County Council of Dorchester County
Legislative Day No. 6
Open Meeting Minutes
May 19, 2026

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on May 19, 2026 at 5:00
p.m., in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland 21613.
A roll call of the members was conducted. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer, Jr.,
President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; and, William V. Nichols. Also present
in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones, County Manager; Keith Benton, Deputy County Manager; Grace
Wittig, Executive Assistant; and, Irene Bames, Executive Operations Manager. Charles
MacLeod, County Attomey, and Ricky C. Travers joined the meeting late.

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously adjourned into Closed Session at 5:01 p.m., pursuant to the following sections of
Title 3 of the General Provisions Article of the Maryland Annotated Code, §3-305(b)(1) to
discuss the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion,
compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of appointees, employees, or
officials over whom it has jurisdiction; §3-305(b)(13) to comply with a specific constitutional,
statutory, or judicially imposed requirement that prevents public disclosures about a particular
proceeding or matter; §3-305(b)(7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice; and, §3-
305(b)(3) to consider the acquisition of real property for a public purpose and matters directly
related thereto. The Written Statement for Closing a Meeting under the Open Meetings Act is
attached hereto.

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) & RESOURCE SHARING AGREEMENT

PUBLIC HEARING ~ BILL NO. 2026-5: FY2026-2027 - ANNUAL BUDGET &
APPROPRIATION ORDINANCE

Ms. Barnes said the third and final public hearing is being conducted on Bill No. 2026-5: FY27
Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance.

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed.

Mr. Jones noted that after the final Budget Work Session, the bill was modified to adjust the final
Board of Education expense, shifting the difference to workers compensation.

The Council adopted Bill No, 2026-5: FY27 Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance via
roll call: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols — aye; and, Travers ~ aye.

ACTION ITEMS:

EVENT REQUEST — TOUCH A TRUCK — CAMBRIDGE-DORCHESTER REGIONAL
AIRPORT FACILITY — DORCHESTER EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES
ADVISORY COUNCIL (DEMSAC)

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Amy Cyr, Vice President, DEMSAC; and, Missy Wilson,
Secretary, DEMSAC, to host a “Touch a Truck” event at the Cambridge-Dorchester Regional
Airport on Saturday, September 12, 2026, from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., to provide a free,
family-friendly opportunity for community engagement while highlighting local EMS, fire, law
enforcement, and other community partners.

The Council acknowledged that Ms. Cyr and Ms. Wilson will provide a certificate of
insurance and coordinate with the public safety teams prior to the event.

REQUEST TO AMEND - STICKER SALES ~ SOLID WASTE,

After a discussion and upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer,
the Council unanimously approved the request of John Cooney, Solid Waste Manager, to amend
the sale of residential trash bag stickers to only be sold at the landfill main office, Finance
Department and online, and return to mailing stickers to past sticker holders, which will enhance
security with the implementation of credit card machines at scales to facilitate the end of cash
sales, however, checks will still be accepted at Golden Hill until a credit card machine is put in
place.

REQUEST TO PROCEED - TAR & CHIP ROADWAYS — PUBLIC WORKS

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Matt Ozman, Public Works Director, to proceed with the
current contractor, American Paving Fabrics, to tar and chip approximately 29 miles of roadway

WORLD'S END TURN DISCUSSION
The Council held a discussion relating to the roadway repair project at World’s End Turn,

After a discussion and upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman
Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to I vote, Councilman Pfeffer opposed, accepted the
recommendation from the State Highway Administration to reallocate state funding from
paving projects in north Dorchester to the Marsh Road, and request the state to reprioritize
the north projects in FY2028.

POLL CONFIRMATIONS

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously confirmed its decision in the interim of meetings, via poll, to submit a Letter of
Intent to the Rural Maryland Prosperity Investment Fund for the Church Creek pump station
rehabilitation project in partnership with the Town of Church Creek, seeking funding for 25% of
the project cost with a required 75% match to be funded with a Federal grant, by a 4 to 0 vote.
