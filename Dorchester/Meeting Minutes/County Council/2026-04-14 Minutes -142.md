County Council of Dorchester County
Legislative Day No. 3
Open Meeting Minutes
April 14, 2026

‘The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on April 14, 2026 at
6:01 p.m, in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland
21613. A roll call of the Members was conducted. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer.
Jr., President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; and, Ricky C. Travers. Also
present in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones, County Manager; Keith Benton, Deputy County
Manager; and, Irene Barnes, Executive Operations Manager. William V. Nichols was absent.
INVOCATION AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Councilman Pfeffer led the invocation and the pledge of allegiance.
CALL FOR ADDITIONS OR DELETIONS TO AGENDA
There were no additions or deletions to the agenda.

FINANCIAL REPORT - GENERAL FUND ~ OPERATING CASH ON HAND.
(ALLOCATED & UNALLOCATED)

Councilman Pfeffer reported total as $ 32,959,893.51.
CONSENT AGENDA

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, approved the Consent Agenda.

* Accounts Payable Disbursement Report ~ Week Ending April 10, 2026
© Councilman Travers abstained from payments made to Simmons Center Market

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, adjourned into Legislative Session.

vote, Councilman Nichols absent, agreed to proceed with the publication of a public hearing.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, adjourned into Open Session.

PUBLIC HEARING — RESOLUTION NO. 688 — ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW ~- COUNTY
CHARTER AMENDMENT

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY,
MARYLAND PURSUANT TO ARTICLE XI-A, SECTION 5 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF
MARYLAND AND PURSUANT TO SECTION 601 OF THE CHARTER OF DORCHESTER
COUNTY, MARYLAND ADOPTING A PROPOSAL TO PLACE A QUESTION ON THE
BALLOT AT THE 2026 GENERAL ELECTION TO AMEND ARTICLE 4, SECTION 409 OF
THE CHARTER OF DORCHESTER COUNTY TO SPECIFY THAT THE REVIEW OF
ADMINISTRATIVE STRUCTURE BE CONDUCTED WITHIN THE FIRST FISCAL YEAR
OF EACH COUNCIL TERM; AUTHORIZING THE COUNTY ATTORNEY TO CERTIFY
THE LOCAL BALLOT QUESTION TO THE STATE BOARD OF ELECTIONS PURSUANT
TO STATE ELECTION LAW; AND PROVIDING THAT THE TITLE OF THIS CHARTER
AMENDMENT RESOLUTION SHALL BE DEEMED A FAIR SUMMARY.

Ms. Barnes said a public hearing is being conducted on Resolution No. 688 to amend Article 4,
Section 409 of the charter of Dorchester County to specify that the review of administrative
structure be conducted within the first fiscal year of each council term.

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council adopted
Resolution No. 688, the roll call was: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer - aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols —
absent; and, Travers — aye.

COUNTY MANAGERS COMMENTS
Mr. Jones: 1) thanked staff for their assistance with preparing the FY27 budget; 2) highlighted
that revenues and expenses relating to the State need to be confirmed; 3) the process of fully
allocating state and federal grant funding was confirmed with the auditors, which has

significantly increased the overall budget: and, 4) there are no changes in the budget relating to
taxes or municipal tax differentials.

COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilman Pfeffer thanked staff for working together to balance the budget, and said the budget
bill will be available on the County website.

meeting at 6:12 p.m.
