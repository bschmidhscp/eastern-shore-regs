### County Council of Dorchester County Open Meeting Minutes December 16, 2025 

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on December 16, 2025 at 5:00 p.m., in Room 110 of<sup>theCountyOfficeBuilding,501CourtLane,Cambridge,Maryland</sup> 21613. A roll call of the members was conducted. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer, Jr., President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; William V. Nichols; and, Ricky C. Travers. Also present in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones, County Manager; Charles MacLeod, County Attorney; and, Irene Barnes, Executive Operations Specialist.

### PUBLIC HEARING 

RESOLUTION NO. 684 —-ESTABLISHING NEW SERVICE AREA - MCKEIL POINT SECTION ONE ~~ WATER & WASTEWATER SERVICES A RESOLUTION, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY OF § 124-2(C) OF THE DORCHESTER COUNTY CODE, ESTABLISHING A NEW SERVICE AREA, TO BE KNOWNAS MCKEIL POINT ~ SECTION ONE AND DEFINING THE LIMITS THEREOF; PROVIDING THAT THE TITLE OF THIS RESOLUTION SHALL BE DEEMED A FAIR SUMMARY AND GENERALLY RELATING TO WATER AND WASTEWATER SERVICES TO BE PROVIDED BY DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND. 

Ms. Barnes said a public hearing is being conducted on Resolution No. 684 to establish a new service area, to be known as McKeil Point Section One, and defining the limits thereof. 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments. 

In response to inquiries posed by Sharon Smith, resident, Pete McElroy, Water & Wastewater Services Director, confirmed the Council’s comments and added that a Memorandum of Understanding was established with the City of Cambridge and there are no concerns at this particular service area. 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for additional public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council unanimously adopted Resolution No. 684; the roll call was: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer— aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols — aye; and, Travers — aye.

### ACTION ITEMS 

BID AWARD RECOMMENDATION ~ LITTER REMOVAL SERVICES ~ SOLID WASTE 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council unanimously approved a recommendation of John Cooney, Solid Waste Manager, to award the bid for the proposal request entitled, Supplying the Dorchester County Solid Waste Litter Removal Services, to the lowest bidder, SVF Groundworks at a daily rate of $600 for two laborers, $800 for three, and $1,000 for four, funded by its FY2026 Operating Budget. 

In response to Council inquiries, Mr. Cooney said he determines the number of laborers needed at the landfill, or transfer stations, based on weather and events, and it is on a will call basis. 

Councilman Pfeffer explained that the County does not source detainees from the Department of Corrections due to the lack of detainees that are allowed to leave the facility to complete this work, but it is always kept in mind. 

#### POLL CONFIRMATIONS 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council unanimously confirmed its decisions in the interim of meetings, via poll, to: 

- e Approve an increase to the L-Gem grant application amount (by $185,414) from $1,189,586 to $1,375,000, which increased the County match (by $9,758) from $62,610 to $72,368, by a 3 to 0 vote 

- e Approve the submission of a $80,000 Resiliency through Restoration Initiative grant application, through the Department of Natural Resources, for the Elliott Island Shoreline Protection Design at Wharf Road project, with no match requirement and a grant award period of 24 months, by a 5 to 0 vote 

FISCAL YEAR (FY) 2027 (JULY 1, 2026-JUNE 30, 2027) BUDGET DISCUSSION 

Mr. Jones asked the Council to begin thinking about and compiling a list of areas of focus ahead ofits FY2027 Goals & Priorities Discussion scheduled on January 6, 2026. 

GRANT SUBMISSION REQUESTS - INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENT AND JOBS ACT (IIJA) —-U.S. DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION (USDOT) - PUBLIC WORKS 

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council unanimously approved a request of Matt Ozman, Public Works Director, to submit the two IJA grant applications, totaling $1,252,595, through the USDOT to fund 100%ofthe engineering costs for bridge maintenance to Hoopers Island and Hip Roof ($686,925) and Shorters Wharf ($565,670).

REQUEST TO PROCEED ~ SOUTH HANGAR LEASE AGREEMENT ~ AIRPORT 

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council unanimously approved a request of Steve Nuwer, Airport Director, to proceed with a Lease Agreement of the South Hangar at the Cambridge-Dorchester Regional Airport with G-Force Avionics, LLC, for a total rent of $229,736 for the initial 10-year term, with a 3% annual rent escalation clause and three 5-year renewal options. 

## COUNTY MANAGER COMMENTS 

There were no comments. 

### COUNCIL COMMENTS 

Councilman Nichols encouraged participation at public County meetings and expressed his appreciation to those who dedicate themselves to the County. 

Councilman Travers explained the importance and benefits to the County of the County Council attending the Maryland Association of Counties (MACo) events, which allows the Council to build new connections and foster collaboration among local and state stakeholders. He congratulated Councilman Pfeffer for being appointed as the MACo First Vice President. 

Councilman Detmer: 1) echoed Councilman Travers’ remarks regarding MACo; 2) commended Hurlock, Cambridge and Vienna on its Christmas parades; 3) commended Dr. Jymil Thompson, Dorchester County Public School Superintendent, for hosting a visit from Linda McMahon, United States Secretary of Education, at Maple Elementary; and, 4) provided an update on the Maryland Blue Crab Solar, LLC development hearings in Linkwood, Maryland. 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Travers, the Council unanimously appointed Michael Knaur to the Agricultural Reconciliation Committee. 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council unanimously agreed to draft a Request for Proposals to determine the cost of<sup>hiringalobbyistto</sup> support Dorchester County at the MACo and the Maryland General Assembly legislative sessions, and to uncover additional grant opportunities. The Council acknowledged that more information is needed to determine what benefit this may be to the County prior to further consideration. 

Councilman Pfeffer: 1) echoed Councilman Detmer on the Maple Elementary School event, adding that he would like to have seen further discussion on the needs of Dorchester County; 2) said he participated in the Hurlock Christmas parade and relayed that the spirit of Christmas was strong; 3) announced that Dorchester County’s 2023 audit is complete and has been posted, and thanked Mr. Jones, Karen Tolley, Finance Director, and the staff that contributed to this accomplishment; and 4) said two new bridges will be built to replace the Chesapeake Bay Bridge at the same approximate location, with final designs to be completed in 2028 and construction to begin in 2032.
