POOL SAFETY BARRIER DISCUSSION

The Council discussed amending §155-50(A)(4)(d) Supplementary Use Regulations of the
County Code requiring “a secure fence at least four feet in height shall enclose any in-ground
swimming pool located within 200 feet of any adjoining property line or public way.”

Jacob Van Schaik, Superior Pools, recommended introducing legislation to amend the Code to
recognize an automatic/powered pool safety cover as an alternative pool safety barrier.

Ms. Webb provided information on a new law passed by the State of Maryland in March 2026,
and recommended replacing “fence” with “barrier” in the County Code for consistency with the
new State law.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council directed
staff to work with Mr. MacLeod to obtain additional information.
