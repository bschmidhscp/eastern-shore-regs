##### County Council of Dorchester County Open Meeting Minutes Legislative Day 11 October 21, 2025 

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on October 21, 2025 at 5:00 p.m., in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland 21613. A roll call of the members was conducted. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer, Jr., President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; and, William V. Nichols. Also present in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones, County Manager; and, Irene Barnes, Executive Operations Specialist. Charles MacLeod, County Attorney, joined by phone. Ricky Travers was absent. 

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, adjourned into Closed Session at 5:01 p.m., pursuant to the following sections of Title 3 of the General Provisions Article of the Maryland Annotated Code, §3-305(b)(1) to discuss the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of appointees, employees, or officials over whom it has jurisdiction; §3-305(b)(13) to comply with a specific constitutional, statutory, or judicially imposed requirement that prevents public disclosures about a particular proceeding or matter; and, §3-305(b)(7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. ###### LEGISLATIVE SESSION 

## INTRODUCTION ~ ELECTRICAL CODE UPDATE 

AN ACT OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND TO AMEND CHAPTER 82 (ELECTRICAL STANDARDS) OF THE DORCHESTER COUNTY CODE BY UPDATING § 82-2 THEREOF TO ADOPT THE 2023 EDITION OF THE NATIONAL ELECTRICAL CODE, AS PUBLISHED BY THE NATIONAL FIRE PROTECTION ASSOCIATION, AND AS UPDATED FROM TIME TO TIME, AS THE MINIMUM STANDARDS FOR ELECTRICAL WORK IN DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND; PROVIDING THAT THE TITLE OF THIS BILL SHALL BE DEEMED A FAIR SUMMARY, AND GENERALLY RELATING TO ELECTRICAL STANDARDS IN DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND 

Ms. Bames said for introduction is legislation to amend Chapter 82 (Electrical Standards) of the Dorchester County Code by updating §82-2 thereof to adopt the 2023 edition of the National Electrical Code, as published by the National Fire Protection Association, and as updated from time to time, as the minimum standards for electrical work in Dorchester County, Maryland. 

Susan Webb, Planning & Zoning Director, said for the County to remain compliant with the State of Maryland a minimumof the 2023 edition must be adopted. 

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, agreed to proceed with the publication of a public hearing. 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, adjourned into Open Session.

##### RECONVENE INTO OPEN SESSION 

##### ACTION ITEMS 

REQUEST TO PIGGYBACK ~SOURCEWELL CONTRACT ~ ROTARY MOWER — AIRPORT 

Upon motion by Councilman Nichols, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, approved the request of Steve Nuwer, Airport Director, to piggyback on a State of Maryland Sourcewell contract with Taylor & Messick, Inc. to purchase a replacement 20’ bat-wing rotary mower for airport grounds maintenance, at the cost of $39,029.64, with 75% ($29,272.00) funded by a MAA Special Grant and the remaining 25% ($9,757.41) funded by the FY2026 Capital Budget line item entitled Rotary Mower. 

SOLE SOURCE REQUEST — 2027 COUNTY LAND PRESERVATION, PARKS & RECREATION PLAN (LPPRP) PREPARATION & SUBMISSION —- RECREATION & PARKS 

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, approved the request of Duc Trieu, Recreation & Parks Director, to sole source with Shore Strategies, LLC for the preparation and submission of the 2027 County LPPRP, at the cost of $41,250.00, funded by FY2026 Program Open Space, with no match requirement. 

The Council acknowledged that the sole source was requested because Shore Strategies, LLC provided these services for the 2022 LPPRP, and it is one of the primary consultants that supports Maryland counties in this process. 

RECRUITMENT & RETENTION PLAN ~ CORRECTIONS 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, agreed to proceed with a recommendation from Jerry Jones, County Manager; Kristin Budd, Human Resources Director and Don Satterfield, Corrections Director, to implement a Recruitment and Retention Plan for Corrections. 

SOLE SOURCE REQUEST — STRATEGIC PLANNING SERVICES 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, approved the request of Mr. Jones to sole source with Integrated Impact Group for Strategic Planning Services for Dorchester County Government to serve as the foundation for long term success, clear goal setting, effective, data-driven decision making and efficient allocation of resources, at the cost of $50,000.00 funded by the Council’s Office FY2026 Operating budget.

MARYLAND ASSOCIATION OF COUNTIES (MACo) LEGISLATIVE COMMITTEE NOMINATIONS

### AUTOMATED EXTERNAL DEFIBRILLATOR (AED) PROPOSAL DISCUSSION 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers directed Mr. Jones to meet with the Dorchester Emergency Medical Services Advisory Committee (DEMSAC) to discuss the vision ofinstalling AED’s at County owned parks and sports complexes. 

The Council acknowledged that Duc Trieu, Recreation & Parks Director, is working on this from a fundraising standpoint. 

GRANT AWARD REQUEST - MARYLAND CENTER FOR SCHOOL SAFETY (MCSS) SHERIFF’S OFFICE 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, approved the request of Marilyn Williams, Grant Writer, on behalf of the Sheriff’s Office, to accept a $91,421.00 School Resource Officer (SRO) Adequate Coverage Grant award through MCSS, to assist with the funding of SRO salaries and overtime, with no match requirement and a performance period of<sup>July1,2025toJune30,2026.</sup>
