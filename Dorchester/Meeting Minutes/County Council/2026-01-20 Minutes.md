### ACTION ITEMS 

# PIGGYBACK REQUEST ~ TAR & CHIP SERVICES — PUBLIC WORKS 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Travers, the Council, by a 3 to 0 vote, Councilman Kramer and Councilman Nichols absent, approved the request of Matt Ozman, Public Works Director, to piggyback on a Caroline County tar and chip contract with American Paving Fabrics, Inc. at $2.29 per square yard, for a three year period, funded by its Operating Budget during the period expended and contingent on future budget approvals. 

COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT EXTENSION REQUEST — U.S. DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE (USDA) — PUBLIC WORKS 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Travers, the Council, by a 3 to 0 vote, Councilman Kramer and Councilman Nichols absent, approved the request of Mr. Ozman to extend a Cooperative Service Agreement with the USDA for beaver management, with an additional expense of $10,327.80, funded by either its FY2026 or FY2027 Operating Budget, depending on the year the invoice is received and contingent on FY2027 budget approvals, as applicable. 

### BOARD OF ZONING APPEALS APPOINTMENT 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council, by a3 to 0 vote, Councilman Kramer and Councilman Nichols absent, reappointed Barbara Knepp to the Board of Zoning Appeals

FY27 CONGRESSIONALLY DIRECTED SPENDING (CDS) PROJECTS DISCUSSION 

The Council discussed water and sewer, the causeway, roads and infrastructure as potential projects to include on a FY27 CDS Projects application, scheduled to open in spring of 2026. 

REQUEST TO ADVERTISE FOR PROPOSALS — COUNTY LOBBYING REPRESENTATION SERVICES 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council, by a3 to 0 vote, Councilman Kramer and Councilman Nichols absent, agreed to advertise a request for proposals for County lobbying representation services, with a submission deadline of February 1, 2026. 

### COUNTY MANAGER COMMENTS 

Mr. Jones said the County is monitoring the weather, and provided a reminder regarding the County community meetings for its Strategic Plan during the week of<sup>January26,2026.</sup> 

### COUNCIL COMMENTS 

Councilman Travers spoke highly of the Ice & Oyster Festival, and congratulated the Chamber of Commerce, X-Lab and all those that were involved in a successful event. 

Councilman Detmer said: 1) echoed Councilman Travers comments regarding the Ice & Oyster Festival, complimenting Bill Christopher for his dedication and leading by example; 2) wished everyone safety and peace during the winter weather: 3) said the strategic planning process has been well received and encouraged participation; and, 4) asked for those expressing their rights not to do so in violent manner, in a threatening way or that interferes with others. 

Councilman Pfeffer said: 1) the general discussion at the Rural Counties Caucus is that one size does not fit all and that the smaller counties do not have the same resources as larger counties; 2) echoed Mr. Jones comments regarding the strategic plan, adding the meeting dates and locations and encouraging participation in discussions relating to the County’s future; and, 3) promoted snow safety and checking in on the elderly and neighbors during the storm. 

Mr. Jones added that the Resident Survey for the strategic planning process went live on the County’s website today. He said that it is not a trap, the County needs residents input, which is welcomed and appreciated. 

### PUBLIC COMMENTS 

There were no public comments.
