The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on October 21, 2025 at

LEGISLATIVE SESSION
INTRODUCTION ~ ELECTRICAL CODE UPDATE

AN ACT OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND TO.
AMEND CHAPTER 82 (ELECTRICAL STANDARDS) OF THE DORCHESTER COUNTY
CODE BY UPDATING § 82-2 THEREOF TO ADOPT THE 2023 EDITION OF THE
NATIONAL ELECTRICAL CODE, AS PUBLISHED BY THE NATIONAL FIRE
PROTECTION ASSOCIATION, AND AS UPDATED FROM TIME TO TIME, AS THE
MINIMUM STANDARDS FOR ELECTRICAL WORK IN DORCHESTER COUNTY,
MARYLAND; PROVIDING THAT THE TITLE OF THIS BILL SHALL BE DEEMED A
FAIR SUMMARY, AND GENERALLY RELATING TO ELECTRICAL STANDARDS IN
DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND.

Ms. Barnes said for introduction is legislation to amend Chapter 82 (Electrical Standards) of the
Dorchester County Code by updating §82-2 thereof to adopt the 2023 edition of the National
Electrical Code, as published by the National Fire Protection Association, and as updated from
time to time, as the minimum standards for electrical work in Dorchester County, Maryland.

Susan Webb, Planning & Zoning Director, said for the County to remain compliant with the
State of Maryland a minimum of the 2023 edition must be adopted.

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council, by a 4 to 0
vote, Councilman Travers absent, agreed to proceed with the publication of a public hearing.

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council, by a 4 to 0
vote, Councilman Travers absent, adjourned into Open Session.

REQUEST TO PIGGYBACK ~ SOURCEWELL CONTRACT ~ ROTARY MOWER —
AIRPORT

Upon motion by Councilman Nichols, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4
to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, approved the request of Steve Nuwer, Airport
Director, to piggyback on a State of Maryland Sourcewell contract with Taylor & Messick,
Inc. to purchase a replacement 20” bat-wing rotary mower for airport grounds maintenance,
at the cost of $39,029.64, with 75% ($29,272.00) funded by a MAA Special Grant and the
remaining 25% (89,757.41) funded by the FY2026 Capital Budget line item entitled Rotary
Mower.

SOLE SOURCE REQUEST — 2027 COUNTY LAND PRESERVATION, PARKS &
RECREATION PLAN (LPPRP) PREPARATION & SUBMISSION - RECREATION &
PARKS

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council, by a 4 to 0
vote, Councilman Travers absent, approved the request of Duc Trieu, Recreation & Parks
Director, to sole source with Shore Strategies, LLC for the preparation and submission of the
2027 County LPPRP, at the cost of $41,250.00, funded by FY2026 Program Open Space, with
no match requirement.

The Council acknowledged that the sole source was requested because Shore Strategies,
LLC provided these services for the 2022 LPPRP, and it is one of the primary consultants
that supports Maryland counties in this process.

RECRUITMENT & RETENTION PLAN ~ CORRECTIONS,

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4
to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, agreed to proceed with a recommendation from Jerry
Jones, County Manager; Kristin Budd, Human Resources Director and Don Satterfield,
Corrections Director, to implement a Recruitment and Retention Plan for Corrections.

SOLE SOURCE REQUEST - STRATEGIC PLANNING SERVICES,

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4
to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, approved the request of Mr. Jones to sole source with
Integrated Impact Group for Strategic Planning Services for Dorchester County Government
to serve as the foundation for long term success, clear goal setting, effective, data-driven
decision making and efficient allocation of resources, at the cost of $50,000.00 funded by
the Council’s Office FY2026 Operating budget.
