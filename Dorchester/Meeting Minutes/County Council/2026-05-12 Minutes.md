# County Council of Dorchester County Legislative Day No. 5 Open Meeting Minutes May 12, 2026 

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on May 12, 2026 at 6:00 p.m., in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland 21613. A roll call of the members was conducted. Present in Room | 10 were: George L. Pfeffer, Jr., President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; and, Ricky C. Travers. Also present in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones, County Manager; Keith Benton, Deputy County Manager; and, Irene Barnes, Executive Operations Manager. William V. Nichols was absent. 

INVOCATION AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 

Councilman Pfeffer led the invocation and the pledge ofallegiance. 

CALL FOR ADDITIONS OR DELETIONS TO AGENDA 

There were no additions or deletions to the agenda. 

FINANCIAL REPORT ~GENERAL FUND ~- OPERATING CASH ON HAND (ALLOCATED & UNALLOCATED) 

Councilman Pfeffer reported the total as $28,363,589.57. 

## CONSENT AGENDA 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, approved the Consent Agenda. 

e Accounts Payable Disbursement Report — Week Ending May 8, 2026 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, adjourned into Legislative Session. 

Noting the advertised time of the Legislative Public Hearing had not passed, and upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, adjourned into Open Session. 

## RECONVENE INTO OPEN SESSION 

# PUBLIC COMMENTS 

Dr. Clifford Coppersmith, Chesapeake College President, thanked the Council for their support and funding of the college’s budget, thanked Karen Smith, Vice President for Administrative Services, Chesapeake College for her services and congratulated her on her retirement.

In response to Councilman Pfeffer, Dr. Coppersmith confirmed that he is aware of the agreement between Dorchester County and Queen Anne’s County (QAC) regarding its share of the funding for the QAC technical building. 

Hannah Collison, Youth Services Coordinator, Dorchester County Public Library (DCPL); Julie Kennedy, Assistant Director, DCPL; Lanett Bagley, Hurlock Branch Manager, DCPL; and, Caitlin McGrellis, Vice President, DCPL Board of Trustees provided an overviewof the services offered to the community and announced the Summer Program theme, “Unearth a Story.” 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Kramer, absent, adjourned into Legislative Session. 

### LEGISLATIVE SESSION 

PUBLIC HEARING~ BILL NO. 2026-5: FY2026-2027 - ANNUAL BUDGET & APPROPRIATION ORDINANCE 

Ms. Barnes said a public hearing is being conducted on Bill No. 2026-5 to provide a second, of three opportunities, for public comments regarding Bill No. 2026-5: FY27 Annual Budget & Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County, for the period of July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027, prior to the Council’s consideration on May 19, 2026 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, adjourned into Open Session. 

## RECONVENE INTO OPEN SESSION 

## COUNTY MANAGER COMMENTS 

Mr. Jones said a Budget Work Session will be held on May 14, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. to review proposed adjustments to the FY27 budget prior to the final public hearing and consideration on May 19, 2026. 

# DEPUTY COUNTY MANAGER COMMENTS 

There were no comments. 

## COUNCIL COMMENTS 

There were no comments. 

## PUBLIC COMMENTS 

There were no comments.
