PUBLIC HEARING ~ BILL NO. 2026-1 - COUNTY INFRACTIONS 

AN ACT OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND TO AMEND CHAPTERS 65 (BUILDING CODE), 82 (ELECTRICAL STANDARDS), 102 (HEATING, VENTILATION, AIR CONDITIONING AND REFRIGERATION), 110 (MINIMUM LIVABILITY CODE), 119 (PLUMBING), AND 134 (STORMWATER MANAGEMENT) OF THE DORCHESTER COUNTY CODE BY PROVIDING THAT VIOLATIONS OF EACH SUCH CHAPTER SHALL BE A COUNTY INFRACTION AND SUBJECT TO A FINE NOT TO EXCEED $5,000; PROVIDING THAT THE TITLE OF THIS BILL SHALL BE DEEMED A FAIR SUMMARY, AND GENERALLY RELATING TO COUNTY INFRACTIONS IN DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND.

<!-- page 4 -->
Dorchester County Council Open Session Minutes of March 17, 2026 Page No. 4 

Ms. Barnes said a public hearing is being conducted on Bill No. 2026-1 to amend Chapters 65 (Building Code), 82 (Electrical Standards), 102 (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration), 110 (Minimum Livability Code), 119 (Plumbing), and 134 (Stormwater Management) of the Dorchester County Code by providing that violations of each such Chapter shall be a County infraction and subject to a fine not to exceed $5,000. 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments. 

In response to Sharon Smith’s, resident, inquiry, Susan Webb, Planning & Zoning Director, explained that the fine was increased to align the County with State standards. She noted that the County is obligated to comply with State law, but not exceed it. Ms. Webb also confirmed that Ms. Smith is correct in understanding that this type of infraction may incur additional fines if it is not addressed. 

Councilman Pfeffer added that Bill 2026-1 serves strictly as a guideline, noting that the County has issued only two maximum fines in the past six years. He emphasized that such fines are used as a last resort. 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council adopted Bill No. 2026-1, the roll call was: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer - aye; Nichols — absent; and, Travers — aye.
