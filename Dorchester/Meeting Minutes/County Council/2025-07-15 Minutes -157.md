PRESENTATIONS
STORM DAMAGE DISCUSSION — PUBLIC WORKS

Matt Ozman, Public Works Director, presented the Council with an overview of recent storm
damage throughout the County, as a result of major rain events. Mr. Ozman provided
information regarding the typical drainage flow in places throughout the County, and reminded
residents to wait at least 48 hours before calling Public Works, because if the flooding clears on
its own, it is not considered an emergency.

EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS DISCUSSION - EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT

Jimmy Windsor, Emergency Management Planner, presented the Council with an overview of
how the County manages emergency alerts, and provided instructions on how to sign-up to
receive alerts:

ACTION ITEMS
REQUEST TO PROCEED — PROPOSAL TO REHABILITATE RUNWAY 16-34 — AIRPORT

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Steve Nuwer, Airport Director, to provide AECOM
Technical Services, Inc. with a notice to proceed with the Cambridge-Dorchester Regional
Airport Runway 16/34 Topographical Survey project, at total cost of $98,350.00, which will be
primarily funded by two Federal Aviation Administration Grants totaling $90,932.00, which is
92.5% of the cost, and the remaining 7.5% of the cost, totaling $7,418.00 will be funded by the
FY25 Capital Budget line item CGE RW 16-34 Pavement Rehab.

SOLE SOURCE ~ GRINDER PUMP PURCHASE — WATER & WASTEWATER,

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Pete McElroy, Water & Wastewater Director, to sole
source the purchase of ten (10) E-One brand “Extreme” model grinder pumps from Freemire &
Associates, Inc. at a total cost of $35,510.00, which will be funded by the FY26 Capital Budget
line item 13-1240 Purchase (10) New E-One Grinder Pump Units.

‘The Council acknowledged that the sole source is being requested because: 1) the department is
working towards standardizing grinder pumps; 2) this company is currently the only authorized
representative of E-One Sewer Systems Products in the surrounding areas; 3) the company
provides extended services well beyond others; and, 4) the company has administered the County
in the past.

REQUEST TO PROCEED ~ PROPOSAL FOR EVENT PRODUCTION ~ TOURISM

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Holly Gilpin, Tourism Director, to proceed with the lowest
bidder, Fan2Sce Production, LLC, at a total cost of $107,155.98, which will be funded by a
FY25 Economic Development Administration (EDA) Grant, for the purchase of products,
services, and support including, but not limited to:

Audio/visual equipment rentals and purchases
Event production and coordination

Artist booking agency services

Insurance coverage including general liability and accommodation-related insurance
Sale and management of event merchandise

‘The Council acknowledged that no other vendor in the region currently offers this combination
of services tailored to Dorchester County’s specific event needs in a single, efficient package,
and a request for proposals was not completed due to time constraints of the EDA grant award
period of July 1, 2025 to September 30, 2025.

In response to Councilman Pfeffer’s inquiry, Ms. Gilpin: 1) confirmed the County will purchase
the stage and sound equipment to use for County events, and rental to non-profits and small
businesses in the area, promoting tourism and economic growth within the County; 2) said
Certificates of Insurance will be required from any business looking to use the equipment; 3) the
business will be responsible for hiring a company to transport, set up, break down, and return the
‘equipment to the County; and, 4) the equipment will be stored at the Tourism and Economic
Development departments.

In response to Councilman Detmer’s request, Ms. Gilpin said she will establish policies and
procedures for use of the equipment.

FY26 CORE PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES FUNDING AGREEMENT ~ HEALTH
DEPARTMENT

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Dr. Casey Scott, Health Officer, Dorchester County Health
Department, to execute the FY26 Core Public Health Services Funding Agreement between the
County and the Maryland Department of Health, with a local match of $858,914.00, funded by
the FY26 Operating Budget to provide local health services at the Health Department.

The Council acknowledged that the original match requirement was $1,189,226.00, however,
through working with Dr. Scott and the State, the match requirement was reduced by
$330,312.00, with the County's commitment to meet its full CORE funding by FY2030.
