#### County Council of Dorchester County Open Meeting Minutes July 15, 2025 

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on July 15, 2025 at 4:33 p.m., in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland 21613. ##### PRESENTATIONS 

STORM DAMAGE DISCUSSION — PUBLIC WORKS 

Matt Ozman, Public Works Director, presented the Council with an overview of recent storm damage throughout the County, as a result of major rain events. Mr. Ozman provided information regarding the typical drainage flow in places throughout the County, and reminded residents to wait at least 48 hours before calling Public Works, because if the flooding clears on its own, it is not considered an emergency.

#### ACTION ITEMS 

REQUEST TO PROCEED — PROPOSAL TO REHABILITATE RUNWAY 16-34 — AIRPORT 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council unanimously approved the request of Steve Nuwer, Airport Director, to provide AECOM Technical Services, Inc. with a notice to proceed with the Cambridge-Dorchester Regional Airport Runway 16/34 Topographical Survey project, at total cost of $98,350.00, which will be primarily funded by two Federal Aviation Administration Grants totaling $90,932.00, which is 92.5%ofthe cost, and the remaining 7.5% of the cost, totaling $7,418.00 will be funded by the FY25 Capital Budget line item CGE RW 16-34 Pavement Rehab. 

SOLE SOURCE ~ GRINDER PUMP PURCHASE — WATER & WASTEWATER 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council unanimously approved the request of Pete McElroy, Water & Wastewater Director, to sole source the purchase of ten (10) E-One brand “Extreme” model grinder pumps from Freemire & Associates, Inc. at a total cost of $35,510.00, which will be funded by the FY26 Capital Budget line item 13-1240 Purchase (10) New E-One Grinder Pump Units. 

The Council acknowledged that the sole source is being requested because: 1) the department is working towards standardizing grinder pumps; 2) this company is currently the only authorized representative of E-One Sewer Systems Products in the surrounding areas; 3) the company provides extended services well beyond others; and, 4) the company has administered the County in the past.

## REQUEST TO PROCEED ~ PROPOSAL FOR EVENT PRODUCTION ~ TOURISM 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council unanimously approved the request of Holly Gilpin, Tourism Director, to proceed with the lowest bidder, Fan2See Production, LLC, at a total cost of $107,155.98, which will be funded by a FY25 Economic Development Administration (EDA) Grant, for the purchase of products, services, and support including, but not limited to: 

- e Audio/visual equipment rentals and purchases 

- e Event production and coordination 

- e Artist booking agency services 

- e Insurance coverage including general liability and accommodation-related insurance e Sale and management of event merchandise 

The Council acknowledged that no other vendor in the region currently offers this combination of services tailored to Dorchester County’s specific event needs in a single, efficient package, and a request for proposals was not completed due to time constraints of the EDA grant award period of<sup>July1,2025toSeptember30,2025.</sup> 

In response to Councilman Pfeffer’s inquiry, Ms. Gilpin: 1) confirmed the County will purchase the stage and sound equipment to use for County events, and rental to non-profits and small businesses in the area, promoting tourism and economic growth within the County; 2) said Certificates of Insurance will be required from any business looking to use the equipment; 3) the business will be responsible for hiring a company to transport, set up, break down, and return the equipment to the County; and, 4) the equipment will be stored at the Tourism and Economic Development departments. 

In response to Councilman Detmer’s request, Ms. Gilpin said she will establish policies and procedures for use of the equipment.
