County Council of Dorchester County
Open Meeting Minutes
December 2, 2025

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on December 2, 2025 at
5:00 p.m., in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland
21613. A roll call of the members was conducted. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer.
Jr., President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; and, Ricky C. Travers. Also
present in Room 110 was: Irene Barnes, Executive Operations Specialist. William V. Nichols
absent. Jerry Jones, County Manager; and, Charles MacLeod, County Attorney, joined the
meeting late.

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Travers, the Council, by a 4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, adjourned into Closed Session at 5:01 p.m., pursuant to the
following sections of Title 3 of the General Provisions Article of the Maryland Annotated Code,
§3-305(b)(1) to discuss the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline,
demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of appointees,
employees, or officials over whom it has jurisdiction; §3-305(b)(13) to comply with a specific
constitutional, statutory, or judicially imposed requirement that prevents public disclosures about
a particular proceeding or matter; §3-305(b)(7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice;
and, §3-305(b)(4) to consider a matter that concerns the proposal for a business or industrial
organization to locate, expand, or remain in the State. The Written Statement for Closing a
Meeting under the Open Meetings Act is attached hereto,

OPEN SESSION

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on December 2, 2025 at
6:21 p.m,, in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland
21613. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer, Jr., President; Mike Detmer, Vice
President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; and, Ricky C. Travers. Also present in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones,
County Manager; Charles MacLeod, County Attorney; and, Irene Barnes, Executive Operations
Specialist. William V. Nichols absent.

Councilman Pfeffer led the invocation and the pledge of allegiance.
CALL FOR ADDITIONS OR DELETIONS TO AGENDA.

1) Request to Proceed ~ Emergency Roof Repairs ~ Public Safety Building ~ Corrections

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, approved the amended agenda.

ACTION ITEMS

FY2027 RURAL LEGACY PROGRAM APPLICATIONS ~- MARYLAND
DEPARTMENT OF NATURAL RESOURCES (DNR) ~ PLANNING & ZONING

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, approved the recommendation of Susan Webb, Planning &
Zoning Director. to submit FY2027 Rural Legacy Program applications to the DNR with the
following order of priority:

1) Nanticoke Rural Legacy Area
2) Harriet Tubman Rural Legacy Area
3) Agricultural Security Corridor — Eastern Shore Heartland Rural Legacy Area

‘The Council acknowledged that these areas are rotated annually to ensure each receives an
equal opportunity.

REAPPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS ~ ADMINISTRATIVE CHARGING COMMITTEE
(ACC)

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Travers, the Council, by a4
to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, reappointed Cherry Moaney to serve a second term of
two years on the ACC.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4
to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, reappointed Vaughn Evans to serve a third and final
term of two years on the ACC.

REQUEST TO PROCEED — REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS — COUNCILS CHAMBERS
EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4
to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, approved the recommendation to proceed with the
Request for Proposals process to determine the cost of replacing the Council’s Chambers
meeting room audio and visual equipment.

DELMARVA WATER TRANSPORT COMMITTEE, INC. PARTICIPATION
DISCUSSION

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Travers, the Council, by a 4
to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, agreed to renew its membership with Delmarva
Water Transport Committee, Inc.

REQUEST TO PROCEED ~- EMERGENCY ROOF REPAIRS ~ PUBLIC SAFETY
BUILDING — CORRECTIONS

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4
to 0 vote, Councilman Nichols absent, approved the request of Don Satterfield, Corrections
Director, to proceed with the lowest estimate received by AROCON Home Improvement at
$69,999.00, to complete emergency roof repairs at the public safety building above the
administrative area and Sheriff's Office funded by the Contingency Fund.

PRESENTATION
CONOWINGO DAM — MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF THE ENVIRONMENT

‘Adam Ortiz, Deputy Secretary, Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE), and Heather
Nelson, Wetlands & Waterways Protection Program Manager, MDE, provided the
presentation to the Council regarding the Conowingo Dam.

A discussion was held between the Council, MDE and Mr. MacLeod.
COUNTY MANAGER COMMENTS

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, agreed to close County offices on December 26, 2025 in
recognition of employees” hard work and dedication.

COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilman Detmer: 1) said the Blue Crab Solar event is being held on December 11, 2025 at
10:00 a.m.; 2) said the court ruling prohibiting private property owners from hunting on their
land while surveying is being completed for an energy transmission project is reprehensible; 3)
congratulated and commended Rescue Fire Company for its 91*'year of holding the Train
Garden; and, 4) paid tribute to the passing of Katie Holbrook Cole, President of Dorchester
Educators.

Councilman Pfeffer: 1) provided his condolences to the family of Katie Holbrook Cole; and, 2)
wished the best to the new and prior leaders of the Town of Hurlock.

PUBLIC COMMENTS

Donald Simon, resident, expressed concerns on who will bear the burden of funding the
Conowingo Dam dredging, and juvenile crime within the school district.

Mr. MacLeod made an additional comment regarding the Conowingo Dam relating to dredging
materials used to rebuild James Island.
