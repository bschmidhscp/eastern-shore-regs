County Couneil of Dorchester County
Open Meeting Minutes
November 4, 2025

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on November 4, 2025 at
5:02 p.m., in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland
21613. A roll call of the members was conducted. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer,
Jr,, President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; William V. Nichols; and, Ricky C.
Travers. Also present in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones, County Manager; Charles MacLeod,
County Attorney; and, Irene Bares, Executive Operations Specialist.

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously adjoumed into Closed Session at 5:03 p.m., pursuant to the following sections of
Title 3 of the General Provisions Article of the Maryland Annotated Code, §3-305(b)(1) to
discuss the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion,
compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of appointees, employees, or
officials over whom it has jurisdiction; §3-305(b)(13) to comply with a specific constitutional,
statutory, or judicially imposed requirement that prevents public disclosures about a particular
proceeding or matter; and, §3-305(b)(7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. The
Written Statement for Closing a Meeting under the Open Meetings Act is attached hereto.

PROCLAMATIONS

OPERATION GREEN LIGHT FOR VETERANS ~ NOVEMBER 4-11, 2025

The Council presented a Proclamation declaring November 4" through 1" as Operation Green
Light for Veterans,

PRESENTATIONS,
UNIVERSITY OF MARYLAND SHORE REGIONAL HEALTH (UMSRH)

Ken Kozel, President, UMSRH; and, Lara Wilson, Director, External Affairs & Community
Liaison, UMSRH, provided the attached presentation to the Council.

Mr. Kozel and Ms. Wilson answered several questions posed by the Council following the
presentation.

PUBLIC HEARING ~ DISPOSITION OF COUNTY PROPERTY - 3 CEDAR STREET,
CAMBRIDGE, MARYLAND

Ms. Barnes said a public hearing is being conducted on the proposed disposition of the County
owned property located at 3 Cedar Street, Cambridge, Maryland, 21613 for a purchase price of
$1.2 million to Twenty-Nine Corporation.

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously agreed to proceed with the sale. The roll call was: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer ~ aye;
Kramer — aye; Nichols ~ aye; and, Travers — aye.

Mr. MacLeod said, as notice, the purchaser is the current tenant of the property, under the lease,
there is a clause for a first right of refusal.

ACTION ITEMS
BID AWARD REQUEST ~ DOG CONTROL BOILER ~ PUBLIC WORKS

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Matt Ozman, Public Works Director, to award the bid for
the proposal request entitled, Dorchester County Dog Control Boiler Replacement RFP, to the
only vendor that submitted a completed bid package, Modern Controls, at the cost of $26,300.00,
funded by the Dog Control FY2026 Operating Budget.

REQUEST TO ADVERTISE FOR PROPOSALS ~ LITTER REMOVAL SERVICES —
SOLID WASTE

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer seconded by Councilman Kramer the Council unanimously
approved the request of John Cooney, Solid Waste Manager, to advertise a request for proposals
entitled, Supplying to the Dorchester County Solid Waste Division Litter Removals Services, to
provide litter pickup services at the landfill for a 30 month period on an as needed basis, funded
by the Solid Waste FY2026 Operating Budget.

BOARD APPOINTMENT ~ RECREATION & PARKS ADVISORY BOARD

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council
unanimously reappointed Jerome Stanley to the Recreation & Parks Advisory Board.

BOARD APPOINTMENT — BOARD OF ZONING APPEALS

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council
unanimously appointed Erick Windsor to fill the Board of Zoning Appeals District 5 expiring
position, currently held by Blaine Williamson.

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council
unanimously appointed James Crist to fill the unexpired term of the District 4 seat on the Board
of Zoning Appeals due to the resignation of Scott Wienhold.

RECRUITMENT & RETENTION PLAN - EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES (EMS)

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council
unanimously agreed to proceed with a recommendation from Jerry Jones, County Manager;
Kristin Budd, Human Resources Director; and, Debbie Wheedleton, EMS Chief, to implement a
Recruitment and Retention Plan for EMS.

POLL CONFIRMATION

Upon motion by Councilman Nichols, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously confirmed its decision in the interim of meetings, via poll, to provide a letter of
support for a $3.5 million FY2027 Governor’s Capital Budget Request application submitted by
the City of Cambridge, in partnership with the Maryland Economic Development Corporation
and the Residences at River Marsh Homeowners Association, for bridge improvements within
the Cambridge, Maryland, by a 5-0 vote.

COUNTY MANAGER COMMENTS,

No comments.
