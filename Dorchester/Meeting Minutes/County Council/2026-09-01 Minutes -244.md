PUBLIC HEARING ~ BILL NO. 2026-10 - ZONING AMENDMENT - TABLE OF
PERMITTED USES BY ZONING DISTRICT - UNCLASSIFIED

Ms. Barnes said a public hearing is being conducted to provide an opportunity for public
comments, prior to consideration of Bill No. 2026-10, to amend Chapter 155, Zoning,

Attachment 1, Table of Permitted Uses by Zoning District, to repeal the Unclassified category,
which provided that non-commercial personal storage uses are permitted in B-1 Neighborhood
Business, B-2 General Business, I-1 Light Industrial, and I-2 Heavy Industrial zoning districts.

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments,

In response to David Beverley, resident, Susan Webb, Planning & Zoning Director, said current
properties that do not meet the new regulation will be noted as legal-nonconforming, and will
only affect new commercial or rezoned properties.

Councilman Pfeffer asked for additional public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was
closed.

In response to Councilman Pfeffer, Ms. Webb provided an overview of the purpose of the bill.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council adopted
Bill No. 2026-10, the roll call was: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer— aye; Kramer — nay; Nichols — absent;
and, Travers ~ aye.

§10-2-Compensation, of the Dorchester County Code, to increase the annual salaries of the
members of the County Council (from $16,000 to $21,000) and the President of the County

RESOLUTIONS
PROPOSED RESOLUTIONS — POLICY AMENDMENTS

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, approved the request to post the following resolutions within
County departments for 10-calendar days to obtain employee comments prior to consideration
the policy amendments at its September 15, 2026 meeting:

1, Inclement Weather/Emergency Closure: To establish clear and consistent procedures
for the operation of Dorchester County government offices during periods of
inclement weather or emergency situations

2. Vehicle Use: To define the purposes for which County vehicles may be used and
under what circumstances private vehicles may be used for County business

3. Thendara Camp & Range: To provide a concise, County-compliant policy to govern
use of the Thendara facility for 4-H archery, shooting, and related recreational
activities, including approved rental use

4. Public Information Act: To comply with the Maryland Public Information Act
(MPIA) by providing the public with access to records maintained by Dorchester
County in the transaction of public business

5. Progressive Disciplinary Action: To address deficient employee conduct or
performance, including violations of County rules, laws, policies, or procedures, and
to encourage compliance with the County's expectations
