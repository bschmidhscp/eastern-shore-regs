# County Council of Dorchester County Open Meeting Minutes January 26, 2026 

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on January 26, 2026 at 4:01 p.m., in Room 110 of the County Office Building, 501 Court Lane, Cambridge, Maryland 21613. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer, Jr., President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; William V. Nichols; and, Ricky C. Travers. Also present in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones, County Manager; and, Irene Barnes, Executive Operations Manager. 

INVOCATION AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 

Councilman Pfeffer led the invocation and the pledge of allegiance. 

CALL FOR ADDITIONS OR DELETIONS TO AGENDA 

There were no additions or deletions to the agenda. 

## ACTION ITEMS 

ENTERPRISE FLEET MANAGEMENT — SHERIFF’S OFFICE 

The following provided information to the Council regarding the Sheriff's Office fleet management plan: 

- e Jerry Jones, County Manager e Karen Tolley, Finance Director 

- « James W. Phillips, Jr., Sheriff, Dorchester County Sheriff's Office (DCSO) 

- e John Stichberry, Major, DCSO 

- e Lee Hagadorm, Lieutenant, DCSO 

- ¢ Bailey Elliott, Client Strategy Manager, Enterprise Fleet Management 

The Council recognized the urgency of finalizing this plan prior to the next County Council meeting is due to the diminishing availability of police pursuit vehicles (PPV) under the current lease program. 

Mr. Jones emphasized that the salaries within the DCSO and the replacement of PPVs do not have a direct correlation, therefore, if the Council only approved half of the requested vehicles, it does not mean that the DCSO salaries will be increased. He said the DCSO is not the only department within Dorchester County that needs a salary review, which will be completed during the budget process. 

Mr. Jones explained that if the County does not accept a vehicle that it has committed to, the County may request Enterprise to rehome the PPV to another interested agency, however the County will be responsible for all costs incurred by Enterprise to do so. 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Travers, the Council, by a 4 to 1 vote, Councilman Nichols opposed, approved the request of Sheriff Phillips, to finalize a fleet leasing plan to replace 27 vehicles through its current agreement with Enterprise Fleet
