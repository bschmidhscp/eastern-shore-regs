#### County Council of Dorchester County Open Meeting Minutes September 15, 2026 

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on September 15, 2026 at 5:01 p.m., in Room 110 of<sup>the CountyOfficeBuilding,501CourtLane,Cambridge,Maryland</sup> 21613. PUBLIC HEARING ~ DISTRIBUTION PLAN —- COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT & REPAIR FUND (CRRF) COMMITTEE 

Ms. Wittig said a public hearing is being conducted to provide an opportunity for public comment prior to consideration of the proposed CRRF Distribution Plan. 

Gregory Meekins, Dorchester County Temporary Shelter Board, expressed appreciation for the County’s contributions, support, and for the committee which was established to address these matters. Mr. Meekins emphasized the importance of assistance for individuals experiencing homelessness and noted that, in some circumstances, it is through no fault of theirown. He indicated that the organization may request additional support in the future. 

Councilman Pfeffer explained that the CRRF refers to the distribution of cannabis tax revenues, which the State of Maryland imposes a 9% tax on sales, which 5% of the revenues collected are distributed to county governments throughout the State.

<!-- page 4 -->
Dorchester County Council Open Session Minutes of September 15, 2026 Page No. 4 

Marilyn Williams, Grant Writer, highlighted a public comment, noting that recent legislation requires the committee to review relevant data to assess community needs and identify the highest-priority areas for consideration. She suggested a modification to include data related to homelessness, substance use, and mental health when evaluating community needs and assigning additional consideration to identified priority areas. 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments. 

Sharon Smith, resident, inquired regarding the amount of funding available for distribution. 

Ms. Williams stated that the CRRF Committee will review the available funds at its September 26, 2026 meeting to determine the amount that may be released, with recommendations to be presented to the Council at its October 6, 2026 meeting. 

Ms. Smith asked whether the process was being conducted for the first time. Ms. Williams clarified that a similar process was conducted the previous year and noted that several years of funding had been accumulated, totaling $819,379. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council approved the CRRF Distribution Plan, with amendments, the roll call was: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols — absent; and, Travers — aye. 

PUBLIC HEARING ~ SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PLAN REVISION 

Ms. Wittig said following a short presentation on the proposed Solid Waste Management Plan revision by Sean O’Donnell, Geosyntec Consultant, a public hearing will be conducted on Resolution 718 to provide an opportunity for public comments prior to consideration. 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments. 

John Hansen, resident, asked whether the proposed plan addresses the potential for contaminants to enter the water supply. 

Mr. O’Donnell stated that groundwater and gas migration are monitored ona daily basis. 

Sharon Smith, resident, asked whether private recycling companies are subject to audits or other oversight to ensure that recyclable materials are appropriately processed and utilized. 

Mr. O’Donnell stated that he was uncertain regarding the extent of State-level oversight but noted that recycling practices have improved nationally. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council adopted Resolution No. 718, the roll call was: Pfeffer - aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols absent; and, Travers — aye.

#### RESOLUTIONS 

The Council acknowledged that Human Resources confirmed that the resolutions were posted within all County departments for 10 days, and all comments were provided for its review.
