MACo conference which established and strengthened connections that will support the future of
the County, including data centers; 2) announced that Councilman Pfeffer is the upcoming
MACo President; and, 3) thanked the Fraternal Order of Police for endorsing his re-election

campaign.

County; and, 4) change is needed regarding juvenile crime.

Management meeting will also be held on August 25, 2026 at the East New Market Volunteer
