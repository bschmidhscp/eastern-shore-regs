### County Council of Dorchester County Open Meeting Minutes November 18, 2025 

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on November 18, 2025 at 4:30 p.m., in Room 110 of<sup>the CountyOfficeBuilding,501CourtLane,Cambridge,Maryland</sup> 21613. A roll call of the members was conducted. Present in Room 110 were: George L. Pfeffer, Jr., President; Mike Detmer, Vice President; Rob Kramer, Jr.; and, William V. Nichols. Also present in Room 110 were: Jerry Jones, County Manager; and, Irene Barnes, Executive Operations Specialist. Charles MacLeod, County Attorney; and, Ricky C. Travers joined the meeting late. 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0 vote, Councilman Travers absent, adjourned into Closed Session at 4:31 p.m., pursuant to the following sections of Title 3 of the General Provisions Article of the Maryland Annotated Code, §3-305(b)(1) to discuss the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of appointees, employees, or officials over whom it has jurisdiction; §3-305(b)(13) to comply with a specific constitutional, statutory, or judicially imposed requirement that prevents public disclosures about a particular proceeding or matter; and, §3-305(b)(7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. #### LEGISLATIVE SESSION 

PUBLIC HEARING ~ BILL NO. 2025-8 - ELECTRICAL CODE UPDATE 

AN ACT OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND TO AMEND CHAPTER 82 (ELECTRICAL STANDARDS) OF THE DORCHESTER COUNTY CODE BY UPDATING § 82-2 THEREOF TO ADOPT THE 2023 EDITION OF THE NATIONAL ELECTRICAL CODE, AS PUBLISHED BY THE NATIONAL FIRE PROTECTION ASSOCIATION, AND AS UPDATED FROM TIME TO TIME, AS THE MINIMUM STANDARDS FOR ELECTRICAL WORK IN DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND; PROVIDING THAT THE TITLE OF THIS BILL SHALL BE DEEMED A FAIR SUMMARY, AND GENERALLY RELATING TO ELECTRICAL STANDARDS IN DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND. 

Ms. Barnes said a public hearing is being conducted on Bill No. 2025-8 to amend Chapter 82 (Electrical Standards) of the Dorchester County Code by updating § 82-2 thereof to adopt the 2023 edition of the National Electrical Code, as published by the National Fire Protection Association, and as updated from time to time, as the minimum standards for electrical work in Dorchester County, Maryland. 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council unanimously adopted Bill 2025-8, the roll call was: Pfeffer —- aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols — aye; and, Travers — aye. 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council unanimously adjourned into Open Session.

### RECONVENE INTO OPEN SESSION 

PUBLIC HEARING ~ LEASE RENEWAL REQUEST ~ BOYS & GIRLS CLUB RECREATION & PARKS 

Ms. Barnes said a public hearing is being conducted to consider a request of Duc Trieu, Recreation & Parks Director, on behalfof the Boys & Girls Club of Metropolitan Baltimore, Inc. to renew its lease of 515 Leonards Lane, Cambridge, Maryland for an additional two year term, through August 31, 2027, with no monthly rent ($0.00) and $30,000.00 in funding for capital improvements, including two extension options of two-years, with a clause relating to negotiations of annual rent/capital improvements was added to the agreement. 

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Nichols, the Council unanimously approved the lease, the roll call was: Pfeffer - aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols — aye; and, Travers — aye.
