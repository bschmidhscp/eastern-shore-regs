LEGISLATIVE SESSION
PUBLIC HEARING ~ BILL NO. 2026-6 —- CRITICAL AREA MAP

Ms. Bares said a public hearing is being conducted to provide an opportunity for public
comments, prior to consideration of Bill No. 2026-6 to amend the Dorchester County Critical
‘Area Program and its associated Official Zoning Maps, as required by the State of Maryland
Critical Area Commission every 10-years to maintain compliance with current laws and
regulations.

Councilman Pfeffer asked for public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was closed.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council adopted
Bill No. 2026-6, the roll call was: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer ~ aye; Kramer ~ aye; Nichols ~ absent;
and, Travers — aye.

PUBLIC HEARING ~ BILL NO. 2026-7 ~ CRITICAL AREA DEFINITIONS

Ms. Bames said a public hearing is being conducted to provide an opportunity for public
comments prior to consideration of Bill No. 2026-7 to amend Chapter 68 (Chesapeake Bay
Critical Area) of the Dorchester County Code by amending the definition of “Critical Area” for
consistency with Maryland Code Annotated, Natural Resources §8-1807, which the current
definition is based on 1972 wetland maps.

Karen Tolley, Church Creek Mayor, said the amendments were adopted by Church Creek.

Councilman Pfeffer asked for additional public comments, hearing none, the public hearing was
closed.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council adopted
Bill No. 2026-7, the roll call was: Pfeffer ~ aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols ~ absent,
and, Travers ~ aye.

was posted within all County departments for 10 calendar days, and no employee comments
were received.

vote, Councilman Nichols absent, adopted Resolution No. 703

COUNTY COUNCIL COMPENSATION DISCUSSION

After a discussion, and upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Travers,
the Council, by a 3 to 0 vote, Councilman Pfeffer abstained and Councilman Nichols absent,
approved the recommendation of the Council’s Office to draft legislation to amend the Couneil’s
salary from $16,000 to $21,000 for members, and $17,000 to $22,000 for the President, with
remaining compensation unchanged.

The Council acknowledged the proposed introduction date of August 4, 2026, and proposed
public hearing date of September I, 2026.

POLL CONFIRMATIONS

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a 4 to 0
vote, Councilman Nichols absent, confirmed its decision in the interim of meetings, via poll, to:

1. Approved a request of the Richardson Maritime Museum, on the evening of July 7, 2026,
to provide a letter of support for its From Hidden Collections to Public Discovery: Phase
II Mini Grant application, through the Maryland Heritage Areas Authority,

GRANT SUBMISSION REQUEST ~ 2027 EDWARD J. BYRNE MEMORIAL JUSTICE
ASSISTANCE (BJAG) — SHERIFF’S OFFICE ~ FINANCE

After discussion, and upon motion by Councilman Travers seconded by Councilman Kramer the

EXPANSION REQUEST ~ THIRD AMENDMENT TO AGREEMENT ~ SANITARY
DISTRICT NO. 4

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council, by a4 to 0

Development officials copied.

COUNTY MANAGER COMMENTS

Mr. Jones said the Council’s Office completed a draft public pack and is working towards
standardization of requests, and Directors will begin presenting departmental updates at Council
meetings to increase public awareness.

Mr. Ozman provided a presentation regarding the status of work orders within Public Works.
Mr, Jones highlighted a meeting with Mr. Ozman, Councilman Kramer and residents of Elliott
Island to discuss infrastructure concerns. He said repairs will be a long-term project, which
Councilman Kramer has presented insightful ways to address the matters, and additional
meetings are scheduled to continue working together.

DEPUTY COUNTY MANAGER COMMENTS

Mr. Benton was absent.

COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilman Detmer said: 1) he supports the public Council meeting pack, and director
presentations, then commended Mr. Ozman for the level of performance to reduce work orders;
3) he has received constituent concerns regarding mosquitos; and, 4) the Brookview Bridge

weight restriction has been lifted, however commercial vehicles are limited to 25 mph when
crossing.

After a discussion regarding data centers, Mr. Jones said he will review surrounding county
processes in implementing a moratorium, determine if a task force is needed and work with staff
to provide additional information by the August 4, 2026 meeting.

PUBLIC COMMENTS

Tawes Harper, Jr., resident, spoke in opposition of increased housing developments.

Don Satterfield, resident, spoke in favor of increasing the Council's salary.

David Bevereley, resident, spoke in favor of a implementing a data center moratorium.
