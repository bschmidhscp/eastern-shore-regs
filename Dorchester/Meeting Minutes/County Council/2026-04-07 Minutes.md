### County Council of Dorchester County Open Meeting Minutes April 7, 2026 

The County Council of Dorchester County convened into Open Session on April 7, 2026 at 5:00 p.m., in Room 110 of<sup>theCountyOfficeBuilding,501CourtLane,Cambridge.Maryland21613.</sup> A roll call of the members was conducted. RESOLUTION — TRANSFER OF CORRECTIONAL OFFICERS TO THE CORRECTIONAL OFFICER'S PENSION SYSTEM (CORS) 

Ms. Wittig said for consideration is a request to post a proposed resolution to become a participating governmental unit in CORS, which is operated and maintained by the Board of<sup>TrusteesfortheMarylandStateRetirementandPensionSystemforeligible</sup> Correctional personnel throughout the State of Maryland, and withdraw eligible Officers from the EPS and transfer them, effective July 1, 2026, to the CORS. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council unanimously approved the request to post the resolution within all County departments for 10 days prior for further consideration at a future meeting. 

RESOLUTION — TRANSFER OF ELIGIBLE EMERGENCY SERVICES PERSONNEL INTO THE LAW ENFORCEMENT EMPLOYEES’ PENSION SYSTEM (LEOPS) 

Ms. Wittig said for consideration is a request to post a proposed resolution to withdraw eligible emergency services personnel from the Employees’ Pension System (EPS) and transfer them to the LEOPS, operated and maintained by the Board of Trustees for the Maryland State Retirement and Pension System, effective July 1, 2026. 

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council unanimously approved the request to post the resolution within all County departments for 10 days prior for further consideration at a future meeting. 

RESOLUTION — PICKUP - MANDATORY EMPLOYEE CONTRIBUTIONS — CORS - LEOPS 

Ms. Wittig said for consideration is a request to post a proposed resolution to affirm that the County will pickup contributions to CORS, LEOPS and EPS, as required by the state. 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer the Council unanimously approved the request to post the resolution within all County departments for 10 days prior for further consideration at a future meeting. 

RESOLUTION NO. 690 - REMOVAL OF OVERTIME THRESHOLD - SHERIFF’S OFFICE 

Ms. Wittig said for consideration is Resolution No. 690 to amend the Dorchester County Personnel Rules & Regulations to remove law enforcement personnel within the Sheriff's Office from the hours in excess of 171 in a 28-day work cycle.
