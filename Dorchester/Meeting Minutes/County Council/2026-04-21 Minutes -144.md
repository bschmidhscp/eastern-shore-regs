RESOLUTIONS

RESOLUTION NO. 691 ~ TRANSFER OF ELIGIBLE EMERGENCY SERVICES
PERSONNEL INTO THE LAW ENFORCEMENT EMPLOYEE’S PENSION SYSTEM
(LEOPS)

Ms. Barnes said for consideration is Resolution No. 691 to withdraw eligible emergency services
personnel from the Employees’ Pension System (EPS) and transfer them to LEOPS, operated
and maintained by the Board of Trustees for the Maryland State Retirement and Pension System,
effective July 1, 2026.

The Council acknowledged that the Human Resources Department confirmed that the resolution
was posted within all County department for 10 days and no comments were received

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council adopted
Resolution No. 691, the roll call was: Pfeffer ~ aye; Detmer ~ aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols ~ aye;
and, Travers ~ aye.

RESOLUTION NO. 692 — TRANSFER OF CORRECTIONAL OFFICERS TO THE
CORRECTIONAL OFFICER’S PENSION SYSTEM (CORS)

Ms. Bames said for consideration is Resolution No. 692 to become a participating governmental
unit in CORS, which is operated and maintained by the Board of Trustees for the Maryland State
Retirement and Pension System for eligible Correctional personnel throughout the State of
Maryland, and withdraw eligible officers from the EPS, transferring them to CORS effective
July 1, 2026.

‘The Council acknowledged that the Human Resources Department confirmed that the resolution
was posted within all County department for 10 days and no comments were received.

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council adopted
Resolution No. 692, the roll call was: Pfeffer ~ aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer ~ aye; Nichols — aye;
and, Travers ~ aye.

RESOLUTION NO. 693 - PICKUP - MANDATORY EMPLOYEE CONTRIBUTIONS
CORS — LEOPS,

Ms. Barnes said for consideration is Resolution No. 693 to affirm that the County will pick-up
contributions to CORS, LEOPS and EPS, as required by the state.

‘The Council acknowledged that the Human Resources Department confirmed that the resolution
was posted within all County department for 10 days and no comments were received.

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council adopted
Resolution No. 693, the roll call was: Pfeffer — aye; Detmer — aye; Kramer — aye; Nichols ~ aye;
and, Travers ~ aye.

REQUEST TO PROCEED ~ POOL PUMP REPLACEMENT ~ RECREATION & PARKS

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Due Trieu, Recreation & Parks Director, to proceed with
lowest quote, received by Hill’s Industrial at the cost of $8,175.55, to replace a deteriorated pool
pump that is no longer repairable, funded by its FY26 Operating Budget

ZONING AMENDMENT DISCUSSION ~ PLANNING & ZONING

Part 1:

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Susan Webb, Planning & Zoning Director, to send a
favorable recommendation to the Planning Commission, at its May 5, 2026 meeting, regarding
amendments to Chapter 155 Zoning, Attachment 2, Density, Lot Size and Bulk Regulations
Table, of the Dorchester County Code, to reduce the minimum lot size for development within
Dorchester County.

Part 2:

Upon motion by Councilman Travers, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council
unanimously approved the request of Ms. Webb to send a favorable recommendation to the
Planning Commission, at its May 5, 2026 meeting, regarding amendments to Chapter 150
Zoning, Section 50 Supplementary Use, of the Dorchester County Code, to modify and clarify
the standards applicable to farm wineries, breweries and distilleries.

The Council acknowledged that if favorable recommendations are received by the Planning
Commission, legislation regarding the amendments will be introduced at a future Council
meeting, and considered after a public hearing is held.

COUNTY PROPERTY TAX EXEMPTION REQUEST ~ HABITAT FOR HUMANITY
CHOPTANK

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Travers, the Council
unanimously approved the request of JoAnn Hansen, Chief Executive Officer, Habitat for
Humanity Choptank, a tax-exempt charitable organization, to grant a County property tax
exemption on 27 properties for the tax year 2026-2027, totaling $3,396.51.
