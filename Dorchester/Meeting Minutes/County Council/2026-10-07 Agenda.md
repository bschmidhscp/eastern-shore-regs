# **COUNCIL MEETING BID AWARD NOTIFICATION**

<u>Request to Proceed - Heated Vent System Replacement – Corrections</u> 

Upon motion by Councilman Kramer, seconded by Councilman Detmer, the Council unanimously approved the request of Don Satterfield, Corrections Director, to proceed with the replacement of Heated Vent units 5, 6 & 7, using a current service agreement with J.L. Gannon’s Refrigeration, Inc. at the cost of $88,556, funded by the FY27 Capital Budget: HVAC Units No. 5-7. 

<u>Bid Award Recommendation – CGE Ramp Pavement Repair Project – Airport</u> 

Upon motion by Councilman Detmer, seconded by Councilman Kramer, the Council unanimously approved the recommendation of Steve Nuwer, Airport to award Bid No. 2026-12 to the sole bidder, David A. Bramble, Inc. at the cost of $64,351, for the CGE Ramp Pavement Repair Project, with 75% funded by a Maryland Aviation Administration grant, and 25% by the FY27 Capital Budget: Taxiway Repair.
