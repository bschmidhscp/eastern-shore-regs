# Dorchester County, MD: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "PC digest", "Council digest", "BOA digest" = `01. Digests/`. State rules (Tiers, BAT, Critical Area, forest 1:1) are in `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`; only local choices are described here. **Code text is current only through 06-03-2025** [Code Contents]; later bills are not codified.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Minor subdivision = up to 7 lots from a parcel as it existed 4/1/1995, no road requirement in the definition** [§ 140-6]. This is the SB 236 number (7) [State Summary §2]. Preliminary and final plat may be combined and the PC Chairman may sign a minor final plat without the full Commission [§ 140-19.B; § 140-21.D(1)]. Sketch plan is mandatory only for majors [§ 140-11.B].
- **Major = 8+ lots** [§ 140-6]. In Tier IV (all AC/RC land) State law allows only minor subdivisions on septic, so **8+ lots on septic in AC/RC looks barred** (Dorchester has no Tier IV exemption: AC yields 3 + 1 per 15 ac, above 1 per 20) [State Summary §2; Attachment 2]. Confirm tier map status.
- **Ag density is thin:** 3 units plus 1 per 15 additional acres (1 per 10 if clustered) on pre-1972 parcels; the first 3 units' acreage is deducted first [Att. 2; § 155-34.D(1)]. Cluster needs 65% open space on parcels over 20 ac [Att. 2].
- **RR is the opportunity:** Table shows no density cap in RR (n/a), only lot size, 40,000 sf (20,000 sf clustered) [Att. 2]. Bill 2026-9 (adopted 2026-08-04) cut RR minimum lot sizes where community water/sewer exist; **new numbers not found in corpus** [Council digest 2026-08-04].
- **Fees are low:** $5,000 building excise tax per new home (no school impact fee found), county-wide [Fees: Res. 695 p.12; § 144-31]. 3-lot minor planning fee about $500 plus recording [Res. 695 p.11].
- **Evidence gap:** PC digest has 0 subdivision items and BOA digest has 0 recorded decisions (agendas only), so approval and variance rates are **not found in corpus**.
- **Risks:** Critical Area RCA (1 per 20 ac), septic/BAT cost, excise-tax suspension history, comp-plan and density-table amendments in motion.

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Minor | Major |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | 7 or fewer lots from 4/1/1995 parcel | 8+ lots, or anything not minor [§ 140-6] |
| Sketch | Optional pre-application conference [§ 140-11.B] | Mandatory; PC approval valid 2 years [§ 140-14] |
| Preliminary | May be combined with final [§ 140-19.B] | PC acts within 1 month; valid 1 year [§ 140-17; § 140-18] |
| Final | Chairman may sign after agencies [§ 140-21.D(1)] | PC meeting; guaranty first [§ 140-21.D(2)] |

- Final plat due within 1 year of preliminary or approval lapses [§ 140-21.A]. Director has 60 days on a complete sketch [§ 140-14.A].
- Agencies: County Engineer, Health Department, Soil Conservation District, Sanitary Commission [§ 140-21.C]. Septic lots meet Health Department rules [§ 140-27].
- **Exemptions:** agricultural division (5+ ac tracts, 5+ ac remainder, no dwelling) and adjoining/exchange plats skip full review, but the lot is not a building lot [§ 140-4.A(2), (3)].
- Roads: public roads serving up to 10 lots use rural section; shared driveway for 2 lots; one interior lot by easement [§ 140-26].
- **Timing signals: not found in corpus.** Minutes are agendas for PC and BOA; PC digest shows 42 items, none subdivision [PC digest].

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Critical Area (CA):** RCA density 1 per 20 ac; private tidal wetlands may count toward density, upland max 1 per 8 ac [§ 68-8.C]. Intrafamily: parcels 7-60 ac of record 3/1/1986 yield 2 lots (7-12 ac) or 3 lots (12-60 ac), family-only [§ 68-15]. Buffer 100 ft minimum, expanded for hydric/erodible soils [§ 68-25]. LDA lot coverage 15% on 36,301-43,560 sf lots [Table 68-7C(10)(e)].
- **Growth allocation:** 1,486 acres remaining of 2,900 [§ 68-12.B]; new LDA in RCA needs 300 ft from tidal edge, Planning Commission, Council and State approval [§ 68-12.C]. Fee $1,200 [Res. 695 p.12].
- **Critical Area updates:** Bills 2026-6 (map) and 2026-7 (definition) adopted 2026-07-21 [Council digest].
- **Septic tiers:** AC/RC = Tier IV; Rural Residential and Village = Tier III; sewered areas Tier I/II [2021 CompPlan p.40]. The tier map is described as "draft to be adopted" [CompPlan p.40]; adoption **not found in corpus**. County "does not currently require" denitrification outside the Critical Area (2021 plan text) [PC Annual Report p.24]; State BAT rule still applies in the Critical Area.
- **Forest conservation:** applies at 40,000 sf; afforestation to 20% (ag/resource) or 15% (other); thresholds 50% AC/RC/RR-C, 25% RR, 20% SR/V [§ 140-63; Table 140-64]. Exempt: lots under 40,000 sf; subdivisions fully in Critical Area; child/grandchild lots with declaration [§ 140-59]. Replacement fund fee $0.10/sf [Res. 695 p.12].
- **Stormwater:** exempt if land disturbance 5,000 sf or less [§ 134-6.C]. **Floodplain:** elevation = base flood + 2 ft freeboard [§ 155-37].
- **Ag preservation:** Right-to-farm plat note required in RC, AC, AC-RCA, RR-C [§ 140-16.B(1)(k)]. MALPF hearing 2026-09-01 [Council digest].
