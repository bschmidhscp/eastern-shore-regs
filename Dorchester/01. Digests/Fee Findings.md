# Dorchester County — Fee Findings

*Prepared 2026-10-11 from the corpus. FY27 = July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027.*

## Impact fee / excise tax
- **Yes: Building Excise Tax, $5,000 per new single-family dwelling, county-wide including incorporated towns** (no in/out-of-town split found). FY27 rate: [Fees: 695-County-Schedule-of-Fees-Amendment p.12, Resolution 695, adopted May 2026]. FY26 was also $5,000 [Fees: FY26-Schedule-of-Fees-PDF p.12, Res. 683].
- $5,000 is the statutory/code maximum per residential unit [§ 144-31(A)(1)]. Nonresidential max $1/sq ft, capped $5,000 per lot [§ 144-31(A)(2)].
- Enabled by Bill 2004-6 (eff. Sept. 15, 2004) [§§ 144-28 to 144-36; Fees: 2004-06-Building-Excise-Tax-Act]. Paid with the building permit [§ 144-33(A)]; refunded if the permit is denied, cancelled or expires [§ 144-33(C)]; unpaid tax is a lien [§ 144-33(F)]. Funds go to schools, sheriff and public safety [§ 144-28(D)].
- **Suspension risk:** the tax was suspended for permits filed 7/1/2024–6/30/2025 [§ 144-36.1; Bill 2024-6]. Earlier suspensions: 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020, 2022, 2023. Code is current only through 6/3/2025, so any FY26 or FY27 suspension bill is not confirmed either way. FY27 budget projects $250,000 of Excise Tax Fund revenue "New Home Construction", which implies it is being collected [Bills: Legislative-Bill-No-2026-5, § 6].
- No separate school or public-facilities impact fee found.
