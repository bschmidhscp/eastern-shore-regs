# § 127-5. Right to farm and real estate transfer disclosure.

Upon any transfer of real property by any means, the transferor shall provide the purchaser or lessee a Real Estate Transfer Disclosure Statement, as prescribed by the Dorchester County Commissioners, specifically advising the purchaser or lessee of the existence of this Right to Farm Ordinance.
