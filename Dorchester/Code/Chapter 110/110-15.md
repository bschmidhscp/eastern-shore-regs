# § 110-15. Premises conditions.

A. Sanitation. All premises shall be maintained in a clean, safe and sanitary condition free from any accumulation of rubbish or garbage.

B. Insect and rodent control. The premises shall be free from infestation of insects, rodents, vermin or other pests.
