# § 110-34. Fire doors.

All necessary and legally required fire-resistant-rated doors or smoke barriers shall be maintained in good working order, including all hardware necessary for the proper operation thereof. The use of door stops, wedges and other hold-open devices which have not received all necessary and legal approvals or which have been denied approval is prohibited.
