# § 93-1. Additional fee to be charged.

*[Amended 6-22-1993 by Ord. No. 224 ]*

The Clerk of the Circuit Court of Dorchester County shall charge an additional fee of twenty-five dollars ($25.) to applicants for marriage licenses, which fee shall be used to fund a local program to deal with domestic violence.
