# § 150-20. Enforcement; violations and penalties.

A. The Dorchester County Sheriff shall enforce the provisions of this chapter.

B. The Dorchester County Sheriff's Department may issue a warning to first time offenders who are accused of violating the provisions of these regulations.

C. Any person, firm or corporation violating any of the provisions of these regulations shall be subject, upon conviction thereof before the District Court of Maryland for Dorchester County, to a fine of not less than $100 nor more than $500 for each offense, and each day that the violation continues to exist shall be considered a separate offense.
