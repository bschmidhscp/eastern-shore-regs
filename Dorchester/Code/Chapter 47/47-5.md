# § 47-5. Nonapplicability of provisions for corporate surety companies.

*[1969, ch. 522, sec. 324E; 1974, ch. 65, sec. 324E]*

The provisions of §§ 47-2 through 47-4 shall not apply to a corporate surety company which is qualified to do business in Maryland under the laws thereof.
