# § 82-10. License required to provide electrical services.

*[Amended 1-21-2025 by Bill No. 2024-11 ]*

No person, firm or corporation shall provide electrical services as an apprentice electrician, journeyperson electrician or master electrician in Dorchester County, Maryland, without having first obtained the appropriate State license from the State Board of Electricians.
