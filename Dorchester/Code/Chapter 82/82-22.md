# § 82-22. Position of Code Official established; administration and enforcement.

*[Amended 1-21-2025 by Bill No. 2024-11 ]*

A. Creation of the position. There is hereby established the position of Code Official. The County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland (the County Council) shall appoint a Code Official to administer and enforce this chapter. Unless otherwise provided by the County Council, the Director of Planning and Zoning shall serve as the Code Official.

B. Administration and enforcement.

  (1) This chapter shall be administered and enforced by the Code Official. The Code Official may be provided with the assistance of such other persons as the County Council may direct, and they may delegate, upon the approval of the County Manager, such duties and responsibilities in connection with the administration and enforcement of this chapter as are appropriate, in their judgment.

  (2) The Code Official shall have the authority to issue permits under this chapter. The Code Official shall have the authority to revoke any permit issued under this chapter for cause.
