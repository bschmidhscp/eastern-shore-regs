# § 82-15. Electrical inspection agencies.

*[Amended 1-21-2025 by Bill No. 2024-11 ]*

Electrical inspection agencies shall be approved by the Code Official to perform inspections in Dorchester County. Inspectors shall hold an approved State license before performing any inspections in the County.
