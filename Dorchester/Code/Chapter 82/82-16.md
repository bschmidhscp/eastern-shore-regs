# § 82-16. Fees.

*[Amended 6-21-2022 by Bill No. 2022-5 ; 1-21-2025 by Bill No. 2024-11 ]*

The County Council of Dorchester County shall establish a schedule of fees for permits required by this chapter. Such fees may be altered or amended from time to time without a public hearing only by the County Council of Dorchester County upon the recommendations of the Code Official.
