# § 115-1. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**NOISE DISTURBANCE** Sounds of sufficient volume, character and duration so as to be productive of actual physical discomfort or annoyance to persons of normal sensibilities.
