# § 137-12. Outlet ditches.

Outlet ditches are those ditches needed and maintained for the drainage of county roads. A right-of-way providing necessary space for maintaining the ditch shall be provided by the property owners. Upon receipt of the drainage right-of-way easement agreement by the County Roads Board, it shall be recorded.
