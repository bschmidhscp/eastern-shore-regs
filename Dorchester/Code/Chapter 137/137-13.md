# § 137-13. Cost and installation of pipe.

Pipe cost shall be at the property owner's expense. Installation shall be made by the County Roads Board at its cost and thereafter maintained by the County. Size of pipe, including diameter and length, is to be determined by the Dorchester County Roads Board with assistance from the Dorchester County Soil Conservation District. If replacement pipe is longer than the original pipe, then the additional footage shall be paid for by the property owner.
