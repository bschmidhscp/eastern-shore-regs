# § 70-1. Policy.

The County Commissioners of Dorchester County and each and every agency, board, commission or unit of county government subject to control and regulation by the County Commissioners shall maintain a work environment without discrimination on the basis of:

A. Political or religious opinion or affiliation, marital status, race, color, creed or national origin;

B. Sex or age, except when sex or age constitutes a bona fide occupational qualification; or

C. The physical or mental handicap of a qualified handicapped individual.
