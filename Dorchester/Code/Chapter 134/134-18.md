# § 134-18. Suspension and revocation.

Any grading or building permit issued by Dorchester County may be suspended or revoked after written notice is given to the permittee for any of the following reasons:

A. Any violation(s) of the conditions of the stormwater management plan approval;

B. Changes in site runoff characteristics upon which an approval or waiver was granted;

C. Construction is not in accordance with the approved plan;

D. Noncompliance with correction notice(s) or stop work order(s) issued for the construction of any stormwater management practice; and

E. An immediate danger exists in a downstream area in the opinion of the Dorchester County Department of Public Works.
