# § 134-17. Fee.

Nonrefundable permit fees will be collected at each phase of stormwater management plan submittal. Permit fees will provide for the cost of plan review, administration, and management of the permitting process and inspection of all projects subject to this chapter. A permit fee schedule shall be established by the Dorchester County Council based upon the relative complexity of the project and may be amended from time to time.
