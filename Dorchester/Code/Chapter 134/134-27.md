# § 134-27. Waiver of liability.

The making of any administrative decision by the County or any of its officials or employees shall not constitute a representation, guarantee or warranty of any kind by the County of the practicality or safety of any proposed structure or use with respect to damage from erosion, sedimentation, stormwater runoff, flood, or any other matter and shall create no liability upon or give rise to any cause of action against the County and its officials and employees.
