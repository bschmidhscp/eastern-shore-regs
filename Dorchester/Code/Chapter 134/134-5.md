# § 134-5. Scope.

No person shall subdivide or develop any land for residential, commercial, industrial, or institutional uses without providing stormwater management measures that control or manage runoff from such developments, except as provided within this section. Stormwater management measures must be designed consistent with the Design Manual and constructed according to an approved plan for new development or the policies stated in § 134-8 of this chapter for redevelopment.
