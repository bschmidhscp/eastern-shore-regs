# § 134-30. Compatibility.

Permits and approvals issued pursuant to this chapter do not relieve the applicant of the responsibility to secure required permits or approvals for activities regulated by any other applicable code, rule, act or chapter. If more stringent requirements concerning regulation of stormwater or erosion and sedimentation control are contained in the other Code, rule, act or chapter, the more stringent regulation shall apply.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: Former Art. XV, Additional Provisions, which article immediately followed this section and consisted of § 134-31, Critical Area protection, was repealed 5-2-2017 by Bill No. 2017-3. See now Ch. 68 , Chesapeake Bay Critical Area.
