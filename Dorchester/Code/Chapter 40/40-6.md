# § 40-6. Requirements and duties of County Roads Engineer.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 10, sec. 455; 1961 Code, sec. 333; 1912, ch. 514, sec. 271; 1922, ch. 184, sec. 271; 1961, ch. 78, sec. 455; 1974, ch. 66, sec. 333]*

A. Residency requirement; specific duties. The County Roads Engineer shall reside in Dorchester County so long as he is a County employee. He shall have charge, control and supervision of the working, repairing and construction of the public roads and bridges in the County and shall make all the specifications for the building of new roads and bridges and shall see that no obstruction, hindrance or injury is permitted upon any road or bridge under his supervision. He shall have charge and control of all equipment, implements and accessories which may be purchased or provided by the County Commissioners for the purpose of this Article and shall employ such labor, equipment and implements as he and the County Commissioners deem necessary in connection with work on public roads and bridges, to be paid for by the County Commissioners. He shall examine and certify to the correctness of all bills and accounts for any work done or materials furnished for any of the public roads or bridges in Dorchester County before they are paid by the County Commissioners, and the County Commissioners shall make no payment until the accounts are certified by the Engineer. He shall be responsible for preparation of an annual budget for the County Roads Board, to be submitted to the County Commissioners. In addition, he shall submit to the County Commissioners budget comparisons on a quarterly basis, reflecting expenditures against the budget.

B. Programs and plans. Programs and plans for projected work for the budget year will be part of the budget.

C. Conflicts of interest. The County Roads Engineer shall not be interested in any firm or corporation from which anything is purchased for the use of the roads and bridges or receive any commission on those purchases.
