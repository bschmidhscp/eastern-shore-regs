# § 40-10. Management and control of teams and road equipment; purchase of road and bridge materials.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 10, sec. 466; 1961 Code, sec. 337; 1912, ch. 514, sec. 279C; 4-12-2005 by Bill No. 2005-5 ]*

It shall be the duty of the County Highway Supervisor and/or Public Works Director to submit to the County Council for their information the price of all bridge and road material recommended by him to be purchased for use in the repair or rebuilding of the roads and bridges, as hereinbefore provided to be repaired and rebuilt, before same shall be purchased by the County Council and also the quantity, price and quality of food required for the teams. It is expressly understood that all materials, supplies, fees, equipment, etc., for use on the County roads and bridges are to be purchased by said County Council, and same shall be inspected and passed upon and all bills certified as correct by said County Roads Supervisor before being paid.
