# § 40-8. Appointment of assistants to County Roads Engineer; duties; suspension.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 10, sec. 456; 1961 Code, sec. 335; 1912, ch. 514, sec. 272; 1922, ch. 184, sec. 272]*

The County Commissioners for Dorchester County shall, subject to the approval of the County Roads Engineer, appoint such assistants to said Engineer in each of the election districts of said County as may be deemed necessary. Under the control and direction of the County Roads Engineer, the assistants shall supervise the road and bridge work in their respective districts and perform such other duties as the County Roads Engineer may prescribe. The assistants shall keep the time of all the employees in their respective districts and a correct record of all materials used and make such reports as may be required thereof and certify bills therefor to the County Roads Engineer, to be approved by him before being presented to the County Commissioners for Dorchester County for payment. For incompetency or willful neglect of any duty imposed upon him by this Article or by the rules and regulations duly made by the County Roads Engineer and of which he shall be given due notice, any assistant may be suspended from service at any time by the County Roads Engineer, who shall thereupon state, in writing, to the County Commissioners for Dorchester County the reason for such suspension, and if the action of the County Roads Engineer shall be sustained by a majority vote of the County Commissioners for Dorchester County, the suspended assistant shall be summarily removed from office and his successor appointed in the manner hereinbefore provided. Each assistant shall devote his entire time to his duties during such days as the County Roads Engineer may determine.
