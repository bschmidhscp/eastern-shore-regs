# § 40-1. County Roads Board.

*[1961 Code, sec. 328; 1947, ch. 557; 1947, Sp. Sess., ch. 15; 1965, ch. 767, sec. 328(b); 1974, ch. 66, sec. 328]*

A. Board established; personnel. A County Roads Board for Dorchester County is created. The Board of County Commissioners of Dorchester County is constituted the County Roads Board. The Clerk to the Board of County Commissioners is constituted Clerk to the County Roads Board, the County Treasurer of Dorchester County is constituted to be the Treasurer to the County Roads Board and the Attorney to the Board of County Commissioners is constituted Attorney to the County Roads Board.

B. Powers. The County Roads Board is possessed of all the powers and authority the County Commissioners possess with respect to roads and bridges in the County under any public general or any public local law of Dorchester County, including the power of condemnation. Whenever the term "Board of County Commissioners" is used in this Article, it shall be taken to mean County Roads Board, except with respect to the levying of taxes.

C. Functions and responsibilities. The County Roads Board created herein shall perform those highway or public works functions assigned by the County Commissioners, and it may be granted responsibility for the following:

  (1) General County public works, buildings, publicly-owned water and sewerage facilities and capital projects as directed by the County Commissioners.

  (2) Roads, highways, bridges and streets, lanes, alleys, footways and culverts.

  (3) Water supply facilities and projects if operated and maintained by the County, unless part of a sanitary district:

  (4) Wastewater collection, treatment and disposal facilities and projects.

  (5) Solid waste collection, recycling and disposal facilities and projects.

  (6) Storm drainage, erosion and sediment control facilities and projects.

  (7) Lighting for roads, highways, alleys and other public places.

  (8) Fire hydrants, mosquito control facilities and programs and snow and ice removal.

  (9) Other functions and duties not inconsistent herewith as assigned from time to time by the County Commissioners.
