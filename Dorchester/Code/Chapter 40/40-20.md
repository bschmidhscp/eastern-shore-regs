# § 40-20. Closing of County public roads.

A. All applications for the closing of public County roads shall be in writing to the County.

B. All such applications shall be accompanied with the following:

  (1) A check from the applicant to pay for the publication expenses; and

  (2) Plats, surveys and legal descriptions for that portion of the County public road requested to be closed.

C. Should the County Council desire to proceed with such request, any member of the County Council may introduce a bill, in legislative session, and thereafter the matter shall proceed in accordance with Article 3 of the Charter of Dorchester County, Maryland.

D. The County Council, on its own initiative, may close a County public road in accordance with this section.
