# § 40-7. Regulations concerning County roads employees.

*[1961 Code, sec. 334; 1941, ch. 287; 1943, ch. 616; 1971, ch. 383; 1974, ch. 66, sec. 334; 1975, ch. 25; 4-12-2005 by Bill No. 2005-5 ]*

A. Every County employee engaged in the construction or maintenance of County roads or bridges shall report to the County Highway Supervisor and/or Public Works Director, except as otherwise provided in this section, pursuant to the County's rules and regulations and policies. The County Highway Supervisor will report directly to the Public Works Director who is responsible to the County Manager and County Council. The County Highway Supervisor shall be subject to County personnel rules and regulations and policies.

B. Compensation and benefits. These employees shall receive the same benefits provided to other Dorchester County government employees and as set forth in the County's annual budget.

C. Determination of vacation and sick leave. Each employee who has worked under the County Highway Supervisor for at least one year shall receive the same vacation, with pay, at a time to be designated by the Supervisor, and shall be entitled also to the same sick leave, with pay, in each year as Dorchester County government employees.

D. (Reserved)

E. Complaints or grievances. Any employee who has any complaint or grievance concerning the terms, conditions or procedures of his employment may file a grievance in accordance with Section I.G. of the Dorchester County Personnel Rules and Regulations.
