# § 40-3. Borrowing power; issuance of certificates of indebtedness.

*[1961 Code, sec. 330; 1947, ch. 557, sec. 452A; 1974, ch. 66, sec. 330]*

The County Roads Board may borrow from time to time such sums of money necessary to carry out the provisions of this Article, but not to exceed $50,000 in the aggregate at any one time, and it may issue its notes or certificates of indebtedness as evidence of the loan.
