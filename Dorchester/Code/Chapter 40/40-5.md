# § 40-5. Appointment and employment of County Roads Engineer.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 10, sec. 454; 1961 Code, sec. 332; 1912, ch. 514, sec. 269; 1922, ch. 184, sec. 269; 1933, ch. 232; 1947, ch. 557, sec. 454; 1949, ch. 347, sec. 454(b); 1974, ch. 66, sec. 332]*

The County Engineer shall be appointed by and be responsible to the County Commissioners. He shall be considered as a County employee, and his salary, benefits, vacation and other details of employment shall be included in and part of the merit plan or system for Dorchester County employees. These benefits shall be part of the employee handbook, as amended annually.
