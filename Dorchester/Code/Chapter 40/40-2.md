# § 40-2. Control of roads and bridges; levy for repair and construction.

*[P.L.L., 1888, Art. 10, sec. 267; 1930, sec. 452; 1961 Code, sec. 329; 1878, ch. 477; 1896, ch. 85; 1912, ch. 514, sec. 267; 1922, ch. 184, sec. 267; 1974, ch. 66, sec. 329]*

The County Commissioners for Dorchester County may control and regulate the public roads and bridges in the County, subject to the provisions of this Article, and may levy such amount annually as may be necessary for the repair of old roads and bridges and for the construction of new roads and bridges.
