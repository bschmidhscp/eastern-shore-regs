# § 40-9. Preparation of road and bridge work schedule.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 10, sec. 465; 1961 Code, sec. 336; 1912, ch. 514, sec. 279B; 1961, ch. 78, sec. 465]*

The County Roads Engineer of Dorchester County shall, on or before the first day of April in each year, make up a schedule of the public roads and bridges to be worked, repaired or rebuilt during that year and the extent of the work and repairs to be done thereon and present the same to the County Commissioners for their approval or rejection, in whole or in part, and said Commissioners may from time to time add to said schedule any road or roads and bridges not included in said Engineer's report that are to be worked, repaired or rebuilt during the year in their respective commissioner districts, the total amount of such schedule not to exceed 75% of the amount levied for such purposes, with the remainder of 25% of said road levy to be kept in reserve for use in making temporary repairs to any and all other roads and bridges in said County as the safety and convenience of public travel may require, and provided, further, that nothing in this Article shall be taken or construed to interfere with or relate to any contract work of any of the roads or bridges in said County now or hereafter to be entered into by said Commissioners.
