# § 40-11. Supervision of contract work.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 10, sec. 467; 1961 Code, sec. 338; 1912, ch. 514, sec. 279D]*

The County Roads Engineer, whenever requested by the County Commissioners to do so, shall take charge of and supervise all contract work on the County roads and bridges entered into by said County Commissioners with the State High way Administration or under the provisions of the Shoemaker Act when not actually engaged in the general road work of said County.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: The provisions of the Shoemaker Act, ch. 225 of 1904, provide for assistance to the counties in building roads.
