# § 155-7. Compliance with ordinance required.

No building, structure, land or part thereof shall hereafter be used, occupied, altered, erected, constructed or reconstructed, unless in conformity with this chapter. Activities not in compliance with this chapter shall be expressly prohibited. It is the intention of this chapter that it be interpreted as excluding any use that is not expressly permitted whether as a permitted, accessory or special exception use.
