# § 155-40. Solid Waste Overlay District (SW).

A. Criteria. A SW District may be established if all of the following criteria are satisfied:

  (1) The SW District is in an AC, I-1 or I-2 District.

  (2) The minimum area for a SW District is 100 acres.

  (3) The district has direct access to a limited access or collector road.

  (4) An SW District at the proposed location will be compatible with the existing and proposed uses and development of adjacent and vicinal properties.

B. Permitted uses. The following uses may be permitted in the SW District pursuant to any specific requirements for those uses in the Table of Permitted Uses and in the supplementary use regulations:

  (1) Rubble landfills and land clearing debris landfills, commercial.

  (2) Sewage sludge storage facilities, outdoor.

C. Procedure for creating an SW District.

  (1) Any individual with a committed financial, contractual or proprietary interest in property meeting the criteria above in § 155-40A may petition the County Commissioners to designate the property as an SW District.

  (2) The application requirements and procedure shall follow the requirements for map amendments in § 155-5B with the following exceptions and additions:

    (a) A finding of mistake in the existing zoning or a substantial change in the character of the neighborhood is not required to apply a floating zone to a property.

    (b) As part of the application the petitioner shall submit a preliminary site plan meeting the requirements for preliminary site plans set forth in Article VIII of this chapter.

    (c) The application shall include a plan of operation explaining such matters as hours of operation; number of truck trips; and associated operations such as washing, sorting, etc.

    (d) As part of the application the petitioner shall demonstrate how the proposal meets all of the purposes and criteria for establishing an SW District in § 155-40A and B.

    (e) As part of its recommendation to the County Commissioners the Planning Commission shall address whether the application meets all of purposes and criteria for establishing an SW District.

  (3) The County Commissioners may grant a petition for an SW District based upon affirmative findings that all of the purposes and criteria of the SW District in § 155-40A and B will be met.

  (4) If the petition is granted the County Commissioners may modify or attach conditions to the preliminary site plan stating the reason for their actions. These modifications or conditions shall be included in the decision and order creating the SW District.

D. Full site plan approval.

  (1) No permit shall be issued for any use in the SW District until the Planning Commission approves a full site plan.

  (2) The full site plan shall conform to the preliminary site plan approved by the County Commissioners for the district, or a new preliminary site plan must be approved by the County Commissioners.

E. Expiration of approval. Creation of an SW District is subject to the reversion clause in § 155-5B(4).
