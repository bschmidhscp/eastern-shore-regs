# § 155-39. Economic and Employment District (EE).

A. Criteria. An EE District may be established if all of the following criteria are satisfied:

  (1) The EE District is in an AC, RR, SR or RCA District.

  (2) The minimum area for an EE District is 10 acres.

  (3) The district has direct access to a limited access or collector road.

  (4) An EE District at the proposed location will be compatible with the existing and proposed uses and development of adjacent and vicinal properties.

  (5) Lot, yard and height requirements.

    (a) Development in an EE District shall meet the minimum lot, yard and height requirements of the I-2 District.

    (b) The preliminary site plan and petition shall clearly note any modifications to the minimum requirements of the I-2 District that are designed to ensure that the proposed use and development will meet the compatibility criteria for establishing an EE District.

  (6) Any proposed use or development of land in an RCA District will meet the requirements of the Critical Area Protection District.

B. Permitted uses. The following uses are permitted in the EE District pursuant to any specific requirements for those uses in the permitted use table and in the supplementary use regulations:

  (1) All uses permitted in the I-1 and I-2 Districts.

  (2) Conference and retreat centers.

  (3) Resort developments, including a compatible mix of uses such as a country club, hotel, entertainment and residential development.

  (4) Large recreational facilities, including racetracks and courses for racing automobiles, dogs, horses, motorcycles, etc.

  (5) Any use which is determined by the County Commissioners to be of the same general character as any of the above uses or is of significant economic or employment benefit, as determined by the County Commissioners.

C. Procedure for creating an EE District.

  (1) Any individual with a committed financial, contractual or proprietary interest in property meeting the criteria above (§ 155-39A) may petition the County Commissioners to designate the property as an EE District.

  (2) The application requirements and procedure shall follow the requirements for map amendments in § 155-5B with the following exceptions and additions:

    (a) A finding of mistake in the existing zoning or a substantial change in the character of the neighborhood is not required to apply a floating zone to a property.

    (b) As part of the application the petitioner shall submit a preliminary site plan meeting the requirements for preliminary site plans set forth in Article VIII of this chapter.

    (c) As part of the application the petitioner shall demonstrate how the proposal meets all of the purposes and criteria for establishing an EE District in § 155-39A and B.

    (d) As part of its recommendation to the County Commissioners the Planning Commission shall address whether the application meets all of purposes and criteria for establishing an EE District.

  (3) The County Commissioners may grant a petition for an EE District based upon affirmative findings that all of the purposes and criteria of the EE District in § 155-39A and B will be met.

  (4) If the petition is granted the County Commissioners may modify or attach conditions to the preliminary site plan stating the reason for their actions. These modifications or conditions shall be included in the decision and order creating the EE District.

D. Full site plan approval.

  (1) No permit shall be issued for any use in the EE District until the Planning Commission approves a full site plan.

  (2) The full site plan shall generally conform to the preliminary site plan approved by the County Commissioners for the district, or a new preliminary site plan must be approved by the County Commissioners.

E. Expiration of approval. Creation of an EE District is subject to the reversion clause in § 155-5B(4).
