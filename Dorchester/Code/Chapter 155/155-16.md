# § 155-16. Duties.

A. Included among the duties of the Planning Commission under this chapter are the following:

  (1) The Commission shall make investigations and recommendations with regard to zoning amendment applications as directed by the County Commissioners as prescribed in § 155-5 of this chapter.

  (2) The Commission shall advise the Board of Appeals on all applications for special exceptions and variances as prescribed in § 155-24 of this chapter.

  (3) The Commission shall approve site plans when required by this chapter or when required by the Board of Appeals.

B. The Commission shall approve subdivision plans as prescribed in Chapter 140 of the County Code.
