# § 125-7. Service standards for pickup and collection.

Licensees shall operate fully in accordance with the following regulations:

A. They shall remove all refuse in containers or bundles in accordance with the approved written statement of service for disposal from every point of pickup and clean up spillage by the collector at pickup points and along the route from the point of pickup to the truck.

B. They shall maintain regular service in accordance with the written statement of service.

C. They shall provide alternate service within forty-eight (48) hours in the event of a mishap or the breakdown of regular equipment or if collection service is missed for any reason.
