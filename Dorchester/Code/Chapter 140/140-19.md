# § 140-19. Purpose; Submission Required; Simultaneous Review.

A. The purpose of the final plat is for final approval by the Commission and recording by the Clerk of the Court, as required by § 140-7 of these regulations.

B. A final plat shall be submitted for all subdivisions, in compliance with the provisions of this chapter, except where variation therefrom may be specified in writing by the Commission. The preliminary and final plat procedures may be executed simultaneously in the case of minor subdivision plats or resubdivisions.
