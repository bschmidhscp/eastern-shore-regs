# § 140-11. Purpose; applicability.

A. The purpose of the sketch plan is to permit a review and discussion of the subdivision in concept with the applicant before the subdivision is fully designed and engineered, alert the applicant to design considerations and other requirements and to alert the applicant to the considerations of other county and state agencies who must eventually review those aspects of the subdivision within their jurisdiction.

B. The sketch plan is mandatory for major subdivisions. Minor subdivisions are encouraged to request a preapplication conference with the Director to discuss the design and other considerations pertinent to the subdivision.
