# § 140-47. Fees.

Fees to cover the costs of considering, examining and checking the several plats and plans required herein and of recording the final plat shall be collected at the time of filing a preliminary plat, in accordance with a fee schedule of charges, adopted by resolution of the Dorchester County Commissioners.
