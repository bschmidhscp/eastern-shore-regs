# § 140-32. Purpose.

The purpose of this article is to establish and define the improvements which will be required to be constructed by the applicant, or provisions made for construction by the applicant, as a condition of final plat approval by the Commission. The construction of all required improvements shall be coordinated in both design standards and construction scheduling so that the required improvements are constructed in the proper place and sequence. As appropriate, there shall be coordination with activities of adjoining incorporated areas.
