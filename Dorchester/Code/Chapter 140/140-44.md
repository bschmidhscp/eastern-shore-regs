# § 140-44. Initiation.

The Commissioners may, from time to time, amend, supplement or change, by ordinance, the regulations herein established. Any such amendment or change may be initiated by a resolution of the Commissioners or by a motion of the Commission. Before taking action on any proposed amendment or change, the Commissioners shall submit the same to the Commission for its recommendation and report. The Commission will report within 60 days after its first meeting subsequent to the proposal being referred to that body.
