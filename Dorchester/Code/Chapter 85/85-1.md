# § 85-1. Levy of additional charge.

*[Amended 10-9-1990 by Ord. No. 189 [1] ; 5-16-2023 by Bill No. 2023-6 ; 3-4-2025 by Bill No. 2025-2 ; 4-15-2025 by Bill No. 2025-3 [2] ]*

There is hereby assessed and levied an additional local 911 emergency telephone charge in the amount of $4.25 per month per telephone billing, to be applied to all telephone bills rendered for switched local exchange access, commercial mobile radio service ("CMRS"), or other 911 accessible service within Dorchester County to help defray the eligible operation and maintenance costs of Dorchester County for the 911 system.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: This ordinance further provided that it shall become effective January 1, 1991. [2] Editor's Note: This bill provided that it shall become effective July 1, 2025.
