# § 56-8. Governmental immunity not waived.

A. The consent of the County Attorney to defend actions or proceedings against county officers and employees may not be construed to deprive any agency, board, commission, department, officer or institution, or any employee thereof of its governmental immunity.

B. The provisions of this chapter shall apply to all actions instituted in court on or after January 1, 1990.
