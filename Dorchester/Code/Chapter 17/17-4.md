# § 17-4. Fourth or Taylors Island Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Fourth or Taylors Island Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning in the Chesapeake Bay at the mouth of the Little Choptank River north of James Island; thence running up the center of the Little Choptank River easterly to the mouth of Parsons Creek; thence running southerly up the center of Parsons Creek to the southerly end of Goose Dam; thence running southeasterly to the head of the Blackwater River; thence running easterly down the center of Blackwater River to Maryland Route 335, Church Creek-Golden Hill Road; thence running southwesterly with the center line of Maryland Route 335 to the intersection of Hip Roof Road at Riggins Corner; thence running with the center line of Hip Roof Road westerly to Smithville Road at Golden Hill; thence running southerly with the center line of Smithville Road to Maryland Route 335, Hooper Island Road; thence running southwesterly with Maryland Route 335 to the center of Great Marsh Creek; thence running westerly up Great Marsh Creek, northerly with Dunnock Island Creek, westerly down Punch Island Creek to the Chesapeake Bay; thence running northerly with the Chesapeake Bay to the mouth of Little Choptank River and the place of beginning.
