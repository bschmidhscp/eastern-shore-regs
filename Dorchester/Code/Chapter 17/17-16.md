# § 17-16. Sixteenth or Madison Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Sixteenth or Madison Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning at the intersection of the center of Little Choptank River and Fishing Creek; thence running southeasterly up the center of Fishing Creek to the mouth of Church Creek; thence running southerly up the center of Church Creek to the mouth of a branch; thence running southwesterly up the center of the branch to intersect Maryland Route 16, the Taylors Island Road, west of Woolford; thence running easterly with the center line of Maryland Route 16 approximately 1,000 feet to the Harrisville Road; thence running southerly with the center line of Harrisville Road to a point; thence running north 75 degrees west 550 feet, south 43 degrees west 1,450 feet, south 35 degrees west 1,200 feet, south 10 degrees west 3,450 feet, due south 600 feet, south 35 degrees west 450 feet to the Blackwater River; thence running northwesterly up the center of the Blackwater River to the head of the Blackwater River; thence running northwest to the southerly end of Goose Dam; thence running northerly through the center of Goose Dam and on down the center of Parsons Creek to the center of the Little Choptank River; thence running northeasterly up the center of the Little Choptank River to the place of beginning.
