# § 17-10. Tenth or Straits Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Tenth or Straits Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning at Harts Bridge at the intersection of Wesley Road and Farm Creek; thence running easterly down the center of Farm Creek to its mouth and the center of Fishing Bay; thence running southerly with the center of Fishing Bay to intersect the Somerset County line in Tangier Sound; thence running with the Somerset County line through Tangier Sound and Holland Straits southwesterly to the Chesapeake Bay; thence running northerly with the Chesapeake Bay, so as to include all islands up to Lower Hooper Island, to the mouth of the Honga River; thence northeasterly and northwesterly up the center of the Honga River to the mouth of Fox Creek; thence running northeasterly and northwesterly up the center of Fox Creek to the mouth of Insley Cove; thence running northeasterly up Insley Cove and Insley Creek to a point; thence running northeasterly at right angles to Wingate Bishops Head Road, to intersect the road at a point 500 feet southeast of Wesley Road; thence running northwesterly with the center line of Wingate Bishops Head Road 500 feet to Wesley Road; thence running northeasterly with the center line of Wesley Road to Farm Creek at Harts Bridge and the place of beginning.
