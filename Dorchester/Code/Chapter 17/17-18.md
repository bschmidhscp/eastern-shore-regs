# § 17-18. Eighteenth or Elliott Island Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Eighteenth or Elliott Island Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning in Fishing Bay at the mouth of Island Creek; thence running easterly up the center of Island Creek to Pokata Creek; thence running southeasterly up the center of Pokata Creek to Elliott Island Road; thence running south 30 degrees east to the Nanticoke River and the Wicomico County line; thence southerly down the center of the Nanticoke River with the Wicomico County line to the mouth of Fishing Bay; thence northerly up the center of Fishing Bay to the place of beginning.
