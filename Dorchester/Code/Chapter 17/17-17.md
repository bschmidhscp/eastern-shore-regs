# § 17-17. Seventeenth or Salem Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Seventeenth or Salem Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning at the intersection of Maiden Forest Road and the Chicamacomico River; thence running southerly down the center of the Chicamacomico River to the Transquaking River; thence running northerly up the center of the Transquaking River to Middletown Branch; thence running northeasterly up the center of Middletown Branch to Drawbridge Road; thence running northwesterly with the center line of Drawbridge Road to Percy May Road; thence running northeasterly with the center line of Percy May Road to United States Route 50 the Cambridge to Vienna Road; thence running with the center line of United States Route 50 northeasterly 2,100 feet more or less to a branch; thence running easterly up the center of the branch to a point; thence running north 67 degrees east 3,250 feet, north 45 degrees east 2,600 feet, due east 4,900 feet to Hawkeye Road; thence running with the center line of Hawkeye Road southeasterly to Maiden Forest Road; thence running with the center line of Maiden Forest Road northerly to the place of beginning.
