# § 17-9. Ninth or Church Creek Election District

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Ninth or Church Creek Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning at the intersection of the center of Fishing Creek and Church Creek; thence running easterly up the center of Fishing Creek to the southeast branch: thence on easterly up the southeast branch to a point 900 feet south of Pig Neck Road: thence running south 61 degrees east by a straight line to intersect the Little Blackwater River at a point where the river branches; thence running southerly down the center of the Little Blackwater River to the Blackwater River; thence running westerly up the center of the Blackwater River to a point; thence running north 35 degrees east 450 feet, due north 600 feet, north 10 degrees east 3,450 feet, north 35 degrees east 1,200 feet, north 43 degrees east 1,450 feet, south 75 degrees east 550 feet to Harrisville Road; thence running northerly with the center line of Harrisville Road to intersect Maryland Route 16, the Cambridge-Taylor Island Road, at Woolford; thence running westerly approximately 1,000 feet with the center line of Maryland Route 16 till it intersects a branch running into Church Creek; thence running northeasterly down the center of the branch to Church Creek; thence running northerly down the center of Church Creek to the place of beginning.
