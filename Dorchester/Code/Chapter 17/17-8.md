# § 17-8. Eighth or Neck Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Eighth or Neck Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning in the Chesapeake Bay at the mouth of the Choptank River, on the Talbot County line; thence running easterly up the center of the Choptank River, with the Talbot County line, to a point opposite the mouth of Lecompte Bay; thence running southwesterly through Lecompte Bay and on up the center of Lecompte Creek to the head of Lecompte Creek; thence running by a straight line southerly to Maryland Route 343 at the head of Gary Creek; thence southerly down the center of Gary Creek to the Little Choptank River: thence running southwesterly and westerly down the center of the Little Choptank River to the Chesapeake Bay north of James Island: thence northerly with the Chesapeake Bay to the place of beginning.
