# § 17-14. Fourteenth or Linkwood Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Fourteenth or Linkwood Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning in the Choptank River on the Talbot County Line opposite the mouth of Indian Creek; thence running southeast and southwest with the center of Indian Creek to a point opposite the center line of Springdale Road (formerly known as "Hollerman County Road"); thence running southeasterly with the center line of the road crossing Maryland Route 16, the Cambridge - East New Market Road, and on with the center line of Springdale Road to intersect Hicksburg Road; thence running with the center line of Hicksburg Road northerly to Red Hill Road; thence running southeasterly with the center line of Red Hill Road to Hawkeye and Hawkeye Road; thence running southeasterly with the center line of Hawkeye Road to a point; thence running due west 4,900 feet to a point, south 45 degrees west 2,600 feet, south 67 degrees west 3,250 feet, to intersect a branch; thence running with the branch southwesterly to United States Route 50; thence running southeasterly with the center line of United States Route 50, 2,100 feet more or less to Percy May Road; thence running southwesterly with the center line of Percy May Road to Drawbridge Road; thence running southeasterly with the center line of Drawbridge Road to Middletown Branch; thence running southwesterly down the center of Middletown Branch to the Transquaking River; thence running northerly up the center of Transquaking River to Drawbridge Road; thence running westerly and northwesterly with the center line of Drawbridge Road to Aireys; thence on northwesterly with the center line of Aireys Road to Cordtown Road; thence running southerly and westerly with the center line of Cordtown Road to Bucktown Road; thence running northwesterly with the center line of Bucktown Road to Old Route 50; thence running easterly with the center line of Old Route 50 to Tates Bank Road; thence running northerly with the center line of Tates Bank Road and on in a straight line to the center of the Choptank River, and the Talbot County line; thence running northeasterly with the center of the Choptank River and the Talbot County line to the place of beginning.
