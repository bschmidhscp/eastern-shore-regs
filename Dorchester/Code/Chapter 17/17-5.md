# § 17-5. Fifth or Lakes Election District

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Fifth or Lakes Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning at the intersection of Maryland Route 335, the Church Creek-Golden Hill Road and the Blackwater River: thence running southeasterly down the center of the Blackwater River to its mouth in Fishing Bay; thence running southerly with the center of Fishing Bay to the mouth of Farm Creek; thence running westerly up the center of Farm Creek to Wesley Road at Harts Bridge: thence running southwesterly with the center line of Wesley Road to Wingate Bishops Head Road; thence running with the center line of the road southeasterly 500 feet to a point; thence running southwesterly at right angles to Wingate Bishops Head Road to the center of Insley Creek; thence running southwesterly down the center of Insley Creek and Insley Cove to the center of Fox Creek; thence running southerly down the center of Fox Creek to Honga River; thence running northerly up the center of Honga River to Great Marsh Creek; thence running northwesterly up the center of Great Marsh Creek to the center of Maryland Route 335, the Hooper Island Road; thence running easterly with the center line of Maryland Route 335 to Smithville Road; thence running northerly with the center line of Smithville Road to Hip Roof Road; thence running easterly with the center line of Hip Roof Road to Maryland Route 335, the Church Creek-Golden Hill Road at Riggins Corner; thence running northeasterly with the center line of Maryland Route 335 to the center of the Blackwater River and the place of beginning.
