# § 17-2. Second or East New Market Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Second or East New Market Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning in the center of the Choptank River on the Talbot County line, and opposite the mouth of Cabin Creek; thence running easterly up the center of Cabin Creek, till it intersects Lords Crossing Road at a point approximately 500 feet south of the Hurlock-Cabin Creek Road: thence running southeasterly with the center line of Lords Crossing Road to Maryland Route 392, the East New Market to Hurlock Road; thence on southeasterly across Maryland Route 392, and with the center line of Shiloh Camp Road to Maryland Route 14, the East New Market to Rhodesdale Road; thence running easterly with the center line of Maryland Route 14 to a point opposite the southeast corner of the Anna P. Corkran Farm, approximately 1,000 feet west of the Rhodesdale-Harrison Ferry Road; thence running southerly at right angles to Maryland Route 14 to intersect Lecompte Road, at the point where Lecompte Road turns from running northeasterly to southeasterly; thence running southwesterly with the center line of Lecompte Road, and on in a straight line, to intersect Centennial Road at a point approximately 1,000 feet west of Kraft Road; thence running northwesterly with the center line of Centennial Road, 1.33 miles to a point; thence running south 70 degrees west 900 feet, south 18 degrees west 1,700 feet, south 30 degrees east 800 feet to intersect Johnson Road; thence on southerly with the center line of Johnson Road to the intersection of Payne Mill Road; thence running westerly with the center line of Payne Mill Road to Hawkeye; thence on northwesterly with the center line of Red Hill Road to Hicksburg Road; thence running southerly with the center line of Hicksburg Road approximately 2,200 feet to Springdale Road (formerly known as Hollerman County Road); thence northwesterly with Springdale Road crossing Maryland Route 16 and on in a straight line northwesterly to intersect Indian Creek; thence running northeasterly, and northwesterly down the center of Indian Creek to its mouth, and on northwesterly to the center of the Choptank River and the Talbot County line; thence running northerly with the center of the Choptank River and the Talbot County line to the place of beginning.
