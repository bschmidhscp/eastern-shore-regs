# § 17-3. Third or Vienna Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Third or Vienna Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning at the intersection of the Nanticoke River and the Marshyhope Creek on the Wicomico County line; thence running southwesterly down the center of the Nanticoke River and with the Wicomico County line to a point east of the southeasterly end of Kraft Neck Road; thence running west to the southeast end of Kraft Neck Road; thence running northwesterly and westerly with the center line of Kraft Neck Road to Steeles Neck Road; thence running southwesterly with the center line of Steeles Neck Road 2,000 feet to a point; thence running northwesterly at right angles to Steeles Neck Road to the center of the Chicamacomico River; thence running northerly up the center of the Chicamacomico River, to the intersection of Maiden Forest Road; thence running southerly with the center line of Maiden Forest Road to the intersection of the Hawkeye-Mays Mill Road; thence running northwesterly with the center line of Hawkeye-Mays Mill Road, to Payne Mill Road at Hawkeye; thence running easterly with the center line of Payne Mill Road, to Johnson Road; thence running northerly with the center line of Johnson Road to a point; thence running north 30 degrees west 800 feet, north 18 degrees east 1,700 feet, north 70 degrees east 900 feet to Centennial Road; thence running with the center line of Centennial Road 1.33 miles to a point approximately 1,000 feet west of Kraft Road; thence running northeasterly to Lecompte Road and on northeasterly with the center line of Lecompte Road to the point where Lecompte Road turns from running northeasterly to southeasterly; thence running northerly at right angles to Maryland Route 14 to the center of Maryland Route 14, the East New Market to Rhodesdale Road at a point opposite the southeast corner of the Anna P. Corkran Farm, approximately 1,000 feet west of the Rhodesdale-Harrison Ferry Road; thence running easterly with the center line of Maryland Route 14 to a point where Maryland Route 331 branches off and runs southerly to Vienna; thence running southerly with the center line of Maryland Route 331 to northwest branch of Chicone Creek; thence running southeasterly and southerly down Chicone Creek to Chicone Bridge Road; thence running northeasterly with the center line of Chicone Bridge Road to Walnut Landing Road; thence running easterly with the center line of Walnut Landing Road and on easterly to the center of the Marshyhope Creek; thence southeasterly down the center of the Marshyhope Creek to the Nanticoke River and the place of beginning.
