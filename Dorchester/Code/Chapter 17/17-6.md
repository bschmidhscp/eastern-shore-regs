# § 17-6. Sixth or Hoopers Island Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Sixth or Hoopers Island Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning in the Chesapeake Bay at the mouth of Punch Island Creek; thence running easterly up the center of the Punch Island Creek, southerly with the center of Dunnock Island Creek, easterly down the center of Great Marsh Creek to the head of Honga River; thence running southerly with the center of Honga River, so as to include Lower Hooper Island to the mouth of the Honga River and the Chesapeake Bay; thence running northerly with the Chesapeake Bay so as to include Barren Island to the place of beginning.
