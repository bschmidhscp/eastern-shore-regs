# § 17-1. First or Fork Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The First or Fork Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning in the unincorporated Town of Reliance at the intersection of the center line of Maryland Route 392, the Hurlock to Seaford Road and the Maryland-Delaware state line; thence running southerly with the center line of the Galestown-Reliance Road and the state line to the center of the Nanticoke River, which is the dividing line of Dorchester and Wicomico Counties; thence running southwesterly down the center of the Nanticoke River and with the Wicomico County line to the center of the Marshyhope Creek; thence running westerly up the center of the Marshyhope Creek to a point opposite the center of Walnut Landing Road; thence running westerly with the center line of Walnut Landing Road, to Indiantown Road, then on westerly and southwesterly following the center line of Chicone Bridge Road to the intersection of Chicone Creek; thence running northerly up Chicone Creek to a point where the creek branches just north of Kelly Road; thence running with the northwest branch of Chicone Creek northwesterly to Maryland Route 331, the Rhodesdale to Vienna Road; thence running with the center line of Maryland Route 331 to the intersection of Maryland Route 14, the East New Market to Brookview Road, at Rhodesdale; thence running easterly with the center line of Maryland Route 14 to the Rhodesdale-Harrison Ferry Road; thence running northerly with the road to the intersection of Mill Creek; thence running northeasterly down Mill Creek to the center of the Marshyhope Creek; thence northerly up the center of the Marshyhope Creek till it intersects the Caroline County line: thence following the Caroline County line, northeasterly, easterly, and southeasterly, to Finchville and the place of beginning.
