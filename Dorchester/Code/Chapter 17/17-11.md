# § 17-11. Eleventh or Drawbridge Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Eleventh or Drawbridge Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning at the intersection of Steeles Neck Road and Kraft Neck Road; thence running easterly with the center line of Kraft Neck Road to Hollands Crossroads and on southeasterly with the center line of Kraft Neck Road to its end; thence running east to the center of the Nanticoke River, and the Wicomico County Line; thence running southerly down the center of the Nanticoke River, with the Wicomico County line to a point; thence running north 30 degrees west to the intersection of Pokata Creek and the Elliott Island Road; thence running northwesterly down the center of Pokata Creek to Island Creek; thence running westerly down the center of Island Creek to its mouth and the center of Fishing Bay; thence running northerly up the center of Fishing Bay to the mouth of the Transquaking River; thence running northerly up the center of the Transquaking River to the Chicamacomico River; thence running easterly and northeasterly up the center of the Chicamacomico River to a point; thence running southeasterly at right angles to Steeles Neck Road to intersect the road at a point 2,000 feet southwest of Kraft Neck Road; thence running northeasterly with the center line of Steeles Neck Road 2,000 feet to Kraft Neck Road and the place of beginning.
