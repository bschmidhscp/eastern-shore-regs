# § 17-7. Seventh or Cambridge Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Seventh or Cambridge Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning in the center of the Choptank River, on the Talbot County line at the extension of the center line of Tates Bank Road; thence running southerly with the extension and the center line of Tates Bank Road to the center of Old Route 50; thence running westerly with the center line of Old Route 50 to the center line of Bucktown Road; thence running southeasterly with the center line of Bucktown Road to a point 500 feet south of the south property line of the Animal Welfare Society of Dorchester County; thence running in a straight line south 49 degrees west to Lot Lane Road; thence running with the center line of Lot Lane Road 4 courses and distances north 82 degrees west 1,100 feet, north 40 degrees west 6,400 feet, north 25 degrees west 900 feet, north 74 degrees west 2,800 feet to Maple Dam Road; thence running northerly with the center line of Maple Dam Road to the east fork of the Little Blackwater River; thence running westerly down the east fork to the place where the Little Blackwater River branches; thence running north 61 degrees west in a straight line crossing Egypt Road and Maryland Route 16, Church Creek Road, to intersect the southeast branch of Fishing Creek, at a point 900 feet south of Pig Neck Road; thence running westerly down the center of southeast branch and on down the center of Fishing Creek to its mouth in the center of the Little Choptank River; thence running northeasterly up the center of the Little Choptank River to the mouth of Gary Creek; thence running northerly up the center of Gary Creek, to intersect Maryland Route 343, Hills Point Road; thence by a straight line northerly to the head of Lecompte Creek; thence running northeasterly down the center of Lecompte Creek and on through Lecompte Bay to the center of the Choptank River and the Talbot County Line; thence running easterly up the center of the Choptank River with the Talbot County line to the place of beginning.
