# § 17-15. Fifteenth or Hurlock Election District.

*[1985, ch. 702]*

The Fifteenth or Hurlock Election District is created and the boundaries shall be: beginning at the intersection of the center of the Choptank River and the mouth of Hunting Creek, where Dorchester, Talbot and Caroline Counties meet; thence running northeasterly up the center of Hunting Creek with the Caroline County line to the old road from Preston to Federalsburg; thence running southeasterly with the old road and the Caroline County line to Maryland Route 318, the Preston To Federalsburg Road, at the intersection of the first county road running southerly therefrom, east of Maryland Route 331, the Preston To Hurlock Road; thence running southerly with the center line of the county road to Elwood Camp Road; thence running westerly with the center line of the Elwood Camp Road to Gravel Branch Road; thence running southeasterly with the center line of Gravel Branch Road to a point just south of Williamsburg Road; thence running southeasterly to a branch, formerly known as "Benjamin Conways Mill Creek," it being the first branch out of the west side of Marshyhope Creek north of Maryland Route 392; thence running easterly and southeasterly down the branch to the Marshyhope Creek; thence running southerly down the Marshyhope Creek to Mill Creek; thence running westerly up Mill Creek to the Rhodesdale-Harrison Ferry Road; thence running southerly with the center line of the Rhodesdale-Harrison Ferry Road to Maryland Route 14, the East New Market to Brookview Road; thence running westerly with the center line of Maryland Route 14 to Shiloh Camp Road; thence running northwesterly with the center line of Shiloh Camp Road to Maryland Route 392, the East New Market to Hurlock Road; thence on across Route 392, and running northwesterly with the center line of Lords Crossing Road to intersect Cabin Creek at a point approximately 500 feet south of the Hurlock-Cabin Creek Road; thence running westerly down the center of Cabin Creek to the center of the Choptank River and the Talbot County line; thence running northerly with the center of the Choptank River and the Talbot County line to the place of beginning.
