# § 1-1. Code legalized.

*[1985, ch. 209]*

Be it enacted by the General Assembly of Maryland, that the 1984 Edition of the Code of Public Local Laws of Dorchester County, being Article 10 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Maryland, published under the direction of the Board of County Commissioners of Dorchester County, is legalized. The Code shall contain all public local laws relating to Dorchester County following the 1985 regular session of the General Assembly of Maryland. The Code shall be deemed and taken in all the courts of the state and by all public officials of the state and of its several political subdivisions to be evidence of the public local laws of Dorchester County in existence at the time of compilation.
