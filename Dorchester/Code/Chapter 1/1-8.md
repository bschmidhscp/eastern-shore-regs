# § 1-8. Filing and recording.

A. Be it further enacted and ordained by the County Commissioners of Dorchester County that following adoption, this ordinance, certified by the President of the County Commissioners and attested by the Administrative Assistant to the Board, shall be filed with the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Dorchester County, who shall record the same as set forth in the Annotated Code of Maryland (1957), Article 25, § 4B (1984 Cumulative Supplement).

B. Following the aforesaid filing and recordation, a fair summary of this ordinance shall be published one (1) time in at least one (1) newspaper of general circulation in Dorchester County.
