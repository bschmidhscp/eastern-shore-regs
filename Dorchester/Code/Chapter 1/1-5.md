# § 1-5. Legalizing and adoption of Code.

Be it enacted and ordained by the County Commissioners of Dorchester County that the 1984 Code of Ordinances and Resolutions of Dorchester County, being Part II, Administrative and General Legislation, Chapters 56 through 157 and Appendix, published under the direction of the Board of County Commissioners of Dorchester County and edited by General Code Publishers Corp., is hereby legalized. The Code of Ordinances and Resolutions shall contain all ordinances enacted by the Board of County Commissioners of Dorchester County subsequent to January 1, 1985, and contain such resolutions as the Board of County Commissioners of Dorchester County may, from time to time, direct. The Code of Ordinances and Resolutions shall be deemed and taken in all the courts of the state and by all public officials of the state and of its several political subdivisions to be evidence of the ordinances and resolutions of Dorchester County in existence at the time of compilation.
