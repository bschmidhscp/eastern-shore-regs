# § 96-6. Forest conservation maintenance and management agreements.

A. Short-term protective agreements.

  (1) A person required to conduct afforestation or re-forestation shall include in the Forest Conservation Plan a binding maintenance agreement for the length of two years.

  (2) The maintenance agreement shall detail how the areas designated for afforestation or reforestation will be maintained to ensure protection and satisfactory establishment of forest.

  (3) The person required to conduct the afforestation or reforestation shall present evidence of a legal right to implement the proposed maintenance agreement on a selected site.

  (4) The maintenance agreement shall not be released until or unless a long-term lease or conservation easement agreement is in place to protect the lands where afforestation or reforestation will occur.

  (5) The maintenance agreement shall provide access by the Department to the afforestation or reforestation site.

  (6) A person required by this chapter to conduct afforestation or reforestation shall furnish surety to the county in the form of a bond, an irrevocable letter of credit or other security approved by the Department. The surety shall:

    (a) Assure that the afforestation, reforestation and associated management plan are conducted and maintained in accordance with the approved forest conservation plan;

    (b) Be in the amount equal to the estimated cost, as determined by the Department;

    (c) Be in a form and of a content approved by the County Attorney; and

    (d) Be provided prior to plat recordation or approval of the grading permit.

  (7) If, upon expiration of the short-term protective agreement, the planting associated with the afforestation or reforestation meets or exceeds the standards of the Dorchester County Forest Conservation Technical Manual, the remaining amount of the cash bond, letter of credit, surety bond or other surety shall be returned or released upon written request of the person required to provide said surety.

  (8) The terms and conditions of the agreement shall be binding on the lot or parcel and may not be terminated upon transfer of title.

B. Long-term protective agreements.

  (1) An applicant under this chapter shall have in effect at all times after project approval approved long-term protective measures to retain as forest all land afforested, reforested or retained under this chapter. This may include one or more of the following:

    (a) Approved forest management plan, prepared by a licensed professional forester and approved by the Department of Natural Resources.

    (b) Forest conservation and management agreement, as provided for in Tax-Property Article, § 8-211, Annotated Code of Maryland.

    (c) Other legally binding protective agreements, including:

      [1] Covenants running with the land.

      [2] Deed restrictions.

      [3] Conservation easements.

      [4] Land trusts.

    (d) Other legally binding agreements shall provide for the protection of all lands forested, afforested or reforested under this chapter and shall limit the uses of the forest to those that are consistent with forest conservation.

  (2) A long-term protective agreement shall be binding for a minimum period of 15 years.

  (3) The terms and conditions of the agreement shall be binding on the lot or parcel and may not be terminated upon transfer of title.
