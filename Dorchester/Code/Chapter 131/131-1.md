# § 131-1. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**SANITARY CONSTRUCTION** The installation of pumps, septic tank systems or other individual sewage disposal systems and the practice of cleaning and disposing of the contents of septic tanks or other sewage disposal systems.
