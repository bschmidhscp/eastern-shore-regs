# § 10-8.3. Appointments.

*[Added 5-6-2003 by Bill No. 2003-6; amended 5-6-2014 by Bill No. 2014-5]*

A. A County Council member who is not reelected at the Primary Election may not make appointments to County boards after September 1 of such election year.

B. A County Council member who is not reelected at a November General Election may not make appointments to County boards and/or committees during the time immediately following the general election and before the newly elected County Council takes its oath of office.
