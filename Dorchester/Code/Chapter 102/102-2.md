# § 102-2. Definitions.

*[Amended 6-4-2024 by Bill No. 2024-5 ]*

As used in this chapter, the following words shall have the meanings indicated:

**ICC** The organization known as the "International Code Council."

**INTERNATIONAL MECHANICAL CODE**

A. The 2021 Edition or current edition of the International Mechanical Code issued by the ICC.

B. Includes interim amendments or subsequent printings of the current edition of the International Mechanical Code issued by the ICC.
