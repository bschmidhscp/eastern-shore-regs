# § 132-1. Use of school bus monitoring cameras.

A. The County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland, as the governing body of Dorchester County, authorizes the use of school bus monitoring cameras.

B. The purpose of this chapter is to authorize all uses to the full extent provided in Annotated Code of Maryland, Transportation Article, § 10-706(c)(1).

C. The terms used in this Chapter have the same meaning as the corresponding terms used or defined in Annotated Code of Maryland, Transportation Article, § 21-706.1.
