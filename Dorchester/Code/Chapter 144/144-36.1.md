# § 144-36.1. Applicability suspended.

*[Added 5-20-2014 by Bill No. 2014-7 ; 4-5-2016 by Bill No. 2006-1 ; amended 5-8-2018 by Bill No. 2018-3 ; 6-15-2020 by Bill No. 2020-3 ; 9-20-2022 by Bill No. 2022-9 ; 11-21-2023 by Bill No. 2023-9 ; 6-18-2024 by Bill No. 2024-6 [1] ]*

The applicability of the building excise tax imposed by this Article X of Chapter 144 of the Dorchester County Code is suspended for a period of one year commencing on July 1, 2024, and ending on June 30, 2025, and to provide that the building excise tax does not apply to building permits filed between July 1, 2024, and June 30, 2025.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: This bill also stated that it is given retroactive application to July 1, 2024.
