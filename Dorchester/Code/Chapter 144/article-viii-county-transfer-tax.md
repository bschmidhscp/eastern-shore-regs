# Article VIII. County Transfer Tax

*[Adopted 6-21-1994 as Res. No. 195 [1] [2] ]*

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: This resolution provided that the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Dorchester County shall collect the County transfer tax on instruments of writing recorded with the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Dorchester County and shall remit the revenues collected to the Treasurer of Dorchester County and that this resolution shall take effect July 1, 1994. It shall remain effective for a period of 20 years and at the end of June 30, 2014, with no further action required by the County Commissioners of Dorchester County, this resolution shall be abrogated and of no further force and effect. [2] Editor’s Note: See also § 10-8.2 , County transfer tax.
