# § 144-55. Statutory authorization.

Pursuant to the authorization contained in § 9-232 of the Tax-Property Article, Annotated Code of Maryland, Dorchester County may grant, by law, a property tax credit against the County property tax imposed on improvements to real property if there is installed in the improvements a complete automatic fire protection sprinkler system, installed in accordance with accepted codes and standards established by the Maryland State Fire Prevention Commission.
