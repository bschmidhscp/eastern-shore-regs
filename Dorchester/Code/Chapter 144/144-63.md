# § 144-63. Definitions.

Words and phrases used in this article shall have their usual meaning, unless otherwise defined in this section:

**BONA FIDE DORCHESTER COUNTY VOLUNTEER FIRE COMPANY** Any fire company within the legal boundaries of Dorchester County that is recognized and in good standing with the Dorchester County Volunteer Firemen's Association.

**DWELLING** As used herein, "dwelling" shall have the same meaning as set forth in Md. Code Ann., Tax Property § 9-105.

**QUALIFYING MEMBER** Any member of a bona fide Dorchester County Volunteer Fire Company that is eligible to reduce the member's Maryland taxable income pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Tax General § 10-208(i-1) and is eligible for the credit authorized under Md. Code Ann., Tax Property § 9-105.
