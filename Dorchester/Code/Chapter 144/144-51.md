# § 144-51. Statutory authorization.

Pursuant to the authorization contained in § 9-311 (b), Tax-Property Article, Annotated Code of Maryland, Dorchester County may grant, by law, a property tax credit against the County property tax imposed on property that is owned by the Cedar Meadows Rod and Gun Club, Incorporated, and property that is owned by the Cambridge Skeet and Gun Club, Incorporated.
