# § 144-39. Qualified erosion control structures.

*[Amended 8-15-2006 by Bill No. 2006-6 ]*

Erosion control structures eligible for this credit are defined as steel bulkheads, timber bulkheads, vinyl bulkheads, concrete walls, stone masonry walls, stone revetments, stone reinforcement, stone breakwaters, jetties, or groins.
