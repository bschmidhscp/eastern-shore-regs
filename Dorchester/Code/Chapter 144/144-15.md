# § 144-15. Filing and recordation; publication.

A. Following adoption, this Article, certified by the President of the County Commissioners and attested to by the Administrative Assistant to the Board, shall be filed with the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Dorchester County, who shall record the same as set forth in Article 25, § 4(b), of the Annotated Code of Maryland (1981 Cumulative Supplement).

B. Following the aforesaid filing and recordation, a fair summary of this article shall be published one time in at least one newspaper of general circulation in Dorchester County.
