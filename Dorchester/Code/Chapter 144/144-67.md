# § 144-67. Application and annual verification.

A. Original application. On or before May 1 of each year, an individual seeking a property tax credit under this article shall submit to the Dorchester County Finance Office ("Finance Office") an application in form and substance the Finance Office requires, including the MSFA-P.2.2. form that each bona fide Dorchester County Volunteer Fire Company provides to its members.

B. Annual verification in subsequent years. In each subsequent year, the Finance Office shall develop procedures to document that the individual and the property remain qualified for the property tax credit under this article.

C. Procedure. The Finance Office is hereby authorized and empowered to develop procedures as may be necessary to administer this article, including determining the form and substance of any documents necessary for such administration.
