# § 144-12. Interest on delinquent county real estate taxes.

A. All annual county real estate taxes are delinquent if not paid on or before September 30 of the tax year and after such date shall bear interest at the rate of 1% per month or fraction thereof, until paid.

B. All on-half-year county real estate taxes are delinquent if not paid on or before 30 days after the date the tax bill is mailed or made available to the taxpayer and after such date shall bear interest at the rate of 1% per month or fraction thereof, until paid.
