# § 144-38. Tax credit granted.

*[Amended 6-3-2025 by Bill No. 2025-7 ]*

Dorchester County grants a real property tax credit in an amount equal to 30% of the total cost of qualified erosion control structures installed on or after July 1, 2006. The maintenance, repair, or replacement of qualified erosion control structures shall not qualify for the tax credit granted hereunder.
