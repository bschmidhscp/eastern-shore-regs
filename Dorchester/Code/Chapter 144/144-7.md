# § 144-7. Rate of tax imposed.

*[Amended 4-29-2003 by Bill No. 2003-3]*

Pursuant to § 12-103(b) of the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, the recordation tax upon every instrument of writing conveying title to real or personal property or creating liens or encumbrances upon real or personal property offered for record and recorded in the office of the Clerk for the Circuit Court for Dorchester County after June 30, 2003, shall be as follows:

A. In the case of instruments conveying title to property, the tax shall be at the rate of $5 for each $500 or fractional part thereof of the actual consideration paid or to be paid.

B. In the case of instruments securing a debt, the tax shall be at the rate of $5 for each $500 of the principal amount of the debt secured.
