# § 144-49. Statutory authorization.

Pursuant to the authorization contained in § 9-245 of the Tax-Property Article, Annotated Code of Maryland, Dorchester County may grant, by law, a tax credit against the County property tax imposed on real property that is owned by and used as the principal residence of an individual who is at least 67 years old and of limited income.
