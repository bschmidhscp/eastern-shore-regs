# § 144-70. Use of funds.

A. In accordance with Md. Code Ann.. Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis § 1-322, funds disbursed to the County from the State Fund shall be deposited into the County Fund and shall be only be used for the following:

  (1) Funding community-based initiatives intended to benefit low-income communities in the incorporated and unincorporated areas of the County;

  (2) Funding community-based initiatives that serve disproportionately impacted areas in the incorporated and unincorporated areas of the County;

  (3) Any other use(s) permitted by Md. Code Ann., Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis § 1-322, as amended from time to time; and

  (4) Administrative expenses related Subsection A(1) through (3) above.

B. Funds shall not be expended for law enforcement agencies or activities.

C. Funds shall be supplemental to, and shall not supplant, funding that would otherwise be appropriated to preexisting County programs.

D. The County Council shall approve the use of funds annually before expenditure thereof may occur. Before approving the use of funds, the Council shall:

  (1) Receive the recommendations of the County Manager regarding proposed expenditure(s) of the funds, which recommendations shall be based upon input received from County staff and community stakeholders as the County Manager deems appropriate; and

  (2) Hold a public hearing to receive public comment regarding the proposed expenditure(s) of the funds, which public hearing may, in the County Council's discretion, be held in conjunction with the public hearing on the County's annual budget or separately.
