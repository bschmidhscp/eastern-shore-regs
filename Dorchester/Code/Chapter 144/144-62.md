# § 144-62. Statutory authorization.

Pursuant to the authorization contained in Md. Code Ann., Tax-Property § 9-260, Dorchester County may grant, by law, a property tax credit against the County property tax imposed on a dwelling located in Dorchester County, Maryland, that is owned by a member of a Dorchester County Volunteer Fire Company if the member is otherwise eligible for the credit authorized under Md. Code Ann., Tax-Property § 9-105.
