# § 144-52. Tax credit granted.

Dorchester County grants a property tax credit each year against the County property tax imposed on real property that is owned by:

A. Cedar Meadows Rod and Gun Club, Incorporated; and

B. Cambridge Skeet and Gun Club, Incorporated.
