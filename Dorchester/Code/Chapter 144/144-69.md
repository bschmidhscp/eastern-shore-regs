# § 144-69. Definitions.

Words and phrases used in this article shall have their usual meaning, unless otherwise defined in this section:

**COUNTY** Dorchester County. Maryland.

**COUNTY COUNCIL** The County Council of Dorchester County.

**COUNTY FUND** The Dorchester County Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund.

**DISPROPORTIONATELY IMPACTED AREA** As used herein, "disproportionately impacted area" shall have the same meaning set forth in Md. Code Ann., Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis § 36-101.

**FUNDS** Monies disbursed to the County from the State Fund.

**STATE FUND** The State of Maryland Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund.
