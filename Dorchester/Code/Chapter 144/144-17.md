# § 144-17. Definitions.

The following words have the meanings indicated:

**BOARD** The Board of County Commissioners for Dorchester County.

**COUNTY** As the context requires, Dorchester County.

**HOTEL** An establishment that offers sleeping accommodations for compensation, and "hotel" includes: an apartment, a cottage, a hostelry, an inn, a motel, a rooming house or a tourist home.

**HOTEL RENTAL TAX** The tax authorized under this article.

**TRANSIENT CHARGE** A hotel charge for sleeping accommodations for a period not exceeding four consecutive months. "Transient charge" does not include any hotel charge for services or for accommodations other than sleeping accommodations.
