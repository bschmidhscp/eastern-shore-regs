# § 144-56. Definitions.

In this article, the following words have the meanings indicated.

**QUALIFIED PROPERTIES** Residential properties that:

A. Include a complete automatic fire protection sprinkler system; and

B. Used as the principal residence of the homeowner; and

C. Constructed between July 1, 2019, and June 30, 2022.[Amended 6-4-2019 by Bill No. 2019-4]
