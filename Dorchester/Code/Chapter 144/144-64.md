# § 144-64. Tax credit granted.

A. Dorchester County grants a $500 tax credit each year against the general County real property tax imposed on a dwelling that is owned by a qualifying member of a bona fide Dorchester County Volunteer Fire Company.

B. If a dwelling is owned by two or more qualifying members, then the annual property tax credit hereunder shall be $1,000.

C. Notwithstanding Subsections A and B above, the property tax credit granted hereunder shall not exceed the actual amount of County property taxes owed on the dwelling.
