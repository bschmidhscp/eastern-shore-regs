# § 144-36. Annual reports.

The Director of Finance shall prepare and submit an annual report on or before July 31 of each fiscal year to the County Council which shall include the following information:

A. Total amount of building excise taxes collected.

B. Amount of funds appropriated from the Building Excise Tax Fund.

C. Amount of funds expended from the Building Excise Tax Fund.

D. Funds remaining in the Building Excise Tax Fund.

E. Total amount of building excise tax refunds.
