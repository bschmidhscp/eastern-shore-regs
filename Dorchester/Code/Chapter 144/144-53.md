# § 144-53. Statutory authorization.

Pursuant to the authorization contained in § 9-311(d) of the Tax-Property Article, Annotated Code of Maryland, Dorchester County may grant, by law, a tax credit against the County property tax imposed on real property that is owned by Habitat for Humanity of Talbot and Dorchester Counties, Inc.
