# § 105-10. Participating agencies.

A. Participating agencies. An agency permitted to participate in County benefit plans which chooses to participate in a retiree benefit plan shall participate in the trust fund. However, a participating agency shall be eligible to participate under Internal Revenue Code Section 115. Each participating agency in the trust fund shall execute an adoption agreement in a form satisfactory to the Board and shall submit any information the Board requires. Except for any obligation to refund assets under Subsection B, legal liability shall not accrue to the County by including any participating agency in the trust fund. Each participating agency shall be fully responsible for its pro rata cost of coverage, including any required annual contribution to the County and its share of administrative expenses.

B. Termination of participating agency. If a participating agency decides to terminate participation in a retiree benefit plan and the trust fund, the agency shall notify the Board in writing. The Board and the participating agency shall agree on a date to end the agency's participation. Any transfer of assets from the trust fund resulting from the termination of an agency's participation shall comply with the Internal Revenue Code and the adoption agreement between the County and the participating agency.
