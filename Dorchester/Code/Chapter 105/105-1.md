# § 105-1. Definitions.

In this article, the following words and phrases have the following meanings:

**CONTRIBUTION** Payment made to the trust fund by the County.

**CUSTODIAN** The Director of Finance.

**INVESTMENT MANAGER** A person or entity who exercises discretion to manage all or part of the assets of an institutional investor.

**OPEB BOARD or BOARD** The Board of Trustees established under this article to manage and invest trust fund assets.

**PARTICIPATING AGENCY** An agency eligible to participate in County benefit plans which elects to participate in any County retiree benefit plan.

**RETIREE BENEFIT PLAN** Any retiree medical, dental, or vision insurance plan administered by Dorchester County, Maryland.

**TRUST FUND** The Other Post Employment Benefit (OPEB) Trust Fund established to pay all or part of the benefits provided under any retiree benefit plan.
