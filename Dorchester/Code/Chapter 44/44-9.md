# § 44-9. Warden of the Dorchester County Detention Center, formerly known as the County Jail.

*[1992, ch. 21; amended 11-15-2016 by Bill No. 2016-5]*

A. Notwithstanding § 2-309(K) of the Courts and Judicial Proceedings Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland or any other provision of this chapter, the County Council, by law, may provide for the appointment of a Director of the Dorchester County Detention Center, formerly known as the County Jail, and assigns responsibility for the operation of the Dorchester County Detention Center to the Director.

B. The law shall specify the qualifications of the Director and shall state that the Director serves at the pleasure of the County Council.[1]
