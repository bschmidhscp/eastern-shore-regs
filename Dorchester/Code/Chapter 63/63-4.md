# § 63-4. Burial Sites Preservation Board.

A. Establishment. There is hereby established a Burial Sites Preservation Board to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

B. Membership and meetings.

  (1) Number of members: The Board shall have a voting membership of not more than nine and not less than three voting members. The Board may designate or appoint an unlimited number of ex officio and other nonvoting advisors. The Board may appoint nonvoting members to various committees or otherwise to provide advice and expertise to the Board.

  (2) Qualifications for voting members.

    (a) Voting members of the Board shall be residents of Dorchester County. A voting member's tenure on the Board shall terminate immediately, without any further action required, when that member ceases to reside in Dorchester County.

    (b) All voting members shall be appointed by the County Council, who shall consider the expertise and experience of available candidates.

    (c) Each member shall serve for a term of four years.

    (d) No member shall serve for more than three consecutive terms.

  (3) The Board shall elect a Chair and Vice Chair from among the voting members at its first meeting in each calendar year.

  (4) The Board shall adopt rules of procedure.

  (5) The members and the Board shall be subject to, and act in accordance with, the Dorchester County Code of Ethics and Chapter 88 of the Code.

  (6) Meetings of the Board shall be held in compliance with the State Open Meetings Act, including provisions governing closed meetings.

  (7) The Board shall meet at least twice a year, and shall meet as soon as practicable at the request of the Chair, the County Planning and Zoning Administrator, or the County Council.

  (8) The Board shall not be empowered to enter into any contracts or hire any compensated employees, staff, advisors, or other personnel except as may be specifically authorized by the County Council. The Board may, with the consent of the County Manager, avail itself of the services of the County's staff and advisors.
