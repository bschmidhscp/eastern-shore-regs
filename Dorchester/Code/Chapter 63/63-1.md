# § 63-1. Title; purpose.

A. This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the "Burial Sites Preservation Code of Dorchester County."

B. The purpose of this chapter is to foster the protection and preservation of burial sites, cemeteries, burial grounds and funerary objects in Dorchester County, including trees, shrubs, and plantings located thereat. Nothing in this chapter shall preclude the routine maintenance of trees, shrubs, and plantings located at burial sites, cemeteries, or burial grounds.
