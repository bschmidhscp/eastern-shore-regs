# § 119-1. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**CODE OFFICIAL** That person designated by the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland, responsible and charged with the administration and enforcement of this chapter.

**PLUMBING** The practice, materials and fixtures used in the installation, maintenance, extension, alteration and removal of all piping, plumbing fixtures, plumbing appliances and plumbing appurtenances connected to, or to be connected to, any of the following:

A. Sanitary drainage or storm drainage facilities.

B. Venting systems and the public or private water supply systems within or adjacent to any building, structure or conveyance.
