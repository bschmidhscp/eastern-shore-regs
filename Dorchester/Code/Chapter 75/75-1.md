# § 75-1. Creation; general purpose.

A. There is hereby created a Department of Correction (the "Department") under the immediate direction of a Director who shall be appointed by the County Council as recommended by the County Manager.

B. The Department shall operate all programs pertaining to detention and rehabilitation of persons under the jurisdiction of the County government awaiting trial or having been convicted of a crime and sentenced by a court of competent jurisdiction for a term in the Dorchester County Detention Center or being held for any other lawful purpose.
