# § 129-1. Disposal restricted.

No used automobile, truck or vehicle tires may be deposited in any sanitary landfill operated within Dorchester County, unless said tires have been dismounted by a resident of Dorchester County from a vehicle, which at the time of dismounting of said tires is physically located within Dorchester County.
