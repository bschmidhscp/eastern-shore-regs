# § 124-5. Water and Wastewater Advisory Board.

There is hereby created a Water and Wastewater Advisory Board.

A. Appointment; term; compensation. There shall be five members of said Advisory Board who shall be appointed by the County Council for two-year terms; provided, however, that the initial terms may be extended for an additional two years in order to create staggered terms. Said Board members shall receive no compensation but may receive an expense allowance, as may be authorized in the budget by the County Council, for attendance at meetings.

B. Duties. The Advisory Board shall have the following responsibilities and duties:

  (1) Advise the County Council on the water and wastewater needs of the County.

  (2) Review amendments to the County Water and Sewerage Plan.

  (3) Make recommendations to the County Council on policies and procedures for the provision of water and sewer services in the County.

  (4) Review proposals for the creation of service areas and shared facilities and provide comments and recommendations to the County Council.

  (5) Review charges and fees for water and wastewater services and make recommendations to the County Council for changes in fees.

  (6) Review the proposed annual budget of the Department, as prepared by the Water and Wastewater Services Director, or designee, and provide comments and recommendations on said budget.

  (7) Such other additional duties as may be assigned, from time to time, by the County Council.

C. Removal of members. The County Council may remove any member of the Water and Wastewater Advisory Board from office for misconduct, incompetency, neglect of duty, or other sufficient cause upon written charges and after a hearing after at least 15 days' notice.

D. Advisory Board sunset. The Advisory Board shall be disbanded, and its functions and responsibilities vested in the County Council as of June 30, 2028, unless this section is amended by the County Council.
