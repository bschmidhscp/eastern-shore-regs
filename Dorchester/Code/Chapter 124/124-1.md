# § 124-1. Establishment of department of water and wastewater services.

There is hereby created the Department of Water and Wastewater Services, which shall be assigned such functions as provided for in this chapter. Until such time as additional functions beyond water and wastewater services are assigned to said Department, the Department may be referred to as the "Department of Water and Wastewater Services." As used in this chapter, the term "Department" shall refer to said Department.
