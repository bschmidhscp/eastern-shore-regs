# § 147-1. Misdemeanors.

The County Council of Dorchester County may provide that violations of ordinances authorized to be adopted or enacted by Dorchester County shall be punishable as misdemeanors, but no penalty shall exceed a fine of $1,000 and imprisonment for six months. Imprisonment in default of fine and costs shall be regulated by the provisions of Article 38, § 4 of the Annotated Code of Maryland.
