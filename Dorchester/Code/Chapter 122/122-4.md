# § 122-4. Terms and conditions of service.

A. Member(s) of the PAB and ACC shall hold their position until their successor(s) are selected and qualified.

B. Members of the PAB and ACC shall be subject to the provisions of Chapter 88 (Ethics) of the Dorchester County Code, as amended from time to time, applicable to nonelected officials.

C. Meetings of the PAB shall comply with the Maryland Open Meetings Act, § 3-101 et seq., of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, as amended hereafter from time to time (the "OMA").

D. With regard to the PAB:

  (1) Except for the Chairperson and subject to Subsection D(3) below, members shall serve a term of four years. No member may serve more than two consecutive terms, including the Chairperson.

  (2) The Chairperson shall serve a term of four years and may serve consecutive terms.

  (3) Initial terms of members other than the Chairperson shall be staggered as follows:

    (a) Two members shall serve an initial term of four years;

    (b) Two members shall serve an initial term of three years; and

    (c) Two members shall serve an initial term of two years.

E. With regard to the ACC:

  (1) Subject to Subsection E(2) below, members shall serve a term of two years and shall not be eligible for reappointment after three consecutive terms of service on the ACC.

  (2) Initial terms of members shall be staggered as follows:

    (a) One civilian appointed by the PAB and one civilian selected by the County Council shall serve an initial term of one year; and

    (b) The three remaining members shall serve an initial term of two years.
