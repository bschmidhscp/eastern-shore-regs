# § 122-7. Budget.

A. The County Council shall prepare a budget for the PAB that shall include:

  (1) An annual appropriation for studies and contracted services relevant to the mission of the PAB supported by grant assistance from the Governor's Office of Crime Prevention, Youth, and Victim Services; and

  (2) A stipend for members per meeting with a per annum maximum amount per member, as the County Council may deem appropriate by resolution.

B. The County Council shall approve a budget for the ACC that shall include:

  (1) An annual appropriation for studies and contracted services relevant to the mission of the ACC supported by grant assistance from the Governor's Office of Crime Prevention, Youth, and Victim Services; and

  (2) A stipend for members per meeting with a per annum maximum amount per member, as the County Council may deem appropriate by resolution.
