Resolution No, 

429 

A resolution of the County Council of Dorchester County amending the provisions of the text and tables of the 2004 Dorchester County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (“Plan”) relevant to the privately-owned water and sewer systems presently serving Linkwood Landing Mobile Home Park (“Linkwood Landing MHP”) to accommodate the expansion of Linkwood Landing MHP. 

Whereas, State law mandates that all Maryland Counties adopt a Comprehensive Water and Sewer Master Plan; and 

Whereas, Dorchester County adopted its most current Plan on March 16, 2004; and 

Whereas, a request was filed to amend the provisions of text and tables of the Plan to accommodate the approved expansion of Linkwood Landing MHP, as indicated on the following exhibits (collectively, the “Proposed Amendments”) , which are attached hereto and incorporated herein: 1. “Proposed Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan Text Amendments Linkwood Landing LLC” (2 pages), 2. “Table 3-2 (S.H.D.-6) Inventory of Existing Public and/or Private Community and other Multi-Use Water Treatment Facilities”, 3. “Table 3-5 Immediate, 5 and 10 Year Priorities for Water Development”, 4. “Table 4-3 (S.H.D.-10) Inventory of Existing Public and/or Private Community and other Multi-Use Sewage Treatment Facilities”, 5. “Table 4-5 (S.H.D.-9) Projected Sewerage Demands and Planned Capacities for Community and Multi-Use Sewerage Systems”, 6. “Table 4-6 (S.H.D.-13) Immediate, 5 and 10 Year Priorities for Sewerage Development”, 7. “Water Service Area Linkwood Landing MHP”, labeled as “Exhibit 3-H”, prepared by Andrews, Miller & Assoc., Inc. and dated April 2006, and 8. “Sewer Service Area Linkwood Landing MHP”, labeled as “Exhibit 4-I”, prepared by Andrews, Miller & Assoc., Inc. and dated April 2006; and 

Whereas, on July 7, 2006 the Dorchester County Planning Commission certified that the Proposed Amendments are consistent with the Dorchester County Comprehensive Plan; and 

Whereas, the County Council of Dorchester County, having held a public hearing on this matter on July 25, 2006, and having given careful consideration to the matter, voted unanimously to adopt the Proposed Amendments.
