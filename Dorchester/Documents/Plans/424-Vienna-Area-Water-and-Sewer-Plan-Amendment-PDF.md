44.3 Vienna (District 3) 

Soils in the northern portion of the Vienna District (north of U.S. Route 50) are derived from course grained Pleistocene deposits. The major soils series are the coarse textured Downer, Hammontown, and Hurlock soils. The moderately well, to well drained soils are generally suitable for individual soil absorption systems. South of U.S. Route 50, Elkton and Othello soils series are encountered. These fine to medium textured soils are poorly drained, have very high seasonal water tables and slow permeability. These conditions preclude the use of individual soil absorption systems. 

The Local Health Department has identified four areas above U.S. Route 50 as known, or suspected septic system failure areas. These are Rhodesdale, Reids Grove, an unnamed settlement on Maryland Route 331 between these communities, and the area west of Vienna. Each of these areas requires a detailed survey to quantify the extent of septic system failure.
