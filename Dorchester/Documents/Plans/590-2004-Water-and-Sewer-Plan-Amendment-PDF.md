# RESOLUTION NO. 590 

A RESOLUTION of the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland amending the 2004 Dorchester County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “Plan”) by modifying the Groundwater Management Area Boundaries Map which is Exhibit 2A in the list of exhibits contained within the Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan. 

WHEREAS, State law mandates that all Maryland counties adopt a Comprehensive Water and Sewer Master Plan; and 

WHEREAS, Dorchester County adopted its most current Plan on March 16, 2004; and 

WHEREAS, a request was filed by the Dorchester County Environmental Health Department (the “applicant”) to amend the aforementioned map by redrawing the northwestern boundary of Area C to follow the Transquaking River, and the area to the west of the river to be designated as Area B11 in the north, and Area B2 in the south; and 

WHEREAS, the hydrogeological conditions in the affected amendment area are indistinguishable from Area B1 on the north and Area B2 on the southwest, and that the management strategies would remain the same; and 

WHEREAS, on July 6, 2016 the Dorchester County Planning Commission certified that the proposed map amendment(s) is consistent with the Dorchester County Comprehensive Plan and voted unanimously to recommend to County Council approval of the amendment; and

<!-- page 2 -->
WHEREAS, the County Council of Dorchester County, having held a public hearing on this matter on September A , 2016 and having given careful consideration to the matter, voted unanimously to adopt the proposed map amendment(s). 

NOW, THEREFORE, the County Council of Dorchester County does hereby amend the Dorchester County 2004 Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan, as set forth on the attached exhibit. 

Adopted thisbb ray of September, 2016.
