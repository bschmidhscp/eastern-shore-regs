THIRD: That the Director of Planning is hereby authorized to change the Dorchester County Critical Area Maps accordingly. 

FOURTH: If Smith Brothers Pile Driving, Inc. should fail to use or begin construction of the expansion of the Taylors Island Marina boat hauling and storage operations (The “Project”), within two (2) years from the date of passage of this resolution or complete the construction of The Project within a reasonable period, as determined in the sole discretion of the County Council, the growth allocations herein awarded are hereby rescinded, null and void and the Critical Area Protection Classifications of the two areas shall revert to their original Critical Area Protection Classifications existing prior to the adoption of this resolution. For the purpose of this section, commence the use or begin construction means either: 

- A. Submission to the county ofa site plan; B. Application for a building permit for structures comprising a substantial portion of the improvements; or 

- (65 Substantial completion of construction. (As determined in the sole discretion of the County Council). 

FIFTH:This resolution shall take effect 60 days after Bill Number 2004-9 Taylors Island Rezoning Marina, is signed or upon Critical Area Commission approval, whichever is later.
