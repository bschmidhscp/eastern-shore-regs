# RESOLUTION NO. 591 

A RESOLUTION of the County Council of Dorchester County amending the 2004 Dorchester County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “Plan") by changing the designation of an area of land located on the west side of Laurie Lane, and shown on Tax Map 50, Grid 12, Parcel 204, Lots 2C and 2D, said land being part of the Brad-Vern Park Part Ill subdivision. For these properties the applicant requests amendments to Chapter 4 Sewage, Chapter 4, Table 4-1 and to amend the Sewer Service Area Map Exhibit 4-E from no planned or denied access to S1, existing to 2 years. S-1 means that community sewer is available now, or will be made available within two years. 

WHEREAS, State law mandates that all Maryland counties adopt a Comprehensive Water and Sewer Master Plan; and 

WHEREAS, Dorchester County adopted its most current Plan on March 16, 2004; and 

WHEREAS, a request was filed by the Dorchester County Environmental Health Department (the “applicant") to amend the aforementioned table and map to permit these two unimproved lots to connect to the Woolford-Madison Sanitary District Sewer Service line; and 

WHEREAS, these unimproved lots were intended to be served bya shared facility sewage disposal system known as a Bermed Infiltration Pond (BIP) that was constructed in 1993 and is now not functioning adequately, and is not capable of accommodating sewage flows generated by any future building construction on these properties; and
