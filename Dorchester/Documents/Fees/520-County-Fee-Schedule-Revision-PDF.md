## RESOLUTION NO, _ 520 

RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland agreed to separate the County’s fee schedule from the 2012-2013 Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County and from future Budget and Appropriation Ordinances on May 15, 2012 in order to allow the opportunity to make any changes or amendments to this schedule, if necessary, periodically throughout the fiscal year. 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland has enacted its 20122013 Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2013 by Bill No. 2012-3 and recognizes this schedule of fees supports the purposes identified in that ordinance in amounts as specified herein. 

NOW THEREFORE, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland hereby accept this “Schedule of Fees” to be effective beginning July 1, 2012 and ending June 30, 2013: 

AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that any amendments to the FY2013 County’s fee schedule approved by this resolution shall be hereafter adopted only by resolution and only after at least one public advertisement in a local news publication of general circulation in the County to be followed by a public hearing conducted by the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland.
