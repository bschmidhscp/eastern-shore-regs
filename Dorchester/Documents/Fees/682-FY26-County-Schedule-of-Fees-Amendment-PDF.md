RESOLUTION NO. 682 

###### RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND TO AMEND THE COUNTY’S SCHEDULE OF FEES 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland agreed to separate the County's fee schedule from the 2012-2013 Annually Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County and from future Budget and Appropriation Ordinances on May 15,2012 in order to allow the opportunity to make any changes or amendments to this schedule, if necessary, periodically throughout the fiscal year; and, 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland adopted Resolution No. 520 establishing this "Schedule of Fees," effective beginning July 1, 2012 and ending June 30, 2013; and, 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland has adopted resolutions since the establishment of this “Schedule of Fees,” to change various fees, the last being Resolution 678 adopted on October 15, 2024; and, 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland enacted its 20252026 Annually Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026 by Bill No. 2025-4 and recognized this schedule of fees supports the purposes identified in that ordinance in amounts as specified herein. 

NOW, THEREFORE, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland, does hereby adopt the following Schedule of Fees that incorporates these amendments:
