###### RESOLUTION NO. 582 

##### RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND TO AMEND THE COUNTY’S SCHEDULE OF FEES 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland agreed to separate the County’s fee schedule from the 2012-2013 Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County and from future Budget and Appropriation Ordinances on May 15, 2012 in order to allow the opportunity to make any changes or amendments to this schedule, if necessary, periodically throughout the fiscal year; 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland enacted its 20142015 Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2015 by Bill No. 2014-6 and recognized this schedule of fees supports the purposes identified in that ordinance in amounts as specified herein; 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland adopted Resolution No. 520 establishing this “Schedule of Fees,” effective beginning July 1, 2012 and ending June 30, 2013; 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland, adopted Resolution No. 548 amending the “Schedule of Fees” on July 16, 2013 to change various fees; 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland, adopted Resolution No. 565 amending the “Schedule of Fees” on February 3, 2015 to change various fees; 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland, wishes to amend the County Schedule of Fees to change various fees for various County services, effective beginning the 15 day of July 1, 2016; 

NOW, THEREFORE, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland, does hereby adopt the following Schedule of Fees that incorporates these amendments:
