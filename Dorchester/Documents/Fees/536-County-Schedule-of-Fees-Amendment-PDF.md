### RESOLUTION NO. ___ 536 

RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND TO AMEND THE COUNTY’S SCHEDULE OF FEES TO INCLUDE A BOARD OF BUILDING APPEALS APPLICATION FEE UNDER APPEAL CASES AND CHANGE THE MINIMUM BUFFER MITIGATION FEE UNDER MISCELLANEOUS 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Dorchester County, Maryland agreed to separate the County’s fee schedule from the 2012-2013 Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County and from future Budget and Appropriation Ordinances on May 15, 2012 in order to allow the opportunity to make any changes or amendments to this schedule, if necessary, periodically throughout the fiscal year; 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland enacted its 20122013 Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of Dorchester County for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2013 by Bill No. 2012-3 and recognized this schedule of fees supports the purposes identified in that ordinance in amounts as specified herein; 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland adopted Resolution No. 520 establishing this “Schedule of Fees,” effective beginning July 1, 2012 and ending June 30, 2013; 

WHEREAS, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland, wishes to amend the County Schedule of Fees to include a Board of Building Appeals Application Fee ($500.00 flat fee) under the Appeal Cases category and change the minimum Buffer Mitigation Fee from $0.40 per square ft minimum to $1.50 per square ft minimum under the Miscellaneous category. 

NOW, THEREFORE, the County Council for Dorchester County, Maryland, does hereby adopt the following Schedule of Fees that incorporates these amendments:
