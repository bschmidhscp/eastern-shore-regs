###### CHARTER AMENDMENT - ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND PURSUANT TO ARTICLE XI-A, SECTION 5 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF MARYLAND AND PURSUANT TO SECTION 601 OF THE CHARTER OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND ADOPTING A PROPOSAL TO PLACE A QUESTION ON THE BALLOT AT THE 2024 GENERAL ELECTION TO AMEND ARTICLE 4, SECTION 409 OF THE CHARTER OF DORCHESTER COUNTY TO SPECIFY THAT THE REVIEW OF ADMINISTRATIVE STRUCTURE BE CONDUCTED WITHIN THE FIRST FISCAL YEAR OF EACH COUNCIL TERM; AUTHORIZING THE COUNTY ATTORNEY TO CERTIFY THE LOCAL BALLOT QUESTION TO THE STATE BOARD OF ELECTIONS PURSUANT TO STATE ELECTION LAW; AND PROVIDING THAT THE TITLE OF THIS CHARTER AMENDMENT RESOLUTION SHALL BE DEEMED A FAIR SUMMARY. 

###### SECTION ONE: 

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF DORCHESTER COUNTY, MARYLAND, ("County Council") acting under Article XI-A, Section 5 of the Constitution of Maryland (the “Constitution") and acting under Section 601 of the Charter of Dorchester County (the “Charter"), that the County Council proposes to amend Article 4, Section 409 of the Charter to read as follows: 

409. Review of Administrative Structure. 

By the year 2006 and at least every four years thereafter, the Council shall [have made] CONDUCT an evaluation of the organizations, functions, powers, and duties of each department, agency, and office in the executive branch of the Government. The evaluation shall BE CONDUCTED WITHIN THE FIRST FISCAL YEAR OF EACH COUNCIL TERM AND address the efficiency and effectiveness of programs, services, and administration. 

###### SECTION TWO: 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the proposed Charter Amendment and Local Question be submitted to the qualified voters of Dorchester County, Maryland at the next general election scheduled for November 5, 2024.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that pursuant to Section 7-103 of the Election Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, the County Attorney shall certify the Local Question and the proposed Charter Amendment to the State Board of Elections on or before August 2, 2024.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that pursuant to Section 601 of the Charter and Article XIA, §5 of the Maryland Constitution, the full text of the proposed Charter Amendment shall be published by the County Council once a week for five successive weeks prior to the election in at least one newspaper published in Dorchester County.

606. GOVERNMENTAL TRANSPARENCY AND PUBLIC ACCESS.

(B) THE COUNCIL SHALL ENABLE THE PUBLIC, ITS REPRESENTATIVES AND THE MEDIA TO HAVE ACCESS TO GOVERNMENT RECORDS WITHOUT UNNECESSARY COST OR DELAY. UNLESS EXEMPT FROM DISCLOSURE, THE CUSTODIAN OF THE GOVERNMENT’S RECORDS SHALL PROVIDE SUCH ACCESS TO REQUESTED RECORDS.
