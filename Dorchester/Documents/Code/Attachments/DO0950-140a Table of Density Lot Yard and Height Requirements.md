## **Table Notes:** 

> <sup>1</sup> In calculating permitted density, the phrases “three units, plus one dwelling unit per 10 acres,” or “three units, plus one dwelling unit per 15 acres,” means that the acreage needed for the first three lots is subtracted from the gross lot or parcel area before the additional one unit per 10 acres or 15 acres is added. For example, assume a 35 acre parcel created prior to March 15, 1972, never subdivided and containing three dwelling units on five acres. The available acreage for further subdivision is 30 acres. This would allow three additional units clustered or two additional units not clustered, for a total of six units in a cluster type subdivision, or a total of five units in a rural type subdivision. 

> <sup>2</sup> The Health Department requirements for minimum lot size, and lot width may exceed the minimum zoning requirements.
