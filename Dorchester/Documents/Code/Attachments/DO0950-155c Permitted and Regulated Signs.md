## **NOTES:** 

> a Sign height is measured from the center line of the right-of-way adjacent to the property to the top of the sign. b  The combined total of all signs on a lot shall not exceed the maximum permitted sign area. c  i.  Flat wall signs may not exceed 50% of a wall surface. 

ii.  One freestanding sign per lot is permitted. The maximum allowable area for a freestanding sign is a maximum of 50 square feet. iii. Shopping centers and industrial parks are exempt from the maximum sign area limit of 400 square feet per lot. d  Permitted business, commercial, institutional and industrial establishments. 

e  Permitted in addition to the permitted individual business, commercial and industrial establishment signs. f  External means only such as by spotlight.
