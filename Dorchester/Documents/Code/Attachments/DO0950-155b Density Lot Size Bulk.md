## **Notes:** 

- (1)  30% open space may be required in cluster development in the critical area as a condition of approval of growth allocation (see Article VII, § 155-38N). 

- (2)  A minimum lot size of 10,000 or 15,000 square feet is permitted where lots are served by community sewer and/or water facilities per the SR District regulations. 

- (3)  Case-by-case basis per Planning Commission review [see Article VII, § 155-34C(2), Cluster development]. 

- (4)  For density and bulk regulations applicable to apartments and single-family attached units see supplementary use regulations. 

- (5)  Intrafamily transfers of land are permitted at the densities set forth in § 140-54 of the Subdivision Regulations. 

A. See supplementary use regulations for density and bulk regulations for specific uses such as apartments, manufactured home parks, planned unit developments, etc. 

B.  See general district regulations for density and bulk regulations applicable to floating and overlay districts (Critical Area, EE, SW Districts).
