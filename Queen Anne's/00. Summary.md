# Queen Anne's County, MD: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "PC digest", "BoCC digest", "BoA digest" = `01. Digests/`. Code is current through 06-13-2026 [Code/Chapter 18:1 History]. Zoning and subdivision rules are combined in Chapter 18:1. State rules (tiers, septic, Critical Area): see `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Ag land is the hard part.** In AG and CS, a plain sliding-scale split gives 1 new lot on the first 100 acres plus 1 per additional 100 acres [§ 18:1-14.D(1)(c); § 18:1-15.D(1)(d)]. Large-lot subdivision allows 1 lot per 20 acres (20-acre minimum) [§ 18:1-14.D(1)(b), E(1)(c)]. Cluster allows 0.125 per acre (1 per 8 ac), needs 16+ acres and 85% open space [§ 18:1-14.D, E]. Illustration, derived: 100 acres yields 1 sliding-scale, 5 large-lot or 12 cluster lots.
- **Minor subdivision is up to 7 lots** [18App-1 "Minor subdivision"]. Administrative or minor approvals can be delegated to the Planning Director, but only the Planning Commission (PC) can approve more than 5 lots or a public road [§ 18:1-162].
- **Impact fee is per square foot, countywide:** about $5.99/sf in FY27, **inferred**, not read from an FY26 or FY27 schedule (see section 6). Roughly $15,000 for a 2,500 sf house, payable at permit.
- **Sewer capacity is the live constraint on Kent Island.** Res. 25-12 froze residential and mixed-use approvals above 1,750 gpd (7 homes) in the Kent Narrows/Stevensville/Grasonville (KN/S/G) area subdistricts, 12 months from 2025-08-12 [Res. 25-12 §§ 2-4]. Small minors under 7 homes are outside it. Extension or end: not found in corpus.
- **Board of Appeals (BoA) is permissive:** 43 of 45 decided cases granted (25 with conditions), 2 denied [BoA digest, 2023-11 to 2026-09].
- **Code rewrite in progress:** a staged "Comprehensive Zoning Code Update" is amending Chapter 18:1 (section 7). Check code history before relying on any threshold.

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Administrative | Minor | Major |
|---|---|---|---|
| Threshold | Lot-line moves, combinations, conforming lots [§ 18:1-167] | Up to 7 lots incl. easements and rights-of-way [18App-1] | Any other [18App-1] |
| Decider | Planning Director unless referred [§ 18:1-165] | Director only if PC delegates; PC if over 5 lots or a public road [§ 18:1-162] | PC |

- **Stages (major):** optional sketch, preliminary plat and engineering, final plat; PC meets at least 10 days after the Director's report [§ 18:1-178, -184, -190]. Preliminary approval lapses 30 months after tentative approval [§ 18:1-184].
- **Improvements:** roads, stormwater, erosion control, water and sewage; flag lots 20 ft wide; no flag sewage reserve areas [§ 18:1-172.B].
- **Timing signal:** Placek's Place (10 lots, NC-20) went from preliminary 2023-10-12 (5-1) to final 2024-02-08, about 4 months [PC digest]. Minors are not minuted: minor timing **not found in corpus**.
- **Rates:** no subdivision denial in the PC digest; its 3 recommended denials were citizen text amendments [PC digest 2024-04-11].

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Critical Area (1,000 ft of tidal water):** RCA density 1 per 20 acres, with tidal wetlands counted only if upland stays at or below 1 per 8 ac [§ 14:1-39.C]. LDA impervious cap 15% of site; 25% per lot of one acre or less in post-1985 subdivisions, but 15% overall [§ 14:1-38.D(8)]. Slopes over 15% prohibited [§ 14:1-38]. New development in the Buffer is barred, with an administrative variance path for 1988 lots of record [§ 14:1-51; § 14:1-68]. Growth allocation is a Commissioners map amendment plus State review [§ 14:1-76].
- **Septic tiers:** Maryland rule applies. QAC's SB 236 minor subdivision is 7 lots [01. Maryland/00. Summary.md], matching the local definition. The county's adopted Tier map: **not found in corpus** (State map showed none adopted in 2017).
- **Forest conservation:** applies at 40,000 sf or more for major and minor subdivisions, not administrative; Critical Area land is excluded [§ 18:2-4]. Reforestation 1:1 (1/2:1 in a Priority Funding Area) [§ 18:2-14]. Fee-in-lieu $1.50/sf approved 2026-06-09 [BoCC digest]. Ord. 26-12 will repeal and replace Chapter 18:2 [PC digest 2026-04-09].
- **Stormwater, floodplain:** Chapters 14:4 and 14:3; thresholds not extracted. **TDRs:** Art. XX; Critical Area TDRs only from RCA land [§ 18:1-100.C].

## 5. Water and sewer

- **Public service only in sanitary subdistricts;** allocation is project-specific, expires if unused, and is not transferable [Plans: Water-and-Sewer-Allocation-FAQ pp.2-3].
- **KN/S/G plant is at or near capacity;** TMDL limits make expansion unlikely. A 300,000 gpd rerating is pending [Res. 25-12 recitals]. Draft policy reserves 50,000 gpd residential plus 100,000 gpd infill from the rerating; adoption not found [BoCC digest 2026-08-11].
- **Allocation is being denied:** Barnstable request (20,500 gpd) denied 1-4 on 2026-09-08 [BoCC digest].
- **Map amendments:** 36 water-sewer items 2023-10 to 2026-09, 9 clearly approved, most others hearings [BoCC digest].
- **Rural lots:** well and septic; BAT required in the Critical Area [01. Maryland/00. Summary.md]. Perc and septic fees: **not found in corpus**.

## 6. Fees

**Impact fee (inferred).** County-wide, per square foot of new residential floor area, no in-town/out-of-town split [Fee Findings; § 18:3-15]. FY25 verified at $5.85/sf (schools $4.77, fire $0.55, parks $0.53) [Fees: FY25-Impact-Fees p.2]. FY26 +2.4% took effect without action [BoCC digest 2025-06-10]. FY27 was held at 0% on 2026-06-09 [BoCC digest]. **The $5.99/sf FY27 figure is inferred** from a State fiscal note for FY26 plus the flat FY27 vote; no county FY26 or FY27 schedule is in the corpus [Fee Findings].

| Item | 3-lot minor | ~10-lot major |
|---|---|---|
| Planning + Public Works application | $800 + $575 = $1,375 | About $6,500 (no sketch) or $8,500 (with sketch) |
| Fire Marshal review | $100 | $100 |
| Impact fee, 2,500 sf homes (inferred) | about $44,900 | about $149,800 |
| Sewer + water connection, if sewered (FY27 KN/S/G) | $48,600 | $162,000 |

Sources: [Fees: Residential-Development-Fee-Sheet-PDF; Minor-Subdivision-Application-PDF]; connection $10,400 sewer + $5,800 water per dwelling [Plans: FY27-KNSG pp.4,10,17]. The 2024 fee sheet is undated by fiscal year; Fee Findings lists the 3-lot Public Works fee as $250, which conflicts with the application form ($500 + $25 per lot = $575). I used the form.

**Excluded:** survey and engineering, perc and septic fees, Soil Conservation District fees [Fees: QASCD-Fee-Schedule-10_6_2025], variance and BoA fees (not found), forest fee-in-lieu, recording, sureties, BAT system, legal and title. No separate excise tax found.
