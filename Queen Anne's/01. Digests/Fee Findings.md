# Queen Anne's County — Fee Findings

*Compiled 2026-10-11. Paths relative to `Queen Anne's/`. FY27 = July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027.*

## Impact fee / excise tax
- **Yes: development impact fee, per square foot of new residential floor area** (schools + fire/EMS + parks), not per dwelling. Applies countywide, including inside towns (collected by County before permit). No in/out-of-town split for residential. [Code § 18:3-15, 18:3-7(B)]
- **FY25 (7/1/24–6/30/25): $5.85/sf** = schools $4.77 + fire $0.55 + parks $0.53 [Fees: FY25-Impact-Fees p.2; Commissioners 2024-06-25: held flat].
- **FY26: +2.4% inflation adjustment allowed to take effect** (Commissioners took no action) [Minutes 2025-06-10]. State fiscal note lists **$5.99/sf** for FY26 [MGA HB 569 fiscal note, https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/2026RS/fnotes/bil_0009/hb0569.pdf, retrieved 2026-10-11].
- **FY27: held flat at 0% increase, so $5.99/sf (inferred: flat vs. FY26)** [Minutes 2026-06-09; § 18:3-6(C)]. The FY26 and FY27 schedule sheets are not posted on qac.org; the page stops at FY25 (https://www.qac.org/300/Building-Fees). Component split for FY26/27 not found.
- Example: 2,500 sf house ≈ $14,975 at $5.99/sf (derived).
- Code table in § 18:3-15 still shows the old base ($3.93/sf); the annual indexed schedule governs [§ 18:3-6(B)].
- Payment: may defer via promissory note and lien: 50% at permit, 25% at framing, 25% before final, plus 3% admin fee [§ 18:3-7(A)(1)(b), Ord. 20-03]. Fee is locked at the rate on the permit application date [§ 18:3-7(A)(2)].
- Exemptions: farm employee dwellings on easement land in AG/CS zones (Ord. 24-14, eff. 2024-12-31); subsidized low-income housing; nonresidential 50% off inside growth area, 25% off outside [§ 18:3-5(D)].
- 2026 HB 569/SB 833 (eff. 7/1/2026) lets school impact fees pay for school replacement; it does not change the amount [fiscal note above; Minutes 2026-05-12].
- No separate school/building excise tax found in corpus.

## Water & sewer charges
Public service only in sanitary subdistricts. Kent Narrows/Stevensville/Grasonville FY27: water connection (allocation) $5,800/dwelling (250 gpd; +2.5%/yr from 1/1/26); sewer connection $10,400/dwelling; permit fees $25 water, $50 sewer (+$3,000 per vacuum valve); Bay Restoration $15/quarter/EDU; inspection fee 6–10% of water/sewer improvement cost [Plans: FY27-KNSG-Water--Wastewater-Rates pp.4,10,17; https://www.qac.org/DocumentCenter/View/26398]. Barclay/Sudlersville: sewer permit $100 [FY27-Barclay-Wastewater-Rates]. Allocation required for any project in a service area [Water-and-Sewer-Allocation-FAQ; CWSP p.199].
