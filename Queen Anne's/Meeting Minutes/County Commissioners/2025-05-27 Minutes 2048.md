# **COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN PUBLIC HEARING:** 

A public hearing was held at 5:40 p.m. in the Commissioner's meeting room. The purpose of the hearing is to consider amending the Queen Anne's County 2011 Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan. In addition to the hearing, an informational presentation was held at 5:40 p.m. on Tuesday, May 13, 2025, also in the Commissioner's meeting room. 

**BARNSTABLE** – This property is currently agricultural land consisting of two farms located on either side of the three improved properties (not included in the development proposal) the center one of which has a street address of 2032 Main Street in Chester. The proposed development is shown on Tax Map 57 as part of parcel 43 (Lowery Farm) and part of parcel 68 (Dream Farm), is approximately 13 acres in size, of which a portion immediately adjacent to Main Street is zoned Town Center (TC) and the other to the rear of the site is zoned Countryside (CS).   The proposal is to amend the property from S-3/W-3 to S-2/W-2 to accommodate a proposed development consisting of 82 townhomes. Flows from the use are anticipated to be 20,500 gpd. Sewer service will be provided via Vacuum Sewer Collection Station ‘G’ whereas water service would be provided by the Stevensville/Chester water system.

# **DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS:** 

# **FY26 SEWERAGE AND WATER RATE INCREASE:** 

In 2011, the Sanitary Commission adopted resolutions that capped the annual rate escalator at 6.5% for sewer fees and 5.5% for water fees for “all subdistricts”. The resolutions had language indicating that these increases will take effect automatically on July 1 “unless the County Commissioners determine by resolution prior to July 1 of each year that the rates will increase by a lesser percentage”. Note this Resolution preceded the creation of the Southern Kent Island (SKI) subdistrict. The intent was to not raise SKI rates until all four phases were constructed unless absolutely necessary. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board agreed to set the annual sewer and water operations & maintenance rate escalator for all Sanitary District rate schedules for the FY 26 budget year at 2.5%, with the exception of the Southern Kent Island subdistrict which shall see no increase in rates.

Ms. Heather Tinelli, Director, Economic Development & Tourism submitted to the board for their approval the request for Economic Development Incentive Funding Workforce Development Fund for the Miltec Corporation.
