# **2023 ANNUAL MDOT TOUR FY 2024-2029 CONSOLIDATED TRANSPORTATION PLAN:** 

Maryland Deputy Transportation Secretary Paul J. Wiedefeld and other representatives of the Maryland Department of Transportation (MDOT) met with Board to discuss the 2024-2029 Consolidated Transportation Plan. The Consolidated Transportation Program (CTP) is Maryland’s six-year capital budget for transportation projects. The Capital Program includes major and minor projects for the Maryland Department of Transportation (MDOT) modal administrations: the Secretary’s Office (TSO); the Maryland Aviation Administration (MAA); the Maryland Port Administration (MPA); the Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA); the State Highway Administration (SHA); the Maryland Transit Administration (MTA) - and authorities that are a part of MDOT, including the Maryland Transportation Authority (MDTA) and the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (WMATA) 

# **2024 LEGISLATIVE INITIATIVES:** 

Senator Hershey, Delegates Ghrist and Jacobs meet with the Board and discussed the County’s current legislative priorities for the upcoming State General Assembly. The following was highlighted 

1) Amend Abandoned Boat Laws to eliminate problems associated with derelict boats being tied up to mooring piles, in some cases for many months or indefinitely, where the County has no recourse to otherwise remedy. 

2) To expand how local Impact fees collected from developers can be used for school “replacement and maintenance” projects and not solely for adding new capacity (ie. new classroom seats). 

3) To consider legislation for using camera systems to enforce and fine motorists that drive in RED X lanes and large vehicles over 5-tons (trucks and/or trucks & trailers) that drive in restricted contraflow lanes on the Bay Bridge.

# **MID-SHORE REGIONAL DETENTION CENTER – PART 1 AND PART 2 PROGRAMMING STUDY PROPOSAL:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby submitted to the Board for their review and approval the Mid-Shore Regional Detention Center Part 1 and Part 2 Programming Study Proposal.  The County has had two productive meetings with representatives of Caroline County and Kent County, as well as staff from the State of Maryland Department of Public Safety & Correction Services and the Department of Budget & Management.  The first step in the design process is to complete what is know as a Part 1 and Part 2 Programming Study.  This study will determine each Counties needs as far as beds, programming space, medical space, etc. and then combine the needs into a single program.  In order to keep this progress progressing, the State needs to have this study completed by March 2024. We have received a proposal from our existing Detention Center architect, Colimore Architects, to conduct the regional study in the amount of $98,900.  Each County has agreed to pay one-third of this fee.  Staff recommends moving forward with this contract.

# **ROAD NAME CHANGE REQUEST:** 

Mr. John Shelton, Planning Supervisor submitted to the Board for their review and approval a request for a road name change request.
