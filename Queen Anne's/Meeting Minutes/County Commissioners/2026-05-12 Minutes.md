# **LEGISLATIVE:** 

# **COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN INFORMATIONAL MEETING – MARLING FARMS & DOMINION SEWER SERVICE AREA MAP:** 

There is a public hearing scheduled for 5:40 p.m. on Tuesday, May 26, 2026, to discuss the following amendment request. This Informational Meeting allows the Commissioners to ask any questions of the applicant and/or staff in preparation for that hearing. 

**Marling Farms & Dominion Sewer Service Area Map** – The amendment seeks to adopt the service area map for the Marling Farms Subdivision and the community of Dominion. The areas in question have been noted as areas of Public Health Concern since at least the 1984 Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan, and every subsequent Plan since. Text briefly defining the problem remains in the 2011 Plan. The service area will carry a sewer service designation of S-3 (4 to 10 years) and the project will require a subsequent map amendment to S-2 should the project proceed. 

The Public Health Concern Maps were removed from the 2011 Plan to accommodate the adoption of the remainder of the Plan. The map needs to be amended back into the Plan in order to pursue a Public Health Exemption from the Smart Growth Coordinating Committee. This is a State committee consisting of the Secretaries of each State department who will judge the merit of the situation based upon a joint MDE and County presentation. As Marling Farms and Dominion are not within the County’s designated Priority Funding Areas, we cannot access State loan or grant funds which are essential to this project without this Exemption. When reaching out to the State in regard to setting the groundwork for the Exemption hearing (MDE has to sponsor the request on behalf of the County) the omission of the service area maps was raised as a fatal flaw, so this amendment is a pre-requisite to pursuing the Exemption. Also noted, the service area map will have to be amended from S-3 to S-2 if the project is to proceed.

# **ACTION ITEMS:** 

# **MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ANNUAL PAYMENT:** 

Mr. Brian Riley, Director, Information & Communications Technologies, submitted to the board for their approval the Maryland Department of Information Technology Annual Payment. 

The ICT department is requesting authorization from the Commissioners for County Administrator Todd Mohn to approve payment on the County’s existing contract with the Maryland Department of Information Technology (DoIT). This covers costing of Network Maryland services utilized during Fiscal Year 2026. Network Maryland provides the County’s core internet services, including standard web access as well as our telephone and 911 Dispatch communications capabilities. The fee on this existing service contract is $112,209.00 for the period of July 2025 through June 2026 with remittance being due May 27, 2026. 

On a motion made by Commissioner McLaughlin, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to authorize the County Administrator to approve the annual payment of $112,209.00 to the Maryland Department of Information Technology for services of the period July 2025 through June 2026. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner McLaughlin asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.
