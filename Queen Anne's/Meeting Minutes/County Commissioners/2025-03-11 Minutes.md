# **MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE MOSQUITO CONTROL AGREEMENT:** 

Mr. Dave MacGlashan, Chief Property Manager, Department of Public Works, submitted to the board for their approval the Maryland Department of Agriculture Mosquito Control Agreement. 

This is the annual budgetary agreement between the State and the County for the 2025 Mosquito Control Cost Share Grant. The total 2025 Mosquito Program Budget is $172,000.  By the terms of the agreement the County is responsible for all costs exceeding the state grant and collection of user fees estimated at $122,000. Last season, we served over 5,000 individual properties. 

On a motion made by Commissioner McLaughlin, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the 2025 Mosquito Control Agreement with Maryland Department of Agriculture and to allocate funding up to $30,000 for the program per the agreement.

# **KENTMORR MARINA PROPERTIES, LLC (DBA KENTMORR RESTAURANT) SKI-3 PUBLIC WORKS AGREEMENT:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director of Public Works, submitted to the board for their approval the Kentmorr Marina Properties, LLC (DBA Kentmorr Restaurant) SKI-3 Public Works Agreement.

# **ECONOMIC AND TOURISM DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION FOR CHESAPEAKE COUNTRY ALL-AMERICAN ROAD LETTER OF SUPPORT:** 

Ms. Heather Tinelli, Director, Economic & Tourism Development, submitted to the board for their approval the Economic and Tourism Development Application for Chesapeake Country All-American Road Letter of Support. 

The Economic and Tourism Development Department is applying for a $50,000 noncapital Maryland Heritage Areas Authority Grant to fund the next phase of the All-American Road project: Navigating Chesapeake Country & Heritage Tourism. If awarded, grant funds will be used to further educate partners, build awareness, and promote the Chesapeake Country AllAmerican Road (CCAAR). This will include three components including a MD250 web page on the CCAAR website, a free online frontline worker training program, and the additional signage for the byway. This project match of $2,500 will be supported through in-kind staff support and volunteer time as well as proposed department operational FY26 budget. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the letter of support for the Chesapeake Country AllAmerican Road MHAA grant.

# **CHESAPEAKE COLLEGE AWARD OF CONTRACT CONSULTANT FOR 10 YEAR FACILITIES MASTER PLAN AND USE OF FUND BALANCE:** 

Chesapeake College needs to prepare a new comprehensive Chesapeake College Master Plan that will establish a framework for the orderly development of all capital improvements to support the mission and the educational plans of the college. The proposed timeline for this project is approximately 10 months. The college had included in the FY25 Maintenance and Repair fund a budget for the Athletic Master Plan Consultant Services in the amount of $75,000.  The college has completed a competitive solicitation and is recommending awarding this contract to Design Collective of Baltimore for a fee not to exceed their bid amount of $242,550. In order to proceed, the college has requested the use of fund balance to supplement the project budget. Chesapeake College Board of Trustees has approved use of fund balance in an amount not to exceed $167,550. Use of Fund Balance also requires approval of the support counties. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the use of fund balance not to exceed $167,550 by Chesapeake College for the Chesapeake College Master Plan. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Wilson asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

# **MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING – METROPOLITAN FIRE CHIEFS’ COUNCIL OF THE STATE OF MARYLAND MUTUAL AID AGREEMENT:** 

Mr. Scott Haas, Director, Department of Emergency Services, submitted to the board for their approval the Memorandum of Understanding – Metropolitan Fire Chiefs’ Council of the State of Maryland Mutual Aid Agreement. 

The Department of Emergency Services recommended that all Queen Anne’s County Fire, Rescue, and EMS services to join the Metropolitan Fire Chiefs’ Council of the State of Maryland Mutual Aid Agreement. This agreement strengthens collaboration and resource-sharing among fire and emergency services statewide, enhancing our collective ability to respond effectively to large-scale emergencies, disasters, and other critical incidents. Recognizing that most jurisdictions do not maintain sufficient resources to manage extreme events independently, this agreement provides a structured framework for counties to supplement their resources during high-demand incidents. Participation in this agreement aligns with our commitment to public safety, operational efficiency, and regional preparedness. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the Memorandum of Understanding between Queen Anne’s County and the Metropolitan Fire Chiefs’ Council of the State of Maryland and authorize the Director of Emergency Services and the Department of Budget and Finance to execute the agreement. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Dumenil, Commissioner Wilson asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.
