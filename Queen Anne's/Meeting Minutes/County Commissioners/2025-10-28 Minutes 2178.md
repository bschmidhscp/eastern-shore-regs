October 28, 2025 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil Christopher M. Corchiarino 

# **MEETING CALLED TO ORDER:** 

The meeting was called to order at 5:34 p.m. 

# **MINUTES:** 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the agenda for the current meeting, the Regular Minutes of October 14, 2025, the Roads Minutes of October 14, 2025, and Closed Session Minutes of October 14, 2025.

# **LEGISLATIVE:** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING – SPECIAL ASSESSMENT CHARGES FOR ROAD CONSTRUCTION HARFORD ROAD AND OLIVE BRANCH ROAD:** 

A public hearing was held regarding special assessment charges for road construction for Harford Road and Olive Branch Road. The Special Benefit Tax Assessment Private to Public Road Upgrade Projects were initiated by property owners along each respective roadway through petition and each project has had a public information meeting. 

Mr. Patrick Thompson, County Attorney, conducted the hearing on behalf of the Commissioners and noted all legal requirements had been met. 

The record will remain open for three weeks.

# **DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS:** 

# **QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY LIBRARY CENTREVILLE BRANCH RAIN GARDEN AND PARKING LOT REHABILITATION CONTRACT AWARD:** 

Mr. Lee Edgar, Chief of Engineering, Public Works, submitted to the Board for their review the Queen Anne’s County Library Centreville Branch Rain Garden and Parking Lot Rehabilitation Contract Award. Sealed bids were received for the Queen Anne’s County Library Centreville Branch Rain Garden and Parking Lot Rehabilitation project. The work is for the renovation of an existing demonstration rain garden, expansion of existing parking area, conversion of existing asphalt pavement to permeable and traditional brick pavers, removal and replacement of existing Portland cement concrete curb, relocation of an existing dumpster enclosure and peripheral site improvements. On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to award the Queen Anne’s County Library Centreville Branch Rain Garden and Parking Lot Rehabilitation contract to Marshall’s Professional Property Management, LLC of Cambridge, Maryland in the amount of $128,098.30, and authorize the Director of Public Works to issue the Notice of Award and execute the Contract on behalf of the County Commissioners.

# **ACTION ITEMS:** 

# **LETTER TO GOVERNOR MOORE - US 301/MD 405 SAFETY IMPROVEMENTS:** 

This letter to Governor Moore urges his support for legislation that would enhance roadway safety at US 301/MD 405 by adding this section of highway to the list of approved automated enforcement areas in the Maryland Code. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Moran, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the Letter to Governor Moore - US 301/MD 405 Safety Improvements. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Moran asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

**LETTER TO GOVERNOR MOORE – ADVANCE STATE FUNDING FOR NEW DETENTION CENTER:**

This letter to Governor Moore is asking for acceleration of State Funding for the Queen Anne’s County Detention Center by one Fiscal Year and begin State funding of the project in FY27 instead of FY28 which would allow for the Architectural and Engineering plans for the replacement of the Detention Center to be funded one year earlier than planned.

**CONSERVATION RESERVE ENHANCEMENT PROGRAM (CREP) – AGREEMENT OF SALE AND PROJECT AGREEMENT – HIDDEN BIRCH FARMS, LLC TAX MAP 46, PARCEL 12:** 

Mr. Joe Pippin, Soil Conservation Specialist, Soil Conservation District, submitted to the Board for their review and approval the Conservation Reserve Enhancement Program (CREP) Agreement of Sale and Project Agreement – Hidden Birch Farms, LLC Tax Map 46, Parcel 12. 

Approval was requested for the submission of 274.584 acres of the 443.592-acre property owned by Hidden Birch Farms, LLC to be submitted to the Maryland Department of Natural Resources for review and final approval and includes the Agreement of Sale and Project Agreement for execution. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the CREP Easement Agreement of Sale and Project Agreement for the Hidden Birch Farms, LLC property.
