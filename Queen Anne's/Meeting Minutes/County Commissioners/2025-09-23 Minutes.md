# **2025 MDOT ANNUAL CONSOLIDATED TRANSPORTATION PLAN TOUR:** 

Maryland Transportation Acting Secretary Samantha Biddle and other representatives of the Maryland Department of Transportation (MDOT) met with the Board to discuss the Consolidated Transportation Plan. The Consolidated Transportation Program (CTP) is Maryland’s six-year capital budget for transportation projects. The Capital Program includes major and minor projects for the Maryland Department of Transportation (MDOT), the Secretary’s Office (TSO); the Maryland Aviation Administration (MAA); the Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA); the State Highway Administration (SHA); the Maryland Transit Administration (MTA) - and authorities that are a part of MDOT, including the Maryland Transportation Authority (MDTA).

# **PROCLAMATION #25-39 – SUICIDE PREVENTION MONTH:** 

Commissioner McLaughlin read Proclamation #25-39 Suicide Prevention Month. September 23, 2025 

3

<!-- page 4 -->
**WHEREAS** , in 2023, nearly 49,300 Americans died by suicide – holding at levels not seen since 1941 – with an average of one life lost every 11 minutes; and 

**WHEREAS,** across the country, 5.5% of adults reported serious suicidal thoughts and more than 42% of U.S. adults personally know someone who has died by suicide, demonstrating the farreaching impact of this public health crisis; and 

**WHEREAS,** in Maryland, 608 residents died by suicide in 2022, at a rate of 1 suicide death for every 10,000 residents, which represents more deaths each year than from homicide; and 

**WHEREAS,** <mark>right here in Queen Anne’s County, the reality is urgent: 18.7% of middle school students and 20.3% of high school students report having seriously considered suicide, a concerning reminder that this crisis is present in the lives of our own children and neighbors;</mark> and 

**WHEREAS** , too many individuals at risk never receive the care they need, due to barriers such as limited access to trained providers, long wait times, and the stigma still surrounding mental health treatment; and 

**NOW, THEREFORE, WE, THE QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY COMMISSIONERS,** do hereby urge all Queen Anne’s County citizens to recognize suicide as a significant public health risk and declare suicide prevention and mental health support a priority and support the development and availability of accessible behavioral health services for all residents, so that no one is left without hope or help; 

**AND FURTHER, WE, THE QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY COMMISSIONERS,** do hereby declare the month of September 2025 as No Matter What…You Matter Suicide Prevention Month in Queen Anne’s County. We urge every resident to take action and check in on the people around them, because even a simple conversation can save a life.

# **SUDLERSVILLE WATER TREATMENT PLANT AND WATER TOWER LEASE:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director, Public Works, submitted to the Board the Sudlersville Water Treatment Plant and Water Tower Lease. 

As part of the action to fully assume the Town of Sudlersville’s water and sewer facilities, and to finalize the full assumption of USDA loans (which paid for the plant and tower), we need to execute a lease for the water facilities. Unlike the sewer facilities, which are on their own individual parcels (which the Town signed over in a fee simple deed on September 3, 2025), the water facilities share lands with the Town Hall. Ultimately the lands will be deeded to the County however, that process will take some time, so the lease satisfies the USDA requirement in the interim. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the Sudlersville Water Treatment Plant and Water Tower Lease with the Town of Sudlersville.

# **ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT INCENTIVE FUND GRANT AGREEMENT – MILTEC:** 

Ms. Heather Tinelli, Director, Economic & Tourism Development, submitted to the Board the Economic Development Incentive Fund Grant Agreement for Miltec. 

At a meeting held on May 19, 2025, the Economic Development Incentive Fund (EDIF) Commission recommended the approval of $2,565.00 in grant funding from the EDIF Workforce Development Fund to Miltec Corporation for training and professional development of its management and staff. The training will include a supply chain course and certification as well as SHRM-CP, a HR management certification. The overall cost of the training is $2,565. This new training initiative at Miltec Corporation is expected to enhance inventory management and efficiencies, as well as provide credentialing to HR staff contributing to the long-term success and stability of Miltec as a key employer in the county. The disbursement of funds was approved. This was reviewed by the County Attorney. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the grant agreement between the Economic Development Incentive Fund and Miltec in the amount of $2,565.00.
