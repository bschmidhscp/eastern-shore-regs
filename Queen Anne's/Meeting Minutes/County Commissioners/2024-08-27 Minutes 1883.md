# **MICHAEL & MELISSA FIRETTI – ADMINISTRATIVE SUBDIVISION #23-12-0401 – AMENDED DEED OF EASEMENT AND AGREEMENT: OPEN SPACE AREA:** 

Mr. Doug Summers, Associate Planner of Planning and Zoning, submitted to the board for their approval the Michael & Melissa Firetti – administrative subdivision #23-12-0401 – amended deed of easement and agreement: open space area. In October 1991, the previous ownership of the subject parcels received County approval for the development of a 21-lot subdivision known as Bloody Point. In support of this subdivision, 11.445 acres of deed-restricted open space was established on Parcel 21 to meet minimum County requirements (M.W.M 17/74). 

The owner is requesting approval to amend the existing deed-restricted open space area on Parcel 21. This is in support of a development plan to reconfigure lot lines to capture a septic reserve area to support a farm employee dwelling on Parcel 21. The proposal calls for the southern parcel line to shift northward while a parcel stem extends south to capture an existing SRA area. This proposal involves an equal land exchange between Parcel 19 & 21. Furthermore, the deedrestricted open space acreage will remain the same and is simply shifting in location. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the Amended Deed of Easement and Agreement: Open Space Area for Michael & Melissa Firetti Administrative Subdivision.

# **MICHAEL D. FOSTER – AMENDED OPEN SPACE PLAN ATE-24-05-0006 – SECOND AMENDED DEED OF OPEN SPACE EASEMENT:** 

Mr. Doug Summers, Associate Planner of Planning and Zoning, submitted to the board for their approval the Michael D. Foster – Amended Open Space plan ATE-24-05-0006 – Second Amended Deed of Open Space Easement. In November 1992 the previous ownership of the subject parcel received County approval for the development of a 9-lot subdivision known as Inverness. In support of the cluster lot portion of this subdivision, the remaining 221.943 acres of land was established as deed-restricted open space, which provided well above the minimum acreage required by County code (S.M. 19/18). Later in July 2002, previous ownership amended the open space area in order to relocate an existing building pad area on site. No reduction or increase of the open space area was completed at this time. The open space area was simply relocated to accommodate the building pad location (S.M. 31/97). 

The owner is requesting approval to amend and reduce the deed-restricted open space area down to the minimum amount required (71.989 ac.) to support the cluster portion of the subdivision. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to table the item until the next meeting. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Moran, Commissioner Corchiarino and Commissioner Dumenil asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

# **BUDGET AMENDMENT #CC-19 – ARPA GRANT REVENUE:** 

Mr. Jeff Rank, Director of Budget and Finance, submitted to the board for their approval Budget Amendment #CC-19, ARPA Grant Revenue. This amendment establishes budget authority in the amount of $897,240, to Aid Agencies for funding the Queen Anne’s County Housing Authority from April 2024 through June 2024. This funding was used to stabilize the Queen Anne’s County Housing Authority operations and ensure continued provisions of vital services during the transition back to Queen Anne’s County. The allocation is being funded with ARPA Grants, which were previously allocated to different projects. The second part of this amendment reduces ARPA funding for three projects and changes to General Capital fund balance. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to approve Budget Amendment #CC-19 – ARPA Grant Revenue.
