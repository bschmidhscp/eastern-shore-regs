# **2024 MDOT ANNUAL CONSOLIDATED TRANSPORTATION PLAN TOUR:** 

Maryland Deputy Transportation Secretary Paul J. Wiedefeld and other representatives of the Maryland Department of Transportation (MDOT) met with the Board to discuss the Consolidated Transportation Plan. The Consolidated Transportation Program (CTP) is Maryland’s six-year capital budget for transportation projects. The Capital Program includes major and minor projects for the Maryland Department of Transportation (MDOT), the Secretary’s Office (TSO); the Maryland Aviation Administration (MAA); the Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA); the State Highway Administration (SHA); the Maryland Transit Administration (MTA) - and authorities that are a part of MDOT, including the Maryland Transportation Authority (MDTA) and the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (WMATA).

# **KRAMER CENTER RENOVATIONS CONTRACT AWARD:** 

Mr. Lee Edgar, Chief of Engineering for the Department of Public Works submitted to the board for their review and approval the Kramer Center Renovations Contract Award. The work involves renovation of the multi-purpose meeting room including removal and replacement of the existing skylight with asphalt shingle roof and appurtenant framing, ceiling finish, acoustical panels & lighting, modification to existing kitchen space, and associated update to plumbing and electrical systems. This fifth and final phase of renovation follows similar update to the remainder of interior spaces including new paint, flooring, acoustic panels, voice/data and lighting upgrades – a comprehensive interior renovation to interior spaces which has been largely unchanged for over thirty years. Bids were solicited in accordance with County procurement policy; seven bids were received.  There was a lower bid, however they were not responsive. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to award the Kramer Center Renovations contract to CarmelFJCo dba KBC of Fairfax, Virginia in the amount of $168,450.00 and authorize the Director of Public Works to issue the Notice of Award and execute the Contract on behalf of the County Commissioners.

# **KENT ISLAND COMMUNITY GARDEN, INC. – LEASE AGREEMENT – 210 WHITE PINE LANE STEVENSVILLE:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director of the Department of Public Works submitted to the board for their approval the Kent Island Community Garden, Inc. – Lease Agreement located at 210 White Pine Lane, Stevensville. 

Since 2015, a community garden has existed located within the Harborview subdivision on the lands of the Galilee Evangelical Lutheran Church on Cecil Drive. The church has decided to sell those lands which will displace the garden. On June 4, Mr. John McKnight, on behalf of the gardening group, reached out to Commissioner McLaughlin explaining their plight and requested assistance to find other lands to relocate their garden. The group requested permission to utilize a portion of county land, which surrounds the offices of the Fire Marshall in Stevensville. General Services and the Fire Marshal agreed, with some stipulations. The County attorney drafted the lease. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the Lease Agreement with the Kent Island Community Garden, Inc. to allow the use of a portion of the County lands located at 210 White Pine Lane for the relocation and continued existence of the community garden currently residing in the Harborview community. 

Commissioner McLaughlin then made an amended motion, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, to additionally provide a $1,500 grant to the Kent Island Community Garden, Inc. The Board unanimously agreed to this amendment. 

Following this, on a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the Lease Agreement with the Kent Island Community Garden, Inc. to allow the use of a portion of the County lands located at 210 White Pine Lane for the relocation and continued existence of the community garden, as well as to grant $1,500 to the Kent Island Community Garden, Inc. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Dumenil asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

# **CHESAPEAKE BAY EDUCATION CENTER – SEWER EXTENSION:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director of the Department of Public Works submitted to the board for their approval the Chesapeake Bay Education Center – Sewer Extension. 

A project was bid to connect the existing facilities at Chesapeake Bay Education Center, which currently relies on a septic system, to County sewer that runs along Perry’s Corner Road via a grinder pump and a 5,000-foot-long service lateral.  Only a single bid was received on September 17th from Teal Construction in the amount of $802,802 which far exceeds the budget of $325,000. The issue is about 3000-feet of the lane to the facility transects tidal marsh. The design called for a single ‘shot’ of 1.5-inch sewer line to avoid any damage to the wetlands.  A 1.5-inch pipe cannot withstand that degree of tension, i.e. it would ‘snap’ in half, so the contractor bid a 4-inch carrier pipe to act as a conduit to carry the 1.5-inch lateral, which greatly inflated the cost. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to reject the bid for the Chesapeake Bay Education Center’s Sewer Extension and direct the Department of Public Works to re-design the project to attempt to meet the approved budget amount.

# **FY26 CAPITAL FUNDING BAYSIDE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL REPAIRS SUPPORT LETTER:** 

Mr. Jeff Rank, Director of Budget and Finance submitted to the board for their approval the FY26 Capital Funding Bayside Elementary School Repairs Letter of Support and funding Commitment letter to the Interagency Commission on School Construction. 

The Board of Education is requesting a support and funding commitment letter to the Interagency Commission on School Construction for a new FY26 capital project “Bayside Elementary School – Storefront Replacement.” Although previously unplanned for the current capital plan, serious maintenance issues have been identified in the aluminum flashing around the doors and windows that need attention. The project is budgeted for $1,800,000, of which half, ($900,000) would be the County’s share. It is being proposed to use leftover bond funds from the Kent Island High School Roof Replacement project and/or reprogramming funds from other Board of Education projects to fund this project next year. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Corchiarino, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the support and funding commitment letter to the Interagency Commission on School Construction which would approve the County’s share of funding for the Bayside Elementary Storefront Replacement Project for Fiscal Year 2026.
