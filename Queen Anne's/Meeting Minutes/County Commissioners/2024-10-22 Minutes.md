# **MESONET LICENSE AGREEMENT:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director of the Department of Public Works submitted to the Board for their approval the Mesonet License Agreement. 

The State of Maryland is developing a ‘Mesonet’ network of real-time weather stations throughout the State and would like to lease 1089-ft2 (33 feet by 33 feet) to install a station on the 

October 22, 2024 

4

<!-- page 5 -->
lands of the Sanitary District. A Mesonet is a network of automated weather stations that monitor and observe ‘mesoscale’ weather events. Mesoscale refers to weather events that are between 1 and 150 miles in size and lasts from several minutes to several hours. Examples include thunderstorms, wind gusts, and heat waves. “The Maryland Department of Emergency Management and The University of Maryland have partnered in efforts to design and implement a world-class meteorological station network, comprised of approximately 70 stations, that will collect, transmit, and archive timely weather condition data. This data will be utilized to gain a better understanding of the atmosphere and rapidly changing conditions while improving forecasts and severe weather warning lead time to the public and decision makers alike. This site would be a benefit to the County’s operations in general and would specifically benefit the Sanitary District as MDE requires the District to have continuous rain event data collection which has proven difficult in the past. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the License Agreement with the University of Maryland for a Mesonet Station to be installed on the lands of the Sanitary District.

Commissioner Dumneil read Proclamation #24-41 for White Cane Awareness Day. 

**WHEREAS** , the white cane, which every blind and low vision citizen of our county has the right to carry, demonstrates and symbolizes the ability to achieve a full and independent life and the capacity to work productively in competitive employment; and 

**WHEREAS** , the white cane, by allowing every blind and low vision person to move freely and safely from place to place, makes it possible for the blind and low vision to fully participate in and contribute to our society and to live the lives they want; and 

**WHEREAS** , every citizen should be aware that the law requires that motorists and cyclists exercise appropriate caution when approaching a blind or low vision person carrying a white cane; and

**WHEREAS** , the State of Maryland, through its public agencies and with the cooperative assistance of the National Federation of the Blind of Maryland, can and should facilitate the expansion of employment opportunities for and greater acceptance of blind and low vision persons in the competitive labor market:

# **FY25 BUDGET – 457 (B) MATCH:** 

Mrs. Beverly Churchill, Director of Human Resources, submitted to the Board for their approval the FY25 Budget – 457 (b) Match. Human Resources would like to proceed with the implementation of the 401(a) match plan to be effective on January 1, 2025.  The Commissioners approved a recommendation to engage services to develop and implement a 401(a) plan on February 27, 2024. This plan would provide a $10 match per pay period for participants in the 457(b) plan. The goal of this match program is to increase participation in our current 457(b) plan and enhance retention of employees with less than five years of service. Employees must continue to contribute their own funds to the currently established 457(b) plan and Voya will be the plan administrator.  The estimated annual cost of this program is $50,000 - $60,000. 

On a motion made by Commissioner McLaughlin, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to authorize the Director of Human Resources to implement the County 401(a) match plan effective January 1, 2025.

# **COUNTY FIREWALL PURCHASE:** 

Mr. Nikolas Urban, IT Network Manager and Ms. Megan DelGaudio, IT Support Manager submitted to the Board for their approval the purchase of the County Firewall. 

The County IT is in imminent need of a new firewall. The current firewall is at the end of Engineering support (June 2024), and will not be supported at all at the end of 2025. With growing bandwidth needs and increased cyberthreats, it is of the utmost importance to ensure that the County protect its IT infrastructure and data. With the consolidation of County IT services to one department, data center redundancy and resiliency is a focal point, and the updated firewall deployment will also support Emergency Services. An RFQ was solicited for pricing and 12 bids were received. The Teneo Group provided the lowest responsive bid and has provided firewall and security consulting services to the County since 2013. The overall project (hardware, subscriptions and professional services) for the first two years totals $296,884.  The equipment will be funded by the County IT Fiber Infrastructure capital budget (with available approved funds totaling $384,258.28 after obligations). Professional Services will be covered using the ITC operating budget and a Homeland Security Grant of $39,865 will be applied to the hardware bringing the total two-year expense down to $257,019.
