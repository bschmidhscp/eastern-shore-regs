# **MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE – SPAY/NEUTER & VACCINATION GRANT – LETTER OF SUPPORT:** 

Ms. Kelly Baker, Chief, Animal Services, submitted to the board for their review the Maryland Department of Agriculture – Spay/Neuter & Vaccination Grant – Letter of Support. 

The Maryland Department of Agriculture will award approximately $850,000 in funds to eligible projects that facilitate, promote, and increase spay and neuter services for cats and dogs in Maryland. The goal of this grant is to provide 14,590 spay and neuter surgeries in 2026 for pets of low-income families. <mark>This program is funded through fees paid by the pet food industry. Since 2013, the program has funded 317 grant projects throughout the state that collectively have completed over 119,500 spay and neuter procedures.</mark> Queen Anne’s County Animal Services successfully participates in this grant program and has performed 233 surgeries on site since 2022. Providing this essential and valued service to our county residents has been very impactful.  This grant does not require a county match. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the letter of support to the Maryland Department of Agriculture for spay/neuter and vaccination grant funds for FY26.

# **SEMIANNUAL PROPERTY TAX PAYMENT SERVICE FEE:** 

Mr. Jeff Rank, Director, Budget and Finance, submitted to the Board the Semiannual Property Tax Payment Service Fee. 

The County Tax Collector is authorized to bill a service fee for the lost interest and administrative costs associated with the semiannual property tax billing if the amount of the service charge is approved by the State Department of Assessments and Taxation. The annual calculation of the service fee is related to the interest we receive from our cash investments, as the semiannual payment results in lost interest to the County. It is estimated that if a service fee based on actual lost interest revenue was implemented, the charge for a typical household would be $17.61 per household. We did not charge a service fee for the 2024-2025 tax year. Once again, we recommend that a service fee not be charged for the 2025-2026 tax year. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to submit to the Department of Assessments and Taxation an Approval Form for The Establishment of County and Municipal Service Fees for Semiannual Property Tax Payments a service charge in the amount of 0%.

# **MUTUAL AID AGREEMENT – COMMANDANT NAVAL DISTRICT WASHINGTON AND QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY:** 

Mr. Scott Haas, Chief, Department of Emergency services, submitted to the Board the Mutual Aid Agreement – Commandant Naval District Washington and Queen Anne’s County. 

This is an updated Emergency Medical Services Mutual Aid Agreement with Commandment Naval District Washington. Since most jurisdictions lack sufficient resources to manage large-scale incidents alone, these agreements ensure that jurisdictions can support one another during high-demand situations. Additionally, the agreements establish automatic mutual aid, allowing for the immediate dispatch and response of requested resources without the need for incident-specific approvals or consideration of jurisdictional boundaries. The agreement has been approved for form and legal sufficiency by the County Attorney.

The Character Counts! pillar celebrated in April is TRUSTWORTHINESS. This pillar encompasses honesty, integrity, and accountability. Our Spotlight honorees this month are trusted daily with the people most precious to us, our children. Being a public-school bus driver is often a thankless task. These individuals are TRUSTED to show up every day and safely deliver our <mark>children to their schools.</mark> 

Over the course of any day, a bus driver might be hugged, high-fived, cussed at, flipped the bird, puked on, coughed on, and hit by back packs, instruments, and gym bags. We thank them for their dedication to our students and we TRUST they will continue to show up and give us their very best.
