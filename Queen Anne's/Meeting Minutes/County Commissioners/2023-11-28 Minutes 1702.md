# **COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT PUBLIC HEARING:** 

The purpose of this hearing is to consider amending the Queen Anne's County 2011 Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan.  The following amendment proposal will be considered: 

# **TOWN OF CENTREVILLE – WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT EXPANSION** 

This text amendment reflects a request to increase the wastewater treatment capacity at the Town of Centreville’s wastewater treatment plant.  The wastewater treatment plant currently has a permitted capacity of 542,000 gallons per day and utilizes groundwater discharge via spray irrigation on a seasonal basis on its lands located at 751 Hope Road (April 1 through November 30) and a surface water discharge into Gravel Run stream the remainder of the year (December 1 through March 31).  This amendment seeks to increase the capacity to 1,000,000 gallons per day with the increase in capacity being surface water discharged year-round.  In order to accommodate this surface water discharge increase, the current wastewater treatment plant

# **COUNTY DEFERRED COMPENSATION BYLAW REVISION AND PLAN ADMINISTRATOR RETENTION:** 

Ms. Beverly Churchill, Director of the Department of Human Resources submitted to the Board for their review and approval the County Deferred Compensation Bylaw Revision and Plan Administrator Retention.   In 2020, we transitioned from three 457(b) deferred compensation vendors to only one, Voya Financial. The deferred compensation plan is a voluntary benefit available to full-time county employees to enhance their retirement income. As a result of this transition, the county established a 457 Governance Committee, which has approved bylaws that they recently made some revisions to. The Committee is asking that these bylaws be reviewed and approved and that Voya Financial also be retained for an additional three years as the county’s 457(b) plan administrator. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the County’s 457 (b) Bylaws as revised and also move to retain Voya Financial as the Deferred Compensation plan administrator for an additional three years.

# **BROADBAND - LONG DRIVEWAY PROGRAM ADDITIONAL FUNDING:** 

Ms. Megan DelGaudio, IT Support Manager, submitted to the Board for their review and approval the Broadband Long Driveway Program Additional Funding. In the interest of continuing to provide broadband to those using the Long Driveway Grant Program (LDGP) and to provide funds to supplement possible state awards to keep the LDGP funded at its current level, there is a request for an additional $250,000 from ARPA or from the Broadband Project 

November 28, 2023 

10

<!-- page 11 -->
funding to be added to the LDGP. So far, the already-provided funding has allowed for 24 households to have service in the coming weeks. There are additional contracts that are on hold pending the approval of these additional funds. Also, the state has advertised a grant that, if awarded, could provide $1,000,000 for the construction of broadband on long driveways. This grant would allow funding of up to $8,000 per driveway. The county is currently offering to pay up to 90% of the build with a cap of $13,500, so additional county funds would still be needed, and state funding could be used in conjunction with the county funds. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to approve $250,000 to continue the implementation of the Queen Anne’s County Long Driveway Program in anticipation of receiving a $1,000,000 State Grant to support the expansion of broadband. As part of the motion, Commissioners Moran and Wilson asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.
