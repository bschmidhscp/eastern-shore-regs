The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present:

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m.

Mr. Todd Mohn, PE, County Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2027 County Budget Presentation.

1. Gregory Gilbert – QAC Library Board Member thanked Commissioners

3. Jennifer Schrecongost – QACPS thanked Commissioners 

4. Raymond Aaron – FEC thanked Commissioners

6. Tamara Pleasant – Community Partnership for students 

7. Amanda Donofrio – School Funding 

8. Billy Faust – Thanked Commissioners 

9. Steve Wilson – County Budget & Ai
