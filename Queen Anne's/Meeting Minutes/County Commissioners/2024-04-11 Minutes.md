# County Commissioners minutes 2024-04-11

<!-- page 1 -->
# April 11, 2024 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil Christopher M. Corchiarino 

# **BUDGET WORK SESSION III:** 

The following Departments met with the Board and reviewed their proposed FY2025 Budget: 

Mr. Jeff Rank, Director and Mrs. Karen Rodgers, Accountant III of Budget and Finance reviewed the FY2025 Budget: 

- Capital Budget 

- Grants to Outside Agencies 

# **FY 2025 BUDGET CHANGES:** 

Mr. Jeff Rank, Director of Budget and Finance submitted to the Board for their review and approval the changes to the FY2025 Budget. Summary of the changes are listed below. All Commissioners were in an agreeance of the submitted changes. 

- Time to Care Act Setup Fee - $7500 in Consultants in HR 

- Reduce General Services, Grass Cutting Services from $125k to $75,000 

- Increase general liability / building / vehicle / etc. insurance by 8.5%, which is ~$75,000 

- Remove extra personnel from DPW $61,400 

- Remove $25,000 from QAC-TV Capital for FY25 

- Add $24,000 for Granicus to IT – Maintenance Agreements 

- Reduce State’s Attorney vehicles from $80,000 to $64,000 

- $7,500 for Queenstown Fireworks (Approved at the 4/9 Commissioner’s Meeting) 

- $250,000 for YMCA pool 

- Economic Development Part Time employee for the Historic Sites Consortium for $53,000 

- $100,000 capital for Grasonville VFD for New Construction only

<!-- page 2 -->
Total changes = +$99,600 for General Fund +$350,000 for Capital Fund 

There being no further business, they adjourned at 6:11 p.m. to meet again on Tuesday, April 23, 2024.
