May 20, 2026 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil Christopher M. Corchiarino

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 

# **QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FY2027 PROPOSED BUDGET PUBLIC HEARING:** 

Mr. Todd Mohn, PE, County Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2027 County Budget Presentation. 

The following persons spoke: 

1. Sarah Skinner – QAC Schools Food Service program 

2. Joe Gannon – Thanked Commissioners 

3. Matt Kibler – Thanked Commissioners for the support 

4. Janet Salazar – Thanked Commissioners 

5. Jay Falstad – Solar projects 

6. Coral Adams – Short staffing in schools 

7. Kirsten Boone – email comment – school funding 

The Board made closing remarks. 

There being no further business, they adjourned at 7:52 p.m. to meet again on Tuesday, May 26, 2026.
