# **MARYLAND ENERGY ADMINISTRATION LOCAL GOVERNMENT MODERNIZATION PROGRAM GRANT AGREEMENTS:** 

July 28, 2026 

1

<!-- page 2 -->
Mr. Lee Edgar, Chief of Engineering, Public Works, submitted to the board for their approval the Maryland Energy Administration Local Government Modernization Program Grant Agreements. 

The Maryland Energy Administration (MEA) has selected Queen Anne’s County to receive up to $2,414,944 in grant funding through the FY2026 Local Government Energy Modernization (L-GEM) Program. The mission of L-GEM is to support local governments as they engage in sustainable, long-term clean energy and energy efficient projects that lead to reduced energy usage and greenhouse gas emissions, increased cost savings, and robust clean energy and economic development. 

Funding has been allocated to the County through six awards across two Areas of Interest – each disbursed via a separate Grant Agreement wherein MEA will reimburse the County ninety percent and fifty-percent respectively for eligible project costs and expenditures. 

Area of Interest 1 (AOI-1) is a formula block grant for energy efficiency and clean energy projects. through which $1,076,242 has been assigned at 90% reimbursement (10% match) within an approximately three year implementation term (June 30, 2029) as follows: 

- **Agreement 1: $171,507 energy efficiency projects + $904,735 renewable energy projects** 

Area of Interest 4 (AOI-4) is a competitive implementation project grant for electrification of building systems through which $1,338,701 has been assigned at 50% reimbursement (50% match) within an approximately two (2) year implementation term (June 30, 2028) as follows: 

- **Agreement 2: $146,250 Department of Public Works 30-ton heat pump RTU + heat pump boiler retrofit** 

- **Agreement 3: $152,000 Grasonville Senior Center air source heat pumps + heat pump water heater** 

- **Agreement 4: $310,893 Health Department heat pump boilers + heat pump water heater** 

- **Agreement 5: $437,746 Liberty Building replace 2 gas-fired boilers with heat pump boilers** 

- **Agreement 6: $291,813 Foxxtown Senior Center replace gas-fired rooftop units with heat pumps** 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the Local Government Energy Modernization Program grant agreements with the Maryland Energy Administration and authorize the Department of Public Works to administer the projects.
