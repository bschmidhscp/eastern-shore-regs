# **VINCIT LOT 19-B SUBDIVISION PLAT – BOE ADMINISTRATION BUILDING:** 

Mr. Lee Edgar, Chief Engineer, Dept. of Public Works, submitted to the board for their review and approval the Vincit Lot 19-B Subdivision Plat BOE Administration Building. The new Administrative Office Building for the Board of Education is sited on a 7.278-acre parcel of land at 115 Vincit Street (lot 19-B). Encumbering approximately two-thirds of this lot the new facility was situated on the north side of the parcel so to preserve future development potential for the remaining one-third of the lot. A major subdivision plat titled, Lot 19-B - The Lands of the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County, provides for the creation of one additional buildable lot from the unused portion of Lot 19-B.  The resultant new Lot 19-E would total 2.541 acres and, following the transfer of remaining Lot 19-B to the Board of Education, could be retained for future development. The proposed subdivision received conditional approval by the Centreville Planning Commission on September 17, and with endorsement by the County Commissioners can proceed to final approval and recordation. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed that the major subdivision plat Lot 19-B – The Lands of the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County be executed and recorded thereby effecting the creation of one additional buildable lot.

# **PROCLAMATION #25-53 – BRING YOUR OWN BAG MONTH:** 

Commissioner Dumenil read Proclamation No. 25-53 – Bring Your Own Bag Month. 

**WHEREAS** , Queen Anne’s County is committed to protecting and preserving the natural beauty of our land and waterways by reducing litter and single-use plastics; and 

**WHEREAS** , single-use plastic bags are a major source of waste and pollution, impacting wildlife, clogging waterways, and contributing to landfill volume; and 

December 9, 2025 

9

<!-- page 10 -->
**WHEREAS** , reusable bags provide a simple and effective alternative that helps reduce waste, conserve resources, and support a cleaner, healthier environment for future generations; and 

**WHEREAS** , the month of December marks the height of the holiday shopping season and provides an ideal opportunity to encourage residents to make environmentally conscious choices; and 

**WHEREAS** , bringing reusable bags while shopping locally helps reduce single-use waste while supporting Queen Anne’s County’s small businesses and strengthening the local economy; and 

**WHEREAS** , Queen Anne’s County and Plastic Free QAC continue their successful partnership in advancing sustainability initiatives, building on the accomplishments of the Single-Use Boat Shrink-wrap Recycling Program, which has diverted over 13 tons of plastic from landfills since its launch in 2023; and 

**WHEREAS** , adopting the habit of using reusable bags promotes sustainability, environmental stewardship, and community pride; and 

**WHEREAS** , the County, in partnership with Plastic Free QAC, is launching a public education campaign to raise awareness and provide resources to help residents transition away from single use bags; 

**NOW, THEREFORE, WE, THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY** , do hereby proclaim December 2025 as “Bring Your Own Bag Month” in Queen Anne’s County and encourage all residents, visitors, and businesses to participate by bringing reusable bags when shopping—especially during the holiday season—and to continue this environmentally friendly practice year-round.

# **DEPARTMENT OF EMERGENCY SERVICES – MOTOROLA VESTA 9-1-1 PHONE SYSTEM REFRESH:** 

Mr. Phil English, Assistant Chief of Communications, Department of Emergency Services submitted to the Board for their approval the Department of Emergency Services – Motorola VESTA 9-1-1 Phone System Refresh. 

The Department of Emergency Services requested approval to refresh the county’s Motorola VESTA 9-1-1 phone system, originally purchased through our authorized vendor, Carousel Industries. This project is a sole source procurement, as Motorola Solutions is the exclusive provider of the VESTA 9-1-1 system and related components. The proposed refresh will maintain system compliance, ensure continued technical support, and enhance operational reliability for our 9-1-1 Center. The upgrade will include updated hardware, software, and licensing necessary to sustain current and future Next Generation 9-1-1 (NG9-1-1) functionality. Funding for this project has been approved in the Fiscal Year 2026 Capital Projects Budget in the amount of $2,205,470.90 and is 100% grant funded through the Maryland 9-1-1 Board. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to authorize the director of the Department of Emergency Services to enter into an agreement with Carousel Industries, in an amount not to exceed $2,205,470.90 for the refresh of the Motorola VESTA 9-1-1 phone system, utilizing the Maryland DoIT PBX IV Contract.  This project will be fully funded by the Maryland 9-1-1 Board. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner McLaughlin and Commissioner Wilson asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.
