# **SAFE STREETS & ROADS FOR ALL GRANT AGREEMENT:** 

Mr. Steve Cohoon, Public Facilities Planner, Dept. of Public Works submitted to the board for their approval and review the Safe Streets & Roads for all Grant Agreement. The Strategic Highway Safety Action Plan is a data driven plan that would use safety investigations, safety assessments, crash data, crash reports, and highway safety modeling to review the cause and locations of crashes throughout Queen Anne’s County. The plan would look at causes or contributing factors related to crashes, such as highway design, excessive speed, distracted driving, impaired driving, and driving behaviors in locations where multiple crashes occur. The plan would then make recommendations on ways to improve safety through engineering, education and enforcement. Once a plan is complete, the County can pursue grant funding to implement the specific recommendations of the plan. To create a SAP the County will need to hire a consultant to coordinate the effort and draft the plan. Creation of a SAP would also include the expertise of multiple agencies at State, County and Municipal levels of government, as well as significant input from the public. The County applied for a Safe Streets and Roads for All (SS4A) grant through Federal Highways (FHWA) for a project budget of up to $350,000 to complete a countywide Safety Action Plan. The grant was awarded to Queen Anne’s County with 80% of the budget ($280,000) being paid with Federal funds and a 20% ($70,000) local match. The 20% local funding match will be provided through County staff time and payroll contributions for work on the project. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the Safe Streets and Roads for All Grant agreement and authorize the County Administrator to execute the agreement.

# **PROCLAMATION #25-54 DIABETES AWARENESS MONTH:** 

Ms. Isabella Corchiarino read Proclamation #25-54 for Diabetes Awareness Month. 

**WHEREAS** , diabetes is a chronic health condition affecting millions worldwide, which can lead to serious complications and diminished quality of life; and 

**WHEREAS** , there are three primary types of diabetes: 

November 18, 2025 

8

<!-- page 9 -->
- **Gestational diabetes** develops during pregnancy in women who did not previously have diabetes. 

- **Type 1 diabetes** occurs when the body's immune system mistakenly attacks and destroys insulin-producing cells in the pancreas. Early signs include increased thirst, frequent urination, sudden weight loss, blurred vision, fatigue, and slowhealing sores. 

- **Type 2 diabetes** results from insulin resistance, where the body doesn't use insulin effectively. 

**WHEREAS** , early detection of type 1 diabetes is crucial. A simple blood sugar test can help prevent delayed or misdiagnosis, which can lead to potentially life-threatening diabetic ketoacidosis; and 

**WHEREAS** , diabetes awareness is essential to educate the public about prevention, management, and treatment; and 

**WHEREAS** , people with diabetes may need to administer insulin injections or perform fingerprick blood glucose tests in public. These are normal management practices that should be conducted without public scrutiny; and 

**WHEREAS** , increased awareness can empower individuals to seek early diagnosis and intervention, reducing the risk of complications and encouraging further research to find better treatments and a cure; and 

**WHEREAS** , November is designated as Diabetes Awareness Month, a time to promote education, awareness, and support for those living with diabetes; and 

**<mark>NOW, THEREFORE WE THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY</mark>** do hereby proclaim the month of November as Diabetes Awareness Month and encourage all citizens to learn more about diabetes, support those affected, and participate in local events aimed at raising awareness and promoting healthy lifestyles.

# **RESOLUTION #25-16 – CONFIRMATION OF CONDEMNATION PROCEEDINGS:** 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND CONFIRMING THE FILING OF CONDEMNATION PROCEEDINGS. 

WHEREAS, The County Commissioners, on July 22, 2025, authorized the filing of condemnation proceedings against property owned by Jemal’s Kent Narrows, L.L.C. and consisting of 13.987 acres and improvements north of US Route 50, east of Chester, and designated as Parcel 428, Grid 10 on Queen Anne’s County Sectional Zoning Map No. 57 (the “Property”); 

WHEREAS, the Property is further described in a deed dated December 15, 1998, and recorded in the Land Records of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland in Liber S.M. No. 670, folio 417; 

WHEREAS, such condemnation proceedings have been filed and the Defendant has questioned the authorization for the condemnation and the process followed; 

WHEREAS, these presents are executed to confirm the authorization to file and proceed with such condemnation; 

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND this 18th day of November, 2025, as follows: 

1. The Property is needed by the County for construction of a recreation center and related amenities. 

2. The County and the owner of the Property have been unable to agree to the terms of purchase of the property.
