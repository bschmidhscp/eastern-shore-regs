# County Commissioners minutes 2025-05-21

<!-- page 1 -->
May 21, 2025 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil Christopher M. Corchiarino 

# **MEETING CALLED TO ORDER:** 

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 

# **QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FY2026 PROPOSED BUDGET PUBLIC HEARING:** 

Mr. Todd Mohn, PE, County Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2026 County Budget Presentation. 

The following persons spoke: 

1. Catherine Brooks – Social Services & Health Department 

2. Edward Ramsey – Education & Infrastructure 

3. Dr. Patricia Saelens – Thanked the Commissioners 

4. Kathleen Lyons – Thanked the Commissioners 

5. Raymond Aaron – Thanked the Commissioners 

6. Shannon Bennett – Thanked the Commissioners 

7. Barbara Dawson – Thanked the Commissioners (via email) 

8. Kim Adams – Support for trail & pedestrian overpass (via email) 

9. Janet Lane – Thanked the Commissioners (via email) 

10. Peter Ayerle – Trail support (via email) 

The Board made closing remarks. 

There being no further business, they adjourned at 7:52 p.m. to meet again on Tuesday, May 27 2025.
