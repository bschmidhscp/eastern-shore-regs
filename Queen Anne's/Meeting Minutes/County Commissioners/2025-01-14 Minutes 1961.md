# **GEM III REAL PROPERTIES LLC – SEWER EASEMENT:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director of Public Works, submitted to the board for their execution the GEM III Real Properties LLC – Sewer Easement. The existing home at 1410 Romancoke Road was granted permission to connect to the SKI-4 collection mains via amendment 11-22 into the Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan. In order to connect to the sewer pipe, the property had to be granted an easement from the Matapeake Estates Homeowners Association as the pipe connecting the home had to transverse the subdivisions’ open space lands. That easement was granted to the property owner, not the County as required. This action of execution of the Easement rectifies that error. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the Easement between GEM III Real Estate and the County to allow the County to maintain the sewer service lateral serving 1410 Romancoke Road.

# **REQUEST FOR ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT INCENTIVE FUNDING – KIDS LANDING PLAYCARE:** 

Ms. Heather Tinelli, Director of Economic Tourism and Development, submitted to the board for their approval the Request for Economic Development Incentive Funding for Kids Landing Playcare. 

Kids Landing Playcare (KLP), is a sensory-smart, play-based center located in the Chesapeake Bay Business Park, Stevensville. The EDIF’s request is for $45,551 to support the acquisition of inclusive, accessible furniture and equipment necessary for KLP's start-up operations. The total project cost is $100,000, with additional funding sourced from local business sponsorships and investors. The project will be conditioned upon the creation of 5 full-time and 4 part-time positions over the next three years. The Economic Development Incentive Fund Commission reviewed KLP's application at their meeting, and subsequently approved the request. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Corchiarino, seconded by Commissioner Moran, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the disbursement of up to $45,551 from the EDIF fund to Kids Landing Playcare for the purchase of furniture and equipment. The disbursement of funds is contingent upon a signed agreement between the EDIF Commission and Kids Landing Playcare, outlining the terms and conditions of the funding and performance of the project in accordance with the application submitted to the EDIF Commission. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner McLaughlin and Commissioner Moran asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

**HB101 – PROPERTY TAX - DEADLINE TO SET COUNTY AND MUNICIPAL CORPORATION TAX RATES – ALTERATION**<sup>**OPPOSITION LETTER:**</sup> 

This is letter **opposes** House Bill 101, which proposes that Counties set property tax rates by June 1 of each year, rather than the current <u>July 1 deadline.</u> This adjustment to our budget process that would significantly affect our County’s financial operations. The bill would require us to accelerate our entire budget calendar by 30 days. This change would not only disrupt our timeline but also set back our public hearing schedule, which could create significant logistical challenges for both county staff and the public. The 30-day shift would have a ripple effect on various processes and coordination, undermining the efficiency of our operations. 

On a motion made by Commissioner McLaughlin, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the HB101 – Property Tax - Deadline to Set County and Municipal Corporation Tax Rates – Alteration<sup>Opposition Letter.</sup> 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Dumenil questions and/or made comments on this topic.
