# County Commissioners minutes 2025-04-01

<!-- page 1 -->
# April 1, 2025 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran 

Jack N. Wilson, Jr. 

Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil (Absent) Christopher M. Corchiarino 

# **BUDGET WORK SESSION II:** 

The Board, along with Mr. Jeff Rank, Director, Mrs. Karen Rodgers, Accountant III, of Budget and Finance along with Mr. Todd Mohn, County Administrator and Mrs. Stephanie Jarrell, Executive Assistant to the County Commissioners and Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2026 Budget and met with the following departments: 

- Steve Chandlee, Director, Parks and Recreation 

- James Miller, Recreation Manager, Recreation 

- Linda Steiner, Manager, Airport 

- Eric Beaufait, Manager, Golf Course 

- Breanne Waters, Fiscal Administrator, Parks and Recreation 

- James Wood, Public Landings 

- Alan Quimby, Director, Department of Public Works 

- Lee Edgar, Chief of Engineering Department of Public Works 

- Shane Moore, Chief of Roads Engineering, Department of Public Works 

- Matt Lucas, Chief Sanitary Engineer, Department of Public Works 

- Ms. Kelly Baker, Chief of Animal Services 

There being no further business, they adjourned at 6:36 p.m. to meet again on Tuesday, April 10, 2025.
