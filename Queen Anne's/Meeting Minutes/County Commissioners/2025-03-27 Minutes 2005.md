# County Commissioners minutes 2025-03-27

<!-- page 1 -->
# March 27, 2025 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil Christopher M. Corchiarino (Absent) 

# **BUDGET WORK SESSION I:** 

The Board, along with Mr. Jeff Rank, Director, Mrs. Karen Rodgers, Accountant III, of Budget and Finance along with Mr. Todd Mohn, County Administrator and Mrs. Stephanie Jarrell, Executive Assistant to the County Commissioners and Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2026 Budget and met with the following departments: 

- Dr. Patricia Saelens, Superintendent, Queen Anne’s County Board of Education 

- Ms. Janet Salazar, Director, Queen Anne’s County Library 

- Mr. Scott Haas, Director, Department of Emergency Services 

- Sheriff Hofmann, Office of the Sheriff 

There being no further business, they adjourned at 6:57 p.m. to meet again on Tuesday, April 1, 2025. 

EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT PRESIDENT
