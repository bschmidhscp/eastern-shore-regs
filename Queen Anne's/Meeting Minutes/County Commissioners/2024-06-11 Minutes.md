# **MIDSHORE BEHAVARIOL HEALTH UPDATE & LETTER OF AGREEMENT:** 

Ms. Kathryn Dilley, Executive Director of MidShore Behavioral Health, and Ms. Sarah Fegan, BH Coordinator, presented to the board the MidShore Behavioral Health Update and reviewed the following: 

- MSBH CAYAS staff participated in the Queen Anne’s County Local Care Team Meetings. 

- MSBH continued to support Crossroads Community, Inc. in the Healthy Transitions program, which is now in year five of five of a SAMHSA Grant. The grant was originally scheduled to end on 6/30/24, but funds were secured for an additional 3 months. This brings the end date for this grant to 9/30/24. This program serves youth 16-25 who have a serious mental health condition to provide transition aged youth programing including supported employment, 7 supported education, social skills groups, etc. in a youth-driven, strengths-based, developmentally sensitive, non-stigmatizing, culturally competent manner. 

- MSBH worked closely in collaboration with Queen Anne’s County Local Addictions Authority Leadership with planning and monitoring behavioral health providers and system needs in the county. 

Also submitted to the board for approval and execution was the Fiscal Year 2025 Letter of Agreement with Mid Shore Behavioral Health, Inc. allowing them to act as the agent in oversight, planning and development of the Public Behavioral Health System in Queen Anne’s County.

# **INFORMATION & COMMUNICATIONS TECHNOLOGY STANDARD OPERATING POLICIES REVISIONS:** 

Mr. Brian Riley, Director of Information Technology submitted to the Board for their review and approval the Information and Communication Technology Standard Operating Procedures #700-001 Information Technology Acceptable Use Policy, #700-004 Passwords Policy and #700-008 Helpdesk Communication Policy and make them effective June 24, 2024. 

Highlights to each Policy change is listed below: 

# **<u>700-001 - Information Technology Acceptable Use Policy</u>** 

1. This revision primarily addresses the removal of the use of any MDM (Mobile Device Management) software within the county at this time. It also contains the addition of Section 5 related to the use of AI (Artificial Intelligence) programs and the approved programs at this time. 

# 2. **<u>700-004 - Passwords Policy</u>** 

This revision primarily addresses the length of time between required password changes along with the required character length. It also addresses additions specific to password usage, sharing of passwords, guest network usage, and MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication). 

# 3. **<u>700-008 - Helpdesk Communication Policy</u>** 

This is a brand-new policy being proposed by the IT department. This is regarding best practices in requesting IT support, 24-hour support, SLA (Service Level Agreement) timers and the like.

# **MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION 2024 PRIORITY LETTER:** 

Mr. Steve Cohoon, Public Facilities Planner submitted to the Board for their review and execution of the Maryland Department of Transportation 2024 Priority Letter for inclusion in the FY 2025-2030 Consolidated Transportation Plan. 

This is the 2024 MDOT priority letter from the Queen Anne’s County Commissioners to Maryland Department of Transportation Secretary, Mr. Paul J. Wiedefeld. The letter outlines transportation priorities for inclusion in the 2025-2030 Consolidated Transportation Plan. The letter is very similar to past years letters and continues to identify the priorities for Queen Anne’s County as: 

- 1) Continue, and to the extent possible, expedite the process to add capacity crossing the Chesapeake Bay to reduce congestion in the Bay Bridge corridor. 

- 2) Construct improvements in the MD 18 corridor from Castle Marina Road to the Kent Narrows 

- 3) Support efforts to expand our bicycle and pedestrian network including planning for a pedestrian overpass to connect the Cross Island Trail with the South Island Trail 

- 4) Construct the US 50 & MD 213 interchange 

- 5) Construct at grade intersection improvements on the US 301 corridor 

- 6) Continued support of our local transit efforts 

- 7) Support regional coordination for transportation projects

# **PROCLAMATION #24-26 TURTLE AWARENESS MONTH:** 

Commissioner Corchiarino read the following Proclamation #24-26 for Turtle Awareness Month and presented to Mr. Jay Falstad. 

# **24-26** 

**WHEREAS,** turtles play a vital role in maintaining the balance of ecosystems, serving as indicators of environmental health and contributing to biodiversity; and 

**WHEREAS** , turtles face numerous threats including habitat loss, pollution, illegal trafficking, and climate change, endangering many species with extinction; and 

**WHEREAS** , the Chesapeake Tidewater Region is home to a number of aquatic, semi-aquatic and terrestrial turtles; and 

**<mark>WHEREAS</mark>** , <mark>Queen Anne’s County is home to 11 of Maryland’s 19 turtle species; and</mark> **WHEREAS** , Sea Turtle species that are found in Maryland are protected by the Endangered Species Act; and 

**WHEREAS** , the waters and woodlands of Queen Anne’s County provide important and necessary habitat for turtle survival and reproduction; and 

June 11, 2024 

9

<!-- page 10 -->
**WHEREAS** , it is our collective responsibility to raise awareness and take action to protect turtles and their habitats for future generations; and 

**WHEREAS** , Turtle Awareness Month presents an opportunity to educate communities about the importance of conservation efforts and to promote respect and empathy towards these remarkable creatures; and 

**NOW THEREFORE, WE THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY** proclaim May and June as Turtle Awareness Months. We encourage all citizens to join in efforts to conserve turtles, preserve their habitats, and support initiatives aimed at ensuring their survival by assisting turtles crossing roadways if the assistance can be done safely, to not disturb nesting turtles or any turtle laying eggs, to protect and promote turtle habitat whenever possible and to not remove or transport turtles to different regions. 

# **LETTER OF SUPPORT FOR ANNE ARUNDEL COUNTY FY24 FEDERAL TRANSIT ADMINISTRATION ELECTRONIC OR LOW-EMITTING FERRY PILOT PROGRAM GRANT APPLICATION:** 

Mrs. Heather Tinelli, Director of Economic & Tourism Development submitted to the board for approval a letter of support for Anne Arundel County FY24 Federal Transit Administration Electronic or Low-Emitting Ferry Pilot Program Grant application. This grant would provide for the purchase of several electric passenger ferries that would establish base routes with connections from Anne Arundel County to Baltimore and Queen Anne’s County.  Anne Arundel County has proposed to cover 100% of the match for this grant and has also indicated that it would include infrastructure funding as part of the application to assist with the establishing this new transportation opportunity. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the letter of support for Anne Arundel County FY24 Federal Transit Administration electronic or low-emitting ferry pilot program grant application.
