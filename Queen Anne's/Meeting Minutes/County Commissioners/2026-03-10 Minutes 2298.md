# **COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AND REPAIR FUNDS ALLOCATIONS FY2027:** 

Mr. Mike Clark, Executive Director, Housing and Family Services, submitted to the Board the Community Reinvestment and Repair Funds Allocations for FY2027. March 10, 2026 

4

<!-- page 5 -->
The Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund (CRRF), established under the Maryland Cannabis Reform Act of 2023, is an initiative designed to reinvest cannabis tax revenue into communities disproportionately impacted by cannabis prohibition. The fund focuses on promoting social equity, economic development, and addressing key community needs. The Local Management Board serves as the CRRC to administer the programs.  Duties include overseeing the public grant application process, recommending fund allocations, and ensuring transparency and community input. Beginning in FY2027, CRRF allocations are being aligned to the County’s fiscal year to improve coordination with other County funding decisions and reporting timelines. Funding levels fluctuate based on the prior-year balance and revenues received from the State. 

# **FY2027 Fund Allocations:** 

The CRRC seeks approval for the following FY2027 allocations, totaling $160,173: 

1. Minority Entrepreneur Training Accelerator (META) Program: $35,000 

   - Operating as Catalyst Q, providing leadership development and business training for minority entrepreneurs. 

2. After-School Programming (Partnering for Youth): $100,000 

   - Providing educational and recreational opportunities for youth. 

3. Homelessness Prevention Efforts **:** $0 

   - Homelessness services are expected to be supported through other County funding sources. 

4. Ad-Hoc Community Needs Funding **:** $0 

   - Due to the available funding level, ad-hoc funding will be reconsidered in FY2028. 

5. Account Balance for Growth: $25,173 

   - This balance will remain in the CRRF account to allow the fund to grow and support future allocations. If needed, these funds may also be considered to supplement homelessness prevention efforts, subject to future CRRC review. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the allocations listed for the FY2027 Community Reinvestment and Repair Funds in the amount of $160,173 effective July 1, 2026. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner McLaughlin asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.
