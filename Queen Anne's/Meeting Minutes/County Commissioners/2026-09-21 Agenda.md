A BILL ENTITLED AN ACT CONCERNING the Level of Service Standard for High Schools Under the Queen Anne’s County Adequate Public Facilities Ordinance; FOR THIS PURPOSE of increasing the level of service standard for high schools under the Queen Anne’s County Adequate Public Facilities Ordinance, Chapter 28 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County to 110% of the state-rated capacity; BY AMENDING §28-7.C.(1)(b) of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County.

# 6:30 p.m. 

# <u>Commissioner’s Sponsored Text Amendment County Ordinance No. 26-14 Cottage Industry –</u> _<u>Can be introduced and scheduled for a Public Hearing</u>_ 

A BILL ENTITLED AN ACT CONCERNING Cottage Industries in Queen Anne’s County, Maryland; FOR THE PURPOSE OF defining, providing for, and regulating Cottage Industry in Queen Anne’s County; defining “Artisan Shop” and “Cottage Industry”; providing for Minor Site Plans for Cottage Industries; permitting Cottage Industry as a use in the Agricultural (AG) District; providing supplemental standards and regulations for screening of materials necessary for Cottage Industries, uses appropriate or inappropriate, minimum lot size, setbacks, outdoor storage, parking, loading, number of employees, signage, refuse collection, and hours of operation; and generally dealing with and regulating Cottage Industry in Queen Anne’s County. 

# 6:35 p.m. 

- <u>Planning Commission Sponsored Text Amendment County Ordinance No. 26-15 Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) –</u> _<u>Can be introduced and scheduled for a Public Hearing</u>_ 

A BILL ENTITLED AN ACT CONCERNING Accessory Dwelling Units in Queen Anne’s County; FOR THE PURPOSE OF defining “Accessory Dwelling Unit;” providing for such Dwelling Units as an accessory to single-family dwelling units; providing setbacks, building height and size limitations in relation to the principal dwelling; requiring compliance with certain Critical Area regulations if located in a Resource Conservation Area; requiring adequate sewage disposal; providing for parking requirements and possible waiver or modification of such parking requirements by the Planning Director; and generally addressing, providing for, and regulating Accessory Dwelling Units in Queen Anne’s County to be in compliance with Maryland Land Use Article §4-501 through § 4-504.
