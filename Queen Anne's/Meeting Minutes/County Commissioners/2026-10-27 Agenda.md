# **LEGISLATION** 

- 5:40 p.m. <u>Public Hearing - Citizen Sponsored Text Amendment County Ordinance No. 2605 Supplemental Standards For Gas Station</u> - Amendment No. 1 

AN AMENDMENT TO A BILL ENTITLED AN ACT CONCERNING Supplemental Standards for Gas Stations to Include Fuel Pumps, Underground Fuel Storage Tanks and Islands, and Canopies in Queen Anne’s County; FOR THE PURPOSE OF amending pending County Ordinance No. 26-05 to allow the Board of Appeals to grant a variance permitting the distance between an exit/entrance to a gas station to any intersection to be no less than 75 feet. 

- 5:45 p.m. <u>Public Hearing - Citizen Sponsored Text Amendment County Ordinance No. 2606 Maximum Impervious Surface Ratio in the Suburban Commercial (SC) District - Amendment No. 1 -</u> 

AN AMENDMENT TO A BILL ENTITLED AN ACT CONCERNING An Increase to the Maximum Impervious Surface Ratio for Commercial and Industrial Uses in the Suburban Commercial (SC) District Which Were in Operation Prior to December 31, 2025, in Queen Anne’s County, Maryland; FOR THE PURPOSE of amending County Ordinance No. 26-06 to increase the maximum impervious surface ration for all uses in the Suburban Commercial (SC) Zoning District, from 0.45 (45%) to 0.65 (65%); BY AMENDING pending County Ordinance No. 26-06.

- 6:10 p.m. <u>Public Hearing - Commissioner’s Sponsored Text Amendment County Ordinance No. 26-14 Cottage Industry</u> 

A BILL ENTITLED AN ACT CONCERNING Cottage Industries in Queen Anne’s County, Maryland; FOR THE PURPOSE OF defining, providing for, and regulating Cottage Industry in Queen Anne’s County; defining “Artisan Shop” and “Cottage Industry”; providing for Minor Site Plans for Cottage Industries; permitting Cottage Industry as a use in the Agricultural (AG) District; providing supplemental standards and regulations for screening of materials necessary for Cottage Industries, uses appropriate or inappropriate, minimum lot size, setbacks, outdoor storage, parking, loading, number of employees, signage, refuse collection, and hours of operation; and generally dealing with and regulating Cottage Industry in Queen Anne’s County. 

- 6:15 p.m. <u>Public Hearing</u> - <u>Planning Commission Sponsored Text Amendment County Ordinance No. 26-15 Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU)</u> 

A BILL ENTITLED AN ACT CONCERNING Accessory Dwelling Units in Queen Anne’s County; FOR THE PURPOSE OF defining “Accessory Dwelling Unit;” providing for such Dwelling Units as an accessory to single-family dwelling units; providing setbacks, building height and size limitations in relation to the principal dwelling; requiring compliance with certain Critical Area regulations if located in a Resource Conservation Area; requiring adequate sewage disposal; providing for parking requirements and possible waiver or modification of such parking requirements by the Planning Director; and generally addressing, providing for, and regulating Accessory Dwelling Units in Queen Anne’s County to be in compliance with Maryland Land Use Article §4-501 through § 4-504. BY ADDING a definition to Chapter 18App – Appendix A: Glossary and REPEALING and REPLACING §18:1-48 of the Code of Public Local Laws in Queen Anne’s County.
