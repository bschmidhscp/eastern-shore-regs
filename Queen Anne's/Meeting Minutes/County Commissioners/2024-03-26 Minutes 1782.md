## **TEAMING AGREEMENT FOR UNIVERSITY OF MARYLAND, BALTIMORE TELE-EMS PROGRAM:** 

Mr. Scott Haas, Director of DES submitted to the Board for their review and approval the Teaming Agreement for University of Maryland, Baltimore Tele-ems Program. Tele-emsThe Department of Emergency Services is requesting authorization to enter into a Teaming Agreement with the University of Maryland, Baltimore, to initiate a pilot Tele-EMS program. "Tele-EMS" stands for "Telemedicine in Emergency Medical Services." It refers to the use of telecommunication technology in emergency medical services to provide remote medical assistance, consultation, and support to patients in emergency situations. Tele-EMS allows healthcare clinicians to assess patients, provide medical advice, coordinate care, and even deliver certain treatments remotely, which can be particularly valuable in situations where immediate on-site medical expertise is limited or unavailable. The term of this agreement is for a period ending on August 31<sup>st</sup> , 2024, and will require reauthorization each year moving forward. The agreement has been reviewed for legal sufficiency by Patrick Thompson. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to authorize the Director of the Department of Emergency Services to execute the Teaming Agreement between the Department of Emergency Services and the University of Maryland, Baltimore.

# **911 SURCHARGE FEE (PROPOSED INCREASE):** 

Mr. Scott Haas, Director of Emergency Services submitted to the Board a request to increase the 911 Surcharge Fee.  The County receives a 9-1-1 surcharge fee from each telecommunications service provider established by the Maryland Public Safety Article §1-310 and §1-311. The County’s fee can range from $0.75 to $2.25 and the State fee is $0.50 making the total charge $1.25 to $2.75 per phone line (per month). We are currently receiving the minimum $0.75 making our 9-1-1 Surcharge $1.25 per phone line.  The fee is utilized to enhance emergency response capabilities, technological upgrades and expanding services to meet the

On an annual basis, each County can request an increase to support the 9-1-1 services within its jurisdiction. The Department of Emergency Services (DES) recently completed an assessment of the fee structure on the mid-shore that illustrated Queen Anne’s County is $0.75 below its surrounding jurisdictions that include Kent, Caroline, Talbot and Dorchester counties. DES is recommending we consider increasing the County fee structure to a $1.50 to be equivalent with the mid-shore counties. The total fee would then become $2.00 per phone line per month effective July 1, 2024. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to authorize the Director of the Department of Emergency Services to request a 911 Surcharge increase from $.75 to $1.50 which will be implemented on July 1, 2024.
