# **COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN INFORMATIONAL MEETING:** 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County will hold a public hearing at 5:40 p.m. on Tuesday, May 27, 2025, in the Commissioner's meeting room located in the Liberty Building, 107 North Liberty Street, Centreville, Maryland 21617.  The purpose of the hearing is to consider amending the Queen Anne's County 2011 Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan. May 13, 2025 

5

<!-- page 6 -->
In addition to the hearing, an informational presentation was held at 5:40 p.m. on Tuesday, May 13, 2025, also in the Commissioner's meeting room. The following proposal will be considered: **BARNSTABLE** – This property is currently agricultural land consisting of two farms located on either side of the three improved properties (not included in the development proposal) the center one of which has a street address of 2032 Main Street in Chester. The proposed development is shown on Tax Map 57 as part of parcel 43 (Lowery Farm) and part of parcel 68 (Dream Farm), is approximately 13 acres in size, of which a portion immediately adjacent to Main Street is zoned Town Center (TC) and the other to the rear of the site is zoned Countryside (CS).   The proposal is to amend the property from S-3/W-3 to S-2/W-2 to accommodate a proposed development consisting of 82 townhomes. Flows from the use are anticipated to be 20,500 gpd. Sewer service will be provided via Vacuum Sewer Collection Station ‘G’ whereas water service would be provided by the Stevensville/Chester water system. 

**ASPIRE at EWING FARM -** This property is currently predominately agricultural land located at 5400 Main Street in Grasonville. It is shown on Tax Map 58-I as Parcel 40, is approximately 112 acres in size, and is zoned Grasonville Planned Residential Neighborhood (GPRN). The proposal is to amend the property from S-3/W-3 to S-2/W-2 to accommodate a proposal consisting of 72 townhomes and 218 single family lots.   Flows from the use are anticipated to be 72,500 gpd.  Sewer service will be provided via a constructed gravity sewer pump station whereas water service would be provided by the Grasonville water system.
