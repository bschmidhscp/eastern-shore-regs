# **DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS – MOOR GRANT FUNDING:** 

Warden Lamonte Cooke, Detention Center, submitted to the Board for their approval the Department of Corrections MOOR Grant Funding Request. 

All correctional facilities across the state of Maryland have been required to offer MAT services since 2023, in alignment with statewide efforts to combat the opioid epidemic. Our facility has actively participated in this initiative for the past several years, providing incarcerated individuals with access to vital treatment, counseling, and post-release support. 

July 8, 2025 

7

<!-- page 8 -->
Currently, our MAT program is managed by a single Registered Nurse. While this position has been instrumental in sustaining the program, there are operational gaps—particularly during weekends, holidays, and other periods of scheduled or emergency absence—where we must rely on assistance from Wellpath, our medical provider, to administer necessary dosing. To address this issue, we have included in our grant proposal the addition of a part-time Certified Medication Technician. This position will provide essential coverage during those times and strengthen the continuity of care for our incarcerated population. Consistent and timely dosing is critical to treatment adherence and to the overall effectiveness of MAT. 

In addition, grant funds will support the addition of a dedicated Peer Support Specialist. While the Detention Center has historically partnered with the Queen Anne’s County Health Department to provide peer services. A dedicated Peer Support Specialist who works exclusively with the population will allow for stronger one-on-one engagement, more reliable support, and improved access to recovery resources and reentry planning. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Moran, the Board unanimously agreed that the Department of Corrections accept the MOOR grant to include funding to hire a part-time contractual Certified Medication Technician and Peer Support Specialist resulting in no cost to Queen Anne’s County.

# **HUMAN RESOURCES POLICIES:** 

Ms. Beverly Churchill, Director, Human Resources, submitted to the Board for their approval Human Resources Policies. 

The Department of Human Resources recommended revisions to the following policies which have been reviewed by the Employee Communication & Morale Committee and the Department Directors. The Career Ladder and Telework policies have also been reviewed by our labor attorney. 

# <u>Character Counts Coaches and Book Buddies:</u> 

It is recommended that the Character Counts Coaches policy be expanded to include the Book Buddies program available through our libraries. The Book Buddies program is a partnership between the Queen Anne’s County Library and Queen Anne’s County Public Schools designed to support third graders who are reading below target grade level. Volunteers visit these students once per week for four weeks during the students’ lunch period to introduce them to a new and engaging book; chosen to support independent readers. The goal is to keep reading fun and to encourage students to read for enjoyment.  At the end of the 4-week program, students get to keep their favorite book to finish at home. 

July 8, 2025 

10

<!-- page 11 -->
# <u>Career Ladder Guidelines:</u> 

It is recommended that a Career Ladder Guidelines policy be adopted to replace the current Desk Audit policy. The Desk Audit policy has been in place for many years and during its initial formation, resulted in the adjustment of a number of positions. It was suspended during the recession for 9 years. since that time, the number of applications and approvals have declined.  In 2023 there were 4 applications with 2 approved.  In 2024 there were 3 applications with 2 withdrawn and 1 denied.  For comparison, in 2023 there were approximately 50 Career Ladder promotions and Market Adjustments.  In 2024 there were approximately 35 Career Ladder promotions and Market Adjustments. 

As a result, it was recommended to convert this policy to a Career Ladder policy, which is a program the Human Resources Department has been working to enhance over the past several years. Career ladders are designed to motivate employees to enhance their skills and knowledge, preparing them for greater success in their chosen career paths and provide a non-competitive advancement process.  Establishing a policy demonstrates our commitment to this program and provides consistency across the County. 

# <u>Telework</u> 

It was recommended by our labor attorney that telework remain within the State of Maryland to avoid any issues with compliance with other state laws regarding personnel management.  The policy has been adjusted accordingly. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to adopt the revised Character Counts & Book Buddies, Career Ladder Guidelines and Telework Policies to be effective in seven business days.

# **COUNTY SECURITY OPERATION CENTER/SECURITY INFORMATION AND EVENT MANAGEMENT:** 

Mr. Brian Riley, Director, Information & Communication Technologies, submitted to the Board for their approval the County Security Operation Center/Security Information and Event Management. 

The County IT has multiple solutions deployed that are monitored manually with disparate alerts being sent to various IT groups as critical issues arise.  In the interest of security and efficiency, consolidation of that management along with having a 24x7 Security Operation Center to review logs that collate alerts through a holistic Security Information and Event Management to facilitate managing critical issues is required. 

July 8, 2025 

12

<!-- page 13 -->
To address this critical gap, County IT evaluated multiple solutions to address our requirements which included Visibility & Coverage, Monitoring & Response, Compliance & Reporting and other requirements specifically to compatibility and ease of integration with existing county network and security assets.  To that end, Cyrebro was selected as the best fit for purpose and thus County IT issued a Request for Quote (RFQ) for procuring Cyrebro, which included onboarding and training, along with a two to five years of support and maintenance. 

The County received five bids, of which three were shortlisted for final evaluation. The three vendors considered were VankySoft LLC, Olakitan LLC, and Teneo Group. Each vendor submitted a complete proposal in accordance with the County’s RFQ, and their submissions were reviewed based on pricing, technical fit, integration approach, and vendor standing. County IT has worked with The Teneo Group to provide firewall and security consulting services since 2013; and, for this reason, we look to utilize their professional services for implementation of the new Cyrebro SOC/SIEM solution. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the ICT Department’s purchase of the Cyrebro SOC/SIEM solution from the Teneo Group for a total purchase price of $60,110 per annum.
