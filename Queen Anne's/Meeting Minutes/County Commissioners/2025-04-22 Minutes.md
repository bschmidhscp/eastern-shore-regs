# **MARYLAND AGRICULTURAL LAND PRESERVATION FOUNDATION MATCHING FUNDS PROGRAM:** 

Ms. Donna Landis Smith and Mr. Joe Pippin, Soil Conservation Aides, Queen Anne’s County Soil Conservation District, submitted to the board for their approval the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation Matching Funds Program. 

Queen Anne’s County is a MALPF certified County which means 80% of all Agricultural Transfer tax collected is retained by the County and 20% is remitted to the State of Maryland. The Agricultural Transfer Tax is used for easement acquisitions. Queen Anne’s County also participates in the Matching Funds Program which would enable the County to receive monies from the State. Queen Anne’s County has participated in the Matching Funds Program since 2001. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to approve $673,689 of ag transfer tax and $166,921 personal property tax (solar), plus an additional $492,723 of general funds towards the MAPLF matching funds program. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Moran, Commissioner Wilson and Commissioner McLaughlin asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

Commissioner McLaughlin read Proclamation No. 25-17, Sexual Assault Awareness Month. 

**WHEREAS** , April 2025 marks Sexual Assault Awareness Month (SAAM): a time to bring attention to the widespread issue of sexual violence and empower communities to take action; and 

**WHEREAS,** Sexual abuse, assault, and harassment affect individuals of every background. To address this, we must promote education around consent, healthy communication, and bystander intervention. By equipping individuals and communities with these tools, we can help prevent violence before it starts and foster a culture of respect and care; and 

**WHEREAS,** Everyone in our communities deserves to live in safe and supportive environments where they are treated with respect. **WHEREAS,** Nationwide, 81% of women and 43% of men reported experiencing some form of sexual harassment and/or assault in their lifetime; and 

**WHEREAS,** One in three female victims and one in four male victims of completed or attempted rape first experienced it between the ages of 11 and 17; and
