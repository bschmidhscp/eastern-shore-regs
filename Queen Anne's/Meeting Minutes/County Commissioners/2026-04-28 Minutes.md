# **PROCLAMATION NO. 26-12 MONTH OF THE YOUNG CHILD:** 

Commissioner Dumenil read Proclamation No. 26-12 Month of the Young Child. 

**WHEREAS** , the month of April has been designated the Month of the Young Child by the State of Maryland; and 

**WHEREAS,** all children are vitally important to the State and to the future of this country; and 

**WHEREAS** , a safe and stable environment for our children is of most importance to Queen Anne’s County; and 

**WHEREAS** , we know that as a community all young children deserve quality early learning environments that support them as capable and competent lifelong learners; and 

**WHEREAS,** we recognize the importance of seeing our children as a positive resource for the present and future of our community; and 

**WHEREAS** , we must ensure that our children are healthy emotionally, physically, intellectually, socially and mentally in order to promote successful growth from infancy into adulthood; and 

**WHEREAS** , we know that as a community children and their families deserve quality and accessible childcare, and early education programs build strong brains during the critical years from birth to age 5; and 

**WHEREAS** , we as a community know the importance of working with agencies and schools in helping and preparing our young children to be ready for school and continue to be successful throughout their school career; and 

**WHEREAS** , we recognize the importance of being intentional in building positive assets and opportunities for our children; and 

**WHEREAS** , we realize that it takes all community members to help our children grow in positive ways and in character; and we encourage all members of our community to: 

April 28, 2026 

6

<!-- page 7 -->
- Recognize the importance of early childhood education and care and support local early childhood programs and initiatives. 

- Celebrate the achievements and potential of our young children and advocate for policies that promote high-quality early learning opportunities for all children. 

- Engage with families and caregivers to foster a positive and supportive environment for young children. 

**WHEREAS** , we recognize that teachers and others who work with or on behalf of young children birth through 2<sup>nd</sup> grade, who make a difference in the lives of young children in Queen Anne’s County deserve thanks and recognition; and 

**WHEREAS** , we call upon all Queen Anne’s County citizens to join together to ensure kindness, love, laughter, respect and encouragement is a daily part of our children’s lives; and 

**NOW, THEREFORE, WE THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY,** do hereby designate the month of April as ‘The Month of the Young Child in Queen Anne’s County.” 

# **PROCLAMATION NO. 26-14 SEXUAL ASSAULT AWARENESS MONTH:** 

Commissioner McLaughlin read Proclamation No. 26-14 for Sexual Assault Awareness Month. 

**WHEREAS** , April 2026 marks Sexual Assault Awareness Month (SAAM), a time to bring attention to the widespread issue of sexual violence and empower communities to take action; and 

**WHEREAS** , sexual assault, abuse, and harassment affect people of every age, gender, race, and background, and every person in our community deserves safety, dignity, and respect; and 

**WHEREAS,** in Maryland, more than half of women (57.2%) have experienced sexual violence in their lifetime, and one in four Maryland men report experiencing some form of unwanted sexual contact in their lifetime, compared to 45.1% of women and 16.9% of men nationally; and 

**WHEREAS,** one in three female victims and one in four male victims of rape or attempted rape first experienced it as a child between the ages of 11 and 17; and 

**WHEREAS,** For All Seasons’ April 2026 Sexual Assault Awareness Month campaign focuses on helping Mid-Shore residents to stay informed, vigilant, and equipped to safeguard themselves and their communities against sexual violence; and

This is the 2026 MDOT priority letter from the Queen Anne’s County Commissioners to Maryland Department of Transportation Secretary Thomson. The letter outlines transportation priorities for inclusion in the upcoming 2027-2032 Consolidated Transportation Plan. The letter continues to identify the major priorities for Queen Anne’s County as: 

- 1) Construct safety improvements at US 301 & MD 405 intersection 2) Expedite the Chesapeake Bay Crossing Tier II NEPA and start engineering and design. 

- 3) Plan for future widening of US 50/301 corridor from Cox Creek to Queenstown 

- 4) Continue partnership with SHA for Ramp Management Program on Kent Island 

- 5) Construct improvements in the MD 18 corridor from Castle Marina Road to Kent Narrows 

- 6) Support planning, design and engineering for a pedestrian overpass to connect the Cross Island Trail with the South Island Trail 

- 7) Support efforts to expand our bicycle and pedestrian network 

- 8) Construct the US 50 & MD 213 interchange 

- 9) Continued support of our local transit efforts 

- 10) Support Chesapeake Bay Passenger Ferry 

- 11) Support regional coordination for transportation projects 

- 12) Support Queen Anne’s County Rail Rehabilitation identified in the Maryland State Rail Plan

Mr. Jeff Rank, Director, Budget & Finance, submitted to the Board the County Commissioners Proposed Budget Release for FY 2027. No action is being taken, this item is for informational purposes. 

The following are changes from the County Administrator’s budget which was presented on March 24<sup>th</sup> :
