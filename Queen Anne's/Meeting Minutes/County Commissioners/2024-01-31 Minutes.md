# **Maryland Road (Portion of Elm St) Special Assessment Charges for Road Construction** 

The purpose of these special assessment charges is to provide an equitable method of distributing the cost of constructing an 8 inch gravel base course, a triple surface treatment surface course, and associated drainage swales for Maryland Road, including a portion of Elm St, between Route 8 and the intersection of Elm St and New Jersey Road to the benefited property owners. 

The total estimated cost of the proposed construction is $525,000. Property owners shall be assessed on a per property owner basis of the total actual project cost. The affected properties includes all lots, which abut the aforementioned roads excepting those lots which currently abut and take access from an existing County or State road. The total project cost includes all engineering, construction, and interest on funds borrowed to finance the construction. Property owners will have the option of paying their assessment via one of the following estimated repayment plans: 



The annual payment option is computed with an interest rate of 5.0% per annum. 

Patrick Thompson, Esquire, conducted the hearing on behalf of the county and noted all legal requirements were met.

# **LETTER OF SUPPORT RE: HB293 – STATE BOAT ACT - ABANDONED OR SUNKEN RECREATIONAL VESSELS - IDENTIFICATION AND REMOVAL:** 

The Board reviewed a letter of support for HB 293. This bill would authorize the Department of Natural Resources to remove abandoned or sunken recreational vessels secured to certain apparatuses or left anchored and unattended for more than sixty days under certain circumstances. This bill would also require the Department of Natural Resources to consider and document certain factors when determining whether a recreational vessel is abandoned or sunken under certain provisions of law. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to sign the letter of support.
