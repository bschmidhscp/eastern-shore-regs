# **COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN PUBLIC HEARING – MARLING FARMS & DOMINION SEWER SERVICE AREA MAP:** 

Mr. Patrick Thompson, County Attorney, conducted the hearing on behalf of the Commissioners and noted all legal requirements had been met. 

**Marling Farms & Dominion Sewer Service Area Map** – The amendment seeks to adopt the service area map for the Marling Farms Subdivision and the community of Dominion. The areas in question have been noted as areas of Public Health Concern since at least the 1984 Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan, and every subsequent Plan since. Text briefly defining the problem remains in the 2011 Plan. The service area will carry a sewer service designation of 

May 26, 2026 

2

<!-- page 3 -->
S-3 (4 to 10 years) and the project will require a subsequent map amendment to S-2 should the project proceed. 

The Public Health Concern Maps were removed from the 2011 Plan to accommodate the adoption of the remainder of the Plan. The map needs to be amended back into the Plan in order to pursue a Public Health Exemption from the Smart Growth Coordinating Committee. This is a State committee consisting of the Secretaries of each State department who will judge the merit of the situation based upon a joint MDE and County presentation. As Marling Farms and Dominion are not within the County’s designated Priority Funding Areas, we cannot access State loan or grant funds which are essential to this project without this Exemption. When reaching out to the State in regard to setting the groundwork for the Exemption hearing (MDE has to sponsor the request on behalf of the County) the omission of the service area maps was raised as a fatal flaw, so this amendment is a pre-requisite to pursuing the Exemption. Also noted, the service area map will have to be amended from S-3 to S-2 if the project is to proceed.

# **RESOLUTION NO. 26-07 – TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON THE APPROVAL OR PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS FOR DATA CENTERS:** 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON THE APPLICATION, APPROVAL OF, OR PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS FOR DATA CENTERS IN QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY; 

WHEREAS, data centers are buildings or group of buildings, that may be large-scale, that house computer systems and infrastructure used to process, store, and transmit vast amounts of digital information and require substantial amounts of electricity, water, and land, and may place significant strains on local energy grids, water resources and public infrastructure, while also raising concerns regarding environmental impacts, noise and compatibility with residential and other land uses; 

AND WHEREAS, the need and desire for data centers have increased creating a greater concern throughout the State of Maryland and nationwide; 

AND WHEREAS, it is apparent that Queen Anne’s County’s existing zoning and other regulatory framework does not sufficiently address and account for the scale, impacts and operational characteristics of contemporary data center development; 

AND WHEREAS, a strategic and temporary moratorium on the application, processing, and approval of data center development would provide the County with the necessary time to conduct comprehensive studies, engage with residents and stakeholders, and develop appropriate policies, performance standard recommendations, and zoning regulations to guide future data center development; 

AND WHEREAS, the impact of data centers necessitates the need for a moratorium to afford the County adequate time to study and draft a Zoning Code update for this land use; May 26, 2026 

10

<!-- page 11 -->
AND WHEREAS, this moratorium is not intended to prohibit data center development permanently but to ensure that where and if such development occurs, it does so in a manner that is safe, sustainable, and in the best interests of the residents of Queen Anne’s County; 

AND THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED this 26th day of May, 2026, by the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland, as follows: 

Section 1. Neither the Queen Anne’s County Department of Planning and Zoning nor any other County Department, Agency, Board, or Commission shall accept, process, or approve any application, permit, or submittal for a data center in Queen Anne’s County for a period of twelve (12) months from the data hereof. 

Section 2. This moratorium is adopted to allow sufficient time for the County to accomplish the following: 

- a. Conduct comprehensive studies and engage with residents and stakeholders; b. Consider any revisions to the County Zoning Ordinance and any other applicable County Ordinances, Policies or Regulations; and 

- c. Consider such other matters as the County Commissioners deem appropriate to address the concerns recited herein. 

Section 3. The County Commissioners shall have and reserve the right to extend or modify this moratorium for such additional period or periods and on such terms and conditions as it deems necessary for the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Queen Anne’s County. 

Section 4.  If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase of this Resolution or of the codes hereby adopted are, for any reason, held to be unconstitutional, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Resolution or those codes. 

Section 5.  This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its enactment. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to adopt Resolution #26-07 Temporary Moratorium on the Approval or Processing of Applications for Data Centers

This additional service offering will help drive higher client volumes and revenue, contributing to local tax growth. The total project cost is estimated at $55,000.00, and Aging Stronger is requesting $50,000.00 in EDIF funding to support this purchase. The remainder will be funded by the owner. Currently, Aging Stronger employs one full-time staff member and three part-time staff members and as a direct result of this equipment purchase, the company will create one additional full-time position over the next three years. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Corchiarino, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to approve an Economic Development Incentive Fund Conditional Loan of up to $50,000 to Aging Stronger for their purchase of a DEXA device. The disbursement of funds is contingent upon a signed agreement between the Economic Development Incentive Fund Commission and Aging Stronger outlining the terms and conditions of the funding and the project’s performance as submitted in the Economic Development Incentive Fund application. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Dumenil asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.
