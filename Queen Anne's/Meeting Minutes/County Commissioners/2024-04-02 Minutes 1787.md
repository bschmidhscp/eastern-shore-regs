# **4-H PARK STORMWATER REPAIRS CONTACT:** 

Mr. Lee Edgar submitted to the Board for their review and approval the 4-H Stormwater Repairs Contract.  The 4-H Park Stormwater Repairs Contract involves installation of new and replacement storm drain, plus appurtenant site grading and stabilization, including approximately 70,000 square feet of asphalt pavement resurfacing. While proceeding with first phase storm drain installation, the contractor encountered a layer of clay beneath most of the existing asphalt areas which would inhibit proper compaction at time of second phase asphalt resurfacing. 

As mitigation for the unsuitable soils, the contractor recommends application of nine inches (9”) of soil cement as the most cost and time-effective means to bridge the clay layer and reinforce the pavement base. Soil cementing involves mixing Portland cement, soil/aggregate and water with the soils in-place. The contractor’s proposal of $308,344.72 would stabilize the subgrade of all 70,000 sq. ft. scheduled for pavement. Additionally, this remedy would be the best course of action to facilitate the targeted completion of site work by May 2024. The 4-H

Park Stormwater Repairs contract is a major component of a Park Infrastructure Upgrade project first initiated by the Queen Anne’s County 4-H Park Association in 2022 and awarded by the County Commissioners in November 2023 to Martin’s Excavation and Hauling, LLC of Centreville, Maryland. The contract is managed by the Department of Public Works in coordination with the 4-H Park Association and civil engineering consultant, Davis, Moore, Shearon and Associates, LLC (DMS). 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to accept the proposal by Martin’s Excavation and Hauling, LLC in the amount of $308,344.72 for the application of 7,226 square yards of soil cement with associated grading and improvements at the Queen Anne’s County 4-H Park and authorize the Director of Public Works to administer the additional budget authority.
