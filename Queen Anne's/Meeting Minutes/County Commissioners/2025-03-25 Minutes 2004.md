# **JARNELL FOSTER LEASE AGREEMENT:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director, Department of Public Works, submitted to the board for their approval the Jarnell Foster Lease Agreement. Residential homeowner Mr. Jarnell Foster in Stevensville is requesting permission to cross County owned land located off Church Street, Stevensville. The request for this crossing is to access a lane that has been there for thirty plus years. This lane will be the access point for a seasonal business of selling crabs. The residential homeowner has filed all necessary permits through the County’s permit office. County staff has determined that this crossing of County property poses no conflict with public utilities and/or County business.  The lease is for one year and was drafted by the County Attorney. The lease indemnifies the County from any liability and requires Mr. Foster to carry business insurance with liability coverage. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Moran, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the one-year lease agreement with Mr. Jarnell Foster giving permission to cross County owned land located off Church Street in Stevensville.

# **HIGH BRIDGE ROAD – BRIDGE REPLACEMENT & OWNERSHIP MOU:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director, Department of Public Works, submitted to the board for their approval the High Bridge Road Bridge Replacement and Ownership MOU. This bridge, located just south of Millington, is unique in that while the County owns the road, the State owns the bridge. The bridge crosses over railroad tracks which the State owns and operates. The State, in the form of the Maryland Transit Authority, wishes to eliminate this obligation and has proposed rebuilding the bridge at their expense, and then transferring the bridge to the County for all future maintenance and ultimately replacement obligations. Should the County choose to not enter into the MOU, the State will remove the bridge effectively closing the road. The MOU has been reviewed by the County Attorney. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to authorize the County Administrator to execute the Memorandum of Understanding between Queen Anne’s County and the Maryland Transit Administration to assume the ownership of the bridge on High Bridge Road once the bridge is replaced as new by the Maryland Transit Authority. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Moran and Commissioner Wilson asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

# **ANNEXATION OF THE LANDS OF PROVIDENCE AT CENTREVILLE, LLC – ZONING WAIVER REQUEST:** 

Mrs. Amy Moredock, Director, Planning and Zoning, submitted to the Board for their approval the Annexation of the Lands of Providence at Centreville, LLC. Zoning waiver request. Known as the “5-year rule”, Md. Local Government Code Ann. § 4-416 Annexation. [Planning and zoning authority] specifies that for 5 years after an annexation, the municipality may not allow development of the annexed land for land use substantially different than the authorized use or at a substantially higher density (not exceeding 50%) than could be granted under the zoning classification of the county applicable at the time of the annexation. Additionally, § 4-416 outlines that, should a county approve the 5-year waiver, then it must also expressly approve the municipality’s proposed zoning classification. The annexed land is zoned Agricultural District under current County zoning, and the Town of Centreville intends to rezone this land for mixed use development under the Traditional Neighborhood Design District (TND). The TND District includes substantially different land uses and exceeds the density limit of 50% from the county zoning, Agricultural District. The TND District is in the process of being amended in the Town. Therefore, the Town has requested a waiver from this standard and consideration of this zoning change which require approval by the County Commissioners. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Moran, the Board unanimously agreed to convey the 5-year zoning waiver and zoning reclassification of

# **LETTER OF SUPPORT – GRANT FOR THE RENOVATION OF THE TUCKER HOUSE:** 

The Queen Anne’s County Historical Society requested a letter of support from for the ongoing renovation of the Tucker House. The Tucker House is a historic landmark of cultural and architectural significance in Queen Anne’s County and the Town of Centreville. The planned renovations will strengthen the Tucker House’s structural integrity while enhancing its role as an educational and cultural resource. The proposed interior plaster restoration and exterior clapboard repairs are essential to completing the vital environmental infrastructure work carried out in previous years. These improvements will ensure that the house continues to be a vibrant and lasting part of our community for generations to come. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the letter of support for the renovation of the Tucker House for 2025 Heritage Fund Grant Preservation Maryland.
