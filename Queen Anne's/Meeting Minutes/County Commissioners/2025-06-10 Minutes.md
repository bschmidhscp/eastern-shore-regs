# **COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN:** 

A public hearing was held at 5:40 p.m. in the Commissioner's meeting room on May 27, 2025. The purpose of the hearing is to consider amending the Queen Anne's County 2011 Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan. In addition to the hearing, an informational presentation was held at 5:40 p.m. on Tuesday, May 13, 2025, also in the Commissioner's meeting room. 

**BARNSTABLE** – This property is currently agricultural land consisting of two farms located on either side of the three improved properties (not included in the development proposal) the center one of which has a street address of 2032 Main Street in Chester. The proposed development is shown on Tax Map 57 as part of parcel 43 (Lowery Farm) and part of parcel 68 (Dream Farm), is approximately 13 acres in size, of which a portion immediately adjacent to Main Street is zoned Town Center (TC) and the other to the rear of the site is zoned Countryside (CS). The proposal is to amend the property from S-3/W-3 to S-2/W-2 to accommodate a proposed development consisting of 82 townhomes. Flows from the use are anticipated to be 20,500 gpd. Sewer service will be provided via Vacuum Sewer Collection Station ‘G’ whereas water service would be provided by the Stevensville/Chester water system. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Moran, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board agreed to approve the map amendment request to upgrade the sewer and water service designations for the Barnstable property from S-3/W-3 to S-2/W-2. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Moran, Commissioner Wilson and Commissioner Dumenil and Commissioner Corchiarino asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

**ASPIRE at EWING FARM -** This property is currently predominately agricultural land located at 5400 Main Street in Grasonville. # **CHARACTER COUNTS SPOTLIGHT – JUNE 2025:** 

Commissioner Dumenil read the June 2025 Character Counts Spotlight for “Fairness”. 

FAIRNESS is the pillar Character Counts! celebrates in June. FAIRNESS encompasses the ideals of treating people justly, giving everyone a fair chance, and believing there should be equal opportunity for all. 

This month, we recognize QACPS School Administration Teams. This includes Principals, Vice Principals, School Counselors, Academic Deans, and Content Specialists. These individuals lead their schools and demonstrate fairness by establishing clear and consistent policies, providing equitable disciplinary practices, and ensuring all students have equal access to resources and <mark>opportunities.</mark> 

They are often their school’s biggest cheerleaders and work hard to advocate for their students and staff. <mark>School administrators create a learning environment where all students and staff feel valued, respected, and have the opportunity to succeed by prioritizing inclusivity, removing barriers to success, and being open to feedback from the community. We’d like to thank them for all they do.</mark>
