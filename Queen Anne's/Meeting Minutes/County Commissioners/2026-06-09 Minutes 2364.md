# **LEGISLATIVE:** 

# **COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN– MARLING FARMS & DOMINION SEWER SERVICE AREA MAP:** 

Marling Farms & Dominion Sewer Service Area Map – The amendment seeks to adopt the service area map for the Marling Farms Subdivision and the community of Dominion. The areas in question have been noted as areas of Public Health Concern since at least the 1984 June 9, 2026 

2

<!-- page 3 -->
Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan, and every subsequent Plan since. Text briefly defining the problem remains in the 2011 Plan. The service area will carry a sewer service designation of S-3 (4 to 10 years), and the project will require a subsequent map amendment to S-2, should the project proceed. 

The Public Health Concern Maps were removed from the 2011 Plan to accommodate the adoption of the remainder of the Plan. The map needs to be amended back into the Plan in order to pursue a Public Health Exemption from the Smart Growth Coordinating Committee. This is a State committee consisting of the Secretaries of each State department who will judge the merit of the situation based upon a joint MDE and County presentation. As Marling Farms and Dominion are not within the County’s designated Priority Funding Areas, we cannot access State loan or grant funds which are essential to this project without this Exemption. When reaching out to the State in regard to setting the groundwork for the Exemption hearing (MDE has to sponsor the request on behalf of the County) the omission of the service area maps was raised as a fatal flaw, so this amendment is a pre-requisite to pursuing the Exemption. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner McLaughlin, the Board unanimously agreed to adopt the Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan for Marling Farms & Dominion Sewer Service Area Map. 

As part of the motion, Commissioner Moran asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.

- Addition of a $700,000 match required to receive state grant funding for the Queen Anne’s County Detention Center project.

Chief Scott Haas, Department of Emergency Services, submitted to the Board for their approval the Rural Health Transformation Program Agreement with Maryland Institute for Emergency Medical Services Systems. The Department of Emergency Services requests approval to enter into a Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) Agreement with the Maryland Institute for Emergency Medical Services Systems (MIEMSS) to support the Queen Anne's County Whole Blood Program.
