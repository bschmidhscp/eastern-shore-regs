# County Commissioners minutes 2024-03-28

<!-- page 1 -->
# March 28, 2024 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil (Absent) Christopher M. Corchiarino 

# **BUDGET WORK SESSION I:** 

The following Departments met with the Board and reviewed their proposed FY2025 Budget: 

Mr. Jeff Rank, Director and Ms. Karen Rodgers, Accountant III of Budget and Finance reviewed the FY2025 Budget with the Departments. 

Dr. Patricia Saelens, Superintendent, Queen Anne’s County Board of Education 

Ms. Janet Salazar, Director, Queen Anne’s County Library 

Mr. Scott Haas, Director, Department of Emergency Services 

Major Dwayne Boardman Office of the Sheriff 

# **LETTER OF SUPPORT:** 

On a motion made by Commissioner Corchiarino, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to sign the letter of support to the Maryland Department of Agriculture requesting funds to spay/neuter and vaccinate pets in Queen Anne’s County.
