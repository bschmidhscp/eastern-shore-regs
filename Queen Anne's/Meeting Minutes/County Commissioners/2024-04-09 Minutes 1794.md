Mr. Jeff Rank, Director of Finance submitted to the Board for their review and approval the semiannual property tax payment service fee. The County Tax Collector is authorized to bill a service fee for the lost interest and administrative costs associated with the semiannual property tax billing if the amount of the service charge is approved by the State Department of Assessments and Taxation.  The annual calculation of the service fee is related to the interest we

# **REQUEST FOR ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT INCENTIVE FUNDING – ANCHORED HOPE HOME CARE:** 

Ms. Heather Tinelli, Director of EDC presented to the board for their review and approval a request for economic development incentive funding – anchored hope home care. The Economic Development Incentive Fund Commission (EDIF) recommends the approval of a conditional loan of up to $26,140.00 to Anchored Hope Home Care, LLC for the start-up of a home health care practice at 203 Romancoke Road.  The funds will be used to purchase equipment and electronic medical record software.  They will specialize in providing skilled nursing care to adult and pediatric clients to keep them safe in their homes while receiving highquality individualized treatment plans.  The business is expected to create 4 full-time equivalent positions and 4 part-time positions when fully staffed.  The EDIF Commission is recommending that the award be conditional upon the creation of the 4 full time positions and subject to credentialing.  The job creation requirement must be met within 3 years of and the business is to remain in full operation in the county for an additional two years. 

On a motion made by Commissioner McLaughlin, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to approve the disbursement of up to $26,140 from the Economic Development Incentive Fund to Anchored Hope Home Care, LLC for the purchase of equipment and electronic medical record expenses for their new residential home health care business in Stevensville. The disbursement of funds is contingent upon a signed agreement between the EDIF Commission and Anchored Hope Home Care, LLC, and will outline the conditions of the agreement and performance of the project in accordance with the application submitted to the EDIF Commission. As part of the motion, Commissioners Dumenil and McLaughlin asked questions and/or made comments on this topic.
