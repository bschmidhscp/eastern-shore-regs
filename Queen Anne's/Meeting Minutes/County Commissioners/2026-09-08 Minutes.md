# **NEXAMP SOLAR POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT REQUEST FOR ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATE EXECUTION:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director, Department of Public Works, submitted to the Board for their approval the Nexamp Solar Power Purchase Agreement Request for Estoppel Certificate Execution. 

In December 2020, the County entered into a Power Purchase Agreement with Nexamp Solar to construct a solar array in Cecil County and provide electricity to the County at a 10% reduced rate. After significant construction delays, the array is now nearing operation. The attached Estoppel Agreement confirms the County’s continued commitment to the Power Purchase Agreement and has been reviewed and approved by the County Attorney. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Corchiarino, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to execute the Estoppel Certificate on behalf of Nexamp (d/b/a Elkton Leeds Solar LLC) and MUFG Bank, Ltd confirming the commitment made by the County on December 10, 2020, by its execution of the Power Purchase Agreement to purchase solar power from Nexamp.

# **BARNSTABLE REQUEST FOR SEWER & WATER ALLOCATION:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby, Director, Department of Public Works, submitted to the Board for their approval the Barnstable Request for Sewer & Water Allocation. 

D & F Development LLC. (dba Barnstable) is the contract purchaser of a portion of two farms located along Rt 18/Main Street in Chester. The developer proposes to construct 82 residential “condominium townhouse” units. The site holds no allocation and will require 20,500-gpd of sewer & water allocation. The cost of the allocation, at current rates, is $1,328,400. In accordance with the latest version of ‘Schedule A’, there is approximately 50,000-gpd of residential sewer allocation remaining and uncommitted of the 3-million-gallon capacity sewerage treatment plant. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board did not grant 20,500-gpd of sewer and water allocation to D & F Development LLC for their proposed 82-unit residential development at a cost of $1,328,400 for which a 10% <u>nonrefundable*</u> deposit will be required within 30 calendar days.

# **GOLF CART USAGE PROSPECT BAY REQUEST AND COUNTYWIDE CONSIDERATIONS:** 

The Commissioners considered establishing a small committee to review the Prospect Bay request to allow golf carts on designated County roads, along with prior and potential future requests. # **DEVELOPMENT REVIEW FEE WAIVER REQUEST:** 

Ms. Heather Tinelli, Director, Economic & Tourism Development, submitted to the Board for their approval the Development Review Fee Waiver Request. 

Under Resolution 14-03, the County Commissioners may reimburse development review fees for projects demonstrating significant economic benefit. The proposed project includes two buildings totaling 96,000 square feet in the Chesapeake Bay Business Park, representing a $12 million investment and an estimated 50–200 new jobs. Given the business park’s near-capacity status and the County’s existing demand for more than 300,000 square feet of commercial space, the project is expected to provide significant economic benefit to Queen Anne’s County and qualify for consideration of fee reimbursement. Staff is asking that the Commissioners remand this request to the economic development commission for its review and recommendation at its next regular meeting. The development review fee is $19,954.
