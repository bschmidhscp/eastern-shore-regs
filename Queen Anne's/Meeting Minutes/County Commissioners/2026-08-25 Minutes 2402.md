Mr. Patrick Thompson, County Attorney, conducted the hearing on behalf of the Commissioners and noted all legal requirements had been met. 

**PROVIDENCE FARM PROPERTY** -  This property was recently annexed into the Town of Centreville. It is predominately agricultural land located between John Andrews Boulevard and Rolling Bridge Road on Ruthsburg Road (Md Rt 304), across from the High School. The property is shown on Tax Map 44F as Parcel 60, is approximately 279 acres in size and is currently zoned Agricultural (County) and would be zoned Traditional Neighborhood Development (Town). The proposal is to amend the property from S-6/W-6 (No Planned Service) to S-3/W-3 (future service) in recognition of its annexation.  A future amendment to S-2/W-2 will be required which would provide the ultimate development proposal particulars. 

The record will remain open for four weeks.

# **BUDGET AMENDMENT NO. CC-2 BOARD OF EDUCATION:** 

This budget amendment increases the FY27 Budget authority for the Board of Education Teachers’ unfunded liability by $173,425. During the FY27 budget preparation process, the Maryland Association of Counties (MACo) advised the County to budget for an increase of approximately 10% over the prior year. However, the actual FY27 invoice exceeded that estimate, resulting in the need for additional budget authority to cover the higher-than-anticipated cost. The total county funding request is $173,425, which is balanced with Prior Year Fund Balance. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Dumenil, seconded by Commissioner Corchiarino, the Board unanimously agreed to approve Budget Amendment No. CC-2 Board of Education.

# **EMERGENCY CAPITAL PROCUREMENT EDC HVAC SYSTEM LETTER OF INTENT:** 

This request is for the Commissioners to sign a letter of intent to assure the Maryland Higher Education Commission that the county intends to provide the local share of funds to design and construct the Chesapeake College Economic Development Center HVAC and Lighting Replacement Project. The EDC HVAC and Lighting Replacement Project require immediate attention. The HVAC units are 32 years old, and the existing duct lining has deteriorated and delaminated, resulting in fiberglass particles being introduced into occupied spaces. Due to these conditions, 21 college employees have been temporarily relocated, and approximately 7,500 gross square feet of the building have been closed and are currently unoccupied.
