# May 21, 2024 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil Christopher M. Corchiarino 

# **MEETING CALLED TO ORDER:** 

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 

# **QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FY2025 PROPOSED BUDGET PUBLIC HEARING:** 

Mr. Todd Mohn, PE, County Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2025 County Budget Presentation. 

The following persons spoke: 

1. Michael Francis - BOE Budget 

2. Patty Saelens - BOE Budget 

3. CC Mitchell - BOE Budget 

4. Sharon Burak - BOE Budget 

5. Bill Faust - FEC 

6. Janet Salazar - Library 

7. Dick Smith - BOE Budget 

8. Stephen Wilson - BOE Budget 

The Board made closing remarks. 

There being no further business, they adjourned at 8:05 p.m. to meet again on Wednesday, May 22, 2024.
