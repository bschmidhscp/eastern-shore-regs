# **2011 COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWERAGE PLAN – INFORMATIONAL MEETING:** 

Mr. Alan Quimby noted that the Informational Meeting allows the Commissioners to ask any questions of the applicant or staff in preparation for hearing. No questions were asked. 

**GEM III REAL PROPERTIES LLC** – This property is currently improved with a singlefamily dwelling located at 1410 Romancoke Road in Stevensville.  It is shown on Tax Map 63 as Parcel 121, is approximately 10.8 acres in size, and is zoned Neighborhood Conservation – 5- acres (NC-5).  The property is currently served via a documented failing on-site sewage disposal system and the remaining soils on the site are deemed to be unsuitable for a replacement system. The proposal is to amend the property from S-6 (No Planned Service) to S-2 (Service On Demand) with an ‘L’ designation (Limited Development) which would require any intensification on this site, other than a replacement of an existing home, to be required to reamend the Plan.  The amendment will allow the property to eventually connect to the immediately adjacent Matapeake Estates subdivision which is part of the Southern Kent Island Sanitary Project – Phase 4.   Flows from the use are anticipated to be 250 gpd. 

No public comment was given.

# **CHARACTER COUNTS MAY 2024 SPOTLIGHT:** 

Commissioners Dumenil and McLaughlin read the following: 

The Character Counts! pillar celebrated in May is CARING. This pillar is rooted in kindness, gratitude, and compassion. Two groups will be recognized tonight. 

First, EBeauty Community, a non-profit organization dedicated to providing free wigs to women undergoing treatment for cancer, alopecia, and other medically related hair loss, was founded in 2011 by Carolyn Keller, a two-time breast cancer survivor from Stevensville MD. EBeauty’s mission is to address the emotional impact of hair loss, often a significant hurdle for patients undergoing chemotherapy. Amid her own experience, Carolyn learned that access to wigs can be limited due to insurance constraints, financial barriers, or even geographic location. Carolyn set out to make a difference and grew EBeauty into the nation’s only wig-exchange program, serving individuals nationwide through its website and developing over 85 partnerships, including hospitals across the country. On May 2<sup>nd</sup> , the EBeauty Community Center for Cancer Support opened here in Queen Anne’s County. This CARING organization is a wonderful addition to our community, and we encourage you to support their mission by volunteering your time, supporting them financially, or sharing your expertise so that their program can reach more women here on the shore.

Austin Reed and Margery P. (Reed) Poitras Subdivision (SUB19-09-0013) 

- This administrative subdivision was approved and recorded in the land records in February 2020 (KBH48/84). 

- Due to a failure to comply with the obligations of the BMP affiliated with the subdivision, the property owner was required to submit and implement that Plan. 

- The Reed Buffer Management Plan was approved and recorded in July 2021 (KBH49/89). 

- There is a two-year window in which a bond is held in order to assure the survivability of plantings. During that two-year timeframe, inspections revealed noncompliance with the approved Plan. Also, during this timeframe, the property transferred to a new owner. 

- Having received no response from Mr. Reed to multiple written and verbal attempts to coordinate BMP compliance, staff received legal guidance from the Planning Attorney. 

- A compliance plan was implemented that included collaboration with the new property owners who have addressed compliance with the Reed BMP. 

- At this time, the zoning inspector has documented compliance. 

- Staff has been coordinating with a representative of Travelers Bond Insurance to call in the bond in order to reimburse the expenses incurred by the current property owners to implement the approved BMP. 

- Travelers Bond Express has agreed that their client has not met the bond obligations and has sent the County a release and assignment for payment of the claim. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Corchiarino, seconded by Commissioner Dumenil, the Board unanimously agreed to revoke Travelers Bond #107465104 which will allow the Department of Planning and Zoning to make payment to the new property owners and close the inspection case affiliated with the approval of Austin Reed and Margery P. # **CHESTERWYE CENTER – RIGHT OF RECOVERY AGREEMENT FOR GRAVEL RUN ROAD FACILITY:** 

It was noted that Chesterwye Center has a 99-year lease with the County for two residential homes situated on Gravel Run Road adjacent to the Grasonville Senior Center. The lease agreement expires in 2088. These facilities are on property owned by the County for the Senior Center. Chesterwye was awarded grant funds in the amount of $288,869 from the Department of Health Capital Projects Grant program for renovation of one of these homes which provides housing for their clients. The grant funds will provide for needed renovation, and installation of Hoyer lift systems, ADA accessible bathrooms, ADA ramps and improvements to critical living areas needs (windows, doors, flooring and roofs).  Chesterwye bid out the proposed work in early 2024 and has selected a contractor. The contractor must be approved by the Maryland State Board of Public Works, and in order to proceed, a Right of Recovery Agreement must be executed since the County is the property owner. Once the complete package can be forwarded to the Board of Public Works, they anticipate a 60 -90 day turn around on approval of the contract, at which time work may proceed.  The Right of Recovery Agreement would require the grant funds to be refunded to the State in the event the facilities are not used for the intended purpose for the next 30 years. 

On a motion made by Commissioner McLaughlin, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, the Board unanimously agreed to approve and execute the Right of Recovery Agreement for the Chesterwye Center facilities on Gravel Run Road.
