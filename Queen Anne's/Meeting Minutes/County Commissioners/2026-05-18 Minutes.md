# County Commissioners minutes 2026-05-18

<!-- page 1 -->
May 18, 2026 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil Christopher M. Corchiarino 

# **MEETING CALLED TO ORDER:** 

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 

# **QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FY2027 PROPOSED BUDGET PUBLIC HEARING:** 

Mr. Todd Mohn, PE, County Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2027 County Budget Presentation. 

The following persons spoke: 

1. Benoit Cosssart – Property taxes 

2. Dr. Matt Kibler – Thanked the Commissioners 

3. Janet Salazar – Thanked the Commissioners 

4. Rebecca Donovan – Mental Health Programs in Schools 

5. Bridget Heekin – Commissioners Budget 

6. Karl Yusavitz – Mental Health/Suicide prevention in the Schools 

7. Jesse Knight – Thanked Commissioners for Lights at Batts Neck 

8. Barbara Dawson – Thanked Commissioners for Parks and Rec Project 

9. Mary Sloane – Mental Health Programs in Schools 

10. Gurpreet Saini – Mental Health Programs in Schools 

The Board made closing remarks. 

There being no further business, they adjourned at 8:20 p.m. to meet again on Tuesday, May 19, 2026.
