# March 31, 2026 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran

The Board, along with Mr. Jeff Rank, Director, Mrs. Karen Rodgers, Budget Analyst, of Budget and Finance along with Mr. Todd Mohn, County Administrator and Mrs. Stephanie Jarrell, Executive Assistant to the County Commissioners and Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2027 Budget and met with the following departments:

- Kim Spence, Board of Elections

- James Miller, Recreation Manager, Recreation

- Eric Beaufait, Manager, Golf Course

- Matt Lucas, Chief Sanitary Engineer, Department of Public Works 

- Ms. Kelly Baker, Chief of Animal Services
