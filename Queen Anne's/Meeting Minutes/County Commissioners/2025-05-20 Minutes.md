# May 20, 2025 

The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County met this date pursuant to its last adjournment and there were present: 

James J. Moran Jack N. Wilson, Jr. Patrick McLaughlin Philip L. Dumenil Christopher M. Corchiarino 

# **MEETING CALLED TO ORDER:** 

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. 

# **QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FY2026 PROPOSED BUDGET PUBLIC HEARING:** 

Mr. Todd Mohn, PE, County Administrator reviewed the proposed FY2026 County Budget Presentation. 

The following persons spoke: 

1. Carolyn Armstrong – Thanked the Commissioners 

2. Dr. Saelens – Thanked the Commissioners 

3. Raymond Aaron – Thanked the Commissioners 

4. Rebecca Donovan – Thanked the Commissioners 

The Board made closing remarks. 

There being no further business, they adjourned at 7:43 p.m. to meet again on Wednesday, May 21, 2025.
