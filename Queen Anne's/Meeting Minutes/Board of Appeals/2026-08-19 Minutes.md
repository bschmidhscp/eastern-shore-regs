FINAL DECISION OF THE BOARD
1. Introduction

The Queen Anne's County Board of Appeals (the "Board") held a meeting on August 19,
2026 at 5:00 p.m. to consider Case No. BOA-26-05-0243 filed by Faith Bible Church of
Sudlersville for conditional use approval to construct a two-story, 35,120 sf. building to function
as a church sanctuary with classrooms and develop a new I-acre cemetery located in the
Agricultural (AG) zoning district. The subject property is located at 407 Dudley Comers Road in
Sudlersville, MD (the "Property"). The Board members present were Chairman, Craig McGinnes,
Vice-Chairman, Howard Dean, and Member Mike Lesniowski.

At the beginning of the hearing, the Board established that all requirements were met
governing the filing of the conditional use application, and proper notice of the August 19" public
hearing. Chairman McGinnes administered the oath to all who wished to testify on the application,
including the Applicant.

Il. Applicant's Request

The Applicant is seeking conditional-use approval under §18:1-14.C.(13) for conditional use
approval to construct a two-story, 35,120 sf. building to function as a church sanctuary with
classrooms and develop a new |-acre cemetery located in the Agricultural (AG) zoning district.
The uses fall under nonprofit and for-profit institutional uses which are a conditional use in the
AG zone.

IV. _ Property Description and Department of Planning & Zoning Recommendations

Doug Summers, County Planner with the Department of Planning & Zoning, presented his
Staff Report on the application. Mr. Summers stated the Applicant is seeking conditional use for
non-profit and for-profit institutional use in the AG zoning district, specifically the Applicant
Proposes to construct a two-story, 35,120 sf. building to house a church sanctuary with classrooms
and to develop a new I-acre cemetery on site.

Mr. Summers provided the Board with an overview of the history of the project, pointing
out that the Applicant previously received conditional use approval on July 2, 2025 (see BOA-23-
11-0169); however, the approval expired, thus requiring the Applicant to return to the Board as the
Code does not have a provision for extensions. He indicated that the current request is substantially
the same as previously approved with the exception of a few minor changes.

Mr. Summers stated the Property is identified as Tax Map 12 Parcel 30 Lot 2 and Tax Map
12 Parcel 34 Lot | and located in the northern portion of the County, about 1.5 miles northwest of
Sudlersville. He indicated there have been no changes in the surrounding area since the previous
approval. He testified that prior to site construction the Applicant proposes an Administrative
Subdivision to consolidate the Lots into resulting Parcel 34 Lot 1 which will be approximately 17
acres.

He stated that there are some environmental features on site, including Red Lion Branch
which is located on the Parcel just west of the site and some non-tidal wetlands and steep slopes
along the stream bank. He testified that no disturbance is proposed with any natural resource areas
or associated buffers. He indicated that the property is not located in the Critical Area nor in the
floodplain. He stated that DNR has noted the presence of a State listed threatened species
downstream of the site and has provided a letter with management guidelines.

Mr, Summers testified that forest conservation would be met on site with the retention of
existing woodland acreage. He indicated that currently there is an existing building and three

<!-- page 4 -->
trailers on site that house the church and the Pre-K-12 school. He stated the proposed use is for
an additional 35,120 sf. building for additional classrooms and storage and the church sanctuary.
He indicated the current proposal is an increase of 1,750 sf. since the previous approval and a
modest increase in impervious coverage of 0.05 ac. He indicated that the proposed connection
between the buildings has also changed as it initially was going to be a covered breezeway and it
is now planned as uncovered sidewalks. He stated that similar to the previous approval the I-acre
cemetery is proposed on the southwest portion of the property.

Mr. Summers testified that all AG district non-residential standards are met. He indicated
that stormwater management and adequate public facilities (APFO) will be addressed at the time
of site plan approval. He stated the property is served by well and septic and the site access will
remain the same off of Dudley Comers Rd. He testified that the plan meets all landscaping
standards including screening for nearby residences.

Mr. Summers concluded by stating that other than the guidance from DNR, no other
agencies reviewing the plan have offered any objections to its approval and staff does not object
to the granting of conditional use approval subject to the conditions as listed in his staff report
submitted as Planning and Zoning Exhibit 1.

V. Applicant's Presentation

Prior to the Applicant proceeding, the Board entered all exhibits (Applicant's 1-6 and P&Z
1) into evidence.

Kevin Shearon, with Davis, Bowen and Friedel, Inc., testified on behalf of the Applicant. He
indicated that the project is substantially the same as that which was previously approved by the
Board (BOA-23-11-0169). He stated the project has continued to progress, however, they had to
change architects which has resulted in some minor changes to the building plan, namely a
slightly larger building and the removal of the breezeway to avoid the requirement of adding
sprinkler systems to the original building. Mr. Shearon indicated that the Applicant continues to
work on the site plan. He stated the conditions regarding the property were outlined in the
original submittal (BOA-23-11-0169) by a letter from DMS and Associates dated March 31.
2025, and those remain the same and are the basis of the request for conditional use. The Board
took notice of the letter and admitted same as Applicant's Exhibit #7.

In response to a question by Board member Lesniowski, Mr. Shearon indicated that the
Applicant is agreeable to the conditions as outlined in the Planning and Zoning staff report.

VI. Testimony from the Public

After the Applicant presented his case, Chairman MeGinnes asked if any members of the public
wished to testify either for or in opposition, to which there was no response.

VII. Findings and Conclusions of the Board

The Board finds the testimony, application and exhibits provided by the Applicant.
including but not limited to Applicant's Exhibits #1-7, as well as the written report and testimony
provided by Mr. Summers, credible and persuasive.

The Board concludes that the evidence justifies approval of the conditional use request
with conditions identified. Based on the evidence presented, and duly considering the applicable
factors from the Queen Anne's County Code, the Board specificatly finds and concludes as follows:

I. The Board specifically adopts its finding as set forth in its decision rendered in BOA -23-
11-0169,

2. The Board finds the proposed use at the Proposed location is consistent with the general
Purpose, goals, objectives, and standards of the Comprehensive Plan, Chapter 18:1, and
any other plan, program, map, or ordinance adopted, or under consideration pursuant to
official notice, by the County.

3. The proposed use, as conditioned. will not result in a substantial or undue adverse impact
on adjacent property, the character of the neighborhood. traffic conditions, parking. public
improvements, public sites or rights-of-way, or other matters affecting the public health.
safety, and general welfare,

4. The proposed use at the proposed location will be adequately served by, and will not impose
an undue burden on, any of the required improvements referred to in Chapter 18:1. Part 7.

5. The application meets the requirements of § 18:1-123(B).

6. The Board considered those factors as identified in Maryland Annotated Code, Land Use
Article § 1-303. in determining that approval of the conditional use will not be contrary to
the Comprehensive Plan,

VIII. Decision

Based on the foregoing findings and conclusions, by a vote of three in favor and none
opposed, the Board grants to the Applicant: conditional use approval for a nonprofit institutional
