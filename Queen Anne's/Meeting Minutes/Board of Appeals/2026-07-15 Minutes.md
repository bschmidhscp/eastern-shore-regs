Introduction 

The Queen Anne’s County Board of Appeals (the “Board”) held a meeting on July 15, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. to consider Case No. BOA-24-12-0203 for a variance application filed by Patrick and Julia Nalls (hereinafter the “Applicants”). Present were Board Members Howard Dean and Michael Lesniowski, and Alternate Member, Barry Waterman. 

At the beginning of the hearing, the Board established that all requirements were met governing the filing of the variance application and proper notice of the July 15" public hearing. Board Member Dean administered the oath to all who wished to testify on the application, including the Applicants. 

##### Applicants’ Request 

The Applicants are seeking a variance to the provision of § 14:1-51.A of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County (the “Code”), to replace in-kind an existing 669 sq. ft. concrete in-ground pool, 718 sq. ft. concrete patio and 473 sq. ft. deck for a total disturbance of 1,860 sq. ft. within the 100 foot Critical Area Buffer at their Property located at 214 Breezy Acres Lane, Queenstown, Maryland (the “Property’). 

The Zoning Inspector denied the Applicants’ zoning certificate application Z24-10-0421 because the proposed pool is within the 100ft setback from tidal water which violates §14-1-51.A of the County Chesapeake Bay Critical Area Act.
