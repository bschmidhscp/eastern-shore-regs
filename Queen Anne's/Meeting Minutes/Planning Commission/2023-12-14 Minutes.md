## 4. UPDATES: 

- (A) Amy Moredock, Planning and Zoning Director, provided the following updates: (1) Correspondence and Testimony Protocol: The Planning Commission's recently approved protocol is posted on the Planning Commission website and staff is following it. 

   - (2) Midshore III Landfill: Letters received by citizens have been provided to the Commission. In response to a Public Information Act request filed with the County, all County files and documents pertinent to the history of the Midshore Landfill project have been collected and are being provided. This is not a Planning Commission or Department issue, and Moredock advised that all responses to inquiries about the Midshore III siting should come from the County Administrator. 

   - (3) Winter MACo: Moredock presented on a panel discussion regarding the benefits of government and non-government organization collaboration. 

   - (4) Pamunkey Regional Jail: members of Queen Anne's, Caroline, and Kent Counties toured the successful multi-jurisdictional facility in Virginia to gather information regarding their model, administration, and legal aspects of operation a regional facility. 

   - (5) Accessory Dwelling Unit Task Force: During the fourth meeting next week there will be a panel discussion with Planning Directors throughout the State who allow and prohibit accessory dwelling units. Surveys will be distributed to Eastern Shore builders, developers and their representatives. 

   - (6) Staff: Robert Owen was recently hired to join the enforcement team. Brian Barnshaw was hired as a new office coordinator.

5. Major Site Plan — Parcel 24 Holding, LLC — Mallard Homes SP # 23-09-0109 — Steve Johnson, County Planner, presented the highlights of the applicant's request for Major Site Plan approval for the construction of a 3-story, 12-unit, residential apartment building on .641 acres of land on Main Street, in Stevensville. 

The Planning Commission took a short break from 9:06 A.M. to 9:15 A.M. to resolve technical issues. 

Joseph Stevens, Esq., on behalf of the applicant, said the MPDUs and setbacks were discussed at Concept Plan review, and we’d be happy to go through that again if you desire. 

Tom Davis, DMS & Associates, said the pump house may be eliminated if Cult Classic expands as they will have to connect to public water and sewer. Mr. Stevens added that the pump may have to remain to serve the fire suppression system at Cult Classic. 

Mr. Stevens said that all units are required to be workforce housing due to receiving the bonus density. 

Mr. Davis stated that the ditch and electric poles make a sidewalk on this and Cult Classic problematic. He added that the Comprehensive Plan identifies the other side of MD Route 18 for sidewalk connectivity. Mr. Stevens added that they are reserving the space for a sidewalk on the property in the event it makes sense in the future. 

Chairman Dobson asked if there were members of the public who wished to be heard, at which time no comments were received. 

Upon review and further consideration, the following motion was made by Commissioner Sylvester, seconded by Commissioner Baker, and passed by unanimous voice vote: 

RESOLVED, that the Planning Commission, regarding the request by Mallard Homes for Major Site Plan approval for one (1), 3-story, 12-unit, residential apartment building, and
