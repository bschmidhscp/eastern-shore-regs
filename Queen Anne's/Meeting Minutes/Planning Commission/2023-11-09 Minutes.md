QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES November 9, 2023 

The Queen Anne’s County Planning Commission met on Thursday, November 9, 2023, at 8:45 A.M. The following members were present: Sharon Dobson, Tom Leigh, Teddy Baker, Kathy Deoudes, Bill Sylvester, and Ben Tilghman. 

Also present were Amy Moredock, Director Department of Planning and Zoning; Rob Gunter, Development Review Principal Planner; Doug Summers, Associate Planner; Steve Johnson, Development Review Planner; John Shelton, Clerk; Christopher F. Drummond, Esquire; and Sharon H. Brinster, Esquire. 

1. Public Comment — No comments received 

2. Meeting Minutes Review — Upon motion made by Commissioner Leigh, seconded by Commissioner Tilghman and passed by voice vote, the October 12, 2023 Planning Commission Minutes were approved as presented. 

3. Extension Requests — None. 

4. UPDATES: 

(A) Legislation and Legal Matters Ms. Amy Moredock, Planning and Zoning Director, provided the following updates: (1) Planning Commission Rules Amendment — Draft amendment presented. Upon review the following motion was made by Commissioner Deoudes, seconded by Commissioner Baker, and passed by unanimous voice vote: 

Resolved, that the Planning Commission, regarding the addition of Article IV, Section 5 Correspondence and Testimony Protocols to the Rules of the Queen Anne's County Planning Commission hereby finds that the current popular avenues of public engagement have created a need to set forth protocols to ensure fair and transparent management of public comments, and the Planning Commission hereby amends the Rules to include new Article IV Section 5 Correspondence and Testimony Protocols as presented, amended and Attached hereto as “Exhibit A” conditioned upon Mr. Drummond’s review and approval of the amendments made this day. 

- (2) Board of Appeals - October 16, 2023 staff led a general training session. The Board voted to change their meeting schedule from “as needed” to a standing meeting on the 34 Wednesday of each month at 5:00 P.M. in the conference room of the Vincit Building. 

- (3) Cannabis Act — (a) MACo Cannabis Symposium attended by Stephanie Jones and Amy Moredock. Survey for local community feedback regarding social equity

- (4) The Maryland Planning Commission Association’s 40" Annual Conference was held in October in the County, and Director Moredock provided a recap including an announcement that the Planning Commission received the Citizen Planners Award. 

- (5) Airport Protection Kent Island Gateway District code and map update became effective October 30, 2023.

Compiling information to standardize and streamline all of those reviews. A final report is due to the Governor in June. 

- (7) Maryland Association of Floodplain and Stormwater Managers 19" Annual Conference — was recently held and included training courses for industry experts. 

- (8) Critical Area Code update - Commission member Sylvester asked for clarification regarding the woodland provisions and the Critical Area Code update. Stephanie Jones is completing the update and hopes to present the draft for Planning Commission review by Spring 2024. 

- (9) County Solid Waste Management Plan — Commission member Sylvester asked for clarification regarding the Centreville Landfill. The site in Centreville was specified in the 1990s in a regional agreement with Kent, Talbot, and Caroline Counties to meet EPA requirements. 

5, Minor Site Plan — Chesapeake Chef Services, LLC — SP #23-04-0103 — Doug Summers, Associate Planner, provided the highlights of the applicant’s request for Minor Site Plan approval to construct a 2,656 square foot building for a commercial food operation and associated parking on .459 acres of land on Saddler Road, in Grasonville. 

David Strouss, McCrone Engineering, provided details of the site design including stormwater management, access and circulation, woodlands, and landscaping. 

Kris Wilson, Studio 50 Design, described the proposed building and compliance with Urban Commercial Design Standards. 

Kurt Peter, Chesapeake Chef Services, said the business was started in his home and there was a need for additional space. He described the “tasting room” for catering customers, take-out deli, and the kitchen space. 

Chairman Dobson askedif there were members of the public who wished to be heard, at which time no comments were received.

Upon review and further consideration, the following motion was made by Commissioner Deoudes, was seconded by Commissioner Sylvester, and passed by unanimous voice vote: 

RESOLVED, that the Planning Commission, regarding the request by Chesapeake Chef Services, LLC, for Minor Site Plan approval! for redevelopment of one parcel on Sadler Road, to include the construction of a 2,656 square foot building for a non-fast-food restaurant with associated commercial food operation and parking on .459 acres of land on Saddler Road, in Grasonville, and as more particularly described in Department of Planning & Zoning file SP #23-04-0103, hereby finds the Minor Site Plan; (1) does meet all requirements of Chapter 18, (2) will not substantially increase traffic hazards or safety concerns due to traffic generated by the proposed use, the location or orientation of the curb cuts, or the layout of internal circulation, (3) does contain a layout of buildings, parking, roads, and utilities that do not substantially increase fire, health, or other public safety hazards, (4) is adequately buffered and screened to minimize the potential adverse impacts to neighboring properties and public rights-of-way, (5) will not substantially increase stormwater drainage or pollution, (6) will not have an unreasonably adverse effect upon property values in the vicinity of the site, (7) will not adversely affect the public welfare and will provide for public safety through compliance with the State Fire Code and with any applicable County or municipal Fire Codes, (8) is compatible with the general character of the surrounding neighborhood, and (9) is consistent with the purpose, goals, and objectives of the Queen Anne’s County 2022 Comprehensive Plan, and hereby grants approval subject to the following conditions; (1) any remaining edits and/or documents required by a reviewing agency, the Departments of Public Works or Planning & Zoning be reviewed and approved, (2) the architecture, lighting, and overall site design must substantially reflect the documents provided, (3) any required legal documents must be approved, signed and recorded prior to obtaining final signatures, (4) all required bonds, sureties, review, and inspection fees must be submitted to the Departments of Public Works and Planning & Zoning as appropriate, and (5) all required signatures must be obtained. 

The Planning Commission took a short break at 9:30 A.M. and reconvened at 9:36 A.M.
