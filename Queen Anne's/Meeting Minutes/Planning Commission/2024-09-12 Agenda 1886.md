## **COUNTY ORDINANCE #24-09** 

§ 18:1-58.J Short-term residential rentals and § 18App-1 Definitions.

## **COUNTY ORDINANCE #24-13** 

§ 18App-1. Definitions. § 18:1-22 Urban Commercial (UC) District, § 18:1-23 Suburban Industrial (SI) District, § 18:1-34 Airport Protection and Kent Island Gateway (APKIG) District, and § 18:1-35 Suburban Industrial Business Employment (SIBE) District. § 18:3-5.D(1)(c) Applicability of Impact Fees, Exempt Development. 

To add the Countryside Zoning District to the list of zonings in which no development impact fees shall be imposed on a farm employee dwelling within a conservation easement.
