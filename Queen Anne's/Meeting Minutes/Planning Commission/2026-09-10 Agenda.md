## **CITIZEN SPONSORED TEXT AMENDMENT APPLICATIONS:** 

**County Ordinance #26-05, Amendment No. 1 –** Three Seas, LLC c/o Brendan Mullaney: 

§ 18:1-58.B Additional uses and regulations 

Amendment No. 1 proposed by the County Commissioners to retain the existing distance of 100 feet and the existing means of measurement from which the entrance and exit from a property that contains fuel pumps, underground fuel storage tanks, and islands, including canopies are required to be from any intersection, but allow for the opportunity for an applicant to seek a variance from the Board of Appeals to reduce the required setback to no less than 75 feet. _Stephanie Jones, Principal Planner_ 

## <u>Staff Report</u> 

**County Ordinance #26-06, Amendment No. 1 –** Deep W, LLC and Robert A. Schauber c/o Brendan Mullaney: 

§ 18:1-21.E Suburban Commercial (SC) District 

Amendment No. 1 proposed by the County Commissioners to amend the maximum impervious surface ratio for commercial and industrial uses in the SC District from 0.45 (45%) to 0.65 (65%) and to remove the operational date of December 31, 2025. _Stephanie Jones, Principal Planner_ 

## <u>Staff Report</u> 

**County Ordinance #26-09, Amendment No. 1 –** Thompson Farms, LLC c/o Jeff Thompson, Managing Partner, and Shirley F. Sheaffer: §§ 18:1-101 and 104 Transfer of Development Rights § 18:1-12 Permitted uses in open space. 

Amendment No. 1 proposed by the County Commissioners adds a prohibition to nonagricultural uses within Open Space once an administrative subdivision has been recorded. This proposal is in addition to County Ord. #26-09 (below). _Stephanie Jones, Principal Planner_
