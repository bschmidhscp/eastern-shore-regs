QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES October 12, 2023 

The Queen Anne’s County Planning Commission met on Thursday, October 12, 2023, at 8:45 A.M. The following members were present: Sharon Dobson, Tom Leigh, Jeff Reiss, Teddy Baker, Kathy Deoudes, and Ben Tilghman. 

Also present were Amy Moredock, Director Department of Planning and Zoning; Rob Gunter, Development Review Principal Planner; Stephanie Jones, Long Range Principal Planner; Rob Tracey, Long Range Planner; Steve Johnson, Development Review Planner; John Shelton, Clerk; Christopher F. Drummond, Esquire; and Sharon H. Brinster, Esquire.

4. Preliminary Major Subdivision — Land Bridge LLC (Placek’s Place) — SUB # 2305-0378 — Rob Gunter, Development Review Principal Planner, provided the history

<!-- page 2 -->
and highlights of the applicant's request for Major Subdivision approval for the creation of 10 new residential lots on 7.834 acres West of Romancoke Road, South of Victoria Drive and Stafford Road, in Stevensville. 

Christopher Drummond, Planning Commission Attorney, said the Bay City Property Owner's Association sued the County and the applicant alleging that Victoria Drive did not go to the property line. He said the Circuit Court agreed with the position of the County and applicant that the public road Victoria Drive does go to the property line, but the Association has appealed to the Appellate Court of Maryland. 

Joseph Stevens, Esq., on behalf of the applicant stated they are back before the Commission because they have made some revisions, and because the Preliminary approval! was called into question and was appealed to the Board of Appeals. He said the Board of Appeals granted our motion to dismiss because it was not a final decision, but provided a 26-page opinion expressing concern about the vote and subsequent correction. 

Barry Griffith, Lane Engineering, described the property history, forests, woodlands, wetlands, and proposed clearing. He said there will be more trees and more protected trees under this revised plan. He added that the subdivision is an infill development and is designed to reflect lot and house sizes that are similar to the neighbors. 

Jarett Byer, Lane Engineering, described the drainage and stormwater quantity and quality management. 

Mark Keeley, Traffic Concepts, stated that a full Adequate Public Facilities Study is not required due to the number of lots proposed. He described the traffic study and results concluding the subdivision would have minimal impact to the intersection at Route 8. 

Chairman Dobson asked if there were members of the public who wished to be heard, at which time the following comments were received: 

(A) Paul Placek, Stevensville, described his ownership of the property and stated that the historic house which has been refurbished will remain on the site (B) John Gott, Bay City, opined that it is irresponsible to approve the project with ongoing litigation. He expressed concern about stormwater runoff, flooding, and traffic. (C) David Blitzer, Bay City, expressed concern regarding traffic, flooding, safety, speeding, and lack of dues provided to Bay City for use of facilities. He added that they are appealing the public road determination. 

(D) Michael Timons, Victoria Drive, expressed concern over the adverse traffic impact that will directly affect him. 

(E) Chuck Krandel, Bay City, said the problem is not the houses but the traffic on roads with no sidewalks. He also expressed concern about the commercial truck traffic during construction and added delivery vehicles that will occur during and after construction.

(F) Michael Driesidel, Bay City HOA President, said they are not against the development but rather the access through Bay City. He said it is a disservice to proceed while there are conflicting views and unsettled litigation. (G) Justine Koehler, Bay City expressed opposition due to traffic and accidents. (H) Kim Adams, Bay City, echoed opposition to use of Bay City Drive, traffic, and safety. (l)Christine Carter, Oregon Road, expressed concern regarding forest removal, stormwater runoff, Route 8 traffic, and pedestrian safety. 

Upon review and further consideration, the following motion was made by Commissioner Deoudes, was seconded by Commissioner Baker, and passed by voice vote with Commissioners Deoudes, Dobson, Leigh, Baker, and Tilghman in favor, and Commissioner Reiss opposed: 

RESOLVED, that the Planning Commission, regarding the request by Land Bridge, LLC for Preliminary Subdivision approval for the subdivision identified as Placek’s Place, to administratively transfer 1.16 acres from tax map 56 parcel 300 to parcel 38 and create ten (10) new residential lots utilizing the large-lot subdivision technique, on 7.734 acres of land West of Romancoke Road and South of Victoria Drive and Stafford Road, in Stevensville, and as more particularly described in Department of Planning & Zoning File #23-05-0378, hereby finds the subdivision to be consistent with the goals and objectives of the Queen Anne’s County Zoning & Subdivision Regulations and the 2022 Comprehensive Plan, and hereby grants approval with the following conditions; (1) any remaining edits and/or documents required by a reviewing agency are reviewed and approved with the Final Subdivision review, (2) any required legal documents must be approved, signed, and recorded, (3) any required bonds, sureties, review and inspection fees must be submitted to the Departments of Public Works and Planning & Zoning as appropriate, (4) the existing septic system on parcel 300 must be abandoned according to the Department of Health standards, (5) the existing well on parcel 300 must be abandoned and sealed by a licensed well driller and a well abandonment report must be submitted to the Department of Environmental Health prior to final signatures, and (6) all required signatures must be obtained.
