5,000SF – 1.0ac <mark>$200.00/</mark> plan 1.0 – 5.0ac <mark>$400.00/</mark> plan 

## **<u>Nursery/Greenhouse SWM Review</u>** 

0.1 – 5.0ac <mark>$500.00/</mark> plan 5.1 – 10.0ac <mark>$750.00/</mark> plan 10.1 – 15.0ac <mark>$1,500.00/</mark> plan Over 15.0ac <mark>$2,250.00/</mark> plan 

## **<u>Poultry House Construction SWM Review</u>** 

<mark>$300.00</mark> fee + <mark>$200.00/</mark> poultry house planned 

_Example:_ The fee for SWM review for a proposed 6 poultry house operation would be: $1,500.00 $300.00 fee + ($200.00 x 6 planned houses) = $1,500.00

Initial Site Preliminary Investigation <mark>$400.00</mark> 

** Includes but is not limited to: Digitally drawing the site with poultry houses, manure storage, storm water management practices to best comply with MDE-AFO Program, Queen Anne’s County setback requirements, and Poultry Integrator requirements; conducting a resources inventory of the site to determine if the site will meet MDE/US-ACE non tidal wetlands compliance, Maryland Historic Trust compliance, State and Federal Sensitive Species compliance, USDA-FSA Food Security Act compliance, etc.: and coordinating with the involved agencies if any of the resources present will require a physical site visit(s) and report.
