# RESOLUTION NO. 26-10 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND AUTHORIZING THE QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND FAMILY SERVICES TO MAKE AN APPLICATION FOR A GRANT FROM THE MARYLAND AFFORDABLE HOUSING TRUST 

WHEREAS, the Maryland Affordable Housing Trust (“MAHT”) is an instrumentality of the State of Maryland created by the Maryland General Assembly in 1992; 

WHEREAS. MAHT promotes affordable housing initiatives through grants to various affordable housing agencies, entities and organizations, including County and local governments, that serve low-income households in Maryland: 

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COU. TY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND this / day of2/t of2/t .2026, as follows: 

day of2/t of2/t .2026, as follows:
