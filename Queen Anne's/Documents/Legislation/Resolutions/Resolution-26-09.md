RESOLUTION NO. 26-09 

A RESOLUTiON OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNES<sup>COUNTY,</sup> MARYLAND CLARIFYING THE IMPACT OF RESOLUTION NO. 25-12 AND<sup>PROVIDING</sup> FOR THE EXTENSION OF CERTAIN DEADLINES IMPACTED BY SUCH RESOLUTION. 

WHEREAS. on August 12. 2025, the County Cormnissioners of Queen Anne’s County. Maryland, by Resolution No. 25-12, imposed a twelve-month Moratorium on certain<sup>residential</sup> and mixed used projects; 

AND WHEREAS, the Queen Anne’s County Zoning Ordinance Section 18:1-93.B(10) provides that Conditional Use approval granted by the Queen Anne’s County Board<sup>of</sup> Appeals<sup>is</sup> void if certain deadlines are not met; 

AND WHEREAS, the effect of Resolution No. 25-12 is to effectively prevent compliance with the deadlines imposed by Code Section 18: l-93.B(10): 

AND WHEREAS, in fairness, the duration of the Moratorium should not be counted in determining when Conditional Use approval shall become null and void: 

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF , 2026, as QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND, this /day of d 

follows: 

A. The duration of the Moratorium imposed by Resolution No. 25-12, including any extension or extensions thereof shall not be counted against the one year deadlines imposed by Section 18:1-93.B(l0)(a) and any person, firm, or entity who had received Conditional Use approval within one year of August 12, 2026 shall not be required to count the time period of the Moratorium in determining compliance with Section 18:l-93.BUO)(a).
