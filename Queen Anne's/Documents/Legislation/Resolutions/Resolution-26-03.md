# RESOLUTION NO. 26-03 

A RESOLUTION OFTHE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE'S COUNTY ESTABLISHING THE PROCESS FOR CONSIDERING VARIANCES UNDER TITLE<sup>5,</sup> SUBTITLE 7 OF THE TRANSPORTATION ARTICLE OF THE ANNOTATED CODE OF MARYLAND (AVIATION OBSTRUCTION REGULATIONS) 

WHEREAS, Transportation Article, Section 5-704 provides that a political subdivision of this State may grant a variance to a rule or regulation adopted under Subtitle 7, of Title<sup>5</sup> of<sup>the</sup> Transportation Article: 

AND WHEREAS, The County Commissioners own and operate the Bay Bridge Airport in the Fourth Election District, Queen Anne's County; 

AND WHEREAS, The County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County, a political subdivision of the State of Maryland believe it advisable to establish<sup>procedures</sup> and<sup>processes to</sup> govern the application fot consideration of, and processing ofany variances; NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN<sup>ANNE'SCOUNTY this\cl*.'dayof</sup> ,<sup>2026,as follows</sup> 

A. Any application for a variance from a rule or regulation adopted under Title 5, Subtitle 7 of the Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland shall be submitted to the County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County together with any required application fee. The Application shall describe in detail the exact variance required, the reasons said variance is necessary and shall demonstrate that the granting of such variance will not endanger the public health, safety, and welfare and shall be accompanied by such plats, drawings, and other information as may be necessary for the County Commissioners to determine the matter. 

B. Upon receipt of an application for variance, the County Commissioners may, but are not required to, request cornment and recommendations on the granting of same from the Queen<sup>Anne's CountyAirport AdvisoryBoard, the</sup> Queen<sup>Anne's County Planning Commission,</sup> and any other individual, public agency, or group. All such comments and recommendations, if requested, shall be submitted to the County Commissioners within thirty (30) days of such request. 

C. The County Commissioners may condition the granting of a variance so as to require the owner of the land or structure to which the variance applies, to install, operate, and maintain at the owner's expense such markers and lights as may be necessary to indicate to aircraft the presence ofan airport hazard. In addition, the County Commissioner may impose other appropriate conditions and require that the applicant incur any costs associated with the remediation of any impacts to the operation of the airport. 

D. The County Commissioners shall notify the applicant in writing of its reasons for the approval or disapproval of an application. No application shall be granted unless the County

Commissioners find and determine that the granting of the same will not endanger the public health, safety, and welfare. 

WITNESS the hands and seals of the County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County, the day and year above written.
