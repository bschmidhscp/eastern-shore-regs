# Res-25-06

<!-- page 1 -->
<!-- Start of picture text -->
25-06<br><!-- End of picture text -->

WHEREAS. Queen Anne’s County recognizes the threat that natural and human-made hazards pose to people and property within our community; and 

WHEREAS, undertaking hazard mitigation actions will reduce the potential for harm to people and property from future hazard occurrences; and 

WHEREAS, the U.S. Congress passed the Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000, emphasizing the need for pre-disaster mitigation of potential hazards; and 

WHEREAS, the Act made available hazard mitigation grants to state and local governments; and 

WHEREAS, an adopted Hazard Mitigation Plan is required as a condition of future funding for mitigation projects under multiple FEMA pre- and post-disaster mitigation grant programs; and 

WHEREAS. Queen Anne’s County, in coordination with its municipalities and relevant stakeholders, developed the 2025 Multi-Jurisdictional Hazard Mitigation Plan to identify and prioritize actions to mitigate hazard risks; and 

WHEREAS, the plan has been reviewed and deemed approvable pending adoption by the Maryland Department of Emergency Management and FEMA; and 

WHEREAS, a public review and comment period was conducted, including a virtual Q&A session on January 7, 2025, to engage community input; and 

WHEREAS, Queen Anne’s County desires to comply with the requirements of the Disaster Mitigation Act and to augment its emergency preparedness and hazard mitigation capabilities. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY. MARYLAND: 

- I. The 2025 Queen Anne’s County Multi-Jurisdictional Hazard Mitigation Plan is hereby adopted as an official plan of Queen Anne’s County. 

2. The County’s departments and agencies are directed to implement the recommended mitigation actions, subject to the avmlability of funding and resources. 

3. The County will continue to coordinate with participating jurisdictions and stakeholders to monitor, evaluate, and update the plan as required.
