# RESOLUTION NO. 26-07 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY MORATORTUM ON THE APPLICATION, APPROVAL OF, OR PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS FOR DATA CENTERS IN QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY; 

WHEREAS, data centers are buildings or group of buildings, that may be large-scale, that house computer systems and infrastructure used to process, store, and transmit vast amounts of digital information and require substantial amounts of electricity, water, and land, and may place significant strains on local energy grids, water resources and public infrastructure, while also raising concerns regarding environmental impacts, noise and compatibility with residential and other land uses; 

AND WHEREAS, the need and desire for data centers have increased creating a greater concern throughoLit the State of Maryland and nationwide; 

AND WHEREAS, ii is apparent that Queen Anne’s County’s existing zoning and other regulatory framework does not sufficiently address and account for the scale, impacts and operational characteristics of contemporary data center development; 

AND WHEREAS, a strategic and temporary moratorium on the application, processing, and approval of data center development would provide the County with thc necessary time to conduct coinprehensivc studies, engage with residents and stakeholders, and develop appropriatc policies, performance standard recommendations. and zoning regulations to guide future data center development; 

AND WHEREAS, the impact of data centers necessitates the need for a moratorium to afford the County adequate time to study and draft a Zoning Code update for this land use; 

AND WHEREAS, this moratorium is not intended to prohibit data center development permanently but to ensure that where and if such development occurs, it does so in a manner that is safe, sustainable, and in the best interests of the residents of Queen Anne’s County; 

AND THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED this day of .2026, by thc County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland, as<sup>follows:t’</sup> 

Section 1. Neither the Queen Anne’s County Department of Planning and Zoning nor any other County Department, Agency, Board, or Commission shall accept, process, or approve any application, permit, or submittal for a data center in Queen Anne’s County for a period of twelve (12) months front the data hereof. 

Section 2. This moratorium is adopted to allow sufficient time for the County to accomplish the following: 

a. Conduct comprehensive studies and engage with residents and stakeholders;

- b. Consider any revisions to the County Zoning Ordinance and any other applicable County Ordinances. Policies or Regulationsz and 

- c. Consider such other matters as the County Commissioners deem appropriate to address the concerns recked herein. 

Section 3. The County Commissioners shall have and reserve the right to extend or modify this moratorium for such additional period or periods<sup>andonsuch</sup> terms and<sup>conditions</sup> as it deems necessary for the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens<sup>of</sup> Queen<sup>Anne’sCounty.</sup> 

Section 4. If any section .subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase of this Resolution or<sup>of</sup> the codes hereby adopted are, for any reason, held to be unconstitutional,<sup>such</sup> decision<sup>shallnot</sup> affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Resolution or those codes. 

Section 5. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its enactment.
