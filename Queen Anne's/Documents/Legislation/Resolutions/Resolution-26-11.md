# RESOLUTION NO.26-li 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, 

MARYLAND ADOPTING A NEW RATE SCHEDULE FOR THE BARCLAY WASTEWATER SUBDISTRICT SERVICE AREA. 

WHEREAS, Queen Anne’s County ([he “County”) owns and operates a wastewater system in the Town of Barclay; 

AND WHEREAS, the County is authorized by, inter alia, Chapter 29 of the Code of the Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County and §9-656 and 9-662 of the Environment Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland to adopt rates and charges to cover the operation, maintenance, and other expenses associated with such system; 

AND WHEREAS, the County has proposed a new Ratc Schedule for such system, has 

made said proposal available to the public and has conducted a public hearing, after publication 

of notice of the same, at which all persons were given an oppor unity to provide public comment 

on the proposed new Rate Schedule. 



by the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland, as follows: 

1. The attached Rate Schedule for the BARCLAY WASTEWATER 

   - SGBDISTRICTSERVICE AREA be and is hereby ADOPTED to he effective October 1,2026. 

Witness the hands and seals of the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County the day and year written above.
