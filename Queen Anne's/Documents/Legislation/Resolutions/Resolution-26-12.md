# RESOLUTION NO. 26-12 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, 

MARYLAND ADOPTING A NEW RATE SCHEDULE FOR THE SUDLERSVILLE WATER AND WASTEWATER SUBDISTRICT SERVICE AREA. 

WHEREAS, Queen Anne’s County (the “County”) owns and operates a water and wastewater system in the Town of Sudlersville: 

AND WHEREAS, the County is authorized by, inter alia, Chapter 24 of the Code of the Public Local Laws of QLIccn Anne’s County and §9-656 and 9-662 of the Environment Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland to adopt rates and charges to cover the operation, maintenance, and other expenses associated with such system; 

AND WHEREAS. the County has proposed a new Rate Schedule for such system, has 

made said proposal available to the public and has conducted a public hearing, after publication of notice of the same, at which all pci-sons were given an opportunity to provide public comment 

on the proposed new’ Rate Schedule. 



by the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland. as follows: 

- I. The attached Rate Schedule for the SUDLERSVILLE WATER AND WASTEWATER SUBDISTRICT SERVICE AREA be and is hereby ADOPTED to be effective October 1, 2026. 

Witness the hands and seals of the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County the day and 

year written above.
