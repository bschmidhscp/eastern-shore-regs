# RESOLUTION NO. 25-11 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND AUTHORIZING THE QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF COMMUNITY SERVICES —HOUSING & COMMUNITY SERVICES TO MAKE APPLICATION FOR A GRANT FROM THE MARYLAND AFFORDABLE HOUSING TRUST 

WHEREAS, the Maryland Affordable Housing Trust (“MAHT”) is an instrumentality of the State of Maryland created by the Maryland General Assembly in 1992; 

WHEREAS. MAHT promotes affordable housing initiatives through grants to various affordable housing agencies. entities and organizations, including County and local governments, that serve low-income households in Maryland; 

- NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND this day of .2025, as follows; 

   - A. The Queen Anne’s County Department of Community Services Housing & Community Services he and is hereby authorized, on behalf of Queen Anne’s County, to apply for, receive and expend grant funds from MAHT to assist in funding the Queen Anne’s County Housing Rehabilitation Program to enhance the safety, livability, and overall well-being of low-income families in the County. 

WITNESS the hands and seals of the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County the day and year above written.
