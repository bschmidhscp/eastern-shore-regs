# RECITALS 

Title VI of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act, as amended by the<sup>Water</sup> Quality<sup>Act</sup> of 1987 (the “Clean Water Act”), authorizes the United States Environmental Protection<sup>Agency</sup> (the “EPA”) to award grants to qualifying states to establish and capitalize state water pollution control revolving funds (“SRFs”) for the purpose of providing loans and certain other forms of financial assistance to finance, among other things, the construction and improvement of<sup>publicly-</sup> owned wastewater treatment facilities and the implementation of estuary conservation management plans and the implementation of nonpoint source management programs. 

As contemplated by the Clean Water Act, the General Assembly of Maryland, at its<sup>1988</sup> session, enacted the Maryland Water Infrastructure Financing Administration Act (previously

known as the Maryland Water Quality Financing Administration Act), codified at<sup>Sections9-1601</sup> through 9-1622 of the Environment Article of the Annotated Code of<sup>Maryland(asamended,the</sup> “Act”), establishing the Maryland Water Infrastructure Financing Administration (the “Administration”) and establishing an SRF designated the Maryland Water Quality Revolving Loan Fund (the “Fund”) to be maintained and administered by the Administration. The Act authorizes the Administration. among other things, to make a loan from the Fund<sup>to</sup> a<sup>“local</sup> government” (as defined in the Act) for the purpose of financing all or a portion of the cost of a “wastewater facility” (as defined in the Act). 

Queen Anne’s County, Maryland a body politic and corporate of the State of Maryland<sup>(the</sup> “Borrower”) is a “local government” within the meaning of the Act. The Borrower has applied to the Administration for loans from the Fund to assist in the financing of<sup>a</sup> certain project<sup>ofthe</sup> Borrower which constitutes a “wastewater facility” within the meaning of the Act. As contemplated by the Act, the Administration and the Borrower will enter into a loan agreement to effect and evidence the loan. The Borrower has determined that it will issue its bond,<sup>whichbond</sup> will constitute a “loan obligation” within the meaning of the Act,<sup>toevidenceitspayment</sup> obligations under the loan agreement.
