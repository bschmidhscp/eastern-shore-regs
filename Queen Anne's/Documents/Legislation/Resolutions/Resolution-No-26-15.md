RITSOLIJTION NO. 26-15 

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE'S COUNTY, MARYLAND. ADOPTING A POLICY REGARDING TEMPORARY SIGNS ON COUNTY PROPERTYAND RIGHTS OF WAY. 

WHEREAS, Queen Anne's County does not currently regulate the placement of signs, including temporary signs, on County property and County rights ofway except for County Parks which prohibit attaching or posting notices or signs on park property except by permit. 

AND WHEREAS, Queen Anne's County wishes to discourage the proliferation of such signs on County owned lands and rights ofway. 

NOWTHEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED this \t! day of Se,fkrvV>0? , 2026, by the County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County that it shall be the policy of Queen Anne's County that no sign, temporary or otherwise, shall be posted, displayed or attached to County property, including County roads and rights ofway unless expressly approved by the County Administrator or his or her designee. This policy shall apply to all County-owned land and property except County Parks which shall continue to be govemed by Section 22-19 of the Code Of Public Local Laws.
