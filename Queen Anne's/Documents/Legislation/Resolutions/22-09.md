22-09 

A RESOLUTION of the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland (the “County Commissioners” or “County”), providing for the acceptance by the County Commissioners of an offer of common-law dedication. By this Resolution, the County Commissioners accept the dedication of two roadway street beds shown as Kent Avenue and Chesapeake Avenue from Orchid Street to Azalea Street on a plat entitled “A Composite Map of Kentrnorr Air Park,” dated July, 1954 and recorded among the Land Records of Queen Anne’s County at Liber T.S.P. 17, folio 225 Plat Book TSP 1, folio 26 (the “1954 Plat”). The offer of dedication was made by the filing of the 1954 Plat among the Land Records of the County, and the acceptance of this dedication at this time is necessary to provide public sewer and water service to certain existing and platted residential lots sold in reference to the 1954 Plat.
