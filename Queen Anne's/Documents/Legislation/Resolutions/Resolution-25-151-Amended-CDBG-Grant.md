# Resolution-25-151-Amended-CDBG-Grant

<!-- page 1 -->
RESOLUTION NO. 25-15.1 

- A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND AUTHORIZING THE QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF COMMUNITY SERVICES - HOUSING & COMMUNITY SERVICES TO MAKE APPLICATION FOR A GRANT FOR THE MARYLAND STATE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT 

WHEREAS, State of Maryland through the Department of Housing and Community Development has solicited applications from eligible jurisdictions to apply for funding under the Maryland Community Development Block Grant Program; and 

WHEREAS, Queen Anne’s County is eligible to apply for funds from the Maryland Community Development Block Grant program through the Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development; and 

WHEREAS, the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County have held the required public hearing(s) related to the formulation of the Queen Anne’s Countys Block Grant Application; and 

WHEREAS, the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County understand and acknowledge that they would be responsible for completion of grant activities and any corrective actions including the repayment of funds if necessary; 

WHEREAS, the Community Development Block Grant (“CDBG”) is an instrumentality of the State of Maryland created by the United States Congress under Title I of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974; 

WHEREAS, CDBG’s objective is to develop viable communities, provide decent housing and a suitable living environment, and to expand economic opportunities, principally for persons of low and moderate income; 

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND this /t. day of44j9ep1&f7025, as follows; 

- A. The Queen Anne’s County Department of Community Services — Housing & Community Services be and is hereby authorized, on behalf of Queen Anne’s County, to apply for, receive and expend grant funds from CDBG in the amount of $136,000.00 to assist in funding repaving and striping efforts at the Fisher Manor and Riverside Estates communities to enhance the safety, livability, and overall well-being of low-income residents.
