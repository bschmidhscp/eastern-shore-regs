# A RESOLUTION REVISING THE BY-LAWS FOR THE ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION AND PROVIDING FOR THE POWERS, TERMS AND AUTHORITY FOR THE MEMBERSHIP 

Whereas, the Board of County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County is vitally concerned<sup>with</sup> the economic vitality of the County and the opportunity to enhance employment, encourage higher wage jobs. ensure a stable tax base and promote the quality of life in Queen<sup>Anne’sCounty.and,</sup> 

Whereas, the Board deems it desirable to encourage economic development for positive growth as a County endeavor, and 

Whereas, the Board has heretofore established the Queen Anne’s County Economic Development Commission through adoption of Resolution No. 17-75 and amended by Resolution No. 18-84; and 

Whereas, the Board has determined that it is advisable to make certain changes to the provisions of Resolution No. 18-84 relating to the membership of the Economic Development<sup>Commissionand</sup> certain revisions to clarify the provisions thereof 

BE IT THEREF( RE RESOLVED, by the Board of County Commissioners oI’Queen<sup>Anne’s</sup> County this day of 2024, acting pursuant to the authority of the Economic 

Development Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland that Sections<sup>I</sup> through<sup>IV</sup> of<sup>ResolutionNo.</sup> 18-84 be and are hereby AMENDED to read as follows: 

SECTION 1. The Queen Anne’s County Economic Development Commission is hereby established, hereinatier referred to as “the Economic Development Commission.” 

SECTION II. PURPOSE. The Economic Development Commission shall have the following powers and duties: 

- I. To confer with and advise the County Commissioners and municipal governing bodies on all Lnatters concerning economic development of the county. 

2. Investigate and assemble information pertaining to economic resources, labor market, industrial sites and opportunities. 

3. Disseminate information in the interest of industrial and commercial development by publication, advertising or other means. 

4. Cooperate with any interested private concern, civic or community organization, municipality, or Federal, State or County governmental agency in the attraction of new industries and in the encouragement of expansion and retention of the existing business base. 

5. Cooperate with representatives from industry sectors and businesses in the County to resolve community problems which they might have and to encourage the management of<sup>such</sup> concerns to have a healthy and constructive outcome for the County’s welfare. 

6. Periodically survey the over-all condition of the County business base and infrastructure requirements to evaluate the County economic climate and recommend services and investments conducive to commercial and industrial expansion while respecting the environmental impact.

7. Implement the Economic Development Commission vision and recommendations hereto fore presented to the County Commissioners. 

SECTION III. DIRECTORS. The Economic Development Commission shall consist of a minimum of eleven (11) members and a maximum of fifteen (15) representing at least one from each district of Queen Anne’s County and interests of economic development of the County. Of these, six members shall represent and/or be employed by one of the six largest employment sectors in Queen Anne’s County (defined by either employment or sales volume). The remaining members shall be selected “at large”. Terms shall be for a period of three (3) years beginning on January01 of each year. No member shall serve<sup>more</sup> than<sup>two</sup> (2) consecutive tenus for a total of six (6) years. 

Subsequent appointments each year shall be made by the County Commissioners<sup>from</sup> Nominees selected by the Economic Development Commission or other qualified<sup>persons.</sup> 

VACANCIES. Vacancies will be advertised and vetted in accordance with the Queen Anne’s County procedures to appoint commissions. Interested and qualified applicants will submit<sup>anapplication</sup> and their written qualifications to the Economic Development Commission for their<sup>review</sup> and recommendation to the County Commissioners. 

ELIGIBILITY. All candidates shall affirm their willingness to serve prior to nomination. All appointments shall be in the sole discretion of the Queen Anne’s County Commissioners. Commission members must adhere to County Ordinance No. 07-01 concerning Boards and Commissions in Queen Anne’s County. The ordinance reads as follows: “Any regular member of a Board or Commission in Queen Anne’s County who misses two (2) consecutive meetings, without notice, each frill twelve month period following appointment shall be asked to resign from such Board or Commission. The Board of Commissioners shall promptly appoint a replacement to fill the unexpired term of any member so resigning. Upon written request, the Board of Commissioners may reappoint the resigned member if good cause is shown, and there is unanimous agreement by the Board of Commissioners that the member be reappointed.” 

LIAISONS. The Economic Development Commission may establish non-voting liaisons with each of the following groups: (I) the elected County Commissioners, (2) government organizations responsible 1br economic, housing or community development in the County, (3) financial institutions or non-hank lenders that provide commercial, long-tenri fixed asset financing in the County, (4) community organizations dedicated to economic development in the County (such as chambers of commerce, foundations, trade associations, colleges or universities), or small business development centers, and (5) businesses Located in the County.
