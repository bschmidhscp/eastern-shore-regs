# Ordinance-24-03_Adopted

<!-- page 1 -->
# **COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. _24-03_____** 

# **A BILL ENTITLED** 

**AN ACT CONCERNING the pier length extension review by the Queen Anne ’ s County Board of Appeals;** 

**FOR THE PURPOSE of amending the accessory pier use to require a variance rather than a conditional use by the Board of Appeals in order to construct a pier at a length greater than 150 feet, as measured from the mean high water line;** 

**BY AMENDING Article VI, § 18:1-41 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne ’ s County, Maryland.** 

# **<u>SECTION I</u>** 

**BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE ’ S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Article VI, § 18:1- 41 of the Code of Public Local Laws be and is hereby AMENDED to read as follows.** 

**Article VI. Accessory Uses** 

**§ 18:1-41. Piers.** 

A pier may not extend into any body of water a distance greater than 150 feet, as measured from the mean high water line, unless the Board of Appeals ~~allows~~ **APPROVES AN APPLICATION FOR A VARIANCE SEEKING** a greater length ~~as a conditional use~~ . In no case may the length of a pier exceed 1/2 of the distance from the mean high water line to the center line of the body of water. The distance is hereby established as the tributary harbor line. All proposed piers must obtain the approval of the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers and the State of Maryland as necessary. 

# **<u>SECTION II</u>** 

**BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall be effective on the forty-sixth (46**<sup>**th**</sup> **) day following its adoption.**
