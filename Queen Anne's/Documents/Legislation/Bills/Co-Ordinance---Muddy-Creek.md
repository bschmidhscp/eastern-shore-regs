# Co-Ordinance---Muddy-Creek

<!-- page 1 -->
COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 25-14 

A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT TO CREATE THE MUDDY CREEK WATERWAYS IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT, TO AUTHORIZE A MAXIMUM EXPENDITURE ON THE PROJECT OF $600,000.00 AND TO LEVY A SPECIAL BENEFIT TAX AGAINST PROPERTIES IN THE DISTRICT TO REPAY SUMS BORROWED AND EXPENDED THEREON AND TO PROVIDE FOR THE COLLECTION THEREOF 

WHEREAS the State of Maryland has authorized local governments to establish waterway improvement districts in accordance with the provisions of Natural Resources Article, Section 8-705; and 

WHEREAS certain property owners have made a written request for the creation of such a district on the waters of Muddy Creek for the purpose of having maintenance dredging performed on the channel in said Creek; 

SECTION ONE: NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Muddy Creek Waterway Improvement District be and the same hereby is created, said District to include the parcels of land listed on Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated herein; and said District to further include the waters of Muddy Creek which are surrounded by the aforementioned properties.  The County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County be and are hereby established as the District Council of the Muddy Creek Waterway Improvement District. 

SECTION TWO: By creating this District, the County authorizes the borrowing of up to $600,000.00 from the Waterway Improvement Fund, which said moneys shall be repaid by the aforesaid property owners over a period of twenty-five (25) years. 

SECTION THREE:  The repayment of the borrowing authorized under Section Two shall be by means of a special assessment or tax against each property owner whose property lies within the District and repayable over a period of twenty-five (25) years without interest as follows: 

- A. The properties listed on Exhibit A shall each be responsible for 1/51 the total construction costs. 

SECTION FOUR: The sums due hereunder shall be valid charges and assessments against the real property indicated and shall be liens upon such property to be collected in the same manner as County taxes are collected.  The lien herein created shall run with the land until paid in full.  The first such payment shall be due and payable on July 1 of the first calendar year in which payment of the borrowing authorized herein is due to the State of Maryland.

<!-- page 2 -->
SECTION FIVE: Be it further enacted that this Bill shall take effect on the fortysixth day following its passage. 

BILL INTRODUCED:  December 9, 2025 INTRODUCED BY:  Commissioner Corchiarino 

PUBLIC HEARING HELD:  January 13, 2026
