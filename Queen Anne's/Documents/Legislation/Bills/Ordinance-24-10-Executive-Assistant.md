# Ordinance-24-10-Executive-Assistant

# **COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 24-10** 

# **A BILL ENTITLED** 

**AN ACT CONCERNING the Executive Assistant and Deputy Executive Assistant to the County Commissioners of Queen Anne ’ s County;** 

**FOR THE PURPOSE of eliminating the provision that the Executive Assistant and Deputy Executive Assistant to the County Commissioners of Queen Anne ’ s County shall serve at their pleasure; making such positions merit system employees; and generally revising the employment status of such employees.** 

**BY AMENDING Section 4-7. A. of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne ’ s County, Maryland.** 

# **<u>SECTION I</u>** 

**BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE ’ S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Section 4-7A. of the Code of Public Local Laws be and is hereby AMENDED to read as follows:** 

**Chapter 4.  Board of County Commissioners** 

**Article II.  Executive Assistant; Deputy Executive Assistant** 

**§ 4-7.  Appointment; bond; oath; duties, compensation.** 

- **A. Appointment.** 

   - **(1) The County Commissioners shall appoint an Executive Assistant and may appoint a Deputy Executive Assistant.** 

   - **~~(2) The Executive Assistant and Deputy Executive Assistant serve at the pleasure of the County Commissioners.~~**
