# COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 26-13 

A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING the Prohibition of Political Signs on County Property; 

FOR THE PURPOSE OF prohibiting political signs on Queen Anne’s County property; BY ADDING Section 18:1-81 A.(2)(l)[6] to the Code of Public Local Laws in Queen Anne’s County, Maryland. 

# SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that a new Section 18:1-81A.(2)(1)[6] be ADDED to the Code of Public Local Laws in Queen Anne’s County to read as follows: 

§18:1-81 A… 

(2)… 

(1)… 

[6] No political signs will be permitted on property owned by Queen Anne’s County <u>or by the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County including, without limiting the foregoing, all County roads and rights of way.</u> 

# SECTION II 

AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance will be effective on the fortysixth (46<sup>th</sup> ) day following its adoption.
