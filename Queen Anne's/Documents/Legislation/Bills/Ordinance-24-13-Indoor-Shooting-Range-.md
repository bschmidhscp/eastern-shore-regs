# Ordinance-24-13-Indoor-Shooting-Range-

<!-- page 1 -->
# COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 24-13 

# A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Indoor Shooting Ranges in Queen Anne’s County; 

FOR THE PURPOSE OF providing a definition for “Shooting Range, Indoor”; providing that Indoor Shooting Ranges are permitted uses in the Urban Commercial (UC) District, the Suburban Industrial (SI) District, the Airport Protection and Kent Island Gateway District, and the Suburban Industrial Business Employment (SIBE) District in Queen Anne’s County; providing that such use shall not be located within 100 feet of any residential use; and otherwise defining, regulating and addressing indoor shooting ranges in Queen Anne’s County; 

BY AMENDING §18 App-i, §18:1-22, §18:1-23; §18:1-34 and §18:1-35 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland; 

# SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Sections 18 App-i, 18:1-22, 18:1-23, 18:1-34 and 18:135 be and are hereby AMENDED to read as set forth on the attached. 

# SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall be effective on the forty-sixth<sup>(46t9</sup> day following its adoption. 

INTRODUCED BY: Commissioner Corchiarino 

DATE: September 24, 2024
