# 2077

# **`COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 04-37`** 

```
A BILL ENTITLED
```

```
AN ACT CONCERNING a Reduction of the Threshold
Requirement for Existing and Projected School Population for
Determination of Adequacy under the Interim Adequate Public
Facilities Ordinance of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland (IAPFO),
Title 28 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s
County (1996 Ed.).
```

```
FOR THE PURPOSE of reducing the threshold for adequacy
under the IAPFO from 120% to 100% of the state-rated capacity
per school affected;
```

```
BY AMENDING Section 28-502(a) of the Code of Public Local
Laws of Queen Anne’s County, (1996 Ed.).
```

# **<u>`SECTION I`</u>** 

```
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S
COUNTY, MARYLAND that Section 28-502(a) of the Code of Public
Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County (1996 Ed.) be amended to
read as follows:
```

# **`28-502. Threshold requirements.`** 

- **`(a) Public Schools.`** 

```
Public schools in the service area of the proposed
development shall be considered adequate if:
```

- **`(1) The existing and projected school population, together with the school population projected to be generated from the proposed development, is 100% or less of the state-rated capacity per school affected or the Board of Education determines that exceeding capacity will not be detrimental to the curriculum or quality of education; or`** 

- **`(2) The County is scheduled to initiate construction within the first two years of the adopted six-year CIP, such additional schools or school improvements as are necessary in combination with existing schools to comply with paragraph (1) above; or`** 

- **`(3) The applicant agrees to undertake school`**

<!-- page 2 -->
```
construction or improvements necessary to  meet
paragraph (1) above; or
```

- **`(4) The applicant agrees to contribute to the financing of specific improvements in accordance with the CIP that will comply with paragraph (1) above.`** 

# **<u>`SECTION II`</u>** 

```
BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall be
effective on the forty-sixth day following its passage.
```
