# COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 26-15 

# A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Accessory Dwelling Units in Queen Anne’s County; 

FOR THE PURPOSE OF defining “Accessory Dwelling Unit;” providing for such Dwelling Units as an accessory to single-family dwelling units; providing setbacks, building height and size limitations in relation to the principal dwelling; requiring compliance with certain Critical Area regulations if located in a Resource Conservation Area; requiring adequate sewage disposal; providing for parking requirements and possible waiver or modification of such parking requirements by the Planning Director; and generally addressing, providing for, and regulating Accessory Dwelling Units in Queen Anne’s County to be in compliance with Maryland Land Use Article §4-501 through § 4-504. 

BY ADDING a definition to Chapter 18App – Appendix A: Glossary and REPEALING and REPLACING §18:1-48 of the Code of Public Local Laws in Queen Anne’s County. 

# SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that a definition of “Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU)” be ADDED to Chapter 18App-Appendix A: Glossary and that §18:1-48 be REPEALED and REPLACED all as shown on “Exhibit A” attached hereto and incorporated herein. 

# SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall be effective on the forty-sixth (46<sup>th</sup> ) day following its adoption.

**ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT (ADU) –** 

- **A. A SECONDARY** **_DWELLING UNIT_ THAT IS ON THE SAME,** **_LOT_ , PARCEL, OR** **_TRACT_ AS THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT_ .** 

- **B. THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT_ MUST BE A** **_SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL DWELLING UNIT_ .** 

- **C. MAY NOT COVER AN AREA OF THE** **_LOT_ GREATER THAN 75% OF THE AREA COVERED BY AND SUBORDINATE IN USE TO THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT_ .** 

- **D. ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT (ADU) INCLUDES A STRUCTURE THAT IS DETACHED OR ATTACHED TO THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT._**

- **A. IN GENERAL. ONE** **_ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT_ PER** **_LOT_ OR PARCEL MAY BE ERECTED OR MAINTAINED SECONDARY TO A** **_SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL DWELLING,_ PROVIDED THAT:** 

   - **(1) THE** **_UNIT_ MAY EITHER BE:** 

      - A) **DETACHED FROM THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT_ OR** ; 

      - **B) ATTACHED TO THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT._** 

   - **(2) A SINGLE UNIT PROVIDING COMPLETE LIVING FACILITIES FOR AT LEAST ONE INDIVIDUAL, INCLUDING, AT A MINIMUM, PROVISIONS FOR SANITATION, COOKING, EATING, AND SLEEPING.** 

   - **(3) NOT LOCATED IN A** **_MULTIFAMILY_ RESIDENTIAL BUILDING.** 

   - **(4) IF THE** **_ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT_ IS DETACHED FROM THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT_ IT SHALL BE LOCATED:** 

      - **A) AT LEAST THREE FEET FROM ANY PROPERTY LINE.** 

      - **B) EXCEPT FOR A(4)(D)[1] AND A(4)D[2]. BELOW SHALL BE LOCATED IN A SIDE OR REAR YARD.** 

      - **C) AT LEAST SIX FEET FROM THE CLOSEST POINT OF THE** **_PRINCIPAL BUILDING_ . D) EXCEPTIONS:** 

         - **1) WHEN THE REAR LOT LINE ABUTS TIDAL WETLANDS, AN** **_ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT_ MAY BE LOCATED IN THAT PORTION OF A FRONT YARD**

**THAT IS NOT WITHIN THE FRONT SETBACK REQUIRED BY THIS SECTION.** 

         - **2) WHEN A PROPERTY ABUTS TWO STREETS, AN** **_ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT_ MAY BE LOCATED IN THAT PORTION OF THE SIDE YARD THAT IS NOT WITHIN THE FRONT YARD SETBACK REQUIRED BY THIS SECTION.** 

   - **(5) THE** **_BUILDING HEIGHT_ SHALL MEET THE REQUIREMENTS OF § 18:1-45 D. (6) MAY NOT COVER AN AREA OF THE** **_LOT_ GREATER THAN 75% OF THE AREA COVERED BY AND SUBORDINATE IN USE TO THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT_** _._ 

   - **(7) SHALL BE EXCLUDED FROM THE CALCULATION OF DENSITY FOR THE** **_LOT_ , PARCEL, OR** **_TRACT_ UPON WHICH IT IS LOCATED.** 

   - **(8) SHALL MEET THE ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT REQUIREMENTS AS FOUND IN § 14:1 IF MAPPED RESOURCE CONSERVATION AREA ON THE CRITICAL AREA OVERLAY MAP OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY.** 

   - **(9) WHERE NOT ON PUBLIC SEWER, AN** **_ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT_ MUST HAVE ADEQUATE ONSITE SEWAGE DISPOSAL AND SUFFICIENT SEWAGE RESERVE AREA APPROVED BY THE QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT IN ACCORDANCE WITH APPLICABLE REGULATIONS.** 

- **B. PARKING** 

   - **(1) ONE OFF-STREET PARKING SPACE IS REQUIRED IN ADDITION TO ANY REQUIRED OFF-STREET PARKING SPACE FOR THE PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT_ . UNITS WITH THREE BEDROOMS OR MORE SHALL HAVE TWO PARKING SPACES.** 

   - **(2) PARKING WAIVER** 

      - **A) THROUGH** **_ZONING APPROVAL_ THE** **_PLANNING DIRECTOR_ SHALL HAVE THE AUTHORITY TO MODIFY THE NUMBER OF REQUIRED PARKING SPACES.** 

      - **B) THE** **_PLANNING DIRECTOR_ MAY CONSULT WITH THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS OR THE MARYLAND STATE HIGHWAY ADMINISTRATION REGARDING ON-STREET PARKING AVAILABILITY AND ROAD WIDTH IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS ROADS DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS MANUAL AND APPLICABLE STATE REGULATIONS AND REQUIREMENTS.** 

      - **C) THE** **_PLANNING DIRECTOR_ MAY CONSIDER THE FOLLOWING FACTORS WHEN REVIEWING A PARKING WAIVER:** 

         - **1) WHETHER SUFFICIENT CURB AREA EXISTS ALONG THE** **_FRONT LOT LINE_ OF THE PROPERTY TO ACCOMMODATE ON-STREET PARKING;** 

         - **2) THE INCREASE IN** **_IMPERVIOUS SURFACE_ DUE TO THE CREATION OF NEW OFF-STREET PARKING AND THE RELATION TO ANY APPLICABLE STORMWATER MANAGEMENT PLANS; AND** 

         - **3) VARIABILITY DUE TO THE SIZE OF THE** **_LOT_ , PARCEL, OR** **_TRACT_ ON WHICH THE** **_ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT_ AND PRINCIPAL** **_DWELLING UNIT_ IS LOCATED.**
