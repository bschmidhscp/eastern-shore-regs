# **AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 24-09** 

# **AN AMENDMENT TO A BILL ENTITLED** 

**AN ACT CONCERNING Short-Term Residential Rentals in Queen Anne ’ s County;** 

**FOR THE PURPOSE of amending County Ordinance No. 24-09 to require that with the property owner or a property manager is located in Queen Anne ’ s County or an adjacent County on the Eastern Shore of Maryland and available at all times during the short-term rental occupancy.** 

**BY FURTHER AMENDING pending County Ordinance No. 24-09, as previously amended.** 

# **<u>SECTION I</u>** 

**BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE ’ S COUNTY, MARYLAND that pending County Ordinance No. 24-09 and is hereby AMENDED and to read as set forth on the attached.** 

# **<u>SECTION II</u>** 

**BET IT FURTHER ENACTED that this amendment shall be effective immediately upon its adoption.**
