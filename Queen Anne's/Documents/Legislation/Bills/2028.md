# 2028

```
COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 05-24
```

# **`A BILL ENTITLED`** 

```
AN ACT CONCERNING the Prohibition on Conversion of Open
Space following Annexation by a Municipal Corporation in Queen
Anne's County.
```

```
FOR THE PURPOSE of prohibiting the conversion of open space
following its annexation by a municipality in Queen Anne's County
and providing that the provisions of this Bill shall apply to
lands annexed after the effective date hereof;
```

```
BY AMENDING Section 18-1-205E.(7) of the Code of Public
Local Laws of Queen Anne's County, 1996 Ed.
```

# **`SECTION I`** 

```
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE'S
COUNTY, MARYLAND that Section 18-1-205E. (7) of the Code of
Public Local Laws of Queen Anne's County, 1996 Ed. be repealed
and reenacted to read as follows:
```

```
'18-1-205.  Contents of instruments.
```

```
E.  Nature and Scope of Covenants.  The instrument shall
contain express provisions that:
```

```
(7)  Any open space restrictions established by the
instrument shall continue in full force and effect following
annexation of the open space by a municipal corporation within
Queen Anne's County and the validity and enforceability of such
open space restrictions shall not be affected by such annexation.
```

# **`SECTION II`** 

```
BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that this Ordinance shall take effect
on the forty-sixth (46th) day following its enactment and shall
only apply to open space lands annexed after the effective date
hereof.
```
