# 25-06-County-Ordinance-Plastic-Bags

<!-- page 1 -->
# COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. .   25-06 . 

A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING the Use of Plastic Bags in Queen Anne’s County; 

FOR THE PURPOSE of prohibiting retail establishments from providing plastic carryout bags to customers; defining certain terms; requiring a fee of at least 10 cents on each paper carryout bag and reusable carryout bag that a retail establishment provides to a customer; providing certain exceptions; providing penalties; and generally prohibiting, regulating, and addressing the use of plastic bags by retail establishments in Queen Anne’s County; 

BY ADDING a new Article VI to Chapter 13 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland; 

# <u>SECTION I</u> 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE'S COUNTY, MARYLAND that a new Article VI be ADDED to Chapter 13 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County to read as set forth on the attached “Exhibit A.” 

# <u>SECTION II</u> 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall take effect on the fortysixth (46<sup>th</sup> ) day following its adoption.
