# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO COUNTY ORDINANCE 26-05 

# AN AMENDMENT TO A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Supplemental Standards for Gas Stations to Include Fuel Pumps, Underground Fuel Storage Tanks and Islands, and Canopies in Queen Anne’s County; 

FOR THE PURPOSE OF amending pending County Ordinance No. 26-05 to allow the Board of Appeals to grant a variance permitting the distance between an exit/entrance to a gas station to any intersection to be no less than 75 feet. 

# SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that County Ordinance 26-05 be AMENDED to read as follows: 

§18:1-58 Additional uses and regulations. 

B. Any fuel pumps, underground fuel storage tanks and islands, including any canopies, shall be at least 25 feet from any street or lot line.  Entrances and exits to streets shall be at least 100 feet from any intersection **, UNLESS THE BOARD OF APPEALS APPROVES AN APPLICATION FOR A** **_VARIANCE_ TO REDUCE THE REQUIRED SETBACK TO NO LESS THAN 75 FEET FROM ANY INTERSECTION.** 

# SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Amendment shall be effective immediately following its adoption and, upon introduction, shall be referred to the Queen Anne’s County Planning Commission for review and recommendation.
