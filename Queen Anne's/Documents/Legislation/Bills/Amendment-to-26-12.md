# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO COUNTY ORDINANCE 26-12 

AN AMENDMENT TO A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING the Queen Anne’s County Forrest Conservation Act, Chapter 18:2 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County 

FOR THE PURPOSE of amending pending County Ordinance 26-12 to provide supplemental requirements for certain tracts of land; providing reforestation at a ratio of 1.5 acres for each acre removed for such tracts; prohibiting fee-in-lieu as respect such tracts; limiting the extent of forest clearing on such tracts. 

BY AMENDING Sections 18:2-8, 18:2-10, 18:2-13 and 18:2-14 of pending County Ordinance No. 26-12. 

# SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Sections 18:2-8, 18:2-10, 18:2-13 and 18:2-14 of pending County Ordinance 26-12 be and are hereby AMENDED to read as set forth on Exhibit A attached hereto. 

# SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Amendment shall take effect immediately upon its adoption.
