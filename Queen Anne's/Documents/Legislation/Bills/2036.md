# **COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 04-02** 

# A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING the adoption of provisions for a Queenstown Resort Development (QRD) District under Title 18 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County (1996 Ed.); 

FOR THE PURPOSE of adding provisions for a Queenstown Resort Development (QRD) District in the Queen Anne’s County Zoning Ordinance (Title 18 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County); establishing the purpose, permitted uses, development standards and development review process for such District; providing for consultation with the Queenstown Planning Commission on proposed projects; and generally dealing with, establishing and regulating a Zoning District to be known as the Queenstown Resort Development (QRD) District. 

BY ADDING a new Section 18-1-35 to Title 18 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County (1996 Ed.). 

# <u>SECTION I</u> 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that a new Section 18-1-35 be added to Title 18 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County (1996 Ed.) to read as set forth on Exhibit A attached hereto. 

# <u>SECTION II</u> 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that existing Sections 18-1-35, 18-1-36, 18-1-37 and 18-1-38 of Title 18 be and are hereby renumbered to 18-1-36, 18-1-37, 18-1-38 and 18-1-39 respectively. 

# <u>SECTION III</u> 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that upon introduction this proposed Ordinance be referred to the Queen Anne’s County Planning Commission for review and recommendation. 

# <u>SECTION IV</u> 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Act shall take effect on the forty-sixth day following its passage. 

INTRODUCED BY:  COMMISSIONER NIEDOMANSKI
