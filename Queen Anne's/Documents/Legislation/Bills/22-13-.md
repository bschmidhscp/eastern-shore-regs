# **COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 22-13** 

# **A BILL ENTITLED** 

**AN ACT CONCERNING the 2022 Comprehensive Rezoning Requests and Rezoning of the Reduced Growth Area;** 

**FOR THE PURPOSE of rezoning twenty (20) parcels of land in Queen Anne ’ s County to conform with the 2022 Comprehensive Plan;** 

**BY AMENDING the official digitally generated and geo-referenced Queen Anne ’ s County Zoning Maps.** 

# **<u>SECTION I</u>** 

**BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE ’ S COUNTY, MARYLAND that the official Queen Anne ’ s County Zoning Maps be and are hereby AMENDED in accordance with the attached 2022 Queen Anne ’ s County Comprehensive Rezoning Summary Table (Attachment A), the County Commissioners having determined, after public hearing, that the same are consistent with the goals and purposes of the Comprehensive Plan.** 

# **<u>SECTION II</u>** 

**BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall be effective on the forty-sixth (46**<sup>**th**</sup> **) day following its adoption.**
