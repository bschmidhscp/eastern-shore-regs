# **COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 04-03** 

AN ACT CONCERNING the adjustment of off-street parking requirements for certain road service uses under Title 18 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County (1996 Ed.); 

FOR THE PURPOSE of eliminating the space adjacent to gas pumps at convenience stores as a parking space that may be counted towards the minimum offstreet parking requirement for such uses. 

# <u>SECTION I</u> 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY that §18-1-83(k)(1)(ii) of Title 18 of the Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County shall be and is hereby repealed. 

# <u>SECTION II</u> 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that, upon introduction, this proposed Ordinance be referred to the Queen Anne’s County Planning Commission for review and recommendation. 

# <u>SECTION III</u> 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Act shall take effect on the forty-sixth day following its passage.
