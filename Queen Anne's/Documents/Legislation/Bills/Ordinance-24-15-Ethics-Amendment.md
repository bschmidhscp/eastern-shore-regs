# Ordinance-24-15-Ethics-Amendment

<!-- page 1 -->
# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO COUNTY ORDINANCE NO.  24-15 

A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Boards and Commissions subject to the Queen Anne’s County Ethics Ordinance; 

FOR THE PURPOSE of revising and updating the Queen Anne’s County Boards and Commissions that are subject to the provisions of Chapter 8 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County (the Queen Anne’s County Ethics Ordinance); 

BY AMENDING Section 8-5 C(1) of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County; 

# <u>SECTION I</u> 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE'S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Section 8-5 C(1) of the Code of Public Local Laws be and is hereby AMENDED to read as follows; 

§8-5. Applicability 

C.  Boards and Commissions 

(1)  Members of the following boards and commissions are subject to the 

provisions of this chapter: 

Administrative Charging Committee Animal Control Commission Board of Appeals – Zoning Board of Building Appeals Board of License Commissioners (Liquor Board) Economic Development Incentive Fund Commission Ethics Commission Housing Authority 

Local Management Board (Community Partnerships for Children and Families) Personnel Board Planning Commission Police Accountability Board Property Tax Assessment Appeals Board 

# <u>SECTION II</u> 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall take effect on the forty-sixth (46<sup>th</sup> ) day following its adoption.
