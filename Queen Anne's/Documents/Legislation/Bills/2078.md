# **`COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. _04-38_____`** 

```
A BILL ENTITLED
```

```
AN ACT CONCERNING the Election of Four County
Commissioners by District rather than At Large;
```

```
FOR THE PURPOSE of providing that the four County
Commissioners elected from County Commissioner Districts of
Queen Anne's County shall be nominated and elected by the
qualified voters of each district and not at large.
```

```
BY repealing and reenacting with amendments Section 4-
101(a) of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne's County
(1996 Ed.).
```

# **<u>`SECTION I`</u>** 

```
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE'S
COUNTY, MARYLAND that Section 4-101(a) of the Code of Public
Local Laws of Queen Anne's County be and is hereby repealed
and reenacted to read as follows:
```

# **`4-101.  County Commissioners; number; election;`** 

```
tenure;
```

# **`(a)  Number`** 

```
There shall be five County Commissioners for Queen
Anne's County.  Four of the County Commissioners shall, at the
time of election, reside in a different County Commissioner
District of the County and shall be nominated and elected by
the qualified voters of that district.  The fifth shall hold
the position of President of the County Commissioners for the
first year of his or her term, may reside in any area of the
County and shall be elected at large.  Following the first
year of their term, the Board of County Commissioners shall
annually, by majority vote select one Commissioner to serve as
President of the County Commissioners.
```
