COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. . 25-05 . 

# A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Indoor Shooting Ranges in Queen Anne’s County; 

FOR THE PURPOSE of allowing indoor shooting ranges as a permitted use in the Town Center (TC) District in Queen Anne’s County; requiring that said uses be located a minimum of 100 feet from any residential use; and generally permitting and regulating indoor shooting ranges in the Town Center (TC) District in Queen Anne’s County; 

BY ADDING Subsection 18:1-28 C. (1)(ss) to the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland; 

SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE'S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Section 18:1-28 C. (1)(ss) be ADDED to the Code of Public Local Laws, to read as follows: 

# 18:1-28 Town Center (TC) District 

C. Uses. 

Permitted uses.

<!-- page 2 -->
(ss) Shooting range, indoor; shall be located at a minimum of 100 feet from any residential use. 

SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall take effect on the forty-sixth (46th) day following its adoption.
