# COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 26-14 

# A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Cottage Industries in Queen Anne’s County, Maryland; 

FOR THE PURPOSE OF defining, providing for, and regulating Cottage Industry in Queen Anne’s County; defining “Artisan Shop” and “Cottage Industry”; providing for Minor Site Plans for Cottage Industries; permitting Cottage Industry as a use in the Agricultural (AG) District; providing supplemental standards and regulations for screening of materials necessary for Cottage Industries, uses appropriate or inappropriate, minimum lot size, setbacks, outdoor storage,  parking, loading, number of employees, signage, refuse collection, and hours of operation; and generally dealing with and regulating Cottage Industry in Queen Anne’s County; 

BY AMENDING Chapter 18-App Appendix A: Glossary and §18:1-14B, 18:1-49, 18:158, and 18:1-141 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County; 

# SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Chapter 18-App Appendix A: Glossary and §18:1-14B, 18:1-49, 18:1-58, and 18:1-141 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County be and are hereby AMENDED to read as set forth on Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated herein. 

# SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall take effect on the forty-sixth (46<sup>th</sup> ) day following its adoption.
