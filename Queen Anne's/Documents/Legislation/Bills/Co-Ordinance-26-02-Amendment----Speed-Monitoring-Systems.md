# AMENDMENT NO.1 TO COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 26-02 

AN AMENDMENT TO A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Speed Monitoring Systems in Queen Anne’s County; 

FOR THE PURPOSE of providing a sunset provision for pending County Ordinance No. 26-02 and providing for the automatic expiration of same unless further action is taken to extend it; 

BY AMENDING Section II of pending County Ordinance No. 26-02. 

# SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Section II of pending County Ordinance No. 26-02 be and is hereby Amended to read as follows: 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED That this Ordinance shall take effect on the forty-sixth (46<sup>th</sup> ) day following its adoption, and that on July 31, 2028, with no further action required by the County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County, This Act shall be abrogated and of no further force or effect. 

# SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED That this Amendment shall take effect immediately upon its adoption.
