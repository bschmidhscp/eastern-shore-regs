# **`A BILL ENTITLED`** 

```
AN ACT CONCERNING the Repeal of the Requirement for
Publication of a Synopsis of Financial Information
Regarding the Queen Anne’s County Sanitary District;
```

```
FOR THE PURPOSE OF repealing the requirement that
Queen Anne’s County annually publish a synopsis of the
receipts, expenses and expenditures under Part 1 of Chapter
24 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County.
```

```
BY Repealing and Readopting with amendments, Section
24-8 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s
County (1996 Ed.).
```

# **`SECTION I`** 

```
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN
ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that Section 24-8 of the Code of
Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County be and is hereby
repealed and readopted to read as follows:
```

```
’24-8 Audit.
```

```
A.  Audit.  The financial accounts relating to the
Sanitary District shall be audited annually by a certified
public accountant to be employed and paid by the County out
of whatever Sanitary District funds as may be available.
```

# **`SECTION II`** 

```
BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Act will take effect
on the forty-sixth (46th) day following its passage.
```
