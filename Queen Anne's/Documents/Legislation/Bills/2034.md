# 2034

<!-- page 1 -->
**COUNTY ORDINANCE NO.  05-29** 

# **A BILL ENTITLED** 

```
AN ACT CONCERNING the Number of Consumer Representatives on the
Queen Anne’s County Department Of Emergency Services Advisory Council.
```

```
 FOR THE PURPOSE of increasing the membership of the Queen Anne’s
County Department Of Emergency Services Advisory Council from eleven
(11) to thirteen (13) and increasing the number of consumer
representatives on the Queen Anne’s County Department Of Emergency
Services Advisory Council from one (1) to three (3).;
```

```
BY AMENDING Section 21-12B(2) and (B)(2)(j) of the Code of Public
Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County, 1996 Ed.
```

# **<u>`SECTION I`</u>** 

```
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY,
MARYLAND that Section 21-12B(2) and (B)(2)(j) of the Code of Public
Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County, 1996 Ed. be repealed and reenacted to
read as follows:
```

```
§21-12.  Emergency Service Advisory Council.
```

```
(2) The County will appoint 13 voting members of the Council.
Voting membership will include representatives as follows:
```

```
(j) Consumers (3).
```

# **<u>`SECTION II`</u>** 

```
BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that this Ordinance shall take effect on
the forty-sixth (46th) day following its enactment and shall only apply
to open space lands annexed after the effective date hereof.
```
