# Co-Ordinance-26-01---Red-Light-Cameras

<!-- page 1 -->
# COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 26-01 

A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Traffic Control Signal Monitoring Systems (Red Light 

Cameras) in Queen Anne’s County 

FOR THE PURPOSE of authorizing Traffic Control Signal Monitoring Systems (Red Light Cameras) in Queen Anne’s County; providing definitions; providing for operation and administration of such systems; providing for trial requirements, civil penalties, and defenses and adjudication; providing for the use and confidentiality of recorded images; and generally authorizing and regulating the use of Traffic Control Signal Monitoring Systems (Red Light Cameras) in Queen Anne’s County; 

BY ADOPTING a new Article VII to Chapter 13 of the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County (Note: Should County Ordinance No. 26-01 not be adopted, the provisions before shall be designated Article VI of Chapter 13 and renumbered to Sections 13-18, et seq.) 

SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND, that a new Article VII of Chapter 13 be ADDED to the Code of 

Public Local Laws to read as follows: 

Article VII Automated Traffic Signal Enforcement 

13-21 AUTHORITY; PURPOSE. 

This Article is enacted by the Board of County Commissioners of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland, pursuant to § 21-202.1 of the Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland and the County’s general police powers.  Its purpose is to promote public safety by reducing traffic signal violations and collisions at signalized intersections.

<!-- page 2 -->
# 13-22 DEFINITIONS. 

All terms used in this Article shall have the meanings set forth in Section 21-202.1 of the 

Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland. 

# 13-23 AUTHORIZATION OF RED LIGHT CAMERAS. 

The County may install and operate Traffic Control Signal Monitoring Systems at approved 

signalized intersections.  All locations shall be subject to approval by the Board of County Commissioners. 

# 13-24 OPERATION AND OWNERSHIP. 

Systems may be owned or operated by the County or through a private vendor.  All such Traffic Control Signal Monitoring Systems shall be operated, administered, and governed by the 

provisions of Section 21-202.1 of the Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland. 13-25 CITATIONS AND PENALTIES. 

Violations are civil offenses subject to a $100.00 penalty, do not result in points, and are not moving violations. 

# 13-26 DEFENSES AND ADJUDICATION. 

Recipients may pay the fine or elect trial in the District Court of Maryland under procedures set forth in Section 21-202.1 of the Transportation Article of the Maryland Code. 

13-27 USE AND CONFIDENTIALITY OF RECORDED IMAGES. 

Images may be used only for enforcement purposes and are confidential except as required by law. 

# SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall take effect on the forty-sixth (46<sup>th</sup> ) day following its adoption.
