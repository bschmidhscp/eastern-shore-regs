# County-Ordinance---Wastewater-Subdistricts

<!-- page 1 -->
# COUNTY ORDINANCE NO. 25-13 

A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING Water and Wastewater Subdistricts in Queen Anne’s County; 

FOR THE PURPOSE of establishing the Sudlersville/Barclay Wastewater Subdistrict and the Sudlersville Water Subdistrict revising the existing Wastewater Subdistricts in Queen Anne’s County; and generally revising, addressing and regulating the Water and Wastewater Subdistricts in the County; 

BY ADDING Sections 24-61 D., 24-91 A. 3, and 24-91 D.  to the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County, Maryland, REPLACING Section 24-91 B. 

# <u>SECTION I</u> 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE'S COUNTY, MARYLAND that the following Sections and Subsections be ADDED to the Code of Public Local Laws of Queen Anne’s County, to read as follows: 

- § 24-61 Enumeration of Water Subdistricts 

- D. Sudlersville Water Subdistrict 

   - (1) There is a Sudlersville Water Subdistrict in the Sanitary District 

   - (2) The Sudlersville Water Subdistrict encompasses the land area within the municipal limits of the Town of Sudlersville. 

- § 24-91 Enumeration of Wastewater Subdistricts 

   - A. Kent Narrows/Stevensville/Grasonville Wastewater Subdistrict 

   - (3) The Kent Narrows/Stevensville/Grasonville Wastewater Subdistrict shall include the subdivisions of Cloverfields and Bay City in the Fourth Election District of Queen Anne’s County and shall also include the land area in the former Prospect Bay Wastewater Subdistrict contained within a boundary box whose corners are defined by the following coordinate pairs of the NAD 83 Maryland State Plan Coordinate System: 

      - (a) X 1538000, Y 461500 

      - (b) X 1544000, Y 461500 

      - (c) X 1544000, Y 447500 

      - (d) X 1538000, Y 447500 

- § 24-91 Enumeration of Wastewater Subdistricts 

   - B. Prospect Bay Wastewater Subdistrict is REPEALED in its entirety and replaced with the following: 

- § 24-91 Enumeration of Wastewater Subdistricts

<!-- page 2 -->
- B. Sudlersville/Barclay Wastewater Subdistrict 

   - (1) There is a Sudlersville/Barclay Wastewater Subdistrict in the Sanitary District. 

   - (2) The Sudlersville/Barclay Wastewater Subdistrict encompasses the land within the municipal limits of the Town of Sudlersville and Barclay in Queen Anne’s County and shall include an area 5’ on each side of the denied access transmission line within Maryland Route 313 (Goldsboro Road) from Sudlersville to Barclay. 

# <u>SECTION II</u> 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED that this Ordinance shall take effect on the fortysixth (46<sup>th</sup> ) day following its adoption.
