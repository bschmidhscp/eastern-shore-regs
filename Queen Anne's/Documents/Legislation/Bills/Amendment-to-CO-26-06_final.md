# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO COUNTY ORDINANCE 26-06 

AN AMENDMENT TO A BILL ENTITLED 

AN ACT CONCERNING An Increase to the Maximum Impervious Surface Ratio for Commercial and Industrial Uses in the Suburban Commercial (SC) District Which Were in Operation Prior to December 31, 2025, in Queen Anne’s County, Maryland; 

FOR THE PURPOSE of amending County Ordinance No. 26-06 to increase the maximum impervious surface ration for all uses in the Suburban Commercial (SC) Zoning District, from 0.45 (45%) to 0.65 (65%); 

BY AMENDING pending County Ordinance No. 26-06; 

# SECTION I 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY, MARYLAND that County Ordinance No. 26-06 be and is AMENDED to read as follows: 

18:1-21 Suburban Commercial (SC) District 

E. Dimensional and bulk requirements. 

(1) Residential uses: not applicable 

(2) Nonresidential uses. 

- a. Maximum, impervious surface ratio. 

[1] All uses: ~~.45~~ .65 

[2] In the growth area, impervious surface ratio allowed can be increased by a maximum of 25% using TDRs in accordance with Chapter 18:1, Part 6, Article XX. 

# SECTION II 

BE IT FURTHER ENACTED That this Ordinance shall be effective immediately following its adoption, and upon introduction shall be referred to the Queen Anne’s County Planning Commission for review and recommendation.
