Dear Mr. Todd: 

This letter is intended to clarify the status of the Southern Kent Island water and sewer service area. The County adopted the 2011 County Water and Sewerage Plan on May 10, 2011 by Resolution No.11-09 and subsequently forwarded the adopted Plan to the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) for review and approval. 

In its letter dated December 7, 2011, the Department denied those portions of the County’s 2011 Plan pertaining to the Southern Kent Island area and the Marling Farms/Dominion area. Specifically, the Department’ letter stated: “The portions of the Plan pertaining to Southern Kent Island and the Marling Farms/Dominion area, which are not approved at this time, are found on pages 1-4, 93-94, 107-110 (where the four maps for the Public Health Sewer Service Areas were located in the draft plan) and 223-227. 

The reason for the Department’s disapproval was that the 2011 Plan eliminated maps of these areas. State regulations require that water and sewerage plans include maps which characterize all areas of the County as areas planned for service or no planned service (COMAR 26.03.01.04). It is the Department’s position that when an amendment is disapproved by MDE, that portion of the Plan being amended reverts to the language and/or mapping in the previous, State-approved Water and Sewerage Plan. Therefore, this letter confirms that the narrative and service area maps contained in the 2006 Queen Anne’s County Water and Sewerage Plan remain in effect. 

The Department appreciates the opportunity to clarify this matter, Please call me at 410-5373567 if you have any questions.
