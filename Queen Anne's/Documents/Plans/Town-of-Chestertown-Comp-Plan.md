# **MEMORANDUM** 

DATE: MARCH 12, 2026 TO: QUEEN ANNE’S COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION CC: AMY MOREDOCK, CFM, PLANNING DIRECTOR FROM: MARY ELLEN GRAY, AICP, SENIOR PLANNER SUBJECT: UPDATE OF THE TOWN OF CHESTERTOWN COMPREHENSIVE PLAN 

The Town of Chestertown is in the process of updating its Comprehensive Plan. The County received the Town <u>of Chestertown Comprehensive Plan draft</u> to review during the 60-day comment period. Maryland Land Use Article 3-203(c) requires that adjoining jurisdictions be notified of changes or updates to comprehensive plans. The 60-day review period allows the public, government agencies, and local jurisdictions to supply comments to the Town. 

The Staff Technical Advisory Committee (STAC) completed a review for consistency between the Town’s and County’s Comprehensive Plans. The STAC review indicated that the Draft Chestertown’s Comprehensive Plan is consistent with the _2022 PlanQAC_ Comprehensive Plan with some minor comments. Based on the review, staff drafted a letter on your behalf to the Town of Chestertown’s Planning Commission and was distributed via email on January 7, 2026 asking for comments given the timing of the due date of the 60-day comment period and the Planning Commission schedule. With no additional concerns, the letter was finalized and sent to the Town on January 12, 2026 and is attached to this memo. 

Please let me know if you have any questions. 

Thank you 

Attachment: January 12, 2026 Letter to Town of Chestertown Planning Chair, John Huchinson

January 12, 2026 

c/o Bradley Moore Planning Commission for the Town of Chestertown John Hutchinson, Planning Chair Town Hall 118 N. Cross Street Chestertown, MD 21620 

- Re: Town of Chestertown Comprehensive Plan: 2045 60-Day Public Review 

Dear Planning Commission Chair Hitchinson, 

Congratulations on completing the draft update of the _Town of Chestertown Comprehensive Plan_ . We thank you for the opportunity to provide comments. Given that Chestertown is a vital neighboring town, Queen Anne’s County Planning Commission and staff conducted a thorough review. 

The Plan is concise, well organized, and aligns with many goals and recommendations of the _2022 PlanQAC Comprehensive Plan (PlanQAC)_ . Please consider addressing the comments below to provide for more consistency between the Town and County’s Comprehensive Plans and continued collaboration between the Town and County’s residents and Comprehensive Plans. 

- Transportation Chapter – Bypass (page 25 and 26) 

   - As reiterated in the draft plan, a new parkway bypass is discussed in the Town of Chestertown’s Comprehensive Plan transportation chapter on page 25 and 26. 

      - <u>Queen Anne’s County Comments</u> 

      - Should this project move forward, we request that this effort be coordinated with Queen Anne’s County at the earliest feasible stage in the process. 

- Municipal Growth Chapter – Annexation (page 64) <u>Queen Anne’s County Comments</u> 

      - As all annexation and growth for the Town of Chestertown is planned in Kent County, providing further clarification that there are no planned annexations in Queen Anne’s County in this section would be helpful. 

- Municipal Growth Chapter – Queen Anne’s Gateway Planning Area (page 66) The Town of Chestertown Comprehensive plan describes this area as encompassing MD Route 213 in Kingstown and a portion of Chester Harbor. The Town of Chestertown’s Comprehensive Plan indicates that Chestertown does not wish to see additional commercial sprawl along MD 213 between Kingstown

<!-- page 3 -->
and Schrader/Union Church Road in Chestertown, to include the proliferation of commercial signage. Chestertown indicates joint planning meetings between the Town and Queen Anne’s County to be welcomed as a way to discuss concerns on both sides of the Chester River. 

- <u>Queen Anne’s County Comments</u> 

- MD 213 is included in the Federally designated Chesapeake County Scenic Byway as described in the Chapter 6 Transportation chapter of _PlanQAC._ It might be helpful to include reference of this designation in the Town of Chesterton’s Comprehensive Plan Municipal Growth Chapter and or the Transportation Element. 

- In addition, it would be helpful to note the _PlanQAC_ Future Land Use Map as described in Chapter 4 Land Use indicates small areas of commercial and mixed use and medium density on the north side of MD 213 in Kingston and very low density residential, low density residential, and medium density residential on the south side. 

- We request as indicated above that should this gateway project proceed, that joint planning meetings between the Town and Queen Anne’s occur to address these issues and other potential concerns as well as coordinate efforts. 

We thank you once again for this opportunity to review and comment on your Town of Chestertown Comprehensive Plan and look forward to future collaboration and discussions on the alignment of our Plans. If you have further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out. 

Sincerely,
