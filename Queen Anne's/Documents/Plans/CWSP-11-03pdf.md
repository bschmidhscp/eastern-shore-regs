### **<u>Map Amendments</u>** 

**SLIPPERY HILL MIXED USE PROJECT –** Tax Map 58F as Parcel 819, is approximately 25 acres in size, and is zoned Grasonville Gateway and Medical Center (GGMC).  The proposal is to construct five three-story apartment buildings (containing a total of 108 apartments), a clubhouse, and two onestory commercial buildings with a total of 20,000-ft2 of office space.   The amendment is to upgrade the water and sewer service designation from W-3/S-3 to W-2/S-2.    Flows are anticipated to be 22,550 gpd.  Water service will be provided via the Grasonville water treatment plant.  Sewerage service will be provided via Collection Station ‘M’.
