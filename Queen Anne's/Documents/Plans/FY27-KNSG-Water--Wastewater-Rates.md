## **<u>Introduction</u>** 

The following document details the potable water and sewerage rate schedule for all user classes within the Kent Narrows, Stevensville, and Grasonville (KNSG) water distribution and wastewater collection systems for FY 2027. A rate schedule is a list of charges and fees that a water and/or sewer customer will have to pay as users within the distribution and collection systems. Specific charges mentioned below are, quarterly billed (every 3 months) user class charges for drinking water and sewerage, one time connection fees for connecting to the county water or sewerage system, water and sewer permit fees, and other miscellaneous customer and developer fees. The rate list is updated and published every year on July 1<sup>st</sup> . The cost of water and sewer is dependent on the planned use of the building being served, aka the user class of the building being served. 

If you are experiencing a problem with your sewer or water system, please contact the <u>Sanitary and Water Maintenance & Operations Division at</u> **410-643-3535** before calling a plumber. 

If you have questions regarding billing, please contact The Queen Anne’s County <u>Finance Department at</u> **410-758-2574** . If you would like to access your water and sewer bill <u>online please go to</u> **qac.org** and navigate to **Online Payments** (Circled in red in the image below) or go directly by clicking the following link: **<u>Pay Taxes & Utility / Special Benefit Assessment Bills | Queen Anne's County, MD - Official Website</u>** and then click “Pay Your Utility Bill or Special Benefit Assessment”.

## **<u>Sewer Notes</u>** 

1. Per unit charges are rounded up to next highest dollar; all others rounded up to next $50.00 increment. 

2. Charge per vacuum valve connection is in addition to any connection (i.e. allocation) charge. 

3. Connection Charge - the Flow Volume, BOD, and SS charge shall automatically increase 2.5% annually beginning on January 1, 2026. 

4. In those areas where a use or proposed use does not fit within one of the classes set forth herein, the average daily flow, SS Loading and BOD Loading, for such use or proposed use shall be assigned and determined by the Chief Administrative Officer and the connection charges shall be determined by use of the above Loading Rates. All Flow Volume Charge, BOD Loading Charges and SS Loading Charges shall be calculated using average daily flow and maximum concentrations of BOD and SS. The quarterly user class charge will be based on the assigned connections at the current rates. 

5. The user rates contained herein shall annually increase by six and one-half percent (6.5%) on July 1 on each year unless the County Commissioners determine by resolution prior to July 1 of each year that the rates will increase by a lesser percentage. 

6. A mixed-use site containing both residential and commercial uses, which is connected to County water, will have its sewer usage charges billed at the rate of the predominate use on the site as determined by the proportion of allocated flow. 

7. Wastewater flow will be based on 85% of potable water flow.  To compute the usage charge, the ‘Usage Rate Per 1000 Gal’ value shown on Page 9 will be reduced by 15%.

## **<u>User Class Definitions</u>** 

User class definitions are the same for water and sewer except all the calculations for unit flows only apply to sewer schedules. Water unit flows are measured by a water meter. 

1. <u>Unit Flows:</u> 

Flow will be determined on a square foot basis using the following guidelines: GPD = gallons per day). In cases where the use is a mixture of the below, the sum of the uses will determine the allocation required 

Office - Gross Sq. Ft. x 0.09 = GPD Retail - Gross Sq. Ft. x 0.05 = GPD Warehouse/Production - Gross Sq. Ft. x 0.03 = GPD Mini-Storage - Gross Sq. Ft. x 0.005 = GPD 

2. <u>Mini Business:</u> 

The flow generated by the above unit flows criteria may not exceed 62 GPD in order to qualify as a mini business user class.  Flows less than 62 GPD will be rounded up to 62 GPD. In cases where the use is a mixture of the above, the sum of the uses will determine the connection required. 

3. <u>Small Business:</u> 

Flow will be determined on a square foot basis by the above unit flows.  The same guidelines that apply to the mini business user class will apply to this category, however the unit flows may not exceed 125 GPD. 

### 4. <u>Business:</u> 

Flow will be determined on a square foot basis by the above unit flows. The flow generated by the above criteria may not exceed 250 GPD in order to qualify as a business user class. 

### 5. <u>Large Business:</u> 

Flow will be determined on a square foot basis by the above unit flows. The flow generated by the above criteria may not exceed 375 GPD in order to qualify as a large business user class. 

**13**

<!-- page 14 -->
### 6. <u>General Commercial:</u> 

Flow will be determined on a square foot basis by the above unit flows. Flow generated by the above criteria may exceed 500 GPD and still qualify as a general commercial user class. Flows more than 500 GPD will be allocated in fractional amounts provided the number of employees does not exceed 40. If the number of employees exceeds 40, the user class will be that of the manufacturing user class. 

### 7. <u>Multi-Retail:</u> 

To be calculated as 10 GPD per 100 square feet with a minimum connection of 250 GPD. This user class is defined as numerous small shops typically in the 300 to 500 square foot range often with shared rest room facilities or being ancillary to a larger, adjacent user.  The Chief Administrative Officer for the Queen Anne's County Sanitary District will determine the connection class for each proposed use. 

### 8. <u>Manufacturing:</u> 

Flows to be computed on a per employee basis.  Use 12 GPD per Employee.  Flow may not be less than 500 GPD in order to qualify as a manufacturing user class. 

### 9. <u>Deli/Carry Out:</u> 

Flows to be computed on a per gross floor area square foot basis.  Use 25 GPD per 100 Square Feet.  Flows can be allocated in fractional amounts.  This user class is defined as relatively small establishments whose primary patrons are carry out customers and whose floor space does not allow many sit-down customers (approximately 1500 square feet or approximately 25 seats).  The Chief Administrative Officer for the Queen Anne's County Sanitary District will determine the connection class. 

### 10. Restaurant/Tavern: 

Flows to be computed on a per gross floor area square foot basis.  Use 37 GPD per 100 Square Feet.  Flows can be allocated in fractional amounts.  This user class is defined as a business used primarily for sit down eating and/or drinking, little or no carry out, having traditionally larger kitchen facilities, and often a liquor license. The Chief Administrative Officer for the Queen Anne's County Sanitary District will determine the connection class. 

**14**

<!-- page 15 -->
### 11. Fast Food: 

Flows to be computed on a per gross floor area square foot basis.  Use 50 GPD per 100 Square Feet. Flows can be allocated in fractional amounts.  This user class is defined as a business having patrons similar in nature to those of the Deli/Carry Out class, but with the more distinguishing features of a larger building size, standardized food preparation, operations scaled for a larger crowd, and usually having characteristics such as a drive through or a franchise license. The Chief Administrative Officer for the Queen Anne's County Sanitary District will determine the connection class. 

### 12. Marina, Commercial: 

A marina wherein slips are provided for rent to the general public.  Use 15 GPD per slip. 

### 13. Community Slips: 

A marina wherein slips are provided for the sole use of residents of the community in which the marina is located in accordance with section 18.1- 43 of the Queen Anne's County Zoning Ordinance.  These slips are not for use by the general public. Use 5 GPD per slip. 

### 14. Transient Slips: 

Boat slips whose sole use is for transient docking for patrons of onshore businesses are exempt from needing connection.  In order to qualify the use must be consistent with section 18.1- 44 of the Queen Anne's County Zoning Ordinance.  No more than 10 slips per property will be exempt. 

### 15. Home Occupations: 

Those uses, which it is determined, will create little if any additional flows will be exempt from purchasing additional connection.  Determination will be made by the Chief Administrative Office of the Queen Anne's County Sanitary District on the basis of information provided by the property owner.

### 16. Home Day Care: 

Those uses licensed by the state and which include 8 or fewer children are exempt from purchasing additional sewer connection fees aside from a normal dwelling. 

17. Rental Apartments 

In order to qualify for these rates, the use must meet the following conditions: 

One-Bedroom Rental Apartment 

Maximum Gross Floor Area to be 900-ft<sup>2</sup> 

Two-Bedroom Rental Apartment 

Maximum Gross Floor Area to be 1,100-ft<sup>2</sup> 

In order to qualify, all dwellings in a given building shall be rental apartments. Threebedroom apartments (or apartments with gross floor areas in excess of 1,100-ft<sup>2</sup> ) will be considered as a normal dwelling. Floor areas in excess of the maximum allowed per user class will be charged at the next higher increment (i.e. a 1,000-ft<sup>2</sup> 1-bedroom apartment will be charged at the 2-bedroom rate). Gross ‘climate controlled’ floor area of the apartment itself will be the determining factor (i.e. excluding decks and patios). 

### 18. <u>Nursing Homes</u> 

This user class is typically used for traditional nursing homes, assisted living facilities, and other institutional uses which provide continuous care for frail and elderly people which are allocated capacity on a per bed basis.  This user class can also be utilized for small, age restricted apartments which meet the following parameters: 

1. Multi-family units with each unit having a maximum floor area of 900-ft.<sup>2</sup> . 

2. Age restricted via deed with minimum age of all occupants of 62. 

3. Maximum number of residents equal 2.

## **<u>Development Services Fee Schedule</u>** 

Any development occurring within the KNSG water and wastewater sub-district shall be required to pay a fee for the inspection of the project. 

Projects that require water improvements will have their fee based upon a percentage of the estimated cost of the water or wastewater improvements.  Projects that require both sewer and water improvements will have their fee determined by the sum of the estimated construction cost of both water and sewer. 

The fee schedule is as follows: 

|**Cost of Project**|**Fee Percentage**|
|---|---|
|$2000 - $50,000|10%|
|$50,000 - $100,000|9%|
|$100,000 - $250,000|8%|
|$250,000 - $500,000|7%|
|$500,000 or more|6%|
