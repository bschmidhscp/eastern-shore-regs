## Map Amendment 

Prospect Bay Water Map - Upgrading water service designation of a single family home owned by Kalvaitis from W-6 to W-2. It is noted the existing well serving the properly must be properly abandoned when no longer regularly used for irrigation. It is further noted that this amendment is to only allow the existing dwelling to connect to public water. Any further development on the property will require its own amendment. 

#### Text Amendment 

4.3.3.2 Eastern Correctional Camp — Replace in its entirety: 

Tax Map 22, Parcel 145, Church Hill 

The State of Maryland operates an existing multi-use wastewater system serving the Eastern Pre-Release Correctional Camp. The population of the camp is approximately 180. The current method of treatment is a lagoon with chlorination with an average daily discharge permit of 20,000 gpd. The existing treatment process is to be replaced by an Enhanced Nutrient Removal process with an average daily flow of40,000 gpd with discharge of the effluent continuing into a tributary of Southeast Creek. 

(end of amendment 11-10)
