## **<u>Introduction</u>** 

The following document details the sewerage rate schedule for all user classes within the Barclay wastewater collection systems for FY 2027. A rate schedule is a list of charges and fees that a water and/or sewer customer must pay as a user within the distribution and collection systems. The specific charges mentioned below are quarterly billed (every 3 months) user class charges for sewerage, one-time connection fees for connecting to the county sewerage system, sewer permit fees, and other miscellaneous customer and developer fees. The rate list is updated and published every year on July 1<sup>st</sup> . The cost of water and sewer is dependent on the planned use of the building being served, aka the user class of the building being served. 

If you are experiencing a problem with your sewer system, please contact the Sanitary <u>and Water Maintenance & Operations Division at</u> **410-643-3535** before calling a plumber. 

If you have questions regarding billing, please contact the Queen Anne’s County <u>Finance Department at</u> **410-758-2574** . If you would like to access your water and sewer bill <u>online, please go to</u> **qac.org** and navigate to **Online Payments** (Circled in red in the image below) or go directly by clicking the following link: **<u>Pay Taxes & Utility / Special Benefit Assessment Bills | Queen Anne's County, MD - Official Website</u>** and then click “Pay Your Utility Bill or Special Benefit Assessment”.

## **<u>Sewer Notes: User Class Definitions</u>** 

### 1. <u>Equivalent Dwelling Unit (EDU):</u> 

A measure of flow that would equate to the accepted standard of 250 gallons per day (GPD) assumed to be produced by a dwelling. No property will be billed at less than 1 EDU. 

2. <u>Business Unit Flows:</u> 

In order to determine the connection/allocation charge for a new use, commercial flows will be determined on a gross square foot basis using the following guidelines. In cases where the use is a mixture of the below, the sum of the uses will determine the allocation required 

(GPD = gallons per day). 

|**Type of Business**|**Unit of Measure**|**Water Usage**|**Total Water**<br>**Usage (GPD)**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Office|Gross Sq. Ft.|X 0.09|= (GPD)|
|Retail|Gross Sq. Ft.|X 0.05|= (GPD)|
|Warehouse/ Production|Gross Sq. Ft.|X 0.03|= (GPD)|
|Mini-Storage|Gross Sq. Ft.|X 0.005|= (GPD)|
|Deli/ Carry-out|Gross Sq. Ft.|X 0.25|= (GPD)|
|Restaurant/Tavern|Gross Sq. Ft.|X 0.37|= (GPD)|
|Fast Food|Gross Sq. Ft.|X 0.50|= (GPD)|



Once the above flow value is derived, it will be divided by 250 to determine the number of EDU’s. That value will be rounded up or down to establish the Ready to Serve Charge. 

### 3. <u>Manufacturing:</u> 

In order to determine the connection/allocation charge for a new use, flows are to be computed on a per-employee basis, using 12 GPD per Employee. Flow may not be less than 2 EDUs in order to qualify as a manufacturing user class. Once the above flow value is derived, it will be divided by 250 to determine the number of EDUs. That value will be rounded up or down to establish the Ready to Serve Charge. 

### 4. <u>Deli/ Carry out:</u> 

In order to determine the connection/allocation charge for a new use, flows are to be computed on a per gross floor area square foot basis. This user class is defined as relatively small establishments whose primary patrons are carry-out customers and whose floor space does not allow many sit-down customers (approximately 1500 square feet or approximately 25

### 5. <u>Restaurant/Tavern:</u> 

In order to determine the connection/allocation charge for a new use, flows to be computed on a per gross floor area square foot basis. This user class is defined as a business used primarily for sit-down eating and/or drinking, little or no carry-out, having traditionally larger kitchen facilities, and often a liquor license. The Chief Administrative Officer for Queen Anne's County Sanitary District will determine the connection class. 

### 6. <u>Fast Food:</u> 

In order to determine the connection/allocation charge for a new use, flows to be computed on a per gross floor area square foot basis. This user class is defined as a business having patrons similar in nature to those of the Deli/Carry Out class, but with the more distinguishing features of standardized food preparation, operations scaled for a larger crowd, and usually having characteristics such as a drive-through or a franchise license. The Chief Administrative Officer for Queen Anne's County Sanitary District will determine the connection class. 

### 7. <u>Other:</u> 

Any uses not defined herein will use the flow parameters as detailed in the KNSG Water and Wastewater Rate Schedules and then converted to an ‘EDU’ basis.

## **<u>Development Services Fee Schedule</u>** 

Any development occurring within the Sudlersville subdistrict shall be required to pay a fee for the inspection of the project. 

Projects that require sewer and water improvements will have their fee based upon a percentage of the estimated cost of the sewer and water improvements. 

The fee schedule is as follows: 

|**Cost of Project**|**Fee Percentage**|
|---|---|
|$2000 - $50,000|10%|
|$50,000 - $100,000|9%|
|$100,000 - $250,000|8%|
|$250,000 - $500,000|7%|
|$500,000 or more|6%|
