**Rural Legacy Areas & Priority Preservation Areas 2008** 

Legend

Priority Preservation Areas as of September 2008. 

The Rural Legacy Areas are designated in a competitive process for grants to purchase conservation easements (and occasionally fee-simple purchases of land) that build on other public and private land protection efforts. 

Note: in 2008 the Rural Legacy Areas were designated as Queen Anne's County's Priority Preservation Areas.
