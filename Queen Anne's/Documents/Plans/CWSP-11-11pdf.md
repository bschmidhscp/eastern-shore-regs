# Map Amendment — Grasonville Sewer Area Map — S-6 to S-2’L’ 

Chesapeake Bay Environmental Center - Tax Map 58H- Parcel 29 and Tax Map 65 - Parcel 12 

This property. owned by the Wildfowl! Trust of NA. Inc.. operates as a public tourist attraction “promoting stewardship, and sustainability through environmental education, habitat restoration, and supports research and volunteerism”. The facility consists of an education building and pavilion and is located at 600 Discovery Lane in Grasonville. The facility's existing on-site disposal system has been deemed failing by the local Environmental Health department. The facility desires to connect to the County's sewer system located within Perry’s Corner Road via a privately owned and operated pressure sewer system. Flows from the use are anticipated to be approximately 500 gpd. Sewer service will be provided via the existing Collection Station *K’. This revised service area would carry an *L’ designation indicating the site cannot be intensified without a subsequent amendment into the Plan.
