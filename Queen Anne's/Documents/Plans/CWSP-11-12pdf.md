## **<u>Text Amendment – Town of Queenstown Water System – Section 3.3.3.7 (Page 57)</u>** 

Insert the below paragraph after existing paragraph 3. 

_“The Town is proposing the following improvements beginning in 2018.  They intend to replace the existing 50,000 gallon water tower located on Wall Street with a new 250,000 gallon tower to be located at an as yet undetermined location.  This tower site will also be utilized to drill a new well into the Mattawan aquifer to avoid Arsenic issues encountered in their existing Aquia wells as well as house a new water treatment plant to provide chlorine disinfection for the water provided by the new well.  In addition the Town will selectively replace various undersized water mains to accommodate the location of the tower and also bring other sub-standard mains up to industry standards.”_
