# **WITNESS:** 

**WHEREAS** , the Declarant(s) is or are the fee simple owner(s) of all those lots, parcels, or tracts of land located in the Fourth Election District of Queen Anne’s County, more particularly known as **Map__** , **Parcel __** , Lots **___, and part of __** , **Block __, ______ Section of __________________________** , as described in a deed dated **____________** recorded among the Land Records of Queen Anne’s County at Liber **__** , Folio ___ and 

**WHEREAS** , said lots are set forth and depicted on a plat known as the **____________________________, registered Surveyor, dated ___________, plat book _________, page __** , recorded among the Land Records of Queen Anne’s County at Plat Book ______, Folio ____ ,and 

**WHEREAS** , said lots are contiguous and share one or more common boundary lines(s) as set forth on the aforesaid plat as **______ Edition of the _____ Section of __________________** and 

**WHEREAS** , the Declarant(s) desires to eliminate the common boundary line(s) between or among the said lots for the purpose of combining the lots into one (1) lot of record, and 

**NOW THEREFORE** , in light of the premises and for no consideration, the Declarant(s) declares and agrees as follows: 

1. The above recitals are not merely prefatory but are incorporated herein as if fully set forth. 

2. The Declarant(s) declares that for all purpose other than the payment of fees or charges imposed by any covenants, agreements, or community association by-law generally affecting or running with and binding lands with in **___________ Section __** , the common boundary line(s) between lots **__________________** , are abolished, null and void, and of no further force and effect with the direct and intended result that lots **________________, _______________** , shall no longer be separate and distinct lots, but henceforth shall be treated, considered, granted and conveyed collectively as one (1) lot to be known as **Lot ___, Block ___, ________________________** . 

3. The Declarant(s) expressly acknowledges that after final approval of this Declaration by the Director of Planning & Zoning, the word “lot” as applied to any land described in the Declaration shall be deemed for purposes of any zoning or subdivision ordinance to mean the resulting **Lot __** established herein.
