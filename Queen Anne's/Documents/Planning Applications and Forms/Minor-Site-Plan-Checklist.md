## **B. Follow Up at End of Review** 

- GIS department review 

- □ Application and/or plat has been signed by the owners of all existing lots affected □ Upon approval: 

   - Signatures from all applicable reviewing agencies have been obtained 

   - □ 10 paper copies of the plat are required and 1 copy of the legal document, if applicable □ Check made payable to the Clerk of Courts for the legals, if applicable 

## **C. Application** 

- Application has been completed accurately and is consistent with the plat 

- □ All submitted information has been stamped and dated “received” □ Correct application fee has been submitted 

- Project has been sent to applicable Incorporated Town if within a 1-mile radius 

- □ Any property deed(s), right-of-way, and/or other easement agreements have been submitted (questions on deed information should be discussed with the PC Attorney) 

- All necessary copies for required reviewing agencies have been submitted 

- □ Proof of notification to adjacent property owners and Fire/EMS Commission □ An APFO study or exemption has been completed and approved, if required □ Application sent to Parks and Recreation for Bike & Pedestrian Advisory Committee if a trail or park is located in the vicinity or adjacent to the property 

- Forest Conservation information, if applicable: county worksheets, Forest Calculation worksheet, Forest Stand Delineation, Forest Conservation Plan, Cost Estimate, 2-Year Maintenance Agreement, Long-Term Protective Agreement and notarized Declaration of Intent if an Intra-Family transfer. 

- Critical Area Project Application and supporting information required as per the Queen Anne’s County Critical Area Ordinance (Environmental Assessment Report, Critical Area Forest Requirements, if applicable) 

- Letters from power and telephone companies indicate service is available and will be provided 

- □ GIS or Google Maps aerials confirm existing conditions □ Use is permitted by right or approved conditional use 

## **D. Plat Information** 

- Name and/or title of project 

- □ Title block that contains Tax Map/Grid/Parcel/Lot, and Election District □ Scale is legible and correct (not less than 1”=100’) □ North arrow with plat oriented north

<!-- page 2 -->
- Location map with north arrow indicating location of project in relation to municipal boundaries and State or County roads 

- □ Name and address of registered surveyor, engineer, or professional responsible for preparing the plan □ Signature and seal of licensed professional and dated □ Name and address of property owner, developer, applicant, contract purchaser, etc. □ Revision block for all revisions and dates □ Purpose and Intent statement □ Blow-up area of any metes/bounds descriptions that are small for the GIS department □ A Legend is provided either on the front page or each page as necessary for all symbols □ Existing and proposed structures and other improvements are depicted on plan along with square footage and use 

- Prior action(s) with date(s) and decision/condition(s) listed on plan 

- □ Ownership, zoning, and use of subject and all adjacent properties □ Location of all easements which affect the property (including, but not limited, to rights-of-ways, easements, restrictions, covenants, TDR restrictions, etc.) 

- Location of outside refuse collection area and type of enclosure/screening are depicted and labeled 

- □ Location and size of any existing or proposed exterior storage and / or display areas shown with dimensions 

- Existing nonconformities have been corrected, and no new nonconformities have been created 

- □ If multi-family or apartment complex is proposed, the location and configuration of buildings, common areas, open space, and utility easements are to be shown 

- Stormwater management facilities have been shown and addressed 

- □ Location and area of proposed open space, common areas, and major utility easements. Proper plat notes have also been provided 

- Existing and proposed wells and septic areas have been graphically depicted 

- □ Existing main/sewer hookups have been graphically depicted □ Sanitary sewer district serving project is indicated □ Note to indicate method of water and sewer service intended for site □ Location, size, height, and description of use of any utility poles, fences, and retaining walls □ Conservation Land map has been checked for MALPF and other easements or restrictions □ MALPF Coordinator notified and all paperwork submitted □ MPDU’s have been addressed, if applicable (§18-1-108) □ For apartment developments: if bonus density is requested, type of moderately priced housing provided (see apartment bonus density standards) 

- □ In Planned Residential Development, location and area of each lot or building site is indicated 

## **E. Site Capacity, Bulk & Density Requirements** 

- Metes, bounds, course and distance description of property lines 

- Easements delineated and labeled, including off-site easements that affect the site with the record owner of the easement, and the description of the facility(s) located or to be located in the easement 

- Gross site area and base site area 

- □ Maximum floor area allowed / existing and proposed floor area □ Maximum impervious area allowed / existing and proposed impervious area □ Maximum required landscape surface area / existing / proposed landscape surface area □ Maximum building height permitted / proposed building height -existing building height, if applicable □ All required setbacks have been shown (front, side, rear, right-of-way)

## **F. Natural Resource Information (§18-1-59 through §18-1-68)** 

- Indication if natural resources were field located with date or scaled from maps 

- □ Location / limits and FIRM# of 100-year floodplain □ Facilities for shoreline protection are indicated □ Soil mapping units have been provided with delineations □ If applicable, location of steep slopes and/or erosion hazard areas □ If applicable, location of streams (intermittent and perennial) and their associated buffers □ If applicable, location of wetlands (tidal and nontidal) and their associated buffers □ If applicable, location of woodland/forest □ Approximate location of all isolated trees with diameter 6” or more and all tree masses □ If applicable, location of habitat of threatened and endangered species □ Area in buffer yards indicated □ Resource protection chart with percentages provided □ Location and aerial extent of all hydric soils and soils with hydric properties □ If tidal/nontidal wetlands are impacted, an Army Corps of Engineers permit is submitted □ A wetlands jurisdictional determination shall be made when any of the three following indicators are present on a lot proposed for development: hydric soils, a water source, hydrophytic vegetation 

## **G. Critical Area (if applicable)** 

- Critical Areas boundaries have been delineated / acreage indicated / designation provided 

- □ Critical Area project application and Environmental Assessment Report provided □ Location of shore buffer, buffer exempt area, 100’ buffer, and/or 300’ buffer □ Critical Area forest conservation requirements have been met 

## **H. Road Access/Parking/Circulation** 

- Minimum required road frontage has been indicated 

- □ Parking calculations are shown with code section noted □ Parking spaces have been graphically depicted with measurements and are numbered □ Pavement type and method of parking stall demarcation have been indicated □ Location and size of parking lot, interior roads, walkways, and loading areas □ Parking space measurements have been indicated □ Loading requirements and calculations indicated with minimum 250 square feet for each space □ Any reduced parking space lengths have been labeled □ Right-of-ways and turn lanes shown, labeled, and widths provided with any proposed widening/restriping □ Curb cuts are depicted on the plan for ingress and egress to/from streets □ Location and size of parking islands, drive aisles, and medians indicated □ Location and dimensions of any new roads, easements/dedications, with deeds/covenants (if required) □ Location and dimensions of curbing indicated □ Existing entrances on the same side and opposite side of any street adjacent to the site □ Location and size of outdoor vehicle and equipment storage areas with location and description of screening 

- Existing and proposed traffic control measures have been depicted and labeled

## **I. Landscaping** 

- Landscape calculations are shown (minimum required/area proposed/area existing) 

- □ On-site landscaping calculations are shown and proper plant unit(s) chosen □ Parking lot landscaping calculations are shown and proper plant unit(s) chosen □ Street buffer calculations are shown and proper plant unit(s) chosen □ Zoning district boundary buffer calculations are shown and proper plant unit(s) chosen □ Landscaping islands are at least 6′ wide and no less than the length of the adjacent parking space □ Landscape schedule includes size, species, plant types, and quantities □ Protective devices are shown to protect landscape planting (both existing and proposed) □ Landscape estimate(s) are provided 

## **J. Architectural Rendering (§18:1-150)** 

- Any design standards/guidelines addressed with narrative included 

- □ Color rendering/photograph of front elevation shown from eye level has been submitted □ Elevation drawings of sides and rear of building(s) shown from eye level have been submitted □ Proposed building materials and color choices have been indicated □ All accessory structures and landscape features have been shown □ Landscaping features are consistent with the landscaping plan in size, maturity, and location 

## **K. Signage** 

- Color rendering/photograph has been submitted 

- Color rendering/photograph has been submitted 

- □ Location of existing and proposed signage with setback from property line has been indicated □ Sign lighting has been addressed □ Architectural base or landscaping is proposed □ Permitted number of signs and maximum signage area (both freestanding and façade) has not been exceeded and is indicated 

- Height of sign(s) has been indicated 

- Signs satisfy required setbacks 

- □ Are there any existing nonconforming signs on site? □ Have existing billboards been shown on the plan? Are they shown “to be removed?” 

## **L. Lighting (§18:1-85)** 

- Lighting study and plan has been provided, if applicable 

- □ Maximum heights have not been exceeded □ Appropriate cutoffs are shown □ No flashing or flickering lights are proposed □ No lights are proposed in buffer yard areas except if lighting a pedestrian walkway □ Cut sheet of light(s) proposed has been provided □ Potential spillover onto adjacent properties is not in excess of 0.25 foot-candles □ Is there adequate lighting to ensure security for publicly used places? 

## **M. Bike / Pedestrian / Community Amenity** 

- Pedestrian circulation / safety addressed? 

- □ Walkways/sidewalks depicted □ Pedestrian signage/traffic control, if applicable □ Check bike and pedestrian map for proposed connections
