### **C. Application** 

- Application has been completed accurately and is consistent with the plat 

- □ All submitted information has been stamped and dated “received” □ Correct application fee has been submitted □ Project has been sent to applicable Incorporated Town if within a 1-mile radius □ All necessary copies for required reviewing agencies have been submitted □ Proof of notification to adjacent property owners and Fire/EMS Commission □ A letter to the local VFD per §18:1-148.D (outreach if necessary) □ Any property deed(s), right-of-way, and/or other easement agreements have been submitted (questions on deed information should be discussed with the PC Attorney) 

- Review packet provided for Parks and Recreation for Bike & Pedestrian Advisory Committee if a trail or park is located in the vicinity or adjacent to the property 

- Forest Conservation information, if applicable: county worksheets, Forest Calculation worksheet, Forest Stand Delineation, Forest Conservation Plan, Cost Estimate, 2-YearMaintenance Agreement, Long-Term Protective Agreement and notarized Declaration of Intent if an Intra Family transfer 

- Critical Area Project Application and supporting information required as per the Queen Anne’s County Critical Area Ordinance (Environmental Assessment Report, Critical Area Forest Requirements, if applicable) 

- Letters from power and telephone companies indicating service is available and will be provided 

- □ Open Space Covenants and or Long-Term Protective Agreements reviewed and signed by the Planning Commission attorney (if required) 

- Road Deed and Covenants (if required) 

- □ MPDUs have been addressed, if applicable §18:1-108 □ If TDRs are being used, all appropriate information has been submitted §18:1-100 through §18:1-107 □ If noncontiguous development is being proposed, all requirements have been met §18:1-97 through §18:1-99 

- If sliding scale, applicant/agent has provided information om the history of the original parcel and any future subdivision potential (§18:1-13(k))

### **D. Plat Information** 

- Conservation Land Map has been checked for MALPF, MET, and other easements or restrictions 

- □ MALPF Coordinator notified and any/all paperwork completed if a MALPF easement exists □ Name of proposed subdivision and/or title of project □ Name and address or property owner(s) on record □ Name and address of registered surveyor, engineer, or professional responsible for subdivision design and the design of public improvements 

- Seal of engineer/professional with a signature and date 

- □ Revision block for all revisions and dates □ Purpose of Intent statement □ Title block that contains Tax Map/Grid/Parcel/Lot, and Election District □ Blow-up area of any metes/bounds descriptions that are small for the GIS department □ For subdivisions in or adjacent to the Agriculture or Countryside zoning district, the following “Right to Farm Plat Statement” must be included on the plat: “There shall be no basis, under the Ordinance, for recourse against the effects of any normal farming operations conducted in accordance with standard and acceptable best management practices. Normal agriculture effects include, but are not limited to noise, odor, vibration, fumes, dust, spray drift, or glare.” 

- Scale is legible and correct 

- □ North arrow is shown, and the top of the plat shall be oriented north □ Location map with the north arrow showing property in relation to towns, and state or county roads or intersections 

- Ownership, zoning, and use of all adjacent properties 

- □ Location of all easements which affect the property including, but not limited, to rights-of-ways, easements, restrictions, covenants, TDR restrictions, etc. 

- Gross area of each lot proposed 

- □ Sequential numbering system of proposed lots □ Any proposed flag lot has at least 35′ of road frontage and a flag stem that is at least 20′ wide □ Site statistics / capacity calculations must include the following information: 

- Gross site area 

- □ Base site area □ Total area in open space □ Area of other dedicated portions of the subdivisions (school sites, parks, etc.) □ Area in buffer yard □ Total permitted net buildable □ Proposed net buildable □ Maximum number of lots allowed □ Total number of lots proposed □ Area within the 100-year floodplain □ Resource protection lands chart 

- □ Existing nonconformities have been corrected, and no new nonconformities have been created □ Property line boundaries with metes, bounds, courses, distances, and curve data indicated and the location of all survey monuments including dimensions of all proposed lots, parcels, open space, and public grounds 

- Proposed public improvements, including roads or other major improvements and unique road names have been assigned

### **E. Road Information** 

- Location, name, and widths of all existing and proposed adjacent or internal roads, rights-of-ways, alleys, and dedications 

- Proposed layout of roads and lots, alignment, and right-of-way widths of proposed roads in relation to existing roads 

- Unique road names have been assigned 

- □ Location of entrance(s) and the manner of access to the subdivision with dimensions □ Existing entrances on the same side and opposite side of any street adjacent to the proposed development 

### **F. Land Description Information** 

- Contours provided at 2-foot intervals for areas proposed for subdivision 

- □ Soil mapping units have been provided with delineations □ Location and method of sewerage disposal □ Location and type of water supply (private well, public water) □ Stormwater management facilities have been shown and addressed □ Location and distance of all required setbacks □ Location and distance of all required buffer yards □ Vegetative buffer has been provided, if applicable (§18:1-76(d)) □ Location of historic sites, buildings, or designated historic areas and natural features as noted in the historic inventory prepared by the Maryland Historic Trust 

### **G. Natural Resource Information (§18:1-59 through §18:1-68)** 

   - Location, size, description and required buffers for all natural resources on site 

   - □ Indication if natural resources were field located with date or scaled from maps □ Location / limits and FIRM# of 100-year floodplain □ If applicable, location of steep slopes and/or erosion hazard areas □ If applicable, location of streams (intermittent and perennial) and their associated buffers □ Stream buffer note □ If applicable, location of wetlands (tidal and nontidal) and their associated buffers □ If applicable, location of woodland/forest □ If applicable, Critical Area boundaries and designations □ If applicable, location of shore buffer □ If applicable, location of habitat and/or threatened and endangered species □ Location and aerial extent of all hydric soils and soils with hydric properties □ If tidal/nontidal wetlands are impacted, an Army Corps of Engineers permit is submitted and issued □ A wetlands jurisdictional determination shall be made when any of the three following indicators are present on a lot proposed for development: hydric soils, a water source, hydrophytic vegetation
