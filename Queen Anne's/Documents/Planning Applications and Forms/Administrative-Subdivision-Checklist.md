## **B. Follow Up at End of Review** 

- GIS department review 

- □ Application and/or plat has been signed by the owners of all existing lots affected by the subdivision □ Upon approval: 

   - Signatures from all applicable reviewing agencies have been obtained 

   - □ 1 mylar and 10 paper copies of the plat are required and 1 copy of the legal document, if applicable □ Recording check made payable to the Clerk of Courts for the plat and legals, if applicable 

## **C. Documents** 

- Application has been completed accurately and is consistent with the plat 

- □ All submitted information has been stamped and dated “received” □ Correct application fee has been submitted □ Project has been sent to applicable Incorporated Town if within a 1-mile radius □ Proof of notification to adjacent property owners and Fire/EMS Commission □ A letter to the local VFD is not necessary unless a buildable lot is created □ All necessary copies for required reviewing agencies have been submitted □ Any property deed(s), right-of-way, and/or other easement agreements have been submitted (questions on deed information should be discussed with the PC Attorney) 

- An APFO study or exemption is not usually required; however, check if part of the larger project will require one 

- GIS or Google Maps aerials 

## **D. Plat Information** 

- Scale is legible and correct 

- □ Name and/or title of project □ Name and address of registered surveyor, engineer, or professional responsible for preparing the plan □ Seal of engineer/professional with a signature and date □ Ownership, zoning, and use of all adjacent properties □ Purpose of Intent statement □ Revision block for all revisions and dates □ Title block that contains Tax Map/Grid/Parcel/Lot, and Election District □ North arrow is shown, and the top of the plat shall be oriented north □ Key map with the north arrow showing property in relation to towns, and state or county roads or intersections 

- A Legend is provided either on the front page or each page as necessary for all symbols 

- □ Statement that any existing nonconformities have been addressed, and no nonconformities are being created

<!-- page 2 -->
- This statement is on the plat: “After this plat is finally approved and recorded, any prior size or configuration of all or any part of the land shown on this plat, or the prior status of any such land as a ‘lot’ under any zoning ordinance or subdivision regulations, is superseded by the size and configuration of the lots shown on the plat.” 

## **E. Land Description Information** 

- Existing lots shown with broken lines and resulting lots shown by solid lines 

- □ Blow-up area of any metes/bounds descriptions that are small for the GIS department □ Property boundary lines with metes, bounds, angles, bearings, dimensions, and curve data □ Course and distance of lines to be removed with note, “line to be removed” □ Gross area is given for existing and proposed lots □ Area of resulting lot(s) calculated to nearest .01± square feet □ Location and width of roads and rights-of-way which adjoin any resulting lot □ Net buildable area calculated if needed □ Critical area designation and location for existing and proposed lot(s) (IDA, LDA, RCA, MBA) □ Conservation Land Map has been checked for MALPF and other easements or restrictions □ MALPF Coordinator notified and any/all paperwork completed if a MALPF easement exists □ Identify each resulting lot by consecutive numbers the same as by which lots were denoted □ Location of proposed on-site well(s) and SRA(s), and, if possible, existing well(s) and SRA(s) □ Areas to be dedicated or for public use are labeled and identified (“drainage easement,” “utility easement,” “buffer yard easement,” etc.) on the plat 

- Front yard minimum setback shown on all resulting lots (more may be provided or altered as permitted*) 

- Side and rear minimum setbacks shown on all resulting lots (as noted above*) 

- □ Lot area and width are the minimum required □ Road frontage is at least the minimum required 

## **F. Required Certifications** (All required to be signed, with typed name of signer and date included) 

- Owner’s Certificate(s) □ Uniform Acknowledgement Act(s) (seal okay from notary) □ Surveyor’s Certificate □ Department of Environmental Health Certificate □ Planning and Zoning Certificate □ Department of Public Works Certificate
