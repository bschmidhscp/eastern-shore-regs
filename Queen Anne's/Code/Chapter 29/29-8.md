# § 29-8. Franchise renewal.

Franchise renewals shall be conducted in accordance with applicable law, including, but not necessarily limited to, the Cable Act, as amended. Grantor and grantee, by mutual consent, may enter into renewal negotiations at any time during the term of the franchise.
