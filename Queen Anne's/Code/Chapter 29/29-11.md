# § 29-11. Initial grantee costs.

Costs to be borne by an initial grantee shall include, but shall not be limited to, any reasonable charges incidental to the awarding or enforcing of an initial grantee's franchise, all costs of publications of notices prior to any public meeting provided for pursuant to this chapter, and any costs not covered by application fees but incurred by the County in its study, preparation of proposal documents, evaluation of all applications, and examinations of the applicant's qualifications. Any payments made to the County pursuant to this section shall not be considered franchise fees.
