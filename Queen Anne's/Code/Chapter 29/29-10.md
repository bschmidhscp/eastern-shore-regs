# § 29-10. County use of grantee facilities.

The County shall have the right, during the term of a franchise, to install and maintain free of charge upon the poles of a grantee any wire or pole fixtures that do not unreasonably interfere with the cable television system operations of the grantee. The County shall indemnify and hold harmless, to the extent permitted by law, the grantee from any claim that might arise due to or as a result of such usage.
