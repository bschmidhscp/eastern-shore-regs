# § 11-2. Exceptions.

*[Amended 2-14-2017 by Ord. No. 16-23 ]*

This chapter does not apply to:

A. An electrical light company, electric railway or railroad company, steam railway or railroad company, or telegraph or telephone company; or

B. An individual performing electrical work for a company listed in Subsection A of this section if the electrical work is an integral part of the plant or service used by the company in rendering its duly authorized service to the public.
