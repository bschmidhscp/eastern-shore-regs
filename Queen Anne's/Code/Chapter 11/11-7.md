# § 11-7. Fee; expenses.

A. The Board shall establish a schedule of fees for all electrical permits and licenses required under Chapter 11, subject to the approval of the Queen Anne's County Commissioners. Such fee schedule may be altered or amended at the discretion of the Board, subject to the approval of the Queen Anne's County Commissioners.

B. Collection. All fees collected under this chapter shall be collected and paid to the official designated by the County Commissioners.

C. Expenses. All expenses of the Board shall be approved by the Board and paid by the County.
