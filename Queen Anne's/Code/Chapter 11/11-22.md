# § 11-22. Violations and penalties.

A. In general. A person convicted under § 11-21 of this chapter is subject to a fine of not less than $25 but not exceeding $1,000 or to imprisonment not exceeding 90 days, or both.

B. Annulment of license. Any conviction under this chapter shall automatically annul a license issued to the person convicted.
