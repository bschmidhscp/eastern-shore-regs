# § 11-13. Display of license.

A. A person granted a license shall be issued a card and he or she shall carry it on his or her person at all times while engaged in any work under said license.

B. Any person holding a master/ restricted electrician license shall display same in a conspicuous place in the office or place of business of the licensee. The licensee shall also display on his/her business vehicle(s) and on any and all advertising that may be done the license number and the name under which he/she is trading.
