# § 11-12. Term of license.

A license issued or renewed under this chapter shall:

A. Be valid for up to two years from the date of issuance or renewal; and

B. Expire on the first July 1 after the effective date of the license or renewal and in an odd-numbered year.

C. Any change of name, address, or employment by a licensee from that which appears on the current license must be reported to the Board, in writing, within 30 calendar days of the change.

D. No duly licensed master/restricted electrician shall represent more than one company or corporation at any one time.
