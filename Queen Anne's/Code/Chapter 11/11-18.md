# § 11-18. License revocation.

A. After a full hearing of all parties in interest, the Board may revoke for proper cause any original license or license renewal.

B. The Board shall have the power to revoke, suspend, or refuse to renew the license of any licensee who shall be found guilty of any one or more the following:

  (1) Fraud or deceit in obtaining a license.

  (2) Aiding, abetting or knowingly combining or conspiring with any person with the intent to evade the provisions of these rules.

  (3) Willful and deliberate violation of the National Electrical Code and the laws of Queen Anne's County and the State of Maryland.
