# § 11-5. Meetings; compensations.

A. Meetings. The Board shall meet at least once a month and shall hold special meetings as the proper and efficient discharge of its business shall require.

B. Compensation. Each member of the Board shall receive compensation for actual service in attending the meetings of the Board, as may be determined by the County Commissioners.
