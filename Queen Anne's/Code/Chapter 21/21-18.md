# § 21-18. Access safes.

*[Added 4-10-2018 by Ord. No. 18-01 ]*

A. Each locked gated business, residence, community and marina in Queen Anne’s County shall be equipped with either a keyed entry system approved by the Queen Anne’s County Fire Marshal or a small safe of a type and design approved by the Queen Anne’s County Fire Marshal that holds keys allowing fire departments and emergency services personnel access to the business, residence, community or marina. Local fire departments and emergency services personnel shall be provided master keys to all such safes in their response area.

B. Any violation of this section shall be a civil infraction subject to a fine of up to $500. Every day that a violation continues shall be a separate offense.
