# § 21-4. Budget.

A. Queen Anne's County shall provide in each annual budget, sums of money necessary to assist with the funding of the Volunteer Fire Departments and such programs for fire suppression and prevention, rescue, emergency medical services, training and any other emergency related services as the County Commissioners may deem appropriate.[Amended 6-11-2013 by Ord. No. 13-13]

B. Budget submissions shall be consistent with the Queen Anne's County Finance Office's policies and guidelines.
