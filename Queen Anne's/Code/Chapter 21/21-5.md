# § 21-5. Length of service awards program.

A. The County shall by resolution establish and fund a length of service awards program for qualified active volunteer members of the Queen Anne's County Volunteer Fire Departments.[Amended 6-11-2013 by Ord. No. 13-13]

B. The program shall be administered by the Commission. Payment shall be made directly to the volunteer entitled to such benefits.
