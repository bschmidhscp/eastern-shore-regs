# § 21-17. Public and semi-public pools.

A. Each public and semi-public swimming pool in Queen Anne's County shall be equipped with a first aid kit and an automated external defibrillator approved by the Maryland Institute for Emergency Medical Services System. The automated external defibrillator shall be tested and maintained as per the manufacturer's specifications.

B. Any violation of this article shall be a civil infraction and subject to the requirements and procedures set forth in Article 25B, § 13C, of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

C. A fine of $500 shall be imposed for the commission of a civil infraction hereunder. A fine of $1,000 shall be imposed for any repeat offense.
