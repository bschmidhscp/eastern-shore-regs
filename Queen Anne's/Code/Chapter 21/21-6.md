# § 21-6. Distribution of state funding from William Amoss Fire, Rescue and Ambulance Fund (508 Funds).

A. State funds allocated to Queen Anne's County through the William Amoss Fire Rescue and Ambulance Fund will be distributed quarterly by the Finance Office to the County's fire and ambulance departments as follows:[Amended 6-11-2013 by Ord. No. 13-13]

  (1) Kent Island Volunteer Fire Department, Inc.: 12%.

  (2) Grasonville Volunteer Fire Department, Inc.: 12%.

  (3) Queenstown Volunteer Fire Company, Inc.: 12%.

  (4) Goodwill Fire Company, Inc. (Centreville): 12%.

  (5) Church Hill Volunteer Fire Company, Inc.: 12%.

  (6) Sudlersville Volunteer Fire Company, Inc.: 12%.

  (7) Crumpton Volunteer Fire Department, Inc.: 12%.

  (8) United Communities Volunteer Fire Dept., Inc.: 12%.

  (9) Queen Anne-Hillsboro Volunteer Fire Co., Inc.: 4%.

B. The County may withhold or delay the distribution of funds hereunder to any department which has not complied with the requirements of the Public Safety Article, Annotated Code of Maryland, Title 8, Subtitle 1.
