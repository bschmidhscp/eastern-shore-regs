# § 21-1. Definitions.

A. As used in this article, the following words have the meanings indicated:

**COMMISSION** The Queen Anne's County Fire and EMS Commission.

**COUNTY COMMISSIONERS** The County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County.

**VOLUNTEER FIRE DEPARTMENT** One of the nine independent volunteer fire companies providing fire suppression, prevention, and/or emergency medical services in Queen Anne's County.[Amended 6-11-2013 by Ord. No. 13-13]
