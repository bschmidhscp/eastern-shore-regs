# § 5-17. Definitions.

A. In general. In this article, the following terms have the meanings indicated.

**GENERAL FUND OF THE COUNTY** That fund known as the general fund as defined by Generally Accepted Accounting Principles promulgated by the Government Accounting Standards Board.
