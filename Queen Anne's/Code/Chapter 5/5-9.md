# § 5-9. Property tax refunds.

*[Added 5-6-2003 by Ord. No. 03-12]*

A. Definitions. All terms used in this section shall have the meanings given in § 7-208 of the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

B. Refunds.

  (1) In the taxable years in which an exemption under § 7-208 of the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland was authorized but not granted, a refund of County property tax and municipal corporation property tax is authorized:

    (a) To a disabled veteran or a surviving spouse for County property tax paid;

    (b) To a disabled veteran for any municipal corporation property tax paid.

  (2) No refund shall be authorized for any taxes paid more than three years prior to the date of refund.
