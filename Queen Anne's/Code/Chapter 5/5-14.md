# § 5-14. Authority established; purposes.

A. Established. The County Commissioners shall serve as the Authority.

B. Purposes. The purposes of the Authority are for:

  (1) The provision of security, maintenance, and amenities within the District; and

  (2) The design, approval, and financing of public improvements in the District.

C. Special taxing district. The Authority is a special taxing district pursuant to Article 25A, § 5(FF) of the Annotated Code of Maryland.
