# § 2-1. Definitions.

In general. In this Code, the following words have the meanings indicated, unless an alternative definition is provided or unless the context clearly indicates an alternative definition is intended.

**COUNTY** Queen Anne's County, Maryland.

**COUNTY COMMISSIONERS** The Board of County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County.

**PERSON** An individual, receiver, trustee, guardian, personal representative, fiduciary, or representative of any kind, and any partnership, firm, association, corporation, or other entity.
