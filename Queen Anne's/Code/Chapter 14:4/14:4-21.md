# § 14:4-21. Preparation of stormwater management plans.

A. Stormwater management plans shall be prepared by a licensed professional engineer, a licensed professional land surveyor, or a licensed landscape architect in Maryland.

B. If a stormwater BMP requires either a dam safety permit from the Administration or small pond approval from the District, the design shall be prepared by a licensed professional engineer in Maryland.
