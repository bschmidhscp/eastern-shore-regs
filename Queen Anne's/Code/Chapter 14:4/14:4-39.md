# § 14:4-39. Maintenance responsibility.

A. The owner of a property that contains private stormwater management facilities installed pursuant to this chapter, or any other person or agent in control of such property, shall maintain in good condition and promptly repair and restore all stormwater management systems, grade surfaces, walls, drains, dams and structures, vegetation, erosion and sediment control measures, and other protective devices in perpetuity. Such repairs or restoration and maintenance shall be in accordance with previously approved or newly submitted plans.

B. A maintenance schedule shall be developed for the life of any stormwater management system and shall state the maintenance to be completed, the time period for completion, and the responsible party that will perform the maintenance. This maintenance schedule shall be printed on the approved stormwater management plan.
