# § 14:4-25. Fees.

Nonrefundable permit fees may be collected for each stormwater management plan submittal. Permit fees will provide for the cost of plan review, administration, and management of the permitting process, and inspection of all projects subject to this chapter. A permit fee schedule shall be established by resolution by the County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County and may be amended from time to time.
