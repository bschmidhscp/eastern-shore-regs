# § 12-17. Piping work.

A. Piping. Any piping beyond a cold water pressure tank must be done under the supervision of a master plumber holding a Queen Anne's County master plumber license.

B. Well work. If installation of piping necessitates any work on a well, an appropriate permit must be obtained from the Board or County Health Department and the work must be inspected by the Plumbing Inspector or Health Department before the work is covered.
