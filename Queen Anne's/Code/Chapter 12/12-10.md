# § 12-10. Special registrations.

A. Registration required; exception.[Amended 4-24-2012 by Ord. No. 11-15]

  (1) A person may not install any appliance, HVAC system, water conditioner, pump or make a propane gas connection without a County registration.

  (2) This section does not apply to a homeowner who installs an appliance other than a gas appliance in or on the homeowner's property.

B. Application. A person desiring a registration under this section shall apply to the Board.

C. Application requirements. An application for a registration must be accompanied by:

  (1) A current State of Maryland master well driller's license, a State of Maryland water conditioner installer's license, State of Maryland gas fitter's license, State of Maryland pump installer's license or State of Maryland HVAC license.[Amended 4-24-2012 by Ord. No. 11-15]

  (2) A certificate of insurance in form and amount as prescribed by the Board.

  (3) Unless the applicant holds a County master plumber license, such fee for registration as may be established by the Board from time to time.[Added 4-24-2012 by Ord. No. 11-15]

D. Issuance; term. A registration issued under this section shall be valid for two years.

E. Registration refusal or revocation. The Board may refuse to issue a registration or may revoke a registration if:

  (1) It is shown that the applicant or registrant is unable or unwilling to properly perform the work; or

  (2) Action is taken on the registrant's certificate of insurance.
