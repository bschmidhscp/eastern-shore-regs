# § 12-4. Duties of Plumbing Board.

In addition to any duties set forth elsewhere, the Board shall:

A. Hear and decide questions in dispute between property owners, or their agents, and the Plumbing Inspector; and

B. Act as adviser to the County Commissioners with respect to the administration and enforcement of this chapter.
