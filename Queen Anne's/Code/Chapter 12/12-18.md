# § 12-18. Maintenance work.

A. In general. Subject to Subsection B of this section, a person employed as a maintenance man in a cannery, oyster house, orange juice plant, milk plant, or similar operation may do maintenance work on the machinery of the company and may routinely service the plumbing system of the plant without being licensed.

B. License required. If maintenance work described in Subsection A of this section requires any new plumbing fixture, new sewer, or addition to an existing sewer handling human waste, the work must be done by a plumber holding a Queen Anne's County plumber license, and shall be governed by the same regulations as other plumbing installations.
