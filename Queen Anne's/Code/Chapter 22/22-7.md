# § 22-7. Employees.

A. In general. The County Commissioners may employ the personnel that the County Commissioners consider necessary to administer properly the functions of the Department of Parks and Recreation.

B. Director of Parks and Recreation.

  (1) If the County Commissioners employ a Director of Parks and Recreation, the person shall have the training, experience, and capacity to initiate and maintain a public park system and a program of public recreation under the person's general supervision.

  (2) The Director of Parks and Recreation serves at the pleasure of the County Commissioners.

C. Salaries. The County Commissioners shall fix the salaries of all personnel.
