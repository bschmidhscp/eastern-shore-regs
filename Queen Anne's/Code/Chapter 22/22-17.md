# § 22-17. Code of conduct.

A. The Code of Conduct promotes, and enforces appropriate athletic conduct and behavior as overseen by the Department of Parks and Recreation and or within the County park system.

B. General powers and duties. The Director of Parks and Recreation Department has the authority to recommend to the County Commissioners suspension or expulsion of leagues, players, coaches and spectators from Queen Anne's County parks and recreation facilities for violations of the Sports Code of Conduct.
