# § 22-16. Definitions.

In this article, the following words have the meanings indicated:

**DEPARTMENT** The Queen Anne's County Department of Parks and Recreation.

**DESIGNEE** Those persons or agencies designated under § 22-24 of this article with responsibility for enforcement of this article.

**DIRECTOR** The Director of the Department.

**SPORTS CODE OF CONDUCT** Refers to appropriate athletic conduct and behavior as determined and adopted from time to time by the Code of Conduct Review Board.
