# § 22-28. Failure to pay or stand trial.

A. Notice. If a person who receives a citation for a violation fails to pay the fine by the date of payment set forth on the citation, and fails to file a notice of intention to stand trial, formal notice of the violation shall be sent to the person's last known address.

B. Additional fine; failure to respond. A person who receives a citation may request adjudication of the case through District Court. The District Court shall schedule the case for trial and summon the defendant to appear.
