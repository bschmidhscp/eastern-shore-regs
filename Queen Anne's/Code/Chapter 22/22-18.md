# § 22-18. Use of County land and recreational facilities.

A. Admittance. Park property and park and recreation programs are open to use by all members of the public regardless of race, sex, national origin, color or creed.

B. Hours of operation.

  (1) The Department will establish hours of operation for park property under its jurisdiction and will post accordingly.

  (2) Any park property may be closed by the Director to the public entirely or for certain uses and such closings shall be posted in advance for public notice.

C. Permits. The Department may issue permits for use of County parks and facilities upon such rules and regulations as may be approved by the County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County.
