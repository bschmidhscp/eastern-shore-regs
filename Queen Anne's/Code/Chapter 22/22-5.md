# § 22-5. Meetings; compensation.

A. Meetings. The Board shall hold the regular and special meetings that the Board considers necessary.

B. Compensation prohibited. All members of the Board serve without compensation but may be reimbursed for reasonable travel expenses associated with serving on the Board.
