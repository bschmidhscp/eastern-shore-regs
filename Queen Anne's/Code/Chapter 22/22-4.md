# § 22-4. Officers.

A. Chairman and Vice Chairman and Secretary. Each year, the Board shall select a Chairman, Vice Chairman and Secretary from the Board's membership.

B. Secretary. The Board shall select a Secretary who may or may not be a member of the Board.
