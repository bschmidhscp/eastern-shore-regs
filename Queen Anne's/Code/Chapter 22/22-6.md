# § 22-6. Report.

A. Annual report. The Board shall submit to the County Commissioners a regular report of the Board's activities, and may recommend further activities and development of the County Parks system and Recreation programs.

B. The Board may advocate to the County Commissioners for budget recommendations that may further the goals and objectives of the Department.
