# § 18:1-152. Report to Commission.

A. When made. Action may not be taken in accordance with this section or §§ 18:1-149 through 18:1-152 of this Chapter 18:1 until any major or minor subdivision necessary to establish the site has been finally approved and the applicant has delivered to the Planning Director:

  (1) Reports containing an affirmative statement by each agency to whom the site plan has been referred that the site plan meets all requirements with which the particular agency is concerned and that the agency has no recommendations;

  (2) The number of paper copies as prescribed by the Department of Planning and Zoning; and

  (3) A disk that contains a CAD drawing illustrating all approved property lines only, if available.

B. Report. After determining that the site plan conforms with all requirements of this Chapter 18:1, the Planning Director shall promptly report those facts to the Planning Commission in writing.
