# § 18:1-163. Application for subdivision approval.

A developer who wishes to subdivide property shall file an application with the Planning Director. The Planning Director may not accept any application that does not contain all information and documentation required under this article. The application shall be accompanied by a nonrefundable fee in an amount prescribed by the County Commissioners.

A. Notice to adjacent property owners. Applications for administrative, major and minor subdivision approval, including applications for any amendment or revision to an approved or recorded subdivision plat, shall include evidence, satisfactory to the Planning Director, that the applicant has provided written notice of the submission and purpose of the application to all adjacent property owners to the mailing address contained in the state property tax records.
