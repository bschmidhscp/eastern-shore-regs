# § 18:1-99. Requirements for approval; covenants.

A. Duties of property owner. In addition to any other requirements of this Chapter 18, including those relating to required improvements, guarantees and other covenants, a property owner involved in an application shall, prior to any approval of a development plan, provide covenants by which land required to remain in open space is restricted to the uses allowed in § 18:1-12 of this Chapter 18:1.

B. Covenants. The covenants shall conform to the requirements of Chapter 18:1, Part 7, Article XXVII.
