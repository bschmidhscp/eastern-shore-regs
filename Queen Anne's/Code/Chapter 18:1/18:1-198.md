# § 18:1-198. Surety bonds.

A. Amount. A surety bond shall be in an amount equal to 125% of the estimated cost of the required improvement as determined by the Planning Commission.

B. Recordation. A bond must be recorded by the owner or developer, and a copy of the recordation fee receipt must be filed with the Planning Director.
