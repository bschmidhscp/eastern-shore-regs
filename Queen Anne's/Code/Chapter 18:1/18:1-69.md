# § 18:1-69. Application of article.

This article contains the landscaping standards required for on-site landscaping, parking lots, district boundaries, and street buffers. The standards:

A. Apply to all multifamily and manufactured home community residential and nonresidential developments; and

B. Do not apply to single-family large-lot subdivisions, or large-lot agricultural or sliding-scale subdivisions in the AG District.
