# § 18:1-199. Letters of credit.

A. Amount. A letter of credit shall be in an amount equal to 110% of the estimated cost of the required improvement as determined by the Planning Commission.

B. Duration. A letter of credit shall be irrevocable for the period that the Planning Commission shall determine, but in no event shall that period be less than the time specified in § 18:1-197 of this Chapter 18:1, plus 30 days.
