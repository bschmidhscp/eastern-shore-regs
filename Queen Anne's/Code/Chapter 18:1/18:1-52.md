# § 18:1-52. Commercial apartments.

A. Commercial apartments are not required to be included in the floor area ratio calculations for a commercial building.

B. Where the maximum building height is 30 feet, a ratio of 1:2 square feet of commercial floor area to apartment floor area is permitted.

C. Where the maximum building height is 45 feet, a ratio of 1:3 square feet of commercial floor area to apartment floor area is permitted.

D. Commercial apartments will not be allowed unless:

  (1) An outdoor balcony or rooftop patio of no less than 50 square feet is provided for each unit;

  (2) In addition to landscaping otherwise required, at least one canopy tree with no less than a two-and-one-half-inch caliper is planted for each unit, planted as close as possible to the units.
