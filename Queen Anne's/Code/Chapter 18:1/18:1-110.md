# § 18:1-110. Department of Planning and Zoning.

A. Establishment. The Department of Planning and Zoning is hereby established.

B. Staff.

  (1) The County Commissioners shall appoint a Planning Director and other professional, enforcement, secretarial, and clerical staff and other employees necessary for the administration and enforcement of this Chapter 18:1.

  (2) All employees shall receive compensation as provided by the County Commissioners.
