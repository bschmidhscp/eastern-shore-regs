# § 18:1-49. Outside storage and display.

A. The outside storage or display of all merchandise, products and raw materials shall meet all required setback lines and may not be allowed on the minimum landscape area as determined under Chapter 18:1, Part 4, Article X, of this Chapter 18.

B. All vehicle or boat repair, painting, or body work activities shall take place within a building or in an area not visible from an adjacent property or public way; except that boats in marinas are exempt from the provisions of this section.

C. Outside storage of material necessary for a permitted home occupation use must be completely screened from the view of adjacent streets and properties.
