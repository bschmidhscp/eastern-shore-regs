# § 18:1-46. Accessory caretaker dwellings.

One caretaker dwelling unit may be provided as an accessory use or structure, provided that:

A. The parcel has a lot area of five or more acres;

B. The structure meets required setbacks applicable to the principal structure; and

C. The structure consists of no more than 50% of the area of the principal building.
