# § 18:1-113. Zoning Administrator.

A. Appointment.

  (1) The County Commissioners may appoint a Zoning Administrator.

  (2) The Zoning Administrator shall be classified as a merit employee.

B. Duties and authority. Subject to the general direction and supervision of the Planning Director, the Zoning Administrator shall:

  (1) Direct and supervise the daily activities of the enforcement, secretarial, and clerical employees of the Department; and

  (2) Perform such other duties and exercise such authority as may be assigned by the Planning Director.
