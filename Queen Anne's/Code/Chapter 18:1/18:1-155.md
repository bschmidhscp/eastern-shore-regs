# § 18:1-155. Form and duration of approval.

A. Form.

  (1) After approval, the Chairman or Secretary of the Commission (or, in the case of a minor site plan, the Planning Director) shall note the approval and its date on the site plan.

  (2) The signature of the Chairman, Secretary, or Planning Director shall constitute final approval of a site plan, and a site plan is not finally approved until the required signature is affixed.

B. Duration. If all construction is not completed within the required time, any approval given under this Chapter 18:1 is void after the time established by the Planning Commission for completion of all construction.
