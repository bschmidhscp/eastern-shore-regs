# § 18:1-43. Community piers.

Community piers are permitted as accessory uses to residential developments, provided that:

A. The community pier facility is located within the residential development on which it is to serve;

B. The number of slips is equal to or less than the number of lots in the development but, in any event, not in excess of the number of slips permitted under § 14:1-43 of this Code;

C. Overnight occupancy is not allowed on any boat docked at the community pier; and

D. The community pier complies with all use limitations and conditions and restrictions set forth in § 14:1-43 of this Code.
