# § 18:1-207. Recording of covenants.

After approval by the Planning Commission, the Planning Director shall:

A. Deliver the instrument containing the covenants to the Recorder of Deeds, together with the required fees furnished by the developer, applicant, or other person for whom the covenants were approved; and

B. Immediately notify such person in writing of the recording.
