# § 18:1-63. Streams and stream buffers.

A. Except as allowed by Subsections B and C below, no development activities are permitted upon designated streams or within designated stream buffers. Stream buffers, except to the extent disturbance is allowed by Subsections B and C below, that are unvegetated at the time new development or redevelopment activities are approved on land on which such a buffer exists, shall be vegetated with native vegetation.[Amended 9-7-2004 by Ord. No. 04-06]

B. Within the AG Zoning District, no more than 20% of areas designated as a stream or a stream buffer may be disturbed for development activity.

C. To preserve natural storm drainage systems, streams and stream buffers may be disturbed if such development activity is part of an approved stormwater management plan.

D. Perennial stream buffers shall be 100 feet, and intermittent buffers shall be 50 feet.

E. Fuel storage in excess of 300 gallons, landfills, effluent disposal uses, except for such uses designed to serve a single-family residential dwelling, fuel, chemical, or asphalt manufacturing or distribution facilities, the confined animal feedlot operations component of a mega farm, and all heavy industrial uses shall be located at least 300 feet from all stream buffers.[Added 9-7-2004 by Ord. No. 04-07]
