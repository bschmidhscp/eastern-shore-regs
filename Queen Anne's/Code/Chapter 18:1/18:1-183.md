# § 18:1-183. Agency reports.

A. Form. Each agency report shall specify the latest revision date shown on the preliminary plat and engineering plans to which the report applies and shall be signed by an authorized representative of the agency.

B. Contents. Each report shall contain either an affirmative statement that the preliminary plat and engineering plans meet all requirements with which the particular agency is concerned and that the agency has no recommendations or:

  (1) Any recommendations for changes in the preliminary plat and engineering plans;

  (2) Any conditions for approval necessary to bring the preliminary plat and engineering plans into compliance with any applicable law, ordinance or regulation or to eliminate any adverse effects of the proposal on those aspects of the general health, safety and welfare of the community for which such agency has special responsibility; and

  (3) Any revisions that the agency requires with respect to the estimates of cost and completion contained in the preliminary plat and engineering plans.
