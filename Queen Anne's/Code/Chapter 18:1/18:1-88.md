# § 18:1-88. Public roads.

*[Amended 12-17-2013 by Ord. No. 13-22 ]*

A. General prohibition. Public road facilities are subject to review and approval by the Department of Public Works in accordance with Chapter 23 of the County Code.

B. Basic characteristics. A public road is required if it provides direct frontage or access to eight or more lots or existing parcels of land.
