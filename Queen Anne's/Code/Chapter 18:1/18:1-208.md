# § 18:1-208. Enforcement authority; right of entry.

A. Enforcement authority:Planning Director. The Planning Director is hereby authorized and directed to enforce the provisions of this Chapter 18:1.

B. Right of entry. The Planning Director, the Deputy Planning Director, the Zoning Administrator, and any employees of the Department designated by the Planning Director, the Deputy Planning Director, or the Zoning Administrator are authorized to enter upon open land in the County for such purposes.
