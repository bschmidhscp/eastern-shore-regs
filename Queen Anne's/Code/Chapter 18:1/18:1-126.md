# § 18:1-126. Nonconforming lots.

A. Authorized uses. Subject to the requirements of this article, a nonconforming lot may be used for any principal use allowed in the zoning district in which the lot is located.

B. Variance. Except as provided in § 18:1-127 of this Chapter 18:1 for certain residential lots, any performance standard of this Chapter 18:1 that cannot be satisfied by a nonconforming lot shall be established by the Board upon application by the owner for a variance.
