# § 18:1-168. Application methods for administrative subdivisions.

An administrative subdivision may be applied for in two methods:

A. Application with a plat as provided in § 18:1-169 of this Chapter 18:1; or

B. Application without a plat as provided in § 18:1-171 of this Chapter 18:1.
