# § 18:1-128. Change of nonconforming uses.

A. In general. A nonconforming use may not be changed to any use other than a use allowed in the zoning district in which it is located, except as provided in this article.

B. [1]Relocation. A nonconforming use, sign, or structure may not be moved, in whole or in part, for any distance whatsoever to any other location on the same or any other lot unless the entire lot and use, sign, or structure shall thereafter conform to the regulations for the zoning district in which it is located after being moved.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: Former Subsection B, Disposal uses, was repealed 7-14-2009 by Ord. No. 09-07. This ordinance also redesignated former Subsection C as Subsection B.
