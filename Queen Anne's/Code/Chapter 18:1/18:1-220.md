# § 18:1-220. Action by Planning Commission.

A. Consideration. In considering any proposed amendment, the Planning Commission shall cause such investigation to be made as it deems necessary and for this purpose may require any person concerned to submit pertinent data and information.

B. Hearing. Before making any recommendation with respect to an amendment involving comprehensive rezoning or an amendment of the Subdivision Regulations, the Planning Commission shall hold at least one public hearing.

C. Report.

  (1) The Commission shall submit its report and recommendation to the County Commissioners within 90 days from the date when it receives the proposal, unless an extension of time is granted by the County Commissioners.

  (2) The recommendations of the Planning Commission shall include discussion of the matters required to be considered by the County Commissioners.
