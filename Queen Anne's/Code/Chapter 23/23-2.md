# § 23-2. Incorporation of additional documents by reference.

For the purpose of this chapter, the following documents are incorporated by reference:

A. The Roads Design and Construction Standards Manual, Queen Anne's County Department of Public Works;

B. Standard Specifications of Construction and Materials, Maryland Department of Transportation, State Highway Administration, 2001 Edition.

C. Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices, Department of Transportation, Federal Highway Administration, December 2000 Edition.
