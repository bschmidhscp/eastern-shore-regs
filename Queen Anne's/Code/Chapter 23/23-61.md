# § 23-61. Road Naming and Addressing Manual; maps.

A. Road Naming and Addressing Manual.

  (1) The Department of Planning and Zoning shall maintain a Road Naming and Addressing Manual that describes the criteria, procedures, and methods used to name roads and to assign address numbers to properties in the County.

  (2) The Manual shall include the development of a system from which the address numbers will be derived.

B. Maps. The Department of Planning and Zoning shall keep a set of maps of the County that displays the address system and the names and route numbers of roads.
