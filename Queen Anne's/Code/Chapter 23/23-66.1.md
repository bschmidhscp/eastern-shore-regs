# § 23-66.1. Legislative purpose; statutory authority; applicability.

A. Purpose. An act to provide standards for the construction, maintenance and repair of sidewalks.

B. Authority. This article is authorized under the provisions of the Local Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, as amended.

C. Jurisdiction. This article shall only apply to the unincorporated areas of Queen Anne's County.
