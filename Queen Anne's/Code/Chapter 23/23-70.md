# § 23-70. Proceedings before District Court.

A. Manner of prosecution. In any proceeding before the District Court, the violation shall be prosecuted in the same manner and to the same extent as a civil infraction under Article 25B, § 13(c), of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

B. Cost of proceedings. If a person is found by the District Court to have committed a violation of this chapter, the person is liable for the costs of the proceedings in the District Court.
