# § 23-1. Terms defined.

Under Chapter 23, Roads, the following terms have the meanings indicated:

**COUNTY ROAD** A road, street or associated rights-of-way and easements which is maintained by the Department of Public Works, Roads Division, and is a component of the County roads system.

**COUNTY ROAD SYSTEM** Those roads which are listed by the Department of Public Works, Roads Division, as an official County road on the Annual Highway Mileage Report as approved by the Maryland Department of Transportation.

**DEPARTMENT** The Department of Public Works of Queen Anne's County.

**DEPARTMENT OF PLANNING AND ZONING** The Department of Planning and Zoning of Queen Anne's County.

**DEVELOPER** Any person, contractor or other agent of the person who commences to effect a development involving construction of or upgrading of roads for that person or another person.

**DIRECTOR** The Director of Public Works of Queen Anne's County.

**EASEMENT** A grant or reservation by the owner of land for the use of such land by others:

A. For a specific purpose or purposes; and

B. Which must be included in the conveyance of land affected by such easement.

**ENGINEER** The Chief Roads Engineer for the Roads Division of the Department.

**FINANCE OFFICE** The Queen Anne's County Office of Budget and Finance.

**GRADING** Any act by which soil is cleared, stripped, stockpiled, excavated, scarified, filled or any combination thereof.

**LOCAL ROAD** A road or street:

A. Designed to provide vehicular access to abutting property; and

B. Designated as such in the transportation element of the Comprehensive Plan.

**OTHER PUBLIC ROAD** A road historically utilized by the general public which is not a part of the County, state or federal road systems.

**PLANNING COMMISSION** The Queen Anne's County Planning Commission.

**PRIVATE ROAD** An improved road or right-of-way held and/or maintained in private ownership and which is not a component of the County, state or federal road systems.

**ROAD** A public or private way for vehicular traffic, including a right-of-way, street, avenue, drive, lane, terrace, boulevard, circle, highway, or other similar term.

**ROADS BOARD** The Roads Board for Queen Anne's County.

**ROADS DESIGN MANUAL** The Roads Design and Construction Standards Manual of Queen Anne's County.

**ROADS DIVISION** The Roads Division of the Department of Public Works of Queen Anne's County.

**ROAD PLAN** A plan for the construction or improvement of any new or existing road.

**ROADWAY STRUCTURE** The stabilized road base, paving, curb and gutter, storm drains, culverts, inlets, drainage ditches and stormwater management systems.

**SHA** The Maryland State Highway Administration.

**STANDARDS**

A. The standards as contained in the Roads Design and Construction Standards Manual of Queen Anne's County.

B. If no County standards exist, then SHA standards.

**SUBDIVISION** The division of a lot, tract, or parcel of land into two or more lots, plots, parcels, sites, or other divisions of land.

**SURETY** Any form of security, including bond, escrow, deposit, collateral, property, or instrument of credit, in an amount and form satisfactory to the Department.

**UTILITIES** All overhead and underground electric, telephone, cable TV, gas or propane lines that service a development.
