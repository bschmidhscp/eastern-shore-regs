# § 23-20. Designation of money.

A. Designation. The money levied under this article shall be designated for construction, reconstruction, and maintenance of County roads, bridges, drains, watercourses, culverts, curbs and gutters, and purchasing and renting of equipment, land, and buildings in connection therewith.

B. Specific purposes. The County Commissioners may not earmark for a specific purpose any of the money levied under this article unless acting in their capacity as the Board.
