# § 23-39. Suspension and revocation.

Any grading or building permit issued by the Department may be suspended or revoked after written notice is given to the permittee for any of the following reasons:

A. Any violation(s) of the conditions of the road construction plan approval.

B. Changes in site characteristics upon which an approval was granted.

C. Construction is not in accordance with the approved plan.
