# § 23-72. Civil action for declaratory judgment or injunction.

A. Instead of or in addition to citation. Instead of or in addition to citing a violator for a civil infraction, the County may enforce this chapter by civil action for declaratory judgment or injunction.

B. Legal fees and costs. In the case of a successful action for declaratory judgment or an injunction, legal fees and costs may be awarded to the County.
