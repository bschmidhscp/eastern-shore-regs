# § 23-43. Security.

A. Security requirements from developer. The Department shall require from the developer a surety or cash bond, irrevocable letter of credit, or other means of security acceptable to and approved by the Department prior to the issuance of any building and/or grading permit for a road construction or road improvement project.

  (1) The amount of the security shall be 125% of the approved engineer's estimated cost of the construction.

  (2) The security required in this section shall include:

    (a) A provision relative to forfeiture for failure to complete work specified in the approved road construction plan;

    (b) Compliance with all of the provisions of this Part 3, the Roads Design Manual other applicable laws and regulations; and

    (c) Any time limitations.

B. Release of the security.

  (1) The security shall not be fully released without:

    (a) A final inspection of the completed work by the Department;

    (b) Submission of as-built plans; and

    (c) Certification of completion by the Department that the roadway and roadway systems comply with the approved road plan and the provisions of this Part 3.

  (2) The Department may adopt procedures for reduction or partial release of the security held by the Department following completion and acceptance of part or all of the project.

  (3) The procedures used for partially releasing securities must be specified by the Department in writing prior to road plan approval.

C. Retainage.

  (1) Retainage in the amount of 5% of the original security shall be held by the Department and kept in force for one year after the acceptance of the road into the County system.
