# § 23-44. When security becomes insufficient or unsatisfactory.

A. Notice. Whenever the County determines that the security provided is insufficient or unsatisfactory, the County shall notify the permit holder.

B. New security.

  (1) Within 10 days after the notice, the permit holder shall deliver to the County a new security that:

    (a) Meets the requirements of this Part 3; and

    (b) Is satisfactory to the County.
