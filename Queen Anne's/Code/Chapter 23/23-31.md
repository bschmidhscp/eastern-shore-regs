# § 23-31. Exceeding weight or speed limit on bridge or culvert.

A. Prohibited. Unless a person has a permit in writing from the Department, a person may not draw, drive, propel, or in any other manner take or cause to be taken, drawn, driven, or propelled over a bridge or culvert a wagon, truck, road engine, road roller, traction engine, threshing machine, or other vehicle of any kind:

  (1) That weighs, with or without a load, more than the number of pounds specified on the sign erected at the entrances or approaches of the bridge or culvert; or

  (2) At a rate of speed greater than the rate specified on the sign.

B. Liability for damages. In addition to any civil or other penalty under this chapter, the owner or person in control of the vehicle is liable to the Board for all damages caused by the violation.

C. Evidence. At the trial of a person charged with violating this section, oral proof of the existence of a sign and its contents is prima facie evidence of the sign's existence and contents.
