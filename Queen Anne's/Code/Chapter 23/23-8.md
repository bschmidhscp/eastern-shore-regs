# § 23-8. Employees.

A. In general. The County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County shall employ the personnel that the Board considers necessary to carry out this Part 2.

B. Compensation. The County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County shall fix the amount of compensation for the personnel and the manner of paying the compensation.
