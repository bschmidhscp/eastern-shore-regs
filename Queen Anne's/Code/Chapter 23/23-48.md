# § 23-48. Improvements to existing County roads.

The Department may require a developer to improve or upgrade an existing County road when a development occurs to ensure that:

A. Consistent planning, design and construction standards are met;

B. The road is suitable for the nature of the traffic proposed by the development; and

C. All public safety concerns are addressed. The nature of the improvements or upgrades may include but are not limited to resurfacing, installation of traffic control devices or lighting, intersection realignments, right-of-way dedication, road widening, sidewalks or pedestrian paths, entrance or access improvements and drainage improvements.
