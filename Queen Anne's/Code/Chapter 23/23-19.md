# § 23-19. Levy by Commissioners.

When making their annual levy, the County Commissioners may levy an amount that they consider necessary for the:

A. Building, repairing, reconstructing, maintenance, and regulation of the use of roads, bridges, drains, watercourses, culverts, and curbs and gutters in the County;

B. Purchase and maintenance of equipment to be used for the purposes stated in Subsection A of this section; and

C. Purchase or rental of land or buildings to be used for the purposes stated in Subsection A of this section.
