# § 24-88. Rules of interpretation; repealer.

A. May. In this Part 3, the term "may" is permissive.

B. Shall. In this Part 3, the term "shall" is mandatory.

C. Inconsistency. Any prior ordinance or resolution of the County that is inconsistent with the provisions of this Part 3 is repealed to the extent of the inconsistency.
