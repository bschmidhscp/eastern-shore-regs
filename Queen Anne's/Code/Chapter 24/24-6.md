# § 24-6. Personnel.

A. In general. The Director of Public Works shall have whatever engineering, legal, advisory, clerical, and other personnel as are:

  (1) Necessary to carry out this Part 1 in the judgment of the Director; and

  (2) Approved by the County Commissioners.

B. Subject to Queen Anne's County Merit System. With the exception of legal counsel, the personnel are subject to the regulations of the Queen Anne's County Merit System.

C. Wages of legal personnel. Legal personnel shall receive wages as may be approved by the County.
