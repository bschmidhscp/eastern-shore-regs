# § 24-119. Discharge of waste.

A. Location. All permit holders shall discharge their waste at the Kent Narrows/ Stevensville/ Grasonville facility or another facility approved by the Queen Anne's County Health Department.

B. Refusal of waste. The Queen Anne's County Sanitary Commission may refuse any waste considered harmful to the treatment facility or receiving body of water.

C. Discharge into sanitary sewer manhole prohibited. A person may not discharge waste into a sanitary sewer manhole.
