# § 24-162. System requirements.

A. In general. The type, capacities, location, and layout of an individual private wastewater disposal system shall comply with all requirements of the Department of Health.

B. Required area. A permit may not be issued for any private wastewater disposal system employing subsurface soil absorption facilities where the area of the lot is less than 20,000 square feet (1,858 square meters).

C. Discharge restriction. A septic tank or cesspool may not be allowed to discharge into any natural outlet.
