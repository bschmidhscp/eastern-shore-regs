# § 24-29. Fixing and levying benefit assessment.

A. In general. Whenever any water supply or sewerage project or part of a project in the Sanitary District is completed, the County shall fix and levy a benefit assessment on all properties in the Sanitary District abutting on the water main or sewer, in accordance with the classification or subdivision of the property.

B. Notice.

  (1) The County shall notify in writing all owners of property of:

    (a) The class or subdivision into which their properties fall;

    (b) The charge determined; and

    (c) A time and place at which the owner will be heard.

  (2) The County shall:

    (a) Mail the notice by registered or certified mail, return receipt requested, to the last known address of the owner;

    (b) Serve the notice in person on any adult occupying the premises; or

    (c) In the case of a vacant or unimproved property, post the notice on the premises.

C. Finality of classification and benefit assessment.

  (1) The classification of and benefit assessed against any property as made by the County is final, subject only to revision at the hearing.

  (2) The County may change the classification of property from time to time as the uses of property change.
