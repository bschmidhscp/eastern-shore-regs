# § 24-133. Inspection process; property owner immunity.

A. Safety rules. While performing the necessary work on private property referred to in § 24-132A of this Part 5, the Director, Chief, or duly authorized employees of the County shall observe all safety rules applicable to the premises established by the company.

B. Immunity.

  (1) The company shall be held harmless for injury or death to the County employees.

  (2) The County shall indemnify the company against loss or damage to its property by County employees and against liability claims and demands for personal injury or property damage asserted against the company and growing out of the gauging and sampling operation, except as such may be caused by negligence or failure of the company to maintain safe conditions as required in § 24-153 of this Part 5.
