# § 24-139. Connection costs; indemnification of County.

A. Connection costs. All costs and expenses incidental to the installation and connection of the building sewer shall be borne by the owner.

B. Indemnification of County. The owner shall indemnify the County from any loss or damage that may directly or indirectly be occasioned by the installation of the building sewer.
