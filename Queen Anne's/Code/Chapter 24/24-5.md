# § 24-5. Director of Public Works.

A. Appointment. The Director of Public Works of Queen Anne's County shall be the chief administrative officer of Queen Anne's County Sanitary District.

B. Duties. The Director of Public Works shall:

  (1) Administer and enforce regulations relating to building and plumbing codes; and

  (2) Execute the policies of the County within this Part 1.
