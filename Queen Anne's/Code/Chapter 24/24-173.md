# § 24-173. Violations and penalties.

Any person violating any of the provisions or requirements of this Part 5 shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction, shall be subject to a fine not exceeding $100 or to confinement in the County jail for not more than 30 days, or both, for each offense. Each day's violation shall constitute a separate offense.
