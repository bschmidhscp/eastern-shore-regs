# § 24-86. Penalties established.

A. In general. A person who violates any of the provisions or requirements of this Part 2 is guilty of a misdemeanor and, on conviction, is subject to a fine not exceeding $100 or to confinement in the County jail for not more than 30 days, or both, for each offense.

B. Separate offense. Each day's violation is a separate offense.
