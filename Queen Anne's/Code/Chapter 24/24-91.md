# § 24-91. Enumeration of wastewater subdistricts.

*[Amended 10-24-2017 by Ord. No. 17-14 ; 9-23-2025 by Ord. No. 25-13 ]*

A. Kent Narrows/Stevensville/Grasonville Wastewater Subdistrict.

  (1) There is a Kent Narrows/Stevensville/Grasonville Wastewater Subdistrict in the Sanitary District.

  (2) The Kent Narrows/Stevensville/Grasonville Wastewater Subdistrict encompasses the land area of the County contained within a boundary box whose corners are defined by the following coordinate pairs of the NAD83 Maryland State Plane Coordinate System, as shown on the map on file at the Sanitary Commission's office and made a part of this Part 3:

    (a) X 1495000, Y 490000

    (b) X 1547000, Y 490000

    (c) X 1547000, Y 466000

    (d) X 1503000, Y 466000

    (e) X 1503000, Y 469000

    (f) X 1495000, Y 469000

  (3) The Kent Narrows/Stevensville/Grasonville Wastewater Subdistrict shall include the subdivisions of Cloverfields and Bay City in the Fourth Election District of Queen Anne's County and shall also include the land area in the former Prospect Bay Wastewater Subdistrict contained within a boundary box whose corners are defined by the following coordinate pairs of the NAD 83 Maryland State Plan Coordinate System:

    (a) X 1538000, Y 461500

    (b) X 1544000, Y 461500

    (c) X 1544000, Y 447500

    (d) X 1538000, Y 447500

B. Sudlersville/Barclay Wastewater Subdistrict.

  (1) There is a Sudlersville/Barclay Wastewater Subdistrict in the Sanitary District.

  (2) The Sudlersville/Barclay Wastewater Subdistrict encompasses the land within the municipal limits of the Town of Sudlersville and Barclay in Queen Anne's County and shall include an area 5' on each side of the denied access transmission line within Maryland Route 313 (Goldsboro Road) from Sudlersville to Barclay.

C. Southern Kent Island Wastewater Subdistrict.

  (1) There is a Southern Kent Island Subdistrict in the Sanitary District.

  (2) The Southern Kent Island Wastewater Subdistrict encompasses the land area of the County contained within a boundary box whose corners are defined by the following coordinate pairs of the NAD83 Maryland State Plane Coordinate System.

    (a) X 1492000, Y 469000

    (b) X 1503000, Y 469000

    (c) X 1503000, Y 438000

    (d) X 1492000, Y 438000
