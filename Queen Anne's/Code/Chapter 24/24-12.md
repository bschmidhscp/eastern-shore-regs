# § 24-12. Removal of obstructions.

A. In general. On reasonable notice from the County, a person who has a building, conduit, pipe, track, pole, or other structure or obstruction in, on, over, under, or through any public road, street, or way which blocks or impedes the construction and establishment of the County's water supply, sewerage, or drainage systems or other works shall promptly shift, adjust, accommodate, or remove structures or obstructions to fully meet the exigencies that caused the action.

B. Cost of changes. The County shall bear the reasonable cost of these changes.

C. Penalty. Any violation of the provisions of this section is a misdemeanor punishable under this Part 1.
