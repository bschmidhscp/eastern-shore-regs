# § 24-118. Suspension or revocation of permit.

A. Grounds for suspension or revocation. The Queen Anne's County Sanitary Commission may suspend or revoke a permit:

  (1) To stop the discharge of any material that presents a hazard to the public health, safety, or welfare, or the safe operation of the waste treatment facility; or

  (2) If a septage hauler is found to have violated any provision of this Part 4.

B. Reinstatement of suspended permits. The Sanitary Commission may reinstate a suspended permit on proof of satisfactory compliance with all provisions of this Part 4.
