# § 24-80. Rate schedule.

A. Establishment. The Sanitary Commission shall establish by resolution a rate schedule for each subdistrict in accordance with this Part 2.

B. Revaluation of rate schedule.

  (1) At least once each year, the Sanitary Commission shall revaluate the rate schedule.

  (2) The Sanitary Commission may adopt new rate schedules as appropriate.
