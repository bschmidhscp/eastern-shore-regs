# § 24-7. Report and statement.

On or before July 1 of each year, the Director of Public Works shall prepare and file with the County:

A. A report on the physical plants and equipment of the Sanitary District;

B. A statement of the activities of the Sanitary District during the preceding fiscal year;

C. A statement of activities proposed to be undertaken during the ensuing fiscal year; and

D. Whatever other statistical and financial matters in connection with the Sanitary District as may be of interest to the County and to the taxpayers.
