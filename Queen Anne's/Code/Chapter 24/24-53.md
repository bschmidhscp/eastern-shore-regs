# § 24-53. Inconsistent laws.

A. Repeal. Any act or part of an act that is inconsistent with this Part 1 is repealed to the extent of the inconsistency.

B. No restriction on power of Department of the Environment and Department of Natural Resources. Notwithstanding Subsection A of this section, this section does not restrict any control that the State Department of the Environment or the State Department of Natural Resources are empowered to exercise in the Sanitary District.
