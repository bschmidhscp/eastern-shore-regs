# § 24-56. Penalties established; continuing violation.

A. District Court. Unless otherwise provided, each act or omission that is designated as a misdemeanor by this Part 1 is punishable by the District Court of Queen Anne's County.

B. Penalty. A person who commits an act or omission designated as a misdemeanor by this Part 1, on conviction, is subject to a fine not exceeding $100 or imprisonment in the County jail for not more than 30 days, or both, in the discretion of the District Court.

C. Continuing violation. If an act or omission is of a continuing nature and is persisted in, in violation of this Part 1 or any regulation adopted under this Part 1, a conviction for one offense may not bar conviction for a continuation of the offense after the first or any succeeding conviction.
