# § 24-4. Sanitary Commission.

*[Amended by Ord. No. 94-08]*

A. Composition and jurisdiction. To carry out this Part 1, the Sanitary District shall be under the jurisdiction and control of the County Commissioners who shall sit as the Sanitary Commission.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: Former Subsection B, Salary of members, which immediately followed this subsection, was repealed 11-12-2013 by Ord. No. 13-23.
