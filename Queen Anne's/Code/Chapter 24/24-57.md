# § 24-57. Terms defined.

Unless the context specifically indicates otherwise, in this Part 2 the following words have the meanings indicated.

**AGRICULTURE** The science or art of cultivating the soil, producing crops, and raising livestock.

**CHIEF** The Chief Sanitary Engineer or an authorized deputy, agent, or representative of the Chief.

**DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS** The Department of Public Works of Queen Anne's County.

**DIRECTOR** The Director of Public Works of Queen Anne's County or an authorized deputy, agent, or representative of the Director.

**DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM** The lines and appurtenances that deliver potable water from a water supply system or storage facility to individual properties.

**EASEMENT** An acquired legal right for the specific use of land owned by others.

**FLOW CHARACTERISTICS** The variations in the flow rate in a particular user. These variations are typically described by such terms as "average daily flow," "peak daily flow," "minimum monthly flow," "maximum hourly flow," etc.

**FRONT FOOTAGE OR FRONTAGE** The length in feet of the property line or lines abutting a water distribution system.

**INDUSTRIAL OR BUSINESS** Any institutional, industrial, or commercial use as defined in §§ 18-1-030, 18-1-031, and 18-1-032 of the Queen Anne's County Zoning Ordinance.[1]

**LIEN** A charge on real or personal property for the satisfaction of a debt or duty.

**LOT** Any plot or parcel of land occupied or intended to be occupied by a principal building or use, or a group of buildings, and its accessory buildings and uses, and having frontage on a road.

**MULTIFAMILY OR ATTACHED DWELLING** Two or more adjoining dwelling units, each of which is separated from the others by one or more unpierced walls from ground to roof.

**PARCEL** A tract or plot of land.

**pH** The logarithm of the reciprocal of the hydrogen-ion concentration.

**POTABLE WATER** Water fit for human consumption and domestic use, which is sanitary and normally free of minerals, organic substances, and toxic agents in excess of reasonable amounts for domestic use.

**RATE SCHEDULE** A schedule, table, or chart detailing specific charges for each service by unit of low volume, front footage, or other measurement.

**RESIDENCE** Any private home, tenant house, apartment, hotel, marina, mobile home, institution, or place where people reside for any period of time.

**ROAD** A public or private right-of-way that provides a public means of access to abutting property.

**SANITARY COMMISSION** The County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County who sit as the Sanitary Commission having jurisdiction over the Sanitary District.

**SANITARY DISTRICT** Queen Anne's County, State of Maryland, exclusive of any incorporated municipality.

**SINGLE-FAMILY DETACHED DWELLING** A dwelling designed for and occupied by not more than one family and having no roof, wall, or floor in common with any other dwelling unit.

**STORAGE FACILITIES** A tank or other reservoir used for the storage of water.

**UNIT** A house, building, mobile home, condominium unit, commercial business, rental or leasehold unit, vessel, pleasure craft, or any similar structure or facility.

**USER CHARGE** A charge levied on users of a water system for the cost of operation and maintenance of the system.

**ZONING ORDINANCE** The Queen Anne's County Zoning Ordinance as may be amended from time to time.[2]
