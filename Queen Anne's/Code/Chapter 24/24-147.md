# § 24-147. Stormwater; other unpolluted discharges.

A. In general. Stormwater, other than that exempted under § 24-146B of this Part 5, and all other unpolluted drainage shall be discharged to such sewers as are specifically designated as combined sewers or storm sewers or to a natural outlet approved by the Chief and other regulatory agencies.

B. Unpolluted industrial cooling water. Unpolluted industrial cooling water or process waters may be discharged, on approval of the Chief, to a storm sewer, combined sewer, or natural outlet.
