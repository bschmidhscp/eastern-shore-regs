# § 24-8. Audit.

*[Amended 11-15-2005 by Ord. No. 05-22 ]*

A. Audit. The financial accounts relating to the Sanitary District shall be audited annually by a certified public accountant to be employed and paid by the County out of whatever Sanitary District funds as may be available.
