# § 15-2. Policy.

The purpose of this chapter is to protect the public health, safety, and welfare in residential structures and premises by:

A. Establishing minimum property maintenance standards for:

  (1) Basic equipment and facilities that are used for light, ventilation, heating, and sanitation for residential structures and premises; and

  (2) Safe and sanitary maintenance of residential structures and premises;

B. Establishing minimum requirements for residential structures and premises for means of egress, fire protection systems, and other equipment and devices necessary for safety from fire;

C. Fixing the responsibilities of property owners, operators, and tenants of residential structures and premises; and

D. Providing for administration, enforcement, and penalties.
