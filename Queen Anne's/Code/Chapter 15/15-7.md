# § 15-7. Density.

A. Maximum/minimum density.

  (1) The maximum density of occupancy of a dwelling unit by individuals may not exceed:

    (a) One individual for each 100 square feet of total habitable room area; or

    (b) A total number of individuals equal to twice the number of its habitable rooms.

  (2) For single-individual occupancy, there must be at least 150 square feet of total habitable room area.

B. Sloping ceiling. To determine the maximum permissible occupancy of a habitable room with a sloping ceiling, the floor area where the ceiling height is less than five feet may not be considered.
