# § 14:3-29. Placement of fill.

A. Disposal of fill, including but not limited to earthen soils, rock, rubble, construction debris, woody debris, and trash, shall not be permitted in special flood hazard areas.

B. Fill shall not be placed in floodways or coastal high hazard areas (V Zones) except as provided in § 14:3-55.

C. Fill proposed to be placed to elevate structures in flood hazard areas (A Zones) that are not coastal high hazard areas (V Zones) shall comply with the floodway requirements in §§ 14:3-40A, 14:3-40B, and 14:3-40C and the limitations of § 14:3-41B.
