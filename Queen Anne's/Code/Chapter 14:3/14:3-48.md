# § 14:3-48. Location and site preparation.

A. Use of fill shall comply with § 14:3-41B.

B. Buildings shall be located landward of the reach of mean high tide.

C. Site preparations shall not alter sand dunes unless an engineering analysis demonstrates that the potential for flood damage is not increased.
