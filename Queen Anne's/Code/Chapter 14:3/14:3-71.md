# § 14:3-71. Violations and penalties.

Violations of these regulations or failure to comply with the requirements of these regulations or any conditions attached to a permit or variance shall constitute a misdemeanor. Any person responsible for a violation shall comply with the notice of violation or stop-work order. Failure to comply shall be punishable by a fine not to exceed $1,000 or imprisonment not to exceed 90 days or both such fine and imprisonment. Each day a violation continues shall be considered a separate offense. Nothing herein contained shall prevent the Queen Anne's County from taking such other lawful action as is necessary to prevent or remedy any violation.
