# § 14:3-51. Accessory structures.

A. Accessory structures shall be limited to no more than 600 square feet in floor area.

B. Accessory structures shall comply with the elevation requirements and other requirements of § 14:3-49 or, if not elevated, shall:

  (1) Be usable only for parking of vehicles or limited storage;

  (2) Be constructed with flood damage-resistant materials below the base flood elevation;

  (3) Be constructed and placed to offer the minimum resistance to the flow of floodwaters;

  (4) Be anchored to prevent flotation;

  (5) Have electrical service and mechanical equipment elevated to or above the base flood elevation; and

  (6) Have walls that meet the requirements of § 14:3-49(D)(3) through (6), and walls shall have flood openings that meet the requirements of § 14:3-41C(4).
