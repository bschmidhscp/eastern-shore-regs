# § 14:3-70. Notice of violation and stop-work order.

If the Floodplain Administrator determines that there has been a violation of any provision of these regulations, the Floodplain Administrator shall give notice of such violation to the owner, the owner's authorized agent, and the person responsible for such violation, and may issue a stop-work order. The notice of violation or stop-work order shall be in writing and shall:

A. Include a list of violations, referring to the section or sections of these regulations that have been violated;

B. Order remedial action which, if taken, will effect compliance with the provisions of these regulations;

C. Specify a reasonable period of time to correct the violation;

D. Advise the recipients of the right to appeal; and

E. Be served in person; or

F. Be posted in a conspicuous place in or on the property and sent by registered or certified mail to the last known mailing address, residence, or place of business of the recipients.
