# § 6-2. Administration; annual appropriation.

A. In general. The Queen Anne's County Court Law Library shall be administered in accordance with rules of the Second Judicial Circuit.

B. Annual appropriation. The County Commissioners shall appropriate each year an amount necessary:

  (1) For maintenance of the Queen Anne's County Court Law Library;

  (2) For the salary of a librarian; and

  (3) Subject to approval of the County Administrative Judge, for books and supplies that the librarian orders.
