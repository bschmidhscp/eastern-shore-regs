# § 6-1. Improper use of Court House grounds.

A. In general. Unless a designated County official consents, a person may not enter, walk, run, ride, or drive on unpaved public grounds adjacent to the Court House in the Town of Centreville, known as "Court House Square," or mutilate, cut, or destroy any flowers, shrubbery, or trees on Court House Square.[Amended by Ord. No. 92-09]

B. Penalty. A person who violates Subsection A of this section is guilty of a misdemeanor and, on conviction, is subject to a fine not exceeding $25 for each offense.
