# § 14:2-15. Permit fee.

A. Fee schedule. The County Commissioners may establish a permit fee schedule for the administration and management of the erosion and sediment control program.

B. Exemption. Capital improvement projects, refuse disposal areas, sanitary landfills, and public works projects are exempt from the permit fee.
