# § 14:2-14. Permit expiration and renewal.

A. Expiration. A building or grading permit shall expire two years from the date of issuance unless extended or renewed by the Queen Anne's County Department of Planning and Zoning.

B. Renewal. Application for permit renewal shall be made at least two months before the permit expiration date.
