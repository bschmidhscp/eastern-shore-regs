# § 14:2-32. Misdemeanor.

A. In general. A person who violates any provision of this Chapter 14:2 is guilty of a misdemeanor and, on conviction in a court of competent jurisdiction, is subject to a fine not exceeding $1,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 90 days, or both for each violation.

B. Costs. The Court may impose costs.

C. Each day a separate offense. Each day on which the violation occurs is a separate offense.
