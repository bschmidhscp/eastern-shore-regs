# § 16-6. Definitions.

In this article, the following words have the meanings indicated.

**ACT AS A PEDDLER** To act as an itinerant or door-to-door peddler or salesperson of goods, wares, or merchandise, by sample or otherwise.

**LICENSE** A license issued by the County Commissioners to act as a peddler.
