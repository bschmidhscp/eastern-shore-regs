# § 16-7. Scope.

This article does not apply to any person who is:

A. Going from door-to-door in any activity or enterprise that is conducted for and on behalf of any nonprofit, charitable, or eleemosynary agency or organization;

B. Going from door-to-door in the sale of farm produce or seafood of any kind that the person has produced or taken in Queen Anne's County; or

C. Selling or delivering newspapers.
