# § 16-5. Prohibited acts; violations and penalties.

A. In general. A person who violates any provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor and, on conviction, is subject to a fine of not less than $25 but not exceeding $100 for each violation.

B. Disposition of fines. All fines collected under this section shall be paid into the County Treasury and used for the general purposes of the County.
