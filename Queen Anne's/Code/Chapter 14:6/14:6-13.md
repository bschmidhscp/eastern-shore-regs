# § 14:6-13. Appeal of notice of violation.

Any person aggrieved by the action of any official charged with the enforcement of this chapter, as the result of issuance of a written notice of violation, or an alleged failure to properly enforce the chapter in regard to a specific application, shall have the right to appeal the action to the Circuit Court of Queen Anne's County. The appeal must be filed in writing within five business days from the date of the notice of violation or determination to the applicant and shall clearly state the grounds on which the appeal is based.
