# § 19-11.1. Right to farm notice and real estate transfer disclosure.

A. Upon any transfer of real property by any means, the transferor shall provide the purchaser or lessee a statement specifically advising the purchaser or lessee of the existence of this Article V which shall be in substantially the form set forth in Appendix A at the end of this chapter.

B. Any person who violates any provision of this section is guilty of an infraction punishable by a civil penalty not exceeding $100. Failure to comply with any provision of this right to farm notice and real estate transfer disclosure section shall not prevent the recording of any document, or the title to real property or any mortgage or deed of trust made in good faith or for value, and it shall not affect the application of this Part 2.
