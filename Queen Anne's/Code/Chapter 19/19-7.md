# § 19-7. Failure to abate nuisance.

A. Penalty. Under § 13C of Article 25B of the Annotated Code of Maryland, a person who fails to abate a nuisance is guilty of a civil infraction and is subject to a fine of not less than $25 but not exceeding $500.

B. Each day a separate offense. Each day that a violation continues is a separate offense.
