# § 14:1-57. Areas unsuitable for mineral extraction.

The provisions of any conditional use permit issued by the Board of Appeals notwithstanding, mineral resource extraction operations, including new wash plants, shall be prohibited in those portions of the critical area unsuitable for surface mining including:

A. Habitat protection areas;

B. Highly erodible soils;

C. Areas where the use of renewable resource lands would result in long-term loss of productivity of forested or agricultural land or degradation of water quality or loss of vital habitat; and

D. The Buffer.
