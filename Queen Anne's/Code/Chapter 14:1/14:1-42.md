# § 14:1-42. General provisions relating to water-dependent facilities.

New or expanded water-dependent facilities may be permitted in the Buffer, provided it can be shown that:

A. They are water-dependent;

B. The project meets a recognized private right or public need;

C. The adverse effects on water quality and fish and wildlife habitat are minimized;

D. Non-water-dependent structures and operations associated with water-dependent projects or activities are located outside the Buffer, insofar as possible; and

E. The facilities satisfy the requirements of § 14:1-34 of this Chapter 14:1.
