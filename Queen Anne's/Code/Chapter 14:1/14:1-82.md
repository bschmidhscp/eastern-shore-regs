# § 14:1-82. Effect of requirements of guarantee.

A. Effect of requirement. If an owner or developer is required under the provisions of this Chapter 14:1 to furnish a guarantee, no project approval shall be given to any part of the project to which the guarantee relates until authorized surety is provided in accordance with this article.

B. Sectional development. If a development activity is approved for implementation or construction in sections or stages, a guarantee is required with respect to all required improvements necessary to the proper operation and function of the section in question, even though such required improvements may be located outside of that section or stage.
