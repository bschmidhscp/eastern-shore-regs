# § 14:1-86. Surety bonds.

A. Amount. A surety bond shall be in an amount equal to 125% of the estimated cost of the required improvement, habitat protection, or enhancement measure as determined by the Planning Commission.

B. Recordation. All bonds must be recorded by the owner or developer, and a copy of the recordation fee receipt must be filed with the Planning Director.
