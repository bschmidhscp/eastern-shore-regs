# § 14:1-73. Amendments to add areas to critical area.

*[Amended 5-21-1999 by Ord. No. 99-02; 10-1-1999 by Ord. No. 99-14]*

A. Procedure. Addition of areas to the critical area shall be processed as amendments to the Official Critical Area Maps as set forth in § 14:1-74 of this Chapter 14:1.

B. Approval of Critical Area Commission required. Such amendment shall be approved by the Critical Area Commission.
