# § 14:1-14. Adoption.

The Queen Anne's County Chesapeake Bay Critical Area Program, as approved by the Critical Area Commission on June 29, 1988, and as amended on February 15, 1989, and June 4, 1996, is hereby adopted as the comprehensive planning guide and statement of the goals and objectives of Queen Anne's County for all development and development activity within the Queen Anne's County critical area.
