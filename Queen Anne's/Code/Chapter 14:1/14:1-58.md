# § 14:1-58. Mineral extraction performance standards.

A. Reclamation. All mined areas shall be reclaimed within one year after mining operations have been discontinued.

B. Vegetated buffer. To the extent possible, a minimum one-hundred-foot vegetated buffer, approved by the Board of Appeals, will be maintained around existing mining operations.

C. End use plan. An end use plan shall accompany any application for a mineral extraction use in the critical area.
