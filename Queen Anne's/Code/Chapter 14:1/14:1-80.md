# § 14:1-80. Definitions.

In addition to the definitions contained elsewhere in this Chapter 14:1, the following words have the meanings indicated:

**AUTHORIZED SURETY** A performance guarantee and/or a maintenance guarantee in a form required by this article and includes, to the extent authorized by this article:

A. A bond issued by a company authorized to provide surety in Maryland;

B. A letter of credit issued by a financial institution authorized to do business in Maryland; or

C. A cash deposit.

**REQUIRED IMPROVEMENTS** Includes required improvements and any other improvements, facilities or work for which a guarantee is given under this Chapter 14:1.
