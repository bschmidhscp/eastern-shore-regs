# § 14:1-55. Other activities.

*[Added 7-28-2009 by Ord. No. 08-10 ]*

A. The use or application of all commercial or chemical fertilizers containing nitrogen or phosphorus between the period of June 15 through September 15 within the one-hundred-foot critical area Buffer is prohibited.

B. This section shall not apply to bona fide agricultural or farm operations.

C. Any violation of this section shall be considered as a land disturbance within the critical area and shall be punishable as provided in § 14:1-100C(6) of this chapter.
