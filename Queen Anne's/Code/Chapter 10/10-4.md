# § 10-4. National Electrical Code amendments.

*[Added 7-6-2004 by Ord. No. 04-10 ; [1] amended 10-13-2020 by Ord. No. 20-11 ]*

The provisions of the National Electric Code are modified and amended so as to exempt smoke detectors from any requirement or requirements for wiring into an arc fault circuit interrupter specifically including the provisions of Section 210.12(B) of the National Electric Code (current edition).

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: This ordinance also renumbered former §§ 10-4 and 10-5 as §§ 10-5 and 10-6 , respectively.
