# § 10-5. National Fuel Gas Code amendments.

*[Added 11-4-2003 by Ord. No. 03-19; [1] amended 10-13-2020 by Ord. No. 20-11 ]*

The provisions of the current edition, National Fuel Code, ANSI, 2223.1 NFPA 54 (the County Fuel Code) are modified and added to as follows:

A. Additions. Section 3.2 and Appendix 1 of the current edition of the Liquefied Petroleum Gas Code, NFPA 58, are added to, incorporated in and made a part of the County Fuel Code.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: This ordinance also renumbered former § 10-4, Violations and penalties, as § 10-5 .
