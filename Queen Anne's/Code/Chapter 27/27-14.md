# § 27-14. Purpose of Personnel Board hearing.

The purpose of a Personnel Board hearing shall be for the Personnel Board to:

A. Obtain all of the facts which form the basis for the appeal;

B. Hear the employee's version of the appeal in full;

C. Receive and review testimonial and other evidence;

D. Determine whether the action or condition which forms the basis for the employee's appeal amounts to a violation of law or policy.
