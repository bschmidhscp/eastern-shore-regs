# § 27-9. Establishment.

A. The classified service of Queen Anne's County is hereby established.

B. All appointments, promotions and personnel transactions within the County's classified service shall be based solely upon merit and fitness ascertained by means of job-related selection practices and techniques and without regard to race, color, religion, political affiliation, marital status, sex, age, physical or mental disability or any other basis prohibited by state or federal law.
