# § 20-9. Use of bulkheads.

A. Loading and unloading. A person shall not park a vehicle at a bulkhead except for loading and unloading.

B. Docking prohibited without permission. A person may not dock any vessel at a bulkhead without the written permission of the County Department of Parks and Recreation, except in the case of extreme weather conditions.
