# § 20-3. Permit required.

Unless a person has a permit, the person may not:

A. Use a public landing for launching a vessel or removing a vessel from the water;

B. Park a vehicle or trailer in any area of a public landing, regardless of the size or type of vehicle; or

C. Otherwise use the County public landing facilities.
