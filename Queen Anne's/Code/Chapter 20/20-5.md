# § 20-5. Display of permits and stickers.

A. In general. Permits shall be evidenced by a sticker issued by the County Department of Parks and Recreation and displayed in accordance with this section.

B. One-day permit. The one-day permit is valid only when displayed on the inside of the vehicle windshield facing out.

C. Annual permit. The animal permit is valid only if it is displayed either on:

  (1) A boat trailer approximately one foot from the trailer hitch on the left side; or

  (2) The left rear bumper of the vehicle utilizing the public landing.
