# § 20-1. Terms defined.

In this chapter, the following words have the meanings indicated.

**ANNUAL PERMIT** A permit that is valid for the calendar year, running from January 1 on one year to the following December 31.

**ONE-DAY PERMIT** A permit that is valid for a single calendar day.

**PERMIT**

A. A public landing use permit issued by the County Department of Recreation and Parks.

B. Includes a one-day permit and an annual permit.
