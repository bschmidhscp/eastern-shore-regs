# § 18:3-3. Effect on other provisions.

A. This Chapter 18:3 may not be construed to alter, amend, or modify any provision of Chapter 18:1 of this Chapter 18. The provisions of Chapter 18:1 of this Chapter 18 shall be operative and remain in full force and effect notwithstanding any contrary provisions, definitions, or intentions that are or may be expressed or implied in this Chapter 18:3.

B. The payment of development impact fees shall not entitle the applicant to a building permit, zoning certificate or certificate of occupancy unless all other applicable land use, zoning, planning, adequate public facilities, forest resource, platting, subdivision, and other related requirements, standards, and conditions have been met. Such other requirements, standards, and conditions are independent of the requirement for payment of a development impact fee.

C. This Chapter 18:3, including the specific Development Impact Fee Ordinances for particular public facilities, shall not affect, in any manner, the permissible use of property, density or intensity of development, design and improvement standards, or other applicable standards or requirements of the land development regulations of the County or any municipal corporation.
