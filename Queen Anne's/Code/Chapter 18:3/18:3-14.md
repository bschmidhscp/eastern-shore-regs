# § 18:3-14. Appeals.

An appeal may be taken by any person aggrieved by a final decision of a County official or the County Commissioners. Appeals from decisions of a County official shall be to the County Board of Appeals and shall be filed and administered in accordance with the provisions of § 18:1-119 of this Chapter 18. Appeals from decisions of the County Commissioners shall be to the Circuit Court for Queen Anne's County.
