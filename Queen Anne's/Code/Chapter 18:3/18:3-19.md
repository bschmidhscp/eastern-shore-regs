# § 18:3-19. Amount of fire protection and emergency medical services impact fee.

All future residential and nonresidential development in the service area shall be subject to the payment of a fire protection and emergency medical services impact fee as provided in § 18:3-7 pursuant to § 18:3-15 of this Chapter 18:3, or as amended pursuant to § 18:3-6.
