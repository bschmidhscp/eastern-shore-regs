# § 18:3-18. Fire protection and emergency medical services impact fee service area.

*[Amended 2-11-2014 by Ord. No. 13-27 ]*

A. The applicable service areas for imposition of a fire protection and emergency medical services impact fee is the entire County, including all municipal corporations.

B. Except as provided by § 18:3-12, the fire protection and emergency medical services impact fee shall be collected and applied in accordance with the Fire Protection and Emergency Medical Services Impact Fee Subarea Map.
