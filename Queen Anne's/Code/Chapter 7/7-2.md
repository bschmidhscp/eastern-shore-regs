# § 7-2. First Election District.

The boundary lines of the First Election District of Queen Anne's County shall run as follows: Beginning at the bridge spanning that branch of Chester River known as Sewell's Branch and sometimes known as Andover Branch, at a place known as Turner's Mills at the end of Kent County Road No. 10 and running thence with the center of the public road known as the Unicorn Branch Road, County Road No. 27 in the Seventh Election District, to Stephens Corners; thence with the Peters Corners Road and the Hackett Corners Road or County Road No. 26 in the Seventh Election District, to Hackett Corners; thence in a straight line in a generally southwesterly direction to the intersection of the center lines of the Maryland Route No. 71 (Blue Star Memorial Highway) and Maryland Route No. 290; thence with Maryland Route No. 290 to Maryland Route No. 300 at Dudley Corners; thence with Maryland Route No. 300 in a westerly direction to the intersection of the Crane Swamp Road; thence in a southerly direction with the center of the Crane Swamp Road, or County Road No. 18 in the Second Election District, to Roberts and the intersection of the last named road with Maryland Route No. 19; thence with the center line of Maryland Route No. 19 to Ingleside; thence with the center line of Maryland Route No. 305 to the intersection of the Charles Walls Road; thence with the Charles Walls Road, or County Road No. 10 in the Second Election District, in a generally southeasterly direction to Maryland Route No. 313; thence with the center line of Maryland Route No. 313 in a generally southeasterly direction to the Caroline County line; thence by and with the Caroline County line in a generally northeasterly direction to the intersection of said Caroline County line with the Kent County, Delaware line; thence with the Kent County, Delaware line to the Kent County, Maryland line at Sewell's Branch; thence in a generally westerly direction with the Kent County, Maryland line to the place of beginning.
