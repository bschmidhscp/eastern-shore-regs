# § 7-3. Second Election District.

The boundary lines of the Second Election District of Queen Anne's County shall run as follows: Beginning at the middle of the mouth of Southeast Creek where the same intersects the Kent County line in the middle of Chester River and running thence with the center of Southeast Creek to the point where Island Creek runs into Southeast Creek; thence with the center line of Island Creek to the point where the same is intersected by Granny Finley Branch; thence with the center line of the Granny Finley Branch to the point where the Granny Finley Branch intersects the Granny Branch Road, County Road No. 8 in the Second Election District; thence with the center line of the said road in a generally southerly direction to Hayden; thence in a generally easterly direction with the center line of the Hayden-Clark Corners Road, or County Road No. 15 in the Second Election District, to Clark Corners; thence in a easterly direction with the center line of the Clark Corners Road, or County Road No. 12 in the Second Election District, to the point where the same intersects Maryland Route No. 305; thence with the center line of Maryland Route No. 305 to the point of intersection with Maryland Route No. 19 at Ingleside; thence with the center line of Maryland Route No. 19 to the point of intersection with the Crane Swamp Road at Roberts; thence with the center line of the Crane Swamp Road, or County Road No. 18 in the Second Election District, to Maryland Route No. 300; thence with the center line of Maryland Route No. 300 in a southwesterly direction to the I B Road; thence with the center line of the I B Road, or County Road No. 19 in the Second Election District, to I B; thence with the center line of the Bowers Corner-Burchard Corner Road and the center line of the Sheriff Meredith Road, to the point where the last mentioned road intersects the Ewingville Road, said point being known as Baxter Corner (said line from I B to Baxter Corner running with the roads which are designated as County Road No. 20 in the Second Election District); and running thence with the center line of the Ewingville Road and the Baxter Road through the Union School Corner to the point where Baxter Road intersects Maryland Route No. 544 at point known as Catlin Corner (said line from Baxter Corner to Catlin Corner, running with the roads which are designated as County Road No. 21 in the Second Election District); thence with the center line of Maryland Route No. 544 in a generally southwesterly direction to the point where the same is intersected by Rosin Creek; thence by and with the center line of Rosin Creek to the point where the same intersects the Kent County line in the middle of the Chester River; thence by and with the center line of the Chester River and the Kent County line to the place of beginning.
