# § 7-8. Seventh Election District.

The boundary lines of the Seventh Election District of Queen Anne's County shall run as follows: Beginning at the bridge spanning that branch of Chester River known as Sewell's Branch and sometimes known as Andover Branch, at a place known as Turner's Mills at the end of Kent County Road No. 10 and running thence with the center of the public road known as the Unicorn Branch Road, County Road No. 27 in the Seventh Election District, to Stephens Corner; thence with the Peters Corners Road and the Hackett Corners Road, or County Road No. 26 in the Seventh Election District, to Hackett Corners; thence in a straight line in a generally southwesterly direction to the intersection of the center lines of Maryland Route No. 71 (Blue Star Memorial Highway) and Maryland Route No. 290; thence with Maryland Route No. 290 to Maryland Route No. 300 at Dudley Corners; thence by and with Maryland Route No. 300 in a southwesterly direction to the intersection of said Road with I B Road; thence with the center line of the I B Road or County Road No. 19 in the Second Election District, to I B; thence with the center line of the Bowers Corner-Burchard Corner Road and the center line of the Sheriff Meredith Road to the point where the last mentioned road intersects the Ewingville Road, said point being known as Baxter Corner (said line from I B to Baxter Corner running with the roads which are designated as County Road No. 20 in the Second Election District); running thence with the center line of the Ewingville Road and the Baxter Road through the Unicorn School Corner to the point where Baxter Road intersects Maryland Route No. 544 known as Catlin Corner (said line from Baxter Corner to Catlin Corner running with the roads which are designated as County Road No. 21 in the Second Election District); thence with the center line of Maryland Route No. 544 in a generally southwesterly direction to the point where the same is intersected by Rosin Creek; thence by and with the center line of Rosin Creek to the point where the same intersects the Kent County line in the middle of the Chester River; thence running by and with the Kent County line and the center of Chester River to the place of beginning.
