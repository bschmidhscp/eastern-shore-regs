# § 13-21. Authority; purpose.

This Article is enacted by the Board of County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County, Maryland, pursuant to § 21-202.1 of the Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland and the County's general police powers. Its purpose is to promote public safety by reducing traffic signal violations and collisions at signalized intersections.
