# § 13-9. Definitions.

In this article, the following words have the meanings indicated.

**DIRT BIKE**

A. Any motorcycle or similar vehicle that is not registered under the Maryland Vehicle Law.

B. Includes:

  (1) A minibike;

  (2) An all-terrain vehicle of either the three- or four-wheel variety; and

  (3) Any other motorcycle or similar vehicle that is not eligible for registration under the Maryland Vehicle Law.

**MINIBIKE** A motor vehicle that:

A. Has a saddle for the use of the rider;

B. Is designed to travel on not more than three wheels in contact with the ground;

C. Is not eligible for registration under the Maryland Vehicle Law; and

D. Has:

  (1) A ten-inch (254 mm) or less nominal wheel-rim diameter;

  (2) Forty inches or less wheel base;

  (3) Twenty-five inches or less seat height, measured at the lowest point on the top of the seat cushion without rider; or

  (4) A propelling engine with piston displacement of 50 cc or less.

**MOTORCYCLE OR SIMILAR VEHICLE**

A. A motor vehicle that is designed to travel on not more than three wheels in contact with ground.

B. Includes:

  (1) A minibike;

  (2) A motor scooter; and

  (3) A bicycle with motor attached.

**UNREGISTERED MOTORCYCLE OR SIMILAR VEHICLE** A motorcycle or similar vehicle that is eligible for registration under the Maryland Vehicle Law but is not in fact registered.
