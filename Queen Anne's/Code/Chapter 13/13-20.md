# § 13-20. Civil penalties.

Unless the driver of the motor vehicle received a citation from a police officer at the time of the violation, the owner and/or driver of the motor vehicle is subject to a civil penalty if the motor vehicle being driven is recorded by a speed monitoring system as being operated in a school zone in excess of the speed limit, with such penalty to be assessed at the maximum amount established in accordance with § 21-809 of the Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, as may be amended from time to time.
