# § 13-11. Prohibited conduct while riding or driving.

No person may drive or ride any dirt bike or any unregistered motorcycle or similar vehicle on any public property that is located in Queen Anne's County including any public roads located in Queen Anne's County nor may any person drive or ride any dirt bike or any unregistered motorcycle or similar vehicle on private property without the permission of the owner of such property.
