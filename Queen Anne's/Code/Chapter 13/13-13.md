# § 13-13. Violations and penalties.

Any person who violates any provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor and, on conviction, is subject to a fine of not more than $500 for each offense. Any person convicted of a second or subsequent violation of this article shall be subject to a fine of not more than $1,000, imprisonment for not more than 90 days, or both fine and imprisonment.
