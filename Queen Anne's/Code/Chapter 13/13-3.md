# § 13-3. Loitering prohibited.

A person may not loiter at, on, or in a public place or place open to the public in a manner as to:

A. Interfere, impede, or hinder the free passage of pedestrian or vehicular traffic;

B. Interfere with, obstruct, harass, curse, or threaten, or to do physical harm to, another member of the public.

C. Make it clear by words, acts, or other conduct that there is a reasonable likelihood a breach of the peace or disorderly conduct will result.
