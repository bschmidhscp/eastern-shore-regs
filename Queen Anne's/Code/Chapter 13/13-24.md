# § 13-24. Operation and ownership.

Systems may be owned or operated by the County or through a private vendor. All such Traffic Control Signal Monitoring Systems shall be operated, administered, and governed by the provisions of § 21-202.1 of the Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.
