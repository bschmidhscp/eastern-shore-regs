# § 13-14. Authorization for use.

The Sheriff of Queen Anne’s County is authorized to use school bus monitoring cameras in consultation with the Board of Education of Queen Anne’s County, in accordance with §21-706.1 of the Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, as amended from time to time.
