# § 9-36. Severability.

If any clause, sentence, paragraph, subdivision or other part of this article shall be adjudged by a Court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid or unconstitutional, such order or judgment shall not affect, impair, or otherwise invalidate the remainder of this article which shall remain in full force and effect except as limited by such order or judgment.
