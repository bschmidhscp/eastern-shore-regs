# § 9-31. Definitions.

The following words, phrases, or terms when used in this article shall, unless otherwise indicated, have the meanings provided below:

**ABUSE OFFENSE** Shall mean the commission of any act that constitutes the criminal offense of:

A. Abuse or neglect of animals under § 10-604 of the Criminal Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland;

B. Attending dogfights or cockfights under § 10-605 of the Criminal Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland;

C. Aggravated cruelty to animals under § 10-606 of the Criminal Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland;

D. Aggravated cruelty to animals - dogfights under § 10-607 of the Criminal Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland;

E. Aggravated cruelty to animals - cockfights under § 10-608 of the Criminal Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland;

F. Killing of dog or cat under § 10-611 of the Criminal Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland;

G. Abandoning domestic animal under § 10-612 of the Criminal Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

**ABUSER** Shall mean any persons 18 years of age or older, including juveniles tried as an adult, who have been convicted of an abuse offense.

**ABUSER REGISTRY** Shall mean the online Registry established by this article for registering any person residing in Queen Anne's County convicted of an abuse offense.

**ANIMAL** Shall mean, generally, any living creature, except a human being as defined by Criminal Law definition under § 10-601(a) of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

**ANIMAL ENJOINMENT** Shall mean any person enjoined by the Court from further possession or custody of other animals under §§ 10-601 through 10-608 of the Criminal Law Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland or otherwise by Court order.

**ANIMAL SHELTER** Shall mean any public or privately owned organization, including, but not limited to, any incorporated humane society, pound, animal protective association or animal rescue group which maintains buildings, structures, or other property for the purpose of harboring animals which may be stray, unwanted, lost, abandoned, or abused and seeks to find appropriate temporary or permanent homes for such animals.

**BOCC** Shall mean the Board of County Commissioners of Queen Anne's County, Maryland.

**COMPANION ANIMAL** Shall mean a domesticated or tamed animal intended to provide companionship, to be used for personal use or enjoyment, or raised or intended to be raised for nonagricultural purposes; not to include a service animal or other animal or wildlife under the exclusive jurisdiction of the state.

**CONVICTION** Shall mean an adjudication of guilt, by any Court of competent jurisdiction, whether upon a verdict or plea of guilty or nolo contendere plea of one or more of the applicable abuse offenses.

**COUNTY** Shall mean Queen Anne's County or its designated agent.

**FARM ANIMAL** Shall mean horses or animals used in the production of human or animal food, feed or fiber regardless of whether or not the animal is actually being used or raised for such purposes.

**PERSON** Shall mean any individual (18 years of age or older, including, when appropriate, juveniles tried as an adult), firm, association, joint venture, partnership, estate, trust, business trust, syndicate, fiduciary, corporation, entity, or any group or combination thereof.

**PET SELLER** Shall mean any individual or person, partnership, firm, corporation or other entity which offers animals for sale or is engaged in the sale, exchange, or other transfer of ownership of animals.

**REGISTRANT** Shall mean any individual or person required by this article to be registered with the State's Attorney's Office for the Queen Anne's County Animal Abuser Registry.

**SERVICE ANIMAL** Shall mean any dog or miniature horse that has been individually trained to do work or perform tasks for people with disabilities as defined under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).
