# § 9-25. Wild animals.

A. Restriction. A person may not keep a wild animal in Queen Anne's County unless a permit to keep the animal has been issued by the Maryland Department of Natural Resources, if required.

B. Registration. If a wild animal is kept in the County, the animal must be registered with the Animal Control Office of Queen Anne's County, in accordance with the provisions of regulations issued by the Commission.
