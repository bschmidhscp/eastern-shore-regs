# § 9-33. Abuser registration requirements.

A person who establishes residency in Queen Anne's County and who was convicted of an abuse offense in another Maryland jurisdiction, on or after the effective date of this article, must, within 10 business days of establishing residency in the County, register on the Queen Anne's County Animal Abuse Registry.
