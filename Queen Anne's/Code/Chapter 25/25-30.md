# § 25-30. Classes of property.

A. Division into classes. The County shall divide the properties benefited into four classes:

  (1) Industrial;

  (2) Business;

  (3) Agriculture; and

  (4) Residential.

B. Subdivision of classes. The County may subdivide each of the classes in whatever manner the County considers to be in the public interest.
