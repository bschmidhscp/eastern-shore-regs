# § 25-44. Minimum standards.

The standards set forth in this article are minimum standards. If the requirements of this Part 4 are stricter than the requirements of another law, then this Part 4 shall apply. If the requirements of another law are stricter than the requirements of this Part 4 then the other law shall apply.
