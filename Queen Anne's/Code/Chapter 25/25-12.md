# § 25-12. Charges.

A. Fee schedule. The expense of household collection shall be charged to property owners according to a fee schedule established by the Director.

B. Bills.

  (1) Bills shall be sent quarterly, semiannually, or annually, as the County may determine, to each property within a solid waste subdistrict.

  (2) Bills are payable at any place that the County Commissioners may designate.
