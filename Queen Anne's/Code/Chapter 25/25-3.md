# § 25-3. Solid waste subdistricts.

The Department may divide the Sanitary District into solid waste subdistricts if the Department determines that the division will:

A. Best serve the needs of the Sanitary District;

B. Promote convenience and economy of operation; and

C. Permit the raising of revenue.
