# § 25-23. Unreasonable amounts of refuse or large items.

It is the intent of this Part 2 that the reasonable accumulation of refuse of each family for the collection period will be collected. The Director may authorize the collector to refuse to collect unreasonable amounts of refuse. The County's collector will not collect large, bulky items, such as appliances, furniture, and similar materials.
