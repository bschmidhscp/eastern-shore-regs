# § 25-31. Fixing and levying benefit assessment.

A. In general. Whenever any solid waste system or part of a system in the Sanitary District is completed, the County shall fix and levy benefit assessments on all properties in the Sanitary District served by the system in accordance with the classification or subdivision of a classification.

B. Notice.

  (1) The County shall notify in writing all owners of property of:

    (a) The class or subdivision into which their property falls;

    (b) The charge determined; and

    (c) A time and place at which the owner may be heard.

  (2) The County shall:

    (a) Mail the notice to the last known address of the owner;

    (b) Serve the notice in person on any adult occupying the premises; or

    (c) In the case of vacant or unimproved property, post the notice on the premises.

C. Finality of classification and benefit assessment.

  (1) The classification of and the benefit assessed against any property as made by the County is final, subject only to revision at the hearing.

  (2) The County may change the classification of property from time to time as the uses of the property change.
