# § 25-52. Violations.

A. In general. If a violation of this Part 4 occurs, the Director shall give written or personal notice specifying the violation to the named operator of the collection service and ordering the operator to conform to this Part 4.

B. Failure to conform. If the operator does not conform within 30 days, the Director shall revoke the permit and the collection service shall cease operation in Queen Anne's County.
