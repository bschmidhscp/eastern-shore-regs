# § 25-48. Entering properties.

The Director and other duly authorized employees of the County bearing proper credentials and identification shall be permitted to enter all properties for the purpose of inspection, observation, measurement, sampling, and testing pertinent to the collection and disposal of solid waste in accordance with the provisions of this Part 4.
