# § 25-11. Rates for solid waste collection.

A. In general. The rates for solid waste collection shall consist of a minimum or ready-to-serve charge.

B. Uniformity. The rates shall be uniform throughout each subdistrict for each class of property, but the Department may make whatever classifications it considers advisable within any subdistrict.
