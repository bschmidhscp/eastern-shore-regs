# § 25-49. Safety of inspector; injury.

A. Safety rules While performing the necessary work on private properties referred to in § 25-48 of this article, the Director or duly authorized employee of the County shall observe safety rules applicable to the premises established by the owner.

B. Liability. The owner shall be held harmless for injury or death to the County employees.

C. Indemnity. Except as may be caused by negligence or failure of the owner to maintain safe conditions, the County shall indemnify the owner against loss or damage to the owner's property by County employees and against liability claims and demands for personal injury or property damage asserted against the owner and growing out of the permit and inspection procedure.
