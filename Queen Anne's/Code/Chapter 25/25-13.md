# § 25-13. Late payment.

A. Results of late payment.

  (1) The charges for household collection shall be due when made and after 30 days from that date shall bear interest at the rate of 1.5% per month.

  (2) If a bill is unpaid after 30 days from the date it was sent, the County, after written notice is left upon the premises or mailed to the last known address of the owner, shall cease to serve the property.

B. Liens.

  (1) Solid waste system assessments shall be liens upon the property served or benefited and, in addition to being enforced by actions at law, may be enforced by a bill in equity against the property served or benefited.

  (2) The liens shall be subject only to liens for state and County taxes.
