# § 25-38. Permit application.

A. In general. A person shall apply in writing to the Director for a solid waste collection permit to collect and haul refuse.

B. Forms. The Department shall prescribe and provide the forms used in an application.

C. Information. An application shall contain:

  (1) The name, address, and telephone number of the applicant;

  (2) The type of equipment to be used;

  (3) The method of confining waste material;

  (4) The service area; and

  (5) Other information as the Director may require to assure compliance with this Part 4.
