# § 25-39. Inspection; issuance of permit.

On receipt of an application for a solid waste collection permit, the Director shall examine the documents and data submitted with the application and conduct an official inspection of all collection equipment to be used by the applicant. If it appears that the proposed solid waste collection service is in compliance with this Part 4 and all other laws of Queen Anne's County, the Director shall issue a permit for the operation of the proposed service within 30 days.
