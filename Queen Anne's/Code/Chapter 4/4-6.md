# § 4-6. Vacancy.

*[Added 10-23-2007 by Ord. No. 07-41 ; amended 2-22-2011 by Ord. No. 11-02 ]*

A. In general. In the event any office of County Commissioner in Queen Anne's County shall become vacant by death, resignation or otherwise, such vacancy shall be filled through the procedure set forth in Article 25, § 16 of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: Former § 4-106, Vacancy, was repealed 4-29-2003 by County Ord. 03-05, effective 4-29-2003.
