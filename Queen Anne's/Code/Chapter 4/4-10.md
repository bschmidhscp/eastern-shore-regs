# § 4-10. County staff and appointments.

*[Amended 9-6-2005 by Ord. No. 05-16 ]*

A. Staff. Except as otherwise provided by law, the County Commissioners may provide for the appointment and removal of County officers and employees and set their compensation.

B. Boards and commissions. No person shall be appointed by the County Commissioners to any board or commission in the County unless such person is a resident of Queen Anne's County and any person so appointed shall, upon relinquishing his residency in Queen Anne's County, be considered to have resigned from such board or commission; provided, however, that no more than two persons, each of whom shall be the owner or principal of a business located in Queen Anne's County, may be appointed to the Queen Anne's County Economic Development Commission regardless of their County of residence. The foregoing provisions shall not apply to any person whose membership on a board or commission is required by governmental law, rule or regulation or by virtue of such person's employment or position.[1][Amended 10-14-2014 by Ord. No. 14-14]
