# § 4-5. Compensation.

*[Amended by Ord. No. 94-08]*

A. Salary. Each County Commissioner is entitled to an annual salary of $25,000.[Amended 11-12-2013 by Ord. No. 13-23]

B. Expenses. Each County Commissioner is entitled to reimbursement for actual expenses incurred in the performance of duties on:

  (1) Submission of a voucher; and

  (2) Approval of the County Commissioners.
