# § 4-4. Meetings; special legislative sessions.

*[Amended by Ord. No. 91-01]*

A. In general. The County Commissioners may meet on the second, third and fourth Tuesdays of each month to enact local laws.[Amended 2-6-2007 by Ord. No. 07-02]

B. Special sessions. In addition to legislative days authorized under Subsection A of this section, the County Commissioners may hold special legislative sessions to enact local laws if:

  (1) The total number of legislative days during the year does not exceed 45; and

  (2) The County Commissioners publish notice of a special legislative session in a newspaper of general circulation in the County at least three days, but not more than 14 days, before the session.
