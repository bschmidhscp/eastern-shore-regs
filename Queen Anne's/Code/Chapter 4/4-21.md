# § 4-21. Duties.

A. Grant process. The Commission shall develop and administer a public grant application process for interested parties to apply for funding from the Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund.

B. Use of funds.

  (1) The Commission shall develop its recommendations, with community input, for the use of funds in the Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund for the upcoming fiscal year. These recommendations, along with a summary of community input and applicants, shall be delivered to the County Commissioners and posted on the County website by March 1 of each year.

  (2) Funds from the Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund may be used for the following purposes:

    (a) Minority Entrepreneur Training Accelerator (Meta) Program;

    (b) After-school programming;

    (c) Homelessness prevention efforts;

    (d) AD-HOC funding by application process as determined by community needs;

C. Annual and biannual reports.

  (1) The Commission shall prepare an annual report detailing the fund allocations, program outcomes, and impact assessments for the prior fiscal year. The report shall be delivered to the County Commissioners by December 1 of each year.

  (2) By December 1 of every second year (even-numbered years), the Commission shall submit a report to the Governor, Senate Budget and Taxation Committee and the House Appropriations Committee detailing fund allocations during the immediately prior two fiscal years.

D. Meeting with County Commissioners.

  (1) The Commission shall meet annually with the County Commissioners or their designee.
