# § 4-17. Membership.

A. Membership shall consist of the current membership of the Local Management Board plus three nonvoting ex officio members.

B. Nonvoting ex officio members. The Commission shall include one representative of each of the following as nonvoting ex officio members.

  (1) Queen Anne's County Department of Economic and Tourism Development;

  (2) Queen Anne's County Department of Community Services; and,

  (3) Queen Anne's County Department of Finance.
