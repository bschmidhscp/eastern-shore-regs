# § 18:2-21. Standards for protecting trees.

A. Protective devices. The County has adopted standards for the protection of trees from construction activity. These devices and procedures are defined and described in the Forest Conservation Technical Manual.

B. General requirements. Before cutting, clearing, grading, or construction begins on a site for which a forest conservation plan is required and prior to the issuance of any grading permit or sediment and erosion control permit, the applicant shall demonstrate to the Department that protective devices have been established.
