# § 18:2-18. Native tree species.

A. General requirements. Tree species used for afforestation, reforestation, and riparian buffer establishment shall be native to the County, when appropriate, and selected from a list of approved species established by the Department or as approved by the Planning Director.

B. Exceptions. This section does not in any way prohibit the use of nonnative species for ornamental and landscaping purposes when the nonnative plantings are in addition to the required reforestation or afforestation. The Department may prohibit the use of species identified as invasive and/or exotic and listed in the Forest Conservation Technical Manual.
