# § 18:2-11. Riparian buffers.

A. For any development that requires afforestation or reforestation, all unforested on-site riparian buffers must be planted in accordance with the Forest Conservation Technical Manual.

  (1) Planting requirements should be concentrated within on-site unforested riparian buffer areas.

  (2) If afforestation and reforestation requirements are met elsewhere, all unforested riparian buffers must still be established on-site unless the applicant demonstrates to the Department that establishment:

    (a) Would be in conflict with allowable uses as established for the riparian buffer;

    (b) Is located on park property and conflicts with the mission and established stewardship practices of the park; or

    (c) Is not suitable for the establishment and retention of the required planting materials, in which case substitute environmental protection measures must be implemented.

B. The riparian buffer must be established on-site in accordance with § 18:2-15.

C. All riparian buffer planting must comply with § 18:2-19, § 18:2-20 and be established within the timeframe outlined in § 18:2-15E.
