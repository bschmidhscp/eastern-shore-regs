# § 18:2-14. Reforestation requirements.

A. General requirements.

  (1) Except as provided in § 18:2-13A(2) of this Chapter, for all existing forest cover measured to the nearest 1/10 acre cleared on the net tract area, the area of forest removed shall be reforested at a ratio of 1 acre planted for every 1 acre removed; and

  (2) For all existing forest cover located in a Priority Funding Area designated under § 5-7B-03 of the State Finance and Procurement Article, and not identified as a priority for retention as described in § 18:2-11A of this Chapter, measured to the nearest 1/10 acre cleared on the net tract area, the area of forest removed shall be reforested at a ratio of 1/2 acre planted for every 1 acre removed.
