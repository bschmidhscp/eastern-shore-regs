# § 18:2-3. Purpose.

A. In general. The intent of the Queen Anne's County Forest Conservation Program is to ensure that high-quality forested areas are retained, sustained, and appropriate areas afforested by requiring consideration and protection of forest resources located outside of the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area.

B. Required under state law. The regulations in this Chapter 18:2 have been developed for the purpose of implementing the Queen Anne's County Forest Conservation Program as required under Title 5, Subtitle 16, of the Natural Resources Article, Annotated Code of Maryland. The goal of these regulations are to minimize loss of forested areas, retention of existing forests, or the creation of new forests.
