# § 18:2-2. Rules of interpretation.

In the interpretation of this Chapter 18:2, the provisions and rules in § 18:1-2 of this Chapter 18 shall be observed and applied, except when the context clearly requires otherwise.

A. General effective date. Except as otherwise provided in this section, this Chapter 18:2 shall take effect in accordance with Title 5, Forest and Parks, Subtitle 16, Forest Conservation, §§ 5-1601 to 5-1612, of the Natural Resources Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

B. Pending matters. Any application filed in accordance with Chapter 18:2 prior to July 1, 2026 shall be recorded prior to July 1, 2027 in accordance with the ordinance in effect on the date on which the particular matter was filed. Otherwise, those applications not recorded as of July 1, 2027 and filed in accordance with Chapter 18:2 must comply with Chapter 18:2, effective as of July 1, 2026.
