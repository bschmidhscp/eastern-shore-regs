# § 26-3. Towing/impoundment of vehicles.

Any vehicle parked in violation of this chapter shall be subject to impoundment and towing by any law enforcement agency or personnel. The vehicle owner shall be responsible for all reasonable costs and expenses of impoundment, towing and/or storage.
