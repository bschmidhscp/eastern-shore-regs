# Maryland — Statewide Rules for Small Residential Subdivisions (layer 1)

*Applies in all 9 Eastern Shore counties. County summaries reference this file instead of repeating it.
Focus: what changes the **cost, timing or lot count** of a 1–20 lot subdivision, and which choices the
State leaves to each county (so county summaries know what to describe locally).
Sources: statutes from mgaleg.maryland.gov and COMAR from regs.maryland.gov, retrieved 2026-10-11; text in
`01. Maryland/Documents/<Topic>/`. Agency guidance is in the same folders. Verify anything decisive in the
source file; statute files show both the old and new text where a change took effect recently.*

## 1. The five things that drive a small rural subdivision

| Driver | State rule | Typical effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Septic tier** | Tier IV allows only a **minor subdivision** on septic; a major subdivision on septic needs **Tier III** and a planning-board hearing [Env. §9-206(g); Land Use §5-104] | Caps lot count on most rural land |
| **Critical Area** (within 1,000 ft of tidal water) | RCA density **1 unit / 20 ac**, no variance; 15% lot coverage; 200-ft buffer for new RCA subdivisions [COMAR 27.01.02.05C; Nat. Res. §8-1808.3(d); §8-1808.10] | Often the binding limit on waterfront land |
| **Perc / soils** | Wet-season testing where soils show high water tables; 10,000 sf sewage area per lot for an initial + 2 replacement systems [COMAR 26.04.02.04B, F; 26.04.03.02C] | Testing window Feb–Apr on most Shore soils; lots fail |
| **BAT septic** | Required for all new construction in the Critical Area; counties may require it elsewhere [COMAR 26.04.02.07A, D] | Higher per-lot cost + annual service contract |
| **Forest conservation** | Applies at ≥40,000 sf; since 1 Jul 2026 every acre of forest cleared is replaced **1:1** (½:1 inside a PFA) [Nat. Res. §5-1602(a); §5-1606(b), Ch. 457 of 2024] | Planting or fee-in-lieu cost; clearing now costs more |

## 2. Septic and the growth tiers (SB 236 of 2012)

- **Tiers.** Counties must adopt Tiers I, III and IV and may adopt Tier II [Land Use §1-506(c)]. Tier IV = not
  planned for sewer, mostly agricultural/resource land [Land Use §1-508(a)(4)]. Tiers are part of the
  comprehensive plan [Land Use §1-509].
- **What can be platted** [Env. §9-206(g)(1)]: Tier I — public sewer only; Tier II — sewer, or a *minor*
  subdivision on septic; Tier III — minor on septic, or a *major* subdivision on septic/shared/community
  systems if the planning board recommends it after a public hearing (with review of service costs and a
  natural resources inventory) [Land Use §5-104(c)–(f)]; **Tier IV — minor subdivision on septic only.**
  The Tier IV cap does not apply if the county's Tier IV zoning yields ≤1 unit/20 ac, verified by MDP
  [Env. §9-206(h)].
- **"Minor subdivision" is the county's number**, frozen in 2012: the local definition in effect on
  1 Jan 2012, or one adopted by 31 Dec 2012 of **no more than 7 new lots**; if neither, **fewer than 5 lots**
  [Env. §9-206(a)(6)]. → *County summaries must state the county's SB 236 minor-subdivision number.*
- **County snapshot (MDP maps — dated, confirm locally):**

  | County | SB 236 minor subdivision (2012) | Tier map / Tier IV exemption (Apr 2017) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Caroline, Cecil | kept existing local definition | tiers adopted; no exemption |
  | Worcester | kept existing local definition | tier map not yet adopted in 2017 |
  | Kent | raised to 7 lots | **Tier IV exemption granted** (≤1 unit/20 ac yield) → minor-subdivision cap lifted |
  | Talbot, Wicomico, Somerset | raised to 7 lots | tiers adopted; no exemption |
  | Queen Anne's, Dorchester | raised to 7 lots | tier map not yet adopted in 2017 (then: no major subdivisions on septic) |

  [MDP, *Minor Subdivision Definition Changes* (2012) and *Tier 4 Exemption Status* (Apr 2017), in
  `Documents/Septic and Sewage/MD_SubdivisionOrdinanceChanges.md`, `MD_T4ExemptionStatus.md` (maps; see the PDFs)]
- **No re-subdivision.** A minor subdivision in Tier II–IV and its remainder may not be further subdivided,
  and the plat must say so; lots can be created over time only up to the minor-subdivision total, with the
  plat tracking what is left [Env. §9-206(l)]. Counties may allow farm-to-farm **transfer of up to 7 lot
  rights** (not from Tier III into Tier IV) [Env. §9-206(m)(2), (5)].
- **Plat sign-off.** No subdivision plat may be recorded without the MDE Secretary's (in practice the county
  health department's) signature [COMAR 26.04.03.02K]; land may not be sold or built on until MDE has the
  plat and the water/sewer method [Env. §9-206(j)].
- **Soils and perc.** Where the soil survey shows moderate/severe limits from seasonal high water tables,
  perc and site tests must be done when the water table is highest [COMAR 26.04.02.04B; 26.04.03.02C].
  Minimum perc rate 1 in./30 min; rates faster than 2–5 min/in. can be refused east of the fall line
  [COMAR 26.04.02.04A(4)]. Each new lot needs a ≥10,000 sf disposal area able to hold an initial and two
  replacement systems [COMAR 26.04.02.04F; 26.04.03.03A(1)].
- **BAT (nitrogen-removing) septic.** Required for new construction and replacements in the Critical Area
  and for systems ≥5,000 gpd; outside the Critical Area it is a county option [COMAR 26.04.02.07A–D].
  Owners must keep BAT under an O&M arrangement for life — inspected and serviced at least yearly
  [COMAR 26.04.02.07E].
- **Bay Restoration Fund fee:** $60/yr per septic system in the Bay/Coastal Bays watersheds (all of the
  Eastern Shore); $5/month per home on sewer [Env. §9-1605.2]. The BRF also funds BAT grants
  [COMAR 26.03.13] — **including new construction**: grant = (BAT cost × eligibility %) − $1,200 (cost of
  an ordinary tank); eligibility is 100% for owners with household income ≤$300,000, 50% above that or for
  businesses (75% for small businesses), so a builder/LLC gets half to three-quarters [MDE *BRF Guidance FY27*, `BRFGuidanceFY27.md`]. Grant money is
  allocated through each county's program and may run out.

## 3. Critical Area (Chesapeake Bay and Atlantic Coastal Bays)

- **Where.** Land within 1,000 ft of tidal water/tidal wetlands; the Atlantic Coastal Bays program (Worcester)
  uses the same law and criteria, with 2002 instead of 1985/86 grandfathering dates
  [Nat. Res. §8-1801 ff.; §8-1808.3(d)].
- **Density by class.** IDA — intensely developed; LDA — 1 unit/5 ac up to 4 units/ac
  [COMAR 27.01.02.04A]; **RCA — max 1 unit/20 ac, density rights tracked on the plat, no variance**
  [COMAR 27.01.02.05C(2)]. Most rural Shore waterfront is RCA.
- **Growth allocation** (reclassifying RCA to LDA/IDA for more density) is capped at 5% of the county's RCA
  acreage [Nat. Res. §8-1808.1(b)], needs a county decision and Commission approval, an environmental report,
  and a 300-ft setback on new LDA/IDA sites [COMAR 27.01.02.06–.06-3]. Slow and discretionary — treat as
  rezoning risk.
- **Intrafamily transfers** (if the county program allows): RCA parcels of record in 1986 (Coastal Bays: 2002),
  7–<12 ac → 2 lots; 12–<60 ac → 3 lots, deed-restricted to family [Nat. Res. §8-1808.2(c)–(f)].
- **Lot coverage:** 15% of the site or of the LDA/RCA portion [COMAR 27.01.02.04C(9), .05C(9);
  Nat. Res. §8-1808.3(d)] (higher fixed allowances for small pre-1985 lots).
- **Buffer.** At least 100 ft from tidal water, tidal wetlands and tributary streams [COMAR 27.01.09.01E];
  **200 ft from tidal water/wetlands for new RCA subdivisions** (reducible only if it would prevent 1/20 ac
  density or an intrafamily transfer) [Nat. Res. §8-1808.10]. A subdivision must **fully plant the buffer**
  where it is not already forested, with a recorded buffer management plan (farms may defer until use
  changes) [COMAR 27.01.09.01-1A–C]. Fee-in-lieu only if planting is infeasible, at least **$1.50/sf**
  [COMAR 27.01.09.01-2F; 27.01.09.01-5].
- **Forest in LDA/RCA:** clearing >20% → replace at 1.5:1; no more than 30% cleared without a variance; sites
  with no forest must be planted to 15% cover [COMAR 27.01.02.04C(6)–(7)].
- **Commission review.** The county must send the Critical Area Commission **every RCA subdivision** and every
  variance; LDA/IDA subdivisions only if they touch the buffer/habitat or use growth allocation
  [COMAR 27.03.01.04A]. Adds comment time to RCA projects.

## 4. Forest Conservation Act

- Applies to a subdivision or grading permit on ≥40,000 sf [Nat. Res. §5-1602(a)]; needs a forest stand
  delineation and forest conservation plan [§5-1604]. Where >50% of the tract is in the Critical Area the
  county may apply Critical Area forest rules instead [§5-1607(d)].
- **Afforestation:** tracts with <20% forest (agricultural/resource and medium-density residential land) must be
  planted up to 20% of net tract area [§5-1606(a)].
- **Reforestation — changed 1 July 2026** (Ch. 457 of 2024): every acre cleared is replaced 1:1, or ½:1 for
  forest inside a PFA not marked for retention; unforested riparian buffers must also be planted
  [§5-1606(b), new text]. The old "break-even" thresholds (¼:1 above, 2:1 below) are gone. Counties may adopt
  MD DNR-approved alternatives if they hold forest cover steady [§5-1606.1].
  → *Check whether each county has updated its FCA ordinance.*
- **Fee-in-lieu** when planting/banking isn't possible: COMAR states **30.5¢/sf inside a PFA, 36.6¢/sf outside**
  (≈$13,300 / $15,900 per acre of required planting), adjusted yearly for inflation by DNR — confirm the
  current year's rate [COMAR 08.19.04.09D; §5-1610(c)]; county funds must charge at least the State rate
  [§5-1610(h)]. Planting must
  happen within 1 year/2 growing seasons, or payment within 90 days, after the project is done [§5-1606].

## 5. Stormwater and sediment control

- Stormwater rules apply above **5,000 sf of disturbance**; projects must use environmental site design (ESD)
  to the maximum extent practicable per the 2000 Design Manual [COMAR 26.17.02.05B(2); 26.17.02.02;
  Env. §4-201.1 ff.]. Waivers are a county policy, approved by MDE [COMAR 26.17.02.05C].
- **Pending rewrite:** MDE published an advance notice and a draft of COMAR 26.17.02 (Sept 2025; listening
  sessions Oct 2025) to update design storms for climate change — likely larger practices per lot. Not yet
  adopted as of retrieval [`Stormwater/2025 September 19 COMAR 26.17.02 - DRAFT.md`].
- Erosion and sediment control plan required (soil conservation district) unless under 5,000 sf **and** 100 cu yd
  [COMAR 26.17.01.05; 26.17.01.02D].

## 6. Wetlands and waterways

- **Nontidal wetlands:** 25-ft regulated buffer, 100 ft where expanded (steep slopes, erodible soils, special
  State concern) [COMAR 26.23.01.01B(14), (33); 26.23.01.04]. Impacts under 5,000 sf (and isolated wetlands
  <1 ac) can usually use a **letter of authorization** instead of a permit — not in the Critical Area
  [COMAR 26.23.03.01]. Mitigation 1:1 emergent, 2:1 scrub-shrub/forested (more via banks)
  [COMAR 26.23.04.03]. Farmed wetlands are largely exempt while farmed [COMAR 26.23.01.02E].
- **Tidal wetlands** (piers, shoreline work): State license/permit [Env. §16-201 ff.; COMAR 26.24].
- **Floodplain/stream work:** MDE waterway construction permit [COMAR 26.17.04]. A joint federal/state
  application covers Corps and MDE permits.

## 7. Planning framework: plans, PFAs, water and sewer

- **Comprehensive plan** with land use, water resources, growth tiers etc.; reviewed every 10 years
  [Land Use §1-406, §1-416, §3-102 ff.]. County subdivision regulations derive from Land Use Title 5.
- **Priority Funding Areas:** State money for growth-related projects (sewer, roads) only inside PFAs
  [State Fin. & Proc. §5-7B-04]; PFA residential areas must allow ≥3.5 units/ac [§5-7B-03]. Rural minor
  subdivisions are outside PFAs: no State infrastructure help and higher FCA fee-in-lieu.
- **County water & sewer plan:** any water/sewer system on a plat must conform to the county plan, and building
  permits need adequate capacity/allocation [Env. §9-512(b)]; plans and amendments are MDE-approved
  [Env. §9-503, §9-507; COMAR 26.03.01]. Extending sewer to a parcel means a plan amendment.

## 8. Agricultural preservation and transfer tax

- **MALPF easement land:** no subdivision except owner/child lots — 1 lot (20–<70 ac), 2 (70–<120 ac),
  3 (≥120 ac), each ≤1 ac (≤2 ac by waiver), non-transferable for 5 years — or **one unrestricted lot** instead
  of all of those [Agric. §2-513(b)]. Check SDAT/MALPF before pricing any farm.
- **Agricultural land transfer tax** when farm-assessed land is sold out of agricultural use: 5% (≥20 ac), 4% or
  3% (<20 ac), plus a 25% surcharge; reduced if the land left farm assessment years earlier
  [Tax-Prop. §13-303]; exempt if the buyer keeps it in farm use under a declaration of intent [§13-305].
  Usually a buyer cost on raw farmland — budget it.

## 9. What each county summary must cover (left to counties)

2. Whether BAT is required outside the Critical Area; septic/perc fees and the testing season.
3. Critical Area program: intrafamily-transfer rules, buffer fee-in-lieu rate, growth allocation left.
