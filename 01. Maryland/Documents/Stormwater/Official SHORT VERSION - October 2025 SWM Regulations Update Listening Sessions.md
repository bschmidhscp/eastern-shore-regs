## **<mark>Background</mark>** 

_Environment Article_ _<mark>§4-201</mark> Code of Maryland Regulations (COMAR) Title 26.17.02_ 

**Purpose** : Manage runoff from land development activities to control surface water pollution and reduce flood risk by: 

   - Reducing stream channel erosion 

   - Minimizing pollution in stormwater runoff 

- Minimizing flood risk from increases in runoff rates and volumes 

- The regulations require approving authorities to: 

- enforce standards for stormwater practice design and construction 

- ● implement programs for inspection, operation, and maintenance

### **Updating the Stormwater Regulations** 

- Outdated regulations and Design Manual: Last major update in 2009 - more than a decade ago 

- Climate drivers: Increased frequency and intensity of rainfall events; outdated precipitation data no longer protective 

- Equity & Consistency: 

   - Clearer criteria and standards for Environmental Site Design (ESD)  to the Maximum Extent Practicable (MEP) 

   - Statewide consistency in flood protection

##### **5 Key Elements of the Proposed Regulation Updates** 

**1.** Updated precipitation data and design storms 

   - Channel protection criteria (Cpv) based on 1-year, 24-hour design storm using Atlas 14 county average value (+) climate change factor 

   - Overbank flood protection criteria (qp10) based on 10-year, 24-hour design storm using Atlas 14 county average (+) climate change factor 

   - Extreme flood protection criteria (qp100) for interjurisdictional watersheds based on 100-year, 24-hour design storm using Atlas 14 county average (+) climate change factor

###### **5 Key Elements of the Proposed Regulation Updates** 

**2.** Standardized flood control statewide: 10-year quantity management using updated design storms and future climate change predictions 

   - qp10 = peak discharge rate for post development conditions based on future climate change projections using a climate change factor managed to pre development conditions determined without a climate change factor 

**3.** Clarified Environmental Site Design (ESD) to the Maximum Extent Practicable (MEP) as i. Full water quality treatment shall be in an ESD practice 

      - ii. Cpv shall be managed based on the discharge point from the development site: 

         1. If required Cpv  ≤ 8,000 ft<sup>3</sup> (4,000 ft<sup>3</sup> in Use Class III/IV watersheds), use an ESD practice 

         2. If required Cpv  > 8,000 ft<sup>3</sup> (4,000 ft<sup>3</sup> in Use Class III/IV watersheds), use a structural practice

### **Key Elements of Design Manual Updates** 

- Ensuring consistency with the updated regulations 

- Removing obsolete or outdated <mark>criteria including criteria referenced from other s</mark> tate agencies 

- Combining all BMPs into 1 chapter 

- Consolidating duplicative practices 

- Updating BMP figures 

- Updating design examples 

- Moving construction, construction <mark>inspection,</mark> and maintenance criteria to an Appendix
