# **Septic Tank, BAT or Holding Tank Parameters:** 

- Septic tank: Two compartment tank or two tanks in series. Minimum capacity calculated per COMAR 26.04.02.05 D-F. 

- BAT (Best Available Technology):  Minimum treatment capacity based on design flow, see COMAR 26.04.02.07. 

- Holding tank: Minimum size based on the design flow (gpd) x 7 days, COMAR 26.04.02.02 L. 

# **Minimum Area Requirements:** 

- Minimum 10,000 square foot sewage disposal area capable of supporting three systems. 

- One initial system and one replacement system.
