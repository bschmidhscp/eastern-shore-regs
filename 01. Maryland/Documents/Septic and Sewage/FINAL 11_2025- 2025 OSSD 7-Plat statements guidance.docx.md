# **<u>Approving Authority Plat Signatory Guidance Templates for Subdivision and Related Record Plats</u>** 

Pursuant to the regulations stipulated in COMAR 26.04.03 regarding water supply and sewerage systems for the subdivision of land, record plats must contain specific statements signed by the Approving Authority for a variety of different applications.  These statements ensure compliance with regulations and promote consistency in enforcement. Below are standardized templates for various scenarios: 

### **General Requirements** 

- Plats must be signed by the Approving Authority and recorded in the land records within **six months** of preliminary plan approval.   Subdivisions served by community water supply and sewerage systems do not have a preliminary plan requirement in COMAR 26.04.03 and therefore the recordation timeframe does not apply in these instances. 

- A written request for an extension may be submitted before the six-month period ends, allowing a **one-time 12-month extension** at the Approving Authority's discretion.
