# Wicomico County, MD: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "PC digest", "Council digest", "BOA digest" = `01. Digests/`. State septic and Critical Area law: see `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`. The eCode360 text is "current through" unknown; enacted 2026 bills below are not yet codified.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Cheapest path: up to 3 lots from a 1957/1968 parent tract.** Excluded from the subdivision procedure; approved by the Director of Public Works and Health Dept, no sketch plat, PC only if requested [§ 200-3A(1), (3); § 200-6B(1)]. Plat fee $225 [§ 141-3A(5)(a)].
- **No impact fee and no excise tax on new homes** (Ch. 130 repealed 2016) [Fee Findings]. Fixed county fees are tiny; survey, engineering, perc and septic dominate cost.
- **Lots 4 to 7 on septic are a "minor subdivision" but go to the Planning Commission** (sketch and preliminary plats required) [§ 200-4B; § 200-6A(2); § 200-7A; PC digest 2026-08-20]. Bill 2026-03 removes the 50% open-space set-aside for them (A-1) [Bill 2026-03].
- **8+ lots on septic appear impossible in Tier IV** (most of the county, mostly A-1) [PC minutes 2026-02-19 p.3]. Major subdivisions need public sewer or Tier III; 12 of 13 major items at PC (2023-10 to 2026-09) were approved [PC digest].
- **A-1 density is thin:** 1 per 15 acres conventional [§ 225-75].
- **Approvals are friendly.** PC digest shows no subdivision denials. BOA: variances 7 of 8 granted [BOA digest].
- **Risks:** Tier IV cap, wet-season perc timing, Critical Area, and a comp plan rewrite (staff called the first draft chapters "a rewrite, not an update") [PC digest 2026-08-20].

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Exempt minor (3 or fewer) | Minor, 4 to 7 lots on septic | Major (8+ or any sewered) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decider | Director of Public Works + Health | Planning Commission | Planning Commission |
| Stages | Plat only | Sketch, preliminary, final (often combined) | Sketch, preliminary, final |

[§ 200-3A; § 200-4B; § 200-6 to 200-8]

- **3-lot conditions:** one-time, from a tract existing 7/9/1957 or 4/1/1968; each lot meets Health Dept or Ch. 225 area; road frontage; **no remnant under 20 acres**; a future street extension is reserved at lot 2 unless waived [§ 200-3A(1), (1.1)].
- **Clocks:** sketch advice valid 1 year; preliminary void after 1 year (1-year extensions); record within 3 years of final [§ 200-10]. PC must act within 60 days or the plat is deemed approved [§ 200-7A(9); § 200-8A(3)]. Sketch submissions are quarterly on paper [§ 200-6C(3)], but sketches are heard monthly in practice [PC digest].
- **Timing signals:** Steeplechase 9 (21 lots): sketch 2026-05-21, preliminary/final 2026-08-20. Raegan's Run (10 lots): sketch 2025-10-16, preliminary/final 2026-03-19, amended 2026-06-18. Pond at Nutters Crossing Ph. 2 (16 lots): 2024-11-21 to 2024-12-19. Bloxham minor (1 lot): one meeting, approved 2026-08-20 [PC digest].
- PC meets the third Thursday; the Commission was reconstituted separately from Salisbury [Council digest 2025-06-17, 2025-08-19].
- **Rates:** 175 PC items 2023-10 to 2026-09; 19 subdivision items; 0 subdivision denials; 1 denial overall [PC digest].

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Critical Area:** RCA 1 dwelling per 20 acres [§ 125-14C(1)]; lot coverage 15% in the Bill 2026-02 rewrite [Bill 2026-02]. Bill 2026-02 (Ch. 125 repeal and re-enactment) and Bill 2026-10 (new Critical Area map) are enrolled but not in the code [Bills]. The state Critical Area Commission appealed a county buffer variance (WA-2508) [BOA digest 2025-05-22, 2026-01-22].
- **Septic tiers:** county minor subdivision = 7 lots [§ 200-4B; 2017 Comp Plan p.223]. Tier I sewer only; Tier II minors only on septic; Tier III major and minor; Tier IV minors only [PC minutes 2026-02-19 p.3]. Tier map is Appendix 6 of the 2017 plan [W&S Master Plan 2021 p.190]. Tier IV share of county: **not found in corpus**.
- **Forest conservation:** thresholds 50% agricultural, 25% medium-density, 20% high-density [§ 126-15]; parcels recorded before 1993 of 39,999 sf or less exempt [§ 126-4A(1)]. Small parcels often file exemptions (FCA(E)) [PC digest 2026-08-20].
- **Stormwater** (Ch. 196): exempt under 5,000 sf disturbed [§ 196-6C]. **Floodplain:** Ch. 149. **Stream buffer outside Critical Area:** advisory ("encouraged"), not a fixed width [§ 225-147B].
- **Ag preservation:** A-1 majors set aside 50% under easement [§ 200-13G]; 14 ag-preservation items at PC [PC digest]. Right to farm: Ch. 186.

## 5. Water and sewer

- **Service:** county urban service districts (Ch. 97) plus city systems (Salisbury, Fruitland, Delmar). Rural parcels are septic and well.
- **Plan amendments:** by Council resolution; 8 in the Council digest 2024 to 2026 (e.g. Res. 52-2025 for Raegan's Run) [Council digest]. The 2021 W&S Master Plan is a "final draft"; the county W&S plan update is under way [PC minutes 2026-02-19].
- **Septic:** wet-season testing usually Nov to Mar; MDE gave a 2026 off-season expedite [Fee Findings; PC minutes 2026-05-21 p.6]. BAT rules outside the Critical Area: **not found in corpus**.
- **Connection charges, EDU fees:** not found in corpus. The "$6,360 per EDU" figure is Sussex County, DE, not Wicomico [Fee Findings].

## 7. Recent changes and watch list

- **Bill 2026-03** (set-aside removed for A-1 minors): approved 2026-04-07, effective 60 days after enactment absent referendum; § 200-4B in the corpus still shows the old text [Council digest 2026-04-07; Bill 2026-03].
- **Bill 2026-08 (ADUs)** passed 2026-08-18: by right where single-family is allowed, capped at 50% of primary dwelling; Health Dept septic capacity may limit uptake [Council digest 2026-07-21, 2026-08-18; PC digest 2026-07-16].
- **Pending:** Bill 2026-13 solar (introduced 2026-10-06); Bill 2026-01 manufactured housing (outcome not found); data-center moratorium Res. 125-2026 approved 2026-08-04; APFO recommended by 1998 plan, not found in code [1998 Comp Plan p.114].
- **Comprehensive plan:** 2017 plan in force; update by Wallace Montgomery (planwicomico.com), Land Use chapter moved to November, **adoption not found in corpus** [PC digest 2026-07-16, 2026-08-20].
