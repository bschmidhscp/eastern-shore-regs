BRIEF DISCUSSION - COMMERCIAL KENNEL (A. Illuminati and L. Carter)

Mr, Illuminati explained to the Commissioners there have been challenges regarding
commercial kennels and how Staff can define them when there is an application for one. The current
County Law states a commercial kennel is a place where dogs and other domesticated animals,
excluding livestock, are bred and raised and are sold or kept for sale or boarded for a fee, it does not
include vet establishments. The specific requirements are a minimum acreage of 5 acres for an outside
run or two acres for an inside run. Animals are kept in a soundproof building that cannot be located
within 200 ft of an existing residential dwelling on an adjacent parcel. They may be located in a C-2
General Commercial District; commercial kennels may be permitted provided that runs for animals are
entirely contained within a building. We are trying to clean up some language in the portion where it
discusses existing residential dwellings on adjacent parcels.

Staff contacted surrounding counties to find out their definitions of a commercial kennel.

1, Somerset County: Their definition stated fifteen (15) dogs or cats in the aggregate permitted
by right with 200 ft. setbacks for the runs. Therefore, the definition is not about the actions on
the property, it is about how many animals are present.

2. Worcester County: They allow five (5) or more dogs and cats in the aggregate and require
special exceptions, as well as 200 ft. setbacks for the runs.

3. Dorchester County: They allow six (6) or more dogs and require special exceptions.

4. Talbot County: They allow six (6) or more dogs and require special exceptions.

5. Sussex County: They allow five (5) or more dogs permitted by right with certain setbacks.

Wicomico County is trying to be more in line with the standard practice, so they looked at the
proposed revisions. The revisions are to strike the word commercial and make the definition more about
the use of the property. For example, a place where ten (10) or more dogs are kept for boarding,
breeding, training, selling, exhibition, or raising.

The Staff should consider a 200 ft. setback or increase it to 500, 800, or 1,000 ft. between now
and the next discussion.
