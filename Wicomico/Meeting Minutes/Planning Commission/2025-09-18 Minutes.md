###### STAFF AND COMMISSION MEMBERS- Self-Introductions 

Ms. Taylor introduced herself. She is the Planning Director for Wicomico County Planning, Zoning, and Community Development. She joined the team last November after retiring from the Maryland Department of Planning (MDP). She was MDP's Eastern Maryland Regional Director, working with alll the Eastern Shore counties and municipalities, but predominantly those on the lower shore from Talbot County down. 

Ms. Taylor introduced Mr. Colin Harrison, the County's Environmental Planner, and noted that moving forward, Mr. Harrison will assist with displaying all the documents on the screen to make viewing easier for the Commission members and the general public. She then introduced Ms. Becky Thayne, who handles subdivision, site plan, floodplain, historic preservation, and several other types of development reviews. Mr. Keith Lackie was introduced as Ms. Taylor's colleague at MDP. He decided to retire from the State, and Ms. Taylor approached him immediately to fill the position of Long Range Planner within the Department. Mr. Lackie will be the primary point of contact for updating the Comprehensive Plan. Ms. Taylor told the Commission members that Departmental staff are very downto-earth, approachable, and easy to understand. If Commission members have any questions or concerns at any time, please do not hesitate to contact the staff. 

Ms. Merchant introduced herself. She has been with the County since August 2021. She is the Senior Department Coordinator in Planning, Zoning, and Community Development. She is the Recording Secretary for the Planning Commission. 

Ms. Siggers introduced herself. She is a Salisbury native and a good friend of Ms. Lori Carter. She has been around this type of forum for a while. 

Mr. Copeland introduced himself. He was a part of the previous joint City-County Planning Commission for about 8 years. He is honored to serve in this capacity for our community. He welcomed the newest members. 

Chairman Dashiell added that he chaired the joint City-County Planning Commission for about 15 years and is happy to be serving as the Chair of the County Planning Commission. He was an attorney in town for years and is a lifelong resident of Wicomico County. 

Ms. Bynum-King introduced herself. She comes to the Commission after retiring from a local municipality after 38 years of service. She said Planning and Zoning is a very integral part of how the communities work and grow. She looks forward to working with Ms. Taylor, her staff, and this Commission as we go forward. 

Mr. Moreno-Holt introduced himself. He served on the previous joint City-Commission for about 2 years. He has worked in Salisbury for about 15 years. He has enjoyed serving on the combined Commission and looks forward to continuing on the County Commission. 

Ms. Denton introduced herself. She grew up in suburban Washington, D.C., and now lives in Willards. She retired from Worcester County as a property researcher. She serves on the Planning and Zoning Appeals Board for the Town of Willards.

###### DISCUSSION OF PLANNING COMMISSION VICE-CHAIR NOMINATIONS 

Ms. Taylor referenced Section 1 “Organization” of the Rules of Procedure, specifically Section 1.2, which states, "The Commission shall elect a Vice-Chair who shall serve in that position through the term of the appointment to the Commission.” The Vice-Chairman would assume the Chairman's responsibilities in their absence, making it a highly responsible position. Ms. Taylor asked if any Commission members were interested or if someone wanted to nominate someone at the table. Please be prepared to do so by the next meeting. 

Chairman Dashiell added that, in the past, the Vice-Chair had assisted in preparing the motions of the Commission's business. He said it is helpful when the Chair is trying to manage the meeting, that when it comes time to make a motion, there has been someone who has been thinking carefully about the motion.

###### DISCUSSION OF NATURAL RESOURCE CONSERVATION ADVISORY COMMITTEE APPOINTMENT 

Ms. Taylor described the advisory group as one that deals with numerous environmental issues. They inquire about various issues occurring in the County; the most recent issues have been related to the landfill. If the public or this group has a particular concern, they may draft a letter to the legislature or to their representative. The term is for three years, and they meet once a month at 2:00 p.m. for approximately an hour. The Chair is Judith Stribling, who was very involved with Friends of the Nanticoke andis a professor at Salisbury University. Ms. Taylor asked the Commissioners to think about this volunteer opportunity and let her know of any interest at the October meeting.
