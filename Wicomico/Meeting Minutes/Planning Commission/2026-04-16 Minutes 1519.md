BURBAGE FARM LLC EASEMENT APPLICATION -— Powellville Road in Pittsville, Maryland - M-51, G-18, 23 & 24, P-165, 147 & 25 (C. Harrison) 

Mr. Colin Harrison approached the table. Mr. Harrison presented the Staff Report. 

> The applicants applied to convey an agricultural preservation easement to the Maryland Agricultural<sup>LandPreservationFoundation</sup> (“MALPF’’}<sup>on</sup> property<sup>located</sup> along<sup>PowellvilleRoad.The</sup> property<sup>sizeis128.38acres.</sup> 

Mr. Harrison explained the qualifying criteria for participation in the MALPF Program, which are as follows: 

Criterion #1: The land is currently being used for producing food or fiber or has the capability to do so. Criterion #2: The majority of the land area of any district should consist of either USDA Soil Capability<sup>Classes|,Il,andIll;USDAWoodland</sup> Groups 1 & 2; or at least 60 percent of the two (2) classifications combined. Criterion #3: An Agricultural Easement generally should not be less than 50 contiguous acres, unless certain conditions are met. 

Criterion #4: Land within the boundaries of a 10-year water and sewerage service district may be included in an Agricultural Easement only if if is outstanding in productivity and of significant size. 

The Planning Staff recommended that the Planning Commission forward a_ favorable recommendation to the County Council for support of the sale of an Agricultural Land Preservation Easement on the Burbage Farm, LLC property based on compliance with the County Comprehensive Plan. 

Mr. Harrison acknowledged and thanked Ms. Kirsten Purnell for her assistance in creating the Zoning,<sup>Aerial,andTax</sup> Maps<sup>fortheeasement</sup> applications being presented. 

As there were no additional comments, Chairman Dashiell requested a motion to forward a favorable recommendation to the County Council for the support of a sale of the Agricultural Land Preservation Easement on the Burbage Farm property based on compliance with the County Comprehensive<sup>Plan.Ms.</sup> Bynum-King<sup>movedto</sup> approve,<sup>whichwasseconded</sup> by<sup>Ms.Dentonand</sup> duly<sup>carried.</sup>
