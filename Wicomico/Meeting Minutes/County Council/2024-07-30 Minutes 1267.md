# County Council minutes 2024-07-30

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in a Special Legislative Session on Tuesday, July 30, 2024 at 9:00 am. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, Josh Hastings, and James Winn. Shanie Shields participated via speaker phone. Shane Baker were absent. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

# Robert A. Benson, Esg., Council Attorney 

Resolution No. 90-2024 — To amend the Charter of Wicomico County for an enhanced governance structure. 

Mr. Paul Wilber, County Attorney, came before Council. Mr. Kevin Karpinski, Esq. participated via Zoom. 

Mr. Benson explained, the County Council previously adopted Resolution No. 70-2024 to amend the Charter of Wicomico County to transition to a Council-Manager form of Government beginning December 1, 2026. He said this Resolution included the entire Charter showing the specific Charter sections to be amended as well as those unchanged. He said Article II, Sections 201 and 201 (A) are currently involved in a voting rights challenge in the United States District Court for Maryland, Case No. 1:23-CV-03325-MJM. He said the litigating parties in that suit have agreed to revise the Resolution to exclude references to the Charter Sections not being amended including Sections 201 and 201 (A). He said no other substantial changes have been made to Resolution No. 70-2024 in this modification. He said Resolution No. 90-2024 only shows the sections of the Charter that have proposed amendments that relate to the change of government structure, and that is the purpose of this meeting. 

Mr. Cannon clarified, Mr. Benson has shared with us that this is simply part of a legal correction we are trying to make in order to make sure that we are following the proper procedures. 

Mr. Hastings said, for clarity, there are various changes where certain areas have been struck and certain areas have been added. He asked, for my own understanding and for the public’s, when does this take place? He clarified, if the question is on the ballot to change our government structure, then this kicks in, or is this before that and to what degree, to which Mr. Benson responded, just by way of clarification, this is identical for all practical purposes to the Resolution adopted by the Council in 702024. He said the difference here is that the decision was made when 70-2024 was put forward to basically reprint the entire County Charter for ease of understanding. He said the purpose of this amendment is to remove any sections that were not being amended, and specially to remove Section 201 and Section 201 (A), which are the two sections that are at issue in the litigation. He clarified, the amended Resolution you have in front of you is identical to the original Resolution adopted in 70-2024 except it excludes any sections not being changed. He said this Resolution, if adopted today, would then be the Resolution that would be advertised and voted on in the November election, and if it were adopted, it would go into effect in December 2026. 

Mr. Cannon said the specific area of this that everyone should be paying attention to is simply what has been highlighted in yellow. He clarified, that is the only correction being made, to which Mr. Benson responded, we changed the recitals, which are the highlighted sections, to explain why the Resolution is being amended. 

Mr. Benson said Section 101 of the Charter is not being changed. He said Section 101 was in the original 70-2024 Resolution, but since it’s not being changed, it’s been removed from this Resolution. He clarified, any section that was not being changed has been removed from this Resolution. He said the

only thing that remains are the specific Charter sections that have amendments in them, to which Mr. Cannon responded, which have already been voted on prior. Mr. Benson said you all voted on this and they were adopted, and it’s the same exact. He said, from my perspective, this does not make any substantive change. He explained, in the litigation, the parties requested for clarification purposes that there be a specific change to clarify that Sections 201 and 201 (A) are not being affected by this Resolution, and since we were going to remove Sections 201 and 201 (A), it just made sense to remove any section that was not being changed. Ms. Shields said I’m looking at what he’s talking about. She then asked, is this what I was talking about when I talked to Mr. Cannon the other evening, to which Mr. Cannon responded, yes. Ms. Shields asked, it’s not going to be in there, so does that satisfy those people, to which Mr. Cannon responded, yes. 

Mr. Holloway said, when the County form of Government changed to the Executive in 2006, there were a lot of changes made to the Charter due to the fact that we had a new form of government with a County Executive. He said over the years up until probably a year or two ago we would run into different issues in the Charter that did not really apply like they should have because we had an Executive form of Government instead of a Council form of Government, and it was always a very simple process of passing a Resolution or Legislation to correct it. He said changing the form of government in the County is a big step, but it’s not such a complicated step that it can’t be rectified if we run into an issue later. 

Mr. Winn clarified, we’re not doing anything else, we’re just talking about 201 and 201 (A), to which Mr. Benson responded, no. He explained, there are other sections that have been removed and those other sections had no changes. He said specifically, we removed any section that was not changed to include Sections 201 and 201 (A) as neither of those were being changed in the original 70-2024 Resolution, and by eliminating them it just reclarified that they are not being changed. 

Mr. Winn said obviously everything else had already been voted on the same day as the budget, to which Mr. Benson responded, this is identical in every other way. Mr. Winn clarified, we are just making a few minor adjustments, to which Mr. Benson responded, to clarify specifically that any sections not being changed have been removed. He said they will remain the same if this is adopted. He clarified, if the voters vote, those sections don’t change in the Charter. 

Mr. Hastings said I don’t want to wordsmith this, and I’m all good with the yellow highlighted areas and continuing to move forward with that piece making sure that part is not included in this, but there are multiple pieces in here that we probably should fix at a future date. He said there are a couple of pieces where it mentions the County Commissioners in Section 402, and there are some other pieces in here that I think at a future date we should probably fix or wordsmith. Mr. Cannon said that is a good point. 

Mr. Hastings clarified, for the record, I obviously still don’t believe we should be moving in this direction, but I am happy to amend this to make sure it gets right for the voters and that it’s set if it does move forward. 

Mr. Cannon asked Mr. Karpinski if he would like to add anything, to which Mr. Karpinski responded, I do not as I think Mr. Benson accurately reflected why this amendment is being put forward. He said it is just to clarify and make sure the voters understand what sections are going to be affected and that there are other sections that are not an issue with regard to this Resolution. 

There being no further discussion, on motion by Mr. Merritt and seconded by Mr. Holloway, Resolution No. 90-2024 was unanimously approved.

## Public Comments: 

Mr. Mike Goldberg came to the podium and said once again, our County Executive has unilaterally entered into a highly controversial MOU. He said this MOU provides Eastern Shore Undercover (ESU) with exclusive access to encrypted Sheriff’s Office communications for dissemination to the public in real time thus potentially compromising operational security. He said the Executive claims that she entered into this MOU in the interest of transparency. He said what an astonishing pronouncement. He said, if she is so interested in transparency, then why was this MOU signed without the knowledge of the County Council, and why was it signed without the knowledge of Sheriff Lewis and while he is away on vacation. He then asked, have any legitimate media outlets been provided with the same access? He said this is just one more example of this Executive acting unilaterally, bypassing any Legislative process, and acting without the knowledge or consent of the Sheriff who is responsible for the lives and safety of his officers. He said Ms. Giordano claims that providing real time police communications is necessary to comply with the Public Information Act (PIA) requirements; hogwash. He asked, then why does she regularly drag her feet and obstruct citizens and the Council from obtaining information through PIA requests? He said to suggest that real time information is necessary to adhere to the PIA requirements is patently false. He said the Executive claimed that personal information will not be released and that ESU may delay posting of information by 15 to 30 minutes. He said her record shows that she will not enforce the terms of the contract. He said don’t forget her failure to enforce the Safari at the Quarry contract. He said, among other unenforced contractual violations, we have yet to see the required financial statement, community impact statement, or a check for the County’s nonexistent share of net profits. He asked, why would we entrust a non-government entity with the ability to compromise officer safety? He said just as with the event promotor for Safari at the Quarry, the proprietor of ESU is a personal friend of the County Executive and is also a significant campaign contributor and defacto minister of propaganda. He said posting real time information about police activity has been the core income-generating service of ESU. He said with encryption, that ability was severely reduced, so to salvage ESU’s Facebook page, it transitioned to being the Julie Giordano Broadcast System. He said, in exchange for the exclusive right to receive and disseminate encrypted information, the County Executive has acted to revive ESU so it can continue to purvey misinformation and render shamelessly biased political support. He said it is hard to imagine a more corrupt deal. He said it is time to end this kind of nonsense. He said I urge every County citizen to vote for the Question A Charter Amendment and eliminate the misfeasance chronically perpetrated by past and future County Executives, and particularly the current one. 

Ms. Monica Brooks came to the podium and said I am a Wicomico County resident and also the President of the Wicomico County NAACP. She said we actually also have an issue with this new MOU, specifically with the way in which it came about. She said, while we have not had a huge issue with Eastern Shore Undercover, just as citizens, so I am saying as an individual citizen, not for Wicomico County NAACP, in disseminating information to everyone. She said however, this particular issue has given us pause because we have concerns about privacy, we have concerns about lack of transparency, and especially how this came about when there was nothing put out to bid and when this was not disseminated to the public. She said we are also going to be requesting to get information from the County as to who they reached out to in order to see who may be available to go into such a contract, if that would be something that is viable to do. She said, specifically, knowing that there are several other media outlets and media companies here that can do the same thing, were they called, were they given consideration? She said three of them are people of color, so we want to know what the County is doing as far as its business, period, with entities of color, with businesses of color, and that diversity is actually in the mix of this for women-owned businesses, minority-owned businesses, and how these issues are happening. She said it is not right for backdoor deals to be done when others can be given equal

## Council Comments:

Mr. Merritt thanked everybody for coming out and said I hope everybody is having a good summer. He said we are weeding through the pits of quicksand as they get put in front of us.

# Council President Comments: 

Mr. Cannon said I would only like to add that we had the passing of Ms. Charlotte Adkins in Powellville. He said she was a great leader in that community and did an excellent job with the auxiliary for the Powellville Fire Department and will certainly be missed. He said they are dedicating the community hall to her, which I think is an excellent idea, and we are glad to see that happening for her and her family. 

There being no further business, on motion by Mr. Hastings, seconded by Mr. Winn, and unanimously approved, the Legislation Session was adjourned at 9:30 a.m.
