# County Council minutes 2025-05-20

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in Legislative Session on Tuesday, May 20, 2025 at 10:00 a.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, Josh Hastings, James Winn, and Shanie Shields. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

On motion by Mr. Merritt and seconded by Mr. Winn, the Consent Agenda consisting of the following items was unanimously approved: 

- Legislative Minutes from May 6, 2025 

- Open Work Session Minutes from April 1, 2025 — Legislation to Dissolve the Current Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission and to Create a New Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission 

- Open Work Session Minutes from April 25, 2025 — Water and Sewer Plan Amendment for Raegan’s Run Subdivision 

- Open Work Session Minutes from April 15, 2025 — Proposed Housing Action Items 

A proclamation was presented to TidalHealth declaring Wicomico County as a Stroke Smart County. 

A proclamation was presented to Life Crisis Center to recognize Children’s Mental Health Matters Week.

Public Hearing: Resolution No. 59-2025 — To authorize the County Executive to execute a lease agreement with Azure Flight Support SBY, LLC for a Fixed Base Operation (FBO). 

Mrs. Hurley said this request is to enter into a lease and operating agreement with Azure Flight Support SBY, LLC, who is the lessee, in which Azure will lease specific parcels of land commonly referred to as the GA terminal, the fuel storage facility, the maintenance hangar, and the ARFF hangar, each located on the southwest portion of the Airport and the improvements thereon including the use of the apron and the vehicular parking areas. 

Mrs. Hurley said the term of the lease will be for a period of twenty years commencing July 1, 2025 and expiring on June 30, 2045 with the option to renew for an additional ten years until June 30, 2055. She said the annual rent for the fuel farm will be $51,153.59 for years 1-5; $102,307.18 for years 6-10, and the annual rent for year 11 and beyond is to be determined. She said ground rent shall be $0.36 per square foot for aeronautical unimproved land. She said the lessee is to pay to the County 90% of landing fees, a fuel flowage fee of $0.14 per gallon for all fuel sold for all aircraft, and a usage fee of $0.08 per gallon for all fuel dispensed for all aircraft. She said the lessee will also pay to the County parking fees equivalent to 50% of revenues collected for use of the apron area for any aircraft to park temporarily or overnight, and will pay to the County 1% of lessee gross revenues attributable to all sales of products and services rendered by lessee at the Airport. She said the initial tenant improvements will be completed in phases subject to FAA and County approval as provided in the lease agreement.
