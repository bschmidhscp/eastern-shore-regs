# Robert A. Benson, Esg., Council Attorney 

Public Hearing: Legislative Bill No. 2025-04: An Act to amend Chapter 186 of the Wicomico County code titled “Right to Farm” to include the right to conduct a commercial seafood operation, establish new membership positions on the Right to Farm and Commercial Seafood Operation Reconciliation Committee, and expand the notification requirement to include the right to conduct commercial seafood operations. 

Mr. Benson said Mr. Winn initially brought this to the Council and the concept when he brought it was to establish a new committee, which would have been a second committee in addition to the Right to Farm Committee. He said, when I looked at Maryland Law with regard to the Right to Farm Act Committee, it also had language in it that allowed the Right to Farm Act Committee to hear seafood related matters as well. He said I suggested to Mr. Winn that, rather than creating a whole new committee, perhaps the better way would be to consider trying to add it to the existing Right to Farm Act Legislation, thereby not having to create a new committee where you would have to appoint additional people to serve on it to sit once or twice a year. 

Mr. Benson said the Bill was drafted using the existing Right to Farm Act Legislation and including seafood operations within it. He said, under Maryland Law, if you regulate or draft a Bill that involves seafood, it has to go to the Maryland Department of Natural Resources to be approved. He said this was submitted to the Director of the Department of Natural Resources, and they did not have any objections to the County moving in this situation, so we are here tonight to have a Public Hearing on the Bill. 

Mr. Cannon opened the Public Hearing.
