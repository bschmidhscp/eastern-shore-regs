Public Hearing: Resolution No. 93-2024 — Amending the FY25 Operating and Capital Budget to use a portion of the expected FY26 Capital Funding for the Wicomico County Free Library, Inc. in the amount of $330,000. 

Mrs. Hurley said the Wicomico Public Library is adjusting its timeline for the renovation of the former Ward Museum moving the design phase to FY25 and construction to FY27. She said to expedite the process, the library is requesting $330,000 from the contingency fund to begin initial designs. 

Mrs. Hurley said a public hearing notice was posted on the County’s website and published in the local newspaper for this public hearing as well as the next two public hearings on the agenda this morning. 

Mr. Cannon opened the Public Hearing. 

Mr. Joe White came to the came podium and said I am a resident of Wicomico County and I was at the initial public hearing for the library, and I stated at that time and would like to restate that we do not have an all-encompassing plan on exactly how much this is going to cost (soup to nuts). He said I am going to reference the fact that the library has already purchased the property in Parsonsburg that had a plan two years ago and that property is still sitting vacant, there is no plan for that, no public plan. He said I think expediting this plan is not going to work out well. He said we do not know all of the ins and outs of what is wrong with the building, and even by Salisbury University’s own admission, they had very large numbers and that is why they felt it was necessary to remove the Ward Foundation out of the building, which is between them and the Ward Foundation. He said I think we are stepping into something that is going to cost these taxpayers more money than we realize, and I would like for Council to consider not expediting this project. He clarified, I am not saying can it, I am just saying don’t speed it up as I think it is going to cause us to run into an issue. 

There were no further public comments. Mr. Cannon closed the Public Hearing.
