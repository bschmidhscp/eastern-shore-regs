# County Council minutes 2024-12-03

The Wicomico County Council met in Legislative Session on Tuesday, December 3, 2024 at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shane Baker, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney.

- Legislative Minutes from November 19, 2024 

- Open Work Session Minutes from October 15, 2024 — Interviews with Nominees for Supervisor of Assessments Position in Wicomico County 

- Open Work Session Minutes from October 15, 2024 — Update from Talkie Communications on Broadband Projects in the County 

- Open Work Session Minutes from October 15, 2024 — Quarterly Work Session with the Board of Education 

- Closed Session Minutes from June 18, 2024 — To Discuss Cybersecurity Audit 

- Closed Session Minutes from August 6, 2024 — Deputy Director of Recreation, Parks & Tourism

Resolution No. 158-2024 — To approve an application for a tax credit for a multifamily residential development property pursuant to Wicomico County Code Section 90-7. 

Mrs. Hurley said this request was originally presented as Resolution No. 126-2024 on October 1 and at that time it did not receive a favorable vote. She said Council has now received a request from the owner’s attorney requesting Council reconsider the application because the County Executive has acknowledged her willingness to modify the application timeframe requirements to permit the processing of the application. She said this application is now before you as new business. 

Ms. Lyndsey Ryan from Booth, Cropper and Mariner came before Council and said I represent the Ross. Mr. Leonard Arvi came before Council and said I am part owner of the Ross. 

Mr. Holloway said last week I requested a list of all the investors. He said this is probably one of the largest tax breaks the County has ever given anybody, and a request for other information, such as tax returns and financial statements, was made and we never received that information, so we made the request again today.

Robert A. Benson, Esg., Council Attorney 

Legislative Bill No. 2024-10 — An Act to amend Chapter 90 of the Wicomico County Code titled “Tax Exemptions” by repealing in its entirety Section 90-7 titled “Hotel and Multifamily Residential Development Property Tax Credit.” 

Mr. Benson said this is better known as the Horizon tax credit. He said, when this was originally enacted by the Council, it had a provision in it that said that the tax credit would be reevaluated by the County Council every three years, and this is the third year. He said, if this is adopted tonight, it would terminate any future tax credits under the Horizon program. 

There being no further discussion, on motion by Ms. Shields, seconded by Mr. Baker, and by roll call vote, Mr. Holloway, aye; Mr. Hastings, aye; Mr. Winn, aye; Ms. Shields, aye; Mr. Baker, aye; Mr. Merritt, aye, and Mr. Cannon, aye, Legislative Bill No. 2024-10 was unanimously approved.
