Laura Hurley, Council Administrator
Mrs. Hurley said there are six Public Hearings on the agenda this morning pertaining to land
preservation easements. She said a Public Hearing notice was posted in the local newspaper for each
one stating that a Public Hearing would be held at 10 a.m. She explained, land preservation easements
are a way to preserve farmland for future food and fiber production as they restrict development on
prime farmland and woodland. She said, once the easement is in place, property owners may still use
the property for agricultural or related uses, but for the most part, they are giving up the development
rights on the property.
