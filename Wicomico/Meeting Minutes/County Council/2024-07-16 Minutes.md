# Robert A. Benson, Esquire, Council Attorney 

Mr. Benson said the Council has received a request that, when Legislative Bills have a Public Hearing, there be an explanation as to the purpose of the Bill, so just so the Council understands, there will be a slight deviation this morning from the normal practice and I am going to try to provide an explanation. He said I would note that on each of the Bills we have before us today there are individuals listed and, should I not provide an explanation that they think is appropriate, perhaps they can come forward and supplement whatever I provide. 

Public Hearing: Legislative Bill 2024-04 — An Act to authorize and empower Wicomico County, Maryland to borrow on the faith and credit of Wicomico County, Maryland an aggregate principal amount not exceeding Ten Million Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($10,250,000) and to evidence such borrowing by the issuance and sale from time to time of one or more series of general obligation bonds of the County, the proceeds thereof to be used by Wicomico County to finance, refinance, or reimburse the costs of a certain project for the Board of Education including costs of planning, design, acquisition, construction, improvement, installation, renovation, rehabilitation, repairing, furnishing and equipping of land, facilities, buildings and structures, together with other costs and financing costs related thereto as further described herein as applicable. 

Mr. Benson explained, the purpose of this Bill is to authorize bonding in the amount of $10,250,000. He said, for the benefit of the public, when the budget was approved, the Council voted to fund the construction of the Fruitland Primary School and $10,250,000 is the portion of funding that was agreed would be bonded, so that is the purpose of this Bill. 

Mr. Cannon opened the Public Hearing. There were no public comments. Mr. Cannon closed the Public Hearing.

Public Hearing: Legislative Bill 2024-05 — An Act to amend Chapter 29 of the Wicomico County Code titled “Finance” to establish Article Ill titled “Community Reinvestment Repair Fund” to establish the use and distribution process for money received from the Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund. 

Mr. Benson explained, the State of Maryland authorized the sale of cannabis in the State of Maryland and there are proceeds generated from that. He said a portion of those proceeds are dedicated to various counties including Wicomico County to be used to fund programs for low-income and other items as identified by the Maryland Office of Social Equity. He said the purpose of this Bill is to provide a mechanism within which the funding would be handled. He said in this Bill the Council is basically saying that the Local Management Board will be the Board that administers the fund and will issue the criteria and make recommendations as to which programs should be funded, and those recommendations would come back to the Council and the Council will fund based on those recommendations.

Public Hearing: Legislative Bill 2024-06 — An Act to amend Chapter 22 of the Wicomico County Code titled “County Property,” Section 22-1 titled “County-Owned Property” to add a provision on the use of County-owned property. 

Mr. Benson explained, the purpose of this Legislation is to add language to the County Code, specifically Chapter 22 that relates to any contracts, licenses, permits, or leases that the County would enter into. He said this Bill came about because of a contract relating to a road event that took place on County property that the Council was not aware of prior to the agreement being entered into.
