# Robert A. Benson, Esg., Council Attorney 

Introduction: Legislative Bill No. 2025-02 — An Act to amend Chapter 37 titled “Ethics Law,” Article II titled “Ethics Commission,” and Section 37-5 titled “Ethics Commission,” to require Special Legal Counsel for the Ethics Commission to avoid a conflict of interest or the appearance of a conflict of interest. Mr. Benson said currently the Ethics Commission is represented by the County Attorney’s office. He said, in reviewing other counties with an Executive Branch the same as Wicomico, most of those counties have a situation where they authorize either independent counsel, or larger counties, like Montgomery or Prince George’s County, appear to have “Ethics Departments” where they have an attorney within the Ethics Department. He said in Harford County, for instance, there is a similar provision where the Ethics Commission is advised by an independent attorney. 

Mr. Benson said this addresses a situation where there is an ethics complaint filed against the Executive or members of the Executive Department who work with the County Attorney. He said, to avoid a conflict of interest, the purpose of this Bill is to authorize independent counsel to represent the Ethics Commission on all ethics complaints. He said in Cecil County I think they have a situation where the Council Attorney represents the Ethics Commission if there is an Executive complaint, but rather than doing it in that manner, this Bill would simply authorize independent counsel. He said the expectation is that the independent counsel would be paid at the same rate the County Attorney would be paid at. 

Mr. Benson said, under Maryland Law, before you can make a change to the Wicomico County Ethics Commission Law, it has to go to the State Ethics Commission. He said this was submitted to the State Ethics Commission in December, and on January 16 the County received a letter back from the State Ethics Commission indicating that this was consistent with State Law and that they did not object. 

Mr. Winn said I agree with the whole thing, but I’m just trying to point a few things out. He said let’s just say the County Attorney doesn’t have any of the information that special counsel has. He clarified, not for any cases that happen with the Executive per se, but for all other cases that happen with the County, will the County Attorney be able to get the records for any incidents so that way they can help deter any claims or anything that happens with any of the ethics violations? He said right now what we have is an Ethics Board and the County Attorney has the Commission and then they have discussion and then, if there is a problem later on, they will have that information later because it’s in their records, but if it is in special counsel the whole time, is the County Attorney not going to be involved at all in any County business? Mr. Baker said I think he will be involved in County business. Mr. Winn clarified, I mean as far as ethics. He asked, is this that we don’t want the County Attorney involved with any ethics at all?
