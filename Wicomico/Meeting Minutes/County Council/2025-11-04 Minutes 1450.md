— To authorize the County Executive to execute (1) an RNG

Mrs. Hurley said this Public Hearing was published on the County website and in the local newspaper.

percent to 50 percent of the project gross revenues adjusted annually by the CPI-Inflation Index.

which means oxygen is present. He said as time progresses, the oxygen decreases and it becomes
anerobic, which means there is a lack of oxygen, and then methane starts to get produced. He said that

environment is going to continue to exist with even less oxygen because now you're capping it.
