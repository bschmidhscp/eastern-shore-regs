Public Hearing: Legislative Bill No. 2025-02
— An Act to amend Chapter 37 titled “Ethics Law,” Article II
titled “Ethics Commission,” Sections 37-5 titled “Ethics Commission” to require Special Legal Counsel for
the Ethics Commission to avoid a conflict of interest or the appearance of a conflict of interest.
Mr. Benson said, prior to the Bill being introduced, this was submitted to the Maryland State Ethics
Commission for their review and comment. He said the Maryland State Ethics Commission responded
and said that they had no comment and that they were not opposed to the Bill.
Mr. Benson explained, the purpose of the Bill is to have the Ethics Commission use special counsel to
represent it for issues that come before the Ethics Commission. He said currently, the Ethics Commission
is represented by the County Attorney. He said there are instances when the County Attorney may have
ethics complaints that involve members of the Executive Branch with which he works, and
I believe it’s
the Council’s thought that it would be better to have an independent counsel to provide legal
representation to the Ethics Commission on all matters.

Mr. Benson said, for clarification, a motion to reconsider is a motion that is recognized by Robert’s Rules
of Order. He said the Council does not use Robert’s Rules of Order as its formal rules of procedure, but it
