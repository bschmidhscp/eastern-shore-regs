Flanders Room of the Wicomico County Youth & Civic Center, Salisbury, Maryland.

Winn, Shane Baker, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings.

Internal Auditor, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney.

items was unanimously approved:
