The Wicomico County Council met in Legislative Session on Tuesday, October 3, 2023 at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland.

# Anjali Chatelle, Internal Auditor 

Ms. Chatelle introduced herself and said I came on board about a month ago. She said I am a certified public accountant in the State of Virginia and now Maryland, and I look forward to serving you all. 

Resolution No. 131-2023 — Concurring with the Declaration from the Internal Auditor on Organizational Independence. 

Ms. Chatelle said the Institute of Internal Auditors publishes an international professional publications framework that comprises the standards for internal auditing. She said attribute standard 1110 entitled Organizational Independence states that the chief audit executive must confirm to the board at least annually the organizational independence for the internal audit activity. She said, in Wicomico County, the equivalent of the Board of Directors is the County Council. She said, in accordance with attribute standard 1110, I am pleased to provide the following declaration that, as Chief Audit Executive for the Office of the Internal Auditor for Wicomico County, Maryland, I declare that to the best of my knowledge and belief there have been no contraventions of auditor independence requirements or any applicable code of professional conduct in relation to the audits performed for Wicomico County.
