# Mr. Cannon opened the Public Hearing. 

Mr. Brian Rigor came to the podium and said I am a Wicomico County resident and the Chief Finance and Operations Officer for Wicomico County Public Schools. He said first I would like to express my appreciation to the Council for support of the Board’s capital plan in recent years. He said, in FY24, the County was able to support our final funding request for the Mardela Middle and High School project as well as funding to support the development of schematic design for a replacement Fruitland Primary School. He said Mardela is currently on track and on budget and we anticipate project completion in advance of the start of the 2025-26 school year and we look forward to faculty and students moving in to completed phase 3 classrooms later this spring. He said the Fruitland Primary project is also currently on schedule as schematic design has been completed and received approval from the Interagency Commission on School construction. He said the FY25 WCPS capital improvement plan was submitted to County Government last fall in order for its priorities to be considered in the County CIP. He said, as usual, our CIP includes a prioritized list of capital projects based on a detailed analysis of data by our planning and construction department. He said also, as usual, our CIP includes projections for capital projects going out five years. He said our first priority for capital funding remains the Fruitland Primary project. He said, at this point, we have selected a construction manager for the project and we are working with the construction manager, the previously selected architect, and stakeholders on design development. He said our request will allow the project to continue moving forward and avoid cost escalations that would be incurred if the project were to be put on hold. He said priorities two, three and four are HVAC systemic renovations at three schools — Fruitland Intermediate, Westside Intermediate, and Salisbury Middle. He said Dr. Stauffer and I will return later today for the work session in case the Council has any questions regarding our CIP requests.

# Anjali Chatelle, Internal Auditor 

Resolution No. 07-2024 — Declaring with the Declaration from the Internal Auditor on Organizational Independence. 

Ms. Chatelle said the Institute of Internal Auditors publishes an International and Professional Practices framework comprising of standards for internal auditing. She said attribute standard 1110 entitled Organizational Independence states that the chief audit executive must confirm to the Board at least annually the organizational independence of the internal audit activity. She said in Wicomico County, the equivalent of the board of directors is the County Council. She said, in accordance with attribute standard 1110, I am pleased to provide the following declaration. She said as Chief Audit Executive for the Office of the Internal Auditor for Wicomico County, Maryland, I declare that to the best of my knowledge and belief there has been no contraventions of auditor independence, requirements, or any applicable code of professional conduct in relation to the audits performed by Wicomico County. 

There being no further discussion, on motion by Mr. Hastings and seconded by Mr. Winn, Resolution No. 07-2024 was unanimously approved.
