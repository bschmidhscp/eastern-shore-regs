# County Council minutes 2025-01-09

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in Special Open Session on Thursday, January 9, 2025 at 5:00 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland to entertain a motion to go into Closed Session. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. Shane Baker was absent. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

Mr. Cannon called the meeting to order. 

There being no further business, on motion by Mr. Merritt, seconded by Mr. Hastings, and unanimously approved, the Open Session was adjourned at 5:01 p.m. to go into Closed Session pursuant to General Provisions Article Section 3-305(b)(8) to consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals about pending or potential litigation to protect the County’s legal position. 

The Wicomico County Council met in a Closed Session on Thursday, January 9, 2025 5:01 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland pursuant to General Provisions Article Section 3-305(b)(8). 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. Shane Baker was absent. 

Present for the Closed Work Session: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator;, Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate; Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney, Frank McKenzie, and Paul Wilber, County Attorney. Kevin Karpinski, Esq., participated via Zoom. The purpose of the Closed Session was to consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals about pending or potential litigation to protect the County’s legal position. No formal action or recorded votes were taken.
