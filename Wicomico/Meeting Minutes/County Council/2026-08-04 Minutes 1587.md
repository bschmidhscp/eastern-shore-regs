Public Hearing: Resolution No. 125-2026 - Establishing a temporary moratorium on the
acceptance, processing, review, approval, and issuance of permits, site plans, development
approvals, and other authorizations for data centers pending the review and adoption of
appropriate County regulations.
Mrs. Hurley said this resolution would establish a temporary moratorium on the acceptance and
processing of applications for new data center developments in Wicomico County. She explained
that the purpose of the temporary pause is to allow the County time to review the State’s pending
Data Center Impact Study and complete the update to the County’s Comprehensive Plan before
considering legislation to address the regulations of data centers. The proposed moratorium would
remain in effect up to one year or until legislation is adopted by the County Council.
