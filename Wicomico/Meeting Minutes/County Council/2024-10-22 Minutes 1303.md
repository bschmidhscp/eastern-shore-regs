# County Council minutes 2024-10-22

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in a Special Legislative Session on Tuesday, October 22, 2024 at 9:00 a.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, and Shanie Shields. James Winn, Shane Baker, and Josh Hastings were absent. 

Present: Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Victor Laws, Esq. 

Resolution No. 141-2024—To institute a temporary ban on any outdoor burning in Wicomico County, Maryland. 

Mr. Lorenzo Cropper, Director of Emergency Services, came before Council and said I talked to the National Weather Service in Wakefield and, at present, no rain, so I think you’re doing what’s best for the community overall to avoid any major mishaps or incidents. 

Mr. Holloway asked, is there any word about water restrictions yet because I know some counties have started to do that, to which Mr. Cropper responded, not as of yet. 

Mr. Cannon said we checked with those associated with Emergency Services to make sure that everybody thought this was in the best interest of the County, so that is a good thing. He clarified, we didn’t come up with this idea ourselves. 

There being no further discussion, on motion by Mr. Merritt and seconded by Mr. Holloway, Resolution No. 141-2024 was unanimously approved. 

## Public Comments: 

There were none. 

## Council Comments: 

Mr. Holloway said be careful with open flames. He said if you’re using equipment outside, be careful not to cause any sparks. He said don’t throw cigarettes along the road. He said it looks like Mr. Cropper said it is probably going to be another week or so before we see any rain, so be careful. 

Mr. Merritt said I want to thank Director Cropper, Assistant Director Hopkins, and Chief Frampton for coming today. He said I know you guys are busy on a normal day and with what we have going on, we are a little bit outside the norm. He said thank you for what you do and for coming in today. He said I hope everybody has a happy and safe Halloween. 

# Council President Comments: 

Mr. Cannon said Emergency Services can be contacted with any questions in regard to the restrictions because there are some exceptions, but not too many. He said, if anyone has any questions, they can certainly contact Wicomico County Emergency Services and they can clarify that for you. 

There being no further business, on motion by Mr. Merritt, seconded by Mr. Holloway, and unanimously approved, the Special Legislative Session was adjourned at 9:04 a.m.
