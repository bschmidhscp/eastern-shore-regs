Laura Hurley, Council Administrator
Mrs. Hurley said we have several Public Hearings on the agenda this morning. She said each Public
Hearing notice was posted on the County’s website and published in the local newspaper.
Public Hearing: Resolution No. 148-2024
— to Approve the Relocation of a Forest Conservation
Easement Containing 2.87 acres located on Tax Map 12, Parcel 1, owned by Heron Ponds Expansion, LLC,
onsite to the same parcel.
Mrs. Hurley said the forest conservation would be relocated on the same parcel of land at a 1:1 ratio.
She explained, the property owner is seeking to relocate the forest conservation easement to create
buildable space for future lot development within the Heron Ponds Subdivision.
Mr. Cannon opened the Public Hearing. There were no public comments. Mr. Cannon closed the Public
Hearing.
There being no discussion, on motion by Mr. Hastings and seconded by Mr. Baker, Resolution No. 148-
2024 was unanimously approved.

Introduction: Legislative Bill 2024-12
— An Act to add Chapter 99 of the Wicomico County Code titled
“Vehicles, County” to establish procedures for the use and operation of County-owned vehicles.
Mr. Benson said Wicomico County currently does not have any significant rules or regulations with
regard to County vehicles, so the purpose of this is to establish a guideline for County vehicles. He said
this Legislation was drafted by Mrs. Hurley initially using similar Legislation from Harford County, and
I
will note that Harford County is a County Executive form of Government the same as Wicomico County.
Mr. Benson said, after Mrs. Hurley drafted it, she sent it to me for my review and
I made minor changes.
He said after my changes, we sent this to the County Attorney’s office in January of this year. He said the
County Attorney indicated he would meet with the County Executive. He said, after the County Attorney

met with the County Executive, the County Attorney’s office submitted a redline version of this bill for

and myself and we reviewed the redline changes and largely the vast majority of the redline changes
proposed by the County Attorney’s office were included in this Legislation.
Mr. Benson said the purpose of this is to basically establish a process whereby the County Council funds
the vehicles, and this requires that the County Executive at the time the budget is submitted provide a
list of vehicles and what Departments and persons those vehicles go to so the County Council
understands what it is funding. He said it requires all vehicles to have County decals so the public knows
when someone is driving a County vehicle and knows when they are being used and what projects are
being done by County vehicles. He said it also provides requirements that County employees have to
meet in order to be entitled to drive vehicles so that someone who has a bad driving record who is not
necessarily someone the County would want driving vehicles would not be permitted to place the
County’s insurance carrier and the public at risk. He said that is what this Legislation is intended to do.

only at budget time.
Mr. Benson said secondly, the County Executive raised an issue about County employees being required
