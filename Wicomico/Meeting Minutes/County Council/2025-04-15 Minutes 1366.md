Public Hearing: Resolution No. 44-2025
— To amend the 2025 Operating and Capital Budget to increase
funding for the Sharps Point Dredge Spoils Hauling and Relocation Project in the amount of $2,300,000.
Mrs. Hurley said this is a request for funds to support the dredging at Sharp’s Point. She explained, the
dredging cycle has been delayed for nearly seven years, which has led to increased sediment buildup.
She said this requires the removal of an estimated 400,000 cubic yards of material, which is significantly
more than the originally projected amount. She said the County is working with the U.S. Army Corps of
Engineers on the project.
