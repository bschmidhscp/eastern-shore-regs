The Wicomico County Council met in Legislative Session on Tuesday, November 19, 2024 at 10:00 a.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shane Baker, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

On motion by Mr. Hastings and seconded by Mr. Baker, the Consent Agenda consisting of the following items was unanimously approved: 

- Legislative Minutes from November 5, 2024 

- Special Legislative Session Minutes from October 22, 2024 

- Open Work Session Minutes from October 15, 2024 — Greater Salisbury Committee Report on Housing Needs in Wicomico County 

- Closed Session Minutes from June 26, 2024 — to Conduct Collective Bargaining Negotiations or Consider Matters Related to the Negotiations

# Laura Hurley, Council Administrator 

Mrs. Hurley said we have several Public Hearings on the agenda this morning. She said each Public Hearing notice was posted on the County’s website and published in the local newspaper. 

Public Hearing: Resolution No. 148-2024 — to Approve the Relocation of a Forest Conservation Easement Containing 2.87 acres located on Tax Map 12, Parcel 1, owned by Heron Ponds Expansion, LLC, onsite to the same parcel. 

Mrs. Hurley said the forest conservation would be relocated on the same parcel of land at a 1:1 ratio. She explained, the property owner is seeking to relocate the forest conservation easement to create buildable space for future lot development within the Heron Ponds Subdivision. 

Mr. Cannon opened the Public Hearing. There were no public comments. Mr. Cannon closed the Public Hearing. 

There being no discussion, on motion by Mr. Hastings and seconded by Mr. Baker, Resolution No. 1482024 was unanimously approved.
