# County Council minutes 2024-07-23

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in a Special Open Session on Tuesday, July 23, 2024 at 9:00 a.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland to entertain a motion to go into Closed Session. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. James Winn and Shane Baker were absent. 

Present: Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

Mr. Cannon called the meeting to order. 

There being no further business, on motion by Ms. Shields, seconded by Mr. Merritt, and unanimously approved, the Open Session was adjourned at 9:01 a.m. to go into Closed Session pursuant to General Provisions Article Section 3-305(b)(8) to consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals about pending or potential litigation to protect the County’s legal position. 

The Wicomico County Council met in a Closed Session on Tuesday, July 23, 2024 at 9:05 a.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland pursuant to General Provisions Article Section 3-305(b)(8). 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present for the Closed Work Session: Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate; Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney; and Paul Wilber, County Attorney. Kevin Karpinski, Esquire, participated via Zoom. 

The purpose of the Closed Session was to consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals about pending or potential litigation to protect the County’s legal position. No formal action or recorded votes were taken.
