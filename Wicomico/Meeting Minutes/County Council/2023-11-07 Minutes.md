# County Council minutes 2023-11-07

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in Legislative Session on Tuesday, November 7, 2023 at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Shane Baker, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James Winn, Jeff Merritt, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Mr. Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

Mr. Cannon asked for a moment of silence for the passing of Lewis Riley. He said Mr. Riley was a great person for Wicomico County who served in a very distinguished position. He said Mr. Riley was on this actual County Council for many years and also served in the House of Delegates, was a State Senator, and had two separate periods in which he served as Secretary of Agriculture for the State of Maryland under two separate Governors. 

On motion by Ms. Shields and seconded by Mr. Baker, the Consent Agenda consisting of the following items was unanimously approved: 

- Legislative Minutes from October 17, 2023 

- Open Work Session Minutes from October 3, 2023 — Letter of Support for State Legislation for Tax Subtraction on Death Benefits for Fallen Law Enforcement Officers/Tax Waiver 

- Open Work Session Minutes from October 17, 2023 — Local Management Board Presentation on Community Plan 

- Open Work Session Minutes from October 17, 2023 — Discussion with Talkie Communications on Broadband Projects 

A proclamation was presented to the Fenix Youth Project in recognition of National Runaway Month. 

Robert A. Benson, Esg., Council Attorney 

Public Hearing: Legislative Bill 2023-15 - An Emergency Act Establishing a Moratorium on the Processing, Review, and Approval of Applicants, Site Plans, Permits, and other Authorizations for the Location, Construction, and/or Operation of Businesses Engaged in Growing, Processing, and/or Selling Cannabis to include On-Site Consumption Establishments for a Period of (6) Six Months to Allow the County Sufficient Time to Enact Reasonable, Thoughtful, and Lawful Zoning Requirements and other Regulations Pertaining Thereto as Deemed Necessary to Protect the Public Health, Safety, and Welfare. Mr. Benson pointed out that this is being presented as an emergency act, and in order for this to be adopted as an emergency act, under Section 311-G of the Charter, five Members of the Council have to vote in the affirmative. 

Mr. Cannon opened the Public Hearing. There were no public comments. Mr. Cannon closed the Public Hearing. 

There being no discussion, on motion by Mr. Baker, seconded by Ms. Shields, and by roll call vote, Mr. Holloway, aye; Mr. Hastings, aye; Mr. Winn, aye; Ms. Shields, aye; Mr. Merritt, aye; Mr. Baker, aye, and Mr. Cannon, aye, Legislative Bill 2023-15 was unanimously approved.
