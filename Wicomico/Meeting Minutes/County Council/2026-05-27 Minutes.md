# Laura Hurley, Council Administrator 

Resolution No. 82-2026 — Extending the date for the adoption of the Annual Budget and Appropriation<sup>Bill fromJune1toJune</sup> 15,<sup>2026.</sup> 

Mrs. Hurley explained, Charter Section 704(F) provides that the Annual Budget and Appropriation Bill shall be adopted by the County Council not later than June 1 or such date as may be set by Resolution pursuant to Section 707 of the Charter, but no later than June 15. Extending the date for the adoption of the Annual Budget and Appropriation Bill will allow Council additional time to further review and discuss the County Executive’s proposed budget. Also, June 1 is on a Monday and the next regularly scheduled Council meeting is on June 2, so this will allow the Council to adopt<sup>the</sup> budget during<sup>a</sup> regular meeting<sup>rather thana</sup> special meeting<sup>unlessitis</sup> needed,<sup>but</sup> again,<sup>nolater thanJune15.</sup> 

There being no discussion, on motion by Ms. Shields and seconded by Mr. Ennis, Resolution No. 82-2026 was unanimously approved.
