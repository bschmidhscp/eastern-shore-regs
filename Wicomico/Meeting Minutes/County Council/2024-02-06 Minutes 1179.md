Ms. Donna O’Hara, Director of Human Resources, and Ms. Pam Oland, Director of Finance, came before Council. 

Mr. Holloway said I see that your prices are $18 for breakfast, $22 for lunch and $40 for dinner. He then asked if they submit receipts for that and then are paid just what they spend, or are they allowed that much anyway, to which Ms. O’Hara responded, they turn in receipts for that. Resolution No. 14-2024 — Approving the new air service incentive program for Salisbury-Ocean City Wicomico Regional Airport. 

Mrs. Hurley said Council had a work session on this request on January 16. 

Mr. Tony Rudy, Director of Aviation, came before Council. 

Mr. Winn asked for an overview of this program. 

Mr. Rudy said it is fairly common for airports around the country to adopt a similar program and we looked at probably a dozen across the country and tried to mirror exactly what other airports are doing. He reported, this is open to all air carriers, Piedmont included, for any new routes to or from the Airport. He said the purpose is to basically offer incentives to carriers to limit their startup costs and to help them grow their passenger volume and start making a profit. He said it is limited for two years and during that time we waive landing fees and we would waive any rent for ticket counter space or any office space they might use, so really it is not costing the Airport anything because we aren’t collecting those fees currently. He said we are also helping out with advertising and marketing costs. He said those costs we currently use for general Airport advertising and marketing and we would target those funds towards the new air service we have. 

Mr. Rudy explained, basically we have two 12-month service periods and we have a provision in there where if any of those funds we expend within either the first 12 months or the second 12 months of service were to stop in one of those periods, we would be able to recover those funds that were spent on advertising and marketing. 

Mr. Merritt asked if Piedmont is able to take advantage of this in any way, to which Mr. Rudy responded, absolutely. He explained, if they started a new cost, granted they are already paying rent for space and I doubt they would be renting more space to cover another flight to another destination, but we can offset landing fees for them. He clarified, we wouldn’t charge them additional landing fees for those extra flights. 

There being no further discussion, on motion by Ms. Shields and seconded by Mr. Winn, Resolution No. 14-2024 was unanimously approved.
