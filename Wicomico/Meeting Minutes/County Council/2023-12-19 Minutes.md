Submission of Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) for Fiscal Years 2025-2029: 

Ms. Pam Oland, Director of Finance, and Ms. Julie Giordano, County Executive, came before Council. Ms. Oland passed out the CIP and Ms. Giordano said, presented in front of you is my proposed Capital Improvement Plan for FY25-29 beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2029. She said I am going to let Ms. Oland take you through some of the highlights and we will go from there. 

Ms. Oland said, as you know, the Charter states we are required to submit the CIP to Council by the 3’ Tuesday in December, which is today, so we are presenting our proposed CIP. She said I stated several times over the last couple of years that in 2025 we are not looking to borrow as much money as we have been in the last couple of years, so we are looking at about $10.7 million and that’s only three projects. She said one project is $4 million to do the roof at the Civic Center as that roof is original to the rebuild and it is time to replace it. She said, since I have been with the County for over ten years now we have talked about replacing that roof and it is time to stop just patching it, we need to fix it. She said there is also $4 million to continue the Fruitland Primary project and there is $2.7 million to do some expansion primarily for medical purposes at the Correctional Facility. She said the rest of the projects are either bond or pay-go funded because we do not have a lot of debt falling off our books in 2025, so anything that we add would be additive to our debt service and to the amount that we have under our debt limit. She explained, we have a debt service policy that we should keep it less than 12 percent of general fund revenue. She said, with the debt we borrowed in 2024, we are at 7.47 percent and our debt limit is at 64 percent with the debt we have borrowed and that number has stayed relatively stable even with the additions, but that is primarily because of growth and assessed value that is based on assessible base. 

Ms. Oland said most of the other projects are ones that have been in here prior. She explained, anything that Council has had in a previous plan would say existing project in the write ups, and anything that is new this year that is coming on would be listed as a new project. 

Ms. Oland said we request Council adopt this plan. She said I know typically Council extends the deadline for adoption, but my request is that Council adopt it in the February or March timeframe so we have a basis to go on what we put in the budget because this is a plan. She clarified, this is a planning document, it is to help the Executive and Council make decisions as to what should be in the budget for FY25. She said we do four additional years, but the main focus should be on 2025 because that is the year we are going to be budgeting for next. 

Ms. Oland said, additionally, I am available for any work sessions Council has, but I am not available on the January 2 Council meeting, but I am available after that for any of the other Council meetings if you would like to have any work sessions to discuss anything that is in here.
