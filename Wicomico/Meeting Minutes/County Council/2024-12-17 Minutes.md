# Robert A. Benson, Esg., Council Attorney 

Public Hearing: Legislative Bill 2024-12 — An Act to add Chapter 99 of the Wicomico County Code titled “Vehicles, County” to establish procedures for the use and operation of County-owned or leased vehicles. 

Mr. Benson said this Bill was proposed by Mr. Holloway and introduced two meetings ago and is here for a Public Hearing this morning. He explained, the purpose of this Bill is that the County currently does not have regulations or requirements as it relates to County vehicles. He said this is modeled after another County and essentially requires decals on County vehicles so County vehicles are properly identified. He said it requires the Executive to provide the County with a list of County vehicles and what Departments they are assigned to once a year at the time the budget is provided so Council knows what it is paying for. He said it sets forth limitations on use of the vehicles such that they have to be used for County business and not for personal business. He said it establishes a policy that employees are not allowed to operate County vehicles when they have alcohol in their system or have used drugs. He said it establishes a procedure that, if a County vehicle is involved in an accident, the accident has to be reported and investigated by the Sheriff’s Department and would require drug testing of the County employee if there is an accident. He said it exempts the Sheriff’s Department as they are a State agency.

# Amendment #2
