The Wicomico County Council met in a Closed Session on Tuesday, July 2, 2024 at 8:34 p.m. in Council
Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland pursuant to General Provisions Article
Section 3-305(b)(8).
In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Shane Baker, Joe
Holloway, James Winn, and Josh Hastings. Shanie Shields was absent.
Present for the second Closed Work Session: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive
Office Associate; Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney; Andrew Illuminati, Esquire; Mr. Paul Wilber,
County Attorney, and Mr. Bunky Luffman, Director of Administration.
The purpose of the second Closed Session was to consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals
about pending or potential litigation to protect the County’s legal position. No formal action or recorded
votes were taken.
On motion by Mr. Baker, seconded by Mr. Merritt, and unanimously approved, the Wicomico County
Council reconvened in Legislative Session at 8:46 p.m.
Resolution No. 82-2024
— To name additional defendants in the oploid lawsuit.
There being no discussion, on motion by Mr. Baker and seconded by Mr. Merritt, Resolution No. 82-
2024 was unanimously approved.
Mr. Benson said, just to be clear, the motion that is being voted on is the one that has Indivior, Mylan
and Zydus and not the one that was in the briefing book that the public may have seen. Mr. Cannon
thanked Mr. Benson for that clarification.
There being no further business, on motion by Mr. Hastings, seconded by Mr. Merritt, and unanimously
approved, the Legislative Session was adjourned at 8:49 p.m.
