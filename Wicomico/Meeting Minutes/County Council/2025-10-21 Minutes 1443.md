The Wicomico County Council met in a Closed Session on Tuesday, October 21, 2025 at 2:10 p.m.
in Councit Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland pursuant to General
Provisions Article Section 3-305(b)(7) and (9}.
In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James
Winn, Shanie Shields, Josh Hastings, and David Ennis.
Present for the Closed Work Session: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive
Office Associate; Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney; Steve Lakin, Assistant Director of
Administration; Paul Wilber, County Attorney; Andrew Illuminati, Esq.; Barry Downey with Smith &
Downey, Cory Huston, and Kaitlin Garvin. The purpose of the Closed Session was to receive legal
advice on the current collective bargaining interpretation issues as they relate to the pension, as
the Council has legal questions concerning the Collective Bargaining Agreement and the
interpretation of the CBA and pension plan, which require advice from legal counsel; and to
discuss matters that relate to negotiations for the Collective Bargaining Agreement, which will have
a direct and material bearing on the conduct of the negotiations, in order to protect the Council’s
collective bargaining strategy. No formal action or recorded votes were taken.
