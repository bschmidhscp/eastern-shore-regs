Public Hearing: Declaring property located at 122 5. Division Street, Salisbury, Maryland, known as the
Downtown Library
— Paul S. Sarbanes Branch, to be Surplus Property.
Mrs. Hurley said the County Executive has proposed declaring the Downtown Library as surplus. She
explained, this property would be exchanged with the State of Maryland University system for property
located at 909 5. Schumaker Drive, which will become the new site for the Library. She said the Library
plans to remain at the Division Street location until renovations at the Schumaker site are complete.

dollar piece of property as surplus to the State for them to let you rent a property for a nominal fee,
which after 30 years you don’t own. He clarified, you have the right to buy, but this is not an exchange.
