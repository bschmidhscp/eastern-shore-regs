and believed MDE acknowledged it was their mistake, working with them on legislation that was

legislation a priority and expected it to go into effect at the very next bill signing.

Ms. Taylor said the recommendation of the Planning Commission was for R10A zoning in the City. The
job of the Planning and Zoning staff of the County is to review annexations based off of State law to
determine if the proposed municipal zoning results in uses that are substantially different than County

a substantial change in the use, and of course the density is greater
