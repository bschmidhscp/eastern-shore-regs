# County Council minutes 2024-04-16

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in Legislative Session on Tuesday, April 16, 2024 at 10:00 a.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shane Baker, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

On motion by Mr. Hastings and seconded by Mr. Merritt, the Consent Agenda consisting of the following items was unanimously approved: 

- Legislative Minutes from April 2, 2024 

- Open Work Session Minutes from March 5, 2024 — Forest Conservation Removal of 0.58 acres on 9115 Old Dagsboro Road, Delmar, Maryland 

- Open Work Session Minutes from March 5, 2024 — Forest Conservation Relocation of 0.41 acres on Mill Pond Land, Salisbury, Maryland 

- Open Work Session Minutes from March 5, 2024 — Capital Improvement Plan for Fiscal Years 2025-2029 

A proclamation was presented recognizing the month of April as Child Abuse Prevention Month. 

A proclamation was presented recognizing the month of April as Sexual Assault Awareness Month. 

A proclamation was presented to recognize Arbor Day in Wicomico County. 

A proclamation was presented to recognize National Trails Day in Wicomico County. 

A proclamation was presented to recognize the month of April as National Volunteer Appreciation Month. 

A proclamation was presented recognizing National Public Safety Telecommunicators Week. 

A proclamation was presented to recognize the Wicomico County Rising Star winner. 

Proclamations were presented to the three Wicomico County 2024-2025 Teacher of the Year finalists. 

A proclamation was presented to the 2024-2025 Wicomico County Teacher of the Year.
