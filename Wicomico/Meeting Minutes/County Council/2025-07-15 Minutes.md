Resolution No. 101-2025 — Amending the FY2025 Operating Budget to reflect an increase for the State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT) Office costs. 

Ms. Pam Oland, Director of Finance, came before Council. 

Mr. Merritt asked Ms. Oland to explain to the public how this occurred. He said apparently, it’s Statewide, not just Wicomico County. 

Ms. Oland explained, we got an email after the start of the fiscal year telling us they underbilled us. She said that was basically the summary, and it said how much it is and that they are going to take it out of the next four quarters that they bill us. She said for our purposes, we set a budget based on an amount that they tell us at the beginning of the year, so in our budget, we had a dollar amount with the new inflator. She explained, remember during the Legislative Session this year, the State decided that we as a County and every county have to cover more of the costs of the State Assessment Office, so we already had an inflated number in our fiscal 26 budget. She said with the fact that we got this after July 1, our budget had already been struck, and it is not an insignificant amount of money to start off the fiscal year in the hole that amount. She said because it blatantly states it in the email that it relates to prior periods, I had a discussion with our auditors and I felt that it was appropriate that it could be something that would be what is called accrued. She said basically, we would put it on the books as a liability in

<!-- page 6 -->
fiscal 2025 because we know about it before our books are closed that we owe this liability related to a prior period. She said as such, we also still had funding left in our contingency account in fiscal 25, so we are requesting that we use those contingency funds. She said there was about $300,000 remaining in contingency, and I have been tracking that all year, and as such, we would like to use the contingency dollar amount to be able to put this on the books. She said it would be on the books during the audit, and then, as we get billed, we wouldn’t take the expense in fiscal 26, we would take it against the expense we already charged in fiscal 25. 

Mr. Merritt said at a recent Zoom meeting with the folks at MACO, they indicated that they would be open to spreading this out over time instead of paying it all at once, to which Ms. Oland responded, well, they are spreading it out over four quarters, but that doesn’t spread it out over time really because that is still within the same fiscal year. She said again, because we have notice of it and because it relates to prior periods, in an accounting purpose, it is a prior expense, and as such, we can take it in fiscal 25 and there are sufficient funds in fiscal 25 to make that accrual with this budget amendment. 

There being no further discussion, on motion by Mr. Merritt and seconded by Mr. Ennis, Resolution No. 101-2025 was unanimously approved.
