Public Hearing: Legislative Bill 2024-07 — An Act to Amend Chapter 225 of the Wicomico County Code titled “Zoning,” Part 8 titled “Special Standards for Particular Uses,” Article XX titled “Uses and Standards for Particular Uses,” Section 225-82.1 titled “Cannabis Businesses” to modify cannabis business zoning regulations in Wicomico County. 

Mr. Benson explained, in 2022, the voters of Maryland legalized the possession and use of cannabis for individuals aged 21 and older. He said the Maryland General Assembly passed Legislation to implement the sale of cannabis in Maryland, and in 2023 Wicomico County passed Legislation to amend the County Zoning Code to address cannabis sale and use as authorized by the General Assembly. He said in April 2024, the General Assembly passed the Cannabis Licensee Locations Restriction Act and in that Act they made changes to what was permitted. He said the purpose of this Bill 2024-07 is to modify the Wicomico County Zoning Code to comply with the new Cannabis Licensee Restrictions Act.

Mrs. Hurley said, per Charter Section 411(d), “Upon veto, the County Executive shall provide in writing a statement of the reasons for the veto. The veto message shall be entered in the journal of the County Council, and not later than at its next Legislative Session day, the County Council may reconsider the Bill. If upon reconsideration at least five members of the County Council vote in the affirmative, the Bill shall be law.” 

Mrs. Hurley said a copy of the Executive’s veto message stating the reasons she is vetoing the Bill was added to the brief book this afternoon and, with Council’s permission, I will enter the veto message into the journal. 

Veto Message: 

Introduction: 

In accordance with Section 411 of the Wicomico County Charter, I am hereby vetoing Legislative Bill 2024-06. My decision is based on several critical concerns which I believe undermine the interests and governance of our county. 

Reasoning: 

1. Overreaching, Targeted, and Reactive Legislation: Legislative Bill 2024-06 represents an overreaching, targeted, and reactive approach to an event that, in reality, caused no issues for the county or the adjacent neighborhood. Throughout the term of the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), my administration and I addressed every concern, from environmental impacts to noise, numerous times. This level of diligence ensured that no negative repercussions were experienced, making this legislation unnecessary and unjustified. 

2. Interference with Executive Authority: The County Executive inherently holds the power to create and execute one-year contracts and agreements. This authority is clearly granted by the Charter of Wicomico County. Legislative Bill 2024-06 potentially interferes with this executive power. undermining the executive branch’s ability to function effectively and efficiently. It is imperative to maintain the balance of power as intended by the Charter. 

3. Impact on Business-Friendly Initiatives: One of my primary missions has been to make Wicomico County more business-friendly. This heavy-handed legislation works counter to that goal. It sends a negative message to current and potential businesses, indicating an unstable and over-regulated environment. Such a perception can deter business investment and growth in our county. 

4. Disruption of Balance of Power: Legislative Bill 2024-06 is yet another attempt by the county council to disrupt the balance of power between the executive and legislative branches. Since the beginning of my term, there has been a consistent theme of power grabs by the council. This bill continues that trend, further straining the cooperative and balanced relationship necessary for effective governance. 

Conclusion: 

For these reasons, I am vetoing Legislative Bill 2024-06. I strongly urge the Council to reconsider their roles and responsibilities as legislators and to respect the separation of powers as outlined in the Charter. Let us work together towards the betterment of Wicomico County, ensuring that our governance is both balanced and effective.
