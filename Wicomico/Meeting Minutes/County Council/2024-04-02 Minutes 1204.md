# County Council minutes 2024-04-02

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in Legislative Session on Tuesday, April 2, 2024 at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shane Baker, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn lngold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

On motion by Mr. Baker and seconded by Mr. Merritt, the Consent Agenda consisting of the following items was unanimously approved: 

- Legislative Minutes from March 19, 2024 

- Open Work Session Minutes from February 20, 2024 — Discussion on Proposed Everside Health Care Center 

- Closed Work Session Minutes from December 5, 2023 — To Consider the Acquisition of Real Property for a Public Purpose and Matters Directly Related Thereto 

- Closed Work Session Minutes from December 19, 2023 - To Consider the Acquisition of Real Property for a Public Purpose and Matters Directly Related Thereto-Health Department 

- Closed Work Session Minutes from December 19, 2023 - To Consider the Acquisition of Real Property for a Public Purpose and Matters Directly Related Thereto-Public Works 

A proclamation was presented to the Wicomico County Department of Social Services to recognize the month of April as Child Abuse Prevention Month. 

A proclamation was presented to the Wicomico County Planning, Zoning, and Community Development Department to recognize the month of April as Fair Housing Month.
