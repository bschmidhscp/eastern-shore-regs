# County Council minutes 2024-06-04

<!-- page 1 -->
The Wicomico County Council met in Special Open Session on Tuesday, June 4, 2024 at 3:30 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland to entertain a motion to go into Closed Session. 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shane Baker, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney. 

Mr. Cannon called the meeting to order. 

There being no further business, on motion by Mr. Merritt, seconded by Mr. Baker, and unanimously approved, the Open Session was adjourned at 3:31 p.m. to go into Closed Sessions pursuant to General Provisions Article Section 3-305(b)(8)(9) to consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals about pending or potential pending litigation to protect the County’s legal position; and to conduct collective bargaining negotiations or consider matters that relate to the negotiations to protect the collective bargaining strategy. 

The Wicomico County Council met in a Closed Session on Tuesday, June 4, 2024 at 3:31 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland pursuant to General Provisions Article Section 3-305(b)(8). 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Shane Baker, Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present for the first Closed Work Session: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate; Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney; Julie Giordano, County Executive; Paul Wilber, County Attorney; Frank McKenzie, GIS Specialist; and Kevin Karpinski, Esquire. 

The purpose of the Closed Session was to consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals about pending or potential pending litigation to protect the County’s legal position. No formal action or recorded votes were taken. 

The Wicomico County Council met in a Closed Session on Tuesday, June 4, 2024 at 3:50 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland pursuant to General Provisions Article Section 3-305(b)(9). 

In attendance: John T. Cannon, Council President; Jeff Merritt, Vice President; Shane Baker, Joe Holloway, James Winn, Shanie Shields, and Josh Hastings. 

Present for the first Closed Work Session: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn Ingold, Executive Office Associate; Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney; Julie Giordano, County Executive; Paul Wilber, County Attorney, and Cheryl Brown, Esquire. 

The purpose of the second Closed Session was to conduct collective bargaining negotiations or consider matters that relate to the negotiations to protect the collective bargaining strategy. No formal action or recorded votes were taken.
