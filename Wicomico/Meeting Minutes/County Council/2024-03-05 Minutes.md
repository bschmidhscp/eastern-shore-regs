The Wicomico County Council met in Legislative Session on Tuesday, March 5, 2024 at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland. 

In attendance: John Cannon, President; James Winn, Shane Baker, Josh Hastings, Joe Holloway and Shanie Shields. Jeff Merritt, Vice President, participated via Zoom. 

Present: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator; Lynn lngold, Executive Office Associate, and Robert A. Benson, Council Attorney.
