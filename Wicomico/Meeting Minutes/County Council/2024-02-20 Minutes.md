# Robert Benson, Esg., Council Attorney 

Introduction: Legislative Bill No. 2024-01 — An Act to Amend Chapter 90 of the Wicomico County Code titled “Tax Exemptions,” Section 90-9, to allow a property tax credit to be granted to an Auxiliary of a Bona Fide Volunteer Fire Company, and to provide that only one tax credit is allowed per dwelling. Mr. Benson said the Council will remember that a prior Bill was adopted in December that granted the tax exemption to volunteer fire companies, but an issue arose after that adoption as to whether or not auxiliaries were considered members of volunteer fire departments. He said, in looking at that, I talked to two or three different fire departments and the answers I got varied. He said some of the fire departments said the auxiliaries were considered members of the fire department, in which case they would be granted the exemption anyway. He clarified, nobody said they weren’t, but some kind of qualified the answer, so the purpose of this is to include auxiliaries of volunteer fire departments as members who would be entitled to take the tax exemption. He clarified, they have to meet the same qualifications that the fire department members do. 

Mr. Benson said the secondary part of this is to make clear that the Council’s intent is that there would only be one credit per dwelling, so if you had two members who both qualified, they would only get one credit and would not be able to add two together and get two credits.

Mr. Benson concluded, this simply acknowledges that auxiliaries are included within volunteer fire departments that are entitled to the exemption and it makes clear that the exemption is one per dwelling regardless of the number of people who might qualify at that dwelling.
