### AGENDA #2 

#WA-2405 - Special Exception - Singlewide Manufactured Home for Mary Kathleen Almony - Property located south of Willards, at 6810 Bent Pine Road in the A-1 Zoning District. M-42, G-3, P-154, L-1 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the first case. 

The applicant, Ms. Mary Kathleen Almony, at 6810 Bent Pine Road, Willards, MD, approached the table. 

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report; the report and the related exhibits were entered into the record. Mr. Meadows stated that the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with eight (8) conditions of approval. 

Mr. David Honick, Attorney at Law, at 1002 Eastern Shore Drive, Salisbury, MD, approached the table. Mr. Honick identified the individual he represented as Mr. Henry Martel with My Sparks, LLC. Mr. Honick was present to discuss why this Special Exception should not be approved. His reasons were as follows: 

1. The property is zoned as Agricultural, and the land should be maintained for sustainable agricultural activity. 

2. There is an unpermitted storage unit next to a Public Drainage Association (“PDA”) ditch; therefore, the applicant is not deserving of the Special Exception. 

3. Agriculture zones are protected by Maryland's Right-to-Farm laws, which is a defense to nuisance suits. A residential development may disrupt agriculture before the home has even been placed in its proper location on the lot. 

4. The proposed structure is likely to lessen the value of surrounding properties, which is not in the best interest of the neighboring residents.
