# Wicomico County Board of Appeals June 27, 2024 

The regular Wicomico County Board of Appeals meeting was held on Thursday, June 27, 2024, in the Council Chambers, Third Floor, Government Office Building at 6:00 P.M. with the following persons in attendance. 

### BOARD MEMBERS: 

Chairman - J. Phillips Wright, Jr. David Ennis Tammy Adkins Jim Morris Michael Hoy 

PLANNING STAFF: 

Andrew Illuminati - County Attorney Janae Merchant - Recording Secretary 

The meeting was called to order at 6:00 P.M. by Chairman J. Phillips Wright, Jr. Chairman Wright reviewed the Emergency Evacuation Statement. Chairman Wright introduced the Planning Staff and Board Members. 

MINUTES - AGENDA #1 

Draft minutes of Thursday, April 25, 2024 meeting were placed on the table for approval. 

Upon a motion by Mr. David Ennis seconded by Mr. Michael Hoy, with all members eligible to vote, the Board unanimously approved the April 25, 2024 minutes as written. 

Chairman Wright requested everyone who planned to testify before the Board to stand and be sworn in or affirmed; Mr. Andrew Illuminati administered the Oath. Chairman Wright instructed the audience when the applicant's case is called the applicant should come

### AGENDA #2 

#WA-2404 - Special Exception - Accessory Apartment - Property located east of Salisbury, at 5721 Argyle Drive in the A-1 Zoning District. M-50, G-2, P-98, L-10D 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the first case. 

The applicant, Ms. Bonnie Sue Smith-Hasson at 5721 Argyle Drive, Salisbury, MD, approached the table. 

Ms. Janae Merchant presented the Staff Report; the report and the related exhibits were entered into the record. Ms. Merchant stated Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with six (6) conditions of approval. 

The Applicant and the Board discussed the layout of the proposed additions. Ms. Smith-Hasson explained it would be for her daughter and family and an in-law suite for her. 

Board Member Mr. Dave Ennis offered a MOTION to approve the applicant’s request for a Special Exception - Accessory Apartment. The motion was SECONDED by Mr. Jim Morris. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the Special Exception - Accessory Apartment. 

### AGENDA #3 

Administrative Matter - Mark Cropper, in accordance with Section 225-157-E of the Wicomico County Code, requests a one (1) year extension to exercise the Board’s approval in case #WA2312 (Royal Farms/Mardela Springs). M-8, G-21, P-441, L-1 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the second case. 

Mr. Illuminati presented the Staff Report and entered the related exhibits into the record. The applicant provided a detailed schedule of submittals and approvals to/from various regulatory agencies and departments. Mr. Illuminati stated the Staff recommended approval of a one-year extension to acquire a building permit. This approval would modify condition number one (1) only; all other conditions are to remain in full force and effect. 

Mr. Mark Cropper, Esq. at 6200 Coastal Highway, Suite 200, Ocean City, MD, and Mr. Jeff Harmon with Becker Morgan at 312 W. Main Street, Suite 300, Salisbury, MD approached the table. 

The Board, Mr. Cropper, and Mr. Harmon discussed what processes had been completed to date and what was still needed. 

Board Member Mr. David Ennis offered a MOTION to approve the request for a one (1) year extension. The motion was SECONDED by Mr. Jim Morris. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the request.

### AGENDA #4 

Administrative Matter - Mark Cropper, in accordance with Section 225-157-E of the Wicomico County Code, requests a one (1) year extension to secure bonding as described in Conditions of Approval (Nos. 1 & 6) in case #WA-2309. M-59, G-9, P-25 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the third case. 

Mr. Illuminati presented the Staff Report and entered the related exhibits into the record. He stated the Staff recommended approval of a one-year extension to acquire a performance bond or other such guaranty to exercise the original approval for the Special Exception. This approval would modify condition number one (1) only; all other conditions are to remain in full force and effect. 

Mr. Cropper remained at the table, he agreed with the modified condition. The Board and Mr. Cropper discussed the difficulty of securing a bond. 

Mr. Illuminati requested a motion to amend condition one (1) to allow this special exception privately owned and operated utility (2MW solar facility) - shall be exercised within two (2) years from the original date of issuance. Condition #6 shall be satisfied on or before May 30, 2025. All other conditions remain unchanged. Board Member Mr. David Ennis offered a MOTION to approve the request for a one (1) year extension. The motion was SECONDED by Ms. Tammy Adkins. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the request. 

There being no further business to come before the Board, the meeting adjourned at 6:52 P.M.
