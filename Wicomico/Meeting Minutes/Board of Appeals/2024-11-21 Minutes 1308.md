## AGENDA #2 

#WA-2411 - Cecilia Wolf - Special Exception - Veterinary Clinic - Property owned by April Holloway and located south of Pittsville at 7040 Morris Road in the A-1 Zoning District. M-32, G-20, P-304, L-1AD 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the first case. 

Chairman Turner instructed the audience that when the applicant's case is called, the applicant should come forward, state their name, provide a mailing address, and state whether the individual has been sworn in or affirmed. 

Dr. Cecilia Wolf, 12444 Murray Road, Whaleyville, MD, and Mr. Christian Larson, 2505 Garsden Court, Phoenix, MD, approached the table. 

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report; the report and the related exhibits were entered into the record. Mr. Meadows stated that the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with seven (7) conditions of approval. 

Dr. Wolf came before the Board of Appeals to request a Special Exception to allow the use of a veterinary clinic on the property located at 7040 Morris Road, Pittsville, MD. The name of the business would be The Guardian Vet Group. She will utilize the existing shell and footprint to include a general veterinary practice and a 24-hour Critical Care Center. The existing signage will be upgraded. Dr. Wolf currently works on small and exotic animals. She would need to purchase a crane before working on large animals. 

Mr. Ron Parker, 7044 Morris Road, Pittsville, MD, approached the table. Mr. Parker owns the market next to the clinic. His first concern was whether clients' large dogs would be kept on a leash. Dr. Wolf indicated that their policy was to keep dogs on leashes and cats/exotics in cages. Mr. Parker's second concern was the gate utilized by both businesses. Dr. Wolf reassured Mr. Parker she would have security set up. They will store CCTV for many days with high resolution, night vision, spotlights, and vestibules. She hoped the safety would extend to his property as well. 

Ms. Judy Robinson, 7036 Morris Road, Pittsville, MD, was also concerned about dogs arriving at the clinic without a leash or hopping out of a car before a leash could be put on them. She’s worried about her dog and cats. Ms. Adkins recommended putting information on their webpage stating that customers must have their pets on a leash. Dr. Wolf advised that if customers did not have a leash, they should contact the clinic before coming in, and they will be met by a Customer Service Representative who will bring out a leash. 

Board Member Mr. David Ennis made a MOTION to approve the applicant’s request for a Special Exception - Veterinary Clinic. Mr. Michael Hoy seconded the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the Special Exception - Veterinary Clinic. Chairman Turner encouraged Dr. Wolf to work with her neighbors to develop solutions to their concerns.

AGENDA #3 

#WA-2412 - Chimere Toadvine - Special Exception - Small Contracting Business (Telecom Cable Company) - Property located south of Parsonsburg at 6088 Forest Grove Road in the A-1 Zoning District and the A-2 Airport Overlay District. M-40, G-21, P-86 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the second case. 

Ms. Chimere Toadvine, 1712 Crestwood Circle, Salisbury, MD, approached the table. 

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report; the report and the related exhibits were entered into the record. Mr. Meadows stated that the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with seven (7) conditions of approval. 

Ms. Toadvine requested approval for a telecom cable company at 6088 Forest Grove Road in Parsonsburg, MD. The company has existed for approximately three (3) years, due to COVID-19 in 2021, some things were overlooked, and they are just coming before the Board for approval. 

Ms. Ann Taylor, 6171 Forest Grove Road, approached the table. Ms. Taylor opposed the Small Contracting Business. Her concerns were the number of employees and whether the septic system was adequate for 20 employees. She also questioned the increase in the neighbor's taxes and whether the building had a fire alarm. 

Mr. Jeff Taylor, 6171 Forest Grove Road, approached the table. Mr. Taylor opposed the Small Contracting Business. His concern was the trucks turning around in the road instead of on her property. The road is not very wide, so it would be safer for the drivers to pull in on the property, unload, and then go back onto the road. 

Mr. Joe Holloway, 32928 Shavox Road, approached the table and expressed concerns from some of the neighbors who did not attend the meeting. His concerns were who determined a minor change vs. a major change. The traffic on the road has increased. He does not believe this type of business should be in a rural area. 

Chairman Turner added that he would like to add an additional condition to limit the business's further growth. He recommended that any unloading be done off Forest Grove Road and on her property. 

Mr. Morris inquired about the number and types of trucks based on the property. Ms. Toadvine responded, saying there are two (2) Ford transit vehicles, four (4) Ford F350 vans with buckets, four (4) Dodge Ram 4500 with a bucket and bed on the back, a Ford F450, and another Ford considereda splicer vehicle.

After further discussions on modifying conditions two (2) and three (3) and adding a condition eight (8), Ms. Tammy Adkins entered a motion to change condition number two (2) by deleting the last two (2) sentences which read “Minor changes in the plans may be approved by the Zoning Administrator. Major changes may require the Board’s approval by public hearing.” Modify condition three (3) to read, “Any changes in the nature of the use, size or number of buildings related to the business, or similar conditions shall require the Board’s approval by public hearing, except as allowed for in Condition #2.” Modifying condition seven (7) to read, “There shall be no parking of company or delivery vehicles along Forest Grove Road.” Finally, move the original seven (7) to a new condition number eight (8). Mr. David Ennis seconded the suggested changes to the conditions. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the changes to the conditions. 

Board Member Ms. Tammy Adkins made a MOTION to approve the applicant’s request for a Special Exception—Small Contracting Business, accepting the modifications of the previously approved conditions. Mr. Michael Hoy seconded the MOTION. Mr. David Ennis opposed the applicant's request. With all other members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the Special Exception—Small Contracting Business with the amended suggested conditions. 

With no further business before the Board, the meeting adjourned at 8:07 P.M. This is a summary of the proceedings of this meeting. Detailed information is in the permanent files of each case as presented and filed in the Wicomico County Department of Planning, Zoning, and Community Development Office.
