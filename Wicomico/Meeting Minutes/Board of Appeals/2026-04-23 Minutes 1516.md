###### AGENDA #2 

#WA-2610 - Riley White, in accordance with Section 225-156 of the Wicomico County Zoning Code, requests a 10-foot front setback variance (Code requires a 50-foot setback - 40-foot setback proposed) for a new double-wide manufactured home on property located south of Willards, at 6625 Bent Pine Road in the A-1 Zoning District. M-42, G-9, P-126 (Clark Meadows) 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the case. 

Ms. Riley White, the Applicant, and Mr. Brent White, her father, approached the table. 

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant requested the Board’s approval to place a new doublewide manufactured home on her property at 6625 Bent Pine Road. The proposed location for the home is partially within the required setback. This 1-acre property is improved with a detached garage and shed that were left by the previous owners. The property has about 156 © feet of frontage along the easterly side of Bent Pine Road. 

Staff's evaluation identified conditions unique to this property and led to a determination that the proposed location for the new home meets the burden of proof for granting the requested variance. Staff recommended the Board grant the variance, subject to the three (3) conditions. 

After a brief discussion between the Board members and Ms. and Mr. White, Board Member Mr. William “Bill” Turner offered a MOTION to approve the Applicant’s request for a 10-foot Setback Variance and adopt Staff's evaluation of the Report as the Findings of Fact by this Board. The evidence and testimony support the conclusion that the requested variance will provide relief from an unreasonable hardship without substantial injury to the public health, safety, or general welfare. This approval shall be subject to the Conditions of Approval, numbers 1 thru 3. The motion was SECONDED by Mr. Jim Morris. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the Variance, subject to three (3) conditions of approval.
