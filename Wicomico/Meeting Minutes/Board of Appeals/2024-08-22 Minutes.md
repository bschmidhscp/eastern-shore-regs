### AGENDA #2 

#WA-2406- Special Exception - Setback Variance for Cheyenne Scearce - Property located east of Fruitland at 4664 Straw Ridge Lane in the R-20 Residential Zoning District. M-48, G- 22, P-456, L-1A 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the first case. 

The applicant, Mr. Cheyenne Scearce, Sr., at 4664 Straw Ridge Lane, Salisbury, MD, approached the table. Mr. Scearce requested a 22 ft. setback variance to construct a 1600 sq. ft. accessory building. 

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report; the report and the related exhibits were entered into the record. Mr. Meadows stated that the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with three (3) conditions of approval. 

Mr. Meadows mentioned there were several conditions on the property that, when combined, caused an undue hardship on the owner's ability to improve it. He said that when the setbacks and septic area are totaled, the buildable area is reduced to approximately 2000 sq. ft., about 19% of the property. Chairman Wright agreed it was logical to grant him his request for the 22 ft. setback. 

Board Member Mr. Dave Ennis made a MOTION to approve the applicant’s request for a Special Exception—Setback Variance. Mr. Bill Turner seconded the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the Special Exception—Setback Variance. 

Chairman Wright requested a recess from 6:16 p.m. - 6:24 p.m.

### AGENDA #3 

#WA-2407 - Special Exception - Wireless Telecommunication Facility for Network Towers, LLC - Property located northwest of Whitehaven at 22659 Capitola Road in the A-1 Zoning District. M-64, G-4, P-39 

Mr. David Ennis recused himself from this case; he is related to the applicant. 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the second case. 

Mr. Mark Cropper, attorney for Network Towers, LLC, at 6200 Coastal Highway, Ocean City, MD; Mr. William Turner, representing Tidewater Properties Appraisers, at 1102 Butterworth Court, Stevensville, MD; and Mr. Jim Golden, representing Network Towers, LLC, at 1 Wainwright Drive, Annapolis, MD approached the table. The applicant requested establishing a network tower site at 22659 Capitola Road, Tyaskin, MD (MD Rt. 352). Verizon Wireless would be the first wireless provider.

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report; the report and the related exhibits were entered into the record. Mr. Meadows stated that the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with eight (8) conditions of approval. 

Mr. Golden explained the benefits of having the tower installed: 

- e Provide overall quality of life by providing connectivity; e Provide students access to online educational opportunities; e Allow employees to work from home; 

- e Provide the infrastructure necessary for other wireless carriers to expand their networks; and 

- e Allow emergency workers operating in the area the ability to communicate more effectively. 

Mr. Cropper explained that the Wicomico County Code requires a 1,000-foot separation between a proposed tower and any abutting/adjacent dwelling. However, the applicant has a 446.2-foot proposed separation. Mr. Golden explained the difficulties of meeting the code requirement. The property has wetlands and a cemetery, and in an attempt to get as far away from the adjacent property line as possible, this was the farthest they could get. He added the towers are designed to fold upon themselves in the unlikely event of a tower failure. 

Mr. Nathan Long, an attorney with Laws, Insley, and Benson, who represented Mr. Linford Martin, who owns the property next to the potential site, approached the table. Mr. Long said that Mr. Martin was not opposed to the cell tower, but they believe a case was not presented today to support the special exception to build it 446 feet away from his property. Mr. Illuminati swore in Mr. Ed Choquette, who resides at 22658 Capitola Road. Mr. Choquette approached the table. His only objection is that he would be able to see quite a bit of the tower outside his front door once it was built. He requested that trees be planted across the front of his property to block the view of the tower. 

Chairman Wright requested a recess from 7:33 p.m. - 7:39 p.m. 

Chairman Wright reconvened the Wicomico County Board of Appeals’ regular monthly meeting. He announced that Mr. Cropper had the floor. Mr. Cropper respectfully requested a 30-day continuance of this matter to examine the site conditions and determine whether it is possible to move this site. He had spoken with Mr. Long, and he agreed to the continuance. Chairman Wright closed off additional testimony for this case, only allowing discussions between Mr. Cropper and Mr. Long. 

Board Member Mr. Bill Turner made a MOTION to grant Mr. Cropper's request for a 30day continuance. Mr. Michael Hoy seconded the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the 30-day continuance. 

With no further business before the Board, the meeting adjourned at 7:43 P.M. 

This is a summary of the proceedings of this meeting. Detailed information is in the permanent files of each case as presented and filed in the Wicomico County Department of Planning, Zoning, and Community Development Office.
