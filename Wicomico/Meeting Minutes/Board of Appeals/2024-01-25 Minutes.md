AGENDA #2 

#WA-9716-24A - Modification of Nonconforming Special Exception - Individual Commercial Establishment (Bennett Farms) - Tilghman Markets, LLC - Property located northwest of Salisbury, at 27720 Ocean Gateway in the A-1 Agricultural - Rural Zoning District. M-28, G-3, P-7 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the first case. 

The applicant, Ms. Elizabeth Tilghman at 21278 River Hill Lane, Tyaskin MD, and Ms. Erica Tilghman at 314 Shipyard Drive, Cambridge, MD, approached the table. 

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report and entered the related exhibits into the record. Mr. Meadows stated Staff recommended approval of the Modification of Nonconforming Special Exception with eight (8) conditions of approval. 

Ms. Elizabeth Tilghman said the State Highway Administration (“SHA”) requested a 400 ft. deceleration lane. She explained the types of products they intend to sell will be very similar to what Bennett Farms Garden Center sold, produce, grocery items, decorative plants, and décor. They also purchased the adjoining property to give extra space to display plant materials and allow for overflow parking. 

Board Member Mr. William “Bill” Turner offered a MOTION to approve the applicant’s request for a Modification of Nonconforming Special Exception - Individual Commercial Establishment. The motion was SECONDED by Mr. Dave Ennis. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the Modification of Nonconforming Special Exception - Individual Commercial Establishment.

Mr. Christopher Smith, attorney who represented Wicomico County during the Civil prosecutions, provided the rulings or status of the cases brought against Ms. Carter. 

Mr. Turner stated the purpose of the hearing was to determine if Ms. Carter would or would not receive permission to operate a commercial kennel at this facility. He also said it was not in the Board’s purview to decide if the dogs stayed or not. 

Board Member Mr. Bill Turner offered a MOTION to deny the request of a Special Exception for a Commercial Kennel. The evidence presented tonight contradicts and goes against the staff's evaluation specifically number five (5) that the proposed use has been designed to minimize possible adverse effects on adjacent properties or on the immediately surrounding areas presented tonight. ##### AGENDA #4 

Administrative Matter - #WA-2303 - (Privately Owned and Operated Utility - Solar) Elk Development, LLC, requests a 1-year extension to exercise the Board’s approval for the Special Exception - Property located at 10070 Sharptown Road, M-8, G-11, P-7 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the third case. 

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report and entered the related exhibits into the record. Mr. Meadows stated Staff recommended approval of the one (1) year extension to exercise the Board’s approval - Special Exception [Privately Owned & Operated Utility (Solar)] subject to all conditions mentioned in January 2023 and modification of number one (1) to read the Special Exception privately owned and operated utility for a 2-megawatt solar facility shall be exercised by the issuance of a building permit on, or before, January 26, 2025. 

Board Member Mr. Dave Ennis offered a MOTION to approve the applicant's request for one (1) year extension to exercise the Board’s approval - Special Exception [Privately Owned & Operated Utility (Solar).] The motion was SECONDED by Mr. Michael Hoy. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the one (1) year extension to exercise the Board’s approval - Special Exception [Privately Owned & Operated Utility (Solar).]. 

It was announced there will be no Board of Appeals meeting in February and there are some availability issues for the March meeting. It was requested that everyone be flexible with dates in March and we would provide a couple of date options in a few weeks for March’s meeting. 

There being no further business to come before the Board, the meeting adjourned at 10:13 P.M.
