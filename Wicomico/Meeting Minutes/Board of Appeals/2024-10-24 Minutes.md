AGENDA #3 

#WA-2408 - Clean Power Wash, LLC requests a Special Exception - Small Contracting Business (Power Wash & Lighting Contractor) on property located northeast of Hebron, at 27000 Ocean Gateway in the A-1 Zoning District. M-19, G-13, P-173 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the second case. 

Mr. Rob Anderson, at 6822 Whetstone Drive, Hebron, MD, approached the table. 

The applicant requested establishing a small contracting business (power washing and lighting company) at 27000 Ocean Gateway. 

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report; the report and the related exhibits were entered into the record. Mr. Meadows stated that the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with seven (7) conditions of approval. 

Mr. Bill Turner asked about the purpose of the additional proposed buildings. Mr. Anderson said the smaller building will store three (3) vehicles to keep them out of the winter elements. He plans to turn the kitchen area into a conference room for meetings and training. The buildings will be similar in style to the existing facility, and the color scheme will be red and black. 

Mr. Michael Hoy inquired about the amount of traffic anticipated for this business. Mr. Anderson stated that the building will not have retail foot traffic. The technicians will arrive for work, get in a company truck, and complete their work. He anticipates 20 - 30 vehicles in and out every day. 

Board Member Ms. Tammy Adkins made a MOTION to approve the applicant’s request for a Special Exception—Small Contracting Business. Mr. Bill Turner seconded the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the Special Exception— Small Contracting Business. 

Mr. Clark Meadowns announced cases #WA-2409 (Salisbury Riverside West Solar, LLC) and #WA-2410 (Salisbury Cross West Solar, LLC) have been postponed at the applicant's request. All interested parties contacting us before the meeting were notified of the postponements. The agenda was revised and published on the County website. Notification will be sent to the applicant stating the postponement was recognized to not exceed beyond the February 2025 Board of Appeals meeting. All interested parties, including those who signed up at the meeting, will also be included in that notification. Notices will need to be readvertised if the applicant requests that the Board hear these projects. Board Member Mr. Bill Turner made a MOTION to approve postponing both cases. Ms. Tammy Adkins seconded the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the postponement.
