##### AGENDA #3 

#WA-2510 - Seth Glinski, in accordance with Section 225-127-A (2) of the Wicomico County Zoning Code, requests a Special Exception - 32 sq. ft. wall sign on property owned by R&A Financial Enterprises and located in Parsonsburg, at 32974 Old Ocean City Road in the Village Conservation Zoning District. M-31, G-21, P-194 (Clark Meadows) 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the first case. 

Mr. Seth Glinski at 29101 Santa Fe Drive, Salisbury, Maryland, approached the table. 

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant seeks the Board’s approval to install a 32-square-foot front elevation sign on the building where his automotive repair business is located. 

Based on evidence acquired from the Applicant and as set forth in the Staff Report, the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with four (4) conditions of approval. 

Mr. Turner inquired if the sign would be illuminated. Mr. Glinski stated the sign would be on a daylight sensor, coming on when the sun sets. There will be no glare to impact traffic. 

Board Member Ms. Tammy Adkins made a MOTION to approve the applicant’s request for a Special Exception. Mr. Michael Hoy seconded the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the Special Exception - a 32-square-foot on-site eesign. eee eee

###### AGENDA #4 

#WA-2511 - David Guy, in accordance with Section 225-67 of the Wicomico County Zoning Code, requests a Special Exception - Type III home-based business (Automotive Repair), on property located north of Willards, at 9360 Bethel Road in the A-1 Zoning District. M-24, G-2, P-88, L-2 (Clark Meadows) 

Mr. Illuminati called the second case. 

Mr. David Guy, of 9360 Bethel Road, Willards, Maryland, approached the table. 

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant seeks the Board’s approval to operate an automotive repair business from a detached shop building located on the Bethel Road property. 

Based on evidence acquired from the Applicant and as set forth in the Staff Report, the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with nine (9) conditions of approval. 

Ms. Adkins recommended that Mr. Guy trim the trees at the end of his driveway for better visibility. Mr. Guy agreed with her suggestion. 

Mr. Turner inquired about the storage of vehicles. After a discussion with the Board members and Mr. Guy, it was decided to create an additional condition. 

Board Member Mr. Bill Turner offered a MOTION to add an additional condition of approval to the original nine (9) conditions, “The overnight parking/storage of vehicles under repair is limited to no more than three (3) vehicles (outside the confines of the garage).” The motion was SECONDED by Tammy Adkins and duly carried. The revised conditions of approval were adopted. 

Mr. Bill Turner made a MOTION to approve the applicant's request for Special Exception - Type Ill Home-Based Business (Automotive Repair). Mr. Jim Morris SECONDED the motion. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the Special Exception with the amended conditions of approval numbers one (1) through ten (10).
