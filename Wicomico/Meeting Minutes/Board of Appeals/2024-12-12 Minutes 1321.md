## AGENDA #2 

ADMINISTRATIVE MATTER: #WA-2315 - Econox Renewables, Inc. - Special Exception - Minor Solar - northeast corner of Hobbs Road at Mt. Hermon Road. M-39, G-21, P-329 

Mr. Clark Meadows called the first case. 

Mr. Clark Meadows explained the purpose of the request. Econox Renewables, Inc. requested approval for a one (1) year extension to exercise the Board's approval for a Special Exception. The Staff recommended the Board accept the Applicant’s request and grant an additional year to acquire a building permit to exercise the original approval for the Special Exception 

Based on evidence acquired from the Applicant and as set forth in the Staff Report, the Board finds that the Applicant has initiated and completed tasks in a timely manner that demonstrate their due diligence in this matter 

Board Member William “Bill” Turner offered a MOTION to grant the one (1) year extension for the Special Exception. The motion was SECONDED by David Ennis. With Tammy Adkins voting AYE, Jim Morris voting AYE, and Chairman Wright voting AYE, the Board approved the one (1) year extension for the Special Exception with the original nine (9) conditions of approval and a modification to number one (1). 

The modification read as follows: “This Special Exception [Minor Solar (not to exceed 2MW)] shall be exercised by the issuance of a Wicomico County building permit within two (2) years from the date of approval. Prior to the issuance of a building permit (that being on, or before, December 4, 2025), the Applicant shall comply with the terms and conditions set forth in Section 225-115.2-F (Decommission) of the Wicomico County Code.” 

With no further business before the Board, the meeting adjourned at 6:19 P.M. 

This is a summary of the proceedings of this meeting. Detailed information is in the permanent files of each case as presented and filed in the Wicomico County Department of Planning, Zoning, and Community Development Office.
