## AGENDA #2 

#WA-2604 - Darin and Tina Kovach, in accordance with Section 225-156 and 225-70-D of the Wicomico County Zoning Code, request a 26-foot setback variance (plat of record requires a 50-foot setback – 24-foot setback proposed) for an addition to the existing residence and a new detached garage. The property is located east of Delmar, at 32125 Melson Road in the A-1 Zoning District. M-21, G-6, P-150, L-1B (Clark Meadows) 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the case. 

Mr. Darin Kovach, at 32125 Melson Road, Delmar, Maryland, approached the table. 

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant requested to construct (1) a 24’ x 32’ addition to his existing home and (2) a detached garage on his property. The proposed locations for the addition and garage are in the easterly part of the lot and extend into the platted setback. Should the Board approve this request, the dwelling and accessory building would be set back 24 ft. from the property line (approximately 36 ft. from the edge of paving) along Hampton Court.  As proposed, the improvements will require the Board’s issuance of a 26-ft setback variance for each project. 

Staff indicated that the Applicant has not met the criteria for approval of the proposed setbacks and recommended that the Board deny the request. 

Mr. Kovach explained that he cannot build on the right side of his house because it would be on the property line. If he were to build behind the house, he would have to cut back three to four feet of his land. This would mean installing a retaining wall or building the garage up by bringing in extra dirt, which would increase stormwater runoff that flows toward his house and other areas. This is why he needs to go off of Hampton Court; to build on that side, he needs the setback as much as possible. 

After a discussion between the Board members and Mr. Kovach, Board Member Ms. Tammy Adkins offered a MOTION to deny the Applicant’s request. The motion was SECONDED by Mr. Jim Morris. The motion was followed by Mr. Michael Hoy, who voted AYE , Mr. William “Bill” Turner, who voted AYE , and Chairman Wright, who also voted AYE , to approve the motion to deny the 26-foot setback Variance and a new detached garage.

## AGENDA #3 

#WA-2605 - Lawrence J. Bonneville, in accordance with Section 225-67 of the Wicomico County Zoning Code, requests a Special Exception – Type II Home-Based Business (canine boarding and day care facility) on property located west of Salisbury, at 6359 White Cove Drive in the R-20 Residential Zoning District. M-37, G-16, P-434, L-6A (Clark Meadows) 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the case. 

Mr. Lawrence Bonneville, at 6359 White Cove Drive, Salisbury, Maryland, approached the table. 

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant requested the Board’s approval for a canine boarding and day care facility to be operated from his home and a detached accessory building located on the White Cove Drive property. The requested use is classified as a Type II Home-based Business and requires the Board's review and approval. 

In consideration of the Staff’s evaluation, the Applicant has not satisfied the criteria for the approval of this Special Exception. Staff recommended that the Board deny the request. 

Mr. Bonneville explained his passion for dogs and his desire to start a home-based business so his wife could stay home and start a family. They would only take in eight (8) dogs since the County deems a kennel to be ten (10) or more dogs. He would like to give the dogs a better life than being crated all day while their owners were at work. He also provided a list of his neighbors and their addresses who support his business desire. This was marked as Exhibit C for the record. 

<u>Mr. Paul Bradshaw</u> (28157 Brockhampton Court, Salisbury, MD) came to the table to speak. Mr. Bradshaw noted that the Applicant has three (3) dogs of his own, so the total number of dogs on the property would be eleven (11) dogs. He asked Mr. Bonneville the size of the building he wished to build. He responded that he would like to build a 14’ x 40'. 

<u>Mr. Michael King</u> (6350 White Cove Drive, Salisbury, MD) came to the table to speak. Mr. King supports Mr. Bonneville’s request. He informed the Board that people in the neighborhood, as well as Mr. Bonneville, take very good care of their dogs. They walk them on leashes and maintain good control. 

Mr. Illumionati clarified that the County defines a kennel as a place where ten (10) or more dogs are kept for boarding, breeding, training, selling, exhibiting, or raising. Therefore, Mr. Bonneville’s three (3) count towards raising, and he could only have up to six (6) additional dogs in his business. 

After a discussion between the Board members and Mr. Bonneville, Board Member Mr. William “Bill” Turner offered a MOTION to deny the Applicant’s request. The motion was SECONDED by Ms. Tammy Adkins. The motion was followed by Mr. Michael Hoy, who voted

## AGENDA #4 

#WA-2606 - S.W. Bloyd Properties, LLC, in accordance with Section 225-67 of the Wicomico County Zoning Code, requests a Special Exception – Group Home (more than eight (8) persons) on property located south of Willards and binding upon the northerly side of Asherwood Branch Road in the A-1 Zoning District. M-42, G-3, P-97, L-7 (Clark Meadows) 

Mr. Illuminati called the case. 

Mr. Brock Parker with Parker & Associates, Mr. Steven Bloyd, owner of the property, and Mr. Seth Underwood, the contractor to build the new facility, approached the table. 

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant requested the Board’s approval to establish an assisted living facility at the referenced location. This unimproved 16-acre lot is currently tilled farmland. The bulk of the property sits back approximately 900 ft. from Asherwood Branch Road. This request regards the development of Lot #7. 

Staff recommended that the Board grant this Special Exception subject to eight (8) conditions. 

Mr. Bloyd stated he currently operates Chesapeake Manor Assisted Living. They have been in existence for 14 to 15 years and are at capacity. In this new facility, they anticipate having 16 to 18 staff members and accommodating 16 residents. Mr. Bloyd also has a facility in Ocean Pines, one of which is an assisted living facility and the other is a memory care facility; both are at capacity. There is a tremendous need for a memory care facility, and this is what Mr. Bloyd would like to build in this area. 

Mr. Bloyd introduced Mr. Seth Underwood. Mr. Underwood is the contractor to build this project. He has built the additions on Chesapeake Manor. He is also an owner-operator of Chesapeake Cottage, just outside of Salisbury, off of St. Luke's Road. 

Mr. Parker mentioned that the modified building plans were submitted to Public Works, and they have fully approved the drawings. If the Board were to approve this project, they are in a position to start construction immediately. 

After a discussion between the Board members and Mr. Bloyd, Board Member Mr. Jim Morris offered a MOTION to grant the Special Exception – Group Home. Mr. Michael Hoy seconded the MOTION . With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the Special Exception, subject to eight (8) conditions of approval.
