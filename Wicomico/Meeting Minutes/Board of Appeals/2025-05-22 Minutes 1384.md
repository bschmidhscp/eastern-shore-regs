AGENDA #2

#WA-2508 - Hugh Cropper IV, on behalf of Jeremiah & Cayla Campbell, in accordance with
Section 125-35 of the Wicomico County Critical Area Resource Protection, requests a Buffer
Variance located at 3039 Whitehaven Road, Tyaskin, MD. The property is located 0.4 miles
south of the intersection of Capitola Road and Whitehaven Road, and 0.4 miles north of the
intersection of Mezick Road and Whitehaven Road. The subject property is in the A-1 Zoning
District as well as within the Resource Conservation Area of the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area.
M-65, G-8, P-162 (Colin Harrison)

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the first case.

Ms. Kristina Watkowski at 9927 Stephen Decatur Highway, Suite F-12 in Ocean City,
MD (standing in for Mr. Hugh Cropper), Mr. Chris McCabe at P.O. Box 66 in Fruitland, MD, Mr.
Brock Parker with Parker and Associates, and Mr. Colin Harrison approached the table.

Mr. Colin Harrison presented the Staff Report; the report and related exhibits were
entered into the record. Mr. and Mrs. Campbell are requesting a Buffer Variance for a
proposed paved driveway from the requirements established in Chapter 125 of the Wicomico
County Code entitled “Critical Area Resource Protection.” The request is for an improved
driveway resulting in impacts of 1,031.08 sq. ft. of permanent lot coverage and 4,797.53 sq.
ft. of temporary disturbance in the Buffer. Section 125-9 of the County Code prohibits the
construction of new roads within the Buffer that are not water-dependent. The proposed lot
coverage in the Buffer and expanded Buffer requires a Variance. No tree or vegetation clearing
is proposed as part of this application. The applicant has proposed the minimum disturbance
necessary in the Buffer to access the proposed site from the existing unimproved driveway.

The Chesapeake Bay Critical Area Commission staff opposed the Variance request as
stated in their comment letter dated May 12, 2025. However, based on evidence acquired

AGENDA #3

#WA-2509 - Salisbury Cross West Solar, LLC, in accordance with Section 225-67 of the
Wicomico County Zoning Code, requests a Special Exception - Minor Solar on property owned
by TWD, LLC, and located east of Fruitland, binding upon the southeasterly side of Nutters
Cross Road, about 3/10 of a mile northwest of its intersection with Coulbourn Mill Road in the
A-1 Zoning District and the A-2 Airport Overlay District. M-58, G-6, P-95 (Clark Meadows)

Mr. Illuminati called the second case.

Mr. Nick Leffner with Kimley-Horn, 215 Washington Ave., Suite 500, Towson, MD, Mr.
Greg Werner with Nexamp, 1133 15 Street NW, Washington, D.C., and Ms. Erin Benson,
counsel from YVS Law, LLC, 185 Admiral Cochran Drive, Annapolis, MD, approached the table.

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was
entered into the record. The Applicant seeks the Board’s approval to construct a 2MW solar
facility on the referenced property.

The general area is predominantly rural-residential in nature, featuring a mix of wooded
parcels, agricultural uses, and residential development. Residential subdivisions and
individual properties are improved with single-family homes surrounding and abutting the
property.

Based on evidence acquired from the Applicant and as set forth in the Staff Report,

the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with seven (7) conditions of
approval.

Ms. Benson requested an extension of one (1) year to obtain a building permit due to
interconnection challenges; therefore, three (3) years total to obtain a building permit for
construction. Mr. Werner added that it was to accommodate the long interconnection queue
at this substation. They want to provide adequate time for Delmarva Power to study the
application.

Ms. Benson stated that the project's scale had drastically decreased since the
presentation in February 2025.

Mr. Werner explained that the project has been pushed into the back of the field and
is now screened by vegetative screening on the front against the road, as well as some
plantings along the side to screen it from some of the rear neighbors. Additional screening will
be provided by the existing sides. They worked on accommodating neighbor concerns, and
they believe this design achieves the balance that everyone was looking for at the February
meeting.

Mr. Leffner added that the project will be surrounded by an 8-foot agricultural-style
fence. The project will have a 40-foot-wide landscape buffer, which exceeds the current county
landscape requirements of three staggered rows. He requested that condition three (3) be
modified to read “Areas located within the solar array shall be planted with Maryland native

ground cover and maintained to establish a pollinator habitat. The ground cover
shall not include any invasive species or noxious weeds.” Adding the words “or naturalized.”

Chairman Wright inquired if the Staff had any objection to modifying condition one (1)
by adding an extension of one (1) year to obtain a building permit due to interconnection
challenges. Mr. Meadows responded, saying the Staff would consider it to be a reasonable
request pending a modification of the condition by the Board.

One individual from the public wished to address the Board: Mr. Robert Buck, who lives
at 4458 Nutters Cross Road. When Mr. Buck looks down his driveway, he sees the driveway
of the property in question. He would like to go on record saying their proposal will not cause
him to stay up nights because of noise, it will not cause him any sinus challenges, nor will it
depreciate the value of his property. He would appreciate it if the Board would grant these
individuals what they have requested.

Board Member Mr. Bill Turner made a MOTION to approve the recommendations to
amend condition one (1) to allow three years from the date of approval for the building permit,
and condition three (3) to include “or naturalized ground cover.” Mr. David Ennis seconded
the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the
amendments to the two (2) conditions.

Mr. Bill Turner made a MOTION to approve the applicant’s request for Special
Exception for a Minor Solar Facility and adopt the Staff’s evaluation and evidence presented,
along with the amended conditions of approval numbers one (1) through seven (7). Mr. David
Ennis seconded the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board
APPROVED the Special Exception.
