### Wicomico County Board of Appeals April 25, 2024 

The regular meeting of the Wicomico County Board of Appeals was held on Thursday, April 25, 2024, in the Council Chambers, Third Floor, Government Office Building at 6:00 P.M. with the following persons in attendance. 

#### BOARD MEMBERS: 

Chairman - J. Phillips Wright, Jr. William ‘Bill’ Turner Michael Hoy David Ennis Tammy Adkins 

#### PLANNING STAFF: 

Andrew Illuminati - County Attorney Clark Meadows - Zoning Administrator Janae Merchant - Recording Secretary 

The meeting was called to order at 6:00 P.M. by Chairman J. Phillips Wright, Jr. Chairman Wright reviewed the Emergency Evacuation Statement. Chairman Wright introduced the Planning Staff and Board Members. 

MINUTES - AGENDA #1 

Draft minutes of Thursday, January 25, 2024 meeting were placed on the table to be approved. 

Upon a motion by Mr. David Ennis seconded by Mr. Michael Hoy, with all members eligible to vote, the Board unanimously approved the January 25, 2024 minutes as written. (Ms. Tammy Adkins recused herself.)

#### AGENDA #2 

#WA-2403 - Special Exception - Accessory Apartment - Property located west of Salisbury and binding upon the southwesterly side of Belgian Way in the Steeplechase Residential Subdivision in the R-20 Residential Zoning District. M-37, G-21, P-452, L-55B 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the first case. 

The applicant, Mr. Donnie Messick at 26155 High Banks Drive, Salisbury, MD, and Ms. Debra Rossi at 921 Johnson Road, Salisbury, MD, approached the table. 

Mr. Clark Meadows presented the Staff Report and entered the related exhibits into the record. Mr. Meadows stated Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception with six (6) conditions of approval. 

The following individuals came forward to voice concerns against the Special Exception: . William Perry at 27635 Arabian Dr, Salisbury, MD . Don Rodgers at 5959 Jumpers Ct, Salisbury, MD 

The Applicant, Interested Parties, and the Board discussed the authority and powers of the neighborhood Homeowners Association (“HOA”). 

Board Member Mr. William “Bill” Turner offered a MOTION to approve the applicant's request for a Special Exception - Accessory Apartment. The motion was SECONDED by Ms. Tammy Adkins. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the Special Exception - Accessory Apartment.

#### AGENDA #4 

Special Discussion: #WA-1404-20A - Modification to the Conditions of Approval (Formerly #WA-7059 & #WA-77174) 

Mr. Clark Meadows announced Mr. Joseph Beach with Howard, Sand, & Gravel entered into an agreement to sell the property to Chaney Enterprises. 

Mr. Meadows indicated conditions of approval for the borrow pit on Route 50 from October 2020 are to be reviewed every five (5) years for the Special Exception. The applicant requested that condition number five (5), "Excavation shall be conducted by Howard Sand & Gravel, Inc. only and not transferable except by review and approval of the Board” be removed. The remaining conditions would continue yet be renumbered. Condition twelve (12) speaks to operations granted on a time-limited basis of 5 years through 10/31/2025. 

Mr. Joseph Beach and Mr. Kyle Murray (Chaney Enterprises) approached the table. Mr. Illuminati administered the Oath to both gentlemen. Messrs. Beach and Murray affirmed the description of the request. 

Board Member Mr. David Ennis offered a MOTION to approve the request to remove condition five (5) and all other conditions would stay intact. The motion was SECONDED by Mr. William “Bill” Turner. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board approved the request. 

## eee

This is a summary of the proceedings of this meeting. Detailed information is in the permanent files of each case as presented and filed in the Wicomico County Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development Office.
