# Wicomico County Board of Appeals November 20, 2025 

The regular Wicomico County Board of Appeals meeting was held on Thursday, November 20, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers, Room 301, on the Third Floor of the Government Office Building. The following persons were in attendance. 

BOARD MEMBERS: 

Chairman - J. Phillips Wright, Jr. William “Bill” Turner Tammy Adkins Jim Morris Lauren White

## AGENDA #2 

Administrative Matter - Modification/Correction of the minutes (approved 5/22/25) in case #WA-2507 (Kathuria Farms & International BioRefineries) to accurately describe the Board’s decision on 3/27/25. (Wright, Turner, and Morris) 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the case. He explained that the Board needed to accurately describe the Board’s decision on March 27, 2025. The last sentence of the last page of the minutes reads “With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the Special Exception.” It was not a Special Exception; it was a decision of the Zoning Administrator. The request this evening was to enter a motion to amend the minutes so that the last sentence reads: “With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board UPHELD the decision of the Zoning Administrator.” 

Board Member Mr. William “Bill” Turner made a MOTION to modify the resolution. Mr. Jim Morris seconded the MOTION. With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the modification of the March 27, 2025, minutes for case #WA-2507. Ms. Tammy Adkins and Ms. Lauren White abstained from voting as they were not present for the March meeting. 

## AGENDA #3 

Administrative Matter - #WA-1404-25A - Extension of time - limited approval for Borrow Pit (Clark Meadows) Mardela Springs Sand and Gravel, LLC, property located east of Mardela Springs and binding upon the northerly side of U.S. Rt. 50 in the A-1 Zoning District. (M-9, G- 15, P-28, 25, 53, 97, and 29) 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati called the case. 

Mr. Kyle Murray, with Chaney Enterprises, at 2661 Riva Road, Annapolis, Maryland, approached the table. 

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant requested the Board’s consideration for a 5-year extension for the existing borrow operation at Mardela Springs Sand & Gravel. The Planning Office records do not indicate any complaints from area residents regarding this activity. Mardela Springs Sand & Gravel has maintained all required permits and bonding for the excavation operation.

Mr. Illuminati called the case. The Applicant's name was clarified. Hebron Sand and Gravel, LLC is the property owner, and Disney Sand and Gravel, LLC is the operating entity. Therefore, the Applicant is Disney Sand and Gravel, LLC. 

Mr. Brock Parker of Parker and Associates, Inc.; Mr. Chris McCabe, President of Coastal Compliance Solutions, LLC; Ms. Jordan Disney; Mr. Darren Disney at 8141 Braggs Lane; and Mr. Mark Cropper, counsel for the Applicant, approached the table. 

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant seeks the Board’s approval to establish and operate a borrow pit on the identified property. 

Based on evidence obtained from the Applicant and as set forth in the Staff Report, the Staff recommended approval of the Special Exception subject to eight (8) conditions. 

Ms. White requested more information on the status of water quality, as stated in the environmental letter, Applicant's Exhibit C. Mr McCabe responded that the water will remain in the pond and will actually improve. It will be discharged at a level in accordance with Mr. Parker's design, and no further application of pesticides, herbicides, or manure will be made.
