## AGENDA #2 

## <u>Administrative Matter</u> – #WA-2508 CBCA Buffer Variance – Withdrawal of prior variance approval (Colin Harrison) 

Mr. Colin Harrison presented the Staff Report. This is an administrative withdrawal of a buffer variance at 3039 White Haven Road. The property owners are Jeremiah and Kayla Campbell. 

The Campbells received Board approval for a buffer variance to construct a driveway that was to meander through the 100-foot buffer. However, the Critical Area Commission appealed the variance to the Circuit Court for Judicial Review. Therefore, the property owners chose to revise their site plan to avoid any 100-foot Critical Area Buffer. The Critical Area Commission reviewed the revised plan and stated, “Considering the project has been redesigned to not impact the Buffer, and it complies with Buffer establishment requirements, then the Commission Staff has no further comments.” Legal Counsel for the Campbells sent an email to the Board's Attorney requesting that the Attorney withdraw the Board's Variance Approval. 

Staff recommended granting an administrative withdrawal of the Board-approved Buffer Variance for Case #WA-2508. 

Board Member Ms. Tammy Adkins made a MOTION to accept the request to withdraw the Variance approval for the Campbells. Mr. Jim Morris seconded the MOTION . With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the request to withdraw the Variance approval. 

Chairman Wright requested that everyone who planned to testify before the Board stand and be sworn in or affirmed; Mr. Andrew Illuminati administered the Oath. Chairman Wright instructed the audience that when the applicant’s case was called, the applicant should come forward, state their name, provide a mailing address, and state whether the individual had been sworn in or affirmed.

Mr. Meadows presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant requested the Board’s consideration for a one (1) year extension to replace a nonconforming manufactured home (destroyed by fire). Ms. Truitt and her son, Ronnie Norman, owners of 1026 Hinman Lane, were displaced in January 2025 after their home was destroyed by fire. The dwelling was a 1966-model singlewide manufactured home. As a legal, nonconforming use in the R-20 zoning district, the manufactured home was authorized to be replaced in-kind within one (1) year from the destruction. Since the fire, Ms. Truitt and Mr. Norman have been cleaning up the property and installing an updated on-site septic system and suitable domestic well service (as required by the County Health Department). The owners continue to move forward with tasks required to reestablish their home on the property, but have not yet obtained a building permit for a replacement home. 

In the year since the fire, the owners have maintained communication with P&Z and have advised the department of their progress in replacing the home. Staff continues to monitor on-site conditions and acknowledges that the owner’s efforts are in good faith. Staff recommended the Board grant a one (1) year extension to replace the noncomforming manufactured home. 

Board Member Ms. Tammy Adkins made a MOTION to accept the request for a one (1)year extension to replace a nonconforming manufactured home, subject to the suggested conditions. Mr. Jim Morris seconded the MOTION . With all members and the Chairman voting AYE, the Board APPROVED the request for a one (1)- year extension. 

## AGENDA #4 

#WA-2601 Vilwell Development, Inc., in accordance with Sections 149-32 and 149-44 of the Wicomico County Floodplain Management Code, requests a Variance – Flood Protection Setback on property located north of Salisbury, at Sandpiper Drive in the R-15 Zoning District. (M-29, G-20, P-253) (Becky Thayne) 

Mr. Illuminati called the case. 

Ms. Becky Thayne, Mr. Rendy Vervill, the Applicant, and Mr. Richard Duvall, counsel for the Applicant, approached the table. 

Ms. Thayne presented the Staff Report, which, along with related exhibits, was entered into the record. The Applicant seeks the Board’s approval to construct a single-family dwelling on Lot 11 of the new Westlake Section Four subdivision, which encroaches into the flood protection setback for Coty Branch Creek. The property is located at the end of the cul-de-sac on Sandpiper Dr in Westlake Section Four, just west of the Wicomico River and north of downtown Salisbury. The lot has 62.3 ft of road frontage on Sandpiper Drive. Westlake Section Four was recorded in 1993 but never developed. 

Staff recommended that the Board grant the Variance subject to five (5) conditions. 

After a brief question period between Mr. Duvall and Mr. Vervill, Mr. Duvall asked the Board to accept the Staff’s recommendation and grant this Variance.
