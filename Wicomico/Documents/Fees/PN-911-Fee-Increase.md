# PN-911-Fee-Increase

<!-- page 1 -->
# **PUBLIC NOTICE** 

# NOTICE OF HEARING 

BEFORE THE COUNTY COUNCIL 

OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, in compliance with Section 1-311 of the Public Safety Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, that the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland will hear public comments on the following proposed Resolution at their regular meeting in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building in Salisbury, Maryland, on: 

# **TUESDAY, APRIL 1, 2025 Commencing at 6:00 p.m.** 

The Wicomico County Department of Emergency Services proposes increasing the 9-1-1 fee from $1.50 to $1.75 to help defray the operational costs of the County’s 9-1-1 emergency telephone system. 

The Wicomico County Council requests that all interested persons appear at the specified time and place to express their views and opinions concerning the matter. 

# WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

BY:  Laura Hurley, Council Administrator 

Salisbury Independent:  March 25, 2025
