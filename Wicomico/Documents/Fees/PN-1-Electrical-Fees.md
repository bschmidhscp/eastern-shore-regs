# PN-1-Electrical-Fees

<!-- page 1 -->
# WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL **P U B L I C    N O T I C E** 

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT A PUBLIC HEARING WILL BE HELD ON: 

Tuesday, July 18, 2023 Commencing at 10:00 A.M. 

in the Council Chambers of the Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland, in conformity with Section 311(d) of the Charter of Wicomico County, on the following Bill, which was introduced for consideration at a Legislative Session of said County for the year 2023.  The following Bill may be called for final reading and consideration commencing immediately after the aforesaid public hearing. 

**Legislative Bill No. 2023-09: –** AN ACT to amend Chapter 138 of the Wicomico County Code, titled “Electrical Standards,” Article II, titled “Registration of Electricians and Inspections,” Section 138-24, titled “Inspections; Permits” and Chapter 141 of the Wicomico County Code, titled “Fees,” Section 141-3, titled “Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development.” 

If the foregoing Bill No. 2023-08 is enacted, it will take effect sixty (60) days from the date it becomes law, subject to the referendum provisions of the Charter of Wicomico County, Maryland.
