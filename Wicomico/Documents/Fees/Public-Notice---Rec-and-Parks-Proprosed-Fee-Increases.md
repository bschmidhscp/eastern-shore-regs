## PUBLIC NOTICE 

NOTICE OF HEARING 

BEFORE THE COUNTY COUNCIL 

OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, that the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland will hear public comments on the following proposed Resolution at their regular meeting in the County Council Chambers,<sup>GovernmentOffice</sup> Building<sup>in</sup> Salisbury, Maryland, on: 

TUESDAY,<sup>JUNE</sup> 2,<sup>2026</sup> 

Commencing<sup>at6:00</sup> p.m. 

The Wicomico County Department of Recreation, Parks, and Tourism proposes increasing the rates and prices<sup>forvarious</sup> programs<sup>andfacilities.The</sup> purpose<sup>of the</sup> proposed<sup>increasesis</sup> to support the continued maintenance, improvement, and operation of recreational programs and parks throughout the County. If adopted, the new rates and prices will become effective July 1, 2026. 

A copy of the proposed rate and price increases is available for review at https://www.wicomicocounty.org/676/County-Council-Public-Notices 

The Wicomico County Council requests that all interested persons appear at the specified time and place<sup>to</sup> express<sup>theirviewsand</sup> opinions concerning<sup>thematter.</sup> 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

BY: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator 

The Daily Times: June 1, 2026
