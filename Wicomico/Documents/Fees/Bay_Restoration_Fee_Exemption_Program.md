# **Bay Restoration Fee Hardship Exemption Program** 

Wicomico County will consider the exemption of residential users from paying the Bay Restoration Fee when they have received a credit on their property taxes through the **Homeowner’s Property Tax Credit Program** and **one** 

of the following conditions is met: 

- Receiving energy assistance subsidy; 

- Receiving public assistance – supplemental security income (SSI) or food stamps; 

- Receiving veterans or social security disability benefits 

The following supporting documentation will be required: 

- Current Real Estate Property Tax Bill showing Homeowner Tax Credit and Bay Restoration fee; 

- Current enrollment verification in energy assistance subsidy; 

- Current enrollment verification of medical assistance, food stamps or other public assistance; 

- Verification of eligibility for veterans / social security disability benefits; 

There are no guaranteed approvals, only properties that are owner occupied and meeting the minimum qualifications will receive approval for the Bay Restoration Fee Hardship Exemption. Please be advised, the Hardship Exemption is only good for one year and property owners must apply yearly. 

For additional information, please call the Wicomico County Executive Office at 410-548-4801
