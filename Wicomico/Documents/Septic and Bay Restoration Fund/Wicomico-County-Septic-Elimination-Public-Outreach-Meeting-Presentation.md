## Project Purpose 

▪ History of failing septic systems 

   - ►17 failed septic systems since 2017 

   - ►11 in the past 4 years per Wicomico County Health Department 

- Failing septic systems could result in contamination of drinking water supply – Treated in public sewer 

- Monetary benefits of project 

   - ►Estimated total project cost - $8.6 million 

   - ►Wicomico County secured a grant from MDE to cover $3 million of the total project cost, $5.6 million in low interest loans 

   - ►Cost of new septic system – Over $35,000

## Survey - What to Expect? 

- Survey roadways, front yards, back yards. Surveyors will require access to homeowners’ front and back yards but will not be digging or causing any disturbances. 

- Needed to determine sewer main alignment, septic tank locations, service line alignment. 

- Nov. 17 start of surveying work. Letters were sent to each homeowner in project area. Surveyors will knock on doors on the day of work for additional notice. 

- Aerial images provided to assist with surveying. 

   - ►Residents can markup septic tank and lateral line locations, well and water service line if known. 

   - ►Prioritized yard features to be avoided, if at all possible.
