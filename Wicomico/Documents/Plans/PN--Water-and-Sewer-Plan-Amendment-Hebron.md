# PN--Water-and-Sewer-Plan-Amendment-Hebron

<!-- page 1 -->
# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, January 7, 2025, at 6:00 pm in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland on an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. The Wicomico County Department of Public Works is seeking a redesignation of property in the area of Old Railroad Road in Hebron, Tax Map 18 and Parcel 25, as shown below from a current designation of W-6 to a new designation of W-1 to be served by the Town of Hebron.  The map amendment will allow the Town of Hebron to seek potential grant funding for the water project, and it does not imply that immediate connection to the Town of Hebron system is imminent. There is no request to update the Sewer Plan map at this time.
