# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, June 16, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the 2010 Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. The Wicomico County Department of Public Works is seeking a redesignation of the Pohanka Automotive Group Kia Dealership located at 1819, 1825, and 1911 N. Salisbury Blvd., Salisbury, MD; Identified as Tax Map 0029, Parcel 0017, Blocks B Lot 1 & 7, Block C Lots 1-7; from a current designation of W-6/S-6 (no planned service) to a new designation of W-1/S-1 (served), to be served by the City of Salisbury.
