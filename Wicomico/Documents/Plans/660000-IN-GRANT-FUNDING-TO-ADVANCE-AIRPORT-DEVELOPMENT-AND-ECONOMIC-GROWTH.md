## **FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE October 20, 2026** 

## **WICOMICO COUNTY SECURES $660,000 IN GRANT FUNDING TO ADVANCE AIRPORT DEVELOPMENT AND ECONOMIC GROWTH** 

_Additional $200,000 Grant Strengthens County's Efforts to Create Shovel-Ready Sites, Attract Businesses, and Expand Aviation Opportunities_ 

**SALISBURY, MD** — Wicomico County Executive Julie Giordano is pleased to announce that Wicomico County has secured an additional $200,000 in Fiscal Year 2026 Site Readiness grant funding, bringing the County's total grant awards for site development initiatives at the Salisbury-Ocean City: Wicomico Regional Airport (SBY) to $660,000. 

The latest award builds upon $460,000 in previously secured funding through the Maryland Economic Development Corporation's (MEDCO) Maryland Business Ready Sites Program, including a $450,000 Site Improvement Grant and a $10,000 Site Characterization Grant. 

These investments are designed to prepare land for future development, improve infrastructure, and position Wicomico County to compete for new businesses and high-quality jobs. 

"This is another major win for Wicomico County and our continued commitment to economic development," said County Executive Julie Giordano. "We aren't just talking about bringing businesses and jobs to our community — we are doing the work to make it happen. By securing grant funding and preparing sites for development, we are creating real opportunities for businesses to invest, expand, and create jobs right here in Wicomico County." 

The initial $450,000 award supports site preparation for future aircraft hangar development at the airport, while the $10,000 Site Characterization Grant supports planning for a proposed business park adjacent to the airfield. The additional $200,000 will further strengthen the County's ongoing site readiness efforts, helping advance the airport's potential as a regional hub for aviation, aerospace, and industrial development.

<!-- page 2 -->
Under County Executive Giordano's administration, the airport has become a major focus of economic development, with continued investments in runway expansion, taxiway improvements, water and sewer infrastructure, hangar development, and aviation workforce training. 

The County has also partnered with Salisbury Wicomico Economic Development (SWED) to advance shovelready development opportunities, reducing obstacles that can delay construction and making Wicomico County more attractive to prospective employers. 

"Economic development doesn't happen by accident," Giordano continued. "It takes planning, partnerships, and a willingness to invest in our future. Every dollar we secure through grants is another opportunity to move important projects forward while reducing the burden on our local taxpayers. Our goal is simple: bring jobs, attract investment, and keep Wicomico County moving forward." 

County Executive Giordano expressed appreciation to MEDCO, SWED, state partners, and local economic development leaders for their continued collaboration and support. 

"Wicomico County is open for business, and we are making sure we have the infrastructure and opportunities in place to prove it," Giordano concluded.
