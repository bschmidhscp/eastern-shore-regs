# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, August 6, 2024, at 6:00 p.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland on an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. LC Management is requesting to reclassify and remap from Water Map 5 and Sewer Map 5 (ten year) Parcels 0223 & 0398 on Tax Map 0030, known as Parson’s Lake Single Family, Parson’s Lake Drive, Salisbury, MD, to being designated as served by public sewer (S-1) and public water (W-1) provided by the City of Salisbury. 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND
