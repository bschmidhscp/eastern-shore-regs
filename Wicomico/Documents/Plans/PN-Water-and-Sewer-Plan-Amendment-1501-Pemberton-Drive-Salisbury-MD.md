# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, April 2, 2024, at 6:00 p.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. David and Jana Layfield are requesting to amend Sewer Map 9 and Water Map 9 to show Parcel 159 on Tax Map 37, known as 1501 Pemberton Dr., Salisbury, MD, being designated as served by public sewer (S-1) and public water (W-1) provided by the City of Salisbury.
