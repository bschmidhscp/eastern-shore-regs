# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, November 5, 2024, at 6:00 p.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland on an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. The Wicomico County Department of Public Works is seeking a redesignation of property in the area of Regency Drive, Presidents Neighborhood and North and South Delano Avenues as shown below from a current designation of W-6/S-6 (no planned service), to a new designation of W- 3/S-3 (priority) to be served by the City of Salisbury.  The map amendment will allow the County to seek potential grant funding for the water/sewer project, and it does not imply that immediate connection to the City of Salisbury system is imminent. 

All those lots, tracts, or parcels of land located along N. Delano Ave., S. Delano Ave., Ocean Gateway, Grant Ave., Tyler Ave., Hayes Ave., E. Lincoln Ave., Pierce Ave., E. College Ave., Buckingham Cir., Oxford Cir., Regency Dr., Gladstone Cir., and Eton Cir. Said area includes Parcels 182, 200, 189, and 181 on Tax Map 28; and Parcels 7, 237, 239, 238, on Tax Map 48; and Parcel 33 on Tax Map 38.
