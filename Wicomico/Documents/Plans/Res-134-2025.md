# Res-134-2025

<!-- page 1 -->
COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

2025 LEGISLATIVE SESSION INTRODUCED: LEGISLATIVE DAY NO. 17 ADOPTED: LEGISLATIVE DAY NO.21 

Resolution No. 134 

INTRODUCED BY: The President of the Council at the request of the County Executive. A RESOLUTION TO CREATE A WATER AND SEWER TECHNICAL ADVISORY COMMITTEE AND ADOPT COMMITTEE BYLAWS. 

WHEREAS, the County Council accepted the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Master Plan via Resolution No. 120-2121 in November 2021, and 

WHEREAS, the County Executive and the Department of Public Works are implementing projects from the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Master Plan; and 

WHEREAS, the process for implementing water and sewer projects requires a high level of technical expertise in planning, engineering, construction, and financing; and 

WHEREAS, the County Executive and the Department of Public Works have determined that it is in the best interest of the citizens to create a Water and Sewer Technical Advisory Committee (WSTAC); and 

WHEREAS, the County Executive and the Department of Public Works have developed bylaws to define the responsibilities of the Water and Sewer Technical Advisory Committee. 

NOW, THERFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland approves the creation of a Water and Sewer Technical Advisory Committee and adopts the bylaws. 

Done at Salisbury, Maryland this<sup>4th</sup> day of November, 2025.

WATER AND SEWER TECHNICAL ADVISORY COMMITTEE BY-LAWS 

Section 1. ESTABLISHMENT AND MEMBERSHIP. 

- A. The Wicomico County Water and Sewer Technical Advisory Committee is hereby created and established as an advisory board to Wicomico County and its Department of Public Works. 

- B. The Wicomico County Water and Sewer Technical Advisory Committee is established as the body which will advise on water and sewer planning, design, construction, financing, operation, and maintenance. 

- C. The Wicomico County Water and Sewer Technical Advisory Committee shall consist of three (3) voting members and one (1) alternate member, each appointed by the Executive and confirmed by the Council. 

- D. Each committee member shall have knowledge or expertise in either engineering, architecture, planning, finance, accounting, building or construction of water and sewer systems. 

- E. The term of a committee member shall be six (6) years expiring on January 1 of the appropriate year with the initial terms of the committee member as follows; 

   - (a) for one committee member, 2 years expiring on the second January 1 after appointment; 

(b) for one committee member, 4 years expiring on the fourth January 1 after appointment; and 

(c) for one committee member, 6 years expiring on the sixth January<sup>1</sup> after appointment; and 

   - (d) for the alternate member, 6 years expiring on the sixth January 1 after appointment. 

- F. At the end of a term, a committee member continues to serve until a successor is appointed. 

- G. A committee member who is appointed after a term has begun serves only for the rest of the term and until a successor is appointed. 

- H. At least one (1) month before the term of a committee member expires, the County Executive shall appoint or reappoint a committee member for confirmation by the Council. 

- I. Vacancies occurring prior to expiration of a term shall be filled in the same manner as the original appointment. 

- J. Employees from the Wicomico County government shall be available, as needed, to provide information and advice to the Committee.

<!-- page 3 -->
Section 2. DUTIES AND FUI\ICTIONS. 

The Committee shall perform the following duties and functions: 

- A. Advise and make recommendations to the Department of Public Works, County Executive and County Council concerning planning, design, construction, extension, operation, maintenance, financing, and rates and fees associated with water and sewer service areas and the associated projects. 

- B. Receive and review planning studies, engineering reports, designs, specifications and budgets prepared by the architects, engineers and/or contractors and to provide technical and practical approaches for the benefit of Wicomico County. 

- C. Receive and review status reports on current contracts and changes thereof. 

- D. Make recommendation on the 5-year and 10-year capital improvements plans and prioritization of water and sewer projects. 

- E. Make recommendations to establish County policies and procedures for provision of water and sewer service in the County. 

- F. Serve on other task forces or committees related to water and sewer projects. 

- G. Receive assistance from personnel in County Departments necessary to perform their duties. 

Section 3. OFFICERS. 

- A. The Committee shall elect a chair person from among its members. 

- B. The election shall be at the January meeting of the Committee, or the first meeting of the calendar year. 

- C. The Chair shall serve for a term of one (1) year, beginning after the election, or until their successor is elected and qualified. 

- D. The Chair shall preside over all meetings of the Committee. The Chair shall represent the Committee at all activities where their presence is desirable or necessary. 

- E. Should the office of the Chair become vacant, the Committee shall select a successor from its membership at the next regular meeting and such selection shall be for the unexpired term of the Chair. 

- Section 4. MEETINGS. 

- A. The Committee shall hold meetings quarterly. 

- B. Meetings can be held in-person or on-line at the discretion of the Chair.

<!-- page 4 -->
- C. Special meetings may be called on an as-needed basis for the purpose of reviewing time sensitive issues. 

- D. With the Chair’s approval, the Committee may also utilize an email vote, when necessary, with the results documented in the following month’s meeting minutes. 

- F. Two (2) voting members of the Committee shall constitute a quorum at every meeting. 

- F. In the event of an absence of a regular committee member, the alternate member may vote at the meeting. 

- G. The Committee may recommend the removal of any voting member who fails to regularly attend Committee meetings. 

- H. Meetings shall be subject to the Open Meetings Act. 

- I. The Department of Public Works shall provide personnel to keep records and comply with the Open Meetings Act provisions. 

Section 5. RULES AND REGULATIONS. 

The Committee may adopt reasonable rules and regulations as necessary to accomplish its purpose and to conduct its business.
