# PN-Water-and-Sewer-Plan-Amendment-Square-at-Merrit-Mill

<!-- page 1 -->
# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, September 17, 2019, at 10:00 a.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. The Square at Merritt Mill is requesting to amend Sewer Map 7 and Water Map 7 to show Parcels 33 and 542 on Tax Map 39 being designated as served by sewer (S-1) and water (W-1) by connecting to the City of Salisbury’s water and sewer system. This project is estimated to use 75 EDUs. 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

____________________________ BY: Dallas Baker, Director of Public Works
