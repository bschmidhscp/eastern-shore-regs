# PN-Capital-Budget-Amendment-Water-and-Sewer-PERs

<!-- page 1 -->
# **NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING** 

BEFORE THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY 

Notice is hereby given pursuant to Article VII, Section 704 (G) of the Charter of Wicomico County that a public hearing will be held before the County Council of Wicomico County in the Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland, on: 

# **TUESDAY FEBRUARY 6, 2024 AT 6:00 P.M.** 

For the purpose of receiving public comment on a proposed amendment to the Fiscal Year 2024 Capital Budget to add two new projects for Public Works-Water/Sewer: (1) Westside Regional Wastewater System Preliminary Engineering Report, in the amount of $150,000-Pay Go and $60,000-Federal Grant. (2) Community of Parsonsburg Preliminary Engineering Report, in the amount of $75,000-Pay Go and $56,250-Federal Grant. 

The Wicomico County Council requests that all interested persons appear at said time and place for the purpose of expressing their views and opinions concerning the matter. 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

BY:__________________________ 

Laura Hurley, Council Administrator
