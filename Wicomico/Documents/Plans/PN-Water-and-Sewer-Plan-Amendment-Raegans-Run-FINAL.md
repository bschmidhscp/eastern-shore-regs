# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, May 6, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. The Bierman Family, LLC is requesting to amend Sewer Map S-4 (3-5 years) to show Parcel 65 on Tax Map 47, located on Riverside Drive, Salisbury, MD, being designated as Sewer Map S-6 (no planned service) to allow for the construction of a 10-lot subdivision with individual well and septic systems. 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

____________________________ BY: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator
