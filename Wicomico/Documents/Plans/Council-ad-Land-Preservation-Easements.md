# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING** 

Under the provisions of Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation, an application has been filed with the Wicomico County Council to sell an easement to the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation on the following property: 

**Delmarva Farms, LLC** – Property located near Eden on the westerly side of Campground Road on Map 46, Parcel 141 consisting of 91.32 acres. 

**Porter Mill Properties, LLC –** Property located north of Hebron on the northerly side of Ocean Gateway on Map 18, Parcel 28 consisting of 129.05 acres. 

**William R. & Teresa B. McCain** – Property located southeast of Fruitland on the southerly side of Union Church Road on Map 58, Parcel 117 consisting of 85.13 acres. 

**MARVIC Associates, LLC –** Property located southwest of Powellville on both sides of Laws Road on Map 60, Parcels 17 and 19 consisting of 167.29 acres. 

**William & Mary Todd –** Property located south of Sharptown on the southerly side of Cross Road on Map 6, Parcel 146 consisting of 87.9 acres. 

# **A HEARING WILL BE HELD BEFORE THE COUNTY COUNCIL** 

**ON TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 15, 2020 AT 10:00 A.M.** in the Council Chambers, Third Floor, Government Office Building, North Division Street and U.S. Route 50, Salisbury, Maryland, to hear comments from the public regarding these applications. 

Agricultural Land Preservation Easements may be sold by landowners whose land meets eligibility requirements of the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation. 

For further information concerning these applications or Agricultural Land Preservation Programs, call the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Department:  Telephone 410-548-4860. Written comments or inquiries may be sent to:  Agricultural Land Preservation, P.O. Box 870, Salisbury, MD 21803-0870. 

The Council reserves the right to close a part of this meting as authorized by Section 10-508(a) of the Maryland Annotated Code.
