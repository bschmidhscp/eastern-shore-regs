# PN-Water-and-Sewer-Plan-Amendment-Delmar-Assisted-Living

<!-- page 1 -->
# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, August 6, 2019, at 6:00 p.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. The Town of Delmar is requesting a Sewer Service Area Map Category change to S-1 (served), as the Delmar Assisted Living Facility, LLC is now connected to the town’s sewer system, and a text amendment to Section 4.2.3 to accurately show the Town of Delmar’s current sewer capacity and needs. This project is estimated to provide 20 EDUs. 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

____________________________ BY: Dallas Baker, Director of Public Works
