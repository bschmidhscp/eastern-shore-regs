# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, May 2, 2023, at 6:00 p.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. The Pond at Nutter’s Crossing is requesting to amend Sewer Map 10 and Water Map 10 to show Parcels 171, 443, 446 and 447 on Tax Map 48 being designated as served by private sewer (S-6) and private water (W-6). 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

____________________________ BY: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator 

Salisbury Independent: April 18<sup>th</sup> and 25<sup>th</sup> , 2023
