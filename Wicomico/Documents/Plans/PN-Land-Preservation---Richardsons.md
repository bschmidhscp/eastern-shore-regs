||WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL<br>NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING|||



In accordance with provisions of Chapter 104 of the Wicomico County Code, applications have been filed to sell easements to the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation on the following properties: 

Sidney & Kay Richardson — Six properties in Pittsville, MD and Willards, MD. 

Sidney & Kay Richardson — Six applications for properties in Pittsville, MD and Willards, MD. Lee & Tracy Richardson — One application for property located in Willards, MD on Old Ocean City Road — M-32, G-14, P-129. 

A HEARING WILL BE HELD BEFORE THE COUNTY COUNCIL ON TUESDAY, AUGUST 16, 2022 AT 10 A.M. in the Council Chambers, Third Floor, Government Office Building, North Division Street and U.S. Route 50, Salisbury, MD, to hear comments from the public regarding these applications. 

Agricultural Land Preservation Easements may be sold by landowners whose land meets eligibility requirements of the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation. 

For further information concerning these applications or Agricultural Land Preservation Programs, call the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Department: Telephone 410-548-4860. Written comments or inquiries may be sent to: Agricultural Land Preservation, P.O. Box 870, Salisbury, MD 2 1803-0870. 

The Council reserves the right to close a part of this meeting as authorized by Section 10-508(a) of the Maryland Annotated Code. 08/02, 08/09’22
