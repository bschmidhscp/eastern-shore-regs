# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, October 18, 2022, at 10:00 a.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. Ryan T. and Brooke C. Adler are requesting to amend Sewer Map 8 and Water Map 8 to show Parcel 574 on Tax Map 48, known as 908 Loch Raven Road, being designated as served by sewer (S-2) and water (W-2) by connecting to the City of Salisbury’s water and sewer system. This project is estimated to use 1 EDU. 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

____________________________ BY: Laura Hurley, Council Administrator
