# PN-Water-and-Sewer-Plan-Amendment-PennTex-Ventures

<!-- page 1 -->
# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, November 5, 2019, at 6:00 p.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. PennTex Ventures is requesting to amend Sewer Map 5 and Water Map 5 to show Parcels 153, 185 and 215 on Tax Map 20 being designated as served by sewer (S-1) and water (W1) by connecting to the City of Salisbury’s water and sewer system. This project is estimated to use 44.64 EDUs. 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

____________________________ BY: Dallas Baker, Director of Public Works
