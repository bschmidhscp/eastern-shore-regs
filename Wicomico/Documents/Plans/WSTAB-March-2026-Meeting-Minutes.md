   - Mr. Illuminati provided guidance on the language within the empowering resolution of the Board’s creation and dialogue for how the Board can create additional by-laws as needed for conducting business.

- VI. New Business 

   - Briefing on County and Sewer Updates 

   - Salisbury Septic Elimination Project 

   - Pittsville-Parsonsburg 

   - Old Ocean City Road Corridor 

      - Ms. Pollack provided a brief history of the most recent 5- year period with discussion of water and sewer needs in the County and desire to take almost 10k OSDS offline and place into municipal services, out of the 18k estimated OSDS in WC. 

      - Priority Funding Areas (PFA), PFA Comment Areas, Tier mapping, and challenges were detailed. 

      - The Board was provided update on the County’s efforts for updating the 2010 CWSP. 

      - Ms. Pollack then spoke on the Salisbury Septic Elimination Project (SSEP); outlining the three target areas, site limitations, existing service conditions, Health Department documentation, and Maryland Dept. of the Environment Water Quality Finance State Revolving Funding MDE WQFA SRF; 3 million in grant, and 5.5 low interest loans.

<!-- page 4 -->
KCI has provided 30% design with 60% design expected in May 20226. Ms. Pollack discussed the plan and relationship with the City of Salisbury, and out-of-town agreement for non-annexation. Mr. McCain asked if a large County vacant parcel could be used to gain access to the line as it goes, being the line would go in front. Ms. Pollack and Mr. Corry then explained that based on funding, the line would be considered Denied Access. 

- Rates and payments from residents. 

- Mr. Baker asked if other pockets are in process of review. Additional updates were provided by Ms. Pollack and Mr. Corry as outlined below. 

- Mr. Whitehead and Mr. Baker asked about additional funding sources, and if growth is considered or hindered. Answers provided for the Salisbury Septic Elimination Project (SSEP) is simply for service of existing dense properties with no likelihood of for growth in this specific project. 

- Ms. Pollack discussed process and procedures of USDA grant funding 

- Pittsville-Parsonsburg rural village; Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) completion to serve Parsonsburg with Town of Pittsville sewer, via an allocation of 50k gallons per day (gpd) is earmarked for this project. Two nearby mobile home parks were also included in the project to relieve long-term on-site sewage disposal concerns. Pittsville needs a full wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) upgrade. Joint application was discussed and not recommended by MDE in previous cycle for State Revolving Fund. However, this January 2027; joint

<!-- page 5 -->
application to be made. Estimated project 8-9 million for this connection. This body will be looked to for assistance in guidance on rate structures. 

- Old Ocean City Road Corridor (OOCRC); request for proposal (RFP) in development with the DPW to include PER for consideration of area from approx. Blind Industries to Walston Switch. Area plagued by failing OSDS and site limitations, but also has potential for growth. 

- Westside Regional Wastewater (WSPER); considering initial options to serve Mardela via Sharptown, the concept later evolved into evaluation for a regional WWTP system for Mardela, Sharptown, Hebron etc. based on Nanticoke and Nanticoke Tributary discharge. Nutrient balance report shows a combined flow, and enhanced treatment would best case scenario for reduction of impacts to watershed. Additional consideration for the addition of San Domingo is being developed. Regional approach estimates are in the 70 million dollars frame and would be a multi-decade approach. Based on scope of project, this project is the lower priority of current concepts in the County. 

- The group began speaking about the Perdue PFAS/PFOS concepts in the area. Until the current MDE/Perdue and legal processes are further along, the County is on the side lines. 

- Additional discussion was held about including the Old Quantico Road/Willing Drive area into focus based on its proximity to City of Salisbury limits. PER development would be the next step.
