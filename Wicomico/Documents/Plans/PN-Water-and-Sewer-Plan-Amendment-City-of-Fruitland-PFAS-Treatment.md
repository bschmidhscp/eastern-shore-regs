# **WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL** 

# **NOTICE** 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **<u>WATER AND SEWER PLAN AMENDMENT</u>** 

A Public Hearing will be held on Tuesday, September 2, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the County Council Chambers, Government Office Building, Salisbury, Maryland for an amendment to the 2010 Wicomico County Water and Sewer Plan. The Department of Public Works in collaboration with the City of Fruitland and its consultant, George, Miles and Buhr, LLC are requesting to provide for the withdraw of Water from the Manokin Aquifer via the installation of two additional confined-aquifer supply wells, the addition of treatment for emerging contaminates (PFAS) and to abandon four existing unconfined supply wells in that system. 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND
