as principal, hereinafter called “Obligor,” and,

Dollars ($ ), for the payment whereof Obligor and Surety bind themselves, their heirs, executors, administrators, successors and assigns, jointly and severally, firmly by these presents. 

WHEREAS, the above bounded Obligor pursuant to Chapter 200, Section 200-23 of the Wicomico County Code desires to record a “Record Plat” and, obtain any necessary building permits which the County requires in order that the Obligor can complete the subdivision in accordance with the Wicomico County Code; and, 

WHEREAS, the public improvements required under the provisions of the Wicomico County Code have not been installed and approved by the County Engineer and/or Director of Public Works; and, 

WHEREAS, the Obligor in accordance with Chapter 200, Section 200-23 of the Wicomico County Code, in lieu of completing the above mentioned public improvements as is required by the Wicomico County Code, desires that the County Council accept a security bond in an amount equal to the estimated cost of installation of the required improvements; and, 

WHEREAS, The County Council of Wicomico County, in Executive Session has approved Obligor’s request and agreed to accept such security bond. 

NOW, THEREFORE, the above bounded Obligor and Surety jointly and severally, covenant and agree to perform said public improvement work in accordance with the Wicomico County Code, including the Stormwater Management, Subdivision and Zoning Chapters. The said Wicomico County Code, Stormwater Management, Subdivision and Zoning Chapters, including, but not limited to Section 200-23, are hereby incorporated into this Agreement. Surety covenants and agrees that in the event Obligor fails to duly perform pursuant to the requirements of the Wicomico County Code and a Public Works Agreement dated , the Surety will pay all costs required to complete the public improvements in accordance with the Wicomico County Code and the aforesaid Public Works Agreement, to the limit of the amount of this bond, together with interest as provided by law to Wicomico County.
