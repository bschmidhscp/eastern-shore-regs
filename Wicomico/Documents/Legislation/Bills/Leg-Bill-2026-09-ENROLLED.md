AN ACT TO AMEND THE AIRPORT TURNING RADIUS OF THE A-2 AIRPORT OVERLAY DISTRICT ON THE WICOMICO COUNTY ZONING MAP 

WHEREAS, Chapter<sup>225oftheWicomico</sup> County Code,<sup>titled</sup> “Zoning,” Part 4, titled “Zoning<sup>Districts,” Article XII,titled</sup> “Special<sup>Districts:</sup> Overlay<sup>Districts,”Section</sup> §225-43,<sup>titled</sup> “A-2 Airport Overlay District,” provides the purpose of the A-2 Airport Overlay District is to “maintain and protect the public safety by preventing the establishment of hazards to aviation and property<sup>inthe</sup> vicinity<sup>ofthe</sup> Salisbury-Ocean City:<sup>Wicomico</sup> Regional Airport, protecting<sup>users</sup> of the airport and preventing unreasonable limitation or impairment on the use and expansion of airport<sup>facilitiesandthe</sup> public<sup>investmentwhichhasbeenmadetherein:;”and</sup> 

WHEREAS, per<sup>the</sup> Adopted<sup>Wicomico</sup> County Zoning Map,<sup>there</sup> must be an 8,000-foot turning<sup>radiusdelineatedfromthe</sup> airport<sup>reference</sup> point,<sup>whichisthe</sup> geometric<sup>centerofthe</sup> airport’s<sup>usable</sup> runways;<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> runway expansion<sup>atthe</sup> airport<sup>resultedintherelocation</sup> of the airport reference point; thus, requiring an amendment to the Wicomico County Zoning Map to properly delineate the parameters of Airport Turning Radius of the A-2 Airport Overlay District; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>theWicomico</sup> County Planning Commission, recommends amending the Wicomico County Zoning Map to reflect the revised Airport Turning Radius. 

Section L BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND,<sup>INLEGISLATIVE</sup> SESSION,<sup>thattheWicomico</sup> County’s Zoning Map<sup>is</sup> hereby<sup>amendedtorelocatethe8,000-foot</sup> Airport Turning<sup>RadiusoftheA-2</sup> Airport Overlay District,<sup>asreflectedinExhibitA,whichisattachedheretoandmade</sup> part<sup>hereof.</sup> 

SECTION II: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT this bill shall be known as Legislative Bill No. 2026-09 and shall take effect sixty (60) days after its enactment,<sup>unless</sup> a proper<sup>PetitionforReferendumthereof shallbefiled</sup> prior to said date: in which event, the Bill shall not take effect until the expiration of thirty (30) days following the approval<sup>ofthisBill</sup> by<sup>a</sup> majority<sup>ofthe</sup> qualified<sup>votersof</sup> the County voting<sup>in</sup> any<sup>such</sup> referendum.
