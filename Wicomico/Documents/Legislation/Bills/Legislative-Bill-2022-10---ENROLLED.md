# ENF OLLED 

### DAF - PROHIBITION 

COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

2022 Legislative Session 

Legislative Day No. 19 

Legislative Bill 2022- 10 

Introduced by: The President of the Council 

AN ACT to amend Chapter 225 of the Wicomico County Code, titled “Zoning” Part 6, titled “Accessory and Principal Uses,” Article XVII, titled “Accessory Uses,” Section 225-62 titled “Outside Storage,” and Article XVIII, titled “Principal Uses,” Section 225-65 titled “Outside Storage,” to prohibit the open top storage of a liquid organic fertilizer or soil amendment AND/OR DAF RESIDUALS. 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland, hereby finds that the open top storage of a liquid organic fertilizer or soil amendment AND/OR DAF RESIDUALS are contrary to the interests of the County and its citizens; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland, has determined that it is right and proper and within its authority to protect its residents from the adverse effects of the storage of said liquid organic fertilizer or soil amendment AND/OR DAF RESIDUALS in open topped tanks STRUCTURES. 

Section I. BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that Chapter 225 of the Wicomico County Code, titled “Zoning” Part 3, titled “Accessory and Principal Uses,” Article XVII, titled “Accessory Uses,” Section 225-62 titled “Outside Storage,” and Article XVIII titled “Principal Uses,” Section 225-65 titled “Outside Storage,” are hereby amended, to read as follows:

## §<sup>225-62OUTSIDESTORAGE.</sup> 

- E. OPEN TOP STORAGE TANK STRUCTURE. 

   1. DEFINITIONS. 

      - OPEN TOP STORAGE TANK AN ABOVE GROUND OPEN TOPPED STRUCTURE, DESIGNED AND USED FOR THE STORAGE OF AN AGRICULTURAL PRODUCT, BY PRODUCT, FERTILIZER OR SOIL AMENDMENT. 

a. OPEN TOP STORAGE STRUCTURE - AN OPEN OR PARTIALLY OPEN STRUCTURE, WHETHER ABOVE GROUND, PARTIALLY ABOVE OR BELOW GROUND LEVEL OR A LAGOON TYPE STRUCTURE, USED FOR THE STORAGE OF AN AGRICULTURAL PRODUCT, BY-PRODUCT, SOIL AMENDMENT AND/OR DAF RESIDUALS. 

   - b. LIQUID ORGANIC FERTILIZER OR SOIL AMENDMENT-A STATE OF MARYLAND APPROVED LIQUID ORGANIC FERTILIZER OR SOIL AMENDMENT PRODUCED FROM POULTRY OR OTHER ANIMAL PROCESSING OR RENDERING PROCESS, INCLUDING DAF RESIDUALS VIA A DISSOLVED AIR FLOTATION PROCESS. 

   - c. DAF RESIDUALS - ORGANIC BYPRODUCT MATERIAL CREATED AT AN ANIMAL PROCESSING FACILITY OR RENDERING FACILITY, INCLUDING MATERIAL COLLECTED BY MEANS OF A DISSOLVED AIR FLOTATION PROCESS. 

2. OPEN TOP TANK STRUCTURE STORAGE OF A LIQUID ORGANIC FERTILIZER OR SOIL AMENDMENT AND/OR DAF RESIDUALS AS DEFINED ABOVE IS PROHIBITED IN ALL ZONING DISTRICTS, EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN PARAGRAPH E.3. BELOW. THIS PROHIBITION IS PROSPECTIVE. 

3. THE PROHIBITION STATED HEREIN SHALL NOT APPLY TO LIQUID ORGANIC FERTILIZER OR SOIL AMENDMENT STORED BY A FARMER OR FARMING BUSINESS ON LAND IN THE AGRICULTURAL RURAL A 1 DISTRICT, OWNED OR LEASED BY SAID FARMER OR FARMING BUSINESS FOR USE ONLY ON LAND OWNED OR TITLED BY SAID FARMER OR FARMING BUSINESS.

THE PROHIBITION STATED HEREIN SHALL NOT PROHIBIT STORAGE AT THE PROCESSING LOCATION WHERE LEGALLY CREATED, NOR A FARMER OR FARMING BUSINESS FROM TEMPORARILY STORING LIQUID ORGANIC SOIL AMENDMENT AND/OR DAF RESIDUALS IN A MOBILE, CLOSED CONTAINER FOR A PERIOD OF NOT MORE THAN FORTY-FIVE (45) CONSECUTIVE DAYS DURING THE APPLICATION PROCESS, PROVIDED THAT THE LAND ON WHICH IT IS STORED IS IN THE WICOMICO COUNTY AGRICULTURAL-RURAL A- 1 ZONING DISTRICT AND THAT THE STORED MATERIAL IS APPLIED TO THE SAME LAND ON WHICH IT IS STORED.
