COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION, 2023 

EMERGENCY LEGISLATIVE BILL 2023-15 

Introduced: October 17, 2023 

By: The Council President 

AN EMERGENCY ACT ESTABLISHING A MORATORIUM ON THE PROCESSING, REVIEW, AND APPROVAL OF APPLICATIONS, SITE PLANS, PERMITS, AND OTHER AUTHORIZATIONS FOR THE LOCATION, CONSTRUCTION, AND/OR OPERATION OF BUSINESSES ENGAGED IN GROWING, PROCESSING, AND/OR SELLING CANNABIS TO INCLUDE ON-SITE CONSUMPTION ESTABLISHMENTS FOR A PERIOD OF (6) SIX MONTHS TO ALLOW THE COUNTY SUFFICIENT TIME TO ENACT REASONABLE, THOUGHTFUL, AND LAWFUL ZONING REQUIREMENTS AND OTHER REGULATIONS PERTAINING THERETO AS DEEMED NECESSARY TO PROTECT THE PUBLIC HEALTH, SAFETY, AND WELFARE. 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Local Gov’t § 10-324, Wicomico County, Maryland (the “County”), as a charter county, “may enact local laws relating to zoning and planning to protect and promote public safety, health, morals, and welfare;” and 

WHEREAS, on November 8, 2022, Maryland voters passed “Question 4” by ballot referendum, making it legal for individuals aged 21 and older to possess and consume cannabis in Maryland on or after July 1, 2023,; and 

WHEREAS, on April 8, 2023, the Maryland General Assembly passed the Cannabis Reform Act (HB556/ SB516) which allowed for cannabis sales to adults 21 and older from licensed dispensaries and established a framework for adult-use cannabis sales beginning July 1, 2023, including the conversion of existing medical cannabis business licenses to both medical and adult-use cannabis, and created a new Maryland Cannabis Administration to oversee see both medical and adult- use program; and 

WHEREAS, on May 3, 2023, the Governor of Maryland signed the Cannabis Reform Act into law; 

and 

WHEREAS, the Cannabis Reform Act provides political subdivisions with the power to establish zoning requirements for and otherwise regulate cannabis subject to certain limitations to include on-site consumption establishments; and 

WHEREAS, the County recognizes the significant planning, zoning, property and secondary effects surrounding or arising from the location and operation of businesses engaged in the growing, processing and/or or sale of cannabis to include on-site consumption establishments; and

<!-- page 2 -->
WHEREAS, the Maryland Cannabis Administration, pursuant to State law, will be awarding the first application round for cannabis business licenses for growers, processors and dispensaries on or before January 1, 2024; and, 

WHEREAS, the County will receive two standard dispensary licenses and is eligible for up to 12 total standard growing and processing licenses and up to 14 micro growing, processing and dispensing licenses in the first application round; and 

WHEREAS, the County currently has no zoning or other regulations for cannabis businesses; and 

WHEREAS, § 225-67 of the County Code provides that unclassified uses are subject to determination by the Board of Appeals and that once a use has been allowed by the Board, it shall be considered classified; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council considers the public interest to be best served by the adoption of zoning regulations through legislative bill establishing the appropriate locations for facilities for the growing, processing and dispensing of cannabis to include on-site consumption establishments; and 

WHEREAS, the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission has held hearings for zoning regulations for cannabis to include adoption of the Director’s Report and a recommendation for zoning requirements and regulations has been made to the County Council; and 

WHEREAS, the County requires additional time to undertake an appropriate study and obtain desired public input before establishing effective County ordinances and other necessary regulations applicable to businesses engaging in the growing, processing and/ or selling of cannabis to include on-site consumption establishments; and 

WHEREAS, the acceptance and processing of an application and/ or site plan and/ or issuance of other zoning approvals or business licenses for the operation of businesses engaged in the growing, processing and/ or selling of cannabis to include on-site consumption establishments, before appropriate study of the matter, obtaining desired public input, and consideration and approval of meaningful and effective County ordinances and/ or regulations governing the matter, would frustrate the County’ s ability to protect the health, safety and welfare of the residents of and visitors to the County and would be adverse to the orderly business development of the County; and 

WHEREAS, the County believes that a moratorium on the issuance of permits, licenses and approvals for facilities relating to the growing, processing and/or selling of cannabis to include on-site consumption establishments and related paraphernalia while the County gives due consideration to state regulations in crafting any zoning or other regulations for such facilities is necessary and appropriate, provided that the suspension continues for a period no longer than necessary to complete the assessment in the exercise of all due haste; and

<!-- page 3 -->
WHEREAS, completion of this process is anticipated to occur within six (6) months following the adoption of this Emergency Act; and 

WHEREAS, in the exercise of its police power as a charter county, Wicomico County, Maryland, through its elected officials, hereby finds that a moratorium bill is needed and deems that a minimum period of (6) six months is necessary for an adequate study of the subject, and for this reason will impose a moratorium on the issuance of (6) six months. 

SECTION I: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, TN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, as follows: 

- A. A Moratorium is hereby established on the processing, review, and approval of applications, site plans, permits, and other authorizations for the location, construction, and/or operation of businesses engaged in growing, processing, and/or selling cannabis to include on-site consumption establishments for a period of (6) six months. 

- B. The Moratorium shall apply prospectively only, beginning on the date this bill is enacted and (6) six months thereafter or upon the effective date of any legislative bill regarding cannabis zoning requirements and other regulations. 

SECTION II: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, based upon the award date of the first application round for cannabis business licenses and the number of licenses Wicomico County is eligible to receive, as stated above, and as there are no zoning requirements or regulations for cannabis businesses in Wicomico County an emergency exists, wherefore this Legislative Bill is passed as an emergency measure necessary for the immediate preservation of public health, safety and welfare which requires immediate action in the public interest, and subject to the laws of Maryland, the Wicomico County Charter, and the Wicomico County Code relating zoning regulations and procedures. 

SECTION III: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, THAT this bill shall be known as Emergency Legislative Bill No. 2023-15 and shall become effective immediately after its final passage. 

Certified correct as passed and adopted by the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland
