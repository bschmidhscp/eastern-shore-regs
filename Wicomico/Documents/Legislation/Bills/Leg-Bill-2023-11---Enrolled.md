COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLANI) LEGISLATIVE SESSION, 2023 BILL NO. 2023-11 

Introduced: July 5, 2023 

BY: The Wicomico County Council. 

AN ACT to amend Exhibit “B”, Exhibit “C” and Exhibit “F” of the Annual Budget and Appropriation Bill, Legislative Bill No. 2023-04, adopted June 96, 2023, to remove the salaries for the Assistant Director of Administration position, the Director of Public Works position and the Deputy Director of Public Works position. 

## PREAMBLE 

On June 96, 2023, the County Council enacted Legislative Bill No. 2023-04, the Annual Budget and Appropriation Bill adopting the Operating Budget (Exhibit B), Governmental and Enterprise Funds Budget (Exhibit C) and Position Control Document (Exhibit F) for fiscal year ending June 30, 2024. 

Pursuant to Charter Section 705.F, the County Council may decrease or delete any items in the budget except those required by the laws of this state or of this county, and except any provisions for debt service on obligations then outstanding or for estimated cash deficits. In the event the Council decreases or deletes any items(s) and the Executive and Council do not agree upon the expenditure of the surplus, the surplus shall be placed in the undesignated fund balance for future appropriation. 

Sections 315 and 413 of the County Charter provide that, within six months after election, the County Executive shall appoint the Director of Administration, Assistant Director of Administration, and all the heads of each department or agency in the executive branch, and the initial appointment of deputy directors, who shall all be subject to County Council confirmation. 

The County Executive has failed to submit the name of any individual to the County Council for confirmation of an appointment to the position of Assistant Director of Administration, Director of Public Works or Deputy Director of Public Works, and the six-month deadline for such appointment and submission has passed. 

To enforce the provisions of Charter Sections 315 and 413, the County Council desires to remove the salaries for the Assistant Director of Administration position, the Director of Public Works position and the Deputy Director of Public Works position from the Fiscal Year 24 budget until an individual has been confirmed by the County Council to serve as the Assistant Director of Administration andior Director of Public Works and/or Deputy Director of Public Works. 

SECTION I. BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that the current Operating Budget, Governmental and Enterprise Funds Budget, and Position Control Document for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2024, as set forth in Exhibits B, C and F of Legislative Bill No. 2023-04, be and are hereby amended in part by removing the salaries for the Assistant Director of Administration position, Director of Public Works position and Deputy Director of Public Works position, from Exhibits B, C and F of Legislative Bill No. 2023-04, which are amended hereby. 

SECTION II. BE IT FURTHER ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT at such time as this Bill

SECTION III. BE IT FURTHER ENACTED ANI ORDAiNED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT this bill shall be known as Legislative Bill No. 2023-1 1 and shall take effect sixty (60) days after its enactment, unless a proper Petition for Referendum thereof shall be filed prior to said date; in which event, the Bill shall not take effect until the expiration of thirty (30) days following the approval of this Bill by a majority of the qualified voters of the County voting in any such referendum.
