COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND LEGISLATIVE SESSION, 2026 

### BILL No. 2026-05 

Introduced:July<sup>7,2026</sup> 

# : ae,rc) ‘LED1 

BY: The Council President 

AN ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 39 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE TO RECOGNIZE THE WICOMICO COUNTY FIRE CHIEFS ASSOCIATION AS A COUNTYWIDE ORGANIZATION REPRESENTING THE FIRE CHIEFS OF THE VOLUNTEER FIRE DEPARTMENTS PROVIDING FIRE PROTECTION AND EMERGENCY SERVICES WITHIN WICOMICO COUNTY AND TO PROVIDE FOR ITS PARTICIPATION IN DISCUSSIONS AND NEGOTIATIONS CONCERNING COUNTYWIDE FIRE SERVICE AGREEMENTS AND POLICIES. 

WHEREAS,<sup>volunteerfire</sup> departments provide<sup>essential fire</sup> protection and emergency services throughout<sup>Wicomico</sup> County;<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>thefire</sup> chiefs<sup>of</sup> the<sup>volunteerfire</sup> departments possess significant operational expertise regarding fire protection, emergency response, staffing, equipment, training, and service delivery;<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>theWicomico</sup> County<sup>FireChiefsAssociation</sup> serves as a countywide organization representing<sup>thefirechiefsof thevolunteerfire</sup> departments serving<sup>Wicomico</sup> County; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Council findsthatthe</sup> public<sup>interestis</sup> served by encouraging communication and collaboration between County officials and the Wicomico County Fire Chiefs Association on matters affecting fire protection and emergency services; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County Council<sup>further</sup> finds that the Wicomico County Fire Chiefs Association should be afforded the opportunity to participate in discussions and negotiations concerning countywide fire service agreements and policies affecting fire protection and emergency services; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County Council intends<sup>that</sup> recognition<sup>of</sup> the Wicomico County Fire Chiefs Association shall supplement, and not diminish or replace, the role of the Wicomico County Volunteer Firemen's Association recognized elsewhere in the County Code. 

SECTION |: BEIT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND,<sup>INLEGISLATIVE</sup> SESSION, that Chapter 39, titled 'Fire and Ambulance Companies and Rescue Squads,’ is amended by adding a new Section 39-2 as follows: 

CHAPTER 39 

FIRE AND AMBULANCE COMPANIES AND RESCUE SQUADS 

§ 39-2. WICOMICO COUNTY FIRE CHIEFS ASSOCIATION. 

1

<!-- page 2 -->
A. THE WICOMICO COUNTY FIRE CHIEFS ASSOCIATION IS RECOGNIZED AS A COUNTYWIDE ORGANIZATION REPRESENTING THE FIRE CHIEFS OF THE VOLUNTEER FIRE DEPARTMENTS PROVIDING FIRE PROTECTION AND EMERGENCY SERVICES WITHIN WICOMICO COUNTY. 

B. THE ASSOCIATION SHALL ADVISE THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE, COUNTY COUNCIL, DIRECTOR OF EMERGENCY SERVICES, AND OTHER COUNTY OFFICIALS ON MATTERS AFFECTING FIRE PROTECTION AND EMERGENCY SERVICES WITHIN THE COUNTY. 

C. THE ASSOCIATION SHALL APPEAR BEFORE THE COUNTY COUNCIL IN A PUBLIC MEETING AT LEAST TWICE EACH CALENDAR YEAR TO INFORM THE PUBLIC OF RELEVANT ISSUES, ACTIVITIES, AND DEVELOPMENTS. 

D. THE ASSOCIATION, THROUGH ITS PRESIDENT, VICE PRESDIENT, AND OTHER REPRESENTATIVES AS DESIGNATED BY THE PRESIDENT AND VICE PRESIDENT OF THE ASSOCIATION, SHALL BE AFFORDED THE OPPORTUNITY TO PARTICIPATE IN DISCUSSIONS AND NEGOTIATIONS CONCERNING COUNTYWIDE FIRE SERVICE AGREEMENTS AND POLICIES AFFECTING FIRE PROTECTION AND EMERGENCY SERVICES. THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND/OR DIRECTOR OF ADMINISTRATION SHALL MEET WITH REPRESENTATIVES DESIGNATED BY THE ASSOCIATION AT LEAST TWO (2) TIMES EACH FISCAL YEAR AND AT LEAST ONE (1) SUCH MEETING SHALL OCCUR PRIOR TO THE EXECUTIVE’S SUBMISSION OF THE COUNTY EXPENSE BUDGET TO ALLOW THE ASSOCIATION TO PRESENT ITS SUGGESTIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS ON THE SALISBURY FHHRE/AMBULANCE, VOEUNTFEER AMBULANCE ANB<sup>VOLUNTEER COUNTYWIDE</sup> AMBULANCE AND FIRE BUDGETS FOR THE UPCOMING BUDGET YEAR. 

SECTION I: BE IT ENACTED that this Legislative Bill shall be known as Bill No. 2026-05 of Wicomico County, Maryland,<sup>andshalltakeeffect</sup> sixty (60) days<sup>afteritsfinal</sup> passage,<sup>unlessa</sup> proper Petition for Referendum thereof shall be filed prior to said date; in which event, the Bill shall not take effect until the expiration<sup>of</sup> thirty (30) days following<sup>the</sup> approval<sup>ofthisBill</sup> by a majority of the qualified voters of the County voting<sup>in</sup> any<sup>suchreferendum.</sup>
