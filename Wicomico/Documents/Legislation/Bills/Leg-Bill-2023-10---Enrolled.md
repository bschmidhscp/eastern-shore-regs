## COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION, 2023 

## LEGISLATIVE BILL 2023-10 

Introduced: July 5, 2023 By: The Council President at the request of the County Executive 

AN ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 225 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE, TITLED “ZONING,” SECTION 225-12, ENTITLED “LOTS DIVIDED BY ZONING DISTRICT BOUNDARY LINES” TO ALLOW USES IN A ZONING DISTRICT THAT COVERS THE MAJORITY OF THE LAND AREA OF A PARCEL TO EXTEND OVER THE ENTIRE PARCEL. 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Wicomico County is empowered to regulate and restrict the use of land and buildings, the density of population, and the location and use of buildings, structures and land for residential or other purposes and to adopt from time to time such other regulations and maps as may be required; and 

WHEREAS, all such zoning regulations may be enacted for the purpose of: preserving and promoting health, safety, and general welfare of the citizens of Wicomico County; guiding the future growth and development of the County generally in accordance with the overall Comprehensive Plan or special area plans in a manner which represents the most beneficial land use relationships among the residential, nonresidential, and public areas; and, providing for the orderly growth and development of the County in a manner which will protect, conserve, and stabilize the value of land, structure, and neighborhoods; and 

WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Commission, after a public hearing, recommended the amendment of Chapter 225 titled “Zoning,” Section 225-12, entitled “Lots Divided By Zoning District Boundary Lines” to allow uses in a zoning district that covers the majority of the land area of a parcel to extend over the entire parcel; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council, after reviewing the record and receiving testimony determined the aforesaid amendment was appropriate. 

SECTION I: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that Chapter 225, Section 225-12, entitled “Lots Divided By Zoning District Boundary Lines”, be and is amended as follows: 

Amend Section 225-12, Lots Divided By Zoning District Boundary Lines. 

- A Whenever a single lot two (2) acres or less in size is located within two or more different zones, the zoning regulations applicable to the zone which constitutes the larger portion of the lot shal-1 apply to the entire lot AS DETERMINED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF PLANNING, ZONING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT MAY APPLY TO THE ENTIRE LOT. 

- B. Whenever a single lot greater than two acres in size is located within two or more different zones, each portion of that lot shall be subject to all the regulations applicable to the zone in which it is located. 

SECTION II: BE IT ENACTED that this Legislative Bill shall be known 2023 10 of Wicomico County, Maryland, and shall take effect sixty (60) days after its final passage, unless a proper Petition for
