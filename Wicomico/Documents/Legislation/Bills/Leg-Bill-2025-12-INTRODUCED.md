AN ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 138 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE TITLED “ELECTRICAL STANDARDS” TO REFLECT THE CURRENT EDITION OF THE NATIONAL ELECTRICAL CODE AND TO AMEND THE PROCESS OF PROMULGATION OF FUTURE EDITIONS. 

WHEREAS, Wicomico County utilizes the National Electric Code, including all appendices thereof, as the respective electrical code for Wicomico County; and 

WHEREAS, from time to time, the National Electrical Code, including all appendices thereof, are respectively amended and updated through the release of a new version; and 

WHEREAS, until Wicornico County adopts the new versions, the old versions control as the electrical code for Wicomico County. 

Section I. BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that Chapter 138 of the Wicomico County Code, titled “Electrical Standards” is hereby amended and shall read as follows: 

Chapter 138 

Electrical Standards 

Article I 

General Provisions 

§<sup>138-2Adoption of standards byreference.</sup> 

There is hereby adopted by the County Council for the purpose of establishing rules and regulations for the installations, renewal, extension and reception of electrical wiring and electrical apparatus in existing buildings, structures or outdoor electrical displays or signs, or in the construction, reconstruction, alteration or repair of buildings, structures or outdoor electrical displays or signs, that certain code known as the “National Electrical Code,” as recommended by the National Fire Protection Association, being particularly the current edition thereof and the whole thereof, hereinafter referred to as the “National Electrical Code”; and the same is hereby adopted and incorporated as fully as if set out at length herein, and from the date on which this chapter shall take effect, the provisions

<!-- page 2 -->
thereof and all amendments thereto shall be controlling for all existing and future electrical wiring and apparatus in buildings, structures or outdoor electrical displays or signs within the corporate limits of Wicomico County, Maryland. 

A. ADOPTION OF THE CODE. 

THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY HEREBY ADOPTS THE PROVISIONS SET FORTH IN THE NATIONAL ELECTRIC CODE (HEREINAFTER REFERRED TO AS ‘NEC”), 2023 EDITION, INCLUDING THE APPENDICES, AS THE ELECTRICAL CODE FOR WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND AND INCORPORATED BY REFERENCE. THE PROVISIONS SHALL BE CONTROLLING FOR ALL BUILDINGS WITHIN THE CORPORATE LIMITS OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, UNLESS OTHERWISE EXEMPTED HEREIN. 

B. PROMULGATION OF NEW EDITION AND ADOPTION BY WICOMICO COUNTY 

1. WITHIN 180 DAYS OF PROMULGATION OF A NEW EDITION OF THE NEC BY THE NATIONAL FIRE PROTECTION ASSOCIATION, THE DEPARTMENT OF PLANNING, ZONING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT’S CHIEF BUILDING INSPECTOR SHALL REVIEW THE NEW EDITION WITH THE WICOMICO COUNTY BOARD OF ELECTRICAL EXAMINERS TO DETERMINE WHETHER ANY MODIFICATIONS ARE RECOMMENDED. 

2. THE CHIEF BUILDING INSPECTOR AND THE PRESIDENT OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY BOARD OF ELECTRICAL EXAMINERS SHALL NOTIFY THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND COUNTY COUNCIL, IN WRITING, OF SUGGESTED MODIFICATIONS TO THE NEW EDITION, IF ANY, AND THE JUSTIFICATION FOR EACH MODIFICATION. 180 DAYS FOLLOWING THE NOTIFICATION BY THE CHIEF BUILDING INSPECTOR AND THE PRESIDENT OF THE BOARD, ANY PROVISION OF THE NEW EDITION, TO INCLUDE APPENDICES, NOT MODIFIED BY LAW, SHALL BE ADOPTED AS THE ELECTRIC CODE FOR WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND AND INCORPORATED BY REFERENCE, BUT FOR THE PROVISIONS IN CONFLICT WITH THE MODIFICATIONS CODIFIED IN §138-2.1. PURSUANT TO SECTION 302 OF THE COUNTY CHARTER, THE NEW EDITION MAY BE MODIFIED AT ANY TIME. 

3. UNTIL A NEW EDITION OF THE NEC IS PROMULGATED BY THE NATIONAL FIRE PROTECTION ASSOCIATION AND ADOPTED PURSUANT TO THIS SUBSECTION, THE FORMER EDITION OF THE NEC IS THE ELECTRIC CODE FOR WICOMICO COUNTY.
