BUDGET CONDITIONS TO LEGISLATIVE BILL 2024-02 

1. The Fruitland Primary School project is to be funded with $10 million in bond funding and $10.5 million in pay-go funding. In accordance with Section 313 B of the County Charter, all issues involving bonding shall be in such amounts as may be deemed advisable by the County Council. Furthermore, Section 313 F provides that any borrowing to finance capital projects must be authorized by a public local law of the Council passed in accordance with the Charter. Additionally, Section 5-104 (a) of the Maryland Education Article requires the County Council to levy and collect a tax on assessable property of the county which, together with other local revenue available, including income tax revenues and bond money, and together with estimated revenues and funds from all sources, will produce the amounts necessary to meet the appropriations made in the approved annual budget of the Board of Education. 

2. Position No. 100654-Grant Administrator shall provide quarterly reports to the County Council in open session.
