§29-26 USE, DISTRIBUTION AND REPORTING 

A. PERMITTED USES. 

1. THE FUND MAY ONLY BE USED FOR: 

A. FUNDING COMMUNITY-BASED INITIATIVES INTENDED TO BENEFIT LOW-INCOME COMMUNITIES; 

B. FUNDING COMMUNITY-BASED INITIATIVES THAT SERVE DISPROPORTIONATELY IMPACTED AREAS; AND 

C. ANY RELATED ADMINISTRATIVE EXPENSES. 

2. PROHIBITED USES. 

A. MONEY MAY NOT BE EXPENDED FROM THE FUND FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES OR ACTIVITIES. 

B. MONEY EXPENDED FROM THE FUND IS SUPPLEMENTAL TO AND MAY NOT SUPPLANT FUNDING THAT OTHERWISE WOULD BE APPROPRIATED FOR PREEXISTING LOCAL GOVERNMENT PROGRAMS. 

B. DISTRIBUTION OF THE FUND 

1. THE COUNTY COUNCIL SHALL AWARD AND APPROPRIATE ALL MONEY RECEIVED FROM THE OFFICE SOCIAL EQUITY FOR THE COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AND REPAIR FUND, WHICH MAY ONLY BE USED TO FUND INITIATIVES IN ACCORDANCE WITH SUBSECTION (A)(l) OF THIS SECTION. THE LOCAL MANAGEMENT BOARD SHALL DISTRIBUTE ALL FUNDS. 

2. THE LOCAL MANAGEMENT BOARD SHALL DEVELOP CRITERIA FOR MAKING AN AWARD PRIOR TO THE SOLICITATION OF PROPOSALS. IN DEVELOPING CRITERIA TO MAKE AN AWARD, THE LOCAL MANAGEMENT BOARD SHALL CONSIDER ANY GUIDANCE PROVIDED BY THE OFFICE OF SOCIAL EQUITY. 

3. A COMMUNITY-BASED ORGANIZATION PREVIOUSLY AWARDED MONEY FROM THE FUND MAY NOT APPLY FOR A NEW AWARD UNTIL ALL REPORTING, IN ACCORDANCE WITH PARAGRAPH I OF THIS SUBSECTION, FOR A PREVIOUS AWARD HAS BEEN COMPLETED AND APPROVED BY THE LOCAL MANAGEMENT BOARD. 

4. ANY MONEY NOT AWARDED THAT FISCAL YEAR SHALL CARRY OVER TO THE NEXT FISCAL YEAR.
