COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION, 2024 

BILL No. 2024-08 

Introduced: September 17, 2024 

- BY: The Council President 

AN ACT TO ADD CHAPTER 163 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE, TITLED “INVASIVE BAMBOO CONTROL ACT” TO ESTABLISH REGULATIONS ON CONTROLLING INVASIVE BAMBOO. 

WHEREAS, controlling invasive bamboo species is crucial for environmental conservation and maintaining ecological balance; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 1-1318 of the Local Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, local governments are authorized to regulate invasive bamboo; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council desires to establish regulations on controlling invasive bamboo. 

SECTION 1: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that Chapter 163, titled, “Invasive Bamboo Control Act” shall be established as follows: 

CHAPTER 163 

INVASIVE BAMBOO CONTROL ACT 

§163-1. DEFINITIONS. 

AS USED IN THIS ARTICLE, THE FOLLOWING TERMS HAVE THE MEANING INDICATED: 

# INVASIVE BAMBOO 

ANY BAMBOO SPECIES FROM THE GENUS PHYLLOSTACHYS. 

PERSON 

ANY NATURAL PERSON, FIRM, CORPORATION, PARTNERSHIP, ORGANIZATION OR GROUP, HOWEVER ORGANIZED. 

§163-2 PROHIBITION. 

- A. NO PERSON SHALL SELL INVASIVE BAMBOO. 

- B. NO PERSON SHALL PLANT INVASIVE BAMBOO. 

§163-3 PUBLIC EDUCATION INITIATIVE. 

- A. PUBLIC AWARENESS CAMPAIGN. THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE SHALL DEVELOP AND IMPLEMENT AN EDUCATIONAL CAMPAIGN TO INFORM THE PUBLIC ABOUT THE DANGERS OF INVASIVE BAMBOO SPECIES. THIS CAMPAIGN SHALL INCLUDE INFORMATION ON THE ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACTS OF INVASIVE BAMBOO, THE

<!-- page 2 -->
IMPORTANCE OF USING NATIVE OR NON-INVASIVE PLANTS, AND THE PROVISIONS OF THIS LEGISLATION. 

- B. DISTRIBUTION OF EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS. EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS SHALL BE MADE AVAILABLE TO THE PUBLIC THROUGH VARIOUS CHANNELS, INCLUDING THE COUNTY’S OFFICIAL WEBSITE, SOCIAL MEDIA PLATFORMS, AND PRINTED MATERIALS DISTRIBUTED AT COUNTY OFFICES AND PUBLIC EVENTS. 

- C. PARTNERSHIPS WITH LOCAL NURSERIES AND GARDEN CENTERS. THE COUNTY SHALL WORK WITH LOCAL NURSERIES, GARDEN CENTERS, AND OTHER RELEVANT BUSINESSES TO EDUCATE CUSTOMERS ABOUT INVASIVE BAMBOO SPECIES AND ENCOURAGE THE SALE OF NATIVE OR NON-INVASIVE ALTERNATIVES. 

SECTION II: BE IT ENACTED that this Legislative Bill shall be known 2024-08 of Wicomico County, Maryland, and shall take effect sixty (60) days after its final passage, unless a proper Petition for Referendum thereof shall be filed prior to said date; in which event, the Bill shall not take effect until the expiration of thirty (30) days following the approval of this Bill by a majority of the qualified voters of Wicomico County voting in any such referendum. 

Certified correct as passed and adopted by the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland this day of (‘j, 2024.
