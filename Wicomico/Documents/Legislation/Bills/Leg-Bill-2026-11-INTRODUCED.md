# Leg-Bill-2026-11-INTRODUCED

## COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION, 2026 

EMERGENCY LEGISLATIVE BILL No. 2026-11 

Introduced: August 18, 2026 

BY: The Council President 

AN EMERGENCY ACT TO ADD CHAPTER 49 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE, TITLED “NURSING HOME” TO ESTABLISH RULES AND REGULATIONS ON THE OPERATION OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY NURSING HOME, THE APPOINTMENT OF A DIRECTOR FOR THE NURSING HOME AND THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NURSING HOME COMMISSION TO AID IN MAINTAINING GOOD GOVERNMENT, HEALTH AND WELFARE OF THE COUNTY. 

WHEREAS, pursuant<sup>toMd.Code</sup> Ann.,<sup>LocalGovernment</sup> §10-206 (a) (2)<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Councilis</sup> authorized to pass an ordinance to maintain the good government, health and welfare of the county; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>Wicomico</sup> County acquired<sup>theWicomico</sup> Nursing<sup>Home</sup> pursuant<sup>to</sup> Chapter 5,<sup>Bill</sup> 5 in June of 1972; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant<sup>tosaid</sup> Legislative<sup>Bill</sup> 5,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Councilisauthorizedtomaintainand</sup> operate<sup>the</sup> nursing<sup>home</sup> including,<sup>butnotlimitedtothe</sup> employment<sup>of such</sup> personnel necessary<sup>to</sup> operate<sup>andmaintaintheWicomico</sup> Nursing<sup>Homeandtoenterinto</sup> agreements,<sup>contractsor</sup> undertakings necessary<sup>for the</sup> operation<sup>of the</sup> Nursing Home;<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Councilhasbeenmadeawareofissues</sup> relating<sup>tothe</sup> operation<sup>of</sup> the nursing home, inciuding<sup>financialand</sup> personnel issues,<sup>suchthatWicomico</sup> County<sup>hasbeen</sup> requested to provide assistance and guidance to the Nursing Home and the Nursing Home Director relating to the operation<sup>of the</sup> Nursing Home;<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS, notwithstanding<sup>thefact that</sup> pursuant<sup>to</sup> Legislative<sup>Bill</sup> 5 referenced above and Wicomico County’s ownership of the Nursing Home, the County Executive has stated that the county has “no say to the operations of the nursing home”; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>inviewof thesuddenand</sup> unexpected<sup>issueswhichhavearisen</sup> relating<sup>to</sup> the operation<sup>of theWicomico</sup> Nursing<sup>Homeandtheconfusion</sup> regarding<sup>theroleof the</sup> County<sup>Executive</sup> and the County Council in the operation of the Nursing Home, the County Council believes that immediate action to establish rules and regulations on the operation of the Nursing Home and to establish a Nursing Home Commission to advise on the operation of the Nursing Home is in the public interest. 

SECTION 1: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND,<sup>INLEGISLATIVE</sup> SESSION,<sup>that</sup> Chapter 49, titled, “Nursing<sup>HomeAct”shall</sup> be established<sup>as</sup> follows: 

CHAPTER 49 NURSING HOME ACT

<!-- page 2 -->
ARTICLE 1. WICOMICO NURSING HOME 

§49-1. CREATION AND DUTIES OF THE NURSING HOME. 

- A. PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY OF THE ANNOTATED CODE OF MARYLAND, LOCAL GOVERNMENT ARTICLE §9-201 A. (3) and WICOMICO COUNTY LEGISLATIVE BILL, CHAPTER 5, BILL 5, WICOMICO COUNTY SHALL HOLD AND OPERATE THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME UNDER THE DIRECTION OF THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR WHO SHALL PERFORM SUCH DUTIES AND CONDUCT SUCH WORK AS THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND THE COUNTY COUNCIL MAY REQUIRE. IT SHALL BE THE RESPONSIBILITY OF THE NURSING HOME TO OPERATE A FACILITY THAT OFFERS NONACUTE INPATIENT CARE TO PATIENTS SUFFERING FROM A DISEASE,<sup>CHRONIC</sup> ILLNESS, CONDITION,<sup>DISABILITYOFADVANCED</sup> AGE,<sup>ORTERMINALDISEASE</sup> REQUIRING<sup>MAXIMAL</sup> NURSING CARE WITHOUT CONTINUOUS HOSPITAL SERVICES AND WHO REQUIRE MEDICAL SERVICES AND NURSING SERVICES RENDERED BY OR UNDER THE SUPERVISION OF A LICENSED NURSE TOGETHER WITH CONVALESCENT, RESTORATIVE, OR REHABILITATIVE SERVICES IN ACCORDANCE WITH ALL APPLICABLE FEDERAL, STATE AND LOCAL LAW. 

## § 49-2 NURSING HOME DIRECTOR. 

- A. THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR SHALL BE APPOINTED BY THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE WITH THE APPROVAL OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL. THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR WILL FUNCTION AS THE HEAD OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY NURSING HOME AND SHALL REPORT TO THE DIRECTOR OF ADMINISTRATION. THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR SHALL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE OVERALL MANAGEMENT AND OPERATION OF THE NURSING HOME. THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR SHALL MEET WITH THE COUNTY COUNCIL QUARTERLY, OR AS DIRECTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL,<sup>TOREPORTONTHEOPERATIONANDCONDITIONOFTHENURSINGHOME.THE</sup> COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND/OR THE COUNTY COUNCIL, WITH THE AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF FIVE (5) MEMBERS OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL, SHALL HAVE THE AUTHORITY TO TERMINATE THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR. 

   - THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR SHALL PREPARE AN ANNUAL BUDGET, TO INCLUDE ALL COSTS, EXPENSES AND PERSONNEL COSTS, FOR THE NURSING HOME. THE ANNUAL BUDGET SHALL BE PRESENTED TO THE DIRECTOR OF ADMINISTRATION FOR REVIEW AND APPROVAL ON OR BEFORE THE FIRST OF APRIL EACH YEAR. A COPY OF THE ANNUAL BUDGET SHALL BE SUBMITTED TO THE COUNTY COUNCIL SIMULTANEOUSLY WITH ITS SUBMISSION TO THE DIRECTOR OF ADMINISTRATION. 

   - THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR SHALL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR ENSURING THAT THE NURSING HOME HAS ALL REQUIRED INSURANCE COVERAGES, INCLUDING, BUT NECESSARILY LIMITED TO WORKERS COMPENSATION, HAZARD AND FIRE, COMMERCIAL LIABILITY AND ERRORS AND OMISSIONS INSURANCE. PROOF OF ALL INSURANCE POLICIES MAINTAINED BY THE NURSING HOME SHALL BE SUBMITTED TO THE DIRECTOR OF ADMINISTRATION AND THE COUNTY COUNCIL AT LEAST ANNUALLY. 

   - THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR SHALL FULLY COOPERATE AND PROVIDE ALL REASONABLE INFORMATION REQUESTED BY THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION.

§ 49-3 NURSING HOME PERSONNEL<sup>SYSTEM.</sup> 

THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE SHALL RECOMMEND A PERSONNEL SYSTEM FOR THE NURSING HOME TO THE COUNTY COUNCIL FOR APPROVAL ON OR BEFORE NOVEMBER 17, 2026, BY RESOLUTION, AFTERA PUBLIC HEARING, WHICH PERSONNEL SYSTEM SHALL HAVE THE FORCE OF LAW. UPON RECOMMENDATION OF THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE, THE COUNTY COUNCIL MAY MODIFY OR REVISE THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME PERSONNEL SYSTEM BY RESOLUTION AFTER A PUBLIC HEARING. 

ARTICLE ll. WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION. 

§49-4. CREATION OF<sup>THE</sup> NURSING<sup>HOMECOMMISSION.</sup> 

THERE IS HEREBY CREATED AND ESTABLISHED THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION TO SERVE IN AN ADVISORY CAPACITY TO THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR, THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND THE COUNTY COUNCIL. 

§49-5. MEMBERSHIP. 

THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION SHALL CONSIST OF SEVEN MEMBERS, ONE OF WHOM SHALL BE A MEMBER OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL, ONE OF WHOM SHALL BE THE DIRECTOR OF ADMINISTRATION OR HIS/HER DESIGNEE, ONE OF WHOM SHALL BE THE WICOMICO COUNTY HEALTH OFFICER OR HIS/HER DESIGNEE, ONE OF WHOM SHALL BE A CURRENT OR RETIRED ACCOUNTANT,<sup>ONEOFWHOMSHALLBEACURRENTORRETIREDMEDICAL</sup> DOCTOR;<sup>ONEOFWHOM</sup> SHALL BE A LOCAL ATTORNEY; AND ONE OF WHOM SHALL BE A CURRENT OR RETIRED REGISTERED NURSE. THE COMMISSION MEMBERS SHALL ELECT A CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FROM THE COMMISSION MEMBERS. THE MEMBERS OF THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION SHALL SERVE WITHOUT COMPENSATION. 

§49-6 TERMS; VACANCIES; REMOVAL. 

- A. THE FAMILY MEMBER COMMISSIONER, THE MEDICAL DOCTOR COMMISSIONER, THE REGISTERED NURSE COMMISSIONER, THE INSURANCE INDUSTRY COMMISSIONER AND THE CURRENTLY EMPLOYED REGISTERED NURSE COMMISSIONER SHALL SERVE INITIAL TERMS OF TWO YEARS AND THEREAFTER SHALL BE APPOINTED FOR TWO-YEAR TERMS OR UNTIL THEIR SUCCESSORS ARE DULY APPOINTED AND QUALIFIED. MEMBERS OF THE NURSING HOME COMMISSION ARE ELIGIBLE FOR REAPPOINTMENT. 

   - VACANCIES OCCURRING IN THE FAMILY MEMBER COMMISSIONER, THE MEDICAL DOCTOR COMMISSIONER,<sup>THEREGISTEREDNURSE</sup> COMMISSIONER,<sup>THEINSURANCEINDUSTRY</sup> COMMISSIONER AND THE CURRENTLY EMPLOYED REGISTERED NURSE COMMISSIONER SHALL BE FILLED FOR THE UNEXPIRED TERMS BY THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND CONFIRMED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL. THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND THE COUNTY COUNCIL, JOINTLY, SHALL HAVE THE AUTHORITY TO REMOVE ANY COMMISSION MEMBER WHEN IN THEIR DISCRETION THE BEST INTEREST OF THE COMMUNITY SHALL BE SERVED. 

# §49-7<sup>CHAIRPERSON</sup> AND<sup>VICECHAIRPERSON.</sup>

<!-- page 4 -->
THE CHAIRPERSON SHALL PRESIDE OVER ALL MEETINGS OF THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION. THE VICE CHAIRPERSON SHALL PERFORM THE DUTIES OF THE CHAIRPERSON IN THE CHAIRPERSON’S ABSENCE OR INCAPACITY. THE CHAIRPERSON OR THE VICE CHAIRPERSON SHALL MEET WITH THE COUNTY COUNCIL AT LEAST ONCE EACH YEAR AND PROVIDE A REPORT ON THE ACTIVITIES OF THE COMMISSION AND THE OPERATION OF THE NURSING HOME. 

§49-8 MEETINGS. 

THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION SHALL CONVENE AT REGULAR MEETINGS AT LEAST ONCE EACH MONTH AND SHALL HAVE SUCH SPECIAL MEETINGS AS THE CHAIRPERSON SHALL CONVENE OR UPON REQUEST OF THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE OR THE COUNTY COUNCIL. WHENEVER POSSIBLE THE MEETINGS SHALL OCCUR AT THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME. 

§49-9 AVAILABILITY OF RESOURCES AND SERVICES. 

THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION SHALL HAVE AVAILABLE TO IT THE RESOURCES AND SERVICES OF THE NURSING HOME, THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR AND/OR WICOMICO COUNTY. 

# §49-10 RESPONSIBILITIES. 

THE WICOMICO NURSING HOME COMMISSION SHALL ACT IN AN ADVISORY CAPACITY AND SHALL REVIEW AND MAKE RECOMMENDATIONS TO THE NURSING HOME DIRECTOR, THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND THE COUNTY COUNCIL WITH RESPECT TO THE FOLLOWING: 

- A. ALL MATTERS CONCERNING COMPREHENSIVE PLANNING, AS WELL AS FORMULATION OF PLANS,<sup>FORTHENURSINGHOMEANDTHECAREOFITSPATIENTS.</sup> 

   - ALL MATTERS CONCERNING THE OPERATION OF THE NURSING HOME INCLUDING STAFFING, MAINTENANCE,<sup>TRAININGANDBILLING.</sup> 

   - ALL MATTERS CONCERNING NECESSARY INSURANCE COVERAGE THAT NEEDS TO BE MAINTAINED BY THR NURSING HOME. 

   - ALL MATTERS CONCERNING AUDITS OF THE NURSING HOME AND ITS OPERATION.
