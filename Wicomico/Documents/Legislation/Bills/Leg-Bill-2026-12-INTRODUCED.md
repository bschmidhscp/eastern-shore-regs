# Leg-Bill-2026-12-INTRODUCED

<!-- page 1 -->
COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

2026 Legislative Session 

Legislative Day<sup>No.18</sup> 

Legislative<sup>Bill2026-12</sup> 

Introduced: September 1, 2026 

Introduced by: The Council President 

AN ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 90 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE, TITLED “TAX EXEMPTIONS” TO ADD A NEW SECTION 90-3 BE TITLED, “SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING TAX CREDIT”. 

WHEREAS,<sup>theWicomico</sup> County<sup>Councilisauthorizedunderthe</sup> Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland to grant tax credits on certain property; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>theWicomico</sup> County<sup>Councildesiresto</sup> provide<sup>alocal</sup> property tax credit to Salisbury Neighborhood Housing Service, Inc. to encourage acquisition, rehabilitation, and transfer of properties for affordable housing. 

Section I. BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that Chapter 90 of the Wicomico County Code, Titled,<sup>“Tax</sup> Exemptions”<sup>is</sup> hereby<sup>amendedtoaddSection</sup> 90-3, titled, “Salisbury Neighborhood Housing<sup>Tax</sup> Credit”,<sup>toreadasfollows:</sup> 

Chapter<sup>90</sup> 

Tax Exemptions 

SECTION 90-3 SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING TAX CREDIT. 

- A. THERE IS HEREBY ESTABLISHED A 100% PROPERTY TAX CREDIT ON REAL PROPERTY OWNED BY SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING SERVICES, INC., (“SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING”) PROVIDED THAT: 

   1. THE PROPERTY IS OWNED WITH THE INTENTION TO REHABILITATE AND TRANSFER IT IN THE NEAR FUTURE FOR AFFORDABLE HOMEOWNERSHIP; 

   - . THE PROPERTY IS USED EXCLUSIVELY FOR DEVELOPMENT, REHABILITATION AND TRANSFER TO A PRIVATE CITIZEN. 

   - . THE PROPERTY IS NOT OCCUPIED BY ADMINISTRATIVE OR WAREHOUSE BUILDINGS OWNED BY SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING;<sup>AND</sup> 

   - . THE PROPERTY QUALIFIES AS “REAL PROPERTY” UNDER COUNTY TAX LAW. 

- B. APPLICABILITY PERIOD AND TERMS OF THE TAX CREDIT.

<!-- page 2 -->
1. THE TAX CREDIT IS NOT AVAILABLE UNTIL TAX YEAR 2028, WHICH BEGINS JULY 1, 2027. 

         - THE TAX CREDIT FOR EACH PROPERTY SHALL BE FOR NO MORE THAN FIVE CONSECUTIVE TAX YEARS OR UPON TRANSFER OF THE PROPERTY. A PROPERTY WHICH WAS NOT TRANSFERRED WITHIN FIVE TAX YEARS IS NOT ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE THE TAX CREDIT AGAIN. FURTHER, UPON THE EXPIRATION OF THE TAX CREDIT, EITHER DUE TO A LAPSE IN TIME OR TRANSFER OF THE PROPERTY, SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING SHALL PAY THE FULL AMOUNT OF TAXES THAT ACCRUED DURING THE TAX CREDIT PERIOD; HOWEVER,<sup>NOINTERESTONTHEACCRUEDTAXESSHALL</sup> BE OWED BY SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING OR 

         - COLLECTED BY WICOMICO COUNTY. 

         - THE TAX CREDIT SHALL BE APPLIED TO THE DISCOUNT AMOUNT AVAILABLE FOR PAYMENT MADE ON OR BEFORE AUGUST 31ST OF EACH FISCAL YEAR ON THE FULL ANNUAL TAX BILL. 

         - IF A PROPERTY RECEIVING THE TAX CREDIT IS REASSESSED WHILE RECEIVING THE TAX CREDIT, THE AMOUNT OF TAXES DUE, FOLLOWING THE REASSESSMENT, SHALL BE BASED ON THE UPDATED ASSESSMENT AMOUNT AS DETERMINED BY THE MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF ASSESSMENTS AND TAXATION. 

         - THIRTY (30) DAYS PRIOR TO TRANSFER OF THE PROPERTY, SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING SHALL NOTIFY, IN WRITING,<sup>THEWICOMICOCOUNTYFINANCEDEPARTMENTTHATA</sup> SALE OF THE PROPERTY WILL OCCUR IN THE NEXT 30 DAYS. 

   - C. APPLICATION AND ADMINISTRATION. 

      1. APPLICATION 

- THE WICOMICO COUNTY FINANCE DEPARTMENT SHALL PREPARE AN APPLICATION FOR THE TAX CREDIT BY MARCH 1, 2027. THE APPLICATION SHALL BE POSTED ON THE COUNTY’S WEBSITE. SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING MUST APPLY FOR THE TAX CREDIT UNDER THIS SECTION BY SUBMITTING THE COMPLETED APPLICATION TO THE WICOMICO COUNTY FINANCE DEPARTMENT BY APRIL 1 EACH YEAR. THE FINANCE DEPARTMENT SHALL DETERMINE WHETHER A PROPERTY QUALIFIES<sup>FORTHETAXDEFERRALBY</sup> JUNE<sup>1EACHYEARAND</sup> SEND WRITTEN NOTICE OF THE DECISION TO SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING. THE NOTICE SHALL INDICATE THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF TAXES CREDITED TO DATE AND HOW MANY YEARS OF POTENTIAL CREDIT REMAIN.

<!-- page 3 -->
2. REPORTING REQUIREMENTS. IN ADDITION TO THE REQUIREMENTS OF SECTION A, TO QUALIFY FOR THE CREDIT, SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING SHALL SUBMIT AN ANNUAL WRITTEN REPORT TO THE COUNTY FINANCE DEPARTMENT BY APRIL 1ST DOCUMENTING: 

i. ALL OF SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING'S REAL PROPERTY HOLDINGS IN WICOMICO COUNTY; AND ii. ALL TRANSACTIONS INVOLVING SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING’S REAL PROPERTY HOLDINGS IN WICOMICO COUNTY, INCLUDING, BUT, NOT NECESSARILY LIMITED TO, THE DATE THE PROPERTY WAS ACQUIRED OR TRANSFERRED,<sup>THEPARTIESINVOLVEDINTHE</sup> ACQUISITION OR TRANSFER, THE PURPOSE OF ACQUISITION AND THE CURRENT USE OF THE PROPERTY. 

3. RIGHT TO DENY CREDIT. THE FINANCE DEPARTMENT SHALL DENY A TAX CREDIT ON ANY PROPERTY THAT DOES NOT MEET ALL ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS SET FORTH IN THIS SECTION. 

- D. NO REFUNDS SHALL BE ISSUED ON TAXES PAID BY SALISBURY NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSING, PRIOR TO JULY 1, 2027. 

SECTION<sup>I:</sup> BE IT FURTHER ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT this bill shall be known as Legislative Bill No. 2026-12 and shall take effect sixty (60) days after its enactment,<sup>unlessa</sup> proper<sup>PetitionforReferendumthereof shallbefiled</sup> prior<sup>tosaid</sup> date;<sup>in</sup> which event, the Bill shall not take effect until the expiration of thirty (30) days following the approval of<sup>thisBill</sup> by<sup>a</sup> majority<sup>ofthe</sup> qualified<sup>votersof</sup> the County voting<sup>in</sup> any<sup>such</sup> referendum.
