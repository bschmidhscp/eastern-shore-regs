COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND 

2025 Legislative Session 

Legislative Day No. 08 

Legislative Bill 2025- 05 

Introduced: April 15, 2025 

Introduced by: The Council President at the request of the County Executive. 

AN ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 117 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE TITLED “BUILDING CONSTRUCTION” TO REFLECT THE CURRENT EDITIONS OF THE INTERNATIONAL RESIDENTIAL CODE, THE INTERNATIONAL BUILDING CODE AND THE INTERNATIONAL ENERGY CONSERVATION CODE AND TO AMEND THE PROCESS OF PROMULGATION OF FUTURE EDITIONS. 

WHEREAS, Wicomico County utilizes the International Residential Code, the International Building Code, and the International Energy Conservation Code, including all appendices thereof, as the respective building codes for Wicomico County; and 

WHEREAS, from time to time, the International Residential Code, the International Building Code, and the International Energy Conservation Code, including all appendices thereof are respectively amended and updated through the release of a new version; and 

WHEREAS, until Wicornico County adopts the new versions, the old versions control as the respective building codes for Wicornico County. 

Section 1. BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, [N LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that Chapter 117 of the Wicomico County Code, titled “Building Construction” is hereby amended and shall read as follows: 

Chapter 117 

Building Construction 

## Article I 

Wicomico County Building Construction 

§<sup>117-2AdoptionofInternationalBuildingCode.</sup> 

A. ADOPTION OF THE CODE. 

The County Council of Wicornico County hereby adopts the provisions set forth in the International Building Code (hereinafter referred to as “IBC”), 2015 2021 Edition, including the appendices, as the building code for Wicornico County, Maryland and incorporated by reference. The provisions shall be controlling for all buildings within the corporate limits of Wicomico County, Maryland, unless otherwise exempted herein.

<!-- page 2 -->
- B. PROMULGATION OF NEW EDITION AND ADOPTION BY WICOMICO COUNTY 1. WITHIN NINETY (90) DAYS OF PROMULGATION OF A NEW EDITION OF THE IBC BY THE INTERNATIONAL CODE COUNCIL, THE DEPARTMENT OF PLANNING, ZONING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT’S CHIEF BUILDING INSPECTOR SHALL REVIEW THE NEW EDITION TO DETERMINE WHETHER ANY MODIFICATiONS ARE RECOMMENDED. 

2. THE CHIEF BUILDING INSPECTOR SHALL NOTIFY THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND COUNTY COUNCIL, IN WRITING, OF SUGGESTED MODIFICATIONS TO THE NEW EDITION, IF ANY, AND THE JUSTIFICATION FOR EACH MODIFICATION. 180 DAYS FOLLOWING THE NOTIFICATION BY THE CHiEF BUILDING INSPECTOR, ANY PROVISION OF THE NEW EDITION, TO INCLUDE APPENDICES, NOT MODIFIED BY LAW, SHALL BE ADOPTED AS THE BUILDING CODE FOR WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND AND INCORPORATED BY REFERENCE, BUT FOR THE PROVISIONS IN CONFLICT WITH THE MODIFICATIONS CODIFIED IN § 1173. PURSUANT TO SECTION 302 OF THE COUNTY CHARTER, THE NEW EDITION MAY BE MODIFIED AT ANY TIME. 

3. UNTIL A NEW EDITION OF THE IBC IS PROMULGATED BY THE INTERNATIONAL CODE COUNCIL AND ADOPTED PURSUANT TO THIS SUBSECTION, THE FORMER EDITION OF THE IBC IS THE BUILDING CODE FOR WICOMICO COUNTY. C. §<sup>117-4AdoptionofInternationalResidentialCode.</sup> 

A. ADOPTION OF THE CODE. 

The County Council of Wicomico County hereby adopts the provisions set forth in the International Residential Code (hereinafter referred to as “IRC”), 2015 2021 Edition, including the appendices, and as such shall be adopted, along with the IBC, as the building code for Wicornico County, Maryland, and incorporated by reference. The provisions shall be controlling for all buildings within the corporate limits of Wicomico County, Maryland, unless otherwise exempted herein.

WITHIN NINETY (90) DAYS OF PROMULGATION OF A NEW EDITION OF THE IRC BY THE INTERNATIONAL CODE COUNCIL, THE DEPARTMENT OF PLANNING, ZONING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENTS CHIEF BUILDING INSPECTOR SHALL REVIEW THE NEW EDITION TO DETERMINE WHETHER ANY MODiFICATIONS ARE RECOMMENDED. 

2. PURSUANT TO SECTION 302 OF THE COUNTY CHARTER, THE NEW EDITION MAY BE MODIFIED AT ANY TIME. 

3. UNTIL A NEW EDITION OF THE IRC IS PROMULGATED BY THE INTERNATIONAL CODE COUNCIL AND ADOPTED PURSUANT TO THIS SUBSECTION, THE FORMER EDITION OF THE IRC IS THE RESIDENTIAL CODE FOR WICOMICO COUNTY. 

C. A LIST OF THE MOST CURRENT EDITION OF THE IRC, ADOPTED PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION B, IS AVAILABLE ON THE COUNTY WEBSITE AND IS AVAILABLE THROUGH VISTING THE DEPARTMENT OF PLANNING, ZONING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT. 

§<sup>117-6AdoptionofInternationalEnergyConservationCode.</sup> 

A. ADOPTION OF THE CODE. 

The County Council of Wicomico County hereby adopts the provisions set forth in the International Energy Conservation Code (hereinafter referred to as “IECC”), 2015 2021 Edition, including the appendices and as such shall he adopted, along with the IBC and IRC, as the building code for Wicomico County, Maryland, and incorporated by reference. The provisions shall be controlling for all buildings within the corporate limits of Wicomico County, Maryland, unless otherwise exempted herein.
