AN EMERGENCY ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 225 OF THE WICOMICO COuNTY CODE, TITLED “ZONING” PART 6, TITLED “ACCESSORY AND PRINCIPAL USES,” ARTICLE XVIII, TITLED “PRINCIPAL USES,” SECTION 225-67, TITLED “TABLE OF PERMITTED USES-DESIGNATIONS” AND PART 7, TITLED “DENSITY AND DIMENSIONAL REGULATIONS,” ARTICLE XIX, TITLED “DENSITY AND DIMENSIONAL REGULATIONS ENUMERATED,” SECTION 22570, TITLED “SETBACK REQUIREMENTS” AND PART 8, TITLED “SPECIAL STANDARDS FOR PARTICULAR USES,” ARTICLE XX, TITLED “USES AND STANDARDS FOR PARTICULAR USES” TO ESTABLISH SECTION §225-103.1 TITLED “MEDICAL CLINIC” TO REGULATE OUTPATIENT MEDICAL AND DENTAL CARE FACILITIES WITH THREE OR MORE PHYSICIANS IN WICOMICO COUNTY. 

WHEREAS, Wicomico County has a severe shortage of family medicine physicians; and 

WHEREAS, TidalHealth Peninsula Regional, Inc. (herein TidalHealth) is a Maryland not for profit regional hospital headquartered in Wicomico County; and 

WHEREAS, TidalHealth is seeking to alleviate the severe shortage of family medicine physicians through establishing a residency clinic which will provide outpatient family medical clinical services to include, but not limited to, primary and specialty care, behavioral health, vital preventive screening, chronic disease management, telehealth services and educational services related to diabetes and other chronic diseases as well as, nutritional and financial counselling; and 

WHEREAS, enumerated uses and standard for a medical facility are necessary to ensure the unique nature of the facility is compatible with the zoning district; and 

WHEREAS, the Wicomico County Planning Commission, has reviewed the request and recommends the incorporation of uses and standards for an outpatient medical or dental care facility with three or more physicians into Chapter 225 of the Wicomico County Code. 

Section I. BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that Chapter 225 of the Wicomico County Code, titled “Zoning” Part 6, Titled “Accessory and Principal Uses,” Article XVIII, Titled “Principal Uses,” Section 225-67, Titled “Table of Permitted Uses-Designations” And Part 7, Titled “Density and Dimensional Regulations,” Article XIX, Titled “Density and Dimensional

§225-103.1 MEDICAL CLINIC 

A. IN THE R-8 RESIDENTIAL DISTRICT, A MEDICAL CLINIC IS PERMITTED, PROVIDED:

<!-- page 4 -->
- (1) A DEVELOPMENT PLAN SHALL BE REQUIRED AND APPROVED BY THE PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION PRIOR TO THE ISSUANCE OF A BUILDING PERMIT OR ZONING AUTHORIZATION FOR A MEDICAL CLINIC. 

- (2) THE REQUIRED MINIMUM LOT SIZE SHALL BE 10 ACRES. 

- (3) THE TOTAL FLOOR AREA OF ALL STRUCTURES SHALL NOT EXCEED 20,000 SQUARE FEET. 

- (4) ALL BUILDINGS AND STRUCTURES SHALL BE SET BACK AT LEAST 100 FEET FROM ALL PROPERTY LINES. 

- (5) A LANDSCAPED BUFFER OF AT LEAST 25 FEET IN WIDTH SHALL BE 

   - PROVIDED ALONG ALL PROPERTY LINES. ALL LANDSCAPING 

   - ELEMENTS SHALL BE DEMONSTRATED ON THE DEVELOPMENT PLAN. 

- (6) THE LOCATION OF EXTERIOR LIGHTING FIXTURES WITH RELATED STANDARDS SHALL BE DEMONSTRATED ON THE DEVELOPMENT PLAN. 

- (7) ALL SIGN ELEVATIONS AND LOCATIONS SHALL BE DEMONSTRATED ON THE DEVELOPMENT PLAN. 

- (8) COMPLY WITH THE PARKING STANDARDS AND REQUIREMENTS AS SET FORTH IN THIS CHAPTER. 

SECTION II: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COIJNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT an emergency exists, to wit: a severe shortage of family medicine physicians, wherefore this Legislative Bill is passed as an emergency measure necessary for the immediate preservation of public health, safety and welfare which requires immediate action in the public interest, and subject to the laws of Maryland, the Wicomico County Charter, and the Wicomico County Code.
