AN ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 37, TITLED “ETHICS LAW”, ARTICLE II, TITLED “ETHICS COMMISSION, SECTION 37-5, TITLED “ETHICS COMMISSION” TO REQUIRE SPECIAL LEGAL<sup>COUNSEL</sup> FOR THE ETHICS COMMISSION TO AVOID A CONFLICT OF INTEREST OR APPEARANCE OF<sup>A</sup> CONFLICT<sup>OF</sup> INTEREST. 

WHEREAS, the Wicomico County Ethics Commission is charged with investigating, reviewing, and providing guidance on matters of ethical conduct within the county government; and 

WHEREAS, the current procedure designates the County Attorney to provide legal advice to the Ethics Commission, which may create a conflict of interest or the appearance of a conflict of interest when the Commission is investigating matters involving the county government; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council recognizes the importance of maintaining public trust in the impartiality of the Ethics Commission’s proceedings; and 

WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of the County to ensure that the Ethics Commission has access to independent legal counsel to provide unbiased advice and representation; and 

WHEREAS, to avoid the appearance of a conflict of interest, the Wicomico County Council seeks to establish a procedure whereby the Ethics Commission retains special independent<sup>legalcounsel,</sup> separate from the County Attorney, and the cost of such legal counsel<sup>will</sup> be borne by the County.

## February 19, 2025 

## County Council: 

I am writing to formally veto Legislative Bill 2025-02, An Act to Amend Chapter 37, Titled Ethics Law, which seeks to mandate the appointment of special legal counsel for the Ethics Commission to avoid confficts of interest or the appearance thereof. While 1 support the intent behind ensuring impartiality in ethics matters, this bill is unnecessary and an imprudent use of county resources. 

The Ethics Commission already possesses the authority to request special legal counsel at its discretion when it deems it necessary. Mandating special legal counsel in all instances, as this bill proposes, would create an unnecessary financial burden on county taxpayers without providing any additional benefits beyond those already available under existing law. 

Our government has a responsibility to be judicious stewards of public funds. Requiring special legal counsel in every case, regardless of the Ethics Commission’s determination of necessity, would divert limited resources away from essential county services. The current system strikes the appropriate balance— allowing the Ethics Commission to seek outside legal assistance when a genuine conflict arises while avoiding an automatic and costly requirement. 

For these reasons, I cannot support Legislative Bill 2025-02 and am vetoing it in the interest of responsible governance and fiscal prudence. I urge the legislature to consider alternative measures that strengthen ethical oversight without imposing unnecessary financial burdens on our constituents. 

Respectfully, 

WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND
