## COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND

AN ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 90 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE, TITLED “TAX EXEMPTIONS” TO ADD A NEW SECTION 90-4 TO BE TITLED, “HABITAT FOR HUMANITY TAX CREDIT”. 

WHEREAS, the Wicomico County Council is authorized under §<sup>9-252</sup> of the Tax-Property<sup>Articleof theAnnotatedCodeof</sup> Maryland<sup>to</sup> grant<sup>taxreliefoncertain</sup> property owned by Habitat for Humanity; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>theWicomico</sup> County<sup>Councildesiresto</sup> provide<sup>alocal</sup> property<sup>taxcredit</sup> to Habitat for Humanity of Wicomico County, Inc. to encourage acquisition, rehabilitation, and transfer of properties for affordable housing. 

Section 1. BEITENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND,<sup>INLEGISLATIVE</sup> SESSION,<sup>that</sup> Chapter<sup>90oftheWicomico</sup> County Code, Titled,<sup>“Tax</sup> Exemptions”<sup>is</sup> hereby<sup>amendedtoaddSection</sup> 90-4, titled,<sup>“Habitat</sup> for Humanity Tax Credit”, to read as follows: 

Chapter<sup>90</sup> 

Tax Exemptions 

SECTION 90-4 HABITAT FOR HUMANITY TAX CREDIT. 

- A. THERE IS HEREBY ESTABLISHED A 100% PROPERTY TAX CREDIT ON REAL PROPERTY OWNED BY HABITAT FOR HUMANITY OF WICOMICO COUNTY, INC.,, (“HABITAT”)<sup>PROVIDEDTHAT:</sup> 

   1. THE PROPERTY IS OWNED WITH THE INTENTION TO REHABILITATE AND TRANSFER IT IN THE NEAR FUTURE FOR AFFORDABLE 

      - HOMEOWNERSHIP ; 

   - . THE PROPERTY JS USED EXCLUSIVELY FOR DEVELOPMENT, REHABILITATION AND TRANSFER TO A PRIVATE CITIZEN. 

   - . THE PROPERTY IS NOT OCCUPIED BY ADMINISTRATIVE OR WAREHOUSE BUILDINGS OWNED BY HABITAT; AND 

   - . THE PROPERTY QUALIFIES AS “REAL PROPERTY” UNDER COUNTY TAX LAW.

<!-- page 2 -->
- B. APPLICABILITY PERIOD AND TERMS OF THE TAX CREDIT. 

      - THE TAX CREDIT IS NOT AVAILABLE UNTIL TAX YEAR 2027, WHICH BEGINS JULY 1, 2026. 

      - THE TAX CREDIT FOR EACH PROPERTY SHALL BE FOR NO MORE THAN FIVE CONSECUTIVE TAX YEARS OR UPON TRANSFER OF THE PROPERTY. A PROPERTY WHICH WAS NOT TRANSFERRED WITHIN FIVE TAX YEARS IS NOT ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE THE TAX CREDIT AGAIN. FURTHER, UPON THE EXPIRATION OF THE TAX CREDIT, EITHER DUE TO A LAPSE IN TIME OR TRANSFER OF THE PROPERTY, HABITAT SHALL PAY THE FULL AMOUNT OF TAXES THAT ACCRUED DURING THE TAX CREDIT PERIOD; HOWEVER, NO INTEREST ON THE ACCRUED TAXES SHALL BE OWED BY HABITAT OR COLLECTED BY WICOMICO COUNTY. 

      - THE TAX CREDIT SHALL BE APPLIED TO THE DISCOUNT AMOUNT AVAILABLE FOR PAYMENT MADE ON OR BEFORE AUGUST 31ST OF EACH FISCAL YEAR ON THE FULL ANNUAL TAX BILL. 

   - . IF A PROPERTY RECEIVING THE TAX CREDIT IS REASSESSED WHILE RECEIVING THE TAX CREDIT, THE AMOUNT OF TAXES DUE, FOLLOWING THE REASSESSMENT, SHALL BE BASED ON THE UPDATED ASSESSMENT AMOUNT AS DETERMINED BY THE MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF ASSESSMENTS AND TAXATION. 

   - . THIRTY (30) DAYS PRIOR TO TRANSFER OF THE PROPERTY, HABITAT SHALL NOTIFY, IN WRITING, THE WCFD THAT A SALE OF THE PROPERTY WILL OCCUR IN THE NEXT 30 DAYS. 

- C. APPLICATION AND ADMINISTRATION. 

### 1. APPLICATION 

- THE WICOMICO COUNTY FINANCE DEPARTMENT SHALL PREPARE AN APPLICATION FOR THE TAX CREDIT BY MARCH 1, 2026. THE APPLICATION SHALL BE POSTED ON THE COUNTY’S WEBSITE. HABITAT MUST APPLY FOR THE TAX CREDIT UNDER THIS SECTION BY SUBMITTING THE COMPLETED APPLICATION TO THE WICOMICO COUNTY FINANCE DEPARTMENT BY APRIL 1 EACH YEAR. THE FINANCE DEPARTMENT SHALL DETERMINE WHETHER A PROPERTY QUALIFIES FOR THE TAX DEFERRAL BY JUNE<sup>1EACHYEARANDSENDWRITTENNOTICEOFTHE</sup> DECISION TO HABITAT. THE NOTICE SHALL INDICATE THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF TAXES CREDITED TO DATE AND HOW MANY YEARS OF POTENTIAL CREDIT REMAIN.

<!-- page 3 -->
2. REPORTING REQUIREMENTS. IN ADDITION TO THE REQUIREMENTS OF SECTION A, TO QUALIFY FOR THE CREDIT, HABITAT SHALL SUBMIT AN ANNUAL WRITTEN REPORT TO THE COUNTY FINANCE DEPARTMENT BY APRIL 1ST DOCUMENTING: 

i. ALL OF HABITAT’S REAL PROPERTY HOLDINGS IN WICOMICO COUNTY;<sup>AND</sup> 

ii. ALL TRANSACTIONS INVOLVING HABITAT’S REAL PROPERTY HOLDINGS IN WICOMICO COUNTY, INCLUDING, BUT, NOT<sup>NECESSARILYLIMITED</sup> TO,<sup>THEDATETHEPROPERTY</sup> WAS ACQUIRED OR TRANSFERRED, THE PARTIES INVOLVED IN THE ACQUISITION OR TRANSFER, THE PURPOSE OF ACQUISITION<sup>ANDTHECURRENTUSEOFTHEPROPERTY.</sup> 

3. RIGHT TO DENY CREDIT. THE FINANCE DEPARTMENT SHALL DENY A TAX CREDIT ON ANY PROPERTY THAT DOES NOT MEET ALL ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS SET FORTH IN THIS SECTION. 

- D. NO REFUNDS SHALL BE ISSUED ON TAXES PAID BY HABITAT FOR HUMANITY OF WICOMICO COUNTY, INC. PRIOR TO JULY 1, 2026. 

SECTION II: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT this bill shall be known as Legislative Bill No. 2025-13 and shall take effect sixty (60) days after its enactment,<sup>unlessa</sup> proper<sup>PetitionforReferendumthereof shallbefiled</sup> prior<sup>tosaid</sup> date;<sup>in</sup> which event, the Bill shall not take effect until the expiration of thirty (30) days following the approval<sup>of thisBill</sup> by<sup>a</sup> majority<sup>of the</sup> qualified<sup>votersof the</sup> County voting<sup>in</sup> any<sup>such</sup> referendum.
