AN ACT TO AMEND THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE, CHAPTER 59 - PERSONNEL RULES, SECTION 59-15 -ACCIDENTAL DISABILITY BENEFIT. 

WHEREAS, the County Executive and the Human Resources Department recommend changes to the County Code, Chapter 59 — Personnel Rules, to amend Section 59-15 (c) regarding accidental disability payments to coordinate language with the Wicomico County Pension Plan and the Wicomico County Collective Bargaining Agreement with the Wicomico County Fraternal Order of Police, Lodge #111; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council endorses the amendment to Chapter 59 — Personnel Rules, Section 5 9-15 regarding accidental disability payments. 

NOW, THERFORE, UPON THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE AND THE DIRECTOR OF HUMAN RESOURCES; 

SECTION I: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that: 

1. The Wicomico County Code, Chapter 59 — Personnel Rules, Section 59-15 (c) is amended to read:

(II) ANY RELATED WORKERS’ COMPENSATION BENEFITS, PAID OR PAYABLE AFTER THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THE CERTIFIED ACCIDENTAL DISABILITY BENEFIT DETERMINATION BY THE MEDICAL ADVISORY BOARD, PROVIDED THAT SUCH BENEFITS: 

(A) ARE PAID OR PAYABLE WHILE AN ACCIDENTAL DISABILITY BENEFIT IS PAID OR PAYABLE; AND 

(B) ARE FOR PERSONAL INJURY OR ILLNESS ARISING OUT OF AND IN THE COURSE OF THE MEMBER’S EMPLOYMENT WITH THE EMPLOYER. 

ANY WORKER’S COMPENSATION BENEFITS PAID OR PAYABLE PRIOR TO THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THE CERTIFIED ACCIDENTAL DISABILITY BENEFIT SHALL NOT REDUCE THE MEMBER’S MONTHLY ACCIDENTAL DISABILITY BENEFIT PAYMENT AMOUNT. 

SECTION II: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that the County Code is hereby amended to include the changes recited in Chapter 59 — Personnel Rules, Section 59-16 (c) set forth above. 

SECTION III: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT this bill shall be known as Legislative Bill No. 2023-05 of Wicomico County, Maryland, and shall take effect sixty (60) days after its final passage, unless a proper Petition for Referendum thereof shall be filed prior to said date; in which event, the Bill shall not take effect until the expiration of thirty (30) days following the approval of this Bill by a majority of the qualified voters of the County voting in any such referendum.
