AN EMERGENCY ACT TO ADD NEW SECTIONS TO THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE TO CODIFY THE OPERATTNG DAYS, HOURS, AND LOCATIONS OF COUNTY SANITARY LANDFILL, CONVENIENCE CENTERS AND RECYCLING CENTERS; TO REQUIRE COMPLIANCE BY ALL COMMERCIAL HAULERS; TO ADD DEFINITIONS; TO PROHIBIT THE UNLAWFUL DISPOSAL OF SOLID WASTE ORIGINATING OUTSIDE OF WICOMICO COUNTY; AND TO PROVIDE PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS. 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Wicomico County is committed to protecting the public health, safety, and general welfare of its citizens through the safe, efficient, and transparent operation of all County-owned solid waste facilities, including the Newland Park Landfill and its convenience centers; and 

WHEREAS, codifying the days and hours of operation for these facilities by ordinance provides consistency for residents and commercial haulers, improves operational planning, ensures adequate staffing, and promotes public accountability by requiring Council action for future changes; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council finds it necessary to prohibit the disposal of solid waste originating outside of Wicomico County in order to preserve landfill capacity, control costs to local taxpayers, and prevent unauthorized or excessive use of County-owned resources; and 

WHEREAS, due to the urgent need to address these matters, the County Council declares this Act to be an emergency measure essential to the immediate preservation of the County’s waste management system and the public interest. 

SECTION I: BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLANI), IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that Chapter 156 of the Wicomico County Code, titled “Garbage, Rubbish and Refuse,” is hereby amended to add new sections to the Wicomico County Code to codify the operating days, hours, and locations of county landfill, convenience centers and recycling centers; to require compliance by all commercial haulers; to add definitions; to prohibit the unlawful disposal of solid waste originating outside of Wicomico County; and to provide penalties for violations as follows:

§<sup>156-4.Presumption of responsibilityfor violation.</sup> 

A. Whenever litter, garbage, rubbish or refuse is thrown, dumped or deposited in violation of this chapter and the identity of the person throwing, dumping or depositing said material cannot be determined, the owner of said material shall be presumed to be responsible for the violation. 

B. The person identified as the recipient of addressed mail, correspondence, envelopes, invoices or shipping tags contained within any litter, garbage, rubbish or refuse unlawfully thrown, dumped or deposited upon any public or private property within the county shall be presumed to be the owner of said litter, garbage, rubbish or refuse and shall be responsible for the violation of this chapter.
