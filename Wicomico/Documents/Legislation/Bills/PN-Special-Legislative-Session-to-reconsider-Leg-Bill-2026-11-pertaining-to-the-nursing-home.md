# WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL 

**P U B L I C   N O T I C E** 

NOTICE OF SPECIAL LEGISLATIVE SESSION 

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland, 

will hold a special Legislative Session on **Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 9:00 a.m.** in the Government Office Building, 125 N. Division Street, Room 301, Salisbury, Maryland 21801. The purpose of the meeting is to reconsider Legislative Bill No. 2026-11 pertaining to the Wicomico Nursing Home. 

The public is invited to attend and observe the meeting. 

Contact: Wicomico County Council Administrator (410-546-4696)
