# Letter Y. 

To clarify that Motorized Vehicles can only be operated on park property in approved areas. 

**<u>Traffic</u>** - (1) the operation of all motorized vehicles on park property shall be in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. (2) Instruction from a Department employee or police officer, by gesture or otherwise, to reduce the speed of a vehicle, to bring it to a stop, to alter its direction, or to remove it from a restricted area shall immediately be obeyed by the operator or owner of the vehicle. (3) No person shall operate any motorized vehicle in excess of twenty- five (25) miles per hour, unless otherwise posted or directed. (5) Buses,</u> trucks, and commercial vehicles with commercial marking or tools of trade, with the exception of taxi cabs and tool trucks on service calls to disabled vehicles, shall not be operated on park property unless a permit has first been obtained from the Department. ##  **Section 179-11 Permits.** 

Letter A. 

To clarify that special permits must have approval by the County Council. 

Permits or written permission required under the provisions of this Chapter for the conduct of any activity or for the use of any park facility or portion thereof, except alcohol or gambling special permits, may be issued to any individual, group, or organization at the discretion of the Department. Requests for special permits for alcohol or gambling shall be forwarded to the Wicomico County Recreation AND PARKS Commission for review and comment. The request, with the recommendation of the Recreation Commission, shall then be forwarded to the County Council of Wicomico County ~~EXECUTIVE f~~ or final action.
