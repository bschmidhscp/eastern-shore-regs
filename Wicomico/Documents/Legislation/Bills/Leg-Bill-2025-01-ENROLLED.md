COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND

Introduced by: The Council President at the request of the Planning Commission 

AN ACT TO AMEND CHAPTER 225 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE, TITLED “ZONING” PART 3, TITLED “BASIC DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATIONS,” ARTICLE VI, TITLED “TERMS DEFINED,” SECTION §225-25 TITLED “DEFINITIONS OF BASIC TERMS”; AND PART 6, TITLED “ACCESSORY AND PRINCIPAL USES,” ARTICLE XVIII, TITLED “PRINCIPAL USES,” SECTION 22567, TITLED “TABLE OF PERMITTED USES-DESIGNATIONS” AND PART 8, TITLED “SPECIAL STANDARDS FOR PARTICULAR USES,” ARTICLE XX, TITLED “USES AND STANDARDS ENUMERATED,” SECTION §225-99 TITLED “KENNELS, COMMERCIAL” TO AMEND THE ZONING REQUIREMENTS FOR THE OPERATION OF A KENNEL. 

WHEREAS, the boarding, breeding, training, selling, exhibition or raising of dogs constitutes activity performed by a kennel; and 

WHEREAS, the regulation of the zoning district, structure location and permissible activities of a kennel are zoning matters; and 

WHEREAS, Wicomico County, Maryland recognizes the importance of maintaining kennels safely and responsibly; and 

WHEREAS, the Wicomico County Planning Commission, recommends amending the Wicomico County Code to reflect current practices in kennel regulation. 

Section I. BE IT ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, THAT CHAPTER 225 OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY CODE, TITLED “ZONING” PART 3, TITLED “BASIC DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATIONS,” ARTICLE VI, TITLED “TERMS DEFINED,” SECTION §225-25 TITLED “DEFINITIONS OF BASIC TERMS”; AND PART 6, TITLED “ACCESSORY AND PRINCIPAL USES,” ARTICLE XVIII, TITLED “PRINCIPAL USES,” SECTION 225-67, TITLED “TABLE OF PERMITTED USES-DESIGNATIONS” AND PART 8, TITLED “SPECIAL STANDARDS FOR PARTICULAR USES,” ARTICLE XX, TITLED “USES AND STANDARDS ENUMERATED,” SECTION §225-99 TITLED “KENNELS, COMMERCIAL” IS HEREBY AMENDED, TO READ AS FOLLOWS:

B. Definitions. As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

Part 8 

Special Standards for Particular Uses 

Article XX 

Uses and Standards for Particular Uses 

§<sup>225-99Kennels,commercial.</sup> 

A. Commercial Kennels in the A-i Agriculture-Rural, TT TOWN TRANSITION or V-C Village Conservation Districts shall: 

(1) Have a minimum acreage as follows: 

(a) Five acres if outside runs; or 

(b) Two acres if no outside runs and animals in soundproof building. 

(2) Not HAVE THE RUNS OR A STRUCTURE, WHICH HOUSES THE DOGS, be located within 2r00 1,000 500 feet of an existing residential dwelling on an adjacent ANOTHER parcel., AS MEASURED FROM THE PROPERTY LNE OF THE KENNEL PROPERTY. 

B. In a C-2 General Commercial District, commercial kennels may be ARE permitted, provided that runs for animals DOGS shall be entirely contained within a building. 

C. A PROPERTY WHICH RECEIVED A SPECIAL EXCEPTION TO OPERATE A KENNEL PRIOR TO JANUARY 1, 2025, MAY CONTINUE AS A NONCONFORMING SPECIAL EXCEPTION AS PROVIDED BY 225-152. ALL OTHER PROPERTIES A PROPERTY WITH TEN OR MORE DOGS PRIOR TO THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF LEGISLATIVE BILL 2025-01, SHALL CONTINUE AS A NONCONFORMING USE AS PROVIDED BY 225-150; HOWEVER, THE ABILITY TO CONTINUE THE NONCONFORMING USE SHALL TERMINATE THREE YEARS FROM THE EFFECTIVE

DATE OF LEGISLATIVE BILL 2025-01 OR NONCOMPLIANCE WITH 225-150(F), WHICHEVER COMES FIRST. 

D. THE BOARD OF APPEALS MAY MODIFY THE SEPARATION REQUIREMENTS IN SUBSECTION (A)(2) AS PART OF THE SPECIAL EXCEPTION APPLICATION. 

SECTION II. IT IS THE INTENTION OF WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL THAT EACH PROVISION OF THIS LEGISLATIVE BILL SHALL BE DEEMED INDEPENDENT OF ALL OTHER PROVISIONS HEREIN. 

SECTION III. IT IS FURTHER THE INTENTION OF THE WICOMICO COUNTY COUNCIL THAT IF ANY SECTION, PARAGRAPH, SUBSECTION, CLAUSE OR PROVISION OF THIS LEGISLATIVE BILL SHALL BE ADJUDGED INVALID, UNCONSTITUTIONAL OR OTHERWISE UNENFORCEABLE UNDER APPLICABLE MARYLAND OR FEDERAL LAW, SUCH ADJUDICATION SHALL APPLY ONLY TO THE SECTION, PARAGRAPH, SUBSECTION, CLAUSE OR PROVISION SO ADJUDGED AND ALL OTHER PROVISIONS OF THIS LEGISLATIVE BILL SHALL REMAIN AND SHALL BE DEEMED VALID AND ENFORCEABLE. 

SECTION 14 IV: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED AND ORDAINED BY THE COuNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT this bill shall be known as Legislative Bill No. 2025-0 1 and shall take effect sixty (60) days after its enactment, unless a proper Petition for Referendum thereof shall be filed prior to said date; in which event, the Bill shall not take effect until the expiration of thirty (30) days following the approval of this Bill by a majority of the qualified voters of the County voting in any such referendum.
