Dear President Cannon: 

The Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) has completed its review of the **Wicomico County Adopted Resolution No. 143-2024 (Amendment** ) to the Wicomico County Water and Sewerage Plan **.** The Amendment includes water category changes from W-6 (no planned service) to W-3 (immediate priority) for 139 properties and sewer category changes from S-6 to S-3 for 206 properties. The properties will be connected to the City of Salisbury’s Water and Wastewater Treatment Plants. The Wicomico County Council adopted the Amendment on November 5, 2024. See enclosed water and sewer changes in the maps below. 

# **<u>Maryland Department of Planning (MDP) Findings</u>** 

The Maryland Department of Planning (MDP) has reviewed this Amendment pursuant to its mandate to advise the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) on local comprehensive plan consistency and other appropriate matters as required by Environment Article Section 9-507 (b)(2) and the Land Use Article Section 1-303 and 1-304. 

1. From both a Land Use and Community Facilities perspective, the Water and Sewerage Plan (WSP) amendment appears to be consistent with the 2017 Wicomico County Comprehensive Plan. The properties are also included in the Salisbury Municipal Growth Area, as per Map 11-3 of the 2010 City of Salisbury Comprehensive Plan and the WSP amendment appears to be similarly consistent with that plan. 

If there are comments or questions regarding MDP’s review, MDE encourages the County to contact MDP. See enclosed MDP’s comments and contact information. 

# **<u>MDE Review and Action</u>** 

MDE has reviewed the proposed amendment in accordance with §9-507 of the Environment Article, Annotated Code of Maryland and its findings are listed below.  These findings and any actions required by the county, or recommended to the County, have been included and taken into consideration in MDE’s final decision.

1. <u>Water Treatment Plant Capacity</u> 

One hundred and thirty-nine (139) properties located at Delano Avenue and Presidents Neighborhood will be served by the City of Salisbury’s water treatment plants (East Main Street and Naylor Mill Road). Table 3 of the 2010 Wicomico County Water and Sewerage Plan (Plan) lists the 2010 projected water demand and planned capacity as 6.9 Million Gallons per Day (MGD) and 12.0 MGD respectively. Table 3 also lists the 2020 projected water demand and planned capacity as 7.66 MGD and 18.0 MGD respectively. Therefore, there is sufficient capacity to serve the 139 properties (0.03 MGD). Please note that the Plan references future treatment plant upgrades. It is uncertain if these upgrades have taken place. In the next update of the Plan, the County is encouraged to incorporate updated information related to the water treatment plant upgrades into the County Water and Sewerage Plan. 

2. <u>Wastewater Treatment Plant Capacity</u> 

Two hundred and six (206) properties located at Delano Avenue, Presidents Neighborhood, Regency Drive and Buckingham Circle will be served by the Salisbury Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP). These properties will generate approximately 0.05 MGD of wastewater flow. The Salisbury WWTP currently has a design capacity of 8.5 MGD and a demand of 5.2 MGD and can accommodate the additional load. 

3. <u>Public Health</u> 

The amendment proposes the connection of 206 properties to the Salisbury WWTP in the areas of Presidents Neighborhood, North and South Delano Avenues, Regency Drive and Buckingham Circle. The Wicomico County Health Department has confirmed that within these areas, seventeen (17) systems have failed since 2017 and needed to be replaced. These areas consist of small lots, poor soil quality and high water tables, making the replacement of onsite sewage disposal systems difficult. To reduce the potential of failing septic systems contaminating the soil and risking the public’s health, the Health Department has advised that the properties in question be added to the City of Salisbury Sewer Service Area. 

4. <u>Water Resources Element (WRE) Guidance Update</u> 

There is an updated Water Resources Element (WRE) guidance. The 2022 update WRE guidance can be found at the following link: 

https://planning.maryland.gov/Pages/OurWork/envr-planning/water-resources-mg/20 22/2022-guidance-update.aspx 

The 2022 guidance includes best practices for protecting receiving waters and for integrating climate change and equity considerations into local water resource planning.  By updating the WRE, a required element of local comprehensive plans, jurisdictions will identify recommendations and strategies necessary for ensuring

5. <u>Water and Sewerage Plan Update</u> Please be reminded that the Wicomico County Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan update is overdue. COMAR requires that the County provide a copy of the Draft Water and Sewer Plan to MDE with enough time prior to County adoption for a thorough review, at least 60 days. This action ensures that MDE’s comments can be incorporated, as appropriate, in the County’s final WSP. 

In accordance with §9-507(a) of the Environment Article, Annotated Code of Maryland, the Department hereby **approves Wicomico County’s Resolution 143-2024 (Amendment)** to **the Wicomico County Water and Sewerage Plan.** See the enclosed maps below. 

This completes MDE’s review, as required by §9-507 of the Environment Article, Annotated Code of Maryland.
