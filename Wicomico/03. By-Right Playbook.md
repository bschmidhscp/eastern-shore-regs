# Wicomico County: By-Right Playbook

Keys: [PC digest] = `01. Digests/Planning Commission Actions.md`; [Fees] = `01. Digests/Fee Findings.md`. State septic, Critical Area, perc and BAT rules: see `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`. Code text is eCode360 retrieved 2026-10-11, "current through" unknown.

## 1. Bottom line

The by-right path is the **"minor subdivision of three lots or fewer"**, which Chapter 200 *excludes* from the normal subdivision procedure [§ 200-3A]. Maximum **3 lots**, once, from a tract that existed 7/9/1957 or 4/1/1968. The **Director of Public Works and the County Health Department** approve the plat; there is no sketch plat and no Planning Commission (PC) review unless the Director or we ask for it [§ 200-3A(1)(h), (3); § 200-6B(1)]. The code requires **no adjoining-owner letters, no sign posting, no published notice and no PC agenda** for this path; the only notice provisions in Ch. 200 attach to PC meetings [§ 200-7A(4); § 200-8A(2)]. Typical time to a recorded plat is about **4 to 8 months (estimate)**, driven by perc/Health Dept timing. A 4th to 7th lot is still a "minor subdivision" but goes to the PC (public meeting, mailed notice to applicant only), so it is outside the no-agenda path.

## 2. Eligibility test

A parcel stays on the path only if ALL are true:

1. **Original tract / cap.** The tract was in existence on **7/9/1957 or 4/1/1968, whichever applies**, and no lots have been taken under this right before. The right is "one-time", "only three lots ... out of any one tract" [§ 200-3A(1), (2)]. Prior lots cut from the parent since that date count (precedent: Basch parent and Bloxham parent each got a "fourth lot" and were sent to the PC [PC digest 2024-01-18, 2026-08-20, 2026-09-17]). Check the deed and plat history back to the base date.
2. **Zoning and density.** Each lot meets the larger of the Health Dept area or Ch. 225 bulk minimums [§ 200-3A(1)(a)]. A-1: SFD lot 20,000 sf or Health Dept area, 100/120 ft width [§ 225-68A; Attach. 3]; A-1 conventional density 1 per 15 ac [§ 225-75]. The code waives the 1998 comp-plan density to preserve the 3-lot right [§ 200-3A.1], but a 2026 staff note applied 1:15 to the parent [PC digest 2026-09-17]. Ask staff (Q1).
3. **Remnant.** The remainder parcel must be **20 acres or more** [§ 200-3A(1)(d)]. Parent tracts under about 20 acres plus the lots cannot use this path.
4. **Frontage and access.** Each lot has frontage on a **publicly maintained road** (on-site water/sewer lots "as may be required" by Ch. 200 and 225) [§ 200-3A(1)(b), (c)]. **No private streets**; every subdivided property is served from a public street [§ 200-11G]. Rural open-section roads assume about 1 acre and 150 ft frontage [§ 200-11, open-section rural road standard]. Flag/panhandle lots and shared-driveway limits: **not found in code** (Bloxham used a shared driveway easement for 2 lots, but it was a PC case) (Q2).
5. **Road class limit.** No more than 3 new lots on any State arterial/collector (SHA prior approval required); no more than 3 on a county major collector; on a minor collector, 3 only unless we pay for road widening [§ 200-11J(2)-(4)]. Interior road needed above 3. Road widening dedication is required [§ 200-3A(1)(f)].
6. **Future street extension.** A single future street right-of-way adjacent to lot 1 or 2 must be shown for access to the balance, **unless waived jointly by the DPW and Planning Directors** [§ 200-3A(1.1)]. Plan the waiver request up front.
7. **Septic tier.** Tier IV and Tier II allow only a minor subdivision on septic; a 3-lot plat is within every tier [Env. § 9-206(g); PC minutes 2026-02-19 p.3]. Each lot needs perc approval and a 10,000 sf area for an initial and two replacement systems [COMAR 26.04.02.04F]. Sewered lots: community sewer lots need frontage and width per Ch. 200/225 [§ 200-3A(1)(c)].
8. **Critical Area.** RCA is 1 dwelling per 20 acres, no variance [§ 125-14C(1)]; a new RCA subdivision gets a 200-ft buffer under the Bill 2026-02 rewrite (enrolled, not in code). LDA/IDA lots need Critical Area review ($50) [§ 141-3A(3)]. Avoid RCA parcels unless the parent is 60+ acres.
9. **No new road, no public sewer extension, no variance or special exception, no rezoning** (see section 6).
10. **Overlays.** A-2 Airport, Paleochannel, Historic Preservation and Floodplain (Ch. 149) overlays can add review [§ 225-43 to 225-46].
11. **Surveyor.** Plat prepared and signed by a Maryland-licensed surveyor [§ 200-3A(1)(g)].

## 3. Step-by-step path

Durations are **estimates** unless cited. Total: roughly 4 to 8 months.

1. **Title and tract-history check (us / attorney / surveyor), 1-3 weeks.** Confirm the 1957/1968 tract, prior lots taken, remnant of 20+ ac, zoning, road class (county vs State), Critical Area, floodplain, tier. Use Tax Map and `01. Maryland/GIS/SOURCES.md`.
2. **Pre-application call with Planning/Public Works, 1-2 weeks.** Confirm the 3-lot count, remnant, and the street-extension waiver (Q1, Q3).
3. **Boundary survey and soils/perc layout (surveyor, civil engineer), 3-6 weeks.** Locate septic areas and wetlands.
4. **Perc and soils testing (Health Dept, Environmental Health, 410-749-1244).** Wet-season testing is **usually Nov to Mar** where the soil survey shows high water tables [Fees; COMAR 26.04.02.04B]; the state gave a 2026 off-season expedite [PC minutes 2026-05-21 p.6]. Missing the window can add 6+ months. Health Dept fees: **not found** (Q4).
5. **Forest conservation (Planning).** Parcels over 39,999 sf are subject unless a certificate of exemption is issued [§ 126-4]; small parcels often use an FCA(E) exemption [PC digest 2026-08-20]. Fee $200 for 1 to 3 lots [§ 141-3A(4)]. If clearing triggers planting and it cannot be done on site, fee-in-lieu is the rate in the Council fee schedule, never below $0.10 per sf [§ 126-17A]; paid before the final plat is recorded [§ 126-17B]. Forest thresholds: 50% agricultural [§ 126-15]. State 1:1 replacement began 7/1/2026 [Maryland Summary].
6. **Stormwater and sediment control (DPW).** Exempt if development disturbs 5,000 sf or less [§ 196-6C]; DPW stormwater fees do not apply when no engineering review is needed [§ 141-4A(2)(f)]. Expect lot-level ESD review at building permit (the builder's cost; we never pull permits). Confirm with DPW whether a 3-lot plat needs a concept plan (Q5).
7. **Prepare the plat (surveyor).** Show 3 lots plus remnant, future street extension (or waiver), road widening dedication, Right to Farm note, "no further subdivision" language where Health Dept requires (state rule: no re-subdivision of a minor in Tier II-IV [Env. § 9-206(l)]).
8. **File with Public Works / Planning and pay the $225 plat fee [§ 141-3A(5)(a)]; plus Critical Area $50 if applicable.** Agency review (DPW roads/engineering, Planning, Fire, Health Dept), estimate 3-6 weeks. The Director may route to PC, or we may request it [§ 200-3A(3)].
9. **Health Dept plat sign-off** (MDE Secretary delegate) is required before recording; not required for lots not for development purposes [§ 200-3A(1)(h); COMAR 26.04.03.02K]. Example: Health final approval came about 2 weeks before a PC hearing [PC digest 2026-09-17].
10. **Approval signatures** by Director of Public Works and Health Dept [§ 200-3A(1)(h)]. No PC meeting.
11. **Recording.** The Director of Public Works files the plat in the land records [§ 200-3A(4)]. Recording fee at the Circuit Court: not found in corpus. Record within the time allowed (the 3-year rule in § 200-10B(4) is written for PC-approved plats; record promptly).
12. **Close with builder** after the plat is recorded and deeds are conveyed.

## 4. Public notice

For the exempt 3-lot path: **none required**. No adjoining-owner letters, signs, publication or hearing appear in § 200-3, § 200-6B or § 141-3. The sketch plat stage, which lists adjoining owners as plat information [§ 200-6D(2)] and is where agency comments are circulated [§ 200-6C(2)], is skipped [§ 200-6B(1)].

Notice appears only if the PC is involved: PC action must happen at a meeting "open to the public," with notice mailed to the applicant or representative at least 5 days before [§ 200-7A(4)]; final-plat notice is a 5-day letter to the applicant, waivable [§ 200-8A(2)]. Agendas are public in practice (e.g., 2026-09-17 agenda listed a minor subdivision). The variant that avoids the agenda is staying at 3 lots and not requesting PC review..

For comparison, a 7-lot PC minor: plat stages $325 + $425 + $500 + $30 x 7 = $1,460, FCA $350, DPW $2,140, stormwater $2,350 (about $6,300) [§ 141-3A(5)(b); § 141-4A]. Not counted: Health Dept perc/plat fees (not found), recording, bonds, legal, water/sewer connection charges (not found), 150% DPW surety if improvements are required.

Consultant studies: boundary survey and plat; perc/soils and septic design (wet season); forest stand delineation or exemption (FCA(E)); wetland/stream delineation where indicated; stormwater concept or exemption letter; Critical Area report if in the 1,000-ft area; title search; State Highway entrance review if on a State road.

## 6. Off-ramps

| Trigger | Result | Design-around |
|---|---|---|
| 4th lot from the original tract | PC review: sketch, preliminary, final; minors 4-7 lots [§ 200-4B; § 200-6A(2)] | Take only 3; stop count at the 1957/1968 tract |
| Remnant under 20 ac | Loses exclusion [§ 200-3A(1)(d)] | Buy more land; size lots accordingly |
| Prior lots already taken | Count exhausted | Title research on every parcel |
| New road or interior road | Not minor-exempt; > 3 lots on State/major collector [§ 200-11J] | Stay on frontage; check road class |
| No public road frontage / private street | Not allowed [§ 200-11G] | Pick frontage parcels |
| Variance (frontage, setbacks) | Board of Appeals, public notice, $300 [§ 141-3A(2)] | Size lots conservatively |
| Special exception / rezoning | Hearing | n/a |
| Lot fails perc / no 10,000 sf area | Lot lost or must be enlarged | Early perc in season |
| Public sewer extension / W&S plan amendment | Council resolution, major path | Septic-only lots |
| Critical Area RCA | 1 per 20 ac, no variance [§ 125-14C(1)] | Avoid RCA |
| Floodplain / wetlands / buffer | Extra review, lost buildable area | Delineate early |
| Airport overlay A-2, Paleochannel | Added review | Screen parcels |
| Director or applicant requests PC | PC process [§ 200-3A(3)] | Do not request |
| Bill 2026-03 | Only affects 4-7 lot minors (set-aside removal) | Not needed for 3 lots |
| Comp plan rewrite, new code | Watch list | Check before contract |
