# § 169-18. Expense credit in tax remittance.

The licensee shall be entitled to apply, and credit against the amount of tax payable by him, an amount equal to 2% of the gross tax to be remitted by him to cover his expense in the collection and remittance of the tax; provided, however, that nothing contained in this section shall apply to any licensee who shall fail or refuse to file his return with said Director of Finance within the time prescribed by this chapter.
