# § 169-13. Appeals.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

Any person aggrieved by any decision or action of the Director under this chapter shall have the right to appeal to the Board of Appeals by filing a written application within 15 days following the effective date of action or decision complained of in such form as the Board of Appeals may prescribe, and the Board of Appeals shall hold a public hearing and shall be empowered thereafter to affirm, abate, reverse or modify the decision or action of the Director.
