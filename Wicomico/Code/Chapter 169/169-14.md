# § 169-14. Levy of tax.

Every person, firm or corporation licensed to operate and conduct a mobile home park or parks in Wicomico County under existing laws and/or regulations pertaining thereto shall pay a monthly tax computed at 15% of the gross monthly charges collected by the mobile home park.
