# § 169-21. Enforcement.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

A. The provisions of this chapter shall be enforced by the Sheriff of Wicomico County and/or the Department, who shall perform such duties as may be assigned to or required of them under this regulation and such other duties as may be assigned to them.
