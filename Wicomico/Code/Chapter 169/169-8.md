# § 169-8. License restrictions.

A. Park licenses shall be issued for the period of one year from July 1 to June 30 in each and every year.

B. No license fees will be prorated, either upon application or upon cancellation, whether voluntary or otherwise.

C. No license issued hereunder shall be transferable, either to another person for the same premises or to other premises by the applicant to whom issued.
