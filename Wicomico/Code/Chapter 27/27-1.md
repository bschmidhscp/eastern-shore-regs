# § 27-1. Printed or typewritten name required. [1959 Code, sec. 25, 1933, ch. 94]

No deed, mortgage, bill of sale, conditional contract of sale, bond or other instrument that may be filed for record in the office of the Clerk of the Circuit Court of Wicomico County shall be admitted to record unless the signature of any signer thereon, whether in the capacity of a party, witness or otherwise, shall be made above the printed or typewritten name of such signer, provided that this section shall not apply to the signature of a Justice of the Peace or notary public thereon.
