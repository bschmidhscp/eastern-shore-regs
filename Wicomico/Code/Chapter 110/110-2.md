# § 110-2. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**OUTDOOR MUSICAL FESTIVAL** Any group or groups of persons participating in or listening to musical entertainment in open spaces and not in a permanent structure.

**PROMOTER** The organizer, operator, producer or the person or persons or corporation staging the outdoor musical festival; and the owner, tenant and lessee of the land upon which the outdoor musical festival is performed.
