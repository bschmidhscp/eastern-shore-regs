# § 110-4. Application.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

The Director of Administration shall not issue a special entertainment permit until the following provisions and requirements shall have been met:

A. Application for said permit shall be made to said Director of Administration at least 21 days prior to the outdoor musical festival. Said application shall be submitted in duplicate to said Director of Administration and shall contain the plans, statements, bonds and assurances as hereinbelow required.

B. The Director of Administration is hereby required to approve or disapprove said application within 10 days from the receipt thereof.

C. In the event same shall be disapproved, the Director of Administration shall notify the promoters immediately by registered mail and shall inform them of the reasons for said disapproval.
