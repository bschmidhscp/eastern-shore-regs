# § 133-1. Authority and purpose.

A. Under authority contained in the Local Government Article of the Maryland Code and for the protection and promotion of the health, safety, comfort, and general welfare of the citizens of Wicomico County, the following chapter is adopted.

B. The enforcement and administration of provisions of this chapter may be delegated, in accordance with § 13-102 of the Local Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, to any organization or municipal corporation in Wicomico County by written contract proposed by the County Executive and approved by the County Council.

C. This chapter is not intended to restrict the authority or duty of the Wicomico County Sheriff's Office.
