# § 179-13. Violations and penalties.

Any person who violates any provision of this chapter or any rule, regulation, directive, restriction, or permit issued pursuant thereto shall be guilty of a civil infraction and shall be subject to a fine of not less than $25 nor more than $1,000. A schedule of fines for violations has been established by resolution of the County Council of Wicomico County. Each day the violation continues shall constitute a separate violation.
