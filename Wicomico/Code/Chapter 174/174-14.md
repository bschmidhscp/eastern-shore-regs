# § 174-14. Definitions.

As used in this article, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**BALLOON** A nonporous bag of air-tight material, generally latex or Mylar, whether filled or unfilled.

**PERSON** Any natural person, firm, corporation, partnership or other organization or group, however organized.
