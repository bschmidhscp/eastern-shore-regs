# § 5-1. Creation and duties of Department.

A. Pursuant to the authority of the Annotated Code of Maryland [formerly Article 1A, § 7-701(e)], there shall be a Department of Aviation under the immediate direction of an Airport Manager who shall perform such duties and conduct such work as the County Executive may require.

B. It shall be the responsibility of the Department to operate the Salisbury-Ocean City Wicomico Regional Airport in accordance with all applicable federal, state and local law.
