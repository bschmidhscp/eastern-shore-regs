# § 5-2. Airport Manager.

The Airport Manager shall be appointed by the County Executive, with the approval of the County Council. The Airport Manager will function as a department head within the County organizational structure and report to the Director of Administration. The Airport Manager is responsible for the overall management and operation of the airport.
