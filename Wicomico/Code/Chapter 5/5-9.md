# § 5-9. Responsibilities.

The Wicomico County Airport Commission shall act in an advisory capacity and shall review and make recommendations to the Airport Manager and the County Executive with respect to the following:

A. All matters concerning comprehensive planning, as well as the formulation of plans for aviation and related services.

B. The creation of a comprehensive plan identifying acquisition, development and rehabilitation projects to support the function and operation of the airport.

C. The utilization of all available facilities to provide aviation services.

D. All matters relating to planning, development, operation, management, fees, budgeting, staffing, and maintenance of the airport with the goal of having the airport operate as a self-supporting enterprise.
