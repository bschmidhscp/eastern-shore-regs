# § 117-6. Adoption of International Energy Conservation Code.

*[Amended 5-6-2025 by Bill No. 2025-05 ]*

A. Adoption of the code. The County Council of Wicomico County hereby adopts the provisions set forth in the International Energy Conservation Code (hereinafter referred to as "IECC"), 2021 Edition, including the appendices and as such shall be adopted, along with the IBC and IRC, as the Building Code for Wicomico County, Maryland, and incorporated by reference. The provisions shall be controlling for all buildings within the corporate limits of Wicomico County, Maryland, unless otherwise exempted herein.

B. Promulgation of new edition and adoption by Wicomico County.

  (1) Within 90 days of promulgation of a new edition of the IEC by the International Code Council, the Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development's Chief Building Inspector shall review the new edition to determine whether any modifications are recommended.

  (2) The Chief Building Inspector shall notify the County Executive and County Council, in writing, of suggested modifications to the new edition, if any, and the justification for each modification. 180 days following the notification by the Chief Building Inspector, any provision of the new edition, to include appendices, not modified by law, shall be adopted as the Building Code for Wicomico County, maryland, and incorporated by reference, but for the modifications currently codified.

  (3) Until a new edition of the IEC is promulgated by the International Code Council and adopted pursuant to this subsection, the former edition of the IEC is the Energy Conservation Code for Wicomico County.

C. A list of the most current edition of the IEC, adopted pursuant to Subsection B, is available on the County website and is available through visiting the Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development.
