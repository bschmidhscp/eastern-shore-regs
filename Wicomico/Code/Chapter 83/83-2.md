# § 83-2. Description.

The County Seal shall be circular in shape, of convenient size and shall bear the words on the outer margin thereof "Wicomico County, Maryland" and in the center thereof, arranged as conveniently as may be, a map of the outline of Wicomico County with the profile of a Wicomico Indian superimposed over said map and the date 1867 to appear below the profile of said Indian.
