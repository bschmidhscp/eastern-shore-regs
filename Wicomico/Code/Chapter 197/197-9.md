# § 197-9. Address numbers.

A. All property subject to this article containing a home, business or other primary use or structure shall have an address number assigned in accordance with the criteria and procedures specified in the road naming and addressing manual. The Department shall keep and maintain the list of properties and addresses.

B. Address numbers shall be assigned in accordance with the criteria and procedures specified in the road naming and addressing manual. Address numbers shall be assigned based on the proposed location of the driveway or entrance to the property.

C. Building permits. An applicant for a building permit shall submit a site plan showing the location of the proposed structure and driveway or entrance to the property. The Department shall assign an address number to a property. The address number shall be assigned and the applicant notified within 10 business days of receiving the application for a building permit.
