# § 197-20. Violations and penalties.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

Any person who violates the provisions of this article is guilty of a civil infraction and shall be subject to a fine of not less than $25 nor more than $1,000. A schedule of fines for violations may be established by resolution of the County Council. Each day the violation continues constitutes a separate infraction.
