# § 197-7. Road naming and addressing manual and maps.

A. The Department shall keep and maintain a road naming and addressing manual which describes in detail the criteria, procedures and methods used to name roads and to assign address numbers to properties in Wicomico County. This manual shall include a grid system from which the address numbers will be derived.

B. The Department shall keep and maintain a set of maps of Wicomico County which display the address grid system and the names of roads.
