# § 197-3. Conditions.

The Department of Public Works, in issuing said permit, shall place such reasonable conditions and restrictions thereon so as to ensure the proper drainage along said county right-of-way and that, when said work is completed, said county right-of-way shall be in the same condition as before the work was started, all of said work to be done at the expense of the owner, contractor or public utility.
