# Article I. Use of Right-of-Way

*[Adopted 11-2-1965 by Res. No. 23 [1] ]*

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: The preamble to this resolution read as follows: "WHEREAS, property owners and/or contractors and public utility companies have been entering the right-of-way of the public roads owned by Wicomico County, Maryland, and placing pipes, wires, gas mains or lines, irrigation pipes, driveway improvements, curbs, gutters and sidewalks thereon which have caused a blocking of stormwater drainage along said County right-of-way, and otherwise damaging said right-of-way; and "WHEREAS, said Wicomico County, Maryland deems it necessary for the health, safety, and general welfare of the citizens of said County to control such works along and under said County right-of-way. "THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY, MARYLAND, That..."
