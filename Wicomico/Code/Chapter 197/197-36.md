# § 197-36. School zones.

Pursuant to the authority granted by § 21-803.1 of the Transportation Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, the County may, by resolution, establish school zones, each consisting of all public roads within a half-mile radius of the named school and the maximum speed limits applicable within the school zone, and within the dates and times specified.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor’s Note: Former § 197-37, Speed-monitoring systems, which immediately followed this section, was repealed 10-17-2017 by Bill No. 2017-11.
