# § 197-23. Payment of fine; election to stand trial; additional penalties.

A. Any fine specified in a citation issued for an infraction of this article is payable to the Director of Finance for Wicomico County.

B. Any person responsible for an infraction of this article shall:

  (1) By the payment date set forth in the citation, pay the fine for the infraction; or

  (2) At least five days before the payment date set forth in the citation, elect to stand trial for the infraction.

    (a) The person responsible for the infraction may elect to stand trial by filing with the Director of Finance for Wicomico County a written notice of intention to stand trial.

    (b) If the person making the election desires the presence at trial of the officer who issued the citation, the notice shall so state.

    (c) If the notice of intention to stand trial does not state that the person making the election desires the presence at trial of the officer who issued the citation, the officer need not appear at the trial.

C. The person responsible for an infraction of this article shall be liable for double the fine assessed by the citation if the fine is not paid by the payment date set forth in the citation; and either

  (1) No timely election is made to stand trial; or

  (2) A timely election is made to stand trial but the person fails to appear for trial.
