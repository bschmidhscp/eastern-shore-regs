# § 197-24. Adjudication of citations.

A. If the fine stated in a citation for an infraction of this article is not paid by the payment date set forth in the citation:

  (1) A copy of the citation accompanied by a request for adjudication may be forwarded to the District Court having venue; and

  (2) Any person responsible for the infraction may be charged with the infraction; and

  (3) Adjudication of the citation shall be governed by the provisions of Chapter 221, §§ 221-7 through 221-11 of the Wicomico County Code.
