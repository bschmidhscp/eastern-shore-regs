# § 197-1. Permit required.

Any property owner, contractor or public utility using the public right-of-way for the purpose of laying pipes, wires, gas mains or lines, irrigation pipes, driveway improvements, curbs, gutters or sidewalks shall first obtain a permit from the Department of Public Works of said county before beginning any such work.
