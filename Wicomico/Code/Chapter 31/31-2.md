# § 31-2. District No. 1, Barren Creek.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the point of intersection of the Nanticoke River and Rewastico Creek and running thence in a northeasterly direction by and with said Rewastico Creek to the Porter Mill Road (County Route No. 437); thence by and with said Porter Mill Road to the Old Railroad Road (County Route No. 430); thence in a northerly direction by and with said Old Railroad Road to Barren Creek Road (State Route No. 467); thence in an easterly direction by and with said Barren Creek Road to the Maryland-Delaware line; thence in a westerly direction along the Maryland-Delaware line to the Maryland-Delaware cornerstone; thence in a northerly direction by and with the Maryland-Delaware line to the point of intersection of the said Maryland-Delaware line with the Cross Road (County Route No. 457); thence in a general westerly direction to the point of intersection of said Cross Road to the road known as Snethen Church Road (County Route No. 452); thence by and with said Snethen Church Road to the Sharptown-Mardela State Road (State Route No. 313); thence by and with the Spring Grove Road (County Route No. 460); in a general northwesterly direction to the Nanticoke River; thence in a general southwesterly direction by and with said Nanticoke River to the point of beginning.
