# § 31-7. District No. 6, Dennis.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at Massey's Crossing on the Pocomoke River and running thence by and with said Massey's Crossing Road (County Route No. 286) in a general southwesterly direction to the Mt. Pleasant Road (County Route No. 215); thence running in a general westerly direction by and with said Mt. Pleasant Road to the Willards-Whiton Road (State Route No. 354); thence running in a southerly direction by and with said Willards-Whiton Road to the Ben Davis Road (County Route No. 272); thence running in a northwesterly direction by and with said Ben Davis Road to the point of intersection with the Morris Road (County Route No. 266); thence running in a southwesterly direction by and with said Morris Road to the Mt. Hermon Road (State Route No. 350); thence running in a general westerly direction by and with said Mt. Hermon Road to the main branch of the Nassawango Creek; thence running by and with said main branch of the Nassawango Creek in a southerly direction to the Wicomico-Worcester County line; thence running by and with said Wicomico-Worcester County line in a general easterly direction to the Pocomoke River and thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Pocomoke River to the point of beginning.
