# § 31-9. District No. 8, Nutters.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1; amended 6-13-1977 by Bill No. 1977-3]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the Nassawango Creek and the Wicomico-Worcester County line and thence running in a westerly direction by and with said Wicomico-Worcester County line to the intersection of said county line and the St. Luke's Road (County Route No. 206); thence running in a general northwesterly direction by and with St. Luke's Road to the Jackson Road (County Route No. 251); thence running in a general easterly direction by and with said Jackson Road to Slab Bridge Creek; thence running in a general northwesterly direction by and with said Slab Bridge Creek to South Division Street Extended (County Route No. 212); thence running by and with said South Division Street Extended in a northerly direction to the Dykes Road (County Route No. 227); thence running by and with the said Dykes Road in an easterly direction to Robins Avenue (County Route No. 216); thence running by and with said Robins Avenue in a northerly direction to the Snow Hill Road (State Route No. 12); thence running by and with said Snow Hill Road in a northwesterly direction to College Avenue Extended (County Route No. 0630); thence running by and with the said College Avenue Extended in an easterly and northerly direction to Beaver Dam Creek; thence running by and with said Beaver Dam Creek in a southeasterly direction to Schumaker Pond; thence running by and with said Schumaker Pond in an easterly direction to Beaver Dam Creek; thence running by and with said Beaver Dam Creek and the center of Parker Pond in a northeasterly direction to the Mt. Hermon Road (State Route No. 350); thence running by and with said Mt. Hermon Road in an easterly direction to the Twilley Bridge Road (County Route No. 244); thence running in a general southeasterly direction by and with said Twilley Bridge Road to the Nassawango Creek; thence running in a general southerly direction by and with said Nassawango Creek to the point of beginning.
