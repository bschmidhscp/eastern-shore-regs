# § 31-11. District No. 10, Sharptown.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the Nanticoke River and the Maryland-Delaware State line; thence running in a southerly direction by and with said Maryland-Delaware State line to the Cross Road (County Route No. 457); thence running in a westerly direction with said Cross Road to the Snethen Church Road (County Route No. 452); thence running by and with the Snethen Church Road in a northwesterly direction to Spring Grove Road (County Route No. 460); thence running in a northwesterly direction by and with said Spring Grove Road to the Nanticoke River; thence running in a general northeasterly direction by and with said Nanticoke River to its intersection with the Maryland-Delaware line at the point of beginning.
