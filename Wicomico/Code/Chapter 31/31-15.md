# § 31-15. District No. 14, Willards.

*[P.L.L. 1930, Art. 23, secs. 82 and 83; 1959 Code, secs. 84 and 85; 1906, ch. 190, secs. 1 and 2; 1965 Code, secs. 93 and 94] [Amended by L. Wic. Co. 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the Maryland-Delaware line with the Tingle Road (County Route No. 143); thence running in a southwesterly direction by and with said Tingle Road to the New Hope Road (County Route No. 162); thence running in a general southeasterly direction by and with said New Hope Road to the Burnt Mill Road (County Route No. 158); thence running in a general southerly direction by and with said Burnt Mill Road to the Poplar Neck Road (County Route No. 129); thence running in a general southeasterly direction by and with said Poplar Neck Road to the Hamblin Ditch; thence running in a general southerly direction by and with said Hamblin Ditch to Old U.S. Route No. 50; thence running in a westerly direction by and with said Old U.S. Route 50 to the White-Rich Road (County Route No. 267); thence running by and with said White-Rich Road in a general southerly direction across the Friendship Road (County Route No. 265) to Pine Branch; thence running by and with said Pine Branch in a southeasterly direction to Savannah Branch; thence running in a general southeasterly direction by and with said Savannah Branch to the Ben Davis Road (County Route No. 272); thence running in a general southeasterly direction by and with said Ben Davis Road to the Willards-Whiton Road (State Route No. 354); thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Willards-Whiton Road to the Mt. Pleasant Road (County Route No. 215); thence running in a general easterly direction by and with said Mt. Pleasant Road to the Massey's Crossing Road (County Route No. 286); thence running in a general northeasterly direction by and with said Massey's Crossing Road to the Pocomoke River; thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Pocomoke River to the Maryland-Delaware line and thence running in a westerly direction by and with the said Maryland-Delaware line to the point of beginning.
