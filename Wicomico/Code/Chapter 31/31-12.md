# § 31-12. District No. 11, Delmar. [P.L.L. 1930, Art. 23, secs. 76 and 77; 1959 Code, secs. 78 and 79; 1898 ch. 74, secs. 1 and 2. 1965 Code, secs. 87 and 88]

*[Amended by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the Maryland-Delaware State line with the Stage Road (County Route No. 176) and thence running by and with said Stage Road in a southerly direction to U.S. Route No. 13; thence running in a southerly direction by and with said U.S. Route No. 13 to Leonard Mill Pond; thence running in a general southwesterly direction by and with said Leonard Mill Pond and Leonard Mill Run to the Naylor Mill Road (County Route No. 431); thence running in a general northwesterly direction by and with said Naylor Mill Road to its point of intersection with the Log Cabin Road (County Route No. 441); thence running in a general northwesterly direction by and with said Log Cabin Road to the Hickory Mill Road (County Route No. 422); thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Hickory Mill Road and the extension thereof, to the Maryland-Delaware line and thence running in an easterly direction by and with said Maryland-Delaware line to the point of beginning.
