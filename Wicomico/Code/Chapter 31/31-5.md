# § 31-5. District No. 4, Pittsburg.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the Maryland-Delaware line and the Tingle Road (County Route No. 143) and thence running in a southwesterly direction by and with said Tingle Road to the New Hope Road (County Route No. 162); thence running in a southeasterly direction by and with said New Hope Road to its intersection with the Burnt Mill Road (County Route No. 158); thence running in a southerly direction by and with said Burnt Mill Road to its intersection with the Poplar Neck Road (County Route No. 129); thence running in a southeasterly direction by and with said Poplar Neck Road to the Hamblin Ditch; thence running in a general southerly direction by and with said Hamblin Ditch to Old U.S. Route No. 50 (same being the road from Salisbury to Ocean City); thence in a westerly direction by and with said Old U.S. Route No. 50 to the White-Rich Road (County Route No. 267); thence running in a southerly direction by and with said White-Rich Road across the Friendship Road (County Route No. 265) to the Pine Branch; thence running in a general southeasterly direction by and with said Pine Branch to Savannah Branch; thence running by and with said Savannah Branch to the Ben Davis Road (County Route No. 272); thence running in a general northwesterly direction by and with said Ben Davis Road to the Morris Road (County Route No. 266); thence running in a general southwesterly direction by and with said Morris Road to the Mt. Hermon Road (State Route No. 350); thence running in a general westerly direction by and with said Mt. Hermon Road to the main prong of Nassawango Creek; thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Nassawango Creek to the Shavox Road (County Route No. 207); thence running in a general westerly direction by and with said Shavox Road to the Forest Grove Road (County Route No. 298); thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Forest Grove Road to Old U.S. Route No. 50; thence in a general easterly direction by and with said Old U.S. Route No. 50 to Broad Street in the village of Parsonsburg; thence running in a northerly direction by and with said Broad Street to the Parsonsburg-Melson Road (County Route No. 130); thence running in a northerly direction by and with said Parsonsburg-Melson Road to the Melson Road (County Route No. 145); thence running westerly by and with said Melson Road to the Melson Delaware Line Road (County Route No. 174); thence running by and with the Melson Delaware Line Road in a northerly direction to the Maryland-Delaware Line; thence running in an easterly direction by and with said Maryland-Delaware Line to the point of beginning.
