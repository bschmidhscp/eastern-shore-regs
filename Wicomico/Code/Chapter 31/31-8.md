# § 31-8. District No. 7, Trappe.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of Wicomico River with Wicomico Creek and thence running by and with said Wicomico River in a northeasterly direction to its intersection with White Creek; thence running by and with said White Creek in a southerly direction to the Walnut Tree Road (County Route No. 308); thence running by and with said Walnut Tree Road in a southeasterly direction to the Allen Road (County Route No. 344); thence running by and with said Allen Road in a southwesterly direction to the intersection of the Old Eden Road (County Route No. 366); thence running by and with said Old Eden Road and the extension thereof in a southerly direction directly into Passerdyke Creek; thence running by and with said Passerdyke Creek and the Wicomico Creek in a westerly direction to the Wicomico River at the point of beginning.
