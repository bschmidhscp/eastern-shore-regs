# § 31-3. District No. 2, Quantico.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the Nanticoke River and Rewastico Creek and proceeding thence by and with said Rewastico Creek to the Porter Mill Road (County Route No. 437); thence by and with said Porter Mill Road in a northeasterly direction to the intersection of Braggs Lane (County Route No. 439); thence by and with said Braggs Lane in a southeasterly direction to the Rewastico Road (County Route No. 433) and proceeding directly across said Rewastico Road and following an old road through the woods in a general southeasterly direction to the Hebron-Quantico Road (State Route No. 347); thence in a northeasterly direction by and with said Hebron-Quantico Road to the point of intersection of said road with the Quantico Creek Road (County Route No. 429); thence in a southeasterly direction by and with said Quantico Creek Road to the Fire Tower Road (County Route No. 428); thence running in a southeasterly direction by and with said Fire Tower Road to Crooked Oak Lane (County Route No. 473); thence running in an easterly direction by and with said Crooked Oak Lane to the Rockawalkin Road (County Route No. 475); thence running in a southerly direction by and with said Rockawalkin Road to the point where it intersects the Salisbury-Nanticoke Road (State Route No. 349); thence continuing in a southerly direction directly across the said Salisbury-Nanticoke Road by and with the Rockawalkin Road (County Route No. 314) to Pemberton Drive (County Route No. 301); thence in a southwesterly direction by and with said Pemberton Drive to the North Upper Ferry Road (County Route No. 320); thence in a southerly direction by and with said North Upper Ferry Road to the Wicomico River; thence in a southwesterly direction by and with said Wicomico River to Green Hill Creek; thence in a general northwesterly direction by and with said Green Hill Creek to the headwaters of the northwesterly fork of said Green Hill Creek; thence in a northwesterly direction to the headwaters of Dennis Creek and from thence running to the Quantico Creek; thence running in a general westerly direction by and with said Quantico Creek to the Nanticoke River and thence by and with said Nanticoke River in a general northeasterly direction to the point of beginning.
