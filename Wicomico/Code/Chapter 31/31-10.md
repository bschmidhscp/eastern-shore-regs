# § 31-10. District No. 9, Salisbury.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the east prong of the Wicomico River and South Division Street; thence running in a northerly direction by and with said South Division Street and North Division Street to U.S. Route No. 13; thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said U.S. Route 13 to the Old Delmar Road (County Route No. 488); thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Old Delmar Road to the intersection of said Old Delmar Road and Naylor Mill Road (County Route No. 431); thence running by and with said Naylor Mill Road in an easterly direction to U.S. Route No. 13; thence running in a northerly direction by and with said U.S. Route No. 13 to Leonard Mill Pond; thence running in a general southwesterly direction by and with said Leonard Mill Pond and Leonard Mill Run to the Naylor Mill Road (County Route No. 431); thence running in a general westerly direction by and with said Naylor Mill Road to its point of intersection with the Log Cabin Road (County Route No. 441); thence running in a general northwesterly direction by and with said Log Cabin Road to the White-Lowe Road (County Route No. 420); thence running in a general southerly direction by and with said White-Lowe Road to U.S. Route No. 50; thence running in a general westerly direction by and with said U.S. Route No. 50 to the Rockawalkin Road (County Route No. 475); thence running in a general southwesterly direction by and with said Rockawalkin Road to State Route No. 349; thence crossing State Route No. 349 and by and with Rockawalkin Road (County Route No. 314) to Pemberton Drive (County Route No. 301); thence running in a general southwesterly direction by and with said Pemberton Drive to the North Upper Ferry Road (County Route No. 320); thence running in a southerly direction by and with said North Upper Ferry Road to the Wicomico River and thence running in a general northeasterly direction by and with the meandering of the Wicomico River to the point of beginning.
