# § 31-6. District No. 5, Parsons.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the Twilley Bridge Road (County Route No. 244) and the main prong of Nassawango Creek; thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Nassawango Creek to the Shavox Road (County Route No. 207); thence running in a general westerly direction by and with said Shavox Road to the Forest Grove Road (County Route No. 298); thence running in a general northerly direction by and with said Forest Grove Road to Old U.S. Route No. 50; thence running in a general easterly direction along Old U.S. Route No. 50 to Broad Street in the Village of Parsonsburg; thence running in a northerly direction by and with said Broad Street to the Parsonsburg-Melson Road (County Route No. 130); thence running in a northerly direction by and with said Parsonsburg-Melson Road to the Melson Road (County Route No. 145); thence running in a westerly direction by and with said Melson Road to the Melson Delaware Line Road (County Route No. 174); thence running in a northerly direction by and with said Melson Delaware Line Road to the Maryland-Delaware line; thence running in a westerly direction by and with said Maryland-Delaware line to the Stage Road (County Route No. 176); thence running in a southerly direction by and with said Stage Road to a point where it intersects U.S. Route No. 13 (Salisbury-Delaware line Road); thence running in a southerly direction along said U.S. Route No. 13 to the Naylor Mill Road (County Route No. 431); thence running in a westerly direction by and with said Naylor Mill Road to the Old Delmar Road (County Route No. 488) (from Delmar to Salisbury); thence running in a general southerly direction along said Old Delmar Road to U.S. Route No. 13; thence running in a general southerly or southwesterly direction by and with said U.S. Route 13 to North Division Street in Salisbury; thence running in a general southerly direction by and with said North Division Street and South Division Street to the east prong of the Wicomico River; thence running by and with the east prong of the Wicomico River, the Beaver Dam Creek, Schumaker Pond and Parker Pond in an easterly direction to the Mt. Hermon Road (State Route No. 350); thence running in a general easterly direction by and with said Mt. Hermon Road to the Twilley Bridge Road (County Route No. 244); thence running in a general southeasterly direction by and with said Twilley Bridge Road to the point of beginning.
