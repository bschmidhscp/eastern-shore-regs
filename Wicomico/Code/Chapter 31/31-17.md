# § 31-17. District No. 16, Fruitland.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 23, secs. 86 and 87; 1959 Code, secs. 88, 89, 90 and 91; 1922, ch. 221, secs. 1, 2 and 3; 1949, ch. 298, sec. 1; 1965 Code, secs. 97, 98, 99 and 100] [Amended by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the St. Luke's Road (County Route No. 206) and the Wicomico-Worcester County line; thence running in a northwesterly direction by and with said St. Luke's Road to the Jackson Road (County Route No. 251); thence running in an easterly direction by and with said Jackson Road to the Slab Bridge Creek; thence running in a northwesterly direction by and with said Slab Bridge Creek to South Division Street Extended (County Route No. 212); thence running by and with said South Division Street Extended in a northeasterly direction to Tony Tank Creek at Coulbourne Mill Pond; thence running in a northwesterly direction by and with said Tony Tank Creek and Tony Tank Lake to the Wicomico River; thence running by and with said Wicomico River in a westerly direction to its intersection with White Creek; thence running in a southerly direction by and with said White Creek to Walnut Tree Road (County Route No. 308); thence running in a southeasterly direction by and with said Walnut Tree Road to the Allen Road (County Route No. 344); thence running by and with the said Allen Road in a southwesterly direction to the Old Eden Road (County Route No. 366); thence running in a southerly direction by and with said Old Eden Road and an extension thereof to Passerdyke Creek; thence running by and with said Passerdyke Creek in a southeasterly direction to its intersection with the Wicomico-Somerset-Worcester County line; thence running in an easterly direction by and with the Wicomico-Worcester County line to the St. Luke's Road at the point of beginning.
