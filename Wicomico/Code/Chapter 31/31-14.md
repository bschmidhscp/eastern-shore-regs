# § 31-14. District No. 13, Camden.

*[P.L.L. 1930, Art. 23, secs. 80 and 81; 1959 Code, secs. 82 and 83; 1904, ch. 133, secs. 1 and 2; 1965 Code, secs. 91 and 92] [Amended by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1; 6-13-1977 by Bill No. 1977-3]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of the Tony Tank Creek with the Wicomico River; thence running by and with said Tony Tank Creek and the north prong of Tony Tank Lake in an easterly direction to its intersection with South Division Street Extended (County Route No. 212) at Coulbourne Mill Pond; thence running by and with South Division Street Extended in a northerly direction to Dykes Road (County Route No. 227); thence running by and with said Dykes Road in an easterly direction to Robins Avenue (County Route No. 216); thence running by and with said Robins Avenue in a northerly direction to the Snow Hill Road (State Route No. 12); thence running by and with said Snow Hill Road in a northwesterly direction to College Avenue Extended (County Route No. 0630); thence running by and with said College Avenue Extended in an easterly and northerly direction to Beaver Dam Creek; thence running in a westerly direction by and with said Beaver Dam Creek to the east prong of the Wicomico River; thence running by and with said east prong of the Wicomico River to the Wicomico River; thence running by and with said Wicomico River in a southwesterly direction to the intersection of Tony Tank Creek to the point of beginning.
