# § 31-16. District No. 15, Hebron.

*[P.L.L. 1930, Art. 23, secs. 84 and 85; 1959 Code, secs. 86 and 87, 1912, ch. 536, secs. 1 and 2. 1965 Code, secs. 95 and 96] [Amended by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1]*

Beginning for the same at the intersection of U.S. Route No. 50 and the White-Lowe Road (County Route No. 420); thence running in a northerly direction by and with said White-Lowe Road to the Log Cabin Road (County Route No. 441); thence running in a westerly direction by and with said Log Cabin Road to the Hickory Mill Road (County Route No. 422); thence running by and with said Hickory Mill Road and the extension thereof in a northerly direction to the Maryland-Delaware line; thence running in a westerly direction by and with said Maryland-Delaware line to the Barren Creek Road (State Route No. 467); thence running by and with the Barren Creek Road in a westerly direction to the Old Railroad Road (County Route No. 430); thence running in a southerly direction by and with said Old Railroad Road to Porter Mill Road (County Route No. 437); thence running in a southwesterly direction by and with said Porter Mill Road to Braggs Lane (County Route No. 439); thence running by and with said Braggs Lane in a southeasterly direction to Rewastico Road (County Route No. 433) and thence proceeding directly across said Rewastico Road in a southeasterly direction following an old road through the woods to the Hebron-Quantico Road (State Route No. 347); thence running in a northeasterly direction by and with said Hebron-Quantico Road to the Quantico Creek Road (County Route No. 429); thence running in a general southeasterly direction by and with said Quantico Creek Road to the Fire Tower Road (County Route No. 428); thence running in a southeasterly direction by and with said Fire Tower Road to Crooked Oak Lane (County Route No. 473); thence running in an easterly direction by and with said Crooked Oak Lane to the Rockawalkin Road (County Route No. 475); thence running in a northeasterly direction by and with said Rockawalkin Road to U.S. Route No. 50; thence running in a general easterly direction by and with said U.S. Route No. 50 to the White-Lowe Road at the point of beginning.
