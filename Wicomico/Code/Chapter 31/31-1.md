# § 31-1. Division into districts; polling places.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1; amended 2-13-1985 by Bill No. 1985-5; 6-14-1993 by Bill No. 1993-15]*

Wicomico County is divided into 15 election districts according to their names, bounds and limits, as fully set forth in §§ 31-2 through 31-18, in each of which districts all elections for public officers shall be held at such location or locations in each district as may from time to time be so designated by the Supervisors of Elections of said Wicomico County. If no suitable location is available in any given district, a suitable location or locations in the adjoining district may be so designated by the Supervisors of Elections of Wicomico County, in accordance with Article 33, § 2-11, of the Annotated Code of Maryland.
