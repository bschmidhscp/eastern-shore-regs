# § 31-19. Plats.

*[Added by L. Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 1; amended 6-13-1977 by Bill No. 1977-3]*

Plats designating the boundaries of the election districts fully described in § 31-2 through 31-18, inclusive, hereinbefore set forth, are recorded among the land records of Wicomico County, Maryland (Plats) in Liber J.W.T.S. No. 487, folios 97-104 inclusive and in Liber A.J.S. No. 876 folio 528.
