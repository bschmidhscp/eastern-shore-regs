# § 163-1. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms have the meaning indicated:

**INVASIVE BAMBOO** Any bamboo species from the genus Phyllostachys.

**PERSON** Any natural person, firm, corporation, partnership, organization or group, however organized.
