# § 225-103.1. Medical clinic.

*[Added 1-9-2025 by Bill No. 2024-13 ]*

A. In the R-8 Residential District, a medical clinic is permitted, provided:

  (1) A development plan shall be required and approved by the Planning and Zoning Commission prior to the issuance of a building permit or zoning authorization for a medical clinic.

  (2) The required minimum lot size shall be 10 acres.

  (3) The total floor area of all structures shall not exceed 20,000 square feet.

  (4) All buildings and structures shall be set back at least 100 feet from all property lines.

  (5) A landscaped buffer of at least 25 feet in width shall be provided along all property lines. All landscaping elements shall be demonstrated on the development plan.

  (6) The location of exterior lighting fixtures with related standards shall be demonstrated on the development plan.

  (7) All sign elevations and locations shall be demonstrated on the development plan.

  (8) Comply with the parking standards and requirements as set forth in this chapter.
