# § 225-84. Crematorium.

Crematoriums require:

A. That a screen be provided to adequately separate this use from adjacent uses or properties.

B. Setback from any residential lot line existing at the time of application: 200 feet.

C. Requirements for air quality/emission control under regulations of the Maryland Department of the Environment.
