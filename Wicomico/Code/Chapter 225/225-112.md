# § 225-112. Sawmill and other wood process facilities, except treatment.

A. Sawmill setback requirements: 600 feet from any dwelling not located on the same premises or from any place of assembly.

B. In the A-1 Agriculture-Rural District, temporary sawmills less than six months shall be used for the cutting of timber grown on the premises.
