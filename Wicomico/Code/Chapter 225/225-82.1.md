# § 225-82.1. Cannabis businesses.

*[Added 11-21-2023 by Bill No. 2023-16 ; amended 8-6-2024 by Bill No. 2024-07 ]*

A. A cannabis business may not locate within:

  (1) 500 feet of a preexisting primary or secondary school in the state;

  (2) 500 feet of a preexisting licensed child-care center or registered family child-care home, playground, recreation center, library, place of assembly or public park;

  (3) 2,640 feet of another cannabis business; or

  (4) 100 feet of a preexisting residence.

B. A cannabis on-site consumption establishment is prohibited within Wicomico County.

C. A cannabis business not enumerated as a permitted use in § 225-67 is prohibited within Wicomico County. A licensed grower cultivating cannabis, exclusively outdoors and in an area zoned only for agricultural use, shall comport with state law and the separation requirements set forth in Subsection A.

D. The separation requirements enumerated above shall be measured from the boundary of the real property owned or leased by an entity in Subsection A. A primary or accessory structure of a cannabis business may not locate within the respective separation areas.

E. The Board of Appeals may modify the separation requirements in Subsection A by special exception.
