# § 225-103. Marinas.

A. Commercial marinas:

  (1) Shall only moor or berth:

    (a) Small boats and pleasure craft; or

    (b) Commercial vessels less than 45 feet in length.

  (2) May provide for their storage, service and repair.

  (3) In the A-1 Agriculture-Rural District, shall be located on a land area of five acres.

B. Industrial marina/shipyard.

  (1) Industrial marina/shipyard shall meet the following standards:

    (a) Land area: two acres;

    (b) Shall be located on an upland site;

    (c) Shall be served by adequate collector roads designed and equipped for the haulout of large vessels; and

    (d) May have interior and exterior areas for construction, repair, restoration, or recovery of vessels.

  (2) Any work performed on commercial metal boats, ships or barges is an industrial use that can only be performed in industrial marina/shipyards or marine terminals.

C. Recreational marinas shall only moor or berth:

  (1) Small boats and pleasure craft; or

  (2) Commercial vessels less than 45 feet in length.
