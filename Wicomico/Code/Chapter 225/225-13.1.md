# § 225-13.1. Zoning authorization requirements.

A. No building or other structure shall be constructed, moved, added to or structurally altered and no land used or use of land changed without a zoning authorization being granted.

B. No zoning authorization shall be issued which is not in conformity with the provisions of this chapter, except after written order from the Board of Appeals granting a variance for such nonconformity.
