# § 225-117. Warehouse.

In an I-1 Light Industrial District:

A. There shall be no open parking of refrigerated vehicles or other vehicles with other continuously running equipment.

B. Wholesale merchandising and warehousing, excluding truck terminals, may be permitted, provided that there is no open parking of refrigerated vehicles or other vehicles with other continuously running equipment.
