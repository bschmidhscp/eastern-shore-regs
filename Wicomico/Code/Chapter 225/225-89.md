# § 225-89. Dwelling - tenant house.

A. A tenant house intended to house farm labor shall be either a single-wide or double-wide manufactured home and shall be on the same parcel as the primary use.

B. One tenant house may be erected per every contiguous 25 acres under single ownership without subdivision.

C. Any additional tenant houses may be permitted by special exception from the Board of Appeals.
