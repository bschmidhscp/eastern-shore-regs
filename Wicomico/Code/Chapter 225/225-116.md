# § 225-116. Theater, outdoor.

A. Any structures, fence or parking area shall be set back at least 100 feet from any highway;

B. There shall be provided a service road, for entrance and exit on the theater property, with access to a public highway at two points, each distant at least 200 feet in opposite directions from the center of the property; and

C. No outdoor theater screen shall be visible in total or in part at any angle from either a state highway or a county road.
