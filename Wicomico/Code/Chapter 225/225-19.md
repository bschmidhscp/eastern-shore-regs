# § 225-19. Interpretation of the Zoning Map.

A. The Board of Appeals is authorized to interpret the Zoning Map and to pass upon disputed questions of lot or district boundary lines and similar questions. If such questions arise in the context of an appeal from a decision of the Zoning Administrator or Planning Commission, they shall be handled as provided in § 225-18, Location and boundaries of districts.

B. An application for a map interpretation shall be submitted to the Board of Appeals by filing a copy of the application with the Zoning Administrator. The application shall contain sufficient information to enable the Board to make the necessary interpretation.
