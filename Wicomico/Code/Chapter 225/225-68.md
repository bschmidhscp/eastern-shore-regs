# § 225-68. Residential density.

A. Every lot created after the effective date of this chapter shall have the minimum number of square feet of land area as required by the Schedule of District Regulations (§ 225-74) or by the Wicomico County Health Department, whichever is greater.

B. The maximum single-family residential density permitted on a tract of land shall be as set forth in the Schedule of Maximum Permitted Residential Densities (§ 225-75). In determining the number of dwelling units permitted on a tract of land, fractions shall be rounded to the nearest whole number.
