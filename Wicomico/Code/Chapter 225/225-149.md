# § 225-149. Steep slopes.

A. No structure, impervious surface or land disturbance should be located on slope with a grade of 15% or greater unless it is determined that the structure, impervious surface or land disturbance is appropriate for stabilization of the slope.

B. A minimum fifty-foot buffer shall be established between development and the crest of slopes in excess of 25%.
