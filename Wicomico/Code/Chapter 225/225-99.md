# § 225-99. Kennels.

*[Amended 2-18-2025 by Bill No. 2025-01 ]*

A. Kennels in the A-1 Agriculture-Rural, TT Town Transition or V-C Village Conservation Districts shall:

  (1) Have a minimum acreage as follows:

    (a) Five acres if outside runs; or

    (b) Two acres if no outside runs.

  (2) Not have the runs or a structure, which houses the dogs, located within 500 feet of an existing residential dwelling on another parcel.

B. In a C-2 General Commercial District, kennels are permitted, provided that runs for dogs shall be entirely contained within a building.

C. A property which received a special exception to operate a kennel prior to January 1, 2025, may continue as a nonconforming special exception as provided by § 225-152. All other properties with 10 or more dogs prior to the effective date of Legislative Bill 2025-01, shall continue as a nonconforming use as provided by § 225-150; however, the ability to continue the nonconforming use shall terminate three years from the effective date of Legislative Bill 2025-01 or noncompliance with § 225-150F, whichever comes first.

D. The Board of Appeals may modify the separation requirements in Subsection A(2) as part of the special exception application.
