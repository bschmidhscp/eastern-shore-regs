# § 225-85. Dwelling - emergency housing.

A. The use of a dwelling for emergency housing shall be limited to one year unless good cause is shown to extend such time.

B. The dwelling shall be located on the same or an abutting lot as a dwelling made uninhabitable by fire, flood, or other natural disaster and is occupied by the persons displaced by such disaster.
