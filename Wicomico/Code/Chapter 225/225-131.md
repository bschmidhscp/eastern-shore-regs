# § 225-131. Access.

Access to lots shall be provided as follows in C-1 Select Commercial, C-3 Regional Commercial, LB-1 Light Business and Institutional and LB-2 Light Business and Residential Districts:

A. Access to the development shall be provided only in such a manner as will not cause traffic congestion nor aggravate existing congestion.

B. Direct access onto a street or major highway shall be reduced or eliminated wherever the approving authority determines that alternative or unified points of access are available to a site resulting in better traffic flow and less traffic congestion.
