# § 225-114. Schools of general instruction.

A. In R-8, R-15 and R-20 Residential Districts, a school may be permitted, provided:

  (1) Lot area: five acres; and

  (2) All buildings, structures and play equipment are set back a minimum of 50 feet from the adjoining property lines.

B. In an R-30 Residential District, a school may be permitted, provided:

  (1) Lot area: 10 acres; and

  (2) All buildings, structures, and play equipment are set back a minimum of 50 feet from the adjoining property lines.
