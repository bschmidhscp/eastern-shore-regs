# § 18-3. Compensation for County Council members.

*[Added 10-15-1981 by Bill No. 1981-20; amended 10-13-1984 by Bill No. 1984-8; 2-13-1990 by Bill No. 1990-3; 2-14-1994 by Bill No. 1994-6; 3-1-2022 by Bill No. 2022-01 ]*

Commencing with the installation of the County Council elected at the general election in November of 2022, compensation to be paid to the members of the Wicomico County Council will be $21,000 a year to each member of the Council; $23,000 to the Vice President of the Council while serving in that capacity; and $25,000 a year to the President of said Council while serving in that capacity. In addition to the above compensation, each member shall receive allowances for traveling and any other necessary expenses actually incurred in the exercise of his official duties. Such allowances for traveling and any other necessary expenses incurred by the members of the Wicomico County Council in the exercise of their official duties shall be provided for in the annual operating budget of Wicomico County.
