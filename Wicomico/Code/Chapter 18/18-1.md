# § 18-1. Creation; membership.

*[Amended 6-15-1981 by Bill No. 1981-19; 2-13-1984 by Bill No. 1984-2; 2-16-1993 by Bill No. 1993-4]*

A. Pursuant to the authority of Article 25A, § 5(AA), of the Annotated Code of Maryland, the Wicomico County Council hereby establishes the Compensation and Allowance Commission for Wicomico County.

B. The Compensation and Allowance Commission for Wicomico County shall consist of 11 members, one of whom shall be designated as Chairman. The members shall be appointed to the Compensation and Allowance Commission no later than six months prior to the date required for the submission of the Commission's recommendation as set forth in § 18-2 of this chapter.
