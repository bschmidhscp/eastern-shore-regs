# § 18-2. Powers and duties.

*[Amended 2-15-1978 by Bill No. 1978-6 ; 6-15-1981 by Bill No. 1981-19 ; 2-13-1984 by Bill No. 1984-2 ; 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

The Commission, pursuant to the Charter of Wicomico County and Article 25A, § 5(AA), of the Annotated Code of Maryland, 1957 Edition, as amended, shall study compensation and allowances paid to the County Executive and the members of the County Council of Wicomico County, Maryland. It shall submit to the County Council its recommendation for compensation and allowances within 15 days after the beginning of the fourth year of the term of each Council. The Commission may recommend an increase or decrease in the compensation and allowances paid to the County Executive and the members of the Wicomico County Council, but in no event shall said compensation or allowances be less than provided in the Charter of Wicomico County. Upon receiving the recommendation made by resolution of the Compensation and Allowance Commission for Wicomico County, the County Council may reduce or reject the Commission’s recommendations, but it shall not increase any item recommended in the resolution. The recommendations contained in the resolution shall become effective upon the adoption by the County Council of an ordinance encompassing the recommendations, but the salaries and allowances specified at the time the Council or County Executive takes office shall not change for that period during which the Council or County Executive was elected. The ordinance making any change in the salary and allowances paid to members of the County Council or County Executive shall be ordained prior to the election for the members of the next succeeding Council and take effect only for the members of the next succeeding Council or next succeeding County Executive.
