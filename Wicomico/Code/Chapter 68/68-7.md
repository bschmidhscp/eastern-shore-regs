# § 68-7. State appropriation.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 23, sec. 176; 1959 Code, sec. 220. 1916, ch. 416, sec. 6]*

Should the State of Maryland make an appropriation to aid in this work without declaring how same shall be applied, then same shall be applied to the amount of debt incurred in each county in draining said swamplands according to the number of acres of swampland in each of said counties.
