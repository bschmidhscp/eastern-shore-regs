# § 37-9. Representation restrictions.

A. A former public official may not assist or represent any person, other than the County, for compensation in any matter involving the County if that matter is one in which the former public official significantly participated as a public official.

B. A former elected official may not assist or represent another person for compensation on a matter that is the subject of legislative action for a period of one year following leaving office.[Amended 5-19-2020 by Bill No. 2020-03]
