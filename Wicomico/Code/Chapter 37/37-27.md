# § 37-27. Exemptions and modifications.

A. The Commission may after consultation with the County Attorney, grant exemptions to or modifications of the provisions of Articles III and V of this chapter for an employee, a member or prospective member of a County board or commission, when the Commission finds that an exemption or modification would not be contrary to the purposes of this chapter, and the application of this chapter would:

  (1) Constitute an unreasonable invasion of privacy; or

  (2) Reduce the availability of qualified persons for public service.

B. The Commission may grant an exemption or modification authorized under Subsection A of this section only on the written request of the employee, board, commission, member, or prospective member involved.
