# § 200-18. Waivers.

*[Amended 1-2-1990 by Bill No. 1989-20; 6-14-1993 by Bill No. 1993-17; 2-14-1994 by Bill No. 1994-5; 6-22-1998 by Bill No. 1998-6 ; 12-18-2012 by Bill No. 2012-13 ]*

The Planning Commission may waive the requirements of Article II of this chapter provided that:

A. Any such waivers may only be permitted where strict adherence to the provisions of these standards would cause practical difficulty for an applicant or subdivider.

B. Such waivers can only be granted where the applicant demonstrates that approving the waiver will not substantially impair the purposes and intent of this chapter or any other County regulation.

C. Any waivers thus granted by the Planning Commission shall be stated in the records of the Commission.

D. No waivers may be granted to subdivisions which have received a sketch plat review or preliminary plat approval prior to February 3, 1998, except as set forth above.
