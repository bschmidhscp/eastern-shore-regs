# § 15-2. Appointment of Director.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

Such Director shall be appointed by the Governor of Maryland, pursuant to the provisions of Public Safety Article, § 14-109 of the Annotated Code of Maryland, upon recommendation of the County Executive.
