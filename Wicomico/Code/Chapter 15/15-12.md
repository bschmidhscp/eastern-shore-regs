# § 15-12. Authorization.

A. Wicomico County shall have the authority to participate in the Maryland Emergency Management Assistance Compact in the event of an emergency or disaster.

B. The county's participation shall be in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth in Article 16A, §§ 37 through 39,[1] and shall constitute an agreement among participating entities which have adopted similar resolutions or ordinances.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: See now Public Safety Article, §§ 14-801 through 14-803.

C. Participation may include requests for and provision of personnel, equipment, materials and other forms of assistance, to any participating political subdivision within the State of Maryland.
