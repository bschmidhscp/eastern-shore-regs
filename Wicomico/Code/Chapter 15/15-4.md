# § 15-4. Responsibilities of Director.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

The Director shall be responsible for coordinating emergency preparation and emergency activities of the county government with other governmental units and shall be responsible for coordinating county activities designed to minimize repair, injury or damage caused during such emergencies, at the direction of the Wicomico County Executive.
