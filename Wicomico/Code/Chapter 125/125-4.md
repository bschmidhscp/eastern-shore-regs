# § 125-4. Official maps.

*[Amended 10-23-2001 by Bill No. 2001-13]*

A. District Maps. The Wicomico County Critical Area shall be delineated on Official Critical Area District Maps prepared as part of the Wicomico County Critical Area Program. The Critical Area District Maps shall be maintained in force as official maps of the county. The Critical Area District Maps shall delineate the extent of the Critical Area District in Wicomico County which is as defined in § 125-3B.

B. Special Buffer Area Maps. Those areas which have been mapped by the County and approved by the Critical Area Commission as special Buffer areas shall be delineated on official Critical Area Special Buffer Area Maps and maintained in force as official maps of the County. These maps shall delineate the extent of special Buffer areas as defined in § 125-11.
