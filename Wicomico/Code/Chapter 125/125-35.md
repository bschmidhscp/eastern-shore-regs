# § 125-35. Authorization.

*[Amended 6-29-1999 by Bill No. 1999-5 ; 7-6-2004 by Bill No. 2004-6 ]*

The Wicomico County Board of Appeals is hereby empowered to grant variances to the provisions of this chapter where, owing to special features of a site or other circumstances, a literal enforcement of provisions would result in unwarranted hardship.
