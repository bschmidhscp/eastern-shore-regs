# § 29-25. Definition of terms.

As used in this article, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**COMMUNITY** Each census block group in Wicomico County, as defined by the current version of the Blueprint for Maryland's Future: Interim Report on Neighborhood Indicators of Poverty, is a separate community.

**COMMUNITY-BASED INITIATIVE** Is a program or service that provides education, vocational education or rehabilitation, job training, health or social services to individuals in the County.

**COMMUNITY-BASED ORGANIZATION** Is a public or private company, corporation, limited liability company, neighborhood group, organization, or partnership that provides educational, vocational education or rehabilitation, job training, health, or social services to individuals in the County.

**DISPROPORTIONATELY IMPACTED AREA** A geographic area identified by the Office of Social Equity that has had above 150% of the state's ten-year average for cannabis possession charges.

**FUND** The Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund established pursuant to § 1-322 of the Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis Article of the Maryland Code.

**LOCAL MANAGEMENT BOARD** The Wicomico County Local Management Board established pursuant to § 8-301 of the Human Services Article of the Maryland Code.

**LOW-INCOME COMMUNITIES** A Tier 4 or Tier 5 community according to the current version of the Blueprint for Maryland's Future: Interim Report on Neighborhood Indicators of Poverty.

**OFFICE OF SOCIAL EQUITY** The independent office within the Maryland Cannabis Administration established pursuant to § 1-309.1 of the Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis Article of the Maryland Code.
