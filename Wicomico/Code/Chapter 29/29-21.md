# § 29-21. Appropriation of County funds required to obtain or use grant funding.

If the expenditure of matching or other funds by the County is required by the terms and conditions of a grant in order to obtain funding thereunder, such funds shall be deemed to be appropriated by amendment of the County budget(s) if such expenditure is expressly stated and identified by amount(s) and purpose(s) in the documentation regarding the grant that has been furnished by the County Executive to the Council prior to its approval of the anticipated or unanticipated grant revenue; provided, however, that such appropriation of County funds is not subject to increase or other change except by subsequent approval by the Council.
