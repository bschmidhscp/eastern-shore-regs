# § 29-26. Use, distribution and reporting.

A. Permitted uses.

  (1) The fund may only be used for:

    (a) Funding community-based initiatives intended to benefit low-income communities;

    (b) Funding community-based initiatives that serve disproportionately impacted areas; and

    (c) Any related administrative expenses.

  (2) Prohibited uses.

    (a) Money may not be expended from the fund for law enforcement agencies or activities.

    (b) Money expended from the fund is supplemental to and may not supplant funding that otherwise would be appropriated for preexisting local government programs.

B. Distribution of the fund.

  (1) The County Council shall award and appropriate all money received from the Office of Social Equity for the Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund, which may only be used to fund initiatives in accordance with Subsection A(1) of this section. The Local Management Board shall distribute all funds.

  (2) The Local Management Board shall develop criteria for making an award prior to the solicitation of proposals. In developing criteria to make an award, the Local Management Board shall consider any guidance provided by the Office of Social Equity.

  (3) A community-based organization previously awarded money from the fund may not apply for a new award until all reporting, in accordance with Subsection B(1) of this section, for a previous award has been completed and approved by the Local Management Board.

  (4) Any money not awarded that fiscal year shall carry over to the next fiscal year.

C. Process for distribution.

  (1) The Local Management Board shall establish an open period in which community-based organizations may submit proposals for the funding of community-based initiatives. The Local Management Board shall advertise the open period on the Wicomico County website and social media accounts.

  (2) Following the closing period for proposals, the Local Management Board shall meet and review the applications over the following four weeks. Following the review period, the Local Management Board shall award the money over the next four to six weeks.

  (3) All money awarded to a community-based organization shall be spent that fiscal year.

D. Reporting.

  (1) Within six weeks of completion of the awarded program, each community-based organization shall write a report outlining the use of the awarded money, the implementation process and how the program has performed to date.

  (2) On or before December 1 every two years beginning in 2024, the County Executive shall submit a report to the Governor and, in accordance with § 2-1257 of the State Government Article, the Senate Budget and Taxation Committee and the House Appropriations Committee on how money received from the fund were spent during the immediately preceding two fiscal years.
