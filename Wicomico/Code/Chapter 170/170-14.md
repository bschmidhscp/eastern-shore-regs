# § 170-14. Violations and penalties.

A. Any person, partnership or corporation who is found to have violated this article shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and shall be fined a sum not exceeding $1,000 or be imprisoned for a period not to exceed six months, or both.

B. Each violation of this article shall be considered a separate offense, and any violation continuing more than one day shall be considered a separate offense.
