# § 149-27. Critical and essential facilities.

Critical and essential facilities shall:

A. Not be located in coastal high-hazard areas (V Zones), Coastal A Zones or floodways.

B. If located in flood hazard areas other than coastal high-hazard areas, Coastal A Zones and floodways, be elevated to the higher of elevation required by these regulations plus one foot, the elevation required by the building code, or the elevation of the two-tenths-percent chance (five-hundred-year) flood.
