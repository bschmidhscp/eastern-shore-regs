# § 149-24. Historic structures.

Repair, alteration, addition, rehabilitation, or other improvement of historic structures shall be subject to the requirements of these regulations if the proposed work is determined to be a substantial improvement, unless a determination is made that the proposed work will not preclude the structure's continued designation as an historic structure. The Floodplain Administrator may require documentation of a structure's continued eligibility and designation as an historic structure.
