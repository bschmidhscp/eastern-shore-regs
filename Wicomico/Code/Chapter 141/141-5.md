# § 141-5. Marriage licenses.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

A. Imposition of fees; purpose. There is hereby imposed an additional fee of $25 for marriage licenses to be used to offset the costs of providing domestic violence programs.
