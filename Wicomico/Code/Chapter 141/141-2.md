# § 141-2. Animals.

*[Added 11-3-1998 by Res. No. 502 ; amended 5-20-2003 by Res. No. 562 ; 4-5-2011 by Res. No. 46-2011 ; 4-16-2019 by Bill No. 2019-01 ]*

A. Rabies Clinic. The fee for vaccination at the Rabies Clinic offered by the Health Department pursuant to § 133-3A(2) shall be $10 for each animal vaccinated.

B. Rabies tags. The cost of the rabies tag provided by the Animal Control Authority pursuant to § 133-3A(3) or a replacement tag pursuant to § 133-4C shall be $3.

C. Dog licenses. The cost of a dog license for Wicomico County issued in the course of a rabies vaccination shall be $0. An administrative fee of $3 shall be payable for all dog licenses issued by the Animal Control Authority as provided by § 133-4A.

D. Breeder license. The cost of a breeder license shall be $50. An administrative fee of $50 shall be payable for all breeder licenses issued by the Animal Control Authority as provided by § 133-24B.
