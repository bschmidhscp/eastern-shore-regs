# § 105-9. Penalty.

*[Amended 10-24-2000 by Bill No. 2000-9]*

A. A person who violates a provision of this chapter for which a criminal penalty is not specified is guilty of a civil infraction and shall be liable for the fine specified for the violation.

B. If the section of this chapter violated does not set forth a specific monetary penalty, then the fine shall be $100 for each violation.

C. Each day the violation continues shall constitute a separate violation.

D. The office of the Sheriff or its designee is hereby authorized to issue citations for civil infractions of this chapter.
