# § 62-4. Members appointed; Chair.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ; 6-17-2025 by Bill No. 2025-07 ]*

A. The Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission shall consist of seven members, one of whom shall be designated as Chair.

B. The County Executive shall appoint each member and the Chair of the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission. The County Council shall confirm the appointments by a majority vote.

C. Terms.

  (1) Subject to Subsection C(3) below, the term for each member of the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission shall be five years.

  (2) Members' terms shall be staggered such that the terms of three members end in three years, and the terms of four members end in five years.

  (3) For the initial term following the establishment of the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission, at the time of the appointment, the County Executive shall delineate three members who shall serve a three-year term, following confirmation by a majority vote of the County Council.

  (4) Members shall serve for no more than two consecutive terms. The County Council may waive this limitation, by a majority vote, upon request of the County Executive.

  (5) If a vacancy occurs during the term of an appointed member, the vacancy shall be filled for the unexpired term in the same manner as is required for appointment under Subsection B of this section.

D. Eligibility.

  (1) Each member must be 21 years of age, or older.

  (2) Each member's principal residence must be in Wicomico County during the term of the appointment.

  (3) Within six months after appointment to the Planning Commission, a member shall complete the education course adopted by the state in accordance with § 1-206 of the Land Use Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland. The failure of a member to complete an education course under this subsection may not invalidate a decision of the Planning Commission or be construed to create a private cause of action by any person, but is grounds for removal of the member at the discretion of the County Executive.

E. Removal; resignation.

  (1) Any member of the Commission may be removed from the Commission by the County Executive, with the majority approval of the County Council, except as otherwise provided in Subsection E(2).

  (2) The County Executive shall remove a member of the Commission for:

    (a) Failing to attend at least 50% of the meetings of the Commission during any consecutive 12-month period;

    (b) Conviction of a crime in accordance with § 8-502 of the State Government Article; or

    (c) Failure to maintain a principal residence in the County during the term of the appointment.

  (3) Members may resign from the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission at any time by notifying the County Executive and the Chair of the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission in writing.
