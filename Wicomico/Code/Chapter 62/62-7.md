# § 62-7. Establishment of Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ; 6-17-2025 by Bill No. 2025-07 ]*

A. There is hereby created a Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development for Wicomico County.

B. The Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development shall be headed by a Director who shall be appointed by the County Executive and confirmed by the County Council.

C. The Department shall have such staff as may be authorized by the County Executive and County Council through the Budget.

D. The Department shall serve as planning and zoning advisors to the Planning and Zoning Commission.

E. The Department shall conduct such activities as may be requested by the Planning and Zoning Commission, the County Executive, or as authorized in any local law or regulation.
