# § 62-3. Authority.

*[Amended 6-17-2025 by Bill No. 2025-07 ]*

A. Authority. Said Commission is hereby authorized to proceed to organize and function as a Planning and Zoning Commission within the contemplation of the aforesaid law.

B. The Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission shall hold at least one regular meeting each month, unless there is no business pending before the Planning Commission. A special meeting may be called, as necessary, at the election of the Chair. The Wicomico County Council, by resolution, shall set the date and time of the regular monthly meeting of the Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission. The date and time of any special meeting shall be set at the discretion of the Chair.

C. In the performance of the Planning Commission's functions, Planning Commission members may enter on any land and make examinations and surveys.

D. The Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission shall adopt rules for the conduct of its business.
