# § 160-89. Applicability.

The provisions of this article shall govern the responsibilities of persons for the maintenance of structures, and the equipment and premises thereof. It is strongly recommended that a lease agreement require an occupant to comply with any provisions of this article. It shall be the responsibility of the occupants to check with the Housing Official to determine if a certificate of occupancy is required.
