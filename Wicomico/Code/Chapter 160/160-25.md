# § 160-25. Recorded statement to constitute lien.

Where the full amount due the County is not paid by such owner within 30 days after the County acted to remedy the violation, then the Finance Office shall cause to be recorded in the Finance Department for the County a sworn statement showing the cost and expense incurred for the work, the administrative fees, the date the work was done and the location of the property on which such work was done. The cost or expense shall be assessed as a lien on the property and shall be entered on the tax records kept by the Department of Finance and shall be collectible in the same manner as taxes on real estate.
