# § 160-87. Plumbing: installation and maintenance.

A. Every plumbing fixture, plumbing stack, waste and sewer line shall be so installed and maintained as to function properly and shall be kept free from obstructions, leaks and defects to prevent structural deterioration or health hazards. All repairs and installations shall be made in accordance with the applicable codes or ordinances.

B. It shall be unlawful for any person to deposit any foreign materials in any water closet/toilet, bathtub, shower, lavatory, kitchen sink or other plumbing fixture which may result in the obstruction of any sanitary sewer.

C. All plumbing facilities shall be maintained in a reasonably clean and sanitary condition to prevent insect and rodent breeding and prevent dangerous or offensive gases and odors.
