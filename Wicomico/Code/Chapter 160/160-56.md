# § 160-56. Decorative features and fences.

A. All cornices, entablatures, belt courses, corbels, terra-cotta trim, wall facings and similar decorative features shall be maintained in good repair with proper anchorage and in a safe condition.

B. All fences shall be constructed of manufactured metal fencing material, wood, masonry or other inert material. Such fences shall be maintained in good condition.
