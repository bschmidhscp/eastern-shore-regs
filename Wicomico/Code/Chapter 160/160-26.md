# § 160-26. Penalty.

A. Any person who shall violate any of the provisions of this chapter shall be subject to a fine not to exceed $1,000 per violation. A schedule of fines for violations of this Code may be established by resolution of the County Council.[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11]

B. Exception. If the owner of a premises fails to comply with a demolition order within the time prescribed, the Housing Official shall have the following methods of recourse:

  (1) The Housing Official shall cause the structure to be razed and removed, either through an available public agency or by contract or arrangement with private persons, and the cost of such razing and removal shall be collected in compliance with procedure set out in § 160-25; and/or

  (2) Any person who fails to comply with a demolition order will be issued a two-hundred-dollar citation each day that the violation continues.
