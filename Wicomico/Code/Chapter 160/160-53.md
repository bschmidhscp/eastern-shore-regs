# § 160-53. Requirements for stairs and stairwells.

A. Every handrail or railing shall be firmly fastened and maintained in good condition. Properly ballasted railings, capable of bearing loads as required by the Building Code, shall be placed on the open portions of the stairs, balconies, landings and stairwells.

B. All stairways having more than three risers above the floor or grade, and all stairs or changes in grade in hospitals, nursing homes, convalescent homes and similar occupancies, shall be equipped with handrails.
