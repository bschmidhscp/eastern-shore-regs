# § 160-68. Dwelling units within certain structures.

A dwelling unit or rooming unit shall not be located within a structure containing an establishment handling, dispensing or storing flammable liquids with a flash point of 110° F. or lower, except as provided for in the Housing Code and/or Fire Prevention Code.
