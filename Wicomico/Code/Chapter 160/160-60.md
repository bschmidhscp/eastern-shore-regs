# § 160-60. Responsibility of owner.

The owner of the structure shall provide and maintain such firesafety facilities and equipment in compliance with these requirements and the Fire Prevention Code. A person shall not occupy as owner-occupant or let to another for occupancy or use any premises which does not comply with the following requirements of this article.
