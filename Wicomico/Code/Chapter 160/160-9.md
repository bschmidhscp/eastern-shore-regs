# § 160-9. Periodic area search.

A. From time to time, the County Executive may designate certain specific areas of the County in need of inspection. Such inspection shall be performed on every structure subject to this Code in the designated area, with request for entry being made pursuant to § 160-8 of this Code.[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11]

B. If entry for inspection is refused, the Housing Official shall be permitted to seek a warrant based upon, but not limited to, the following factors:

  (1) The passage of time since the last inspection of the structure;

  (2) The type of structure to be inspected (i.e., single-family dwellings, multiple-family dwellings, apartments);

  (3) The age of the structure;

  (4) Condition of the structures in the surrounding areas;

  (5) Evidence of blighted conditions in the surrounding areas; and

  (6) Evidence of past violations in the structure.
