# § 160-61. Egress.

Every dwelling unit, boardinghouse, and/or rooming unit shall have one or more means of egress, with minimum head room of six feet, six inches leading to a safe and open space at ground level. It shall be maintained as a safe, continuous and unobstructed passageway to an open area at grade.
