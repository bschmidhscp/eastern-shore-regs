# § 160-84. Bathtub, shower and lavatory sink.

A. All dormitory housing, dwelling, and rooming units shall provide a room separate from habitable rooms which is equipped with a bathtub or shower and a lavatory supplied with hot and cold running water and connected to a sewer system.

B. Every lavatory basin and bathtub or shower shall be supplied with heated and unheated water on demand.

C. At least one bathtub, or shower and lavatory shall be supplied for each eight persons or fraction thereof residing within a boardinghouse, including members of the operator's family whenever they share the use of the facility provided.
