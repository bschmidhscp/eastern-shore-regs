# § 160-91. Disposal of garbage and rubbish.

All garbage and rubbish shall be placed in a refuse container to maintain clean and sanitary conditions. Each occupant of a dwelling or dwelling unit shall dispose of all his rubbish in a clean and sanitary manner by placing it in the garbage disposal facilities or approved garbage storage containers. In all cases, the occupants shall be responsible for the availability of garbage storage containers and the disposal of rubbish.
