# § 160-33. Variations and modifications.

A. The Board, after hearing an appeal, may vary the application of any provision of this Code to any particular case when, in its opinion, the enforcement thereof would cause undue hardship and would be contrary to the spirit and purpose of this Code or public interest or when, in its opinion, the interpretation of the Housing Official should be modified or reversed.

B. A decision of the Board to vary the application of any provision of this Code or to modify an order of the Housing Official shall specify in what manner such variation or modification is made, the conditions upon which it is made and the reasons therefor.
