# § 160-86. Water-heating facilities.

Every dwelling unit shall have supplied water heating facilities which are properly installed, operated and maintained in a safe and good working condition and are properly connected to the bathtub or shower, sink and lavatory basin. Such water heating facilities shall be capable of heating water to a temperature of 110° F. and capable of meeting normal demands at every required outlet, even though the dwelling's heating facilities are not in operation. All gas-fired water heaters shall. be vented to the outside. Gas and liquid fuel water heaters are not permitted to be located in bedrooms.
