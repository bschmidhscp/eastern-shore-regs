# § 104-15. Mineral extraction.

A. A county agricultural district application or county easement application may not be approved if surface or subsurface mineral extraction (sand, gravel, shale, limestone, crude petroleum, natural gas, clay, fertilizer minerals, deep mined minerals, including bituminous coal) may occur in the future or currently takes place, or where extraction has occurred but the land has not been reclaimed in accordance with state and county law.

B. Before the county will consider such property for inclusion in its agricultural preservation program, the reclamation of the property shall be implemented by the landowner in accordance with state and county law. In addition, a soil and water conservation plan must be approved and implemented for the property.
