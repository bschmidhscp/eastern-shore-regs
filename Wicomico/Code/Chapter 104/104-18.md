# § 104-18. Application of other laws.

The establishment of agricultural preservation districts and the sale of agricultural preservation easements shall be subject to the terms of the Wicomico County Zoning Ordinance,[1] the Wicomico County Subdivision Regulations[2] and all other laws and regulations of Wicomico County, Maryland.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: See Ch. 225 , Zoning. [2] Editor's Note: See Ch. 200 , Subdivision of Land.
