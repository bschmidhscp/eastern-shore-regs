# § 64-3. Advisory power.

The Wicomico County Areawide Planning Organization is a review and advisory organization and does not replace nor assume any of the authority, duties nor responsibilities of the Salisbury-Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission nor of the Planning Commissions of any municipalities in Wicomico County.
