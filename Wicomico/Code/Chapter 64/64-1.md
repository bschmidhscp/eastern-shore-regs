# § 64-1. Establishment; membership.

The Wicomico County Areawide Planning Organization is hereby established and recognizes its membership to include the county and all such municipalities in the county which, by resolution of such municipality, shall become members; and that membership shall be composed of representatives from the county and from each municipality according to bylaws to be adopted by the Organization, except that each representative shall be an elected official of the county or municipality or such other person or persons as the legislative body may so designate.
