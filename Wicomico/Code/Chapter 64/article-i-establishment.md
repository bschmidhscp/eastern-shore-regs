# Article I. Establishment

*[Adopted 10-17-1972 by Res. No. 147 [1] ]*

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: The preamble to this resolution read as follows: "WHEREAS, Counties and Municipalities are empowered by Articles 66B and 25A of the Annotated Code of Maryland, enacted in 1927 and in 1933 as amended, to engage in and carry out comprehensive planning and" "WHEREAS, an Areawide Planning Organization is a prerequisite for eligibility to participate in certain grant and loan programs with the federal governments; and" "WHEREAS, the Salisbury-Wicomico County Planning and Zoning Commission is a duly established body and agency of the Mayor and Council of the City of Salisbury and of the Wicomico County Council; and" "WHEREAS, the formation of an Areawide Planning Organization with membership open to all municipalities in Wicomico County will further promote cooperative arrangements and coordination among its members and will study such areawide problems and plans as are common among its members;" "NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that..."!
