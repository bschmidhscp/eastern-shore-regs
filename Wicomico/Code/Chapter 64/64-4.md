# § 64-4. County representatives.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

The representatives of Wicomico County to the Wicomico County Areawide Planning Organization shall be:

A. The member of the Commission who is a County Councilman.

B. The lay member of the Commission who is appointed by the County Executive and confirmed by the County Council.

C. The Chairman of the Commission who is appointed by the Wicomico County Executive and confirmed by the County Council and by the Mayor and Council of the City of Salisbury.
