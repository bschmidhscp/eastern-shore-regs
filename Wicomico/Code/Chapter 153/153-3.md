# § 153-3. Possession of red fox pelt.

*[1959 Code, sec. 97. 1939, ch. 447]*

It shall be unlawful for any person at any time to have in his possession in Wicomico County any red fox pelt or pelts. Any person violating the provisions of this section shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall be fined not more than $50 for each such violation.
