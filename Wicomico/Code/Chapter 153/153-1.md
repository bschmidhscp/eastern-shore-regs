# § 153-1. Shooting foxes or dogs.

*[P.L.L. 1888, Art. 23, sec. 51; 1930, sec. 91; 1959 Code, sec. 94. 1868, ch. 75; 1908, ch. 349, sec. 51 (p.1083); 1924, ch. 478; 1959, ch. 75, sec. 91; 1961, ch. 581]*

Any person who shall shoot a fox that is being pursued by a dog or dogs in charge of a person or persons hunting same for sport in the limits of Wicomico County, and any person who shall shoot said dog or dogs, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, on conviction thereof before a Justice of the Peace, shall be fined not more than $50 and stand committed until the fine and costs are paid; provided, however, that this section shall not apply to any landowner shooting a fox upon his own property.
