# § 153-2. Female foxes.

*[P.L.L. 1888, Art. 23, sec. 52; 1930, sec. 92; 1959 Code, sec. 95. 1868, ch. 75; 1908, ch. 349, sec. 52 (p. 1083); 1959, ch. 75, sec. 92]*

Any person who shall dig out or in any manner take from any den a female fox or her young whelps or kill or in any manner cause the death of such female fox or her young whelps during the period of time in which she is suckling them shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, on conviction before a Justice of the Peace, shall be fined not less than $25 nor more than $50 for every female fox or whelp so dug or taken out of any den or killed or caused to be killed and shall stand committed until such fine and costs are paid.
