# § 99-7. Retroactive applicability.

*[Added 7-15-2025 by Bill No. 2025-09 ]*

The provisions of this chapter, as amended by Legislative Bill No. 2025-09, shall apply retroactively to the effective date of Chapter 99, titled "Vehicles, County," as established by Legislative Bill No. 2024-12.
