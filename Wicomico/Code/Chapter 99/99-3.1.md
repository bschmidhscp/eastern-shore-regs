# § 99-3.1. Exemption for the Circuit Court.

*[Added 7-15-2025 by Bill No. 2025-09 ]*

The Circuit Court for Wicomico County is exempt from the requirement that mileage reimbursement be limited to situations where County vehicles are unavailable and, notwithstanding anything to the contrary in § 99-3E, employees of the Circuit Court for Wicomico County shall be eligible to receive mileage reimbursement for the use of personal vehicles for official business.
