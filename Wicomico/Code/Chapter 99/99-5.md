# § 99-5. Vehicle pools; use of private vehicles for County business.

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary herein, the County Executive shall establish intra-department vehicle pools in the executive branch, in which all vehicles not assigned to a specific department, agency or individuals shall be maintained.
