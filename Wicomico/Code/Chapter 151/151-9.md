# § 151-9. Permit fees.

A. The permit fee required to be paid at the time of the issuance of any permit under this chapter shall cover a period of one year from June 1 to the succeeding May 31 and shall be the sum of $1,000.

B. No permit fees will be prorated, either upon application or upon cancellation, whether voluntary or otherwise.

C. No license issued hereunder shall be transferable, either to another person for the same premises or to other premises by the applicant to whom issued.
