# § 138-2. Adoption of standards by reference.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ; 9-16-2025 by Bill No. 2025-12 ]*

A. Adoption of the code. The County Council of Wicomico County hereby adopts the provisions set forth in the National Electric Code (hereinafter referred to as "NEC"), 2023 edition, including the appendices, as the electrical code for Wicomico County, Maryland, and incorporated by reference. The provisions shall be controlling for all buildings within the corporate limits of Wicomico County, Maryland, unless otherwise exempted herein.

B. Promulgation of new edition and adoption by Wicomico County.

  (1) Within 180 days of promulgation of a new edition of the NEC by the National Fire Protection Association, the Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development's Chief Building Inspector shall review the new edition with the Wicomico County Board of Electrical Examiners to determine whether any modifications are recommended.

  (2) The Chief Building Inspector and the President of the Wicomico County Board of Electrical Examiners shall notify the County Executive and County Council, in writing, of suggested modifications to the new edition, if any, and the justification for each modification. 180 days following the notification by the Chief Building Inspector and the President of the Board, any provision of the new edition, to include appendices, not modified by law, shall be adopted as the electric code for Wicomico County, Maryland, and incorporated by reference, but for the provisions in conflict with the modifications codified in § 138-2.1. Pursuant to § 302 of the County Charter, the new edition may be modified at any time.

  (3) Until a new edition of the NEC is promulgated by the National Fire Protection Association and adopted pursuant to this subsection, the former edition of the NEC is the electric code for Wicomico County.

C. A list of the most current edition of the NEC, adopted pursuant to Subsection B, is available on the County website and is available through visiting the Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development.
