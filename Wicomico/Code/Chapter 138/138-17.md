# § 138-17. Reciprocity.

*[Amended 7-18-2023 by Bill No. 2023-08 ]*

The Board shall, upon application therefor and the payment of the regular fee and without examination, issue a certificate of registration as electrician limited to any person who holds a certificate of qualification or registration issued to him by the proper authorities of any County, state or territory or district of the United States, or of any country, provided that the requirements where the aforesaid registration was issued do not conflict with the provisions of this chapter and are of a standard not lower than that specified in this chapter for the classification to be issued by this Board, and further provided that the same rights are given to electrical contractors of this County.
