# § 138-2.1. Modifications to the National Electrical Code.

*[Added 9-16-2025 by Bill No. 2025-12 ]*

The following modifications are made to the National Electric Code:

A. Section 210.8(f): Outdoor Outlets. This section is modified as follows:

  Exception Number 2: GFCI protection shall not be required for listed HVAC equipment.

  Exception Number 3: GFCI protection shall not be required for listed fresh water and waste water equipment.
