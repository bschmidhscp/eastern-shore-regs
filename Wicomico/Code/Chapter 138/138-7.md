# § 138-7. Meetings; officers; quorum; majority required.

A. The Board shall hold regular meetings at least once each month and special meetings at such times as the Board or the President shall determine.

B. The Board shall elect annually a President, Vice President and Secretary-Treasurer.

C. Four members of the Board shall constitute a quorum for purposes of doing business.

D. In all proceedings of the Board, a majority of all of the members thereof shall be necessary to bind the Board.

E. No member shall be elected as President of the Board for more than two consecutive years.[Added 10-14-1980 by Bill No. 1980-31]
