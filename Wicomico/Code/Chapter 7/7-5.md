# § 7-5. Burden of proof.

A. Original jurisdiction applications. The burden of proof is one of a preponderance of the evidence and is on the applicant to show, by competent, material and substantial evidence that he or she is entitled to the relief requested and that the request meets all prescribed standard and requirements.

B. Appeal hearings. The burden of proof shall be on the appellant to show that the action taken by the administrative agency or official was clearly erroneous, and/or arbitrary and capricious, and/or contrary to law.
