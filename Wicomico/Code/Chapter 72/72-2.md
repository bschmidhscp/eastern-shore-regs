# § 72-2. Fees for entries.

*[P.L.L., 1888, Art. 23, sec. 93; 1930, sec. 197; 1959 Code, sec. 222. 1880, ch. 213]*

He shall receive for making said entries only such fees as are now allowed by law for docket entries to Registers of Wills.
