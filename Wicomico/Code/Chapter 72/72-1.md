# § 72-1. Index books.

*[P.L.L., 1888, Art. 23, sec. 92; 1930, sec. 196; 1959 Code, sec. 221. 1880, ch. 213]*

The Register of Wills of Wicomico County is authorized to procure two large index books, one to be known as "Index to Guardianships" and the other as "Index to Administrations"; in the former of which books he shall hereafter regularly enter and record, in alphabetical order, the names of all guardians, wards, dates of guardianships, penalty of each guardian bond, names of the sureties on the same, date of each guardian account and the date of the filing of the receipt and release of guardian from each ward, in each and every case; and in the other book he shall enter and record the name of each administrator or executor, the name of each surety on each bond, penalty of said bond, name of decedent, date of administration, date of the passage of each administration account and date of each distribution.
