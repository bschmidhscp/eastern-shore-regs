# § 213-2. License required; fee.

*[Amended 10-17-1972 by Ord. No. 39A ]*

No person shall engage in business as a transient merchant in Wicomico County unless he has secured and holds a current license as such issued upon application by the Sheriff of Wicomico County. There shall be a fee of $250 charged for the issuance of each and every license.
