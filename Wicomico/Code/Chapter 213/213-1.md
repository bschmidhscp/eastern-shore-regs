# § 213-1. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**TRANSIENT MERCHANT** Any person who displays samples, models, goods, wares or merchandise in any hotel or motel, inn, rooming house, store, club, storehouse, house or other place for the purpose of securing retail sale of such goods, wares or merchandise and who does not own said premises or is not the holder of a formal lease thereon; provided, however, that this definition shall not include farmers or the holders of booths in any fair, exposition or trade show.
