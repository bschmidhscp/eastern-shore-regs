# § 213-6. Violations and penalties.

*[Amended 3-6-1973 by Ord. No. 39B ]*

Any person violating the provisions of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall be fined not more than $500 for each offense, and every day that a violation of this chapter shall continue shall constitute a separate and distinct offense.
