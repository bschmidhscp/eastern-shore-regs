# § 70-3. Purpose and duties.

A. The Wicomico County Recreation and Parks Commission shall act in an advisory capacity to the Director of Recreation, Parks and Tourism and shall review and make recommendations to the Department and Director with respect to:

  (1) All matters concerning comprehensive planning, policy interpretation, budgeting, operations, maintenance, capital improvements, as well as the formulation of plans and policies for public recreation, the establishment of recreation programs and the creation of recreation policies.

  (2) The creation of a comprehensive plan identifying acquisition, development and rehabilitation projects to support the public's demand for access to athletic facilities, play areas, tidal tributaries, freshwater ponds, and natural areas in the form of neighborhood, community and county-wide parks. It shall ensure that these sites are well maintained and distributed throughout the county to best serve all residents.

  (3) The utilization of all available facilities to provide programs to all age groups, races, sexes and mental abilities.

  (4) The Board may review the Department of Recreation, Parks and Tourism's recreation and parks budget in an advisory manner with the Director of the Department with special regard to the institution of new activities and programs where deemed advisable or the discontinuance of old features if such is desirable.

B. The Commission shall support the missions and initiatives of the Tourism Board and the Wicomico County Youth and Civic Center.
