# § 70-5. Meetings.

A. The Commission shall hold regular monthly meetings. Members of the Commission shall be required to attend a minimum of 60% of these meetings per year. Special meetings may be held at any time or place at the call of the Chairman or upon the written request of two members of the Commission.

B. Five members of the Commission shall constitute a quorum at every meeting.

C. At all regular meetings of the Commission, the following shall be the order of business:

  (1) Roll call;

  (2) Reading and acting upon the minutes of the previous meeting;

  (3) Report of the Director of Recreation, Parks and Tourism;

  (4) Reports from other staff members;

  (5) Report of any committees meeting during the month;

  (6) Unfinished business;

  (7) New business.
