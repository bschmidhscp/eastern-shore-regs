# § 70-4. Officers.

A. The officers of the Commission shall be a Chairman, Vice Chairman and a Secretary. The officers, with the exception of the Secretary, shall be elected from among the commission's membership. The election shall be at the June meeting of the Commission, and the officers shall serve for terms of one year, beginning July 1, or until their successors are elected and qualified.

B. The Chairman shall preside at all meetings of the Commission. He/she shall represent the Commission at all activities where his/her presence is desirable or necessary. He/she shall represent the commission on the Department of Recreation, Parks and Tourism's Board of Directors. In consultation with the Director of the Department of Recreation, Parks and Tourism, the Chairman may appoint from time to time, committees, standing or special, as may be authorized by the Commission.

C. The Vice Chairman shall perform the duties of the Chairman in the Chairman's absence or incapacity, and in case of the resignation or death of the Chairman, the Vice Chairman shall perform such duties as are imposed upon the Chairman until such time as the board shall elect a new Chairman. The Vice Chairman shall represent the Commission on the Department of Recreation, Parks and Tourism's Board of Directors.

D. The secretary shall be a staff member assigned by the Director of Recreation, Parks and Tourism to record the minutes of each meeting and maintain such other records as may be from time to time, designated by the Board.

E. Should the office of Chairman or Vice Chairman become vacant. The Board shall select a successor from its membership at the next regular meeting, and such selection shall be for the unexpired term of said office.
