# § 70-2. Establishment and membership.

A. The Wicomico County Recreation and Parks Commission is hereby created and established to serve in an advisory capacity to the Director of the Department of Recreation, Parks and Tourism.

B. The Wicomico County Recreation and Parks Commission shall consist of not more than 15 members, to be appointed by the County Executive and confirmed by the county council of Wicomico County, who have demonstrated an interest in recreation in the county.

C. Each member appointed or reappointed shall serve a three-year term or until a successor is appointed.

D. Vacancies occurring prior to expiration of a term shall be filled in the same manner as an original appointment.
