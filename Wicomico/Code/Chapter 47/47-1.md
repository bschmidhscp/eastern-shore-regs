# § 47-1. Authorization; levy.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

In order to provide for the maintenance and development of a library system in Wicomico County adequate to serve the needs of the citizens thereof, the County is hereby authorized and empowered to acquire, create and maintain a public library or libraries, with branch libraries and library stations.
