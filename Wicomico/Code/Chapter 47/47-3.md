# § 47-3. Control of library system.

*[Amended 10-15-1979 by Bill No. 1979-14 ; 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

The public library system for Wicomico County shall be under the control of a Board of Library Trustees which shall be appointed by the County Executive and the County Council from nominees submitted by the board of Library Trustees.
