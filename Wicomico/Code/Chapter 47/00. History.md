# Chapter 47. Library System

*[HISTORY: Adopted by the County Council of Wicomico County 6-14-1975 by Bill No. 47 . [1] Amendments noted where applicable.]*

STATUTORY REFERENCES

Libraries — See Education Article, Title 23, Annotated Code of Maryland.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: The preamble to this bill read as follows: "WHEREAS, the County Council for Wicomico County finds it advisable and necessary for the good and welfare of the citizens of Wicomico County to maintain a public library system in and for said county; and "WHEREAS, the present Board of Library Trustees of the Wicomico County Free Library, Inc., have advised the County Council for Wicomico County and it feels that the acquisition of the property now owned and operated by the said Wicomico County Free Library, Inc. by the County Council for Wicomico County and the maintenance of such library system by said County Council would greatly enhance the prospects for the growth and development of a library system in Wicomico County; "SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF WICOMICO COUNTY MARYLAND, IN LEGISLATIVE SESSION, that..."
