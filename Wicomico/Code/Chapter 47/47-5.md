# § 47-5. Membership.

*[Amended 10-15-1979 by Bill No. 1979-14 ; 11-12-2008 by Bill No. 2008-7 ]*

Each member of the Board of Library Trustees shall serve for a term of five years and until a successor is appointed and qualified. The terms of the members of the Board shall be staggered as required by the terms of the members serving on the Board as of July 1, 1979. The members shall be chosen on the basis of character, ability and demonstrated interest in library matters and shall be residents of Wicomico County.
