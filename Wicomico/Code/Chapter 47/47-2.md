# § 47-2. Acquisition of land.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

For the purpose of maintaining such library system, the County may acquire by gift, purchase or condemnation real or leasehold property and may erect thereon, out of specially appropriated funds, buildings for the use of the library and may sell at public or private sale any such property when the same is no longer needed for library purposes.
