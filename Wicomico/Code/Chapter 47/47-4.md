# § 47-4. Powers and responsibilities.

*[Amended 10-15-1979 by Bill No. 1979-14 ]*

The Board of Library Trustees shall have the powers and responsibilities as set out in the Title 23, Subtitle 4, County Public Libraries, of the Education Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, 1978 Edition.
