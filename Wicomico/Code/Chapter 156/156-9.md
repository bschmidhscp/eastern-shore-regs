# § 156-9. Qualification; denial of permit.

A. To qualify for a permit, an applicant shall:

  (1) Submit an application to the Department in accordance with this chapter.

  (2) Possess a valid motor vehicle driver's license and shall be at least 18 years old.

B. The Department may refuse to issue or renew a permit if the applicant:

  (1) Does not meet a qualification for the permit;

  (2) Makes a material false statement on the application; or

  (3) Has had a permit revoked for inappropriate use within the previous year.
