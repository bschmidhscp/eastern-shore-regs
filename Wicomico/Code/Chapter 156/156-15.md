# § 156-15. Authority and amendments.

The County Council reserves the authority to amend the hours of operation, solid waste facility list, or tipping fee by resolution. Any temporary or emergency changes to hours due to weather, safety, or staffing may be made by the Director of Public Works or such other authorized designee, after prompt notice to the Council. The County Council reserves the right to confirm or reject the temporary or emergency change.
