# § 156-14. Landfill, convenience center, and recycling center hours of operation.

A. Wicomico County shall operate solid waste facilities in accordance with the Maryland Department of the Environment refuse disposal permit, to include, but not necessarily limited to, landfill(s), convenience centers, and recycling centers. The locations and hours of operation for the solid waste facilities shall be as set forth herein below. The days and hours of operation established in this section reflect the solid waste facility schedule in effect as of the introduction of Legislative Bill No. 2025-11 on July 1, 2025.

B. Newland Park Landfill:

  (1) Convenience center hours: daily, 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

  (2) Scale hours (weigh station): Monday through Saturday, 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

  (3) Office hours: Monday through Friday, 7:15 a.m. to 3:15 p.m.

C. Convenience centers (all other locations):

  (1) Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday: 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

  (2) Sunday: 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

  (3) Closed: Tuesdays and Thursdays.

D. Recycling centers: Recycling centers shall operate during the same day and hours as their co-located convenience centers.

E. Locations of convenience and recycling centers:

  (1) Airport - 31966 Fooks Road.

  (2) Bivalve - 20906 Nanticoke Road.

  (3) Delmar - 27774 Waller Road.

  (4) Fruitland - 27778 Walnut Tree Road.

  (5) Hebron - 8301 Old Railroad Road.

  (6) Mardela Springs - 9290 Athol Road.

  (7) Parsonsburg - 698 Eastside Road.

  (8) Quantico - 25202 Nanticoke Road.

  (9) Sharptown - 12287 Twiford Road.

  (10) Wango - 34333 Mount Hermon Road.

F. 24-hour recycling centers. The following recycling centers are available to the public 24 hours a day, seven days a week:

  (1) Delmar - 29750 Foskey Lane.

  (2) Fruitland - 27778 Walnut Tree Road.

  (3) Jersey Road - 1130 Jersey Road.

  (4) Pittsville - 7478 Perdue Street.

  (5) Willards - 7300 Truitt Street.

G. Holiday closures:

  (1) All County solid waste facilities shall be closed on the following holidays unless otherwise posted: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

  (2) Convenience and recycling centers may be closed on any County-declared holiday approved by the Wicomico County Council; however, when operational needs arise, facilities may remain open during a holiday as determined and publicly posted by the Department of Public Works.
