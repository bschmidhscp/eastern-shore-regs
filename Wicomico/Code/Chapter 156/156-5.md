# § 156-5. Penalties.

*[Amended 10-24-2000 by Bill No. 2000-9 ; 8-5-2025 by Bill No. 2025-11 ]*

A. Penalty.

  (1) Any person or commercial hauler who, in the opinion of the Director of the Department of Public Works or the Director's designee, disposes of out-of-County solid waste at a Wicomico County solid waste facility shall be subject to the following penalties. The Director of the Department of Public Works or the Director's designee is hereby authorized to issue citations for civil infractions, as follows:

    (a) For a first offense, the person or commercial hauler shall be guilty of a civil infraction, punishable by a fine of not more than $1,000, and be placed on a two-year probationary period.

    (b) For a second offense within the two-year probationary period, committed before October 1, 2025, any person or commercial hauler shall be guilty of a civil infraction, punishable by a fine of not more than $1,000, or $2,500 for an offense that occurs on or after October 1, 2025.

    (c) For a third offense within the two-year probationary period, committed before October 1, 2025, any person or commercial hauler shall be guilty of a civil infraction, punishable by a fine of not more than $1,000, or $5,000 for an offense that occurs on or after October 1, 2025, and an administrative penalty suspension from utilizing any solid waste facility operated by Wicomico County for a period of three consecutive operating days.

    (d) For a fourth offense within the two-year probationary period, committed before October 1, 2025, any person or commercial hauler shall be guilty of a civil infraction, punishable by a fine of not more than $1,000, or $5,000 for an offense that occurs on or after October 1, 2025, and an administrative penalty prohibiting the utilizing of any solid waste facility operated by Wicomico County.

  (2) Notice.

    (a) The Director of the Department of Public Works or the Director's designee shall provide the person or commercial hauler written notice of any penalty imposed pursuant to Subsection A(1) of this section. The notice shall include:

      [1] The date, time, and location of the violation;

      [2] A description of the facts supporting the penalty;

      [3] The specific penalty imposed;

      [4] The effective date of the penalty; and

      [5] Information regarding the right to appeal.

    (b) The person or commercial hauler may appeal the penalty to the Wicomico County Board of Appeals by filing a written notice of appeal with the Director of the Department of Public Works or the Director's designee within 10 days of the date of the notice.

    (c) The imposition of any administrative penalty shall be stayed until the right to appeal is waived in writing, the time for appeal has expired or, if an appeal is filed, until the Board of Appeals issues a final decision, whichever is later.

    (d) Reinstatement. Notwithstanding the penalties set forth herein, an individual or commercial hauler who has been prohibited from utilizing any solid waste facility operated by Wicomico County may petition the Director of Public Works or the Director's designee for reinstatement. Reinstatement may be granted upon a showing of a change in circumstances, including but not limited to a change in ownership of the business.
