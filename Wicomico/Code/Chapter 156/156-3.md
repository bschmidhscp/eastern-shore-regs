# § 156-3. Unlawful disposal.

*[Amended 8-5-2025 by Bill No. 2025-11 ]*

A. It shall be unlawful for any person to throw, dump or deposit or to cause or permit to be thrown, dumped or deposited any litter, garbage, rubbish or refuse on any public or private property other than property designated by the County or state for the disposal of such litter, garbage, rubbish or refuse.

B. It shall be unlawful for a commercial hauler to dispose of out-of-county solid waste at a Wicomico County solid waste facility.

C. It shall be unlawful for a commercial hauler to dispose of yard waste, brush, trees, tree stumps or shrubs at any location other than the Newland Park Landfill.
