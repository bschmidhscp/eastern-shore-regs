# § 156-11. Display of permit.

A. Permits must be attached to the windshield of the vehicle either in the lower corner, driver's side, or behind the rear view mirror (not in the shaded area).

B. Failure to display an appropriate permit may result in denial of the right to dispose of refuse at the facility.
