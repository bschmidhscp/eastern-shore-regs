# § 205-9. Display of driver's permit; term.

A. Each person who holds a driver's permit shall conspicuously display the permit in the taxicab while operating a taxicab in the county.

B. Each driver's permit issued under this chapter shall expire on the date one year from the date of issuance. The initial issuance of permits shall be on a staggered basis as determined by the Department.
