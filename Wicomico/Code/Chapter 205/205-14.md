# § 205-14. Notice and opportunity for hearing.

A. Upon determination to revoke or suspend a owner's or driver's permit, the Department shall:

  (1) Notify the holder of the permit of the Department's decision in writing by certified mail; and

  (2) State the reasons for the Department's action.

B. The holder of an owner's or driver's permit revoked or suspended by the Department may, within 10 days of the decision, appeal the Department's decision to the Board of Appeals. The hearing before the Board of Appeals shall be conducted within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the notice of appeal.[Amended 7-6-2004 by Bill No. 2004-6]
