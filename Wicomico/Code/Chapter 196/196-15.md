# § 196-15. Preparation of stormwater management plans.

A. The design of stormwater management plans shall be prepared by any individual whose qualifications are acceptable to Wicomico County. Wicomico County may require that the design be prepared by either a professional engineer, professional land surveyor, or landscape architect licensed in the state, as necessary to protect the public or the environment.

B. If a stormwater BMP requires either a dam safety permit from MDE or small pond approval from the SCD, Wicomico County shall require that the design be prepared by a professional engineer licensed in the state.
