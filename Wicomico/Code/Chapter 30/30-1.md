# § 30-1. Definitions.

In general. In this chapter the following words have the meanings indicated.

**CERTIFIED GENERAL REAL ESTATE APPRAISER** An individual who is certified as a real estate appraiser under Title 16 of the Business Occupations and Professions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

**CLEAN ENERGY FINANCING AGREEMENT** An agreement between a property owner and a clean energy lender providing for the terms and conditions of a clean energy loan.

**CLEAN ENERGY LENDER** A private lender providing a clean energy loan under the Program.

**CLEAN ENERGY LOAN** Any loan made by a private lender to a property owner under the Program.

**CLEAN ENERGY LOAN OBLIGATION** All indebtedness and obligations of a property owner to a clean energy lender under a clean energy financing agreement.

**COMMERCIAL PROPERTY** Has the meaning stated in § 1-1101 of the Local Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

**PROGRAM** The Clean Energy Loan Program.

**PROGRAM ADMINISTRATOR** Any person or entity chosen by the County to manage the Program.

**PROPERTY OWNER** An owner of a commercial property.
