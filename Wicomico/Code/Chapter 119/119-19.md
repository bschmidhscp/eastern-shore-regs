# § 119-19. Service availability and record request.

A grantee shall provide cable service throughout its entire franchise area pursuant to the provisions of this chapter and its franchise agreement, and shall keep a record for at least three years of all requests for service received by the grantee. This record shall be available for inspection by the county at the local office of the grantee during regular office hours.
