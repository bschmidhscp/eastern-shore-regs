# § 36-2. Responsibilities.

It shall be the responsibility of this Commission to advise the County Council and County Executive on all matters with regard to environment, to accept and investigate any and all complaints from the citizens of the county regarding the environment and to forward the same to the appropriate governmental agency for the most expeditious handling.
