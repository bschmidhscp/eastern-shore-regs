# § 126-23. Variances.

A. The Board of Appeals is authorized to grant variances from the provisions of this chapter where the Board finds that such provisions would result in an unwarranted hardship for the applicant.[Amended 7-6-2004 by Bill No. 2004-6]

B. Any applicant for a variance shall have the burden of proving to the Board that:

  (1) There are special conditions peculiar to the property that would cause the unwarranted hardship.

  (2) The enforcement of these rules will deprive the applicant of rights commonly enjoyed by others in similar areas.

  (3) The granting of the variance will not confer on the applicant a special privilege that would be denied to other applicants.

  (4) The variance request is not based on conditions or circumstances which are the result of actions by the applicant.

  (5) The variance request does not arise from a condition relating to land or building use, either permitted or nonconforming, on a neighboring property.

  (6) The granting of a variance will not adversely affect water quality.

C. The Board of Appeals shall make written findings of fact that the applicant has met the requirements of this article before the Department may grant final approval of a variance.[Amended 7-6-2004 by Bill No. 2004-6]
