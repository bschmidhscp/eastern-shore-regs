# § 126-2. Applicability.

A. Unless exempted by Article III of this chapter, any person making application for a building permit, subdivision plat, stormwater management plan, project plan and grading or sediment control permits on parcels of land 40,000 square feet or greater after the effective date of this chapter shall file a forest conservation plan with the Department.

B. This chapter shall apply to all lands under the authority of Wicomico County and all lands within the corporate limits of any municipality that has entered into an agreement with Wicomico County, Maryland, as permitted by Title 5 of the Natural Resources Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11]
