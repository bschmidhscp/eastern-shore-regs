# § 126-31. Wicomico County Forest Conservation Manual.

The current edition of the State of Maryland Forest Conservation Manual is hereby adopted by reference to be known as the "Wicomico County Forest Conservation Manual." All provisions of the manual that are not specifically noted as standards or minimum requirements shall be considered as recommendations.
