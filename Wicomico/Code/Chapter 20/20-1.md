# § 20-1. Creation; function.

A. There is hereby created a Department of Correction under the immediate direction of a Director, who shall be appointed by the County Executive and confirmed by the County Council.[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11]

B. The Department shall operate all programs pertaining to detention and rehabilitation of persons under the jurisdiction of the county government awaiting trial or having been convicted of a crime and sentenced by a court of competent jurisdiction to a term in the Wicomico County Detention Center, or being held for any other lawful purpose.
