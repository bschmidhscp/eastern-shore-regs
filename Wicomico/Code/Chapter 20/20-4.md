# § 20-4. Custody of inmates at other correctional facilities.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

Should the facilities of the County Detention Center be inadequate to provide for all inmates lawfully committed therein, the Director may, with the approval of the County Executive or his designated agent, make arrangements for the temporary custody of the inmates at other correctional facilities within the state.
