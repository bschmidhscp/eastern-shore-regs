# § 221-3. Procedure.

A. Issuance and enforcement of a citation for a civil infraction of the Wicomico County Code shall be governed by the procedures established by this chapter.

B. Issuance and enforcement of a citation for parking violation shall be governed by the procedures established by that chapter.
