# § 221-12. Equitable relief.

A. The county may seek equitable, including injunctive, relief against any person or entity violating any provision of the Wicomico County Code relating to health, housing, fire, building, electric, licenses and permits, plumbing, animal control and zoning.

B. A petition for equitable, including injunctive, relief under this section may be filed in the District Court.
