# § 9-2. Bids.

*[P.L.L., 1930, Art. 23, sec. 11; 1959 Code, sec. 4. ch. 219, sec. 2] [Amended 2-15-1978 by Bill No. 1978-6]*

The County Council of Wicomico County is hereby authorized and empowered to advertise, in such newspapers as it deems best, for bids to erect such bridges as may be necessary to carry out the requirements of §§ 9-1 and 9-2. Said bridges may be either pivot or lift. Bids shall be accompanied by plans and specifications. Said Council shall have the right to reject any or all bids received by it. In case all bids are rejected, said County Council may contract, without further advertising, for erection of said bridges.
