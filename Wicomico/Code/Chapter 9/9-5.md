# § 9-5. Bridges authorized.

*[Added by Wic. Co., 1966, ch. 2]*

The County Council of Wicomico County does hereby authorize and empower any municipality located within said Wicomico County to construct and maintain bridges within the corporate limits of any municipality over any rivers, streams or other waterways situate therein, suitable for the use of the general public, said municipality to pay for all costs of such construction and maintenance, subject, nevertheless, to all applicable federal and state laws and regulations pertaining thereto.
