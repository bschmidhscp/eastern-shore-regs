# § 146-2. Open fire regulations.

*[Added 10-5-2010 by Bill No. 2010-10]*

A. Open fires are controlled by the provisions of the Code of Maryland Regulations (COMAR) and the enforcement of these regulations by the Wicomico County Health Department (the Department).

B. Upon receipt of an application for an open fire permit, the Department, in accordance with the provisions of COMAR, may issue or approve a permit in writing allowing an open fire.
