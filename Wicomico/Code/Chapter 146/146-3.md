# § 146-3. Civil infraction.

*[Added 10-5-2010 by Bill No. 2010-10]*

A. Any person, firm, corporation or agent who shall conduct an open fire incident controlled by the provisions of COMAR without the permit required, or in violation of such permit, shall be guilty of a civil infraction.

B. The Director of the Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development or his designee is hereby authorized to issue citations for civil infractions of this chapter.
