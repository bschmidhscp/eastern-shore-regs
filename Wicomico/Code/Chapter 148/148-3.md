# § 148-3. Water supply and sewage systems.

*[Amended 12-18-2012 by Bill No. 2012-13 ]*

The Department of Planning, Zoning and Community Development shall require new or replacement water supply systems and/or sanitary sewer systems to be designed to minimize or eliminate infiltration of floodwaters into the systems and discharges from the systems into floodwaters and require on-site waste disposal systems to be located so as to avoid impairment of them or contamination from them during flooding.
