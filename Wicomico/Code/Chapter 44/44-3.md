# § 44-3. Members designated.

The following persons be and hereby are appointed as Commissioners of the Wicomico County Housing Authority to serve from the 21st day of September 1965 for a number of years set forth opposite their respective names:

A. Raymond S. Smethurst, Jr., one year.

B. Charles H. Mack, two years.

C. Ralph O. Dulany, three years.

D. James Leonard, four years.

E. Jesse M. Parker, five years.
