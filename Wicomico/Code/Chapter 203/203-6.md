# § 203-6. Exemption granted.

Pursuant to the right and authority conferred by Subsection (24) of Section 9 of Article 81 of the Annotated Code of Maryland (1957 Edition) and amendments thereto,[1] raw materials on hand and manufactured products in the hands of manufacturers in Wicomico County, Maryland, be and the same are hereby exempted from Wicomico County taxation.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: For current provisions, see § 7-226 of the Tax - Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.
