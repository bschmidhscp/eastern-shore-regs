# § 203-13. Rate of tax.

*[Amended 6-15-1993 by Res. No. 419 ; 4-17-2012 by Bill No. 2012-2 ]]*

Pursuant to the power, authority and requirement set out in § 10-106 of the Tax - General Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, a County income tax rate of 3.2% of an individual’s Maryland taxable income is established commencing January 1, 2013.
