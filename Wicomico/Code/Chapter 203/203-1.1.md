# § 203-1.1. Tax collected.

*[Added 2-2-2016 by Bill No. 2016-02]*

Pursuant to § 12-109(b)(1) of the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, the Director of Finance is designated to collect recordation tax in Wicomico County.
