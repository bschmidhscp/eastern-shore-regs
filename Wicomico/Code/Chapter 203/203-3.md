# § 203-3. Interest.

*[Amended 11-12-2008 by Bill No. 2008-6 ]*

All County property taxes are due and payable without interest as of the first day of July in each taxable year. These taxes are overdue and in arrears on the first day of the succeeding October and from and after October 1 shall bear interest at the rate of 1% per month or fraction thereof. Taxes that remain overdue and in arrears on the first day of the succeeding January shall bear interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or fraction thereof until paid.
