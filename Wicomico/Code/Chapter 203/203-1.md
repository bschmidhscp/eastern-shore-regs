# § 203-1. Tax imposed.

The following rate of tax shall be applicable to instruments recorded with the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Wicomico County:

A. In the case of instruments conveying title to property, the tax shall be at the rate of $3.50 for each $500, or fractional part thereof, of the actual consideration paid or to be paid.

B. In the case of instruments securing a debt, the tax shall be at the rate of $3.50 for each $500 of the principal amount of the debt secured.
