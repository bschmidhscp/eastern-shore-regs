# § 144-8. Applications for permits.

Applications for permits required by this chapter shall be made on forms to be supplied by the Fire Marshal. A fee of $5 shall be charged to the applicant for each permit issued under the terms of this chapter. The Fire Marshal shall account monthly and pay over to the Director of Finance all fees collected by him as required by this chapter.
