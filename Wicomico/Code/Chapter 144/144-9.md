# § 144-9. Terms defined.

*[Amended 2-15-1978 by Bill No. 1978-6]*

Wherever the words "Chief of Fire Department" or "Chief of Bureau of Fire Prevention" appear, substitute the words "Fire Marshal"; and wherever the word "municipality" is used, it shall be held to mean Wicomico County; and wherever "N.F.P.A." is used, it means "National Fire Protection Association"; and wherever "A.I.A." is used, it means "American Insurance Association.
