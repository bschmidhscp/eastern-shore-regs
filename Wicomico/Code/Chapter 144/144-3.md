# § 144-3. Inspectors.

*[Amended 10-17-2006 by Bill No. 2006-11 ]*

The County Executive may appoint, as inspectors, such other persons as may be necessary for the enforcement of this chapter, who shall be thoroughly experienced in fire prevention and life safety regulations in connection with hazardous materials and processes.
