# § 140-4. Exemptions.

The following activities are exempt from the provisions of this chapter:

A. Agricultural land management practices and agricultural BMPs;

B. Clearing or grading activities that disturb less than 5,000 square feet of land area or disturb less than 100 cubic yards of earth; and

C. Clearing or grading activities that are subject exclusively to state approval and enforcement under state law and regulations.
