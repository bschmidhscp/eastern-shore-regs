# § 140-5. Variances.

The Wicomico County Soil Conservation District may only grant a variance from the requirements of the Standards and Specifications when strict adherence will result in exceptional hardship and not fulfill the intent of this chapter. The owner/developer shall submit a written request for a variance to the Wicomico County Soil Conservation District. The request must state the specific variance sought and the reasons for the request. The Wicomico County Soil Conservation District shall not grant a variance unless and until sufficient information is provided describing the unique circumstances of the site to justify the variance.
