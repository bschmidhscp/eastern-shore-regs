# § 140-23. Severability.

If any portion, section, subsection, sentence, clause or phase of this chapter is for any reason held invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent jurisdiction, such portion shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and independent provision and such holding shall not affect the validity of the remaining portion of this chapter, it being the intent of Wicomico County, Maryland that this chapter shall stand, notwithstanding the invalidity of any portion, section, subsection, clause or phrase, hereof.
