# § 140-14. Approval suspension and revocation.

The Wicomico County Soil Conservation District may suspend or revoke any approvals after providing written notification to the applicant based on any of the following reasons:

A. Any violation(s) of the terms or conditions of the approved erosion and sediment control plan;

B. Noncompliance with violation notice(s) or stop-work order(s);

C. Changes in site characteristics upon which plan approval were based; or

D. Any violation(s) of this chapter or any rules and regulations adopted under it.
