# § 135-5. Construal of maps.

The Drug-Free School Zone Maps approved and adopted by this article shall continue to constitute an official finding and record as to the location and boundaries of the areas on or within 1,000 feet of property owned by or leased to any elementary or secondary school or school board which are used for school purposes until such time, if any, that this article shall be amended to reflect any additions or deletions with respect to the location and boundaries of school property and the Drug-Free School Zones.
