# § 183-32. Right of appeal.

A. The owner of a building or structure or any other person may appeal from a decision of the Plumbing Official refusing to grant a modification of the provisions of this code covering the manner of construction or materials to be used in the erection, alteration or repair of a plumbing system to the Wicomico County Plumbing Board.

B. Application for appeal. Application for appeal may be made when it is claimed that the true intent of this code or the rules legally adopted thereunder have been incorrectly interpreted, the provisions of this code do not fully apply or an equally good or better form of construction can be used.
