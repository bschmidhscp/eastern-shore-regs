# § 183-39. Violations and penalties.

A. it shall be a misdemeanor for any person to violate any provision of this article, and, upon conviction thereof, such persons shall be punished by a fine of not more than $1,000 or imprisoned for not more than six months, or both. Each day that a violation exists or continues shall constitute a separate offense.

B. Violation of the provisions of this article may result in the revocation of the individual, firm, partnership or corporation's permit to operate as a scavenger.

C. The imposition of the penalties herein prescribed shall not preclude Wicomico County, Maryland, and/or the owner and/or the manager of an approved facility from instituting appropriate action to prevent, restrain, correct or abate violations of this article or the rules and regulations of the approved facility.
