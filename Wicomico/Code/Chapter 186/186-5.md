# § 186-5. Establishment; membership; terms of office; meetings.

A. There is hereby created and established the Wicomico County Agricultural and commercial seafood operation Reconciliation Commission, hereinafter referred to as "WCACSOC," which shall arbitrate and mediate disputes involving agricultural operations conducted on agricultural lands and commercial seafood operation practices and issue opinions on whether such agricultural and commercial seafood operations are conducted in a manner consistent with generally accepted agricultural and commercial seafood management practices.

B. Membership.

  (1) The WCACSOC shall be composed of nine residents of the County, all of whom shall be appointed by the County Executive and confirmed by the County Council. The County Executive shall designate the chairman. The Committee shall be composed of the following representatives:

    (a) The Wicomico County Farm Bureau;

    (b) The Coastal Board of Realtors;

    (c) The Wicomico County Soil Conservation District;

    (d) The Agricultural Preservation Advisory Board;

    (e) The Delmarva Poultry Industry-Growers Committee;

    (f) Chamber of Commerce - AG Committee;

    (g) Homeowner's Association, who is a resident of Wicomico County.

    (h) Two representatives from the waterman community to represent commercial seafood operation interests.

  (2) A municipality representative shall serve as an alternate member to the WCACSOC. This representative shall be rotated between the incorporated municipalities of Wicomico County on a yearly basis.

  (3) The County extension agent shall serve as an ex-officio member.

C. Terms.

  (1) The Committee members shall serve a four-year term, except the municipality representative who shall serve a one-year term; however, the initial appointments shall be as follows:

    (a) Three members shall be chosen to serve four-year terms;

    (b) Three members shall be chosen to serve three-year terms;

    (c) Two members shall be chosen to serve two-year terms;

    (d) One member shall be chosen to serve a one-year term; and

    (e) The municipality alternate member shall be chosen to serve a one-year term.

  (2) After these initial appointments, all terms, except the municipal representative, shall be for a full four-year term.
