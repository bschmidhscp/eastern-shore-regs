# § 186-3. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**AGRICULTURAL LAND** All real property within the boundaries of Wicomico County that is:

A. Lying in the Agricultural-Rural Residential District; or

B. Carried on the tax rolls of the State Department of Assessments and Taxation as agricultural; or

C. All other land that has been used as an agricultural operation continuously for one year.

**AGRICULTURAL OPERATION** Includes, but is not limited to, all matters set forth in the definition of "operation" in § 5-308(a) of the Courts and Judicial Proceedings Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, as amended from time to time; the production of all matters encompassed within the definition of "farm product" in § 10-601(c) of the Agriculture Article, as amended from time to time; the cultivation and tillage of the soil; the spreading of manure, lime, fertilizer and the like; composting; spraying; production, harvesting and processing of agricultural crops; raising poultry and other fowl; production of eggs; production of milk and dairy products; production of livestock, including pasturage; production of bees and their products; production of fruit, vegetables and other horticultural crops; production of aquatic plants; aquaculture; production of timber; any commercial agricultural procedure performed as incident to or in conjunction with such operations, including preparation for market, delivery to storage or to market or to carriers for transportation to market; usage of land in furtherance of educational and social goals, (including, but not limited to, 4-H clubs and Future Farmers of America), agrotourism and alternative agricultural enterprises; and the like.

**COMMERCIAL SEAFOOD OPERATION** An operation for the harvesting, storage, processing, marketing, sale, purchase, trade or transport and associated activities of any seafood product, including the delivery, storage and maintenance of boats, supplies and equipment and charter boat fishing and related arrival and departure activities, equipment and supplies.

**GENERALLY ACCEPTED AGRICULTURAL AND COMMERCIAL SEAFOOD MANAGEMENT PRACTICES** Those methods used in connection with agricultural or commercial seafood operations which do not violate applicable federal, state or local laws or public health, safety and welfare and which are generally accepted agricultural practices in the agriculture industry or generally accepted practices in the commercial seafood industry. Generally accepted agricultural and commercial seafood management practices include practices which are recognized as generally accepted management practices and those methods which are authorized by various governmental agencies, bureaus and departments, such as the Wicomico County Cooperative Extension Service of the University of Maryland, the Wicomico County Soil Conservation Districts and the like. If no generally accepted agricultural or commercial seafood management practice exists or if there is no method authorized by those agencies mentioned herein which governs a practice, the practice is presumed to be a generally accepted agricultural or commercial seafood management practice.
