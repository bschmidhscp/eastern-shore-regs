# § 186-1. Findings and purpose.

A. Agriculture is the largest industry in Wicomico County and it adds many positive benefits to the quality of life for the residents of Wicomico County. Commercial seafood operations are a historically important industry in Wicomico County and also adds positive benefits to the quality of life for residents of Wicomico County. Therefore, it is the declared policy of the County to preserve, the County of its agricultural and seafood industries by limiting the circumstances under which agricultural and commercial seafood operations may be deemed to constitute a nuisance, trespass, or other interference with the reasonable use and enjoyment of land, including, but not limited to, smoke, odors, flies, dust, noise, chemicals, or vibration, provided that nothing in this chapter shall, in any way, restrict or impede the authority of the state and of the County to protect the public health, safety, and welfare, nor shall it restrict or impede private covenants.

B. It is in the public interest to promote a better understanding between agricultural and commercial seafood operations and their noncommercial neighbors, concerning the possible inconveniences of agricultural and commercial seafood operations which follow generally accepted agricultural or commercial seafood practices and do not endanger public health or safety.

C. An additional purpose of this chapter is to promote a good neighbor policy by advising purchasers and users of property adjacent to or near agricultural or commercial seafood operations of the inherent potential problems associated with such use. These potential problems include, but are not limited to, noises, odors, dust, flies, chemicals, smoke, vibration and hours of operation that may accompany agricultural or commercial seafood operations. It is intended that, through mandatory disclosures, purchasers and users will better understand the impact of living near agricultural or commercial seafood operations and be prepared to accept attendant conditions as the natural result of living in or near rural areas and waterways. However, this chapter shall be effective regardless of whether disclosure was made in accordance with § 186-7 herein (Right to farm and commercial seafood operation notice and real estate transfer disclosure).
