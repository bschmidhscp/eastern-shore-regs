# Wicomico County — Fee Findings

*Prepared 2026-10-11 from the corpus (eCode360 WI0638, retrieved 2026-10-11, "current through" unknown).*

## Impact fee / excise tax
- **None in force for new homes.** Former Ch. 130 (Development Impact Fees, Bill 2006-2) was repealed 11-1-2016 by Bill 2016-12 [Ch. 130 History].
- § 203-22 only authorizes the Council to adopt impact fees by ordinance; no rate ordinance is in the code [§ 203-22].
- No excise tax found. A 1% transfer tax (Bill 2000-5) was rejected by referendum 2000 and refunded [§ 203-23 note].
- Comp plan only recommends studying road impact fees [Comp Plan 1998 p.114].

## Health dept / septic

Wicomico Health Dept (Environmental Health) handles perc tests and plat sign-off; its applications page lists no fees (https://www.wicomicohealth.org/environmental-health/applications-information/, retrieved 2026-10-11). Phone 410-749-1244. Wet-season testing normally Nov–Mar; MDE gave a 2026 off-season expedite policy [PC minutes 2026-05-21 p.6].

## Water and sewer charges
- No county connection-fee schedule in corpus. Code § 97-13 lets the Commission set "reasonable" connection charges by street width [§ 97-13]. Urban service districts also levy annual front-foot benefit assessments [§ 97-10; § 97-8].
- The W&S Master Plan's "$6,360 per EDU" connection charge is Sussex County, DE, a comparison example, not Wicomico [W&S Master Plan 2021 p.180].
- Municipal (Salisbury, Fruitland, Delmar) rates are separate. W&S plan amendment review fee: $200 + advertising (2010) [Fees: Review-Fees p.1].
