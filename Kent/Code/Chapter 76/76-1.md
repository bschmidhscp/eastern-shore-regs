# § 76-1. Title and purpose.

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the "Kent County Cable Television Franchise Act" (hereinafter "chapter"). The purposes of this chapter are to establish the terms and conditions under which a cable television or open video system occupying the public rights-of-way must operate within Kent County, Maryland (which may hereinafter be referred to as "County," "franchising authority" or "grantor"); to provide for the payment of a franchise fee to the County for use of public rights-of-way and the costs associated with administering and regulating the system; and to enhance the County's authority to grant a cable television system franchise while managing the public rights-of-way.
