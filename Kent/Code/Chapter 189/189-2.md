# § 189-2. Appointment; salary; discharge.

A. The Board of County Commissioners of Kent County may appoint a Director from time to time. He shall be selected on the basis of his executive, administrative and fiscal abilities and his knowledge and experience of housing, plumbing and electrical codes, public grants, rental projects and subsidies for housing programs.

B. The Director shall receive an annual salary to be established by the County Commissioners.
