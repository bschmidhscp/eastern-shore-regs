# Chapter 182. (Reserved)

[Former Ch. 182, Floodplain Management, adopted 4-7-1992 by Bill No. 4-92, as amended 12-6-1994 by Bill No. 6-94, was repealed 4-18-2023 by Bill No. 1-2023. The floodplain management regulations of Kent County are included in the Kent County Land Use Ordinance, which is on file in the County offices and available for inspection during regular business hours.]
