# § 148-13. Modification of regulations.

A. The County Commissioners shall have authority to adopt regulations to carry out and promote the intent and purpose of this chapter as stated heretofore in § 148-1 and provided, further, that said regulations are fair and reasonable.

B. Procedure for issuance of regulations. The County Commissioners, before issuing any regulations, shall send a copy of the proposed regulations to all known interested governmental agencies or participating parties who may have an interest in the proposed regulations and, in addition, shall publish notice of intent to issue regulations on a given subject matter in any of the Kent County newspapers for thirty (30) days prior to the issuance of the regulations. The County Commissioners shall invite comments, ideas and suggestions, if any, in compliance with § 148-1 and this section.
