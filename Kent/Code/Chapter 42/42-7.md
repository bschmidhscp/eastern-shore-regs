# § 42-7. Duties and responsibilities.

A. Provide recommendations on a variety of topics, including parkland acquisition, services, and program offerings.

B. Offer guidance on parks and recreation issues, encompassing planning, policy development, and program implementation.

C. Evaluate budgets and proposals to ensure they align with the objectives of the Parks and Recreation department.

D. Act as a liaison between the community and the Parks and Recreation department.

E. Assist in the development and promotion of new parks, programs, and activities to enhance community engagement.

F. Recommend policies, rules, and regulations for facilities, including participation fees for programs.

G. Monitor the effectiveness of parks and recreation programs to ensure they meet community needs.

H. Propose rules and regulations to guide the operations of parks and recreation.

I. Foster collaboration among county departments and municipal and state governments.

J. Establish bylaws for governance and business conduct and recommend rules and regulations for the use and access of recreational programs and facilities.

K. Perform additional duties as assigned by the County Commissioners as necessary.
