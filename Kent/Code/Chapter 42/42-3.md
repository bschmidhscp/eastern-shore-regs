# § 42-3. Composition and compensation.

A. The Parks and Recreation Advisory Board shall consist of a minimum of seven and a maximum of nine members, excluding the Director of Parks and Recreation and the Deputy Director of Public Works, who will serve as nonvoting ex officio members. The membership will comprise individuals with a strong interest in recreation and parks.

B. Advisory Board members may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred in the performance of their duties, subject to approval by the County Commissioner.
