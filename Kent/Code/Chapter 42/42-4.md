# § 42-4. Terms and removal.

A. At least one member shall be a County Commissioner, serving for the duration of their term in office. Additionally, at least one member must be a Board of Education member, also serving for their term, and one member shall be a secondary school student from Kent County, serving as long as they are enrolled. All other members will serve a term of four years or until their successors are appointed.

B. A member may be recommended by the Parks and Recreation Advisory Board to the County Commissioners for removal. A member missing three consecutive meetings may be recommended for removal.
