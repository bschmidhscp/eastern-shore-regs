# § 42-5. Officers and quorum.

A. The officers of the Parks and Recreation Advisory Board shall include a Chair and a Vice Chair, both elected from among the Board's members. Each officer shall serve a term of two years with an opportunity to be reappointed or until a successor is elected. The specific responsibilities of the officers are detailed in the bylaws of the Parks and Recreation Advisory Board.

B. A quorum for the Parks and Recreation Advisory Board shall consist of a majority of its members.
