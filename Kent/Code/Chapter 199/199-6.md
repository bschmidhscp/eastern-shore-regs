# § 199-6. Meetings; rules; record.

*[Amended 12-6-1994 by Bill No. 6-94]*

The Commission shall hold at least one (1) regular meeting each month. It shall adopt rules for the transaction of business and shall keep a record of its resolutions, transactions, findings and determinations, which records shall be a public record.
