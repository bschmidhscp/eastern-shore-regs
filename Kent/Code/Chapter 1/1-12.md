# § 1-12. Changes in previously adopted bills.

A. In compiling and preparing the legislation for adoption and revision as part of the Code, certain grammatical changes and other minor changes were made in one (1) or more of said Acts. It is the intention of the County Commissioners that all such changes be adopted as part of the Code as if the bills so changed had been previously formally amended to read as such.

B. In addition, the following changes, amendments or revisions are made herewith, to become effective upon the effective date of this Act. (Chapter and section number references are to the Acts as they have been renumbered and appear in the Code.)[1]
