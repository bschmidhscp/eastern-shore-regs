# Chapter 178. (Reserved)

[Former Ch. 178, Erosion and Sediment Control, adopted 4-3-1990 as Ch. 6, Art. III, of the 1989 Code, as amended 3-3-1992 by Bill No. 2-92; 12-6-1994 by Bill No. 6-94; and 6-6-1995 by Bill No. 5-95, was repealed 4-18-2023 by Bill No. 1-2023. The erosion and sediment control regulations of Kent County are included in the Kent County Land Use Ordinance, which is on file in the County offices and available for inspection during regular business hours.]
