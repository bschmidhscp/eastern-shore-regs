# § 21-4. Terms; removal.

A. Commission members shall serve for terms of three years, or until a successor is appointed. Members may not be appointed for more than two successive full terms (filling a vacant position during the term shall not be construed as a full term).

B. If a member is currently serving on the Economic Development Commission or Tourism Development Advisory Board, that member may finish their term as a member of the Economic and Tourism Development Commission. If that member is serving in their first term, that member may be reappointed for a second term.

C. A member may be recommended by the Commission to the County Commissioners for removal. A member missing three consecutive meetings may be recommended for removal.
