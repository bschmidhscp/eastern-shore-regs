# § 3-11. Compensation of boards.

*[Added 6-6-2017 by Bill No. 1-2017 ]*

Members of boards, commissions and committees appointed by the County Commissioners of Kent County shall not be paid any compensation for their services, except where otherwise provided in this Code, but may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred in their duties as designated by the County Commissioners.
