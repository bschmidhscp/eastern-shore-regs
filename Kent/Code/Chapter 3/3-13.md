# § 3-13. Notices.

*[Amended 12-6-1994 by Bill No. 6-94 ]*

It shall be considered sufficient compliance in Kent County as respects any notice or advertising required by law to be published that such notice or advertising be printed and published in one or more newspapers published in Kent County.
