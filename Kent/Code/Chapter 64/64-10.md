# § 64-10. Feral cat colonies.

No person shall maintain a feral cat colony without first registering the colony with the Humane Society of Kent County. Those persons maintaining feral cat colonies on lands actively used in agriculture and zoned either Agricultural Zoning District (AZD) or Resource Conservation District (RCD), or on land constituting a farm as defined by the Land Use Ordinance, shall be exempt from the registration requirement. A registration certificate shall be issued to the colony caretaker responsible for the colony and shall contain the following information:

A. The name, telephone number, and address of the person to whom the certificate is issued.

B. The date of issuance.

C. The number of cats in the colony.

D. The address (location) of the colony.
