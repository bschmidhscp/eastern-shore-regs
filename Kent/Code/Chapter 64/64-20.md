# § 64-20. General conditions related to physical siting; removal at Board's discretion.

A. The Board shall set conditions addressing how the physical facilities relate to the site's size and shape, considering the proposed size, shape, and arrangement of buildings, structures, and secure enclosures to be licensed on each specific property.

B. The Board shall review and establish specific setbacks from all property lines that are within 1,000 feet of all buildings and attached runs for the hounds.

C. The Board may also set forth general conditions to address:

  (1) Anticipated traffic patterns;

  (2) Impacts, if any, on public services and public facilities;

  (3) Exterior displays, lighting, storage, signs, and other appurtenances;

  (4) Increased landscaping to provide screening of physical facilities;

  (5) Consistency with the Critical Area Program; and

  (6) Consistency with the Comprehensive Plan and Land Use Ordinance.

D. The Board may add, change, or remove conditions during any review, including conditions required during previous reviews.
