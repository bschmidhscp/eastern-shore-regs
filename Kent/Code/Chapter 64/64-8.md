# § 64-8. Dog and cat waste.

The owner of or person responsible for every dog or cat shall be responsible for the removal of any excrement deposited by the owner's animal on public property, recreation areas or private property other than the owner's. The owner of or person responsible for every dog or cat shall not allow excrement deposited by animals to accumulate on his property so as to cause a health hazard or become a public nuisance.
