# § 64-19. Physical siting requirements.

A. All Fox Hunting Club Dog Kennel facilities must be located on the property in a way that reasonably avoids or reduces Public Nuisances as defined in § 64-12.

B. Only Fox Hunting Clubs as defined in Part II may operate Fox Hunting Club Dog Kennels. Applicants must submit formal documentation showing that they operate as part of a Fox Hunting Club.

C. All Fox Hunting Club Dog Kennel facilities must have a secure enclosure that includes the buildings and attached runs for the hounds.
