# § 152-27. Reduction of taxes under lease agreement.

The lessor of real property eligible for tax credits under § 152-22 shall reduce by the amount of the tax credits computed under s§ 152-25 the amount of taxes for which the eligible business entity is contractually liable under the lease agreement.
