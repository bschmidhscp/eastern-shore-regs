# § 152-34. Qualifications for tax credit.

To qualify for a property tax credit under this section, a business entity shall obtain new, improved, or expanded premises in a commerce zone by:

A. Purchasing newly constructed premises;

B. Constructing new premises;

C. Causing new premises to be constructed; or

D. Improving existing premises for occupation by the business entity.
