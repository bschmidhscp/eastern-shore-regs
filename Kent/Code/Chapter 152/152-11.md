# § 152-11. Taxes to be liens.

All state and county taxes on real estate shall be liens on the real estate in respect of which they are levied from the date they become payable.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: Former Section 9-23 of the 1989 Code, Taxes to be liens; personal property, which immediately followed this section, was deleted 12-6-1994 by Bill No. 6-94.
