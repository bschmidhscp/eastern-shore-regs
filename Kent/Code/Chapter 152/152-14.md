# § 152-14. Evidence of payment.

Payment of the tax authorized to be imposed by this article shall be evidenced by the fixing to or stamping on the instrument of writing offered for recordation a legend stating that such tax has been paid and the amount of payment and containing the signature of the Clerk or an authorized facsimile thereof. Any instrument so executed shall be prima facie evidence that the tax imposed by this article has been paid.
