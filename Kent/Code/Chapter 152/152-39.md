# § 152-39. Definitions.

In this article, the following words have the meanings indicated.

**QUALIFIED PROPERTIES** Residential properties that:

A. Include a complete automatic fire protection sprinkler system; and

B. Are used as a principal residence of the homeowner; and

C. Have completed construction between January 1, 2020, and December 31, 2027. [Amended 9-19-2023 by Bill No. 4-2023]
