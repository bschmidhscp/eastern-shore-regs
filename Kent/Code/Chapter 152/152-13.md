# § 152-13. Rate.

The tax authorized by this article shall be levied at the rate of not more than 1/2 of 1% of the actual consideration paid or to be paid for the conveyance of title and shall be collected by the Clerk of the Circuit Court prior to his accepting any such instrument for recordation.
