# § 152-28. Refund of tax credits earned.

The business entity shall refund to the County and state tax credits earned, if, during the three taxable years succeeding any year in which a credit was earned, the business entity fails to satisfy the applicable thresholds to qualify for a property tax credit required under § 152-25 of this article.
