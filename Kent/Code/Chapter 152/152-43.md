# § 152-43. Establishment; purposes.

A. There is a Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund, into which shall be paid all funds received from the State Cannabis Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund established pursuant to § 1-322 of the Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

B. The Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund is a special, non-lapsing fund.

C. The revenue paid into the fund shall only be used only as set forth in Chapter 15, Community Reinvestment and Repair Commission, § 15-7C.
