# § 152-42. Tax credit granted.

A. Kent County grants a tax credit against the County real property tax imposed on a manufacturing, commercial, or industrial building that:

  (1) Is located in an arts and entertainment district designated as such under § 4-704 of the Economic Development Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland; and

  (2) Is wholly or partially constructed or renovated, after the date of the designation described above, to be capable for use by a qualifying residing artist or an arts and entertainment enterprise.

B. The real property tax credit shall be applied to the increase in assessment resulting from construction or renovation and shall be 100% in each of the following fiscal years not to exceed more than 10 years.

C. An application for the tax credit created by this article shall be filed by the property owner on or before the June 1 preceding the fiscal year for which the tax credit is sought.

D. Kent County may designate an arts and entertainment district manager who may promulgate forms for applications for the tax credit hereunder and may require such verification of qualifications hereunder as such office may deem appropriate.
