# § 152-29. Verification.

The business entity shall submit the following information to verify that the business entity is not subject to §§ 152-27 and 152-28 of this article:

A. Certification as to the size of the premises occupied.

B. Annual employment records certifying the number of full-time employees and the date that each was employed.
