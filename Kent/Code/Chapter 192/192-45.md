# § 192-45. Cut-in certificate.

No electric light or power company shall attach its power lines or electric meters to any consumer's property within the borders of Kent County unless the work has been installed by an electrician licensed by the State of Maryland and registered in Kent County, Maryland, under this Part 2 and until a temporary or permanent meter cut-in certificate has been issued by the inspector or an appropriate fifteen-day card issued in accordance with the guidelines of the local utilities.
