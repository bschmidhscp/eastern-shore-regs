# § 192-46. Violations and penalties.

A. Any person, firm or corporation who or which shall install, alter or replace any water supply, wiring, appliances or devices for electric light, heat, protective signaling or power without having a valid and current license issued by the State of Maryland and a valid and current registration issued by Kent County, Maryland, except under the direct supervision of a person having such valid license and registration, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall be liable for a fine of not less than $100 nor more than $1,000 or to imprisonment of not more than 90 days, or both, in the discretion of the court, and any such conviction shall ipso facto revoke and annul any registration that may have been issued to such person. Each day's violation shall constitute a separate offense.

B. The County Commissioners of Kent County may enjoin such person, firm or corporation from engaging in electrical work in violation of this Part 2 by action for injunction or other equitable relief in a court of competent jurisdiction or may recover the monetary penalty by civil action in such court.
