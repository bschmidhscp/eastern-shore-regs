# § 192-37. Fees; insurance.

A. The registration fees and renewal fees for electrical registrations shall be according to a schedule to be established from time to time by resolution of the Board of County Commissioners.

B. Before the registration is issued or reissued, the applicant shall furnish the County with prima facie evidence of adequate business liability insurance commensurate with that which is required by the State of Maryland. All registered master electricians shall be required to carry coverage equal to or exceeding the requirements by the State of Maryland for master electricians.
