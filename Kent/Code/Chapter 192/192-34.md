# § 192-34. Applications.

Applications may be procured in person or by mail from the office of the Kent County Department of Planning, Housing, and Zoning during normal working hours and, upon submission, must be accompanied with the following:

A. Photocopy of a current Maryland state-wide master electrician's license.

B. Photocopy of a driver's license (or another form of picture identification).

C. Current fee established by the County.

D. Certificate of insurance for $1,000,000 per incident and $2,000,000 general aggregate, which shall list the certificate holder as:

  County Commissioners of Kent County, Maryland

  ATTN: Electrical Office

  400 High Street

  Chestertown, MD 21620
