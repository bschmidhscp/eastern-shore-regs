# § 192-38. Registrations and renewals.

A. All registrations shall be displayed in a prominent place in the principal office or establishment of the registrant.

B. No registration shall be transferable.

C. Registrations shall be issued on a biannual basis and are valid as of the first day of July of the odd year on and prior to issuance. The full fee shall be paid irrespective of the date of issuance.

D. All registrants may display on the vehicles used in the performance of their work the words "Registered Electrician - Kent County, Maryland" and the number assigned to them in letters not less than two inches in height. This is to be done within 90 days of issuance of the registration.
