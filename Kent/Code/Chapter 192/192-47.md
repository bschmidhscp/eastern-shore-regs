# § 192-47. Definitions.

As used in this Part 3, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**HEALTH OFFICER** The legally designated authority of Kent County or the State of Maryland or his authorized representative.

**PERSON** Any individual, firm, corporation, association, partnership, institution or public body, and includes the plural as well as the singular.
