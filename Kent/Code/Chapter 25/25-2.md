# § 25-2. First District.

The First Election District shall begin at the point on the boundary line between the States of Maryland and Delaware, where Kent County joins Queen Anne's County; and running thence by and with said line northwardly to the line of Cecil County, then westerly with the division line between Cecil County and Kent and with Sassafras River to Mill Creek, then up Mill Creek to the head thereof and southerly to the public road leading from Lambson's to Chesterville, then with said public road through Chesterville and to Chester River at Crumpton, then up Chester River and with the division line between Kent and Queen Anne's Counties to the beginning. The first election district is divided into two (2) voting precincts, to be known as the "Millington Precinct," comprising all that part of said first district which lies south of the Baltimore and Delaware Bay Railroad, with the voting place at Millington, and the "Galena Precinct," comprising all that part of said district which lies north of said Baltimore and Delaware Bay Railroad, with the voting place at Galena.
