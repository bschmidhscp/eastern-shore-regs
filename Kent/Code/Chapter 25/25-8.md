# § 25-8. Seventh District.

The Seventh Election District shall begin at the mouth of Radcliff Creek on Chester River, and run up said creek and stream to the public road from Chestertown to Fairlee; then with that public road to the public road from Aldridge gate to Lankford; then with that public road and the public road from Lankford to Price's mill to the headwaters of the West Fork of Lankford Bay, then down and with said bay, and up Chester River to the beginning, with the voting place at Pomona.
