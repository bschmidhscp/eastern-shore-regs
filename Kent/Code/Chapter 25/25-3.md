# § 25-3. Second District.

The Second Election District shall begin at Mill Creek on Sassafras River, and running down Sassafras River to the public road at Betterton, then with said public road from Betterton to the intersection of said road with the public road from Coleman to Lynch's, then with that public road to Lynch's and thence with the public road to Riley's mill on Morgan Creek, then with Morgan Creek to Chester River, then up Chester River to the public road at Crumpton, then with said public road to Chesterville and with the public road towards Lambson to the line of the first district, then with that line northerly to Mill Creek, and with Mill Creek to the beginning on Sassafras River, with the voting place at Kennedyville.
