# § 25-7. Sixth District.

The Sixth Election District shall begin at Buck Neck Landing on Worton Creek, and running with the public road towards Hanesville to Militoto; thence with the public road towards Chestertown to Eliason's corner; thence with the public road to the road from Fairlee to Chestertown; then with the public road to the road leading from Aldridge gate to Lankford; then with the public road to Lankford; and with the public road from Lankford to Price's mill; and to Rees' Corner and to Swan Creek bridge; and thence westerly to Chesapeake Bay; and up and with said bay to Worton Creek; and up Worton Creek to the beginning, with the voting place at Fairlee.
