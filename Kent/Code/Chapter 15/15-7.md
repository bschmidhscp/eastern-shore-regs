# § 15-7. Duties; grant process; allocation process; use of funds.

A. Grant process. The Commission shall develop and administer a public grant application process for interested parties to apply for monies from the Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund.

B. Allocation process. The Commission shall develop recommendations, with community input, for the use of the funds in the Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund for the upcoming fiscal year. These recommendations, a summary of community input, and a summary of applicants shall be delivered to the County Commissioners for approval by resolution by May 1 each year. Following County Commissioner approval, monies may be disbursed from the Fund. The Commission's recommendations are advisory in nature and do not require the County Commissioners to award any particular grant.

C. Use of funds.

  (1) Funds from the Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund may only be used:

    (a) To fund community-based initiatives intended to benefit low-income communities;

    (b) To fund community-based initiatives that serve disproportionately impacted areas, as defined by § 36-101 of the Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland;

    (c) For any other use permitted under § 1-322 of the Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, as amended; or

    (d) For related administrative expenses.

  (2) Funds from the Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund may not be expended:

    (a) For law enforcement agencies or activities; or

    (b) To supplant funding that otherwise would be appropriated for preexisting local programs.
