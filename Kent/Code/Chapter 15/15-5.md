# § 15-5. Meetings; quorum; voting.

A. Meetings. The Commission shall meet at the call of the Chair as frequently as required to perform its duties, but not less than four times per year. All meetings shall comply with the Maryland Open Meetings Act.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: See § 3-101 et seq. of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

B. Quorum. A simple majority of the members of the Commission shall serve as a quorum.

C. Voting. An affirmative vote by the majority of a quorum is needed for any official action.
