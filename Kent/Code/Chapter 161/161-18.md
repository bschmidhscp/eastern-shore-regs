# § 161-18. Findings and requirement.

A. Legislative findings. It is the legislative finding of the County Commissioners that it is necessary for the existing and future health, safety and welfare of the public in general that all sanitary facilities serving 20 or more single-family units or equivalent dwelling units in separate ownership, producing or designed to produce greater than 5,000 gallons per day of effluent or water, be provided through sanitary service areas where such facilities shall be owned and operated by the County Commissioners.

B. Required. Wherever sanitary facilities serve, or are proposed to serve, 20 or more single-family units or equivalent dwelling units in separate ownership, producing or designed to produce greater that 5,000 gallons per day of effluent or water, a sanitary service area shall be required to be established in accordance with the provisions of this article.
