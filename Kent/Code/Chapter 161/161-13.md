# § 161-13. Violations and penalties.

A. Intentional violation. Any intended violation of the provisions of this article shall constitute a misdemeanor.

B. Inadvertent violation. Any inadvertent violation of the provisions of this article shall constitute a civil infraction.

C. Penalties. Any person who intentionally violates this article shall, upon conviction thereof, be fined not more than $1,000 or be imprisoned for not more than 30 days, or both. Each and every day such violation occurs shall be considered a separate offense. The owner of the land upon which the violation exists, as well as the developer and any other person who commits, participates in, assists in or maintains such violation, may be found guilty of a separate offense and suffer the penalties herein provided.

D. Correction of violation. The County Commissioners or the court may order the violation removed or corrected and may issue such additional orders as the County Commissioners or court may deem appropriate to safeguard against future violations of this article.
