# § 46-4. Powers and duties.

It shall be the duty of the Association to recommend to the Board of County Commissioners of Kent County the location or locations to establish ditches, drains or canals and to cause to be constructed, straightened, widened or deepened any ditch, drain or watercourse for the purpose of draining wet, swampy or overflowed lands or lands subject to overflow and to assist the Board of County Commissioners of Kent County in performing its duties as set forth under Article 25, § 52 et seq., of the Annotated Code of Maryland (1957 Edition) pertaining to draining lands.
