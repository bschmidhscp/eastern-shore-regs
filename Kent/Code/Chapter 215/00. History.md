# Chapter 215. (Reserved)

[Former Ch. 215, Subdivision of Land, adopted 4-3-1990 as Ch. 6, Art. II, of the 1989 Code, as amended 12-6-1994 by Bill No. 6-94, was repealed 4-18-2023 by Bill No. 1-2023. The subdivision regulations of Kent County are included in the Kent County Land Use Ordinance, which is on file in the County offices and available for inspection during regular business hours.]
