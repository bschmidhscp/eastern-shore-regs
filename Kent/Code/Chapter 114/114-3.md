# § 114-3. Violations and penalties.

Any person violating the provisions of this chapter is subject to a fine of not less than one hundred dollars ($100.) nor more than one thousand ($1,000.00) dollars or imprisonment of not more than six (6) months, or both such fine and imprisonment.
