# **<u><mark>PETE LANDON, DIRECTOR, AND LOGAN QUINN, CHIEF OF EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES, OFFICE OF EMERGENCY SERVICES, AND JOANNE MCNAMARA, DIRECTOR, GRANTS & CONTRACTS</mark></u>** 

Ms. McNamara provided a brief overview of the grant opportunity for the Whole Blood Program. Mr. Quinn provided background information regarding the Whole Blood Program initiative. The Maryland Institute of Emergency Medical Services Systems (MIEMSS) and Maryland Department of Health (MDOH) invited local jurisdictions to submit applications for grant funding to initiate a Whole Blood Program, funded by an allocation of Rural Health Transformation Funds. The purpose of the Whole Blood Program is to bridge the gap between injury and hospital treatment and improve survival for patients suffering from severe menorrhagic shock by enabling pre-hospital blood transfusion administered directly by Emergency Medical Services (EMS) clinicians at the scene of the injury or en route to the hospital. MIEMSS and MDOH offered awards to the Emergency Services Departments of Cecil, Dorchester, Kent, Queen Anne's, and Somerset Counties, as well as Ocean City and Salisbury. Kent County's Office of Emergency Services seeks to accept its awarded allocation of $57,226.00 to initiate this program. On motion by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved and accepted the Grant Award Agreement as presented.

# **PUBLIC HEARING PROPOSED FISCAL YEAR 2027 COUNTY BUDGET** 

In compliance with and according to Maryland Annotated Code Tax-General and Tax Property Articles, the County Commissioners of Kent County held a public hearing on Tuesday, June 2, 2026, in the Commissioners’ Hearing Room, R. Clayton Mitchell Jr., Kent County Government Center, 400 High Street, Chestertown, Maryland, on the Proposed Fiscal Year 2027 Budget beginning July 1, 2026. 

County Commissioners Ronald H. Fithian, Albert Nickerson, and John F. Price were in attendance, as were Shelley L. Heller, County Administrator, G. Robert Mowell, County Attorney, Bob Gast, Chief Finance Officer, Pat Merritt, Chief Finance Officer, Office of Finance, and Lacey M. Townsend, County Clerk. 

Bob Gast, Chief Finance Officer, Office of Finance, reviewed the Proposed Fiscal Year 2027 Budget via a presentation. Mr. Gast explained the categories of the County’s revenue sources. Kent County’s property tax rate is $1.022 per $100.00 of the assessable base. The maximum income tax rate this year remains the same at 3.30%. Mr. Gast reviewed funding levels by category and function. Mr. Gast reviewed expenditure highlights, capital projects schedule, potential future revenue, and the Kent County Middle School project funding. Personnel actions included a 5% salary increase effective July 1, 2027. The budget includes funding for five new public safety positions and a newly implemented grade schedule in Emergency Services. Some additional expenditure highlights included the County’s health insurance increase and pension plan expense increase, a new strategic plan for Economic and Tourism Development, a teacher’s pension plan increase, an increase in the allocation to volunteer fire companies, and an increase in public library funding. Mr. Gast provided a brief overview of the County’s capital project expenditures. The County’s efforts to address the budget challenges included the following: 

- Property Tax Rate $1.022 per $100.00 of the assessable base (No change) 

- Income Tax Rate 3.3% (No Change) 

- Public Utilities Property Tax Rate $2.555 per $100 of assessable base (No Change) 

- Business Personal Property Tax Rate on Public Utilities $2.555 per $100 of assessable base (No Change) 

- Water and Sewer Rates increase 4% 

- Five New Positions 

- Capital Projects/Expenditures 

- Upcoming State Mandates 

Each Commissioner provided remarks on the proposed budget. Commissioner Price read a statement into the record. 

On a motion by Commissioner Price, and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved to go into a Public Hearing.  The public hearing for the Fiscal Year 2027 Proposed County Budget commenced at 6:49 p.m.

# **<u>THE VIEW HOSPITALITY GROUP, LLC DBA THE VIEW AT WORTON CREEK, CLASS B, BEER, WINE, AND LIQUOR LICENSE</u>** 

Mr. Mowell stated that an application was submitted for a Class B, Beer, Wine, and Liquor License for View Hospitality Group, LLC d/b/a The View at Worton Creek. The hearing was duly advertised in the Kent News on May 7, 2026, and May 14, 2026. 

On a motion by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved to go into a Public Hearing. The hearing commenced at 6:08 p.m. 

Mr. Mowell administered the Oath to Bonnie S. Pearsall, Inspector, Kent Alcohol and Tobacco Enforcement (KATE), Owner, Eric Walters, representing The View Hospitality Group, LLC, dba The View at Worton Creek, and anyone else wishing to testify. 

Inspector Pearsall stated that a legal review of the application for a Class B, Beer, Wine, and Liquor License had been completed. A Sales and Use Tax License was issued to the applicant as an individual, but needs to be issued under the Establishment's name and address, which prompted a Hold from the Office of the Comptroller. As of the time of the Public Hearing, the Sales and Use Tax License has been corrected and released by the Comptroller’s Office. However, the applicant must also provide proof of inspection from the Fire Marshal confirming the establishment's maximum capacity. The establishment also needs a food service license, which cannot be issued until the fire marshal inspection has been completed. The applicant has demonstrated good-faith efforts to address these items to make the necessary corrections. It is under the Board’s discretion to approve the issuance of an alcohol beverage license contingent upon receiving all necessary documents required. Mr. Walters read a statement into the record regarding his business and the intentions of the operations. No other comments were offered in favor of or in opposition to the application. 

On a motion by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved to go out of the Public Hearing and back into open session. The public hearing concluded at 6:12 p.m. 

On a motion by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the Class B, Beer, Wine, and Liquor License application for The View Hospitality Group, LLC dba The View at Worton Creek, contingent upon the receipt of a Fire Marshal’s inspection report, the issuance of a Food Service License, a corrected Sales and Use Tax License, and the Office of the Comptroller's release of hold.
