# **<u><mark>CARLA GERBER, DIRECTOR, PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING</mark></u>** 

<mark>Ms. Gerber explained that questions have been asked about the timing and process for site plan review for utility-scale solar facilities, especially those large enough to require a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity (CPCN). The Unified Development Ordinance permits applications that are “substantially complete” to be scheduled for review by the Technical Advisory Committee and Planning Commission. Substantially complete is not defined. The Planning Commission has tried to be fair and flexible, allowing concurrent review by scheduling these applications for concept or preliminary review while they are working through the CPCN process. We have required the final order for the CPCN before scheduling the final site plan review.</mark> 

<mark>The following questions were posed to the Commissioners by Ms. Gerber:</mark> 

- <mark>Is it inefficient and wasteful for the County to review projects before getting a CPCN on the chance that the Public Service Commission (PSC) requires changes to the site plan?</mark> 

- <mark>Does it make it easier for the PSC to approve the CPCN if they think that the County has already approved the site plan?</mark> 

- <mark>Does it provide the County an opportunity to influence the PSC decision if the Planning Commission requests changes to the site plan?</mark> 

<mark>Mr. Mowell offered further insight into the information Ms. Gerber provided. Discussion took place. Janet Christensen-Lewis, County Resident, provided comments regarding the solar panel projects throughout the County and the approval process required.</mark>

# **<u>AMENDMENT TO THE KENT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN, SERVICE FOR THE EVERTON WAREHOUSE PROJECT</u>** 

The public hearing commenced at 6:19 p.m. in the County Commissioners’ Hearing Room, R. Clayton Mitchell, Jr., Kent County Government Center, 400 High Street, Chestertown, MD. Mr. Mowell proceeded to read the public notice into the record. The public hearing is required by Title 9, Subtitle 5 of the Environmental Article of the Maryland Annotated Code to amend the comprehensive water and sewer plan. The purpose of the public hearing was to review and receive comments on a proposed amendment to the 2018 Kent County Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan for Kent County. 

Dan Mattson, Director, Public Works, provided an overview of the amendment. Mr. Mowell stated that the amendment proposes extending the County Water and Sewer Service Area to include the Everton Warehouse Project (Map 31, Parcel 6), and that it has been determined that the amendment is consistent with the Kent County Unified Development Ordinance and Comprehensive Plan. The project is located within a designated County Priority Funding Area (PFA). In addition to available water capacity, the County Commissioners and the Town of Millington executed an agreement on February 24, 2026, allocating 34 Equivalent Dwelling Units (EDUs) of existing sewer capacity to support the project. 

The proposed update to the Kent County Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan may be examined at the Department of Public Works, Water and Wastewater, 709 Morgnec Road, Chestertown, Maryland, from 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday.

# **<u>SPECIAL CLASS C LICENSE</u>** 

The Rock Hall Volunteer Fire Company (RHVFC) applied for a Multiple Event Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license for twelve events per year. The application was approved by the Commissioner during their January 6, 2026, meeting. Multiple Event License number 2026 ME-6 was issued on January 9, 2026. Since the approval and issuance of their license, the RHVFC has had an influx of events scheduled and would like to revise their license to a Twenty-four event per year Multiple Event Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license. There was no need to make them complete a whole new application; they were asked to complete the first page again to revise the request from twelve to twenty-four events per year and to provide an updated list of events. The RHVFC complied with the request and submitted the required documents, which were provided to the Commissioners for review along with their original application. They will have to pay an additional fee of $200.00 for the Twenty-four event per year, Multiple Event Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license. On a motion by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the revision to the Multiple Event Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application for the Rock Hall Volunteer Fire Company.
