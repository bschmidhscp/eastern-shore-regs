# **<u>MATTER NO. 1: LEGAL ADVICE AND LITIGATION</u>** 

Topic to be discussed: The status and strategy for pending litigation before the Appellate Court of Maryland. 

Reason for closing: The details of potential litigation should be closed to the public because discussing legal strategy and potential claims in public would harm the government's position, revealing weaknesses, negotiating tactics, and sensitive details that could prejudice the case. 

G. Robert Mowell, the County Attorney, updated the Commissioners on the status of an appeal currently before the Appellate Court of Maryland. A discussion ensued. 

At 8:07 p.m., a motion was made by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson; the Commissioners unanimously adjourned the closed session and reconvened in open session. 

# **<u>OPEN SESSION</u>** 

Upon returning to the open session, Commissioner Price summarized the matter discussed in the closed session with the County Attorney, during which legal updates and advice were provided. Regarding Matter No. 1: Legal Advice and Litigation, Commissioner Price stated that no action was taken at that time. 

At 8:08 p.m., on a motion made by Commissioner Nickerson and seconded by Commissioner Price, the Commissioners unanimously approved adjourning the meeting. The Board is scheduled to meet again on Tuesday, March 24, 2026, at 10:00 a.m.
