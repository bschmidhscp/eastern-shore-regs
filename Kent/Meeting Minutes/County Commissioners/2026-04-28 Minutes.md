# **<u><mark>JOANNE MCNAMARA, DIRECTOR, GRANTS AND CONTRACTS, AND JILL COLEMAN, DIRECTOR, PARKS AND RECREATION</mark></u>** 

<mark>Ms. McNamara and Ms. Coleman explained a planned project to enhance a Kent County Park. The Maryland Department of Transportation (MDOT) Recreational Trails Program (RTP) provides federal funding to develop and maintain recreational trails and related facilities in Maryland to enhance pedestrian, biking, and other recreational uses. The Department of Parks and Recreation, in conjunction with the Department of Public Works, seeks to submit an application for funding to develop Kent County's first Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) compliant nature trail at a County-owned and operated park. This trail would be located at the site of an existing trail in Turner's Creek Park, a 0.2-mile Overlook Trail, and the project would involve grading the trail to meet ADA-compliance requirements related to slope, meeting width requirements to allow wheelchairs and other accessibility devices, and surfacing the path with firm, stable, slip-resistant paving to allow for the use of mobility aid devices. The total project cost is $121,000.00, with a 20% cost-share contribution from the County. The departments are also planning to leverage other grant funding sources to lessen the impact on the County’s general fund. O</mark> n motion by Commissioner Price, and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously <mark>approved the submittal of a grant application to MDOT's Recreational Trails Program for funding to create an ADA-compliant trail at Turner's Creek Park.</mark>
