# June 9, 2026 

A meeting of the County Commissioners of Kent County was called to order at 10:00 a.m., with the following present: Ronald H. Fithian, President, Albert H. Nickerson, and John F. Price. The meeting was convened with the Pledge of Allegiance. Commissioner Fithian asked everyone to remain silent for a former Kent County Detention Center employee, Andy Cox, who has just been admitted to the hospital and is not doing well. 

Shelley L. Heller, County Administrator, G. Robert Mowell, County Attorney, and Rose Osborn, Administrative Specialist, Economic and Tourism Development, were also present. 

# **<u>BOB GAST, CHIEF FINANCE OFFICER, AND PAT MERRITT, CHIEF FINANCE OFFICER, OFFICE OF FINANCE</u>** 

The following resolutions were submitted for approval, allowing for the adoption of Kent County’s Fiscal Year 2027 Budget: 

1. <u>Resolution 2026-10 Establishing the Kent County Levy Tax: Taxes are levied upon the</u> assessable property in Kent County, Maryland, for the Fiscal year July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2027.  These taxes exclude any taxes levied by the State and Municipalities.  Real Property is $1.022 on each One Hundred Dollars ($100.00) of real property subject to taxation for County purposes.  Public Utilities is $2.555 on each One Hundred Dollars ($100.00) of property subject to taxation for County purposes.  Business Personal Property Taxes on Public Utilities are $2.555 on each One Hundred Dollars ($100.00) of real property subject to taxation for County purposes in accordance with Chapter 152, Taxation, Article IX, Business Personal Property Taxes on Public Utilities of the Code of Public Laws of Kent County, Maryland. On a motion made by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously adopted resolution 2026-10, Establishing the Kent County Levy Tax. 

2. <u>Resolution 2026-11 County Income Tax: The county income tax of 3.30 percent of an</u> individual’s Maryland taxable income is levied upon the residents of Kent County for the calendar year beginning January 1, 2027. On a motion made by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously adopted Resolution 2026-11, County Income Tax. 

3. <u>Resolution 2026-12 Establishing Water and Sewer Service Rates with Exhibit A: The water</u> and sewer rates increased by 4%. On a motion made by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously adopted Resolution 2026-12, Establishing Water and Sewer Rates for FY2027. 

4. <u>Resolution 2026-13 Adoption of the FY2027 Budget, General Fund with Exhibit A: The</u> Fiscal Year 2027 General Fund Budget is $80,923,944.00. On a motion made by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously adopted resolution 2026-13, Adoption of the Kent County FY2027 Budget, General Fund.

If approved by the Commissioners, the letter will be sent to the Maryland Stadium Authority (MSA) and the Interagency Commission on School Construction (IAC). The goal is to allow the Board of Education (BOE) to move forward with bidding for the Middle School project, with the aim of obtaining concrete construction figures for the Commissioners to consider for their budget, likely making a final decision in July. It is important to note that this is not a commitment or approval of the project moving forward.
