# **<u><mark>CHEEMOANDIA BLAKE, ELECTION DIRECTOR, KENT COUNTY BOARD OF ELECTIONS</mark></u>** 

<mark>Ms. Blake explained that there is a statewide pollbook modernization project. Pat Merritt, Chief Finance Officer, Office of Finance, joined Ms. Blake for the discussion. Each Local Board of Elections (LBE) must select a payment method. Kent County would need 55 pollbooks. The total cost is $127,325.00 for 55 pollbooks at $2,315.00 each. The state cost share is $63,662.50, and the local cost share is $63,662.50 (cost share equals $1,157.50 per pollbook). The Commissioners were provided four payment options: Option 1, to remit payment to the State Board of Elections over three fiscal years beginning in FY2027; Option 2, to finance the cost through the State Treasurer’s Office over five fiscal years, noting that interest rates and administrative fees may change and apply; Option 3, to pay the full amount in FY2027; and Option 4, to pay the full amount which can be absorbed within the current budget. Discussion took place. O</mark> n a motion by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved <mark>reallocating existing Board of Election funds for this current year, 2026, to cover this expense, as discussed.</mark>

# **<u>SHELLEY L. HELLER, COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR</u>**

# **<u>SHELLEY L. HELLER, COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR REPORT</u>** 

<mark>Ms. Heller stated that Mr. Mattson was in attendance to report on an emergency procurement for the Water and Wastewater Division. Mr. Mattson explained that a planned purchase was recently added to the division’s capital budget; however, an inspection conducted of the Tolchester Pump Station #2 identified an emergent condition, necessitating reclassification from a standard to an emergency purchase in the amount of $77,290.00. The purchase remains within the approved capital budget. A memorandum and purchase order request related to this emergency purchase have been submitted for reference. The final repair to the pump station should be completed within the next 3 to 4 months.</mark>
