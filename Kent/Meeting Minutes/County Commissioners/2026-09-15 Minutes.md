# **<u>DAN MATTSON, DIRECTOR, PUBLIC WORKS</u>** 

<u>Local Government Energy Modernization Grant - Energy Planning & Feasibility Study Proposal Award: Kent County received a Local Government Energy Modernization (L-GEM) Grant</u> to support energy-efficiency and renewable-energy planning and improvements at County facilities. Ameresco, Inc. assisted the County with development of the successful grant application. Following the grant award, County staff identified the need for an integrated planning study to guide investment of the grant funds, and requested a scope and fee proposal from Ameresco. The County-developed scope includes: 

- Assessment of eight County buildings to identify and prioritize actionable energy-efficiency, operational and capital improvements 

- Development of planning-level costs, anticipated energy savings, return on investment and implementation priorities 

- Solar feasibility assessment at the Nicholson Landfill 

- Floating-solar feasibility assessment at the Worton-Butlertown Wastewater Treatment Plant lagoons 

- Rooftop-solar feasibility assessments at the Detention Center and Emergency Management Building

<u><mark>Department of Natural Resources Waterway Improvement Fund Grant Application - Rock Hall Dredging Project: Kent County's Director of Public Works has been working collaboratively</mark></u> <mark>with the Town of Rock Hall, in concert with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) to help move forward a project that will be implemented by USACE for Rock Hall Harbor dredging maintenance. While the actual dredging of the channel will be completed by USACE, the local jurisdiction is responsible for the placement of the dredge spoils. USACE has delayed initiation of the dredging project from its original target initiation date to occur in Federal FY26 to an initiation date targeted in Federal FY27; with a dredging project window from approximately October 2027April 2028. To ensure that the County is prepared to place the dredging materials and to ensure that the project is facilitated without delay, the County has already entered into a contract with David A. Bramble, Inc. in July 2026 to prepare a dredged material placement site at the Orem Pit, including to provide construction of the necessary containment cell to be ready for acceptance of dredged material. Department of Public Works now seeks to apply to Maryland Department of Natural Resource's Waterway Improvement Fund grant opportunity for funding to support the cost of tipping fees related to the placement of dredged material. Receiving this funding award will ensure that funds are in place to cover costs anticipated to be incurred next year. On motion by Commissioner Price, and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the proposed grant application as presented.</mark>

<u><mark>Lindsey McCullough, Justice Reinvestment Coordinator-Local Government Justice Reinvestment Commission Board Recommendation: The Justice Reinvestment Act (JRA)</mark></u> <mark>established the Local Government Justice Reinvestment Commission to advise the Justice Reinvestment Oversight Board on local impacts of the Act. The Commission is charged with making recommendations on certain grants to local governments and creating performance measures to assess their effectiveness. The Commission consists of one representative from each jurisdiction, appointed by the local governing body. Most counties have recommended either someone from the Sheriff’s Office, their detention center, or their State’s Attorney. The Commissioners will send a letter of recommendation. # **<u><mark>SHELLEY HELLER, COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR REPORT</mark></u>** 

<u>Maryland Public Service Commission-Proposed Solar Project in Kent County:</u> Residents and other interested members of the public are invited to comment on a proposal by applicant Halo Rock Hall LLC to build a 5.0 MW solar photovoltaic generating facility in Kent County. The hearing will take place on Monday, September 21, at 6:30 p.m. at the Rock Hall Volunteer Fire Company, 21500 Rock Hall Avenue, in Rock Hall, MD. The Maryland Public Service Commission has delegated this case to its Public Utility Law Judge Division; Chief Public Utility Law Judge Kristin Case Lawrence will preside over the hearing. In May 2025, the developer applied to the Commission for what is known as a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity (CPCN), which grants an applicant the authority to construct an energy generating station in Maryland. According to the application, the developer proposes to build the project on approximately 17 acres of a 41.1-acre property located on Edesville Road, in Rock Hall, Maryland. In addition to taking comments from the public, the hearing will include a brief presentation by the applicant, brief statements by the Power Plant Research Program of the Maryland Department of Natural Resources, and the Commission’s Technical Staff, as to their respective roles and recommendations in the case. The hearing will also include a virtual option for those unable to attend in person. Attendees can register (by September 17, 2026) from the ‘File a Public Comment’ page of the Commission’s website, https://psc.maryland.gov/online-services/file-a-public-comment/:
