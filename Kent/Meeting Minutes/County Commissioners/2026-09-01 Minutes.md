# **<u>G. ROBERT MOWELL, COUNTY ATTORNEY</u>** 

<u>Code Home Rule 4-2026 Purchasing: Chapter 49 of the Public Local Laws of Kent County governs</u> County purchasing.  Joanne McNamara, Director of Grants and Contracts, conducted a comprehensive review of our existing purchasing laws and created completely new legislation with input from all other departments.  The proposed laws are designed to modernize outdated procurement systems to maximize taxpayer-dollar efficiency, increase transparency, and accelerate project delivery. Joanne McNamara provided an overview and history of the legislation. Mr. Mowell provided an overview of the legislative bill. On motion by Commissioner Price, and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved to introduce Code Home Rule Bill 4-2026 and for the Bill to be scheduled for Public Hearing. 

# **<u>JAMIE WILLIAMS, DIRECTOR, ECONOMIC AND TOURISM DEVELOPMENT</u>** 

<u>2027 Economic and Tourism Development Strategic Plan: Recommendation for Selection of Consultant: During the FY2027 Economic and Tourism Development budget process, $30,000 was</u> approved to develop the 2027 Economic & Tourism Development Strategic Plan. The County Commissioners adopted the 2022 Economic and Tourism Development Strategic Plan on February 8, 2022, by resolution. A Request for Proposals was prepared with support from the Director of Grants and Procurement to identify a qualified consulting partner. Thirteen proposals were received and evaluated by a four-person review team. After thorough scoring and discussion, the review team selected the highest‑scoring proposal and recommends that the Commissioners approve the recommended consultant. The recommended vendor, PPR Strategies, is a Frederick, Maryland– based consulting firm specializing in economic development, tourism strategy, stakeholder engagement, and place‑based marketing. With more than two decades of experience working with local governments, tourism offices, and economic development agencies across Maryland and the Mid‑Atlantic, PPR Strategies brings strong familiarity with Maryland’s economic landscape and the unique character of the Eastern Shore, key strengths for this project. While the County received several highly qualified submissions, PPR Strategies earned the highest overall score based on their

# **<u>DAN MATTSON, DIRECTOR, PUBLIC WORKS</u>** 

<u>Recommendation of Award for RFP #PW 26-12 Janitorial Services for County Facilities:</u> The Department of Public Works issued RFP #PW 26-12, Janitorial Services for County Facilities, on April 20, 2026. Thirteen (13) proposals were received and evaluated against the RFP's official Basis of Award: Price (40%), Experience & Qualifications (25%), Staffing Plan & Supervision (15%), Quality Control Plan (10%), and References & Past Performance (10%). Staff recommends award to Associated Building Maintenance Co., Inc. (Crofton, MD), based on a combination of the lowest responsive price and the strongest overall qualifications and reference checks among the vendors evaluated. The current vendor, Sentral Services, LLC, has continued service on a month-tomonth basis at current FY26 rates pending completion of this process. Formal notice of termination has been issued, with services concluding September 30, 2026. The new contract is anticipated to begin October 1, 2026. Proposed contract includes all County standard General Terms & Conditions. Carrie Klein was present on behalf of Dan Mattson; some discussion took place. On motion by Commissioner Price, and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously award the contract for RFP #PW 26-12, Janitorial Services for County Facilities, to Associated Building Maintenance Co., Inc., with the Director of Public Works authorized to execute any subsequent contract renewals on behalf of the County.
