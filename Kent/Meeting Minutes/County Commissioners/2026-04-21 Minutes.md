# **<u><mark>SHELLEY L. HELLER, COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR</mark></u>** 

<u><mark>FY2027 Budget Request for Reimbursement of Continued Test Well Monitoring in Chestertown: In the Special Work Session held on April 14, 2026, the County and the Town of</mark></u> <mark>Chestertown discussed continuing to monitor test wells near the site of the hospital oil spill of 1987. Previously, the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) had required the hospital to test numerous sentinel monitoring wells. A sentinel monitoring well is a specialized groundwater well located between known contamination sources (the oil spill) and drinking water supplies to detect pollutant migration. MDE's requirements have ended, but the Town would like to continue monitoring for pollutants. The cost for FY2027 is approximately $20,000.00 to test seven wells on a quarterly basis. The Commissioners tentatively discussed paying half the cost to ensure that the drinking water wells are not drawing contaminated water. Meghan Efland, Mayor, Town of Chestertown, joined the conversation and provided additional comments. O</mark> n a motion by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners, by unanimous vote, approved, including $10,000.00 <mark>in the FY2027 budget to help the Town of Chestertown pay for the quarterly monitoring of the wells and events.</mark>
