<mark>Ms. Merritt and Ashley Albia, Treasury Accountant, gave an overview of House Bill (HB) 0059 Tax Sale Criteria. In April of 2025, HB0059 was approved by the Governor, and its changes were incorporated into the Tax - Property Article, section 14-811. This bill mandated changes to tax sale criteria and allowed the County discretion to establish additional criteria. Additionally, this bill requires the County to establish a registry for interested parties or to designate the Tax Sale Ombudsman to withhold property from tax sale. Staff provided a schedule comparing the previous criteria for withholding properties from tax sale with the criteria established in HB0059. Ms. Albia requested the Board to approve staff recommendations for withholding limits as follows:</mark> 

1. <mark>Residential (owner-occupied) less than $1,000.00</mark> 

2. <mark>Residential (occupied heir of deceased owner) less than $1,000.00</mark> 

3. <mark>Residential (non-owner-occupied) less than $250.00</mark> 

4. <mark>Non-Residential less than $250.00</mark> 

<mark>These limits would allow for more timely collection of delinquent County and municipal water and sewer accounts. Discussion took place. On a motion made by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the recommended criteria for withholding properties from tax sale indicated on the schedule provided.</mark>

<mark>On behalf of the County Commissioners of Kent County, on January 30, 2026, Ms. Heller submitted letters to The Honorable Cory V. McCray, The Honorable Guy Guzzone, Chair, and Members of the Budget and Taxation Committee expressing strong support for Senate Bill (SB) 0288. The bill, with sponsor amendments, prevents a significant reduction in Highway User Revenue in FY2028. Kent County emphasized that these funds are critical to maintaining local roads and bridges, ensuring traffic safety, and supporting storm response. As a rural jurisdiction without independent authority to raise transportation revenue, Kent County relies on predictable funding for multi-year projects and to avoid costly emergency repairs. The letters urged a favorable report on SB0288 to preserve funding stability and advocate for long-term restoration of Highway User Revenues. On February 6, 2026, a similar letter was sent to the Honorable Mark Eldelson and to the Members of the Appropriations Committee and the Environment and Transportation Committee regarding HB0559.</mark>

# **<u>SPECIAL CLASS C LICENSE</u>** 

Kent Cultural Alliance submitted a Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application to sell beer and wine for a concert, For the Love of Artists: Sue Matthews in Concert, on February 14, 2026, from 6:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. at 211 High Street, Chestertown. On a motion by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application for Kent Cultural Alliance. 

The Betterton-Still Pond Lions Club submitted a Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application to sell beer and wine for a Fundraiser on March 14, 2026, from 5:30 p.m. to 10:30 p.m. at Betterton Fire Hall. On a motion by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application for the Betterton -Still Pond Lions Club. 

The Dogwood Festival of Galena submitted a Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application to sell beer only for a Festival on May 9, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. at 105 Sycamore Lane, Galena. On a motion by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application for the Dogwood Festival of Galena. 

Each application was provided for review. No comments were received either in support of or in opposition to any of the applications presented for approval. 

At 11:26 a.m., a motion was made by Commissioner Nickerson, seconded by Commissioner Price, and the Commissioners unanimously approved adjourning the meeting. The Board is scheduled to meet again on Tuesday, February 24, 2026, at 10:00 a.m.
