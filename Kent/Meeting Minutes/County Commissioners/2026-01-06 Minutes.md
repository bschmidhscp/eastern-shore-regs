# **<u>HUMAN RESOURCES</u>** 

Michael Sutton, Director of Human Resources, advised that the <mark>Disciplinary Action policy has not been revised since being published in 2017. Human Resources (HR) recommends that the policy be retitled "Progressive Corrective Action." The revised policy modernizes Kent County’s approach by shifting from a disciplinary model to a corrective, improvement-focused framework. It introduces clearer criteria, formal HR involvement, improved documentation practices, and a more consistent timeframe for evaluating repeat issues while preserving the County’s discretion to act decisively in serious situations. On a motion made by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously adopted the amended Progressive Corrective Action policy by Resolution 2026-1.</mark> 

<mark>Mr. Sutton requested approval to offer a conditional offer of employment to the recommended candidate to fill the Systems of Care Coordinator vacancy in the Local Management Board. # **<u>SPECIAL CLASS C LICENSES</u>** 

The applications noted below were reviewed and met the requirements to be approved for a multiple-event license. Part of those requirements includes providing a list of events scheduled to take place throughout the year. This practice helps track the number of permitted events allowed throughout the year. Both applicants provided a list of events planned for the year so far and will provide an updated list as additional events and dates are confirmed. Bonnie S. Pearsall, Inspector, Kent Alcohol and Tobacco Enforcement (KATE), will schedule visits to educate each fire department about their responsibilities and the requirements for obtaining a Multiple Event License. Staff recommended approval. 

Galena Volunteer Fire Company, Inc. (GVFC) has applied for a Beer, Wine, and Liquor Special Class C Multiple Event License to cover twelve (12) events for the 2026 calendar year. There were no objections.
