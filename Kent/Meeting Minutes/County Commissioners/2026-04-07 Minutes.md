# **<u>REDESIGNATION OF CHESTERTOWN ENTERPRISE ZONE APPLICATION</u>** 

Mr. Mowell read the protocols for the public hearing and the public hearing notice into the record. The public hearing was duly advertised. The rules for a public hearing were in effect. Two sign-in sheets were made available to the public: an attendance sheet and a testimony log. 

The public hearing commenced at 6:18 p.m. in the County Commissioners’ Hearing Room, R. Clayton Mitchell, Jr., Kent County Government Center, 400 High Street, Chestertown, MD.  A public hearing to discuss a Chestertown Enterprise Zone Redesignation Application. 

Mr. Mowell gave an overview of the Enterprise Zone (EZ) Designation. The EZ program provides real property tax credits for businesses and state income tax credits for employees located in a Maryland Enterprise Zone in return for job creation and capital investments. The Maryland Department of Commerce provided the data needed for the redesignation of the Chestertown Enterprise Zone (EZ). Maryland EZs must be redesignated every 10 years. Chestertown remains eligible. Ms. Abbott is updating the 2016 application, which is due April 15, 2026. As part of the process, a joint public hearing with the Town is required. Eligibility is determined by the poverty rate for Census Tract 9503, which currently stands at 13.6% (2026). For comparison, it was 20.3% in 2016, 5.1% in 2021, and 5.9% in 2022, an example of the significant fluctuations and inconsistencies often seen in Census data for Kent County. 

The Town of Chestertown was required to hold a public hearing regarding the Redesignation Chestertown Enterprise Zone Application. Meghan E. Efland, Mayor, Town of Chestertown, provided an overview of the public hearing held on April 6, 2026, by the Town of Chestertown. Jamie Williams, Director of Economic and Tourism Development, provided additional background information. 

On a motion by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved to conclude the public hearing at 6:22 p.m.
