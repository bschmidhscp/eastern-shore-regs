<u><mark>Road Division: Mr. Mattson discussed the Contract Change Order with Beltway</mark></u> <mark>International Trucks LLC due to an increase in added tariffs of $7,000.00. <u><mark>Water and Wastewater Division: Mr. Mattson discussed an A</mark></u> greement Between Kent County and the Town of Millington – Sewer Allocations. This item was previously discussed at the September 30, 2025, County Commissioners meeting, and the provided staff report summary remains valid. Execution of the Agreement was delayed due to Millington’s process of securing a Town Attorney, followed by additional delays because the selected attorney had previously served as the County’s interim attorney and reviewed the Agreement on behalf of Kent County. The review is now complete, and the Town of Millington has approved the Agreement. Changes since the prior presentation are minimal and primarily involve wording adjustments (e.g., “intermunicipal” changed to “interjurisdictional,” addition of “in good faith” where appropriate). The Agreement will serve, among other purposes, as a de facto reservation of EDUs for the Everton Warehouse project, Millington Senior Village, and Elementary School Redevelopment. <u>Water and Wastewater Division: Mr. Mattson informed the Commissioners that</u> $2,971,500.00 had been allocated for twelve water and wastewater capital improvement projects. While several projects have been completed under budget, others are delayed by design requirements, permitting, and equipment procurement. To meet project completion timelines, the department requests approval to reallocate capital funds to complete the remaining projects, expand the scope of select FY2026 projects, and fund projects in the capital improvement plan that could not be completed with FY2025 funds. A reallocation summary and supporting spreadsheet were submitted for the Commissioners’ review. With four months left in the FY2026 budget year, the department seeks authorization to make further adjustments to these projects as needed. Discussion took place. On a motion by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the reallocation of FY2026 funding for water and wastewater capital projects as requested and authorized the Public Works Director to further reallocate these funds as needed for the designated projects to meet contractual requirements. 

Mr. Mattson briefed the Commissioners on departmental operations and projects.

The Board of License Commissioners met today with the following present: Ronald H. Fithian, presiding, Albert H. Nickerson, and John F. Price.

# **<u>CONSENT ITEM</u>** 

On a motion made by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved Consent Item #2 - Liquor Minutes, February 10, 2026. 

# **<u>SPECIAL CLASS C LICENSE</u>** 

The Timothy Kern Memorial Fund submitted a Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application to sell beer only for a sporting clays event fundraiser on March 28, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at Hopkins Hunting Sporting Clays, 13003 Turners Creek Road, Kennedyville. The application was reviewed. No comments were received either in support of or in opposition to the application. On a motion by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application for Timothy Kern Memorial Fund. 

# **<u>PUBLIC HEARING PROTOCOL</u>** 

G. Robert Mowell, County Attorney, affirmed Commissioner Fithian’s suggestion in waiving the reading of the public hearing protocols. The rules for a public hearing were in effect. 

# **<u>TRACKSIDE BAR & GRILL, LLC, CLASS B, BEER, WINE, AND LIQUOR LICENSE</u>** 

The hearing commenced at 10:14 a.m. The public notice was provided for the record. Notice, an application has been made by Member Javier Colon Algarin, representing Trackside Bar & Grill, LLC, for a Class B, Beer, Wine, and Liquor License on the premises known as Trackside Bar & Grill, which shall authorize the holder thereof to keep for sale and to sell Beer, Wine, and Liquor at the place therein described, for on-premises consumption, or elsewhere. The hearing was duly advertised. 

Mr. Mowell administered the Oath to Bonnie S. Pearsall, Inspector, Kent Alcohol and Tobacco Enforcement (KATE), and anyone else wishing to testify. The applicant was not present. 

A legal review of the application for a Class B, Beer, Wine, and Liquor License was completed. Inspector Pearsall informed the Commissioners that the applicant had met all the requirements for the application to be heard, and a pre-hearing inspection had been completed. Staff recommended approval of the application with a contingency noted. No comments were offered in favor of or in opposition to the application. There being no objections, on a motion by Commissioner Price, seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the Class B, Beer, Wine, and Liquor License for Trackside Bar & Grill, LLC, contingent upon the receipt of a corrected Sales and Use Tax License. The public hearing concluded at 10:07 a.m.
