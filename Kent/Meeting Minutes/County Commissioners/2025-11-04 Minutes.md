# County Commissioners minutes 2025-11-04

November 4, 2025 

A meeting of the County Commissioners of Kent County was called to order at 6:00 p.m., with Ronald H. Fithian presiding, Albert H. Nickerson, and John F. Price. The meeting was convened with the Pledge of Allegiance. Commissioner Fithian asked everyone to observe a moment of silence for no particular reason and to keep everyone in their prayers across the country. 

Shelley L. Heller, County Administrator, and G. Robert Mowell, County Attorney, were also present. 

# **<u>CONSENT ITEMS</u>** 

On a motion by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved five consent items as presented. 

1. Regular Session Minutes, October 28, 2025 2. Liquor Minutes, October 28, 2025 3. Closed Session Minutes, October 28, 2025 

4. Rosemary Ramsey Granillo, Director of Kent County Local Management Board – Contract Renewal with the Queen Anne’s County Department of Health for the Healthy Families Mid-Shore Home Visiting Program for $147,626.00 

5. Ms. Granillo, FY2026 Contract with Findlay House Global, LLC, Beyond Your Mental Program for $82,000.00 

# **<u>KENT COUNTY DETENTION CENTER</u>** 

<mark>At the October 21, 2025, Commissioners meeting, a discussion took place regarding the future of the Kent County Detention Center (KCDC) and the need to create a work session or an agenda item to permit the detention center employees to attend and receive updates and accurate information regarding plans for the facility, incarcerated individuals, and employees. Previously, on August 19, 2025, the Board voted, by a majority, to continue implementing measures to reduce the number of incarcerated persons housed at KCDC and other cost-saving measures.</mark> 

<mark>Each Commissioner offered the pros and cons of current operations and future plans. A lengthy discussion occurred. In attendance from KCDC were Director of Operations Corey Sheppard; Captain Steve Wallace; Sergeant Chuck Dobson; Sergeant Kermit Burrell; Correctional Officer (CO) Clarence Johnson; CO Angela Norfleet; Corporal Eric Pinkett;</mark> and C <mark>O/Administrative Assistant M</mark> onaca Stickland <mark>. Pat Merritt, Chief Finance Officer, Office of Finance, was also present for the discussion. Due to unforeseen circumstances, personnel from the Kent County Sheriff’s Office were unable to attend the meeting. Attendees were invited to ask and answer questions about the operations at KCDC. Kevin Stickland, a resident of Kent County, also offered information relevant to the discussion. Ms. Merritt provided estimated costs; however, more information and further review of expenses are needed to advance the discussion.</mark>

November 4, 2025 

The Board of License Commissioners met today with the following present: Ronald H. Fithian, presiding, Albert H. Nickerson, and John F. Price. 

Shelley L. Heller, County Administrator, G. Robert Mowell, County Attorney, and Bonnie S. Pearsall, Inspector, Kent Alcohol and Tobacco Enforcement, were also present. 

# **<u>CONSENT ITEM</u>** 

On a motion made by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved Consent Item #2 - Liquor Minutes, October 28, 2025. 

# **<u>SPECIAL CLASS C LICENSE</u>** 

The Maryland Ducks Unlimited submitted a Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application to sell beer only for a Sporting Clay Fundraiser on November 7, 2025, from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at Hopkins Hunting and Sporting Clays, 13003 Turners Creek Road, Kennedyville. The application was provided for review. No comments were received either in support of or in opposition to the application. On a motion by Commissioner Price and seconded by Commissioner Nickerson, the Commissioners unanimously approved the Special Class C (Per Diem) liquor license application for Maryland Ducks Unlimited. 

# **<u>PUBLIC HEARING PROTOCOL</u>** 

Commissioner Fithian waived the reading of the public hearing protocols. The rules for a public hearing were in effect. 

# **<u>HONK HONK, LLC TRADING AS BETTY’S BAR, CLASS D, BEER, WINE, AND LIQUOR LICENSE</u>** 

The hearing commenced at 6:04 p.m. G. Robert Mowell, County Attorney, read the public notice into the record. Notice, an application has been made by Members Andrew Kuechler, Emmett Duke, and Katelyn Boardman, representing Honk Honk, LLC, for a Class D, Beer, Wine, and Liquor License on the premises known as Betty’s Bar, which shall authorize the holder thereof to keep for sale and to sell Beer, Wine, and Liquor at the place therein described, for on-premises consumption, or elsewhere. The hearing was duly advertised. 

Mr. Mowell administered the Oath to Bonnie S. Pearsall, Inspector, Kent Alcohol and Tobacco Enforcement (KATE), Andrew Kuechler, Member of Honk Honk, LLC, and anyone else wishing to testify. 

Mr. Mowell stated that a legal review of the application for a Class D, Beer, Wine, and Liquor License had been completed. Inspector Pearsall informed the Commissioners that the applicants had met all the requirements for approval of the application, except for pending background check results for two members. Staff recommended approval of the application with
