# **<u>RESOLUTION 2025-18 COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT FUNDS APPLICATION AUTHORIZATION, MILLINGTON WASTEWATER CONVEYANCE SYSTEM IMPROVEMENTS</u>**

Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Program is a federally funded program designed to assist local governments with activities directed toward neighborhood revitalization, housing opportunities, economic development, and improved public services. CDBG funding is highly competitive and must meet one of the following national objectives: (1) activities that will benefit low and moderate-income (LMI) persons or households; or (2) aid in the prevention or elimination of slums or blight; or (3) meet an urgent or immediate threat to community health and welfare. 

Ms. McNamara provided pertinent information about available funding and discussed the project-specific details. Kent County Department of Public Works (DPW) seeks to apply for CDBG funding to support an infrastructure improvement project related to the wastewater conveyance system in Millington that is necessary to complete the relocation of the Millington Wastewater Treatment Plant from its current location in Town to a County parcel outside of the flood plain. The Town has been highly supportive of this transfer, as the current plant, located on Town property and operated by the Town via contractor, is subject to flood risk and has received significant damage in the past. The current plant is also aged well beyond its useful life. The project will improve the wastewater conveyance system in the Town so it can transport wastewater to the new location once it is constructed. The Town of Millington is home to 610 residents, the majority of whom are low to moderate income as defined by DHCD. A complete copy of the SYF2026 application was submitted as part of the public hearing materials. Ms. McNamara further explained that the County intends to apply for the maximum allowable amount of $800,000.00. Dan Mattson, Director, Public Works, offered a technical explanation concerning the conveyance system. 

The proposed application was discussed, and the Commissioners asked questions about the project and funding availability, and stated the County’s commitment to supporting this critical project.
