# Department of Planning, Housing, and Zoning 

## **MINUTES** 

Meeting: Kent County Board of Zoning Appeals Date: November 21, 2022 Time: 5:00 P.M. Location: County Commissioners Hearing Room, 400 High Street, Chestertown, Maryland 

|**Agenda Item**|**Sitting for the Board**|**Action Taken**|**Vote**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**22-80 Green's Septic and**<br>**Excavation, LLC – Special**<br>**Exception**<br>The applicant is requesting a<br>special exception to operate a<br>septic maintenance and<br>excavation business except for<br>the associated office uses on a<br>property zoned Village. A new<br>pole building will be constructed<br>to store equipment used for the<br>business. The property is located<br>at 10252 Fairlee Road,<br>Chestertown.<br>The Kent County Planning<br>Commission forwarded a<br>favorable recommendation to the<br>Board of Appeals in regard to this<br>application.<br>Applicant/Representative<br>Steven Green, property owner,<br>and Harry Smith Jr., LS, Delmarva<br>Survey, were sworn in and<br>presented a summary of the case.<br>Public Comment<br>No correspondence was received<br>on this application.<br>Planning Staff<br>Carla Gerber, AICP, Deputy<br>Director of Planning was sworn in.|Dr. Albert Townshend,<br>Chairman<br>Joan Horsey, Member<br>John Massey, Member<br>David Hill, Alternate Member<br>Mr. Christopher Drummond,<br>Attorney for the Board<br>Campbell Safian, Clerk|Mr. Massey made a motion to<br>approve the special exception for<br>Green's Septic and Excavation, LLC,<br>located at 10252 Fairlee Road based<br>on the testimony that was<br>presented, both in writing and orally,<br>with the one condition that the final<br>site plan approval be granted by the<br>Planning Commission. The special<br>exception will lapse after the<br>expiration of one year if no<br>substantial construction in<br>accordance with the plans herein<br>presented occurs.<br>The motion was seconded by Ms.<br>Horsey; the motion passed with all in<br>favor.|Unanimous<br>Approval|
|**MINUTES: October 17, 2022**||Dr. Townshend moved to approve<br>the minutes. Mr. Massey seconded<br>the motion; the motion passed with<br>all in favor.|Approved|
