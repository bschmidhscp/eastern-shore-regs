**MINUTES** 

Meeting: Kent County Board of Zoning Appeals Date: April 18, 2022 Time: 5:00 P.M. 

Location: County Commissioners Hearing Room, 400 High Street, Chestertown, Maryland 

|**Agenda Item**|**Sitting for the Board**|**Action Taken**|**Vote**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**#22-09 25809a Still Pond Neck,**<br>**LLC – Special Exception**<br>The applicant is requesting a<br>special exception for a utility-<br>scale solar energy system in the<br>Agricultural Zoning District (AZD)<br>on an 85-acre farm owned by<br>Raymond and Joyce Stoltzfus. The<br>property is located at 26001 Still<br>Pond Neck Road in the Third<br>Election District and is on the<br>southwest corner of the<br>intersection of Still Pond Neck<br>Road and Still Pond Road.  The<br>proposed 1 MW array of panels<br>will encompass approximately 5<br>acres on the northwestern corner<br>of the property, and the entrance<br>will be from Still Pond Neck Road.<br>The surrounding area is<br>predominantly farmland and<br>woodland.|Dr. Albert Townshend,<br>Chairman<br>Joan Horsey, Member<br>John Massey, Member<br>David Hill, Alternate Member<br>(observing)<br>Mr. Christopher Drummond,<br>Attorney for the Board<br>Michael Pelletier, Clerk|Mr. Massey moved to deny the<br>application on the ground that the<br>proposed “area of use” exceeded<br>five acres; therefore, the application<br>did not meet the requirements of<br>Section 7.57.25(j).<br>The motion to deny was seconded by<br>Ms. Horsey; the motion passed with<br>all in favor.|Unanimous<br>Denial of<br>Application|
|The Kent County Planning<br>Commission has issued a<br>favorable recommendation to the<br>Board of Appeals in regard to this<br>application.||||
|Applicant/Representative<br>Anthony Kupermisth, Esq. with<br>McCallister, Detar, Showalter and<br>Walker, appeared on behalf of the<br>applicant.<br>Mr. Kupersmith was sworn in and<br>presented a summary of his case.<br>Cory McCandless and Bruce<br>Wilson with SGC Power were<br>sworn in on behalf of the<br>applicant.||The Applicant’s Representatives<br>provided testimony and evidence in<br>support of the application.<br>Mr. Kupersmith introduced<br>Applicant’s Exhibit 1 “MHT Project<br>Review Form” and Applicant’s Exhibit<br>2 “DNR Environmental Review” to be<br>incorporated into the record.<br>Mr. Kupersmith provided a closing<br>argument in support of the<br>testimony before the Board.||
|Ted Hastings with Becker Morgan<br>Group was sworn in on behalf of<br>the applicant.||||
