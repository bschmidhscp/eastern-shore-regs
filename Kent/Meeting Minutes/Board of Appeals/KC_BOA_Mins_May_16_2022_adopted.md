|**22-22 Marci Tarrant Johnson –**<br>**Variance – Pier Length**<br>The applicant is requesting a<br>variance to remove and replace a<br>nonconforming, 95-foot pier that<br>has been determined to exceed<br>25% of the waterway. In<br>accordance with the method for<br>measurement of waterway width,<br>as specified in the Kent County<br>Land Use Ordinance (LUO), the<br>proposed replacement would<br>exceed the allowable 25% of the<br>waterway width. The LUO<br>prohibits in-kind replacement for<br>nonconforming structures that<br>are voluntarily removed by the<br>property owner.|Dr. Albert Townshend,<br>Chairman<br>Joan Horsey, Member<br>John Massey, Member<br>Mr. Christopher Drummond,<br>Attorney for the Board<br>David Hill, Member (observing)<br>Carla Gerber, Acting Clerk|Ms. Horsey moved to approve the<br>application of Marci Tarrant Johnson<br>requesting a variance to remove and<br>replace the nonconforming 95-foot<br>pier that has been determined to<br>exceed 25% of the waterway located<br>at 22059 Harrington Park Road in<br>Rock Hall; the variance will lapse<br>after one year, if no substantial<br>construction is undertaken in<br>accordance with the plans<br>presented.<br>The motion was seconded by Mr.<br>Massey; the motion passed with all<br>in favor.|Unanimous<br>approval|

|**General Discussion**<br>Per the previous request by the<br>Board of Zoning Appeals, the<br>Department of Planning, Housing,<br>and Zoning is preparing an<br>application for a zoning text<br>amendment to clarify “area of<br>use” for utility-scale solar energy<br>systems in the Agricultural Zoning<br>District (AZD) and Resource<br>Conservation District(RCD).||The Board was in favor of language<br>that explicitly limits the area of use<br>to those components of the energy<br>system that are within the<br>boundaries of the required perimeter<br>security fence, all of which could not<br>exceed 5 acres.||
