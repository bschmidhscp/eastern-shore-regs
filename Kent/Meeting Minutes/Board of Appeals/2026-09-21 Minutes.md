# **APPLICATIONS FOR REVIEW** 

_26-44: Thomas Parks — Special Exception — Cottage Industry — Automobile and Marine Repair 12901 Irelands Corner Road, Galena_ 

**Public Notice Information:** The Clerk read into the record that public notice for the application was published in the Kent County News on September 10, 2026. Letters were sent to adjacent property owners on August 11, 2026, and the property was posted on August 19, 2026; no correspondence was received. The Chair reviewed the hearing procedure for the record. 

**Applicant Testimony:** Thomas Parks testified that he worked for a Chevrolet dealership for 31 years and is looking to retire and slow down, and that restoring automobiles is his hobby. He testified that he is requesting a special exception to operate a cottage-industry automobile and marine repair business, involving long-term restoration projects rather than day-to-day, high-turnover mechanical work. He testified that the business would be located in an existing accessory structure of approximately 2,000 square feet, representing about 60% of the size of the living area, that the use would be secondary to his residential use of the property, and that he lives in the house and would work only in the shop. He testified that the structure is set back off the road and screened by trees on approximately 90% of the site, that no outdoor storage of materials, vehicles, or tires is planned, and that all work would be performed indoors, with no on-site painting. He testified that he does not currently plan to have employees but might have one in the future, and confirmed the shop has capacity for a maximum of about two vehicles at a time. In response to Board questions, he agreed that a condition prohibiting outdoor storage would be acceptable. A Board member noted that because a special exception runs

**Staff Report:** Paul Keen, Associate Planner, testified that the Kent County Planning Commission reviewed the application at its September 3, 2026, meeting and forwarded a favorable recommendation as documented in a September 15, 2026, letter and a September 3, 2026, staff report. He testified that no correspondence was received and that there were no objections raised at the Planning Commission meeting. Staff confirmed there are no additional recommended conditions beyond no outdoor storage of materials/vehicles and the applicant's proposed hours of operation and noted that the applicant's materials also address indoor storage of waste fluids. 

**Board Attorney:** Karen Ruff, Esq., advised the Board that the applicant's stated business model and statement of intent — including hours of operation, no outdoor storage, and indoor storage of waste fluids — should be incorporated as part of any approval, since that is the operation being approved and any subsequent change would require amendment. 

**Applicant Rebuttal:** Mr. Parks added that automobile restoration has been his personal hobby as well, that he has restored three cars over the past ten years on weekends without any issues with neighbors, and that he does not intend to leave the property. 

**Board Discussion:** Board members indicated they did not see a problem with the application providing that operations adhere to the applicant's stated use and intent, business model, the Planning Commission's recommendation, and staff recommendation. 

**Motion:** Mr. Davis moved to approve special exception, Case Number 26-44, to Thomas Parks for Cottage Industry — Automobile and Marine Repair, at 12901 Irelands Corner Road, Galena, Maryland, Map 7, Parcel 48, First Election District, based on the evidence in the record, with the Board of Appeals adopting the findings of fact report dated September 3, 2026, and the Planning Commission recommendation letter dated September 15, 2026, with the following conditions: no outdoor storage; hours of operation 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday; and no storage of waste fluids outside. Mr. Hill seconded the motion, and the motion passed unanimously.
