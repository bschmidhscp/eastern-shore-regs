# **MINUTES** 

Meeting: Kent County Board of Zoning Appeals Date: March 13, 2023 Time: 5:00 P.M. Location: County Commissioners Hearing Room, 400 High Street, Chestertown, Maryland 

|**Agenda Item**|**Sitting for the Board**|**Action Taken**|**Vote**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**23-07 Darrell and Carla Morgan –**<br>**Variance – Critical Area Clearing**<br>The applicants are requesting a<br>variance to clear in excess of the<br>30% maximum allowance for<br>clearing in the Critical Area. The<br>applicants propose to clear<br>12,845 square feet (69%) of an<br>18,691 square foot, fully wooded<br>parcel to accommodate the<br>installation of a detached single-<br>family home, a driveway, and a<br>septic system.  The property is<br>identified as Map 27, Parcel 481<br>on Clarissa Road.<br>The Kent County Planning<br>Commission forwarded a<br>favorable recommendation to the<br>Board of Appeals in regard to this<br>application.|Dr. Albert Townshend,<br>Chairman<br>David Hill, Member<br>John Massey, Member<br>Mr. Christopher Drummond,<br>Attorney for the Board<br>Beth Grieb, Clerk|Mr. Massey made a motion to grant<br>a critical area clearing variance to<br>Darrell and Carla Morgan located on<br>Clarissa Road in the Chesapeake<br>Landing Subdivision to allow up to<br>69% of the parcel to be cleared. The<br>approval is contingent upon the<br>following conditions: An approved<br>Critical Area Forest Clearing plan to<br>mitigate at a ratio of 3:1 for the area<br>cleared.  Fee in lieu of planting is<br>acceptable.  The variance will lapse<br>after the expiration of one year if no<br>substantial construction in<br>accordance with the plans herein<br>presented occurs. The motion is<br>made on the findings of facts both<br>oral and written.<br>The motion was seconded by Mr.<br>Hill; the motion passed with all in<br>favor.|Unanimous<br>Approval|
|Applicant/Representative<br>Buck Nickerson, LS, Extreme<br>Measures, LLC, was sworn in and<br>presented a summary of the case.||||
|Public Comment<br>Correspondence was received on<br>February 14, 2023, from Tay<br>Harris, Natural Resources Planner,<br>of the Maryland Critical Area<br>Commission. The CAC concurred<br>with Staff’s recommendation.||||
|Planning Staff<br>Mark Carper, LEED Green<br>Associate, Associate Planner, was<br>sworn in and recommends<br>approval with conditions.||||

|**23-09 Scott and Shari Smith –**<br>**Variance – Side Yard Setback**<br>The applicants are requesting a<br>side yard setback variance to<br>construct a residential addition.<br>The addition would connect the<br>existing detached garage to the<br>dwelling, placing the entire<br>primary structure three feet from<br>the side lot line.  The property is<br>located at 26933 Mallard Road,<br>Chestertown.<br>The Kent County Planning<br>Commission forwarded a<br>favorable recommendation to the<br>Board of Appeals for the side yard<br>setback variance of twelve feet<br>with the condition that no further<br>encroachment shall occur within<br>the side yard setback beyond the<br>proposed addition over the<br>existing concrete pad.|Dr. Albert Townshend,<br>Chairman<br>John Massey, Member<br>David Hill, Member<br>Mr. Christopher Drummond,<br>Attorney for the Board<br>Beth Grieb, Clerk|Mr. Hill made a motion to approve<br>the variance for the side yard setback<br>to connect the detached garage to<br>the existing dwelling provided that<br>no further encroachment shall occur<br>within the side yard setback beyond<br>the proposed addition over the<br>existing concrete slab. The variance<br>for Scott and Shari Smith located at<br>26933 Mallard Road will lapse after<br>the expiration of one year if no<br>substantial construction in<br>accordance with the plans herein<br>presented occurs.<br>The motion was seconded by Mr.<br>Massey; the motion passed with all<br>in favor.|Unanimous<br>Approval|
