|**#21-38 Bonnie Plants, LLC (D.**<br>**Drury) – Special Exception –**<br>**Migrant Worker Housing**<br>The applicant is requesting a<br>special exception to construct a<br>building to house migrant laborers<br>on its 20.39-acre property located<br>at 12515 Augustine Herman<br>Highway. The applicant has been<br>growing herbs and vegetable<br>plants in greenhouses for over 15<br>years and would like to house the<br>H2A guest workers it hires onsite.<br>The building will house up to 24<br>workers from February through<br>July and will meet all Department<br>of Labor requirements, per the<br>applicant’s testimony.|Dr. Albert Townshend,<br>Chairman<br>Joan Horsey, Member<br>John Massey, Member<br>Mr. Christopher Drummond,<br>Attorney for the Board<br>(remote)<br>Michael Pelletier, Clerk|Mr. Massey made a motion to<br>approve the special exception for<br>construction of migrant worker<br>housing with the following<br>conditions:<br>a) The applicant secure final site<br>plan approval from the<br>Planning Commission;<br>b) The applicant work with<br>Department staff to develop a<br>landscaped screen between<br>the dormitory and the<br>property of the neighbor to the<br>west; and<br>c) The applicant actually plant<br>the landscaped screen and<br>maintain it as required by the<br>Planning Commission.|Unanimous<br>Approval|
|The property is located 12515<br>Augustine Herman Highway in the<br>Second Election District and is<br>zoned Agricultural Zoning District<br>(AZD) and Crossroads Commercial<br>(CC).||The motion was seconded by Ms.<br>Horsey; the motion passed with all in<br>favor.||
|Applicant(s)/Representative(s):<br>David Drury, proprietor of Bonnie<br>Plants, LLC.||||
|Mr. Drury was sworn in and<br>presented his case.||||
|Planning Staff:<br>Carla Gerber, Deputy Director,<br>was sworn in.<br>No correspondence was received.||||
