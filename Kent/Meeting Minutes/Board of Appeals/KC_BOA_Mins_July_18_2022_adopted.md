|**22-27 Tucker White – Special**<br>**Exception – Oversized Accessory**<br>**Structure**<br>The applicant is requesting a<br>special exception to construct a<br>pole building with a floor area of<br>1,968 square foot and a mean<br>height of approximately 22 feet<br>on his property located at 21779<br>Sunnyside Avenue, Rock Hall. The<br>total height of the proposed<br>building is approximately 23.5<br>feet. A special exception is<br>required for accessory buildings<br>that exceed floor area of 1,200<br>square feet or 17 feet in height on<br>parcels less than 5 acres.<br>The Kent County Planning<br>Commission forwarded a<br>favorable recommendation to the<br>Board of Appeals in regard to this<br>application.|Dr. Albert Townshend,<br>Chairman<br>Joan Horsey, Member<br>John Massey, Member<br>Mr. Christopher Drummond,<br>Attorney for the Board<br>David Hill, Alternate Member<br>(observing)<br>Carla Gerber, Acting Clerk|Mr. Massey moved to approve the<br>application by Tucker White for a<br>building with a footprint of 1,248<br>square feet and a floor area of 1,968<br>square feet at 21779 Sunnyside<br>Avenue, Rock Hall. The decision is<br>based on the findings of fact in the<br>application and orally that the<br>application is consistent with the<br>Comprehensive Plan, there will be no<br>unique adverse impacts to the<br>neighborhood, and there is support<br>for the application from the<br>neighbors. This special exception will<br>lapse after one year if no substantial<br>construction is begun.<br>The motion was seconded by Ms.<br>Horsey; the motion passed with all in<br>favor.|Unanimous<br>Approval|
