# **MINUTES** 

Meeting: Kent County Board of Zoning Appeals Date: January 13, 2020 Time: 7:00 P.M. Location: County Commissioners Hearing Room, 400 High Street, Chestertown, Maryland 

|**Agenda Item/Case**|**Sitting for the Board**|**Action Taken**|**Vote**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**MINUTES: December 16, 2019**||Minutes approved as presented|Unanimous<br>Approval|
|**19-59 – Robert Fry and Judy**<br>**Gifford –**The applicants are<br>requesting a 363-foot variance from<br>the rear yard setback requirement<br>and a 227-foot variance from the<br>front yard setback requirement in<br>order to construct a 48 x 124-foot<br>waste management structure on<br>their 61.73-acre dairy farm.<br>Currently, the County’s Land Use<br>Ordinance requires waste<br>management structures to be a<br>minimum of 600-feet from all<br>parcel boundary lines.<br>The property is located at 12246<br>Locust Grove Road, Kennedyville,<br>Maryland in the Second Election<br>District and is zoned Agricultural<br>Zoning District “AZD”.<br>Applicants:<br>Dr. Robert Fry|Dr. Albert Townshend,<br>Chairperson<br>Joan Horsey, Member<br>John Massey, Member<br>David Hill, Alternate<br>Member sitting in observing<br>capacity only<br>Mr. Christopher Drummond<br>Attorney for the Board<br>Sandy Adams, Clerk|Based on the applicant’s<br>testimony and verification of<br>the following:<br>•<br>There is a practical<br>difficulty as the structure<br>cannot meet the required<br>setback requirement<br>anywhere on the property.<br>•<br>It is environmentally safe in<br>protection of the Bay.<br>•<br>The request is in<br>compliance with the<br>Comprehensive Plan.<br>•<br>The request is in<br>compliance with Best<br>Management Practices.<br>•<br>The structure will not create<br>a detriment to the adjacent<br>properties.<br>•<br>The structure will not<br>change the character of the<br>neighborhood.|Unanimous<br>Approval|
|Rob Tracey, Community Planner<br>All were sworn in.||Ms. Horsey made a motion to<br>approve the variance based on<br>the following finding(s) of fact:||
|No correspondence has been<br>received.<br>There were no protestants in the<br>audience.||• The proposed 48 x 124-foot<br>waste management structure<br>will be used for the storage<br>of manure to comply with<br>dairy farm Best Management<br>Practices and to meet the<br>requirements associated with<br>Maryland’s Nutrient<br>Management Guidelines.<br>Mr. Massey seconded the<br>motion; all were in favor.||
|**Adjourn**||At 7:18 PM, Dr. Townshend<br>asked for motion.<br>Ms. Horsey made a motion to<br>adjourn the meeting,and Mr.|Unanimous<br>Approval|
