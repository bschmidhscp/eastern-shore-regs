# Kent County: By-Right Playbook

Keys: "UDO" = Chapter 222 Unified Development Ordinance, `Code/Unified Development Ordinance/`. Density tables are in `Documents/Legislation/Bills/Article 2 Districts - Effective 2025-07-25 (REV 2025-08-15).md` (cited "Art. II"). "Fees" = `Documents/Fees/Planning Health Dept Fees - September 1 2024.md`. State rules (septic tiers, perc, Critical Area, FCA) are in `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md` and are not re-explained here. Items marked **estimate** are not in the corpus.

## 1. Bottom line

The path is the **Minor Subdivision**: up to **7 lots total** (original/remainder counts), decided by the **Planning Director** (or designee) after one Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) review, in one step, with no Planning Commission and no public hearing [UDO Art. IV §§ 1.7.1, 2.4.2]. **Some notice cannot be avoided:** after TAC review the Department mails a notice to adjacent owners (address of record, SDAT) with a 10-day comment window; no sign, no newspaper ad, no Planning Commission agenda [Art. IV § 1.6.1(A)]. Typical time to a recorded plat is about **4 months (range 3 to 9)**, an **estimate** because the corpus has no minor-subdivision timing evidence. The real constraint is density, not process: AZD allows 1 unit per 30 acres, so 3 lots needs roughly 90+ acres and 7 lots roughly 210 acres [Art. II § 2.3; Art. IV § 2.3]. In AZD only 2 lots may front an existing public road, so a 3-lot deal on existing frontage needs a waiver or a private road [Art. II § 2.3 fn 3].

## 2. Eligibility test

A parcel must pass all of these to stay on the path.

1. **Zoning and density** [Art. II]:
   - AZD: 1 per 30 ac (0.033). RCD and RC: 1 per 20 ac [§§ 3.3, 4.3]. CAR and CR: 1 per ac [§§ 5.3, 6.3]. Village: 5 per ac single-family [§ 7.3, per 00. Summary; § 1.1 says 8, use § 7.3]. Accessory dwelling units do not count in AZD or RCD.
   - Yield is run on the **original tract**, and the remainder counts as a lot [UDO Art. IV § 2.3.1, 2.3.4].
2. **Minor threshold:** 7 or fewer lots from the original lot, counting every lot recorded since **12/23/1969** and the remainder. 8 or more is a major (3-stage Planning Commission) [Art. IV §§ 2.3.1, 2.4.2, 2.4.3].
3. **Cumulative density count dates:** outside the Critical Area, lots recorded since **8/1/1989** count against density; inside the Critical Area, since **12/1/1985** [Art. IV §§ 2.3.2, 2.3.3]. Deliverable: a subdivision history back to 12/23/1969 in the narrative [Art. IV § 2.6.1(J)].
4. **Lot minimums** [Art. II]: AZD, RCD, RC: 3/4 ac on septic (1/2 ac on a community system in AZD and RCD), 75 ft minimum lot width. CAR and CR: 1/2 ac, 75 ft. No separate frontage minimum is stated in these tables (width only); confirm (Section 7).
5. **AZD only: 10% rule.** Lots may occupy at most 10% of the property; parcels of 100 acres or more are not counted toward the percentage [Art. II § 2.3 and fn 2].
6. **AZD only: road frontage cap.** Max **2 lots fronting an existing public road**; lots created before 12/3/2002 do not count toward the 2. The Planning Commission may waive [Art. II § 2.3 fn 3].
7. **No new public road.** Private roads are allowed only in minors, serve at most 7 parcels, need a 50-ft right-of-way, county road standards, and "The road is private and not the responsibility of Kent County" on the plat and in every deed and contract [Art. IV §§ 2.4.2 note, 2.7.25]. Every lot must abut a public road or approved private road [§ 2.7.20]. New public roads are for majors; avoid.
8. **Access forms:** no flag-lot or panhandle rule and no shared-driveway lot limit found in the UDO. Treat as unresolved (Section 7).
9. **Septic:** Kent has a Tier IV exemption (April 2017) so the State's minor-subdivision septic cap does not apply [01. Maryland § 2]; confirm it is still current. A **major** in Tier III needs a newspaper-advertised PC hearing [Art. IV § 1.6.3(C)]; a minor does not. Every lot needs passing perc and soils (Section 3, step 3).
10. **Critical Area:** RCD allows 1 per 20 ac, and RCD land only supports intrafamily lots as follows: parcel of record on 3/1/1986, 7 to <12 ac = 2 lots, 12 to <60 ac = 3 lots, with deed covenant and attestation; no third-party sale unless rules are met [Art. IV § 2.5]. Anything in RCD, CAR, CCA, M, ECCA, MXDCA goes to the State Critical Area Commission and the County may not approve until it replies or the waiting period ends [Art. IV § 1.4, § 2.2]. Lot coverage 15% (25% per lot for 1-acre-or-smaller lots) [Art. VI § 2.4.1, per 00. Summary]. Not by-right: growth allocation ($500 fee [Fees p.2]).
11. **Open space or fee in lieu:** 1/10 acre of recreation land per dwelling unit, or a fee equal to the assessed value of that land plus equipment, at the Director's option [Art. IV § 2.7.11]. The 40% open-space rule applies to **majors** in CR only [§ 2.7.12].
12. **No conversion triggers:** no variance, special exception, rezoning, new road, W&S plan amendment, or forest-retention alteration (needs a Board of Appeals variance) [see Section 6].

Plat notes: AZD or RCD or lots abutting a farm must carry the statement acknowledging Chapter 84 "Farming" [Art. IV § 2.6.2(J)]. Lot letters, not numbers, for resubdivisions [§ 2.4.4]. Add the private-road legend if applicable [§ 2.6.3(M)].

## 4. Public notice

- **Applicable notice for a minor:** a regular-mail notice to adjacent owners after TAC review, with a 10-day comment period. "No other notice is required." [Art. IV § 1.6.1(A)]. There is no posting of a sign (posting is for majors, special exceptions, variances [§ 1.6.2(A)]), no newspaper advertisement (hearings only [§ 1.6.3]) and no Planning Commission agenda unless the Director chooses to refer it [§ 2.4.2].
- **Online:** the Department posts agendas, minutes and packets [§ 1.6.4]; TAC agendas are therefore visible.
- **Citizen Participation Plan:** the narrative includes a CPP, with citizen participation meetings held once prior to initial submittal [Art. IV § 2.6.1(L); § 1.5.1]. Whether a minor needs the meeting is not stated; ask (Section 7).
- **Can any variant avoid the mailed notice?** None found. Lot line adjustments follow the same procedures as a minor [§ 2.4.1(C)] and a no-new-lot adjustment is not a subdivision, but it creates no lots. A plot plan exception applies to single dwellings, not subdivision [§ 1.7.3(E)].
- **Appeals:** any person may appeal the Director's decision to the Board of Appeals within 30 days; that appeal is a noticed public meeting [Art. IV § 1.15.3]. Standing is broad [§ 1.16]. Treat the 30-day window as a risk before selling lots.
- **A PC waiver** (for example the AZD 2-road-front-lot limit) is not advertised or noticed [§ 1.11] but is a Planning Commission agenda item (agenda is posted online).

## 6. Off-ramps

| Trigger | Result | How to design around |
|---|---|---|
| 8 or more lots counting the remainder and every lot since 12/23/1969 | Major: 3-stage PC, 20-day mailed notice and posted sign [Art. IV §§ 1.6.1(B), 1.6.2, 2.4.3] | Cap at 7 including remainder; confirm history |
| More lots than density allows | Cannot subdivide; needs rezoning or lot-transfer remedies not in code | Size the parcel to yield; AZD 30 ac per lot |
| 3rd+ road-front lot in AZD | Needs PC waiver [Art. II § 2.3 fn 3] or private road | Use pre-2002 lots; private road (50 ft ROW); lay out interior lots; ask staff |
| Private road serving more than 7 parcels; or an 8th parcel | Waiver; the 8th parcel must front a public road [Art. IV § 2.7.25(A)] | Keep to 7 |
| Lots over 10% of property in AZD (parcels under 100 ac) | Fails 10% rule [Art. II § 2.3] | Use larger tract or smaller lots at 3/4 ac |
| Variance (lot width, setback, buffer, forest retention) | Board of Appeals, newspaper ad, mailed notice, posting [Art. IV §§ 1.6, 1.9]; forest retention alterations require a variance [§ 6, note] | Design to standards; shift lines |
| Special exception | Same as variance | Avoid |
| Critical Area growth allocation | County action; $500 [Fees] | Avoid RCD/CAR density increases |
| Critical Area Commission objection | County cannot approve until notice or waiting period ends [§ 1.4] | File early; stay within 1 per 20 ac |
| Failed perc / no area for primary plus reserve | Lot lost | Test before contract close; option terms |
| Water/sewer plan amendment or sewer service | Separate county process; allocation fee [Fees] | Stay on septic and wells |
| New public road | Major subdivision track | Use private road or existing frontage |
| Referral to PC at Director's discretion | Public meeting, longer timeline [§ 2.4.2] | Clean application; pre-submission meeting |
| Appeal of Director decision within 30 days | Noticed Board of Appeals meeting [§ 1.15.3] | Address neighbors; record plat only after the appeal window closes (estimate of risk) |
| Plat not recorded in 6 months | Approval expires [§ 1.12.1] | Have sureties and deeds ready; extension by PC [§ 1.13.2] |
| Intrafamily (RCD) lots | Third-party sale blocked [§ 2.5] | Do not use the RCD intrafamily route for an arm's-length sale |
| Future ordinance change | A new zoning map/ordinance is planned [Art. II § 1.3(C)]; pre-UDO approvals lapse in 6 months on adoption [§ 1.12.5] | Monitor; file before changes |

## 7. Open questions to ask staff

1. **Notice and CPP:** Is the Citizen Participation Plan meeting required for a minor subdivision, and when is the 10-day adjoining-owner notice sent relative to TAC? Any practical way to shorten it?
2. **Road frontage:** In AZD, how does staff apply the 2-lot road-frontage cap on a new minor (3 to 7 lots) with only existing public frontage? Is a private road or PC waiver the usual route, and how long does the waiver take?
3. **Flag/panhandle lots and shared driveways:** Are they allowed, with what stem width, and how many lots may share a driveway?
4. **Timing:** What is the real time from complete application to signed plat for a minor, and has a minor been approved since the UDO took effect 2025-07-25?
5. **Septic and perc:** Is the Tier IV exemption still in effect? Environmental Health perc fee, lead time, and whether Health will accept winter data outside Feb to Apr.
6. **Fees and filings:** Are the 2024 fees ($90 + $50 per lot; $1,800 stormwater) still current for FY27? Soil and Water Conservation District sediment fees? Which "concluding items" (Soil and Water Conservation District signature, sureties, FCP easements) are required at the minor stage?
