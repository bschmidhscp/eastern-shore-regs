# **INSTRUCTIONS FOR CONNECTION TO THE KENT COUNTY WATER AND/OR SEWER SYSTEM** 

These instructions are issued by the **Kent County Department of Public Works (DPW) – Water and Wastewater Division** and apply to all requests for connection to the County water and/or sewer system. Compliance is mandatory. 

# **1. Application and Permits** 

The following must be submitted prior to any work: 

- Application for Water and/or Sewer Connection 

- Plumber’s Permit 

Each connection requires a separate application and a non-refundable $50.00 fee. No work may begin until all required documentation and fees are approved. 

# **2. Site Visit and Materials** 

Applicants must schedule a pre-construction site visit by calling **410-778-3287** with at least **48 hours’ notice** . The site visit will determine permit requirements, connection locations, and required materials. 

# **County Responsibilities** 

- Water meter 

- Grinder pump pit, control panel, cable, and grinder pump (if required) 

# **Property Owner Responsibilities** 

- All pipes, fittings, valves, meter pit, and related connection materials 

# **3. Utility Locates** 

Prior to excavation, contact **Miss Utility (800-441-8355)** at least **48 hours in advance** . All markings must be observed. 

# **4. Connection Inspections** 

Inspections must be scheduled at least **48 hours in advance** by calling **410-778-3287** . 

- Inspections are **not conducted on Mondays or Fridays** 

- Lines and trenches must remain open until inspected 

Any trench backfilled prior to inspection must be re-excavated at the owner’s expense.

<!-- page 2 -->
# **5. Grinder Pump and Meter Installation** 

Following approval of inspections: 

- Water meters and grinder pumps are installed by Kent County DPW 

- **48 hours’ notice** is required 

# Additional requirements: 

- An adult (18+) must be on site for meter installation 

- Grinder pumps require electrical service and running water **prior to installation** 

# **6. Compliance and Owner Responsibility** 

No materials will be ordered and no connection permitted until all required documentation and fees are received. Failure to comply may result in delays or denial. 

The property owner is responsible for proper installation of all appurtenances and shall guarantee construction for one (1) year from final acceptance by Kent County DPW. 

# **7. Questions** 

Kent County Department of Public Works – Water and Wastewater Division Phone: 410-778-3287 Email: watergenmb@kentgov.org

# **NOTICE TO CONTRACTORS AND PLUMBERS** 

# **Grinder Pump Service Connections – HDPE Requirements** 

Issued by the **Kent County Department of Public Works – Water and Wastewater Division** , this notice applies to all service connections to **low-pressure grinder pump systems** where **high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe** is utilized. 

To maintain system integrity and standardization, **all new grinder pump connections to the main shall comply with the following requirements** . 

# **Required Materials** 

All connections shall include: 

1. **Tapping Saddle** – Central Plastics electro-fusion self-tapping tee, sized to the main (2", 3", or 4" x 1.5"), **SOR 11 IPS** , with brass cutter. 

2. **Saddle to Lateral Connection** – **1.5" x 1.5" SDR 11 IPS compression coupling** . 

3. **HDPE Pipe** – CSR Polypipe or approved equal, **1.5" SDR 11 IPS** , installed: 

   - From the main to the curb stop valve; and 

   - `o` From the curb stop valve to the grinder pump. 

4. **Grinder Pump Adapter** – **1.25" stainless steel x Polypipe adapter** . 

5. **Adapter Coupling** – **1.5" x 1.25" SOR 11 IPS compression coupling** . 

6. **Curb Stop Valve** – **1.5" compression x compression curb stop valve** , installed approximately five (5) feet from the grinder pump. 

7. **Curb Box** – **Cast iron adjustable curb box** , installed over the curb stop valve and set to finished grade. 

# **Contractor Responsibilities** 

The contractor or plumber shall ensure: 

- Adequate manpower, equipment, and materials are available; 

- Excavations are maintained in a **safe, dry, and clean condition** suitable for County personnel. 

County personnel **will not enter or assist with unsafe excavations** . If additional County equipment or manpower is required, or if County personnel are required on site longer than **two (2) hours** , the contractor or property owner will be billed at the County’s standard rates. 

# **Contact Information** 

Kent County Department of Public Works – Water and Wastewater Division Phone: 410-778-3287 

Compliance with this notice is mandatory for all applicable grinder pump service connections within Kent County.

# **Authorization Conditions** 

This authorization is valid for **ninety (90) days** from the date of issuance and is subject to the following conditions: 

1. **Notification** 

The plumber shall contact the Kent County Water and Wastewater Division at **410-778-3287** at least **48 hours in advance** of any excavation or installation involving County water or sewer lines. 

2. **Installation and Restoration** 

   - Pipe shall be bedded and covered to a minimum of **one (1) foot above the pipe** using sand or select gravel at roadway crossings and shoulders. 

   - Select fill shall be used from one (1) foot above the pipe to within twelve (12) inches of finished grade. 

   - Disturbed roadway and shoulder areas shall be restored with **twelve (12) inches of crusher run or gravel** . 

   - All disturbed areas shall be stabilized and reseeded with grass seed and straw or approved matting. 

3. **Compliance** 

All work shall comply with applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations, including the Kent County Plumbing Code, Kent County Public Works Water and Sewer Construction Standards, Maryland Occupational Safety and Health Administration (MOSH), Maryland Public Service Commission (PSC), Maryland State Highway Administration (SHA), and Kent County Zoning Regulations.

4. **Maintenance Responsibility** 

The plumber shall be responsible for the repair and restoration of disturbed areas in accordance with **Kent County Roads Division Specifications** for a period of **one (1) year** from the date of work completion. 

5. **Indemnification and Damages** 

The plumber agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the **Kent County Department of Public Works** , **Water and Wastewater Division** , and the **County Commissioners of Kent County, Maryland** , from all claims or damages arising from the authorized work. The plumber shall be responsible for all costs related to damage to roadways or County property. 

6. **Final Acceptance** 

All work shall be completed to the satisfaction of the Kent County Department of Public Works, Division of Water and Wastewater.
