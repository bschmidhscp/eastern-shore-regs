# Kent County Small Business Grant Proposal

<!-- page 1 -->
The purpose of this funding opportunity is to support businesses in expanding access to essential services that benefit Kent County residents or improve the ability of small local businesses to serve underserved populations. 

# **Program Goals:** 

- Strengthen small businesses that provide essential services or benefits to the community. 

- Encourage projects that improve quality of life, expand access to resources, or support community well-being. 

- Foster economic growth through sustainable, community-driven initiatives. 

# **Eligible Applicants:** 

- Eligibility is limited to small, locally owned businesses operating within Kent County. 

- A small business, for eligibility purposes of this application, is defined as a for-profit business entity with fewer than 15 employees. Sole proprietors are eligible. 

- Start-ups are not eligible. Applicants must be a business that is currently in operation and serving Kent County. 

- Businesses must be in good standing with the State of Maryland and Kent County. 

# **Eligible Essential Services:** 

- Access to essential services includes services that benefit the health, safety, or wellbeing of residents, with examples including, but not limited to, childcare, healthcare, transportation, and educational services. Lodging, retail, and restaurants are not eligible for this funding program. 

- Priority may be given to projects that support underserved populations, including groups that often face barriers to accessing services or resources, such as seniors or low-income families. 

# **Eligible Uses of Funds:** 

- Equipment, technology, or supplies needed to expand or enhance community-serving operations. 

- Improvements that increase accessibility or customer safety, including but not limited to minor renovations that enhance the ability of Kent County residents to access the essential services provided by the business. 

- Funding for existing, established small local businesses to launch a new pilot program or services that directly address an essential community need. 

- Business modernization efforts that support community engagement. 

- Marketing costs are allowable when they do not make up a significant share of the budget and are necessary to support an eligible pilot program launch or to promote new equipment or technology funded under the program.

<!-- page 2 -->
# **Grant Amounts:** 

- Up to $32,000 in total grant funding is expected to be available across all applicants 

- Individual awards between $1,000 and $5,000. 

- Matching funds are not required; however, inclusion of a matching contribution could increase competitive advantage. 

# **Selection Criteria:** 

Applications are scored on: 

- Clear identification of a community need. 

- Direct benefit to the community. 

- Feasibility and readiness of the proposed project. 

- Sustainability or long-term community impact. 

- Strength of business capacity to execute the project. 

# **Application Requirements:** 

- Project description. 

- Description of the community need being addressed. 

- Budget and timeline. 

- Expected measurable outcomes (number served, services added, improved access, etc.). 

# **Timeline:** 

- Application release: June 15, 2026. 

- Application deadline: July 15, 2026 

- Award decisions: within 4 weeks. 

# **Reporting Requirements:** 

- Grant Reporting - Grantees are required to submit a final grant report to Kent County Economic and Tourism Development by February 28, 2027. Reporting templates are available at www.kentcounty.com/grants. 

• Reimbursement Request - Grantees must submit a reimbursement request showing proof of expenses by January 31, 2027. 

- Expenses must be incurred June 1, 2026, through January 31, 2027. Reimbursement request forms are available at <u>www.kentcounty.com/grants.</u>
