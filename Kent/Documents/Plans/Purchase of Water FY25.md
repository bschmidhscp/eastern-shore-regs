Getting Water from Kent County Fire Hydrants 

- 1) Email this completed and signed form to <u>watergenmb@kentgov.org at least 24 hours before water</u> is needed. 

- 2) The person operating the hydrant must possess a valid State of Maryland water distribution operator certification, (See COMAR **<u>Chapter 26.06.01. General Provisions</u>** ). Therefore, a county employee will operate and oversee the filling of tanks and containers. 

- 3) When using a Kent County fire hydrant in filling tanks of any kind you will need to have an air gap in place while filling from our hydrants. This is a picture of a simple air gap.
