# Rock Cover Option Web posting

<!-- page 1 -->
Kent County standardizes its grinder pump sewer system around the Environment One (E/One) pumps to provide ease of maintenance and common replacement parts.  Kent County sewer customers with grinder pump units now have an opportunity to purchase decorative rock covers from E/One.  These are the only covers approved for use on E/One grinder pump units.  If customers are interested in purchasing a cover, please contact us at 410-778-3287 for pricing and installation.
