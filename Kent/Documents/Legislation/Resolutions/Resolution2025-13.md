# RESOLUTION 2025-13 

COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF KENT COUNTY, MARYLAND 

A RESOLUTION TO ADOPT SOLAR APP+™ AS REQUIRED BY MARYLAND SENATE BILL 783, RELATED TO ISSUANCE OF CERTAIN RESIDENTIAL, ROOFTOP SOLAR PERMITS; WAIVING THE BUILDING PERMIT FEES FOR SUCH PERMITS; AND, DIRECTING THE DEPARTMENT OF PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING TO COLLECT A REVISED ELECTRICAL INSPECTION FEE TO COVER THE COSTS OF INSPECTIONS PERFORMED BY THE MIDDLE DEPARTMENT INSPECTION AGENCY, WHICH IS KENT COUNTY’S RECOGNIZED INSPECTION AGENCY, SET FORTH IN §192-42, INSPECTION AUTHORITY, IN THE PUBLIC LOCAL LAWS OF KENT COUNTY, MARYLAND. 

WHEREAS, Maryland Senate Bill 783, adopted in 2024, requires that “on or before August 1, 2025, each county and municipality shall implement solar permitting software for features supporting the tracking and approval of residential building permits for: (1) residential solar energy systems; (2) residential energy storage systems; (3) main electric panel upgrades; and (4) main electric panel derates;” and 

WHEREAS, Maryland Senate Bill 783 also provides for certain exemptions from implementation of permitting software requirements; however, Kent County is not currently eligible for exemption; and 

WHEREAS, the State originally identified the Solar APP+™ program, developed by the National Renewable Energy Laboratory, as the State’s preferred program to implement this requirement; and 

WHEREAS, in order to collect payment for building permits issued by Solar APP+™, the County must utilize and integrate the Stripe Billing program into its financial systems, as well as the Solar APP+ program to issue permits; and. 

WHEREAS, in consultation with County staffand the County’s software consultants, the County’s current financial and permitting software is unable to be integrated with these two particular programs; and 

WHEREAS, the County can select a web-based version of Solar APP+™, which is an option that does not require integration of the Solar APP+™ program with the existing permitting software; however, the County cannot collect permit fees in either system without using the Stripe Billing program; and 

WHEREAS, the County Commissioners desire to comply with the State’s requirements; however, due to the lack of compatibility with the existing County software, there are limitations to implementation. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the County Commissioners of Kent County, Maryland, that the web-based Solar APP+™ program is hereby adopted for Kent County to issue building permits for residential, rooftop solar, and any accompanying improvements, as set forth in State Law; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, by the County Commissioners of Kent County, Maryland, that the building permit fees related only to those building permits issued via Solar APP+™ are hereby waived, because such permit fees cannot be collected by Kent County utilizing the Stripe Billing program; and 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, by the County Commissioners of Kent County, Maryland, that the Department of Planning, Housing, and Zoning is directed to develop and collect a reimbursement fee for the Middle Department Inspection Agency (MDIA), which may be revised from time to time based on evolving practices, to cover the cost of MDIA inspections related to permits issued via Solar APP+™; and 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, by the County Commissioners of Kent County, Maryland, that if any of the exemptions provided in Maryland Senate Bill 783 were to apply to Kent County in the future, then this Resolution is no longer effective, until such time as the State of Maryland revises Senate Bill 783, or adopts other legislation that again requires implementation of the Solar APP+™ program in the County. 

ADOPTED this 22nd day of July 2025.
