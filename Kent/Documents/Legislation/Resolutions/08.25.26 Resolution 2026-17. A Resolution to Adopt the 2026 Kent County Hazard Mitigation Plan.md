2026 KENT COUNTY HAZARD MITIGATION PLAN 

AUGUST 25, 2026 

A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS TO ADOPT THE KENT COUNTY, MARYLAND 2026 KENT COUNTY HAZARD MITIGATIONPLAN, BASED ON THE STANDARDS CONTAINED IN 44CFR, PART 201, AS AUTHORIZED BY THE DISASTER MITGATION ACT OF 2000. THESE CRITERIA ADDRESSTHE PLANNING PROCESS, HAZARD IDENTIFICATION AND RISK ASSESSMENT, MITIGATION STRATEGY AND PLAN MAINTENANCE REQUIREMENTS. 

WHEREAS The Board of County Commissioners has reviewed the Kent County, Maryland Hazard Mitigation Plan and support the adoption of<sup>thisplan;</sup> 

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners understand that the Kent County, Maryland Hazard Mitigation Plan has received a satisfactory rating by FEMA for all required criteria; 

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners are dedicated to supporting the Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000, and are committed to reducing future disaster losses and building community resiliency; 

NOW, THEREFORE, THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF KENT COUNTY, MARYLAND, this 25 day of August 2026, do hereby adopt the Kent County, Maryland Hazard Mitigation Plan, based on standards contained in 44CFR, Part 201, as authorized by the Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000, and call on all government agencies and municipalities of Kent County to also adopt the 2026 Kent County, Maryland Hazard Mitigation Plan. 

THIS RESOLUTION, having been duly posted in accordance with the policy of the Board of County Commissioners for advertising for the adoption of resolutions, shall become effective upon the adoption of this Resolution.
