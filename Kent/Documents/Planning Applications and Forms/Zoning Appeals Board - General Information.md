# Zoning Appeals Board - General Information

<!-- page 1 -->
# **What Does the Board of Zoning Appeals Do?** 

The **Board of Zoning Appeals** is responsible for reviewing and making decisions on: 

- **Special exceptions** 

- **Variances** 

- **Interpretations of zoning regulations** 

- **Appeals of decisions or determinations** made by the Zoning Administrator under the Land Use Ordinance 

# **How to File an Appeal** 

To appeal a zoning decision, or to request a variance, special exception, or interpretation: 

1. **Submit a completed appeals form** to the **Kent County Planning Office** 

2. Include the appropriate **filing fee** : 

   - **$350** for variances, special exceptions, or interpretations 

   - **$250** for appeals of decisions made by the Zoning Administrator 

3. Make checks payable to: **County Commissioners of Kent County** 

**Note:** The Planning Department and Board of Appeals are not required to fill out this form. It must be completed by the applicant or their agent. If staff assists with the form, they cannot be held responsible for its contents. 

For questions, contact the Clerk at **(410) 778-7467** . 

# **Administrative Hearings** 

Certain minor zoning matters may be reviewed administratively by the **Planning Director** or their designee, including: 

- **Yard setback variances** up to 50% of the required distance 

- **Accessory structures** located: 

   - In the front yard of waterfront parcels 

   - In the front yard of through or corner lots
