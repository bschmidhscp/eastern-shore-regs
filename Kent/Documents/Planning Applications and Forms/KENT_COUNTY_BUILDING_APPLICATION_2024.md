## **BUILDING PERMIT APPLICATION DETAILS** 

- C **hecklists are available** on our website: <u>https://www.kentcounty.com/planning/building-permits</u> 

   - Accessory Building (Shed, Garage, Pole Buildings) 

   - Additions, Deck, Porch, Breezeway, & Renovations 

   - New Single-Family Dwelling (New Home) 

   - Pier 

   - Swimming Pool / Hot Tubs 

   - Solar 

**IMPORTANT:** _If you are a_ **_<u>contract purchaser</u>_** _<u>, the permit cannot be issued until the property transfers.</u>_ 

- _If the property owner(s) listed on this application does not correspond with the current Maryland Tax Assessment Records, a copy of the recorded deed will need to be submitted with the application._ 

- _If you_ **_<u>plan to purchase</u>_** _the property and fill out the application in your name, you will need a signed letter from the current owner authorizing you to submit the application. A copy of the contract is also sufficient to process a permit._ 

- _If you are a_ **_<u>contractor submitting an application for the owner,</u>_** _you will need a signed letter from the owner authorizing you to submit the application._ 

- _If the_ **_<u>property is owned by a Corporation, LLC, or Partnership,</u>_** _then you will need to provide documentation on who is authorized to sign on behalf of the Corporation, LLC, or Partnership._ 

**Construction Plans:** Two (2) sets of construction plans – hard copies for Accessory Building, Decks/ Porches/Ramps, New Single-Family Dwellings, Swimming Pools, and Solar projects will be required. 

**Construction Plans:** Three (3) sets of construction plans – hard copies for an Addition or Renovation – projects including adding or replacing windows and exterior doors will be required. 

**<mark>What Building Codes are used?</mark> Effective May 29, 2023,** <u>these codes must be listed on the construction plans with a ResCheck Report. This report determines if the building design meets the requirements of the code.</u> 

- 2021 IRC 

- NFPA 101 Life Safety Code 2018 

- 2021 IECC International Energy Conservation Code 

- 2021 International Plumbing Code 

- 2021 IBC 

- NFPA 1 Fire Code 2018 

- 2021 International Mechanical Code (IMC) 

### **<mark>Effective May 1, 2024</mark>** 

- NFPA 70 National Electrical Code Handbook 2023 

**Improvements:** Check all improvements that are appropriate to the proposed building. MDIA (Middle Dept. Inspection Agency) will review and inspect all building projects. For all structural, electrical, mechanical, and HVAC questions, please call MDIA at 410/822-8300. 

## **CONTRACTOR/BUILDER:** 

- A homeowner can be listed as the builder of the project for his/her own house, or the property owner 

- may list themselves as OWNER under Builder. The property owner will be responsible for the builder/contractor they choose. This excludes Mortgage Companies. 

<u>Note: If the property owner acts as the builder/contractor, an Affirmation of Landowner as home</u> builder/contractor form is required.

**BUILDING PERMIT APPLICATION DETAILS Continued…** 

 For electrical work, the electrician **<u>must be licensed through the State of Maryland</u>** and **<u>must be registered</u>** as a master electrician with Kent County Government - Maryland. Please contact Mike O’Neil at <u>MONeil@kentgov.org or check out our website: https://www.kentcounty.com/planning/plans</u> for the forms - Kent County Master Electrician Registration/Renewal 

 For plumbing work, a homeowner may not do his/her own plumbing work in his/her own home. The plumber **<u>must be licensed through the State of Maryland</u>** and **<u>must be registered as</u>** a master plumber with Kent County Government- Maryland. Please contact Mike O’Neil at <u>MONeil@kentgov.org</u> or check out our website: <u>https://www.kentcounty.com/planning/plans</u> for the forms - Kent County Master Plumber Registration/Renewal.
