5. 

###### Lot Coverage Checklist: 

Please complete the attached lot coverage checklist. The amount of Lot Coverage permitted on your property varies according to the size of your property. Lot Coverage calculations include all areas where water cannot infiltrate, such as sidewalks, buildings, driveways, and roof eaves. Decks with spaces that allow water to pass freely and access paths to the water within the Critical Area Buffer do not count against lot coverage totals. For assistance, do not hesitate to contact the Department of Planning. 

6. 

###### Buffer Enhancement/Management Plan or Critical Area Clearing Plan: 

Before any trees can be removed within the Critical Area and/or the Critical Area Buffer, a mitigation plan must be obtained from the Department of Planning. In addition, if any new lot coverage is proposed on a parcel that contains a buffer, then a Buffer Enhancement Plan is required. For assistance, do not hesitate to contact the Department of Planning. 

###### 7. 

###### Nonconversion Agreementfor Water Dependent Structures (if applicable): 

Please complete this form if your proposed accessory building is to be located within the Critical Area Buffer for a bone fide water-dependent use. This document must be recorded with the Kent County Clerk of Circuit Court for buildings constructed for bone fide water-dependent uses. The building may only be used for a specified water-dependent purpose as approved by the Kent County Department of Planning. The owner may not change or alter the structure to another use. The original document must be on file in the Planning Department. 

8. 

###### Floodplain Documentation: 

- If the proposed accessory building is located within the 100-Year Floodplain, then please complete the following pertinent to floodplain review: . Venting Affidavit: Buildings that are elevated and have fully enclosed areas in the floodplain must include at least two water equalizing vents on different walls with an area of at least one square inch for every square foot of enclosed area subject to flooding. This form must be notarized, and the original document must be on file in the Planning Department. 

- . Declarationof Land Restriction: This document shall be recorded with the Kent County Clerk of Circuit Court for fully enclosed garages or storage spaces in areas subject to flooding. This declaration indicates that the enclosed areas subject to flooding will not be converted into habitable space, will be used for parking and limited storage only, and that the venting will be maintained. The original document must be on file in the Planning Department.
