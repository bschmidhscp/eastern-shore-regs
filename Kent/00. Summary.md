# Kent County, MD: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "UDO" = Chapter 222 Unified Development Ordinance, effective 2025-07-25 (REV 2025-10-01) [`Code/Unified Development Ordinance/`; density tables in `Documents/Legislation/Bills/Article 2 Districts`]. The eCode Chapter 222 is a stub with no rules. "PC digest", "BOA digest" = `01. Digests/`. State rules: see `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Density is the binding limit.** Agricultural (AZD) is 1 unit per 30 acres, and RCD and RC are 1 per 20 acres [UDO Art. II §§ 2.3, 3.3, 4.3]. A 3-lot minor needs roughly 90+ acres of AZD, and the original/remainder lot counts toward the total [UDO Art. IV § 2.3.1]. Lots made since 8/1/1989 (1985 in the Critical Area) count against the parcel [Art. IV § 2.3.2-3].
- **Extra AZD limits:** lots may cover at most 10% of the property (parcels 100+ acres exempt), and at most 2 lots may front an existing public road [UDO Art. II § 2.3, fn 2-3].
- **Minors are administrative:** up to 7 lots, Planning Director decides after TAC review, one step [UDO Art. IV §§ 1.7.1, 2.4.2]. Eight or more lots is a 3-stage Planning Commission (PC) process [Art. IV § 2.4.3].
- **No impact fee or building excise tax exists** [Fee Findings; Code Ch. 152]. County plan fees are tiny: $240 for a 3-lot minor [Fees: Sept 2024 p.2].
- **Septic:** Kent has an MDP Tier IV exemption (April 2017), so the State's minor-subdivision cap on septic does not apply [01. Maryland/00. Summary § 2]. Confirm it is still current.
- **Little subdivision activity visible:** 0 subdivision items in 191 PC digest rows (Aug 2023 to Oct 2026); 35 new dwelling permits countywide in 2023 [PC digest 2024-12-05]. Precedent for 4+ lot timing is **not found in corpus**.
- **Risks:** thin yield, Critical Area (RCD 1 per 20 ac), stormwater review cost, sewer rare outside towns. **Opportunity:** no impact fees; 7-lot administrative path; Village and Intense Village districts with higher density.

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Lot line adjustment | Minor | Major |
|---|---|---|---|
| Threshold | No new lot | 7 or fewer lots from the original parcel | 8 or more lots |
| Decider | Planning Director | Planning Director (may refer to PC) | Planning Commission |
| Steps | One | One, after TAC | Concept, Preliminary, Final, each after TAC |

All from [UDO Art. IV §§ 1.7, 2.4]. Counts include lots made since 12/23/1969 and the remainder [§ 2.3.1].

- **TAC** meets monthly; pre-submission conference recommended, not required [§ 1.5]. Minors get a mailed notice to adjoining owners (10-day comment); majors get 20-day mailed notice, posting, and advance notice [§ 1.6].
- **Private roads** only in minors, max 7 parcels served unless waived [§ 2.4.2 note]. Open space or fee in lieu: 1/10 ac per dwelling [§ 2.7.11].
- **Lapse:** signed plats must be recorded within 6 months of approval [Art. IV, Lapse of Approvals § 1].
- **Critical Area projects** go to the State Critical Area Commission, and the County cannot approve until notice or the waiting period ends [§ 2.2].
- **Timing:** not found in corpus. Minors are not minuted; the PC digest shows no subdivision items. Appeals of PC decisions are due within 30 days [Art. IV § 1.14].

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Critical Area** (1,000 ft of tidal water; RCD, CAR, CCA, M, ECCA, MXDCA): lot coverage 15%; for subdivisions of 1-acre-or-smaller lots, 15% overall and 25% per lot [UDO Art. VI § 2.4.1]. Buffer 100 ft [§ 2.7]; RCD density is the State's 1 per 20 acres. Growth allocation follows County policy and COMAR [§ 2.18]; fee $500 [Fees p.2].
- **Septic tiers:** 2018 Comp Plan uses Tiers 1, 1A, 2, 2A, 3, 4, 4A [Comp Plan p.30-31, Map 4]. Major subdivisions in Tier III need a PC public hearing [UDO Art. IV § 1.6.3(C)]. Kent's Tier IV exemption applies [01. Maryland § 2]. Perc, soils and Health Department sign-off: State rules apply.
- **Forest conservation** (outside Critical Area; AZD, RC, CR, V, IV, C, EC; disturbance over 40,000 sf): afforest to 20% in AZD/RC/CR; reforest 1/4 ac per acre cleared above 50% forest, 2 acres per acre below [UDO Art. VI §§ 3.2, 3.4]. County says its standards exceed the State's [§ 3.1]; the State's 1:1 rule effective 1 Jul 2026 is in the Maryland summary.
- **Stormwater:** formal plan required for minor subdivisions; $1,800 review [Fees p.2]. Design standards are in Art. VI § 5 (not extracted).
- **Floodplain:** Art. VI § 6; thresholds not extracted.
- **Ag preservation:** Priority Preservation Area in AZD; 13 MALPF applications on file, up to 16 allowed [PC digest 2026-05-07]. No APFO found.

## 5. Water and sewer

- Service is in the towns and a few County systems; the 2018 Water and Sewer Plan governs [Documents/Plans]. Rural lots are septic with wells.
- **Allocation fee** (where the County serves): $11,800 sewer + $4,400 water = $16,200 per dwelling (250 GPD); undated table [Fees: allocation fee options p.1]. Connection application $50 [Fees: Connection Packet p.1].
- **FY27 rates:** residential $159.73 water + $207.05 sewer + $15.00 Bay Restoration Fee = $381.78 per year; vacant $183.40 [Fee Findings, web retrieved 2026-10-11]. Rates rose 4% [Res. 2026-12, Commissioners digest 2026-06-09].
- **Recent W&S plan amendments:** Millington/Everton (34 EDUs, Res. 2026-09, 2026-05-05); new Millington WWTP, 350,000 gpd, site bought 2026-04-14 [Commissioners digest]. Both are growth-area, not rural.
- **BAT/septic costs:** BAT required in the Critical Area [01. Maryland § 1]. Local BAT cost, BRF grant eligibility: **not found in corpus**.
