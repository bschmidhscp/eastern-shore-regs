# Kent County: Fee Findings

*Compiled 2026-10-11. Fiscal year (FY) 2027 = July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027.*

## Impact fee / excise tax
- **None found.** Chapter 152 (Taxation) has no development impact fee, school or public-facilities excise tax, or building excise tax. It covers only transfer tax, recordation tax, short-term rental tax and credits [Code: Ch. 152 Contents].
- Transfer tax: up to 0.5% of consideration [§ 152-13]. Recordation tax: $3.30 per $500 [§ 152-19]. These are paid at conveyance, not as a new-home fee.
- Impact fees appear only as a possible tool in comp-plan and minutes discussion (e.g. [Minutes: PC 2026-06-04 p.2]). No adopted fee was found.
- Developer agreements may include "impact fees and other fees" [§ 19-6]. This is a negotiated item, not a fixed schedule.

## Health dept / septic
- Environmental Health fee: $200 per new single-family dwelling [Fees p.1].
- The subdivision rows have a second column of Environmental Health amounts ($75 for minor subdivision, lot-line adjustment and concept plan; $0 for preliminary and final). The PDF text is run together, so confirm the column alignment [Fees p.2].
- No separate perc test fee schedule was found. Confirm with the county.

## Water & sewer charges
- **Allocation fee (one-time, per dwelling, 250 GPD):** $11,800 sewer + $4,400 water = $16,200 combined. A 1-bedroom apartment is $6,480 and a 2-bedroom apartment is $11,340 [Fees: allocation fee options p.1; undated]. Applies only where the county serves the lot.
- **Connection application:** $50 non-refundable per connection. The owner pays for pipes, fittings and meter pit. The county supplies the meter and grinder pump [Fees: NEW CUSTOMER CONNECTION PACKET072026 p.1].
- **Annual rates, FY27:** residential water $159.73 + sewer $207.05 + Bay Restoration Fee $15.00 = $381.78. Vacant lot is $183.40 [https://www.kentcounty.com/government/departments/public_works/water_and_wastewater/allocation_inquiries_request_fees_and_rate_schedule.php, retrieved 2026-10-11].
- Septic lots pay the state Bay Restoration Fund fee, set statewide; see the Maryland summary.
