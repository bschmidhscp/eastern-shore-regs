### What is a Comprehensive Plan? 

- Blueprint for the future: 20-year horizon 

- Establishes a vision for growth, change and preservation for the next 20 years 

- Guides decisions on land use, transportation, housing, economic development, environmental issues, and historic preservation 

- Coordinates local planning efforts 

- Fulfills State of Maryland requirements

<!-- page 5 -->
### What is a Comprehensive Plan? 

- Main Components: 

   - Identifying Needs and Opportunities 

   - Supporting Goals and Recommendations 

   - Guiding Future Development 

   - Outlining Implementation 

- Public Input: 

   - Helps to prioritize needs and opportunities 

   - Helps to establish community vision for growth and preservation 

   - Helps to identify desired development patterns

<!-- page 6 -->
### What a Comprehensive Plan is NOT: 

A comprehensive plan is not : 

- A set of regulations 

- An ordinance 

- The same as the zoning code (zoning helps to implement the Plan)

## Visioning Overview 

What is a vision statement? 

- Communicates what the County wants to be like in the next 20 years 

- Provides overall “big picture” guidance for the entire Comprehensive Plan 

- Is supported by all the goals and policies in the Comprehensive Plan 

- Is often formatted as a short paragraph

## Draft Vision Statement 

Worcester County is committed to preserving its diverse rural and coastal heritage and historical roots, while striving to create an inclusive and secure community that offers ample opportunities for residents and visitors alike. Our vision encompasses sustainable economic growth, harmonizing with our existing agricultural community, and ensuring the preservation of our natural resources.
