As the next item of business, the Planning Commission reviewed the agenda for the Technical Review Committee meeting scheduled for January 8, 2025. Ms. Tremblay was present for the review to answer questions and address concerns of the Planning Commission. 

No comments were forwarded to the Committee. 

### III. Site Plan Review: Worcester County Commissioners Vehicle Storage Facility - County Project 

Timothy Metzner from Davis, Bowen, and Friedel presented the site plan and building elevations to the Planning Commission for a 12,275 square foot building that will be used for vehicle storage at the Worcester County fire training center in Newark. Mr. Metzner stated the site is currently surrounded by privacy fencing and will continue to be after the project is complete. Mr. Metzner also stated that a future fire tower will be built on the site. 

After a brief discussion, on a motion made by Ms. Wimbrow and seconded by Ms. Knight, the Planning Commission approved the site plan as submitted. 

### IV. Text Amendment 

As the next item of business, the Planning Commission reviewed a proposed text amendment prepared by Mrs. Keener at the request of the Worcester County Commissioners. The proposed text amendment reviewed the uses associated with the Commercial Marine District and evaluated ways to strengthen the protection of the commercial fishing industry in the Worcester County Zoning code. Mrs. Keener presented the proposed text amendment. 

Following a brief discussion, a motion was made by Ms. Knight, and seconded by Ms. Wimbrow, the Planning Commission gave a favorable recommendation to the Worcester County Commissioners for the proposed text amendment as submitted. 

##### Vv.
