### I. Call to Order 

# Il. Comprehensive Plan Work Session 

As the first item of business, the Planning Commission met with Michael Bayer, Cassie Williams and Angela Fleck from Wallace Montgomery to discuss the draft Natural Resources and Sensitive Areas Element of the proposed Worcester County Comprehensive Plan. 

Ms. Williams is an environmental scientist with Wallace Montgomery. She is focusing on the Natural Resources and Sensitive Areas chapter. She identified the main goals listed in the chapter, along with possible action items below each one. She noted that these were to be used as a starting point, and that the Planning Commission will eventually go through them to see what is actionable, reasonable, and feasible. 

During the discussion, the Planning Commission made the following comments and/or recommendations: 

- e The Planning Commission had some discussion on the electrification requirement for buildings over 35,000 square feet, which is a state law that will impact buildings using fossil fuels, including many of the hotels and condominium buildings in Ocean City. While not directly referenced in this section, a note will be made to add language where appropriate. An education component for energy use is encouraged. 

- e The Planning Commission requested that the St. Martin’s River be added to the Physiography section. 

- e Clarifying the difference in NRCS data set for the soil survey and percentages versus County data set and percentages. Worcester County uses County data for septic, MALPF,

Once input is received on their projected growth boundaries, then the data will be input into the Water Resources Element. In the upcoming months, the Transportation chapter will be reviewed in February, and the Economic Development and Tourism chapter in January. 

Mr. Bayer noted that all the current chapters are working drafts; as major changes are made to a respective chapter, it will be shared with the Planning Commission and updated on the County’s website.
