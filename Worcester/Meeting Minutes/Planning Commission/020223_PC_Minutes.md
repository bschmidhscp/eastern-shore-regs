# WORCESTER COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES - February 2, 2023 

##### Ill. 

Residential Planned Communities § ZS 1-315 

## A. Refuge at Windmill Creek 

As the next item of business, the Planning Commission reviewed the Step I submittal for Refuge at Windmill Creek, consisting of 90 single family lots. Located on the northwest side of Beauchamp Road, north of Racetrack Road (MD Route 589), Tax Map 15, Parcels 127 and 259, Tax District 3, R-1 Rural residential and RP Resource Protection Districts 

Present for the review was Ronnie Carpenter of Carpenter Engineering. Mr. Carpenter presented the proposal to the Planning Commission. He explained the number of lots from the previously submittal. He further explained that the only changes made were two lots needed to be reconfigured due to the necessitation for a sewer pump station. The changes allow for County access to an outlot where the pump station is located. The access is fee simple and will be dedicated to Worcester County. Mr. Barbierri questioned which lots had changed to which Mr. Carpenter responded Lots 17 and 18. Mr. Church questioned why the previous approval was allowed to expire. Mr. Carpenter responded that the project did not have the water EDUs required at the time of expiration. Ms. Tremblay added that the road name, Windmill Creek Lane, had been previously approved and could be incorporated into the Planning Commission’s recommendation. 

Following the discussion, a motion was made by Ms. Ott, seconded by Ms. Knight, and carried unanimously to make a favorable recommendation for Step I approval with incorporation of the road name. 

IV. Adjourn — A motion to adjourn was made by Ms. Ott and seconded by Ms. Knight.
