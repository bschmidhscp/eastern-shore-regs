Recycling Department Over Expenditure 
Public Works is requesting Commissioner approval of an over-expenditure at the Solid 
Waste, Recycling Division for the Overtime Pay account, 100.1206.6000.400 in the amount of 
$5,500.00. On November 21, 2025, the baling machine in the Recycling Division broke down and 
was out of service until December 18, 2025.  During that time, paper and cardboard became backed 
up to the point it was overflowing out of the doors of the Recycling building.  With the overflow 
of material and the increase of cardboard from the holiday, 4 to 6 person crews were working 10-
hour days to catch up.  
Headway has been made on cardboard to the point the compactor and loading dock can 
now be accessed allowing the shipment out of the baled cardboard.  If approved, the over-
expenditure request would allow two more weeks of overtime, including Saturdays. The increased 
hours will allow crews to get caught up with the excess cardboard and paper. 
Funds are available in the Recycling account – 100.1206. 6110.420, Tools and Supplies to 
cover this over-expenditure. Please let me know if there are any questions.

On behalf of Sheriff Matthew Crisafulli, the Sheriffs Office respectfully requests approval to 
proceed with the FY26 purchase of a pneumatic breaching tool in the amount of$38,230.78. 
This purchase was included in the budget amendment approved by the Worcester County 
Commissioners during the December 2, 2025 meeting, and funds have been transferred to 
General Ledger Account 100.1101 .030.6110.190 (Supplies & Equipment- Law Enforcement 
Equipment) to fully cover the cost. 
The Worcester County Sheriffs Office worked in coordination with Mr. Ed Welch, Worcester 
County Procurement Officer, to secure pricing through a publicly solicited cooperative contract. 
The equipment is manufactured by Holmatro, Inc., which holds a proprietary design for this 
technology and distributes through an assigned geographic dealer network. Chesapeake Fire & 
Rescue Equipment, Inc. is the authorized Holmatro dealer servicing Worcester County. This 
procurement was sourced through cooperative contract #PS2571 0, resulting in a 2% discount and 
satisfying formal competitive bid requirements and Worcester County financial policy. 
The pneumatic breaching tool is a tactical device designed to provide rapid, controlled forced 
entry into locked or barricaded doors and other entry points using compressed air. This 
capability enhances operational safety and efficiency during high-risk law-enforcement 
activities, including warrant service and exigent circumstances.

Re: Over-Expenditure Request - FY2027 Ammunition Purchase 
Mr. Young, 
On behalf of Sheriff Matthew Crisafulli, the Sheriffs Office respectfully requests approval of an 
over-expenditure in the FY26 budget in the amount of$76,632.42 to allow the Worcester County 
Sheriffs Office to place an ammunition order necessary for operational and training use in 
FY27. 
Historically, the Sheriffs Office has pre-ordered bulk ammunition prior to the start of the fiscal 
year to account for extended manufacturer and distributor lead times, which typically range from 
six (6) to seven (7) months, sometimes longer. Under this practice, ammunition was neither 
shipped nor billed until the start of the applicable fiscal year. We have been advised that placing 
such pre-orders during the current fiscal year for a future fiscal-year need is not in compliance 
with County financial policy. 
To ensure full compliance with County Government fiscal requirements and to avoid similar 
issues going forward, we are requesting approval of an over-expenditure in FY26 so the order 
may be placed appropriately at this time, in consideration of our operational and supply-chain 
realities from the distributor.
