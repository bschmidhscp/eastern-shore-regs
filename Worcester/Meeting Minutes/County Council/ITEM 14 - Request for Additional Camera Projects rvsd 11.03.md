- To: Weston Young, Chief Administrative Officer 

From:    Brian Jones, IT Director 

- Re: Camera System Project/Additions 

Date: October 27, 2025 

As you know, the County is currently undertaking a comprehensive upgrade of our camera system. During the initial planning phase, several departments expressed interest in adding new cameras or upgrading existing systems that are not already part of the county infrastructure. The priority of the original RFP was to replace the dated systems we had in place. 

Fortunately, the overall project is tracking well under budget. Given this, I’ve invited all departments to submit updated camera requirements so we can evaluate and potentially fulfill them using the remaining funds. The original RFP focused solely on replacing an end-of-life system that had reached its capacity. 

We are requesting a sole source procurement for this phase of the project, as the new equipment must integrate seamlessly with our existing camera servers and software network. Attached is a spreadsheet summarizing the departments requesting additional coverage, along with a detailed cost breakdown. 

The estimated total cost for this expansion is **$424,513.55** , which includes Cradlepoints, and network switches required to provide internet connectivity to County facilities. 

This project will be funded through **GL 100.1090.070.9010.040** .
