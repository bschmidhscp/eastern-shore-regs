write to you today not just as a concerned citizen, taxpayer, and parent, but as a
passionate advocate for the future of our community. Our education system lies at the
very heart of that future, serving as the cornerstone upon which the prosperity and
progress of our county rest. Yet, itis a system in dire need of support, a support that
only your commitment to fully funding the education budget can provide.

Investing in education is not merely an expenditure; itis an investment in our collective
tomorrow. It is an investment in the bright minds of our youth who will one day lead our
county forward. It is an investment in the teachers who tirelessly dedicate themselves to
shaping the minds of the next generation. And it is an investment in the economic
vitality and social cohesion of our community.

By fully funding the education budget, we affirm our commitment to providing every
child with an equal opportunity to succeed, regardless of their background or
circumstances. We ensure that our schools have the resources they need to offer a well-
rounded education that prepares students not just for the challenges of today, but for
the opportunities of tomorrow.

But the benefits of fully funding education extend far beyond the classroom. A strong
education system is the bedrock of a thriving economy. It attracts businesses and
industries looking for a skilled workforce, driving job creation and economic growth. It
reduces crime rates and improves public health outcomes, leading to a safer and more
prosperous community for all. All of which are vital for Worcester County as a whole.

However, underfunding education undermines these aspirations. It places an undue
burden on teachers, forcing them to do more with less and often leaving them burnt out
and disillusioned. It deprives students of essential resources and opportunities, widening
the achievement gap and perpetuating cycles of poverty and inequality. And it sends a
message to our youth that their education is not a priority, sowing seeds of apathy and
disengagement.

We cannot afford to let this happen. We owe it to ourselves, to our children, and to
future generations to prioritize education and provide it with the support it needs to
thrive. So | implore you, commissioners, to fully fund the education budget. Let us invest
in our future today, so that tomorrow may be brighter for us all.
