APPROVAL REQUESTED: A variance to the side yard setback from 7 feet to 0 feet (to 
encroach 7 feet) for an existing residence with a proposed attached gazebo in a campground 
subdivision in the A-2 Agricultural District. 
 
CODE REFERENCES: Zoning Code §§ ZS 1-116(c)(4), ZS 1-202(c)(18), ZS 1-318(d)(1)B and 
ZS 1-305. 
  
PROPERTY HISTORY: The property is improved with a doublewide manufactured residence 
that was approved in 2004 and has the following record on file: 
 
07/09/2004 
Permit No. 92726 – Issued 07/14/2004 – C/O issued 01/24/2005 – Permit to replace 
an existing unit with a new doublewide SF home [Note – There is no record on file 
for the original unit.] 
 
COMMENTS: The County Commissioners approved Bill 24-08, effective January 31, 2025, that 
increased the number of detached accessory buildings allowed on each campsite in a campground 
subdivision (such as White Horse Park) from one to two; the Bill also requires that there be a 
minimum 3’ separation distance between buildings/structures on the same lot or any other lot. The 
applicants bought this property in February 2019 and there is an existing 8’ x 8’ shed on their lot 
that has been there since at least 2004. They would like to install a 10’ x 12’ gazebo in front of 
the shed and attach it to their residence, as they are unable to meet the 3’ separation distance from 
the residence if it is detached. Once attached, the gazebo will become part of the principal structure 
and will be approximately 0.14’ from the property line, requiring a setback variance. 
 
For the variance request, the Board must make findings that the applicant has demonstrated the 
following: 
 
1. Special conditions and circumstances exist which are peculiar to the land, structure or building 
involved.

surrounding neighborhoods; will cause no objectionable noise, vibration, fumes, odors, dust, 
glare or physical activity; and will not have a detrimental effect on ground- or surface water 
quality.

Thank you for providing information regarding the above referenced variance application. The 
applicant is requesting a variance to the provisions of the Worcester County Critical Area Code, 
which limits the length of piers across tidal wetlands to 100 feet, to permit the construction of a 
217-foot long pier over tidal wetlands at 13022 Riggin Ridge Rd (Tax Map 27, Parcel 570, Lot 
28B). The property is within the Critical Area on lands designated Intensely Developed Area 
(IDA).

is required for the portion of the pier within the Buffer. The applicant must also still receive 
Authorization from the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers before a permit can be issued. The 
application materials did include a Tidal Wetlands License from the Maryland Department of the 
Environment dated March 16, 2026 (Tidal Authorization Number: 26-PR-0069).
