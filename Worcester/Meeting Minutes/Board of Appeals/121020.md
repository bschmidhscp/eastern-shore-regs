# **NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING WORCESTER COUNTY BOARD OF ZONING APPEALS** 

# **AGENDA** 

_THURSDAY, DECEMBER 10, 2020_ 

Pursuant to the provisions of the Worcester County Zoning Ordinance, notice is hereby given that a public hearing will be held in-person before the Board of Zoning Appeals for Worcester County, in the Board Room (Room 1102) on the first floor of the Worcester County Government Center, One West Market Street, Snow Hill, Maryland. Masks and social distancing will be required.
