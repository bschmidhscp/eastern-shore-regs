# Worcester County: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "PC digest", "Council digest", "BOA digest" = `01. Digests/`. State rules (SB 236, Critical Area, forest law) are in `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`; only local choices appear here. Code current through 11-18-2025 [Coverage Report].

## 1. Bottom line

- **Minor subdivision = up to 5 lots out of the parcel as it stood 7/25/1967** (cumulative). Staff approves it; no Planning Commission hearing [§ ZS 2-104; § ZS 2-302(c)]. **6 or more lots is "major"** [§ ZS 1-311(a)].
- **Hard ceiling on septic:** Worcester never adopted SB 236 tier maps, and the Commissioners have said a property "is not eligible for a major subdivision" on private septic [Council minutes 2025-11-04 p.19]. Practical rule: 6+ lots need public sewer. Confirm with staff.
- **No impact fee or excise tax** found; planning fees are small [Fee Findings]. Costs are engineering, perc testing and sewer/septic.
- **Ag land (A-1, A-2, RP, 84% of county) yields at most 5 lots** (6 with a rural cluster, "extremely rare") [Council minutes 2025-11-04 p.541]. Only minors are listed uses in A-1/A-2 [§ ZS 1-201(b)(7); § ZS 1-202(b)(7)].
- **Variances are easy:** 314 of 328 decided BOA cases granted (95.7%) [BOA digest 2017-10 to 2026-07].
- **Risks:** Coastal Bays Critical Area (RCA 1 per 20 ac), Ocean City / Ocean Pines sewer capacity, 2026 Comp Plan not yet adopted, Route 589 map-amendment moratorium [Res. 26-20].
- **Opportunity:** growth areas with sewer (V-1 5/ac, R-2 4/ac, R-1 1/ac) plus clustering at 1.35x density [§ ZS 1-307(c)(2)].

## 2. Zoning districts

| District | Density / lot | Subdivision allowed |
|---|---|---|
| A-1, A-2 | 40,000 sf min, 200 ft wide [§ ZS 1-201(b)(5); 1-202(b)(5)] | Minor and rural cluster; A-2 also consolidated rights (6 or fewer lots reviewed as minor) [§ ZS 1-309(c)(9)] |
| E-1 Estate | 1 per 2 net ac, 80,000 sf [§ ZS 1-203(b)(4)] | Minor, rural cluster, major cluster [§ ZS 1-203] |
| RP Resource | 40,000 sf [§ ZS 1-215(c)(1)] | Minor only, by special exception [§ ZS 1-215(c)(3)] |
| V-1 Village | 5/ac, 10,000 sf [§ ZS 1-204(b)(2)] | Major and minor [§ ZS 1-204(b)(5)] |
| R-1 | 1/ac, 40,000 sf [§ ZS 1-205(b)(2)] | Major and minor |
| R-2 / R-3 / R-4 | 4 / 6 / 8 per ac; 10,000 / 10,000 / 5,000 sf [§ ZS 1-206 to 1-208] | Major and minor |

Cluster in E, V, R districts: 4 ac minimum, 1.35x density, Planning Commission approval [§ ZS 1-307(c)]. Rural cluster (A, E): 40,000 to 90,000 sf lots, one extra lot, 200 ft from road, minor review [§ ZS 1-308(b)]. Every new lot needs 35 ft frontage on a public or approved private road [§ ZS 2-502(d)(4)]. Agricultural-protection setback applies to new lines [§ ZS 1-305(r)].

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Minor (up to 5) | Major (6+) |
|---|---|---|
| Decider | Department of Development Review and Permitting (DRP) = Planning Commission approval | Planning Commission (TRC first) |
| Stages | Plat only; sketch not required | Sketch (optional, required for cluster), preliminary, construction plans, final, record plat [§ ZS 2-401] |

- **Minor clock:** DRP reports within 30 days of acceptance; Environmental Programs (septic) reviews in parallel; rights vest only on recordation [§ ZS 2-302(b); § ZS 2-303(c)]. DRP may force major review [§ ZS 2-304]. Actual minor timing: **not found in corpus** (minors are not minuted).
- **Major clocks:** TRC acts within 60 days of a complete submission [§ ZS 2-403(c)(3)]; preliminary valid 2 years [§ ZS 2-403(d)]; construction plans 1 year [§ ZS 2-404(d)]; final plat 2 years, plus up to 2 [§ ZS 2-405(g)].
- **PC digest 2023-01 to 2026-10:** 5 decided major preliminary plats (Beech Tree Place 9 lots 2023-01-05; Sea Oaks Village 2 76 units; Triple Crown II 30; Refuge at Windmill Creek 90 lots 2023-09-07; The Elms 20 units 2024-03-07), all approved or favorable, none denied; none since March 2024. Beech Tree Road was accepted into the county road inventory 2026-02-03 [Council digest], about 3 years after PC approval.
- **PC overall 2023-2026:** 47 approved and 50 recommended approval vs 3 recommended denial (rezoning, site plan, text amendment mix) [PC digest].

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Septic tiers:** local minor limit is 5 lots, frozen under SB 236 [01. Maryland summary, county snapshot]; no tier map [Council 2025-11-04]. Draft 2026 plan says BAT will be required for all new septic [2026 Comp Plan p.218]; adoption not found, so treat BAT as likely.
- **Critical Area** (Chesapeake and Atlantic Coastal Bays, 1,000 ft): RCA 1 dwelling per 20 ac, no variance [§ NR 3-108(c)(3)]; 100 ft buffer, no new development [§ NR 3-104]; lot coverage 15% of site, subdivision lots of 1 ac or less capped at 15% overall [§ NR 3-107(c)(6)]; slopes over 15% barred [§ NR 3-107(c)(5)]. Growth allocation by Commissioner map amendment; 369 ac remained in the Coastal Bays pot in 2023 [PC digest 2023-10-05; § NR 3-112]. Map update recommended by PC 2026-06-04 and expected to lose ~300 ac of Critical Area [PC digest 2024-12-19, 2026-06-04].
- **Forest conservation:** applies at 40,000 sf or more; afforest to 20% in A-1, A-2, RP, E-1, 15% in R districts [§ NR 1-408(a)(1)]; reforest 2:1 below the floor [§ NR 1-408].
- **Stormwater:** exempt under 5,000 sf cumulative disturbance [§ NR 1-104(b)(3)]. **Floodplain:** show on plat [§ ZS 2-303(b)(16)]. **Ag preservation:** Rural Legacy (Dividing Creek, Bishopville-Showell) and MALPF active; 11 MALPF applications endorsed 2026-08-06 [PC packet 2026-10-01].

## 5. Water and sewer

- **Categories** S/W-1 to S-6/W-6 ("no planned service") [Council packet 11-07-23 p.512]. 11 sanitary service areas; EDU = 250 gpd [Council digest 2025-03-18]. Water/sewer plan not posted; amendments only via hearing notices [Coverage Report].
- **Capacity (live issue):** available EDUs Ocean Pines 9,700, Mystic Harbour 3,000, Riddle Farm 925, Assateague Pointe 535, Landings 400; systems over 80% of permits [Council digest 2026-06-02]. Ocean City sewer purchase of 170,000 gpd (est. $1.08M to $1.3M; MDE cut Landings plant permit to 36,000 gpd) [Council digest 2025-09-16]; West Ocean City SSA expanded [Res. 25-32]. Ocean Pines plant fell short on cold-weather nitrogen removal [Council digest 2026-03-17].
- **Amendments:** 5 PC and 28 Council digest rows 2023 to 2026, mostly Riddle Farm, Mystic Harbour, West Ocean City.
- Connection or equity charges for new lots: **not found in corpus**. Unimproved-lot accessibility charge, e.g. $204 per quarter per EDU, Assateague Pointe [Res. 26-14].
