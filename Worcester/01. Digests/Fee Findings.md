# Worcester County — Fee Findings

*Prepared 2026-10-11. Citations: [Res 25-11] = Fees/Resolution 25-11 DRP FY26 Fees (eff. 7/1/2025); [Env 2024] = Fees/Schedule-Fees-2024 (rev. 6/24/2024); [Res 26-14] = Fees/Assessments and Charges (FY27 W&S).*

## Impact fee / excise tax
- **None found.** No development impact fee, school/public-facilities excise tax or building excise tax appears in the code (searched "impact fee", "excise tax", "adequate public facilities") or in county fee resolutions. ZS 1-118(7) only lists "imposing impact fees and exactions" as a Commissioner power; none has been adopted [§ ZS 1-118].
- The 2006 comp plan only proposes to "explore" an APFO, impact fees and an excise tax [Plans: finalcomp31406 p.70]; the 2026 draft plan says impact fees "will be pursued" for water resources [Plans: 2026 Comp Plan PC Adopted p.244]. Recent RPC/rezoning terms make developers responsible for any impact fees "if assigned" [Council 2026.05.19 p.200]. A state bill on earlier vesting/impact-fee timing failed in 2026 [Council 2026.06.16 p.233].
- Amount per home: $0 today. Watch for future adoption.

## Health dept / septic (Environmental Programs) [Env 2024]
- Sketch plan: no fee. Minor subdivision review, private W&S: $180; public W&S up to 5 lots $100 (>5: $200).
- Major subdivision plat review: $150 min (first 5 lots) + $25/lot over 5.
- Sewage evaluation (perc): $450 per lot/unit (minor); major: $350 each for 6 or fewer lots, $1,800 min for more.
- Sewage permit: $400 new system; $325 drainfield replacement; $475 non-conventional. Well: $160.
- Forest conservation: minor subdivision $150; major $250 + $25/lot. Critical Area review same structure.
- Stormwater plan review schedule is in the corpus (Fees/Stormwater Management Plan Review Fee Schedule). FY26 Env. revision was in packet images (2025.05.20 pp.7–14) and not read; confirm.

## Water & sewer charges
- FY27 (7/1/2026–6/30/2027) quarterly user rates, assessments and "unimproved lot accessibility" charges per EDU by service area (e.g. Ocean Pines $219.45 min W&S; $186.53 accessibility) [Res 26-14].
- One-time connection/equity charges for new lots: not found — confirm with county (Public Works). County may collect "connection charges" tied to legacy debt [§ PW 5-203(c)].
