# Worcester County By-Right Playbook

Citations: § = Worcester County Code (`Worcester/Code/`); [Fee Findings] = `01. Digests/Fee Findings.md`; [MD Summary] = `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`; [Summary] = `Worcester/00. Summary.md`. Estimates are labeled.

## 1. Bottom line

The path is a **minor subdivision**: up to **5 lots** (6 with a rural cluster in A districts) counted cumulatively from the parcel as it stood **July 25, 1967** [§ ZS 1-311(a)]. The **Department of Development Review and Permitting (DRP)** decides, and its approval counts as Planning Commission approval; there is no hearing [§ ZS 2-302(c)]. **No public notice of any kind is required**: the minor-plat procedure (§§ ZS 2-301 to 2-304) has no adjoining-owner letter, sign, publication or agenda step. Typical time to a recorded plat is roughly **3 to 6 months (estimate)**, dominated by perc testing and septic sign-off. The main risk is that DRP may convert a minor into a major or send it to the Planning Commission at its discretion [§ ZS 2-304].

## 2. Eligibility test

A parcel stays on the path only if every box checks.

- [ ] **Zoning:** minor subdivisions are a permitted use in A-1 and A-2 [§ ZS 1-201(b)(7)] and also in E-1, V-1, R-1 to R-4 [Summary §2]. In RP only by special exception (hearing, so off the path) [§ ZS 1-215(c)(3)].
- [ ] **Lot size (A-1/A-2):** 40,000 sf minimum, 200 ft width; front setback 35 ft, sides 20 ft, rear 50 ft [§ ZS 1-201(b)(5)]. E-1: 1 per 2 net acres, 80,000 sf [§ ZS 1-203(b)(4)]. V-1 and R districts: see [Summary §2].
- [ ] **Cumulative cap:** total lots created out of the single parcel as of **7/25/1967** may not exceed 5, counting earlier subdivisions of the same original tract [§ ZS 1-311(a), "Subdivision, Minor/Major"]. Undeveloped lots transferred to a bona fide conservation entity are not counted. Pull the full chain of title and prior plats before buying.
- [ ] **Rural cluster option (A, E):** lots 40,000 to 90,000 sf, one extra lot beyond minor, compact layout, lot lines 200 ft or more from the road, 20 ft access easement; reviewed as a minor [§ ZS 1-308(b)(3),(5),(6),(7)]. Not available if 3 or more non-conforming lots were previously recorded from the 1967 parcel [§ ZS 1-308(b)(8)]. Rural cluster lots are set back from the road, so they do not fit a pure road-frontage plan.
- [ ] **Septic:** every lot needs a passing perc and 10,000 sf area for an initial plus two replacement systems [MD Summary, COMAR 26.04.02.04F]. Worcester never adopted SB 236 tier maps; the Commissioners have said a property is not eligible for a major subdivision on private septic, so 6 or more lots effectively need public sewer [Summary §1; Council minutes 2025-11-04 p.19, confirm with staff]. Expect BAT (nitrogen-removing) systems; required in the Critical Area, likely countywide under the draft 2026 plan [MD Summary; Summary §4].
- [ ] **Critical Area:** RCA allows 1 dwelling per 20 acres, no variance [§ NR 3-108(c)(3)], so RCA parcels do not work. Avoid the 100 ft buffer [§ NR 3-104]. LDA/IDA: lot coverage limits (15% of site; lots of 1 ac or less capped at 15% overall) and no slopes over 15% [§ NR 3-107(c)(5),(6)]. Growth allocation is a Commissioner map amendment (off the path) [§ NR 3-112].
- [ ] **Frontage and no new road:** each new lot needs **35 ft of frontage on a public or approved private road**, otherwise a variance [§ ZS 2-502(d)(4)]. Roads and access: a minor that creates a new road or an access easement serving more than 2 lots must be tied to State Plane coordinates [§ ZS 2-303(a)(2)]. Plan on frontage lots only.
- [ ] **No remnant:** all of the tract should be taken up in lots, roads, or other uses [§ ZS 2-502(b)(7)]. Remainder over 5 ac is only described generally; 5 ac or less must be surveyed and shown as a lot [§ ZS 2-303(a)(2),(3)].
- [ ] **Flood and ag setbacks:** FEMA zone statement on the plat [§ ZS 2-303(b)(16)]; agricultural-protection setback to ag structures within 200 ft of new lines [§ ZS 1-305(r); § ZS 2-303(b)(14)].
- [ ] **Not in a moratorium or plan overlay** [§ ZS 2-502(b)(8)]; Res. 26-20 (Route 589 map amendments) is not a subdivision bar [Summary §7].

## 3. Step-by-step path

Fees are FY26 (eff. 7/1/2025); FY27 DRP schedule was not found [Fee Findings]. Durations are estimates unless a code clock is cited.

1. **Pre-application call** (DRP staff, Environmental Programs). Confirm zoning, 1967 parent parcel history, cumulative count, Critical Area status and sewer category. No fee; sketch plan is optional and free [Fee Findings]. 1 to 2 weeks (estimate).
2. **Boundary survey and 1967 lot-count research** (surveyor, title). Every new lot, and a remainder of 5 ac or less, must be surveyed by a Maryland PLS or PLS-property line surveyor [§ ZS 2-303(a)(2)]. 3 to 6 weeks (estimate).
3. **Perc / soils testing** (Environmental Programs; engineer or soil scientist). Apply for sewage evaluation, $450 per lot [Fee Findings]. Test when the water table is highest, generally **Feb to Apr** on Shore soils [MD Summary, COMAR 26.04.02.04B, 26.04.03.02C]; verify the county's season with Environmental Programs. Outside the window, expect to wait for the next season. Typical 3 to 8 weeks inside the window (estimate), up to 9 months if missed.
4. **Forest conservation** (DRP). Applies at 40,000 sf or more [§ NR 1-403(a)]; exempt inside the Critical Area (governed by Critical Area law) [§ NR 1-403(b)(2)]. A simplified FSD/FCP by a surveyor, forester or landscape architect is submitted with the plat and shown on it [§ NR 1-405(a)-(c)]. DRP responds within 15 days [§ NR 1-405(d)]; approval needed before recordation [§ NR 1-405(e)]. Fee $150 [Fee Findings]. Afforestation floors 20% (A-1, A-2, RP, E-1), 15% in R districts [§ NR 1-408(a)(1)]. Fee-in-lieu is the applicant's choice and needs no BZA approval [§ NR 1-405(c)(9)]; rate 30.5 cents per sf of required planting inside a priority funding area [§ NR 1-413].
5. **Wetland delineation / floodplain check** if mapped features exist; unverified wetland line triggers a plat note [§ ZS 2-303(b)(18)D].
6. **Submit minor plat package to DRP:** executed application, 4 paper plats (or electronic at DRP's discretion), fee $250, FCP, perc results [§ ZS 2-302(a)(1)-(2); Fee Findings]. Environmental Programs minor review fee: $180 private W&S ($100 public up to 5 lots). Plat contents per § ZS 2-303(b), including signature blocks for the Planning Commission and MDE designee [§ ZS 2-303(b)(19),(20)].
7. **Acceptance and review.** DRP may decline an incomplete plat [§ ZS 2-302(a)(3)]. After acceptance DRP reviews zoning compliance, forwards immediately to Environmental Programs, and reports comments **within 30 calendar days** [§ ZS 2-302(b)]. 4 to 8 weeks including one revision round (estimate).
8. **Stormwater / sediment control.** Developments disturbing 5,000 sf or less cumulatively are exempt [§ NR 1-104(b)(3)]. No stormwater plan is required at plat stage per § ZS 2-303; plans are reviewed with each house permit (stormwater plan $275 minimum per house) [Summary §6]. If we sell lots without pulling permits, this cost falls to the builder. Confirm with DRP that no ESD concept is demanded with the plat.
9. **Construction plans (only if roads, drainage or utilities are required):** $150 for a minor [Fee Findings]. Avoided if frontage lots on existing roads.
10. **Road dedication.** Lot lines are set 25 ft from the centerline of an existing county road; land between is dedicated [§ ZS 2-502(c)(2)B]. Include the dedication statement on the plat [§ ZS 2-303(b)(18)C].
11. **Sign-off and Mylars.** After DRP approval, submit 3 Mylars to Clerk of Court standards and 10 paper copies signed in ink by owner and surveyor, plus recording fees [§ ZS 2-302(c)(1)]. Environmental Programs signs; then a Planning Commission officer signs [§ ZS 2-302(c)(1)]. 2 to 4 weeks (estimate).
12. **Recording.** DRP records the plat in the land records of Worcester County at the Circuit Court; $5 per sheet [§ ZS 2-303(c); Fee Findings]. **Rights vest only on recordation**; after recording lots may be sold and permits issued [§ ZS 2-302(c)(2); § ZS 2-303(c)]. 1 to 2 weeks (estimate).

**Total: low 2.5, typical 4, high 8 months, estimate.** No minor-subdivision timing is minuted in the Planning Commission record; only the 30-day code clock (step 7) and 15-day FCP clock (step 4) are cited. Major-subdivision timing is much longer (TRC 60 days per stage) and is not our path.

## 4. Public notice

**None required on this path.** § ZS 2-302 sets submission, 30-day staff review, DRP approval (constituting PC approval), signature and recording, and contains no adjoining-owner letter, sign posting, published notice, or Planning Commission agenda step. Searches of the code for notice or posting in connection with minor plats returned nothing applicable. Notice appears in Worcester only where a hearing exists: BZA variances and special exceptions (adjoiner letters; certified letters for adjoiners since April 2023 [Council packet 030723, p.187]) and Commissioner map amendments (15-day posting and mailed notice [Council digest]). Those are off-ramps, not part of the path. The one caveat: DRP may "at any time" forward a minor plat to the Planning Commission, which would put it on an agenda [§ ZS 2-304]. A rural cluster minor is also reviewed under the same minor procedure with no notice [§ ZS 1-308(b)(7)].

Stormwater plan is a building-permit item.

## 6. Off-ramps

| Trigger | Result | Design around it |
|---|---|---|
| 6th lot from the 1967 parcel (cumulative) | Major subdivision, Planning Commission, and effectively needs public sewer [§ ZS 1-311(a); Council 2025-11-04 p.19] | Count the chain of title first; stay at 5 (or 6 via rural cluster in A/E) |
| DRP finds "major impact" | Forced to major review or PC [§ ZS 2-304] | Pre-meet with staff; keep access and impacts simple |
| Variance (e.g., frontage under 35 ft, width, setback) | BZA public hearing with notice; 95.7% granted historically [Summary §1] but it is a hearing | Design lots to meet 35 ft frontage and 200 ft width |
| Special exception (RP zone) | BZA hearing [§ ZS 1-215(c)(3)] | Avoid RP |
| New road or access easement serving 3 or more lots | Plan tied to State Plane; road standards and PC role [§ ZS 2-303(a)(2); § ZS 2-502(c)] | Frontage lots on existing road; flag lots share driveways only with cross easements |
| Failed perc, no 10,000 sf area | Lot dropped | Test early in season; size lots with margin |
| Rezoning or map amendment | Commissioner hearing | Buy in a zone that already allows the use |
| Growth allocation (Critical Area) | Commissioner map amendment [§ NR 3-112] | Avoid RCA/LDA reliance |
| W&S plan amendment or sewer category change | Hearing with notice | Stay on private septic and well within 5 lots |
| Rural cluster non-conformance (3 prior lots) | Cluster unavailable [§ ZS 1-308(b)(8)] | Use standard minor |
| Moratorium or code change before recordation | Rights vest only on recording [§ ZS 2-303(c)] | Move quickly; 2026 Comp Plan and zoning rewrite pending |

## 7. Open questions to ask staff

1. Actual elapsed time from acceptance to recording for recent minors (DRP, Environmental Programs); is 30 days the real turnaround?
2. Official perc/wet-season window and whether Environmental Programs accepts consultant-run tests or uses its own staff.
3. Does DRP require any stormwater or ESD submission at plat stage for a 3 to 5 lot minor, or is it entirely at building permit?
4. Is BAT required on all new septic systems now, and what is the exact lot-size or setback implication?
5. How does DRP determine the 1967 parent parcel and cumulative count (research method, prior plat database), and how often has it used § ZS 2-304 to force major review?
6. Current FY27 fee schedule, and any sewer EDU or connection charges for lots in sewered areas (Ocean Pines, Mystic Harbour, etc.) and whether the Route 589 moratorium or the 2026 Comp Plan adoption affects minors.
