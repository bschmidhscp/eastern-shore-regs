# § PW 1-101. General powers of County Commissioners.

The County Commissioners of Worcester County shall have control over the public roads, bridges, drains, watercourses, public landings, culverts and curbs and gutters along the same in Worcester County, subject only to the existing rights and duties of the State Roads Commission of Maryland.
