# § PW 1-207. Utilities in rights-of-way.

*[Added 4-6-1993 by Bill No. 93-4; amended 11-14-1995 by Bill No. 95-15; 5-16-2000 by Bill No. 00-4; 5-15-2001 by Bill No. 01-6]*

Subject to applicable construction standards as established by resolution of the County Commissioners, utility lines may be located underground within the right-of-way, provided that only water lines, sewer lines, stormwater management drainage lines, and crossovers for other utilities may be located within the paved section of the right-of-way, that electrical and gas utilities may not be located less than ten feet beyond the paved section, and that in the case of utility lines in the paved section, all required laterals must be provided upon initial installation of the utility line. This shall not prohibit later installation of laterals upon replatting or changes in service. No utility structures or other obstructions may be located within fifteen feet of a fire hydrant.
