# § PW 5-204. Powers of County Commissioners.

*[Added 10-5-1993 by Bill No. 93-19]*

(a) Exercise powers of Sanitary District. Upon the abolishment of the Sanitary District and Sanitary Commission,[1] the County Commissioners shall exercise all the powers of a water or sewer authority or Sanitary District or Commission.

> Note: [1] The Sanitary Commission was abolished by Bill No. 93-19.

(b) Power to regulate, acquire, operate and set fees. The County Commissioners may:

  (1) Adopt, by resolution, regulations for water and sewerage management.

  (2) Acquire, construct, operate and maintain water and sewerage systems as the County Commissioners consider to be in the public interest and necessary to protect the general health and welfare.

  (3) Set, by resolution, rates, fees and assessments for water and sewerage services and benefits.
