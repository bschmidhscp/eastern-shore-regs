# § PW 2-104. Revocation or suspension of license.

(a) Notice and hearing. A commercial hauler license may be revoked or suspended by the County Commissioners after notice and hearing.

(b) Cause. Causes for revocation or suspension are as follows:

  (1) Fraud or misrepresentation in obtaining a license.

  (2) Violation of any law, statute, regulation, rule or standard relating to the license or any activity conducted thereunder.
