# § CL 1-203. Violations and penalties.

Upon conviction of a violation of this Subtitle, a person shall be subject to a fine not to exceed one hundred dollars or imprisonment for not more than thirty days for the first conviction and a fine not to exceed two hundred dollars or imprisonment for sixty days for the second conviction.
