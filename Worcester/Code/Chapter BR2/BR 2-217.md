# § BR 2-217. Appeals.

(a) Court review. Any person who is an interested party and is aggrieved by any decision of the Board of Electrical Examiners may appeal the same to the Circuit Court for Worcester County. If, upon the review of the proceedings of the Board, it shall appear to the Court that additional testimony is necessary for the proper disposition of the matter, it may take evidence or appoint a referee to take such evidence as it may direct and report the same to the Court with his findings of fact and conclusions of law, which shall constitute a part of the proceedings upon which the determination of the Court shall be made. The Court may reverse or affirm, wholly or partly, or may modify the decision brought up for review.

(b) Court review procedure. An appeal to the Circuit Court of Worcester County, Maryland, shall be taken in the manner provided by the Maryland Rules 7-201 et seq.
