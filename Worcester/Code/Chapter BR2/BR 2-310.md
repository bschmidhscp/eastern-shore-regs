# § BR 2-310. Subsequent amendments.

To the extent that any other ordinance or parts of ordinances are inconsistent with the provisions of this ordinance, the terms of this ordinance shall prevail as to floodplain management issues. This ordinance shall be amended as required by the Federal Emergency Management Agency, Title 44, Code of Federal Regulations, as from time to time amended. All subsequent amendments to this ordinance are subject to the approval of the Federal Emergency Management Agency and the Maryland Department of the Environment.
