# § PH 2-101. Recruitment program.

(a) Funding to recruit physicians. The County Commissioners may expend funds for the recruitment of physicians to serve the needs of the people of Worcester County and the sojourners therein.

(b) Physician Recruitment Program. The County Commissioners may establish a Physician Recruitment Program to provide for the recruitment of physicians.
