# § PW 3-102. Engineers.

Before undertaking said work, said County Commissioners of Worcester County shall select a suitable engineer and the County Commissioners of Wicomico County shall likewise select a suitable engineer, and said two engineers shall jointly proceed to lay out a swamp district in each of said counties and ascertain the number of acres of land in said swamp district and also the number of acres in each county and shall make suitable plots of the same and shall lay off the swampland in each county into divisions or zones according to the benefits it will receive, in their judgment, from said drainage and the total cost, in their judgment, of draining said land.
