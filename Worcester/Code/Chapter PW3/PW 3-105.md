# § PW 3-105. Benefit assessments.

The County Commissioners of each of said counties are hereby fully authorized and empowered to levy in their respective counties, upon the swamplands and lands drained in each district, such sum of money as may be necessary to pay each county's proportion in payment of said work, and the County Commissioners of each county are hereby fully authorized to levy such sum of money in one year or extend it over a period not exceeding five years in equal amounts each year, with an additional sum each year to pay the interest upon the unpaid portions of said money to be levied. Said County Commissioners in each of said counties are hereby authorized, in making said levy upon said swamplands and lands drained, to have the right to levy upon each swamp district or zone in each of said counties such proportion of said taxes as they may deem proper according to benefits received so as to make said tax fair and equitable.
