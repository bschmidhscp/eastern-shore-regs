# § CG 2-101. Election; number; qualification.

*[Amended 5-18-1993 by Bill No. 93-6; 8-20-2002 by Bill No. 02-11]*

At the general election to be held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November 2002 and every fourth year thereafter, there shall be elected seven County Commissioners who shall be qualified voters of the county and have resided at least two years in Worcester County immediately preceding their election.
