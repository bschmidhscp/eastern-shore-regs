# § CG 2-201. Commissioners' meetings.

*[Added 8-19-1986 by Bill No. 86-6; amended 8-20-2002 by Bill No. 02-11]*

The Commissioners shall meet at least two days each month and shall remain in session so long as may be necessary to properly transact the business of the County as established on the meeting agenda. The Commissioners shall meet at such other times after proper notice as a majority of the entire Board may determine.
