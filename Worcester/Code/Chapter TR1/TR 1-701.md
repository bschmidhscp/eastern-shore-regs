# § TR 1-701. Imposition and rate of tax.

(a) Tax imposed. A recordation tax, as authorized by and subject to Title 12 of the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, is imposed upon instruments of writing recorded with the Clerk of the Circuit Court of Worcester County.

(b) Rate. Effective September 1, 1991, the recordation tax rate in Worcester County shall be two dollars and twenty cents for each five hundred dollars or fraction of five hundred dollars of the consideration payable or of the principal amount of the debt secured for an instrument of writing. Effective September 1, 1997, the recordation tax rate in Worcester County shall be three dollars and thirty cents for each five hundred dollars or fraction of five hundred dollars of the consideration payable or of the principal amount of the debt secured for an instrument of writing. The consideration includes the amount of any mortgage or deed of trust assumed by the grantee.[Amended 6-10-1997 by Bill No. 97-11]
