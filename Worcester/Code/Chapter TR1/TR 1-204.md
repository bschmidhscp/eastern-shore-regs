# § TR 1-204. When taxes are deemed to be in arrears; notice to delinquent taxpayers.

On the first day of October next succeeding each levy, taxes shall be deemed to be in arrears, and interest shall be charged and collected on all taxes not then paid from the first day of October, the date when said taxes become due and in arrears; and within ninety days after the first day of October, the Treasurer shall deliver or mail to the last known post office address of each delinquent taxpayer an account of his assessment and the taxes and interest due thereon, with a notice of said delinquency thereto attached that, unless payment is made in full, the same will be collected by process of law; and it shall be the duty of the Treasurer to enforce the payment of all taxes remaining unpaid not later than two years after said taxes become in arrears, by sale of either real or personal property.
