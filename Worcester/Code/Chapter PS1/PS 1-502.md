# § PS 1-502. Open burning.

(a) Generally. A person may not cause or permit open burning except as provided in this Subtitle.

(b) Discretion of Control Officer or Fire Marshal. A person may not knowingly cause or permit open burning as allowed by this Subtitle when, in the sole discretion and judgement of either the Control Officer or the Fire Marshal:

  (1) A condition of air stagnation exists;

  (2) Any national or state ambient air quality standard may be violated;

  (3) The open burning causes unreasonable interference with a person's health, safety, comfort, or use or enjoyment of their real property;

  (4) The open burning creates a nuisance; or

  (5) The open burning is conducted without a required permit issued pursuant hereto.

(c) Supervision. All open burnings as allowed by this Subtitle shall remain under supervision until completely extinguished. Supervision shall include the tending thereof by sufficient personnel who are adequately equipped and trained.
