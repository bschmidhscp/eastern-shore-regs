# § ZS 1-332. Unsafe buildings and storm damage.

Nothing in this Title shall prevent the temporary strengthening to a safe condition of any part of any building or structure declared unsafe by proper authority, to protect from the elements a structure or building damaged by an act of nature or to protect from trespass until such time as it shall be restored or fully demolished in accordance with this Title.
