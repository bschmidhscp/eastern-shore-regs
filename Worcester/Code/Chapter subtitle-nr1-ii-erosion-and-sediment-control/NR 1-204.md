# § NR 1-204. Exemptions.

(a) Exemptions. The following activities are exempt from the provisions of this Subtitle:

  (1) Agricultural land management practices, agricultural BMPs and construction of agricultural structures.

  (2) Clearing and grading activities that disturb less than five thousand square feet of land area and disturb less than one hundred cubic yards of earth.

  (3) Clearing and grading activities that are subject exclusively to state approval and enforcement under state law and regulations.
