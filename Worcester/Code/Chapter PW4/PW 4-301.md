# § PW 4-301. Works of improvement authorized.

The Board of County Commissioners of Worcester County is hereby authorized and empowered to carry out, maintain and operate the following works of improvement: (1) soil conservation; (2) water conservation; (3) flood prevention (including structural and land treatment measures); (4) agricultural phases of conservation, development, utilization and disposal of water, including group drainage projects.
