# § PW 4-303. Economic development projects.

(a) Authorities. The Board of County Commissioners of Worcester County is hereby authorized and empowered to carry out, maintain, operate and make appropriation for the following types of economic development projects: (1) industrial parks; (2) sewer projects; (3) water projects; (4) drainage projects; (5) utility projects; (6) erosion projects; (7) transportation projects; (8) such other projects similar in nature as approved from time to time by the County Commissioners.

(b) Funding appropriations. The County Commissioners may make grants or appropriations for financial assistance to other political subdivisions in Worcester County for economic development projects located in Worcester County upon such criteria as the Commissioners may from time to time determine by resolution.
