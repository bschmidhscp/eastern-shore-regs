# § PS 6-201. Name, purpose and intent.

(a) Name. The regulations hereby adopted shall constitute and shall hereafter be known as the "Worcester County Public Safety Radio Coverage Code" and may be cited and is hereinafter referred to as the "Public Safety Radio Coverage Code."

(b) Purpose and intent. It is the intent of this Subtitle to ensure the uninterrupted operation of Worcester County's public safety, law enforcement, other emergency-related and County operational wireless communications systems. It is the purpose of this Subtitle to require, as herein provided, that all persons or entities constructing, erecting or maintaining buildings and structures or equipment in Worcester County do so in a manner which does not interfere with the effective use of the County's wireless communications network and provides for the appropriate facilities necessary to eliminate interference and provide for adequate public safety radio coverage.
