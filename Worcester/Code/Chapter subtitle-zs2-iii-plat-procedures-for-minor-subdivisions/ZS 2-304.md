# § ZS 2-304. Processing of minor subdivisions as major subdivisions.

In the event that the Department determines that a proposed subdivision meeting the definition of a minor subdivision will or may have a major impact upon the County or surrounding properties, it may require that the subdivision be processed as a major subdivision subject to all requirements of Subtitle ZS2:IV hereof. In addition, the Department may at any time it deems necessary forward a minor subdivision plat to the Planning Commission for its review and/or approval, subject to the requirements of Subtitle ZS2:III.
