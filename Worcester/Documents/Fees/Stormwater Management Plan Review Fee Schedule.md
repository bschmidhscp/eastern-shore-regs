STORMWATER MANAGEMENT PLAN REVIEW 

FEE SCHEDULE 

### (1) Single Family Dwellings: 

   - A. $3.00 per 1,000 square feet of disturbance, with a minimum fee of $275.00 

   - B. Revisions to approved plans: $100.00 

- (2) Agricultural Structures and Uses; Restoration and Mitigation Projects 

   - A. $3.00 per 1,000 square feet of disturbance/stormwater design area, with a minimum fee of $400.00 

   - B. Revisions to approved plans: $175.00 base fee, plus $50.00 per hour beyond first hour of review. 

- (3) Multi-Family, Commercial, Industrial and Institutional Structures and Uses: 

   - A. $4.00 per 1,000 square feet of disturbance/stormwater design area, with a minimum fee of $700.00. 

   - B. Revisions to approved plans: $250.00 base fee, plus $50.00 per hour beyond the first hour of review. 

## (4) Waivers: $200.00 flat fee. 

Effective 7/1/25 - DMB
