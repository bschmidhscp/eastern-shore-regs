**<mark>3.0 – QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS</mark>** The following questions and answers are provided as a matter of information to clarify issues raised about the <u>Proposal Documents.</u> **<u>Item Questions and Answers</u>** In Section IV, Task 3, the County requests an evaluation of allocation between Base Fee and Usage Fees. In the County’s rate schedule (as presented in the “Notice of Public Hearing Worcester County Water and Wastewater Enterprise Funds FY 2027 Requested Budgets and Assessments”), only the SSA Edgewater Acres appears to have a “Domestic Water and Sewer Base Fee”. Can the County provide a clarification on what it refers to as the Base Fee in the context of 1 this Rate Study? 7 of the 11 service areas have a base fee and a usage fee. The 4 that do not are either non-metered areas of the County or are sewer-only districts (Assateague Pointe, Briddletown, Lighthouse Sound, and West Ocean City). We would like to evaluate the art and science of effectively charging the base fee and the <u>usage fee with the caveat that most areas are seasonal and usage does drop off, outside of irrigation.</u>

|For example, is it more prudent to charge a higher usage fee that in the summer months customers so a<br>relatively high bill, but in the winter months when minimal usage is occurring, they see the<br>corresponding drop.
