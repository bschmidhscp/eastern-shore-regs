# RESOLUTION NO. 26-2] 

## RESOLUTION BUDGET AMENDMENT 

## FOR FISCAL YEAR 2027 

Recitals 

- A. Worcester County Code PW Section 5-310, the County Commissioners adopted a Resolution on June 2, 2026, adopting the expense budget and establishing the tax rate for Fiscal Year 2027. 

- B. The County Commissioners have identified an additional expense to be included in Fiscal Year 2027 expense budget. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE RESOLVED by the County Commissioners of 

Worcester County, Maryland that: 

1. The County Commissioners adopt a budget amendment for Fiscal Year 2027 that the Fiscal Year 2027 budget be modified as follows: 

Increase in the following expense line items: 

e 570.9010.030 Capital Equipment Other WWW Equipment $ 331,300 

Increase in the following revenue line items: 

e 570.5975.100 Transfer From (To) Reserves $ 331,300 

AND BE RESOLVED that this Resolution will become effective September 15, 2026.
