# § 172-24. Conditions.

A commercial or industrial business is eligible to apply for a tax credit under this article if it:

A. Makes a substantial investment in Talbot County;

B. Is currently or will be doing business in Talbot County; and

C. Enters into an agreement with Talbot County under § 172-27 of this article.
