# § 172-25. Amount and duration of credit.

A. Subject to Subsection C of this section, and except as provided in § 172-28 of this article, the tax credit under this article shall be granted annually for a term not to exceed 10 years beginning with the first tax year in which the real property tax would increase as a result of an increase in assessment due to new construction or an improvement to an existing facility.

B. As part of the procedure under § 172-26 of this article, the County Council shall establish the tax credit in an amount expressed as a percent of the additional County tax imposed on real property that is a result of an increase in assessment due to the new construction or improvement.

C. Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, the tax credit under this article may not exceed the amount of County property tax imposed on the property that is a result of an increase in assessment due to the new construction or improvement.
