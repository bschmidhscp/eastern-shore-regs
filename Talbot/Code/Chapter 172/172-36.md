# § 172-36. Eligibility.

In order to be eligible for a clean energy loan, the property owner, or in the case of multiple owners of the same property, property owners, shall:

A. Have a 100% ownership interest in the commercial property for which improvements are proposed;

B. Demonstrate that the most recent property taxes, assessments, and charges on the property have been paid;

C. Provide a copy of written notice to all current holders of a mortgage or deed of trust who have a priority recorded lien on the property and written proof of express consent to the clean energy loan as a priority lien by all current holders of a mortgage or deed of trust on the property;

D. Obtain an energy audit approved under program guidelines demonstrating that the savings projected to be obtained from the improvements over the life of the improvements equal or exceed the principal and aggregate interest to be paid over the term of the loan; and

E. Establish that the owner(s) of the commercial property is able to repay the loan provided under the clean energy loan program, in a manner substantially similar to that required for a mortgage loan under Md. Code Ann., Commercial Law Art., §§ 12-127, 12-311, 12-409.1, 12-925, and 12-1029.
