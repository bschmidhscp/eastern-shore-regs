# § 172-29. Annual report.

A. The Economic Development Office shall report annually to the County Manager and the County Council concerning the implementation of the tax credit under this article.

B. The report under this section shall:

  (1) Be submitted no later than October 1 of each year; and

  (2) Include, for each recipient of a credit under this article during the previous fiscal year:

    (a) The name of the recipient; and

    (b) The number of jobs created as a result of the activity that qualified the recipient for the credit and the salary for each such job; and

    (c) The amount of the credit granted.
