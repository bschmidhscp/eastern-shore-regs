## Relevance to small subdivisions

None of the enacted bills changes minor subdivision thresholds, lot sizes or density. Bill 1627 matters
for parcels carrying reserved-land / reserved-development-rights agreements tied to MALPF easement
options. The steady stream of CWSP amendments shows that adding individual parcels to sewer service
areas is a routine, repeatable process in Talbot.
