# § 107-5. Issuance of license; term and display; lost or stolen licenses.

A. Upon furnishing the information required under § 107-3, the applicant shall be issued a license by the Talbot County Sheriff as prescribed by the County Council of Talbot County at the fee established by the Council.

B. A license issued under this chapter shall be good for one year, beginning on January 1 and expiring on December 31 of the same year, unless revoked as provided for in this chapter.

C. Each licensee shall at all times have on his person, while engaged in the purpose licensed under this chapter, the license issued hereunder and shall exhibit the same to any County official or police officer upon request.

D. If a peddler's license is lost or stolen, such shall be reported to the Talbot County Sheriff's Department within 10 days. Upon notification, the Department shall issue a replacement license at a cost to be set by the Common Council in the annual budget process.
