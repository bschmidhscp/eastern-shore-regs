# § 190-1. Title and authority.

1.1 Title and effective date.

  A. This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the "Talbot County Zoning, Subdivision, and Land Development Ordinance" or "Chapter 190."

  B. The effective date of this amended Chapter 190 is November 10, 2018.

1.2 Statutory authority. Talbot County is organized pursuant to Art. XI-A of the Maryland Constitution and derives its authority for planning, zoning, and subdivision from the Land Use Article, Annotated Code of Maryland.

1.3 Critical Area. The County's local Critical Area Program is adopted pursuant to Natural Resources Article § 8-1801, et seq., Maryland Annotated Code. The Code of Maryland Regulations (COMAR) Title 27, Critical Area Commission for the Chesapeake and Atlantic Coastal Bays, also applies to the Critical Area.
