# § 148-11. Publication of required notice.

Any notice of publication required by this chapter shall be:

A. Published at least once each week for three consecutive weeks in one or more newspapers published in the County, having a circulation in the area affected by such notice, or by giving written notice to all persons affected thereby.

B. By posting of such notice in conspicuous public places in the area to be affected.
