# § 148-24. Authority.

*[Amended 7-24-2001 by Bill No. 837 ]*

The provisions of this chapter are enacted pursuant to the authority contained in both the County Charter and the laws of Maryland, including but not limited to Md. Code Ann., Environment Art., § 9-704.
