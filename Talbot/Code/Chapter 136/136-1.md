# § 136-1. Salaries established.

The salary of the Orphans Court Judges for Talbot County shall be established at the beginning of each term, at $5,500, to include an annual cost-of-living increase of 2%. The Chief Judge shall receive an additional $500 per annum. Once established, the salary of the Orphans Court Judges shall not be increased or decreased during the remainder of the term of office.
