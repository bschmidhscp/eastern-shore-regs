# § 97-16. Reimbursement not to be considered salary or compensation.

The salary and/or compensation for any elected official shall be only that amount set forth in an ordinance adopted for the purpose of setting forth said salary and/or compensation. Any other sums paid to an elected official as reimbursement for expenses incurred in connection with performance of his/her duty as an elected official shall not be deemed salary or compensation.
