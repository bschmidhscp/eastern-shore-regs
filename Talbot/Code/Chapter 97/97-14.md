# § 97-14. Interest and penalties on unpaid taxes.

*[Added 6-15-1982 by Bill No. 172 ]*

A. All taxes in arrears after the date set forth in Md. Code Ann., Art. 81, § 48, as the same may be amended from time to time by the General Assembly, shall bear interest from the date set forth in said code section at the rate of 2/3 of 1% for each month or fraction of a month thereafter until paid in full.

B. Any County taxes unpaid upon the date described in Subsection A above shall bear a penalty in the amount of 5/6 of 1% for each month or fraction of a month thereafter until paid in full.
