# § 97-17. Definitions.

For the purposes of this article, the following terms now have the meanings set forth below:

**COUNCIL** The Talbot County Council.

**COUNTY** Talbot County, Maryland.

**EMPLOYEE** All salaried employees of the County Council, but shall also include the County Attorney and Deputy County Attorney.

**OFFICIAL** Members of the County Council, Board of Appeals, the Planning Commission and all other persons appointed to their positions by the County Council, whether or not compensated.[Amended at time of adoption of Code (see Ch. 1, General Provisions, Art. I)]
