# § 64-4. Violations and penalties.

Any person violating these regulations shall be fined not more than $100 for each offense, and each and every day of operation without a permit shall constitute a separate offense. Notice of violation must be in writing. Penalties will be assessed for food service facilities at $25 for each 30 days past due.
