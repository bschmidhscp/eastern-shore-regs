# § 64-5. Additional license fee; purpose.

*[Amended 6-22-1993 by Bill No. 509 ]*

The Clerk of the Circuit Court shall charge an additional fee of $25 to applicants for marriage licenses, which fee shall be used to fund a local program to deal with domestic violence.
