# § 64-22. Appeals.

*[Amended at time of adoption of Code (see Ch. 1, General Provisions, Art. I)]*

Any person aggrieved by a final decision of a County official or the County Council may take an appeal. Appeals from decisions of a County official shall be to the County Board of Appeals in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 20, Board of Appeals, of the Talbot County Code. Appeals from decisions of the County Council shall be to the Circuit Court for Talbot County.
