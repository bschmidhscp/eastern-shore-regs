# § 64-7. Future domestic violence programs.

If the Mid-Shore Council on Family Violence, Inc., should cease to exist or fail to expend its funds as required hereby, the County Manager shall hold all accrued funds until the County Council for Talbot County, Maryland, approves a successor organization to administer domestic violence programs.
