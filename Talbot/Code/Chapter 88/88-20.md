# § 88-20. Transfer of ownership.

A property owner who has received a repair order, condemnation order, demolition order, confirmed order, modified order, or final order, or upon whom a civil citation has been served, may not sell, transfer, mortgage or otherwise dispose of the premises until:

A. The provisions of the order or citation have been complied with; or

B. The property owner shall first furnish the grantee, transferee or mortgagee a true copy of the notice or order issued by the Code Official and shall furnish to the Code Official a signed and notarized statement from the grantee, transferee or mortgagee, in which he/she acknowledges receipt of the notice or order. The property owner shall remain responsible for the repairs, improvements, or corrective actions unless the notarized statement from the grantee, transferee or mortgagee states that he/she fully accepts and assumes the responsibilities, without condition, for making the corrections or repairs as required by the notice or order.
