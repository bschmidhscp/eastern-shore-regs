# § 60-16. Exemptions and modifications.

*[Amended at time of adoption of Code (see Ch. 1, General Provisions, Art. I)]*

The Commission may grant exemptions and modifications to the provisions of § 60-6 and § 60-14 of this chapter to nonelected officials and employees, when the Commission finds that an exemption or modification would not be contrary to the purposes of this chapter, and the application of this chapter would:

A. Constitute an unreasonable invasion of privacy; and

B. Significantly reduce the availability of qualified persons for public service.
