# § 56-2. Adoption of standards.

*[Amended 7-8-2008 by Bill No. 1117 ; 6-14-2022 by Bill No. 1499 ]*

The National Electrical Code, as published by the National Fire Protection Association, and as updated from time to time, is hereby adopted as the minimum standard for electrical work in Talbot County, Maryland.
