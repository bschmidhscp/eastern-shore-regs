# § 56-14. Fees.

*[Amended 6-14-2022 by Bill No. 1499 ]*

The Board shall establish a schedule of fees for a Talbot County registration as a master electrician or a Talbot County license as an electrician limited as required under the code, subject to the approval of the County Council. Such fee schedule may be altered or amended at the discretion of the Board, subject to the approval of the County Council.
