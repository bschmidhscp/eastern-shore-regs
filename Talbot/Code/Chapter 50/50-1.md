# § 50-1. Definitions.

In this chapter, the following words have the meanings indicated:

**AGREEMENT** A development rights and responsibilities agreement.

**COUNTY COUNCIL** The Talbot County Council.

**PLANNING COMMISSION** The Talbot County Planning Commission.
