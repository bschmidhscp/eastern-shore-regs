# § 50-7. Amendment of agreement.

A. Subject to Subsection B of this section and after a public hearing, the parties to an agreement may amend the agreement by mutual consent.

B. The parties may not amend an agreement unless the Planning Commission determines that the proposed amendment is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan.
