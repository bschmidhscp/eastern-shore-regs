# § 91-9. Deduction to compensate for collection.

The person collecting the tax may apply and credit against the amount of tax payable an amount equal to 1 1/2% of the gross tax to be remitted to the County, to cover the expense of collection and remittance of the tax. However, this section does not apply to any person who fails or refuses to file a return with the County within the time prescribed within this chapter.
