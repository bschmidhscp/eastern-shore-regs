# § 58-1. Applicability.

*[Amended 10-8-2024 by Bill No. 1578 ]*

The provisions of this chapter apply to every chapter, article, and section of this Code except Chapter 8 (Airport); Chapter 11 (Alcoholic Beverages); Chapter 15 (Animals); Chapter 56 (Electrical Standards); Chapter 102 (Parks and Recreation); Chapter 121 (Public Landings); and Chapter 159 (Smoking and Tobacco Products).
