# § 58-4. Persons responsible for violations.

The following persons may each be held jointly or severally responsible for a violation:

A. Persons who apply for or obtain any permit or approval;

B. Contractors;

C. Subcontractors;

D. Property owners;

E. Managing agents; or

F. Any other person who has committed, assisted, or participated in the violation.
