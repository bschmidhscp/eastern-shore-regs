# § 155-6. When effective.

*[Added 9-19-2006 by Bill No. 1003 ; amended 9-14-2021 by Bill No. 1492 ]*

In accordance with Article III, Section 35, of the Maryland Constitution, any increase or decrease in compensation is to become effective for the Sheriff’s term beginning in or after the year 2022.
