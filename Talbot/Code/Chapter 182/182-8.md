# § 182-8. Funding of program.

A. The Volunteer Emergency Service Longevity Award Program pension benefits program shall be funded as may from time to time be provided in the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance of the County Council.

B. In lieu of direct funding, the County Council may from time to time purchase a benefits program from an insurance or annuity company, financial institution or other private vendor to provide some or all of the benefits described above.
