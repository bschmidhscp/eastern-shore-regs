# § 182-4. Periodic adjustments.

Periodically, the Director and the Director of Finance shall review the benefits prescribed in this chapter, and such review may result in a recommendation to increase or decrease these benefits. Revised benefits, subject to approval of the County Council through the budget process, shall be effective on the first pay period in the new fiscal year.
