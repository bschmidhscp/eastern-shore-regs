# § 20-2. Composition.

The Board of Appeals shall consist of five members and three alternates appointed by the Council for three-year terms. The Board shall elect a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson annually at the first meeting of each calendar year. An alternate member shall act in the place of an absent or disabled Board member. Members of the Board may receive such compensation as deemed appropriate by the Council and may be removed by the Council for malfeasance in office upon written charges and after public hearing.
