# § 20-11. Notice of public hearing.

A. Content. The Secretary shall schedule and provide public notice of the date, time, and place of the hearing on each Application. The notice shall include the property address, a brief description of the property, a summary of the relief requested, and shall be published over the signature of the Secretary in a suitable format approved by the Board. The Applicant shall bear the expense of publication.

B. Advertisement. All public hearings shall be advertised in a newspaper of general circulation in the County not more than 15 days' prior to the date of the hearing.
