# § 20-5. Forms and fees.

Applications shall be filed on a form approved by the Board and accompanied by the filing fee as established by the Council. An Application that does not contain an appropriate Application form, filing fee, and accompanying material required by §§ 20-6 and 20-7 shall not be accepted for filing and shall be returned with the required, but missing or incomplete, material identified.
