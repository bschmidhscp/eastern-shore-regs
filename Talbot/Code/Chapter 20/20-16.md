# § 20-16. Order of presentation.

The order of presentation of evidence shall be as follows, with cross examination following all witnesses' direct testimony and examination by the Board at any time:

A. Presentation of the official record of exhibits by the Board, notation of any objections to any part of the record, and other preliminary matters;

B. Presentation of testimony and exhibits by the Applicant;

C. Presentation of testimony and exhibits by County officials and staff;

D. Presentation of testimony and exhibits by other persons opposed to the Application;

E. Rebuttal evidence by the Applicant;

F. Surrebuttal by parties opposing the Application;

G. Formal receipt into evidence of the record by the Board; and

H. Closing statements by each side.
