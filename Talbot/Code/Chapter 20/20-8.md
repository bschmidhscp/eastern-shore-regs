# § 20-8. Service of Application for administrative appeal.

Upon filing an Application for administrative appeal, the Applicant shall serve a copy on the County department head, or chairman of any County commission or agency responsible for interpretation, administration, and enforcement of the ordinance, rule, or regulation involved in the Application as well as any opposing parties and/or their attorney or agent(s).
