# § 24-1. Establishment; term; qualifications; compensation.

*[Amended 8-24-2001 by Bill No. 837 ]*

A. There is hereby created a Public Works Advisory Board which shall consist of five residents of the County who have been qualified voters of the County for at least two years, no more than one of whom shall reside in the same election district.

B. Members of the Board shall be appointed by the Council for terms of four years from the first day of May of the year of their appointment or until their successors are appointed and qualified.

C. Vacancies, except those at the expiration of a term, shall be filled in the same manner as original appointments and for the unexpired term.

D. Members of the Board shall receive no compensation for their services except reasonable and necessary expenses as may be provided in the budget.
