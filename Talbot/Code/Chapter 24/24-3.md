# § 24-3. Organization; open meetings.

The Board shall annually elect its Chairman and Vice Chairman. The County Engineer shall be the Executive Secretary to the Board and shall attend all meetings of the Board. Three members of the Board shall constitute a quorum for the conduct of its business. All meetings of the Board shall be open to the public.
