# § 34-40. Records required.

A. The grantee shall at all times maintain:

  (1) A record of all interruptions of service experience for the preceding period prior to a performance review.

  (2) A full and complete set of plans, records and as-built maps showing the exact location of all cable communication system equipment installed or in use in the County, exclusive of subscriber service drops.

  (3) A comprehensive record of all personnel transactions and utilization of contractors, subcontractors, vendors and suppliers by race and sex.

B. The County may impose reasonable requests for additional information, records and documents from time to time.
