# § 34-11. Franchise renewal.

Upon completion of the term of any franchise granted under this chapter, the franchise may be renewed under such terms and conditions as the parties may agree; provided, however, that any such renewal process shall be subject to, and governed by, any existing federal or state laws or regulations regarding same.
