# § 34-21. Performance evaluation.

A. The County and the grantee shall, at the discretion of the County, hold scheduled performance evaluation sessions annually. All such evaluation sessions shall be open to the public.

B. Special evaluation sessions may be held at any time during the term of the franchise at the request of the County.

C. During the review and evaluation by the County, the grantee shall fully cooperate with the County and shall provide such information and documents as the County may need to reasonably perform its review.
