# § 70-27. General requirements.

This section's requirements shall:

A. Apply in special flood hazard areas that are not identified as coastal high hazard areas (V Zones) and Coastal A Zones. These flood hazard areas, referred to collectively as "A Zones," include special flood hazard areas along nontidal waters, landward of coastal high hazard areas (V Zones), and landward of Coastal A Zones (if delineated).

B. Apply to all development, new construction, and substantial improvements (including repair of substantial damage).
