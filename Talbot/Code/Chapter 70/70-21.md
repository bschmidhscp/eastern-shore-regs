# § 70-21. Critical and essential facilities protection.

A. New critical and essential facilities shall be located outside coastal high hazard areas (V Zones), Coastal A Zones or floodways.

B. If located in special flood hazard areas other than coastal high hazard areas, Coastal A Zones or floodways, they shall be elevated to the higher of the elevation required by these regulations plus one foot, the elevation required by the building code, or the elevation of the 0.2% probability (500-year) flood.
