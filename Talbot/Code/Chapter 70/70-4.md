# § 70-4. Compliance required.

These regulations apply to all special flood hazard areas within the unincorporated areas of Talbot County, as identified in § 70-12, Flood Insurance Rate Map use and interpretation.

A. All development shall fully comply with these regulations and all other applicable regulations.

B. A permit is required for development in a special flood hazard area. Failure to obtain a permit shall be a violation of these regulations and shall be subject to penalties in accordance with § 70-49, Enforcement.

C. Permits issued on the basis of plans and applications approved by the Floodplain Administrator authorize only the specific activities set forth in the approved plans and applications. Development activities contrary to that authorization are a violation of these regulations.
