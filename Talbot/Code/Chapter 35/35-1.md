# § 35-1. Fund established.

There is a Community Reinvestment and Repair Special Revenue Fund (the "Fund"), into which shall be paid all monies received from the State Cannabis Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund established pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis § 1-3A-03. Monies from the Fund may only be used for the purposes expressly set forth in this article and shall be held separately from other County revenue and general funds.
