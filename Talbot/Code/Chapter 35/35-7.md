# § 35-7. Meetings.

The Commission shall meet at the call of the Chair, as frequently as required to perform its duties, but no less than four times per year. All meetings shall comply with the Maryland Open Meetings Act.[1] The Commission may adopt Rules of Procedure and Bylaws, not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter, to govern its affairs.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: See Md. Code Ann., General Provisions Art. § 3-101 et seq.
