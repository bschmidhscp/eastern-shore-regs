# § 35-3. Administration and use of the Fund.

A. The Talbot County Community Reinvestment and Repair Commission defined in Article II of this chapter shall administer the Fund in accordance with the County's fiscal policies.

B. Permitted uses. Monies from the Fund shall only be used for funding community-based initiatives, with or without nonprofit designation, intended to benefit:

  (1) Disproportionately Impacted Areas, which are defined as geographic areas identified by the State Office of Social Equity that had above 150% of the State's ten-year average for cannabis possession charges from 2013-2022;

  (2) Low-income communities;

  (3) Any related administrative expenses incurred by the Commission in administering the Fund, which shall not exceed 15% of the monies received from the State Cannabis Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund in the fiscal year for which such expenses are incurred; and

  (4) Any other uses permitted under State law.

C. Examples of community-based initiatives for which monies from the Fund may be used include, but are not limited to, the following:

  (1) Behavioral health crisis response services;

  (2) Education and after-school programs;

  (3) Truancy and absentee intervention programs;

  (4) Housing and homelessness prevention initiatives;

  (5) Transportation improvements in high-density areas that have public transportation;

  (6) Job training and workforce development programs;

  (7) Community child care and recreational services; and

  (8) Programs that benefit individuals and families impacted by incarceration.

D. Prohibited uses. Monies from the Fund shall not be expended for law enforcement agencies or activities. Additionally, monies from the Fund are supplemental to, and shall not supplant, funding that otherwise would be appropriated for County programs that existed prior to July 1, 2023.
