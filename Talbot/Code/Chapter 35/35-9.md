# § 35-9. Grant process.

A. Subject to the provisions set forth in Article I of this chapter, the Commission shall develop and administer a public grant application process for interested parties to apply for monies from the Fund, as well as criteria for making an award thereof, prior to the solicitation of proposals. In developing criteria to make an award, the Commission shall consider any guidance provided by the Maryland Office of Social Equity.

B. A qualifying grantee previously awarded monies from the Fund may not receive additional monies until all reporting from the previous award has been completed and approved by the Commission.
