# § 8-2. Function and duties of department.

The Easton Airport will be maintained as an airport as provided for in the Md. Code Ann., Transportation Article, §§ 5-101 to 5-1105, as amended. The department shall have such other duties as may be prescribed by directive of the County Manager or by legislative act of the County Council not inconsistent with the County Charter.
