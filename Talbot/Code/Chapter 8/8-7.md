# § 8-7. Annual budget.

The County fiscal or budget year begins on the first day of July and ends on the 30th day of June of the succeeding year. Notwithstanding the creation of separate enterprise fund accounts for the Easton Airport, the Airport Manager shall submit an annual budget for the facility in accordance with applicable County regulations. The proposed budget shall be submitted to the Airport Board for its review prior to submittal to the County Manager.
