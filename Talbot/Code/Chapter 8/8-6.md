# § 8-6. Duties and functions of Airport Board.

The Board shall make recommendations to and advise the Airport Manager and the County Council relating to policies, planning, development, operation, management, fees, budgeting, staffing, and maintenance of the Easton Airport with the goal of having the Airport operate as a self-supporting enterprise. The Board shall meet as needed, but at least quarterly, and minutes will be kept of all meetings. All meetings shall be open to the public unless applicable Maryland law permits an executive or closed session. The Board shall report on its activities to the County Council annually and at such other times as may be requested by the Airport Board or the County Council.
