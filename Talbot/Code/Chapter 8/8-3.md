# § 8-3. Airport Manager.

The Easton Airport will operate as a department administered by a facility manager, who will function as a department head within the County organizational structure. The Airport Manager shall be appointed by the County Manager, with the approval of the County Council, after the recommendations and/or comments of the Airport Board have been received.
