# § 8-5. Airport Board membership.

The Airport Board shall consist of five members, each of whom shall be an adult resident of Talbot County. The members of the Airport Board shall be appointed by the County Council. Each member shall serve for a four-year term or until a successor is appointed, except that the first term, which shall commence June 1, 1993, shall be as follows: one member for one year, one member for two years, two members for three years and one member for four years. Members shall be eligible for reappointment. The Board shall annually select a Chairman and any other officers deemed necessary. Members shall serve without compensation but shall be entitled to reimbursement for reasonable expenses duly approved by the County.
