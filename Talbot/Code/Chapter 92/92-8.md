# § 92-8. Conflict with other provisions.

A. Whenever any provision of this chapter conflicts with any other provision of law, rule, or regulation covering the same subject matter, whether set forth in this chapter or elsewhere, that provision which is more restrictive or imposes the higher standard or requirement shall govern.
