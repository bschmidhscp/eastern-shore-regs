# § 92-4. Noise standards.

A. It shall be unlawful within the County to make, continue to make, permit, or cause to be made or continued a noise disturbance or a noise level in excess of 60 dBA during the daytime or 55 dBA during the nighttime, except as specifically stated herein.

B. Prominent discrete tones, cyclically varying sound, or periodic or repetitive noises shall not exceed a sound level that is five dBA lower than the applicable level allowed in § 92-4A above.

C. In the VM Zone, as defined in Chapter 190 of the Talbot County Code, restaurants, bars and nightclubs required to obtain a noise compliance plan shall not exceed 60 dBA during the daytime for outdoor amplified music.
