# § 92-9. Word usage.

A. Rules of construction.

  (1) Words used in the present tense include the future; words in the singular number include the plural, and words in the plural number include the singular unless the natural construction of the wording indicates otherwise.

  (2) The words "shall" and "must" are mandatory, and the words "may" and "should" are permissive.
