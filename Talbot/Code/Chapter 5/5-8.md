# § 5-8. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following words and phrases shall be defined as follows:

**ADVISORY BOARD** The Talbot County Agricultural Preservation Advisory Board.

**COUNTY COUNCIL** The County Council of Talbot County.

**FOUNDATION** The Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation.

**PLANNING COMMISSION** The Talbot County Planning Commission.
