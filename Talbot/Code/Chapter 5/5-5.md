# § 5-5. Official maps of Agricultural Preservation Districts.

A. Official maps of Agricultural Preservation Districts shall be kept and shall be revised from time to time to reflect the establishment, alteration and abolition of Agricultural Preservation District boundaries. The official map shall be made current at least once a year by June 30 of each year and shall be published at the same scale as the Department of Assessments and Taxation Tax Maps. The official maps and all revisions thereto shall be certified by the Clerk to the County Council.

B. Copies of all official Agricultural Preservation District maps shall be located in the offices of the Talbot County Planning Office and shall be available for public inspection.
