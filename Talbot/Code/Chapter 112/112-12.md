# § 112-12. Violations and penalties.

Any person, firm or corporation violating any provision of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and shall upon conviction be fined not less than $50 nor more than $200 for the first offense and for the second or subsequent offense by a fine of not less than $100 nor more than $500.
