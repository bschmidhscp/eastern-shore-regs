# § 112-1. Definitions.

*[Amended 8-13-1985 by Bill No. 225 ]*

For the purpose of this chapter, "sanitary construction" shall mean the installation of all plumbing, water supply wells and pumps, septic tank systems or other individual sewage systems and the practice of cleaning and disposing of the contents of septic tanks or other sewage disposal systems, abandonment of any sewage or septic disposal system and connection to a regional sewage system.
