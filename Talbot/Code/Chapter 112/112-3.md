# § 112-3. Minimum Board of Health standards.

*[Amended 1974 by Bill No. 9 ; at time of adoption of Code (see Ch. 1, General Provisions, Art. I)]*

The requirements of the Sewage Disposal and Certain Water Systems for Homes and Other Establishments in the Counties of Maryland Where a Public Sewage System is Not Available (COMAR 26.04.02) of the State Secretary of Health shall be the minimum requirements of the Talbot County Board of Health.
