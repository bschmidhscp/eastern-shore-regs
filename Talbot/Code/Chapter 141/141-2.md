# § 141-2. Conditions to be same as for public school transportation; defraying of costs.

This transportation, subject to the approval of the County Council, shall be provided by the Board of Education as aforesaid, for all the children attending schools described herein, upon the same terms and conditions established by the Board of Education of Talbot County for children attending public schools, provided that the County Council of Talbot County shall make such provisions in its budget by appropriate tax levy as will be necessary to defray the cost of such transportation, which authority is hereby granted to said Council so that it may, in its discretion, provide such transportation as is herein authorized.
