# § 141-1. Transportation of children to schools not receiving state aid.

All children who are of public school age, who attend schools in Talbot County which do not receive state aid and who reside on or along or near the public highways of Talbot County, on which there is operated a public school bus or conveyance provided by the Board of Education of Talbot County for transporting children to and from the public schools of Talbot County, shall receive, subject to the approval of the County Council, transportation on such buses or conveyances. Such transportation, upon the approval of the County Council, shall be provided for them by the Board of Education of Talbot County from a point on the said public highway nearest or most accessible to their respective homes to a point on the public highway nearest or most accessible to their respective schools.
