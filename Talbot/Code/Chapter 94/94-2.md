# § 94-2. Applicability.

This article applies to:

(1) Any claim in any proceeding that any act by a County representative violates or would violate:

  (a) The Constitution of the United States or any federal laws or regulations enacted thereunder;

  (b) The Constitution of the State of Maryland or any State laws or regulations enacted thereunder; or

  (c) The County Charter.

(2) Any claim in any proceeding that a County ordinance, rule, or regulation, either on its face or as applied, violates or would violate:

  (a) The Constitution of the United States or any federal laws or regulations enacted thereunder;

  (b) The Constitution of the State of Maryland or any State laws or regulations enacted thereunder; or

  (c) The County Charter.
