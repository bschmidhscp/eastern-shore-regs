# § 94-6. Word usage.

In this article, unless such construction would be unreasonable, the singular includes the plural and vice versa; the masculine gender includes the feminine and vice versa; the conjunctive shall also be taken in the disjunctive and vice versa; and the use of any tense of any verb shall be considered also to include with its meaning all other tenses of the verb so used. "Shall" is mandatory; "may" is permissive. "Herein," "hereby," "hereunder," "hereof," "hereinbefore," "hereinafter" and other equivalent words refer to this article and not solely to the particular portion thereof in which any such word is used. Wherever the words "include," "includes," or "including" are used in this article, they shall be deemed to be followed by the words "without limitation." The phrase "relates to," "arises from," "upon which the claim is based," or "to which the claim relates," as used in this article, means: constitute(s), refer(s) to, reflects(s), concern(s), pertain(s) to, or any way logically or factually connect(s) any proceeding, ordinance, rule, regulation, decision, act, or failure to act by the County or a County representative to any claim subject to this article.
