# § 42-1. Creation of Department.

There is hereby created the Talbot County Department of Correction. The Department shall operate all programs pertaining to detention and rehabilitation of persons under the jurisdiction of the County government awaiting trial or having been convicted of a crime and sentenced by a court of competent jurisdiction to a term in the Talbot County Detention Center or being held for any other lawful purpose.
