# § 42-4. Temporary custody of inmates.

Should the facilities of the County Detention Center be inadequate to provide for all inmates lawfully committed therein, the Director may, with the approval of the County Manager or a designated agent, make arrangements for the temporary custody of the inmates at other correctional facilities within the state.
