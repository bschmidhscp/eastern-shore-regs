# § 164-6.3. Permit suspension and revocation.

Any permits or approvals issued to the applicant for the project by the County associated with an approved stormwater management plan may be disapproved, suspended or revoked after written notice to the permittee for any of the following reasons:

A. Any violation(s) of the conditions of the approved stormwater management plan;

B. Changes in site runoff characteristics for which a waiver was granted;

C. Construction other than in accordance with the approved plan;

D. Noncompliance with correction notice(s) or stop-work order(s) issued for any stormwater management practice or system; or

E. An immediate danger exists in a downstream area.
