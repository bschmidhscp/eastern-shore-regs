# § 164-1.2. Incorporation by reference.

For the purpose of this chapter, the following documents are incorporated by reference:

A. The 2000 Maryland Stormwater Design Manual, Volumes I and II (Maryland Department of the Environment, April 2000), and all subsequent revisions, is incorporated by reference by Talbot County and shall serve as the official guide for stormwater management principles, methods, and practices.

B. United States Department of Agriculture Natural Resources Conservation Service Maryland Conservation Practice Standard Pond Code 378 (January 2000).

C. Talbot County Stormwater Management Process and Implementation Guide, as approved by resolution of the County Council.
