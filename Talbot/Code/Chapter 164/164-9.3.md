# § 164-9.3. Maintenance responsibility.

A. The property owner or any other person or agent in control of property that contains stormwater management systems shall maintain all ESD practices, grade surfaces, walls, drains, dams and structures, vegetation, erosion and sediment control measures, and other protective devices in good condition and shall promptly repair and restore the same to comply with all applicable requirements.

B. A maintenance schedule is required for the life of any structural stormwater management facility or system and ESD practice. It shall set forth the maintenance to be completed, the time period for completion, and the party responsible to perform the maintenance, and shall be set forth on the approved stormwater management plan.
