# § 164-5.3. Preparation.

A. The design of stormwater management plans shall be prepared by an individual whose qualifications are acceptable to the County Engineer. The County Engineer may require that the design be prepared by either a professional engineer, professional land surveyor, or landscape architect licensed in the state, as necessary to protect the public or the environment.

B. If a stormwater BMP requires either a dam safety permit from MDE or small pond approval from the Talbot County Soil Conservation District, the design shall be prepared by a professional engineer licensed in the state.
