# § 102-4. Powers and duties of Department.

The Department shall be responsible for the organization and conduct of public recreation and public parks in all areas of the County not presently controlled by one of the municipal subdivisions. It shall also be responsible for the organization and conduct of public parks, playgrounds, and other recreational facilities open to the public which are so located, excepting Hog Neck Golf Course. It shall encourage development of cooperative programs and joint use of facilities among the various political subdivisions of the County and act as a clearinghouse for coordination of ongoing programs. It shall coordinate use of the facilities with the Board of Education. The Department shall have such other duties as may be prescribed by directive of the County Manager or by legislative act of the County Council not inconsistent with the County Charter.
