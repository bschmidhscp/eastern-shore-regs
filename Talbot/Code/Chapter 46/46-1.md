# § 46-1. Location and days.

Pursuant to the authority granted to the County Council by Md. Code Ann., Estates and Trusts Art., § 2-106, to prescribe the location and days that the Orphan's Court for Talbot County shall hold court, the Orphan's Court shall hold its hearings in the courtroom in the North Wing of the Court House, 11 North Washington Street, Easton, Maryland, on each Tuesday, as necessary.
