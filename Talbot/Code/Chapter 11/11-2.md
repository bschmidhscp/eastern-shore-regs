# § 11-2. Purpose.

*[Amended at time of adoption of Code (see Ch. 1, General Provisions, Art. I)]*

The County Council declares that the purpose of this chapter is to regulate the retail sale of alcoholic beverages within Talbot County, pursuant to the authority granted in Md. Code Ann., Alcoholic Beverages Art., § 30-103. The regulations, provisions, restrictions and penalties contained in this chapter are for the protection, health, welfare and safety of the citizens of Talbot County.
