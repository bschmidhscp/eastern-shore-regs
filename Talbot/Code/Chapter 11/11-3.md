# § 11-3. Administration by Board of Liquor License Commissioners.

This chapter shall be administered and enforced by the Talbot County Board of Liquor License Commissioners (the "Board"). The Board may adopt such written rules and regulations as it deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter, subject to the approval of the County Council. Chapter 60, Ethics Provisions, Talbot County Code, shall apply to members of the Board.
