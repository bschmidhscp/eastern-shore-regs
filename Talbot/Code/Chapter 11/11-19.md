# § 11-19. Supplemental municipal regulation.

Municipalities within Talbot County may restrict the retail sale of alcoholic beverages within their respective jurisdictions through adoption of local zoning ordinances. Enforcement of any ordinance so enacted shall be the responsibility of the municipality.
