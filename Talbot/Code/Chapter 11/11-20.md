# § 11-20. Conflict with other regulations.

*[Amended at time of adoption of Code (see Ch. 1, General Provisions, Art. I)]*

Any law enacted by the Talbot County Council pursuant to the grant of express powers in Article 25, § 3(ee),[1] or Md. Code Ann., Alcoholic Beverages Art., § 30-103, shall prevail over any provision of the Code of Public General Laws of Maryland regulating the retail sale of alcoholic beverages. However, unless and until the Talbot County Council enacts a law which is contrary to a provision of the Code of Public General Laws regulating the retail sale of alcoholic beverages, the provisions of the Code of Public General Laws shall remain in effect.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: Art. 25 was repealed by Acts 2013, C. 119, § 1, eff. 10-1-2013.
