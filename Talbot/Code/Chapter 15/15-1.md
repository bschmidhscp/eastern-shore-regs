# § 15-1. General.

This chapter is intended to promote the public health, safety, and welfare in connection with the care, custody, and control of animals. State law also establishes certain licensing fees and requirements, and certain criminal acts with regard to the care and treatment of animals. This chapter is intended to provide supplemental additional regulation. Nothing in this chapter is intended to supersede, modify, or repeal any existing state law, rule, or regulation, except to the extent that state law delegates the power to the County Council to do so and the Council has exercised that authority expressly or by necessary implication.
