# § 121-13. Closure of public landings.

If the Administrator determines that the condition of any public landing or portion thereof may endanger the safety of any person using the landing, the Administrator may close the landing. Notice to that effect shall be prominently posted. Use of the public landing when closed is prohibited, and any such use is a violation of this chapter.
