# § 121-12. Camping, loitering, or sleeping in vehicles or vessels.

No person shall camp, loiter, or sleep in a vehicle or vessel at any public landing between the hours of 11:00 p.m. and 4:00 a.m. Such activity shall be a violation of this chapter. The Director of Parks and Recreation may issue a written waiver from the requirements of this section.
