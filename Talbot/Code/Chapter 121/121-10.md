# § 121-10. Swimming.

*[Amended 8-24-2001 by Bill No. 837 ]*

Swimming at any public landing is prohibited in any area not specifically designated for swimming by the Administrator. Any person swimming at a public landing in an area not designated for swimming by the Administrator does so at his or her own risk. Swimming in any area other than a designated area is a violation of this chapter.
