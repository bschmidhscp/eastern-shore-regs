# § 134-19. Appeals to courts.

*[Amended at time of adoption of Code (see Ch. 1, General Provisions, Art. I)]*

Any person aggrieved by the decision of the Board of Appeals under this chapter may appeal the decision to the Circuit Court, and thereafter to the appellate courts of this state in accordance with the provisions of the Maryland Rules of Procedure.
