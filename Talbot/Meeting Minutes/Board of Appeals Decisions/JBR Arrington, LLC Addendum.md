ADDENDUM TO DECISION
TALBOT COUNTY BOARD OF APPEALS

Appeal No. 21-1718

The Talbot County Board of Appeals, via this Addendum, wishes to clarify its decision
dated May 29, 2021. granting a special exception to JBR ARRINGTON ROAD, LLC. The
decision states on page 12 that “[b]ased on the testimony, application and exhibits, upon motion
and seconded, the Board approved the requested special exception modification and variances,
by a vote of four to one.” No variance was requested, and the resolution on page 17 indicates
only a special exception was approved. Additionally, all five members of the Board of Appeals
present for the special exception hearing in Appeal No. 21-1718 signed the decision without
indicating the member who was not in favor of approving the special exception request.

The Board of Appeals wishes to clarify its decision in Appeal No. 21-1718 by clarifying

that Paul Shortall voted against the approval of the special exception.
