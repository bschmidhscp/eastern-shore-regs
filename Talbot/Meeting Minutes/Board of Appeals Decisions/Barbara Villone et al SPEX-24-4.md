# BEFORE THE TALBOT COUNTY BOARD OF APPEALS 

IN THE MATTER OF * CASE NO. SPEX-24-4 BARBARA M VILLONE, et al. * SPECIAL EXCEPTION * * * * * * * * * * * * * 

The Board of Appeals (the “Board”) held a hearing on November 18, 2024 in the Bradley Meeting Room, Court House, South Wing at 11 N. Washington Street, Easton, Maryland to consider the application of Barbara M. Villone, et al. (the “Applicant•’). The Applicant requested approval of a Special Exception for the expansion and major modification of an existing nonconforming use in reference to the proposed demolition and reconstruction of a second primary dwelling on a single parcel of land. Chairman Frank Cavanaugh. Vice Chairman Louis Dorsey, Jr., Board Members Patrick Forrest, Jeff Adelman, Zakary Krebeck, and Board Attorney Lance M. Young were present. Board Secretary Christine Corkell and Andrew Nixon, Planner, appeared on behalf of the County. 

# STATEMENT OF THE CASE 

The project is proposed on the Presquile Farm (“Property”). The existing structure on the farm dates back to the<sup>19th</sup> century. The farm is on a neck of land almost entirely surrounded by water. The Property was subdivided from a larger parcel in 2010. That subdivision plat is evidence of three existing dwellings on the Property. In 2009, one of the dwellings removed a kitchen, which had the effect of reclassifying it as an accessory dwelling structure. There are two remaining primary dwelling structures on the Property, which is nonconforming residential use. 

The Applicant proposes to demolish and reconstruct one of the primary dwellings, which was originally constructed on or about 1900. Section 190-48.3W) of the Talbot County Code permits the expansion of structures utilized for nonconforming uses so long as the expansion is limited to no more than 20% of the gross floor area of the nonconforming use or 3.000 square feet, whichever is greater. 

# SUMMARY OF TESTIMONY 

The Applicant was represented by attorney Brendan Mullaney and Brett Ewing of Lane Engineering. Mr. Mullaney explained that the nonconforming status is derived from the fact that multiple primary residences are not permitted on the Property. The dwelling that the Applicant wishes to reconstruct was built around 1900 long before such restrictions were in place. Most of the Property is in agricultural use.

<!-- page 2 -->
The Planning Commission recommend approval. The Critical Area Commission Planner, Annie Sekerak, opined that the Applicant has the burden of proof demonstrating that the primary dwelling structures are nonconforming. 

The reconstruction will be done in conformance with § 190-48.3W) with regard to the gross floor area and/or square footage. It will also he compliant with all other zoning restrictions and requirements. The dwelling is very dilapidated in its current state. The reconstruction will place the dwelling even further from the Mean High Water line, which benefits the environment. The reconstruction will have little impact on adjacent properties. It is around 1,300 fcet away from the nearest neighbor. Mr. Mulaney represented that the nonconforming use has never been abandoned for a 12 month period of time. 

Mr. Ewing testified that the entire site compound is over 100 years old. The replacement building envelope is 160’ from the water at its closest point and there are no buffer violations. The Applicant will comply with all standards in the permitting process. 

Andrew Nixon, the County Planner, testified regarding the nonconforming status of the dwelling. The County staff consider the use nonconforming, in part, because the 2010 recorded plat certifies the existence of the two primary dwellings existing prior to the enactment of the zoning ordinance. There was no testimony that contradicted Mr. Mulaney and Mr. Nixon’s testimonies regarding nonconformity. 

# FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

The structure was built on or around 1900 and has been used continuously as a primary residence since that time. The Board is satisfied with the evidence and testimony of Mr. Mulaney and Mr. Nixon in that regard.
