- IV. <u>Introduction of Administrative Resolution:</u> 

An ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING A TALBOT 250 COMMISSION was read into the record by the Clerk and brought forward for introduction.  Prior to introduction, Mr. Lesher noted that the State has established a Maryland 250 Commission for the purpose of coordinating activities commemorating the 250<sup>th</sup> anniversary of the founding of the United States of America and is encouraging jurisdictions throughout the state to do so as well.  He stated that at his request, the Office of Law had drafted the administrative resolution now before the Council for consideration and Council discussion ensued.  Sarah Kilmon, Talbot County Communications Manager and Public Information Officer, stated that the intent of the establishment of a Talbot 250 Commission is to coordinate and promote Talbot 250 events.  Additionally, she stated that the website, talbot250.org, will host information on historical events, stories, and the people of Talbot County who helped shape its history over the past 250 years.  Upon motion by Mr. Lesher, seconded by Ms. Haythe, the Council approved the administrative resolution by voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye 

The Administrative Resolution is effective immediately.

VII.       Public Hearings: 

Resolution No. 364, A RESOLUTION TO FORMALLY CLOSE WALLYS WAY AND BETHANY LANE AS COUNTY ROADS AND AUTHORIZE THE CONVEYANCE OF CERTAIN REAL PROPERTY DESCRIBED IN A DEED DATED DECEMBER 14, 1984, AND RECORDED AMONG THE LAND RECORDS OF TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND AT LIBER J.T.B. NO. 594, FOLIO 423, TO CHAMBERLAIN, LLC, FOR NO MONETARY CONSIDERATION, was read into the record by the Clerk and brought forward for public hearing. Prior to the public hearing, Patrick Thomas, County Attorney, stated that the County owns a 50foot strip of land which bisects a parcel of land owned by Chamberlain, LLC which is included in the County’s road inventory as Bethany Lane.  He stated that Chamberlain, LLC had contacted the County to request closure of the roads which are the subject of the resolution and to convey the land to Chamberlain, LLC so they can transfer the property.  Mr. Thomas stated that he had conferred with Brian Moore, Superintendent, Roads and County Facilities, who had advised that the roads are not necessary, are on private property, are not being used, and the County does not want to maintain them and therefore has no objection to the conveyance.  Members of the public were afforded an opportunity to comment on the legislation.  The Council approved Resolution No. 364 by voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp - Aye Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye
