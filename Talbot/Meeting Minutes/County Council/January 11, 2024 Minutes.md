# TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND 

County Council **MINUTES** January 11, 2024 

Present – President Chuck Callahan, Vice President Pete Lesher, Keasha N. Haythe, Lynn L. Mielke, Dave Stepp, County Manager Clay Stamp, and County Attorney Patrick Thomas 

I. <u>Agenda – The Agenda of Thursday, January 11, 2024 was approved upon motion by Ms. Mielke,</u> seconded by Mr. Lesher, with the Council voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp - Aye (via telephone) Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye (via telephone) 

II. <u>Minutes – The Minutes of Tuesday, December 12, 2024 were approved upon motion by Mr.</u> Lesher, seconded by Ms. Mielke, with the Council voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp - Aye (via telephone) Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye (via telephone) 

III. <u>Disbursements – Disbursements of Tuesday, December 19, 2023, Tuesday, December 26, 2023,</u> Tuesday, January 2, 2024, and Tuesday, January 9, 2024, were approved upon motion by Mr. Lesher, seconded by Ms. Mielke, with the Council voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp - Aye (via telephone) Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye (via telephone) 

### IV. <u>County Manager’s Report:</u> 

- A. <u>Request from County Manager – Mr. Stamp requested Council approval to declare</u> Tuesday, January 16, 2024 a Special Legislative Day.  Mr. Lesher stated that the County Charter allows the Council to designate one additional Tuesday as a legislative day beyond the two Tuesdays specified in the Charter.  County Attorney, Patrick Thomas, outlined the specific reasons for declaring January 16<sup>th</sup> as a Special Legislative Day. Upon motion by Mr. Lesher, seconded by Ms. Mielke, the Council approved declaring Tuesday, January 16, 2024 as a Special Legislative Day by voting 5 – 0 as follows:
