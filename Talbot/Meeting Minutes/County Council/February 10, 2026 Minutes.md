- IV. <u>Presentation of Annual Report for Talbot County – Sarah Kilmon, Communications Manager – Ms.</u> Kilmon briefed the Council on the 2025 Annual Report for Talbot County which provides an overview of County government operations using the five (5) pillars of Talbot County Government: Education and Recreation; Public Safety, Health, and Welfare, Infrastructure; Environmental Stewardship and Economic Stability and focusing on the themes of Progress, Partnerships and Purpose.  Ms. Kilmon noted various infrastructure projects which have recently been completed or are currently in progress; public safety initiatives, and economic development efforts.  She stated that infrastructure projects include the ongoing Chapel District Elementary School and the St. Michaels Library renovation and expansion projects; the completion, in 2024, of the gymnasium at the Talbot County Community Center which has allowed for an expansion of programming for the Department of Parks and Recreation and year-round use of the ice rink; Goldsborough Neck and Airport Road improvement projects; countywide broadband expansion by Easton Utilities Commission in partnership with Talbot County; and improvements at Hog Neck Golf Course.  She stated that Public Safety initiatives include the construction of a North EMS Station in Cordova which will decrease the emergency response time in the northern portion of the county; an upgrade of the Computer-Aided Design (CAD) system for the County’s 9-1-1 Center and completion of a new Public Safety Complex and sallyport on Marys Court in Easton; a new facility for the Talbot County Health Department which was completed in 2024 which provides much needed space for new programs and initiatives to better serve the community; the launching of the Mobile Integrated Health (MIH) program which provides individuals an opportunity to receive care in their homes

V. <u>Proclamation:  American Heart Month – February 2026 – The Clerk read a proclamation into the</u> record which spoke of the prevalence of heart disease as the leading cause of death of Americans each year, and preventive measures which can reduce risk of the disease including, maintaining a healthy weight, eating balanced and nutritious meals, increasing physical activity, and encouraging citizens to participate in healthier lifestyle initiatives to help eradicate the deadly disease.  The proclamation also spoke of sudden cardiac arrest and the partnership of the Talbot County Paramedic Foundation, the Talbot County Volunteer Fire and Rescue Association, the Department of Emergency Services, and the University of Maryland Shore Regional Health to address cardiac care through public education and provision of state-of-the-art lifesaving services and devices. Upon motion by Ms. Mielke, seconded by Ms. Haythe, the Council approved the proclamation by voting 4 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Lesher – Aye 

Mr. Stepp – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye 

Ms. Haythe – Aye 

Tina Kintop, EMS Division Chief, Talbot County Department of Emergency Services, expressed her appreciation to the Council for their support of American Heart Month and the entities which partner with the Department of Emergency Services on outreach to the public, preparedness and prevention initiatives, including placement of automated external defibrillators (AEDs) in Talbot County businesses and along parks and trails, and providing CPR certification classes.  She stated that the Mobile Integrated Health Program provides preventative care related to heart disease by focusing on chronic disease management, education, medication oversight and community resources to support a healthier lifestyle to help individuals stay out of the hospital. She stated that, to date, approximately 300 AEDs have been placed in public locations throughout the county, 26 of which are outdoor temperature-controlled units to ensure year-round accessibility.  Director Kintop stated that the annual CPR marathon will be held on Friday, March 20, 2026, at the Talbot County Health Department, 510 Cadmus Lane in Easton and encouraged residents to participate. Information can be found at www.talbotcountymd.gov/lifesaver. Council members expressed their appreciation to Director Kintop, DES, and its partner agencies for all they do to provide a safer environment for the citizens of Talbot County. 

Mr. Stepp presented the Proclamation to Director Kintop who accepted on behalf of the Department of Emergency Services and its partner organizations.
