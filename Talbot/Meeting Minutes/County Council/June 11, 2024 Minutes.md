A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN FOR THE PURPOSES OF APPROVING AN ALTERNATE LOCATION FOR THE PUMP STATION AND EXPANDING THE POTENTIAL ROUTE FOR THE FORCE MAIN TO PROVIDE SANITARY SEWER SERVICE TO THE UNIVERSITY OF MARYLAND MEDICAL CENTER SHORE REGIONAL MEDICAL CENTER, TO BE LOCATED AT 10000 LONGWOODS ROAD, EASTON, MARYLAND, AND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMMUNITY CENTER, LOCATED AT 10028 OCEAN GATEWAY, EASTON, MARYLAND, AS INITIALLY SET FORTH IN RESOLUTION NO. 184, ADOPTED ON SEPTEMBER 13, 2011 was read into the record by the Clerk and brought forward for introduction. XII. 

<u>Work Session – Discussion of Update of Comprehensive Plan, Report of the Review and the Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan – The Council held a work session at 2:30 p.m. in the</u> Bradley Meeting Room with Ray Clarke, County Engineer, and Brennan Tarleton, Planning Officer.  Ms. Haythe was unable to be in attendance.  The purpose of the work session was to brief Council on the process of developing the 2024 Report of the Review.  Mr. Clarke stated that the Council recently approved Resolution Nos. 338, 347, and 348 and same have been forwarded to the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) for review and comment; MDE has the option to deny, make recommendations to modify, or approve the resolutions.  Once a response has been received from MDE, all pertinent information will be incorporated into a Draft 2024 Report of the Review and copies provided to the Planning Commission, Public Works Advisory Board and the municipalities for their review and comment, and posted on the County’s website, for review and comment by the public.  He stated that once finalized, the Draft 2024 Report of the Review will be submitted to the Council for consideration of approval through a numbered resolution. He stated that once the 2024 Report of the Review has been approved by the Council, the information will be incorporated into the update of the Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan.  Mr. Tarleton stated that at the same time that the Department of Public Works is finalizing the 2024 Report of the Review, the update to the County’s 2016 Comprehensive Plan will begin.  He stated that the Request for Proposals will be advertised in the near future and he anticipates having a consultant on board for the project by late September or early October 2024.  He state that the schedule for completion of the update to the Comprehensive Plan includes a 6-month buffer to account for unforeseeable issues which may arise.  Council discussion ensued with Mr. Clarke and Mr. Tarleton regarding various factors which are needed to complete the Draft 2024 Report of the Review.  Mr. Clarke stated that any resolutions not approved by MDE will not be included in the 2024 Report of the Review.  He stated that it is the intent of the Department of Public Works update the Report of the Review every three (3) years and Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan every ten (10) years going forward.  The work session concluded at 5:25 p.m.
