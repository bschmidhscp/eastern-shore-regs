# TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND 

County Council **MINUTES** November 25, 2025 

Present – Vice President Pete Lesher, Keasha N. Haythe, Lynn L. Mielke, Dave Stepp, County Manager Clay Stamp, and County Attorney, Patrick Thomas.  President Chuck Callahan was absent. 

- I. <u>Agenda – Mr. Lesher requested and received unanimous consent for approval of the Agenda of</u> Tuesday, November 25, 2025. 

- II. <u>Minutes – Mr. Lesher requested and received unanimous consent for approval of the Minutes of</u> Tuesday, October 28, 2025, and Tuesday, November 4, 2025. 

- III. <u>Disbursements – Mr. Lesher requested and received unanimous consent for approval of the</u> Disbursements of Tuesday, November 11, 2025, Tuesday, November 18, 2025, and Tuesday, November 25, 2025. 

- IV. <u>Council Commendation to Hannah Ward, Maryland 9-1-1 Board Communication Specialist of the Year –  Holley Guschke, 9-1-1 Division Chief, Department of Emergency Services, read the</u> statement submitted to the Maryland 9-1-1 Board for the nomination of Hannah Ward, who was selected by her peers, for consideration as Talbot County’s Communication Specialist of the Year. The statement spoke of Ms. Ward’s work ethic, her ability to handle high pressure situations with a positive attitude and calm demeanor, her tireless dedication to her job, the professionalism and compassion by which she performs her job, her willingness to assist her co-workers, and many other positive attributes. Council members expressed their congratulations to Ms. Ward.  The Clerk then read a Council commendation into the record which spoke of Ms. Ward’s selection as Talbot County’s Maryland 9-1-1 Board Communication Specialist of the Year.  Upon motion by Mr. Stepp, seconded by Ms. Mielke, the Council approved the commendation by voting 4 – 0 as follows: 

   - Mr. Lesher – Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye 

   - Ms. Haythe – Aye 

Ms. Haythe presented the Council Commendation to Ms. Ward.  Holley Guschke, Brian LeCates, Director, Department of Emergency Services, and County Manager, Clay Stamp were also in attendance.

## VI. 

<u>Proclamation:  40</u><sup>th</sup> <u>Anniversary of Festival of Trees – The Clerk read a proclamation into the</u> record in recognition of the 40<sup>th</sup> anniversary of the Festival of Trees which supports the Talbot Hospice Foundation and its mission to provide professional, team-oriented care to patients faced with a life-limiting illness in an atmosphere of comfort and dignity.  The proclamation stated that since its inception 40 years ago, the Festival of Trees and its various events have raised more than $3.7 million for the Talbot Hospice Foundation. ## IX. <u>Public Hearings:</u> 

Bill No. 1621, A BILL TO AMEND CHAPTER 190 (ZONING, SUBDIVISION, AND LAND DEVELOPMENT), SECTIONS 190-25.2 (TABLE OF LAND USES) AND 190-29 (COMMERCIAL USES), REGARDING FUNERAL HOMES AND CREMATORIA, was read into the record by the Clerk, brought forward for public hearing, and members of the public were provided an opportunity to comment on the legislation.  Written comments on Bill No. 1621 will be accepted until Noon on Friday, December 5, 2025.  Bill No. 1621 is eligible for vote on Tuesday, December 9, 2025. 

Bill No. 1622, A BILL TO AMEND CHAPTER 190 (ZONING, SUBDIVISION, AND LAND DEVELOPMENT) OF THE TALBOT COUNTY CODE FOR THE PURPOSES OF PROVIDING FOR CLASSES OF SHORT-TERM RENTAL LICENSES AND AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS PERTAINING TO SHORT-TERM RENTAL LICENSES, INCLUDING: 1) THE COMPOSITION AND TERM OF THE SHORT-TERM RENTAL REVIEW BOARD; 2) CONTENTS OF NEW APPLICATIONS; 3) HEARINGS, DECISIONS, AND APPEALS; 4) RENEWAL; 5) GROUNDS FOR DENIAL; AND 6) ADDITIONAL RESTRICTIONS, was read into the record by the Clerk and brought forward for public hearing.  Prior to the public hearing, County Attorney, Patrick Thomas, stated that the legislation represents the initial proposal Council had forwarded to the Planning Commission, with the exception of the following three (3) changes as recommended by the Planning Commission:  the proposed increase in the minimum night stay for certain zoning districts from three (3) nights to seven (7) nights has been removed; the proposed requirement for a landline telephone has been removed; and the proposed requirement to maintain a log of rentals has been removed.  Members of the public were then afforded an opportunity to comment on the legislation. Written comments on Bill No. 1622 will be accepted until Noon on Friday, December 5, 2025.  Bill No. 1622 is eligible for vote on Tuesday, December 9, 2025.
