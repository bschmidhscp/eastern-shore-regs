# TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND 

County Council **MINUTES** August 13, 2024 

Present – President Chuck Callahan, Vice President Pete Lesher, Keasha N. Haythe, Lynn L. Mielke, Dave Stepp, County Manager Clay Stamp, and County Attorney Patrick Thomas. 

- I. <u>Agenda – The Agenda of Tuesday, August 13, 2024, was brought forward for vote.  Mr. Stepp</u> requested that _Item V., Update by Mid Shore Behavioral Health_ , be removed from the Agenda as Kathryn Dilley, LCSW-C, Executive Director, Mid Shore Behavioral Health, Inc., was unable to be in attendance; the motion was seconded by Mr. Lesher.  The Council approved the Agenda, as amended, by voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe - Aye 

- II. <u>Minutes – Mr. Callahan requested and received unanimous consent for approval of the Minutes of</u> Tuesday, June 25, 2024. 

- III. <u>Disbursements – Mr. Callahan requested and received unanimous consent for approval of the</u> Disbursements of Tuesday, July 30, 2024, Tuesday, August 6, 2024, and Tuesday, August 13, 2024. 

- IV. Introduction of Amendment to Bill No. 1577, AN EMERGENCY BILL TO AMEND BILL NO. 1231 FOR THE PURPOSE OF COORDINATING THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF MARYLAND ROUTE 662C TO FACILITATE TIMELY CONSTRUCTION OF A NEW HOSPITAL AND REGIONAL MEDICAL CENTER BY SHORE HEALTH SYSTEM, INC. AND MINIMIZE IMPACTS TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON OCEAN GATEWAY (U.S. ROUTE 50) DURING THE 2025 SUMMER TRAVEL SEASON, was brought forward for introduction.  Prior to introduction, County Attorney, Patrick Thomas, stated that the proposed amendment, which, in his legal opinion, is non-substantive, includes the draft amendment to the road transfer agreement between the Maryland State Highway Administration and the County as Exhibit “A”, and the road inspection agreement between Shore Health System, Inc. and the County as Exhibit “B”.  If introduced, the amendment will be posted on the website for viewing by the public prior to the scheduled public hearing on Bill No. 1577 on Tuesday, August 27, 2024.   The amendment, known as Amendment No. 1, was introduced by Mr. Callahan, Ms. Haythe, Mr. Lesher, and Mr. Stepp. The public hearing on Amendment No. 1 will be held in conjunction with the public hearing on Bill No. 1577 on Tuesday, August 27, 2024, at 5:30 p.m. in the Bradley Meeting Room, South Wing, Talbot County Courthouse, 11 North Washington St., Easton, Maryland 21601.

- VI. <u>County Manager’s Report:</u> 

   - A. <u>Request from Finance Office – Requested Council approval of a United States</u> Department of Agriculture (USDA) Loan Resolution related to the County’s financing/refinancing of improvements for wastewater facilities and the St. Michaels wastewater collection system improvements; the resolution is required so the County can issue bonds in the amount of $88,000 as outlined in Bill No. 1576.  Council discussion ensued with County Engineer, Ray Clarke, who stated that funding in the amount of the loan has already been paid to the County by the Town of St. Michaels; once the loan has closed, the funding will be utilized to pay off the loan.  Upon motion by Ms. Haythe, seconded by Mr. Stepp, the Council approved the loan resolution by voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye

Council discussion ensued with Mr. Stamp and Ray Clarke, County Engineer, regarding the specifics of the project related to the change order; Mr. Clarke stated that the sallyport was not part of the original contract. Upon motion by Mr. Stepp, seconded by Mr. Lesher, the Council approved Change Order No. 8 by voting 5 – 0 as follows:

VII. <u>Public Comments:     There were no public comments.</u> 

VIII.        Council Comments: 

Ms. Haythe - No comments. Mr. Lesher - Mr. Lesher stated that he is looking forward to the MACo (Maryland Association of Counties) Summer Conference later this week and the opportunity for Council and staff to receive continuing education on best practices in county government and to learn what is being done in the way of innovative practices in other governmental bodies both throughout Maryland and across the nation.  He stated that the University of Maryland’s program, Academy for Excellence in Local Government, is a boot camp of sorts for Council members to get up to speed on topics such as risk management, county budgeting, ethics, Open Meetings, and the Public Information Act.  Mr. Lesher stated that he has taken all of the

- Ms. Mielke - Ms. Mielke stated that she had recently attended the Talbot County Farm Bureau picnic, and, in her opinion, it was great to converse with the farmers of Talbot County; she appreciates all their good works and the good food.  She stated that what stood out to her was the number of young ladies who are involved in agriculture, one of whom, among her other projects, had raised her own yellow tomatoes which were served at the picnic.  Ms. Mielke stated that she was encouraged to know that agriculture in Talbot County is alive and well.  Ms. Mielke concluded her comments by congratulating the Talbot County Farm Bureau. 

- Mr. Stepp - Mr. Stepp expressed his appreciation to the residents of Tunis Mills who met with the Council last week to discuss their concerns about the proposed bridge replacement project.  He stated that it was good to hear everyone’s thoughts on what they would like to see with regard to a new improved bridge, and, in his opinion, it was a great meeting. 

- Mr. Callahan - Mr. Callahan stated that by the next time the Council convenes, he will be a married man.  He stated that he had recently gone fly fishing for the first time in the mountains of Montana and, in his opinion, the area was beautiful, and the trip was phenomenal. 

After Mr. Callahan’s comments, Mr. Stamp provided an update on the following projects:  the Roads Department has been working diligently to address issues on Airport Road by widening the shoulder on each side, after which contractor David A. Bramble, Inc. will overlay the road; Easton Airport is bringing fill dirt from a property just north of the Airport to build up the elevation for the runway modernization project; and the Roads Department had recently replaced several culvert pipes along Airport Road, Goldsborough Neck Road, Hailem School Road and Hiners Lane. 

## IX. <u>Public Hearing:</u> 

Resolution No. 361, A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT FAMILY NETWORK BYLAWS TO PROVIDE FOR A TREASURER’S DESIGNEE TO ATTEND MEETINGS IN THE TREASURER’S ABSENCE, was read into the record by the Clerk and brought forward for public hearing.  Prior to the public hearing County Attorney, Patrick Thomas, stated that the resolution was requested by the Talbot Family Network Board of Directors to allow the treasurer to appoint a designee to represent them at meetings when he or she is unable to be in attendance. Council discussion ensued with Nancy Andrew, Executive Director, Talbot Family Network who stated that, if approved, the resolution would allow for the appointment of the treasurer’s designee, as is already outlined in Talbot Family Network’s Bylaws for the other ex-officio members. Members of the public were provided with an opportunity to comment on the legislation. The Council approved Resolution No. 361 by voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye

Bill No. 1576, A BILL TO AUTHORIZE TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND (THE “COUNTY”) TO BORROW NOT MORE THAN EIGHTY EIGHT THOUSAND DOLLARS ($88,000) IN ORDER TO FINANCE AND REFINANCE IMPROVEMENTS TO CERTAIN WASTEWATER FACILITIES IN THE COUNTY AND TO EFFECT SUCH BORROWING BY THE ISSUANCE AND SALE OF ONE OR MORE SERIES OF ITS GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS PAYABLE FROM AD VALOREM TAXES TO BE LEVIED BY THE COUNTY; EXEMPTING THE BONDS FROM THE PROVISIONS OF SECTIONS 19-205 AND 19-207 OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT ARTICLE OF THE ANNOTATED CODE OF MARYLAND; AUTHORIZING THE CONSOLIDATION OF SEPARATE SERIES OF BONDS INTO ONE OR MORE BONDS; AUTHORIZING THE REFUNDING OF SUCH BONDS AND RELATING GENERALLY TO THE ISSUANCE AND SALE OF SUCH BONDS FOR SUCH PURPOSES, was read into the record by the Clerk, brought forward for public hearing, and members of the public were provided an opportunity to comment on the legislation. Mr. Callahan – Aye 

Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye 

Bill No. 1576 was read into the record by the Clerk and brought forward for vote.  The Council approved Bill No. 1576 by voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye 

Bill No. 1576 will become effective as outlined in the legislation. 

Upon motion by Mr. Stepp, seconded by Ms. Haythe, the Council voted to adjourn and to reconvene on Tuesday, August 27, 2024, at 2:30 p.m. in Open Session for a tentative work session on a proposal to amend the Talbot County Accommodations Tax; to convene into Closed Session at 3:30 p.m. as listed on the statement for closing that meeting, and for the regularly scheduled meeting at 5:00 p.m. by voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan - Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher - Aye Ms. Mielke – Aye Ms. Haythe - Aye
