## V. <u>Introduction of Administrative Resolution:</u> 

An ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION CHANGING THE DESIGNATED RESIDENT AGENT FOR TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, was read into the record and brought forward for introduction.  Prior to introduction, County Attorney, Patrick Thomas, stated that under Maryland State law, each county is required to designate a resident agent for the purposes of accepting service on behalf of the County. VII. <u>Council Discussion of Proposed Text Amendment to Chapter 190 Concerning Short-Term Rental Licenses and Short-Term Rental Review Board – County Attorney, Patrick Thomas, stated that the</u> request for a text amendment was made by Mr. Stepp.  He stated that the proposed text amendment would amend sections 190.63.2.G.2a(iii) and 190.63.A.4a(iii) of the Talbot County Code to provide that applicants for new and renewal of short-term rental licenses provide notice to the 50 properties nearest to the short-term rental dwelling, or owners of all properties with a property line within 1,000 feet, including line of sight within 1,000 feet across waterways, whichever is less.  He stated that the Code currently requires that notices be sent to owners of all properties with a property line within 1,000 feet, including line of sight within 1,000 feet across waterways.  Mr. Stepp stated that the intent of the request for the proposed text amendment is to make the process less onerous for both new and renewal applications.  Council discussion ensued.  Upon motion by Mr. Stepp, seconded by Ms. Haythe, the Council approved forwarding the proposed text amendment to the Planning Commission for review and recommendation by voting 3 – 2 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher – Nay Ms. Mielke – Nay Ms. Haythe – Aye

## VIII. <u>Council Discussion of Proposed Text Amendment to Chapter 190 Concerning Signs – County</u> 

Attorney, Patrick Thomas stated that the proposed text amendment was requested by Attorney Zach Smith on behalf of his client, Preston Automotive, who recently purchased the Toyota dealership on U.S. Rt. 50 in Easton.  Mr. Thomas stated that Preston Automotive would like to display a flag with their frog logo on it, which represents their non-profit entity, but which is also associated with the business, and is currently prohibited under the County’s current zoning code.  Mr. Thomas stated that the proposed text amendment would permit use of a flag by Preston Automotive and other similar businesses.  Council discussion ensued with Mr. Smith who outlined reasons for the request and various facets related to same, including zoning regulations within the town of Easton. Upon motion by Mr. Stepp, seconded by Ms. Haythe, the Council approved forwarding the proposed text amendment to the Planning Commission for review and recommendation by voting 5 – 0 as follows: 

Mr. Callahan – Aye Mr. Stepp – Aye Mr. Lesher – Aye Ms. Mielke –Aye Ms. Haythe – Aye

- K. <u>Council Consideration of Maryland Senate Bill 791 (SB791),</u> _<u>Correctional Services and Public Safety - Immigration Enforcement – Prohibitions (Community Trust Act)</u>_ – County Attorney, Patrick Thomas, read a summary of the legislation as outlined in the fiscal note for same.  He stated that SB791, as it would apply to Talbot County specifically, would prohibit the Detention Center employees from communicating with the federal government regarding certain individuals subject to a detainer, and if they were to violate that, then that individual could bring an action against the County or the employee for actual damages, punitive damages, and injunctive relief.   Council discussion ensued with Mr. Thomas.  Mr. Stamp inquired of Council if they wished to take a position on the legislation and forward that position to the Legislature. - XII. <u>Work Session with Department of Economic Development and Tourism on Draft Strategic Plan –</u> Dan Gundersen, FM, HLM, Senior Vice President, Camoin Associates; Lindsay Ortiz, Director of Strategic Planning, Camoin Associates; Debbie Walsworth, Chairperson, Economic Development Commission; Cassandra Vanhooser, Director, Department of Economic Development and Tourism – The Council held a work session with representatives of consultant, Camoin Associates on Tuesday, February 24, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. in the Bradley Meeting Room, South Wing, Talbot County Courthouse, 11 North Washington Street, Easton, Maryland.  Prior to the presentation by Camoin and Associates, Ms. Vanhooser stated that the draft Strategic Plan is the culmination of approximately two years of work by the Department of Economic Development and Tourism and a year of work with Camoin and Associates.  Ms. Walsworth stated that not only is she personally invested in Talbot County, but as the owner of an accounting firm with approximately 4,000 clients, she has her pulse on the economics of the community.  She stated that over the last 18 months, she had been involved in all the meetings to gather input from businesses and members of the public and that the sentiment most expressed by the community was that the Strategic Plan should sustain the culture, economy and way of life in the county that its residents and visitors have come to appreciate and want to see carried into the future.  She stated that, in her opinion, the draft Strategic Plan is focused, practical, builds on things that currently exist and strives to make improvements where possible, and  aligns the workforce with industry.  Mr. Gundersen and Ms. Ortiz utilized a PowerPoint presentation to brief the Council on the approach used to develop the draft Strategic Plan using the guiding principles of:  collaboration across communities and within the entrepreneurial ecosystem unique to Talbot County; well-planned growth; agriculture as the
