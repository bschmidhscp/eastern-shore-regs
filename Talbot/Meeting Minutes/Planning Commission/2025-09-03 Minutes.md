## **8. Commission Matters** 

- **a.** Commissioner Spies inquired about the County involvement in the Rails for Trails construction process. Brennan Tarleton responded that a lot of the process is funded through the Maryland Transportation Authority (MDOT).  The State has been updating the County Council on the process.  Community concerns can be directed to the County Councell for consolidation.  This would allow the county to have a consolidated format of community concerns to provide MDOT at a possible future listening session. Brennan will reach out for a direct point of contact for questions regarding Rails for Trails.
