**5. Discussion Items** — 

      - **a. The Planning Commission unanimously voiced new concerns regarding the December 4, 2024, recommendation to the County Council on draft legislation concerning cannabis.  The Planning Commission would like to send a letter to the County Council to clarify the Planning Commissions position including the following:** 

1. Separate the Cannabis Grower and Processor (if cannabis grown on-site) in the land use table to only permit Cannabis Processing by Special Exception 

2. Change the minimum lot size for both Growers and Processors from 20 acres to 40 acres 3. The Planning Commission strongly supports the bill as drafted requiring minimum setbacks of 500 feet for both the Cannabis Grower and Processor land use as well as the language regarding odor control.  The legislation as written allows for a reduction of the 500-foot minimum setback to not less than 200 feet if permission is obtained from all owners of the residential properties within the 500-foot setback for both Cannabis Growing and Processing.
