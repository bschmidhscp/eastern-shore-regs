**5. Discussion Items –** 

   - **a.** Planning Commission members and staff discussed the intent and direction of the memo regarding the Farm Based Recreation 

   - **b.** A guest in the cannabis industry will be scheduled to speak with the Planning Commission at an upcoming Planning Commission meeting. The Planning Commission would like a future work session with the Talbot County Council regarding the Farm Based Recreation and the Cannabis Text Amendment. 

   - **c.** Comprehensive Plan updates 

      1. The Talbot County Department of Planning and Zoning, seeks the services of an experienced consultant to assist the County in the completion of an implementable update to the County’s Comprehensive Plan. All proposals are due by September 13, 2024, at 11 am. 

2. Planning and Zoning staff will be organizing a Selection Committee to help in this process.  The Planning Commission will be asked to choose one or two Planning Commission members to a participate.
