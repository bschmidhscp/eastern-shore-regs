# **APPENDIX B TALBOT COUNTY RIGHT-TO-FARM NOTICE (Chapter 128, Right to Farm)** 

The Talbot County Comprehensive Plan recognizes agriculture as a significant part of Talbot County life, both as a major industry and as the most important contributor to the County's unique rural character. As a result of agricultural operations, you may be subject to certain inconveniences or discomforts, including but not limited to smoke, noise, vibration, odors, fumes, dust, pests, glare, the operation of machinery of any kind during any twenty-four-hour period (including aircraft), the use of irrigation, vibration, the storage and disposal of manure, application of fertilizer, pesticides, and other agricultural chemicals. Talbot County has determined that inconveniences or discomforts associated with such agricultural operations are generally temporary in nature and shall not be considered to be an interference with reasonable use and enjoyment of land and residential or accessory structures, if such operations are conducted in accordance with generally accepted agricultural practices. If you have any questions concerning this policy, please contact the Talbot County Office of Planning and Zoning for additional information.
