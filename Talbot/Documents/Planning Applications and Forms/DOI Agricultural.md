Article 73, Forest Conservation, Section 73.3(B), of the _Talbot County Code_ establishes a list of activities that are **Exempt** from the County’s Forest Conservation Ordinance, provided the applicant files a **Declaration of Intent** with the Department of Planning and Zoning assuring compliance with all requirements of the Forest Conservation Act.  Based on the information provided by the applicant, stating that the property will be used for agriculture and agricultural related purposes, it has been determined that the proposed activity is **Exempt** from the Talbot County Forest Conservation Ordinance. 

The following activities/uses are permitted under this **Exemption** : 

1. Agricultural activities not resulting in a change of land use; 

2. That erection, alteration, or expansion of agricultural support buildings and other related structures using accepted Best Management Practices; and 

3. The cutting, clearing, or grading of more than 40,000 square feet of forest per year. 

The applicant must include a sketch map of the property that shows the area to be cleared. 

I hereby verify that the property will be used for the proposed activity stated above and that no regulated activity shall occur on the above-referenced property within five (5) years of the effective date of this Declaration of Intent. 

- I understand that: 

   - a. The conditions upon which this **Declaration** were granted may not be changed without approval by the Department of Planning and Zoning; and 

   - b. Any regulated activity, as defined by the Talbot County Forest Conservation Ordinance, that is not the subject of this **Declaration of Intent** , will require additional approval.
