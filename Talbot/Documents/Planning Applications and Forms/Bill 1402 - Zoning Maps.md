A BILL TO AMEND THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAPS OF TALBOT COUNTY,
MARYLAND (SPECIFICALLY, MAPS 1, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 22, 24, 30, 31, 32, 33,
38, 39, 40, 40A, 41, 42, 43, 44, 44A, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49, 51, 55, AND 56) AND TO REZONE
THE AFFECTED LANDS CONSISTENT WITH THE 2016 TALBOT COUNTY
COMPREHENSIVE PLAN

WHEREAS, Talbot County adopted a new Comprehensive Plan in 2016 pursuant to Bill 1329;
and,

WHEREAS, Land Use Article § 1-417, Md. Code Ann., requires charter counties to implement
comprehensive plans by adopting local land use and zoning laws, including zoning maps; and,

WHEREAS, Talbot County desires to implement the 2016 Comprehensive Plan through the
adoption of revised zoning maps as set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF TALBOT
COUNTY, MARYLAND that:

SECTION ONE: The Official Zoning Maps of Talbot County, Maps 1, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12, 14,
16, 22, 24, 30, 31, 32, 33, 38, 39, 40, 40A, 41, 42, 43, 44, 44A, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49, 51, 55, and 56
shall be and are hereby amended as shown and described on the maps attached hereto as Exhibit
“A” and incorporated by reference herein.

SECTION TWO: The digital version of the Official Zoning Maps of Talbot County shall be
amended to reflect the above map amendments. In the event of a conflict between the printed
and digital versions of the map amendments, the digital version controls.

SECTION THREE: The Talbot County Department of Planning and Zoning shall ensure that,
where applicable, each project submittal utilizes the digitally generated, geo-referenced zoning
boundary as shown on the Official Zoning Maps of Talbot County, Maryland (Digital Version).

SECTION FOUR: The zoning classification amendments resulting from the adoption of this
Ordinance may not affect the initial construction of a development project or activity if, prior to
the effective date, the development project or activity receives growth allocation, preliminary
subdivision approval, site plan approval, or any other final approval, or is vested.

SECTION FIVE: If any provision of this Ordinance or the application thereof to any person or
circumstance is held invalid for any reason in a court of competent jurisdiction, the invalidity
does not affect other provisions or any other application of this Ordinance which can be given
effect without the invalid provision or application, and for this purpose the provisions of this
Ordinance are declared severable.

SECTION SIX The Publishers of the Talbot County Code, the Department of Planning and
Zoning, or the Talbot County Office of Law, in consultation with and subject to the approval of

SECTION SEVEN: This Ordinance shall take effect sixty (60) days from the date of its
passage.
