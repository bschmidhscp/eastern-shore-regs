# **Mobile/Manufactured Home Park License Application** 

In accordance with the _Talbot County Code_ , Chapter 91, Mobile/Manufactured Home Rental Communities, an annual license is required to operate a mobile/manufactured home rental community in Talbot County.  This application is made for a permit to maintain and operate a mobile/manufactured home park for the license year, July 1, 20____ to June 30, 20____.

8. Application fee as determined by fee schedule adopted by County Council.  Check shall be made payable to:  Talbot County, Maryland. 

9. Applicant shall make the premises and rental records available for inspection by the designated Minimum Livability Code Official of Talbot County during normal business hours. 

10. The applicant shall conform to any and all rules and regulations and amendments thereto of the _Talbot County Code_ in connection with such permit, it being understood that this permit may be revoked for failure to do so.
