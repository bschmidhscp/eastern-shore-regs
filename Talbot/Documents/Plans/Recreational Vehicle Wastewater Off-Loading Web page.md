# **<u>Recreational Vehicle Wastewater Off-Loading Procedures</u>** 

The Talbot County provides for economical and environmentally protective offloading and treatment of recreational vehicle wastewaters at the Talbot County Bio-Solids Utilization Facility (TCBUF) located at 9786 Klondike Road, Easton Maryland. 

If you want to arrange for this service do the following: 

- Pay a flat rate of $25.00, preferably by check, and bring same to the Talbot County Department of  Public Works, located at 28712 Glebe Road, Suite 3, Easton, Maryland (410-770-8170) 

- You will be given directions to the TCBUF (approximately 10-15 minute travel from our office) and issued a receipt for payment.  Your payment receipt will be required when you off-load your wastewater at the TCBUF. 

- Once you arrive at the site you will be served by our Environmental Operations Division employees who will assist with the safe off-loading of your vehicle. 

# **_It’s simple!  It’s safe! It’s Environmentally Responsible!_**
