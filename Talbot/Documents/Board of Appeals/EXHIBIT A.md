Rule 3. Hearsay

(1) incompetent;
(2) irrelevant;

Rule 5. Rules of privilege
The presiding officer shall apply a privilege that law recognizes.
Rule 6. Scope of evidence
On a genuine issue in any hearing on any application, each party is entitled to:
(1) call witnesses;
(2) offer evidence, including rebuttal evidence;
(3) cross-examine any witness that another party or the Board calls: and
(4) present siummation and argument.
Rule 7. Documentary evidence
The presiding officer may receive documentary evidence:
(1) in the form of copies or excerpts: or
(2) by incorporation by reference.
Rule 8. Official notice of facts
(1) The Board may take official notice of a fact that is:
(i) judicially noticeable; or
(ii) general, technical, or scientific and within the specialized knowledge of the agency.
(2) Before taking official notice of a fact, the presiding officer:

(i) before or during the hearing, by reference in a preliminary report, or otherwise, shall
notify each party; and

(ii) shall give each party an opportunity to contest the fact.
Rule 9, Evaluation

The Board may use its experience, technical competence, and specialized knowledge in the
evaluation of evidence.
