# Frequently Asked Questions _FAQ_

<!-- page 1 -->
# **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ’s)** 

**Question:** Can I apply for my building permit by mail? **Answer** :  No, the building permit must be applied for in person as we must review the application and send the applicant to various departments to collect fees 

**Question** :  When can I apply for my building permit? **Answer** :  Permits may be applied for Monday through Friday from 8:00 am until 4:00 pm.  We will not take any new applications after 4:00 pm 

**Question** :  Can I pay for my permit once the permit is ready to be issued? **Answer** :  No, the building permit fee must be paid the day the permit is applied for 

**Question** :  Can I come and pick up my building permit once it is issued? **Answer** :  No, the permit can not be picked up.  We mail all of our building permits unless special arrangements have been made. 

**Question** :  What forms of payment does your department collect? **Answer** :  We are only able to accept checks as form of payment for permits, renewals and re-inspection fees.  We do not keep cash in our office, nor do we have a credit card machine. 

**Question** :  How long are permits good for? **Answer** :  Permits are good for 18 months.  The first inspection must be called in within 9 months. 

**Question:** What is the cost for a building permit? **Answer** : Building permit fees are based on the value of the construction of the project. 

**Question:** When can I schedule inspections for my building permit? **Answer** :  Inspections may be called into our office Monday through Friday from the hours of 8:00 am until 3:30 pm (3:30 pm is in the cut off time for obtaining an inspection for the following business day).
