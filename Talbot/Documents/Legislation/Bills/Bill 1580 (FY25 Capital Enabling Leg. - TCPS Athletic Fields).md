A BILL TO AUTHORIZE THE IMPROVEMENT OF ATHLETIC FIELDS AT PUBLIC SCHOOLS WITHIN TALBOT COUNTY 

BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, as follows: 

SECTION 1: The improvement of athletic fields at public schools within Talbot County is hereby authorized as a capital project. The scope of work includes the planning, design, preparation, installation, and construction necessary to replace and/or improve athletic fields at public schools within Talbot County. The scope of work excludes any and all purchases of products or services that may be considered repair or maintenance, including, but not limited to, mowing, grooming, aerating, topdressing, seeding, fertilization, weed control, and pest management. Approval of this capital project includes authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all associated transactional costs and for incidental and customary costs and charges associated with, and reasonably necessary for, the completion of the project. 

SECTION 2: The County Council of Talbot County is hereby authorized to expend up to $116,679 for this project. The project will be funded by $116,679 in funds from the Capital Projects Fund. 

SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.
