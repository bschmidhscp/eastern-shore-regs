# A BILL TO AUTHORIZE REPLACEMENT OF THE AUDITORIUM LIGHTING SYSTEM AT EASTON HIGH SCHOOL 

BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, as follows: 

SECTION 1: The replacement of the auditorium lighting system at Easton High School is hereby authorized as a capital project. The scope of work includes, but is not limited to, the purchase of products and services necessary for the replacement of the auditorium lighting system at the Easton High School. Approval of this capital project includes authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all associated transactional costs and for incidental and customary costs and charges associated with, and reasonably necessary for, the completion of the project. 

SECTION 2: The County Council of Talbot County is hereby authorized to expend up to $817,321 for this project. The project will be funded by $817,321 in funds from the Capital Projects Fund. 

SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.
