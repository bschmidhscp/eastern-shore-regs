## ARTICLE II 

Board of Economic Development and Tourism 

§<sup>24-4.Boardcreated.</sup> 

The Talbot County Board of Economic Development and Tourism shall be and the same is hereby created as an advisory board. 

§<sup>24-5.</sup> Membership; terms;<sup>vacancies.</sup> 

- A. The Talbot County Board of Economic Development and Tourism shall consist of 17 members. The Board shall adopt bylaws and rules of procedure to govern its meetings. Members of the Board shall receive no compensation for their services. 

- . The Director of Economic Development and Tourism and the Airport Manager shall serve as ex officio, nonvoting members. 

- . The 15 appointed, voting members shall consist of representatives from various County

§<sup>24-6.Dutiesand</sup> functions; participation<sup>of members.</sup> 

- A. The Board shall develop plans, goals, objectives, and programs for the promotion and management<sup>ofeconomic</sup> development<sup>andtourisminthe</sup> County<sup>andrecommend</sup> proposed policies<sup>and</sup> legislation regarding<sup>economic</sup> development<sup>andtourismtothe</sup> County<sup>Council.</sup>

- The Board shall encourage development<sup>of</sup> cooperative programs among the various municipalities, organizations<sup>and</sup> segments<sup>of businessesandthetourism</sup> industry throughout the County and federal, State, and local agencies, boards, departments and commissions, and shall act as a clearinghouse for coordination of ongoing programs. 

- .<sup>TheBoard</sup> shall act as an advisory and consultive body to the County Council regarding policy, planning,<sup>and</sup> funding<sup>issues</sup> affecting<sup>economic</sup> development<sup>and</sup> tourism.
