A BILL TO AMEND BILL NO. 1474 TO AMEND THE APPROPRIATION FOR
IMPROVEMENTS TO AIRPORT ROAD

WHEREAS Bill No. 1474 authorized the improvement of Airport Road.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County,
Maryland, as follows:

SECTION 1: The improvement of Airport Road is hereby authorized as a capital project.
The scope of work includes, but is not limited to, surveying and right-of-way acquisition
for the future design, engineering, property acquisition, drainage improvements, and
construction and widening of Airport Road between Maryland Rt. 50 and Goldsborough
Neck Road in order to improve traffic safety. Approval of this capital project includes
authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all
incidental and customary costs and charges associated with and reasonably necessary for
design, engineering, construction and completion of the project.

SECTION 2: The County Council of Talbot County (the “County Council”) is hereby
authorized to expend up to $2,900,000 for the Airport Road project. The project will be
funded by Development Impact Fees of approximately $700,000, funds from the Capital
Projects Fund of approximately $1,450,000, and Cost Sharing funds of $750,000.
Acceptance of federal and State grant funds, the use of cost sharing funds received by the
County for this project, Development Impact Fees, and the use of funds in the Capital
Projects Fund is authorized. The County Council is authorized to use federal and State
grants, cost sharing funds received by the County for this project, Development Impact
Fees, and funds in the Capital Projects Fund or a combination of federal and State grants,
cost sharing funds received by the County, Development Impact Fees, and funds in the
Capital Projects Fund not to exceed $2,900,000 for this project. This represents an increase
of $1,200,000 over prior authorizations. The increase is funded by additional Capital
Projects funds of $1,200,000.

SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.

A CAPITAL PROJECT FOR THE CONNECTION OF BAR NECK AND

WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT

WHEREAS, Bill No. 1418 authorized the connection of the Bar Neck and Fairbank
communities to the Region V (Tilghman) Wastewater Treatment Plant.;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County,
Maryland, as follows:

SECTION 1: The connection of the Bar Neck and Fairbank communities to the Region V
(Tilghman) Wastewater Treatment Plant is hereby authorized as a capital project. The
scope of work includes, but is not limited to, the design, engineering, property acquisitions

businesses located in Bar Neck and Fairbank communities to the Region V (Tilghman)

expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all associated
transactional costs and for incidental and customary costs and charges associated with and
reasonably required for financing, planning, design, construction, and completion of the

$2,190,000 for the completion of this project. The project will be funded by grants, if

increase of $190,000 over prior authorizations. The increase is funded by additional federal
and State grants, if awarded or by Long Term Borrowing of $190,000. Acceptance of
federal and State grants for this capital project is hereby authorized. Borrowing to finance
this capital project for this project is hereby authorized. All costs incurred for this capital
project shall be repaid from revenues generated from fees paid by the users of this sewer
collection system.
