A BILL TO AMEND BILL NO. 1607 FOR THE PURPOSE OF AMENDING THE
APPROPRIATION FOR THE REPLACEMENTS AND IMPROVEMENTS AT
COUNTY COMMUNITY POOLS AND ASSOCIATED PROPERTY
WHEREAS, Bill No. 1607 authorized the replacements and improvements of County
community pools and associated property.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County,
Maryland (the “County Council”), as follows:
SECTION 1:
The replacements and improvements at County Community Pools and
associated property are hereby authorized as a capital project. The scope of work includes,
but is not limited to, the planning, design, engineering, purchase of materials and
equipment, construction, installation, and furnishing necessary to complete replacements
and improvements at George Murphy Pool and Bay Hundred Pool. Approval of this capital
project includes authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the
appropriation, for all associated transactional costs and for incidental and customary costs
and charges associated with, and reasonably necessary for, the completion of the project.
SECTION 2: The County Council is hereby authorized to expend up to $193,000 for this
project. Acceptance of federal and State grants is authorized. The project will be funded by
$193,000 in funds from the Capital Projects Fund. This represents an increase of $33,000
over prior authorizations. The increase will be funded by additional funds from the Capital
Projects Fund of $33,000.
SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.

enhancements to County facilities.

Maryland (the “County Council”), as follows:
SECTION

authorized as a capital project. Prior authorization for this project included, but was not

devices, construction of new facility entry points, and reconstruction of office entrances at

to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all associated

project. The project will be funded by $760,000 in funds from the Capital Projects Fund.
This represents an increase of $220,000 over prior authorizations. The increase will be
funded by additional funds from the Capital Projects Fund of $220,000.
