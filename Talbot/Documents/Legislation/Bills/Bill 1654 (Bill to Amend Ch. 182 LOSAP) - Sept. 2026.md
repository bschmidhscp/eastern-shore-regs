## §<sup>182-6.Point</sup> system. 

In order to qualify for the benefits pursuant to this chapter, points shall be credited to each volunteer in accordance with the following standards:

- .<sup>One</sup> point<sup>shallbecreditedfor</sup> every<sup>three</sup> hours of attendance in a recognized Countyapproved training course, provided<sup>thatnotmorethan20</sup> points may<sup>becreditedfor all</sup> training courses attended per year. 

- . One point shall be credited for each company or County drill attended in its entirety, provided that not more than 25 points may be credited for all drills attended per year. 

- . One point shall be credited for each official company or County meeting pertaining to the County<sup>fireservicesorrescueservices</sup> attended, provided<sup>thatnotmorethan15</sup> points may<sup>be</sup> credited for all meetings attended per year. 

- .<sup>One</sup> point<sup>shallbe</sup> credited for<sup>eachcall</sup> to which a volunteer responds, provided that not more than 40 of the required points may be credited for all calls responded to per year. 

- . Twenty-Five points shall be credited for completion of a one-year term as an appointed or elected officer in any of the fire or rescue service organizations of the County, or the Volunteer Firemen's Association, provided that not more than one office shall be counted in any calendar year. 

- One point shall be credited for every three hours of acceptable collateral duties, approved by the Director, such as, but not limited to, apparatus and building maintenance, official standby and fire prevention or fund-raising details, provided that not more than 20 points may be credited for all collateral duties performed per year.
