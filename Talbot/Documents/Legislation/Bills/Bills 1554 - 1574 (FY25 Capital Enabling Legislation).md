A BILL TO AMEND BILL NO. 1427 FOR THE PURPOSE OF AMENDING THE
APPROPRIATION
FOR
THE
CONSTRUCTION
OF
NEW
RECYCLING
STATIONS IN TALBOT COUNTY
WHEREAS, Bill No. 1427 authorized the construction of a recycling station for the Bay
Hundred Area.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County,
Maryland, as follows:
SECTION 1:
The construction of new recycling stations in Talbot County is hereby
authorized as a capital project. Prior authorization for this project included, but was not
limited to, the purchase of land and the planning, design, construction and equipping of a
recycling station for the Bay Hundred area. The scope of work is hereby extended to
include, but will not be limited to, the purchase of land and the planning, design,
construction and equipping of new recycling stations in Talbot County. Approval of this
capital project includes authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of
the appropriation, for all associated transactional costs and for incidental and customary
costs and charges associated with, and reasonably required for, the completion of the
project.
SECTION 2: The Talbot County Council is hereby authorized to expend up to $420,000
for this project. Acceptance of federal and State grants is authorized. This project will be
funded by $420,000 in funds from the Capital Projects Fund. This represents an increase
of $220,000 over prior authorizations. The increase will be funded by additional funds from
the Capital Projects Fund of $220,000.
SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.

A BILL TO AUTHORIZE IMPROVEMENTS TO THE VACUUM COLLECTION
SYSTEM SERVING THE REGION II MARTINGHAM SEWER SERVICE AREA
BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, as follows:
SECTION
1:
Improvements to the vacuum collection system serving the Region II
Martingham Sewer Service Area is hereby authorized as a capital project. The scope of
work includes, but is not limited to, the preparation, design, engineering, replacement,
construction, and installation necessary to improve the vacuum collection system serving
the Region II Martingham Sewer Service Area. Approval of this capital project includes
authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all
associated transactional costs and for incidental and customary costs and charges
associated with, and reasonably necessary for, the completion of the project.
SECTION 2: The Talbot County Council is hereby authorized to expend up to $1,000,000
for this project. Acceptance of federal and State grants and the use of long-term borrowing
is authorized. The project will be funded by long-term borrowing of approximately
$1,000,000. All costs incurred for this capital project shall be repaid by the users of the
wastewater treatment system serving Region II Martingham Sewer Service Area.
SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.

A BILL TO AMEND BILL NO. 1538 FOR THE PURPOSE OF AMENDING THE
APPROPRIATION FOR IMPROVEMENTS TO AND EXPANSION OF THE
CHAPEL DISTRICT ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
WHEREAS, Bill No. 1538 authorized the improvements to and expansion of the Chapel
District Elementary School.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County,
Maryland, as follows:
SECTION 1:
The improvements to and expansion of the Chapel District Elementary
School is hereby authorized as a capital project. Prior authorization for this project
included, but was not limited to, engineering and architectural services associated with
design and preparation phases, construction, and furnishings. The scope of work is hereby
extended to include the cost of issuance of any bonds or other long-term borrowing needed
to finance the project if long-term borrowing is utilized. Approval of this capital project
includes authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount ofthe appropriation,
for all associated transactional costs and for incidental and customary costs and charges
associated with, and reasonably necessary for, the completion of the project.
SECTION 2: The Talbot County Council is hereby authorized to expend up to $27,800,000
for this project. The County Council is authorized to utilize long-term borrowing not to
exceed $26,900,000. The project will be funded by approximately $26,900,000 in long
term borrowing and $900,000 in funds from the Capital Projects Fund. This represents an
increase of $26,900,000 over prior authorizations. The increase will be funded by long
term borrowing of approximately $26,900,000.
SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.
