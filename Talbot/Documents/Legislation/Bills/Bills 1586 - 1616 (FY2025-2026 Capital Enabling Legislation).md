WHEREAS, Bill Nos. 1549, 1479 and 1244 authorized replacing the roof of the North and
South Wings of the Talbot County Courthouse.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County,
Maryland (the “County Council”), as follows:
SECTION 1: The replacement and improvement ofroofs of buildings and structures owned
by Talbot County is hereby authorized as a capital project. Prior authorization for this
project included, but was not limited to, removal and replacement of all, or sections of, the
slate roofing system of the North and South Wings of the Talbot County Courthouse. The
scope ofwork is hereby extended to include, but will not be limited to, the planning, design,
construction, removal and replacement of all, or sections of, roofs of buildings and
structures owned by Talbot County. Approval of this capital project includes authorization
to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all associated
transactional costs and for incidental and customary costs and charges associated with, and
reasonably required for, the completion of the project.
SECTION 2: The County Council is hereby authorized to expend up to $895,000 for this
project. Acceptance of federal and State grants is authorized. The project will be funded by
$895,000 in funds from the Capital Projects Fund. This represents an increase of $300,000
over prior authorizations. The increase will be funded by additional funds from the Capital
Projects Fund of $300,000.
SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.
