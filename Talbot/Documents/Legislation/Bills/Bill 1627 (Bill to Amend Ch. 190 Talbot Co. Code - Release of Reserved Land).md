73.3 Release or relocation. 

- A. Release. A grantor or his successors may petition the County Council for partial or complete release of a reserved land agreement or reservation of development rights agreement ifJj the parcel’s zoning classification changes; or (ii) the parcel is subject to an option contract with the State of Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation (MALPF) that was entered into between April 1, 2024, and March 31, 2026, is binding upon the owner of the parcel, and will establish a perpetual easement over the parcel. 

- B. Relocation. The boundaries of lands subject to the agreement shall be delineated on the final plat. A major revision plat shall be submitted for the relocation or adjustment of reserved land acreage. The relocation shall be of an equal area and shall be within the boundaries of the original parcel.
