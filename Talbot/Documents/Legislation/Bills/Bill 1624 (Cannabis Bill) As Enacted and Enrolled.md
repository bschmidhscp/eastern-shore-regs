# §<sup>190-78TermsDefined</sup> 

* * * 

# CANNABIS 

- A. “Cannabis” means the plant cannabis sativa 1. and any part of the plant, including all nonsynthetically derived extracts, cannabinoids, isomers, acids, salts, and salts of isomers, whether growing or not, with delta-9 tetrahydrocannabinol concentration greater than 0.3 percent on a dry weight basis. 

- B. “Cannabis” includes cannabis products. 

- C “Cannabis” does not include hemp or hemp products, as defined in Md. Code Ann., Agriculture, 14-101. 

4

<!-- page 5 -->
# CANNABIS DISPENSARY 

An entity licensed under Md. Code Ann., Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis, Division III (Cannabis). Title 36 (Medical and Adult-Use Cannabis) that acquires, possesses, repackages, transports, sells, distributes, or dispenses cannabis or cannabis products, including tinctures, aerosols, oils, and ointments, related supplies, and educational materials for use by qualifying patients, caregivers, or consumers through a storefront. 

# CANNABIS GROWER 

An entity licensed as a cannabis grower under Md. Code Ann., Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis, Division III (Cannabis), Title 36 (Medical and Adult-Use Cannabis) that cultivates or packages cannabis and is authorized by the Maryland Cannabis Administration to provide cannabis to other cannabis licensees and registered independent testing laboratories. 

# CANNABIS PROCESSOR 

An entity licensed as a cannabis processor under Md. Code Ann., Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis, Division III (Cannabis), Title 36 (Medical and Adult-Use Cannabis) that transforms cannabis into another product or extract and packages and labels the cannabis product and is authorized by the Maryland Cannabis Administration to provide cannabis to licensed dispensaries and registered independent testing laboratories. 

# CANNABIS PRODUCT 

A product that is composed of cannabis, cannabis concentrate, cannabis extract, or any other ingredient and is intended for use or consumption, including edible products, oils, tinctures, and any other product produced and regulated under COMAR Title 14 (Independent Agencies), Subtitle 17 (Maryland Cannabis Administration). 

# CANNABIS-INFUSED PRODUCTS 

Oil, wax, ointment, salve, tincture, capsule, suppository, dermal patch, cartridge, or any other product containing cannabis concentrate or usable cannabis that has been processed so that the dried leaves and flowers are integrated into other material.

# GREENHOUSE, RETAIL 

A structure designed and used for growing plants other than cannabis that are displayed and sold to the general public on the site.
