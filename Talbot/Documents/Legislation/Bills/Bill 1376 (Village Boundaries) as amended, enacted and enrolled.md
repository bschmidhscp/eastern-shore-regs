# Bill 1376 (Village Boundaries) as amended, enacted and enrolled

COUNTY COUNCIL
OF

TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND

2017 Legislative Session, Legislative Day No.: October 24, 2017

Bill No.: 1376 *AS AMENDED*

Expiration Date: December 28. 2017

Introduced by: Mr. Bartlett, Mr. Callahan, Ms. Price, Ms. Williams

A BILL TO AMEND THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAPS OF TALBOT COUNTY,
MARYLAND (SPECIFICALLY, MAPS 1, 4, 5, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 22, 24, 31, 32, 40, 40A,
41, 42, AND 48) AND THE CATALOG OF LIMITED DEVELOPMENT AREAS
(“LDA’S”) AND INTENSELY DEVELOPED AREAS (“IDA’S”) OF TALBOT COUNTY,
MARYLAND (SPECIFICALLY, LDA MAPS 32, 42, 48, 52, 53, 58, 69, AND 70) TO
MODIFY VILLAGE CENTER ZONING DISTRICT (“VC ZONE”) BOUNDARIES FOR
THE VILLAGES OF BOZMAN, CLAIBORNE, CORDOVA, COPPERVILLE,
LONGWOODS, MCDANIEL, NEWCOMB, ROYAL OAK, SKIPTON, TUNIS MILLS,
WITTMAN, WILLIAMSBURG AND WYE MILLS AS RECOMMENDED IN THE 2016
TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN (THE “COMPREHENSIVE PLAN”),
AND TO REZONE AFFECTED LANDS REMOVED FROM THE VC ZONE TO
ZONING CLASSIFICATIONS CONSISTENT WITH THE COMPREHENSIVE PLAN
AND IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE SURROUNDING AREAS

Introduced, read first time, ordered posted, and public hearing scheduled on Tuesday.
November 14, 2017 at 6:30 p.m. at the Bradley Meeting Room, South Wing, Talbot County
Courthouse, 11 North Washington Street, Easton, Maryland 21601.

A BILL TO AMEND THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAPS OF TALBOT COUNTY,
MARYLAND (SPECIFICALLY, MAPS 1, 4, 5, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 22, 24, 31, 32, 40, 40A,
41, 42, AND 48) AND THE CATALOG OF LIMITED DEVELOPMENT AREAS
(“LDA’S”) AND INTENSELY DEVELOPED AREAS (“IDA’S”) OF TALBOT COUNTY,
MARYLAND (SPECIFICALLY, LDA MAPS 32, 42, 48, 52, 53, 58, 69, AND 70) TO
MODIFY VILLAGE CENTER ZONING DISTRICT (“VC ZONE”) BOUNDARIES FOR
THE VILLAGES OF BOZMAN, CLAIBORNE, CORDOVA, COPPERVILLE,
LONGWOODS, MCDANIEL, NEWCOMB, ROYAL OAK, SKIPTON, TUNIS MILLS,
WITTMAN, WILLIAMSBURG AND WYE MILLS AS RECOMMENDED IN THE 2016
TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN (THE “COMPREHENSIVE PLAN”),
AND TO REZONE AFFECTED LANDS REMOVED FROM THE VC ZONE TO
ZONING CLASSIFICATIONS CONSISTENT WITH THE COMPREHENSIVE PLAN
AND IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE SURROUNDING AREAS

WHEREAS, the current Official Zoning Maps of Talbot County, Maryland, consisting of
Maps | through 64, including 40A and 44A (the “Official Zoning Maps”), and the Catalogue of
Limited Development Areas and Intensely Developed Areas of Talbot County, Maryland,
consisting of Maps 1 through 83 (the “Critical Area Maps”), were adopted pursuant to Talbot
County Bill 1295; and,

WHEREAS, the 2016 Talbot County Comprehensive Plan (the “Comprehensive Plan”)
recommends adjusting the boundaries of certain villages consistent with policies in the
Comprehensive Plan concerning village character and scale (see Appendix A to the
Comprehensive Plan); and,

WHEREAS, through this Bill, the County desires to undertake comprehensive
remapping of the villages of Bozman, Claiborne, Cordova, Copperville, Longwoods, McDaniel,
Newcomb, Royal Oak, Skipton, Tunis Mills, Wittman, Williamsburg, and Wye Mills to
implement the Comprehensive Plan recommendations and to promote the health, safety, and
welfare of the community.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND that:

SECTION ONE:
A. The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.
B. Official Zoning Maps 1, 4, 5, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 22, 24, 31, 32, 40, 40A, 41, 42,

and 48 shall be and are hereby amended as shown and described on the maps attached
hereto as Exhibit “A” and incorporated by reference herein.

Cc. Critical Area Maps 32, 42, 48, 52, 53, 58, 69, and 70 shall be and are hereby
amended as shown and described on the maps attached hereto as Exhibit “B” and
incorporated by reference herein.

SECTION TWO: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that the digital version of the Official Zoning
Maps shall be amended to reflect the above map amendments. In the event of a conflict between
the printed and digital versions of the map amendments, the digital version controls.

SECTION THREE: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that the Department of Planning and Zoning
shall ensure that, where applicable, each project submittal utilizes the digitally generated, geo-
referenced zoning boundary as shown on the Official Zoning Maps of Talbot County, Maryland
(Digital Version).

SECTION FOUR: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that upon adoption of this ordinance, the
Department of Planning and Zoning shall provide the Official Zoning Maps of Talbot County,
Maryland in paper and digital format to the Critical Area Commission for the Chesapeake and
Atlantic Coastal Bays for final approval;

SECTION FIVE: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that the zoning classification amendments
resulting from the adoption of this ordinance may not affect the initial construction of a
development project or activity if, prior to the effective date, the development project or activity
receives growth allocation, final subdivision approval, final site plan approval, or any other final
approval, or is vested.

SECTION SIX: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that if any provision of this ordinance or the
application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid for any reason in a court of
competent jurisdiction, the invalidity does not affect other provisions or any other application of
this ordinance which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and for this
purpose the provisions of this ordinance are declared severable.

SECTION SEVEN: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that the title and a summary of this
ordinance shall be published once on the first publication date after enactment of the ordinance
in accordance with County Charter § 213 (c). The title is not a substantive part of this ordinance.
If the ordinance is amended, the title may be administratively revised if required to conform the
title to the content of the ordinance as finally enacted.

SECTION EIGHT: BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that the Publishers of the Talbot County
Code or the Talbot County Office of Law, in consultation with and subject to the approval of the
County Manager, shall make non-substantive corrections to codification, style, capitalization,
punctuation, grammar, spelling, and any internal or external reference or citation to the Code that
is incorrect or obsolete, with no further action required by the County Council. All such
corrections shall be adequately referenced and described in an editor's note following the section
affected.

SECTION NINE: AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, that this ordinance shall take effect sixty

(60) days from the date of its passage and upon approval by the Critical Area Commission if
such approval is required by law.
