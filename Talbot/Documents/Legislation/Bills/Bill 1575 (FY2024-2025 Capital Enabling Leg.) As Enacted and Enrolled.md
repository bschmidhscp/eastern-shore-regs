A BILL TO AUTHORIZE THE REPLACEMENT AND IMPROVEMENT OF THE CART BRIDGE AT THE NINTH HOLE OF THE TALBOT COUNTY HOG NECK GOLF COURSE 

BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, as follows: 

SECTION 1: The replacement and improvement of the cart bridge at the ninth hole of the Talbot County Hog Neck Golf Course is hereby authorized as a capital project. The scope of work includes, but is not limited to, the engineering services, surveying, planning, design, preparation of construction documents, permitting, excavation, demolition, and construction necessary to replace and improve the cart bridge at the ninth hole of the Talbot County Hog Neck Golf Course. Approval of this capital project includes authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all associated transactional costs and for incidental and customary costs and charges associated with, and reasonably necessary for, the completion of the project. 

SECTION 2: The Talbot County Council is hereby authorized to expend up to $200,000 for this project. Acceptance of federal and State grants is authorized. The project will be funded by $200,000 in funds from the Recreation Facilities Fund. 

SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.
