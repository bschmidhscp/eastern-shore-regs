WHEREAS, pursuant to § 620 of the Charter of Talbot County (the “Charter”), no
obligations of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”) may be authorized in any fiscal year for
or on account of any capital project not included in the County budget as finally enacted for that
year; but upon receipt of a recommendation in writing from the County Manager, the County
Council of Talbot County (the “County Council”) may, after public hearing and with the
affirmative vote of four-fifths of the entire County Council, amend the County budget in
accordance with the recommendation without increasing the total amount of the appropriation
therefor; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 615 of the Charter, inter-project transfers of appropriations
between capital projects in the capital budget may be authorized by legislative act of the County
Council upon request of the County Manager, but no new project may be created nor any
abandoned except in accordance with § 620 of the Charter; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 213(b) of the Charter, the County Council is authorized to
introduce and pass emergency bills affecting public health, safety, or welfare, provided that such
bills shall be plainly designated as emergency bills and contain, after the enacting clause, a
declaration stating that an emergency exists and describing the claimed emergency in clear and
specific terms; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 213(d) of the Charter, an emergency bill may only be passed
by an affirmative vote of at least four-fifths of the County Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 214 of the Charter, an emergency bill shall take effect on the
date of its passage.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF TALBOT
COUNTY, MARYLAND that:

SECTION ONE: The County Council has determined that an emergency exists due to the
failure of the ice rink floor at the Talbot County Community Center (the “TCCC”). The repairs
need to commence as soon as possible to ensure the ice rink’s availability for the 2023 summer
camp season as well as the 2023-2024 winter sports season, and the Director of Parks and
Recreation has requested the County Council to take prompt action to appropriate the funds to
make the necessary repairs. Delaying the completion of the repairs an additional sixty (60) days
would jeopardize the use of the ice rink for the 2023 summer camp season as well as the 2023-
2024 winter sports season, at a substantial detriment to the TCCC and the community at large.

<!-- page 3 -->
SECTION TWO: The TCCC Ice Rink Floor Removal and Replacement is hereby
authorized as a capital project. The scope of the work includes, but is not limited to, the removal
of the existing floor and the installation of a new floor.

SECTION THREE: The County Council is hereby authorized to expend up to $950,000
for this capital project. Approval of this capital project includes authorization to expend funds,
not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation, for all incidental and customary costs and
charges associated with, and reasonably necessary for, the design, engineering, construction,
financing, and completion of the project. This capital project will be funded by federal and State
grant funds if available and capital project funds if grant funds are not available or do not cover
the full cost of this capital project.

SECTION FOUR: The inter-project transfer of $950,000 from the capital project
authorized by Bill No. 1171 for the extension of water and sewer lines to the TCCC to the capital
project authorized by this Bill is hereby authorized.

SECTION FIVE: Consistent with §§ 615 and 620 of the Charter, the Capital Budget for
Fiscal Year 2022-2023 is amended as follows:
