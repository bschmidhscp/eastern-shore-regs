AN EMERGENCY BILL TO AMEND BILL NOS. 1244 AND 1479 FOR THE
PURPOSE OF EXPANDING THE SCOPE OF WORK OF THE CAPITAL
PROJECT TO REPLACE THE ROOF OF THE SOUTH WING OF THE TALBOT
COUNTY COURTHOUSE TO ALSO INCLUDE REPLACING THE ROOF OF
THE NORTH WING OF THE TALBOT COUNTY COURTHOUSE WITHOUT
INCREASING THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF THE APPROPRIATION THEREFOR

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 620 of the Charter of Talbot County (the “Charter”), no
obligations of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”) may be authorized in any fiscal
year for or on account of any capital project not included in the County budget as finally
enacted for that year; but upon receipt of a recommendation in writing from the County
Manager, the County Council of Talbot County (the “County Council”) may, after public
hearing and with the affirmative vote of four-fifths of the entire County Council, amend
the County budget in accordance with the recommendation without increasing the total
amount of the appropriation therefor; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 213(b) of the Charter, the County Council is authorized
to introduce and pass emergency bills affecting public health, safety, or welfare, provided
that such bills shall be plainly designated as emergency bills and contain, after the enacting
clause, a declaration stating that an emergency exists and describing the claimed
emergency in clear and specific terms; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 213(d) of the Charter, an emergency bill may only be
passed by an affirmative vote of at least four-fifths of the County Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 214 of the Charter, an emergency bill shall take effect
on the date of its passage.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND that:

SECTION ONE: The County Council has determined that an emergency exists
due to the identification of leaks and deteriorated condition of the slate roof of the North
Wing of the Talbot County Courthouse (the “North Wing”), making it hazardous to repair
and causing further damage to the roof and building. The repairs need to commence as
soon as possible to prevent damage to the structure of the North Wing and eliminate the
hazard presented by the brittle roof slates. The Director of Facilities Maintenance has
requested the County Council take prompt action to extend the scope of the roof
replacement of the South Wing of the Talbot County Courthouse (the “South Wing”) to
also include replacement of the roof on the North Wing. Delaying the completion of the
repairs an additional sixty (60) days would jeopardize the condition of the infrastructure
and lead to further deterioration of the slates, extending and increasing the hazard they
present, as colder temperatures and weather conditions of winter approach.

SECTION TWO: Pursuant to Bill Nos. 1244 and 1479, the County Council
authorized the expenditure of up to $595,000 in funding from the Capital Projects Fund for

<!-- page 3 -->
the replacement of the slate roof on the South Wing. Such work is nearly complete, and
the contractor engaged by the County is able to also replace the slate roof on the North
Wing without exceeding the authorized expenditure of $595,000.

SECTION THREE: The replacement of the slate roof on the North Wing is
hereby authorized as a capital project. The scope of work includes, but is not limited to,
removal and replacement of all, or sections of, the slate roofing system at the North Wing.
Approval of this capital project includes authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the
total amount of the appropriation, for all incidental and customary costs and charges
associated with and reasonably necessary for design, engineering, construction and
completion of the project.

SECTION FOUR: The County Council is hereby authorized to expend up to
$595,000 in funding from the Capital Projects Fund for this project, such amount consisting
of the amount authorized under Bill No. 1479. No new expenditures are authorized
hereunder.

SECTION FIVE: The level of funding in the Capital Budget for Fiscal Year 2023-
2024 will remain unchanged, as follows:
