A BILL TO AUTHORIZE A FEASIBILITY STUDY FOR, AND POSSIBLE FUTURE
CONSTRUCTION OF, A TALBOT COUNTY VISITORS CENTER

BE IT ENACTED BY the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, as follows:

SECTION 1: A feasibility study for, and possible future construction of, a Talbot County Visitors
Center is hereby authorized as a capital project. The scope of work includes, but is not limited to,
a feasibility study to determine the need for a visitors center and appropriate site locations as well
as for engineering and architectural services associated with any design and preparation phases,
construction, and furnishing of a Visitors Center, if to be constructed. Approval of this capital
project includes authorization to expend funds, not to exceed the total amount of the appropriation,
for all incidental and customary costs and charges reasonably necessary for the completion of the
project.

SECTION 2: The County Council of Talbot County is hereby authorized to expend up to $50,000

in federal and State grants for this project. Acceptance of federal and State grant funds is hereby
authorized.

SECTION 3: This Bill will take effect sixty (60) calendar days from the date of enactment.
