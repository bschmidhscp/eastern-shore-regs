# RESOLUTION 

A RESOLUTION TO ADOPT THE 2002 REPORT OF THE REVIEW OF THE 1992 UPDATE COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWERAGE PLAN FOR TALBOT COUNTY; PURSUANT TO THE POWER AND AUTHORITY CONTAINED IN THE ENVIRONMENTAL ARTICLE, TITLE 9, SUBTITLES, OF THE ANNOTATED CODE OF MARYLAND. 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County, State of Maryland, by Resolution Number 67, introduced on May 4, 1993, and duly adopted as effective on June 1, 1993, has adopted the December 1992 Update of the Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan as the current plan in effect; and 

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of each incorporated town in the County, the County Office of Environmental Health, the County Engineer, the Department of State Planning and the Maryland Department of the Environment have all contributed to this 2002 Report of the Review of the 1992 Update Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan; and 

WHEREAS, those comments which are found to be acceptable and do promote the public health, safety and welfare of Talbot County have been incorporated and made a part of this 2002 Report of the Review of the 1992 Update Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan; and 

WHEREAS, the water and sewer service maps for the incorporated Towns of Easton, Oxford, St. Michaels and Trappe shall be based on the 1992 Update of the Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan and all adopted Resolutions up through August 2002; and 

WHEREAS, the Easton Club East development located on Tax Map 35, Parcels 3, 5, 58, and 104, Tax Map 35 Parcel 62 inclusive of all lots, and Tax Map 43, Parcel 13 shall be reprogrammed from S-2 and W-2 to S-i to W-i thus having immediate priority status for water and sewer service in the Town of Easton; and 

WHEREAS, the 2002 Report of the Review of the Talbot County Plan shall be consistent with the currently adopted Talbot County Comprehensive plan of 1997; and 

WHEREAS, upon adoption of the new Talbot County Comprehensive Plan, Talbot County shall initiate the development of a future Report of the Review of the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan to be submitted to the Maryland Department of the Environment and the Maryland Department of Planning eighteen (18) months after adoption of the Talbot County Comprehensive Plan; and 

WHEREA$ Talbot County intends to pursue State funding for expanding the capacity of the St. Michaels Wastewater Treatment Plant from 500,000 to 800,000 gallons per day to meet existing service obligations and to provide for adequate infrastructure for planned growth as designated by the County’s Comprehensive Land Use Plan; and 

WHEREAS, the Region IT (St. Michaels) Wastewater Treatment Plant shall be constructed for a design flow of 1.0 million gallons per day, but will be permitted to discharge 800,000 gallons per day; and

<!-- page 3 -->
WHEREAS, as State funding is predicated on compliance with Priority Funding Area law, the expanded capacity of the St. Michaels Wastewater Treatment Plant will only be used to serve Priority Funding Areas; 

NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland as follows: 

Section 1. Adoption of this amendment, by the County Council of Talbot County, shall authorize the construction of the projects in accordance with and subject to the approval of the Municipal, County, State and/or Federal agencies, as may be required, effective from the date of adoption. 

Section 2. The 2002 Report of the Review of the 1992 Update of the Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan shall revise the goals, procedures, and the programmed areas for existing and future water and sewer service in Talbot County: 

Be It Further Resolved that this Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its date of passage.
