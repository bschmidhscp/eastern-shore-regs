A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER
AND SEWER PLAN FOR THE PURPOSE OF ADDING OR MODIFYING THE TALBOT
COUNTY CAPITAL PROJECT FOR FISCAL YEARS 2023 AND 2024 TO ADD A NEW
CAPITAL PROJECT IN THE AMOUNT OF $4.718 MILLION FOR WATER SYSTEM
IMPROVEMENTS FOR THE TOWN OF OXFORD

WHEREAS, the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”)
contains capital projects for water and sewer service in Talbot County, Maryland, (the “County”);
and

WHEREAS, the Town of Oxford (the “Town”), working with its consulting engineering
firm, George, Miles and Buhr, LLC (“GMB”), identified improvements to the Oxford Water
System (the “Water System”), including a possible new water well, new water meters and billing
software, water treatment improvements, water storage improvements, and improvements to the
distributions system (the “Water System Improvements”) as presented in Section 7.0 of the
Preliminary Engineering Report - Oxford Water System Renovation prepared by GMB dated
September 2021 (the “PER”); and

WHEREAS, the Water System Improvements are estimated to cost $4,718,000; and

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County Council (the “County Council”) desires
to amend the CWSP for the purpose of including the Water System Improvements as a capital
project identified by the Town for Fiscal Years 2023 and 2024 in Table 8 of the CWSP, entitled
“Oxford Water System Capital Improvement Projects;” and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-
506(a)(1)(i), the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot
County Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as the Talbot County Public
Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area; and

WHEREAS, on October 5, 2022, the Planning Commission certified that the proposed
CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md.
Code Ann., Envir. § 9-506(a)(1)(ii).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.
SECTION TWO: This project will complete improvements to the Water System as presented

in Section 7.0 of the PER. The Water System Improvements will maintain the drinking water
quality for the Town and increase the reliability and resiliency of the Water System.

SECTION THREE: The Water System serves 766 equivalent dwelling units (“EDUs”)
consisting of residential and commercial properties and a population of 651 individuals.

SECTION FOUR: The engineering estimate to complete the Water System Improvements is
$4,718,000.

SECTION FIVE: The Town is working to submit applications for funding requests to the
Maryland Department of the Environment and possibly other federal funding sources.
