WHEREAS,<sup>onOctober</sup> 22, 2002,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>CouncilofTalbot</sup> County (the “County Council”) adopted<sup>ResolutionNo.</sup> 100,<sup>which</sup> updated<sup>theTalbot</sup> County Comprehensive<sup>Water</sup> and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”) through the 2002 Report of the Review; and 

WHEREAS, the Maryland Department of the Environment (“MDE”) approved Resolution No. 100 on February 20, 2003; and 

WHEREAS, Stephanie<sup>C.</sup> Allen,<sup>JonathanJ.</sup> Allen,<sup>Ted</sup> Derheimer,<sup>andKristenHawn</sup> (the “Owners”’)<sup>aretheownersof certainreal</sup> property<sup>locatedat5586Leeward</sup> Lane, Tilghman, Maryland,<sup>shownonTax</sup> Map<sup>44asParcel</sup> 35,<sup>Lot7</sup> (the “Property”);<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> Property consists of<sup>2.394</sup> acres<sup>andis</sup> improved<sup>with</sup> a 4,752-square foot single-family dwelling; and 

WHEREAS, Stephanie<sup>C.</sup> Allen, on behalf of<sup>the</sup> Owners,<sup>hassubmitted</sup> an Application for an Amendment to the CWSP for Change of Priority Status to change the sewer classification of the Property from “Unprogrammed” to “S-1” Immediate Priority Status; and 

WHEREAS, the County Engineer has evaluated and confirmed the feasibility of extending public<sup>sewer to the</sup> Property<sup>and</sup> requested<sup>that the Talbot</sup> County<sup>Office ofLaw</sup> prepare this amendment to the CWSP to facilitate extension of public sewer thereto; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>inaccordancewiththe</sup> requirements<sup>ofMd.Code</sup> Ann.,<sup>Envir.</sup> § 9- 506(a)(1)(1),<sup>the</sup> proposed<sup>CWSPamendmentsetforthhereinhasbeensubmittedtotheTalbot</sup> County Planning<sup>Commission</sup> (the “Planning Commission’),<sup>aswellastheTalbot</sup> County<sup>Public</sup> Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>onOctober</sup> 7, 2026,<sup>the</sup> Planning<sup>Commissioncertifiedthatthe</sup> proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-506(a)(1)(ii). 

NOW, THEREFORE,<sup>BEITRESOLVEDBYTHECOUNTYCOUNCILOF</sup> TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows: 

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein. 

SECTION TWO: Subject to the terms and conditions herein, the CWSP is amended to reclassify<sup>and</sup> remap<sup>the</sup> Property<sup>from</sup> “Unprogrammed”<sup>to“S-1”Immediate</sup> Priority Status,<sup>as</sup> shown on the map attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference as if fully set forth.

SECTION THREE: Sewer service for the Property shall be from the Region V Wastewater Treatment Plant. The proposed sewer extension shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the current Region V Service Policy and design guidelines. 

SECTION FOUR: The Property is hereby granted an allocation of one (1) equivalent dwelling<sup>unit</sup> (“EDU”)<sup>of sewer</sup> capacity.<sup>The</sup> peak<sup>flowallocationtothe</sup> Property<sup>shallbe</sup> limited to two hundred fifty (250) gallons per day per EDU. 

SECTION FIVE: The Owners shall be responsible for contracting and paying for all required permits, easements,<sup>construction</sup> work,<sup>andallbenefitandconnection</sup> charges<sup>in</sup> accordance with a Public Works Agreement (“PWA”) approved by the County. The PWA for the Property<sup>shallrunwithandbindthe</sup> Property<sup>servedandshallbefiled</sup> among<sup>theLandRecords</sup> of Talbot County, Maryland within sixty (60) days from the date of approval of the building permit. 

SECTION SIX: The Owners shall be responsible for paying a connection fee as defined for the Region V Wastewater System before commencing construction to connect the Property to the force main. Such connections shall be subject to periodic charges, tariffs, and policies as may be adopted from time to time. 

SECTION SEVEN: The Owners shall be solely responsible for all costs incurred for design, engineering, construction, inspection,<sup>and</sup> testing<sup>that</sup> may<sup>be</sup> reasonably required, as determined by the County Engineer, to connect the Property to the force main, including, without limitation, any material, pumps, saddles, or other equipment, and for all costs incurred for ongoing maintenance and repair. 

SECTION EIGHT: No water or sewer service shall be available to any area beyond the existing Property<sup>tobeserved.Noother</sup> properties, lots, or parcels, including any future reconfiguration<sup>orrecombinationof the</sup> Property,<sup>shallbeentitledtoserviceor</sup> capacity,<sup>unless</sup> and until the CWSP is amended to permit such service. 

SECTION NINE: The design shall be consistent with the design standards for similar projects<sup>inthe</sup> Region<sup>VService</sup> Area,<sup>shallbe</sup> subject<sup>toreviewand</sup> approval by<sup>the</sup> County Engineer,<sup>andshallinclude</sup> design features, components,<sup>and materialsasthe</sup> County Engineer or their designee may reasonably require, including the ability to isolate the connection. 

SECTION TEN: The Owners shall be solely responsible for all remediation, mitigation, damages, charges, fines, penalties,<sup>orothercosts</sup> imposed, levied,<sup>orassessedat</sup> any<sup>time</sup> by any federal, State, or<sup>localenforcement</sup> agency for any environmental damage or violation of law caused by or resulting from the Owners connection to the force main. The Owners shall indemnify and hold the County harmless from and against all such claims, actions, suits, damages, losses, or expenses,<sup>of</sup> any kind, nature,<sup>or</sup> description<sup>whatsoever.</sup> 

SECTION ELEVEN: This Resolution shall not modify, excuse, or supersede any other requirements<sup>for</sup> ongoing compliance<sup>withall</sup> applicable federal, State,<sup>andlocal</sup> statutes, ordinances, rules,<sup>or</sup> regulations, including<sup>withoutlimitation</sup> all conditions and requirements of all permits and approvals necessary for connection to the force main.
