A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE OFFICIAL ROAD NAME LIST FOR TALBOT
COUNTY TO CHANGE THE NAMES OF “LINCOLN ROAD” AND “SHELLMILL
AVENUE” TO “BELLEVUE SCHOOL ROAD” AND “TURNER AVENUE”,
RESPECTIVELY, IN THE VILLAGE OF BELLEVUE, TALBOT COUNTY,
MARYLAND, PURSUANT TO § 131-5(C) OF THE TALBOT COUNTY CODE

WHEREAS, “Lincoln Road” and “Shellmill Avenue” were established as private roads
in the Village of Bellevue, Maryland, pursuant to a plat titled “MAJOR REVISION & LOT
LINE ABANDONMENT PLAT ON THE LANDS OF SUSAN R. PAYNE, ET AL. & ALMA
FREYA FARLEY HAYES,” prepared by Lane Engineering LLC, last revised March 25, 2021,
and recorded among the Plat Records of Talbot County, Maryland in Plat Book K.M.D. No. 89,
Page 87; and

WHEREAS, Lincoln Road and Shellmill Avenue are listed in the Official Road Name
List for Talbot County (the “Road Name List”); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 131-5(A) of the Talbot County Code, the Road Name List
shall be approved by resolution of the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County
Council”); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 131-5(C) of the Talbot County Code, following its adoption,
the Road Name List may be amended from time to time by the County Council to change the
name of a listed road following a public hearing; and

WHEREAS, the fee simple owners of Lincoln Road and Shellmill Avenue have
requested that the County Council change the names thereof to “Bellevue School Road” and
“Turner Avenue,” respectively, as depicted on a plat titled “SPECIAL PURPOSE PLAT: ROAD
NAME CHANGE ON THE LANDS OF LONG IRON INVESTMENT GROUP, LLC,
PAQUIN DESIGN BUILD, INC., SAMANTHA BETH BURKA AND ADAM HORDELL”,
prepared by Lane Engineering, Inc., dated July 14, 2023, and attached hereto as Exhibit A (the
“Special Purpose Plat”); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 131-5(C) of the Talbot County Code, the County Council
held a public hearing as set forth herein regarding the foregoing proposed amendments to the
Road Name List, thus affording the public an opportunity to comment thereupon.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the County Council of Talbot County,
Maryland, that the Official Road Name List for Talbot County shall be and is hereby amended to
change the name of “Lincoln Road” to “Bellevue School Road” and to change the name of
“Shellmill Avenue” to “Turner Avenue,” in the Village of Bellevue, Talbot County, Maryland,
as depicted on the Special Purpose Plat.
