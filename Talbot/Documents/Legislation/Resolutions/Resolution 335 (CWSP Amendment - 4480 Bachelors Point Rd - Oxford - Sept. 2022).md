A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER
AND SEWER PLAN FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECLASSIFYING AND REMAPPING
CERTAIN REAL PROPERTY LOCATED AT 4480 BACHELORS POINT ROAD,
OXFORD, MARYLAND, SHOWN ON TAX MAP 53 AS PARCEL 77, FROM “W-2” AND
“S-2” TO “W-1” AND “S-1” IMMEDIATE PRIORITY STATUS FOR WATER AND
SEWER SERVICE FROM THE OXFORD WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEM

WHEREAS, Hazen Marshall and Megan Hauck (the “Owners”) are the owners of certain
real property located at 4480 Bachelors Point Road, Oxford, Maryland 21654, shown on Tax Map
53 as Parcel 77, consisting of 2.96 acres of land, more or less (the “Property”); and

WHEREAS, the Property is served by a failing septic system, and the Owners are
constructing a single-family residence on the Property; and

WHEREAS, to remediate the failing septic system, the previous owners of the Property
entered into an annexation agreement with the Town of Oxford (the “Town”) in order to be eligible
for the Town’s municipal water and sewer service which required the Property to connect to such
service within three years; and

WHEREAS, on February 27, 2007, the Town approved Resolution No. 0714, entitled “A
Resolution of the Commissioners of Oxford to Annex Certain Lands Located on the South and
East Side of the Town of Oxford on the East Side of Bachelor’s Point Road, in the Third Election
District, Talbot County, Maryland and consisting of 5.35 Acres of Land, More or Less, and to
Provide for the Terms and Conditions of the Annexation to the Town of Oxford;” and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-
506(a)(1)(i), the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot
County Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as the Talbot County Public
Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area; and

WHEREAS, on , 2022, the Planning Commission certified that the
proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required
by Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-506(a)(1)(ii).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.

SECTION TWO: Figure 9 (“Talbot County, Town of Oxford, Water Service Area”) and
Figure 20 (“Talbot County, Town of Oxford, Sewer Service Area”) of the Talbot County
Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”), attached hereto and incorporated herein by
reference as Exhibit “A,” is hereby amended to reclassify and remap that certain real property
located at 4480 Bachelors Point Road, Oxford, Maryland 21654, shown on Tax Map 53 as Parcel
77, consisting of 2.96 acres, more or less, from the current classification of “W-2” and “S-2” to
“W-1” and “S-1” immediate priority status for water and sewer service, as shown and described

adoption.
