# ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING THE OFFICE OF COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIPS FOR TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County Council”) desires to formally establish an Office of Community Partnerships for Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”) to strengthen oversight, accountability, and strategic alignment of County investments in human services and community nonprofits; to serve in a convening and coordinating role among County agencies, nonprofit partners, the designated Community Action Agency and community stakeholders; and to advance shared goals and measurable outcomes that improve the well-being of children, families, seniors, and vulnerable populations, as more fully set forth in this Administrative Resolution. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, that: 

1. The County Council hereby establishes the Office of Community Partnerships for Talbot County, Maryland (the “OCP”) for the following purposes: 

a. Strengthening oversight, accountability, and alignment of County investments in human services and community nonprofits; 

b. Serving as a convening and coordinating office to foster collaboration among County agencies, nonprofit partners, the designated Community Action Agency and community stakeholders; and 

c. Supporting the evaluation and refinement of County funding processes, including consideration of structured grant mechanisms where appropriate; and 

d. Advancing shared goals, measurable outcomes, and sustainable community impact through coordinated planning and equitable stewardship of public resources. 

2. The OCP is intended to serve primarily in a strategic, coordinating, and partnership capacity within the County’s human services system in support of the many non-profits working to support human service needs in the County. 

3. The OCP shall be established as a coordinating office within the County government. The OCP Director shall serve as a County Department Head and shall oversee designated human services coordination functions, including Talbot Family Network (“TFN”) as the Local Management Board, the Community
