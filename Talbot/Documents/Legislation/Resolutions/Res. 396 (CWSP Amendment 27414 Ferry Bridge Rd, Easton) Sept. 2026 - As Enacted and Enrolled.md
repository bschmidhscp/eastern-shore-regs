SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.

SECTION TWO: Subject to the terms and conditions herein, the CWSP is amended to reclassify<sup>and</sup> remap<sup>the</sup> Property<sup>from</sup> “Unprogrammed”<sup>to“S-1”Immediate</sup> Priority Status, as shown on the map attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference as if fully set forth. 

SECTION THREE: Sewer service for the Property shall be from the Region I Wastewater Treatment Plant. The proposed sewer extension shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the current Region I Service Policy and design guidelines. 

SECTION FOUR: The Property is hereby granted an allocation of one (1) equivalent dwelling<sup>unit</sup> (“EDU”)<sup>of sewer</sup> capacity.<sup>The</sup> peak<sup>flow allocationtothe</sup> Property<sup>shall belimited</sup> to two hundred fifty (250) gallons per day per EDU. 

SECTION FIVE: The Owner shall be responsible for contracting and paying for all required permits, easements,<sup>construction</sup> work,<sup>andallbenefitandconnection</sup> charges<sup>in</sup> accordance with a Public Works Agreement (“PWA”) approved by the County. The PWA for the Property<sup>shallrunwithandbindthe</sup> Property<sup>servedandshallbefiled</sup> among<sup>theLandRecords</sup> of Talbot County, Maryland within sixty (60) days from the date of approval of the building permit. 

SECTION SIX: The Owner shall be responsible for paying a connection fee as defined for the Region I Wastewater System before commencing construction to connect the Property to the force main. Such connections shall be subject to periodic charges, tariffs, and policies as may be adopted from time to time. 

SECTION SEVEN: The Owner shall be solely responsible for all costs incurred for design, engineering, construction, inspection,<sup>and</sup> testing<sup>that</sup> may<sup>be</sup> reasonably required, as determined by the County Engineer, to connect the Property to the force main, including, without limitation, any material, pumps, saddles, or<sup>other</sup> equipment, and for all costs incurred for ongoing maintenance and repair. 

SECTION EIGHT: No water or sewer service shall be available to any area beyond the existing Property<sup>tobeserved.Noother</sup> properties, lots, or parcels, including any future reconfiguration<sup>orrecombinationof the</sup> Property,<sup>shallbeentitledtoserviceor</sup> capacity, unless and until the CWSP is amended to permit such service.

SECTION TEN: The Owner shall be solely responsible for all remediation, mitigation, damages, charges, fines, penalties,<sup>orothercosts</sup> imposed, levied,<sup>orassessedat</sup> any<sup>time</sup> by any federal, State,<sup>orlocalenforcement</sup> agency<sup>for</sup> any<sup>environmental</sup> damage or violation of law caused by or resulting from the Owner’s connection to the force main.
