A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER
AND SEWER PLAN FOR THE PURPOSE OF ADDING OR MODIFYING THE TALBOT
COUNTY CAPITAL PROJECT FOR FISCAL YEARS 2024 AND 2025 TO ADD A NEW
CAPITAL PROJECT IN THE AMOUNT OF $12.0 MILLION FOR THE PURPOSE OF
REPLACING AND/OR REHABILITATING THE TOWN OF EASTON SEWER
COLLECTION SYSTEM TO MITIGATE INFLOW AND INFILTRATION

WHEREAS, the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”)
contains capital projects for water and sewer service in Talbot County, Maryland, (the “County”);
and

WHEREAS, Easton Utilities serves approximately 7,000 customers via 120 miles of
sewer lines and force mains and twenty (20) pump stations that convey wastewater to the Easton
Wastewater Treatment Plant upgraded with Enhanced Nutrient Removal technologies; and

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County (the “County Council”) desires to
amend the CWSP for the purpose of including the capital project identified by Easton Utilities for
Fiscal Years 2024 and 2025 into Table 16 of the CWSP, entitled “Easton Sewerage System Capital
Improvement Projects;” and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-
506(a)(1)(i), the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot
County Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as the Talbot County Public
Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area; and

WHEREAS, on October 4, 2023, the Planning Commission certified that the proposed
CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md.
Code Ann., Envir. 506(a)(1)(ii).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.
SECTION TWO: This project will add sewer replacement and rehabilitation projects noted

over a ten (10) year period starting in 2024 and ending in 2034 in locations throughout the Town
of Easton.

SECTION THREE: Easton Utilities shall seek funding through the Maryland Department of
the Environment through grants and loan funding that could fund one or more proposed sewer
replacement and/or rehabilitation projects in the same fiscal year.

SECTION FOUR: The proposed sewer replacement and/or rehabilitation projects are needed
to mitigate inflow and infiltration.
