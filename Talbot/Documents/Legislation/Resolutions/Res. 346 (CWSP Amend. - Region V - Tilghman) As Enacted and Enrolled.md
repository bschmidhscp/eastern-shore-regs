A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER
AND SEWER PLAN FOR THE PURPOSE OF ADDING OR MODIFYING THE TALBOT
COUNTY CAPITAL PROJECT FOR FISCAL YEARS 2024 AND 2025 TO ADD A NEW
CAPITAL PROJECT IN THE AMOUNT OF $8.0 MILLION FOR THE PURPOSE OF
REPLACING AND/OR REHABILITATING THE REGION V (TILGHMAN) SEWER
COLLECTION SYSTEM TO MITIGATE INFLOW AND INFILTRATION

WHEREAS, the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”)
contains capital projects for water and sewer service in Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”);
and

WHEREAS, the Region V (Tilghman) Wastewater System serves approximately 600
customers via 7.8 miles of sewer lines and force mains and up to five (5) major pump stations that
convey wastewater to the Region V (Tilghman) Wastewater Treatment Plant; and

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County (the “County Council”) desires to
amend the CWSP for the purpose of including the capital project identified by the Talbot County
Sanitary District for Fiscal Years 2024 and 2025 into Table 27 of the CWSP, entitled “Talbot
County Region V Sanitary District (Tilghman Village) Capital Improvement Projects;” and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-
506(a)(1)(i), the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot
County Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as the Talbot County Public
Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area; and

WHEREAS, on October 4, 2023, the Planning Commission certified that the proposed
CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md.
Code Ann., Envir. 506(a)(1)(ii).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.

SECTION TWO: This project will add a sewer replacement and rehabilitation project to
rehabilitate and/or replace sewer lines, rehabilitate and/or replace manholes, and raise roads
elevations, when possible, to mitigate inflow and infiltration throughout the Region V (Tilghman)
Gravity Sewer Collection System after funding has been obligated.

SECTION THREE: The Talbot County Sanitary District shall seek funding through the
Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) and the USDA Rural Development through
grants and loan funding for the purpose of replacing and/or rehabilitating sewers and manholes
along with completing road improvements to mitigate inflow and infiltration.

SECTION FIVE: The engineering estimate to complete all the work as presented in the
