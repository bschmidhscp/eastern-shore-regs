A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER
AND SEWER PLAN TO ADD OR MODIFY THE TALBOT COUNTY CAPITAL
PROJECT FOR FYs 2023 AND 2024 TO ADD A NEW CAPITAL PROJECT IN THE
AMOUNT OF $8.6 MILLION FOR THE PURPOSE OF REPLACING THE TOWN OF
EASTON’S GLEBE WATER TREATMENT PLANT WITH UPGRADED
TECHNOLOGY

WHEREAS, the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”)
contains capital projects for water and sewer service in Talbot County, Maryland, (the “County”);
and

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County, (the “County Council”) desires to
amend the CWSP for the purpose of including the capital project identified by Easton Utilities for
Fiscal Years 2023 and 2024 into Table 7 of the CWSP, entitled “Easton Water System Capital
Improvement Projects;” and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-
506(a)(1)(i), the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot
County Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as to the Talbot County
Public Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area;
and

WHEREAS, on April 20, 2022, the Planning Commission certified that the proposed
CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md.
Code Ann., Envir. 506(a)(1)(ii);

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.

SECTION TWO: This project will replace the Town of Easton Glebe Water Treatment Plant
with a new water treatment plant with upgraded technology. The proposed plant will improve
drinking water quality for the Town of Easton and will increase the reliability and resiliency of the
drinking water system.

SECTION THREE: The proposed Town of Easton Glebe Water Treatment Plant is currently
in the schematic design phase, with GHD, Inc. having completed the design report on October 5,
2021.

SECTION FOUR: The proposed Town of Easton Glebe Water Treatment Plant serves 1,632
commercial properties and 6,877 residential properties, with an estimated 9,000 equivalent
dwelling units (EDUs) being served by this water treatment plant.

SECTION FIVE: The engineering estimate to complete this project is $8.6 million.
