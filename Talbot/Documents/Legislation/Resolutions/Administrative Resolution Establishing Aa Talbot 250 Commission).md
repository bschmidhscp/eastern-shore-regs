# ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING A TALBOT 250 COMMISSION 

WHEREAS, on July 4, 2026, the United States of America will celebrate the<sup>250th</sup> anniversary of its founding (the “Semiquincentennial”); and 

WHEREAS, the Semiquincentennial is a momentous occasion that should be commemorated with a variety of celebrations, events, and activities that reflect the nation’s diversity and strength and the role that Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”) played in its founding; and 

WHEREAS, notable examples of the role the County played in the nation’s founding include, but are not limited to, the following: 

- May 24, 1774 — County citizens assembled on the Courthouse lawn to protest Great Britain’s closing of the Port of Boston and resolved “to act as friends to liberty and the general interest of mankind.” The County was the first locality in Maryland to release a statement in support of its sister city in Boston. 

- October 24, 1781 — County native Tench Tilghman, aide-de-camp to General George Washington, arrived in Philadelphia, carrying news to the Continental Congress that Lord Charles Cornwallis of the British Army surrendered, marking a major turning point in the Revolutionary War. 

- 1818 — Frederick Douglass, born into slavery in the County as Frederick Augustus Washington Bailey, continued the work of the revolutionaries when he self-liberated from Baltimore and helped lead the fight to “secure the blessings of liberty” for all Americans, regardless of race or gender. 

WHEREAS, in consideration of the foregoing, the County will be a significant venue for celebrating the Semiquincentennial; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County Council”) recognizes the importance of engaging the County’s citizens, businesses, educational institutions, and other various organizations

in celebrating the Semiquincentennial and fostering a greater understanding and appreciation of American history, culture, and achievements; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council is desirous of formally establishing a Talbot 250 Commission for the purposes of planning and coordinating celebrations, events, and activities for the Semiquincentennial, as more fully set forth in this Administrative Resolution. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, that: 1. The County Council hereby establishes the Talbot 250 Commission (the “Commission”) for the following purposes:

3. The members set forth in 2(b) through (h), 2(k), and 2(1) above shall be nominated through a public application process and, to the extent practicable, reflect the racial, gender, and cultural diversity of Talbot County, Maryland. 

4. Administrative support for the Commission shall be provided by the County Manager’s Office. 

5. Legal support for the Commission shall be provided by the Office of Law. 

6. Commission members serve at the pleasure of the County Council and may be removed at any time by a majority vote of the County Council. 

7. The Commission shall meet at least once quarterly, with additional meetings as needed. A majority of the Commission members appointed shall be present to constitute a quorum for the transaction of Commission business. 

8. The Commission shall provide reports at least once per quarter at regularly scheduled County Council meetings. 

9. Commission meetings shall comply with the Maryland Open Meetings Act, Md. Code Ann., General Provisions § 3-10 1, et seq., as amended hereafter from time to time. 

10. Within three months after the final Semiquincentennial event, the Commission shall submit a final report evaluating the success of the County’s commemoration of the Semiquincentennial and making recommendations for future related events. The Commission shall dissolve following its submission of its final report to the County Council. 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Administrative Resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption.
