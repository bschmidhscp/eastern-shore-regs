A RESOLUTION REGARDING THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE
WATER AND SEWER PLAN TO CLARIFY AND CONFIRM THE WATER AND
SEWER CLASSIFICATIONS OF CERTAIN PARCELS INCORRECTLY
SHOWN IN EXHIBITS A AND B TO RESOLUTION NO. 281, AS AMENDED

WHEREAS, in accordance with Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-503(a), the County Council
of Talbot County (the “County Council”) has adopted a plan that deals with water supply systems
and sewerage systems in Talbot County, inclusive of the municipal corporations located therein,
which is titled the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-503(c)(1), the County Council is
authorized to adopt a revision to the CWSP if it considers such an amendment necessary; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-503(d), before the County Council
adopts an amendment to the CWSP, it shall first conduct a public hearing thereon, which may be
conducted jointly with other public hearings or meetings, with notice provided to the principal
elected official of any affected municipal corporation at least 14 days in advance; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-506(a), before the County Council
may adopt an amendment to the CWSP, it shall submit the proposed amendment to the Talbot
County Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”) for review and comment, and the
Planning Commission must certify that the proposed amendment is consistent with the Talbot
County Comprehensive Plan; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Envir. §§ 9-503(c) and 9-507(a), once the
County Council adopts an amendment to the CWSP, it must then submit the proposed amendment
to the Maryland Department of the Environment (“MDE”) for final review and approval; and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the foregoing statutory authority, on August 11, 2020,
the County Council adopted Resolution No. 281, as amended (“Resolution No. 281”), for the
purposes of amending the CWSP, inter alia, to add certain water and sewer capital projects
related to the Lakeside Planned Unit Development (the “Lakeside Project”) and existing systems
in the Town of Trappe (the “Town”) and to reclassify and remap certain properties associated
therewith, specifically identified therein as Tax Map 54, Parcel 304, Tax Map 55, Parcels 14, 15,
17, 19, 44, 65, 83, and 85, and Tax Map 59, Parcel 4 (the “Properties”); and

WHEREAS, or about November 4, 2020, MDE approved the amendments to the CWSP
as set forth in Resolution No. 281; and

WHEREAS, the Properties’ water and sewer classifications were correctly shown in
Exhibits A and B to Resolution No. 281; however, the water and sewer classifications of certain
other parcels within the Town, which parcels were unrelated to Resolution No. 281 and were not
referenced in the text thereof (the “Unrelated Parcels”), were incorrectly shown in Exhibits A and
B, though their water and sewer classifications were not changed thereby; and

<!-- page 3 -->
WHEREAS, pursuant to correspondence from MDE dated April 24, 2023, May 9, 2023,
and July 21, 2023, MDE requested, in part, that the County Council address the Unrelated Parcels’
incorrect water and sewer classifications as shown in Exhibits A and B to Resolution No. 281,
thus eliminating any confusion or uncertainty regarding the same; and

WHEREAS, the County Council is desirous of addressing the Unrelated Parcels’
incorrect water and sewer classifications as shown in Exhibits A and B to Resolution No. 281 as
requested by MDE; and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-
506(a)(1)(i), this Resolution has been submitted to the Planning Commission, as well as the Talbot
County Public Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the
area; and

WHEREAS, on 2023, the Planning Commission certified that this
Resolution is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-
506(a)(1)(ii).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.

SECTION TWO: Exhibit A entitled “Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan Town of Trappe Proposed Water Service Area & Water System Facilities,” attached hereto
and incorporated herein by reference as if fully set forth, hereby supersedes and replaces Exhibit
A to Resolution No. 281, solely to the extent Exhibit A to Resolution No. 281 is inconsistent with
the Exhibit A attached hereto.

SECTION THREE: Exhibit B entitled “Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan Town of Trappe Proposed Sewer Service Area & Sewer System Facilities,” attached hereto
and incorporated herein by reference as if fully set forth, hereby supersedes and replaces Exhibit
B to Resolution No. 281, as amended, solely to the extent Exhibit B to Resolution No. 281 is
inconsistent with the Exhibit B attached hereto.

SECTION FOUR: This Resolution does not alter, amend, or otherwise change any water
or sewer classifications for any parcel whatsoever. The sole purpose of this Resolution is to clarify
and confirm the water and sewer classifications of the Unrelated Parcels incorrectly shown in
Exhibits A and B to Resolution No. 281, thus eliminating any confusion or uncertainty regarding
the same. The water and sewer classifications shown on Exhibits A and B attached hereto,
respectively, accurately depict the current water and sewer classifications for the Town of Trappe
pursuant to Resolution No. 100, as amended, and as subsequently amended pursuant to Resolution
No. 259, as amended, and Resolution No. 281.

<!-- page 4 -->
SECTION FIVE: This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon the date of its
adoption.
