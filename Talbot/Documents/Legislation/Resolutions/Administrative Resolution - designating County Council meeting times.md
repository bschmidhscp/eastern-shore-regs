ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION TO CHANGE THE STARTING TIME OF LEGISLATIVE SESSIONS OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND 

WHEREAS, pursuant to § 212(g) of the Charter of Talbot County (the “Charter”), the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County Council”) shall adopt and publish Rules of Procedure not inconsistent with the provisions of the Charter and may amend the same from time to time; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant to the foregoing authority, the County Council has adopted Rules of Procedure; and 

( WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 1(B)(1) of the Rules of Procedure, the County Council shall meet at least twice a month on Tuesday at a time determined by a majority of the County Council, with the second and fourth Tuesdays of each month being reserved for legislative sessions; and 

WHEREAS, legislative sessions of the County Council typically start at 6:00 p.m.; and 

WHEREAS, on June 13, 2023, the County Council adopted an administrative resolution for the purpose of changing the starting time of its legislative sessions to 5:00 p.m., the intent of which was to have legislative sessions start earlier during the summer months; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council is desirous of changing the starting time of its legislative sessions back to 6:00 p.m. during the fall, winter, and spring months only. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, that legislative sessions of the County Council in the months of October through May shall start at 6:00 p.m. unless otherwise specified by a majority of the County Council. 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that legislative sessions of the County Council in the months of June through September shall start at 5:00 p.m. unless otherwise specified by a majority of the County Council. 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Administrative Resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption. 

- Cc INTRODUCED by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland at a Regular Meeting on September 12, 2023, at which meeting copies were available to the public for inspection.
