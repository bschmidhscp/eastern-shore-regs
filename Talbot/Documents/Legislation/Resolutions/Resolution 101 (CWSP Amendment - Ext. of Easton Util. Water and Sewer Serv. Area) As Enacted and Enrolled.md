# RESOLUTION No. 101 

A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN, AS ADOPTED BY TALBOT COUNTY RESOLUTION 67, TO EXTEND THE SERVICE AREA OF THE WATER AND SEWERAGE SYSTEMS OF THE EASTON UTILITIES COMMISSION TO CERTAIN AREAS WITHIN THE SERVICE LIMITS OF THE TOWN OF EASTON, IN THE FIRST ELECTION DISTRICT, TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND; PURSUANT TO THE POWER AND AUTHORITY CONTAINED IN THE ENVIRONMENTAL ARTICLE, TITLE 9, SUBTITLE 5, OF THE ANNOTATED CODE OF MARYLAND 

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County, State of Maryland, by Resolution Number 67, introduced on May 4, 1993, and duly adopted as effective on June 1, 1993, has adopted the December 1992 Update of the Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan as the current plan in effect; and 

WHEREAS, said plan, as one of its goals, encourages the growth of the County population centers around presently or potentially adequate water and sewerage systems; and 

WHEREAS, the owners of certain areas which are contiguous to and adjoining the service districts of the water distribution and sewerage collection systems of the Town of Easton, in the First Election District. Talbot County. Maryland have requested inclusion of said area for immediate priority classification for extension of services; and 

WHEREAS, the Easton Utilities Commission has authorized the requested extension of its services as consistent with the use and intent of existing utilities, planned improvements and upgrade of existing utilities, and the legislated policies and enactments relating thereto; and 

WHEREAS, the Easton Utilities Commission has demonstrated and verified that sewer allocations to its Priority (S-l) Schedule will be properly planned, controlled, and regulated such that available treatment plant capacity, based on permitted and actual sewer demand, will not be exceeded; 

NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland as follows: 

Section 1. The Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan shall be amended to include an immediate priority (W- 1) water service extension in the jurisdiction of Easton Utilities water distribution system to the following area: 

- a) Cooke’s Hope Village and the Galloways as present on Tax Map 42, Parcel 56 in 1995 that presently is represented as Tax 42 parcels 268 and all lots, Parcel 282, and Parcel 162 and all lots.
