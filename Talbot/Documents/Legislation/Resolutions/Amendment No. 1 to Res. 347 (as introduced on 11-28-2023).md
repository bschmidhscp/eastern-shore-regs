Purpose: This amended is intended to delete the phrase “The phases represent defined land uses
only and do not represent the timing or sequence of development” from the proposed amendments
to the Trappe Area Water System — Future Planning and Trappe Area Sewer System — Future
Planning sections of the CWSP.

Amendment not substantive: The amendments proposed herein are non-substantive, as they do
not enlarge or narrow the scope of Resolution No. 347 as introduced to such an extent that
Resolution No. 347, if adopted with the foregoing amendments, could be said to be misleading in
a substantial manner. Furthermore, such amendments do not defeat the original purpose of
Resolution No. 347 as introduced. See Ajamian v. Montgomery County, 99 Md. App. 665, 684-
685 (1994).
