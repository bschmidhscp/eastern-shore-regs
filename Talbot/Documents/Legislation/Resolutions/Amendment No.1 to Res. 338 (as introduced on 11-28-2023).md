Purpose: This amendment is intended to expressly incorporate Md. Code Ann., Environment § 9-
506(a)(1)@i), pursuant to which the County Council must submit a proposed CWSP amendment
to the Talbot County Planning Commission for a certification that the amendment is consistent
with the County’s Comprehensive Plan.

Amendment not substantive:

The amendments proposed herein are non-substantive, as they do

not enlarge or narrow the scope of Resolution No. 338 as introduced to such an extent that
Resolution No. 338, if adopted with the foregoing amendments, could be said to be misleading in
a substantial manner. The amendments proposed herein merely reinforce what Md. Code Ann.,
