A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND
SEWER PLAN FOR THE PURPOSE OF ADDING OR MODIFYING THE TALBOT
COUNTY CAPITAL PROJECT FOR FISCAL YEARS 2023 AND 2024 TO ADD A NEW
CAPITAL PROJECT IN THE AMOUNT OF $1.5 MILLION FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF
A NEW SOUTH WELL FOR THE TOWN OF EASTON

WHEREAS, the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”) contains
capital projects for water and sewer service in Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”); and

WHEREAS, Easton Utilities has completed a preliminary project estimate to construct a new
drinking water well for the Easton Water System, referred to as the new South Well Project, for the
amount of $1,117,500.00 (increased to $1.5 million for budgeting purposes); and

WHEREAS, the pilot hole for the new South Well will be drilled to a depth of 1,100 feet, with
the finished well having an estimated depth of 1,050 feet and a diameter of eight (8) inches; and

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County, (the “County Council”) desires to amend
the CWSP for the purpose of including the South Well Project as a capital project identified by Easton
Utilities for Fiscal Years 2023 and 2024 into Table 7 of the CWSP, entitled “Easton Water System
Capital Improvement Projects;” and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-506(a)(1)(i),
the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot County Planning
Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as the Talbot County Public Works Advisory
Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area; and

WHEREAS, on July 6, 2022, the Planning Commission certified that the proposed CWSP
amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md. Code Ann.,
Envir. 506(a)(1)(ii).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF TALBOT
COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan shall be and
is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.

SECTION TWO: This project will construct a new South Well to be added to the Town of
Easton drinking water wells. The construction of the new South Well will improve drinking water
quality and quantity for the Town of Easton and will increase the reliability and resiliency of the
Town’s drinking water system.

SECTION THREE: The engineering estimate to complete this project is $1,117,500.00 with
a budgetary estimate of $1.5 million.

SECTION FOUR: On January 31, 2022, Easton Utilities submitted an application to the
Maryland Department of the Environment (“MDE”) seeking grant and loan funds for this capital
project.

SECTION SIX: The Talbot County Office of Law, in consultation with and subject to the
approval of the County Manager, may make non-substantive corrections to codification, style,

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Resolution shall take effect immediately upon the
date of its adoption.
