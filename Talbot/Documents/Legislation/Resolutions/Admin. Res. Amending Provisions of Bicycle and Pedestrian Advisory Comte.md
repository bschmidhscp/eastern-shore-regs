# ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS REGARDING CITIZEN REPRESENTATIVES ON THE BICYCLE AND PEDESTRIAN ADVISORY COMMITTEE 

WHEREAS, on September 24, 2024, the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County Council”) adopted an Administrative Resolution (the “September 24 Administrative Resolution”) for the purpose of establishing a Bicycle and Pedestrian Advisory Committee (the “Committee”); and 

WHEREAS, Section 2(f) of the September<sup>24th</sup> Administrative Resolution provides that the County Council shall appoint seven (7) citizen representatives to the Committee, subject to certain residency requirements; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council is desirous of amending certain provisions regarding the citizen representatives on the Committee, as more fully set forth herein. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, that: 

1. Section 2(f) of the September<sup>24th</sup> Administrative Resolution be and it is hereby amended to read as follows: 

2. (f) Seven (7) to eleven (11) citizen representatives appointed by the County Council including at least. one (1) resident from each of the municipalities of Easton, Oxford Queen Anne, St. Michaels, and Trappe; one (1) resident from the unincorporated area of the County in the Chapel District or the Easton District; and one (1) residentfrom the unincorporated area ofthe County in the Trappe District St. Michaels District, or Bay Hundred District. Four (4) citizen representatives shall serve for an initial term of two (2) years and thereafter serve for a term ofthree (3) years. All other citizen representatives shall serve for a term ofthree (3) years. 

2. All other sections of the September<sup>24th</sup> Administrative Resolution not amended hereunder shall remain in full force and effect. 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Administrative Resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption.
