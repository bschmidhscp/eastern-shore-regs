# ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION CHANGING THE DESIGNATED RESIDENT AGENT FOR TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Local Gov’t § 1-1301(b)(1) and (c), certain governmental entities, including counties, are required to designate a resident agent for service of process who is a resident of the State of Maryland, a Maryland corporation, or an officer of the governmental entity; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Local Gov’t § 1-1301(d), a governmental entity shall designate or change its resident agent by filing with the State Department of Assessments and Taxation (“SDAT”) a certification of the person who the governmental entity’s charter authorizes to accept service of process therefor or, absent a charter designation, a certified copy of the resolution of the governmental entity’s governing authority that authorizes the designation; and 

WHEREAS, the Charter of Talbot County, Maryland does not designate an individual authorized to accept service of process on behalf of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”); and 

WHEREAS, in accordance with the foregoing, on November 9, 2004, the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County Council”) adopted an Administrative Resolution for the purpose of designating the County Manager as the County’s resident agent; and 

WHEREAS, on June 28, 2016, the County Council adopted a subsequent Administrative Resolution for the purpose of specifically designating R. Andrew Hollis, who was then serving as the County Manager, as the County’s resident agent; and 

WHEREAS, the current County Manager is Clay B. Stamp, a resident of the State of Maryland; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council desires to change the County’s resident agent from Mr. Hollis specifically to the County Manager generally so as to avoid the need for future Administrative Resolutions in the event of a change in the individual serving as County Manager. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, that the following Resident Agent is hereby designated:

<!-- page 2 -->
County Manager for Talbot County, Maryland Talbot County Courthouse 11 North Washington Street Suite 13 Easton, Maryland 21601 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that upon adoption of this Administrative Resolution, and upon any change in the individual serving as the County Manager, the President of the County Council shall be authorized to execute any and all documentation necessary to effectuate the change of the County’s resident agent in accordance herewith and file the same with SDAT along with a certified copy of this Administrative Resolution. 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Administrative Resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption.
