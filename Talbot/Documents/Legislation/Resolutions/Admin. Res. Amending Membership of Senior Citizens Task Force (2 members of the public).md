ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION AMENDING THE MEMBERSHIP OF THE SENIOR CITIZENS TASK FORCE TO INCLUDE TWO MEMBERS OF THE PUBLIC 

WHEREAS, on August 12, 2025, the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County Council”) adopted an Amended Administrative Resolution (the “August<sup>12th</sup> Administrative Resolution”) for the purpose of establishing a Senior Citizens Task Force (the “Task Force”); and 

WHEREAS, Section 2 of the August 12 Administrative Resolution sets forth the membership of the Task Force, which is comprised of representatives from the public and private sectors as well as nonprofit organizations; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council is desirous of amending the membership of the Task Force to include two citizen representatives, as more fully set forth herein. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, that: 

1. Section 2 of the August 1<sup>2th</sup> Administrative Resolution be and it is hereby amended to read as follows, with additions in bold, underlined font and deletions in strikethrough: 

2. The Task Force shall consist of the following individuals, who shall serve for the terms indicated below ana except as otherwise indicated, be entitled to vote on all matters before the Task Force: 

a. An individual currently serving on the Talbot County Commission on Aging or another quafl/led representative recommended thereby; 

b. An individual currently serving on the Board ofDirectors ofDelmarva Community Services, Inc. or another qualled representative recommended thereby; 

c. An individual currently serving on the Board ofDirectors of Talbot Interfaith Shelter or another qualified representative recommended thereby; 

d. An individual currently serving on the Board ofDirectors ofthe Neighborhood Service Center or another quafl/led representative recommended thereby;
