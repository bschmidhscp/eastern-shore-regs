ADMINISTRATIVE RESOLUTION AMENDING THE MEMBERSHIP OF THE SENIOR CITIZENS TASK FORCE TO INCLUDE A REPRESENTATIVE OF TALBOT COUNTY’S FAITH COMMUNITY AND A REPRESENTATIVE OF THE TALBOT COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL SERVICES - ADULT SERVICES 

WHEREAS, on August 12, 2025, the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County Council”) adopted an Amended Administrative Resolution (the “August 1<sup>2th</sup> Administrative Resolution”) for the purpose of establishing a Senior Citizens Task Force (the “Task Force”); and 

WHEREAS, on September 9, 2025, the County Council adopted an Administrative Resolution (the “September 9’ Administrative Resolution”) for the purpose of amending Section 2 of the August 12th Administrative Resolution to add two citizen representatives to the Task Force; and 

WHEREAS, the County Council is desirous of again amending the membership of the Task Force to include a representative from Talbot County’s faith community and a representative of the Talbot County Department of Social Services — Adult Services, as more fully set forth herein. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, that: 

1. Section 2 of the August 1<sup>2th</sup> Administrative Resolution, as amended pursuant to the September 9 Administrative Resolution, be and it is hereby amended to read as follows, with additions in bold, underlined font and deletions in strikethrough: 

2. The Task Force shall consist of the following individuals, who shall serve for the terms indicated below and, except as otherwise indicated, be entitled to vote on all matters before the Task Force: 

a. An individual currently serving on the Talbot County Commission on Aging or another qualIed representative recommended thereby; 

b. An individual currently serving on the Board of Directors ofDelmarva Community Services, Inc. or another qualled representative recommended thereby; 

c. An individual currently serving on the Board ofDirectors of Talbot Interfaith Shelter or another qualified representative recommended thereby;

d. An individual currently serving on the Board ofDirectors ofthe Neighborhood Service Center or another quafl/led representative recommended thereby; 

e. An individual currently serving on the Board ofDirectors ofMid-Shore Behavioral Health or another quafl/led representative recommended thereby, 

f A representative of the Talbot County Health Department Senior Care Program, g. A representative of the Talbot County Mobile Integrated Health Program; ii. A representative of Talbot County ‘s faith community; i. A representative of the Talbot County Department ofSocial Services — Adult Services; 4-j A member of the Easton Town Council; A member of the County Council; and 

f- L Two citizen representatives, who shall be residents ofthe County and at least 65 years ofage. 

2. All other sections of the August 1<sup>2th</sup> Administrative Resolution, as amended pursuant to the September<sup>9th</sup> Administrative Resolution, not amended hereunder shall remain in full force and effect. 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Administrative Resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption.
