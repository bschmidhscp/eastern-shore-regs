WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County (the “County Council”) adopted Resolution
No. 94 on January 22, 2002 for the purpose of amending the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and
Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”) to authorize a shared sanitary facility (the “Facility”) for the benefit of the
residents of The Preserve at Wye Mills subdivision (the “Preserve”); and

WHEREAS, the County Council adopted Resolution No. 100 on October 22, 2002 for the
purpose of updating the CWSP through the 2002 Report of the Review, which identified the Facility
in Table 28 and Figure 30; and

WHEREAS, the Maryland Department of the Environment (“MDE”) approved Resolution
Nos. 94 and 100 on June 13, 2002 and February 20, 2003, respectively; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Chapter 152 (Shared Sanitary Facilities) of the Talbot County Code
(the “County Code”), on March 16, 2004, the Talbot County Health Department (the “Health
Department” or the “Approving Authority”) and the Talbot County Department of Public Works
(“DPW” or the “Controlling Authority”) entered into a Shared Sanitary Facility Agreement (the
“Agreement”) with Dolvin’s Farm, LLC and The Preserve at Wye Mills Property Owners Association,
Inc. (the “POA”) for the Facility, which Agreement is recorded among the Land Records of Talbot
County, Maryland at Liber M.A.S. No. 1231, folio 912; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-1110, Code of Maryland Regulations
(“COMAR”) Title 24, Subtitle 04, Chapter 05, § 152-12(C) of the County Code, and Section 9 of the
Agreement, the County is authorized and empowered to purchase or acquire by gift any shared sanitary
facility, or parts thereof, necessary for the purpose of providing adequate sanitary utility service for
the residents of Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”), which acquisition shall proceed through the
CWSP amendment process, and shall be executed under the terms of a Public Works Agreement
(PWA) to which all owners are party; and

WHEREAS, the Facility is currently owned and operated by the POA; and

WHEREAS, the POA has raised concerns with the County regarding the state of the Facility
due to a lack of maintenance and operation by their contract operators; and

WHEREAS, the Facility’s existing rotating biological contactor system (the “RBC”) was
designed to comply with biological nutrient removal standards of 10 mg/l of total nitrogen, with
effluent being disposed of through spray irrigation; and

WHEREAS, the RBC has been unable to comply with the Facility’s MDE-issued discharge
permit since 2006 or 2007 due to extremely low flows and a poor engineering design; and

<!-- page 3 -->
WHEREAS, MDE has advised the County and the POA that the County would be eligible for
grant funding to upgrade and replace the RBC with a new enhanced nutrient removal membrane bio-
reactor wastewater system if the County were to acquire the Facility; and

WHEREAS, the Talbot County Engineer has evaluated and confirmed the feasibility of the
County’s acquisition of the Facility, through DPW as the Controlling Authority, and has requested that
the Talbot County Office of Law prepare this amendment to the CWSP to facilitate the same; and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-506(a)(1)(i),
the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot County Planning
Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as the Talbot County Public Works Advisory
Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area; and

WHEREAS, on , 2022, the Planning Commission certified that the proposed
CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md. Code
Ann., Envir. § 9-506(a)(1)(ii);

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF TALBOT
COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan shall be and
is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.

SECTION TWO: Subject to the terms and conditions herein, the Facility shall be transferred
to the County as a gift from the POA, at no cost to the County.

SECTION THREE: The transfer of the Facility shall be set forth in a Transfer Agreement
between the POA and the County and shall comply in all other respects with Chapter 152 of the County
Code.

SECTION FOUR: The County shall work with the POA and MDE in preparing all the
required documents and agreements to facilitate the transfer of the Facility’s ownership from the POA
to the County, and the appropriate County officials are hereby authorized to execute any and all such
documents and agreements.

SECTION FIVE: The County shall establish a new sanitary district to be referenced as “The
Preserve at Wye Mills Sanitary District.”

SECTION SIX: Table 28 of the CWSP is amended to delete The Preserve at Wye Mills from
Table 28. Shared Sanitary Facilities in Talbot County.

SECTION SEVEN: The CWSP is amended to add The Talbot County Region IV — Preserve
at Wye Mills Sanitary District on page CHAPTER TWO 61-A, with Figure 30 being amended to show
the S-1 (immediate priority status) sewer service area for Region IV — The Preserve at Wye Mills
Sanitary District and the location of the Facility and spray irrigation field on page CHAPTER TWO
61-B.

SECTION EIGHT: All new lots to be connected to the Region IV — The Preserve at Wye
Mills Sanitary District shall be assessed a connection fee of Fifteen Thousand Dollars ($15,000.00) for
the property to be connected to the Facility before commencing construction to connect the same. Such

<!-- page 4 -->
connection shall be subject to periodic charges, tariffs, and policies as may be adopted by the County
Council from time to time.

SECTION NINE: This Resolution shall not modify, excuse, or supersede any other
requirements for ongoing compliance with all applicable federal, State, and local statutes, ordinances,
tules, or regulations.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Resolution shall take effect immediately upon the
date of its adoption.
