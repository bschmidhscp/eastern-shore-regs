A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMPREHENSIVE WATER AND SEWER PLAN FOR THE PURPOSES OF APPROVING AN ALTERNATE LOCATION FOR THE PUMP STATION AND EXPANDING THE POTENTIAL ROUTE FOR THE FORCE MAIN TO PROVIDE SANITARY SEWER SERVICE TO THE UNIVERSITY OF MARYLAND MEDICAL CENTER SHORE REGIONAL MEDICAL CENTER, TO BE LOCATED AT 10000 LONGWOODS ROAD, EASTON, MARYLAND, AND THE TALBOT COUNTY COMMUNITY CENTER, LOCATED AT 10028 OCEAN GATEWAY, EASTON, MARYLAND, AS INITIALLY SET FORTH IN RESOLUTION NO. 184, ADOPTED ON SEPTEMBER 13, 2011 

WHEREAS, Md. Code Ann., Environment § 9-501, et seq. requires each County to develop and maintain a comprehensive plan for adequately providing throughout the County, including all towns and municipal corporations in the County, water supply systems, sewerage systems, solid waste disposal systems, solid waste acceptance facilities, and systematic collection and disposal of solid waste and litter; and 

WHEREAS, on October 22, 2002, the County Council of Talbot County (the “County Council”) adopted Resolution No. 100, which updated the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”) through the 2002 Report of the Review; and 

WHEREAS, the Maryland Department of the Environment (“MDE”) subsequently approved Resolution No. 100 on February 20, 2003; and 

WHEREAS, on September 13, 2011, the County Council adopted Resolution No. 184 (“Ri 84”) for the purpose of incorporating into the CWSP proposed infrastructure extensions and improvements to the Easton Utilities water and sewer system within the Town of Easton (the “Town”), as follows: 1) to extend Easton Utilities’ water system to certain real property located at 10000 Longwoods Road, Easton, Maryland (the “Hospital Property”) for the proposed relocation of the University of Maryland Medical System (“UMMS”) Shore Regional Medical Center (the “Hospital”) from its current location in the Town, including extension to the Talbot County Community Center, located at 10028 Ocean Gateway, Easton, Maryland (the “TCCC Property”); 2) to extend Easton Utilities’ sewer system to the Property and the TCCC; and 3) to add a cross-town water connector; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Ri 84, the Hospital Property was to include an elevated water storage tank and a new force main pump station; and 

WHEREAS, Ri 84 resulted from the collaborative efforts of the Town, the County, Easton Utilities, and UMMS representatives to develop plans for extension of municipal water and sewer service from Easton Utilities to the Hospital Property and to the TCCC Property (collectively, the “Properties”); and 

WHEREAS, Exhibit B-i to R184 depicted the proposed pump station located at the northern end of the Hospital Property by Hiners Lane and the force main extending south along Longwoods Road between Routes 662 and 322, crossing at Route 322, and continuing through the Town using existing easements to tie in to the collector force main at Chapel Road; and

WHEREAS, the Town has submitted an Application to amend the CWSP for the purposes of adding an alternate location for the wastewater pump station at the southern end of the Hospital Property and expanding the potential force main route to serve the Properties, which would allow maximum flexibility to determine the most appropriate route from economic, engineering, and rights-of-way acquisition perspectives; and 

VHEREAS, the County Engineer has evaluated and confirmed the feasibility of adding an alternate location for the wastewater pump station and expanding the potential force main route as aforesaid, and has requested that the Talbot County Office of Law prepare this amendment to the CWSP to facilitate the same; and 

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9- 506(a)(1)(i), the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot County Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as the Talbot County Public Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area; and 

WHEREAS, on July 3, 2024, the Planning Commission certified that the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required by Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-506(a)(1)(ii). 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows: 

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein. 

SECTION TWO: The CWSP is hereby amended as shown in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference as if fully set forth to add an alternate location for the wastewater pump station and to expand the potential force main route to serve the Properties. 

SECTION THREE: The flow rates of the wastewater pump station and the size of the force main shall be adequately designed to serve the Properties. 

SECTION FOUR: No properties, lots, or parcels other than the Properties to be served hereunder shall be entitled to sewer service unless: 1) any such properties, lots, or parcels are already eligible for sewer service from the Town under the CWSP; or 2) the CWSP is amended to permit such service. 

SECTION FIVE: This Resolution shall not modify, excuse, or supersede any other requirements for ongoing compliance with all applicable federal, State, and local statutes, ordinances, rules, or regulations, including without limitation all conditions and requirements of all permits and approvals necessary for connection to the force main. 

SECTION SIX: This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon the date of its adoption.
