WHEREAS, the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer Plan (the “CWSP”)
contains capital projects for water and sewer service in Talbot County, Maryland (the “County”);
and

WHEREAS, the County Council of Talbot County (the “County”) desires to amend the
CWSP for the purpose of including the capital project identified by the Talbot County Engineer
for Fiscal Years 2023 and 2024 into Table 27 of the CWSP, entitled “Talbot County Region V
(Tilghman) Sanitary District Capital Improvement Projects;” and

WHEREAS, in accordance with the requirements of Md. Code Ann., Envir. § 9-
506(a)(1)(i), the proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein has been submitted to the Talbot
County Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”), as well as to the Talbot County
Public Works Advisory Board, for review for consistency with planning programs for the area;
and

WHEREAS, on 2022, the Planning Commission certified that the
proposed CWSP amendment set forth herein is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan as required
by Md. Code Ann., Envir. 506(a)(1)(ii);

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF
TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewer
Plan shall be and is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION ONE: The above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully set forth herein.

SECTION TWO: A Preliminary Engineering Report completed by Rummel, Klepper and
Kahl Engineering in 2020 evaluated the option of upgrading the Region V (Tilghman) Wastewater
Treatment Plant (“WWTP”) on Seth Avenue with Enhanced Nutrient Removal (ENR)
technologies or expanding the Region II (St. Michaels) WWTP to include wastewater flows from
the Region V Sanitary District along with eliminating septic systems in and around the Villages of
Sherwood, Wittman, McDaniel, and Claiborne. With projected sea level rise, the overall impacts
to roads and the wastewater infrastructure were more costly than extending sewer from the Region
Il WWTP along with eliminating septic systems in and around the villages between St. Michaels
and Tilghman Island.

SECTION THREE: This project consists of engineering, design, and construction of a new
WWTP located on the site of the existing Region II (St. Michaels) WWTP, installing force mains

from the Region V (Tilghman) Wastewater Treatment to the newly constructed Region II (St.
Michaels) Wastewater Treatment Plant, and extending sewer to small lots in and around the
Villages of Sherwood, Wittman, McDaniel, and Claiborne between St. Michaels and Tilghman
Island.

SECTION FOUR: As presented in the 1992 Update of the CWSP, the small lots in and
around the Villages of Sherwood, Wittman, McDaniel, and Claiborne between St. Michaels and
Tilghman Island are located where the groundwater table is high and the soils are poorly drained.
Many of these lots are less than two acres, which leaves insufficient area to properly install a
functioning septic system with 10,000 square feet being needed to support the drainfield.

SECTION FIVE: As part of this capital improvement project, over 22,500 pounds of total
nitrogen (“TN”) will be removed from the annual loading of nitrogen pollution allocated to Talbot
County. As part of the Total Maximum Daily Loads, Talbot County needs to remove 37,050
pounds of TN by 2025. This project, along with other septic system elimination projects being
completed by Talbot County, will remove over 42,850 pounds of TN, thus exceeding the goal of
removing 37,050 pounds of TN by over 5,800 pounds.

SECTION SIX: On January 31, 2022, the County submitted an application to the Maryland
Department of the Environment (MDE) seeking grant and loan funds for this capital project.
