A RESOLUTION TO AMEND THE 911 FEE FOR TALBOT COUNTY, MARYLAND BY
INCREASING THE FEE FROM $0.75 PER MONTH TO $1.50 PER MONTH

WHEREAS, on June 11, 1985, the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland (the
“County Council”) adopted a resolution establishing a local 911 fee of thirty cents ($0.30) per
month to be applied to all bills rendered for switched local exchange access service within Talbot
County, Maryland (the “County”) to finance the operation and maintenance of the County’s 911
system; and

WHEREAS, on October 2, 1990, the County Council adopted a resolution establishing an
additional charge of twenty cents ($0.20) per month to pay for the operation and maintenance of
the County’s 911 system, for a total charge of fifty cents ($0.50) to be applied to all bills rendered
for switched local exchange access service within the County; and

WHEREAS, on August 29, 1995, the County Council adopted a resolution establishing a
charge of fifty cents ($0.50) per month to be applied to all current bills for switched local exchange
access service, wireless telephone service or other 911 accessible service within the County after
January 1, 1996 to finance the operation and maintenance of the County’s 911 system; and

WHEREAS, the General Assembly of Maryland enacted Chapter 451, effective October
1, 2003, amending Md. Code Ann., Public Safety § 1-311 (1997 Repl. Vol.) to authorize the
counties of Maryland to increase the local 911 fee to seventy-five cents ($0.75) per month to cover
eligible operation and maintenance costs; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. 114, adopted and effective May 11, 2004, the
County Council increased the local 911 fee to seventy-five cents ($0.75) per month to cover the
total amount of eligible operation and maintenance of the County’s 911 system; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Public Safety § 1-311(c)(2), if revenues
attributable to a county’s 911 fee for a fiscal year do not provide the revenues necessary to cover
the county’s operational costs for that fiscal year, the county may, for the following fiscal year,
impose a county 911 fee sufficient to cover the county’s projected operational costs for the 911
system for the fiscal year for each switched local exchange access service, commercial mobile
radio service (“CMRS”), or other 911 accessible service provided; and

WHEREAS, the County Council has determined that the local 911 fee should be increased
to one dollar and fifty cents ($1.50), such increase being sufficient to assist in covering the eligible
operation and maintenance of the County’s 911 emergency telephone system.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the County Council of Talbot County,
Maryland that the local 911 fee applied to all current bills rendered for switched access service,
commercial mobile radio service (CMRS), or other 911 accessible service within the County is
hereby increased from seventy-five cents ($0.75) per month to one dollar and fifty cents ($1.50)
per month.

<!-- page 3 -->
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the above recitals are hereby incorporated as if fully
set forth herein.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that said 911 fee shall continue in effect until repealed
or modified by resolution of the County Council.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that a copy of this resolution shall be forwarded to the
Public Service Commission, the Maryland 911 Board, and, no later than 60 days before the
implementation of the fee change, the Comptroller of Maryland certifying said additional local
911 fee.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Resolution shall take effect immediately upon
its date of adoption.
