WHEREAS, the Board for Martingham Utilities Cooperative has requested an amendment to the Comprehensive Water & Sewer Plan to permit Region II (St. Michaels) Wastewater System to accept treated effluent from the Martingham Utilities Cooperative on a temporary basis; and 

WHEREAS, the Board for Martingham Utilities Cooperative has indicated that it will, in cooperation with the Talbot County Department of Public Works and the Maryland Department of the Environment, continue to review and explore options to establish a permanent solution to the problems associated with their discharge permit. 

NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the County Council of Talbot County, Maryland, pursuant to Title 9, Subtitle 5, of the Environment Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, that: 

Section 1. The Talbot County Comprehensive Water and Sewerage Plan is hereby amended to extend sewer service to Martingham Utilities Cooperative to accept up to 19,000 gallons per day, approximately, of treated effluent from Martingham Utilities Cooperative into the Region II Wastewater Treatment Plant, on a temporary basis, for a period up to and including three (3) years from the effective date of this Resolution. During this period, the County Engineer and Maryland Environmental Service shall retain authority, management, and control over daily average flows, and may temporarily cease acceptance of effluent, from time to time, based on available storage capacity at the Region II Wastewater Treatment Plant. Unless amended, modified, or extended in the interim, this Resolution shall automatically expire by its terms without further action on the 3 anniversary of its effective date, and thereafter shall have no continuing force or effect. 

Section 2. As a condition for the continued extension of sewer service during this temporary three-year period, the Board for Martingham Utilities Cooperative will, in cooperation with the Talbot County Department of Public Works and the Maryland Department of the Environment, continue to review and explore options to establish a permanent solution to the problems associated with their discharge permit and shall report the status and results of their efforts to the County Council from time to time as requested; and 

Section 3. The Talbot County Department of Public Works shall submit to the Talbot County Health Department reports identifying the allocation of sewer and the remaining capacity of the Region H Wastewater Treatment Plant;

Section 4. This amendment authorizes construction of a temporary connection to the Region TI Wastewater Treatment Plant, at Martingham Utilities Cooperative’s expense, subject to review and approval by the Talbot County Department of Public Works, the Maryland Department of Environment, and any other State, local, or federal agencies. Design and construction shall be in full compliance with applicable design and technical requirements, rules, and regulations of local, State, and federal authorities, and subject to the approval of all County, State, and/or Federal agencies, as applicable. 

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon acceptance by the Maryland Department of the Environment as an amendment to Talbot County’s Comprehensive Water & Sewer Plan.
