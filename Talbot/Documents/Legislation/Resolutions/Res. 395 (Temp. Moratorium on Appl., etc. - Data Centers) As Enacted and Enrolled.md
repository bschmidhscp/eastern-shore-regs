A RESOLUTION TO ADOPT A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON THE PROCESSING, REVIEW, AND APPROVAL OF APPLICATIONS, SITE PLANS, PERMITS, AND OTHER AUTHORIZATIONS FOR THE LOCATION, CONSTRUCTION,<sup>AND/OROPERATIONOFDATACENTERSFORAPERIODOF</sup> TWELVE (12) MONTHS TO ALLOW THE COUNTY SUFFICIENT TIME TO ENACT REASONABLE, THOUGHTFUL,<sup>ANDLAWFULZONING</sup> REQUIREMENTS<sup>AND</sup> OTHER REGULATIONS PERTAINING THERETO AS DEEMED NECESSARY TO PROTECT THE PUBLIC HEALTH, SAFETY, AND WELFARE 

WHEREAS, pursuant<sup>toMd.Code</sup> Ann.,<sup>LocalGov’t</sup> § 10-324,<sup>Talbot</sup> County, Maryland (the “County’),<sup>as</sup> a Charter county, may enact<sup>locallaws</sup> relating to zoning<sup>and</sup> planning<sup>to</sup> protect and promote public safety, health, morals, and welfare; and 

WHEREAS, as a local jurisdiction under Division I of the Land Use Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, the County exercises planning and zoning authority, maintains a Planning Commission,<sup>aBoardof</sup> Appeals,<sup>anda</sup> Zoning<sup>Administrator,andhas</sup> adopted<sup>a</sup> Zoning Ordinance and a Comprehensive Plan; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>CouncilofTalbot</sup> County, Maryland (the “County Council’) recognizes<sup>thatthe</sup> location,<sup>construction,and</sup> operation<sup>of datacentersraise</sup> significant<sup>concerns</sup> for the communities in which they are located, including sustainability, energy and water usage, noise, vibration,<sup>and</sup> impacts<sup>onresidents’</sup> quality<sup>of</sup> life;<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>as</sup> public<sup>infrastructureandenvironmental</sup> impacts<sup>ofdatacenterscomeinto</sup> focus,<sup>itisclearthat</sup> regulation<sup>is</sup> needed, including,<sup>butnotlimited</sup> to, mitigation<sup>ofthe</sup> large amounts of public utility usage of data centers, such as water and electricity, in order to maintain the public health, safety, and welfare; and WHEREAS,<sup>to</sup> leverage<sup>thebenefitsofdatacenterscan</sup> provide<sup>tothelocal</sup> economy while protecting the environment and local quality of life, the County Council desires to establish a comprehensive approach to data centers, including,<sup>but</sup> not<sup>limited</sup> to, defining a<sup>“data</sup> center,” designating zoning<sup>districtsinwhichdatacenterswillbe</sup> permitted,<sup>whether</sup> by right,<sup>with</sup> conditions, or by special exception, establishing bulk and area regulations, landscaping requirements,<sup>andnoise</sup> limitations;<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Councilconsidersthe</sup> public<sup>interesttobebestserved</sup> by sufficiently<sup>deliberatedlocallawsand</sup> regulations regarding<sup>datacentersinthe</sup> unincorporated areas of the County; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County currently<sup>lackslocallawsand</sup> regulations regarding<sup>data</sup> centers; and 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Council</sup> recognizes<sup>the</sup> potential negative impacts<sup>of locallaws</sup> and regulations regarding data centers stemming from inadequate and hurried deliberations; and

<!-- page 3 -->
WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Councilhasdeterminedthat</sup> enacting hastily prepared<sup>locallaws</sup> and regulations regarding data centers would potentially create confusion, unintended consequences,<sup>unreasonableand</sup> inequitable<sup>outcomes,and</sup> litigation,<sup>andwouldotherwisebe</sup> prejudicial<sup>tothe</sup> public health, safety,<sup>and</sup> welfare;<sup>and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Council</sup> requires<sup>sufficienttimefor</sup> appropriate study<sup>and</sup> public input<sup>toensuretheestablishmentofeffectiveanddurable</sup> zoning<sup>textamendmentsandother</sup> necessary<sup>locallaws</sup> regulations applicable<sup>todatacenters;and</sup> 

WHEREAS,<sup>the</sup> County<sup>Councilhasdeterminedthata</sup> temporary moratorium,<sup>foraterm</sup> of twelve (12) months, to be a reasonable length of time for the due consideration of zoning text amendments and other local laws and regulations for the safe, practical, and legal implementation of data centers in the County. 

NOW, THEREFORE,<sup>BEITRESOLVED</sup> by<sup>the</sup> County<sup>CouncilforTalbot</sup> County, Maryland,<sup>that:</sup> 

1. Definition. As used herein, the term “data center” means and refers to a building, group of buildings, or portion thereof whose primary use is the housing and operation of computer servers, computer storage equipment,<sup>andassociatedinfrastructurethatbusinesses</sup> or other organizations<sup>useto</sup> organize, process, store,<sup>anddisseminate</sup> large<sup>amountsofdata.Theterm</sup> “data center” does not mean and refer to, and this moratorium does not prohibit, the housing and operation<sup>ofsuch</sup> computer<sup>servers,</sup> computer storage equipment,<sup>andassociatedinfrastructure</sup> that is accessory and subordinate to a lawful, principal use on the same property and serves only the occupant thereof. 

2. Moratorium established. A temporary moratorium is hereby established on the processing, review,<sup>and</sup> approval<sup>of</sup> applications,<sup>site</sup> plans, permits,<sup>andotherauthorizations,</sup> including<sup>butnotlimitedto</sup> zoning, occupancy,<sup>and</sup> building<sup>certificatesand</sup> permits<sup>and</sup> approvals,<sup>fordatacentersfora</sup> period<sup>of twelve</sup> (12)<sup>months.</sup> 

> 3. Recommendations and legislation. This temporary moratorium is established to allow a reasonable time within which County staff,<sup>withtheassistanceof</sup> subject matter experts<sup>if</sup> deemed appropriate by the County Council, can prepare recommendations for review by the Planning Commission;<sup>the</sup> Planning<sup>Commissioncan</sup> prepare<sup>recommendationsforthe</sup> County Council;<sup>andthe</sup> County<sup>Councilcanconsiderandenact</sup> legislation<sup>to</sup> efficiently<sup>and</sup> fairly establish zoning requirements and other local laws and regulations regarding data centers and consider such other related matters as the County Council, the Planning Commission, and County staff deem appropriate. 

4. Applications. During the duration of this temporary moratorium, the County shall not process, review,<sup>or</sup> approve any application,<sup>site</sup> plan, permit,<sup>orotherauthorization,</sup> including<sup>but</sup> not limited to any zoning, occupancy, or building certificate or permit, or application for a zoning text amendment within the County’s jurisdiction, for a data center. This temporary moratorium shall apply to applications currently pending before the County, if any, as well as applications filed after the effective date.

<!-- page 4 -->
5. Effective date. This temporary moratorium shall be effective immediately upon adoption<sup>of thisResolution.</sup> 

6. Amendment; extension. The County Council reserves the right to amend or extend,<sup>for</sup> up<sup>totwo</sup> (2)<sup>additionalsix(6)month</sup> periods,<sup>this</sup> temporary<sup>moratoriumasitdeemsnecessary</sup> to protect the public health, safety,<sup>and</sup> welfare and<sup>infurtheranceof the</sup> purposes<sup>setforthherein.</sup> 

7. Recitals. The recitals to this Resolution are not merely prefatory but are a substantive part<sup>hereof.</sup> 

> 8. Severability. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase of this Resolution is,<sup>for</sup> any reason,<sup>heldtobeunconstitutionalorotherwise</sup> unlawful,<sup>suchdecisionshallnotaffect</sup> the validity of the remaining portions of this Resolution, which can<sup>be</sup> given<sup>effectwithoutthe</sup> invalid section, subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase, and to that end,<sup>all</sup> provisions<sup>ofthis</sup> Resolution are hereby declared to be severable.
