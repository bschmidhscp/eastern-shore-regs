# Talbot County, MD: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "PC digest" and "Council digest" = `01. Digests/`; "Amendments" = `Code/00. Amendments Not Yet in Code.md`. Code text is current through Bill 1624.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Minors (up to 3 lots, no new road) are the easy path:** Planning Director decides, preliminary and final plat can be combined [§ 190-66.2; § 190-68.3]. Application fee about $1,300 [Fees: Planning Fees p.1]. Density is the real limit.
- **Ag-district density is thin:** 3 units plus 1 per 20 acres on parcels of 6+ acres, counted from the 6/22/1991 parcel; the 3-lot minor cap is cumulative [§ 190-8.7; § 190-78]. Clustering and reserved land are mandatory in AC/CP/WRC [§ 190-8.8].
- **Septic drives cost and risk:** Tier IV is over 80% of the county [2016 CompPlan p.57]; new septic needs nitrogen-removing BAT, est. $19,055 to $31,512 [2016 CompPlan p.121; Fees: BAT Ranking]. Sewer is rare.
- **Impact fees:** $9,356 per home outside towns, $8,080 inside (FY26), paid at building permit; a likely FY27 inflation bump is not in the corpus [Fees: FY 26 Impact p.1; § 64-13.C].
- **Board of Appeals is permissive:** 147 of 151 decisions (2016–2026) granted, 123 with conditions; 3 denied. 58 were Critical Area variances [BOA digest]. Variances cost time and conditions, rarely the project.
- **Risks:** Critical Area (RC is 1 per 20 acres; growth allocation needs Council plus State), pending Comprehensive Plan rewrite, and 3 to 6 months over 2 to 3 PC meetings for 4+ lots [PC digest 2024-11-06, 2025-03-05].
- **Opportunities:** TR with sewer (4 per acre) and village districts (1 per acre) [Tables II-9, II-10]; Bill 1627 eases release of reserved land under a MALPF option [Amendments].

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Minor | Small-scale | Major |
|---|---|---|---|
| Threshold | 3 or fewer new and cumulative lots, no road | 4 to 7 new lots; unsewered Tier II and Tier IV; or road with fewer than 8 lots | 4+ lots or any road; Tier I, sewered Tier II, Tier III |
| Decider | Planning Director (may refer to PC) | PC | PC |

All from [§ 190-78; § 190-66.2].

- **Stages:** sketch, preliminary, final, each via Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) and a final compliance meeting [§ 190-66.1; § 190-67.3]. Pre-application meeting is mandatory for major and small-scale [§ 190-54.1.B].
- **Clocks:** each approval valid 12 months plus one 12-month extension [§ 190-67.8]; 9 months to cure a noncompliance notice [§ 190-68.2.D].
- **Final plat needs:** title search, approved construction, stormwater and sediment plans, developer agreements, right-to-farm covenants [§ 190-70.2].
- **Timing signals:** SUB-24-1 sketch 2024-11-06 to approval 2025-02-05; SUB-25-1 sketch 2025-03-05 to final 2025-09-03; SUB-25-2 sketch 2025-05-07 to preliminary 2025-07-02; SP-24-2 continued 2024-04-02 [PC digest]. PC bylaws need 3 votes; a 3-member meeting forced a postponement 2026-03-04 [PC digest].
- Minor timing is **not found in corpus** (Director decisions are not minuted). PC digest shows only 9 major subdivision items 2023-10 to 2026-08.

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Critical Area** (1,000 ft of tidal water) [§ 190-15.2]: lot coverage 15% in RCA/LDA; subdivisions with lots of 1 ac or less capped at 15% overall, 40% per lot [§ 190-15.6.C]. Buffer at least 100 ft, no by-right coverage [§ 190-15.11]. Slopes of 15%+ prohibited [§ 190-15.6.B]. Forest clearing limited to 20% (30% with 1.5x replanting) [§ 190-15.6.A]. IDA allows only VM among residential districts [Table III-2]. Growth allocation: Council map amendment plus State approval; fees $2,500 to $10,000 [§ 190-55.5; Fees: Planning Fees p.1].
- **Septic tiers (SB 236):** in Tier IV and unsewered Tier II the code recognizes only minor (≤3) and small-scale (4–7 lot) subdivisions — "major" is defined only for Tiers I, sewered II and III [§ 190-78]. State law allows only locally-defined "minor" subdivisions on septic in Tier IV (state default 5 lots, local definitions up to 7), so **8+ lots on septic in Tier IV appear not possible**; the "5+" in [GIS SOURCES #12] is the state default. Confirm with staff. Tier III is infill only; sewer there is "not intended" for new development [Bill 1378, Policy 6.21].
- **Forest conservation:** threshold 50% in AC/CP/WRC/TC; 25% unsewered, 20% sewered in TR and villages; reforest 1/4 ac per acre above, 2 ac per acre below [§ 73-11].
- **Stormwater** (Ch. 164) thresholds not extracted. **Floodplain:** show structures cannot avoid it [§ 70-14].
- **Ag preservation:** reserved land and right-to-farm plat notes [§ 190-73; § 190-8.6.G]. 16 MALPF applications forwarded for FY27 [PC digest 2026-06-03].

## 5. Water and sewer

- **Categories:** S/W-1 (immediate priority), 2, 3, unprogrammed [Res. 383, 384]. GIS layer: [GIS SOURCES #14].
- **Adding parcels:** Council resolution after PC review. PC digest lists 66 W&S items: 10 (2023 Q4), 22 (2024), 27 (2025), 7 (2026 to April). Many are existing homes, flagged "not subdividable" (Res. 388, 389) or "no remaining development rights" (Res. 393) [Council digest 2025-12-09, 2026-04-14]. S-1 status does not create lots.
- **Extensions:** Rt. 33 to St. Michaels (bonds $7,018,942, done early 2027) [Council digest 2025-07-22]; Bozman/Neavitt; Claiborne; Calhoon MEBA. County CWSP update consultant awarded $243,600 [Council digest 2026-08-11].
- **BAT/BRF:** annual BAT inspection required [§ 146-3]; O&M $200 to $357 per year after year 2 [Fees: BAT Ranking]. BRF grants are income-based, need a sanitary permit first [Septic: BRF FY2027]. New-construction eligibility **not found in corpus**.

Sources: [Fees: Planning Fees p.1-2; FY 26 Impact p.1]. Impact fees are collected at permit [§ 64-12.F].

**Estimate, 3-lot minor, non-Critical Area:** about $1,300 to $2,200 planning fees at plat, plus $28,068 impact fees at permit. **Excluded:** survey and engineering, soil and perc tests, Health Department plat fees, Soil Conservation District E&S and stormwater fees (restructured 2026-04-14), recording, sureties, BAT system, building excise tax (rate not found), legal and title, FY27 adjustment. The Council's motion to waive the FY27 impact-fee increase failed 2-2, so the automatic adjustment likely applies [Council digest 2026-07-14].
