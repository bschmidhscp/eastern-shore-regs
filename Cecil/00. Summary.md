# Cecil County, MD: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "PC digest", "BOA digest", "Council digest" = `01. Digests/`; "Zoning" = standalone Zoning Ordinance PDF (`Code/Zoning Ordinance/`, mirrored in `Documents/Code/`); "Sub Regs" = Subdivision Regulations (2011, amended through 2017). State law (SB 236 tiers, Critical Area, BAT): see `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`; only Cecil's local choices are described here. **The eCode has no zoning or subdivision text; use the PDFs.**

## 1. Bottom line

- **Best feature: minor subdivisions are dense and administrative.** In NAR, SAR and RR a minor subdivision gets **1 unit per acre**, versus 1 per 10 ac (NAR), 1 per 20 ac (SAR) and 1 per 3 ac (RR) for a major [Zoning §§ 22.2, 23.2, 24.2; Art. VIII notes 5]. The Director approves minors on a combined preliminary/final plat, no Planning Commission hearing [Sub Regs § 2.4.1-2.4.2].
- **Cap is 5 lots, cumulative:** a subsequent plat that brings lots off the parent to five or more triggers major procedures [Sub Regs § 2.4.3]. Whether the 5th lot is still "minor" is ambiguous (§ 2.4.2 says "up to five"; § 2.4.3 says "five or more"). Confirm with staff.
- **No impact fee or excise tax on new homes** [Fee Findings; Council minutes 2026-06-09]. Minor plat fee about $200 [Fees: 847 p.1].
- **Majors are slow:** Director O'Connor put the full process at about 18 months [Council minutes 2026-06-09]. Rural major density is thin (60% open space in NAR/SAR) [Zoning §§ 22-23].
- **Risks:** Critical Area RCA 1 per 20 ac [Zoning § 193.3]; perc testing before approval [PC minutes 2026-01-21]; 2010 comp plan; public sewer, if used, costs $12,000 per ELU plus any subdistrict assessment [Fee Findings].
- **Surprise:** a 3-lot, 30.4 ac NAR subdivision (LaPointe) went to the Planning Commission as a "major subdivision" (Tier III, outside the Master W&S Plan) rather than a staff-approved minor [PC digest 2026-05-18]. Reason not found in corpus.

## 2. Zoning districts

| District | Minor sub density | Major sub density | Min lot (no community facilities) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NAR (north ag) | 1 per ac | 1 per 10 ac | 20,000 sf; 80 ft | 60% open space; bufferyards [Zoning § 22; Art. VI] |
| SAR (south ag) | 1 per ac | 1 per 20 ac | 20,000 sf; 80 ft | 60% open space [Zoning § 23] |
| RR | 1 per ac | 1 per 3 ac | 20,000 sf; 80 ft | Large lot max 40% of tract [Zoning § 24.2] |
| LDR | n/a (same as major) | 1 per ac; 2 with community facilities | 20,000 sf; 80 ft | [Zoning § 25.2] |
| UR, ST, VR, RM, MH, RMU | same as major | 1 to 14 per ac by type; PUD higher | 6,000 to 20,000 sf | Mostly growth-area, sewered [Art. VIII] |

Other: ≥10 lots require 15% common open space (PDR payment in lieu allowed) [Zoning § 22.3]. Frontage 100 ft (25 ft on a shared access lot) [Art. VI]. Intrafamily lot: one lot from a pre-ordinance parcel under 2 ac, no transfer outside family for 5 years, not in RCA [Zoning § 22.5]. Minor subdivision acreage is deducted from the parent for later major density, but remaining minor lots served by an internal road may be added as a bonus of up to 5 lots [Sub Regs § 2.4.1].

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Minor | Major |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | Up to 5 lots, no new street, cumulative [Sub Regs § 2.4.2-2.4.3] | 5+ lots or any new street |
| Decider | Director, Land Use and Development Services | Planning Commission |
| Stages | Combined preliminary/final plat | Concept, preliminary, final; Technical Advisory Committee (advisory only) [Council minutes 2026-06-09] |

- **Remainder:** if under 5 ac remains, the whole tract must be platted [Sub Regs § 2.4.4].
- **Expirations conflict:** Sub Regs say concept 3 yrs, preliminary 3 yrs [§ 4.0.9, 4.1.16]; the Director told Council concept 3 yrs, preliminary 2 yrs, final none [Council minutes 2026-06-09]. Use the Director's figure as the current practice and verify.
- **Timing signals:** a 3-lot preliminary/final plat was approved at a single PC meeting on 2026-05-18 [PC digest]. Of 32 PC digest items (Oct 2025 to Sep 2026), 5 were major subdivisions: 2 approved, 3 shown only on agendas or continued. Minor timing: **not found in corpus**.

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Critical Area:** RCA density 1 unit per 20 ac (private wetlands may count if upland is 1 per 8 ac) [Zoning § 193.3-4]; lot coverage 15% for parcels over 1 ac, and lots of 1 ac or less in post-1985 subdivisions capped at 15% overall; small-lot table up to 31.25% [Zoning Art. XI, pp.234-235]. Statewide rules: Maryland summary.
- **Septic / tiers:** the county adopted a tier map [PC digest 2026-05-18; Plan 1217]. Per MDP the county kept its pre-2012 local "minor" definition and has no Tier IV exemption [Maryland summary]. Tier IV is not mentioned in the corpus; effect on the 5-lot cap **not found**. Perc evaluation must precede septic permit and building permit sign-off [PC minutes 2026-01-21].
- **Forest conservation:** county regulations under Nat. Res. §5-1601 [Sub Regs definitions p.8]; the LaPointe plat used an exemption (§ 3.2K) via a declaration of intent [PC digest 2026-05-18; Sub Regs § 2.3.5]. Thresholds **not found**.
- **Stormwater:** Ch. 325; minimum-control determination $100, minor-subdivision ESD narrative $100 [Fees: 656]. LaPointe deferred SWM to building permit [PC digest].
- **Ag preservation:** NAR and SAR are the preservation zones [Plan 1217 p.~98]. County TDR (Ch. A386) and PDR (Ch. A385; amended by Bill 2025-09). Floodplain overlay: Zoning Part III (not read).
- **Habitat:** bog turtle noted near LaPointe; MDNR coordination was a condition [PC digest 2026-05-18].

## 5. Water and sewer

- Governed by the 2019 Master Water and Sewer Plan (categories S-1 existing to S-3 future, 2-3 yrs) [2019 Master W&S Plan]. W&S Plan amendment count 2023-26: **not found** (Legislation Index shows no W&S flags).
- Growth areas (Tier I, S-1/W-2 sewered) host the big projects, e.g. Northwoods Section 5, 66 lots [PC digest 2026-05-18]. Rural minors use well and septic.
- **Charges:** Major Facilities Fee $12,000 per ELU (220 gpd), paid at occupancy permit [Wastewater Division Fee Schedule 8/1/2021, note 1]; Little Elk Creek benefit assessment $35,265 per ELU from 2027 (Triumph Industrial Park only) [Council digest 2026-08-18]. Water connection fees: not found.
