# Cecil County, MD: By-Right Playbook

Keys: "Sub Regs" = `Documents/Planning Applications and Forms/Subdivision-Regulations-PDF.md`; "Zoning" = `Code/Zoning Ordinance/`; "Fees 847/656" = `Documents/Fees/`; "PC 2026-05-18" = `Meeting Minutes/Planning Commission/2026-05-18 Minutes.md`. State rules (tiers, perc, forest conservation, Critical Area, BAT): see `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`. Anything marked (estimate) is not in the corpus.

## 1. Bottom line

The path is the **Minor Subdivision**, a combined preliminary/final plat, up to **5 lots**, no new street, **approved by the Director of Land Use and Development Services** with no Planning Commission hearing [Sub Regs §§ 2.4.1-2.4.2]. The written rules require **no public notice of any kind** for it: the adjoining-owner certified letters and on-site sign apply to plats going to the Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) or Planning Commission, and a minor skips both [Sub Regs § 3.8, § 4.0 notice paragraph, § 4.1.20(a), § 4.2.3]. Typical time to a recorded plat is about **4 months (estimate; range 2 to 8)**, driven by perc testing. Two cautions: (a) the cap is cumulative, so lots already cut from the pre-1976 original parcel count against the 5 [Sub Regs § 2.4.3, "Original Parcel" definition]; (b) a 3-lot NAR plat (LaPointe, May 2026) was nonetheless run as a "major subdivision" through TAC, notice and the Planning Commission [PC 2026-05-18], reason not stated. Confirm the lot count and process with staff before buying.

## 2. Eligibility test

Pass all of these or the parcel is off the path.

- [ ] **Zoning and density.** In NAR, SAR and RR a minor subdivision gets **1 dwelling per acre** (major is 1 per 10, 1 per 20, 1 per 3 ac) [Zoning § 22.2.a, § 23.2, § 24.2; Art. VIII table]. LDR and village/urban districts use the same density as major [Zoning Art. VIII note 5]. Caution: Art. VIII note 5 says minor density "shall be the same as major," which conflicts with the 1-per-acre text in Art. III; the LaPointe staff report applied 1 per 10 ac to a 3-lot NAR plat [PC 2026-05-18]. Raise with staff.
- [ ] **Lot minimums.** 20,000 sf and 80 ft width for single-family detached (NAR; the same in SAR/RR/LDR per the Maryland-county summary) [Zoning Art. VI]. Septic lots in practice need more area for the 10,000 sf sewage reserve (see State summary).
- [ ] **Road frontage.** 100 ft; may drop to **25 ft on a local road** per the Roadway Classification Map [Zoning Art. VI, note 6; PC 2026-05-18 table "100/25"]. Collector/arterial frontage also triggers an expanded building setback (100 ft in NAR, Firetower Road example) and no direct access from arterials [Zoning Art. VI note 1; Sub Regs § 7.4.5].
- [ ] **Cumulative cap.** Lots off the "original parcel" (any lot recorded or existing before **1976-04-15**) are counted; once a later plat brings the total to **five or more**, major procedures are required [Sub Regs § 2.4.3; definition p.~11 "Original Parcel"]. Staff's LaPointe history list (minor subdivisions #343, #1365, #1809 and a prior major) shows they trace the whole chain [PC 2026-05-18]. Wording conflict: § 2.4.2 says "up to five," § 2.4.3 says "five or more." Underwrite **4 lots per original parcel** until staff confirms 5.
- [ ] **Remainder rule.** If under 5 ac remains in the original tract, the whole tract must be platted; over 5 ac may stay unplatted [Sub Regs § 2.4.4]. A tax-map sketch of all land and prior lots is required.
- [ ] **No new street.** Any new street makes it a major [Sub Regs § 2.4.2]. Existing road frontage only.
- [ ] **Access.** Flag/pipestem/panhandle lots are allowed, but no more than **two** such lots may have adjoining driveway entrances to a public road; handles over 5 ac lots may be required at 50 ft wide, otherwise 25 ft minimum [Sub Regs § 7.4.2]. Shared access: a 50 ft shared access easement serving 2 lots was accepted [PC 2026-05-18]. Off-site road must not be unsafe (sight distance, grades over 10%, pavement too narrow) [Sub Regs § 2.7]. State road frontage needs SHA entrance approval [§ 2.7(e)].
- [ ] **Septic and tier.** Cecil kept its pre-2012 local minor definition (up to 5 lots) and has no Tier IV exemption [State summary § 2]. Tier IV: minor on septic only. Tier III: minor on septic, major only after a PC hearing. Tier I/II: sewer, or minor on septic. Every lot needs health-department approval of well and septic [Sub Regs § 2.8(a)]. No re-subdivision of a Tier II-IV minor and its remainder (plat note) [Env. § 9-206(l)].
- [ ] **Critical Area.** RCA is 1 unit per 20 ac, no variance [Zoning § 193.3-4]. A parcel inside the 1,000-ft area needs the Critical Area environmental assessment on the plat and 20 plan copies instead of 19 [Sub Regs § 2.4.2(e); Fees 847 p.3]. Do not plan 4-5 lots on RCA land. LDA and IDA parcels need case-by-case review (not read).
- [ ] **No variance, special exception or rezoning needed**, and no W&S Master Plan amendment (parcel is not in a service area, or the category already fits) [Sub Regs § 2.8].
- [ ] **Not an add-on or intrafamily lot** (those are separate, cheaper tracks) [Sub Regs § 2.3; Zoning § 22.5].

## 3. Step-by-step path

Durations are estimates unless cited. Meeting calendar from Fees 847 p.3: TAC 1st Wednesday, PC 3rd Monday (neither needed for a minor).

1. **Title and original-parcel research (us / attorney, 1-3 weeks).** Pull the deed chain back to 4/15/1976 and every prior minor/major plat off the parcel (staff keep numbered records such as "Minor Subdivision #1809"). Fixes the true remaining lot count.
2. **Pre-application call with Land Use and Development Services (free, 1 week).** Confirm lot cap, density reading, tier, Critical Area, W&S category, process (see Section 7). No formal pre-app fee found.
3. **Boundary survey and topography (surveyor/engineer, 3-6 weeks).** Required: contours at 2 or 5 ft extending 50 ft beyond the boundary, features, utility easements within 200 ft, soils, wells and septic areas, perc data, Critical Area information, signed and sealed by a Maryland surveyor [Sub Regs § 2.4.2(a)-(e); § 4.1.20(a)].
4. **Soils and perc (Health Department / Environmental Health, 4-12 weeks).** A land-evaluation site test with test pits, then approval; the survey must show test pits, buildings, wells, septic and topography first, and fieldwork alone is not approval [PC 2026-01-21 pp.5-6 (cited in Fee Findings)]. **Season: late winter to early spring** (high water table, State rule, about Feb-Apr) [COMAR 26.04.02.04B; State summary § 1]. Health fee: not found. Plan the whole job around this window; missing it costs up to 10 months (estimate).
5. **Forest conservation.** County Forest Conservation Regulations under Nat. Res. § 5-1601 ff. [Sub Regs definitions]. File a **Declaration of Intent** to claim an exemption (the LaPointe plat claimed § 3.2K) [Sub Regs § 2.3.5; PC 2026-05-18]. If no exemption applies: Forest Stand Delineation and a forest conservation plan (thresholds not found; State rule starts at 40,000 sf, replacement 1:1 for clearing since 7/1/2026) [State summary § 1]. Fee in lieu per sq ft: not found.
6. **Stormwater (Dept. of Public Works).** Submit the **minor-subdivision ESD narrative** ($100 first submittal, $50 resubmittal) [Fees 656]; full SWM may be deferred to building permit, as in LaPointe [PC 2026-05-18]. Ch. 325 [§ 325-13].
7. **Other agencies (estimate 2-4 weeks, parallel).** Soil Conservation District if erosion and sediment control facilities are required [Sub Regs § 2.4.2(d)]; Critical Area review if mapped; MDNR habitat coordination if rare species (bog turtle condition in LaPointe); SHA entrance if state road; Division of Water and Sewer Planning signature that the on-site systems conform to the Master W&S Plan [PC 2026-05-18].
8. **Submit the combined preliminary/final minor plat to Land Use and Development Services (apply on county forms via the online portal; fee $50 + $50/lot).** Plat meets all § 4.2 final-plat content plus the § 2.4.2(a)-(g) extras [Sub Regs § 2.4.2]. Fees due at submittal [Fees 847 p.1]. Review (estimate): 3-6 weeks per round; incomplete plats are returned.
9. **Director approval and signatures.** Required signature blocks before recording: Chief, Development Plans Review Division (certifies stormwater and any public works items), Health Department approving authority, then the Director [Sub Regs §§ 4.2.3-4.2.9]. No TAC, no Planning Commission [§ 4.2.3]. If the plat shows a dedication (e.g., a road-widening strip) it must be shown and dedicated before recording [PC 2026-05-18, Firetower example].
10. **Record at the Circuit Court for Cecil County.** Record only after all signatures; pay recording fees within 2 years of signatures or the approval is void [Sub Regs §§ 4.2.9, 4.2.11, 2.4.2]. Furnish recorded copies to agencies [§ 4.2.10]. Circuit Court recording fee: not found.
11. **Sell the lots.** No lots may be sold before the plat is recorded and the Maryland sale/building restrictions in Env. § 9-206(j) are met (MDE must have the plat). We pull no building permits; buyer handles Health permit, driveway permit ($40 [Fees 665]) and building permit.

Total (estimate): 2 months best case (perc in season, clean file) to 8 months (perc season missed or resubmittals); typical 4.

## 4. Public notice

- **Minor subdivision path: none required in the text.** The certified-mail letters to immediately contiguous owners apply "prior to the submittal of a concept plat to the TAC" [Sub Regs § 4.0 notice paragraph, p.~25], and the on-site public notification sign is a completeness requirement for **TAC and Planning Commission** submissions [Sub Regs § 4.1.20(a); Fees 847 p.3]. A minor goes only to the Director, with no TAC or PC review [§§ 2.4.1, 4.2.3]. No newspaper notice and no hearing found.
- **Caveat.** In the LaPointe 3-lot plat, Planning & Zoning wrote that the plat "is in compliance with § 3.8 ... regarding public notification" and it was heard at the Planning Commission under a combined preliminary/final plat [PC 2026-05-18]. Whether that was because it was treated as major (cumulative count, tier, or practice) is not stated. Treat "no notice" as the code reading and confirm in the pre-app call.
- If notice is required (major track): contiguous owners by certified mail with name, address, phone, acreage, zoning and lot count, proof submitted by the TAC deadline, plus an installed site sign [Sub Regs § 4.0; Fees 847 p.3]. No variant avoids it other than staying a true minor.

## 6. Off-ramps

| Trigger | Result | Design around it |
|---|---|---|
| Cumulative count reaches 5 or more off the original parcel | Major procedures (concept/prelim/final, PC) [Sub Regs § 2.4.3] | Do the title/history research first; stay at 4 until staff confirm |
| New street, or internal road | Major [§ 2.4.2] | Use existing frontage with shared access easement |
| Third driveway entrance in a row of flag lots | Violates § 7.4.2 | Cap adjoining entrances at two lots |
| Tier III/IV septic rules | Tier IV allows only minor; Tier III major needs PC hearing [State summary § 2] | Stay minor |
| Parcel in RCA | 1 per 20 ac, no variance [Zoning § 193] | Choose non-Critical Area land |
| Lot below 20,000 sf, 80 ft width, or frontage short | Variance (Board of Appeals hearing) [Zoning Art. VI] | Draw to minimums; use the 25 ft local-road frontage |
| Perc failure on a lot | Lot lost or needs a different system | Test every lot before closing; option the land |
| Rezoning, special exception, or W&S plan category change | Public hearings, months [Zoning Part I] | Buy land already zoned/served for the plan |
| Remainder under 5 ac | Whole tract must be platted [§ 2.4.4] | Plan the remainder or include it |
| Wetlands, floodplain, stream buffers, habitat (bog turtle) | Conditions or lot loss [PC 2026-05-18] | Delineate before contract |
| County treats the plat as "major" anyway (LaPointe) | TAC + notice + PC | Ask staff in writing before purchase |

## 7. Open questions to ask staff

1. Is the fifth lot off the original parcel still a minor ("up to five" § 2.4.2 vs "five or more" § 2.4.3)? Do the remaining lands count if sold unplatted?
2. Why was the 3-lot LaPointe plat (Firetower Road, NAR, Tier III) processed as a major subdivision with PC review, and what would have made it a staff-approved minor? Do minors ever get signs, adjoining-owner letters or TAC/PC review?
3. Which density governs minors in NAR/SAR: 1 per acre (Zoning § 22.2.a) or note 5 of Art. VIII (same as major)? Does a minor with 1-acre lots on septic in Tier III/IV conflict with the tier map?
4. Realistic review time and fee for a minor plat today (current FY27 schedule), whether record plat review is charged separately, and who stamps the Health Department block and when.
5. Forest conservation: current thresholds and exemptions (the § 3.2K exemption), and fee in lieu per square foot.
6. Health Department: current perc/site evaluation fee, wet-season testing window, and test-pit witnessing rules; any extra rules for 4-5 septic lots in a row.
