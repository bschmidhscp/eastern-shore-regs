RESOLUTION NO. 04-2026
Appointment — Public Safety Pension Plan Board of Trustees — Stephen Brownhill and Colin Shaw

WHEREAS, the Plan and Trust were established by Resolution on April 16, 2002, by the Board of County
Commissioners of Cecil County for the benefit of the Cecil County’s Public Safety Employees; and

WHEREAS, the purpose of the Plan is to attract and retain the highest quality public safety employees
and to provide the Plan’s participants with retirement benefits in accordance with the terms, conditions, and
provisions of the Plan, as set forth in the Plan document, the Trust Agreement, and applicable law; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Article 7 Administration of the Plan. Section 7.1 Board of Trustees, the County
Executive shall appoint the Board of Trustees as the administration of the Plan; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees has oversight and day-to-day administration authority of the Public
Safety Pension Plan; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees members shall be required to select, monitor, and diversify the assets
of the Pension Plan in accordance with the standards outlined in the Plan and Trust Agreement; and

WHEREAS, Article 7, Section 7.1 Board of Trustees, Subsection (a) (iii) specifies that the Board of
Trustees will consist of the Cecil County Sheriff or his or her designee; and

WHEREAS, Article 4, Section 412(b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County Executive
shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the County Council; and

WHEREAS, the County Executive has reappointed Stephen Brownhill and appointed Colin Shaw to terms
which expire December 31, 2028, to the Public Safety Pension Plan Board of Trustees.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND
confirms the reappointment of Stephen Brownhill and the appointment of Colin Shaw are confirmed to terms
which expire December 31, 2028, to the Public Safety Pension Plan Board of Trustees.

AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, that this

Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, Maryland.

Article 2 - Requirements for Participation

2.1 Who Must Participate.

(a) Every Covered Employee who becomes a Covered Employee on or after
July 1, 2002 must participate in the Plan, subject to all the provisions of the Plan from time to
time in effect.

(b) Every Covered Employee must complete, sign and file with the Board of

Trustees any and all additional forms or other instruments that may be required by the rules
adopted from time to time by the Board of Trustees.

2.2 Election’ to Participate.

(a) Each person who is a Covered Employee on April 15, 2002 or who
becomes a Covered Employee between April 15, 2002 and May 10, 2002. must elect either (i) to
cease participating in the State Plan and become a Participant or (ii) to continue to participate in
the State Plan. Such election must be made in the form and within the time limits established by
the County and is irrevocable. If any Covered Employee makes an election described in this
paragraph and subsequently separates from service as a Covered Employee or dies before July 1.
2002. such Covered Employee's election will be automatically revoked and such Covered
Employee will not be eligible to participate in the Plan.

(b) Upon the effective date of the election described in paragraph (a)(i), the
Participant will be eligible for benefits in accordance with the Plan and will no longer be eligible
for any benefits under the State Plan.

(c) Upon the withdrawal of a Participant from the State Plan on June 30, 2002
pursuant to an election made under paragraph (a)(i), the accumulated contributions of such
Participant and the amount of reserve in the State Plan from contributions of the County on
account of such Participant shall be transferred to the Trust Fund promptly. Upon such transfer,
such amounts shall be treated as employee and employer contributions, as applicable, for all
purposes under the Plan.

Every Covered Employee who elects to participate in the Plan in accordance with
Section 2.2 is a Participant as of the Effective Date. Every Covered Employee who becomes a

3.2. Normal Retirement Benefit.

(a) Each Participant accrues benefits at the rate of two percent (2%) of
Average Annual Compensation times each year or fractional year of Creditable Service, up to a
maximum of twenty-five (25) years.

(b) The annual Normal Retirement Benefit payable to a Participant on or after
his Normal Retirement Date is equal to his accrued benefit, calculated pursuant to paragraph (a).
as of the date of his separation from service as a Covered Employee.

(c) The maximum Normal Retirement payable to any Participant is fifty
percent (50%) of the Participant’s Average Annual Compensation.

review all information provided in the application and the written opinion provided by
the Medical Advisory Board and make a determination as to whether the applicant is
disabled. The determination of the Disability Review Board shall be communicated to the
Participant.
