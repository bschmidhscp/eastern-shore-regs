Appointment—Board of Appeals — Devon Perry and Michael Linkous WHEREAS, a local jurisdiction may establish by local law a planning commission with the powers and 

duties, pursuant to Section 2-101. Authority to establish, Subtitle 1. Organization and General Authority, Title 2. Planning Commission, Division |. Single-Jurisdiction Planning and Zoning, in the Land Use Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland; and 

WHEREAS, the Board of Appeals shall consist of five regular members and one alternate member, who must be residents of Cecil County; pursuant to Section 9-704 Board of Appeals, Subtitle 7. Cecil County, Title 9. Single-Jurisdiction Planning and Zoning, in the Land Use Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland; and 

WHEREAS, the Board of Appeals shall be appointed by the County Executive and approved by the County Council, pursuant to Section 298. Board of Appeals Established, Part II. Board of Appeals, Article XVI. Administrative Mechanisms, of<sup>theCecilCounty ZoningOrdinance;and</sup> 

WHEREAS, the power and duties of the Board of Appeals are to hear and decide appeals when it is alleged that there is an error ia any order, requirement, decision, or determination made by<sup>theZoning</sup> 

Administrator or any other administrative official in the enforcement of the zoning ordinance; and to hear and decide special exceptions as provided in the zoning ordinance; and to authorize upon application a variance from the provisions of the zoning ordinance; and 

WHEREAS, Article 4, Section 412 (b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County Executive shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the Council; 

WHEREAS, the County Executive has reappointed Devon Perry and Michael Linkous, to terms which will expire on August 15, 2029, to the Board of Appeals. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, 

MARYLAND, that the reappointments of Devon Perry and Michael Linkous are confirmed to terms which will expire on August 15, 2029, to the Board of Appeals. 

AND, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, that this 

Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, Maryland.
