Resolution No. 30-2026 

Appointment - Building Board of Appeals — Paul Keeley 

1 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 157-1 (B) of the Code of Cecil County, Maryland, the 2021 2. International Residential Code was adopted as an addition to the Building Code of Cecil County, 3. Maryland, and 

4 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section R112 of the 2021 International Residential Code, a Building 5 Board of Appeals is established to hear and decide appeals of orders, decisions, or determinations 6 made by the building officials relative to the application and interpretation of the Residential Code; 7 and 

8 WHEREAS, members of the Building Board of Appeals shall be qualified by experience and 9 training to pass judgment on matters pertaining to building construction and are not employees of 10 ~— Cecil County; and 11 WHEREAS, members shall serve for five years or until a successor has been appointed; and 12 WHEREAS, vacancies shall be filled for an unexpired term in the same manner in which the 13 original appointment occurred; and 14 WHEREAS, Article 4, Section 412 (b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County 15 Executive shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the 16 = Council; and 17 WHEREAS, the County Executive has appointed Paul Keeley who is replacing Adrian Aiken, to 18 aterm which will expire on April 21, 2031 to the Building Board of Appeals. 19 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, 20 MARYLAND, that the appointment of Paul Keeley who is replacing Adrian Aiken is confirmed to a 21 term which will expire on April 21, 2031, to the Building Board of Appeals. 22 AND, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, 23 that this Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, 24 ~~ Maryland.
