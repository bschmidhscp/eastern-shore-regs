WHEREAS, the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act, WIOA, Public Law 113-128, was adopted by Congress to reform and strengthen the workforce investment system of the Nation, to put Americans back to work, and make the United States more competitive in the twenty-first century; and WHEREAS, the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act, Section 107 of HR 803, establishes a local workforce development board in each local area of a State to carry out the functions ofthis program; and 

WHEREAS, the local workforce development board is composed of a majority of business representatives in the local area, while not less than twenty per cent of the members shall be representatives of the workforce within the local area, who shall include representatives of labor organizations, community-based organizations, organizations that have demonstrated experience and expertise in addressing employment, training or education needs of eligible youth, education and training, governmental and economic and community development and elected officials; and 

WHEREAS, the local board, in partnership with the chief elected officials for the local area, shall develop and submit a local comprehensive plan to the Governor to provide programs that will improve employment and economic conditions within the locality; and 

WHEREAS, Cecil County and Harford County have been designated as the Susquehanna Regional Workforce Area by the Governor of Maryland; and 

WHEREAS, the Susquehanna Workforce Network, Inc. shall be governed by a Workforce Board and consist of a minimum of 51% membership who represent the private sector; and 

WHEREAS, the Workforce Board will provide policy guidance and oversight for the workforce development initiatives in the Susquehanna region. 

WHEREAS, the Workforce Board will be responsible for developing a strategy to continuously improve and strengthen the workforce development system through innovation in, and alignment and improvement of, employment, training, and education programs to promote economic growth; and 

WHEREAS, members of the Workforce Board from Cecil County shall be appointed by the County Executive of Cecil County, subject to the confirmation of the County Council of Cecil County; and 

WHEREAS, the County Executive has reappointed Sandra Edwards, and Andrew Meyer, and appointed Jeff Sellers, to terms which will expire on June 30, 2029, to the Susquehanna Workforce
