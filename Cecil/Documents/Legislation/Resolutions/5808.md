WHEREAS, the Local Management Board in Cecil County is known as the Human Services Agency; and 

WHEREAS, Article 4, Section 412 (b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County

WHEREAS, the County Executive has reappointed Arsalan Sheikh, to a term which expires on April 1, 

2031, to the Local Management Board. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, 

MARYLAND, that the reappointment Arsalan Sheikh is confirmed to a term which will expire on April 1, 2031 to 

the local Management Board. 

AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, that this 

Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, Maryland.
