# **CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF FINANCE FISCAL IMPACT NOTE** 

## **<u>SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION</u>** 

**SPONSOR** : Council President at the request of the County Executive 

**RESOLUTION NO. 03-2026 – Supplemental Appropriation – Fund Balance – Department of Public Works – Roads Division – Asphalt Overlay** 

**<u>SYNOPSIS:</u>** A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026, to move expenditure authority from the fund balance to the Department of Public Works – Roads Division, Asphalt Overlay account in the amount of $2,800,000 for the benefit of additional asphalt overlay of county roads. 

**<u>FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY:</u>** The approval of this resolution will reduce the fund balance by $2,800,000. 

## **<u>FISCAL ANALYSIS:</u>** 

The $2,800,000 in additional funding will enable Roads Division to resurface an additional +/- 10 miles of county roadways depending on bid prices.
