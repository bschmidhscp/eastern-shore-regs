# **COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2026-19** 

# **RESOLUTION NO.  56-2026** 

**Title of Resolution** : Operation Green Light for Veterans 

**Synopsis:** A Resolution to designate Cecil County as a Green Light for Veterans program participant and encourage businesses and residents to display a green light on November 1-22, 2026 to salute and honor Veterans in observance of Operation Green Light for Veterans. 

**Introduced by:** <u>Council President at the request of the County Executive</u> 

**Introduced and ordered posted on:  October 6, 2026** 

**Scheduled for Consideration on:  October 20, 2026** 

**By:** _________________________________ Council Manager 

Notice of time and title of Resolution having been posted by October 6, 2026 at the County Administrative Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd, Elkton, and consideration by the County Council of Cecil County having been scheduled on October 20, 2026 **<u>.</u>**

**WHEREAS,** Cecil County seeks to honor these individuals who have made countless sacrifices for freedom by placing themselves in harm’s way for the good of all; and

**WHEREAS,** studies indicate that 44-72 percent of service members experience high levels of stress during transition from military to civilian life; and 

**WHEREAS,** Active Military Service Members transitioning from military service are at a high risk for suicide during their first year after military service; and 

**WHEREAS,** the National Association of Counties encourages all counties, parishes and boroughs to recognize Operation Green Light for Veterans; and 

**WHEREAS,** Cecil County appreciates the sacrifices of our United States Military Personnel and 

believes specific recognition should be granted.
