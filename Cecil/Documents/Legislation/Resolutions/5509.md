8 WHEREAS, pursuant to Title 10 Human Services, Subtitle 201 of the Annotated Code of 9 Maryland, the Maryland Department of Aging was created as a principal executive department to 10 administer programs mandated by the federal government throughout the state through the Area 11 Agencies on Aging; and

WHEREAS, the Cecil County Commission on Aging oversees the provision of services to seniors as the advisory council to the Area Agency on Aging in Cecil County; and

WHEREAS, the Commission on Aging identifies, assesses, and advocates for the needs of the growing older population in Cecil County; encourages the development of services designed to address those needs; and supports the mission of the Senior Services and Community Transit of Cecil County of developing and coordinating community-based systems of services for all older adults in the County; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 412(b) of the Charter of Cecil County, the County Executive shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the Council; and 

WHEREAS, the County Executive has reappointed Denise Hill, Julianne Graham, Lisa Beeman, Jamie McCloskey, and Mark Hill to terms which expire on June 30, 2028 to the Commission on Aging.
