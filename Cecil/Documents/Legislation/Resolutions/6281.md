Title of Resolution: Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds — Administrative Office of the Courts Security Administration — Court Security Officer Support Grant Program — Circuit Court

## SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION 

SPONSOR: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

RESOLUTION NO. 50-2026 - Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds — Administrative Office of the Courts Security Administration — Court Security Officer Support Grant Program — Circuit Court 

SYNOPSIS: A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $173,155.32 to the Circuit Court — Court Security Officer Support Grant Program, to record a grant awarded through the Administrative Office of the Courts. Grant funds will be used to hire three (3) additional courtroom bailiffs. 

FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY: Net fiscal impact to the County will be $O (ZERO DOLLARS) for Fiscal 2027. 

FISCAL ANALYSIS: County Executive is revising the FY27 Circuit Court — Court Security Officer Support Grant Program revenue estimate for Cecil County to establish the following amount: the addition of $173,155.32 in grant revenue to the Court Security Officer Support Grant Program to provide for a grant awarded through the Administrative Office of the Courts Security Administration.
