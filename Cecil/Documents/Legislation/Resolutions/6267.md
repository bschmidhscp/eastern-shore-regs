LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2026-17 RESOLUTION NO. 47-2026 

Title of Resolution: Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds — Department of Community Services — Community Partnerships Division 

Synopsis: A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $100,000.00 to the Community Services Grants Fund — Department of Community Services, Community Partnerships Division, Fund 109, Department 523, to record a grant awarded through the Opioid Restitution Trust Fund. Grant funds will be used to provide trauma therapy support to children and families impacted by substance use disorder.
