Appointment — Economic Development Commission — John “Jack” Schammel 

WHEREAS, the Economic Development Commission was created to advance the general economic welfare 

- 2 of the people through the establishment of programs and activities that result in new economic activity, job 3. growth, and expansion of the local tax base, and that relate to manufacturing, distribution, research and 

- 4 development, corporate and regional offices, agriculture, and tourism; and to cooperate with and offer assistance 5 to existing industries and businesses in orderto facilitate economic expansion, and to promote and encourage the 

- 6 establishment and location of new industries and businesses in Cecil County; and to develop and implement 7 — workforce development initiatives intended to foster and develop gainful employment opportunities for all who are 8 nowor may hereafter be residents of Cecil County, pursuant to Chapter 16, Article 1, Section 16-1 Creation, in the 

   - Code of Cecil County; and 

WHEREAS, the Economic Development Commission is composed of not fewer than 23 nor more than 33 members who should reasonably represent the various sectors within Cecil County and shall be selected because of their known experience and interest in the economic development of Cecil County, pursuant to Chapter 16, Article 1, Section 16-2 Membership; and 

WHEREAS, members of the Commission shall serve for three years from the effective date of their appointment and are eligible for reappointment, pursuant to Chapter 16, Article 1, Section 16-3 Term of Office; and 

WHEREAS, the Economic Development Commission shall make an annual report to the County within 90 days of the end of the fiscal year that shall assess the performance of the Commission and staff and make recommendations for the improvement and advancement of the economic welfare of the people of Cecil County, pursuant to Chapter 16, Article 1, Section 16-5 Annual Report; and 

WHEREAS, Article 4, Section 412(b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County Executive shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the Council; and 

WHEREAS, the County Executive has appointed John “Jack” Schammel to replace Bernard DeMarinis to a term which expires on January 7, 2029, to the Economic Development Commission.
