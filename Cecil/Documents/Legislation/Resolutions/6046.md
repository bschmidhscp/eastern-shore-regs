RESOLUTION NO. 27-2026 

Appointments - Department of Social Services Board — David Rudolph, Dianne Fox, and Georgia Galicki 

1 WHEREAS, pursuant to the Human Services Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Title 3, 2. Local Departments of Social Services, Subtitle 5. Local Boards, Section 3-503, defines the duties and 3 functions of a Local Social Services Board as an advisory board to the local director for the local 4 application and implementation of State policies or procedures, which includes evaluating and making 5 recommendations to improve current services and develop new services as needed in the county; and 6 serving as the liaison with the local government authority and residents to communicate the 7 objectives, policies and programs of local social services and public assistance administration; and 8 WHEREAS, Article 4, Section 412(b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County 9 Executive shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the County 10 Council; and 11 WHEREAS, the County Executive has reappointed David Rudolph, Dianne Fox, and Georgia 12 Galicki, to terms which will expire on June 30, 2029 to the Department of Social Services Board. 13 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, 14 MARYLAND, that the reappointments of David Rudolph, Dianne Fox, and Georgia Galicki are confirmed 15 to terms which will expire on June 30, 2029, to the Department of Social Services Board. 16 AND, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND that 17 this Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, 18 Maryland.
