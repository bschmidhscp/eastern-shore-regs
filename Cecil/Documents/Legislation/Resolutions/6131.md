## COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2026-14 RESOLUTION NO. 36-2026 

Title of Resolution: Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds — Community Services — National Community Care Corps Funding 

Synopsis: A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $37,745.00 to the Community Services Grants Fund — Department of Community Services, Fund 109, Department 523, to record a grant awarded from the Department of Health and Human Services National Community Care Corps Program, administered through the Oasis Institute. Grant funds will be used to recruit, train and support volunteers providing assistance to older adults, caregivers and individuals living with disabilities served through the Department’s Aging and Disabilities Division. 

Introduced by: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced and ordered posted on: July 21, 2026 

Scheduled for Consideration on: August 4, 2026
