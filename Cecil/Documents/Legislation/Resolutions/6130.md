## COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2026-14 RESOLUTION NO. 35-2026 

Title of Resolution: Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds —- Community Services — Human Services Division — Housing Navigator Funding. 

Synopsis: A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $71,017.00 to the Community Services Grants Fund — Department of Community Services, Human Services Division, Fund 109, Department 533, to record a grant awarded from the Department of Housing and Urban Development, through the Maryland State Department of Housing and Community Development. Grant funds will be used to support a part-time Housing Navigator covering both Cecil and Harford Counties. 

Introduced by: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced and ordered posted on: July 21, 2026 

# Scheduled for Consideration on: August 4, 2026
