WHEREAS, the Cecil County Planning Commission was established and consists of six members plus an alternate who shall serve in the absence of a regular member; and a member of the County Council who serves as anex-officio, non-voting member; pursuant to Section 295; Article XVI, of the Cecil County Zoning Ordinance; and WHEREAS, each Cecil County Planning Commission member serves three-year terms and must be a resident of Cecil County; and

WHEREAS, the duties of the Cecil County Planning Commission are to review, evaluate, and approve or disapprove plans for subdivisions in accordance with the Zoning Ordinance and Subdivision Regulations; and to review and make recommendations to the Cecil County Council regarding proposed changes or amendments to the Comprehensive Plan, Chesapeake Bay Critical Area Program, Zoning Ordinance, Subdivision Regulations, Road 

Code, Master Water and Sewer Plan, acquisition and development of lands for open space or recreation purposes, historic sites or districts, land use or development arising from State or Federal programs or policies, Forest Conservation Regulations; and to review the Capital Improvement Program annually; and to make 

recommendations to the Board of Appeals regarding applications for special exceptions; and 

WHEREAS, Article 4, Section 412(b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County Executive shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the County Council; and 

WHEREAS, the County Executive has reappointed Rob Carey and Gary Rasnake to terms which expire on August 17, 2029 to the Cecil County Planning Commission. 

- NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, 

- that the reappointment of Rob Carey and Gary Rasnake are confirmed to terms which expire on August 17, 2029, to the Cecil County Planning Commission. 

24 AND, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND that this 25 Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, Maryland.
