# **CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF FINANCE FISCAL IMPACT NOTE** 

## **<u>SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION</u>** 

**SPONSOR** : Council President at the request of the County Executive 

**RESOLUTION NO.  10-2026 – Supplemental Appropriation – Wastewater Enterprise Fund – Department of Public Works – Wastewater Services – Washington Street Force Main Repair** 

**<u>SYNOPSIS:</u>** A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriations Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the Fiscal Year ending June 30, 2026, to move expenditure authority from the Wastewater Enterprise Fund balance to the Department of Public Works – Wastewater Services, in the amount of $200,000 for expenses related to the emergency repair of the Washington Street Force Main. 

**<u>FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY:</u>** The approval of this Resolution will reduce the Wastewater Services Fund balance by $200,000. There is a $0 impact on the County General Fund. 

**<u>FISCAL ANALYSIS:</u>** County Executive is revising the Fiscal Year 2026 revenue estimate for Cecil County to increase the Fund Balance for the Department of Public Works – Wastewater Services budget by $200,000 in order to allow the following appropriation within the Wastewater Enterprise Fund. There is no fiscal impact on the General Fund.
