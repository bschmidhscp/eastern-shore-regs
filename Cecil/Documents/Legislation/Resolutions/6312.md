## **COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2026-19 RESOLUTION NO.  55-2026** 

**Title of Resolution** :  Supplemental Appropriation – Grant Funds – Maryland Rural Health Transformation Program Fund – Departments of Emergency Services and Community Services 

**Synopsis:** A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $1,144,170.00, with $399,170.00 to be allocated to the Department of Emergency Services Grants Fund – Mobile Integrated Healthcare Program – Fund 198; $475,000.00 to be allocated to the Community Services Grants Fund – Cecil Transit Division – Fund 109, Department 522; and, $270,000.00 to be allocated to the Community Services Grants Fund – Human Services Division – Fund 109, Department 532, to record a Rural Health Transformation Program grant awarded by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) through the Maryland Department of Health and Cecil County Health Department.  Grant funds will be used to support the Mobile Integrated Healthcare Program, increase transportation access to healthcare and address food insecurity within Cecil County, respectively. 

**Introduced by:** <u>Council President at the request of the County Executive</u> 

**Introduced and ordered posted on:  October 6, 2026** 

**Scheduled for Consideration on: October 20, 2026** 

**By:** Council Manager 

Notice of time and title of Resolution having been posted by October 6, 2026 at the County Administrative Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd, Elkton, and consideration by the County Council of Cecil County having been scheduled on October 20, 2026.
