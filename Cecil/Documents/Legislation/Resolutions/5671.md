Supplemental Appropriation — General Fund Balance — State of Maryland — State Department of Assessments and Taxation WHEREAS, in accordance with Section 505 of the Cecil County Charter, on April 1, 2025, the County 

Executive submitted a proposed budget to the County Council for Fiscal Year 2026; and 

WHEREAS, in accordance with Section 506 of the Cecil County Charter, the County Council held public hearings on the proposed budget submitted by the County Executive for Fiscal Year 2026; and 

WHEREAS, in accordance with Section 507(b) of the Cecil County Charter, on June 3, 2025, the Budget for Cecil County was adopted for Fiscal Year 2026; and 

WHEREAS, last year the State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT) identified an administrative discrepancy in previous reimbursement bills to counties during Fiscal Year 2026 (See Exhibits A, B, 

&C); and 

WHEREAS, three prior end-of-year deficits were inadvertently applied as credits to subsequent billing 

cycles rather than being issued as additional charges; and 

WHEREAS, this resulted in a cumulative underbilling for Cecil County; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Chapter 604 of the Laws of the State of Maryland (Budget Reconciliation and 

Financing Act of 2025) Cecil County will reimburse the State of Maryland for 90% of the costs of administering certain programs within SDAT; and 

WHEREAS, such appropriation is necessary to provide $275,495 to account 00119200-512000 of the County Executive’s Fiscal Year 2026 assigned fund balance for the General Fund for the administrative discrepancy; and 

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland for Fiscal Year 2026 is hereby amended for the supplemental appropriation and by amending the current budget to appropriate $275,495 to account 00119200-512000 of additional expenditures in the General Fund to reimburse SDAT. 

AND, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, that this 

24 

Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, Maryland.
