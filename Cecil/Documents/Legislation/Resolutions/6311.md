**Appointment - Planning Commission – Christopher Wiggins** 

**WHEREAS,** a local jurisdiction may establish by local law a Planning Commission with the powers and 

- 2 duties set forth in this division, pursuant to Section 2-101 in the Land Use Article of the Annotated Code of 3 Maryland; and 

- 5 

- 6 

**WHEREAS,** the Planning Commission was established and consists of six members plus an alternate who shall serve in the absence of a regular member; and a member of the County Council who serves as an ex-officio, non-voting member; pursuant to Section 295; Article XVI, of the Cecil County Zoning Ordinance; and 

**WHEREAS,** each Planning Commission member serves three-year terms and must be a resident of Cecil County; and 

**WHEREAS,** the duties of the Planning Commission are to review, evaluate, and approve or disapprove plans for subdivisions in accordance with the Zoning Ordinance and Subdivision Regulations; and to review and make recommendations to the County Council regarding proposed changes or amendments to the 

Comprehensive Plan, Chesapeake Bay Critical Area Program, Zoning Ordinance, Subdivision regulations, Road 

Code, Master Water and Sewer Plan, acquisition and development of lands for open space or recreation 

purposes, historic sites or districts, land use or development arising from State or Federal programs or policies, 

- 15 

Forest Conservation Regulations; and to review the Capital Improvement Program annually; and to make 

recommendations to the Board of Appeals regarding applications for special exceptions; and 

**WHEREAS,** Article 4, Section 412(b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County Executive shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the County Council; and 

**WHEREAS,** the County Executive has appointed Christopher Wiggins who is replacing Christopher Bannon as the alternate to a term which expires on August 17, 2027 to the Planning Commission. 

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND,** 

- 23 

that the appointment of Christopher Wiggins who is replacing Christopher Bannon as the alternate is confirmed to a term which expires on August 17, 2027, to the Planning Commission. 

   - **AND, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND** that this 

- 25 Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, Maryland.
