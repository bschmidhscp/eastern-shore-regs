Appointment - Local Management Board — Laura Hannan 1 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 8-301, Title 8, Subtitle 3, of the Human Services Article of the 2 Annotated Code of Maryland, each county shall establish and maintain a local management board to 3. ensure the implementation of a local interagency service delivery system for children, youth, and families; 4 and 

5 WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 8-302, a local management board may be composed of public and 6 private community representatives who share the responsibility for implementing a community-based, 7 _ interagency, family-focused service delivery system for children, youth, and families; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 8-303, a local management board shall strengthen the decisionmaking capacity at the local level; design and implement strategies that achieve clearly defined results for children, youth, and families; maintain standards of accountability for locally agreed upon results; influence the allocation of resources across the system; build local partnerships to coordinate services; and create an effective system of services, supports, and opportunities that improve outcomes for all; WHEREAS, the Local Management Board in Cecil County is known as the Human Services Agency; 

and 

WHEREAS, Article 4, Section 412 (b) of the Charter of Cecil County provides that the County Executive shall appoint all members of boards and commissions, subject to confirmation by the Council; and WHEREAS, the County Executive has appointed Laura Hannan, to a term which expires on June 17, 2031, to the Local Management Board. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, 

MARYLAND, that the appointment of Laura Hannan is confirmed to a term which will expire on June 17, 2031 to the Local Management Board. 

AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, that this 

23 Resolution shall take effect on the date of its passage by the County Council of Cecil County, Maryland.
