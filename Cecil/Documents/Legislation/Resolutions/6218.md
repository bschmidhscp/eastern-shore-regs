FISCAL IMPACT NOTE 

# SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION 

SPONSOR: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

RESOLUTION NO. 44-2026 — Approval —- Axon Equipment Enhancement Package for Cameras, Tasers, VR Training, License Plate Readers, Drone Support, etc. — Multiple Year Contract 

SYNOPSIS: A Resolution to approve a ten (10) year agreement between the County on behalf of the Cecil County Sheriff's Office, Detention Center, State’s Attorney, Circuit Court, and Animal Services and Axon Enterprise, Inc. for Camera’s, Tasers, VR, Drone, etc. 

FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY: There is no additional fiscal impact in FY2027 as the resolution is supported by the approved budget for FY2027 for payments required during the first year of the agreements. Remaining payment requirements will be incorporated into future budgets for FY2028 through FY2036. 

#### FISCAL ANALYSIS: 

Cecil County Sheriff’s Office previously contracted with Upstate Wholesale and Rok for provision of body-warn cameras and related software and peripheral equipment. The County has experienced functionality issues with current equipment and does not wish to extend this contract beyond its current maturity on October 31, 2026. Multiple County agencies and departments utilize these camera systems and related devices which are a critical component of law enforcement. As solutions were researched and all impacted departments were consulted, Axon Entereprise, Inc. emerged as the best choice to resolve ongoing video concerns. Axon is an existing provider of taser and other services to the County, so a global review of all Axon services and equipment was completed. This resulted in proposal of new agreements for five agencies/departments to have Axon provide body cameras, tasers, license plate readers, VR training, and drone support software. 

The scope of services or equipment required by each department is segregated into five separate agreements with a total cost of $13,761,395.67 over a term of ten years. Required payments for FY2027 will be funded via the county’s approved budget for the first year. Remaining payments will be incorporated into future budgets. 

This obligation is more than $20,000 and includes appropriations for future fiscal years and, as such, requires County Council approval.
