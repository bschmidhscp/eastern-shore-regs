# **CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF FINANCE FISCAL IMPACT NOTE** 

## **<u>SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION</u>** 

**SPONSOR** : Council President at the request of the County Executive 

**RESOLUTION NO.  11-2026 – Supplemental Appropriation – General Fund Balance – Department of Public Works – Roads Division - Snow Removal** 

**<u>SYNOPSIS:</u>** A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriations Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the Fiscal Year ending June 30, 2026, to move expenditure authority from the General Fund Balance to the Department of Public Works – Roads Division, in the amount of $750,000 for expenses related to the removal of snow and ice from County roadways. 

**<u>FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY:</u>** The approval of this resolution will reduce the fund balance by $750,000. 

**<u>FISCAL ANALYSIS:</u>** Roads Division Fiscal Year 2026 budget provided $300,000 in the Snow Removal account for the removal of snow and ice from County roadways. There have been multiple significant winter weather events which have caused the budgeted amount to be exceeded. Therefore, appropriation is necessary to provide $750,000 of the County Executive’s Fiscal Year 2026 assigned fund balance for the General Fund for expenditures.
