# COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2022-09 

BILL NO. 2022-06 As Amended 

Title of Bill: Residential Noise Ordinance 

Synopsis: An Act to add new Chapter 250, Noise in Residential Areas, of the Code of Cecil County; to prohibit certain noises in certain areas during specified hours; to establish certain exemptions to this prohibition; to establish fines for violating this prohibition; and generally relating to noise. 

Introduced by: Council President on behalf of the Council 

Introduced and order posted on: April 19, 2022 

Public hearing scheduled on: May 17, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. 

Consideration scheduled on: June 7, 2022
