# COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2022-04 

BILL NO. 2022-01 

Title of Bill: Supplemental Appropriation — Fund Balance — Health Department — Roof Replacement 

Synopsis: A Bill to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2022, to appropriate expenditure authority within the Health Department Roof Capital Projects Budget in the amount of $30,000 from the fund balance. 

Introduced by: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced and order posted on: February 15, 2022 

Public hearing scheduled on: March 15, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. 

Consideration scheduled on: April 5, 2022
