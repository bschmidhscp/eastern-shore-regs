##### BILL NO. 2024-09 

Title of Bill: Amendment — Electrical Standards 

Synopsis: A Bill to amend Chapter 191, Electrical Standards, of the Code of Cecil County by amending local electrician licensing requirements in accordance with State law; amending the references from The Department of Permits & Inspections to The Department of Land Use and Development Services; and amending references from the Board of Electrical Examiners to the Board of Electricians. 

Introduced By: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced and order posted on: February 20, 2024 

Public hearing scheduled on: March 19, 2024 at 7:00 pm

WHEREAS, Title 6 of Business Occupation and Professions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland establishes a licensing program for individuals who provide or assist providing electrical services, in order to safeguard the life, health, property and public welfare of the citizens of the state; and
