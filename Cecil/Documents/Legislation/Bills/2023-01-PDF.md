SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION 

SPONSOR: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

BILL NO. 2023 -01- Supplemental Appropriation — Fund Balance — State’s Attorney’s Offices — Renovation Completion 

SYNOPSIS: A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2023, to provide the State’s Attorney's Office budget within the General Fund expenditure appropriation authority to authorize $250,000 to be expended for completion of the State’s Attorney's Office Renovation. 

FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY: The approval of this bill will have a net fiscal impact on the fund balance of $250,000. 

## FISCAL ANALYSIS: 

This bill will reduce the general fund balance by $250,000 and increase the SAO CIP Budget by $250,000.
