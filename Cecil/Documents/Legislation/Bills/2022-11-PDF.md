BILL NO. 2022-11 As Amended 

Title of Bill: Taxation — Tax Credit — Sworn Officers 

Synopsis: A Bill to implement a Cecil County Sworn Officers Tax Credit Program to establish a property tax credit for certain Sworn Officers, the criteria for the property tax credit created; and generally relating to property tax discounts and credits. 

Introduced by: Council President on behalf of County Executive 

Introduced and order posted on: July 26, 2022 

Public hearing scheduled on: August 16, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. 

Consideration scheduled on: September 6, 2022 

By: SavmonCouncil ManagerViarac We
