## TENTH AMENDMENT TO THE 

# CECIL COUNTY PENSION PLAN FOR PUBLIC SAFETY EMPLOYEES 

This TENTH AMENDMENT to The Cecil County Pension Plan for Public Safety Employees is made by Cecil County, Maryland (the “County”). The County maintains The Cecil County Pension Plan for Public Safety Employees, effective as of July 1, 2002, as amended and restated effective December 31, 2015, and as amended from time to time (the “Plan”). 

WHEREAS, the County, pursuant to the authority granted under Section 11.1 of the Plan, wishes to amend certain Plan provisions as more particularly set forth herein. All Plan amendments shall be effective on the date on which the County Council bill approving this Tenth Amendment becomes effective, unless otherwise set forth herein. 

NOW, THEREFORE, the Plan is amended as follows, effective as set forth herein: 

Article 13 — Deferred Retirement Option Plan 

13.1 Purpose and Duration 

(a) A deferred retirement option plan (“DROP”) program was created for Covered Employees under the Plan effective , _, to offer them an alternative method of accruing benefits in the Plan. The DROP provision is effective under this Plan on » __and shall sunset on [five years past effective date], as further described in Section 13.10 below. A Covered Employee’s election to participate in the DROP program shall not affect the Covered Employee’s active employment status with the County. As a result, the Covered Employee’s eligibility for County benefits shall be determined based on the Covered Employee’s active employment status. 

(b) The DROP program is intended to add flexibility to the Plan for Covered Employees. It provides a Covered Employee who elects to participate in the DROP program access to a lump sum benefit when the Covered Employee retires, in addition to the Covered Employee’s monthly annuity benefit payment. A Covered Employee may retire for all purposes of the Plan and defer receipt of retirement benefits into a DROP Account while continuing employment with the County as a Full-time public safety employee. The DROP program is intended to be cost neutral to the Plan. 

13.2 Eligibility, Duration of DROP Participation, Selection of Survivor Option and Waiver 

(a) Any Full-time Covered Employee who is eligible for, but has not yet commenced, a Normal Retirement Benefit is eligible to participate in the DROP program. 

(b) Before a Covered Employee may participate in the DROP program, the Covered Employee shall voluntarily and irrevocably: 

(i) designate a participation period ofno more than sixty (60) consecutive months;

<!-- page 6 -->
(ii) make an election as to how to receive the Covered Employee’s Normal Retirement Benefit from the Plan; 

(iii) designate a Beneficiary to receive amounts payable upon the Covered Employee's death, as applicable; 

(iv) cease accruing benefits under the Plan (other than through the DROP Account) starting on the day the Covered Employee enters the DROP program; 

(v) elect to have DROP benefits credited to a DROP Account as provided in this Article 13; 

(vi) elect to accrue benefits solely under the terms of this Article 13 starting on the day the Covered Employee enters the DROP program; 

(vii) receive benefits from the Plan when the Covered Employee leaves the DROP program and retires, as provided in this Plan; 

(viii) agree to leave County employment as a Full-time public safety employee on or before the end of the Covered Employee's designated DROP participation period; and 

(ix) waive the Covered Employee's right to assert a claim or bring an action against the County or the Plan, based on age discrimination or any other employment discrimination law, arising out of the Covered Employee's participation in the DROP program or the requirement that the Covered Employee terminate employment at the end of the Covered Employee's DROP period. 

13.3. Termination of DROP Participation 

(a) A Covered Employee who is participating in the DROP program may leave the DROP program at any time before the end of the Covered Employee's designated DROP participation period by voluntarily terminating County employment as a Full-time public safety employee. 

(b) The Plan will stop the Covered Employee's participation in the DROP program when any of the following events occurs: 

(i) the Covered Employee's designated DROP participation period ends; 

(ii) the Covered Employee's County employment as a Full-time public safety employee is terminated for cause; but, if the termination for cause is reversed, the Covered Employee’s DROP Account will be credited with all contributions the Covered Employee would have received during the time between the termination and the reversal under Section 13.4(c) but for the termination, the Covered Employee's participation will be reinstated for the remainder of the Covered Employee's designated DROP

## 13.4 DROP Benefits and Accounts 

(a) ADROP Account is a "nominal" account established within the Plan on behalf of each DROP participant. All benefits accrued by a Covered Employee under this Article 13 are accounted for in the Covered Employee's DROP Account. A DROP participant does not have a claim on the Plan's assets with respect to the Covered Employee's DROP Account, and the Plan will not set aside any assets for any DROP participant that are separate from other Plan assets. 

(b) All amounts credited to a Covered Employee's DROP Account are fully vested. 

(c) A Covered Employee's DROP Account will be credited with: 

(i) An amount, credited monthly, which is calculated in the same manner as the Covered Employee's monthly annuity benefit payment. This amount will increase each year by a cost-of-living adjustment (“COLA”) to be determined by the County each budget year. 

(ii) An amount, credited annually, representing the Covered Employee’s monthly annuity benefit payments. 

(iii) Interest on the above amounts, equal to four percent (4%) annually compounded monthly, unless and until the Board of Trustees approves a different percentage.

13.6 Designation of Beneficiary 

(a) Before entering the DROP program, the Covered Employee shall designate a Beneficiary for the Covered Employee's DROP Account. The Covered Employee may change this Beneficiary designation at any time before the amounts in the Covered Employee's DROP Account are fully distributed, and before any joint and survivor annuity payments begin. 

(b) If the Covered Employee's DROP Account Beneficiary dies before the Covered Employee does, and the Covered Employee then dies before designating a new Beneficiary, the Plan will distribute all amounts in the Covered Employee's DROP Account to the Covered Employee's estate. 

13.7 Payment of Benefits 

(a) When a Covered Employee simultaneously leaves the DROP program and retires, the Covered Employee will: 

(i) begin receiving the Covered Employee's monthly annuity benefit payment; and (ii) be entitled to receive the amounts credited to the Covered Employee's DROP Account. 

(b) A Covered Employee who leaves the DROP program shall elect, before the Covered Employee’s retirement date, to receive the Covered Employee’s entire DROP Account in a single lump sum distribution to be paid within sixty (60) days of the Covered Employee’s retirement date. Section 13.8 Compliance with Applicable Provisions of the Internal Revenue Code 

(a) The DROP program is not intended to jeopardize the Plan's tax-qualified status under the Internal Revenue Service's rules and regulations. The Administrator may adopt rules and regulations to assure that the DROP program complies with applicable federal laws and regulations. 

(b) Despite any other provision in this Plan, benefits provided under this Article are subject to the requirements of the Internal Revenue Code and regulations issued under the Code for the Plan to remain a tax-qualified retirement plan, including the following: 

(i) The limitations of Internal Revenue Code Section 415 relating to the amount of benefits that may be paid. 

(ii) The limitations of Internal Revenue Code Section 401(a)(17) relating to the amount of compensation that can be taken into account for benefit accrual. 

(iii) The limitations of Internal Revenue Code Section 401(a)(9) relating to the time that benefit payments must begin. 

(iv) The rules of Internal Revenue Code Section 401(a)(31) relating to the rollover of benefits. 

(v) The limitations of Internal Revenue Code Section 401(a)(25) relating to "definitely determinable" benefits. 

Section 13.9 Employment Status during DROP Participation 

A Covered Employee who elects to participate in the DROP program has all ofthe rights, privileges, and benefits, and is subject to all other terms and conditions of active employment, except as provided in this Article 13.
