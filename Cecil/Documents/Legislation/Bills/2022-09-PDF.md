COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2022-14 

BILL NO. 2022-09 As Amended 

Title of Bill: Fire Companies Volunteer — Emergency Services Expenses - Reimbursement 

Synopsis: An Act to add to Chapter 49, Fire Companies, Volunteer, of the Code of Cecil County; to include authorization for the member volunteer fire and fire and ambulance companiesofthe Cecil County Firemen’s Association to obtain reimbursement of certain costs to render services during the normal course of emergency service and/or rescue operations. 

Introduced by: Councilman Coutz 

Introduced and order posted on: June 21, 2022 

Public hearing scheduled on: July 26, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. 

Consideration scheduled on: August 2, 2022
