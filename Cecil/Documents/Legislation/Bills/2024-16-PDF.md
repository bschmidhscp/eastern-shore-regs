## COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2024-14 

BILL NO. 2024-16 AS AMENDED 

Title of Bill: Supplemental Appropriation — Amendment to the Fiscal Year 2025 — Fiscal Year 2029 Capital Improvement Program — Extend Water Service to Wilson Road. 

Synopsis: A Bill to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriations Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2025, to add the Extend Water Service to Wilson Road project, to the Fiscal Year 2025 Capital Budget and to record private funding from Southern States LLC for the design and construction of the project in the amount of $4,300,000. This project will extend public water service to Wilson Road to serve homes in the vicinity of the former Southern States facility having high levels of nitrates in the water wells. Southern States has agreed to fund design and construction costs, meaning that there will be no impact on the County’s fund Balance. 

Introduced by: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced and ordered posted on: August 6, 2024 

Public hearing scheduled on: September 3, 2024 at 7:00 pm 

Consideration scheduled on: September 17, 2024 

By: (Oy Council Manager 

### PUBLIC HEARING 

Notice of time and place of public hearing and title of Bill having been posted by August 6, 2024 at the County Administration Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd., Elkton and having been published according to the Charter on August 21, 2024 and August 28, 2024, a public hearing was held on September 3, 2024 and concluded on September 17,2024.
