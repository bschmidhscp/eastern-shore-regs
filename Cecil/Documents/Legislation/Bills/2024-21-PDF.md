COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND LEGISLATIVE SESSION DAY 2024-16 

BILL NO. 2024-21 

Title of Bill: Establishment — Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund 

Synopsis: A Bill to add a new Section 45-4, the Cecil County Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund, to Chapter 45, Finance and Taxation, of the Cecil County Code, as amended; to create a Fund for receipt of revenue from the State of Maryland pursuant to the Annotated Code of Maryland Acholic Beverages Article §1-322 and used for purposes outlined therein.; and generally related to finance and taxation. 

Introduced by: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced, read first time and ordered posted on: September 3, 2024 

Public hearing scheduled for: October 1, 2024 at: 7:00 p.m. 

Consideration scheduled on: October 15, 2024 

Council Manager 

# Public Hearing 

Notice of time and place of public hearing and title of Bill have been posted by September 3, 2024 at the County Administration Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd., Elkton and having been published according to the Charter on September 18, 2024 and September 25, 2024, a public hearing was held on October 1, 2024, and concluded on October 1, 2024.
