COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2024-02 

BILL NO. 2024-01 

Title of Bill: Establishment — Commission on Veterans Affairs 

Synopsis: To establish a Cecil County Commission on Veteran Affairs for the purpose of advancing the general welfare of our veterans through the advocacy and advisement of our veteran programs at the local, State and Federal level. These activities will help promote the health and wellness of our veterans as well as provide education about programs, resources, and opportunities for economic assistance to our veterans and their spouses. The Commission will help promote employment opportunities and encourage local businesses to hire and retain veterans and to educate businesses in workforce development initiatives intended to foster and develop gainful employment opportunities for all veterans of Cecil County. 

Introduced by: Council President Gregory, Vice President Meffley, Councilwoman Hamilton and Councilwoman Culberson 

Introduced and order posted on: January 16, 2024, postponed to January 23, 2024 

Public hearing scheduled on: February 6, 2024 at 7:00 p.m. 

Consideration scheduled on: February 20, 2024
