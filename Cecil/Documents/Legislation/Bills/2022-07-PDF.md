## SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION 

SPONSOR: Council President Meffley at the request of the County Executive 

BILL NO. 2022-07 — Supplemental Appropriation — Fund Balance — Courthouse — Parking Lot Capital Project 

SYNOPSIS: A Bill to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2022, to move expenditure authority from the fund balance to the Courthouse Parking Lot Capital Projects Budget in the amount of $120,000. The additional funding is needed due to increased constructions costs. 

FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY: The approval of this bill will have a net fiscal impact on the fund balance of $120,000. 

#### FISCAL ANALYSIS: 

The FY2022 Approved Capital Improvement Program (CIP) for the Courthouse Parking Lot was submitted in March 2021, and since then, constructions costs have risen and the additional funds are required to complete the project.
