# COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND 

LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2025-15 

BILL NO. 2025-09 

Title of Bill; Amendment — Cecil County Code — Chapter A385 — Purchase of Development Rights Program and Fund. 

Synopsis: An Act to amend the Cecil County Code, Chapter A385, Purchase of Development Rights Program and Fund — by adding a new Section C to Section A385-2, Funding, for the purpose of developing a new funding source for the County Purchase of Development Rights Program and County Purchase of Development Rights Fund, for the purpose of providing an additional funding source to assist landowners wishing to retain their properties in agricultural use. 

Introduced by: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced and ordered posted on: August 5, 2025 

Public Hearing Scheduled on: August 19, 2025 at 7:00 p.m. 

Consideration scheduled on: September2, 2025

WHEREAS, Cecil County Government believes it to be in the best interest of Cecil County to encourage the preservation and protection of agricultural land and implement and effect local government planning activities aimed at preserving agricultural land; and 

WHEREAS, Cecil County Government desires to amend the Cecil County Code, Chapter A385, Purchase of Development Rights and Fund, for the purpose of developing a new funding source for the County’s Purchase of Development Rights Program and County Purchase of Development Rights fund, for the purpose of providing an additional funding source to assist landowners wishing to retain their properties in agricultural use. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND, that the proposed amendment to the Cecil County Code, Chapter A385, Purchase of Development Rights Program and Fund, Section A385-2, Funding, by adding a new Section C, is hereby adopted as follows: 

- A. The Purchase of Development Rights Fund shall receive its funding from the County's recordation tax and from optional payments made in lieu of open space required in the Northern Agricultural Residential (NAR) and Southern Agricultural Residential (SAR) Zoning Districts and other sources of revenue streams as deemed appropriate. 

- B. The payment made in lieu of open space shall be equal to the appraised value of the acreage that is required to be provided in the NAR and SAR fifteen-percent common open space standards.
