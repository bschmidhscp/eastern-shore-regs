WHEREAS, Pursuant to Chapter 337. Taxation, Article X, Tax Credit for Sworn Officers, Section 337-50, Tax Credit for Sworn Officers, in order to amend the criteria of tax credits for certain individuals, in Cecil County, Maryland; and 

WHEREAS, the Annotated Code of Maryland, Section 9-260, Tax Property Article, authorizes the governing body of a county to grant, by law, a property tax credit against the county property tax imposed on the dwelling of an eligible individual; and 

WHEREAS, the property tax credit allowed under the Annotated Code of Maryland, Section 9-260, Tax Property Article may not exceed $2500 per dwelling; or the amount of property tax imposed on the dwelling; and 

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Annotated Code of Maryland, Section 9-260, Tax 

Property Article, the County Council may provide, by law, the amount of the credit under this section, the duration of the credit, additional eligibility requirements for sworn officers to qualify for the credit, procedures for the application and uniform processing of requests for the credit, and any other provisions necessary to carry out this section; and
