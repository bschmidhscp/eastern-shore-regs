# **CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF FINANCE FISCAL IMPACT NOTE** 

**<u>SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION</u>** 

**SPONSOR** : Council President at the request of the County Executive 

## **BILL NO. 2024-22 – Supplemental Appropriation – Amendment to the Fiscal Year 2025 – Fiscal Year 2029 Capital Improvement Program – Child Advocacy Center** 

**SYNOPSIS:** A Bill to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriations Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2025, to add the Renovate Child Advocacy Center project to the Fiscal Year 2025 Capital Budget and to record Community Development Block Grant funding for the design and construction of the project in the amount of $672,000. 

**FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY:** The approval of this bill will have no impact on the fund balance. 

## **FISCAL ANALYSIS:** 

This project will renovate the existing Child Advocacy Center building to convert existing front and rear porch space to provide additional interview and therapy rooms totaling an estimated 800 square feet. The roof of the existing building will be replaced, and a backup generator installed to power the entire building. The Department of Community Services has secured Community Development Block Grant funding for the project, meaning that there will be no impact on the County’s fund balance.
