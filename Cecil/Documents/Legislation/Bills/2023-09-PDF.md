# SUMMARY OF LEGISLATION 

SPONSOR: Council President at the request of the County Executive 

BILL NO. 2023-09 — Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds to Capital Projects — Construct Elkton West Sanitary Sewer Subdistrict 

SYNOPSIS: A Bill to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2024, to record $3,550,101 in grant funding from the Maryland Department of the Environment for the Construct Elkton West Sanitary Sewer Subdistrict capital project. 

FISCAL IMPACT SUMMARY: The approval of this bill will have a net fiscal impact on the fund balance of zero, as the grant funding will have offsetting expenses. 

FISCAL ANALYSIS: 

This bill will record $3,550,101 in state grant funding and increase the Construct Elkton West Sanitary Sewer Subdistrict capital project budget by a corresponding amount. The additional funding is needed to allow the project to be completed.
