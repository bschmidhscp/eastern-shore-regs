# COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY, MARYLAND 

## LEGISLATIVE SESSION 2023-08 

BILL NO. 2023-07 

Title of Bill: Amend - Critical Area Maps Program — Summary Draft Maps 

Synopsis: A Bill to adopt the Critical Area Maps Program by approving all elements of the summary draft map including, the shoreline and landward boundary of tidal wetlands, the digitally generated and georeferenced Critical Area 1,000-foot boundary line; and Critical Area classifications of all areas newly included in the Critical Area. 

Introduced by: Council President on behalf of the County Executive 

Introduced, read first time and order posted on: April 18, 2023 

Public Hearing scheduled on: May 9, 2023 at 7:00 pm 

Consideration scheduled on: May 16, 2023 

ouncil Manager 

Notice of time and place of public hearing and title of Bill have been posted by April 18, 2023 at the County Administration Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd., Elkton and having been published accordingto the Charter on April 19, 2023 and April 26,2023 a public hearing was held on May 9, 2023, and concluded on May 9, 2023. 

Council Manager
