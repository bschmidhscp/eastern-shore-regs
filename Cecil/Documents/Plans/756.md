# A. **INSTUCTIONS AND CONDITIONS OF ACCEPTANCE** 

- a. DEMO PERMITS WITH NO REBUILD/REPLACEMENT 

   - i. Sewer line must be dug up and capped at the property line. 

   - ii. Once the line has been dug up and capped at the property line the property owner or contractor must notify the Cecil County Wastewater Division at 410-996-6260 for an inspection prior to covering up the pipe. 

      1. The location must be marked for future use. 

iii. Wastewater staff will complete inspection and return paperwork to the wastewater office to be processed thru Hansen. 

- b. DEMO PERMITS WITH REBUILD/REPLACEMENT 

   - i. Sewer line must be Cut off and capped at a convenient location on the property. 

      1. The line cannot be capped under the structure to be demolished. 

   - ii. Once line has been cut off and capped the property owner or contractor must notify the Cecil County Wastewater Division at 410-996-6260 for an inspection prior to covering the pipe. 

      1. The location must be marked for future use. 

   - iii. Wastewater staff will complete inspection and return paperwork to the wastewater office to be processed thru Hansen. 

- c. If no clean out is available at the property line, one will be required to be installed prior to the issuing the permit for use and occupancy of the new structure. 

# B. **SEWER BILLING** (SELECT AN OPTION) 

- a. OPTION 1 

   - i. Property owner can choose to continue paying the Quarterly Sewer billing and keep sewer allocations for future use, or 

- b. OPTION 2 

   - i. Choose to be removed from Quarterly Sewer Billing and relinquish all rights to sewer allocation, Major Facility Fee, or Benefit Assessment paid.  Once removed from the Quarterly Sewer billing any future sewer service to the property will be treated as NEW, and will have to go through the process of requesting sewer allocation and paying any fees in place at that time.
