# NOTES: 

1. EXCEPT AS NOTED. MANHOLE TAPERS. RISERS. AND BASES SHALL BE FURNISHED IN STRICT ACCORDANCE WITH ASTM DESIGNATION C478 (LATEST) FOR PRECAST REINFORCED CONCRETE MANHOLE SECTIONS. 

2. WHEN TYING INTO AN EXISTING MANHOLE. RECHANNEL GENCH AS PER COUNTY STANDARDS. 

3. PROVIDE CARBON ODOR CONTROL 01SH. 

4. INVERT OF PIPE TERMINUS SHALL BE EQUAL TO OR HIGHER IN ELEVATION THAN FORCE MAIN PRIOR TO THE FIRST 22-1/2" BEND. THE LENGTH OF SPOOL PIECE BETWEEN BENDS SHALL BE INDICATED ON THE CONSTRUCTION ORAWINGS TO ENSURE THE PROPER ELEVATIONS NOTED ABOVE. 

- ‘S. THE TOP OF THE BENCH SHALL BE SLOPED TOWAROS THE CHANNEL. THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN EDGE OF THE MANHOLE AND EDGE OF THE CHANNEL SHALL BE NO LESS THAN TWO COURSES OF BRICK. 

6. THE SECOND FITTING MAY NOT BE NECESSARY IF THE CONSTRUCTION ORAWINGS INDICATE THIS CONDITION. GENERALLY THE FIRST FITTING MAY NOT BE NECESSARY (AS DETERMINED BY THE DIVISION OF WATER AND SEWER) IF THE APPROACHING GRADE OF THE FORCE MAIN ODES NOT REQUIRE THE FITTING. 

7. TESTING OF THE FORCE MAIN SHALL BE PERFORMED AFTER THE BRICK CHANNEL ANO BENCH HAVE GEEN COMPLETED.
