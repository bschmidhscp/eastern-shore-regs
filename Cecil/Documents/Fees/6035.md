# Resolution Cecil County Code Part IV - General Legislation 

Chapter 211 – Electrical Standards 

Whereas, the Board County Commissioners are authorized to levy various fees, license and taxes within Cecil County; and 

Whereas, under the provisions of Chapter 211-26 Fees, of the Cecil County Code the following schedule of fees shall become effective October 1, 2012, 

# <u>Electrical Permit Fees</u> 

General $30.00 Low Voltage $20.00 

- a) In cases where any owner or authorized agent are required to obtain a permit under the Code and where there are multiple structures, buildings or facilities, etc., on the property and work is to be done in each structure, building facility, or etc., a permit will be required for each. 

- b) For the first permit the fee shall be as listed above, for each additional permit fee shall be $5.00 

- c) These fees only apply if all permit applications are applied for and obtained at the same time.
