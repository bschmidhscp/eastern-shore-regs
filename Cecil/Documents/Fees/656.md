The following fees apply for the **Standard Plan** and **each Phase** of the Joint Agency ESD Review 

- **1) Determination of Minimum Control Requirements Fee (always required) $100 initial submittal $50 resubmittal** 

- **2) Minor Subdivision requiring ESD Narrative $100 initial submittal $50 resubmittal** 

- **3) Stormwater Variances    $400 initial submittal $200 resubmittal** 

- **4) A $300 Micro Scale Practice Declaration Fee is required for practices less than $7,500.00 (except BMP Group 6)** 

- **5) Every three (3) Rain Barrels and each cistern will be considered a separate practice**
