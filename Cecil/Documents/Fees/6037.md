Mechanical Code Fee Schedule 

<u>Residential Uses:  (includes 1 & 2 family and multi-family dwellings)</u> 

(a) New Residential Installations: 

|1.) Heating and Cooling System………………………….$75.00|
|---|
|2.) Heating System Only…………………………………….$50.00|
|3.) Cooling System Only…………………………………….$50.00|



The minimum fee for any permit in the residential class shall be $50.00 

# <u>Non-residential Installations</u> 

The permit fee for the installation, repair or replacement of all mechanical equipment requiring a permit under County regulations shall be calculated from the following table based on the estimated cost of the work, including equipment, labor and material. The Department shall require verification of such costs. 

|Estimated Costs|Permit Fee|
|---|---|
|$0.00 to $5,000…………………………………………|$50.00|
|$5,001 to $7,500……………………………………….|$75.00|
|$7,501 to $11,000……………………………………..|$90.00|



For all estimated cost in excess of $11,000, the fee shall be $90.00 plus $0.005 time the amount in excess of $11,000. 

FEE OF $250.00 WILL BE ASSESSED BY THE DEPARTMENT PRIOR TO THE ISSUANCE OF THE CERTIFICATE OF USE AND OCCUPANCY FOR PROCEEDING WITHOUT PROPER PERMITS OR INSPECTIONS. WHEN NO USE AND OCCUPANCY PERMIT IS REQUIRED ALL OUTSTANDING FEES OR PENALTIES MUST BE PAID PRIOR TO RECEIVING ANY NEW PERMITS.
