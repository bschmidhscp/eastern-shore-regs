# Cecil County — Fee Findings

*Compiled 2026-10-11 from the corpus. Figures are as published; no FY27 schedule was found.*

## Impact fee / excise tax
- **None on new homes.** Council Vice President Meffley stated the County "does not have the authority to impose impact fees"; the Administration director noted impact fees affect affordability [Council minutes 2026-06-09, p.~3].
- No impact fee or building excise tax in the code: Ch. 337 (Taxation) and Ch. 202 (Fees) contain none [Code Ch. 337, Ch. 202]. The 2010 comp plan and Growth Study only discuss impact fees/excise taxes as options [Plans: 2938 p.185; Growth-Study p.55-58].
- **Only a deed transfer fee:** 0.5% of consideration (or $25 if zero consideration), first-time-buyer owner-occupant exemption [§ 202-1].
- Roads/other exactions are imposed case by case at plan review (same minutes).

- Stormwater review (updated 9/1/17): $100 min-control determination; $100 minor-subdivision ESD narrative; $400 SWM variance; BMP fees $50-$300 [Fees: 656].

## Health dept / septic
Perc/septic site evaluation (PERC) is required before preliminary approval [PC minutes 2026-01-21 pp.5-6]. Health Dept fee amounts: **not found — confirm with county.** Bay Restoration Fee (state): $15 per month on sewer/water bill for sewer users; $60/yr for septic [Fees: 590].

## Water & sewer charges
- **Wastewater Major Facilities Fee (MFF): $12,000 per ELU residential**, one-time, per 220 gpd ELU; charged on occupancy permit; was $6,000 for homes permitted before 7/6/2021 (Res. 41-2021) [Plans: Wastewater Division Fee Schedule eff. 8/1/2021, notes 1, 8; Fees: Sanitary District Policy Memo 1 (12/1/2017)].
- Benefit Assessments may apply by sanitary subdistrict (check with Development Plans Review) [same, note 2].
- User rate $13.65 per 1,000 gal (0-100,000 gal) metered, 9,000-gal quarterly minimum [same].
- Water connection charges: not found — confirm with county.
