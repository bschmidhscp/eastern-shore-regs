# § 70-3. Performance evaluations.

Each year, prior to July 1, a performance evaluation of the County Health Officer for the preceding twelve-month period shall be completed in writing by the County Executive, using a standard Cecil County Personnel Office form,[1] including the employee evaluation form which is applicable to department heads. The County Executive shall then review its evaluation with the County Health Officer.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: See Ch. A384 , Personnel Policies and Procedures.
