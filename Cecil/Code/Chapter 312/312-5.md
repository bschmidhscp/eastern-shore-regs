# § 312-5. Variances.

The Cecil Soil Conservation District may only grant a variance from the requirements of the Standards and Specifications when strict adherence will result in exceptional hardship and not fulfill the intent of this chapter. The owner/developer shall submit a written request for a variance to the Cecil Soil Conservation District. The request must state the specific variance sought and the reasons for the request. The Cecil Soil Conservation District shall not grant a variance unless and until sufficient information is provided describing the unique circumstances of the site to justify the variance.
