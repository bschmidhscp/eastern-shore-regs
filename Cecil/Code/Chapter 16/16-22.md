# § 16-22. Meetings; rules and regulations.

*[1998, ch. 182, § 1]*

A. The Commission shall meet as frequently as required to perform its duties, but no less than two times each year.

B. The Commission shall adopt rules and regulations necessary to govern its procedure and business.
