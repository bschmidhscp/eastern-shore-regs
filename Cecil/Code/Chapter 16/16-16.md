# § 16-16. Borrowing funds.

*[1968, ch. 697, § 1; 1970 Code, Sec. 1-8(j); 1977, ch. 841, § 1; 1988, ch. 227, § 2]*

Cecil County may borrow funds for the purposes of this article and issue individual notes, bonds or other evidence of indebtedness for the repayment of the funds and of interest thereon.
