# § 16-24. Account and Board created.

A. There is hereby created a Landfill Closure Investment Account, which shall be for the purpose of accumulating funds to pay long-term liabilities resulting from, by way of example and not limitation, the closure of the County's landfill, including the fulfillment of state and federal laws regarding post-closure requirements.

B. There is hereby created a Landfill Closure Investment Account Board of Directors (the Board). The Board shall be comprised of membership as set forth in § 16-25, shall serve a term as specified in § 16-26, and shall have the powers set forth in § 16-29 of Cecil County Code Chapter 16, Article IV.
