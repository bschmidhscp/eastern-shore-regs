# § 16-13. Initiation or maintenance of recreation programs.

*[1968, ch. 697, § 1; 1970 Code, Sec. 1-8(g); 1977, ch. 841, § 1; 1988, ch. 227, § 2]*

The Board, subject to the approval of the County, may contract with private organizations for the initiation and maintenance of public recreation programs.
