# § 16-12. Control of land, buildings or facilities.

*[1968, ch. 697, § 1; 1970 Code, Sec. 1-8(f); 1977, ch. 841, § 1; 1988, ch. 227, § 2]*

The control of any land, buildings, or other acceptable facilities shall be in accord with agreements reached between the Board and the person, corporation, or agency having jurisdiction over such properties. No power or authority conferred by this article shall be construed to abridge or limit the power of Cecil County, the Board of Education, or of any governmental agency, or of any person or corporation, to refuse to permit or to limit the use of any ground, building, or facility under their control, ownership or jurisdiction.
