# § 16-33. Role of the Board.

A. The Board shall function in an advisory role and render recommendations to the County administration through the Director of Emergency Services on operational issues, which will sustain the career/volunteer relationship that will render the best delivery of emergency medical services to meet the needs of Cecil County.

B. The Board shall establish a working relationship with the Cecil County Firemen's Association through their committees, and entertain a formal position from the Association on matters of relevant importance that impact their daily operations.

C. Medical guidelines and personnel related matters are not within the purview of the Board.

  (1) Medical guidelines are assigned to the local EMS Medical Director.

  (2) Personnel-related matters are assigned to the Department of Emergency Services.

D. The Board shall investigate and make recommendation to the County administration of other emergency services that may benefit the community.
