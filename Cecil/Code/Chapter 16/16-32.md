# § 16-32. Organization.

A. The Board shall annually select a member to serve as President and a Secretary for a one-year term. Each officer may be reelected to one additional term.

B. The President shall determine the meeting date, time, and location; and notifications of meetings shall be provided by the Department of Emergency Services.

C. The Board shall meet no less than four times annually.
