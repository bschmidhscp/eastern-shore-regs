# § 16-37. Staff; meeting compensation.

A. The Cecil County Department of Community Services, or its successor agency, shall provide staff support to the Commission.

B. The meetings of the Commission shall be called by the Chairperson. The Commission shall meet regularly and as often as necessary to perform its duties but not less than six times per year.

C. The members shall receive no compensation for their service but may be reimbursed for reasonable and necessary expenses incurred in the discharge of their duties.
