# § 16-31. Membership.

A. The Board will consist of 15 voting members.

  (1) Nine members, one from each of the nine volunteer fire/EMS companies;

  (2) Five members, one from each of the five Councilmanic districts;

  (3) One representative of the IAFF Local.

B. The Board will also include three ex-officio members.

  (1) Director of the Department of Emergency Services;

  (2) Local physician EMS Medical Director;

  (3) One member of the Cecil County Firemen's Association.

C. Members will be appointed by the County Executive, and confirmed by the County Council, to a term of three years.
