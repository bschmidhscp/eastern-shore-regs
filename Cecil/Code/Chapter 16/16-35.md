# § 16-35. Membership.

A. The Commission shall consist of nine members appointed by the County Executive subject to confirmation by the County Council, except for the members, appointed by the Council, and shall consist of:

  (1) Two members of County Council;

  (2) Four members who are veterans and shall be a member of any bona-fide and nationally recognized veterans' group such as, but not limited to, the following:

    (a) Veterans of Foreign Wars;

    (b) Women's Army Corps Veterans Association;

    (c) American Legion;

    (d) Military Order of the Purple Heart;

  (3) Three members of the general public that have experience in veterans affairs.

B. The County Executive may also designate the following as ex-officio members:

  (1) The Garrison Commander of Aberdeen Proving Ground, or the Commander's designee;

  (2) A representative from Cecil County Health Department or Cecil County Community College familiar with veterans' educational and health issues;

  (3) A representative from the Cecil County Chamber of Commerce;

  (4) A representative from the Susquehanna Workforce Network.

C. All members of the Commission, with the exception of the ex officio members, shall be appointed for three-year terms and shall continue to serve until their reappointment or until their successors are appointed. Vacancies, except those at the expiration of a term, shall be filled in the same manner as the original appointments and for the unexpired term. All members of the Board shall serve without compensation, except that they may be reimbursed for necessary and proper expenses incurred while performing their duties thereon.

D. The Commission shall select from its membership a Chair, Vice Chair, and a Secretary-Treasurer each to serve at the pleasure of the Commission. Upon the death, disability, resignation, or removal of any member of the Commission, the County Executive, in accordance with the Charter, shall appoint a person representing the original group for the unexpired term. The Commission has the power to establish and appoint advisory groups and committees to assist in its program of activity, which membership is not restricted solely to members of the Commission.
