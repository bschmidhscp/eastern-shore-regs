# § 16-27. Meeting procedures.

The Board shall meet regularly and may hold special meetings at the call of the Chair or a majority of members of the Board. The Board shall adopt such rules and regulations as it may deem necessary to govern its procedures and business. A majority of the members shall constitute a quorum. The Board is empowered to employ such employees and/or consultants as its business may require, with costs to be paid from the fund upon approval of a majority of quorum of the trustees.
