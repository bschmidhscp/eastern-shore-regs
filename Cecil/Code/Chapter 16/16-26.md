# § 16-26. Term.

The members of the Board shall serve for as long as the employee holds their position with Cecil County. They shall serve without compensation but shall be reimbursed for necessary travel and other expenses incurred in the performance of their duties. The Board shall select from its own membership a Chair, Vice Chair, and a Secretary-Treasurer to serve a term of one year and to be eligible for reelection, provided that no one person shall hold the position of Chair for more than three consecutive terms. Upon the death, disability, resignation, or removal of any member of the Board, the duties shall be assumed by the next individual holding the relevant position.
