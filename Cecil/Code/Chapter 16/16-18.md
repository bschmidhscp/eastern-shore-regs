# § 16-18. County Planning Commission not affected.

*[1968, § 697, § 1; 1970 Code, Sec. 1-8(1); 1977, ch. 841, § 1; 1988, Ch. 227, § 2]*

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this article, the authority of the Cecil County Planning Commission, as set forth under § 3.08 of Article 66B, Annotated Code of Maryland, shall not be diminished or impaired.
