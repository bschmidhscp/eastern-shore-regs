# § 325-2. Documents incorporated by reference.

For the purpose of this chapter, the following documents are incorporated by reference:

A. The 2000 Maryland Stormwater Design Manual, Volumes I and II (Maryland Department of the Environment, April 2000), and all subsequent revisions, which shall serve as the official guide for stormwater principles, methods, and practices.[Amended 11-13-2012 by Ord. No. 2012-12]

B. USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service Maryland Conservation Practice Standard Pond Code 378 (January 2000).
