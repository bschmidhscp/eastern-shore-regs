# § 325-18. Plan and permit suspension and revocation.

Any grading permit issued by the Department of Public Works and any building permit which requires Department of Public Works review and/or approval may be suspended or revoked. The Department of Public Works may suspend the review of a plan currently under review and/or require revisions to a plan previously approved. None of the above actions shall be taken by the Department until after written notice is given to the permittee for any of the following reasons:

A. Any violation(s) of the conditions of the stormwater management and/or erosion and sediment control plan approval.

B. Changes in site runoff characteristics upon which an approval was granted.

C. Construction is not in accordance with the approved plan or permit.

D. Noncompliance with the notice of violation(s) or stop-work order(s) issued for the project/site.

E. An immediate danger exists in a downstream area in the opinion of the Department of Public Works.
