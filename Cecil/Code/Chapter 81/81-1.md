# § 81-1. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**EMERGENCY** May consist of an unruly person or group which demonstrates a potential for violence, a hostage situation, a fire, a flood, an accident or a similar circumstance where prompt police action is required or more than one officer or specialized equipment is required.
