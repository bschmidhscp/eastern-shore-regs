# § 310-1. Notice of state sludge permit application.

Upon receipt of the notice from the Maryland State Department of the Environment of a proposed sludge permit application, the Department of Planning and Zoning of Cecil County shall identity the geographic location of the proposed sludge site and the adjoining property parcels and property owners.
