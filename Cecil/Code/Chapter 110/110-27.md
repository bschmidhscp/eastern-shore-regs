# § 110-27. Procedure for establishment.

A. In cases where the Cecil County Department of Public Works has inspected the stormwater management facilities and determined that they fail to meet applicable standards, and where the homeowners' association or other responsible party is unable or unwilling to voluntarily make the repairs necessary to bring the facilities into compliance, the Department will send written notices to the owners of properties served by the facilities requesting their written assent to the creation of a Stormwater Management Facility Maintenance District.

B. The Department will tally the responses. Failure of a property owner to submit a response by the specified deadline shall constitute a vote against the establishment of the Stormwater Management Facility Maintenance District.

C. If at least 2/3 of property owners in the proposed district assent to the creation of such a district, the Cecil County Council shall conduct a public hearing to consider the matter.

D. Notice of the time and location of the public hearing shall be provided by:

  (1) First-class mail to each owner of property within the proposed district, at the mailing address listed on the assessment records; and

  (2) Notice in a newspaper of general circulation in the County for two consecutive weeks.

E. At the public hearing conducted under this section:

  (1) The Department will provide a list of all the properties served by the facilities and a map showing the proposed boundaries of the district; and

  (2) The Department will describe the scope of the required repairs, the estimated total cost, and the estimated individual levies; and

  (3) The Council shall receive public comment regarding the establishment of the proposed district and set a date for a final hearing that may only be changed pursuant to notice as provided above.

F. At a final hearing conducted under this section, the Cecil County Council may:

  (1) Establish the district; and

  (2) Designate the area within the district; and

  (3) Establish the project scope and estimated cost; and

  (4) Determine if interest is to be charged and, if so, the rate of interest; and

  (5) Establish individual levies and the time period for property owners to reimburse the County for its costs.
