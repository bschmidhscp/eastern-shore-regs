# § 110-21. Administration.

For the purposes of administering the district, the Cecil County Council constitutes the District Board. The District Board is a body corporate by the name of "Winding Brook Special Taxing District," with the right to use a common seal, to sue and be sued, and to do any and all other corporate acts for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this article.
