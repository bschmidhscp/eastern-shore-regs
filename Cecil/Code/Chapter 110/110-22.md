# § 110-22. Levy of tax.

Cecil County shall levy annually upon the property in the Winding Brook Special Taxing District a sum sufficient to pay the costs of trash collection, mowing of common areas, and general maintenance of the district. This levy shall be collected as all other taxes in the County are collected and shall be paid over to the Director of Finance.
