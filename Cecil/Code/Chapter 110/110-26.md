# § 110-26. Apportionment of costs.

A. Repair and maintenance costs to bring existing facilities up to current standards shall be apportioned equally among the properties served by those facilities.

B. In cases where the County opts to retrofit stormwater management facilities to achieve restoration credits or obtain other co-benefits such as flood control or mitigation, property owners shall only be responsible for the cost to bring the facilities up to current standards, while the County shall bear the additional cost created by retrofitting rather than just repairing the facility.
