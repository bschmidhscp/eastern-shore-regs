# § 110-9. Funds for roads and drainage.

*[1984, ch. 278, § 1; 1988, ch. 227, § 2]*

For the purpose of providing funds for the design, construction, establishment, and improvement of roads and drainage in the district:

A. Cecil County may issue bonds upon the full faith and credit of the County; and/or

B. The District Board may borrow funds from any source.
