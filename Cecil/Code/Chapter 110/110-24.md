# § 110-24. Purpose.

The purpose of the Stormwater Management Facility Maintenance District is to pay the cost of repairing and maintaining in perpetuity stormwater management facilities serving the properties within the district to ensure they meet current standards as defined by the Maryland Department of the Environment and the Code of Cecil County.
