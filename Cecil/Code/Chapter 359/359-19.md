# § 359-19. Violations and penalties.

A. Any person violating any of the provisions of this article shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall be fined according to the District Court of Maryland Schedule of Fines.[Amended 11-13-2012 by Ord. No. 2012-12]

B. Each day during which a violation of this article continues shall be deemed a separate offense.
