# § 359-24. Sale of impounded vehicles.

Whenever any motor vehicle or part thereof is in the custody of a designee of the County and whenever the owner or person entitled to the possession thereof cannot be located and fails to claim such motor vehicle or part thereof for a period of 30 days after such motor vehicle or part thereof came into the custody of the County, it may dispose of the motor vehicle and distribute the proceeds of sale according to the Annotated Code of Maryland.
