# § 359-14. Definitions.

For the purposes of this article, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings respectively ascribed to them by this section:

**EMERGENCY VEHICLE** A vehicle so designated or authorized by the Administrator of the Maryland Department of Transportation Motor Vehicle Administration.

**FIRE COMPANY** Any volunteer or compensated fire company serving the County, including those operated by the federal government or from out of state.

**FIRE LANE** A lane required to be permanently open for the ingress or egress of fire and other emergency vehicles only, in order that fires and other disasters may be prevented or controlled and exitways kept unobstructed for life safety.

**FIRE MARSHAL** The State Fire Marshal or any deputy State Fire Marshal.

**SAFE AND EXPEDITIOUS MANNER** The use of any safe method by the proper authorities which is calculated to remove the obstruction in the shortest period of time.
