# § 359-13. Special purpose parking areas.

The County shall have the authority to establish special purpose parking zones, and it shall be unlawful for any person to park any vehicle or tractor or to allow the same to remain parked in any such designated zone, except as specifically provided in such zone. Each special purpose parking area shall be designated by resolution of the County Council, which resolution shall include the name of the street, the times for parking (time limit/hours), the side of the streets and the location of the street.
