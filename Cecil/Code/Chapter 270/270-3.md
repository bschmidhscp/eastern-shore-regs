# § 270-3. Inspections.

A. The Division of Permits and Inspections shall be notified by the plumber to perform an inspection before covering any indoor or outdoor underground installations and rough plumbing work. The Plumbing Inspector shall be requested by the plumber to perform a final inspection at such time as all plumbing work is complete.

B. Inspections for plumbing installations can only be scheduled by the individual or his or her representative who acquired the plumbing permit.

C. A notice of at least 24 hours is required to schedule any inspection.

D. All connections to a municipal water or sewer line shall be inspected by the Cecil County Department of Public Works or any municipality which regulates such lines or their agent prior to covering of the work performed.
