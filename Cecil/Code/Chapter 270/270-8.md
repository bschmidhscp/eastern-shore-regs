# § 270-8. Traps and cleanouts.

The maximum distance of fixture traps from vents shall be the following:


| Size of Fixture Drain (inches) | Distance from Trap to Vent (feet) |
|---|---|
| 1 1/2 | 5 |
| 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 10 |
