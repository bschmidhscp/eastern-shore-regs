# § 285-3. Administration.

A. The Director of Public Works for Cecil County shall be responsible for the overall administration of the commercial refuse haulers licensing program in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.

B. The Cecil County Director of Finance shall be responsible for the issuance and renewal of commercial refuse hauler's licenses to those commercial refuse haulers who have paid the required fee to the Cecil County Director of Finance and who meet all other requirements as determined by the Director of Public Works or his/her designee.
