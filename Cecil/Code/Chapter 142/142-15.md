# § 142-15. Reports of animal bites.

Any hospital or physician who treats an individual for an animal bite shall immediately telephone and report the incident to the local police or Sheriff's Department and to the Cecil County Health Department. An animal bite report form shall be completed as required by COMAR 10.06.02.05B.
