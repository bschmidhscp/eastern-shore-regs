# § A387-14. Emergency meetings.

A. An emergency meeting may be called by the Council President, by four Council members, or by the County Executive. Upon notification to the Council of an emergency meeting, the notice of the emergency meeting must be posted on the County website and shall include the purpose, subject, location and time of the emergency meeting.

B. In the event that the County website or Internet access is unavailable, notices would be sent out to area media for broadcasting on local stations.
