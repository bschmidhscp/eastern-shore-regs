# § A387-38. Petition for hearing.

A. A bill that has not been scheduled for a public hearing by the Council President may at any time be the subject of a petition demanding such a hearing.

B. A petition for a hearing may be made by motion by any Council member. The motion is to include the date, time and place of the proposed public hearing. The motion will stand adopted by an affirmative vote of at least three Council members.
