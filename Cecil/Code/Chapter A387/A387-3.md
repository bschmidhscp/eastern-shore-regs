# § A387-3. Suspension of rules.

A. Except for a rule that is required by law, the Charter, or ordinance, a motion to suspend one or more of these rules requires the affirmative vote of at least four members for adoption.

B. A separate suspension of the rules is required for each proposition.

C. A rule that is required by law, the Charter or ordinance cannot be suspended.
