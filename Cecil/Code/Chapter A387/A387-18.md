# § A387-18. Meeting time and location.

A. Council meetings, termed a "legislative day," shall be scheduled to convene at 7:00 p.m. on the first and third Tuesday of each month, and shall be held in the County Administration Building in accordance with § A387-10B.

B. Other Council meetings may be scheduled as needed, as per § A387-12.

C. Legislative meetings scheduled by the Council to convene at a location other than in the County Administration Building may be authorized by the affirmative vote of three Council members at least one week in advance.

D. No meeting shall continue past the hour of 12:00 midnight unless these rules of procedure are suspended, as per § A387-3.
