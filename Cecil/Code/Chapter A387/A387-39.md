# § A387-39. Scheduling bills for public hearing.

A. Except for emergency legislation, no bill may be scheduled to be heard sooner than three weeks after its introduction.

B. Except as provided in this Policy, if the Council President has submitted a bill to a County agency, board or commission for review, a public hearing on the bill cannot be convened until the report has been received by the Council President and distributed to the Council members. Copies of any such reports shall be available to the public.

C. If pursuant to this Policy an entity fails to report on a bill submitted to it, a public hearing on the bill may be commenced by an affirmative vote of at least three Council members, even in the absence of the report.
