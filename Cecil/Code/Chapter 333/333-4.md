# § 333-4. Amendments, additions and deletions.

A. The following sections of the 2021 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code (ISPSC) are hereby revised and amended as follows:

  (1) Delete Section 304 entirely.

  (2) Delete Section 305.1.

  (3) Insert:

    305.1 General. The provision of this section shall apply to the design barriers for restricting entry into areas having pool and spas.

  (4) Chapter 3. Rename Section 305.2 to "Applicability" and replace the text with the following:


| (A) | A public swimming pool or spa shall be surrounded by a barrier that complies with Sections 305.2.1 through 305.7, except that the top of the barrier where measured on the side of the barrier that faces away from the swimming pool or spa shall be not less than: |  |
|---|---|---|
|  | [1] | 72 inches (1828 mm) above grade for a Class A, B, or D swimming pool or spa; |
|  | [2] | 60 inches (1524 mm) above grade for a Class C or E swimming pool or spa; and |
|  | [3] | 36 inches (914.4 mm) above grade for a wading pool. |
| (B) | Non-public outdoor swimming pool and spa and indoor swimming pools shall be surrounded by a barrier that complies with Sections 305.2.1 through 305.7. |  |

B. In Section 307.1.4, where "International Building Code" appears, substitute "Maryland Accessibility Code" and where "ICC A117.1" appears, substitute "Maryland Accessibility Code."
