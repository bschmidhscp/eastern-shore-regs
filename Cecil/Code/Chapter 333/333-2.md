# § 333-2. Scope.

A. Except as provided in Subsection B of this section, the standards shall apply to all swimming pools and spas for which an application for a building permit is received by on or after December 31, 2023.

B. The standards adopted under this chapter do not apply to a swimming pool or spa built and in operation before December 31, 2023, unless there is an alteration or repair that results in a substantial and material structural change to the swimming pool or spa that occurs on or after December 31, 2023.

C. If there is an alteration or repair to a swimming pool or spa built and in operation before December 31, 2023, that results in a substantial or material structural change, the alteration or repair shall conform to the standards.
