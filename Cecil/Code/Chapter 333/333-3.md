# § 333-3. Definitions.

**SUBSTANTIAL AND MATERIAL STRUCTURAL CHANGE** Means an alteration or repair that:

A. Increases the volume of the swimming pool or spa by 15% or more; or

B. Decreases the flow capacity of the swimming pool or space by 15% or more.
