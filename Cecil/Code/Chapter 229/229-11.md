# § 229-11. Applicable standards.

The 2021 edition of the International Mechanical Code shall be the code in effect and will be enforced within Cecil County. Any provisions of the Maryland Building Performance Standards that deal directly with mechanical installations as adopted by the State of Maryland shall also be complied with.
