# § A383-5. Uniform contracting procedure.

The Roads Division shall adopt a uniform procedure for contracting of snow and ice removal in Cecil County, pursuant to the terms outlined in the Snow Removal Requirements and Procedures, Snow Removal Contract Agreement, Notice to Contractors and Letter of Interest, all of which are incorporated by reference into this article.
