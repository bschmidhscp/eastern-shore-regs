# § A383-1. Responsibility; policy.

The Roads Division of the Cecil County Department of Public Works is responsible for snow and ice removal on the County road system. The Roads Division will endeavor to keep the County road system in as safe a traversable condition as possible. This operation will be conducted as economically as possible.
