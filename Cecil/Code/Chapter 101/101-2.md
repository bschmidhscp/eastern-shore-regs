# § 101-2. Traveling expenses for members of Board of Education.

*[1961 Code, § 437; 1970 Code, Sec. 20-2]*

Each member of the County Board of Education of Cecil County shall receive annually the sum of $600 for traveling and other expenses incident to attending the meetings and transacting the business of said Board.
