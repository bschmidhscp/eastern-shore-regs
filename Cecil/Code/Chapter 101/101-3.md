# § 101-3. School bus safety cameras.

*[Added 11-2-2021 by Bill No. 2021-14 ]*

A. Definitions. As used in this section, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**BOARD** The Cecil County Board of Education.

**SCHOOL BUS** A bus operated by the Board to transport students.

**SCHOOL BUS SAFETY CAMERA** A camera placed on a school bus that is designed to capture a recorded image of a driver of a motor vehicle committing a violation authorized by the Annotated Code of Maryland, Transportation Article, § 21-706.1.

**SCHOOLS** Cecil County Public Schools.

**SHERIFF** The Cecil County Sheriff.

**VIOLATION** A violation of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Transportation Article, § 21-706.1.

B. The Board may install, operate, and maintain school bus safety cameras on school buses as permitted by the Annotated Code of Maryland, Transportation Article, § 21-706.1.

C. A person who commits a violation recorded by a school bus safety camera is subject to a civil penalty authorized by the Annotated Code of Maryland, Transportation Article, § 21-706.1.

D. The registered owner of a motor vehicle will be sent a citation by the Sheriff's Office, which will include a fine of $250 for each violation.

E. The schools must use any fines collected by the County for a violation recorded by a school bus safety camera:

  (1) To recover the costs of installing, operating, and maintaining school bus safety cameras.

  (2) For public safety purposes, including pedestrian safety programs.

  (3) Any revenue remaining will be assigned to the Cecil County Sheriffs Office for their use.
