# § 10-13. Borrowing for capital expenditures.

*[1969, ch. 714, § 1; 1970 Code, Sec. 1-7(g)]*

Cecil County, from time to time, may borrow money on the faith and credit of Cecil County for capital expenditures imposed or incurred for the construction, reconstruction, repair, or enlargement of an airport or airports in Cecil County.
