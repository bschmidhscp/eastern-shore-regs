# § 10-11. Control of airport.

*[1969, ch. 714, § 1; 1970 Code, Sec. 1-7(e); amended 11-13-2012 by Ord. No. 2012-12 ]*

Following such studies, the Cecil County Council may establish by ordinance and construct an airport or airports in Cecil County, under the provisions of this article. In that event, it or they shall be operated and maintained under the control, direction, and supervision of the Airport Authority.
