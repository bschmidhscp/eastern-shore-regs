# § 10-5. Directors.

*[1961 Code, § 101; 1970 Code, Sec. 3-7; 1974, ch. 240, § 3]*

The affairs of said Association shall be managed by seven Directors to be elected by the incorporators hereinbefore named at a meeting to be held at the County seat of Cecil County, after a majority of them have been notified of said meeting and of the time and place of holding the same, to serve for one year or until their successors, thereafter to be elected annually by the members thereof, are elected and qualified.
