# § 10-4. Judges to have use of books.

*[1961 Code, § 100; 1970 Code, Sec. 3-7; 1974, ch. 240, § 3]*

The Judges of the Circuit Court of Cecil County shall have the use of any and all of the books of the Association as fully as any of the members thereof.
