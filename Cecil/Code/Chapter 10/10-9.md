# § 10-9. Responsibilities.

*[1969, ch. 714, § 1; 1970 Code, Sec. 1-7(c); 1988, ch. 227, § 2]*

The Airport Authority shall study the need and possibilities for an airport in Cecil County, the rules, regulations, and requirements of the Federal Aviation Administration and the State Aviation Administration in relation to any such facility, the possibilities for obtaining state or federal funds for the construction and operation of an airport, possible sites for an airport, and such other matters as properly are related to, and fall within the scope of, the intent and purpose of this article.
