# § 10-2. Authority to purchase or sell property.

*[1961 Code, § 97; 1970 Code, Sec. 3-5; 1974, ch. 240, § 3]*

Said corporation, under the above name, shall be capable in law of purchasing, holding, selling and conveying property, real, personal and mixed, as far as shall be necessary for the purpose for which it is incorporated.
