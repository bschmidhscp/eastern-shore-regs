# § 92-4. Budgetary appropriations; accounting practices.

A. No County purchase shall be made without a sufficient budgetary appropriation of funds.

B. Accounting for County purchases shall be in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles and in compliance with the standards established by the Governmental Accounting Standards Board or, in the absence of a GASB standard, the Financial Accounting Standards Board.

C. All unexpended County appropriations, except those for capital improvement projects, lapse at the end of each fiscal year.
