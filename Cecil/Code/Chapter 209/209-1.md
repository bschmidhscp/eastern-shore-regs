# § 209-1. Shooting or killing fox pursued by hounds or dogs.

*[1961 Code, §§ 223, 224; 1970 Code, Sec. 10-1]*

A. Any person or persons who shall shoot, kill or wound any fox or foxes while being pursued by hounds or a hound within the limits of Cecil County shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall pay a fine of $10 for every such offense, together with the costs of prosecution, and upon failure to pay the same shall be committed to the County jail until the fine and costs are paid.

B. It shall not be lawful to shoot, shoot at, trap, set a trap for, injure or kill any fox in Cecil County while such fox is being pursued by dog or dogs.
