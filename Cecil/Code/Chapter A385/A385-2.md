# § A385-2. Funding.

*[Amended 11-13-2012 by Ord. No. 2012-12 ; 9-2-2025 by Bill No. 2025-09 ]*

A. The Purchase of Development Rights Fund shall receive its funding from the County's recordation tax and from optional payments made in lieu of open space required in the Northern Agricultural Residential (NAR) and Southern Agricultural Residential (SAR) Zoning Districts and other sources of revenue streams as deemed appropriate.

B. The payment made in lieu of open space shall be equal to the appraised value of the acreage that is required to be provided in the NAR and SAR 15% common open space standards.

C. Ground-mounted solar projects located on a site that is located within a priority preservation area, rural legacy area, defined heritage area, historic structure or site listed on or property eligible for the National Register of Historic Places or County-designated historic district, or located along a designated scenic byway, prior to the issuance of a building permit, shall contribute a payment commensurate with the most recent top 25% per acre appraised value completed by Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation (MALPF). These funds shall be used to fund the purchase and preservation of development rights per § A385-2A.
