# § A385-6. Landowner responsibilities.

*[Amended 11-13-2012 by Ord. No. 2012-12 ]*

The landowner shall be responsible for providing a boundary survey and for the resolution of any property boundary or title problems prior to settlement. The landowner shall also be responsible for the implementation of the soil and water conservation plan. The landowner shall obtain approval from the Department of Planning and Zoning or the Department of Parks and Recreation for all children's lots and dwellings on land withheld prior to easement sale. All subsequent owners shall be subject to deed of easement restrictions.
