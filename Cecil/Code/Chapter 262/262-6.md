# § 262-6. Law enforcement hold.

A. When contacted by law enforcement or by the Sheriff's Pawn Unit with a request to have an item placed on hold that is still within the required thirty-day hold period and/or the business is still in possession of an article, precious metal object, or secondhand property, the dealer, secondhand dealer, or pawnbroker shall hold that item for investigative purposes until it is determined by law enforcement or the Sheriff's Pawn Unit that the item will either be seized, it is determined that the item is no longer needed for investigative purposes, or 45 days, whichever shall first occur.

B. In circumstances where law enforcement is able to determine that an item has been stolen or is evidence related to a crime or criminal investigation, law enforcement may seize the item from a dealer, secondhand dealer, or pawnbroker and hold the item until the criminal proceedings reach a final decision or the criminal investigation is closed without charges. In all such instances, the seizure shall be documented on a form provided by the Sheriff's Pawn Unit.

C. When contacted by law enforcement or by the Sheriff's Pawn Unit to have an item/transaction placed on hold or if the item is seized in accordance with this section, the dealer, secondhand dealer, or pawnbroker (and its employees) shall keep that information confidential.
