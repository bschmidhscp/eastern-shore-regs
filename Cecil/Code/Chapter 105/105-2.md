# § 105-2. Copyright and guidelines for use.

*[Added 7-19-2011 by Ord. No. 2011-09 ]*

A. A certificate of registration was issued under the seal of the Copyright Office in accordance with Title 17, United States Code, and attests that registration has been made for the County Seal. The effective date of registration is November 15, 2010.

B. The Cecil County Council shall by resolution develop guidelines for use of the County Seal.
