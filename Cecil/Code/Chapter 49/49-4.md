# § 49-4. Reimbursement of emergency services expenses.

*[Added 7-26-2022 by Bill No. 2022-09 ]*

A. The Cecil County Firemen's Association Company members are authorized to charge and receive reimbursement for certain costs from insurance companies of insured parties who are associated with emergency services response to and mitigation of motor vehicle collisions, accidents, and rescues within the County or any area within which the Association's members are authorized to provide such services.

B. Definitions. The following terms shall have the meanings as indicated:

**ASSOCIATION** The Cecil County Firemen's Association.

**CHARGE** The reimbursement charge for certain emergency services response and mitigation supplies and equipment, medical care and/or transportation provided by an Association member in the performance of their duties.

**EMERGENCY** 911 call for service.
