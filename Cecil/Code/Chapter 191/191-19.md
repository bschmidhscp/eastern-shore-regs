# § 191-19. Fees.

*[Amended 4-2-2024 by Bill No. 2024-09 ]*

All permit and license fees shall be established by resolution by the County Council as provided for by law. Copies of the fee schedule shall be available in the offices of the Cecil County Council, Board of Electricians and the Division of Permits and Inspections. Any fees for inspections shall be established and assessed by the agency providing the inspection service.
