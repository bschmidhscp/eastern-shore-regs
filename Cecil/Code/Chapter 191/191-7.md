# § 191-7. Officers.

*[Amended 5-6-2014 by Bill No. 2014-09 ]*

The Board shall, at their annual November meeting, elect a Chairperson, a Vice Chairperson and any other officers. The Vice Chairperson shall, in the absence of the Chairperson, assume all duties and responsibilities of the Chairperson. Any sitting officers may be elected to consecutive terms.
