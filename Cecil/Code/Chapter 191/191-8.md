# § 191-8. Salaries and compensation.

*[Amended 4-2-2024 by Bill No. 2024-09 ]*

Each member of said Board shall receive compensation at a rate the County determines for attending meetings and other expenses incurred in connection with his/her official duties. Any compensation and expenses of said Board shall be paid out of the budget of the Division of Permits and Inspections.
