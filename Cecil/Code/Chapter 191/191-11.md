# § 191-11. Legal advice and assistance.

*[Amended 5-6-2014 by Bill No. 2014-09 ; 4-2-2024 by Bill No. 2024-09 ]*

The County Attorney and/ or designee shall act as legal advisor to the Board of Electricians and render such legal assistance as the Board may request from time to time. The County Attorney and/or designee shall represent the County and assist in any prosecution of violations of this chapter.
