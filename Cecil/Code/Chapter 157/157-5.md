# § 157-5. Saving clause.

Nothing in this chapter or in the Building Code hereby adopted shall be construed to affect any suit or proceeding pending in any court or any rights acquitted or liability incurred or any cause or causes of action acquired or existing under any act or ordinance hereby repealed, nor shall any just or legal right or remedy of any character be lost, impaired or affected by this chapter.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: Former § 200-6, Emergency shelters, was repealed 6-15-2010 by Ord. No. 2010-04.
