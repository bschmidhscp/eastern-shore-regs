# § 157-4. Fees.

*[Amended 6-15-2010 by Ord. No. 2010-04 ; 11-20-2019 by Bill No. 2019-19 ]*

All fees shall be established by resolution by the Cecil County Council as provided for by law. Copies of the fee schedule will be available in the office of the Cecil County Council and the Division of Permits and Inspections.
