# § 157-1. Adoption of standards by reference.

*[Amended 2-15-2005 by Ord. No. 2005-01 ; 9-18-2007 by Ord. No. 2007-05 ; 6-15-2010 by Ord. No. 2010-04 ; 7-17-2012 by Ord. No. 2012-03 ; 8-18-2015 by Bill No. 2015-13 ; 11-20-2019 by Bill No. 2019-19 ; 4-2-2024 by Bill No. 2024-04 ]*

A. A certain document, copies of which are on file in the Division of Permits and Inspections, being marked and designated as the "2021 International Building Code (IBC)," is hereby adopted as the Building Code of Cecil County, Maryland, for the control of buildings and structures as herein provided; and each and all of the regulations, provisions, penalties, conditions and terms of the 2021 International Building Code (IBC) are hereby referred to, adopted and made a part hereof, as if fully set out in this chapter, with additions, insertions, deletions and changes, if any, prescribed in § 157-2 of this chapter.

B. Further, the document marked and designated as the "2021 International Residential Code" be and the same is hereby adopted as an addition to the Building Code of Cecil County, Maryland, and each and all regulations and provisions and terms of the 2021 International Residential Code are hereby referred to, adopted and made a part hereof, as if fully set out in this chapter, with the additions, insertions, deletions and changes, if any, prescribed in § 157-2 of this chapter.

C. In addition to the codes listed in Subsections A and B above, local jurisdictions are responsible for the implementation and enforcement of the Maryland Building Performance Standards.
