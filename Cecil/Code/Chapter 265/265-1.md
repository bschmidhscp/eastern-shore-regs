# § 265-1. Permit required.

A. It shall be unlawful for any person to publicly sell, hawk or peddle any articles of merchandise within the unincorporated boundaries of Cecil County without having first obtained a permit from the Cecil County Department of Permits and Inspections, except as exempted herein.

B. In the areas of the County which are incorporated, it is the responsibility of the peddler/hawker to check for license or permit requirements within those jurisdictions.
