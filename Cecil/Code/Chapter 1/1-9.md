# § 1-9. Repealer.

All ordinances or parts of ordinances inconsistent with the provisions contained in the Code adopted by this ordinance are hereby repealed; provided, however, that such repeal shall only be to the extent of such inconsistency, and any valid legislation of the Cecil County which is not in conflict with the provisions of the Code shall be deemed to remain in full force and effect.
