# § 1-1. Legislative intent.

The ordinances and public local laws of Cecil County of a general and permanent nature, adopted by the Board of County Commissioners of Cecil County and the State Legislature, as revised, codified and consolidated into chapters and sections and consisting of Chapters 1 through 365, together with an Appendix, are hereby approved, adopted, ordained and enacted as the "Code of Cecil County, Maryland," hereinafter known and referred to as the "Code."
