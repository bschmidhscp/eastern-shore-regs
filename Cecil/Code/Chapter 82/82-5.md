# § 82-5. Resignation and removal.

A. Any member of the CCPAB or CCACC may resign at any time by providing written notice to the Cecil County Executive.

B. In addition to the grounds set forth above, a member of the CCPAB or CCACC appointed by the Cecil County Executive may be removed in accordance with the following provisions:

  (1) The Cecil County Executive retains the authority to remove any member at any time when the member:

    (a) No longer meets any of the membership criteria in § 82-4;

    (b) Violates the Cecil County Code of Ethics;

    (c) Fails to maintain confidentiality;

    (d) Violates the State's Open Meetings Act;

    (e) Is removed upon the recommendation of a majority vote of the CCPAB when, in its discretion, the best interest of the community, Board or Commission would not be served by continued membership; or

    (f) Just cause.

  (2) The Chairperson shall notify the Cecil County Executive if a member of the CCPAB or of the CCACC misses three scheduled meetings in any twelve-month period without an excused absence. The Cecil County Executive shall remove and replace such member.
