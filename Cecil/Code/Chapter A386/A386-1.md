# § A386-1. Purpose.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish a County transfer of development rights program that will create a bank to purchase and sell development rights. It is the purpose of this chapter to provide another method to assist landowners wishing to retain their properties in agricultural use and assist landowners wishing to realize higher densities in the growth area.
