# § A381-1. Title and purpose.

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the "Cecil County Cable Service Franchising Act" (this "chapter"). The purposes of this chapter are to: (i) establish the terms and conditions under which a grantee occupying the public rights-of-way must operate within Cecil County; (ii) provide for the payment of a franchise fee to the County for use of the public rights-of-way and other costs associated with administering and regulating cable service; and (iii) enhance the County's authority to grant a cable service franchise while managing the public rights-of-way.
