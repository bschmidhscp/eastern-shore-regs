# § A381-6. Franchise renewal.

Franchise renewals shall be conducted in accordance with applicable law, including, but not limited to, the Cable Act, as amended. The County and a grantee, by mutual consent, may enter into renewal negotiations at any time during the term of a franchise agreement.
