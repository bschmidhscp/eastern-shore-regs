# § 318-16. Appeals.

A. Any person whose Cecil County commercial refuse hauler's license or whose privilege of using Cecil County solid waste disposal facilities has been suspended or revoked by decision of the Chief of the Solid Waste Management Division for violation of these rules and regulations may appeal that decision to the Cecil County Director/Deputy Director of Public Works.

B. Any person whose Cecil County commercial refuse hauler's license or whose privilege of using Cecil County solid waste disposal facilities has been suspended or revoked for violation of these rules and regulations may appeal decisions by the Cecil County Director/Deputy Director of Public Works to the Circuit Court for Cecil County; otherwise, all decisions shall be final.
