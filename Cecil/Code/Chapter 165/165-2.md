# § 165-2. Snow removal in cemeteries.

A. A funeral director shall first make arrangements with the responsible group for the County or state equipment to enter the cemetery for snow removal for funerals only. The funeral director shall be responsible to the responsible group for having the caretaker at the entrance of the cemetery to direct the route of the equipment.

B. If this procedure is followed, the County shall not stand responsible or liable for any damage caused by the equipment in the snow removal process in the cemetery.
