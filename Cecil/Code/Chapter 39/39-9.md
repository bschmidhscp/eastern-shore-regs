# § 39-9. Advisory opinions.

A. Any person subject to this chapter may request an advisory opinion from the Commission concerning the application of this chapter.

B. The Commission shall respond promptly to a request for an advisory opinion and shall provide interpretations of this chapter based on the facts provided or reasonably available to the Commission in an expeditious manner, not to exceed 60 days from the request date.

C. In accordance with all applicable State and County laws regarding public records, the Commission shall publish or otherwise make available to the public copies of the advisory opinions, with the identities of the subjects deleted, provided content of such disclosure would not otherwise indicate the identity of the subject. Opinions may be released in their entirety if the subject waives confidentiality.

D. The Commission may adopt additional policies and procedures related to the advisory opinion request process.
