# § 39-31. Retroactive gift disclosure.

On or before April 30 of each year during which an official or employee holds office, those officials and employees specified in § 39-29 of this article shall file a statement disclosing gifts received during the preceding calendar year from any person who contracts with or is regulated by Cecil County, including the name of the donor of the gift and the approximate retail value at the time of receipt.
