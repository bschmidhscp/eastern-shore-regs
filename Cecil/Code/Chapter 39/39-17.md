# § 39-17. Use of prestige of office.

A. An official or employee may not intentionally use the prestige of office or public position for the private gain of that official or employee or the private gain of another, including, but not limited to, influencing the award of a local contract to a specific person or entity, initiating a solicitation for a person or entity to retain the compensated services of a particular lobbyist or firm, or using public resources or title to solicit a political contribution regulated in accordance with the Election Law Article, Annotated Code of Maryland, and/or the provisions of this Code.

B. This section does not prohibit the performance of usual and customary constituent services by an elected official without additional compensation.
