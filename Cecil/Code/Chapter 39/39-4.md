# § 39-4. Membership; terms.

A. There shall be a Cecil County Ethics Commission, which is an independent agency of Cecil County Government that consists of five members.

B. Three members, including the Chair, shall be appointed by the County Executive; two members shall be appointed by the County Council.

C. The members of the Cecil County Ethics Commission are volunteers and shall serve a four-year staggered term.

D. No more than three members of the Commission shall be affiliated with the same political party.

E. A Commission member may serve until a successor is appointed and qualified.
