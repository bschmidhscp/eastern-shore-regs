# § 39-40. Severability.

If any section, sentence, clause, or phrase of this chapter is held invalid or unconstitutional by any Court of competent jurisdiction, the ruling shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this chapter.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: The former provisions of Art. VII , Enforcement, which immediately followed, were repealed 5-5-2026 by Bill No. 2026-05.
