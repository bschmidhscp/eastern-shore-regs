# § 39-20. Participation in procurement.

A. An individual or a person who employs an individual who assists a County agency or unit in the drafting of specifications, an invitation for bids or a request for proposals for a procurement may not submit a bid or proposal for that procurement or assist or represent another person directly or indirectly who is submitting a bid or proposal for the procurement.

B. The Commission may establish exemptions from the requirements of this section for providing descriptive literature, sole-source procurements, and written comments solicited by the procuring agency.
