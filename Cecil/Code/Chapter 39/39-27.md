# § 39-27. Interests of person making statement.

For the purposes of § 39-26A, B and C of this chapter, the following interests are considered to be the interests of the individual making the statement:

A. An interest held by a member of the individual's immediate family, if the interest was, at any time during the reporting period, directly or indirectly controlled by the individual.

B. An interest held by a business entity in which the individual held a 30% or greater interest at any time during the reporting period.

C. An interest held by a trust or an estate in which, at any time during the reporting period: the individual held a reversionary interest or was a beneficiary; or, if a revocable trust, the individual as a settlor.
