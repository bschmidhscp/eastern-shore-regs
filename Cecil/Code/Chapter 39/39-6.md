# § 39-6. Role of County Attorney.

A. The County Attorney shall assist the Commission in carrying out the Commission's duties.

B. If a conflict of interest under Article III of this chapter or other conflict prohibits the County Attorney from assisting with the Commission in a matter, the County shall provide sufficient funds for the Commission to hire independent counsel for the duration of the conflict.
