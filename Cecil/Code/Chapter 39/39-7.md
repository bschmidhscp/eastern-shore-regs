# § 39-7. Budget and expenses.

A. The Commission, with the assistance of the Director of Administration, shall develop and submit a budget to the County Executive on or before February 1 of each year.

B. The Commission shall be reimbursed for expenses reasonably incurred in connection with the work of the Commission.
