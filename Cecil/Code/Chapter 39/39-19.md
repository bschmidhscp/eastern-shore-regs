# § 39-19. Disclosure of confidential information.

Other than in the discharge of official duties, an official or employee or a former official or employee may not disclose or use confidential information that the official or employee or former official or employee acquired by reason of the official's or employee's public position and that is not available to the public, for the economic benefit of the official or employee or former official or employee or that of another person.

A. An official or employee shall not retaliate against an individual for reporting or participating in an investigation of a potential ethics violation.
