# § 225-1. Adoption of standards.

*[1968, ch. 214, § 1; 1970 Code, Sec. 11-1; 1982, ch. 228, § 1; 1988, ch. 227, § 2]*

The Cecil County Council may adopt a Housing Code, which code shall stipulate minimum housing standards in order to make dwellings fit for human habitation.
