# § 365-16. Connection specifications.

The connection of the building sewer into the public sewer shall conform to the requirements of the building and plumbing codes or other applicable rules and regulations of the County. All connections of building sewers into the public sewer shall be performed by persons authorized by the County. The prescribed procedures and materials must be approved by the County before installation.
