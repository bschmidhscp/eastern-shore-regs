# § 365-4. Pollution of waters prohibited.

It shall be unlawful to discharge to any natural outlet within the County or in any area under the jurisdiction of said County any sewage or other polluted waters, except where suitable treatment has been provided in accordance with subsequent provisions of this Part 2.
