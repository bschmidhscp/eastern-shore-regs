# Article X. Standards Adopted


| [By virtue of Chapter 97 of the Code of Cecil County, the Cecil County Council may establish standards for the construction of private and public water and sewer treatment facilities, collection systems and distribution systems. The updated Cecil County Water and Sewer Standards, as amended, are available for inspection in the office of the Department of Public Works.] |
