# § 365-31. Right of entry.

The Director of the Department of Public Works and other duly authorized employees or representatives of the County bearing proper credentials and identification shall be permitted to enter all properties for the purpose of inspection, observation, measurement, sampling and testing in accordance with the provisions of this Part 2.
