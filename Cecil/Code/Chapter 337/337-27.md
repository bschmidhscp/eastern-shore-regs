# § 337-27. When deferred taxes due.

The total amount of real property taxes deferred and interest accrued are due and payable when:

A. The homeowner ceases to own the property;

B. The homeowner ceases to occupy the property as his or her principal place of residence;

C. The property becomes subject to a tax sale; or

D. The homeowner fails to submit a timely application for deferral. An application for deferral must be submitted each year a deferral is sought.
