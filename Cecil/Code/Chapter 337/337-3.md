# § 337-3. Definitions.

As used in this article, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**AGRICULTURAL LAND** Real property subject to an easement or other interest that has, pursuant to Title 2, Subtitle 5, of the Agriculture Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, been approved for the establishment of an agricultural district or real property which has been permanently conveyed or assigned to the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation.
