# § 337-34. Imposition of tax; rate.

A. The County Council, at the request of the County Executive, may impose, by resolution, a hotel rental tax.

B. That the County hotel rental tax for the taxable year beginning July 1, 2017, and ending June 30, 2018, is hereby imposed on the population centers currently identified by the County and subject to the hotel rental tax.

C. That the rate of County hotel rental tax for Fiscal Year 2018 is hereby fixed at 6%.
