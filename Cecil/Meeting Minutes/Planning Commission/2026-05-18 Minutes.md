**FY 2027 - Capital Improvement Program** 

- **DPW – Roads & Bridges: Repair McCauley Road Bridge over Basin Run – Pg. 39 of 76.** 

- **Parks & Recreation: Indoor Recreation Center – Pg. 48 of 76.** 

Scott Flanigan, Director of the Department of Public Works, appeared and presented an overview of the program. 

Mr. Flanigan explained that there are two additions to the fiscal year 2027 Capital Improvement Program, those being McCauley Road Bridge repair and the proposed Indoor Recreation Center located on North East Road. 

- McCauley Road is a single span bridge constructed in 1885. The bridge is structural deficient with a weight restriction on it. The repairs will repair and replace steel cords, floor beams and other elements as need while maintaining the bridges historic character. This will extend the life of the bridge by 25-50 years. The FY 2027 CIP proposes to fund both the design and construction in fiscal year 2027. 

- The indoor recreation center will be an approximately 77,000 square foot recreation center with various sporting amenities including but not limited to, a six lane running track, long jump runway and landing pit, triple jump runway with landing pit, pole vault runway, high jump area, multi-use courts for basketball, volleyball, and pickleball, an auxiliary gym for wrestling matches, yoga classrooms, office space for facility, restrooms, concession of premade snacks and drinks. Preliminary planning and design has been funded using operating funds. The FY2027 CIP seeks funding for design and construction in fiscal year 2027. Should the Council approve this project, the completion of the center should be in late summer or early fall of 2028.
