## COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY LEGISLATIVE SESSION MINUTES LSD 2026-12 

June 16, 2026 

The County Council of Cecil County met in Legislative Session at the Elkton Municipal Building, 100 Railroad Avenue, Elkton, MD. The following members of the Council were present: 

Al Miller, Council President 

Robert Meffley, Council Vice President Rebecca Hamilton, Council Member Donna Culberson, Council Member Dawn Branch, Council Member 

NOTE: The recording with transcription of this meeting is available on the County website at www.cecilcountymd.gov 

## CALL TO ORDER 

The meeting of the County Council of Cecil County of June 2, 2026, was called to order by Council President Miller at 7:00 p.m. 

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 

President Miller led all those in attendance in the Pledge of Allegiance. 

ROLL CALL 

Council Manager Henson called the roll and noted that five Council members were present. 

# OPENING PRAYER/MOMENT OF SILENCE 

Pastor Shaun Taylor, Porters Grove Baptist Church, Rising Sun, offered the opening prayer. 

## APPROVAL OF AGENDA 

On a motion made by Vice President Meffley, seconded by Councilwoman Branch, the Council moved to approve the agenda ofJune 16, 2026. The motion was approved unanimously. 

### PRESENTATION 

MD 250" Moment — Reading of “Letter to the Future” by County Executive Adam Streight County Executive Adam Streight read a “Letter to the Future”, commemorating Cecil County’s recognition of the United States’ 250' Anniversary. 

MD 250" Moment — Reading of “Treachery and Consequences in Revolutionary Times” by Councilwoman Rebecca Hamilton 

Councilwoman Hamilton read “Treachery and Consequences in Revolutionary Times”, highlighting Cecil County's history during the American Revolution. 

### PROCLAMATION 

Retirement — Gene Daley, Commander, VFW Post 6027 North East 

Council and County Executive Streight presented a Proclamation to Gene Daley, Commander, VFW Post 6027 North East, commemorating his retirement. Executive Streight read the Proclamation into the record of the minutes. 

## APPROVAL OF MINUTES 

On a motion made by Councilwoman Culberson, seconded by Councilwoman Hamilton, the Council moved to approve the minutes ofthe June 2, 2026 Legislative Session. 

## PUBLIC COMMENTS 

Council Manager Henson stated that there were no speakers.

Councilwoman Hamilton reminded all in attendance to vote during Early Voting, or during the Primary Election, which takes place on June 23%. 

Councilwoman Culberson stated that the last day of Early Voting is June 18°".

Vice President Meffley reminded all in attendance that Juneteenth will be recognized on June 19th, 

Vice President Meffley offered congratulations to Elkton Mayor Rob Alt, who was inducted into the Maryland Municipal League (MML) Hall of Fame on June 16". He stated Mayor Alt was recognized for 20 years of service to the Elkton community. 

President Miller offered congratulations to Eagle Scout recipient Jack Edlers. President Miller reminded all in attendance to vote during Early Voting or the Primary Election. President Miller announced that the Cecil County Fair will commence on July 24*.
