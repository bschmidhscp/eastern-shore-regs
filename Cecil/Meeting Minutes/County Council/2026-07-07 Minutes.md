# County Council minutes 2026-07-07

<!-- page 1 -->
COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY WORK SESSION MINUTES 

July 7, 2026 

OPEN SESSION — Elk Room 

4:30 P.M. 

NOTE: The recording and transcription of this meeting are available on the County website at www.cecilcountymd.gov 

PRESENT: Council Members: Al Miller, Donna Culberson and Dawn Branch; Council Manager L. Michelle Henson; Maggie Tome, IT Solutions Developer; members of the Administration and members of the public. 

### Call to Order 

President Miller called the meeting to order at 4:30 p.m. and announced that there were three Council Members present which constitute a quorum. 

## Approval of Minutes 

On a motion made by Councilwoman Culberson, seconded by Councilwoman Branch, the Council moved to approve the minutes of the Council Work Session of June 16, 2026. The motion passed unanimously. 

### Board of Health 

President Miller recessed the Work Session at 4:31 pm to conduct the Board of Health meeting. 

President Miller reconvened the Work Session upon adjournment ofthe Board of Health meeting at 5:37 pm and resumed the Work Session agenda. 

Review of Legislative Agenda 

Introduction of Resolutions 

Resolution No. 31-2026 

Council Manager Henson stated for the record: 

Resolution No. 31-2026 Appointment — Planning Commission — Rob Carey and Gary Rasnake Director of Administration Schneckenburger requested Council’s support to reappoint Rob Carey and Gary Rasnake to the Planning Commission. 

Resolution No. 32-2026 

Council Manager Henson stated for the record: 

Resolution No. 32-2026 Appointment — Plumbing Board — Ronald Dixon, Jesse Foster and Mark 

#### Freese 

Director of Administration Schneckenburger requested Council’s support to reappoint Ronald Dixon, Jesse Foster and Mark Freese to the Plumbing Board, stating they served the Board well. Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated that Resolution No. 32-2026 addressed three expiring terms. 

Resolution No. 33-2026 

Council Manager Henson stated for the record: Resolution No. 33-2026 Appointment — Economic Development Commission — John “Jack” Schammel 

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated Cecil County Chamber of Commerce Chief Executive Officer Jack Schammel’s appointment to the Economic Development Commission is in response to the retirement of current member Danny DeMarinis. 

### Resolution No. 34-2026 

Council Manager Henson stated for the record: 

Resolution No. 34-2026 Appointment — Susquehanna Workforce Network Board — Sandra Edwards, Andrew Meyer, and Jeff Sellers 

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated that Resolution No. 34-2026 provides for the reappointment of Sandra Edwards and Andrew Meyer and the appointment of new member Jeff Sellers, who is replacing current member Danny DeMarinis.

Resolution No. 27-2026 Appointment — Department of Social Services Board — David Rudolph, Dianne Fox, and Georgia Galicki

# Council Manager Report 

Council Manager Henson stated that the report was placed in Council’s meeting packet. Council Manager Henson noted that the Maryland Association of Counties (MACo) requested input regarding the MACo Legislative Priorities List for 2027. 

# Council Member Items 

Council deferred Council Member Items to the Legislative Session. 

## Adjournment 

President Miller requested a motion to adjourn the Work Session. Councilwoman Culberson made motion to adjourn the Work Session, which was seconded by Councilwoman Branch. 

President Miller adjourned the work session at 5:44 pm. 

President Miller announced that the next Work Session will be held on Tuesday, July 14, 2026 at 4:30 pm in the Elk Room of<sup>the County AdministrationBuilding.Thesessionwillbelivestreamed</sup> on the County Council’s webpage at www.cecilcountymd.gov
