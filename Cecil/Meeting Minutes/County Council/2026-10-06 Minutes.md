COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY
LEGISLATIVE SESSION MINUTES

LSD 2026-19

October 6, 2026

The County Council of Cecil County met in Legislative Session at the County Administration
Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd., Elk Room, Elkton, MD. The following members of the Council
were present:

Al Miller, Council President

Bob Meffley, Council Vice President
Rebecca Hamilton, Council Member
Dawn Branch, Council Member

NOTE: The recording of this meeting is available on the County website at cecilcountymd.gov

CALL TO ORDER

The meeting of the County Council of Cecil County of October 6, 2026, was called to order by
Council President Miller at 7:02 p.m.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
President Miller led all those in attendance in the Pledge of Allegiance.

ROLL CALL
Council Manager Henson called the roll and noted that four Council members were present.

OPENING PRAYER/MOMENT OF SILENCE
Pastor Hyang Ahn, Ebenezer Global Methodist Church, offered the Opening Prayer.

APPROVAL OF AGENDA

On a motion made by Councilwoman Hamilton, simultaneously seconded by Vice President
Meffley and Councilwoman Branch, the Council moved to approve the agenda of October 6,
2026. The motion was approved unanimously.

PRESENTATIONS

MD 250% Moment — Reading of “19*" Century Tourism in Rising Sun” — President Al Miller
President Miller read “19*" Century Tourism in Rising Sun’, illustrating the popularity of Rising
Sun as a vacation destination in the 1800's.

President Miller expressed appreciation to Tourism Manager Sandy Turner for her coordination
of the MD 250" scripts.

Proclamations

Breast Cancer Awareness Month

Councilwoman Branch read the Breast Cancer Awareness Month Proclamation into the record
of the minutes.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES
On a motion made by Vice President Meffley, seconded by Councilwoman Hamilton, the Council
moved to approve the minutes of the September 15, 2026 Legislative Session.

PUBLIC COMMENTS

Mr. Sean Brimm spoke of the economy, at-large voting, solar energy and the upcoming election.
Ms. Teresa DiFabbio spoke of Divine Grace.

Ms. Michelle Ravert spoke of advocacy between the County and the Office of Delegate and data
centers.

Ms. Karen Kelly spoke about dialogue between the public and Council.

PRESIDENT AND COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilwoman Branch thanked the audience for attending the meeting.

Councilwoman Branch noted that she attended the Cecil College Scholarship Breakfast on
September 23%, stated the event provides the opportunity to speak directly to scholarship
recipients.

Councilwoman Branch stated she attended the Cecil County Public Schools (CCPS) Citizen
Advisory Committee (CAC) meeting on October 5'", adding that she represents Council as an ex-
officio member of the committee. She stated that the committee discussed recently passed
legislation that bans cell phone use by students at the beginning of the 2027-2028 school year.
She added that the CAC also discussed the State’s new policy that will test student reading
proficiency, noting that this policy will also go into effect during the 2027-2028 school year.
Councilwoman Branch, noting Council’s meeting rules, advised Ms. Kelly that her questions were
not being ignored during the Public Comment period.

Councilwoman Hamilton thanked the audience for attending the meeting, adding her
appreciation for public involvement with local government. She noted that agreement is not
always guaranteed, adding that it is better to have conversations than no communication.
Councilwoman Hamilton stated she attended the Pregnancy and Resource Center Banquet on
September 29*, stating her appreciation for the faith-based organization. She noted that 58
lives were saved this year, adding that the center is a great resource in Cecil County.

Vice President Meffley thanked the audience for attending the meeting, stating that Council is
eager to hear what citizens have to say.

Vice President Meffley stated he attended the Upper Shore Regional Council (USRC) Economic
Development workshop on September 16*, stating that representatives from businesses
throughout the region attended. He stated that the USRC distributed a survey to Cecil, Kent and
Queen Anne’s Counties to determine economic development priorities.

Vice President Meffley stated he attended the Cecil College Foundation Scholarship Breakfast
on September 23 and expressed his appreciation for the opportunity to speak directly with
students.

Vice President Meffley offered his congratulations to Mr. Ed Durham, recipient of the Mary A.
Maloney Distinguished Service Award, which was presented during the Scholarship Breakfast.

President Miller thanked the audience for attending the meeting.

President Miller stated that Cecil Cares provided many volunteer opportunities throughout Cecil
County on October 3° and 4", adding that he participated in cleanup activities at the Fair Hill
Nature Center on October 4",

President Miller stated that Council welcomes questions and comments but is not compelled to
answer queries regarding specific situations over which Council has no jurisdiction.

President Miller advised the audience that the best way to learn about government processes
is to attend meetings, noting that agendas and minutes are posted to the County’s website.

Resolution No. 55-2026

Council Manager Henson stated for the record:

Resolution No. 55-2026 Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds — Maryland Rural Health
Transformation Program Fund — Departments of Emergency Services and Community Services

A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County,
Maryland for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $1,144,170.00,
with $399,170.00 to be allocated to the Department of Emergency Services Grants Fund —
Mobile Integrated Healthcare Program — Fund 198; $475,000.00 to be allocated to the
Community Services Grants Fund — Cecil Transit Division — Fund 109, Department 522; and
$270,000.00 to be allocated to the Community Services Grants Fund — Human Services Division
— Fund 109, Department 532, to record a Rural Health Transformation Program grant awarded
by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) through the Maryland Department of
Health and Cecil County Health Department. Grant funds will be used to support the Mobile
Integrated Healthcare Program, increase transportation access to healthcare and address food
insecurity within Cecil County, respectively.

Introduced by Council President at the request of the County Executive.

Introduced and ordered posted on October 6, 2026; and scheduled for consideration on October
20, 2026.

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated that Resolution No. 55-2026 recognizes grant
funds for the Departments of Emergency Services and Community Services. He noted that Health
Officer Karyl Rattay spearheaded the effort to obtain the grants.

Resolution No. 50-2026

Council Manager Henson stated for the record:

Resolution No. 50-2026 Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds — Administrative Office of
the Courts Security Administration — Court Security Officer Support Grant Program — Circuit
Court

A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland

for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $173,155.32 to the Circuit
Court — Court Security Officer Support Grant Program, to record a grant awarded through the
Administrative Office of the Courts. Grant funds will be used to hire three (3) additional courtroom

bailiffs.
Introduced by Council President at the request of the County Executive.
