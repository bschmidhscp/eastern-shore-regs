## PRESIDENT AND COUNCIL COMMENTS 

Councilwoman Culberson provided Emergency Response Services Intel for the previous week, reporting 252 calls, 15 of which were mental health related; 36 domestic violence calls, 20 of which were physical; and 3 overdoses, none of which were fatal. She stated that during 26 of the past 33 weeks, there were 0 overdose related fatalities. Councilwoman Culberson stated that Mt. Zoar AME Church in Conowingo will recognize its 145 anniversary in December. She stated the AME Church property includes a cemetery in which soldiers who fought in the Civil War were laid to rest. 

Councilwoman Hamilton thanked the members of the audience for attending the meeting. 

Councilwoman Branch thanked the members of the audience for attending the meeting. Councilwoman Branch noted that the new school year commenced on August 31° and wished students and school staff a safe and successful year. Councilwoman Branch thanked first responders for their service. 

President Miller noted that the recent rains have been beneficial to farmers, adding that roadside weeds have also benefitted. He noted that harvest will begin soon, and requested roadside maintenance of weeds and low branches, which can impact large farm equipment as farmers travel roads.
