## CALL TO ORDER 

The meeting of the County Council of Cecil County of August 4, 2026, was called to order by Council President Miller at 7:00 p.m. 

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 

President Miller led all those in attendance in the Pledge of Allegiance.

Council Manager Henson called the roll and noted that five Council members were present.

Pastor Ben Dixon, Porters Grove Baptist Church of Rising Sun, offered the opening prayer. 

## APPROVAL OF AGENDA 

On a motion made by Councilwoman Branch, seconded by Councilwoman Culberson, the Council moved to approve the agenda of August 4, 2026. The motion was approved unanimously. 

### PRESENTATIONS 

MD 250% Moment — Reading of “Autos Arrive!” — President Al Miller 

President Miller read “Autos Arrive!”, highlighting the impact of automobiles in Cecil County. 

## PROCLAMATION 

Cecil Quilters 

County Executive Adam Streight and Council presented a Proclamation to the Cecil Quilters in recognition of their creation and donation of a quilt commemorating the 250" Anniversary of the United States. A copy of the Proclamation was read and placed in the record of the minutes. 

### PUBLIC HEARING 

Bill No. 2026-06 

President Miller opened the Public Hearing at 7:11 pm. Council Manager Henson stated for the record: 

Bill No. 2026-06 Establishment of the Sanitary Sewer Benefit Assessment for the Little Elk Creek 

Sanitary Subdistrict 

An Act to establish the Sanitary Sewer Benefit Assessment for the Little Elk Creek Sanitary Subdistrict in the amount of $35,265 per Equivalent Living Unit (ELU) which shall be applied to all sewer connections in the Little Elk Creek Sanitary Subdistrict commencing July 21, 2027 and continuing through June 30, 2057. 

Introduced by Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced and ordered posted on July 21, 2026; Public Hearing scheduled on August 4, 2026; and scheduled for consideration on August 18, 2026

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated that the County extended sewer service to the Triumph Industrial Park (TIP) several years ago. 

Vice President Meffley queried if new businesses within the TIP would be assessed the fee established by Bill 2026-06. Director of Public Works Scott Flanigan responded in the affirmative. President Miller queried members of the audience for comments regarding Bill No. 2026-06. Mr. Troy Cooper stated that the County did not extend sewer service to the property he owns in the TIP therefore his property should not be assessed. 

Mr. Laki Paliouras expressed concerns that buildings within the TIP had been constructed over sewer lines, resulting in difficult maintenance and repair. He stated the County should assume responsibility for maintenance and repair of all sewer lines within the TIP. Mr. Robert Hodge expressed support for establishment of the Sanitary Sewer Subdistrict, adding that the County should assume ownership and responsibility for all the sewer lines within the TIP. 

President Miller closed the Public Hearing at 7:23 pm.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTIONS Resolution No. 35-2026 

Council Manager Henson stated for the record: Resolution No. 35-2026 Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds — Community Services — Human Services Division — Housing Navigator Funding 

A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $71,017.00 to the Community Services Grants Fund — Department of Community Services, Human Services Division, Fund 109, Department 533, to record a grant awarded from the Department of Housing and Urban Development, through the Maryland State Department of Housing and Community Development. Grant funds will be used to support a part-time Housing Navigator covering both Cecil and Harford counties. Introduced by Council President at the request of the County Executive Introduced and ordered posted on July 21, 2026; and scheduled for consideration on August 4, 2026 ' 

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated that Resolution No. 35-2026 provides for receipt of grant funding for a part-time housing navigator who will provide housing assistance to citizens who are currently in shelters. Councilwoman Hamilton queried if this is a new position. Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated this is a new, part-time position. 

President Miller noted that there is no fiscal impact to the County. 

President Miller requested a motion to approve Resolution No. 35-2026. Councilwoman Culberson made a motion to approve Resolution No. 35-2026, which was seconded by Councilwoman Branch. Roll Call Vote: Hamilton — Y, Culberson —Y, Branch —Y, Meffley -Y, Miller —Y The motion was approved 5-0.
