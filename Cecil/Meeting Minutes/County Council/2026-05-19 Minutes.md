COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY LEGISLATIVE SESSION MINUTES LSD 2026-10 

May 19, 2026 

The County Council of Cecil County met in legislative session at the County Administration Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd., Elk Room, Elkton, MD. The following members of the Council were present: 

Al Miller, Council President Robert Meffley, Council Vice President Rebecca Hamilton, Council Member Dawn Branch, Council Member 

NOTE: The recording with transcription of this meeting is available on the County website at www.cecilcountymd.gov 

## CALL TO ORDER 

The meeting of the County Council of Cecil County of May 19, 2026, was called to order by Council President Miller at 7:00 p.m. 

### PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 

President Miller led all those in attendance in the Pledge of Allegiance. 

ROLL CALL 

Council Manager Henson called the roll and noted that four Council members were present. 

# OPENING PRAYER/MOMENT OF SILENCE 

Reverend Zach Wright, Bethel Lutheran Church of North East, Maryland offered the opening prayer. 

## APPROVAL OF AGENDA 

On motion made by Vice President Meffley, seconded by Councilwoman Branch, the Council moved to approve the agenda of May 19, 2026. The motion was approved unanimously. 

## PRESENTATION OF PROCLAMATIONS 

Emergency Medical Services Week — May 17-23, 2026 

Council recognized May 17-23, 2026 as Emergency Medical Services Week. 

County Executive Adam Streight read the Proclamation into the record of the minutes and joined County Council in presenting the Proclamation to Department of Emergency Services Director Pat Campbell. 

National Public Works Week — May 17-23, 2026 Council recognized May 17-23, 2026 as National Public Works Week. 

Councilwoman Hamilton read the Proclamation into the record of the minutes. County Executive Streight joined Council in presenting the Proclamation to Department of Public Works Director Scott Flanigan. 

## APPROVAL OF MINUTES 

On a motion made by Vice President Meffley, seconded by Councilwoman Branch, the Council moved to approve the minutes of the May 5, 2026 Legislative Session. 

## PUBLIC COMMENTS 

Ms. Michelle Ravert spoke regarding water quality at Harbor View. 

Ms. Ravert advised Council that she initiated a formal complaint with the Maryland Attorney General’s Office regarding at large voting in Cecil County. 

## PRESIDENT AND COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilwoman Hamilton stated she attended the Cecil County School of Technology (CCST) Scholarship Night event on May 6" and will attend the Scholarship Awards Event at Elkton High School on May 20**.

Vice President Meffley stated he also attended the Cecil College Commencement Event, stating he was impressed with the student and guest speakers.

President Miller stated he attended the Cecil College Commencement Event on May 17", stating the event was well done. President Miller expressed congratulations to, and pride in his daughter, who received her Nursing Pin. 

President Miller noted that Early Voting will take place June 11 — 18" at the County Administration Building. He stated that the June 16‘ Work Session and Legislative Session will be conducted at the Elkton Municipal Building, adding that he will request a vote from Council on June 2"4 regarding meeting location.
