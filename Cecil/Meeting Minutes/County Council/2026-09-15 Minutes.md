## September 15, 2026 

The County Council of Cecil County met in Legislative Session at the County Administration Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd., Elk Room, Elkton, MD. The following members of the Council were present: 

Al Miller, Council President

### CALL TO ORDER 

The meeting of the County Council of Cecil County of September 15, 2026, was called to order by Council President Miller at 7:00 p.m. 

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 

President Miller led all those in attendance in the Pledge of Allegiance. 

ROLL CALL 

Council Manager Henson called the roll and noted that five Council members were present. 

# OPENING PRAYER/MOMENT OF SILENCE 

Pastor Sean Hurm, Cherry Hill United Methodist Church, offered the Opening Prayer.

On a motion made by Councilwoman Branch, seconded by Councilwoman Culberson, the Council moved to approve the agenda of September 15, 2026. The motion was approved unanimously.

### APPROVAL OF MINUTES 

On a motion simultaneously made by Councilwoman Branch and Vice President Meffley, seconded by Councilwoman Culberson, the Council moved to approve the minutes of the September 1, 2026 Legislative Session. 

### PUBLIC COMMENTS 

Council Manager Henson stated that there were no speakers. 

### PRESIDENT AND COUNCIL COMMENTS 

Vice President Meffley stated that he was honored to attend the 911 Ceremony in Chesapeake City on September 11". 

Vice President Meffley stated he attended an event honoring the Sentman family on September 11". He stated the family business has been sold, and the last day of operation is October 2™, He expressed his desire for another entity to keep the business running, noting that the family did not want to sell the enterprise. 

Vice President Meffley stated that the Maryland Rural Development Corporation (MRDC) is seeking a circuit rider to draft grants. He stated that the MRDC provides Head Start programs; noting that the Cecilton facility serves 29 children. He added his appreciation that Secretary Day from the Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development toured the Head Start facility in Greensboro during the MRDC meeting. 

Councilwoman Branch advised those in attendance that Council, during the earlier Work Session, discussed a proposed ballot question regarding the deadline for presentation of the County’s annual budget. 

She stated the proposed question is specific to Cecil County, noting that when the State of Maryland’s budget is not finalized until the last minute, Cecil County’s budget process can be negatively impacted. 

Councilwoman Hamilton stated that the Cecil County Chamber of Commerce (Chamber) will conduct a Candidate Forum on September 30" at 6:00 pm at Elkton High School. She stated the Chamber will hold subsequent, virtual forums for Delegate and Senate candidates. Councilwoman Hamilton announced that the “Remembering Our Fallen” National Traveling Military Memorial will be on display at Cecil Arena September 18 — 20. 

Councilwoman Culberson thanked the members of the audience for attending the meeting. Councilwoman Culberson noted that she did not receive Emergency Services intel prior to the meeting. 

President Miller thanked the audience for attending the meeting. President Miller commended Land Use and Development Services (LUDS) Director Stephen O'Connor for his Work Session presentation regarding solar projects in Cecil County.
