Fund Balance to the State of Maryland — State Department of Assessments and Taxation in the amount of $275,495 for the additional funds to correct an administrative discrepancy. Introduced by Council President at the request of the County Executive 

Introduced and ordered posted on March 3, 2026; and scheduled for consideration on March 17, 2026 

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated that Resolution No. 07-2026 was discussed during the earlier Work Session, and deferred questions to Deputy Director of Finance Andrew Ondish. 

Councilwoman Hamilton, referring to Deputy Director Ondish’s presentation during the earlier Work Session, stated that Cecil County received notice from the State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT) that an adjustment of $100,000 was identified as a credit when it should have been identified as a charge in Fiscal Year 2025 (FY25) and that the subsequent adjustment was slightly more than what was credited. Deputy Director Ondish concurred. 

Councilwoman Hamilton requested clarification of how the $275,495 identified in Resolution No. 07-2026 was determined. Deputy Director Ondish stated that SDAT advised the County of the billing error in 2025, after the County’s budget was passed. He added that SDAT did not provide the final amount due until September 2025, at which time the County received an invoice. Deputy Director Ondish stated that, during the time that SDAT became aware of the error, the County was advised of an increase in Local County Cost Share Billing from 50% to 90%. He stated this resulted in a $247,000 shortfall of the $800,000 that was originally budgeted for Local County Cost Share Billing. 

Councilwoman Hamilton queried if the requested $247,000 would satisfy the County’s obligation. Deputy Director Ondish stated that the final obligation cannot be determined until the State of Maryland Budget is adopted.
