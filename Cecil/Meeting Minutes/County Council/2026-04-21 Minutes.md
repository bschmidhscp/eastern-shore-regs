Town of Elkton (the Town) Director of Planning Jeanne Minner stated the property referenced in Annexation A1-2026 is owned by PECO Energy, adding that the Town’s Attorney confirmed that the Town could initiate annexation without a Petition for Annexation from the property owner. She stated that annexation of this property avoids creation of an unincorporated island. She stated that the Maryland Department of Planning did not provide comments regarding the annexation. 

Director of Administration Dan Schneckenburger stated that Administration had no opposition to the annexation, adding that Belle Hill Road is now owned by the Town. 

President Miller queried those in attendance for comments in favor of, or opposition to Resolution No. 13-2026. Hearing no comments, President Miller queried Council for questions or comments. 

Vice President Meffley queried if the Town’s Mayor & Commissioners (M&C) approved the Annexation. Director Minner replied that the M&C approved Annexation A1-2026 on April 15", contingent upon Council’s approval of the waiver of the 5-year rule. 

Councilwoman Hamilton stated that Council received a letter from Town Attorney David Beste regarding the Town’s authority to annex the PECO property without a Petition for Annexation. She queried Council Attorney Lance Young for his opinion regarding Town Attorney David Beste’s letter. Council Attorney Young concurred with Town Attorney Beste’s opinion that the Town could initiate annexation on tax-exempt property without a Petition for Annexation from the owner. 

Councilwoman Hamilton queried the purpose of rezoning ofthe parcel. Director Minner stated the purpose of rezoning the parcel was to provide consistency in zoning designations. Councilwoman Hamilton queried the development plans for the parcel. Director Minner stated that no development will occur on this parcel. Councilwoman Hamilton queried if the rezoning request was dependent upon Resolution Nos. 14-2026 and 15-2026. Director Minner answered in the affirmative. 

Hearing no additional comments, President Miller closed the Public Hearing at 7:12 p.m.

Resolution No. 15-2026 

President Miller opened the Public Hearing at 7:17 p.m. Council Manager Henson stated for the record: Resolution No. 15-2026 — Annexation — Town of Elkton — 17 Swallow Drive (Tax Map 027B, Parcel 0808) 

A Resolution to approve the request of the Town of Elkton for a waiver of the five-year hold in accordance with § 4-416 of the Local Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland. The request from the Town of Elkton is regarding property owned by JD Stonewall Ayars Development, LLC, located on the south side of Belle Hill Road and north of Route 279, consisting of approximately 3.5910 acres of land, identified on Tax Map 0278, Parcel 0808, which is being annexed into the Town of Elkton pursuant to Town of Elkton Annexation Resolution A3-2026. The property's proposed rezoning from Cecil County’s High Density Residential District (RM) to the Town of Elkton’s Highway Interchange Commercial (C3) zoning upon its annexation will result in substantially different uses, or substantially higher density, exceeding 50%, than could be granted for proposed development under the County’s RM zoning. 

Introduced by Council President at the request of the County Executive. 

Introduced and ordered posted on April 7, 2026; Public Hearing scheduled on April 21, 2026; and consideration scheduled on May 5, 2026. 

Director Minner stated this is the final parcel of the previously annexed land identified as the Ayars property. 

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated that Administration had no opposition to Resolution No. 15-2026. 

President Miller queried those in attendance for comments in favor of, or opposition to Resolution No. 15-2026. Hearing no comments, President Miller queried Council for questions or comments. 

Councilwoman Culberson queried the development plans for the parcel. Director Minner stated that the development plans included logistics or warehouses. 

Councilwoman Hamilton queried if the property was included in the Town’s Enterprise Zone. Director Minner answered in the affirmative. 

Councilwoman Hamilton queried the property owner’s plans for the property in the event Council denied the request. Director Minner stated the property owner would wait for the fiveyear period for the parcel to become C3. 

President Miller confirmed that the M&C approved Annexation A3-2026. Director Minner replied that the M&C approved Annexation A3-2026 on April 15th, contingent upon Council’s approval of the waiver of the 5-year rule. 

Vice President Meffley expressed support for Resolution No. 15-2026, stating that the Town’s infrastructure capacity does not support high-density residential development in this area. Councilwoman Hamilton queried if the Town’s infrastructure will support the planned commercial development. Director Minner answered in the affirmative. Hearing no additional comments, President Miller closed the Public Hearing at 7:21 p.m.
