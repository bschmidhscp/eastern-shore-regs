COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY LEGISLATIVE SESSION MINUTES LSD 2026-03 

## February 3, 2026 

The County Council of Cecil County met in Legislative Session at the County Administration Building, Elk Room, 200 Chesapeake Blvd., Elkton, MD. The following members of the Council were present: 

Al Miller, Council President Robert Meffley, Council Vice President Rebecca Hamilton, Council Member Donna Culberson, Council Member Dawn Branch, Council Member 

NOTE: The recording with transcription of this meeting is available on the County website at www.cecilcountymd.gov 

## CALL TO ORDER 

The February 3, 2026 Legislative Session of the Cecil County Council was called to order by Council President Miller at 7:00 p.m. 

PLEDGE OF ALLIEGIANCE 

President Miller led all those in attendance in the Pledge of Allegiance. 

ROLL CALL 

Council Manager Henson called the roll and noted that five members were present. 

# OPENING PRAYER/MOMENT OF SILENCE 

Mr. Brian Hamson, Area Director, Fellowship of Christian Athletes, Perryville Maryland offered the Opening Prayer 

## APPROVAL OF AGENDA 

On a motion made by Councilwoman Branch, seconded by Councilwoman Culberson, the Council moved to approve the agenda of February 3, 2026. The motion was approved unanimously. 

## APPROVAL OF MINUTES 

On a motion made by Vice President Meffley, seconded by Councilwoman Culberson, the Council moved to approve the minutes of the Legislative Session of January 20, 2026. 

### PUBLIC COMMENT 

Mr. Sean Brimm requested posting of updated Ethics Commission meetings and minutes to the County website. Mr. Brimm spoke of national issues including US Immigration and Customs Enforcement, and elections. 

PRESIDENT AND COUNCIL COMMENTS 

Councilwoman Branch, referring to the recent winter storm event, stated the Town of North East acknowledged that clearing of snow was delayed due to equipment failure. Councilwoman Branch announced that the Town of North East Municipal Election is scheduled for February 9". 

Councilwoman Branch announced that a Texas Road House restaurant is coming to the North East area, on Route 40 near the Pat’s restaurant. 

Councilwoman Culberson stated she had not yet received an Emergency Services Intel report. Councilwoman Culberson announced the Fire and Ice event in Port Deposit is scheduled for February 28", adding that she will attend with the Veterans Affairs Commission.

Councilwoman Hamilton commended the Department of Public Works Roads Division for plowing during the recent winter storm event. Councilwoman Hamilton commended the Department of Parks and Recreation for clearing of parking lots and trails during the winter storm event, adding that she saw a person using the walking trail at the Brantwood facility earlier in the day. 

Vice President Meffley stated he attended County Executive Streight’s Fiscal Year 2027 (FY27) Public Budget Hearing on January 22", during which residents talked about the budget and issues including US Immigration and Customs Enforcement in Cecil County and tax relief at the County level. 

Vice President Meffley stated he recently attended a meeting of the Landlord Association of Cecil County, adding that the group had been dormant for several years. He stated that discussion included State regulations regarding landlord tenant issues and requirements that Landlords face prior to selling a rental property. 

President Miller, noted that this was his first experience as Council President during a winter storm event, adding that County Administration, the Department of Public Works Roads Division and Emergency Services met daily prior to and during the storm. He commended all County departments for working together during the storm event. 

President Miller stated he attended the Maryland Association of Counties (MACo) Rural Counties Coalition meeting on February 2". He stated his commitment to have Cecil County’s voice heard in Annapolis. 

### INTRODUCTION OF RESOLUTIONS 

Resolution No. 04-2026 

Council Manager Henson stated for the record: Resolution No. 04-2026 Appointment — Public Safety Pension Plan Board of Trustees — Stephen Brownhill and Colin Shaw A Resolution to confirm the reappointment of Stephen Brownhill and the appointment of Colin Shaw, to terms which expire December 31, 2028, to the Public Safety Pension Plan Board of Trustees. 

Introduced by Council President at the request of the County Executive Introduced and ordered posted on February 3, 2026; and scheduled for consideration on February 17, 2026 

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated Resolution No. 04-2026 provides for the reappointment of Capt. Brownhill and appointment of Mr. Shaw, who replaces Tyler Adams on the Public Safety Pension Plan Board of Trustees. 

## CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTIONS

## ADJOURNMENT 

On a motion made by Councilwoman Culberson, seconded by Councilwoman Branch and approved by all present, President Miller adjourned the meeting at 7:17 p.m.

The Legislative Session will be livestreamed on the County's webpage at www.cecilcountymd.gov The recording and transcription will be uploaded to the County's website as soon as possible.
