# County Council minutes 2026-10-13

COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY WORK SESSION MINUTES 

October 13, 2026 OPEN SESSION — Elk Room 4:30 P.M. 

NOTE: The recording of this meeting is available on the County website at cecilcountymd.gov 

PRESENT: Council Members: Al Miller, Bob Meffley, Rebecca Hamilton and Dawn Branch; Council Manager L. Michelle Henson; Council Executive Assistant Christine Palmer; members of the Administration and members of the public. 

## Call to Order 

President Miller called the meeting to order at 4:30 p.m. and announced that there were four Council Members present which constitute a quorum. 

# Approval of Minutes 

On a motion made by Vice President Meffley, seconded by Councilwoman Branch, the Council moved to approve the minutes of the Council Work Session of October 6, 2026. The motion passed unanimously. 

## PRESENTATION 

Update - Family Violence (CAC Human Trafficking, the Bridge), Anne Bean, Chief of Human Services Division, Marion Gill, Coordinator of Family Violence Programs, and Erica Fielder, Trafficking Regional Navigator 

Director of Community Services David Trolio, Chief of Human Services Anne Bean, Coordinator of Family Violence Programs Marion Gill and Human Trafficking Regional Navigator Erica Fields provided an overview of Cecil County Family Violence Programs via Power Point Presentation. A copy of the presentation was placed in the record of the minutes. Director Trolio stated the goal of Family Violence Programs (Program) is to reduce trauma to children. 

Director Trolio advised Council that the Child Advocacy Center has been expanded, adding that a ribbon-cutting ceremony will be conducted soon. 

Director Trolio stated that Cecil County is the first county in Maryland to hire a Human Trafficking Regional Navigator (Navigator). 

Chief Bean stated that the Navigator position grew from groups partnering to develop resources, laying the foundation to be chosen as one of 3 pilot programs in Maryland. Chief Bean added that the Navigator is embedded in the Child Advocacy Center (CAC) and the Bridge facility. Chief Bean provided an overview of services that fall within the Program, stating that the services provided work together to serve separate goals. 

Coordinator Gill provided an overview of the CAC, stating that the County funds the medical component via contracted forensic nurse through Christiana Care, anda County-employed Victim Advocate. 

Navigator Fielder provided an overview of her role, which came about to comply with the Child Sex Trafficking Screening and Services Act of 2019. 

Vice President Meffley queried if services offered through the Program are coordinated with the Department of Social Services. Coordinator Gill responded that overlap of services is mitigated. Councilwoman Branch commended the practice of housing all entities within one location, and limiting the number of interviews a child experiences. Coordinator Gill stated that the process of limiting the number of interviews a child experiences has proven successful and has become a standard procedure. 

President Miller expressed appreciation to the team of people involved in the Program. Discussion ensued regarding grant funding associated with the Program. Vice President Meffley expressed appreciation to everyone involved in the Program. County Executive Adam Streight noted that CAC and services offered through the Program are very important to Law Enforcement and commended the team for their dedication. Director of Administration Schneckenburger expressed appreciation for the efforts of the Program team.
