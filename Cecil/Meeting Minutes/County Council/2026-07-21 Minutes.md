COUNTY COUNCIL OF CECIL COUNTY LEGISLATIVE SESSION MINUTES LSD 2026-14 July 21, 2026 

The County Council of Cecil County met in Legislative Session at the County Administration Building, 200 Chesapeake Blvd., Elk Room, Elkton, MD. The following members of the Council were present: 

Al Miller, Council President 

Bob Meffley, Council Vice President Donna Culberson, Council Member Dawn Branch, Council Member 

NOTE: The recording of this meeting is available on the County website at cecilcountymd.gov 

### CALL TO ORDER 

The meeting of the County Council of Cecil County of July 21, 2026, was called to order by Council President Miller at 7:00 p.m. 

#### PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE 

President Miller led all those in attendance in the Pledge of Allegiance. 

#### ROLL CALL 

Council Manager Henson called the roll and noted that four Council members were present. 

## OPENING PRAYER/MOMENT OF SILENCE 

Pastor Shaun Taylor, Porters Grove Baptist Church of Rising Sun, offered the opening prayer. President Miller requested a moment of silence to recognize the passing of former County Executive Tari Moore. 

### APPROVAL OF AGENDA 

President Miller requested Council’s approval to amend the agenda to provide for consideration of Resolution No. 39-2026 Charter Amendment — Budget and Finance — Preparation of the County Budget. On a motion made by Councilwoman Culberson, seconded by Councilwoman Branch, the Council moved to amend the agenda of July 21, 2026. The motion was approved unanimously.

### APPROVAL OF MINUTES 

On a motion made by Vice President Meffley, seconded by Councilwoman Branch, the Council moved to approve the minutes of the July 7, 2026 Legislative Session. 

#### PUBLIC COMMENTS 

Mr. Sean Brimm spoke about an application regarding a proposed Charter School. 

### PRESIDENT AND COUNCIL COMMENTS 

Councilwoman Branch thanked the audience for attending the meeting. Councilwoman Branch stated that she attended the Chesapeake City Volunteer Fire Co. Awards Banquet on July 11". She thanked all first responders for their service. 

Vice President Meffley stated he attended the Firemen’s Association meeting on July 20* during which issues such as maneuverability of roads and cul-de-sacs and costs of equipment were discussed. He stated the Firemen’s Association utilizes an application which provides live emergency call information for responders. 

Vice President Meffley stated he spoke with Director of Land Use and Development Stephen O'Connor regarding initiation of requirements for installation of elevators during construction of 3-story buildings. 

Vice President Meffley stated that the Fair Hill Nature Center Board is pursuing playground equipment for the facility. He commended Executive Director Laura Hannan for her efforts to expand programs, including historical refurbishment of the caretaker’s house and remodeling of the barn to create classrooms. 

Vice President Meffley stated he attended the Chesapeake City Volunteer Fire Co. Awards Banquet on July 11" with Councilwoman Branch and Executive Streight. 

Councilwoman Culberson, referring to previous comments regarding the proposed Charter School, stated that the application did not ask for a waiver of State oversite; adding that the proposed Charter School requested autonomy over its planned curriculum. She added that the former North East Middle School is the preferred site for the proposed Charter School however other sites are being considered. Councilwoman Culberson provided Emergency Response Services Intel for the period ending July 13‘, reporting 283 calls, 19 of which were mental health related; 60 domestic violence calls, 35 of which were physical; and 14 overdoses, and no fatalities. Councilwoman Culberson commended the efforts of the Health Department and Voices of Hope in reducing overdose deaths. 

President Miller thanked the audience for attending the Legislative Session. President Miller announced that the Cecil County Fair runs from July 24 to August 15. He expressed appreciation to the volunteers and members of the business community who make the fair a success each year, adding that the fair highlights 4H, Future Farmers of America and Cecil County Agriculture.

Resolution No. 35-2026 Supplemental Appropriation — Grant Funds — Community Services — Human Services Division — Housing Navigator Funding 

A Resolution to amend the Annual Budget and Appropriation Ordinance for Cecil County, Maryland for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027, in order to provide a total of $71,017.00 to the Community Services Grants Fund — Department of Community Services, Human Services Division, Fund 109, Department 533, to record a grant awarded from the Department of Housing and Urban Development, through the Maryland State Department of Housing and Community Development. Resolution No. 39-2026 Council Manager Henson stated for the record: 

Resolution No. 39-2026 Charter Amendment — Budget and Finance — Preparation of the County Budget 

A Resolution amending Article 5, Budget and Finance, Section 504, Preparation of the County Budget, to provide that on or before the second Monday of April each year, the Executive shall prepare and submit a proposed budget to the Council for the ensuing fiscal year. The proposed budget shall present a complete financial plan for the government reflecting anticipated revenues from all sources, expenditures, and any surplus or deficit in the general or special funds of the government. The proposed budget shall be reproduced and made available to the public. Introduced by Council President at the request of the County Executive Introduced, considered and unanimously approved on July 21, 2026 pursuant to Section A38749 D of the Cecil County Code. 

Director of Administration Schneckenburger stated that Resolution No. 39-2026 provides for presentation of the County Budget after the General Assembly convenes its session. He stated this will provide County Government with specific data regarding State-related revenue and expenses, resulting in a clear budget for Council to review. President Miller advised Council that the Code of Cecil County, Policies and Procedures, Section A387-49, Process for Resolutions, Paragraph D was included in Council’s meeting packets for reference.
