# Pending rule changes — do they threaten a 2–5 lot minor subdivision on septic? (2026-10-16)

*Reviewed by Claude for Brian and Ben from county minutes, digests, legislation and posted plan drafts. Rights vest
when the plat records, so a change adopted mid-deal can apply to us. Each assessment is also on every deal's
"Approval watch" on the Deal Board (rules/<County>.yaml `rule_change_assessment`).*

**Bottom line:** no county's pending rewrite cuts rural minor subdivisions. Several move the other way (pro-housing).
The real constraint is the Health Department's septic approval and the wet-season perc window, plus two cost
changes: Worcester's planned BAT requirement and the state's 1:1 forest replacement (now priced). Septic-tier remaps
don't hurt us: minors are allowed in Tier IV in every county; only majors are barred.

**Statewide, already in force:** Forest Conservation Act changes (Ch. 457 of 2024), from 1 July 2026 — every acre of
forest cleared is replaced 1:1 (½:1 in a PFA). Now priced on every deal: ~0.5 ac cleared per lot × the tract's wooded
share × the county fee-in-lieu rate (median ~$5K on Pursue deals).
