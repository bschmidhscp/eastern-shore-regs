# Somerset County, MD: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "ZO" = Zoning Ordinance PDF (printed page numbers), "CA Ord." = Critical Area Ordinance 05-2026 (PDF pages), "Comm digest" = County Commissioners digest (includes Sanitary Commission items). Statewide rules: `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md` (cited as [MD]).

**Biggest gap:** the County **Subdivision Ordinance is not in the corpus** (ZO refers to it but it was not found online) [ZO p.4; Coverage Report]. Minor/major process rules below come from the fee form, digests and the 2025 plan, not code text.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Cheapest entitlement cost in the corpus:** no impact fee or excise tax found; a 3-lot minor costs **$300** in subdivision fees [Fees: Res 1218 p.3; Fee Findings]. Confirm with DTCS (410-651-1424).
- **Minor subdivision = 1 to 7 lots; major = 8+** [Fees: Subdivision Application 2025 p.1]. This matches Somerset's SB 236 number of 7 [MD §2].
- **Density in AR is not capped per acre in the ZO:** minimum lot is 1 acre (43,560 sf) with 100 ft width [ZO § 6.2 p.68]. Septic perc, the tier map and Critical Area are the real limits.
- **Critical Area covers about 42% of the county** [2025 CompPlan p.137]; RCA allows 1 unit per 20 acres [CA Ord. p.58].
- **Sewer is thin:** public sewer serves just under half of existing dwellings [2025 CompPlan p.189]; a Sanitary District EDU allocation and a Crisfield approval may gate lots [Comm digest 2023-03-09].
- **Zoning appeals are permissive:** 61 of 64 decided Board of Zoning Appeals (BZA) cases granted, 2019 to 2026 [BZA digest].
- **Risks:** no Subdivision Ordinance text to verify process; the county says its subdivision rules need updating [2025 CompPlan p.288]; 2012 tier map may be remapped toward Tier IV [PC digest 2025-07-10]; pending zoning, forest and sewer changes (section 7).
- **Market signal:** modular homes at $280,000 to $310,000 planned at Somerset Grove, 79 lots [PC digest 2025-05-01].

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Minor | Major |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | 1 to 7 lots [Application p.1] | 8+ lots [Application p.1] |
| Stages | One application, sketch/prelim/final not itemized | Sketch, preliminary, final [Res 1218 p.3] |
| Decider | **not found in corpus** | Planning Commission (PC) [PC digest 2025-02-06, 2025-05-01] |

- **Minor lots with sewer** go to the Sanitary Commission, whose chairman signs the plat after EDU approval [Comm digest 2024-01-11, 2025-10-09].
- **Roads:** minor collector or local street subdivisions over 2 lots need an internal road unless each lot has 600 ft of frontage [2025 CompPlan p.234]. Flag-lot access of 50 ft vs 100 ft required needed a BZA variance [BZA digest 2026-03-20].
- **Timing signals:** Crisfield Highway minor tabled 2023-02-09 and 2023-03-09 awaiting a 1-EDU approval from Crisfield, signed 2023-04-13 [Comm digest]. Thornton: phone poll 2023-11-14, signed 2024-01-11 [Comm digest]. Thornton 4-lot preliminary (1.45 ac) approved at PC 2024-05-02 [PC digest]. PC acted on only 2 major subdivisions in 76 items, 2023-10 to 2026-07: 20-lot Industrial Park (2025-02-06) and 79-lot Somerset Grove (2025-05-01; a 2005 approval was never built) [PC digest].
- **PC rate:** of 40 site plan, rezoning, subdivision and plat items with a decision, none denied; 4 tabled [PC digest]. Many rows are discussion only.
- **Intrafamily (Critical Area):** parcels of record 3/1/1986, 7 to under 12 ac: 2 lots; 12 to under 60 ac: 3 lots; DTCS Director approves [CA Ord. p.87-88].
- Subdivision review timing: **not found in corpus.**

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Critical Area (CA-1):** RCA 1 unit per 20 ac; 100 ft Buffer, 200 ft for new subdivisions on tidal water; buffer fee-in-lieu $1.50 per sf; protective easement recorded with plat [CA Ord. p.58, 110, 103, 114]. Growth allocation: 977.01 acres remain; no more than half may go to RCA; 300 ft from tidal water [CA Ord. p.71]. Extra review fee $150 [Fees: Res 1218].
- **Septic tiers:** 2012 tier map adopted, no Tier IV exemption [MD §2]. Tier IV cap on septic is 7 lots, so 8+ lots on septic outside Tier III needs sewer or a Tier III hearing [MD §2]. The Miller rezoning staff note says max 7 lots without water and sewer [PC digest 2026-04-02]. Tier IV share of land: **not found in corpus** (map in `Documents/Code Related/TierMap.pdf`).
- **Forest conservation:** applies at 40,000 sf; 2026 update draft raises replacement to 1:1 while still showing old 1/4 and 2 acre ratios; fee-in-lieu 30 and 36 cents per sf [Forest Ord. draft p.19, 22, 51-52, 57]. Adoption **not found in corpus.**
- **Stormwater:** county plan fee $2,000 + $25 per lot, waiver review $200 [Fees: Res 1218 p.3]. Thresholds **not found in corpus**; state rule applies at 5,000 sf [MD §5].
- **Floodplain:** separate ordinance not in corpus [ZO p.4].
- **Ag preservation:** MALPF and county easement program; PC forwarded top 16 FY2027 applications [PC digest 2026-06-04]. TDR is optional: sending AR/CO, receiving R-1/R-2/R-3/MRC and adjacent AR; not combined with cluster; none in RCA [ZO § 8.16 p.108-110].
- **BAT outside Critical Area, perc season, fees:** not found in corpus; see [MD §2].

## 5. Water and sewer

- **Systems:** seven treatment plants serve about 5,297 dwelling units, slightly under half the county; Princess Anne ENR upgrade about $6 million [2025 CompPlan p.137, 189]. Capacity table uses 2007 data and is stale [2025 CompPlan p.191].
- **County W&S Plan:** **not found online or in corpus** [Fee Findings; Coverage Report]. No W&S plan amendment resolutions in corpus; the 11 water-sewer rows in the Comm digest are all discussion of the Rumbley-Frenchtown petition (104 residents; $30,000 engineering bid) [Comm digest 2026-03-24].
- **Sanitary District controls hookups:** EDU allocations are requested and renewed annually (list renewal 2023-12-14; Fairmount request 2024-11-14); reservation fee is 10% of EDUs requested [Fees: Exhibit 1 FY27 p.2; Comm digest]. Minor subdivisions routinely add 1 to 2 EDUs [Comm digest 2024-01-11, 2025-10-09].
- **Charges:** residential connection minimum $2,625 water + $5,000 sewer, plus overruns; extensive construction +$2,000; Princess Anne minimum quarterly bill $178.50 [Fees: Exhibit 1 FY27 p.1; Plans: Rate Sheet FY27].
- **Debt service and assessments** on lots can be large: $197,017.83 waived for Westfield Estates [Comm digest 2025-04-10].
- Secondary growth villages (Chance, Dames Quarter, Oriole, Rumbley and others) are infill areas; avoid areas with septic failures [2025 CompPlan p.229].
