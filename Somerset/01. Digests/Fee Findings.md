# Somerset County — Fee Findings

*Compiled 2026-10-11. Somerset has no online code library; fees are set by Commissioner resolution under Zoning Ordinance § 14.1.*

## Impact fee / excise tax
**None found.** No development impact fee, school excise tax or building excise tax appears in the current P&Z fee schedule (Res 1218, effective Jan 27, 2026) [Fees: Res 1218 pp.1-4], the zoning ordinance, the 2025 comp plan, or Commissioner minutes. The only mentions are Crisfield's plan listing impact fee/excise tax as options the County *could* adopt [Plans: 10_CMP_Crisfield p.72], and state ADU/reporting bills (HB 698) in PC packets. Web search found no Somerset charge. Treat as "no county impact fee," but **confirm with county** (DTCS 410-651-1424).

## Water & sewer charges (Sanitary District, FY27, 7/1/26-6/30/27) [Fees: Exhibit 1 FY 27 pp.1-2]
- Residential connection: water $2,625 + sewer $5,000 minimum, plus cost overruns; extensive construction +$2,000; fire service $4,025. Commercial/multi: varies by project.
- Allocation reservation fee 10% of EDUs requested.
- Quarterly rates (Princess Anne): minimum $178.50 for 0-6,000 gal [Plans: Water and Sewer Rate Sheet FY27]; Smith Island $260.50/EDU; Westover $242.25/EDU.
