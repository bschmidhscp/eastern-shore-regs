# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY, MARYLAND RESOLUTION NO. _ 12/2 

A Resolution of the County Commissioners for Somerset County to amend the fee Schedule for the following areas: Planning and Zoning Fees, Building Permit Fees, and other Development Fees 

Whereas, the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance (Ordinance #1212), provide for the establishment of a Schedule of Fees, and; 

Whereas, the Schedule of Fees shall undergo annual review at an increment no longer than every five (5) years and may be amended by the Board of County Commissioners; and; 

Whereas, Resolution #1181 effective March 1, 2023, was adopted by the County Commissioners for Somerset County to establish for development activities reviewed by the Department of Technical and Community Services, and; 

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County that Resolution #1181 enacting the fee schedule be amended as follows:
