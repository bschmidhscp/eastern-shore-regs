# **Somerset County Sanitary District Inc. Water/Sewer Rates 7/1/24 to 6/30/25 EXHIBIT 1** 

- _The Somerset County Sanitary District does require 24 hours notice on all Work Order request, unless deemed an emergency._ 

- _When a check is returned for any reason, full payment is required within 7days after notification.  Only cash or a money order will be accepted.  If full payment, including return check fee, is not received by the due date, water disconnection will occur.  If water is not served, collection action will be taken. Anytime two return checks are written, the account will receive a cash only status, meaning only cash or money orders will be accepted._ 

- _Hook Up fees are subject to cost overruns.  The cost overrun policy is stated on the Application for Water and/or Sewer Connection.  The Application must be filled out in full in order to accept payment._ 

- _Any hookups in the Crisfield Peripheral are subject to City of Crisfield’s approval and any connections fees associated with City._ 

- _Certain hookups may be required to sign a Request for Services Agreement.  This form is required when engineering, legal and other services may be necessary for the services requested._ 

- _All miscellaneous charges are due at the time of disconnection, reconnection or work order request._ 

- _If water is disconnected for nonpayment, full payment plus the reconnect fee of $70.00 will be required. Payments must be made in the office by 3:00pm to have service restored that day.  Payments made after 3:00pm will not have service restored until the next business day._
