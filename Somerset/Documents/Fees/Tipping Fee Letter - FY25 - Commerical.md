Subject: Revised Landfill Fees for Fiscal Year 2025 

(July 1, 2024 - June 30, 2025) 

Please be advised that the tipping fee for all commercial users of the Somerset County Landfill has remained the same ($75.00 per ton) for the next fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2025. Scrap tires will continue to be charged as follows. Passenger tires-$4.00 each; Truck tires-$12.00 each; Off-road heavy equipment tires $100.00 per ton. Concrete and asphalt 12” or less in diameter is accepted at no charge. Any concrete with wire or rebar will be charged the normal tipping fee rate. Tree limbs and trimmings must be under 12” in diameter. Stumps and tree parts over 12” in diameter are not accepted.
