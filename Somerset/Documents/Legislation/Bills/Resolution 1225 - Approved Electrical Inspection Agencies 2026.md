# Resolution 1225 - Approved Electrical Inspection Agencies 2026

<!-- page 1 -->
## BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 

# FOR SOMERSET COUNTY, MARYLAND RESOLUTION NO. Ms 

WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 765 implemented and enforced the National Electric Code (NEC) as 

the local Electrical Code for Somerset County, Maryland; and 

WHEREAS, the Electrical Code (Ordinance 765) Section IX ‘Inspections’ that the Board of 

County Commissioners for Somerset County shall designate one or more non-governmental organizations 

as an approved Inspection Agency for the enforcement of the National Electric Code within Somerset County, Maryland. 

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset 

County that the following organizations are designated as approved Inspection Agencies for the 

enforcement of the National Electrical Code within Somerset County, Maryland: 

First State Inspection Agency United Inspection Agency American Inspection Agency 28773 Dupont Boulevard, Suite 2 716 N Bethlehem Pike, Suite 300 30555 W. Beach Road Millsboro, Delaware 19666 Lower Gwynedd, Pennsylvania 19002 | Dagsboro, Delaware 19939
