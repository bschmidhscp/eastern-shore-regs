## **BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY, MARYLAND ORDINANCE NO.** 

AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND THE SOMERSET COUNTY COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AND REPAIR FUND WHICH IMPLEMENTS §1-3A-03, ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND CANNABIS, OF THE ANNOTATED CODE OF MARYLAND TO ESTABLISH THE PURPOSE AND USE FOR WHICH MONEY RECEIVED FROM THE STATE OF MARYLAND’S COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AND REPAIR FUND MAY BE USED. 

**Whereas,** on November 8, 2022 Maryland voters passed “Question 4” by ballot referendum, making it legal for individuals aged 21 and older to possess and consume cannabis in Maryland on or after July 1, 2023, and; 

**Whereas,** on April 8, 2023, the Maryland General Assembly passed the Cannabis Reform Act (HB556/ SB516) which allowed for cannabis sales to adults 21 and older from licensed dispensaries and established a framework for adult-use cannabis sales beginning July 1, 2023, including the conversion of existing medical cannabis business licenses to both medical and adult use cannabis, and created a new Maryland Cannabis Administration to oversee see both medical and adult- use program; and 

**Whereas,** on May 3, 2023, the Governor of Maryland signed the Cannabis Reform Act into law, and; 

**Whereas,** the Cannabis Reform Act established a Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund, the purpose of which is to provide financial support to community-based organizations that serve communities which were the most impacted through the disproportionate enforcement of the cannabis prohibition before July 1, 2022 and; 

**Whereas,** Somerset County will receive a percentage of the Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund, but must adopt a law establishing the purpose for which money received from the Fund may be used and;

# **EXHIBIT A:** SOMERSET COUNTY COMMUNITY REPAIR AND REINVESTMENT FUND 

### **<u>SECTION 1</u>** 

**AS USED IN THIS ORDINANCE, THE FOLLOWING TERMS SHALL HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED:** 

- **Community** – each census block group in Somerset County, as defined by the current version of the Blueprint for Maryland’s Future: Interim Report on Neighborhood Indicators of Poverty, is a separate community. 

- **Community-based Initiative** – is a program or service that provides education, vocational education or rehabilitation, job training, health or social services to individuals in the county. 

- **Community-based Organization** – is a public or private company, corporation, limited liability company, neighborhood group, organization, or partnership that provides educational, vocational education or rehabilitation, job training, health, or social services to individuals in the county. 

- **Disproportionately Impacted Area** – a geographic area identified by the office of social equity that has had above 150% of the state’s 10-year average for cannabis possession charges. 

- **Fund** – the community reinvestment and repair fund established pursuant to §1-322 of the alcoholic beverages and cannabis article of the Maryland Code. 

- **Low-Income Communities** – ~~HUD-designated Qualified Census Tract (QCT); Somerset County has the following QCTs, Tract 9301.01 (parts of Princess Anne) and 9306 (parts of Crisfield)~~ means a geographic area, or the residents of a geographic area, that is either: 

   - Designated as a Qualified Census Tract (QCT) by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development under 26 U.S.C. §42(d)(5)(B)(ii); or 

   - For a Political Subdivision in which two or fewer census tracts are designated as Qualified Census Tracts, an area in which at least 51 percent of households earn at or below 80 percent of the area median income, as determined by the most recent data published by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. 

- **Office of Social Equity** – the independent office within the Maryland cannabis administration established pursuant to §1-309.1 of the alcoholic beverages and cannabis article of the Maryland.

### **<u>SECTION 2</u>** 

#### **1. PERMITTED USES:** 

a. The fund may only be used for: 

- i. Funding community-based initiatives intended to benefit low-income communities; 

- ii. Funding community-based initiatives that serve disproportionately impacted areas; 

- iii. Any related administrative expenses up to 15%. 

#### **2. PROHIBITED USES:** 

- i. Money may not be expended from the fund for law enforcement agencies or activities. 

- ii. Money expended from the fund is supplemental to and may not supplant funding that otherwise would be appropriated for pre-existing local government programs. 

#### **3. DISTRIBUTION OF THE FUND:** 

- a. The Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County shall award and appropriate all money received from the Office of Social Equity for the Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund to the Somerset County Health Department. The Somerset County Health Department shall distribute all funds, which may only be used to fund initiatives in accordance with subsection (a)(l) of this section. 

- b. The Somerset County Health Department shall develop criteria for making an award prior to the solicitation of proposals. In developing criteria to make an award, the Somerset County Health Department shall consider any guidance provided by the Office of Social Equity. 

- c. A community-based organization previously awarded money from the fund may not be given a new award until all reporting for a previous award has been completed and approved by the Somerset County Health. 

- d. Any money not awarded that fiscal year shall carry over to the next fiscal year.

#### **4. PROCESS FOR DISTRIBUTION:** 

- a. The Somerset County Health Department shall establish an open period in which community-based organizations may submit proposals for the funding of community-based initiatives. The Somerset County Health Department shall advertise the open period by means of the following: 

   - i. Somerset County website 

   - ii. Somerset County Health Department website 

   - iii. Social media pages for Somerset County and the Somerset County Health Department 

   - iv. Public Service Announcements (PSA) 

   - v. News releases: 

      - a. As funding allows, paid advertising Following the closing period for proposals, the Somerset County Health Department will use their Grant Agreement and Monitoring Policy as their documented process for issuing awards. 

      - b. All money awarded to a community-based organization shall be spent in the fiscal year it was awarded. 

#### **5. REPORTING** 

- a. Within two (2) weeks of completion of the awarded program, each communitybased organization shall write a report outlining the use of the awarded money, the implementation process and how the program has performed to date. 

- b. On or before December 1 every two years beginning in 2024, the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County shall submit a report to the governor and, in accordance with §2-1257 of the state government article, the senate budget and taxation committee and the house appropriations committee on how money received from the fund were spent during the immediately preceding two (2) fiscal years. 

- c. Following the submission to the Governor’s Office, a comprehensive report will be compiled to showcase all programs awarded and will be available for the public on the Somerset County website and the Somerset County Health Department website.
