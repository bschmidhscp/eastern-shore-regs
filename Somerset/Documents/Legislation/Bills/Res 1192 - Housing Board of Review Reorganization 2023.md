# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY, MARYLAND RESOLUTION NO. 1D 

A Resolution of the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to reorganize and reestablish the Housing Board of Review to assist the Department of Technical and Community Services with the implementation of a Housing Rehabilitation Program throughout the County. 

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners supports and actively seeks ways to assist 

homeowners in the County in efforts to ensure that their residential units are safe, sanitary and decent; and 

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners has applied for and received funding from the Maryland Department ofHousing and Community Development for Community Development Block Grant funding to support a Housing Rehabilitation Program to assist eligible homeowners in the County; and 

WHEREAS, the County has previously appointed a Housing Board of Review consisting of knowledgeable County residents to assist the Department of Technical and Community Services with the review of applications from County homeowners seeking financial assistance with housing rehabilitation; and 

WHEREAS, the County has previously adopted Resolution 1046 establishing a Housing Board 

of Review with seven (7) county residents; and 

WHEREAS, the County seeks to redefine the membership of the Housing Board of Review to include four (4) knowledgeable Somerset County staff and three (3) citizens, selected and appointed by the Board of County Commissioners 

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners deem it necessary to reorganize and reestablish 

the Housing Board of Review in order to clarify its responsibilities and terms of appointment;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of County Commissioners for 

Somerset County does hereby reorganize and reestablish the Housing Board of Review to assist the Department of Technical and Community Services with reviewing and approving applications for financial assistance from eligible homeowners seeking to rehabilitate their homes, in accordance with the “Somerset County Housing Rehabilitation Grant Program Handbook” as may be amended to comply with State of Maryland requirements, subject to the following: 

1. This Resolution hereby repeals and replaces Resolution #1046 as adopted by the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County. 

2. This Resolution establishes a Housing Board of Review to consist of seven (7) members. 

3. Four (4) of the seven (7) members shall be knowledgeable staff from Somerset 

   - County, compromising; 

      - a. DTCS Housing Rehabilitation Specialist 

      - b. DTCS Code Enforcement Officer or Building Codes Inspector 

      - c. DTCS Director or designee 

      - d. County Engineer or designee 

4. The three (3) remaining members, all residents of Somerset County, shall be 

appointed by the County Commissioners. 

   - a. These Members shall serve an initial two-year term 

   - b. After the initial appointment, all subsequent appointments shall serve three- 

      - year terms. 

5. A minimum quorum of this board shall consist of one (1) citizen and three (3) 

Somerset County Staff.
