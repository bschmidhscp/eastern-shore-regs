## SHALL BE ENACTED AND READS FOLLOWS: 

SECTION I 

- A. A tipping fee of $75.00 per ton shall be charged for all solid waste accepted at the Somerset County Landfill except the following: 

   1. No charge for noncommercial wastes, 500 pounds or less, providing a transfer station user permit has been purchased and displayed on the vehicle. A noncommercial user without a transfer station user permit shall pay at the rate of $75.00 per ton with a minimum charge of<sup>$1.00.</sup>

2. The City of Crisfield, Town of Princess Anne, Crisfield Wastewater Treatment Plant, and the Somerset County Sanitary District shall be charged $75.00 per ton effective July 1, 2025. 

3. Tires shall be charged as follows: 

a. Passenger vehicles and 1/2 ton pick-up trucks - $4.00 per tire. b. All tires’ smaller than passenger vehicle tires - $4.00 per tire. c. All truck tires, except pick-up trucks - $12.00 per tire. d. All tractor and heavy equipment tires - $.033 per pound ($100.00 per ton). 

- B. Transfer Station fees for use of<sup>thefiveCountyTransferStations:</sup> 

Each household: First vehicle - $64.00 yearly fee. Second additional permit will require a $64.00 charge. Limit - 2 permits per household. 

A permit issued by the Solid Waste Department must be displayed on each vehicle entering the Transfer Stations. A $20.00 fee will be charged for lost replacement permit stickers. A fee of $25.00 will be charged for all returned checks. First and second transfer station permits will be reduced quarterly using the following formula: 

July 1 - September 30 - $64.00 October 1 - December 31 - $48.00 January 1 - March 31 - $32.00 April 1 - June 30 - $16.00 

- C. Commercial waste haulers are not exempt from the five hundred (500) pound tipping fee exclusion noted in Section I-A-1 of<sup>thisResolution.</sup> 

- D. A commercial waste hauler is a person, company, or entity hauling refuse for hire, either for cash or other remuneration, in Somerset County to the Somerset County Landfill. 

#### SECTION II 

Noncredit customers will pay before leaving the landfill site, and credit customers, after receiving approval for credit, will be billed monthly for tonnage delivered to the landfill during the month. Payment shall be made within 30 days or the last business day of the month following the period of<sup>servicecoveredbythebilling.</sup> 

All credit customers shall file an application and secure a $5,000.00 security bond to Somerset County or provide a security deposit equivalent to two months' estimated tonnage. If the estimated two months! tonnage equates to $50.00 or less, a minimum of<sup>$50.00security</sup> deposit will be required upon filing of application for credit. 

##### SECTION III 

The Board of County Commissioners may exempt the fee schedule, for a limited period of time, as outlined in this resolution for emergencies that may be created by acts of God. 

#### SECTION IV 

Based upon the action of<sup>theBoardof CountyCommissionersRecordedintheirMinutesof</sup> meeting dated March 26, 2024, all sections of this Resolution shall be effective July 1, 2024.
