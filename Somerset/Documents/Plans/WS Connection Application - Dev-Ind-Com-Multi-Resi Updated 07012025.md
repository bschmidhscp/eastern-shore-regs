The property owner is responsible for obtaining all of the proper permits for plumbing installation from the Somerset County Department of Technical & Community Services, and all other government agencies having jurisdiction over the work.  The property owner or designated contractor shall notify the Sanitary District when Middle Department Inspection Agency, Inc. is called for inspection of any service lines from the property line connection point to the building.  When installation involves mains, Lift Stations or other infrastructure, the Sanitary District shall be notified when the inspection and certifying authority is called. 

#### **<u>The applicable fee shall be paid in full and a copy of the design and approval for the Fire Service shall be submitted to the Sanitary District before application for any water or fire service will be approved.</u>** 

The property owner and designated contractor shall accept and abide by the Rules and Regulations of the Somerset County Sanitary District, including but not limited to Section XV (known as the “Pretreatment Ordinance”) and shall adhere to the Somerset County Sanitary District Construction Standards and Specifications.  The property owner and its contractor shall ensure that all water and sewer lines and all fixtures are in good repair.  In the event that it becomes necessary for the Sanitary District to enforce any of its rights arising hereunder, the Sanitary District shall be entitled to its costs, including an award of reasonable attorney’s fees and court costs.
