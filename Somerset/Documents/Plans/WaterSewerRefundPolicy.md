# WaterSewerRefundPolicy

<!-- page 1 -->
Somerset County Sanitary District Inc. Water and Sewer Billing Refunds 

A customer can request a refund for one fiscal year of water and sewer billings, if the customer can provide information, such as an electric bill, to prove that the property was not occupied during the time the credit was requested.  Any requests for refunds past one fiscal year will be denied. 

Approved by Sanitary Commission on: May 28, 2009
