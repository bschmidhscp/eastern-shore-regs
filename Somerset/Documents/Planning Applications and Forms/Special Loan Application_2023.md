The information requested by the Department of Housing and Community Development (the "Department") is necessary in determining your eligibility for a Special Loan Programs loan. Your failure to disclose this information may result in the denial of your application for a loan. Availability of this information for public inspection is governed by the provisions of the Maryland Public Information Act, State Government Article, Sections 10-611 et. seq. of the Annotated Code of Maryland. This information will be disclosed to appropriate staff of the Department, the staff of the local administrator for the loan, and participating mortgage lender, if any, for purposes directly connected with administration of the loan and the loan program. **<u><mark>Maryland Housing Rehabilitation Program - Single Family Description</mark></u>** 

### **<u><mark>Mission:</mark></u>** 

<mark>The Maryland Housing Rehabilitation Program aims to provide affordable funding to address critical health</mark> and safety issues and bring properties into an agreement with applicable building codes and standards. 

<mark>Eligible Types of Housing:</mark> 

Program funds may be used to assist in the rehabilitation of owner-occupied single-family homes. 

### **<u><mark>Addressable Items:</mark></u>** 

- Mold Remediation 

- <mark>Gas leaks</mark> 

- <mark>Roof repair and replacement, Gutters</mark> 

- <mark>Asbestos abatement</mark> 

- <mark>Vermiculite removal</mark> 

- <mark>Lead abatement</mark> 

- <mark>Combustion appliance repair and replacement for Health and Safety concerns, including gas ranges, domestic water heaters, and space heat.</mark> 

- <mark>Chimney Repair</mark> 

- <mark>Foundation and sub-space water-proofing, and drainage, inclusive of bulk water mitigation by drain tile, sump pump, and minor grading.</mark> 

- <mark>Radon mitigation</mark> 

- <mark>Electrical hazard, Knob, and tube removal, inclusive of necessary service upgrades.</mark> 

- <mark>Electrical upgrades for future home electrification. (<100A to 200A upgrades)</mark> 

- <mark>Plumbing issues</mark> 

- <mark>Water and well</mark> 

- <mark>Septic and sewer</mark> 

- <mark>Windows and doors only for H&S, water intrusion, and security.</mark> 

- <mark>Structural repair - atrophy, rot, insect, settling, storm-related</mark> 

- <mark>Access for disabled and seniors, inclusive of Chair lifts, handicap ramps, bars, railings, and doors.</mark> 

- <mark>Fall and injury prevention (handrails, rubber treads, bathroom grab bars)</mark> 

- <mark>Ductwork upgrades and installations (primarily for solid fuel-burning households- Mainly in Allegany and Garrett counties)</mark> 

- <mark>Building shell stabilization (i.e. repair/replace missing sheetrock on exterior walls, point up deteriorated masonry that allows for water infiltration)</mark> 

- <mark>Pest treatments (rats, roaches, and mice)</mark> 

- <mark>Incipient failure of critical infrastructure due to being beyond the life expectancy of the equipment.</mark> 

### **<u><mark>Eligible Applicants:</mark></u>** 

<mark>Household income of owner-occupants of single-family homes cannot exceed 80 percent of the statewide or</mark> Washington, D.C. Metropolitan Statistical Area median income. 

### **<u><mark>Provide Contractor and a Scope of Work:</mark></u>**

### **<u><mark>Available Funding and Loan Terms:</mark></u>** 

<mark>Loans have interest rates based on the income of tenants served and the projected income available to repay the loan. Generally, the maximum loan is $50, 000. Loans in excess of $5,000 or with deferred payments are secured by a mortgage. Interest rates range from 0% to 6% and are based on the applicant's ability to pay. The maximum loan term is 30 years. Loans that serve families with incomes at or below 50 percent of the statewide or Washington, D.C. Metropolitan Statistical Area median income may have deferred payments if necessary for project feasibility.</mark>
