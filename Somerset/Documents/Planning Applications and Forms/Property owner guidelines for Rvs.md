# Property owner guidelines for **<u>RV’s, CAMPERS and TENTS</u>** 

in Somerset County, Maryland. 

Common question: 

**Can I bring my RV or camper to my vacant lot?** 

Answer: **<u>No</u>** 

- **<u>RV’s, Campers and Tents are not permitted as the sole use of a property.</u>** 

- **<u>Parking of RV’s, Campers and placement of tents is not permitted as the sole use of a property.</u>** 

Common question: 

**Are there times I can bring my RV or Camper?** 

Answer: **<u>only in specific situations</u>** 

- A Seasonal Permit is available during hunting season on parcels located in AR zoning and CO zoning only. Parcels must be **larger than 5 acres** . Hunting Season runs from September thru Feb. **No Seasonal permits will be issued in Residential zoned areas. No electrical service allowed.** 

- A valid building permit for a dwelling has been issued on the property. RV will require separate temporary permit. 

- Parking of RV or Camper is allowed on a lot improved with an **<u>owner occupied dwelling.</u>** 

**Be aware that Citations, fines of $500.00 and court action will be pursued for violators.** 6/16/2022
