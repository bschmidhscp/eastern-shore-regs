The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 17<sup>th</sup> day of January 2025 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

###### **<u>Board Members Present:</u>** 

Isaac Ballard – acting Chairman Paul Emely AC Alrey Andrew Baines William Cottman **<u>Others Present:</u>** William Cornish, Assistant Director Jesse Drewer, Director Carter Finney, Planning and Zoning specialist Andrew Illuminati, Board Attorney 

Isaac Ballard opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Isaac Ballard called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Mr. Ballard asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the October 18, 2024 meeting, Mr. Alrey asked that the minutes be corrected to show that Mr. Carter Finney attended the last meeting. The motion was made by Mr. Cottman and seconded by Mr. Baines 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** SE 2025-2547 Samantha Brillinger 3991 Smith Island RD 

**SE 25-2547:** The applicant is seeking approval to open a restaurant in the MRC zoning 

**Testimony:** Mr. Ballard read the ad into the record. Ms. Samantha Brillinger came forward and was sworn in. 

Mr. Ballard asked the applicant to explain her request. 

Mrs. Brillinger testified that she would like to open a small coffee shop to serve drinks, salads and pastries to tourist that visit Smith Island. For now, it will be a take away only, so there will be no dining on the premises.

<!-- page 4 -->
Mr. Ballard then asked if the pictures of the Kitchen in the staff report gave an accurate representation of what they planned to do. 

Mrs. Brillinger confirmed that they were indeed. She then informed the board that she had already spoken to the health department about any other additional equipment she needed in order to have her kitchen meet commercial standards. She also mentioned they also wanted to add a walkway and deck to the front of the building. 

Mr Ballard declared that the photographs of the kitchen were exhibit 1. 

Mr. Baines inquired about the dining area, before being told that the restaurant was intended to be takeaway only at this point. 

Mr. Emely then inquired whether the customers would have to go through the kitchen. 

Mrs. Brillinger then said there would be a counter constructed where the people would order and then be served from. 

William Cornish read the staff report into record. Mr. Ballard made this exhibit 2. 

Mr. Alrey requested clarification on the rear porch which was planned. 

Mrs. Brillinger then replied that it was an exit for the staff. 

Mr. Ballard then asked if there were any other questions for the board, all replied no. 

Mr. Ballard thanked Mrs. Brillinger for coming 

There were no further questions. 

Mr. Illuminati than asked the applicant if she was aware of the conditions of the request, those being a permit was required to begin construction and that a drive thru was never allowed to be constructed on the property. 

Mrs. Brillinger confirmed that she was aware of said conditions. 

Mr. Alrey than requested that an additional condition be added that Mrs. Brillinger also give the board before and after photos of the property after renovations were complete. 

The condition was added.

<!-- page 5 -->
###### **<u>Findings:</u>** 

**SE 24-2540** , The Board voted unanimously to approve the request for a Special Exception Subject to the conditions in the staff report 

Paul Emely – Yes AC Alrey – Yes Andrew Baines – Yes Isaac Ballard – Yes William Cottman - Yes 

###### **Other business** 

Mr. Alrey made the announcement that the University of Maryland Eastern Shore was having a bootcamp for entrepreneurs on the 18<sup>th</sup> of January. 

He also announced that the EPA was setting up a resiliency round table for the three lower counties on the 22<sup>nd</sup> and 25<sup>th</sup> of January from 9 Am to 4 Pm. 

**The Board of Zoning Appeals voted to adjourn the meeting.** 

**The meeting adjourned at 8:00 pm**

### **STAFF REPORT** 

###### **Meeting of February 21, 2025** 

**Case No.: SE 2025-2548 Applicant: Robert Hajir Property Owner: Mohsen Farms LLC Location: 30868 McCormick Swamp Road Tax Map: Map 16D / Grid 19 / Parcel 129 Zoning: Medium-Density Residential District (R-2) Floodzone: X Critical Area: N/A Property Size: 9.91 Acres Request: Special Exception Approval** 

###### **I.** **<u>SUMMARY OF REQUEST:</u>** 

Robert Hajir, representative of Mohsen Farms LLC, is requesting Board of Zoning Appeals approval for the construction of three poultry houses located on McCormick Swamp Road. Section 5.7(A)6 requires a Special Exception to be approved by the Board subject to conditions. 

###### **II.** **<u>DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPERTY AND SURROUNDING AREA:</u>** 

The property is located within an unincorporated area of Somerset County and is zoned Medium Density Residential (R2) in the East Princess Anne Election District. Site is located on both the north and south of McCormick Swamp road 500 feet to the South of Hampden RD. the Parcel is 9.91 Acres. The property is improved with an existing building. The Parcel is surrounded by primarily unimproved fields and is directly south of a mobile home park. Sites is not within the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area or the floodplain. 

###### **III.** **<u>CODE CRITERIA:</u>** 

Two sections of the Zoning Ordinance (#1205) are relevant to this request; 

- **1) Section 5.7 – Table of Uses:** 

Poultry Houses require a special exception

<!-- page 9 -->
###### **2) Section 11.2 – Special Exceptions:** 

When hearing evidence necessary for granting of any Special Exception, the Board shall consider all pertinent facts in the case, and render a decision in accordance with the following principles: 

- (1) The proposed use conforms in all respects to minimum requirements of the district in which it is located; 

- (2) The proposed use will not adversely affect the health, safety, and general welfare of residents of the area; 

- (3) The proposed use will not interfere with the adequate and orderly provision of public facilities necessary to service the area or the proposed Special Exceptions; 

- (4) The proposed use will not create congestion in the road or undue traffic hazards and that adequate ingress and egress is provided; 

- (5) The proposed use will not adversely affect the area and surrounding property due to adverse environmental characteristics including undue smoke, odor, noise, improper drainage inadequate access; 

- (6) The proposed use will not adversely affect the established character of the area; and 

- (7) The proposed use is consistent with the guidelines established for such uses in the County’s Critical Area Program, where applicable. 

- (8) The proposed use is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan or a local Area Plan adopted by the Board of County Commissioners. 

###### **IV.** **<u>ISSUES/PLANNING CONCERNS:</u>** 

None. 

###### **V.** **<u>CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:</u>** 

- **1)** A Building Permit and Zoning Certificate are required prior to any construction. **2)** Owner must meet all applicable Local, State and Federal requirements. 

- **3)** Building Permits must be obtained within 3 years of approval. 

###### **VI.** **<u>STAFF RECOMMENDATION:</u>** 

Staff respectfully requests that the Board of Zoning Appeals consider approval of the Special Exception with the stated conditions.
