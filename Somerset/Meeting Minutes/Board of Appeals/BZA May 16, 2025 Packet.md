### STAFF REPORT 

###### Meeting of May 16, 2025 

Case No.: SE 2025-2551 Applicant: Sara Matthews on behalf of The Trilogy Group Property Owner: _ Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County Location: Revells Neck Rd Tax Map: Map 32/Grid 04 / Parcel 149 Lot 3 Zoning: General Industrial District (I-2) Floodzone: x Critical Area: N/A Property Size: 2.02 Acres Request: Special Exception Approval 

###### I. 

#### SUMMARY OF REQUEST: 

Sara Matthews is requesting Board of Zoning Appeals approval for the creation of a cannabis growing operation, located on Revells Neck Rd. Section 5.7(E) subsection 33 requires a Special Exception to be approved by the Board subject to conditions. 

###### II. 

##### DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPERTY AND SURROUNDING AREA: 

The property is located within an unincorporated area of Somerset County and is zoned General Industrial District (I-2) in the Westover Election District. Site is located on the northside of Revells Neck road 3000 feet to the west side of Ocean Hwy. the Parcel is 2.02 Acres. The property is unimproved. The Parcel is surrounded on all sides by unimproved fields. Sites is not within the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area or the floodplain. 

###### Ill. CODE CRITERIA: 

Two sections of the Zoning Ordinance (#1205) are relevant to this request; 1) Section 5.7(f)33 — Usage Table: 

Growing Cannabis in the I-2 Zoning district requires a special exception from the board of appeals.

<!-- page 8 -->
- 2) Section 11.2 - Special Exceptions: When hearing evidence necessary for granting of any Special Exception, the Board shall consider all pertinent facts in the case, and render a decision in accordance with the following principles: 

   - (1) The proposed use conforms in all respects to minimum requirements of the district in which it is located; 

   - (2) The proposed use will not adversely affect the health, safety, and general welfare of residents of the area; 

   - (3) The proposed use will not interfere with the adequate and orderly provision of public facilities necessary to service the area or the proposed Special Exceptions; 

   - (4) The proposed use will not create congestion in the road or undue traffic hazards and that adequate ingress and egress is provided; 

   - (5) The proposed use will not adversely affect the area and surrounding property due to adverse environmental characteristics including undue smoke, odor, noise, improper drainage inadequate access; 

   - (6) The proposed use will not adversely affect the established character of the area; and 

   - (7) The proposed use is consistent with the guidelines established for such uses in the County’s Critical Area Program, where applicable. 

   - (8) The proposed use is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan or a local Area Plan adopted by the Board of County Commissioners. 

###### IV. ISSUES/PLANNING CONCERNS: 

The applicant does not currently own the property, but is in active negotiation with the county to purchase it pending BZA approval. 

Vv. CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL: 

- 1) A Building Permit and Zoning Certificate are required prior to any construction. 

- 2) Owner must meet all applicable Local, State and Federal requirements. 

- 3) Building Permits must be obtained within 3 years of approval. 

- 4) A site plan must be reviewed by the Somerset County TAC Committee and approved by the Planning Commission. 

- 5) Applicant will be subject to the Somerset County Industrial Park Business Owners Association regulations and requirements.

<!-- page 9 -->
###### VI. STAFF RECOMMENDATION: 

Staff respectfully requests that the Board of Zoning Appeals consider approval of the Special Exception with the stated conditions.
