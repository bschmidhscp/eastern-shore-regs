# SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES December 18, 2020 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 18th day of December 2020 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

Board Members Present: 

Brian Laird - Chairman Donald Price Andrew Baines William Cottman Paul Emely Isaac Ballard 

Others Present: Mary Phillips, Zoning Specialist 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Subject of Appeal: SE-20-2513 

SE 20-2513: James B Beauchamp owner of Meadow Edge Farms, LLC is requesting a Special Exception for placement of a single wide mobile home on property located at 8921 Courthouse Hill Rd. The property can be identified on tax map 34, grid 23, parcel 27 in the 4" Election District and is zoned AR. The request is per section 5.7(b)2 and section 5.8(g)6 iii of the Somerset 

## Testimony: 

The Case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. James Beauchamp of Courthouse Hill Rd was sworn in. Mr. Beauchamp explained that he would like to place a single wide mobile home on 8921 Courthouse Hill Rd as a temporary resident while he builds his home. Mr. Beauchamp explained that there was at one time back in the 80’s there was a mobile home on this property, and the property currently has septic, well, and electric. 

Chairman Laird asked if anyone in attendance in opposition of the request. No one in attendance. 

Findings: SE 20-2513 James B Beauchamp 

All Board members have been out to the site. 

The Board voted to grant the Special Exception for the placement of a single wide Mobile Home.
