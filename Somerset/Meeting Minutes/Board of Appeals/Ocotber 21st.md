The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 21st day of October 2022 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

Board Members Present: Brian Laird - Chairman William Cottman Isaac Ballard Andrew Baines Donald Price Paul Emely 

##### Others Present: 

Mary Phillips, Zoning Administrator/ Assistant Director Will Cornish, Zoning Specialist Charly Sager, GIS Specialist 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Mr. Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the previous meeting, there being none Mr. Laird for a vote to approve the minutes from the October 21, 2022 Meeting. Minutes were approved by unanimous vote. 

Case #1 

Subject of Appeal: Special Exception 2022- 2525 Williams, Hudson 

SE 2022-2525; Tanya Williams and George Hudson, owners of a 9.267 AC parcel located on Champ Rd identified as Parcel 119 on Map 22 in the 2" Election District have requested a Special Exception for the placement of a Hunting/Fishing cabin of 200 sq. ft or less, for use as temporary residence with no connection to utilities. Special Exception is required by Section 5.7(b)9 of Ordinance # 1174.

## BOARD OF ZONING APPEALS MEETING MINUTES: October 21, 2022 

Chairman Laird read the case advertisement into the record. Mr. George Hudson, 209 N Railroad Ave Wyoming De accompanied by Ms. Tanya Hudson, came forward. Mr. Hudson was sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mr. Hudson express to the Board his intention. Mr. Hudson explained they are mainly bow hunters and have been searching for property for a retreat. He is seeking to place a 12 ft wide by 16 ft wide prebuilt cabin with an open covered porch on the parcel. Ms. Williams, 209 N Railroad Ave Wyoming De. was sworn in. Ms. Williams stated they are mainly bow hunters and may use rifle on occasion. The deer stands will be located in the rear of the property farther away from the road. Mr. Laird read a letter from MDE regarding the presence and location of nontidal wetlands on the parcel, into the record. The letter was entered as Exhibit 1, and the accompanied maps as Exhibit 2. Two pictures of the proposed cabin were entered as Exhibit 3. The Site Plan was entered as Exhibit 4. Ms. Mary Phillips, DTCS asked that the Staff Report be entered into the record. Ms. Phillips noted that according to GIS , the proposed cabin will be approximately 360’ north of the nearest residence on the south and approximately 430’ to the nearest residence on the north. Chairman Laird asked if there were any questions from Board members. Being none, Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in attendance in opposition to the request. 

Mr. Carlton Huntington, 10361 Champ Rd. came forward and was sworn. Mr. Huntington expressed that he is not opposed the cabin itself but is concerned what it may do to his property values. Chairman Laird expressed his opinion that the cabin will be low impact on the area. Mr. Andrew Baines stated that the building will be new and portable. Mr. Issac Ballard expressed that the cabin will have sufficient buffer to shield it from the road and neighboring property. Chairman Laird called the applicants to come forward again. Ms. Phillips stated conditions which needed to be included in approval are a Zoning certificate and building permit are required, any change or enlargement to the structure would require the structure to be upgraded and meet all conditions for a single-family residence and floodplain regulations. Mr Hudson stated he planned to keep tree clearing to a minimum and dress up the site with plantings of grasses and dress up the driveway entrance as well. Chairman Laird asked if there were any additional comments or questions from the Board and there are none. 

### Findings: SE 2022-2525 Williams Hudson 

All Board members have been out to the site. 

The Board voted approve a Special Exception for the placement of a Hunting Fishing Cabin on the site per the submitted site plan and conditions.
