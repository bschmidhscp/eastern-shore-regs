**SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals Meeting Minutes** November 17, 2023 



The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 17<sup>th</sup> day of November 2023 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

# **<u>Board Members Present:</u>** 

Brian Laird - Chairman Isaac Ballard Paul Emely Andrew Baines William Cottman 

# **<u>Others Present:</u>** 

William Cornish, Zoning Specialist Jesse Drewer, Director Andrew Illuminati Esq, Board Attorney 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Mr. Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the October 20, 2023 meeting, there being none Mr. Laird for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. 

Minutes were approved by unanimous vote. 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** Special Exception 2023-2538 309 E Carroll Street, LLC, 14110 Backbone Road. 

**SE 23-2538:** The applicant proposes a replacement nonconforming Single Wide Mobile Home on a .506 Ac Parcel in AR Zoning District. 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Ms. Christy Bierman, representative for 309 E Carroll Street LLC came forward and was sworn. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain her request. 

Ms. Bierman stated that she purchased a new single wide for the property in September, the old single wide on the property was removed earlier this year. She further stated that the trailer would not hinder the character of the neighborhood, will have new shutters and a new deck.

<!-- page 2 -->
Mr. Illuminati clarified that the staff report as read will be entered into the record as exhibit #1. 

Mr. Cornish read the conditions if an approval was granted. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in opposition or in favor in attendance. Being none 

Chairman Laird asked for any further questions from Board members. 

Being none – Chairman Laird thanked Ms. Bierman for coming. 

**<u>Subject of Variance:</u>** Variance 2023-1059 Cheryl A. & Keith O’Neal, 26528 Harbor Road. 

**VAR 23-1059:** The applicant proposes a lot reconfiguration of a nonconforming lot of record on a 3,580 sq ft in the MRC Zoning District. 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Mr. Keith O’Neal, applicant, came forward and was sworn. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain his request. 

Mr. O’Neal stated that he is here because he wishes to consolidate half of the vacant lot adjacent with his home, to allow for more off-street parking and more beach access. Mr. O’Neal stated that the other half of the lot would be consolidated by his neighbor who was also present tonight. 

Mr. Illuminati stated that the staff report will be entered into the record as exhibit #1. 

Mr. Drewer read the conditions if an approval was granted. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in opposition or in favor in attendance. Being none. 

Chairman Laird asked for any further questions from Board members. 

Being None – Chairman Laird thanked Mr. O’Neal for coming. 

**<u>Subject of Variance:</u>** Variance 2023-1060 Carmen Santiago Hall and George A. Hall, 26536 Harbor Road. 

**VAR 23-2538:** The applicant proposes a lot reconfiguration of a nonconforming lot of record on a 3,705 sq ft. in the MRC Zoning District. 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Ms. Carmen Santiago Hall, applicant, came forward and was sworn.

<!-- page 3 -->
Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain her request. 

Ms. Santiago Hall reiterated what Mr. O’Neal said, the vacant lot between their properties would allow for more parking and beach access. 

Mr. Illuminati stated that the staff report will be entered into the record as exhibit #1. 

Mr. Drewer read the conditions if an approval was granted. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in opposition or in favor in attendance. Being none. 

Chairman Laird asked for any further questions from Board members. 

Being None – Chairman Laird thanked Ms. Santiago Hall for coming. 

# **Findings:** 

SE 23-2538, the Board voted unanimously to approve the request 

Paul Emely – yes Andrew Baines – yes William Cottman – yes Isaac Ballard – yes Brian Laird – yes 

VAR 23-1059, the Board voted unanimously to approve the request, 

Paul Emely – yes Andrew Baines – yes William Cottman – yes Isaac Ballard – yes Brian Laird – yes 

VAR 23-1060, the Board voted unanimously to approve the request, 

Paul Emely – yes Andrew Baines – yes William Cottman – yes Isaac Ballard – yes Brian Laird – yes
