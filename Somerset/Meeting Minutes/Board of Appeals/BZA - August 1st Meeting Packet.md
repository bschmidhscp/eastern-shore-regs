**Administrative Appeal of CAVAR-25-016 Board of Zoning Appeals Critical Area Variance request of Somerset County, Maryland Island Sunset, LLC for 11190 Roland Parks Road** 

### **<u>DECISION AND FINDINGS OF FACT</u>** 

Island Sunset, LLC, (“Applicant”) is the owner of certain real property identified as Somerset County Tax Map 0020, Parcel 25, ID # 11-003338 (the “Property”), located at 11190 Roland Parks Road in Somerset County, Maryland. The Property is 20 acres ( +/871,200 square feet) and is located within Somerset County’s Critical Area (“Critical Area”). The property was previously improved with multiple structures; however, in January 2025 the structures were destroyed by a fire. The Applicant seeks to rebuild the property through consolidating the lot coverage from the various former structures into a primary structure, associated decking and a shed. The entirety of the property is within the Critical Area and practically all of the property is burdened by the 100 foot buffer to include the majority of the proposed development. 

On July 18, 2025 a hearing was held and the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals (“Board”) received evidence from the Applicant through exhibits and witnesses who testified. Additionally, the Somerset County Department of Technical and Community Service (the “County”) presented evidence. The parties’ evidence centered around the ten variance provisions, set forth in the Somerset County Critical Area Ordinance, Chapter 1-115(B), which must be satisfied for a variance to be granted. Those provisions are: 

(1) Due to special features of the site or special conditions or circumstances

<!-- page 4 -->
peculiar to the applicant's land or structure, a literal enforcement of the Somerset County Critical Area program would result in an unwarranted hardship to the applicant; 

(2) A literal interpretation of the Somerset County Critical Area program would deprive the applicant of a use of land or a structure permitted to others in accordance with the provisions this Ordinance; 

(3) The granting of the variance would not confer upon the applicant any special privilege that would be denied by this Ordinance to other lands or structures in accordance with the provisions of the Somerset County Critical Area program; 

(4) The variance request is not based upon conditions or circumstances that are the result of actions by the applicant; 

(5) The variance request does not arise from any conforming or nonconforming condition on any neighboring property; 

(6) The granting of the variance would not adversely affect water quality or adversely impact fish, wildlife, or plant habitat within the Somerset County Critical Area program; 

- (7) The granting of the variance would be in harmony with the general spirit and intent of the Critical Area law, the provisions of this Ordinance, and the Somerset County Critical Area program; 

- (8) That the applicant has the burden of proof and the burden of persuasion to overcome the presumption of nonconformance pursuant to sub-section (5); 

- (9) That based on competent and substantial evidence, the approving authority shall make written findings as to whether the applicant has overcome the presumption of nonconformance as established above; and 

(10) That the written findings may be based on evidence introduced and

<!-- page 5 -->
testimony presented by the applicant, the County or any other government agency, or any other person deemed appropriate by the County. 

After receiving the evidence, the Board asked questions of the Applicant and County in relation to the requested variance. Following the questions, and without public opposition, the Board unanimously voted to grant the variance sought. 

Based on the evidence received, the Board finds as a matter of fact that the existing lot coverage, prior to the fire, was 4,860 square feet, of which 4,034 square feet was located within the 100 foot buffer. Further, the Board finds that the proposed lot coverage, based on the submitted preliminary plans (excluding Decks C and D, which will be constructed per Critical Area requirements to allow for pervious areas), is 4,464 square feet, of which 3,863 square feet is located within the 100 foot buffer. Based on the plans submitted, the percentage of lot coverage is .51%. The Board finds that the survey submitted by the Applicant accurately reflects the 100 foot buffer and properly accounted for the terrain of the property to include steep slopes, nontidal wetlands, hydric soils and highly erodible soils in calculating lot coverage. The Board does not give any weight to the letter, dated May 13, 2025, submitted by the State of Maryland Critical Area Commission (“the Commission”). The Commission had the opportunity to participate at the hearing before the Board and did not. At that time, the Commission could have further explained the letter, rebutted Applicant’s contentions that the proposed plans meet the provisions of County and State law and, most importantly, been subject to examination by the Board. 

The specific finding of the Board is that the Applicant’s variance request satisfies each of the variance provisions set forth in Chapter 1-115.  Applying the provisions, the Board finds: 

**(1) Due to special features of the site or special conditions or circumstances peculiar to the applicant's land or structure, a literal enforcement of the Somerset County Critical Area program would result in an unwarranted hardship to the applicant.**

<!-- page 6 -->
The property is an island located entirely within the Critical Area and has been subject to heavy shoreline erosion. The property has limited areas of development due to its shape, which also contributes to special conditions. The Board observed the property prior to the hearing. The Board, as reflected in the record, adopts those observation as to the uniqueness of this property. Due to shoreline erosion and the limited ability to develop, the consolidation of the two buildings into a single building addresses the peculiarity of this parcel. Without consolidation of the preexisting square footage into a single primary structure, the Applicant would experience an unwarranted hardship. The Board finds that the Applicant has satisfied this provision. 

**(2) A literal interpretation of the Somerset County Critical Area program would deprive the applicant of a use of land or a structure permitted to others in accordance with the provisions this Ordinance;** 

The property is delineated as Resource Conservation Area and located within the Conservation Zoning District. The Applicant has reduced the lot coverage while utilizing portions of the original foundation. A literal interpretation of the law would deprive the applicant use of the land, which is permitted to others. The Board finds that the Applicant has satisfied this provision. 

**(3) The granting of the variance would not confer upon the applicant any special privilege that would be denied by this Ordinance to other lands or structures in accordance with the provisions of the Somerset County Critical Area program;** 

The property was previously developed with 4,860 square feet of lot coverage, of which 4,034 square feet was located within the 100 foot buffer. Based on the submitted preliminary plans (excluding Decks C and D,

<!-- page 7 -->
which will be constructed per Critical Area requirements to allow for pervious areas), the lot coverage is 4,464 square feet, of which 3,863 square feet is located within the 100 foot buffer. The Applicant’s proposed development of the property is consistent with the Ordinance and does not confer a special privilege that would be denied to others. The Board finds that the Applicant has satisfied this provision. 

**(4) The variance request is not based upon conditions or circumstances that are the result of actions by the Applicant;** 

The variance request is based on the special features of the site, as outlined in provision 1, which are not the result of actions by the Applicant. Additionally, there is no evidence that the Applicant contributed to or caused, directly or indirectly, the fire which destroyed the previous development of the parcel. The Board finds that the Applicant has satisfied this provision. 

**(5) The variance request does not arise from any conforming or nonconforming condition on any neighboring property;** 

The Applicant and the County agree that the variance request does not arise from any conforming or nonconforming condition on any neighboring property. The Board agrees and finds that the Applicant has satisfied this provision. 

**(6) The granting of the variance would not adversely affect water quality or adversely impact fish, wildlife, or plant habitat within the Somerset County Critical Area program;** 

The Board finds that the proposed development of the property does not expand the impervious area of the lot. There is no evidence to suggest that the granting of the variance would adversely affect water quality or impact fish, wildlife, or plant habitat on the property.  The Board finds that the

<!-- page 8 -->
Applicant has satisfied this provision. 

**(7) The granting of the variance would be in harmony with the general spirit and intent of the Critical Area law, the provisions of this Ordinance, and the Somerset County Critical Area program;** 

“The goals of the Critical Area law are to: (1) Minimize adverse impacts on water quality that result from pollutants that are discharged from structures or run off from surrounding lands; (2) Conserve fish, wildlife and plant habitat; and (3) Establish land use policies for development in the Critical Area which accommodate growth as well as address the environmental impacts that the number, movement, and activities of people may have on the area.” Somerset County Critical Area Ordinance, Chapter 1-102(c). The spirit and intent of the Critical Area Law is to accommodate growth and protect the surrounding area. The proposed property variance does not further develop the property, but coalesces the lot coverage of the previous development, in a manner to minimize adverse impacts to the property, surrounding water and ecosystem. The Board finds that the Applicant has satisfied this provision. 

**(8) That the applicant has the burden of proof and the burden of persuasion to overcome the presumption of nonconformance pursuant to sub-section (5)** ; 

The Board finds that the Applicant has overcome the burden of proof and burden of persuasion as to nonconformance as the proposed development reduces the lot coverage in comparison to the original development and the first seven provisions are satisfied.  As a result, the Board finds that the Applicant has satisfied this provision. 

( **9) That based on competent and substantial evidence, the approving authority shall make written findings as to whether the applicant has**

**overcome the presumption of nonconformance as established above;** 

The Board finds that the evidence presented by the Applicant was competent. The Board issues this Decision and Findings of Fact, regarding the variance request, to satisfy this provision. 

**(10) That the written findings may be based on evidence introduced and testimony presented by the applicant, the County or any other government agency, or any other person deemed appropriate by the County.** 

All legal requirements pertaining to a public meeting were met. The Board considered the evidence, both physical and testimonial, submitted by the Applicant and the County. The findings contained within this decision are based solely on the evidence presented at the hearing and the site visit by the Board members. 

For these reasons, a variance for the property, in accordance with the variance request application, on the real property identified as Somerset County Tax Map 0020, Parcel 25, ID # 11-003338, located at 11190 Roland Parks Road in Somerset County, Maryland, is hereby GRANTED.

2. Afforestation to mitigate any impervious surface created and to mitigate for all disturbance to the Buffer, measured as, a calculation of square feet disturbed, on at least a 3 to 1 ratio. 

3. Implementation of any mitigation measures that relate to Habitat Protection Areas as delineated in the County Critical Area Ordinance, or as recommended by

4. Develop the final site plan in accordance with the submitted preliminary site plan and representations made on the record. The deck shall be constructed so that all decking is pervious.

6. Health Department approval is required. 

7. Comply with all County, State and Federal ordinances and laws in developing the property. 

8. All conditions shall be completed within two years from the date of the variance decision.
