# **SOMERSET COUNTY BOARD OF ZONING APPEALS MEETING MINUTES** _February 21, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 21<sup>st</sup> day of February 2025 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Board of Appeals Members Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Brian Laird, Chairman Jesse Drewer, Director Issac Ballard Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Admin. Paul Emely AC Alrey Andrew Baines **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** William Cottman Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Chairman Laird called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Mr. Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the January 17, 2025 meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. The motion was made by Mr. Alrey and seconded by Mr. Emely 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** SE 2025-2548 Mohsen Farms 30868 McCormick Swamp RD 

**SE 25-2548:** The applicant is seeking approval to construct 3 Chicken Houses in the R-2 zoning 

**Testimony:** Mr. Laird read the ad into the record. Mr. Robert Hajir of 31177 McCormick Swamp Rd came forward and was sworn in. 

Mr. Laird asked the applicant to explain his request. 

Mr. Hajir testified that there used to be 3 poultry houses on the lot in question. They were torn down as they were in disrepair and needed to be replaced. Most of the infrastructure is still there including the foundations, feed, generator, well, and manure shed with nutrient management plan. He also noted that they are already storing their manure from their plant in the existing manure shed on site. Wants to put a maximum of 20,000 birds in each house.

Mr. Laird then asked if they had purchased the old ADF plant to run their existing chicken processing plant out of. 

Mr. Hajir confirmed they had, he also noted that his company had been running for nine years. The company processes 50,000 birds a week and sells all over the country. They employ already 53 and will need to employ at least one or two more to run the houses. 

Mr. Laird then asked for clarification that the company intended to place the new houses in the existing footprints from the old houses. 

Mr. Hajir then confirmed this, and noted they intended to continue using the existing manure shed. They already have someone with an existing nutrient management plan and noted that if needed they will get their own nutrient management plan. 

Mr. Laird than asked if any members of the board had any questions. 

Mr. Emely wanted confirmation that the new houses would be the same size as those previously on the lot. 

To which Mr. Hajir replied in the affirmative. 

Mr. Cottman then inquired about the age of the chickens they were processing. 

Mr. Hajir then explained they were typically 38-40 days old. 

Mr. Cottman than asked whether they sell whole chickens or just parts. 

Mr. Hajir replied that the do a mixture of both, and that their business is roughly split half and half. 

Mr. Alrey then expressed concern about the home located across the street from the intended site. 

It was then explained the house belongs to another chicken farming family, and that the home sits empty for the majority of the year, only really used for family get togethers. Mr. Hajir also noted that the manure shed is already in use and there have been no complaints about the smell. Mr. Hajir also noted that he lives only half a mile down the road himself, so if anything were to happen he would be in close proximity to respond. 

Mr. Laird then asked if they were anyone in the audience in opposition. 

There was none. 

William Cornish read the staff report into record. Mr. Laird made this exhibit 1.

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** SE 2025-2549 David King & Gerald Philpott of 27999 Coulbourne Creek Rd 

**SE 25-2549:** The applicant is seeking amendment to Special Exception 15-2478 for the purpose of increasing the number of guests they are allowed to accommodate to 20. 

**Testimony:** Mr. Laird read the ad into the record. Mr. David king and Mr. Gerald Philpott of 27999 Coulbourne Creek Rd came forward and were sworn in. 

Mr. Drewer read the Staff report into the record 

Mr. Laird asked the applicants to explain their request. 

Mr. King explained that the initial special exception granted only allowed them to operate their bed and breakfast out of their main house, and limited them to only renting 5 rooms. Mr. King went on to explain that they also owned a 5-room guest house with all separate facilities that they would like to extend their bed and breakfast use to. 

Mr. Philpott then added that the expanded use would require absolutely no additional construction. He also added that they had operated at their current capacity for the past 9 years with absolutely no complaints. 

Mr. Alrey then inquired about whether the existing driveway, and parking area could handle the increase in traffic. 

Mr. Philpott then explained they have no neighbors in a two-mile radius. 

Mr. King then explained they had parking for 23. 

Mr. Ballard and Mr. Baines both expressed that they had been to the property before and that the place was beautiful. 

Mr. Laird than asked if there were any other questions 

There were none.
