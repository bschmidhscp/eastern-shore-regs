SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning “:ppeals MEETING MINUTES November 1, 2024 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 19th day ofNovember 2021 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

Board Members Present: 

Donald Price Andrew Baines William Cottman Paul Emely Isaac Ballard 

## Others Present: 

Will Cornish, Zoning Specialist Catherine Skeeter , Planner 

Vice Chairman Ballard opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Subject of Appeal: SE 2021-2523 George E Harmon Jr 

SE 2021- 2523: George E Harmon Jr, owner of property located at 26608 Crackertown Rd, Crisfield MD is shown on Tax Map 64I, Grid 19, Parcel 807 in the 8" Election District. The property is zoned R2. The owner has requested a Special Exception for a replacement Mobile Home per Section 4.4 Nonconforming structures and Section 11.3 of the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance # 1144. The original case was opened on October 15, 2021 and continued until Nov 19, 2021 

# Testimony: 

Isaac Ballard opened the meeting at 7:30 pm. The case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. George Harmon Jr was sworn in. Vice Chairman Ballard asked Mr. Harmon to express to the board his request. Mr. Harmon explained that he purchased the property on Crackertown Rd and wished to replace the existing mobile home which is in disrepair with a 1999 model. Mr Harmon presented pictures to the board and they were entered into the record. 

Vice Chairman Ballard asked if any Board memebres had any questions, being none, Mr Ballard asked if anyone in attendance was in opposition of the request. No one in attendance.

Findings: SE 21-2523 George Harmon Jr.
