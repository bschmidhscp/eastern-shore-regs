The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 18 day of October 2024 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

Board Members Present: 

Brian Laird - Chairman Isaac Ballard Paul Emely AC Alrey Andrew Baines William Cottman Others Present: William Cornish, Zoning Specialist Jesse Drewer, Director Mary Philips, Assistant Director Andrew Illuminati, Board Attorney 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. Chairman Laird called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Mr. Laird asked if there were any changes to the corrected minutes from the August 16, 2024 meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. Isaac Ballard made a motion to approve, second by Andrew Baines. 

Subject of Appeal: Agricultural Relocation 2024-196 Cynthia Ann Conner Farm Market RD 

AR 24-196: Owner is requesting Board of Zoning Appeals approval for the relocation of a 2-acre Forest Conservation Area. Owner is proposing to dissolve the unimproved subdivision into 1 large parcel. 

Testimony: Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Ms. Cynthia Ann Conner came forward and was sworn in. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain her request.

<!-- page 4 -->
Ms. Conner testified that she would like to relocate the forest conservation area of an approved site pan. She wants to move it to the perimeter of the property. She presented a larger site plan, which was submitted as exhibit 1 (one). She mentioned she wanted to relocate it because the original site plan was never developed. 

Mrs. Philips read the staff report into the record. Chairman laird made this Exhibit 2. 

Chairman Laird asked if the board had any questions, all replied no. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in the audience who wished to testify in favor of the request. 

No one came forward. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in the audience who wished to testify in opposition to the request. 

No one came forward. 

Chairman Laird thanked Ms. Conner for coming. 

Subject of Appeal: SE 2024-2546 Cobb Heritage LLC 11587 Pine Pole Rd, and 11752 Pine Pole RD 

SE 24-2540: The applicant is seeking approval for expansion of the existing nonconforming use on both parcels. 

Testimony: Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Mr. Todd Baker of 1901 broad street Pocomoke City Md came forward and was sworn in. 

Mr. Todd Baker expressed that the land was once occupied by commercial research and development farm. The buildings were used to do testing for the poultry industry. They have since moved to more traditional Chicken farming. They will have 2 flocks of 40,000 and will produce eggs for major food distributers. Mr. Baker repeatably stressed that all of their poultry farming would be handled more in line with traditional breeders. 

Mr. Chris Custis PO Box 786 Princess Anne Md came forward. Mr. Custis presented an aerial view of the properties. He expressed they would be clearing minimal trees. And that their site plan would meet all the requirements of any other agricultural use. Chairman Laird made this exhibit 1. 

Mr. Custis then expressed that the zoning of the properties was Agricultural, and that the taxes were being accessed for an agricultural use. He also expressed that all state agencies had

<!-- page 5 -->
considered the farm an Agricultural use. He also noted all poultry was to be sold at market, and that the Chicken houses were relatively small for modern houses. 

After studying the aerial AC questioned what was behind the Chicken houses 

Mr. Custis explained that he was looking at woods. 

There were no further speakers. 

Mrs. Philips then entered the staff report into the record this was made exhibit 3. 

Chairman Laird asked if there were any questions. 

Mr. Baines inquired how many new employees there would be 

Mr. Baker stated there are currently 12 and would be 30 or more. 

Mr. Ballard asked if the new chicken houses would be double sized. 

Mr. Baker clarified there are two models one would be ~ 270 square feet and the other ~240 square feet. 

There were no further questions. 

###### Findings: 

AR 24-296, The Board voted unanimously to approve the request to relocate the forest conservation area Subject to the conditions in the staff report

Request: Applicants are requesting Special Exception approval for conversion of an existing residential structure into a restaurant without drive-through service. 

I. SUMMARY OF REQUEST: 

Sam Brillinger, representative of Polster Aram Etal, is requesting Board of Zoning Appeals approval for the establishment of a restaurant without a drive through located on Smith Island Road. Proposal is to convert existing residential structure into restaurant. Section 5.7(d)33 requires a Special Exception to be approved by the Board subject to conditions. 

##### II. DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPERTY AND SURROUNDING AREA

The property is located within an unincorporated area of Somerset County and is zoned MaritimeResidential-Commercial (MRC) in the Smith Island Election District. Site is located on the north east corner of the intersection of Smith Island Rd and Tyler RD. the Parcel is .482 Acres. The property is improved an existing residential dwelling. The Parcel is surrounded by primarily residential structures and is across the directly opposite the Ewell Fire department. Sites is within the intensely developed area of the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area and the flood zone. There are several single-family homes in the vicinity with an average age of 100 years. 

IV. CODE CRITERIA: 

Two sections of the Zoning Ordinance are relevant to this request. Each section is addressed below. 

###### Somerset County Zoning Ordinance #1205 

###### Section 11.2 Special Exceptions. 

When hearing evidence necessary for granting of any Special Exception, the Board shall consider all pertinent facts in the case, and render a decision in accordance with the following principles: 

   - (1) The proposed use conforms in all respects to minimum requirements of the district in which it is located; 

   - (2) The proposed use will not adversely affect the health, safety, and general welfare of residents of the area; 

   - (3) The proposed use will not interfere with the adequate and orderly provision of public facilities necessary to service the area or the proposed Special Exceptions; 

   - (4) The proposed use will not create congestion in the road or undue traffic hazards and that adequate ingress and egress is provided; 

   - (5) The proposed use will not adversely affect the area and surrounding property due to adverse environmental characteristics including undue smoke, odor, noise, improper drainage inadequate access; 

   - (6) The proposed use will not adversely affect the established character of the area; and 

   - (7) The proposed use is consistent with the guidelines established for such uses in the County’s Critical Area Program, where applicable. 

   - (8) The proposed use is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan or a local Area Plan adopted by the Board of County Commissioners.
