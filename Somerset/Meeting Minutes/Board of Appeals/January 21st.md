SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES January 21, 2022 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 21st day of January 2022 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

### Board Members Present: 

Brian Laird - Chairman Donald Price William Cottman Paul Emely Isaac Ballard 

Andrew Baines- Absent 

#### Others Present: 

Mary Phillips, Zoning Administrator/ Assistant Director Catherine Skeeter, Planner Will Cornish, Zoning Specialist 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Case #1 

Subject of Appeal: Administrative Review 2021-195 Michael Rawlings 

AR 2021-195; Michael Rawlings of 13513 Bobtown Rd, Princess Anne MD 21853, have applied for a Administrative Review variance for the construction of a 12 ft high fence on property located at 13513 Bobtown Rd. Princess Anne MD (Tax Map 08 Grid 03 Parcel 261) Request a review the of Zoning Administrators determination of 8 ft height limitation for fencing. 

### Testimony: 

Mr. Laird read the case advertisement into the record. Mr. Michael Rawlings came forward and was sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mr. Rawlings express to the Board his intention. Mr. Rawlings stated he is seeking to construct a 12 ft high fence on his property on Bobtown Rd. to hide his equipment and to provide a place for equipment racks which will be attached to the inside of the fencing. Mr. Rawlings stated that due to the height of his equipment, that the extra height is needed to hide his equipment from the neighborhood. Mr. Laird entered the staff report into the record.

Mr. Laird asked Mr. Rawlings to explain pictures of the fencing he is proposing and the areas of his lot to be enclosed. He has provided photos for the board. The pictures where entered into the record. Mr. Rawlings explained that he currently has large equipment in front of the house that will be moved to the rear yard once the fencing is erected. The front yard will be kept clear of equipment once the fence is completed. Mr. laird questioned why8 ft height was not adequate. Mr. Rawlings explained that the size and height of his equipment would not be shielded from view with 8 ft. 

Ms. Phillips explained that requirements for residential fencing height is not addressed in the code and that where fencing is addressed, the height limitation is 8 ft. 

Mr. Laird asked the Board members if they had any questions or comments. 

Mr. Price discussed the area Mr Rawlings will have to construct a shed or pole building inside the fenced yard. 

Mr. Ballard expressed his pleasure to see a property owner wanting to keep his equipment out of view and his yard in order. 

Mr. Laird asked if there was anyone in attendance in opposition that wished to come forward. There was no one in opposition in attendance. 

Findings: AR 2021-195 Michael Rawlings 

All Board members have been out to the site. 

The Board voted approve a 12 ft height fence located on 13513 Bobtown Rd. Princess Anne MD, 

Board Vote: Brian Laird-Yes 

Bill Cottman — Yes Donald Price — Yes Paul Emely- Yes Isaac Ballard- Yes 

#### Case #2 

## Special Exception Request - SE 2021-2524 11120 Somerset LLC 

SE 2021-2524 Applicant 11120 Somerset LLC has applied for a Special Exception for an increase of a Non-Conforming use, for a 15 ft x 100ft addition to an existing commercial building located at 11120 Somerset Ave Princess Anne MD. (Tax Map 23, Grid 6, Parcel 60) 

Testimony: Mr. Brian Laird — Chairman, read the advertisement into the record.

Mr. Christopher Drummond, Esq representing the owner of 11120 Somerset LL came forward and was sworn. 

Mr. Laird asked Mr. Drummond to explain the request. 

Mr. Drummond explained that the owner was currently out of State and unable to attend. Mr. Drummond summarized a sequence of events which lead to the application for the Special exception request. Construction was commenced and a stop work order was placed by the County Building Inspector. It was discovered that the Town of Princess Anne had erroneously issued a permit for the addition to the building which is out of their jurisdiction. 

When application was correctly made to the County Planning Office it was discovered the Residential Zoning Classification which made the building a Non conforming use. 

Mr. Drummond explained that the Building was constructed in the early 1970’s and has always been commercial. He further explained that after the Zoning update in 2012 the property and adjoining properties were classified as residential. 

Mr. Drummond explained that the procedure to correct the zoning is a lengthy one and that the quickest remedy is to come before the Board and a rezoning will be pursued later. 

Ms. Phillips gave a short history of the zoning for the parcel and how the mapping appears to be a mistake. 

Mr. Drummond explained the need for the Board to approve the expansion of the Non-conforming use. 

Mr. Laird asked for comments or questions of the Board members. There were none. 

Mr. Laird asked if there was anyone in opposition to the request who would like to speak. There was no one in attendance in opposition. 

Board Vote: Brian Laird-Yes Bill Cottman — Yes Donald Price — Yes Paul Emely- Yes Isaac Ballard- Yes 

On a motion made by Chairman Laird and seconded by Mr. Ballard, the Board of Zoning Appeals voted to adjourn the meeting. 

The meeting adjourned at 8:03 P.M. 

Respectfully Submitted, 

Brian Laird, Chairman/AW
