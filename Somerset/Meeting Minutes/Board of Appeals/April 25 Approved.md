# **<u>Minutes</u>** 

April 25, 2025 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 25<sup>th</sup> day of April 2025 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

## **<u>Board of Appeals Members Present:</u>** 

**<u>Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** 

Brian Laird, Chairman Jesse Drewer, Director Isaac Ballard Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Administrator A.C. Alrey Andrew Baines **<u>Board of Appeals Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Paul Emely Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Chairman Laird called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Chairman Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the February 21, 2025 meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. The motion was made by Mr. Alrey and seconded by Mr. Ballard 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** SE 2025-2550 Burton – 28590 Deal Island RD 

**SE 25-2550:** The applicant is seeking approval to conduct a farm school in AR zoning 

**Testimony:** Mr. Laird read the ad into the record. Mrs. Nadine Burton of 28590 Deal Island Rd came forward and was sworn in. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain her request. 

Mrs. Burton testified that she works at the University, and runs the farm on the side, where she grows food that is not native to the area. she would like to open a school that would teach the farmers how to cook the untraditional crops she grows. 

**_11916 Somerset Avenue, Suite 211 • Princess Anne, Maryland 21853_** Telephone (410) 651-1424 • Fax (410) 651-2597 Email: dtcs@somersetmd.us

Mr. Baines inquired as to what types of crops she is currently growing. 

Mrs. Burton answered she grows Jamaican spinach, squash, okra, jute leaves, pumpkins, and other Caribbean foods. 

Mr. Laird than asked if there were any other questions 

Mr. Alrey then mentioned he had taken a bus tour of the property before, and found it very impressive. 

Mr. Baines added that the farm had a partnership with the food bank 

Mr. Laird then asked if there was anyone who came to speak in favor of Mrs. Burton 

Shelly Anne Henry of 20360 Urban Commons came forward. She is Nadine’s daughter and delivers the crops to the food bank. She testified that people at the food bank have asked about how to properly prepare the crops in the past. This indicates there is a real need for the service Ms. Burton is proposing at the location. 

Mr. Laird than asked if any one else wanted to speak in favor of the proposal 

Prem Bhanda of 163 Peter Parkway Fruitland came forward. He works for University of Maryland Eastern Shore ads an agro-tourism ambassador. He believes Mrs. Burton’s project will expand tourism in the county, and increase employment. 

Mr. Laird asked if anybody wanted to speak in opposition of the request 

No one stepped forward 

Mr. Laird asked if the board had any questions 

Mr. Emely had none 

Mr. Ballard mentioned that Mrs. Burton ran a good operation 

Mr. Baines questioned If Mrs. Burton ran all the houses her self 

Mrs. Burton said it was primarily her and her daughter. During peak months they employ up to 5 people. 

Mr. Alrey questioned if her driveway needed improvements to handle the increased traffic 

Mrs. Burton replied that there is already existing parking and her driveway is long enough to handle the increased traffic 

Mr. Cornish read the staff report into the record.
