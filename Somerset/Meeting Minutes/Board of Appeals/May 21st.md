The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 15th day of May 2021 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

#### Board Members Present: 

Brian Lair, Chairman Donald Price Andrew Baines William Cottman Paul Emely Isaac Ballard 

Others Present: Mary Phillips, Zoning Administrator / Assistant Director 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Subject of Appeal: SE 2021-2520 Doug Reynolds / Reynolds Excavating 

SE 2021-2520: George E Harmon Jr, owner of property located at 26608 Crackertown Rd, Crisfield MD is shown on Tax Map 64I, Grid 19, Parcel 807 in the 8"" Election District. The property is zoned R2. The owner has requested a Special Exception for a replacement Mobile Home per Section 4.4 Nonconforming structures and Section 11.3 of the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance # 1144. The original case was opened on October 15, 2021 and continued until Nov 19, 2021 

### Testimony: 

Brian Laird, Chairman, opened the meeting at 7:30 pm. The case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. Doug Reynolds was sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mr. Reynolds to express to the board his request. Mr. Reynolds explained that he purchased the property on Backbone Rd and wished to establish a Borrow Pit / Strip Mining Operation.. Mr. Reynolds provided a plan by Chris Custis. Mary Phillips of DTCS stated that the members had been provided with the plan and the plan was entered as Exhibit 1. The plan showing the proposed site is the subject of the current request, along with proposed setbacks and buffer areas from the road and adjoining property lines. Mr. Reynolds pointed out that the operation will start at the rear of the property which is buffered by existing trees. An Erosion and Sediment

Control plan for Forest Harvest Operations has been filed with the Soil Conservation District. In response to the Board’s question about future development, Mr. Reynolds stated that the wooded area next to the development to the West will be preserved, The State of MD owns land on the East which provides for another buffer and the operation will maintain a 350’ setback buffer with a vegetated berm along Backbone Rd. as shown on the submitted plan. 

Mr. Reynolds stated that access will be from the existing farm lane on Backbone Rd. 

There appeared no other adjoining property owners either in person or by letter before the Board to express opposition or support to the Application for the Special Exception. 

## Findings: SE 21-2520 Douglas Reynolds 

All Board members have been out to the site. 

The Board voted to grant the Special Exception to establish a Borrow Pit / Strip Mining Operation subject to the following conditions: 

1. Prior to the issuance of a Zoning Certificate, all permits from MDE for Surface Mining shall be obtained. 

2. Prior to the issuance of a Zoning Certificate, applicant shall obtain approval the Somerset County Forest Conservation program. 

3. A350’ setback from Backbone Road shall be maintained. 

4. The proposed vegetative buffer shall be installed per submitted plans. 

5. Applicant shall comply with all Federal, State and Local laws including but not limited to all requirements of the Zoning Ordinance and the County’s adopted Building Code. 

Board Vote: Brian Laird-Yes Bill Cottman — Yes Donald Price — Yes Andrew Baines — Yes Isaac Ballard- Yes Paul Emely- Yes 

On a motion made by Chairman Laird and seconded by Mr. Price, the Board of Zoning Appeals voted to adjourn the meeting. 

The meeting adjourned at 8:10 P.M.
