# **SOMERSET COUNTY BOARD OF ZONING APPEALS MEETING MINUTES** 

_January 17, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 17<sup>th</sup> day of January 2025 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Board of Appeals Members Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Issac Ballard, Acting Chairman Jesse Drewer, Director Paul Emely Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Admin. AC Alrey Carter Finney, Planning and Zoning Specialist Andrew Baines William Cottman **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** 

**<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Isaac Ballard opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Isaac Ballard called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Mr. Ballard asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the October 18, 2024 meeting, Mr. Alrey asked that the minutes be corrected to show that Mr. Carter Finney attended the last meeting. The motion was made by Mr. Cottman and seconded by Mr. Baines 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** SE 2025-2547 Samantha Brillinger 3991 Smith Island RD 

**SE 25-2547:** The applicant is seeking approval to open a restaurant in the MRC zoning 

**Testimony:** Mr. Ballard read the ad into the record. Ms. Samantha Brillinger came forward and was sworn in. 

Mr. Ballard asked the applicant to explain her request. 

Mrs. Brillinger testified that she would like to open a small coffee shop to serve drinks, salads and pastries to tourist that visit Smith Island. For now, it will be a take away only, so there will be no dining on the premises. 

Mr. Ballard then asked if the pictures of the Kitchen in the staff report gave an accurate representation of what they planned to do.
