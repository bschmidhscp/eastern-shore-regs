The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 21<sup>st</sup> day of November 2025 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Board of Appeals Members Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Acting Chairman Isaac Ballard Jesse Drewer, Director A.C. Alrey Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Administrator William Cottman Andrew Baines Paul Emely **<u>Board of Appeals Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Acting Chairman Ballard opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Mr. Ballard called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Mr. Ballard asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the September 19<sup>th</sup> meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. The motion was made by Mr. Alrey and seconded by Mr. Cottman 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** VA 2025-1062 Island Sunset LLC 11190 Roland Parks Rd 

**SE 25-1062:** The applicant is seeking exemption to parking and landscaping requirements 

**Testimony:** Mr. Drewer read the ad into the record. Mr. Frank Parks of 11190 Roland Parks Rd came forward and was sworn in. 

Mr. Ballard asked the applicant to explain his request. 

Mr. Cornish read the staff report into record. Chairman Ballard made this Staff Exhibit #1. 

Mr. Parks testified They had been preforming the retreats on the island for years. They have roughly 5 or 6 visits a year of 40 to 50 visitors.

###### **I.** **<u>SUMMARY OF REQUEST:</u>** 

The applicant requests permission to replace a Single-Wide Mobile Home on a parcel per Section 4.4 – Nonconforming Uses. The current mobile home was permitted in 2005 through Permit #118110, at that time it was already replacing a non-conforming mobile home. The current home is 1056 sqft per assessment records. The replacement is 28’ x 56’ and 1568 sqft doublewide. A Double-Wide Mobile Home (DWMH) is not allowed in the current C-2 District but the proposed one can be approved because there is an existing nonconforming mobile home on the property. 

###### **II.** **<u>DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPERTY AND SURROUNDING AREA:</u>** 

The property is zoned C-2 (General Commercial) and is located on the southwest side of Sawmill Road approximately 150’ to the southwest side of Ocean Highway. The parcel is partially wooded with the exception of the building site which is buffered from view by trees along Sawmill Road. 

The property is located within an unincorporated area of Somerset County.  The property is bordered on the north, east, and west by wooded land.

<!-- page 7 -->
###### **III.** **<u>CODE CRITERIA:</u>** 

Two sections of the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance (#1212) are relevant to this request. Each section is addressed below. 

###### **1) Section 4.4 – Nonconforming Structures:** 

**Nonconforming Structures -** Where a lawful structure exists at the effective date of adoption or amendment of this Ordinance that could not be built under the terms of this Ordinance because of lot area, yards or other dimensional requirements, such structure may be continued subject to Section 6 of this section, provided it remains otherwise lawful, and subject to the following provisions: 

- **a)** No such structure may be enlarged or altered in a way which increases its nonconformity except by authority of the Board of Zoning Appeals. 

- b) Should such structure be destroyed to an extent of more than 80 percent of the structure’s replacement costs at time of destructions as determined by the owner’s insurance company report and/or as reviewed by the Board of Zoning Appeals, it shall not be reconstructed except in conformity with the provisions of this Ordinance or upon authority of the Board of Zoning Appeals. 

   - (1) However, this Ordinance shall not prohibit the replacement of a destroyed residents, provided: 

      - a. The resulting dwelling is completed within twenty-four (24) months after the destruction, and 

      - b. No new or increased nonconformities are created. 

- c) A nonconforming structure shall not be moved ina manner that would result in increased or new nonconformities. 

- d) The above provisions do not apply to replacement poultry houses on existing poultry operations or poultry operations that have not been inactive for more than three (3) years. Existing setbacks for replacement will be extended to a new poultry house to allow consistency on the property and to avoid economic hardship for growers wishing to expand their operations. The footprint of the nonconforming poultry house may be increased to meet current grower requirements.

###### **2) Section 11.2 – Special Exceptions:** 

When hearing evidence necessary for granting of any Special Exception, the Board shall consider all pertinent facts in the case, and render a decision in accordance with the following principles: 

- (1) The proposed use conforms in all respects to minimum requirements of the district in which it is located; The proposed use is confined to minimum setback requirements for the C-2 Zoning District. 

- (2) The proposed use will not adversely affect the health, safety, and general welfare of residents of the area; No anticipated adverse impacts within the surrounding neighborhood. 

- (3) The proposed use will not interfere with the adequate and orderly provision of public facilities necessary to service the area or the proposed Special Exceptions; No anticipated interference. 

- (4) The proposed use will not create congestion in the road or undue traffic hazards and that adequate ingress and egress is provided; Use will not create congestion and is not anticipated to generate any additional vehicle trips per day. 

- (5) The proposed use will not adversely affect the area and surrounding property due to adverse environmental characteristics including undue smoke, odor, noise, improper drainage inadequate access; Proposed use will have no additional adverse impact. 

- (6) The proposed use will not adversely affect the established character of the area; and Proposed use is not anticipated to have negative impact to the neighborhood; the site is secluded and buffered from the road. 

- (7) The proposed use is consistent with the guidelines established for such uses in the County’s Critical Area Program, where applicable. The parcel is not located within the Critical Area 

- (8) The proposed use is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan or a local Area Plan adopted by the Board of County Commissioners. The proposed use is allowed per the adopted Zoning Ordinance, through Special Exception or Approval of the Board of Appeals in certain situations, which is consistent with the adopted 2025 Comprehensive Plan.
