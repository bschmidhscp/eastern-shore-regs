## **SOMERSET COUNTY BOARD OF ZONING APPEALS** 

Brian Laird, Chair | Jesse Drewer, MPA, DTCS Director Isaac Ballard, Vice-Chair William Cornish, CFM, DTCS Assistant Director 

# **<u>Minutes</u>** 

November 21, 2025 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 21<sup>st</sup> day of November 2025 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

### **<u>Board of Appeals Members Present:</u>** 

**<u>Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** 

Acting Chairman Isaac Ballard Jesse Drewer, Director A.C. Alrey Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Administrator William Cottman Andrew Baines Paul Emely **<u>Board of Appeals Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Acting Chairman Ballard opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Mr. Ballard called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Mr. Ballard asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the September 19<sup>th</sup> meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. The motion was made by Mr. Alrey and seconded by Mr. Cottman 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** VA 2025-1062 Island Sunset LLC 11190 Roland Parks Rd 

**SE 25-1062:** The applicant is seeking exemption to parking and landscaping requirements 

**Testimony:** Mr. Drewer read the ad into the record. Mr. Frank Parks of 11190 Roland Parks Rd came forward and was sworn in. 

Mr. Ballard asked the applicant to explain his request. 

Mr. Cornish read the staff report into record. Chairman Ballard made this Staff Exhibit #1. 

Mr. Parks testified They had been preforming the retreats on the island for years. They have roughly 5 or 6 visits a year of 40 to 50 visitors. 

**_11916 Somerset Avenue, Suite 211 • Princess Anne, Maryland 21853_** Telephone (410) 651-1424 • Fax (410) 651-2597 Email: dtcs@somersetmd.us

<!-- page 2 -->
Mr. Ballard asked if the board had any questions for the applicant. 

Mr. Cottman inquired as to whether the location of the structure was to be the same one discussed at a previous hearing. Mr. Parks confirmed it was. 

Mr. Ballard then asked roughly how many people would typically be in attendance. 

Mr. Parks stated that the typical attendance can vary from 30 to 60 people. 

Mr. Ballard then asked if they were anyone in the audience in opposition. 

There was none. 

Mr. Drewer read the staff report into record, including the conditions of approval. 

Mr. Alrey made a motion to accept the variance with all stated conditions. Mr. Cottman seconded the motion 

The board voted to unanimously approve the request congruent on the following conditions of approval 

Mr. Illuminati requested that the board clarify whether the applicants three years to begin construction began at the conclusion of the hearing, or if they wanted to start the clock at the date that the applicants appeal process ended with the courts 

The board decided the three years began today. 

Mr. Ballard thanked Mr. Parks for his time. 



### **<u>The conditions are as follows:</u>** 

- 1) A Building Permit and Zoning Certificate are required prior to any construction. 

- 2) Owner must meet all applicable Local, State and Federal requirements. 

- 3) Building Permits must be obtained within 3 years of approval. 

- 4) Review by TAC Committee and Planning Commission is required for any construction on the site.
