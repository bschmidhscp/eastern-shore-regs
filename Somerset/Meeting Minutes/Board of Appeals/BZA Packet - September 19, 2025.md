Chairman Laird asked the applicants to explain their request. 
 
Mr. Honick explained the importance of the Critical Area law, and that the applicant he was 
representing only required a small deviation in the Critical Area regulations to protect the applicant 
from severe flooding, in his quest to rebuild his burnt down structure and continue his religious 
mission. 
 
Mr. Parks then testified he was the director of the Fisher of Men Foundation, and that Island Sunset 
LLC was the entity that owned the Fisher of men Foundation. The Fisher of Men Foundation has 
now bought out two thirds of Island Sunset LLC and the other third are owned by a partnership of

in question.

burned down.

had four separate structures on the property that he wished to combine into a single larger structure.

not be required. But due to his previous structures were in a low laying area, he wished to move 
the new structure to an area of higher ground.

Exhibit Two.

current hearing. He then added the staff report from hearing CAVAR 25-016 to the record as 
Applicant Exhibit 3. Also added were the applicants site plan for the request. And a letter from the 
Critical Area Commission recommending denial of Mr. Parks request authored by Jamileh 
Soueidan and addressed to Environmental Planner Catherine Skeeter. These were all submitted as 
part of Exhibit 3. But will also be referenced as separate exhibits later.

Variance. Mr. Honick then began to go through the standards with Mr. Parks in order to address 
all of the required questions. The Questions and Answers were as followed:
