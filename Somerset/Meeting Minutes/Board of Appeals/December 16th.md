The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 16"" day of December 2022 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland.

Brian Laird - Chairman William Cottman Isaac Ballard Donald Price Paul Emely

## Case #1 

Subject of Appeal: Special Exception 2022-2526 Village Inn Ventures DBA Tangier Sound Brewing Co 

SE 2022-2526: Applicant, Village Inn Ventures DBA Tangier Sound Brewing Co, requests permission to establish a Microbrewery within the existing, recently rebuilt, structure at 11118 Somerset Ave, Princess Anne. Section 5.7(d)23 allows for “Microbreweries, Brewpubs, and Distilleries” within a C-2 district upon Special Exception. 

Testimony: Chairman Laird read the case advertisement into record. Mr. Jeffrey Lee Phillips, of 6079 Walston Switch Rd, Salisbury, MD, came forward. Mr. Phillips was sworn in and asked by Mr. Laird to express his intentions to the Board. Mr. Phillips explained the concept for the brewery, detailing the plans for on-site brewing, tasting, dining, and an outdoor seating area. Mrs. Mary Phillips entered the application for brewer’s notice from the Md Dept of the Treasury which Mr. Phillips has already secured into record, as exhibit 1. Mrs. Phillips formally entered the Staff

Case #2 and Case #3 heard simultaneously due to interconnection of requests. 

Subject of Appeal: Var 2022-1057 & Var 2022-1058 

VAR 2022-1057 11120 Somerset Ave LLC: The applicant is requesting a Setback Variance for the purpose of a Lot Line Reconfiguration with adjoining Parcel 99. The current building was permitted in 1972 per Zoning Permit # 72-610 under C3 Zoning and a setback Variance was granted in 1972 for a 33’ variance which resulted in a 2’ Rear setback and a 10.66’ Variance which resulted in a 4.34’ Setback. The Zoning District was changed during the 2001 Comprehensive Rezoning from C3 to R-2, which was deemed a mistake and Rezoned in March 2022 as C-2. The required rear setback for C-2 Zoning is 25’ from the rear property line and the required side setback is 15 feet. The lot line relocation will result in an adjusted portion of the rear line requiring a variance of 23’ for a 2’ setback for a length of 28.60’, a 13’ variance for a 2’ side setback along a portion of the new side property line for a length of 30.42’, and an 11’ variance for another portion of the new side lot line resulting in a 4’ setback adjacent to P 99 for a length of 114.30’. 

VAR 2022-1058 Village Inn Ventures, DBA Tangier Sound Brewing: The applicant is requesting a Setback Variance for the purpose of a Lot Line Reconfiguration with adjoining Parcel 60. The current building was recently constructed in the same footprint of an older, failing structure. No evidence could be found ofan existing variance on the property for the old structure’s proximity to the lot line, nor could a building permit be found for the original structure. It is likely that the original structure predated the creation of the Planning Office. The Zoning District was changed during the 2001 Comprehensive Rezoning from C3 to C-2.
