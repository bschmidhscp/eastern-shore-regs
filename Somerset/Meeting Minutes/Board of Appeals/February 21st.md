# SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES February 21, 2020 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board ofZoning Appeals meeting held on the 21* day of February 2020 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

Board Members Present: 

Brian Laird - Chairman Donald Price Andrew Baines William Cottman Paul Emely Isaac Ballard 

Others Present: Mary Phillips, Zoning Specialist 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Subject of Appeal: SE 20-2510 Greg & Lorilee Dunn 

2020-2510; Greg Dunn and Lorilee Dunn of 11190 Messick Rd., Dames Quarter, MD 21821, have applied for a Special Exception on property located at 11190 Messick Rd. Dames Quarter, MD (Tax Map 12 Grid 23 Parcel 102) Owners request a Special Exception for use as temporary vehicle storage similar to Sec 5.7(d) 29. Section 5.4 allows for determination of similar uses not addressed by the zoning ordinance. 

The Case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. Gregg Dunn & Mr. Cody Dunn were sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mr. Greg Dunn to express to the Board his intention. Mr. Greg Dunn is requesting a Special Exception to use his parcel as a temporary vehicle storage. Mr. Cody Dunn explained that they tow vehicles for different police departments and by law; they are required to keep the vehicles until the police departments and or the state release them. He went on the explained that the Maryland Regulations require certain vehicles to be held for a designated amount of time depending on the reason for the vehicle being towed, but the state usually requires a minimum of six (6) months. Mr. Cody Dunn went on to discuss how they keep the vehicles out of the buffer and make sure they are kept neat and tidy. Mr. Greg Dunn explained that they do not keep parts off the vehicle and that they do not
