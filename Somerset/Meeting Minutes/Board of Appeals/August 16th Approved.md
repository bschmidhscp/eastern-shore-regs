**SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals Meeting Minutes** August 16, 2024 



The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 16<sup>th</sup> day of August 2024 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

# **<u>Board Members Present:</u>** 

Brian Laird - Chairman Isaac Ballard Paul Emely AC Alrey Andrew Baines **<u>Others Present:</u>** William Cornish, Zoning Specialist Jesse Drewer, Director Andrew Illuminati, Board Attorney 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Chairman Laird called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Mr. Laird asked if there were any changes to the corrected minutes from the May 17, 2024 meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. Isaac Ballard made a motion to approve, second by Andrew Baines. 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** Special Exception 2024-2545 Steven Smith, Patrick Poloney Log Cabin Lane 

**SE 24-2545:** The applicant requests a Special Exception for the placement of a hunting / fishing cabin on a 6.77 acres Parcel located on Log Cabin Rd in Agriculture Residential (AR) Zoning District. 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Mr. Patrick Poloney came forward and was sworn in. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain his request.

<!-- page 2 -->
Mr. Patrick Poloney testified that he would like to place a hunting and fishing cabin on the property. He submitted a corrected plan for the building that met the size requirements of the cabin, along with a picture of what the proposed completed cabin would look like. Theses were submitted as exhibit one (1) and two (2) respectively. Mr. Poloney stated he planned to stay in the cabin seasonally, and use it as a winter getaway. He will be completely off grid and subsist off of solar panels 

Mr. Drewer read the staff report into the record. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in the audience who wished to testify in favor of the request. 

No one came forward. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in the audience who wished to testify in opposition to the request. 

Mr. Shawn Chaney of 7406 River Road came forward. Mr. Chaney expressed his concern regarding the cabin.  Mr. Chaney claimed the owners were trying to put a hunting cabin where it would be illegal to hunt. Mr. Chaney believed that there would be a lack of game considering the parcel was surrounded by water on three sides. Mr. Chaney also expressed concerns about the disposal of human waste. Mr. Chaney’s final concern was the critical area. He held the belief that areas in the critical area shouldn’t be developed. 

Mr. Ben Wiley 28922 Log Cabin Road came forward. Mr. Wiley questioned how many trees would be cut down and how the property would be accessed. Mr. Wiley also expressed concerns about the disposal of human waste. 

Mr. Claude Leonard Thompson 28861 Log Cabin Road came forward. Mr. Thompson explained an example of a time before when hunters were renting a nearby property. The hunters were negligent and destroyed the property.  Mr. Thompson expressed concern that the cabin would lower property values on the road. 

There were no further speakers. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to address the concerns expressed by the attendees. 

Mr. Patrick Poloney plans to use a composting toilet. He doesn’t plan to hunt on the land he intends to use it to canoe and kayak on the river. At the most he plans to go fishing, but acknowledges he may not even do that. He will not have to take out many, if any trees as the highest part of the land is already cleared. 

Mr. Emely asked if he intended to rent the cabin out considering there was space for multiple people in the cabin.

<!-- page 3 -->
Mr. Poloney answered he has no intentions of renting the cabin out. He only intended to have his son and grandson out to the cabin. The extra beds were included in the price of the cabin and the diagram shown was just the base layout of the cabin as spec from the manufacturer. 

Mr. Alrey asked where he planned to place the toilet and where he planned to source water. He also asked about how Mr. Poloney planned to utilize any appliances. 

Mr. Poloney answered the composting toilet would be in the cabin. He planned to bring bottled water. He continued that his basic appliances could be run off his solar panels. He would also possibly use a wood stove in the future. 

Mr. Alrey then questioned Mr. Poloney about ventilation if a wood stove were to be utilized. 

Mr. Poloney answered there was ample ventilation through the large windows. And that if he decided to go for a wood stove the installers would install piping to let the smoke out. 

Chairman Laird asked if there were any additional questions from Board members. 

Mr. Baines wanted confirmation that there would only be a single cabin placed on the lot. 

Mr. Poloney confirmed this. 

Opposition requested to ask more questions 

Chairman Laird objected as they had already had time to speak. 

# **<u>Findings:</u>** 

**SE 24-2542** , The Board voted unanimously to approve the request for a Special Exception Subject to the conditions in the staff report 



# **Other business** 

Mr. Drewer informed the board about the Zagars intention to purchase a property next to their current one in order to hunt without having to construct a cabin from the prior BZA meeting in May. 

Mr. Ballard expressed shock that the Zagars had continued using the property even after their special exception was denied.

<!-- page 4 -->
Mr. Drewer confirmed that citations have already been placed on the property and that the Zagars had reached back out to the county on the last day of their citation timeline. 

Mr. Emely wanted it clarified that the couldn’t use the current cabin while they were in the process of purchasing the new one. He also inquired about the fines the county could leverage if they continued using the current cabin. 

Mr. Drewer confirmed that the counties maximum fine was $1,000.00. 

The board decided to give the Zagars until the end of the year to purchase the adjoining property. This is predicated on the Zagars discontinuing use of their cabin immediately. If the refuse to discontinue usage of the cabin in violation, county staff is instructed to move forward with prosecution by submitting the citation to district court. 

**The Board of Zoning Appeals voted to adjourn the meeting.** 

**The meeting adjourned at 8:31 pm** 

Respectfully Submitted, 

Brian Laird, Chairman/AW
