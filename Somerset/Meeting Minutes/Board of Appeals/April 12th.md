SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES April 12, 2019 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 12" day of April 2019 at 7:30PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

## Board Members Present: 

Chairman Brian Laird Isaac Ballard Andrew Baines Donald Price William Cottman 

Paul Emely 

Others Present: Gary R. Pusey, Director DTCS

SubjectFood Systemsof Appeal:Inc. Variance 2019-1044 George E. Young III, on behalfof Lankford Sysco 

George E Young III, on behalf of Lankford Sysco Food Systems Inc, located at 33239 Costen Rd. has applied for a setback variance of 25.45” from the required 65’ measured from the centerline of Peach Orchard Rd. for the construction of a new vehicle maintenance building. The parcel can be located on Tax Map #51, Block #3, Parcel #163 in Dublin Election District and is zoned [2 industrial General Commercial. Section 5.6(a) of the County Zoning Ordinance requires a sixty five (65) foot setback measured from the road centerline for structures on rights of ways of less than fifty (50) feet in width. Peach Orchard Road is a thirty (30) foot right of way. The proposed building will be 39.55’ from the centerline of Peach Orchard Rd. 

## Testimony: 

The case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. George E. Young III of Pocomoke City MD was sworn in. Mr. Young advised the Board that he was the Surveyor/Engineer for this proposed new vehicle maintenance building. Mr. Young stated the reason for needing the setback variance of 25.45” was due to two (2) drainpipes.

Subject of Appeal: Variance 2019-1045 Williams and Carol Leach 

William and Carol Leach have applied for a side yard setback variance for a proposed storage shed on their property located at 23666 Thomas Price Rd. in Rock Creek Subdivision, Chance, MD (Tax Map 20, Grid 1, Parcel 300, Lot 22). The property is zoned Residential One (R-1) and is located within the CBCA-LDA. The proposed shed will be located outside of the Critical Area 100’ Buffer. Section 5.6b.1 requires an 8’ side yard setback and a 5’ setback is proposed, requiring a 3’ side yard setback variance. 

## Testimony: 

The case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. William Leach and Mrs. Carol Leach both of Good Rd Airville PA were sworn in. Mr. Leach advised the Board he would like to place a 12x28 (336 sqft) pre-constructed storage shed in their side yard. Mr. Leach advises the Board that when the shed is placed it will be placed on concrete pillars to allow for run off. Mr. Leach explained that the storage shed would only be used for his lawn mowers and yard equipment. 

There were no persons in attendance in opposition to the request. 

## Findings: Variance 2019-1045 

The Board voted unanimously to approve a side yard setback variance of 3’.

Subject of Appeal: Special Exception 2019-2499 Dana and David Zucker 

Dana and David Zucker are the Owners of the Historical Clifton Estate on the Manokin River at 28154 Revels Neck Rd., Westover, MD (Tax Map 31, Grid 3, Parcels 0099, 0106, and 0116). The Owners are Applicants seeking a Special Exception to operate a Bed and Breakfast (B&B) on the above property. The property is zoned Agricultural Residential (AR) and is within the 100’ Buffer of the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area (CBCA), Resource Conservation Area (RCA). The Applicants intend to operate a B&B utilizing three existing dwellings on the property, namely the Main House, the former Manager’s House and the former Guest House. Section 5.2.4.9 requires a Special Exception from the BZA for a B&B in the AR District. 

## Testimony: 

Mr. David Zucker and Mrs. Dana Zucker of Revels Neck Rd Princess Anne MD were sworn in. Mrs. Dana Zucker advised the board that they would like to have a Bed and Breakfast with two (2) guesthouses and three (3) suites in the main house. Mrs. Zucker explained their plan was to bring people to the area, enjoy community events, and bring revenue to the county. Mrs. Zucker stated that there hopes were to have people come and book duck hunts. 

There were no persons in attendance in opposition to the request.

Subject of Appeal: Variance 2019-1043 Dana and David Zucker 

Applicants, Dana and David Zucker incorporate by reference all the relevant facts stated above in SE 2019-2499. Section 5.8b.1 of the Somerset County Zoning Code establishes a B&B limit of 5 Guest Rooms. Applicants would like to establish and operate three (3) suites and two (2) separate Bedrooms in the above-described three (3) dwellings on Parcels 99 and 106 for a total of ten (10) Guest Bedrooms. Accordingly, Applicants are requesting a Variance of five (5) Bedrooms raising the permitted number to ten (10) Guest Bedrooms. 

## Testimony: 

Mr. David Zucker and Mrs. Dana Zucker of Revels Neck Rd Princess Anne MD were sworn in. Mrs. Zucker advised the board they would like to establish and operate three (3) suites and two (2) separate bedrooms in the above-described three (3) dwellings on Parcel 99 and 106 for a total of ten (10) guest bedrooms. 

There were no persons in attendance in opposition to the request. 

Findings: Variance 2019-1043
