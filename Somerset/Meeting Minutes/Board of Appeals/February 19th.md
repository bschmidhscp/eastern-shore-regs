SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES 

February 19, 2021 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 19th day of February 2021 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland.

Brian Laird - Chairman Donald Price Andrew Baines William Cottman Paul Emely Isaac Ballard

On a motion made by Chairman Laird and seconded by Mr. Ballard, the Board of Zoning Appeals voted to approve the minutes of the January 15", 2021 Board of Zoning Appeals Minutes.

The Case advertisement was read into the record by Chairman Laird. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. Adam Gibson was sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mr. Gibson to present the staffs findings, procedure and determination to the Board for the NOV issued on July 31, 2020. Mr. Gibson explained the staff's procedure when addressing violations of the Critical Area. Mr. Gibson noted that the property was currently in violation of their Stop Work Order and would like to amend the NOV to include this in the mitigation requirements. Staff presented their case through four main points: 

1. The Initial Complaint 

2. Site Inspection and Contact 

3. Stop Work Order, Survey and Site Evaluation 

4. Notice of Violation and the Applicant’s Appeal 

After Mr. Gibson explained these points, he yielded the remainder of the staff's time to Kathryn Durant, of the Critical Area Commission (CAC). Ms. Durant gave a brief summary of the CAC’s letter to the Board of Zoning Appeals, in support of Somerset County DTCS staff’s actions. The letter was submitted into the record. Ms. Durant stated that staff accurately determined the complaint to be a violation and correctly determined the mitigation requirements. Ms. Durant, on behalf of the CAC staff, requested that the board uphold the staff's decision to pursue the Critical Area Violation in question and deny the request for appeal by the applicant. 

Mr. Derek Hills was sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mr. Hills, as the legal counsel to the applicant, to present the applicant’s case for appeal. Mr. Hills submitted a legal brief into the record. Mr. Hills began his presentation by explaining that, although permits were not obtained, the mitigation orders received did not match the environmental disturbance committed by Edward Rote. He stated that Mr. Rote was trying to improve the property by removing underbrush. He stated that Mr. Rote did remove four trees that he bumped into while removing the underbrush, and Mr. Rote accepts responsibility for removing these four trees, but he noted that this removal did not occur in the Buffer. He also claimed that a significant portion of the removal was conducted before Malcolm Rote bought the property in 2017. Citing administrative errors and the evidence presented via the brief and witnesses in attendance, Mr. Hills asked that this case be overturned and subsequently dropped by Somerset County DTCS. 

Chairman Laird clarified with Mr. Hills that he agreed that clearing was done on the property without a permit and Mr. Hills confirmed that was true, and that once his clients learned there was a violation all work stopped. 

Chairman Laird asked if anyone in attendance wanted to speak on behalf of the Applicants. The following members of the public spoke in support of the appeal request: 

1. John Jordan - 11884 Edge Hill Terrace, Princess Anne, MD 21853 — Submitted three pictures into the record of the site that in his opinion was cleaning the property and was not land clearing or land disturbance.

Mr. Laird stated that all testimony has now been taken and it was time for the Board to discuss the request and make a decision. Mr. Laird stated in his opinion that although the Applicant was removing vegetation that he thought would be an improvement to the property, it didn’t appear that proper procedures were followed as a permit wasn’t obtained. Discussion ensued among the members concerning possible fines and how the penalties would be enforced, but the Board determined that was not the subject of this hearing, and the task for the Board to consider was whether a violation occurred and if Staff acted appropriately to issue a Notice Of Violation for unauthorized clearing in the Critical Area, outside of the Buffer. 

The Board voted to DENY the request of Appeal, and affirmed that the Staff acted appropriately to issue a Notice of Violation for unauthorized clearing of vegetation in the Critical Area. There was one dissenting vote; the Board’s vote is as follows: 

Board Vote: Brian Laird-Yes Bill Cottman — Yes Donald Price — Yes Andrew Baines — No Isaac Ballard- Yes Paul Emely- Yes
