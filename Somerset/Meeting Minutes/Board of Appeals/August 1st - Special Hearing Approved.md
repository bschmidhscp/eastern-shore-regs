Chairman Laird called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 



**<u>Subject of Hearing:</u>** Findings of fact for Administrative Appeal 2025-197 Island Sunset LLC 11190 Roland Parks Rd 

**AA 25-197:** The applicant was granted an appeal of the denial decision of Administrative Critical Area Buffer Case CAVAR 25-016 Per section 1-115(H) of the Somerset County Critical Area Ordinance, appeals to Critical Area Variance. The appeal request was to allow expanded intrusion into the 100’ Critical Area Buffer for the construction of a “dwelling,” associated decking, and a shed. 

**Testimony:** Mr. Illuminati went over the history of the case, and presented his written finding of facts for the board’s approval. The findings basically said the following: 

The board agreed with the site plan created by Chris Custis showing the delineation of the 100foot critical are buffer, the Site location and the previous location.

It also listed the conditions that must be met for any Critical Area Variances, and the board’s reasoning for why they felt said conditions had been met. 

Finally, it listed the eight conditions that the board agreed they must meet. 

1. Reforestation on site to offset disturbed forest on equal area basis 

2. Afforestation to mitigate for any created impervious surface in the buffer at 3:1 ratio 

3. Any mitigation measures recommended by state or county agencies 

4. Develop a final site plan. Meeting standards discussed at previous meeting 

5. A building permit and zoning certificate are required 

6. Health Department approval is required 

7. Comply with all local, state and federal laws while developing property. 

8. All conditions will be completed in two years of variance decision 

Mr. Illuminati then when on to describe how the legal proceedings would go if anyone were to appeal the decision, and all the time limits about when to do so. 

Mr. Alrey made a motion to accept the findings of fact. The motion was seconded by Mr. Baines. The motion passed unanimously. 



## **<u>Findings:</u>** 

**AA 25-197** , The Board voted unanimously to approve the findings of fact for the administrative appeal 

Andrew Baines- Yes AC Alrey – Yes William Cottman - Yes Isaac Ballard – Yes Paul Emely - Chairman Laird – Yes
