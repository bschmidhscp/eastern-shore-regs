# Oct 20th Approved-Signed

<!-- page 1 -->
**SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES** October 20, 2023 



The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 20<sup>th</sup> day of October 2023 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

# **<u>Board Members Present:</u>** 

Brian Laird - Chairman Isaac Ballard Paul Emely William Cottman 

# **<u>Others Present:</u>** 

William Cornish, Zoning Specialist Mary Phillips, Assistant Director/Zoning Administrator Jesse Drewer, Director 

Andrew Illuminati Esq, Board Attorney 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** Special Exception 23-2537 Thomas Hornsby/dba. Hornsby Enterprises, 31350 Curtis Chapel Rd. 

**SE 23-2535:** The applicant proposes to Accessory retail store on a 137.76 Ac Parcel in AR Zoning District. 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Mr. Thomas Hornsby, representative for Hornsby Enterprises 31350 Curtis Chapel Rd came forward and was sworn. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain his request. 

Mr. Hornsby explained the nature of his business. Mr. Hornsby testified that due to the conditions of the MALPF Ag Easement in place that he did not feel a Special Exception was necessary as he felt the Ag Easement took away all residential development rights and what he is doing is all agriculture related. Mr. Hornsby provided a fact sheet from the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation and his price sheet for his products for board members to review. 

Mr. Hornsby stated that he is in the process of transferring the FDA / Health Department approvals he has for his business on Post Office Road to the new farm on Curtis Chapel Road but this zoning issue is delaying the paperwork.

<!-- page 2 -->
Ms. Mary Phillips of DTCS explained that the establishment of the Agriculture Preservation Easement did not remove the underlying zoning and zoning requirements. Mr. Andrew Illuminati, Board Attorney concurred with DTCS assessment of the requirement for the Special Exception. 

Ms. Phillips asked to enter the staff report and the hand out presented by Mr. Hornsby into the record. Handout accepted as exhibit #1, Staff report as exhibit #2. 

Mr. Hornsby asked staff what is the difference of the existing poultry houses selling chickens and him selling other products. Ms. Phillips replied that the chickens are trucked to the processing plant and packaged and sold offsite. This request is for individual persons to come to the property to purchase a product. One is wholesale, one is retail. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in opposition or in favor in attendance. Being none 

Chairman Laird asked for any further questions from Board members. 

Being none – Chairman Laird thanked everyone for coming. 

# **Findings:** 

SE 23-2537, the Board voted unanimously to approve the request, 

Vote: Paul Emely –   yes William Cottman – yes Brian Laird -    yes Isaac Ballard - yes 

Mr. Illuminati clarified that the vote was in favor of the special exception subject to the conditions in the staff report, to which the board replied yes. 

# **The Board of Zoning Appeals voted to adjourn the meeting.** 

The meeting adjourned at 8:08 pm 

Respectfully Submitted, 

**Brian Laird/AW** 

Brian Laird, Chairman/AW
