August 21, 2026 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 21<sup>st</sup> day of August 2026 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

#### **<u>Board of Appeals Members Present:</u>** 

Brian Laird-Chairman Paul Emely William Cottman Andrew Baines Michael Junge 

#### **<u>Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** 

Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Administrator Donna Cannon – Administrative assistant 

**<u>Board of Appeals Legal Counsel Present</u>** Christopher M Smith, Esq. 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M.

Chairman Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the July 17<sup>th</sup> Meeting. Their being none, Mr. Cottman made a motion to approve the minutes. The motion was seconded by Mr. Baines. 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** SE 2026-2556 Tull Farms Holdings LLC Polks Road

Legal representative, Mr. Smith gave a short summary of the findings of fact prepared by Mr. Illuminati. 

After that the board took a vote on whether to accept the findings of fact.
