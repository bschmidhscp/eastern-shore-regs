**SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES** August 18, 2023 



The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 18<sup>st</sup> day of August 2023 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

# **<u>Board Members Present:</u>** 

Brian Laird - Chairman Isaac Ballard Paul Emely William Cottman 

# **<u>Others Present:</u>** 

William Cornish, Zoning Specialist Mary Phillips, Assistant Director/Zoning Administrator 

Andrew Illuminati Esq, Board Attorney 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Chairman Laird made the announcement of the news of the passing of board member Donald Price earlier in the morning. Chairman Laird turned the floor over to Rev. Isaac Ballard, Board Member to provide a prayer for Mr. Price. 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** Special Exception 23-2535 Sheedy / Clayton Homes, Fairmount Rd. 

**SE 23-2535:** The applicant proposes to place a Single Wide Mobile Home on a parcel as a principle residence in a MRC Zoning District. 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Mr. Charles Shade, representative for Clayton Homes 38409 Sussex Highway Seaford De came forward and was sworn. Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain their request. 

Mr. Shade explained he represents Clayton Homes and the buyers. Mr. Sheedy is the current owner of the property and Clayton homes will be purchasing the property on behalf of the buyers. Mr. Shade testified that the mobile home will be compliant with all required setbacks. Mr. Shade further testified that the property was formerly improved by a single wide mobile home and that there is no additional impact or congestion to the neighborhood and would not create and drainage issues to neighboring properties. 

Chairman Laird inquired if this property is served by Public water and Sewer? Mr. Shade responded in the affirmative and has been in contact with Earl Ludy of the Sanitary District regarding the connections. Mr. Shade explained details shown on the site plan in regards to the sanitary connections.

<!-- page 2 -->
Ms. Phillips asked to enter the Site Plan into the record. 

Mr. Shade continued to explain the site plan and noting the building site is not within the Critical Area or the flood zone. 

Chairman Laird asked if there were any additions question from the board members. There were none. 

Ms. Phillips asked to enter the Staff Report into the record and noted the suggested four conditions of approval shown on page 3. 

# **Findings:** 

SE 23-2535, the Board voted unanimously to approve the request, with staff recommendations / conditions. 

Vote: Paul Emely –   yes William Cottman – yes Brian Laird -    yes Isaac Ballard - yes 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** Special Exception 23-2536, Amanda Roth 

**SE 23-2536** : The applicant wishes to establish a commercial kitchen to serve off sight sales on property located at 11288 Hodson White Rd in an MRC (Maritime Residential Commercial) Zoning District. A Special exception is required per Section 5.7(d)7, Commercial Uses of Zoning Ordinance # 1144. 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. 

Ms. Amanda Roth 11288 Hodson White Rd Dames Quarter Md was welcomed to the front and was sworn. Ms. Roth also had two persons accompanying her; Ms. Wendy Penn, 30630 UMES Blvd Princess Anne Md and Mr. Shaun R Roth 11288 Hodson White Rd. Dames Quarter Md. came forward and were sworn. Ms. Roth explained her request before the Board. Ms. Roth told the Board of her Food Truck business and what products she sells through her company “Sisters with a Twist” which she runs with her sister. Ms. Roth stated her business is baked goods and sweet treats. Ms. Roth stated that her type business requires a commercial kitchen to meet State of Maryland regulations. 

Mr. Shaun Roth explained a series of pictures of the proposed kitchen layout and building to be renovated, and also pictures of the truck being renovated. Ms. Roth provided copies of the pictures for the record. Chairman Laird entered the pictures as exhibit #1and #2 

Ms. Roth explained the layout of the commercial kitchen she plans to install in a renovated outbuilding. Ms. Roth stated she has been in contact with the Health Department regarding testing for a required septic system for the kitchen. Ms. Roth hopes to have 2 ovens and no food will be fried. 

Chairman Laird asked if any Board members had any questions.

<!-- page 3 -->
Board member William Cottman asked if Ms. Roth would be traveling around to different job sites. 

Ms. Roth replied yes and through her research of State of Maryland regulations she can travel up to a 90-mile radius of her commercial kitchen. 

Mr. Cottman asked if the food is cooked onsite then taken on the truck to other locations? Ms. Roth answered yes. 

Mr. Roth told Board members how some states allow all food to be prepared in the truck but the State of Maryland requires a base of operations. 

Ms. Roth testified she has looked for an available commercial kitchen to use and has even reached out to the Universities with no luck. 

Board Member Paul Emely asked if supplies would be delivered or would she be bringing them herself. Ms. Roth stated she would be buying and transporting her own supplies so there is no additional truck traffic. 

Board Member Isaac Ballard asked if customers could place orders. Ms. Roth testified she could take orders, but she would be delivering them. 

Ms. Phillips asked to enter the Staff Report into the record as Exhibit #3 

Ms. Phillips also entered a copy of the Mobile Food regulation requirements per the State of Maryland as exhibit #4 

Ms. Phillips referred to the Staff Report and the eight principles that should be considered when granting a Special Exception and the 4 Conditions of Approval recommended by staff. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in opposition or in favor in attendance. Chairman Laird asked for any further questions from Board members. 

Being none – Chairman Laird thanked everyone for coming. 

# **Findings:** 

SE 23-2536, the Board voted unanimously to approve Ms. Roth’s request, with staff recommendations / and 4 conditions. 

Vote: Paul Emely –   yes William Cottman – yes Brian Laird -    yes Isaac Ballard - yes
