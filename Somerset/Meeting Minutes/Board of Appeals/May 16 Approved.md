## **<u>Board of Appeals Members Present:</u>** 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Chairman Laird called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Chairman Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the April 25, 2025 meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. The motion was made by Mr. Alrey and seconded by Mr. Baines 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** SE 2025-2551 Matthews Sara – 28590 Revells Neck RD 

**SE 25-2551:** The applicant is seeking approval to conduct a Cannabis growing facility in I2 zoning 

**Testimony:** Mr. Laird read the ad into the record. Sara Matthews of 2401 Queen Mitchel Rd Gambrills Maryland came forward and was sworn in. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain her request.

Ms. Matthews represents the trilogy group who intend to purchase a lot in the county industrial park. She testified that she intends to open an indoor cannabis growing operation. She intends for the facility to bring sustainable agricultural jobs to Somerset County. If the facility proves sustainable it will provide up to 75 jobs when the complex is fully operational. 

There will be no unauthorized access to the site. There will be fencing and cameras to secure the facility, and they project will use water retention/recycling policies so there will be little impact on local utilities. The project is fully licensed by the Maryland Cannabis Commission. 

Mr. Cottman wanted clarity as to whether they would be proessing the cannabis in a similar manner as a dispensary would. 

Mrs. Burton answered that the facility will only be used for growing the cannabis. 

Mr. Alrey inquired as to whether there would be any tours of the facility to help boost the county’s agro-tourism sector. 

The applicant responded that there was some possibility of that in the future, but due to the security required at the site access will be restricted. 

Mr. Cottman inquired about the size of the facility 

Ms. Matthews answered that the company has a license to grow up to 300,000 square feet, but they intend to start at a smaller scale in order to perfect their process before potentially scaling in the future. 

Mr. Cottman then asked if there will be an open-air green house. 

The applicant explained that there will be a steel building around the green houses, obscuring any view of the cannabis. 

Mr. Ballard questioned whether there would be a secure fence around the facility. 

The applicant answered in the affirmative. 

Mr. Emely then asked where the closest similar project was located, and if there were any on the Eastern Shore. 

The applicant answered SunMed, a facility located in Cecil county. That follows a similar business model. They are the largest growers in Maryland. 

Mr. Illuminati then gave a short overview of the history of state legislation that enabled the legalization of cannabis, and permitted the applicant to make their request. That was made exhibit one.

Mr. Emely inquired as to whether there were any special regulations that must be followed due to the facility being in close proximity to a state prison. 

Mr. Laird than asked if there were any other questions 

Mr. Cornish read the staff report into the record, making a note to correct a typo about the size of the lot from 2.2 acres to 9 acres. The staff report was made exhibit two. 

Mr. Laird then asked if there was anyone who came to speak in favor of the request 

Mr. Laird asked if anybody wanted to speak in opposition of the request 

Neal Widdowson came forward. 

He questioned who would pay for the infrastructure at the site. 

There was brief discussion before Mr. Alrey pointed out that the BZA was simply voting on the approval of the use of the site. Any details about the site plan would be worked out by the planning commission. 

Mr. Laird asked if the board had any questions 

There were none. 

Mr. Alrey made a motion for the approval of the use of the property as a Cannabis growing facility 

Mr. Ballard Seconded 

The board voted unanimously to approve the request
