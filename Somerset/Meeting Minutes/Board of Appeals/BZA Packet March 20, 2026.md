Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Mr. Laird called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Chairman Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the November 21<sup>st</sup> meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. The motion was made by Mr. Ballard and seconded by Mr. Cottman 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** VA 2026-1063 Oakwood Homes 8888 Sawmill RD 

**SE 25-1063:** The applicant is seeking Expansion of a non-conforming use 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Jason Weisenburg of Oakwood Homes located at 10797 Iron hill Rd Delmar DE came forward and was sworn in. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain his request. 

Mr. Weisenburg Explained that there was an existing single wide on the lot that had been already been placed through a special exception many years ago. The land was zoned commercial which did not allow the placement of single-family homes. But the family had been living in the area since before the establishment of the county’s zoning code.

The applicants intended to expand the use from a single wide in to a double wide, as the previous single wide had been on the property for 15 years, and was reaching its end of life. 

Chairman Laird asked if the board had any questions. 

Mr. Emely inquired about whether the trailer would be placed on the lot in a vertical or horizontal orientation. 

Mr. Weissenberg confirmed it would be oriented the same way as the existing trailer. 

Chairman Laird then asked if there were anyone in the audience in opposition.

Chairman Laird then asked if there was anyone in support of the request.

Mr. Cornish read the staff report into record, including the conditions of approval. This was submitted as exhibit one. 

Mr. Illuminati clarified to the applicant that they should not remove their existing home until they were absolutely ready to replace it, as the board did not have the authority to increase the 90-day deadline. 

The board voted to unanimously approve the request congruent on the following conditions of approval

- 1) A Building Permit and Zoning Certificate are required prior to any construction. 

- 2) Sanitary District approval is required 

- 3) All state, and local approvals must be received. 

- 4) Building Permits must be obtained within 1 year of approval. 

- 5) The new mobile home must be placed within 90 days of the removal of the previous home.

###### **I.** **<u>SUMMARY OF REQUEST:</u>** 

The applicant requests permission to create a nonconforming lot (Parcel 98, Lot 4) from a subdivision of existing agricultural Parcel 98.  The subdivision plat proposes a road frontage width for Lot 4 of only 50.1’, rather than the code-required 100’ width.  Due to the specific peculiarities of the parent tract, the proposed Lot 4 cannot accommodate the 100’ width without encroaching into the established operations already present on the parent tract (remainder of Parcel 98, known now as lot 3, and the adjacent Parcel 12, lot 2). Following initial submittal of the subdivision plat, the road frontage width of lot 4 has been increased to the proposed 50.1’, which allows adequate access to the set-back lot, while also preserving the adjacent lots’ functions (setbacks from existing and future structures, proposed wells, and septic reserve areas). 

###### **II.** **<u>DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPERTY AND SURROUNDING AREA:</u>** 

The property is zoned AR (Agricultural Residential) and is located on the southern side Charles Boston Road. The parent tract is partially wooded with cleared area to the east of the existing home site.  The cleared area of Parcel 98, along with a portion of Parcel 12, is the location of proposed Lot 2, planned for an additional home.  Lot 4 is proposed to cut between the two dwelling lots via a stem of 50.1’ width, until the lot opens up into the actual acreage behind Lot 2.

<!-- page 7 -->
The property is located within an unincorporated area of Somerset County.  The property is bordered on the north, east, and west by a mixture wooded, farmed, and residential land. 

###### **III.** **<u>CODE CRITERIA:</u>** 

One section of the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance (#1212) is relevant to this request. This section is addressed below. 

###### **Section 11.3 – Special Exceptions:** 

A variance shall only be granted if the applicant proves to the satisfaction of the Board of Zoning Appeals, based upon the written application and testimony that all of the following standards will be met, as applicable: 

- (1) That owing to conditions peculiar to the property, a literal enforcement of the ordinance would result in unnecessary and undue hardship and/or in practical difficulty; Lot 4 is being created for agricultural purposes only.  Adjoining Lots 3 and 2 restrict the road frontage available to satisfy the 100’ width requirement for Lot 4.  Access could not be created without negatively impacting the adjacent lots. 

- (2) That special conditions and circumstances exist which are peculiar to the land or structure involved and which are not commonly applicable to other lands or structures in the same district; 

The depth of the parent tract is unusually large, with little available road frontage, making subdivision of adequate width a challenge, especially while attempting to accommodate existing and proposed development setbacks. 

- (3) That literal interpretation of the provisions of this Ordinance would deprive the applicant of rights commonly enjoyed by other properties in the same district under the terms of this Ordinance; 

There is no alternative to allow the desired use of all properties without a variance for the lot width of Lot 4.  Without such a variance, access to the agricultural Lot 4 would require cutting further into the adjacent lots, limiting their use. 

- (4) That the special conditions and circumstances do not result from the actions of the applicant; 

   - The circumstances of this lot width issue are not the result of the poor decisions of the property owner. 

- (5) That greater profitability or lack of knowledge of the restrictions shall not be considered as sufficient cause for a variance 

Access of the subdivided Lot 4, and allowance of appropriate utilization of adjacent lots is the impetus for this variance request. 

- (6) That the presence of a nonconforming use on neighboring lands and/or of a permitted use in another district shall not by itself be considered grounds for the issuance of a variance; No nonconforming use on a neighboring property is encouraging the seeking of this variance. 

- (7) That the granting of the variance will be in harmony with the purposes of this Ordinance, and will not be injurious to the neighborhood, or otherwise detrimental to the public welfare;
