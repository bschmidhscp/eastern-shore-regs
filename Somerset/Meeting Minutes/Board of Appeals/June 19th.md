SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES June 19, 2020 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 19" day of June 2020 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

## Board Members Present: 

Brian Laird - Chairman Donald Price Andrew Baines William Cottman Paul Emely Isaac Ballard 

Others Present: Mary Phillips, Zoning Specialist Gary Pusey, Director 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Subject of Appeal: VAR 2020-1051 James Nelson 

2020-1051: James Nelson, 28425 Revells Neck Rd Westover, MD 21871 have applied for a Variance on property located at 29303 Revells Neck Rd, Westover, MD (Tax Map 32 Grid 01 Parcel 111) Owners wish to expand Agricultural structures on a residential lot improved with a single family dwelling being the primary use. Chapter 17 of the Zoning Ordinance defines Farm as being 5 acres or more. Request is for a 2.5-acre variance. 

Testimony: 

The Case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. James Nelson was sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mr. James Nelson to express to the Board his intention. Mr. Nelson is requesting to add another grain bin to the property for the storage of his grains. His explained that currently there is one bin on the property, of which he wishes to add another bin of the same style to the property. 

Chairman Laird asked the Board if they had any questions or concerns for the applicants. Mr. Price asked if any other neighbors have complained or had any concerns. Mr. Nelson explained that he had spoken with the neighbors and he had not heard of any complaints from anyone. Mr. Nelson also explained that the fans used on the grain bins are quite and do not put out much noise at all.

Subject of Appeal: SE 2020-2511 Calvert Crossland, LLC 

2020-2511: Pursuant to section 5.7 (d) 3 of Somerset County Zoning Code, Calvert Crossland, LLC has applied for a Special Exception to construct a 195’ Monopole Cellular Tower on land owned by Covington Conclusion, LLC. The site is 200’ northwest of an existing dirt farm lane near to Big Monie Rd and intersecting Mt. Vernon Rd (Tax Map 08, Grid 16, and Parcel 23). The parcel is zoned Agricultural Residential (AR), and is not within the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area or 100 year flood Plain. 

## Testimony: 

The Case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mr. Steve Smythurst and Ms. Barbara Pivec were sworn in. Chairman Laird asked them to express to the board their request. Ms. Pivec is a partner with Calvert Crossland, LLC and they currently have a contract with Verizon Wireless. Verizon has identified the need for a cell tower in the area of Mt. Vernon back in 2016. Ms. Pivec explained that in the binders she has provided hasa lot of information regarding the research that was completed along with all the site plans.

Ms. Pivec went on to explain how they picked a location for the cell tower. A series of test/studies were completed one which was what has called a Balloon Test. Pictures are provided in the binder which soa series a pictures, with a red balloon placed at the exact height of the cell tower. Ms. Pivec explained that the photos were taken from eleven different positions along Mt. Vernon Road and Covington Lane. This was to be able to show the visibility of the cell tower once it was up. 

Ms. Pivec explained while looking at a property they went thru all the zoning standards and comprehensive plan to make sure they met all the guidelines. The current location has an existing driveway, which has given them the opportunity to bring it off the main road way and behind the existing wood line. Ms. Pivec explain that the site has been through the FAA/FCC to determine if there would be an impact to local and regional airports. There are no airports and or flight paths within five miles of the site. Ms. Pivec also explained that if the tower were to ever become inoperable for more than six months then they would take the tower down. This is an agreement in the land lease that they have with the property owners. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in attendance that in support of the project. Mr. Howard Anderson of Big Monie Rd came forward in support of the project and was sworn in. 

Mr. Anderson explained that he was in support of this project. There is a huge need for the tower in the community. Mr. Anderson explained that the only visible part of the tower is what will be above the existing tree line. Mr. Anderson also stated that this tower would not have any flashing lights. Ms. Pivec spoke up to explain that there would be an emergency light on the tower but only on a motion sensor base. Ms. Pivec also explained that there would be a six-foot fence with a onefoot barbed wire across the top. The tower will have landscaping around then fencing. 

Mr. Emely asked about the lighting on the tower and his thoughts were that anything over one hundred feet required lighting. Ms. Pivec explained the based on the current regulations anything over two hundred feet is what required the lighting. This tower would only be (195’) one hundred ninety-five feet in height. 

Chairman Laird asked if there was anyone in attendance in opposition of the project. Mr. David Garner and Mrs. Kimberly Garner of 12512 Mallard Lane came forward and were sworn in. 

Mrs. Garner explained that she had concerns about this project and based on her just finding out about the project she would like to see if postponed so she can look more into the risk of having this tower so close. Mrs. Garner explained that based on the little research she was able to do before the meeting there are a lot of health risk having this tower so close to a development. Mrs. Garner also explained that there is a lot of wildlife in that area and some, which are endangered animals. Chairman Laird explained to Mrs. Garner that the property was advertised in both the local papers and the property was posted in the timeframe that is required. Therefore, with that being done he saw no reason to postpone the meeting. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to please come back up and address the oppositions concerns. Mrs. Steve Smythurst came forward and explained that he had been doing cell towers for over
