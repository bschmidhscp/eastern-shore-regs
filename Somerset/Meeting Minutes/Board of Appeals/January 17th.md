SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES January 17, 2020 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 17" day of January 2020 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

Board Members Present: 

Brian Laird - Chairman Donald Price Andrew Baines William Cottman Paul Emely Isaac Ballard 

Others Present: 

Robert Spery, Zoning Administrator 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Subject of Appeal: Variance 2019-1049 Joseph and Amber Fuerneisen 

VAR 2019-1049; Joseph and Amber Fuerneisen of 30046 Bryan Hall Rd., Marion, MD 21838, have applied for a Setback Variance on property located at 30046 Bryan Hall Rd. Marion, MD (Tax Map 58 Grid 16 Parcel 283) Request a 166’ rear yard setback variance per Section 5.7(d) 21 schedule of allowed uses -Kennel and Section 11.3 Variances per Ordinance # 1144 entitled Somerset County Zoning Ordinance for the establishment of a private kennel within an existing building 

# Testimony: 

The Case advertisement was read into the record. The Board determined the exact location of the parcel. Mrs. Amber Fuerneisen was sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mrs. Fuerneisen to express to the Board her intention. Mrs. Fuerneisen stated she is seeking 166’ rear yard setback variance for a private dog kennel on Bryan Hall Rd. There is an old chicken house on the property that is 34? from the rear property line. For this, Mrs. Fuerneisen stated she is seeking the 166’ setback variance. Mrs. Fuerneisen is going to use the old chicken house for the dog’s kennels. There are no neighbors in the rear. Mrs. Fuerneisen raises Siberian Huskies and she has provided photos for the board.
