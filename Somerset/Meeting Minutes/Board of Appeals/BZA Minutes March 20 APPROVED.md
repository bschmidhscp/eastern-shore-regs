# **<u>Minutes</u>** March 20, 2026 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 20<sup>th</sup> day of March 2026 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Board of Appeals Members Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Brian Laird Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Administrator Andrew Baines Catherine Skeeter Senior Planner William Cottman Paul Emely 

**<u>Board of Appeals Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Mr. Laird called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Chairman Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the November 21<sup>st</sup> meeting, there being none Mr. Laird asked for a vote to approve the meeting minutes. The motion was made by Mr. Cottman and seconded by Mr. Baines. 

**<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** VA 2026-1065 Carol Wink Charles Boston RD 

**VA 26-1065:** The applicant is seeking the creation of a non-conforming lot. 

**Testimony:** Chairman Laird read the ad into the record. Carol Wink of 10750 Charles Boston Rd Princess Anne Md came forward and was sworn in. 

Chairman Laird asked the applicant to explain her request. 

Mrs. Wink Explained that they are in possession of a n existing 40-acre farm that they intend to subdivide out parcels of for their grandchildren to put new homes on. The remainder of the parcel is intended to never be developed. The remainder of the lot will be a flag lot with a 50-foot access road. The code necessitates 100 feet of road frontage, but they would prefer it to be 50. 

**_11916 Somerset Avenue, Suite 211 • Princess Anne, Maryland 21853_** Telephone (410) 651-1424 • Fax (410) 651-2597 Email: dtcs@somersetmd.us

<!-- page 2 -->
Mrs. Wink continued that the existing road is only 20 feet and she would prefer that would be all that is necessary. 

Chairman Laird then asked if there was ever intended to be more than light traffic on the driveway. To which the applicant responded that there should never be any increased traffic on the driveway. 

Mr. Cornish then read the staff report into the record. Said staff report was made exhibit one. 

Legal then interjected that the advertisement only mentioned a 50-foot right of way, so that was all the board had the ability to give. 

Discussion then ensued between the applicant and her family about whether the timing would work out with coming to another meeting to ask for the 20-foot or stick with the 50-foot ask. In the end they decided to stick with the 50-foot ask. 

Chairman Laird then asked if there were anyone in the audience in opposition. 

There was none. 

Chairman Laird then asked if there was anyone in support of the request. 

There was none 

The board voted to unanimously approve the request congruent on the following conditions of approval 

Mr. Laird thanked the applicants for their time. 



### **<u>The conditions are as follows:</u>** 

- 1) A subdivision plat must be finalized with all necessary modifications, and signatures of involved parties, and then recorded in the land records within one year of the variance approval 

- 2) The subdivision plat cannot be finalized for 30 days following the decision of the board of appeals in the event there is an appeal of the decision made. 

### **<u>Findings:</u>** 

**SE 25-1065** , The Board voted unanimously to approve the request for a variance Subject to the conditions in the staff report.
