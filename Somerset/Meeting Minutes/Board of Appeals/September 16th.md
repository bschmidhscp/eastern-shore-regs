SOMERSET COUNTY Board of Zoning Appeals MEETING MINUTES September 16, 2022 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 21st day of January 2022 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

Board Members Present: Brian Laird - Chairman Andrew Baines Donald Price Paul Emely 

William Cottman - Absent Isaac Ballard- Absent 

Others Present: Mary Phillips, Zoning Administrator/ Assistant Director Will Cornish, Zoning Specialist 

Chairman Laird opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Mr. Laird asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the previous meeting, there being none Mr. Laird for a vote to approve the minutes from the January 21, 2022 Meeting. Minutes were approved by unanimous vote. 

Case #1 

Subject of Appeal: Variance 2022-1054 Burgstahler / Bell 

VAR 2022-1054; Barbara Burgstahler, represented by Clarence Bell of 5402 Bivens Rd, Marion MD 21838, have applied for a variance for the construction of a 4 ft wide by 106 ft long Deck across the front of a building located 28338 Crisfield Marion Rd. Marion MD (Tax Map 57 Grid 16 Parcel 322) Request is for a 38’ variance from the 40’ required in a C2 Commercial District. Structure is legal Non-conforming and is approximately 19’ from the centerline of Crisfield Marion Rd. 

### Testimony: 

Mr. Laird read the case advertisement into the record. Mr. Clarence Bell came forward and was sworn in. Chairman Laird asked Mr. Bell express to the Board his intention. Mr. Bell explained
