### **STAFF REPORT** 

#### **Meeting of April 25, 2025** 

**Case No.: SE 2025-2550 Applicant: Burton Nadine Malvina Property Owner: Burton Nadine Malvina Location: 28950 Deal Island Rd Tax Map: Map 22 / Grid 06 / Parcel 167 Zoning: Agricultural Residential District (AR) Floodzone: X Critical Area: N/A Property Size: 2.02 Acres Request: Special Exception Approval** 

#### **I.** **<u>SUMMARY OF REQUEST:</u>** 

Nadine Burton, is requesting Board of Zoning Appeals approval for the creation of a home occupation with more than 10 daily visitors, a farm school, located on Deal Island Rd. Section 5.8(D)10 requires a Special Exception to be approved by the Board subject to conditions. 

#### **II.** **<u>DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPERTY AND SURROUNDING AREA:</u>** 

The property is located within an unincorporated area of Somerset County and is zoned Agricultural Residential (AR) in the West Princess Anne Election District. Site is located on the northside of Deal Island road 4000 feet to the eastside of Venton RD. the Parcel is 2.02 Acres. The property is improved with an existing building. The Parcel is surrounded on three sides by unimproved forest and is directly west of three houses with an average age of 75 years. Sites is not within the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area or the floodplain. 

#### **III.** **<u>CODE CRITERIA:</u>** 

- Two sections of the Zoning Ordinance (#1205) are relevant to this request; **1) Section 5.8(d) – Home Occupations:** 

Home occupations that may receive more than 10 daily visitors require a special exception.

<!-- page 9 -->
#### **2) Section 11.2 – Special Exceptions:** 

When hearing evidence necessary for granting of any Special Exception, the Board shall consider all pertinent facts in the case, and render a decision in accordance with the following principles: 

- (1) The proposed use conforms in all respects to minimum requirements of the district in which it is located; 

- (2) The proposed use will not adversely affect the health, safety, and general welfare of residents of the area; 

- (3) The proposed use will not interfere with the adequate and orderly provision of public facilities necessary to service the area or the proposed Special Exceptions; 

- (4) The proposed use will not create congestion in the road or undue traffic hazards and that adequate ingress and egress is provided; 

- (5) The proposed use will not adversely affect the area and surrounding property due to adverse environmental characteristics including undue smoke, odor, noise, improper drainage inadequate access; 

- (6) The proposed use will not adversely affect the established character of the area; and 

- (7) The proposed use is consistent with the guidelines established for such uses in the County’s Critical Area Program, where applicable. 

- (8) The proposed use is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan or a local Area Plan adopted by the Board of County Commissioners. 

#### **IV.** **<u>ISSUES/PLANNING CONCERNS:</u>** 

None. 

#### **V.** **<u>CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:</u>** 

- **1)** A Building Permit and Zoning Certificate are required prior to any construction. 

- **2)** Owner must meet all applicable Local, State and Federal requirements. 

- **3)** Building Permits must be obtained within 3 years of approval. 

- **4)** No product will be permitted to bs sold on-site, unless it Is clearly accessory to the service being provided on site. 

- **5)** No materials connected with the home occupation may be stored outside of an enclosed building. 

- **6)** The use must not be open to the public between nine (9) p.m. and seven (7) a.m. 

- **7)** A maximum of two (2) trucks are permitted to operate from the lot for business purposes, the use is not permitted to routinely require deliveries or pickup of items by tractor-trailer trucks.

<!-- page 10 -->
#### **VI.** **<u>STAFF RECOMMENDATION:</u>** 

Staff respectfully requests that the Board of Zoning Appeals consider approval of the Special Exception with the stated conditions.
