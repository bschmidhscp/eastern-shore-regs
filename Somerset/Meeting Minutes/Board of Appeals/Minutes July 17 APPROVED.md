The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals meeting held on the 17<sup>th</sup> day of July 2026 at 7:30 PM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland.

Vice Chair Ballard opened the Meeting at 7:30 P.M. 

Chairman Ballard called the meeting to order and gave an explanation on how the meeting will proceed. 

Chairman Ballard asked if there were any changes to the minutes from the April 17<sup>th</sup> , meeting. **<u>Subject of Appeal:</u>** SE 2026-2557 Bonnie Barnes-Paige 3033 William Maddox Road 

**SE 26-2557:** The applicant is seeking the establishment of a flea market. 

**Testimony:** Mr. Drewer read the ad into the record. Ms. Bonnie Barnes Paige of 5311 Overhill RD Baltimore Maryland came forward and was sworn in. 

Chairman Ballard asked the applicant to explain their request. 

> **_11916 Somerset Avenue, Suite 211 • Princess Anne, Maryland 21853_** Telephone (410) 651-1424 • Fax (410) 651-2597 Email: dtcs@somersetmd.us

<!-- page 2 -->
- Applicant wishes to establish an open-air flea market on the property. she wishes for locals to have a space to sell local goods, and produce. She chose the location do to its proximity to the water and ample space for parking an greenery 

- Mr. Ballard asked what she intends to do with the dilapidated structure currently on the property. 

- The applicant explained they had already had surveyors and engineers look at the structure and were simply waiting for their final report on the property. Ms. Barnes-Paige agreed if the structure was to far gone she would demolish the structure. 

- Mr. Baines commented that he had visited the dwelling while it was inundated with flood waters, and he believed the structure needed to come down. 

- Mr. Laird said that he was under the impression that her market would be open-air, and that people would be packing their goods and tables up before leaving with them. 

- Mr. Cottman commented that she would be putting substantially more money into the project if she tried to save the home versus tearing it down. 

- Mr. Junge had questions about the narrowness of the road, and where people would be turning if they missed her driveway. 

- This led to a discussion about lots further down the streets, and whether they were open to the public for people to turn down them. at the end of the discussion it was determined that all property further down from the structure were private property. 

- Mr. Drewer read the staff report into the record. The staff report was made staff exhibit one. 

- Chairman Ballard asked if there was anyone in attendance who wanted to speak in opposition of the request. 

- Mr. Perry Brown of 3016 William Maddox road came forward. 

- Mr. Brown opposed the project because of Traffic concerns, the existing structure and flooding. 

- Not only does Mr. Brown live adjacent to the property, he also owns several properties in the area. he is worried that people will use his properties to turn around in. this is a fear of his as some people already do it. He also believes people will end up parking in the surrounding neighbors’ yards. 

- He also stated that the he had lived in the area since 1997 and the house had been abandoned when he first moved in. 

- He also was worried about the environmental impact as the area frequently flooded with debris flowing into nearby yards.

- After Mr. Brown completed his testimony Mr. Cornish read a personal letter from Andrew J. Kuntz and Michelle K Wallace of 3937 Chelseas Ct Crisfield Maryland into the record. The letter expressed opposition due to the flooding issue and the perceived disrespect of the applicant’s contractors. The letter was made opposition exhibit one. 

- Mr. Ballard then asked if there was anyone there in support of the project. To which no one stepped forward. 

- Ms. Barnes-Paige came forward again to respond to the opposition. She agreed with Mr. Brown about the debris problem on the site. But she says she has spent time, and effort cleaning the property. and claims she will have a dumpster on site in order to allow people to clean up after themselves. 

- Ms. Barnes-Paige also stated there was space on the accommodate increased parking. 

- Mr. Junge inquired about restrooms on the site. The applicant suggested porta potties, or possibly putting a new sceptic on the property. 

- Mr. Junge then inquired about the hours of operation of the market. The applicant said her intended hours were 7-2 on weekends. 

- Mr. Cottman noted that the market would face tough times during flooding. 

- Mr. Laird noted the applicant could keep an eye on the weather reports, and cancel the market whenever the weather looked bad. 

- Mr. Ballard then brought up the hours of operation on a Sunday, noting that a busy market may be a nuisance for church goers when the times overlapped. 

- After that a small discussion ensued about grading the property before Ms. Barnes finished her testimony.

- Mr. Laird abstained, due to missing the previous meeting, but all other board members signaled their agreement with the proposal.
