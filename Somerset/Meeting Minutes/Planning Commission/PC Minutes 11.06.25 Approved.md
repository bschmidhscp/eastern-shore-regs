## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Administrative Solar Project Review:** SP25-261 Secluded Field Solar 1 Mt. Vernon Rd, Princess Anne (Tax Map 15E, Grid 8, Parcel 52). 

Mr. William Cornish presented a summary of the Staff Report for the record. 

Mr. Ted Hastings, of Becker Morgan, was present for the meeting on behalf of Secluded Solar. Mr. Hastings provided a brief narrative of the planned development.  Mr. Hastings stated that the project is actually three separate systems that are co-located, and that the systems can be built at any time or order. That means the development could be either completed all at once or built in phases. 

Issues discussed among the representative and Planning Commission included the requested landscaping island waiver – in which the applicant requested to not include a landscaped island abutting the building. Due to space constraints and degree of landscaping provided elsewhere on the property, the Planning Commission granted the request to waive the landscaped parking island requirement abutting the building.

**b. Commercial Site Plan Approval:** SP24-251 Higher Calling Ministries, 30614 Antioch Avenue (Tax Map 15F, Grid 17, Parcel 484). 

Representatives Mr. Evan Young and the Pastor of the Church were present to discuss the proposed subdivision. Mr. William Cornish entered the Staff Report into the record and provided a synopsis of the request: 

_The proposed Church Building will be located in front of the three existing Higher Calling Ministries buildings, at 30614 Antioch Ave. The property is already improved with three existing structures – all to remain. The full Site Development Plan calls for a new primary church building of 6,232 sq. ft, as well as the creation of extensive parking to service the new and existing structures. Also, details pertaining to Stormwater Management, Sediment and Erosion Control, and Landscaping are included in the Site Plan. The preliminary plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on December 9, 2024. A revised subdivision was resubmitted on October 20, 2025_ 

Mr. Young provided a narrative to the Planning Commission, stating the full intention of consolidating several buildings that the church is currently using into one larger building. 

Further topics discussed included the stormwater management, which as of now, is planned to be constructed to Maximum Standards, and is being adequately coordinated with the county engineer. 

The commission also had questions about the parking lot, and whether it had to be paved or would be kept gravel. In the end it was decided that the lot could initially be constructed with gravel before being paved with asphalt as the church gained more funds.
