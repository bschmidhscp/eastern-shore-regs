# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _June 5, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on June 5, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Matt Webster Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Admin. Jeremy Mason (via phone) Catherine Skeeter, Planner Janet McIntyre Skip Colborn Laverne Stewart **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Nelson Brice Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Chairman Mr. Matt Webster opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** May 1, 2025 & May 27, 2025 meetings 

Mr. Nelson Brice made notice of the multiple name-related errors in the May 1<sup>st</sup> minutes.  Staff assured members that all names would be corrected. 

On a motion by Mr. Brice, seconded by Mr. Skip Colborn, the amended May 1, 2025 minutes were approved. 

On a motion by Mr. Colborn, seconded by Mrs. Janet McIntyre, the May 27, 2025 minutes were approved.

# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _June 5, 2025_ 

Mr. Webster asked if there were any opponents or proponents present to speak.  None were present. Mr. Cornish noted the submission of two letters for support.  Mr. Cornish read the two letters of support for the record. 

Mrs. McIntyre moved to close the Public Hearing. 

**b. Recommendation to County Commissioners:** Critical Area Growth Allocation for Kingsbay Mansion LLC/David Carlin King & Gerard Philpott 

Mr. David King came up to speak as the applicant.  Mr. King further explained the reasoning for their request, specifically noting the need for a place for people in the area (and beyond) to come and eat at a nice restaurant.  Mr. King assured the Planning Commission that the expansion of their business would only positively impact the area, and would not lead to any negative impacts to the community or the environment. 

Mr. Colborn asked about the plan to accommodate restaurant attendee parking.  Mr. King and Mr. Philpott (coming up from the back) assured the Planning Commission that adequate parking is already available on the property, primarily along the existing long driveway.  Also, staff confirmed that site issues could be further fleshed out once the applicants come back to the Planning Commission for the approval of their restaurant plan. 

Mr. Colborn asked staff how much Growth Allocation had been recently used, and if the applicant had adequately met all application standards.  Staff assured Mr. Colborn that the applicants had met the standards to proceed through the process, and shared that very little of the County’s Growth Allocation acreage has been utilized. 

On a motion by Mr. Brice, seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission moved to forward a favorable recommendation of the Growth Allocation application to the County Commissioners. 

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS** 

Staff shared that nothing was yet confirmed for the July Planning Commission meeting.
