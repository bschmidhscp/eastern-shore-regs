# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _October 2, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on October 2, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Matt Webster Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre William Cornish, Assistant Director Skip Colborn Laverne Stewart Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Jeremy Mason Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Chairman Matt Webster opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** September 4, 2025 meeting 

On a motion by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, seconded by Mr. Jeremy Mason, the September 4, 2025 minutes were approved. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Site Plan Approval:** SP25-260 Sarah Matthews, Trilogy Group LLC, 4360, Crisfield Highway, Crisfield, Maryland – Map 64, Grid 12, Parcel 51 lot 2 

Mrs. Sara Matthews, Mr. Cliff Matthews, and Mr. Skyler Fogle, with Trilogy Group LLC, presented the site plan and concept for the proposed Cannabis Growing Facility.  The site details and a mock up of the office interior were presented. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer read the Staff Report into the record: 

_“The Department of Technical and Community Services received a Site Plan submittal from Mrs. Sara Matthews of Trilogy Group, LLC on September 2, 2025. The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for the change of use and renovation of the existing auto repair building, on the already developed commercial site. The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 8.15b(3). A preliminary Site Plan and narrative were reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on September 8, 2025. A revised Site Plan was submitted on September 22, 2025 for Planning Commission consideration (a further revised Site Plan was submitted on October 2_<sup>_nd_</sup> _)._

_The proposed cannabis growing facility will be located behind and inside the existing ‘Jed’s Auto’ facility, which will be renovated from its current use, at 4630 Crisfield Highway. The property is already improved with a combined 17,400 sq. ft. warehouse and administrative building, existing commercial access to MD413, and landscaping. The Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals met on Friday, September 19th to review and discuss a Special Exception Request for this project, which was unanimously approved._ 

_Items for PC Discussion and Consideration:_ 

- _a. The stormwater management pond for this site is located on an adjoining parcel. Within one year of approval, the applicant should submit for a lot consolidation to ensure the stormwater management pond remains with the facility._ 

   - _I. Revised lot consolidation plat should also remove the 50’ service road which was removed by the Planning Commission in 2023 for the Johnston property on the corner of MD413 and Plantation Road._ 

- _b. Exterior lighting on the proposed building and property should be shown on the site plan._ 

- _c. The Site Plan is lacking basic property information, and some details are confusing. If approval is granted, it should be conditioned that the Site Plan be brought into compliance with the basic standards for submittal._ 

_It is recommended that the Planning Commission consider approval of the proposed_ 

_Site Plan, with the following conditions:_ 

- _All required revisions to the plat be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures._ 

- _Stormwater Management Maintenance and Inspection Agreement shall be submitted and approved by the County Engineer.”_ 

Mr. Brice asked the applicant how many Employees they plan to have.  Mrs. Matthews answered they currently only have her, but they plan for about a dozen. 

Mr. Mason then inquired if Trilogy group has any other facilities. The applicants explained their license only allows one business per license holder. 

Mr. Webster then asked for a short rundown of how their business works. The applicants went into great detail breaking down their business plan, and the ins and outs of their field. This discussion included the security and licensing. Mr. Colborn asked for clarification of their license and its exact limits. The applicants explained that the maximum facility they could operate under their license was 300,000 Square feet. 

Mr. Colborn questioned the signage, with Mr. Mason following up about the screening of the property. Trilogy intends for the signage to be as discreet as possible, and the facility will be screened according to state standards.

Mr. Webster questioned whether there would be an increase in traffic at the facility, to which Mrs. Matthews explained they would be taking weekly deliveries at the most. 

Mr. Colborn inquired whether expansion of their facility would be possible with the lot reconfiguration that would combine the parent parcel with the storm water pond. The applicant answered that yes that was a possibility. 

**On a motion by Mr. Nelson Brice, seconded by Mr. Jeremy Mason, the Planning Commission moved to approve SP25-260 for Trilogy Group, LLC, with the following discussed conditions:** 

1. Within one year of approval, the applicant should submit for a lot consolidation to ensure the stormwater management pond remains with the facility. 

   - a. Revised lot consolidation plat should also remove the 50’ service road which was removed by the Planning Commission in 2023 for the Johnston property on the corner of MD413 and Plantation Road. 

2. A building permit must be obtained before any construction 

3. All required revisions to the plat be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures 

4. Lighting should be addressed on the site plan. 

5. Stormwater Management Maintenance and Inspection Agreement shall be submitted and approved by the County Engineer 

6. That all local, State, and Federal regulations are adhered to. 

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS:** 

Mr. Drewer mentioned that there would not be a TAC meeting for October, and at this time there was not expected to be a November meeting, unless a prior TAC reviewed site plan was resubmitted for Planning Commission review and approval. 

## **D. ADJOURN** 

**On a motion by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, and seconded by Mr. Jeremy Mason, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the August 7, 2025 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 6:46 p.m. 

Respectfully Submitted, 

William Cornish, Assistant Director
