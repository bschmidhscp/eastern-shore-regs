# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _February 1, 2024_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission meeting held on February 1, 2024, at 5:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Matt Webster Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre Will Cornish, Zoning Specialist Laverne Stewart Catherine Skeeter, Planner Nelson Brice Skip Colborn **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Mr. Matt Webster opened the meeting at 5:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

January 4, 2024 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mrs. Janet McIntyre and seconded by Mrs. Laverne Stewart. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Subdivision/Condominium Revision:** SA23-555 Lots 47 & 48 Manokin Village Condominium Regime Phase III (Tax map 23C, Grid 15, Parcel 175) – _Continuance from January 4, 2024 Planning Commission meeting with opportunity for public comment_ 

_*this case was heard out of agenda sequence*_ 

Mr. Bret Davis was not present for the meeting, and a further continuance was proposed, to allow for proper discussion with the applicant. 

Two members of the public, with an interest in the case (one owns a lot in Manokin Village) were present to speak on the matter - Mr. Nasir Badr and Mr. Saif el Islam Hamed.  Both stated they had no objection to Mr. Davis’ proposal for a duplex.  They were also in favor of being able to build in styles other than mobile homes within the development. 

The Public Comment/Hearing portion of the meeting was closed at 6:02 p.m. 

Mr. Skip Colborn spoke of hesitancy to allow for the “duplex” style because it would be inconsistent with the original vision for the mobile home development.  Mrs. McIntyre stated

that she is in favor of allowing additional styles of development because the development is in poor conditional and with a willing developer eager to make changes, the development could be improved and the tax base could grow. 

**On a motion by Mrs. Stewart, seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission moved to afford a further continuance of the case, to be heard again during the next Planning Commission meeting.** 

**b. Site Plan:** SP23-244 T & T Auto, Holland Crossing Rd (Tax Map 65, Grid 2, Parcel 166) 

Representative Mr. Evan Young of Hampshire, Hampshire, & Andrews, as well as applicants Mr. Clifton Taylor and Ms. Danielle Crockett were present for the presentation and discussion of this case. 

Ms. Catherine Skeeter entered the Staff Report into the record and provided a synopsis of the request: 

_The Department of Technical and Community Services received a submittal from Mr. Evan Young of Hampshire, Hampshire and Andrews, on behalf of Mr. Clifton G. Taylor, IV, on October 27, 2023.  Mr. Taylor is seeking site plan approval for the development of a mixed-use commercial/residential structure. The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 8.15b(3). The Site Plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on January 8, 2024.  A revised Site Plan was submitted on January 22, 2024._ 

_The proposed Commercial/Residential mixed-use structure will be located on Holland Crossing Rd, adjacent to the existing T&T Auto at 27571 Holland Crossing Rd.  The proposed structure will include a performance auto shop and living space.  The structure will be set back from the road a fair distance.  The lot of concern is 12.52 acres, and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial), and is currently fully wooded._ 

_The site plan appears to adequately address ingress/egress, parking, lighting, façade appearance, and landscaping.  Signage and dumpster placement are addressed in the site plan notes.  BUT they are not planning to have a sign on the property and they are planning to utilize the existing dumpster on the adjacent lot._ 

_Staff recommends approval of this site plan, with consideration for possible contributions for inclusion by the Planning Commission._ 

Mr. Young further elaborated on the proposed structure’s use and emphasized the non-publicfacing nature.  Mr. Taylor confirmed that he would be the primary employee, and that access to the property would not be intended for the public. 

Mr. Colborn inquired about the material of the long driveway, with confirmation from Mr. Taylor that it would be stone/gravel, rather than asphalt.  Applicants also confirmed that culvert install has been approved by County Roads.  Planning Commission members had no further concerns.

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS** 

## **a. Comprehensive Plan Update:** Smith Planning & Design 

Virginia Smith and Eric Messick of Smith Planning and Design were present to discuss the status of the ongoing Comprehensive Plan Update.  SP&D ran through a PowerPoint presentation outlining the timeline status, Technical Committee (TC) meetings, project website, and public survey result status. Particular attention was paid to the topics of the morning’s TC meeting: Community Facilities and Transportation.  They mentioned that the ‘working draft’ chapters are on the website and discussed next steps. 

## **<u>ADJOURNMENT:</u>** 

**On a motion by Mrs. Stewart, and seconded by Mr. Brice, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the February 1, 2024 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 6:06 p.m.
