## **B. NEW BUSINESS** 

**a. Site Plan:** SP25-253 Dodson Mini Storage (Jim Dodson), Crisfield Highway/Crisfield-Marion Road (Tax Map 64, Grid 12, Parcel 51) 

Mr. Jesse Drewer read a summary of the Staff Report for the record. 

_“The Department of Technical and Community Services received a submittal from Mr. Chris Custis of Chris D. Custis Surveying on February 19, 2024, on behalf of Mr. Jim Dodson. The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for the creation of a new Mini Storage operation, comprised of two phases. The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 8.15b(3). The Site Plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on March 3, 2025. A revised Site Plan was submitted on March 20, 2025, and a further revised update was submitted on April 3, 2025, for Planning Commission consideration._

_The proposed phased mini storage development plans for the reuse of an existing structure (with a new addition attached) as well as the construction of a new building, totaling 88 units. Phase two will include three additional new mini storage buildings, totaling 140 more units (phase one + phase two = 228 total units). The plan also includes a Lot Reconfiguration to re-combine lots 4A and 5 into the newly named Lot 4B. There is an existing Forest Conservation Area on the northern portion of the property, the result of a 1998 subdivision. The property is located at the west quadrant of the intersection of Crisfield Hwy (MD413) and Crisfield Marion Rd (MD667). There are two platted, but not utilized, access points to the property off of MD667, and a utilized ROW, which is the planned only access for the mini storage, off of MD413. The property is ideally zoned as General Commercial.”_ 

Mr. Chris Custis, of CDC Surveying was present to discuss the Site Plan, as was the applicant Mr. Jim Dodson.  Mr. Custis and Mr. Dodson gave a rundown of the proposal and timeline. 

Mr. Colborn questioned the location of the driveway in relation to the adjacent property line, hoping that space could be found to accommodate a buffer of plantings along the driveway.  Mr. Custis provided assurance that the area could be utilized as a planting buffer.  Drainage was also a concern of Mr. Colborn’s, raising the issue of the capacity of the planned stormwater basin.  Mr. Colborn asked about the frontage along 413 and whether planting could be placed here.  Mr. Custis and Mr. Dodson described the area as not being ideal for planting for a number of reasons.  The turn onto 413 from 667 is already hazardous and the addition of plants would only exacerbate this. Also, the area is not far enough away from the proposed gravel drive and plantings would impede utilization of the driveway and storage building. 

Mr. Colborn asked about the accessibility of the complex for disabled people, suggesting that at least one of the end units nearest the provided handicap parking be up to ADA standards.  Mr. Dodson agreed with this recommendation. 

Mr. Colborn questioned the location of an existing sign along 413, which is not associated with the property, but a SHA sign.  The sign was not reflected on the Site Plan. 

Mr. Brice made a motion to approve the Dodson Mini Storage Site Plan with staff recommendation and the conditions that landscape buffers of 5’ be provided along the access driveway and 667 property line, any SHA requirements be followed, and that an ADA accessible unit be provided. The motion was seconded by Mrs. Stewart and passed unanimously.
