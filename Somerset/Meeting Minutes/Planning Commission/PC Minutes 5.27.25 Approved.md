# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _May 27, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on May 27, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Matt Webster Jesse Drewer, Director Jeremy Mason Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Admin. Janet McIntyre Catherine Skeeter, Planner Skip Colborn Steve Raab **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Nelson Brice Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Chairman Mr. Matt Webster opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. NEW BUSINESS** 

**I. Public Hearing:** Proposed Zoning Code Text Amendments 

Mr. Jesse Drewer provided a summary of the proposed zoning code text amendments – entering for the record the provided packet of changes and cover sheet highlighting each section. 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati provided some insights into the reasoning for the solar-related changes, also sharing that Somerset will be the first County to amend the code to comply with the new State standards recently signed into law.  Mr. Illuminati provided some perspective as to probable implications of the solar text amendments including the likelihood of the western side of the County receiving far more solar sites than the eastern side, due to infrastructure variation from the east to west.  Mr. Illuminati also shared that the site plans will still come to the Planning Commission. 

Mr. Drewer highlighted the new language related to allowing special events in the Critical Area – this language concurs with the updated text in the Critical Area Ordinance. 

Mr. Drewer noted a change to clarifying language for the placement of mobile/manufactured homes, in concurrence with the building code.  Mr. Cornish provided some clarity on the need for the new language to limit the placement of dilapidated homes.

For the proposed text amendment relating to allowing the County to utilize website and other massmedia options for advertising, Mr. Drewer stated the need for this option to improve access to information and provide flexibility for the office.  Mr. Illuminati shared that this would not apply to everything, as many hearing types would still require newspaper advertising. 

Mr. Webster asked if there were any proponents or opponents to the proposed Zoning Code Text Amendments.  Seeing none, the public hearing was closed at 6:15. 

**II. Recommendation to County Commissioners:** Proposed Zoning Code Text Amendments 

Mr. Skip Colborn kicked off discussion of the proposed zoning amendments.  First asking about the allowance of solar in R1 but not R2 or R3 districts.  Mr. Cornish provided the explanation that any zone allowing mixed use, which R2 and R3 do, solar would not be permitted.  Mr. Colborn also questioned the reasoning behind prohibiting solar on brownfields.  Staff could not provide exact reasoning, just that it was directly from the State’s language.  Mr. Colborn asked about fencing requirements, and whether a minimum height standard should be set.  Mr. Illuminati stated that the Commission would still have oversight on Site Plan approvals, and setting a minimum could cause issues down the road.  Mr. Colborn lastly asked about the language in the new solar requirements that state a “qualified landscape architect” prepare landscaping plans, citing the contradiction to other landscaping plan requirements elsewhere in the code.  Staff and other members of the Planning Commission agreed that allowing landscape plans to be prepared by surveyors/engineers, rather than only landscape architects, in other applications would remain appropriate with no need to change that standard. 

Mr. Steve Raab made a motion to recommend the proposed zoning code text amendments, with the addition of building code requested language, to the County Commissioners.  The motion passed unanimously.

**I** . ‘Regular’ Meeting on Thursday, June 5<sup>th</sup> at 6:00 p.m. 

Mr. Drewer shared that the June meeting has only one item – a Public Hearing for the Growth Allocation application from Kingsbay Mansion.
