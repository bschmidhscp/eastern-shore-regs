# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** 

_May 2, 2024_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission meeting held on May 2, 2024, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Jeremy Mason Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre Catherine Skeeter, Planner Skip Colborn Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Mr. Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

March 7, 2024 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mr. Nelson Brice and seconded by Mr. Skip Colborn. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Commercial Site Plan:** SP23-243 AutoZone Store #5836, 12208 Brittingham Lane (Tax Map 15C, Grid 8, Parcel 307). 

Mr. Jesse Drewer presented a summary of the Staff Report for the record. 

Mr. Carmen DiDiano, of MDM Surveyors & Engineers, was present for the meeting on behalf of AutoZone.  Mr. DiDiano provided a brief narrative of the planned store development, and provided a large picture of the façade plan.  Mr. DiDiano stated that the building is their largest prototype, featuring a corner entry, with an expected 3-5 employees, opened 8-9:00 p.m. daily, with propane, water and sewer, and on-site underground stormwater management. 

Issues discussed among the representative and Planning Commission included the requested landscaping island waiver – in which the applicant requested to not include a landscaped island abutting the building. Due to space constraints and degree of landscaping provided elsewhere on the property, the Planning Commission granted the request to waive the landscaped parking island requirement abutting the building. 

Mr. DiDiano also mentioned that the existing trees on the property would be retained, and the ditch on the northern property line would be cleaned out to support their stormwater management.  Mr. Skip Colborn voiced concern for the additional traffic along Brittingham Lane, citing a problematic curb up the road adjacent to the new Royal Farms.  Mr. DiDiano assured the Planning

**On a motion by Mr. Colborn, seconded by Mr. Nelson Brice, the Planning Commission unanimously moved to approve the Commercial Site Plan for AutoZone, with standard conditions.** 

**b. 4 Lot Preliminary Subdivision Approval:** SA24-557 Thornton Properties, LLC., Old Princess Anne Road (Tax Map 151, Grid 23, Parcel 469). 

Owners/Representatives Mr. Paul Thornton and Mr. Brannigan Thornton were present to discuss the proposed subdivision. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer entered the Staff Report into the record and provided a synopsis of the request: 

_The proposed subdivision will be located on Old Princess Anne Rd approximately 1150 ft north of the intersection of Old Princess Anne Rd and Mulder Branch Rd. in Princess Anne. Property is bounded on the North by single family residential properties, South by the corporate limit of the Town of Princess Anne and the Somerset Meadows Apartment complex. Parcel borders the railroad right of way on the west and does not have access to Somerset Ave.  The proposal is to subdivide a 1.45 ac parcel into 4 lots. 3 lots will front and have access off a new 30 ft wide private road which will be 288 ft in length and terminate at a cul de sac turnaround. The existing house, which fronts Old Princess Anne Rd will keep its current access. The Subdivision regulations provide minimum construction requirements for private roads. The preliminary plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on April 8, 2024. A revised subdivision was resubmitted on April 23, 2024_ 

Mr. Paul Thornton provided a narrative to the Planning Commission, stating the full intention of creating a beautiful new subdivision, built to high standards, with the goal of improving the area. 

Further topics discussed included the private road, which as of now, is planned to be constructed to County Road Standards, and the planned water and sewer service which is being adequately coordinated with the Sanitary District. 

Mr. Colborn and Mr. Mason both expressed support for the effort to construct infill in this particular area. 

**On a motion by Mr. Brice, seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to approve the Preliminary Subdivision.**
