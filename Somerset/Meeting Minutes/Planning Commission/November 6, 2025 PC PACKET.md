B. NEW BUSINESS: 
a. Site Plan Approval: SP25-260 Sarah Matthews, Trilogy Group LLC, 4360, Crisfield Highway, 
Crisfield, Maryland – Map 64, Grid 12, Parcel 51 lot 2 
 
Mrs. Sara Matthews, Mr. Cliff Matthews, and Mr. Skyler Fogle, with Trilogy Group LLC, 
presented the site plan and concept for the proposed Cannabis Growing Facility.  The site details 
and a mock up of the office interior were presented. 
 
Mr. Jesse Drewer read the Staff Report into the record: 
 
“The Department of Technical and Community Services received a Site Plan submittal from Mrs. Sara 
Matthews of Trilogy Group, LLC on September 2, 2025. The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for the 
change of use and renovation of the existing auto repair building, on the already developed commercial 
site. The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 
8.15b(3). A preliminary Site Plan and narrative were reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) 
meeting on September 8, 2025. A revised Site Plan was submitted on September 22, 2025 for Planning 
Commission consideration (a further revised Site Plan was submitted on October 2nd).

The proposed cannabis growing facility will be located behind and inside the existing ‘Jed’s Auto’ facility, 
which will be renovated from its current use, at 4630 Crisfield Highway. The property is already improved 
with a combined 17,400 sq. ft. warehouse and administrative building, existing commercial access to 
MD413, and landscaping. The Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals met on Friday, September 19th 
to review and discuss a Special Exception Request for this project, which was unanimously approved. 
 
Items for PC Discussion and Consideration: 
 
a.  The stormwater management pond for this site is located on an adjoining parcel. Within one year 
of approval, the applicant should submit for a lot consolidation to ensure the stormwater 
management pond remains with the facility. 
I. 
 Revised lot consolidation plat should also remove the 50’ service road which was removed 
by the Planning Commission in 2023 for the Johnston property on the corner of MD413 
and Plantation Road. 
b.  Exterior lighting on the proposed building and property should be shown on the site plan. 
c.  The Site Plan is lacking basic property information, and some details are confusing. If approval 
is granted, it should be conditioned that the Site Plan be brought into compliance with the basic 
standards for submittal. 
 
It is recommended that the Planning Commission consider approval of the proposed 
Site Plan, with the following conditions: 
•  
All required revisions to the plat be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures. 
•  
Stormwater Management Maintenance and Inspection Agreement shall be submitted and approved 
by the County Engineer.” 
 
Mr. Brice asked the applicant how many Employees they plan to have.  Mrs. Matthews answered 
they currently only have her, but they plan for about a dozen.   
 
Mr. Mason then inquired if Trilogy group has any other facilities. The applicants explained their 
license only allows one business per license holder. 
 
Mr. Webster then asked for a short rundown of how their business works. The applicants went into 
great detail breaking down their business plan, and the ins and outs of their field. This discussion 
included the security and licensing. Mr. Colborn asked for clarification of their license and its 
exact limits. The applicants explained that the maximum facility they could operate under their 
license was 300,000 Square feet. 
 
Mr. Colborn questioned the signage, with Mr. Mason following up about the screening of the 
property. Trilogy intends for the signage to be as discreet as possible, and the facility will be 
screened according to state standards.

Administrative Approval Review – Solar Facility 
 
 
A. BACKGROUND:  
Pursuant to new state legislation (SB931) regarding solar facilities, projects are now required to 
be administratively reviewed and expedited. Projects must meet standardized criteria outlined in 
state law for landscaping, fencing, and setbacks. Counties are required to develop and adhere to 
an expedited review process, which for Somerset County will be a review by the Technical 
Advisory Committee and a review of the administrative approval by the Planning Commission. 
While not technically necessary and the commission cannot impose any further restrictions, staff 
feel the review by the Planning Commission will keep the public aware and informed of upcoming 
and ongoing projects. 
The Department of Technical and Community Services received a Site Plan submittal from Ted 
Hastings of Becker Morgan Group on behalf of the applicant on October 22, 2025. Section 5.7e(28) 
of the Zoning Code allows Solar Energy Generation Stations that produce less than 5MW as a 
permitted use in the R-2 Zoning District. A preliminary Site Plan and narrative were reviewed at 
a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on September 8, 2025.  A revised Site Plan was 
submitted on October 22, 2025 for Administrative Approval and Planning Commission 
consideration. 
 
B. PROJECT OVERVIEW:  
The proposed solar facility will be three (3) co-located community solar systems with a maximum 
of five (5) MW of capacity located on what is currently an agricultural field, located on Mt. Vernon 
Road.

C. PROJECTS SPECIFICS: 
a. Access: The single existing farm property access drive is to be utilized and improved 
with access to/from Mt. Vernon Road. An access permit from SHA may be required 
for proposed driveway improvements  
b. Stormwater Management: The project has been reviewed by the County Engineer 
and proposes the use of a solar disconnection on land that is currently farmed, which is 
an allowed non-structural practice, which when combined with the landscaping buffer, 
compliments the site. 
c. Landscaping: Currently existing woods surround the property on three sides, with 
additional landscaping proposed along the front of the property facing Mt. Vernon 
Road. 
d. Parking: No proposed parking or loading areas, temporary parking and loading areas 
for construction are addressed in the attached logistics plan. 
e. Lighting: No visible light will emit from the site during dusk to dawn operations. 
f. Fencing: Fencing is required around the perimeter of the facility and is shown on the 
site plan between the system and the landscaping buffer.

INTRODUCTION. 
 
Secluded Field Solar 1, LLC (“Applicant”) submits this Supplemental Narrative in support of its 
application for Site Plan approval.   
 
The Applicant is the lessee of those certain lots or parcels of land located on and accessible to Mt. 
Vernon Road, Princess Anne, Maryland 21853, further described as Tax Map 15, Grid 8, Parcel 
226, consisting of approximately 48.72 acres, and Tax Map 15E, Grid 8, Parcel 52, consisting of 
approximately 84.12 acres, which parcels are owned by Joseph E. Reading and Jay O. Reading 
pursuant to a deed dated April 8, 2015, recorded among the Land Records of Somerset County, 
Maryland in Liber 896, folio 520 (collectively, the “Property”). 
 
The Property is bounded to the northeast by Mt. Vernon Road (Md. Route 362) and two residential 
parcels, and to the south, east, and west by mostly wooded lands, substantially buffering the 
Property from nearby residences and developments.  The Property is zoned R-2 Medium Density 
Residential District and under active tillage for grain production. 
 
The Applicant seeks to establish a utility scale community solar project with up to five (5) MW of 
capacity on a portion of the Property well removed from Mt. Vernon Road and nearby residences, 
together with an access road, security fencing, and customary equipment and utility 
interconnection infrastructure (the “Project”), as further shown and depicted on the enclosed Site 
Plan attached here as Exhibit A. The Project will consist of three (3) separately metered solar 
arrays. One solar array will have 1.9 MW, the second solar array will have 2.0 MW, and the third 
solar array will have 1.1 MW, for an aggregate of 5 MW for the entire Project. 
 
In accordance with Section 8.15 of the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance (“Ordinance”), the 
Applicant has submitted the Site Plan for approval by Planning Commission. Section 8.15 of the 
Ordinance requires the Applicant to obtain approval of the Site Plan from the Planning 
Commission prior to commencing the Project.  The Site Plan meets the Ordinance design 
requirements for approval as further described below, including but not limited to the bulk 
regulations, visual standards, lighting, and fencing requirements for a Solar Energy Generation 
Station.
