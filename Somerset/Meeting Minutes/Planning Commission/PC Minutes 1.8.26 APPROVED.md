# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _January 8, 2026_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on January 8, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Matt Webster Jesse Drewer, Director Jeremy Mason Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Admin. Skip Colborn Catherine Skeeter, Planner Steve Raab Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Chairman Matt Webster opened the meeting at 5:01 p.m. 

## **1) APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** December 4, 2025 Meeting 

On a motion by Mr. Nelson Brice, seconded by Mr. Skip Colborn, the December 4, 2025 minutes were approved. 

## **2) NEW BUSINESS** 

**A. Administrative Solar Project Review:** SP-25-263 TurningPoint Energy (TPE) SO19 Solar – 27315 Crisfield-Marion Road (Tax Map 65, Grid 01, Parcels 111, 130 & 132) 

Mr. Jesse Drewer read a summary of the Staff Report, for the record, and further explained the administrative nature of the already-approved solar project, noting that the plans are coming before the Planning Commission simply for notification purposes. 

Ms. Franny Yuhas and Mr. Chris Martin were present virtually via Teams to discuss the project and field concerns. 

Mr. Colborn asked whether landscaping would be irrigated, or watered for survivability through other means.  Mr. Martin shared that other similar projects have typically utilized a water truck and that survivability assurance will be further supported by the landscaping surety and bonding.

## **3) OTHER BUSINESS** 

**A. Findings of Fact Approval for REZ-25-196:** Chesapeake Renewable Energy LLC Ocean Highway, (Tax Map 42, Grid 13, Parcel 017) 

Mr. Drewer summarized the intent of the Findings of Fact, noting that the document was prepared by Mr. Illuminati to reflect the Planning Commission’s recommendation during their December meeting to favorably forward the rezoning case to the County Commissioners.  Mr. Illuminate confirmed the stance that the Findings reflect the “mistake” argument rather that the “change of character” argument. 

**On a motion by Mr. Colborn, seconded by Mr. Raab, the Planning Commission moved to forward the Findings of Fact favorable recommendation of the rezoning of a portion of Map 42, Parcel 107, Lot 4 from AR to I-2, to the County Commissioners for their further review and approval.** 

Chairman Webster signed the Findings of Fact and associated memo. 

## **B. Housing Expansion and Affordability Act of 2024 (HB538) Discussion** 

Mr. Drewer shared the implications of the approved HB538 Housing Expansion and Affordability Act.  Mr. Raab asked whether singlewides would be permissible regardless of their age.  Mr. Illuminate shared that the bill’s language references “new” manufactured or modular dwellings, so age restrictions would still apply.  Mr. Mason voiced his concern for how this legislation would actually address affordability within Somerset County.  Mr. Colborn asked whether this legislation
