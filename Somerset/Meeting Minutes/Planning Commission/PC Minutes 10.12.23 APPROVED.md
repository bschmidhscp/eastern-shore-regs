**SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** October 12, 2023 

# eae 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission meeting held on October 12, 2023, at 6:00 P.M. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Jeremy Mason (Chair) Jesse Drewer, Director Matt Webster (Vice Chair) Mary Phillips, Assistant Director/Zoning Administrator Janet McIntyre Catherine Skeeter, Planner Skip Colborn Steve Raab **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Laverne Stewart Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Mr. Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

August 3, 2023 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mr. Steve Raab and seconded by Mr. Skip Colborn. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

## **a. Election of Chair and Vice-Chair** 

- On a motion by Mr. Raab, seconded by Mrs. Janet McIntyre, Mr. Mason was selected to continue as Chair of the Planning Commission. 

- On a motion by Mr. Mason, seconded by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, Mr. Matt Webster was selected to continue as Vice Chair of the Planning Commission. 

**b. Presentation and Update of the Comprehensive Plan Update:** Smith Planning and Design will present and discuss the progress of the update. 

Michelle King and Eric Messick of Smith Planning and Design were present to discuss the status of the ongoing Comprehensive Plan Update.  SP&D ran through a power point presentation outlining the update timeline status, public meetings with both Princess Anne and Crisfield, Technical Committee meetings, project website, public survey result status, Land Use and Economic Vitality analysis methodologies, Draft Chapter 3 and 4 Goals and Recommendations, and next steps. 

Multiple questions arose from the Planning Commission regarding the methodology of the public survey dissemination, the interpretation of results, and the potential for further expanding the reach of the survey by continuing to post it in various online locations as well as increase the number of

in person survey box locations. SP&D was receptive of concerns and expressed interest in potentially modifying methodology to reach as much of the public as possible. Mrs. McIntyre expressed concern with an issue of an ad following the completion of the online survey, urging SP&D to find a way to remove the ad from the website. **c. Zoning Code Text Amendments:** Proposed Amendments to Section 5.7(e) Industrial Uses to include cannabis as an Industrial Use, and Section 17 Definitions for references to cannabis. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer presented to the Board proposed changes to the Zoning Ordinance to now include reference specifically to cannabis definitions and allowance in only Industrial zoning districts within the County.  Mr. Drewer explained that the County Commissioners adopted a temporary Moratorium on businesses engaged in the growing, processing, and/or selling of cannabis to include on-site consumption establishments.  The proposed verbiage of the text amendment was reviewed by Planning Commission members, with the inclusion of “on-site” consumption leading to discussion.  Mr. Andrew Illuminati explained how the Planning Commission may opt to remove the allowance of on-site consumption within the code.  Mr. Raab moved to strike the mention of “on-site consumption” from the code, with a second from Mr. Webster.  Mrs. McIntyre voted against this motion, but the motion carried as the other members voted in its favor. 

The next step in the process of adopting the text amendments to the Zoning Ordinance is to hold a Public Hearing in front of the Planning Commission to allow for public input before the Planning Commission formally adopts the amendment.  This hearing has been scheduled for November 16, 2023 at 6:00 P.M. 

## **<u>OTHER BUSINESS</u>** 

Mr. A.C. Alrey, the new Director of Business Development for the City of Crisfield, was present to introduce himself and establish a relationship with the Planning Commission, hoping to work in conjunction moving forward. 

## **<u>ADJOURNMENT:</u>** 

**On a motion by Mr. Webster, and seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the October 12, 2023 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 7:13 P.M.
