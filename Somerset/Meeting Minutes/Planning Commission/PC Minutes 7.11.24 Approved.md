# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _July 11, 2024_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission meeting held on July 11, 2024, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

## **<u>Planning Commissioners Present:</u>** 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Jeremy Mason Jesse Drewer, Director Matt Webster Catherine Skeeter, Planner Skip Colborn Steve Raab Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Mr. Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

June 6, 2024 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mr. Steve Raab and seconded by Mr. Matt Webster. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Commercial Site Plan:** SP24-246 Mountaire Hatchery, 30700 King Miller Road (Tax Map 23, Grid 12, Parcel 153) 

Mr. John Shahan and Mr. Ed Taylor of AWB Engineers, were both present to speak on the case. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer entered the Staff Report into the record, reading a summary of the request. 

_“The lot is located at the intersection of King Miller Road and US Route 13. The proposed use of the site is an addition to the existing building to expand the ‘chick room’ and provide additional office space, and a breakroom. As noted on the Site Plan, there will be an increase of thirty-one (31) parking spaces for customers and staff. The existing ‘amenities trailer’ on the proposed building addition will be relocated near King Miller Road.”_ 

Mr. Taylor and Mr. Shahan detailed the scope of the project, siting that the proposed addition will allow for new office space, freeing up other area in the existing portion of the building for expanded operations.  The planned exterior style was also described, siting that it will be matched to the existing façade well.

<!-- page 2 -->
Mr. Skip Colborn asked the applicants to explain their reasoning for requesting a landscape waiver for the required parking islands.  The applicants sited potential issues for snow removal.  Upon further discussion, the applicants and Planning Commission agreed to only require the parking islands on the length of the parking lot most near Rt. 13.  At least 3 parking islands will be needed to satisfy the code. 

Mr. Drewer stated that the requirements for Forest Conservation have been satisfied on the Site Plan.   Mr. Colborn requested that the Site Plan incorporate a more detailed landscape plan, to include plant location/quantity/type.  This expanded landscape plan is to be resubmitted and administratively approved as a condition of Site Plan approval. 

The applicants also made note of a change from the original Site Plan submission – the existing amenities trailer, which serves as additional office space, will be relocated on the site to the southern side of the lot, adjacent to the central access point on King Miller Rd. 

**On a motion by Mr. Colborn, seconded by Mr. Webster, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to approve the Site Plan, with standard Staff Recommendations/Conditions, also requiring the addition of 3 landscape islands on the length of the parking lot nearest Rt. 13, and the addition of a landscape legend outlining the planting scheme on the plan.** 

**b. Commercial Site Plan:** SP23-242 Horn Island Ventures, (Tax Map 32, Grid 24, Revised Parcel 239, Lot C) 

Applicant Mr. Chet Rohrbach, and Mr. Evan Young of George E. Young Engineers & Surveyors DBA Hampshire, Hampshire, & Andrews were present to discuss the case. 

Mr. Drewer read a summary of the Staff Report for the record. 

_“The undeveloped lot, which recently underwent a lot reconfiguration to combine two lots, is located on US 13 Northbound, just west of Old Princess Anne Rd, and with Tomato Blvd on the western property line. The Site Plan proposes phased construction of (27) 2,500 sq ft mini-storage buildings and an associated 900 sq ft office building.”_ 

Mr. Rohrbach and Mr. Young detailed the plan and the phased construction.  Stormwater Management will all be incorporated into phase I of the project.  Mr. Colborn asked for an explanation of the Stormwater Management techniques to be utilized in the plan, and asked about the status of the review by the County Engineer.   Mr. Young provided a description of the planned system and stated that Mr. John Redden, County Engineer, has done a preliminary review of the plan.  Mr. Young also brought up the landscaping shown on the plan, stating that though it is not currently shown, a landscape buffer is in fact planned for the western property line along Tomato Blvd, and that the eastern property line is not appropriate for a landscaped buffer due to ditch location restraints and placement of the planned storage units.  Mr. Colborn agreed with the

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS** 

a. Maryland Planning Commission Association – Regional Workshop July 25 at the Crisfield Library 

Mr. Drewer informed the Planning Commission of the upcoming July 25<sup>th</sup> Regional Workshop put on by the Maryland Planning Commissioners Association at the library in Crisfield.  Mr. Drewer encouraged attendance, stating that local meetings of this nature are not common. 

## **<u>ADJOURNMENT:</u>** 

**On a motion by Mr. Webster, and seconded by Mr. Nelson Brice, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the July 11, 2024 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 6:32 p.m.
