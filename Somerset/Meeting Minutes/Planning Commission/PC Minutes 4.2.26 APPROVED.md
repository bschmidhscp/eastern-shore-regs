# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _April 2, 2026_

## **2) NEW BUSINESS** 

**A. Public Hearing - Rezoning:** REZ-26-194 Theodore Miller 11105 Brownstone Road (Tax Map 12, Grid 03, Parcel 002) 

Mr. Will Cornish read a summary of the Staff Report, for the record, and further explained how the applicant had come before the Planning Commission in 2025, but upon unfavorable discussion amongst the Planning Commission at that time, it was decided that a better argument for the rezoning could be made once the 2025 Comprehensive Plan was adopted, which would include the property within a Priority Preservation Area. 

Mr. Cornish made note of the opposition letter received, but no other opponents or proponents were present to voice input on the rezoning proposal.

Mr. Colborn voiced concern for the loss of potential development that could come as a result of the rezoning.  Staff shared that the property is not within a water and sewer service area, and that residential development would be capped at a maximum subdivision of 7 lots.  Mr. Mason spoke to show consideration for both sides of the argument. 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati clarified the argument for the case, siting a change in character of the area as the justification for the request, rather than a mistake in the zoning.  Mr. Illuminati also shared that the Planning Commission will simply need to forward their recommendation to the County Commissioners, who will then hold an additional Public Hearing prior to coming to a formal determination. 

On a motion by Mrs. Janet McIntyre, seconded by Mr. Steve Raab, the Public Hearing closed at 5:12 p.m. 

Applicant, Mr. Theodore Miller, was present to discuss the request with the Planning Commission. Mr. Miller explained to the Board the historic agricultural use, as well as his desire for the land to remain in such use in perpetuity, hoping to apply for a permanent preservation easement through the MALPF program. 

Mr. Colborn again questioned the reasoning for the rezoning, noting the potential for further residential development. 

On a motion by Mr. Nelson Brice, seconded by Mrs. McIntyre, the Planning Commission moved to forward a favorable recommendation for the rezoning to the Board of County Commissioners. Yeas: Nelson Brice, Janet McIntyre, Steve Raab; Nays: Skip Colborn, Laverne Stewart 

**B. Site Plan Review:** SP-25-265 Princess Anne Redevelopment Company, LLC (Hawk Plaza) – Clipper Circle (Tax Map 23, Grid 05, Parcel 166) 

Ms. Catherine Skeeter read a summary of the Staff Report, for the record. 

Mr. Bret Davis was present to discuss the Site Plan for Hawk Plaza. 

Mr. Colborn asked about the proposed landscaping, concerned for the lack of plantings shown around the apartment, though plants were shown around the recreational amenity areas.  Mr. Davis assured the Planning Commission that plantings would be placed around the apartment building to match the style of the plantings around the existing apartment buildings on the parcel. 

Mr. Colborn also asked for confirmation that the dog park area was to be fully fenced – Mr. Davis confirmed.

Mr. Mason voiced support for the project, citing it as a good addition to the existing neighborhood. 

On a motion my Mrs. Stewart, seconded by Mr. Raab, the Planning Commission unanimously moved to approve the Hawk Plaza Site Plan with the following conditions: 

1. All required revisions to the plat be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures. 

2. Any exterior lighting on the proposed buildings should be shown on the site plan. 

3. Prior to the issuance of a Zoning Certificate, Stormwater Management and Sediment and Erosion Control Approval shall be obtained. 

4. All local, State, and Federal regulations shall be adhered to. 

## **3) OTHER BUSINESS** 

Mr. Drewer shared that there would be an April 6, 2026 TAC meeting for a major subdivision and an industrial site plan.  Also, a May Planning Commission meeting may be needed, depending on the re-submittal of TAC-reviewed projects. 

## **4) ADJOURN** 

**On a motion by Mrs. Stewart, and seconded by Mr. Raab, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the April 2, 2026 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 5:39 p.m.
