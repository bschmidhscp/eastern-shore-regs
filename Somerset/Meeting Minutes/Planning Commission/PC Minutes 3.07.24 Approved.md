## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

February 1, 2024 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mrs. Laverne Stewart and seconded by Mr. Skip Colborn. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Subdivision/Condominium Revision:** SA23-555 Lots 47 & 48 Manokin Village Condominium Regime Phase III (Tax map 23C, Grid 15, Parcel 175) – _Additional Continuance from February 1, 2024 Planning Commission meeting_ 

Mr. Bret Davis was not present for the meeting, but rather his brother Mr. Kirk Davis was present as his representative. 

Issues discussed among the representative and Planning Commission included assurance that an HOA/Condo Association is active, the potential issue of clashing styles within the development, and the likelihood of further improvement of the development spurred by this request. 

Mr. Andrew illuminati, on behalf of the County, prepared a statement outlining the amended conditions to be applied to the development standards for Manokin Village.  The Planning Commission agreed that Mr. Illuminati’s drafted statement was appropriate, suggesting the addition of mention of a zero-lot-line. 

**On a motion by Mr. Steve Raab, seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission moved to approve the request for the revision of the building types allowed within the Manokin Village Condominium Regime Phases I-III, based on the drafted Statement by Mr. Illuminati.**

<!-- page 2 -->
**b. Site Plan:** SP24-245 Avatar Self Storage/Rajkumar Thangavelu, Bardwell Drive/Ocean Highway (Tax Map 23, Grid 18, Parcel 136, Lot 1B) 

Owner/Representative Mr. Rajkumar Thangavelu was present to discuss the Site Plan. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer entered the Staff Report into the record and provided a synopsis of the request: 

_The Department of Technical and Community Services received a submittal from Mr. Rajkumar Thangavelu on January 3, 2024.  Mr. Thangavelu is seeking site plan approval for the development of a self-storage facility, with a planned site for a future cell tower.  The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 8.15b(3).  The Site Plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on January 8, 2024.  A revised Site Plan was submitted on February 25, 2024._ 

_The proposed Avatar Self Storage will be located on Bardwell Dr, at the end of Market Ln. in Princess Anne.  Market Ln runs parallel to Rt. 13, and the site of the self-storage is very visible from Rt. 13.  The lot has been recently reconfigured to eliminate the section of the property to the north of Bardwell Dr (created Lots 1A and 1B).  The proposal is depicted as phases 1, 2, and 3.  The lot of concern is 17.4 acres, per recent subdivision, and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial)._ 

Further issues discussed included additional needed landscaping along Rt. 13, and likely impactful modifications to the current Site Plan as a result of Stormwater Management review comments from the County Engineer. 

**On a motion by Mrs. Stewart, seconded by Mr. Webster, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to table further review and approval of an updated Site Plan, until the April (or later, if needed) Planning Commission meeting.**
