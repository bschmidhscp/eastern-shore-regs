# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _December 4, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on December 4, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Matt Webster Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Admin. Skip Colborn Catherine Skeeter, Planner Laverne Stewart Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Chairman Matt Webster opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **1. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** November 6, 2025 Meeting 

On a motion by Mr. Skip Colborn, seconded by Mrs. Janet McIntyre, the November 6, 2025 minutes were approved. 

## **2. NEW BUSINESS** 

**A. Public Hearing - Rezoning:** REZ-25-193 Chesapeake Renewable Energy LLC Ocean Highway, (Tax Map 42, Grid 13, Parcel 017) 

Mr. Webster asked for applicant representation to come forward.  Mr. Lance Young of MacLeod Law Group, LLC, on behalf of the applicant was present to speak to the legal grounds for the rezoning request.  Mr. Young provided an overview of the site’s history, citing the issue of prior zoning not adequately taking the environmental limitations of the site into account when deeming only a portion of the parcel I2.  Mr. Young also spoke of similar cases which act as precedent to allow such a rezoning. 

Board members of Chesapeake Renewable Energy LLC, Mr. Mark Schroeder and Mr. Mark Frizzera, also came forward to speak on the case.  Both took time to discuss the constraints of the property, such as wetlands.  The section of the property that is zoned I2 already is limited, while the section that is AR has greater development potential.  They hope the rezoning will allow for greater economic growth potential.

The applicants also noted the consistency of their request with the County’s goals for the area based on it being a growth area planned as industrial. 

Mr. Webster opened the floor first to proponents, and hearing none, next to opponents. 

Mrs. Tammy Truitt spoke to the Planning Commission to express strong opposition to the requested rezoning.  Mrs. Truitt referenced the Chesapeake Renewable Energy LLC website, on which they cite Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) as a potential future business venture of interest.  Mrs. Truitt shared the potential dangers of BESS and warned of the negative impacts such a facility could have on the County. 

Mrs. Lisa Muir spoke to express opposition to the proposed rezoning, also noting the dangers of BESS and her fear that such a system would be placed on the newly zoned I2 portion of the property. 

Mr. Howard Overholt shared his desire to keep AR land AR, and to not allow actions within the County that could negatively impact farmland. 

Mr. Wayne Muir spoke to express disinterest in the proposed rezoning and to note the lack of economic development solar projects have brought to the County.  Mr. Muir also noted the misnomer “solar farm” where solar is wrongfully deemed an agricultural product.  Mr. Muir similarly expressed discontent with the potential of BESS. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer quickly shared after all public comment ceased, that BESS are already a permitted use in all zoning districts, and that this case had no bearing on the potential development of the site with a BESS facility. 

Mr. Will Cornish formally entered the Staff Report into the record. 

On a motion by Mrs. McIntyre, seconded by Mrs. Stewart, the Planning Commission moved to close the public hearing at 6:53 p.m. 

## **B. Discussion and Recommendation on REZ-25-193** 

Mrs. McIntyre voiced her support of the request, noting the need to clean up the zoning of the parcel. 

Mr. Webster made note of his support of the request as it matches the intentions of the future growth of the County as noted in the Comprehensive Plan.

Mr. Colborn made note of the need for any future development needing to come back to the Planning Commission for review and approval. 

**On a motion by Mrs. McIntyre, seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission moved to forward a favorable recommendation of the rezoning of a portion of Map 42, Parcel 107, Lot 4 from AR to I-2, to the County Commissioners for their further review and approval. One vote in opposition came from Mr. Nelson Brice, but the motion carried.** 

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS** 

Mr. Drewer shared that the next meeting is scheduled for January 8<sup>th</sup> at 5 p.m., during which the Planning Commission will be tasked with formally forwarding their favorable recommendation for the rezoning to the County Commissioners, along with the corresponding Findings of Fact document to be prepared by the Planning Commission’s legal counsel. 

## **D. ADJOURN** 

**On a motion by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, and seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the December 4, 2025 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 7:09 p.m. 

Respectfully Submitted, Catherine Shecter Catherine Skeeter, Planner
