**c.** **<u>Solar Energy Floating (SEF) Zone Approval:</u>** REZ-25-191 Nexamp, Inc., 10960 Kemps Nursery Road (Tax Map 22, Grid 06, Parcel 284) 

Mr. Drewer read a summary of the Staff Report and case history for the record. 

_“The Somerset County Zoning ordinance requires all solar energy facilities generating more than two (2) megawatts in electricity go through the SEF Floating Zone process. Pending a recommendation by the Planning Commission, a public hearing will be held by the County Commissioners on the request. If the floating zone is granted, the applicant would then be permitted to submit for the standard site plan review process by staff and the Planning Commission._ 

_The applicant proposes a solar energy facility that generates 5 megawatts of electricity. It is proposed to be 3 independent systems with 1 1MW and 2 2MW facilities. The proposed solar energy facility will be located on approximately 30 of the 136 acres that comprise the site. Located approximately 0.4 miles down Kemps Nursery Road, the property and surrounding area are primarily agricultural in nature. The property is zoned as Agricultural Residential and complies with the conditions outlined in section 7.5c of the zoning code.”_ 

Attorney Ms. Sharon Van Emberg and Mr. Erick Alves de Sa were present to discuss the proposal. Ms. Van Emberg explained that since their first Planning Commission appearance, the property had been surveyed to reveal that the southern portion suspected to belong to the property owner in fact was a part of the property.  As such, in the revised proposal the panels have been moved south on the parcel, further from the road, more out of sight, and on less productive farm land.  Mr. Alves de Sa went through a quick PowerPoint presentation further outlining Nexamp and the project specifics.  The panels will now jut into the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area, Mr. Alves de Sa confirmed that he has been in contact with Critical Area Commission staff to ensure the development is in compliance with any Critical Area requirements.  Also, the parnel area will be within the floodplain, which will require additional standards be met.  Mr. Alves de Sa outlined the proposed landscaping on the eastern boundaries of the site, and highlighted the existing wooded buffers surrounding most of the project area. 

Mr. Jimmy Beauchamp voiced his general support for the new location of the panels, but still wanted to share that farm land being lost would be a negative, with the new siting leading to a greater area of farm land lost than the original.  Mr. Beauchamp also inquired about the maintenance of the grassed areas outside of the project fences.  Mr. Alves de Sa stated that the area outside of the fences would be maintained by Nexamp for this particular site.
