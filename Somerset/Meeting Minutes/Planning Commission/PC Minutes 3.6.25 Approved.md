**4) Public Hearing for a petition for a Utility Scale Solar Energy Facility (SEF) Floating Zone – REZ-25-191 Nexamp, Inc., 10960 Kemps Nursery Road (Tax Map 22, Grid 06, Parcel 284):** 

Mr. Webster opened the public hearing at 6pm.  Mr. Jesse Drewer read the notice of public hearing for the record, followed by a reading of the Staff Report (and attached exhibits) for the record: 

_The Department of Technical and Community Services received an application from Bohler Engineering on November 26, 2024, on behalf of the applicant, Nexamp Inc. The applicant is seeking Utility Scale Solar Energy Facility (SEF) Floating Zone Approval for property located at 10960 Kemps Nursery Road. Following the procedure set forth in the Somerset County Zoning Code section 7.7, an application is submitted to the Board of County Commissioners who, following a vote, refer the application to the Planning Commission. The request was initially heard by the County Commissioners on December 3rd where it was denied. Following discussions over the next month with staff, the matter was brought back up to the County Commissioners for a referral recommendation on January 28, 2025, where it was forwarded to the Planning Commission for a public hearing and discussion._ 

_The Somerset County Zoning ordinance requires all solar energy facilities generating more than two (2) megawatts in electricity go through the SEF Floating Zone process. Pending a recommendation by the Planning Commission, a public hearing will be held by the County Commissioners on the request. If the floating zone is granted, the applicant would then be permitted to submit for the standard site plan review process by staff and the Planning Commission._ 

_The applicant proposes a solar energy facility that generates 5 megawatts of electricity. It is proposed to be 3 independent systems with 1 1MW and 2 2MW facilities. The proposed solar energy facility will be located on approximately 30 of the 136 acres that comprise the_ 

_site. Located approximately 0.4 miles down Kemps Nursery Road, the property and_ 

_surrounding area are primarily agricultural in nature. The property is zoned as Agricultural_ 

_Residential and complies with the conditions outlined in section 7.5c of the zoning code [Staff Exhibit #1] which states:_ 

- _I. Utility scale solar energy facilities will only be considered on parcels designated as ‘Agricultural Residential’ (AR) or ‘General Industrial’ (I-2)._ 

- _II. Siting of utility scale solar energy facilities will not be considered for placement within 1,500 feet of the right-of-way for U.S. Route 13, Ocean Highway or M.D. Route 413, Crisfield Highway._

- _III. Utility scale solar energy facilities may not be considered for parcels consisting of 50% or more prime agricultural soils as determined by the Natural Resources Conservation Service Soil Service Soil Survey as Class I, II, or III, and as further advised by the Somerset County Soil Conservation District._ 

- _IV. Development Cap. No more than 1,800 acres of utility scale solar will be considered within the County as defined by the limits of development for each utility scale solar energy facility developed or proposed._ 

_It should be noted that the above condition III, after consulting with legal counsel, is not explicitly met, as the site contains greater than 50% prime agricultural soils. Per the Somerset County Soil Conservation District [Staff Exhibit #2], the site is fully comprised of Class I-III Soils._ 

_Section 7.5(A), which is the statement of intent for the SEF states, “it is intended that utility scale solar energy facilities are not placed on prime agricultural lands, are aesthetically attractive, are placed so as to protect the commercial viability of the U.S. Route 13 and M.D. Route 413 corridors, and are compatible with the surrounding neighborhood.” The Commission must determine whether, in light of the prime soils analysis, a recommendation for an SEF is appropriate based on the conceptual site plan. [Applicant Exhibit #2]._ 

_It is recommended that the Planning Commission consider approval of the proposed Utility Scale Solar Energy Facility (SEF) Floating Zone, with the following conditions:_ 

- _All conditions imposed by the Planning Commission be met._ 

- _If the Floating Zone is approved by the County Commissioners, the full site plan is to be reviewed by the Technical Advisory Committee and Planning Commission prior to approval._ 

On behalf of the applicant, attorney Sharon Van Emburgh was present to speak.  Also present for Nexamp was Mr. Erik Alves de sa, the development manager for the project.  Ms. Van Emburgh outlined Nexamp’s desire to be a good neighbor, and to go through the County process, rather than the State process which would cut out the County from conversations.  Ms. Van Emburgh further discussed the SEF and the ideal location of the project on Kemp Nursery Road.  Ms. Van Emburgh invited the property owner Ms. Kathleen Keller up to speak.  Ms. Keller shared her story and desire to place solar on a portion of her property.  The remaining acreage of her property, outside of the area where her dwelling stands, will continue to be leased for farming. 

Mr. Alves de sa presented a PowerPoint to the Planning Commission to overview the Nexamp mission and project specifics. 

- Size, capacity, construction, decommissioning, appearance 

- Project location 

- Landscape proposal 

- Utility-Scale vs. Community-Scale Solar

Mr. Alves de sa mentioned that it is an area of study without much support, but the lack of typical agricultural tilling would reduce soil impacts from standard crop rotation.  Mr. Mason asked if the maintenance of the

## **5) Discussion and Recommendations for REZ-25-191:** 

Mrs. McIntyre made a motion to make a favorable recommendation for the application of a SEF along to the County Commissioners - motion was seconded by Mr. Colborn.  Opposed: Mr. Mason, Mrs. Stewart, Mr. Brice.  Motion failed. 

Planning Commission members further discussed the soil classification issue, as well as the need to forward a recommendation to the County Commissioners.  Mr. Illuminati stated that the process just requires a recommendation be made to the County Commissioners, whether that be for or against the granting of the SEF.  Mr. Illuminati also outlined the potential other process, had Nexamp decided to go through the CPCN process, rather than through the County process, through which local regulations would have been bypassed. 

Mr. Brice made a motion to table the decisions for a recommendation to a later meeting of the Planning Commission to allow for further information to be prepared and submitted by the applicant – motion seconded by Mrs. Stewart.  For: Mr. Colborn, Mr. Mason.  Opposed: Mrs. McIntyre.  Motion passed.
