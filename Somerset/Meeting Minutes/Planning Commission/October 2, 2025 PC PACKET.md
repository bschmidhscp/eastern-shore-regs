### **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _September 4, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on September 4, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

###### **<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** 

Jeremy Mason Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre Catherine Skeeter, Planner Skip Colborn Laverne Stewart Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Steve Raab Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Vice Chairman Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

**A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** August 7, 2025 meeting 

On a motion by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, seconded by Mr. Steve Raab, the August 7, 2025 minutes were approved. 

###### **B. NEW BUSINESS** 

**I. Site Plan Approval:** SP25-258 Holly Grove Mennonite Church, Inc. 7313 Mennonite Church Road, Westover, Maryland 21871 – Map 50, Grid 03, Parcel 138 

Mr. Jesse Drewer read the staff report for the record. 

_“The Department of Technical and Community Services received a Site Plan submittal from Mr. Nathan Noble of Wilkins-Noble, LLC on April 11, 2025, on behalf of Holly Grove Mennonite Church, Inc. The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for the creation of a new Gymnasium/Storage building on the already developed school site. The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 8.15b(3). The Site Plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on July 7, 2025.  A revised Site Plan was submitted on July 31, 2025 for Planning Commission consideration._ 

_The proposed Gymnasium/Storage Building will be located behind the existing Holly Grove Christian School, at 7313 Mennonite Church Rd.  The property is already improved with a school building, adequate parking, ball fields, and a number of sheds/outbuildings.”_

### **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _September 4, 2025_ 

Mr. Bruce Bowden was present as the representative for Holly Grove, with Mr. AJ King present via phone.  Mr. Bowden provided a brief detailing of the proposed building, citing the need for the facility to serve the growing school. 

Mr. Skip Colborn asked about the status of the Stormwater Management review – Mr. John Redden’s TAC comments were of concern.  Mr. King shared that the existing Stormwater Management pond’s capacity will be increased to accommodate the new need. Mr. Redden was present virtually and able to chime in about the landscape buffer comment, stating that it was just a standard comment and that Planning Commission’s view that no additional landscaping be needed is fine.  Mr. King stated that no landscaping trees would be feasible between the ball fields and the new building, as it would result in a visibility challenge for safety purposes. 

**On a motion by Mr. Steve Raab, seconded by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, the Planning Commission moved to approve SP25-258 for Holly Grove Mennonite Church, Inc, with the following discussed conditions:** 

1. All required revisions to the plat be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures 

2. Forest Conservation Plan Approval must be obtained. 

3. Stormwater Management and Sediment and Erosion Control Approval shall be obtained. 

4. As requested by Staff and Planning Commission, a note should be added to the Site Plan regarding the parking lease/agreement in place for Holly Grove’s utilization of the lands of John Murphy for parking. 

**II. Site Plan Approval Revision:** SP24-248 Waterway Industrial Park (Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County), Revells Neck Road – Map 32, Grid 04, Parcel 149, Lot B 

Mr. Drewer summarized a memorandum prepared for a request to eliminate the western property line’s landscaping requirement previously approved for the County Industrial Park Site Plan/Subdivision Plat.  Also, Mr. Drewer made the board aware of the official name of the site: Waterway Industrial Park. 

Mr. Kevin Aydelotte with Parker and Associates (who have prepared the plat/site plan) was present to speak on the Industrial Park project as a whole, and describe the changes to the proposal that need Planning Commission approval. Mr. Aydelotte stated that the western property boundary buffer has been eliminated, since it is adjacent to the County-owned borrow pit, and that the Revells Neck Rd property line landscaping will consist of crepe myrtles and knock out roses. 

Mr. Colborn brought up the reduction of the front landscaping from 15’ to 10’ and the lack of adequate plant materials, specifically the reliance on crepe myrtles and knock out roses.  Mr.

### **<u>STAFF REPORT</u>** 

**Meeting of October 2, 2025** 

**Case No.: SP 25-260 Applicant: Trilogy Group (Sara Matthews) Property Owner: John E & Annette Humphreys (Jed’s Auto) Location: 4630 Crisfield Highway, Crisfield, Maryland 21817 Tax Map: Map 64 / Grid 12 / Parcel 51 / Lot 2 Zoning: General Commercial District (C-2) Property Size: 4.002 Acres (Stormwater Pond adjacent lot is 5.32 Acres) Request: Site Plan Approval – Cannabis Growing Facility** 

###### **A.** **<u>BACKGROUND:</u>** 

The Department of Technical and Community Services received a Site Plan submittal from Mrs. Sara Matthews of Trilogy Group, LLC on September 2, 2025. The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for the change of use and renovation of the existing auto repair building, on the already developed commercial site. The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 8.15b(3). A preliminary Site Plan and narrative were reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on September 8, 2025.  A revised Site Plan was submitted on September 22, 2025 for Planning Commission consideration (a further revised Site Plan is in the works, and should be ready by the October 2<sup>nd</sup> meeting date). 

###### **B.** **<u>PROJECT OVERVIEW:</u>** 

The proposed cannabis growing facility will be located behind and inside the existing ‘Jed’s Auto’ facility, which will be renovated from its current use, at 4630 Crisfield Highway.  The property is already improved with a combined 17,400 sq. ft. warehouse and administrative building, existing commercial access to MD413, and landscaping. 

The Somerset County Board of Zoning Appeals met on Friday, September 19<sup>th</sup> to review and discuss a Special Exception Request for this project, which was unanimously approved. 

###### **C.** **<u>PROJECTS SPECIFICS:</u>** 

- a. Access: The existing property access drive is to be utilized.  A 50’ gravel driveway is currently established around the majority of the building. As required by the Maryland

###### **D.** **<u>DISCUSSION POINTS:</u>** 

- a. The stormwater management pond for this site is located on an adjoining parcel. Within one year of approval, the applicant should submit for a lot consolidation to ensure the stormwater management pond remains with the facility. 

   - I. Revised lot consolidation plat should also remove the 50’ service road which was removed by the Planning Commission in 2023 for the Johnston property on the corner of MD413 and Plantation Road. 

- b. Exterior lighting on the proposed building and property should be shown on the site plan. 

- c. The Site Plan is lacking basic property information, and some details are confusing.  If approval is granted, it should be conditioned that the Site Plan be brought into compliance with the basic standards for submittal. 

###### **E.** **<u>RECOMMENDATION/CONDITIONS:</u>** 

- a. It is recommended that the Planning Commission consider approval of the proposed Site Plan, with the following conditions: 

   - All required revisions to the plat be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures. 

   - Stormwater Management Maintenance and Inspection Agreement shall be submitted and approved by the County Engineer. 

   - Additional recommendations as applied by the Planning Commission, be addressed on the Site Plan.

## Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) Submission 

#### **<u>Project Information</u>** 

Property Address: 4630 Crisfield Highway, Marion Station, MD 21838 Applicant: Trilogy Group, LLC (in care of John & Annette Humphreys, Owners of Record) Project: Cannabis Cultivation Facility – Standard Grower License (Indoor & Greenhouse) Zoning: Commercial (C-2), Somerset County – Subject to Special Exception for Agricultural Production Uses. 

Occupancy Classification: F-1 (Factory/Industrial, Moderate Hazard) per International Building Code §306.2. 

#### **<u>Project Description</u>** 

The proposed project consists of retrofitting an existing 17,400 sf warehouse and 50×60 paint booth into a 5,000–6,000 sf indoor canopy cultivation facility. A future phased expansion will add modular greenhouses. This submission is limited to the initial indoor canopy capacity. 

The facility is classified under F-1 occupancy (factory/industrial, moderate hazard) per IBC §306.2, consistent with Somerset County Ordinance Sec. 22-138.2. F-1 standards require fire separations, compartmentalization, and designated egress routes, which are incorporated into the design. The site will be secured by perimeter fencing, access control, and 24-hour surveillance per MCA regulations. 

#### **<u>Zoning & Land Use Analysis</u>** 

- Current Zoning: Commercial (C-2) 

- Permitted Uses: Warehousing, light industrial, service uses 

- Special Exception Required: Agricultural/cannabis cultivation must be approved by Board of Appeals under Somerset Zoning Ordinance Article VIII, Sec. 8-102 

- Occupancy Compliance: All proposed indoor and greenhouse uses will meet F-1 occupancy standards under IBC §306.2 (factory/industrial moderate hazard) 

- Compliance with Standards: 

- Setbacks (50 ft from property line) maintained 

- Parking provided (20+ stalls) 

- Driveway access consistent with SHA/County road requirements 

- Utility yard and stormwater management designed per County code

#### **<u>Environmental Considerations</u>** 

Storm water Management: Site plan incorporates graded drainage and detention per Somerset County Stormwater Ordinance (Chapter 106) 

- Flood Zone: Portion of lot in Zone A3/B; facility footprint is outside floodplain. Building elevation complies with FEMA and County Floodplain Ordinance 

- Odor Control: Facility equipped with carbon filtration and negative pressure rooms per MCA Odor Mitigation Guidance 

- Waste Disposal: Plant waste rendered unusable per COMAR 10.62.30.05 (grinding + mixing with >50% inert material) 

- Nutrient Management: Fertigation system with spill containment, compliance with Maryland Dept. of Environment (MDE) nutrient regulations 

#### **<u>MCA Compliance – Standard Grower</u>** 

- License Type: Standard Grower, conditional approval attached 

- Canopy: Initial 5,000–6,000 sf indoor canopy 

- Security Plan: 

- 24-hour video surveillance with 90-day storage (§ COMAR 10.62.09) 

- Limited access areas with keycard system 

- Security vault for currency and product storage 

- Operational Standards: 

- Seed-to-sale tracking system (METRC) 

- Monthly reporting of cultivation, waste, and sales to MCA
