# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** November 16, 2023 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission meeting held on November 16, 2023, at 6:00 P.M. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

## **<u>Planning Commissioners Present:</u>** 

Jeremy Mason (Chair) Matt Webster (Vice Chair) Janet McIntyre Steve Raab Laverne Stewart Nelson Brice 

**<u>Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** 

Jesse Drewer, Director Catherine Skeeter, Planner 

**<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Erica Witz, Esq. 

Mr. Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

October 12, 2023 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mr. Steve Raab and seconded by Mrs. Laverne Stewart. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. PUBLIC HEARING: Somerset County Zoning Code Text Amendments:** Proposed Amendments to Section 5.7(e) Industrial Uses to include cannabis as an Industrial Use and Section 17 Definitions for references to cannabis 

Mr. Jesse Drewer read into record the Notice of Public Hearing which ran in the County Times. Mr. Drewer presented a brief staff summary of proposed changes, which had previously been presented and discussed among the Planning Commission during the October 12<sup>th</sup> meeting. 

Mr. Jeremy Mason asked public attendees if they would like to speak.  No public comment was received. 

Mr. Raab made a motion, seconded by Mr. Matt Webster, to approve the proposed Text Amendments to the Zoning Code.  Mrs. Stewart made a motion, seconded by Mr. Nelson Brice, to forward a favorable recommendation to the County Commissioners.

<!-- page 2 -->
## **C. OTHER BUSINESS** 

Mr. Drewer informally brought up the potential for an amendment to either the zoning or nuisance code relating to domestic fowl.  However, official discussion on this subject is dependent on an upcoming discussion among the County Commissioners on how they would like this addressed, if the zoning code is amended it will be back before the Planning Commission, however if it is a nuisance code amendment, that is done strictly with the County Commissioners. Concerns about the potential issues enforcing such a regulation were raised by Planning Commission members, as well as multiple public attendees. 

Mr. Drewer informed the Planning Commission of the proposed agenda for the scheduled December 7<sup>th</sup> meeting, in which Smith Planning and Design will be present to give an update on the Comprehensive Plan progress. 

## **<u>ADJOURNMENT:</u>** 

**On a motion by Mrs. Stewart, and seconded by Mr. Raab, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the November 16, 2023 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 6:09 P.M. 

Respectfully Submitted, Catherine Skeeter, Planner
