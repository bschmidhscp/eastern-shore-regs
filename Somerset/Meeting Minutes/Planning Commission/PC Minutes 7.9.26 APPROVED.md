# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _July 9, 2026_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on July 9, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Jeremy Mason (Vice Chairman) Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Administrator Eugene ‘Skip’ Colborn Catherine Skeeter, Senior Planner Steve Raab Laverne Stewart **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Vice Chairman Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 5:00 p.m. 

## **1) APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** June 4, 2026 Meeting 

On a motion by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, seconded by Mr. Skip Colborn, the June 4, 2026 minutes were approved. 

## **2) NEW BUSINESS** 

**A. Public Hearing:** Proposed Amendments to the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance 

Mr. Jesse Drewer read the Public Notice, for the record. 

Mr. William Cornish gave a summary of the proposed text amendments pertaining to: _split zoned parcels, ADUS, retreats, food trucks, personal service business, height regulations in the floodplain, accessory structures, and definitions._ 

Mr. Drewer read a comment letter received by Ms. Chris Widdowson, in which she expressed concern for lacking language in the code regarding solar setbacks from swales and ditches, as well as visual buffer planting widths.

**B. Site Plan Review:** SP-26-268 Southern Connection Seafood, Inc. 4884 Crisfield Highway (Tax Map 65, Grid 01, Parcel 403) 

Mr. Drewer read the Staff Report for the record: 

_“The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for the addition of two new 40’x120’ stand alone storage structures, to support the already-existing business operations of Southern Connection Seafood._ 

_The proposed two storage buildings will be located adjacent to the existing cold storage building, and be utilized for dry storage. To note: the existing cold storage building did not come before the Planning Commission prior to its construction, though this should have been a requirement due to its nature of expanding a commercial operation. Concrete was already poured for the storage building nearest the cold storage building, but the process was halted by this office to ensure proper approvals were secured prior to the continuation of construction. Also, of note: prior to coming through for Planning Commission review, the applicant was required to go through a lot consolidation to eliminate lot lines that previously cut through the site. The Lot Reconfiguration Plat was recorded in the land records on May 22nd._ 

_Several unpermitted structures/additions have taken place over the years on this site. Planning and Zoning has required the applicant to come before Planning Commission now in an effort to remedy these outstanding issues as well as approve the new proposed structures, with any recommendations from the Planning Commission._ 

_It is recommended that the Planning Commission consider approval of the proposed Site Plan, with the following conditions:_ 

- _All required revisions to the plan be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures_ 

- _Stormwater Management and Sediment and Erosion Control Approval shall be obtained._ 

- _Forest Conservation requirements shall be addressed._ 

- _Additional recommendations as applied by the Planning Commission, be addressed on the Site Plan.”_

Mr. James Patrick Reese, Jr. approached as the representative of the project.  Mr. Reese shared further the nature of the request, siting frustration with the process.

**C** . **Site Plan Review:** SP-26-269 2Jims Billiards 4840 Crisfield Highway (Tax Map 65, Grid 07, Parcel 405) 

Ms. Catherine Skeeter read the staff report for the record: 

_“The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for a new proposed Billiards Hall within an existing commercial building, which has been vacant for years._ 

_The site plan does not propose any additional structures on the property, but rather quantifies the existing structures, proposed parking, and the placement of other site features. Planning and Zoning notified the property owner of the requirement to come before the Planning Commission prior to opening the business, as the owner was unaware of the need to do so for a new use in an existing structure. The Health Department was also notified of the proposal and has a process that must be followed (regarding well and septic capacity/functionality) prior to the business opening._ 

_As the structure is existing, should leniency to code standards, as typically applied to new development, be granted?_ 

_It is recommended that the Planning Commission consider approval of the proposed Site Plan, with the following conditions:_ 

- _All required revisions to the plan be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures_ 

- _Additional recommendations as applied by the Planning Commission, be addressed on the Site Plan.”_

Owner, Mr. Scott Tawes, and business operator, Mr. Jim Davis, were both present and approached to discuss the proposal. 

Mr. Tawes shared that the building will have no exterior alterations, with the footprint not changing, and that the interior has been updated. 

Mr. Colborn asked about ADA accommodations and exterior lighting, with Mr. Tawes sharing that ADA entrances were recently updated to meet standards, and Mr. Davis stating that front exterior lights are already present.

Mrs. Stewart stated that the property looks very nice, and aside from the parking not being physically delineated on the ground, she saw no issue with the proposal.

**D. Site Plan Approval Revision:** SP-24-248 Waterway Industrial Park (Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County) Revells Neck Road (Tax Map 32, Grid 04, Parcel 149, Lot 

Mr. Drewer summarized the reasoning for this coming back before the Planning Commission, siting edits related to the number of lots, location of the Forest Conservation Area, SWM updates, lot line adjustments, and the proposed utilization of an existing ROW. 

Mr. Colborn expressed concern for the number of new intersections the Industrial Park proposes onto Revells Neck, as well as the generally poor design of the road and lot layout.

Mr. Drewer shared that the Planning Commission should expect to revisit the Zoning Code Text Amendments at next month’s August 6<sup>th</sup> meeting, as well as anticipate a new Site Plan for a proposed restaurant.
