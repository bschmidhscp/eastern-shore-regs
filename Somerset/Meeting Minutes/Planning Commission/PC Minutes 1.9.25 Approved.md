# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _January 9, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on January 9, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

## **<u>Planning Commissioners Present:</u>** 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Matt Webster Jesse Drewer, Director Jeremy Mason Will Cornish, Assistant Director and Zoning Admin. Skip Colborn Catherine Skeeter, Planner Steve Raab Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Erica Witz, Esq. 

Mr. Matt Webster opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A.** **<u>APPROVAL OF MINUTES:</u>** 

November 7, 2024 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mr. Steve Raab and seconded by Mr. Jeremy Mason. 

## **B.** **<u>NEW BUSINESS:</u>** 

**a. Proposed Zoning Code Text Amendment: Cannabis** – Section 5.7(d) Commercial Uses, 5.7(e) Industrial Uses, Section 5.7 Supplemental Use Regulations, and Section 17 Definitions. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer read the legal memo prepared by legal counsel, Mr. Andrew Illuminati, Esq., for the record. 

Discussion commenced among Planning Commission members.  Mr. Skip Colborn expressed his desire for a compromise, stating that many within the County are not in favor of the change.  Mr. Colborn brought up the issue of liquor vs. beer and wine, referencing the licensing process for their sale.  Mr. Colborn questioned whether the County could support a cannabis dispensary, as well as mentioned the stigma associated with locals patronizing such a business, citing the likelihood that a potential cannabis dispensary would more-so serve populations from outside of the County. Traffic/parking concerns were raised by Mr. Colborn, as well.   Mr. Colborn stated his belief that a cannabis dispensary would not be appropriate in the C-1 commercial district, as well as within MRC. 

Mr. Steve Raab asked for a renewed synopsis of the Staff’s recommendations.  Mr. Drewer provided the following summary of the proposed amendment to the table of permitted uses and supplemental use regulations:

<!-- page 2 -->
_Growing permitted by special exception in C-1, C-2, OC, I-1, and I-2 zones; processing would be by special exception in the C-1, C-2, OC, I-1, and I-2 zones; and selling would be permitted by special exception in AR and MRC, permitted in C-1 and C-2, and not permitted in the I-1 and I-2 zones; on-site consumption would not be permitted in any zoning district._ 

_A cannabis business may not locate within: 500 feet from a pre-existing primary or secondary school; 500 feet from a pre-existing childcare center, registered family childcare home, playground, recreation center, library, place of assembly, or public park; 2,640 feet from another pre-existing cannabis business or 100 feet from a pre-existing residence._ 

On a motion by Mr. Raab, seconded by Mr. Brice, the Planning Commission voted to approve the proposed zoning code text amendment to modify, as staff and legal counsel recommended, the table of permitted uses and supplemental use regulations for cannabis businesses. 

## **C.** **<u>OTHER BUISINESS:</u>** 

**a. Comprehensive Plan Update** – Smith Planning and Design (Land Use and Water Resources draft chapters 

Virginia Smith and Michele King of Smith Planning and Design were present (virtually via Teams) to provide the Planning Commission and public a status update on the progress of the new Comprehensive Plan.  A PowerPoint slide deck was displayed for review.  Discussion points: 

- Chapter 9 “Water Resources” 

   - Should be consistent with the County’s Water and Sewer Plan which is also undergoing an update, making it challenging to accomplish consistency. 

   - Service areas established during Technical Committee workshop 

- Chapter 10 “Land Use” 

   - Land use data sources and procedures for utilization 

   - MDP’s updated data 

   - Draft land use maps for the plan 

   - Growth Areas 

- Next Steps 

   - Incorporate or generate future water & sewer demand projections for Water Resources. 

   - Technical & Community Service staff review of Growth Areas and PFA Amendments. 

   - Finalize Future Land Use Map. 

   - Chapter 10 Land Use draft submittal for review and comment. 

   - New Chapter – Priority Preservation Areas 

   - Cohesive Draft Comprehensive Plan
