# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _December 7, 2023_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission meeting held on December 7, 2023, at 6:00 P.M. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Jeremy Mason (Chair) Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre Mary Phillips, Assistant Director/Zoning Administrator Skip Colborn Catherine Skeeter, Planner Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Mr. Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

November 16, 2023 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mrs. Janet McIntyre and seconded by Mr. Nelson Brice. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Presentation and Update of the Comprehensive Plan Update:** Smith Planning and Design will present and discuss the progress of the update. 

Virginia Smith and Michelle King of Smith Planning and Design were present to discuss the status of the ongoing Comprehensive Plan Update.  SP&D ran through a PowerPoint presentation outlining the timeline status, Technical Committee (TC) meeting, project website, and public survey result status. Particular attention was paid to the topic of the morning’s TC meeting: Affordable Housing.  They mentioned that the ‘working draft’ chapters for 2-4 were on the website and the next steps. 

Following the PowerPoint presentation, questions from the planning commissioners included aging in place for homeowners, programs offered by the County for low income homeowners, and the importance of workforce development for the county.

<!-- page 2 -->
Commissioner McIntyre asked about school seniors completing the survey, which Ms. Smith replied would be considered as an option after the holidays, along with a stronger emphasis on social media for survey completion, by the County and partners of the Comprehensive Plan update. SP&D encouraged Planning Commission members to review further draft chapters as they become available in the coming weeks.  Their next visit to the Planning Commission will be February 1<sup>st</sup> , following another Technical Committee meeting that morning. 

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS** 

Mr. Drewer mentioned the 2024 Meeting Calendar was in the members packet, the meetings will continue to be on the first Thursday of every month, with one exception for July 11, 2024, due to the 4<sup>th</sup> of July holiday. 

Mrs. Phillips mentioned that there will be a January 4<sup>th</sup> meeting, in which a change of condition proposal for the Manokin Village condominium development will be presented. 

## **<u>ADJOURNMENT:</u>** 

**On a motion by Mr. Brice, and seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the November 16, 2023 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 7:10 PM. 

Respectfully Submitted, Catherine Skeeter, Planner
