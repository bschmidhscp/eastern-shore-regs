**NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION AMENDMENTS TO THE SOMERSET COUNTY ZONING ORDINANCE** Tuesday, May 27, 2025 at 6PM County Commissioners’ Meeting Room, Room 111 11916 Somerset Avenue Princess Anne, Maryland 

In accordance with Section 15 of the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance, Somerset County is considering amending sections of the Zoning Ordinance that pertain to Solar Energy Generating Systems, Energy Storage Devices, and Special Events.  The relevant sections of the Ordinance are: Section 5.7 Table of Uses, Section 5.8 Supplemental Use Regulations, Section 7 Overlay and Floating Districts; Public Hearing Requirements in Sections 10, 11 & 15 and Section 17 Definitions. 

The Planning Commission will hold this public hearing prior to making a recommendation to the Board of County Commissioners for its consideration. The County Commissioners will hold a public hearing prior to making a final decision. 

**<u>Fair Summary</u>** 

The proposed changes include: 

- Amending section 5.7(e), Industrial Uses Uses to remove all current solar energy regulations and replace with new solar energy generating station and energy storage device language 

- Adding Special Events to Section 5.7(f) miscellaneous uses to concur with the Critical Area Ordinance. •  Revising and adding use regulations for solar energy generating stations and energy storage devices to Section 5.8 Supplemental Use regulations 

- Removal of Section 7.5 Utility Scale Solar Energy Floating Zone (SEF). 

- Revising language in the Public Hearing Requirements in Sections 10, 11, & 15. 

- Adding new definitions to Section 17, Definitions. 

A detailed listing of the proposed revisions may be obtained by calling the Somerset County Department of Technical and Community Services at 410-651-1424, or by visiting the department website at https:// www.somersetmd.us/departments/departments_-_n_-_z/planning_and_zoning/planning_commission.php

- **5.2** **<u>Purposes of Each Zoning District</u>** <u>. Each district is intended to serve the following major</u> purposes, in addition to serving the overall purposes and objectives of this Ordinance. 

   - a. <u>Base Zoning Districts.</u> 

      - (1) <u>Agricultural Residential District (AR)</u> - To provide for a full range of agricultural activities and to protect agricultural land from encroachment by incompatible land uses, thereby encouraging a stable agricultural economy. The agricultural district also permits low density residential and community service uses. This district also provides a land reserve for future development. 

      - (2) <u>Conservation District (CO) - To identify and conserve the natural resources and</u> concentrations of wetlands so that they may remain a haven for wildlife and conservation. Within such areas many important natural processes are performed. To permit very low intensity uses in portions of this District that may prove suitable. This will help to facilitate the efficient and economical provision of governmental services at selected development areas elsewhere in the County, thus creating reasonable service areas where practical.

- g. <u>Mobile, Manufactured, and Other Homes.</u> 

   - (1) <u>Definition of Mobile/Other Home. A dwelling unit manufactured in a factory</u> on a chassis that is intended to be towed to a site in one (1) or two (2) substantial pieces, and which is not designed to be supported by a conventional perimeter foundation. This definition shall apply regardless of whether a dwelling is still mobile. 

   - (2) <u>Purposes. These Mobile/Manufactured Home provisions are primarily intended</u> to: 

      - i. Recognize that these homes offer affordable housing for County residents **;** 

      - ii. Make sure that these homes are compatible with nearby site built housing and the character of the zoning district **;** 

      - iii. Recognize that newer higher quality double-wide units can be constructed in a manner that is closely similar in appearance to site built homes **;** 

      - iv. Require special approval in certain cases to make sure that the housing is compatible, and e) restrict certain types of these homes to higher density districts or Mobile/Manufactured Home Parks. 

   - (3) Minimum Construction Standards. New or replacement manufactured or mobile homes intended for human habitation not certified at the time of its construction as meeting the HUD Manufactured Housing Standards are not permitted in any location within Somerset County. 

   - (4) <u>Minimum Floor Area. Any newly placed Mobile/Manufactured Home shall</u> include a minimum total dwelling floor area of 720 square feet, not including garages, storage rooms and porches, unless a more restrictive floor area is established by another provision of this Ordinance. Such 720 square feet may be measured based upon the outside dimensions of the dwelling. 

   - (5) <u>Categories of Mobile</u> **<u>,</u>** <u>Manufactured and Other Homes. <u>Planned Unit Development (PUD).</u> An area of land developed as a single entity, in approved phases, which is totally planned to provide for a variety of residential, commercial, and related uses, as well as common areas or open space.
