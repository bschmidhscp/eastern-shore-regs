## **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _August 7, 2025_ 

_The proposed Somerset Primary Care will be located on Crisfield Hwy, very near the city limits of Crisfield. The lot is currently unimproved, having historically been used for the storage of boats, vehicles, and trailers.  The lot has preexisting impervious cover (gravel) over 37.6% of the lot.  The lot is within the Critical Area (Limited Development Area), which would typically only allow for 15% impervious area. Due to the historic use, and the proposed new use leading to the reduction of impervious to only 24.4%, the exceeding of the typical 15% has been deemed permissible (P&Z Staff discussed at length with Critical Area Commission Staff).  Concerns for lot coverage have been taken into consideration on the revised Site Plan by reducing the sprawl of the building and parking area.  Also, revised plans reflect efforts to preserve the existing wooded areas - no tree clearance is proposed.  Additional improvements to the existing conditions will include Storm Water Management on-site and landscaping.  Additionally, the parcel is ideally located near the City, and adjacent to the Rt. 413 Hiker/Biker Trail._ 

_Items for PC Discussion and Consideration:_ 

_1. Parking Aisles: Segments of parking spaces over 10 must be broken up by 6’ wide parking dividers, to be either paved for pedestrian connectivity, or perhaps landscaped.  The divider should be centralized within the aisle, and ideally provide connection to a sidewalk.  Dividers could be accommodated by losing 1 space from each aisle, leaving a total of 28 spaces, which is still greater than the minimum required 23._ 

_2. Landscaping: No new landscaping is proposed along the long edge of the parking aisle adjacent to Rt. 413.  Though this area mostly falls under tree canopy, there is an opportunity for improved curb appeal, and parking screening, if this space were to be landscaped further with shrubs or understory plants._ 

_3. Lighting should be addressed on the Site Plan._ 

_4. Due to its location directly adjacent to the Rt. 413 Hiker/Biker Trail, a bike rack is recommended to accommodate greater accessibility for individuals not travelling via vehicle._ 

_Staff recommends approval.”_ 

Mr. Webster asked the Planning Commission to discuss the parking divider issue.  Only 2 spaces would need to be eliminated.  Ms. Skeeter described the proposal to place a parking divider on each aisle.  Mr. Colborn chimed in to discuss the location and landscaping of the dividers.  Ms. Skeeter proposed that the divider closer to the building be not landscaped, but rather a sidewalk to allow for greater walkability and accessibility to the sidewalk from the parking area.  Mr. Raymond Strang, architect of the project, contributed to the discussion that it would be ideal to improve the access to the building by having the divider nearest the building not landscaped.  It was agreed upon that the divider nearest 413 be landscaped, and the one nearest the building not be landscaped to function as a walkway.  Dr. Howard Haft came up to gain a better understanding of this recommendation. 

Landscaping along the parking lot, under the pine trees adjacent to 413 was discussed. The Planning Commission did not see it necessary to add any supplemental landscaping to this area.

## **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _August 7, 2025_ 

The proposed lighting, not referenced on the Site Plan, was discussed.  Mr. Huey shared that at least 4 lights are proposed.  Mr. Drewer shared that standards in section 8.14 of the Zoning Code pertaining to lighting shall be met. 

Mr. Webster asked about Staff’s recommendation for a bike rack.  The applicant agreed that a bike rack can be provided. 

Mr. Colborn recommended that irrigation be provided to encourage survivability of the proposed plantings, though not explicitly required per code. 

Mr. Strang brought up the possibility that proposed landscaping areas be accomplished with raised planter beds.  Mr. Strang also showed the Planning Commission the concept on the interior of the Primary Care facility. 

Mrs. McIntyre asked about their timeline.  Applicants shared that expected building completion projection would be eight months after building permit acquisition. 

Dr. Haft further shared the plan of the office to serve the local community through long-term medical care with physician continuity. 

**On a motion by Mrs. Janet McIntyre, seconded my Mrs. Laverne Stewart, the Planning Commission moved to approve SP25-255 for Somerset Primary Care, with the following discussed conditions:** 

1. All required revisions to the plat be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures. 

2. Prior to the issuance of a Zoning Certificate, Stormwater Management and Sediment and Erosion Control approval shall be obtained. 

3. A landscaped parking divider shall be installed in the westerly parking row (adjacent to MD 413) to ensure there are no more than 10 consecutive parking spaces. 

4. A parking divider shall be installed in the easterly parking row (adjacent to the building) to ensure there are no more than 10 consecutive parking spaces. 

5. Lighting should be addressed on the site plan. 

6. Due to the close proximity to the Route 413 Hiker/Biker Trail, a bike rack is to be installed for individuals not travelling via vehicle. 

7. Prior to the issuance of a Zoning Certificate, Somerset County Sanitary Commission shall provide approval for water and sewer. 

8. That all local, State, and Federal regulations are adhered to.

## **STAFF REPORT** 

**Meeting of September 4, 2025 Case No.: SP 25-258 Applicant: Wilkins-Noble (c/o Nathan Noble) Property Owner: Holly Grove Mennonite Church, Inc. Location: 7313 Mennonite Church Rd, Westover, MD 21871 Tax Map: Map 50 / Grid 3 / Revised Parcel 138 Zoning: Agriculture Residential (AR) Property Size: 39.133 Acres (pending lot reconfiguration) Request: Site Plan Approval – Gymnasium/Storage Building** 

#### **A.** **<u>BACKGROUND:</u>** 

The Department of Technical and Community Services received a Site Plan submittal from Mr. Nathan Noble of Wilkins-Noble, LLC on April 11, 2025, on behalf of Holly Grove Mennonite Church, INC. The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for the creation of a new Gymnasium/Storage building on the already developed school site. The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 8.15b(3). The Site Plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on July 7, 2025.  A revised Site Plan was submitted on July 31, 2025 for Planning Commission consideration. 

#### **B.** **<u>PROJECT OVERVIEW:</u>** 

The proposed Gymnasium/Storage Building will be located behind the existing Holly Grove Christian School, at 7313 Mennonite Church Rd.  The property is already improved with a school building, adequate parking, ball fields, and a number of sheds/outbuildings. 

#### **C.** **<u>PROJECTS SPECIFICS:</u>** 

- a. Access: The existing property access drive is to be utilized.  A 24’ gravel driveway is proposed around a portion of the building. 

- b. Stormwater Management: Proposed SWM measures shown on the plan – must undergo full review and approval from County Engineer. 

- c. Forest Conservation: A Forest Conservation Plan was prepared and submitted on August 26<sup>th</sup> , with the FCA review underway and review fee outstanding.

TO: Somerset County Planning Commission Members FROM: Jesse Drewer, Director Ge DATE: August 26, 2025 

SUBJECT: **Site Plan Approval Revision** – Waterway Industrial Park Site Plan Landscaping 

<u>______________________________________________________________________________</u> 

At the February 6<sup>th</sup> Planning Commission Meeting, the original site plan and major subdivision for what was then the Somerset County Industrial Park, were reviewed and approved by the Planning Commission. At the March 6<sup>th</sup> Planning Commission meeting, due to some confusion about the landscaping buffer, the recommendation to amend buffer was made to reflect a 10ft western property ling and 15ft frontage landscaping buffer. 

Since this last came to the Planning Commission, the park name was decided to be Waterway Industrial Park, with the 3 roads to be named Kings Creek Drive, Back Creek Drive, and Jones Creek Drive. As the subdivision and site plan are nearing final completion, it has been requested to amend the landscaping buffer (as shown on the attached site plan) to reflect a 10ft front landscape buffer.
