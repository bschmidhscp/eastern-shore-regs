**a. Revised Plat:** SA24-556 Johnston’s, LLC., Plantation Road and Crisfield Highway (Tax Map 64, Grid 12, Parcel 164, Lot 1). 

Mr. Evan Young, of Hampshire, Hampshire & Andrews, and owner Alex Johnston were present for the case.  Mr. Drewer entered the Staff Report into the record, reading a summary of the case. 

_The Department of Technical and Community Services received a submittal for a site plan from Mr. Alex Johnston in December 2022 for the construction of a commercial building and associated parking. The site plan was scheduled to be reviewed by the Planning Commission in January 2023 however the plan was rescheduled for February 2023.  Discussion during the February meeting by Commission members resulted in the applicant withdrawing his plan subject to further revision._ 

_In March of 2023, The Maryland Department of Environment DNR contacted the applicant for unauthorized disturbance of regulated nontidal wetlands. The applicant has worked over the course of the last year to satisfy the Dept of Environment and has obtained a letter of authorization for impacts to Nontidal Wetlands buffer due to the construction of his building. A revised Site plan was submitted in April 2024 and a TAC meeting reviewed the submittal on May 8, 2024. The Site Plan is contingent on direct access to Plantation Rd. The Md Dept of Environment has issued a letter in support of the Plantation Rd access to avoid additional environmental impacts. The removal of denied access to Plantation Rd for Lot 1 per the original Subdivision Plat is a necessary modification to accommodate the proposed commercial structure and minimize environmental impact._ 

Mr. Webster asked for confirmation that the County, including the Roads Department, was for the Plantation Road access.  Per the TAC comment letter, no County objections were presented that would outright prohibit the approval of the Plantation access.  Mr. Webster expressed a concern for lack of sufficient comment from Mr. Woody Barnes, Director of County Roads, that would support the safety and legitimacy of the requested access on Plantation.  Mrs. McIntyre spoke up to support the fact that the new business will likely not contribute a significant amount of new traffic, and that the new access should not be issue. 

**On a motion by Mr. Colborn, seconded by Mrs. Stewart, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to approve the Preliminary Subdivision, with the condition that Mr. Barnes (County Road Department) does not object on the grounds of traffic impact.** 

**b. Revised Commercial Site Plan:** SP24-247 Johnston’s, LLC., Plantation Road and Crisfield Highway (Tax Map 64, Grid 12, Parcel 164, Lot 1) 

Mr. Evan Young, of Hampshire, Hampshire & Andrews, and owner Mr. Alex Johnston were present for the case.  Mr. Drewer entered the Staff Report into the record, and noted that since it is the same property as the previous case, much of the content overlaps. 

_The lot is located at the intersection of Plantation Rd and Crisfield Highway (MD 413). The proposed use of the site is a welding and fabrication shop, as noted on the Site Plan, with five (5) parking spaces for customers, a dumpster, and turn around space for deliveries._

Mr. Webster asked whether work would be taking place on the site beyond the confines of the proposed structure, siting concern for unattractive views of the property.  Mr. Johnston explained that the bulk of active work would need to take place inside, but that occasional storage of projects or some tasks would be possible outside.  Mr. Colborn questioned the 15’ landscape buffer on the west side and the swale along Crisfield Hwy.  Upon discussion with Mr. Young and Mr. Colborn, the idea of adjusting the swale locale or width was raised to allow for a hedgerow along the Plantation Rd frontage, in order to screen the view of the property from passersby. 

The public was given the opportunity to provide input on the case.  Mr. Charles Laird was present to speak on the matter as a concerned nearby resident.  Mr. Laird expressed concern with the magnitude of truck traffic, possible issues with trucks turning in and out of the lot, and the visual appeal of this type of business in an area near the highly-tourist-trafficked Janes Island State Park. The concern for screening of the business will require a resubmission to Staff for review of a revised Landscape Plan. 

**On a motion by Mrs. McIntyre, seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to approve the Site Plan, with the cited Staff Recommendations/Conditions, and the additional condition that Sanitary Commission approval be expressly obtained.** 

**c. Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation (MALPF) Applications and Ranking:** Review applicant list and ranking procedures. 

Ms. Catherine Skeeter provided a synopsis of the MALPF program, detailing updates to the annual submission schedule and ranking procedures, as well as provided a list and map of the FY25/26 applicants.
