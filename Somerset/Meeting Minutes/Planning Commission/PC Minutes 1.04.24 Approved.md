# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _January 4, 2024_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission meeting held on January 4, 2024, at 6:00 P.M. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Jeremy Mason (Chair) Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre Mary Phillips, Assistant Director/Zoning Administrator Matt Webster Catherine Skeeter, Planner Nelson Brice Skip Colborn **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Mr. Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 6:02 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

December 7, 2023 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mrs. Janet McIntyre and seconded by Mr. Skip Colborn. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Subdivision/Condominium Revision:** SA23-555 Lots 47 & 48 Manokin Village Condominium Regime Phase III (Tax map 23C, Grid 15, Parcel 175) 

Mr. Bret Davis was present as the sole representative for the submittal.  Mr. Davis provided an updated Site Plan, and proof of holding a Condominium Association meeting. 

Mrs. Mary Phillips gave a brief rundown of the request and history of the Manokin Village Condominium Regime, entering the Staff Report, and attachments, into the record. 

_The Department of Technical and Community Services received a submittal from Mr. Bret Davis requesting a building permit to construct a duplex unit on two vacant lots in Manokin Village Condominium Regime. The proposed duplex will consist of two 3-bedroom, 2 bath single family dwellings with attached garage and will share a common wall along the shared property line of Lot 47 and Lot 48. Applicant acquired 17 unimproved units within the development in December 2022._ 

_Manokin Village Condominium Regime was approved as a Mobile Home Park development by Special Exception by the Board of Zoning Appeals on July 1, 1988. The property was zoned Agriculture at the time of approvals. The Manokin Village Condominium Regime Phases I, II and III were approved by the Somerset County Planning and Zoning Commission on August 1, 1991 subject to the conditions imposed_

<!-- page 2 -->
_by the Board of Zoning Appeals. The location of future Phases IV, V, VI and VII were designated on the original plats. A Comprehensive rezoning occurred in Somerset County in 1992 and the property was rezoned to R3 - High Density Residential._ 

_Phase IV was approved on May 6, 1993 however a reconfiguration survey was approved in September 2013 which eliminated Phase IV and future phases V, VI and VII. There are currently 44 Lots total; 10 lots are improved with single family manufactured homes; 34 lots are currently vacant. The road system is private until such time as they are brought into compliance with county standards and accepted into the county maintenance system. Current approvals provide for one single family manufactured home per lot. A Critical Area Report dated June 1988, and prepared for the TAC review and Board of Zoning Appeals provided a typical lot detail with setbacks from all property lines._ 

Mrs. Phillips discussed the lack of explicit mention of the terms “duplex” or “zero lot line” in the County’s Zoning Code.  Also, the complication of the fact that the development is under a Condominium Regime, rather than a standard subdivision.  Mrs. Phillips stated, with support from Mr. Illuminati, that the Condominium Declaration should be amended to allow for additional building types beyond mobile homes.  Also, BZA findings from 1988, within which the development was described as solely for mobile home development, were discussed. 

Mr. Bret Davis spoke to express his intentions and desires with the proposed duplex construction and potential future further development on other lots.  Mr. Davis spoke on the fact that the Condominium Regime documents do not restrict stick-built houses, nor do they restrict duplexes. 

Discussion ensued among the Planning Commission members and Mr. Bret Davis.  Topics of discussion included: 

- Condominium Association notification of potential new building type allowance 

- Concerns for stylistic consistency within the development 

- Condominium Association forfeiture 

- Other phases of the development 

- Freedom to develop remaining lots in any residential style 

- Condominium Association common areas being privately owned 

As the debate died down, final action items were proposed, to be further reviewed for approval at the February 1<sup>st</sup> Planning Commission Meeting.  The 1988 BZA findings will be amended to allow for additional building types, beyond mobile homes.  Owners within the Manokin Village Condominium Regime must be notified of the February 1<sup>st</sup> meeting. 

**On a motion by Mrs. McIntyre, seconded by Mr. Brice, the Planning Commission moved to afford a continuance of the case, to be heard again during the February 1**<sup>**st**</sup> **Planning Commission meeting.** 

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS**
