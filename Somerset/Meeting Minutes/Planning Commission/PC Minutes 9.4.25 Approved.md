# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _September 4, 2025_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission Public Hearing and meeting held on September 4, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** 

Jeremy Mason Jesse Drewer, Director Janet McIntyre Catherine Skeeter, Planner Skip Colborn Laverne Stewart Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Steve Raab Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Vice Chairman Jeremy Mason opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** August 7, 2025 meeting 

On a motion by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, seconded by Mr. Steve Raab, the August 7, 2025 minutes were approved. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS** 

**I. Site Plan Approval:** SP25-258 Holly Grove Mennonite Church, Inc. 7313 Mennonite Church Road, Westover, Maryland 21871 – Map 50, Grid 03, Parcel 138 

Mr. Jesse Drewer read the staff report for the record. 

_“The Department of Technical and Community Services received a Site Plan submittal from Mr. Nathan Noble of Wilkins-Noble, LLC on April 11, 2025, on behalf of Holly Grove Mennonite Church, Inc. The applicant is seeking Site Plan approval for the creation of a new Gymnasium/Storage building on the already developed school site. The Zoning Code requires site plan approval for all new commercial development, per section 8.15b(3). The Site Plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on July 7, 2025.  A revised Site Plan was submitted on July 31, 2025 for Planning Commission consideration._ 

_The proposed Gymnasium/Storage Building will be located behind the existing Holly Grove Christian School, at 7313 Mennonite Church Rd.  The property is already improved with a school building, adequate parking, ball fields, and a number of sheds/outbuildings.”_

Mr. Bruce Bowden was present as the representative for Holly Grove, with Mr. AJ King present via phone.  Mr. Bowden provided a brief detailing of the proposed building, citing the need for the facility to serve the growing school. 

Mr. Skip Colborn asked about the status of the Stormwater Management review – Mr. John Redden’s TAC comments were of concern.  Mr. King shared that the existing Stormwater Management pond’s capacity will be increased to accommodate the new need. Mr. Redden was present virtually and able to chime in about the landscape buffer comment, stating that it was just a standard comment and that Planning Commission’s view that no additional landscaping be needed is fine.  Mr. King stated that no landscaping trees would be feasible between the ball fields and the new building, as it would result in a visibility challenge for safety purposes. 

**On a motion by Mr. Steve Raab, seconded by Mrs. Laverne Stewart, the Planning Commission moved to approve SP25-258 for Holly Grove Mennonite Church, Inc, with the following discussed conditions:** 

1. All required revisions to the plat be confirmed prior to final approval and signatures 

2. Forest Conservation Plan Approval must be obtained. 

3. Stormwater Management and Sediment and Erosion Control Approval shall be obtained. 

4. As requested by Staff and Planning Commission, a note should be added to the Site Plan regarding the parking lease/agreement in place for Holly Grove’s utilization of the lands of John Murphy for parking. 

**II. Site Plan Approval Revision:** SP24-248 Waterway Industrial Park (Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County), Revells Neck Road – Map 32, Grid 04, Parcel 149, Lot B 

Mr. Drewer summarized a memorandum prepared for a request to eliminate the western property line’s landscaping requirement previously approved for the County Industrial Park Site Plan/Subdivision Plat.  Also, Mr. Drewer made the board aware of the official name of the site: Waterway Industrial Park. 

Mr. Kevin Aydelotte with Parker and Associates (who have prepared the plat/site plan) was present to speak on the Industrial Park project as a whole, and describe the changes to the proposal that need Planning Commission approval. Mr. Aydelotte stated that the western property boundary buffer has been eliminated, since it is adjacent to the County-owned borrow pit, and that the Revells Neck Rd property line landscaping will consist of crepe myrtles and knock out roses.

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS** 

Mr. Drewer shared that the Comprehensive Plan will be before the County Commissioners for a Public Hearing on September 9<sup>th</sup> , with adoption expected at the September 23<sup>rd</sup> . 

## **D. ADJOURN**
