# **SOMERSET COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES** _October 3, 2024_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Planning Commission meeting held on October 3, 2024, at 6:00 p.m. in the meeting room of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Planning Commissioners Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Jeremy Mason (via phone) Jesse Drewer, Director Matt Webster Catherine Skeeter, Planner Skip Colborn Janet McIntyre Nelson Brice **<u>Planning Commission Legal Counsel Present:</u>** Andrew Illuminati, Esq. 

Mr. Matt Webster opened the meeting at 6:00 p.m. 

## **A. APPROVAL OF MINUTES:** 

July 11, 2024 Meeting Minutes: motion to approve by Mr. Skip Colborn and seconded by Mr. Nelson Brice. 

## **C. OTHER BUSINESS** (heard out of order) **:** 

a. Election of Chair and Vice-Chair 

Brief deliberation resulted in a consensus that Chairman Jeremy Mason and Vice-Chairman Matt Webster would switch roles.  Mr. Webster’s tenure as Chair will span 2 years, as will Mr. Mason’s as Vice-Chair. 

## **B. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**a. Commercial Site Plan:** SP24-245 Avatar Self Storage/Rajkumar Thangavelu, Bardwell Drive/Ocean Highway (Tax Map 23, Grid 18, Parcel 136, Lot 1B) 

Mr. Rajkumar Thangavelu was present to speak on the case. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer entered the Staff Report into the record, reading a summary of the request. 

_“The proposed Avatar Self Storage will be located on Bardwell Drive, at the end of Market Lane in Princess Anne. Market Lane runs parallel to Route 13, and the site of the self storage is very visible from Route 13. The lot has been reconfigured to eliminate the section of the property to the north of Bardwell Drive (created Lots 1A and 1B). The proposal is depicted as phases 1, 2, 3, and 4. The lot of concern is 17.4 acres, per recent subdivision, and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial).”_

<!-- page 2 -->
Mr. Colborn asked the applicant about the landscape stock to be utilized.  The applicant agreed with Mr. Colborn’s input that the planned use of deciduous and younger plants should be reconsidered to mix in larger evergreens. 

Additional clarifying questions were raised.  Mr. Thangavelu assured that there would be no office on the site, and phase 1 (3 buildings) would contain roughly 150-200 units. 80-90% capacity of phase 1 would trigger the progression of phase 2. 

Mr. Drewer mentioned the planned cell tower development site on the lot, which is planned for Planning Commission review at the November 7<sup>th</sup> meeting. 

**On a motion by Mr. Mason, seconded by Mrs. McIntyre, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to approve the Site Plan, with standard Staff Recommendations/Conditions.** 

**b. Commercial Site Plan:** SP24-249 Atlantic Tractor, 7321 Ocean Highway, Pocomoke City, MD 21851 (Tax Map 51, Grid 4, Revised Parcel 125A - per recent lot consolidation) 

Applicant Mr. Evan Young of George E. Young Engineers & Surveyors DBA Hampshire, Hampshire, & Andrews and builder Mr. Byler were present to discuss the case. 

Ms. Catherine Skeeter read a summary of the Staff Report for the record. 

_“The Department of Technical and Community Services received a submittal from Mr. Evan Young of George E. Young Engineers & Surveyors DBA Hampshire, Hampshire & Andrews on July 1, 2024, on behalf of Stevenson Family, LLC. The applicant is seeking site plan approval for the construction of a new maintenance building, behind the primary existing building on the site, to be utilized as part of the Atlantic Tractor business operation. The Site Plan was reviewed at a Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) meeting on July 8, 2024. A revised Site Plan was submitted on September 23, 2024 for Planning Commission consideration, and a lot reconfiguration was submitted on August 27, 2024 for Staff review._ 

_The lot, currently home to Atlantic Tractor and undergoing a lot reconfiguration to combine three lots, is located on US 13 Northbound, just west of Peach Orchard Rd, and with Ocean Highway Seafood & Productions on the western property line. The Site Plan proposes construction of a 6,000 sq ft maintenance building, following the demolition of a portion of an existing rear building.”_ 

Mr. Byler and Mr. Young detailed the plan, citing the need for a larger structure to contain tractors and combines.  The applicants clarified that the structure would not be a part of the public-facing store, but rather for employee use only.  Lighting will be provided on the new building’s exterior. 

Mr. Colborn encouraged the applicants to reconsider the planting stock, making account for the maximum size of the planned trees and for the use of evergreen.

**On a motion by Mr. Brice, seconded by Mrs. McIntyre, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to approve the Site Plan, with the cited Staff Recommendations/Conditions.** 

**c. HB805 Cannabis Zoning Update:** Discussion on “Must, May and Absolutely Now Cannot Do” with Board Attorney Andrew Illuminati 

Mr. Andrew Illuminati presented a PowerPoint for the Commission to outline the need for the zoning text amendment, to come into compliance with State regulations for cannabis. 

Meeting attendee Mr. Robert Hajir of Princess Anne was allotted time to speak on the matter.  Mr. Hajir expressed grave concern for the updating of the ordinance to come into compliance with the State’s regulations, stating that the Planning Commission has the option to defy the State without serious repercussion, for the sake of the County. 

Mrs. McIntyre solely expressed her contempt for the notion that the Planning Commission should take part in an effort to defy the State.  Planning Commission members agreed to move forward with hearing out public concern at the future Public Hearing. 

**d. Zoning Code Text Amendment:** Proposed Amendments to 5.7(d) Commercial Uses to address Telecommunication Towers and Section 17 Definitions for references to Telecommunication Towers 

Mr. Drewer outlined the reasoning behind the proposed amending of the zoning code further to expand the verbiage around wireless telecommunications facilities, which are not currently explicitly referenced. 

It was agreed that both zoning text amendments will move forward with a Public Hearing on November 7<sup>th</sup> followed by any other typical Planning Commission cases. 

## **<u>ADJOURNMENT:</u>** 

**On a motion by Mrs. McIntyre, and seconded by Mr. Colborn, the Planning Commission voted to adjourn the October 3, 2024 Planning Commission Meeting.** 

The Planning Commission adjourned at 7:28 p.m. 

Respectfully Submitted, 



Catherine Skeeter, Planner
