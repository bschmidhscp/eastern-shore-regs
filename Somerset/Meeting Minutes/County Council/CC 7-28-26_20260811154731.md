At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Public Hearing open to receive public comment on a proposed ordinance and resolution for Somerset County Wharfs and Docks. Mr. Leatherbury read the notice of public hearing into record as follows: 

#### PUBLIC HEARING 

An Ordinance to Appeal Ordinance No. 1066 and Resolution No. 1183 and Enact an Ordinance and Resolution to the use of County Wharfs and Docks and establish fines and 

procedures for issuance of such fines 

Notice is hereby given that the Board of County Commissioners will hold a Public Hearing on: 

Tuesday, July 28, 2026 at 2:00 P.M. In the County Commissioners Meeting Room Somerset County Office Complex, Room 111 11916 Somerset Avenue Princess Anne, Maryland 21853 

##### Fair Summary 

The purpose of the public hearing is to Repeal Ordinance No. 1066 and Resolution 1183 and Enact an Ordinance and Resolution regarding the use of Somerset County Wharves and Docks and establish fines for violation of the ordinance as outline the procedures for issuance of such fines. 

The meeting will be held in-person to receive public comment. Individuals wishing to make oral or written comments are encouraged to attend the hearing. Anyone wishing to testify will be allowed to speak in person. The proposed Ordinance and Resolution will be made available at the public hearing. The document can also be viewed following the public hearing, during regular office hours, weekdays 8:30 am. — 4:30 p.m., at the Office of the Somerset County Commissioners, Room 111, Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland.

Mr. Jesse Drewer, DTCS Director came forward. 

First, Mr. Drewer advised that Mr. Nelson Brice has expressed his interest to serve on the Agricultural Preservation Board, which has a vacancy. Being that Mr. Brice currently serves on the Planning Commission (representing District 5), Mr. Drewer consulted the County Attomey to confirm there would not be a conflict of interest to allow Mr. Brice to serve on both boards. Mr. Drewer advised that member Matt Webster serves on both boards as well. Attorney Simpkins advised that it would not be a conflict of interest. Mr. Drewer recommended Mr. Brice to fill the vacancy. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to appoint Nelson Brice to serve as a member on the Agriculture Preservation Board. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr. Drewer presented the a grant to update the FY27 Critical Area Ordinance for approval. He explained that the ordinance is overdue for its 10-year update, Notice was given last year that the update was forthcoming. Updating the plan can be a burden on smaller counties; and on July 22, 2026 the office was notified that Somerset County was awarded this $25,000 one-time grant to start the process over the next year. Mr. Drewer requested the Boards approval to accept the grant and allow staff to proceed with updating the ordinance. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to accept the Critical Area Ordinance Update grant in the amount of $25,000. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of hands, therefore the motion carried 5-0. President Charles Laird asked Mr. Drewer to remain at the meeting. 

Ms. Jennifer Trager, Catholic Charities/Seton Center came forward at this time to present the bi-annual update regarding Bethany House, formally known as the Homeless Shelter. Ms. Trager began with good news, sharing that they have already surpassed the number of families placed this year (8) as compared to last year (7). She advised there were 68 residents served last year, compared to 58 this year. Currently they are housing 17 residents, consisting of 2 families.
