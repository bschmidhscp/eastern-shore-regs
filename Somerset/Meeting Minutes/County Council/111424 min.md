Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of November 14, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, November 14, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, and Maxine Landon, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Ashley Stern, CPA, PKS & Associates, and Josh Taylor, P.E., Davis, Bowen & Friedel. The Crisfield (Peripheral Area) Board position is currently vacant. There were no members of the public. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

##### General Information 

Ashley Stern, CPA/Partner, PKS & Company, presented the Commission with the Fiscal Year 2024 Audit Report of the Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. Ms. Stern started the presentation by crediting the Sanitary Staff for their administrative and organizational skills, making the Audit a pleasurable experience. Ms. Stern reviewed the draft of the report with Mr. Stockus and Ms. Mister prior to this meeting. Ms. Stern stated that this year the District had two Audits, Independent Auditor's Report on Internal Control over Financial Reporting as well as an Independent Auditors’ Report on Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards. Both Audit Reports were in accordance with the auditing standards generally issued by the Comptroller General of the United States. The Auditors conducted the Audit consistent within the planned scope of timing. There were no instances of noncompliance material to the financial statements of the District, which would be required to be reported in accordance with Government Auditing Standards. Ms. Stern stated the audit report is presented with an unmodified opinion, which is the highest opinion available; she also stated the rarity of receiving such opinion. The Commission had no questions for Ms. Stern. A copy of the FY 2024 Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. Audit may be obtained on the Somerset County Website, www.somersetmd.us The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Board for review. The first quarter revenues exceeded expenses by $967,785.00, the surplus is partially due to reimbursements received after the end of Fiscal Year 2024 from the United States Department of Agriculture and the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) for the Smith Island Clean Water Project. The Board had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

Mr. Stockus presented a draft of the 2025 Debt Service Budgets. The Sanitary District Management is proposing no change in the sewer rate of $30.35 per quarter. The District is proposing the water rate of $21.65 per in Princess Anne and $20.25 in Fairmount and Crisfield, a decrease from current rate of $4.00/quarter ($16.00/year). This decrease in water rate was possible because two capital loans in Crisfield were paid off this year. The

#### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project since cancelling the Pilot Testing Agreement/Proposal with Fluence. Jason Loar, P.E., DBF, is meeting with Matthew Marshall, MDE, to discuss the best alternative listed in the preliminary engineering report for enhanced nutrient removal. 

Mr. Stockus reported the District has officially received notification from MDE regarding their FFY 2024 Intended Use Plan for the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF). The Princess Anne Color Removal Project ranked 5" on the priority scale, funding $5,090,200 of the requested $6,590,000 (leaving a shortage of $1.5 million). The funding package includes DWSRF base loan of $750,000, BIL (Bipartisan Infrastructure Law) general loan of $1,795,100, and principal forgiveness of $2,545,100. MDE will be sending the District requirements and first steps in the DWSRF process in the coming weeks. 

Mr. Padgette was not present but sent an update to the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating the countertops and floors are next to be installed, followed by plumbing. The Lab is projected to be complete by the end of March 2025. 

##### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP) stating the Subaqueous Force Main (SFM) project progress meeting was held on October 15'* 2024 on-site. After a month’s long hiatus, Crofton Construction is set to start plowing/drilling to install the SFM from Tylerton to Rhodes Point. Regarding the WWTP Upgrade project, a progress meeting was held on-site November 6" 2024 where construction of the Plant is 73% complete.

#### Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular session minutes dated October 10" 2024 were previously distributed. On a motion by Ms. Landon and seconded by Mr. Williams, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular session minutes dated October 10" 2024. 

### Approval of Commercial Loan Maturity Extension 

The District opened a Line of Credit with Hebron Savings Bank (HSB) in June 2023 for financing and refinancing the costs of capital projects. HSB has presented the District with a Commercial Loan Maturity Extension Agreement to outspread the term for the Line of Credit past the current maturity date. Ms. Somers made a motion to authorize the Chairman to execute the Commercial Loan Maturity Extension Agreement between the District and Hebron Savings Bank. Mr. Williams seconded the motion, the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

Princess Anne - Action 

# Approval of WWTP EQ pump rebuild 

Mr. Padgette submitted a request for approval to have the WWTP Equalization Tank rebuilding. Mr. Padgette explained the pump is considered essential equipment in the treatment system process. Mr. Padgette stated there was no time to advertise or solicit bids, Hill’s Industrial quoted the repair at $5,412.44, the cost to replace would have been $16,714.80. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the quote from Hill's Industrial for $5,412.44 for the EQ pump rebuild. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

### Request for Irrigation credit 

Ronald Boulden, 12484 Chestnut Circle, has requested an irrigation credit for reseeding his property during the September 2024 quarter. Mr. Boulden provided time-stamped pictures of his yard’s growth during the process. A meter audit was able to confirm the usage of those days and consumption decreasing back to normal usage. The Irrigation credit policy was established on May 9"" 2024 for situations such as lawn reseeding and sod installation. Management's recommendation was to credit the September 2024 quarter sewer based on his average consumption. Ms. Somers made a motion based on Management's recommendation to credit the sewer portion as stated. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

#### Smith Island — Action 

#### Authorization to list property 

Mr. Stockus approached the Board with the option of selling 3323 Marsh Road which sits the Rhodes Point Pump Station. The overgrown lot, needing a maintenance contract for landscaping services, was discussed during the October 10 2024 Commission meeting. Mr. Stockus stated during that meeting, the District will need to revise and reissue a RFQ for leveling the property, which may include barging equipment. Then the District would need to issue a RFQ for a maintenance (mowing) contract. Since the meeting Mr. Stockus inquired if it would be possible to sell the property with the caveat of maintaining a right-of-way easement to the Pump Station. The property, which currently assesses at $20,200, previously had a structure and has the ability to be developed. The Commission were in agreeance, this is a good option to eliminate the extra expense with maintaining the lot. Ms. Landon made a motion to list 3323 Marsh Road for sale with Wilson Reality contingent upon acceptance of utility easement to the District. Mr. Williams seconded the motion which carried unanimously to approval. 

#### Fairmount — Action 

## Request for EDU Allocation 

John and Barbara Myers are requesting an allocation for one residential EDU (Equivalent Dwelling Unit) of water and sewer for their property located at 27320 Fairmount Road. The Field Staff verified water and sewer mains are accessible at this property. Once this property is allocated an EDU the Office can proceed with the connection application process. Ms. Landon made a motion to allocate one EDU for water and sewer to 27320 Fairmount Road. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval. 

### Request for leak credit 

Richard Diggs, 7939 Halls Creek Road, is requesting a sewer credit for a leaking toilet and hose bib he repaired on 10/15/24. Mr. Diggs repaired his leaks immediately after receiving his September 2024 quarter bill which had tripled from the previous quarter. Mr. Diggs was able to provide the District with photos of his repair and his consumption has resumed to normal since repairing. Management is recommending crediting the sewer portion of Mr. Diggs September bill based on his average consumption. Ms. Somers made a motion to credit the sewer based on Management's recommendation. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.
