<mark>BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY</mark> 

<mark>October 10, 2023</mark> 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird, called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Darryl Webster and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report</u>** 

On Tuesday, October 10, 2023 from 2:21 p.m. until 4:02 p.m., President Charles Laird, upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, opened in Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.  Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., were also present. By show of hands the motion carried 5-0. 

Also present were County Administrator/Clerk Ralph D. Taylor, Executive Aide Lory Ebron, Ms. Tammy Zink, HR Director, and Attorney Robin Cockey (by phone). Topics discussed: (1) Probationary to Permanent Employee Status (2) Discuss EMS Division Coordinator hire (3) Interim HR Director (4) Rescind Motion to hire Housekeeper (5) Housekeeper Vacancy (6) Deputy County Administrator Vacancy (7) Finance Director Vacancy. Actions taken during closed session: (1) Approval to move probationary employees to permanent status (2) Approval to hire EMS Division Coordinator (3) Motion to rescind hiring EMS Division Coordinator (4) Approval for Interim Director (5) Approval to rescind motion to hire Housekeeper (6) Approval to hire Deputy County Administrator (7) Approval to hire Finance Director. 



President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance.

Next, Mr. Cavanaugh discussed an issue that has occurred with the Court House. He advised that rain water is leaking in, deteriorating the mortar joints, and causing damage, and must be repaired. Four companies were contacted for a proposal, but only one company, Laird Masonry responded, with a proposal of $29,500 to make the repairs. Judge Powell has advised that there will be no court cases held during the weeks of November 6-22, 2023, and repairs can be made at that time. Admittingly pricy, and also an unbudgeted expense, Mr. Cavanaugh recommends the work be performed before further damage occurs. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve the bid submitted from Laird Masonry for $29,500 to make needed repairs to the courthouse as presented. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. President Charles Laird abstained; therefore, the motion carried 4-0-1.

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Planning and Zoning Director came forward. First, Mr. Drewer presented the resolution for a 6-month moratorium regarding the growing, processing and/or selling of cannabis until further discussions can be held, and a public hearing can be scheduled to present the recommendations for zoning changes. Mr. Taylor read the summary of the resolution into record. The public hearing was held by the Commissioners on September 26, 2023 to receive public comment. No comments were received during the meeting, or the 10-day period following the hearing. Mr. Drewer noted that a preliminary discussion is planned for the next P & Z meeting scheduled for Thursday, October 12, 2023 to discuss proposed amendments to the Zoning Ordinance. Mr. Drewer advised that the Planning Commission will hold a public hearing regarding the proposed amendments, after which it will come to the Commissioners. It is felt that the process will be completed within 3 months; therefore, the 6-month moratorium should be a sufficient amount of time. Mr. Drewer then requested the Boards approval for Resolution #1191 a Temporary moratorium on the approval of businesses engaged in the growing, processing and/or selling of cannabis to include on-site consumption establishments. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve Resolution #1191 as presented. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr. Drewer presented the resolution drafted (#1192) to reorganize and reestablish the Housing Board or Review, to assist the Department of Technical and Community Services with the implementation of a Housing Rehabilitation Program throughout the county. Mr. Drewer explained the reason for the reorganization in that the previous board of seven members, consisting of Somerset County citizens only, had only three remaining members. In order to continue the program, a full board must be in place. It was also felt that this board should consist of staff who are knowledgeable in this area. The resolution will reorganize this board to consist of four knowledgeable staff members, and three citizens. It was noted that the remaining three citizen members (Rosalie White, Eugene Borden and Jackie Wheatley-Demoss) have agreed to continue serving. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve Resolution No. 1192 regarding the Housing Review Board reorganization as presented. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0.
