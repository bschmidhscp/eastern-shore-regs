# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY AUGUST 5, 2025** 

At 2:56 pm, Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board. Present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird Commissioner Craig Mathies were absent for today’s Special Session meeting. 

Also in attendance were Interim County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for August 5, 2025</u>** 

On August 5, 2025, from 2:57 p.m. until 4:40 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) to consult with counsel for legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Darryl Webster, and Commissioner Eldon Willing, President Charles Laird and Commissioner Craig Mathies attended via teleconference. Interim County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, DTCS Director Jesse Drewer, EMS Chief Joel Dixon, EMS Deputy Chief Corey Polidore, Landfill Superintendent Gary Kitching, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Legal, Action Taken: Table discussion for future meeting; (2) Topic: Proposed EMS Facility, Action Taken: Proceed; (3) Topic: Scale House Attendant, Action Taken: Hiring; (4) Topic: Finance Director, Action Taken: Hiring; (5) Topic: Paramedics, Action Taken: Hiring; (6) EMTs, Action Taken: Hiring. 

The Board was advised for the need of a Closed Session.
