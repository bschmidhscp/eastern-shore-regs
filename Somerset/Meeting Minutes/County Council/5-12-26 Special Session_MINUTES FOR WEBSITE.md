BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY MAY 12, 2026** 

At 2:23 pm, President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in a Work Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent today. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for May 12, 2026</u>** 

On May 12, 2026, from 2:23 p.m. until 3:55 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; (7) to consult with counsel for legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent today. County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, DTCS Director Jesse Drewer, Sheriff Ronnie Howard, Chief Deputy Paul Keplinger, Lt. Ray Johns, Attorney Robin Cockey, Emergency Services Director Yvette Cross, HR Director Jamie Manaia, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, Roads Superintendent Mike Marvel, EMS Deputy Chief Cory Polidore, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Legal; Action Taken: None; (2) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: None: (3) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Consensus for Step Increase; (4) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Promotion; (5) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Hire Part-Time Employee; (6) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Approval of contribution for the new FMLA-type insurance program; (7) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Consensus for 24-hour staffing policy for EMS; (8) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: County Administrator Salary. 

The Board was advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:23 pm, on a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; (7) to consult with counsel for legal advice.
