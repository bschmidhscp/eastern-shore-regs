## BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY APRIL 28, 2026** 

At 6:31 pm, President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in a Work Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Commissioner Craig Mathies, was absent today. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for April 28, 2026</u>** 

On April 28, 2026, from 6:31 p.m. until 7:38 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Commissioner Craig Mathies was absent today. County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Sheriff Ronnie Howard, Chief Deputy Paul Keplinger, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Hiring/Paramedic; (2) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Employee Part-Time to Full-Time Status: (3) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: None. 

The Board was advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 6:31 pm, on a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
