‘The proposed map updates are required by the State of Maryland Critical Area Commission by House Bill 1253 (2008). The State of Maryland Critical Area Program was established in 1984 as a direct response to growing concern over the decline of the quality and productivity of the waters of the Chesapeake Bay and its tributaries. The decline was found to have resulted, in part, from the cumulative effects of human activity that caused increased levels of pollutants, nutrients, and toxins, and also from declines in protective land uses such as forest land and agricultural land in the Bay region. 

The Critical Area Maps and Ordinances for each county are reviewed on a 6-year basis, and the adoption of the maps is the first step in the process to review, and update if necessary, the Critical Area Ordinance.

Mr. John Redden, County Engineer came forward to present State Clearinghouse Review MD20240131-0066 - FY2023 Flood Mitigation Assistance (FMA) Grant Program: Southern Crisfield Flood Mitigation Project-FMA (Phase 1 and 2). Mr. Redden explained that this concept plan involves creation ofa perimeter dike system (Block) and an interior drainage system (Relieve) to protect the City. The dike system consists of raising roads, constructing earthen berms and raising bulkheads while the drainage system consists of improving pump stations, constructing stormwater management facilities, replacing/removing tide gates and reconstructing wetlands. This is proposed in two phases, Phase | to the south and Phase 2 to the north. This project could be extremely beneficial to the City, but it may have detrimental impacts to those areas just outside
