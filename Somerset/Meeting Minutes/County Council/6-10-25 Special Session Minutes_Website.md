# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY JUNE 10, 2025** 

At 2:00 pm, Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, and Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were Interim County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for June 10, 2025</u>** 

On June 10, 2025, from 2:01 p.m. until 2:41 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by by all present members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was not present for today’s meeting. Interim County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, Human Resources Director Jamie Manaia, Internal Auditor Sharon Muir, Budgets/Grants Administrator Cindy Sapp, EMS Coordinator Joel Dixon, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Salary, Action Taken: Approval of Pay Scale; (2) Topic: Tax Collection Clerk, Action Taken: Hiring. 

Vice President Randy Laird asked for Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

The Board was advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:01 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated
