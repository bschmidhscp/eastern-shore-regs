BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

January 14, 2025 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present 

Closed Session Summary Report 

On Tuesday, January 14, 2025 from 2:23 p.m, until 2:44 p.m., President Charles Laird, upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, and seconded by Commissioner Willing opened in Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. Commissioner Craig Mathies Sr., and President Charles Laird voted aye by show of hands, therefore, the motion carried 4-0. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Mr. Ernest J. Leatherbury, Jr., Deputy Administrator, Ms. Lory Ebron, Executive Aide, Ms. Yvette Cross, Emergency Services Director and Ms, Jamie Manaia, HR Director were also present. Topics discussed: (1) Emergency Medical Service Coordinator Hire (2) Employee Work from Home request (3) Consent to Assignment of Franchise Agreement request. Approved: (1) Approval to hire Emergency Medical Service Coordinator (2) Approval to allow work from home (3) Approval to sign Consent to Assignment of Franchise Agreement.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 1-14-25 

Commissioner Willing made a motion to approve the meeting minutes from December 17, 2024 as presented. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion 

carried 4-0. 

Ms. Katie Douglas, Program Lead/Big Brothers Big Sisters of the Eastern Shore, Inc., came forward to accept a proclamation recognizing January 2025 as National Mentoring Month. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Department of Technical and Community Services came forward at this time to accept sealed bids received for the Rehabilitation of the Ewell Recreation Center New HVAC System. No bids were received, therefore Mr. Drewer advised that he will re-advertise. 

Mr. Drewer next presented a request received from EDC Executive Director Danny Thompson for the waiver of Submission and Review Fee's (approximately $1,380) for future Somerset County Industrial Park, located on Revell’s Neck Road. It was noted this is a county project. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to waive the fees for the Somerset County Industrial Park project as requested. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Mr. Woody Barnes, Public Works Director, came forward at this time to present a request to hire a person for grant writer services regarding the Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities (BRIC) Grant Application. Ms. Jennifer Merritt with IT With Merritts, LLC presented a proposal for one (1) years-service in the amount of $36,000 and will provide grant application document development, presentations, communications support, coordination and meetings, and grant matching funds research. Mr. Barnes noted that he is familiar with Ms. Merritt’s work provided in the City of Crisfield which was instrumental. Commissioner Mathies questioned if the position was advertised. Mr. Barnes said it had not been advertised, noting that typically it probably would have been. Commissioner Mathies felt for competitive reasons regarding salary, the position should be advertised, noting he is not against the hiring for this position. No action was taken, and the matter will be discussed again at a future meeting. 

Mr. Scott Sturgis, Director Dog Control, came forward at this time to present the Volunteer Liability Waiver form for approval. It was explained that the form has been reviewed by the county attorney, and would allow volunteers to walk dogs at the facility following a Dog Walking Program. Mr. Sturgis noted that applicants (volunteers) will be reviewed, and will walk the dogs on the best days for the office, weather permitting. Also, dogs will be evaluated for safety reasons,

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve the Dog Walking Program and Volunteer Liability Waiver form as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Mr. Taylor continued with correspondence and discussion items. 

Ms. Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc., requested the Board of County Commissioners approval to re-appoint Reverend Charles Bagley to serve another six (6) year-term on the Somerset County Sanitary District Board. Mr. Bagley’s term expires the end of January 2025, and he has agreed to serve another 6-year term. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to reappoint Reverend Bagley to another six-year term on the Sanitary District as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

A letter was received from Maryland Department ofTransportation advising that the State Highway Administration will seek entrance onto private property to conduct environmental and engineering studies for wetland mitigation sites in the Tangier and Pocomoke-Western Lower Delmarva Watersheds. The county will be notified if a site is selected. 

The Maryland State Retirement Agency has notified Somerset County Government to expect and audit between December 2024 and February 2025. 

Comcast has notified the county of channel changes to the Fuse TV and FM HD channels. Correspondence sent was reviewed as well. 

Under discussion items, Mr. Taylor presented the recommended Environmental Health Fee increases, initially presented by Health Officer Danielle Weber at a previous meeting. As requested, the current fee vs proposed new fee recommendation was presented in side-by-side format. Being the full board was not present, no action was taken. 

A request was received from Daniel R. Webster, Assistant Chief/Treasurer of the Deal Island Chance Volunteer Fire Company to install a boat lift in Slip #1 at the Deal Island Marina. The lift will be installed at no cost to the county, and would be protection from high waves, and 

save on cost of maintenance.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 1-14-25 

Commissioner Willing made a motion to approve the request presented to install a boat lift in Slip #1 at the Deal Island Marina. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

The proclamation prepared proclaiming Monday, January 20, 2025 as Reverend Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Day in Maryland was approved by a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

At this time, Mr. Robert J. McIntyre, 28101 Mt. Vernon Roads, requested to address Board. Mr. Melntyre questioned why there are only 3 people serving on the Property Tax Assessment Appeals Board, and why there has been no movement in five (5) months. Mr. McIntyre was advised that the Property Tax Assessment Appeals Board appointments are made by the Governor's Office. Mr. McIntyre asked who the contact is for the appointment's office. 

With no further business, at 2:23 p.m., the Board was advised of the need for a Closed Session. Upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, and seconded by Commissioner Willing the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. Commissioner Craig Mathies Sr., and President Charles Laird voted aye by show of hands, therefore, the motion carried 4-0. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

The Commissioner and Closed Session meetings held today concluded at 2:44 p.m.
