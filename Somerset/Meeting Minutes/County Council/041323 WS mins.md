Somerset County Sanitary Commission 

Work Session Minutes of April 13, 2023 

A Work Session meeting of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, April 13" 2023 at 11:00 a.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member and Barbara Somers, Member. Adam Riggin, Chairman and Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman were absent. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, Manager, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent and Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide. 

Ms. Mister presented the Commission with following budget highlights.
