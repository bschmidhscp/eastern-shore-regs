BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY AUGUST 20, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird call the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for August 20, 2024</u>** 

On August 20, 2024, from 2:36 p.m. until 3:16 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, Director of Solid Waste and Maintenance Charles Cavanaugh, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Legal; Actions Taken: Confidential. 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

No minutes were presented today. 

At this time, the Board invited Dr. Tasker-Mitchell, Superintendent of Somerset County Schools, and Tracie Bartmey, Director of Schools, to come forward. Dr. Tasker-Mitchell began by providing a brief introduction of herself and her background in the education system before

moving into her priorities for Somerset County. The Board was advised of a 100 Day Entry Plan which includes a deep look into Somerset County Schools by evaluating student performance as well as how to recruit and retain teachers. Dr. Tasker-Mitchell then shared current standings/test scores for Somerset County, advising how Somerset County schools are ranked 22<sup>nd</sup> out of 24 in the State of Maryland, and continued with her vision to build a strong climate and emotional support system for students to improve their educational experience. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing inquired about Somerset Technical High School and asked for a concrete answer as to why private school students and homeschool students have not been permitted in the past to enroll in the vocational classes offered; stating how the school in being very underutilized, especially with students nowadays being encouraged to go to trade instead of college. Dr. Tasker-Mitchell advised that a meeting was held recently with the principal of Somerset Technical High School, of whom agreed that the community was not fully aware of what the school offered. The principal provided a presentation for Dr. Tasker-Mitchell and staff to show all that was available that the school has to offer for students. Dr. Tasker-Mitchell then advised that there is a process for using the Somerset Technical High School and how she would like to discover the reason behind parents choosing to homeschool their children and figure out a way to get those students enrolled back into the district. Commissioner Eldon Willing advised he would still like solid answer and suggested that by permitting private school students and homeschooled students permission to enroll in Somerset Technical High School that it may offer encouragement for those students to want to return to public schools. 

Commissioner Craig Mathies addressed the Blueprint and advised how he would like to see transparency with how those funds are being utilized and where they are being placed. 

Vice President Randy Laird addressed concerns raised from parents of special needs students; stating how when parents attend IEP meetings they advise they aren’t able to get the assistance they need without a hired professional; which isn’t something all parents can do. Dr. Tasker-Mitchell advised that all students should receive equal treatment and how her plan involves rebuilding trust with parents and building a solid relationship. One solution is to hire the right people, as it is a very challenging job, and ensure the services and programs provided are for all students. 

Vice President Randy Laird then addressed School Resource Officers, advising how the Board is fully funding them, and requested that Dr. Tasker-Mitchell monitor and report if SROs
