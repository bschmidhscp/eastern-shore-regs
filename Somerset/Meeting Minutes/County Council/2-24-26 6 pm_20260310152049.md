At 6:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Special Session Meeting of the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster.

also present.
