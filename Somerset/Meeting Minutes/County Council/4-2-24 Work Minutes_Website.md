# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **WORK SESSION TUESDAY APRIL 2, 2024** 

At 1:17 p.m., upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the Board entered into Somerset County Commissioners Work Session. Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Following a brief recess, the Board invited City of Crisfield Mayor Taylor and Coastal Engineer Jacob Berninger to come forward to discuss the Clearinghouse Review Project MD20240131-0066/Southern Crisfield Flood Mitigation Project. Also in attendance for this discussion were County Engineer John Redden, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, Director of Solid Waste and Maintenance Charles Cavanaugh, and Stormwater Engineer John Aldrich (via teleconference). Mr. Berninger provided the Board with a PowerPoint presentation regarding the Crisfield Mitigation Strategy and the current analysis to correct the flooding issues in Crisfield; broken into stages with stage one projected to cost $39,948,811 and stage two projected to cost $29,401,440. A discussion then was held with Mr. Redden, Mr. Barnes, and Mr. Cavanaugh expressing a concern of the proposed solutions as blocking the water from currented affected areas may cause flooding in areas not affected at this time. Commissioner Darryl Webster inquired of any success stories with this proposed plan, to which he was advised there were none for this exact plan (however, individual aspects of the plan have seen success). 

During this discussion, Commissioner Darryl Webster, Commissioner Craig Mathies and Mr. Taylor all stepped out of the board room at various times before shortly returning to the meeting. 

At the conclusion of the discussion, President Charles Laird inquired as to a timeline for the letter of support to which Mayor Taylor advised it was too late for one during this go around, but there may be need for one in the future and she would like for it to state that the County accepts the conceptual design that was presented today. A brief discussion was held regarding the funding
