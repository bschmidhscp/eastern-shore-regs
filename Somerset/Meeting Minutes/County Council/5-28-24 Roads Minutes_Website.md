**SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY MAY 28, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also present for today’s meeting were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Vice President Randy Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the April 16, 2024 meeting were presented for approval; however, the Board requested more time to review. 

Mr. Barnes advised that, due to an unfortunate error in the advertisement, today’s scheduled public hearings will be postponed and re-advertised for June 11, 2024. 

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

A discussion was held regarding Bridge #SO-11 Sign Post Road over Back Creek and emergency repairs needed on the deck and railing that will be $74,695.00 in materials alone. Mr. Barnes advised that there is only $50,000.00 budgeted for bridge repairs, as well as leftover funds from the bulkheading repairs (as most of those repairs were completed in-house). A brief discussion was held on how to proceed with this much-needed repairs. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to authorize the purchase of the materials in the amount of $74,695 by utilizing the $50,000.00 from the bridge funds and the remaining amount from the bulkheading funds). By a vote in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

A discussion was held regarding the construction of a truck turn around at the Messick Farm. Mr. Barnes advised that he did not budget for this sort of request; however, considering a road and a spur will need to be constructed in the future, Mr. Barnes suggested this request go through the Solid Waste Department’s budget. The Board gave a consensus for Mr. Taylor to discuss this with Mr. Cavanaugh.

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to authorize Mr. Barnes to advertise for a Highway Worker I/MEO I. 

# **WATERWAYS** 

An update was provided on the Dames Quarter Bulkheading Project with Mr. Barnes advising it is not complete yet due to flooding. Work on this is on hold until more favorable weather conditions. 

A discussion was held regarding the Smith Island County Dock. Mr. Barnes advised that the involved parties have now received a second violation notice but there is still no resolution. The situation has escalated now to vehicles being parked on the county docks and the owners locking them so they cannot be moved. The Board inquired about installing cameras and signage to help address these issues as well as enlisting the Sheriff’s office to help enforce the ordinance. The Board was advised by Mr. Barnes that the Sheriff’s office and DNR have informed him that this is a civil matter and they cannot assist. 

Mr. Taylor left the meeting and returned with the ordinance to review with the Board. 

The Board discussed the possibility of issuing fines for violators. The discussion concluded with the Board requesting Mr. Taylor to have County Attorney Kirk Simpkins review the current ordinance and make it more enforceable. Vice President Randy Laird also requested that Mr. Barnes place signs on the docks. 

A motion was then made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the minutes from the April 16, 2024 meeting. By a vote in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried.
