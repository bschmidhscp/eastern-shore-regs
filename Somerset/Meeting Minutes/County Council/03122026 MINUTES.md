Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of March 12, 2026 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, March 12, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, arrived at the meeting at 1:05 p.m. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

### Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating that the District is continuing design and funding correspondence with Davis, Bowen, & Friedel, Inc. (DBF) and Maryland Department of Environment (MDE). Mr. Taylor expressed that while this project is still in the early phase, it is important that the District ensures adequate grant funding and understands how any loans needed will affect the debt service rates in the future. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating the District is still working with MDE and DBF on the preliminary design phase. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Lead Service Line Project by stating the District is finalizing the contract with Electroscan (data collection and management program) for this project. The District will confirm approval from MDE of the contract with Electroscan before purchasing the equipment to ensure reimbursement. 

#### Smith Island Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) upgrade is reaching the end of completion. The District has scheduled the final punch list inspection for 3/25/2026, which will include representatives from the District, GMB (George, Miles, & Buhr), MDE, USDA, and M2 Construction. Mr. Taylor expects to have a completion date status after the punch list is developed.

## Fairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission in regards to the Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension Request/Petition by stating that two bids were obtained by the District's on-call engineers, DBF & GMB, with a quote for engineering services for the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) and Engineering Report (ER). Once the RFP is approved, the District will have a cost for the PER and ER to present to the County Commissioners, and they will then determine whether they will be funding the reports. Bids will be discussed during the action section of the meeting. 

### Public Input 

Richard Crumbaker wanted to make the Sanitary District commissioners aware that the City Council of Crisfield made an announcement last night that they will be holding a public meeting on 3/22/2026 regarding their sewer plant. They will specifically talk about how to unload or have someone else manage their sewer plant, and they will want Josh to attend that meeting.

### Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular session minutes dated February 12" 2026 were previously distributed. On a motion by Ms. Somers and seconded by Mr. Williams, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular session minutes dated February 12" 2026. 

Approval of Policy & Resolution — Reserve Fund Policy 

In the February 2026 meeting, The Reserve Fund Policy was presented to the Commission. This fund provides financial stability and a source of funding for appropriations that have become unfunded due to revenue shortfalls or other foreseen financial circumstances. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the Reserve Fund Policy and Resolution; Mr. Williams seconded the motion which carried unanimously to approval. 

## Approval of Resolution of Vehicle Loan Agreement w/ Hebron Savings Bank 

The Vehicle Loan Agreement with Hebron Savings Bank will help the Sanitary District finance the purchase of a dump trailer from J&S Equipment, Inc. in the amount of $18,495.00. Mr. Taylor let the Commission know that approval for the financing of the dump trailer has been received by the County Commissioners. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the resolution of the vehicle loan agreement with Hebron Savings Bank

## Approval of Brivo Quote — Office Security System Upgrade 

The County recently made security updates to the County Complex building and hired the company, Pye-Barker Fire & Safety, to manage and monitor their security system. Since the District's security is tied to the County’s system, management recommends that the District updates the security company to the same as the County. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the two Brivo quotes for the office security system upgrade totaling $6,707.00. Ms. Landon seconded the motion which carried unanimously to approval. 

Smith Island Subdistrict — Action 

# Approval of Engineering Amendment No. 11 - SICWP 

George, Miles, & Buhr, LLC has presented the District with an amendment to the SICWP Owner-Engineer Agreement for additional construction phase services. Mr. Taylor explained to the Commission that the need for increase in services is due to the inability to achieve the Final Completion milestone date and additional time is needed. The proposed additional constriction phase services provided by GMB for the WWTP Upgrade contract are as follows: 

1. Construction Inspection $36,750.00 

2. Reimbursable Expenses $9,792.00 Total: $46,542.00 

The above tasks result in an increase to the budget for Engineering Service in the amount of $46,542.00. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the Engineering Amendment No.11 from GMB for the Smith Island Clean Water Project in the amount of $46,542.00. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

Fairmount Subdistrict — Action 

## Approval of RFP for PER/ER — Rumbley—Frenchtown Sewer Extension 

The District received two bid responses to provide a preliminary engineering report and environmental report (PER/ER) for the Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension project. The first bid was received from DBF in the amount of $30,000, and the second bid from GMB in the amount of $40,000. This report will provide the necessary information for the District to learn if the area can be serviced and if it would be financially feasible.
