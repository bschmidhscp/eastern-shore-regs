# **SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY JANUARY 21, 2025** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also present for today’s meeting were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the October 29, 2024 open meeting were presented for approval. A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the minutes as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Darryl Willing, the motion carried. 

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

A discussion was held regarding the recent snow events that occurred January 6<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> with Mr. Barnes advising of how the Roads Department addressed county roads during and after the snow, as well as minor repairs to the plow trucks during the event. 

An update was provided on Bridge #11 on Sign Post Road. Repairs on this bridge started on November 11, 2024 with the replacement of three panels, a new wooden guard rail, and repairs of the abutment wall on the south end of the bridge. The bridge is now operational for normal weight restrictions. Mr. Barnes also advised that road repair work from the bridge to Revells Neck Road is complete. 

Mr. Barnes advised that the replacement fuel tank for the County Roads Department is scheduled to be delivered and installed by February 17<sup>th</sup> .

# **DISCUSSION** 

Vice President Randy Laird advised of a possible drainage issue at the corner of Ape Hole Road and Byrdtown. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing expressed concerned over some icy areas on State Roads during the weather event. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster inquired if the County Roads had surplus sand for any future weather events, to which Mr. Barnes advised they do. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing inquired about a possible blockage in a culvert he requested be addressed (either by State or the County) and Mr. Barnes advised that upon investigation there were no blockages in the culvert itself. 

The meeting was then opened for public comment and Mr. Bob McIntyre addressed the Board advising of the unfavorable conditions on Polks Road and requesting more sand to make it safer during these snow events. 

With no further business to discuss, a motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and carried on a vote in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster to adjourn the Somerset County Roads Board meeting at 2:14 p.m.
