Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of May 9, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, May 9, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Succeeding Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Josh Taylor, P.E., Davis Bowen & Friedel, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. ### Approval of Irrigation Credit Policy 

Management has created an Irrigation Credit Policy and Resolution for the Board’s review and approval following the March 14'" 2024 Commission meeting where the Board voted to issue a customer a one-time courtesy sewer credit for water usage to reseed and germinate his yard. Ms. Mister stated to the Board, she considered the Board’s comments during the March SCSD Commission meeting when drafting the proposed Irrigation Credit Policy. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the Irrigation Credit Policy as presented (below). Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion unanimously carried to approval. 

Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. Irrigation Credit Policy 

The District recognizes that the installation and maintenance of landscaping, including sod, can result in significant water usage. To ensure fairness and accuracy in billing, customers incurring extra water usage related to landscaping, including but not limited to lawn reseeding or sod installation, are encouraged to follow these steps to prevent sewer charges for the water used: 

1. Consider Installing an Irrigation Meter: For customers who anticipate regular lawn watering, installing an irrigation meter is the most effective solution. To request the installation of an irrigation meter, customers must submit a water connection application and pay the associated fees. An irrigation meter is specifically designed to measure and bill only for water used for irrigation purposes. 

2. Alternative Option for Occasional Users: We understand that the cost of an irrigation meter may not be feasible for occasional users. Therefore, we offer an alternative solution. Customers can notify our office In advance of their intention to water their landscaping. To request a credit for sewer charges, customers must complete a Request for Irrigation Credit form. Supporting documentation such as time-stamped pictures or a letter from the sod company confirming irrigation is required. Once the landscaping watering is complete, customers must contact our office to schedule a meter audit. A one-time trip fee will apply for this service. If the dates of the water usage align with the provided documentation, a sewer credit (an “Irrigation Credit") may be issued for the water consumed during landscaping watering. Customers without an irrigation meter are eligible for one (1) Irrigation Credit per year. 

While this policy is primarily designed for situations such as lawn reseeding and sod installation, this policy can also apply to other outdoor water-only activities, such as power washing a house. In the event that this policy Is utilized for such activities, it is imperative to follow the same procedures stated above and submit appropriate documentation to be eligible for sewer credit consideration.
