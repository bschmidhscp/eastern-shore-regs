Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of April 11, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, April 11, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, and Josh Taylor, P.E., Davis Bowen & Friedel. Adam Riggin, Chairman, was absent. There were no Public Attendees. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

##### General Information 

Mr. Stockus was happy to announce, Barbara Somers, Commissioner of the Fairmount/Rumbley Area, has been reappointed by the Somerset County Commissioners to serve another six-year term with the Sanitary District Commission Board. The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Board for review. The third quarter revenues exceeded expenses by $265,840.31. The Commission had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

#### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Ludy updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade stating electrical service is scheduled for the Pilot Study which is expected to be onsite by the end of May 2024. 

Mr. Stockus informed the Commission there has been no update in status regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project since the last Commission meeting. The District is still waiting for the release of the Intended Use Plan for the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund from the Maryland Department of the Environment. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating inspections have been completed and drywall has been installed. Project completion is projected for June 1% 2024. 

Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP). During the WWTP Upgrade progress meeting held on 4/3/24 M2 Construction stated fiberglass doors, all exterior windows, and electrical conduit were installed. During the Subaqueous Force Main progress meeting also held on 4/3/24 Crofton Construction provided an update on work being done stating equipment has been successfully barged

### Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular session minutes dated March 14'" 2024 were previously distributed. On a motion by Ms. Landon and seconded by Ms. Somers, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular session minutes dated March 14" 2024. 

### Approval to purchase Handheld 

Ms. Mister informed the Board, the District's only handheld meter audit tool needs repairs in order to function properly. These repairs are estimated to take a couple months. The District priced a new multifunction handheld, which can perform meter audits as well as meter readings. She stated that this handheld would also provide us a backup meter reader and audit tool for these types of situations. The new handheld cost is $13,000.00. The handheld is a crucial piece of equipment for billing. It was Management's recommendation to purchase a new handheld and have the current handheld as backup once it’s repaired. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the purchase of a new handheld at the cost of $13,000.00. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

### Approval of new position 

Mr. Ludy is recommending a new position be created for the PA WWTP, titled Water & Sewer Maintenance Operator. This position would replace the current Water and Sewer Operator Trainee position that is currently vacant. This position will require a Journeyman’s Electrician License at time of hire, with requirements of obtaining State of Maryland Wastewater Collection System Operator’s Certificate within a year of hire. This position will perform electrical troubleshooting and repairs currently being contracted out, which will save the expense of outsourcing. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the new position of Water & Sewer Maintenance Operator for advertisement, Mr. Williams seconded the motion to unanimous approval.

Princess Anne — Action 

# WWTP Influent Pump repair 

Mr. Ludy informed the Commission, the influent Pump at the PA WWTP is inoperable and in need of repairing. It is necessary to have this pump operational for heavy flows from excessive rainfall and flooding. This pump is essential to keeping the flows down, eliminating sewer overflows. The quote from Hill's Industrial for the repair is in the amount of $19,393.00. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the quote from Hill’s Industrial as presented for the WWTP Influent pump repair. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

#### Approval of Site Plan 

MDM Surveyors & Engineers, on behalf of AutoZone Store, have submitted a site plan for approval of their proposed business to be located at 12208 Brittingham Lane. A Utility plan was submitted to Davis, Bowen & Friedel on November 9'" 2023. Josh Taylor reviewed and commented on November 14" 2023 with 15 items to address before moving forward with approval. The plan presented today has been reviewed by Earl Ludy but has not been reviewed by our Engineers. Mr. Taylor stated he is certain they have addressed all the items listed but will confirm in writing. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve site plan for AutoZone contingent upon receiving letter from Davis, Bowen & Friedel stating all necessary revisions have been completed. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

##### Crisfield — Action 

### Request for credit 

Jonathon Pruitt, SP Rentals LLC, is requesting a credit to the water and sewer bill for his property located at 26642 Mariners Road. Mr. Pruitt stated in his letter to the Board, he discovered the water meter has been malfunctioning since June of 2023 resulting in being billed based on estimated usage since (18,391 gallons/quarter). Mr. Pruitt is requesting the meter be replaced and he be refunded based on minimum usage (12,000 gallons/quarter) for the last three quarters as he feels it is unjustifiable to bill an estimated over-usage without providing concrete evidence that the property has consumed more than minimum usage. Ms. Mister stated the District replaced the water meter at this property on April 4"" 2024 after receiving his request letter to the Commission. It was Management's recommendation to take no action on his request for credit/adjustment today (citing the Estimated Meter Policy). However, the Office will make an adjustment (if
