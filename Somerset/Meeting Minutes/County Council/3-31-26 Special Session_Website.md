## BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY MARCH 31, 2026** 

At 2:01 pm, President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent today. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for March 31, 2026</u>** 

On March 31, 2026, from 2:01 p.m. until 2:17 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; (7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent today. County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Attorney Kirk Simpkins, DTCS Assistant Director Will Cornish, HR Director Jamie Manaia, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Legal Discussion, Action Taken: None; (2) Topic: Personnel, Action Taken: Probationary to Permanent Employee Status; (3) Topic: Personnel, Action Taken: Accept resignation; (4) Personnel, Action Taken: Approval to close Transfer Station on Easter/keep open on Good Friday. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked for Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance.

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:01 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; (7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice.
