# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY MARCH 5, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Commissioner Craig Mathies was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for March 5, 2024</u>** 

On March 5, 2024, from 3:05 p.m. until 3:54 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Commissioner Craig Mathies was absent for today’s meeting. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, DTCS Assistant Director Mary Phillips, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Personnel; Actions Taken: Promotion; (2) Personnel; Actions Taken: Extended Probation/Performance Improvement Plan. 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Ms. Christine Bozick, Therapist Supervisor for Life Crisis Center, as invited to come forward to accept a proclamation for Professional Social Work Month. 

Ms. Bozick then left the meeting.

Next, Mr. David King and Mr. Jerry Philpott were before the Board to present a request for special exceptions with regard to Kingsbay Mansion. President Charles Laird also invited Mr. Danny Thompson (Director of EDC), Mr. Jesse Drewer (Director of DTCS), and Ms. Mary Phillips (Assistant Director of DTCS) to come forward for the discussion. A PowerPoint presentation was provided with information pertaining to the Carlin Company, the history of Kingsbay Mansion, and the local decline of restaurants/attractions that are negatively impacting their business; therefore, they are requesting the following special exceptions: 1) That Kingsbay Mansion be designated as a hotel; allowing the remaining suites to be included as part of Kingsbay Mansion for rent as separate room night stays and provide for future expansion; 2) To operate the West Wing bar, mansion dining rooms, and ballroom as a restaurant and bar; and 3) To obtain a liquor license to sell and serve alcohol on the premises only (in the West Wing, dining rooms, and outdoor events). The Board was advised that with these special exceptions granted, it would allow Kingsbay Mansion the opportunity to expand their operations throughout the year and assist with providing employment opportunities within Somerset County. A discussion was held regarding these requests with the Board advising Mr. King and Mr. Philpott to follow through with the Growth Allocation Application and hire a consultant to help them through the lengthy process, and provide a brief synopsis to Mr. Taylor with regards to the liquor requests. 

Mr. King and Mr. Philpott then left the meeting. 

The Board was advised for the need of a formal vote for the phone poll conducted with regard to the approval of permitting gas cards/fobs to all of the fire companies in Somerset County. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the distributions of gas cards/fobs to all of the fire companies in Somerset County. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster; the motion carried.
