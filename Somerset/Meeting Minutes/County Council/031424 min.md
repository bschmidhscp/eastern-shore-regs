Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of March 14, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, March 14, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Adam Riggin, Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Richard Crumbacker, County Times. Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman was absent. Members of the Public included Jason Wallace and Kirk Davis, present for their appointments. 

Mr. Riggin called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

##### General Information 

Adam Riggin, Chairman, was recognized for 10 years of service with the Sanitary District Commission Board. Mr. Riggin was presented with a plaque from Mr. Stockus and his fellow Sanitary District Commissioners. 

Mr. Stockus informed the Board, it was recently announced the Low-Income Household Water Assistance Program (LIHWAP) will be ending soon as the funds have nearly depleted. Ms. Mister has appealed by letter to Senator Marybeth Carozza, Senator Chris Van Hollen, and Delegate Charles Otto addressing the end of LIHWAP and requesting any additional funding opportunities available for assistance programs within the state. 

Ms. Mister informed the Commission, on behalf of the Maryland State Retirement and Pension System (MSRA), the public auditing firm UHY Advisors, Inc. recently performed a compliance review of SCSD. The review was to determine if the District is compliant with state laws, rules, regulations, and administrative policies relating to enrolling members in the System and reporting payroll data to MSRA. There were no findings to report, the District Staff were credited in the compliance letter with properly enrolling and accurately reporting the payroll data to MSRA. 

## Princess Anne- Information 

Mr. Ludy updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade stating a meeting is scheduled to discuss the Pilot Testing Agreement/Proposal which is planned for summer through fall 2024. 

Mr. Stockus informed the Commission there has been no change in status regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project since the last Commission meeting.

#### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP). During the WWTP Upgrade progress meeting held on 3/6/24 M2 Construction stated insulation has been installed, drywall work has begun, and electrical conduit was beginning to be installed. During the Subaqueous Force Main progress meeting also held on 3/6/24 Crofton Construction provided an update on work being done. HDD #1 from the Tylerton drill site began on March 1* and the pipe pullback was completed on March 5*Crofton indicated that approximately 150' of pipe remains protruding from the exit point. Crofton has begun preparing equipment and materials for mobilization. The current revision of the Wetlands License only permits work at Tylerton and only until March 10*, additional time has been requested of MDE in order to allow work to continue to the next step of drilling at Rhodes Point.

Management previously distributed to the Board copies of two proposals from Jeff Smith & Associates, Inc., a job recruiting agency designed to use its best efforts to find suitable candidates for employment. They have provided the District with two contract options in

Ms. Landon made a motion to revise the Superintendent position advertisement to include $10,000 sign-on bonus and negotiable salary. ### Emergency Repair 

Mr. Ludy informed the Commission, the sludge pump at the PA WWTP is inoperable. This pump moves the sludge to the belt press for dewatering, removing solids to keep the plant in compliance with the National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System Permit. The quote from Hill’s Industrial for the repair is in the amount of $5,910.25. Ms. Somers made a motion to retroactively approve the quote from Hill’s Industrial as presented for the sludge pump repair. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

### Appointments — Action 

Request for sewer credit - Appeal 

Insert from SCSD Commission meeting minutes: 

Jason Wallace, 30043 Kristwood Way, requested the Board consider granting him a credit for some of the sewer usage charged to his water and sewer bill during the quarters of 9/30/23 and 12/30/23. Mr. Wallace stated in his request, during the 9/30/23 quarter he reseeded his yard and was watering to get it germinated and growing. During the 12/30/23 quarter, he topped off his pool. Management's recommendation was to take no action for the reason that the District are unable to verify how his water consumption was used. The Commission took no action on Mr. Wallace’s request, citing Management's recommendation. 

Mr. Wallace was present for his appointment to discuss the Commission’s decision to take no action on his request. Mr. Wallace explained he used his water to reseed and germinate his property and had receipts of seed purchase with photos of the reseeding. Management recommended taking no action due to lack of documentation that the water was specifically used for the purpose stated, and fear of setting a precedence. However, after much deliberation, the consensus was to give Mr. Wallace a one-time courtesy credit for the sewer portion of his 12/30/23 quarter with the acknowledgement in the future Mr. Wallace must either pay for an irrigation system to be installed at his expense or have the District read his meter before and after irrigating at his expense. Mr. Wallace agreed to the options stated for future reference. Ms. Somers made a motion to grant Mr. Wallace a one-time courtesy sewer credit (based on average usage) for his 12/30/23 quarter bill. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. *Due to Mr. Williams’ working relationship with Mr. Wallace he was excluded from deliberation and voting on this matter.
