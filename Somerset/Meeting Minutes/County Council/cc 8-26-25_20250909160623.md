BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY August 26, 2025 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Darryl Webster, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr. and Commissioner Eldon Willing. 

Interim County Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

Closed Session Summary Report 

On Tuesday, August 26, 2025 at 2:05 p.m., until 3:11 p.m., President Charles Laird, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing entered into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction, and (7) consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. All those present voted in favor, therefore, the motion carried 5-0. 

Interim County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury, Jr., HR Director Jamie Manaia, Mr. Kirk Simpkins, Mr. Danny Thompson, Ms. LeeAnn Linton, Mr. Jesse Drewer, Mr. Woody Bames, and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

Topics discussed: (1) Consult with Counsel (2) Potential Litigation (3) Consideration to change job description (4) Acting Director of Finance. Approved: (1) Approval for Attorney to draft letter (2) Decision regarding potential litigation/confidential (3) Approval to change job description for County Engineer (4) Approval to designate Acting Director of Finance.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 8-26-25 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve the minutes as presented. Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr, Jesse Drewer, Department of Technical and Community Services Director came forward at this time to request consideration to approvea letter of support request received from Smith Island United. Mr. Drewer explained that Smith Island United will be submitting a grant application in the amount of $ 260,000 that will be used for two projects; a storage building for the Tylerton Fire Boat and rehabilitation of three owner-occupied properties. They have requested a letter of support from the Board of County Commissioners. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to authorize President Charles Laird to sign the letter of support requested by Smith Island United as presented. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. By showofhands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr. Danny Thompson, Executive Director, Economic Development came forward to announce a groundbreaking project in the State of Maryland; the delivery of medications by drones. The project will be entering the testing phase, from Crisfield-Somerset County Airport to deliver medications from local Somerset County pharmacies and they are looking for volunteers living around the Crisfield area to test demo the program by having their medications delivered to their home by drone. Commissioner Webster asked if he can share the flyer on social media to promote the program. Mr. Thompson advised that would be beneficial as he is working on getting the word out. 

Mr. Leatherbury continued with correspondence at this time. 

First, Ms. Claudia Nelson, Director of the Somerset County Department of Social Services has announced her plans to retire on or about November 1, 2025. 

Last, the letter sent jointly to the Board of Education and Superintendent Dr. TaskerMitchell was reviewed.
