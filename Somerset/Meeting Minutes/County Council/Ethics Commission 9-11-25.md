# Ethics Commission 9-11-25

<!-- page 1 -->
# SOMERSET COUNTY ETHICS COMMISSION 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111 

Princess Anne, MD 21853 

Minutes of the September 26, 2024 Meeting of Somerset County Ethics Commission 10:00 a.m. 

Members Present: 

Staff Present: 

R. Dean Goldsborough Kirk Simpkins, Attorney Ernest Satchell Lory E. Ebron, Executive Aide William McInturff Elmer Barkley Rita Jones 

## Members Absent: None 

A meeting of the Somerset County Ethics Commission was convened on today’s date at 10:00 a.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. The annual 2024 Ethics Commission meeting was called to order by Chairman Goldsborough. The following business was addressed: 

Attorney Simpkins advised that the first order of business was to approve the minutes from the previous meeting held on September 28, 2023. The minutes from the September 28, 2023 open session meeting were presented for approval. Chairman Goldsborough called for a vote in favor. All said aye. There were no opposed or abstentions. The minutes were approved unanimously as presented. 

Mr. Simpkins next presented the 2024 Annual Certification Form (Form 26). He requested approval to submit Form 26 as presented certifying the stated certifications and providing the requested information. 

Mr. Mclnturff made a motion to accept Form 26 as presented and file the form with the State Ethics Commission. Ms. Jones seconded the motion. Those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Goldsborough asked ifanyone had any new business to present. No concerns were noted. 

With no further business, at 10:04 a.m., the meeting of the Ethics Commission adjourned upon a motion made by Mr. Satchell and seconded by Mr. McInturff. The motion carried unanimously by those present.

R. Dean Goldsborough, Chair
