BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY SPECIAL SESSION Tuesday July 8, 2025 

At 2:22 p.m., the Board entered into Special Session. Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Darryl Webster and Commissioner Craig Mathies. 

Also, in attendance were Interim County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., Public Works Director Woody Bames and Executive Aide Lory Ebron. 

Closed Session Summary Report for July 8, 2025 

On July 8, 2025, from 2:50 p.m. until 3:34 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., and seconded by Commissioner Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. President Charles Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster voted in favor by show of hands, therefore the motion carried 5-0. 

Present at the Closed Session meeting were Interim County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., EMS Chief Joel Dixon, HR Director Jamie Manaia, and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron. Topics discussed were: (1) Salary for recent EMS Assistant Chief and Office Assistant IIT hires (2) Disciplinary Suspension/Probation. Actions Taken: (1) Approval for recent EMS hire salaries (2) Approval for disciplinary suspension/probation.

###### Capital Projects 

Mr. John Redden (attending by phone), Mr. Jesse Drewer, Mr. Woody Barnes and Mr. Joel Dixon were present to discuss consideration of contract approval for the EMS Headquarters project. Mr. Redden explained that after looking at several different sites, the decision was made to chose the field owned by the County near the Civic Center as the new site for the EMS facility. Being presented today for the project were two contract proposals. The first from AWB regarding design services (architectural and engineering) for the new facility in the amount of $117,960. It

was explained that drawings provided that showed the design, similar to what Talbot County (plans provided) has. Attomey Simpkins has reviewed the proposal and found it legally sufficient. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve the proposal from AWB for architectural and engineering design for $117,960 as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By showofhands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr. Redden presented the proposal from Parker and Associates for site work to be performed at the chosen site for the EMS Facility. The site work proposal is for $39,750. Attorney Simpkins also reviewed this proposal and found it legally sufficient. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve the site design proposal received from Parker and Associates for site work in the amount of $39,750 as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of<sup>hands,themotioncarried5-0.</sup> 

Mr. Clint Sterling came forward at this time to present the proposal received for site and construction plans regarding the Somerset Athletic Complex on Sam Bames Road. Mr. Redden explained that two years ago a concept plan to expand the complex was considered that would include additional fields, paths, additional parking, tot lot, pickleball and tennis courts and additional bathroom facility as envisioned by Mr. Sterling. The design work proposal, at a cost of $74,275 was shown to the Board, maximizing what can be done on the site. Mr. Sterling advised that a grant for $1 million from the Local Parks and Playground Infrastructure will be used to fund the project, advising that the need exists. Mr. Sterling added that the approval has been received from Board of Public Works as well and Attorney Simpkins has reviewed the proposal for legal sufficiency. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the proposal for site design from Parker and Associates in the amount of $74,275 for site and construction plans for the Somerset Athletic Complex. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0.

##### Solid Waste Department 

Mr. Barnes commended Mr. Gary Kitching who has done a tremendous job at the Landfill. Trash pick-up has made a huge difference on the hills, fences and entire facility using man-power to coordinate and get it completed. Maryland Department of the Environment has recently performed an inspection, complimenting the work done and stating that the county is heading in the right direction. Mr. Barnes explained that wood chips have been removed, as required and they are in the process of removing scrap metal debris. The plan has been updated to include allowable things that were being done, but were not a part of the original plan. 

##### Drainage Department 

Drainage work has been completed on Champ Road, Riley Roberts Road, Champ Wharf Road, Tulls Comer Road and Sackertown Road. 

Last, Mr. Barnes advised that he has visited all county facilities, creating a list of concerns, and advising that most of the concerns have been addressed. He stated his efforts are to fix things correctly so to eliminate the need of having to keep going back to re-fix. 

Commissioner Webster asked if staff is tracking county dumping at the landfill. Mr. Barnes advised that staff is monitoring the trucks more closely by engaging with the drivers to learn where their trash is coming from. They are also spot checking. He also explained that he is working on the new landfill location, with the expectancy to be at full capacity by 2030. The time frame for the new site is 3-10 years. 

Commissioner Willing asked about off-road drainage on the Jones property. Mr. Barnes explained that the property is on the list, which is sorted in order of<sup>priority.AllCommissioners</sup> expressed their gratitude to Mr. Barnes for what he is doing.
