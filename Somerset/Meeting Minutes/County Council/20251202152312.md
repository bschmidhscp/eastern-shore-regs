BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, FOR SOMERSET COUNTY November 18, 2025 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Interim County Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 11-18-25 

Ms. Yvette Cross, Emergency Services Director, came forward to present the recommendation to award the bid regarding the UPS Replacement in the 911 Center Radio Room. She explained that after advertising the solicitation for bids in Delmarva Now and eMaryland Marketplace, one bid was received from Carber Power Inc., in the amount of $70,708.00; a vendor who the county has used before, and who has worked closely with our IT department. Additionally, this vendor provides ongoing maintenance for the current units in place. Following review of the bid submission, it was found to satisfy all operational standards and was in compliance with the State’s run time requirements for 911 Centers. Ms. Cross advised that $70,000 was budgeted for this project, which was bid at $70,708 and therefore $708.00 over budget. She requested the Boards approval to award the project to Carber Power and authorize the additional allocation of $708.00 to fully fund the project. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to accept the proposal from Carber Power in the amount of $70,708 and authorize the additional allocation as presented. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, DTCS Director, came forward at this time. 

Mr. Drewer first presented a State Clearinghouse Review, MD20251030-0696 for coding consideration. He explained that this is the CDBG Somerset County Housing Rehabilitation Grant targeted to low-and moderate-income individuals. Mr. Drewer recommended a C5 code (It is consistent with our plans, programs, and objectives). 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve coding Clearinghouse Review project MD20251010-0696 as C5. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr. Drewer presented a letter received from the Maryland Department of the Environment about proposed changes to COMAR pertaining to nontidal wetland mitigation regulations and proposed to better align state regulations with federal regulations. The proposed changes have the potential to benefit development in the county through the allowance of creating wetland mitigation banks within the county. 

Last, Mr. Drewer reminded the Board of vacancies on various boards and commissions 

under his department as follows: 

- Agricultural Reconciliation Board- 2 seats open for civilians unaffiliated with farming or forestry operations - Agricultural Preservation Advisory Board — 1 seat open

Mr. Josh Taylor, Sanitary District General Manager came forward. He explained that he had previously attended a meeting to obtain County guarantee approval to purchase three trucks for the Sanitary District, totaling $150,000. However, after purchasing a F350 and a F150, it was found that a third used truck was not needed, and following re-evaluation, a dump trailer for hauling solids is now being requested. The cost for a dump trailer is $18,495, which will cost $2,000 less than the used truck. Mr. Taylor requested board approval to modify the loan request to purchase the dump trailer instead of a used truck as presented. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve the loan modification to purchase a 

dump trailer in place of another used truck. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Woody Barnes, Public Works Director, came forward, with Mr. John Redden, County 

Engineer attending by phone, to present the proposals received regarding EMS Station 

renovations. Bids were solicited and received from four vendors as follows: 

- George, Miles & Buhr, LLC $42,250.00 - Davis, Bowen & Friedel Inc $87,800.00 - Becker Morgan Group $135,000.00 - AWB Engineers $138,800.00 

Mr. Barnes explained the work will consist of design and documentation of approximately 

7500 square feet apportioned for vehicle apparatus storage, offices, living quarters and support areas for EMS use and meeting ALL code and structural uses. The time frame allows for 30-day submittal, and 75-day construction. The varying range of bid submissions was questioned. Mr. Barnes advised the higher bid submissions included structural and exterior design. Mr. Barnes recommended the bid be awarded to George, Miles & Buhr for $42,250.00. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to award the bid to George, Miles and Buhr for $42,250.00. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Leatherbury continued with correspondence and discussion items.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 11-18-25 

Letters were sent to Mr. Jack Mills, Humane Society, approving their request for additional appropriation, Holly Grove Mennonite Church approving their building fee waiver request and to Frank Parks approving their BZA application fee waiver request. 

A letter of support was requested regarding the Industrial Park project to submit an extension regarding unspent funds. The extension was formally requested by Mr. Danny Thompson, EDC, but also requires a letter of support from the Board of Commissioners. 

Commissioner Willing made a motion to approve the letter of support regarding an extension request for unspent funds for the Industrial Park as submitted. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Last, Mr. Leatherbury requested approval to nominate Mr. John French as the Somerset County Representative to serve on the new Somers Cove Marina Advisory Commission. It was noted that Mr. French served previously, and has expressed his interest to continue serving. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to appoint John French to serve on the Somers Cove Marina Advisory Commission. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0.
