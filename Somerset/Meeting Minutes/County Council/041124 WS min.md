## Somerset County Sanitary Commission 

Work Session Minutes of April 11, 2024 

A Work Session meeting of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, April 11" 2024 at 11:00 a.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member and Barbara Somers, Member. Adam Riggin, Chairman was absent. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, Manager, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, and Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide. 

Ms. Mister presented the Commission with following budget highlights.
