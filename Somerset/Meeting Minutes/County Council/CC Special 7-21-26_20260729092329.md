BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, FOR SOMERSET COUNTY SPECIAL SESSION Tuesday, July 21, 2026 

At 2:20 p.m., the Board entered into Special Session, for the purpose of entering into Closed Session. Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Darryl Webster and Commissioner Craig Mathies. President Charles Laird was absent from today’s meeting. 

Also, in attendance were County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory Ebron. 

Closed Session Summary Report for July 21, 2026 

On July 21, 2026, from 2:20 p.m. until 3:36 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Craig N Mathies, Sr., the board entered into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. President Charles Laird was absent from today’s meeting. All those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 4-0. Present for the mecting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Darryl Webster and Commissioner Craig Mathies. County Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr., Executive Aide Lory Ebron, Ms. Jamie Manaia, HR Director, Mr. Woody Bames, Public Works Director, Mr. Cory Polidore, Deputy EMS Chief, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, EDC Director Danny Thompson. Topics discussed were: (1) Employee Resignation (2) Hiring for Full Time Paramedic, Part Time Paramedic (3) Job Description for MEO III/ Crew Leader (4) Employee Promotion (5) Letter of Support Request (6) Consult with Counsel. Actions Taken: (1) Approval to hire Full Time Paramedic (2) Approval to hire three Part Time Paramedics (3) Approval for Job Description for MEO III/Crew Leader (4) Approval to promote employee (5) Approval for Letter of Support (6) Approval following legal counsel.
