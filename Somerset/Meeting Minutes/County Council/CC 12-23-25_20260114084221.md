Chief Joel Dixon, joined by Assistant Chief Cory Polidore presented two policies drafted for consideration of Board approval. The first policy presented was titled “Ambulance Hardship Assistance Guidelines”. Mr. Dixon explained that this policy is designed to aid by allowing a

<!-- page 3 -->
reduction of the ambulance transport fee due to certain definable circumstances of the person receiving the service who may not have the means to pay the ambulance transport fee due to circumstances out of their control. Guidelines are provided within the policy, with it explained that the reduction will be based by percentage in accordance with Federal poverty guidelines. 

The second policy, titled “Volunteer Member Billing Policy” was created to recognize the assistance and dedication that active volunteer firefighters and their families, as well as lifemembers have provided to Somerset County. It was explained that the guidelines proposed will allow for the discount of the ambulance service fee for an eligible volunteer member or family member. It was noted that if no insurance exists, the fee will be written off. Attorney Simpkins has reviewed both policies, and found them acceptable. President Charles Laird commended both policies. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve the Ambulance Hardship Assistance Guidelines policy as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of<sup>hands,the motioncarried5-0.</sup> 

Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., made a motion to approve the Volunteer Member Billing Policy as presented. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

The Board was advised for the need of a Closed Session.
