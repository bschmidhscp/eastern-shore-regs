Board of Commissioners Meeting 7-29-25 

Vice President Randy Laird called the Public Hearing to open at 2:03 p.m. to receive public 

comment on the Ewell Recreation Grant Close Out. Mr. Drewer was asked to read the notice of Public Hearing into record as follows: 

NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING Second Hearing for MD-24-CD-05 

Tuesday, July 29 at 2pm 

County Commissioners Meeting Room 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111 Princess Anne, Maryland 21853 

Somerset County will conduct a Public Hearing to provide information on the progress of Rehabilitation of the Ewell Recreation and Community Center (MD-24-CD-5) which was funded under the Maryland Community Development Block Grant Program which is a federally funded program designed to assist governments with activities directed toward neighborhood and housing revitalization, community development, and improved community facilities and services. 

Efforts will be made to accommodate the disabled and non-English speaking residents with 3 days advance notice to Jesse Drewer, DTCS Director (410-651-1424). 

Emest J. Leatherbury, Deputy County Administrator 

Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County 

Mr. Drewer explained that in March 2024, Somerset County was awarded a $120,000 Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) for the rehabilitation of the Ewell Recreation and Community Center. An additional grant award was received in April 2025 ($16,795) to complete the second phase. The breakdown of funding for this project was $131,795 received for the public facility funds and $5,000 for administration. Mr. Drewer detailed the work completed during each phase, advising that the total cost for Phase I was $99,695 completed in October 2024 and Phase Il, completed in June 2025, was completed at a cost of $32,100. The grant project administration expenses totaled $2,291.93, therefore, the grand total expended for this project was $134,086.93, spent of the $136,795.00 grant total. A more detailed breakdown was provided within the agenda packet as well. 

Vice President Randy Laird asked if there were any proponent present wishing to speak 

There were none.

Ms. Muir advised that written public comment will be taken for 10 day's until Friday, August 8, 2025 at 4:30 p.m. Vice President Randy Laird declared the public hearing closed at 2:06 p.m.

Mr. Drewer also reminded the commissioners that two vacancies remain on this board; one for plumber and one for any construction trade. This is a seven-member board who serve three-year terms.

Mr. Robert Wells, 11966 Jeffrey Lane, Princess Anne MD, a concerned tax payer, requested to address the board. Mr. Wells first questioned the $6 Million budgeted by the county to operate the newly acquired EMS department, yet Fruitland and Allen continue to respond to calls for Somerset County. He stated he knows there are kinks in the system, but $6 Million is into the project and he’s not seeing results. Second, he questioned where the rest of the money will be coming from to cover the services in the future. Third, he questioned the county’s hiring practices for selecting people for positions, noting that this is probably a personnel issue that can’t be discussed here. The SWOT committee formed intended to have qualified people from the county hired for these positions. He advised that he applied for a position and was told he was not properly dressed for the position, noting that he was dressed as he is today, inquiring if his dress is considered appropriate. He also stated that the SWOT analysis recommended an oversight committee be formed, which has not been done yet. These are concerns he had. He closed his comments requesting to receive direction on how to address the county’s hiring process, and thanked the Board for their time. 

With no further business, a motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing to adjourn the meeting at 2:23 p.m. and enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
