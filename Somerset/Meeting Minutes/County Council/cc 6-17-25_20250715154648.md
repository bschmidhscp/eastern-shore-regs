Mr. Drewer explained that on May 27, 2025 the Somerset County Planning Commission held a Special Session Public Hearing regarding proposed text amendments required regarding the Somerset County Zoning Code in order to be in compliance with state law recently passed (SB931/Renewable Energy Certainty Act). It was noted that 95% of the changes involve new “solar” language, however the changes will: 

- Eliminate the Solar Energy Facility (SEF) Floating Zone 

- - Update definitions for solar facilities and energy storage devised - Establish new zoning regulations for solar energy facilities 

Mr. Drewer advised that these revisions will bring Somerset County’s Zoning Ordinance in compliance with SB931. Additionally, there were two “housekeeping” items included in the revision that will clarify language for the placement of mobile/manufactured homes and include a definition of Special Events for the concurrence with the Critical Area Ordinance. 

President Charles Laird asked if there were any proponents present wishing to speak. There 

were none. 

President Charles Laird asked if there were any opponents present wishing to speak. There were none.
