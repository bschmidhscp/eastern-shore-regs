BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

November 4, 2025 

At 6:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Interim County Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

## Closed Session Summary Report 

On Tuesday, November 4, 2025, at 7:03 p.m., until 7:38 p.m., President Charles Laird, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird entered into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies were also present. All those present voted in favor by show ofhands, therefore, the motion carried 5-0. 

Also present were Interim County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury, Jr., Attorney Kirk Simpkins, Internal Auditor Sharon Muir, HR Director Jamie Manaia, EMS Deputy Chief Cory Polidore and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron. Topics discussed: (1) Consult with Counsel (2) Employee suspension. Approved: (1) Approval to suspend employee.
