Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of October 12 2023 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, October 12, 2023 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Chairman, Adam Riggin, Vice-Chairman, Charles Bagley, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, and Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, there were no Public Attendees. 

Mr. Riggin called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

###### General Information 

Ashley Stern, CPA/Partner and Alyssa Revell, Manager, PKS & Company, presented the Commission with the Fiscal Year 2023 Audit Report of the Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. Ms. Stern started the presentation by crediting the Sanitary Office Staff for their promptness, as well as their administrative and organizational skills. Ms. Stern reviewed the draft of the report with Mr. Stockus and Ms. Mister prior to this meeting. Ms. Stern stated that this year the District had two Audits, Independent Auditor's Report on Internal Control over Financial Reporting as well as an Independent Auditors’ Report on Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards. Both Audit Reports were in accordance with the auditing standards generally issued by the Comptroller General of the United States. The Auditors conducted the Audit consistent within the planned scope of timing. There were no instances of noncompliance material to the financial statements of the District, which would be required to be reported in accordance with Government Auditing Standards. Ms. Stern stated the audit report is presented with an unmodified Opinion, which is the highest opinion available. The Commission had no questions for Ms. Stern. A copy of the FY 2023 Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. Audit may be obtained on the Somerset County Website, www.somersetmd.us 

Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project by stating the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) is still being reviewed by the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE). 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project, stating Josh Taylor, DBF, has forwarded the letter stating DBF’s recommendation for treatment by combining Princess Anne’s two largest wells with carbon and blending that water with the PASE tower water to Alex McManimen at MDE for review. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating construction will begin in November with completion projected by the end of January 2024.

##### Advertisement of Position 

Mr. Stockus is requesting the Broad’s approval to advertise the position of Field Operations Superintendent. Earl Ludy is planning to retire from the SCSD July 2024, Mr. Ludy’s position as the Superintendent is essential to the District’s Operation. This position may be challenging to fill with the training and experience required, Management would like to be proactive in the fulfillment of this position by advertising the Superintendent position prior to Mr. Ludy’s retirement in an effort to train the new hire as much as possible before Mr. Ludy’s exit. Mr. Bagley made a motion to approve the advertisement for the position of Field Operations Superintendent (effective immediately). Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

##### Princess Anne — Action 

# WWTP Influent Pump Repair 

Mr. Ludy requested the Board approve an estimate from Hill’s Electric to repair an Influent Pump at the WWTP. The estimate is for $8,831.48, a new pump is $25,574.40. This Pump repair is needed to move the influent sewage out of the raw sewage wet well and not backup the sewer mains in Princess Anne. The Pump was originally installed in 2002 and has never been serviced. Mr. Bagley made a motion to approve the estimate of $8,831.48 for the repair of the WWTP Influent Pump. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

Fairmount — Action 

#### Request for sewer credit 

Sister Winifred, Ascension Hermitage and Sanctuary, is requesting a sewer credit for their property located at 27548 Fairmount Road. Sister Winifred explained in her letter, on June 16'" an employee was tending to their horses when a thunderstorm erupted he left quickly and forgot to turn off the outside hose. Sister Winifred discovered the outside spigot running the next morning and turned off. The Sanitary District performed an audit confirming the usage. Management is recommending crediting the sewer portion ($25.40) based on their average usage since the water did not enter the sewer system. Mr. Bagley made a motion to credit the sewer based on Management's recommendation. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval. 

### 2:00 p.m. Appointment 

Bret and Kurt Davis, Owners of Simple Fiber, were present to request the Commission consider letting their company use the Dr. William Hytche Blvd Pump Station Site as a

central equipment building location for their company to provide internet to a wider range in Princess Anne than their current location. They received a grant from the County to extend these internet services throughout Princess Anne. They explained they would provide their own internet, electric, and simply need space to set a concrete pad for a 10 x 20 foot sized building. Bret Davis offered free fiber internet to the Sanitary District for lifetime in exchange for using the land. Kurt Davis described some of the work they have done in the past with other agencies, such as the City of Salisbury, to provide fiber internet to different areas on the Shore. Mr. Ludy expressed that the lot they are requesting to use would not be large enough for an additional structure. He instead suggested they view the Irving Well Site, also own by the Sanitary District, as that lot is adequate in size for their purpose. Bret and Kurt Davis thanked the Commission for their time and stated they would look into the Irving Street lot and could possibly have plans and a proposal to present to the Commission next month. Assuming they will pursue the lot on Irving Street, the Commission were unanimously open to negotiating a deal, in depth at a later date, in an effort to provide high speed fiber internet to the area, with the understanding that all expenses associated would be at Simple Fiber's expense.
