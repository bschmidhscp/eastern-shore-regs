# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY APRIL 2, 2024** 

At 1:00 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for the start of today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for April 2, 2024</u>** 

On April 2, 2024, from 1:01 p.m. until 1:17 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Commissioner Craig Mathies was absent for today’s meeting. Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez, and Attorney Robin Cockey were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Personnel/Legal; Actions Taken: Confidential. 

Vice President Randy Laird asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

The Board was advised for the need of a closed session. 

Therefore, at 1:01 p.m., on a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of
