# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY JUNE 25, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m. the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County met in Special Session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Commissioner Eldon Willing was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Craig Mathies to lead those in attendance in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

At this time, the Board invited MACo President, County Executive Johnny Olszewski, Executive Director Michael Sanderson, and Legislative Director Kevin Kinnally to come forward for the MACo Annual Visit. Mr. Sanderson provided information on the State Budget and their multi-year commitment to education; advising that the State’s blueprint is different from how the County’s are expected to fund schools. He also addressed revenue structures for transportation funding issues but advised that the Highway User Revenue funds are in good standing. Mr. Kinnally then addressed the Board, advising of a multi-year initiative for fire fighter retention; stating how they worked on a bill to provide free tuition at community colleges and discounts at four-year colleges. He then addressed the Governor’s affordable housing initiative before moving into the issue of solar and how it should not solely be on the Eastern Shore. A brief discussion was held in regards to different options for solar on the Western Shore. Mr. Kinnally then spoke on the cannabis zoning bill and provided follow-up information as to the distance from sensitive sites and the limits not being stronger that those in place for alcoholic beverage sellers. 

The Board then entered into a discussion regarding their frustrations with solar and, in turn, the loss of farmland/production of food. A brief discussion was held about EMS services provided to ECI, and the MACo representatives were advised that ECI was utilizing a private firm until they were dropped due to allegedly not paying for the services, to which they are now utilizing the EMS services within the County but not paying for those as well; creating a burden for the County. A discussion was held in regards to the State’s Blueprint, the School Board, and funding. 

The Board then thanked Mr. Olszewski, Mr. Sanderson, and Mr. Kinnally for attending today’s meeting and the information they provided.

SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS SPECIAL SESSION: TUESDAY JUNE 25, 2024 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Services, and Mr. Rick Nelson, Chesapeake Housing Mission, were now before the Board to present an amendment to the CDBG funding request. Mr. Drewer advised that in an effort to fully expand the Housing Rehabilitation Grant, and to serve low-to-moderate income citizens of the County, they would like to request to an amendment to the grant to reallocate $50,000.00 of the funds to the construction of ramps that will be built in partnership with Chesapeake Housing Mission. Mr. Nelson expressed his thanks to the Board for their previous funding throughout the years and advised that the goal of the Chesapeake Housing Mission is to get folks in and out of their homes safely. The Board inquired if the previous funding provided had been spent in the County, to which Mr. Nelson advised that 90% of the funds were spent within the County (some materials were purchased outside of the County but they do their best to stay local). Mr. Drewer advised that a public hearing will be needed to the amendment to the grant. 

Upon the conclusion of the discussion, a motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to authorize President Charles Laird’s signature on the letter to Ms. Cindy Stone requesting the reallocation of funds. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Drewer and Mr. Nelson left the meeting. 

Ms. Danielle Weber, Somerset County Health Officer, Ms. Christie Taylor, Somerset County Health Department Director of Administrative Services, and Ms. Faith Redmond, Director of Finance, were now before the Board to request the purchase of a John Deere Gator (with a trailer) with the extra collection money in the Environmental Health Department. Of the three quotes they received, the lowest price is $15,152.27. This Gator would assist the Environmental Health staff with traveling on properties where the grass is very high and not landscaped yet (areas where trucks cannot travel and/or would get stuck). 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to approve the purchase of the Gator and trailer for $15,152.27 using the extra collection money in the Environmental Health Department. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Ms. Weber, Ms. Taylor, and Ms. Redmond left the meeting.

SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS SPECIAL SESSION: TUESDAY JUNE 25, 2024 

Ms. Sharon Muir, Internal Auditor, and Ms. Jamie Manaia, Director of Human Resources, were now before the Board to present the Time to Care Act Collaborative Consultant Agreement. Ms. Muir provided a brief summary for the Board of the Time to Care Act and the Board’s actions to-date. The key objective of this collaborative is to identify the premium rate from the most qualified private insurance carrier before participating employers need to petition the Department of Labor to opt-out of the State Plan. The County has received a request from Bolton (a MD based employee benefits and actuarial firm that is supporting the TCA Collaborative in the procurement process) to provide employee census data that will be included in the Request for Proposal. Ms. Muir then advised that she is requesting the President’s signature on the Consultant Confidentiality Agreement submitted to the County by Bolton for protection of the County’s confidential employee census information. Ms. Muir further advised that this document has been reviewed by County Attorney Kirk Simpkins and has found it to be legally sufficient. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to authorize President Charles Laird’s signature on the Maryland Time to Care Act Public Sector Collaborative Consultant Confidentiality Agreement. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Ms. Muir and Ms. Manaia left the meeting. 

Warden Muir was now before the Board to request the renewal of the Wellpath medical contract for the Somerset County Detention Center. Warden Muir advised that while they have received outstanding healthcare from Wellpath, they have increased the price of agreement by $332,386.00. With that, he would like to reach out to Prime Care and received a quote from them to see if they could provide medical services at a lower cost. 

At the conclusion of the discussion, the Board inquired about roadside trash clean up. Warden Muir advised that they are actively working on roads. 

Following this, a motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to accept and authorize the President’s signature on the Wellpath medical contract renewal for the upcoming fiscal year. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Warden Muir left the meeting.

SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS SPECIAL SESSION: TUESDAY JUNE 25, 2024

Therefore, at 3:08 pm, on  a motion made by Vice Randy Laird and seconded Commissioner Craig Mathies, and carried on a vote in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Darryl Webster the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
