# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY DECEMBER 10, 2024** 

At 2:10 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird call the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez.

Mr. Taylor acknowledged Senator Marybeth Carozza and Delegate Charles Otto and thanked them for attending today’s meeting to discussed Proposed Legislation for 2025. He then began by revisiting a proposal to reintroduce an annual fee on approved properties to support emergency medical services (EMS); building on legislation that passed last year authorizing an Emergency Services Advisory Council and funding for volunteer fire companies. The Board advised of the urgent need to address funding shortfalls as volunteer EMS services struggle with declining membership and coverage gaps, leading to delays in emergency response times. They clarified that the request is for authority to impose a dedicated EMS fee (or tax) in the future, not for immediate implementation. Transparency on how funds would be used was emphasized, with a preference for a dedicated EMS tax over a general property tax to improve public understanding. While there are no plans for the county to take over EMS services, the Board stressed the need for a long-term strategy and expressed concerns about the state’s limited support. 

A request was presented to resubmit the bill regarding allowing veterans' organizations and service clubs to purchase alcohol from retail outlets instead of being restricted to specific distributors. Delegate Otto advised that pushback is expected due to the state's well-established three-tier alcohol distribution system. Senator Carozza advised that there are no simple alcohol bills and the consideration of working directly with the alcohol board to resolve the issues without the need for legislation. 

Next, Mr. Taylor presented a proposal to increase salaries for County Commissioners and the Sheriff to bring them in line with other counties. Under the proposal, Commissioners' salaries would rise from $20,000 to $24,000, with the President's salary increasing from $22,000 to $26,000. The Sheriff’s salary would increase from $85,000 to $105,000. These proposed salaries (should they pass through legislation) would not go into effect until the next election. 

Lastly, Mr. Taylor advised of the need for an update to the Somerset County Maryland Code of Public Local Laws, which has not been updated since 2016. 

Senator Carozza inquired of any updates regarding the meeting planned with Secretary Scruggs. Mr. Leatherbury advised that the Commissioners’ office is working with the Secretary’s office to reschedule this meeting. A brief discussion was held regarding the critical staffing shortages at ECI.

Vice President Randy Laird inquired about Highway User Funds. Senator Carozza advised that the budget situation is severe and this topic will be addressed during the MACo Winter Conference. 

The Board briefly addressed issues concerning EMS services and the importance of this legislation for the safety and well-being of Somerset County citizens. 

The Board thanked Senator Carozza and Delegate Otto once more for attending today’s meeting to discuss proposed legislation. 

Senator Carozza and Delegate Otto left the meeting. 

Mr. Will Cornish, Assistant Director of Technical and Community Services, was now before the Board to present Ordinance #1205; the proposed Zoning Code Text Amendment regarding Antenna & Wireless Telecommunication Facilities. Mr. Cornish advised that no comments have been received from the pubic hearing held on November 19, 2024. 

With that, a motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve Ordinance #1205 as presented. By a round of ayes in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster the motion carried. 

Mr. Cornish left the meeting. 

The meeting was then opened for public comments and Mr. Bob McIntyre approached the podium echoing the Board’s concern regarding EMS services. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 3:00 pm, on  a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and carried on a vote in favor by round of ayes by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
