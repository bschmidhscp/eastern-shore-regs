#### BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

May 7, 2024 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present.

Next, Mr. Charles Cavanaugh, Director of Solid Waste and Maintenance came forward; first presenting the results of the review performed regarding bids received for the Somerset County Sheriff's Office HVAC Renovations. Mr. Cavanaugh advised that County Engineer John Redden has reviewed both bids received (Joe Richardson/$689,000 and Wilfre Company/$715,000) and has recommended the low bidder, Richardson at $689,000 be awarded the project. It was noted that the project has been budgeted into the FY25 budget (advising that it was initially budgeted for $650,000); and it will be at least 6 weeks to get equipment, with the project probably starting after July 1. Commissioner Mathies questioned if this is a project that must be done. Mr. Cavanaugh explained that the current HVAC system in the building is not efficient, utilizing a 45-year old boiler. Additionally, there are humidity problems with the current system, and this new more efficient system will be a propane system with new equipment. Duct work will not be replaced. President Laird stated that this is as a county building needing a renovated HVAC system. Mr. Cavanaugh added that Gipe and Associates designed the proposed system, which will be more efficient. 

Following discussion, a motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird to approve the bid received from Joe Richardson in the amount of $689,000 for HVAC renovations at the Somerset County Sheriff's Office building. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Next, Mr. Cavanaugh presented for discussion, the sole bid received for the Somerset County Landfill replacement of four sediment pond dewatering and drawdown devices. Muller, Inc., submitted a bid in the amount of $76,000, with Mr. Cavanaugh noting the $80,000 had been budgeted. He did advise that certificate of insurance was not included with the bid as submitted, but has since been received. Mr. Cavanaugh also advised that a second bid was received; but it was 15 minutes late (cut off time was stated as 12 pm, and posted in three different areas in the specs). Muller Inc., is out of Reston Virginia, and Mr. Cavanaugh advised he was not able to find other jobs done by this company. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve the bid received for the landfill sediment pond dewatering and drawdown devices from Muller Inc for $76,200. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Last, Mr. Cavanaugh discussed the two bids received regarding the Princess Anne Library HVAC Replacement Project. He advised that the equipment is ancient, expensive and failing, Advising that $30,000 had been budgeted, and following review of both bid submissions, he made

Next, the Board was made aware that Mr. John Crockett of Somerset Heating and Air has contacted the Department of Technical and Community Services (DTCS) expressing his interest to fill the vacancy on the Building and Plumbing Codes Appeals Board, which currently has two vacancies; one for plumber and one for any construction trade (Builder, HVAC, Plumber or Electrical). A letter of support request was received from the nephew of Private First-Class Donald Harold Selby, Mr. Brian Jones, regarding his efforts to have a sign dedication on a state road in honor of his uncle’s service. PFC Selby was killed in action during the Korean War, and his remains have never been recovered. The letter, if approved, will be included in the application being submitted by Mr. Jones. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve the letter of support as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Also, a letter of support request was received from the City ofCrisfield, who is applying for a grant for $50,000 to support a Community Rating System Coordinator that will serve to help the community needs regarding flood mitigation and insurance aspects of the National Flood Insurance Program. This position is similar to the one our own DTCS department is working towards creating as well. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the letter of support for the City of Crisfield as presented. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

A request to waive landfill tipping fees was received from the Princess Anne Volunteer Fire Company who are replacing roof shingles on their firehouse. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve the tipping fee waiver as requested by the Princess Anne Fire Company. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands

Last, Mr. Taylor presented the current Somerset County Boat Slip Rental Fees for the county. The last increase to slip fees was made in 2019, and were increased across the board by $25.00. Comparison to other county fees was reviewed, and it was noted that Somerset County fees were falling below what other counties are charging. 

Following discussion, Commissioner Mathies made a motion to increase the yearly boat slip fees as follows; boat slips having water or electric will increase by $100 annually, and slips without water and electric will increase by $75.00. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0.
