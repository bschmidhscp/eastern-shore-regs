SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS SPECIAL SESSION: TUESDAY FEBRUARY 20, 2024 

# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY FEBRUARY 20, 2024** 

At 2:38 p.m., the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County met in Special Session on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing; carried by a unanimous show of hands in favor by all present Board members. Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for February 20, 2024</u>** 

On February 20, 2024, from 2:55 p.m. until 3:55 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for today’s meeting. Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, Attorney Robin Cockey, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Legal; Actions Taken: None; (2) Personnel; Actions Taken: Employment. 

Mr. Vernon Spriggs, Executive Director of Somerset County LMB, was before the Board to request the current LMB Bylaws to be re-signed (due to a change in Executive Directors) as they are required to be submitted to Inuit for accounting purposes. The Board inquired if there were any significant changes to the Bylaws to which Mr. Spriggs advised there were not.

Commissioner Craig Mathies helped to further clarify the reasoning for this action; stating that with the change in leadership at the LMB and this action is legally required. 

A motion was then made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the Board’s signatures on the LMB Bylaws as presented. Commissioner Craig Mathies abstained from the vote. Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster voted in favor; therefore, the motion carried. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the Board’s signatures on Resolution #1197 as presented.  Commissioner Craig Mathies abstained from the vote. Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster voted in favor; therefore, the motion carried. 

Mr. Spriggs and Mr. Ballard left the meeting. 

Mr. Leatherbury advised of correspondence sent and received. 

Under discussion items, Mr. Leatherbury advised of three Somerset County Legislative Letters of Support (HB0975 – Fire, Rescue, and Emergency Medical Services; SB0829/HB976 – Increase to Somerset County Fire Companies; HB977 – Sale of Property on Revells Neck Road) and requested the Board’s approval for the President’s signature on those letters. 

A motion was made by Commissioners Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve President Charles Laird’s signature on the Somerset County Legislative Letters of Support as presented. Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster voted in favor; therefore, the motion carried. 

Mr. Leatherbury then advised of two Somerset County Letters of Opposition (SB0537 – Cannabis – License Locations – Restrictions; HB1407 – County Tier 1 Renewable Sources – Generating Systems, Capacity, and Generation – Prohibition, Studies, and Plans) and requested the Board’s approval for the President’s signature on those letters. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve President Charles Laird’s signature on the Somerset County Legislative Letters of Opposition as presented. Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster voted in favor; therefore, the motion carried. 

The Board was advised for the need of a closed session.
