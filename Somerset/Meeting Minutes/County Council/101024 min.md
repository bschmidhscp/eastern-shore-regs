Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of October 10, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, October 10, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, and Maxine Landon, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, Chris Derbyshire, P.E., George Miles & Buhr and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. The Crisfield (Peripheral Area) Board position is currently vacant. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Information 

Anthony Stockus was presented with a plaque recognizing 10 years of service with the Sanitary District as the General Manager. Mr. Stockus served as a Board Member on the SCSD Commission for 8 years prior to succeeding Robin Street as the General Manager in 2014. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

During the September 2024 SCSD Commission meeting Mr. Stockus stated to the Board, regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project, Pilot Testing Agreement/Proposal by Fluence (water treatment solution corporation) is no longer happening. Mr. Stockus stated he is planning to meet with Jason Loar, P.E., DBF, to discuss the alternatives listed in the preliminary engineering report for enhanced nutrient removal. 

Mr. Stockus stated the District has officially received notification from MDE regarding their FFY 2024 Intended Use Plan for the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF). The Princess Anne Color Removal Project ranked 5" on the priority scale, funding $5,090,200 of the requested $6,590,000 (leaving a shortage of $1.5 million). The funding package includes DWSRF base loan of $750,000, BIL (Bipartisan Infrastructure Law) general loan of $1,795,100, and principal forgiveness of $2,545,100. In the coming weeks MDE will be sending the District requirements and first steps in the DWSRF process. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating the electric and plumbing connections are outstanding and the cabinet inspections are delayed.

### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP) stating the WWTP Upgrade progress meeting was held on October 2" 2024 onsite where construction of the Plant is 68% complete. Regarding the Subaqueous Force Main (SFM) project, construction is set to begin again on the Island. A progress meeting for the SFM is scheduled for October 15'" 2024 on-site. 

Robert Floros, resident on Marsh Road, reached out via email to SCSD concerned with the condition of the lot owned by the District on Marsh Road. Mr. Floros stated the lot was severely overgrown and encroaching into his yard. He also offered to bush hog the property himself for a fee. Ms. Landon also questioned why the lot had not been maintained as agreed upon when the mowing request for qualifications (RFQ) was rewarded last year. Ms. Landon provided pictures of the current condition. Mr. Stockus explained he was unaware of how severe the lot condition was until recently. He also explained the RFQ for mowing this lot was done incorrectly and only included maintaining the lot, cleaning up the debris and removing stumps were not included. Mr. Stockus stated the District will need to revise and reissue a RFQ for leveling the property, which may include barging equipment. Then the District will issue a RFQ for a maintenance (mowing) contract. Mr. Stockus assured Ms. Landon the District will address the lot’s condition.

## **<u>Approval of Plans</u>** 

Parker and Associates have presented the District with a Site Plan for approval of 1 EDU of water and sewer for Skipjack Seafood Market to be built on Market Lane. Josh Taylor, DBF, and Rich Pagette reviewed the plans and had requested a grease trap be installed.

### Crisfield — Action 

# Request for revised payment agreement 

Deborah Jackson, 4312 Crisfield Highway, is requesting a new payment plan to help her afford her water bill. Ms. Jackson’s usage during the March 2024 and June 2024 quarters were very high and unexplainable. The District reread and audited the meter to ensure it was reading correctly. Usage since has resumed to normal. Unfortunately, the bill must stand if there’s no explanation for the usage or leaks detected and repaired. Ms. Jackson currently has a payment agreement with the Office Manager for a repaired leak last year. Management recommended offering a revised 2-year interest free payment plan. Ms. Landon made a motion to amend the current payment plan for 4312 Crisfield Highway by renewing the interest free 2-year term upon approval with the current balance. Mr. Williams seconded the motion, the motion carried unanimously to approval.
