# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY OCTOBER 13, 2026** 

At 2:22 pm, President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was not present for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly 

Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for October 13, 2026</u>** 

On October 13, 2026, from 2:22 p.m. until 2:33 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Vice President Randy Larid, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent for today’s meeting. County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Hiring; (2) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Salary approval; (3) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Approval of stipend refusal request; (4) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Approval of Interim Health Director. 

The board was advised for the need of a closed session. 

Therefore, at 2:22 p.m., a motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to enter into Closed Session by authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the board, the motion carried.
