# Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of May 11" 2023 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, May 11, 2023 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Adam Riggin, Chairman, Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, and Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant. There were no Public Attendees. 

## Mr. Riggin called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

### General Business — Information 

The Sanitary District held a budget work session prior to this meeting. The Consensus of the Board was to move forward with advertising the following rates for quarterly water and sewer service, bulk water sales, and septage hauler fees for Fiscal Year 2023. With last year’s 6.5% inflation rate and another 3.3% inflation predicted for 2023, the District is recommending an across the board rate increase of 8% to both water and sewer. 

### SOMERSET COUNTY SANITARY COMMISSION PUBLIC HEARING 2:00 P.M. JUNE 15, 2023 

A public hearing will be held at 2:00 PM. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Ave., Princess Anne, MD 21863 for the purpose of establishing the fees for quarterly water and sewer service, bulk water sales. and septage hauler fees in Princess Anne, Westover, Smith Island, Fairmount and Jacksonville Subdistricts for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2023, Written comments may be mailed to 11916 Somerset Ave. Box 1, Princess Anne, MD 21853, phone 410-651-3831. The proposed rates for Jacksonville-Crisfield outside city limits are pending determination by the City of Crisfield. A separate public hearing was held by the City of Crisfield on May 10, 2023 at the Stratton-Corbin Library located at 4 East Main St., Crisfield, MD 21817. The rates determined by the City of Crisfield will be multiplied by a factor of 1.2.

During the 2/9/23 Commission meeting, the Board approved the Sanitary District to apply for participation in the Maryland Water Assistance Program — Coronavirus State Fiscal Recovery Funds which provides relief to water systems for outstanding water debt from residential customers. The District received notice of application approval on 4/27/23 for a grant award in the amount of $14,156.02 to be allocated to customers eligible. For additional information about this program please visit dbm.waterassistance@maryland.gov. 

The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Commission for review. The third quarter expenses exceeded revenues by $90,412.36; however, the District is awaiting project reimbursements in the amount of $58,601.05. The Commission had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

Ms. Marshall informed the Commission of a new service offered by our customer payment credit card provider, Truist Bank Credit Card System, for customers paying by credit card. The District can now offer the ability to store credit card information and/or set up recurring payments through Truist Bank. Customers who wish to utilize these features will be required to complete a Credit Card Authorization form consenting the District to store their information and/or charge recurring payments through Truist Bank. Once service is setup, the District will shred all credit card information and the information will be stored by Truist Bank Credit Card System. To obtain this form please visit somersetmd.us/government/sanitary_district/. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus informed the Commission, there has been no movement since the last session regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR Upgrade Project. 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project stating our Engineers at Davis, Bowen & Freidel are drafting a letter to MDE stating their recommendation, the combination of treating Princess Anne’s two largest wells with carbon and blending that water with the PASE tower water. DBF believe this treatment method would greatly reduce the tannins causing the dark color in Princess Anne water. Thereafter, we will be requesting letters of support to be addressed to the Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) from the Somerset County Commissioners, the Town of Princess Anne, and Property Owners in Princess Anne. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating Bid Opening for the Laboratory Cabinets as well as the Fume Hood, Safety Cabinets & Water Still is scheduled for June 2" 2023. Mr. Ludy anticipates project completion by the end of September 2023.

## Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission work and regular session minutes dated April 13" 2023 were previously distributed. On a motion by Mr. Bagley and seconded by Ms. Somers, it was unanimously carried to approve the work and regular session minutes dated April 13" 2023. 

# Revised Toilet & Interior Faucet Leak Policy 

The toilet & interior faucet leak policy has been revised to require the customer fill out an application form and provide information such as the type of leak and when the leak was fixed. The revision also clarifies the credit will be based on half of the gallons billed with a maximum overall credit of $200.00. Mr. Bagley made a motion to approve the revised toilet & interior faucet leak policy. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

# Revised Water & Sewer Billing Leak Credits Policy 

The water & sewer billing leak credits policy, which excludes toilet and interior faucet leaks, has been revised to require the customer fill out an application form and provide information such as the type of leak and when the leak was fixed. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the revised water & sewer billing leak credits policy. Mr. Bagley seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

## Revised Meter Verification Transfer Policy 

The revised meter verification transfer policy has been revised to allow the option of requesting a refund for requested readings within 45 days prior to the regular quarterly reading that consists of a flat rate or minimum charge. Ms. Marshall explained, these situations occur when property settlements fall through or settlement dates get pushed back for unseen reasons. Mr. Bagley made a motion to approve the revised meter verification transfer policy. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

## Princess Anne — Action 

## Request to waive late charges 

The Maryland State Highway Administration is requesting the Sanitary Board waive their late fee of $1.12 charged to their account for payment received after the due date. Although it is a small amount, the Commission took no action for fear of setting presentence. 

## Request for courtesy credit 

Roy Davison III, property owner of 11980 Somerset Avenue, is requesting a courtesy credit for water usage caused by an outside hose left partly on by his grandson. After receiving his water and sewer bill, Mr. Davison immediately checked his home for leaks and recognized the hose dripping outside and tightened the faucet. The District billing office was able to confirm the decrease in usage by performing a meter audit. Since the water did not enter the sewer system, Management's recommendation was to issue a courtesy leak credit to the sewer portion of Mr. Davison’s bill. Mr. Bagley made a motion to grant a courtesy sewer credit for 11980 Somerset Avenue based on the water and sewer billing leak credit policy. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.

## Approval of Emergency Well Repair 

Mr. Ludy informed the Commission, the field personnel were redoing all the piping in the Rest Area Well due to the damage from continuous exposure to chlorine gas and condensation; during this maintenance, they found damage to the piping and wellhead that extended into the concrete. Mr. Ludy stated these repairs, which are beyond the Sanitary Staff capability, are needed to place the well back in service to insure Princess Anne water supply is adequate. Mr. Ludy received a cost estimate from Somerset Well Drilling for $21,910.00. Mr. Bagley made a motion to approve the Emergency Rest Stop Well repair at the estimated price of $21,910.00. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion was unanimously carried to approval. 

Smith Island — Action 

# Change Order #1 

Change Order #1 for the SICWP — Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) Upgrade project was submitted by GMB to the Sanitary District for Board approval. The change order consists of two changes, a non-compensatory time extension and cost escalation of $77,639.60 for materials since original contract. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve Change Order #1 for the SICWP — WWTP. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval. 

# Amended Letter of Conditions (USDA) 

The Commission was presented with the fourth Amendment to the Letter of Conditions with the USDA for the SICWP. The Sanitary District requested and is receiving additional funding assistance to support the Smith Island Clean Water Project. The USDA is providing an additional grant in the amount of $389,000. The USDA funding is currently a loan in the amount of $83,000 and two RUS grants totaling $4,611,000. The approval of the fourth Amendment to the Letter of Conditions will bring the USDA grant total funding to $5,000,000. This Amendment changes the original Letter of Conditions dated September 4, 2018, and subsequent amendments dated June 23, 2021 and August 11, 2022. Mr. Bagley made a motion to authorize the Chairman to execute the Amendment to the Letter of Conditions (and Letter of Intent). Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.
