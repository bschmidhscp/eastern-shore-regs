# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY OCTOBER 29, 2024** 

At 2:13 p.m., President Charles Laird call the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for October 29, 2024</u>** 

On October 29, 2024, from 3:31 p.m. until 4:06 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (3) to consider the acquisition of real property for a public purpose and matters directly related thereto. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, Director of Solid Waste and Maintenance Charles Cavanaugh, County Engineer John Redden, Director of Technical and Community Services Jesse Drewer, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Property; Actions Taken: Confidential. 

Dr. Ava Tasker-Mitchell, Superintendent of Somerset County Schools, Mr. Jon Hill, Supervisor of Capital Planning, Facilities & Security for Somerset County Schools, were now before the Board to discuss the Capital Improvement Program for FY2026. 

Upon the start of the meeting, Vice President Randy Laird left the room for a brief moment before joining again.

Mr. Hill began his presentation by advising of the following priorities detailed in their 

Capital Improvement Plan: 

- Priority #1: Request for Major Construction Funding; Crisfield Academy and High School – Limited Renovations are required to improve building performance at Crisfield Academy and High School and reconfigure administration areas to provide a more secure reception area adjacent the existing main vestibule. 

- Priority #2: Request for Major Construction Funding; Greenwood Elementary School – Construct a replacement school to combine Princess Anne (PAES) and Greenwood (GES) Elementary School populations in a single facility on the GES property to support an increase in capacity and implementation of State and local educational initiatives. 

- Priority #3: Request for Major Construction; Somerset Intermediate School – Construct a two-story classroom addition to move 8th grade level to Intermediate School including support spaces, MEPFP extensions, exterior site improvements for increased traffic and secondary emergency access road. 

- Priority #4: Request for Major Construction; Washington Academy & High School – Limited Renovation including new fire alarm, security system upgrades, HVAC system heat pump replacement and mechanically clean geothermal piping system and install central filtration system, new generator and fire suppression tank, ADA exterior improvements, Science Classroom and casework replacement throughout, new water filtration system, gymnasium floor replacement, locker room expansion for additional equipment lockers, locker and restroom new epoxy floors, restroom plumbing fixture and partition replacement. Reconfigure main entrance vestibule with addition to provide enhanced secure vestibule and administrative suite reception areas for improved security and better utilize suite area. 

- Priority #5: Request for Systemic Project Funding; Carter Woodson Elementary School – Limited Renovation focusing on mechanical, plumbing and fire alarm systemic renovations. Also includes structural evaluation of interior CMU wall cracking and control joints, and exterior entrance canopy column corrosion. The current Feasibility request is to define scope and understand needs for future systemic renovation project. 

Following a discussion regarding these priorities, a letter of support was presented for the Board’s consideration; advising the Executive Director of the Public School Construction Program that the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County have received and reviewed the FY2026 Capital Improvement Plan and are in support of the project requests. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to authorize President Charles Laird’s signature on the letter of support. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Dr. Tasker-Mitchell, Mr. Hill, and ??? left the meeting.
