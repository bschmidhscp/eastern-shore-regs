Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of June 4, 2026 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, June 4, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Ms. Elliott called the meeting to order at 1:10 p.m. 

#### General Information 

The District presented the Commission with a first read of the proposed Background Check Policy for their review and discussion. The Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) recently selected the SCSD for an audit of records. The District passed the CJIS audit with one requirement moving forward, establish a policy for the background checks. Management explained to the Board that the District conducts criminal background checks as part of its responsibility to protect public health and promote safe drinking water and sewer systems. The District shall not conduct background checks on individuals at the initial application phase; instead, the District may only conduct a background check on an individual after presenting them with a conditional offer of employment. The policy applies to all Criminal History Record Information (CHRI) obtained for employment-related background checks on prospective or current employees. The consensus from the Board was to move forward with the policy as an action item at the next SCSD Commission meeting.

#### Crisfield Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor informed the Board that an article in the April 22"4 2026, Somerset County Times states the potential for sale of all or portions of the City of Crisfield’s water and wastewater systems and infrastructure. If that happens, it will affect the Sanitary District due to the Crisfield Peripheral Area that is serviced. On April 24' 2026, once the City of Crisfield held a public meeting and the news article was issued regarding this situation, the District filed a Maryland Public Information Act (MPIA) Request for financial documentation related to operation and maintenance of their water and wastewater utilities. The request was made in response to customer questions our staff was receiving concerning future services in the peripheral areas. The City was responsive to the MPIA request, and Management sat down with representatives from the City and reviewed their responses and requested additional information. The District will review that information once received and will continue discussions with the City regarding options for Sanitary District assistance with their water and sewer systems operation, as well as water/sewer service to the peripheral areas.
