# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY FEBRUARY 3, 2026** 

At 2:10 pm, President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were Interim County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for February 3, 2026</u>** 

On February 3, 2026, from 2:10 p.m. until 2:43 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Interim County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, HR Director Jamie Manaia, Assistant Warden Jamie Balderson, EMS Chief Joel Dixon, EMS Deputy Chief Cory Polidore, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Personnel, Action Taken: Selection of Applicants; (2) Topic: Personnel, Action Taken: Hiring; (3) Topic: Personnel, Action Taken: Approval of Vacation Pay. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:10 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner 

Craig Mathies, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
