**SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY APRIL 16, 2024** 

At 1:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also present for today’s meeting were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Vice President Randy Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the January 23, 2024 and the February 20, 2024 open and closed meetings were presented for approval. A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the minutes as presented. On a vote in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

Mr. Barnes advised of a request from landowners on Halls Creek Road to place a gate on their property to close the road to the public as they are having issues with trespassers and people dumping trash. The Board discussed this request and Mr. Barnes advised that relinquishing the sand and gravel portion of the road would be beneficial to the County (as they would no longer be responsible for the maintenance); stating the assessed value is $7,623.00. The Board gave a consensus to move forward with the necessary public hearing for the process of closing public access on the requested portion of Halls Creek Road. 

Mr. Barnes advised of a request from landowner to purchase the end of Edelen Webster Road in Deal Island (from the “end of maintenance” sign to the bulkheading; a distance of 129 feet). The landowner has agreed to pay the assessed value of the requested road ($677.25). During a brief discussion, Mr. Barnes advised that (if the landowner was granted with this request) the bulkheading would become their responsibility; to which they are aware of that. The Board gave a consensus to move forward with the necessary public hearing for this request. 

A discussion was held in regards to the Smith Island Roads project. Mr. Barnes advised of pot holes and other winter wear, following up with the repairs they can do now (patching) as a

A discussion was held regarding recent weather patterns and the problems arising due to the amount of rain received in a short span of time. The Roads Department has fielded many phone calls regarding these issues; however, oftentimes the answer to the solution is not cleaning/digging the roadside ditches, but rather off-road drainage. The Board then entered into a discussion about off-road drainage and tidal gates. 

Mr. Taylor stepped out of the board room. 

# **WATERWAYS** 

Mr. Barnes advised that they will be finalizing the paving portion of the Dames Quarter (Messick Road) Project next week. 

A discussion was held in regards to damage to the Crisfield dock with Mr. Barnes asking around and no one admitting to the damages. However, the incident happened once more and the company responsible for those damages also admitted to causing the damages before. The cost for repairing these damages (mooring pile and ladder) will be reimbursed to the County. 

Mr. Taylor returned to the meeting. 

Lastly, Mr. Barnes advised of a request to utilize the parking lot at Wenona Harbor for the Blessing of the Fleets. The Board gave a consensus to approve this request.
