Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of June 15" 2023 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, June 15, 2023 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Adam Riggin, Chairman, Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, Josh Taylor, P.E., Davis, Bowen, and Friedel, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Board Member, Maxine Landon was absent. There were no Public Attendees. 

Mr. Riggin called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR Upgrade Project stating Maryland Department of Environment has requested a pilot study be included in the Preliminary Engineering Report for the Plant upgrade. 

As mentioned during May’s Commission meeting regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project, Mr. Stockus stated Josh Taylor, Engineer, Davis, Bowen & Freidel, is drafting a letter to MDE with his recommendation for treatment by combining Princess Anne’s two largest wells with carbon and blending that water with the PASE tower water. This treatment method would greatly reduce the tannins causing the dark color in Princess Anne water. Thereafter, we will be requesting letters of support to be addressed to the Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) from the Somerset County Commissioners, the Town of Princess Anne, and Property Owners in Princess Anne. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating Bidders have been awarded for all aspects of the project with the exception of engineering services. 

Mr. Ludy informed the Commission Southern Corrosion Inc. has inspected the PASE, Irving Avenue, and Hickory Road water towers and concluded no issues requiring immediate attention. All tanks were adequately covered with screening and the interiors showed no signs of foreign debris. 

Ms. Mister informed the Board of water and sewer availability recently discovered to property located at 10950 Market Lane. The Office received a call from the Property Owner (who recently purchased this property) inquiring if water and sewer was available. After performing a field consult, a manhole and hydrant were discovered, confirming availability of both water and sewer. After consulting with Legal Counsel, we are not able to back bill for Debt Service because it was transferred on 4/18/23, however, we will be billing Debt Service for this property starting 7/01/23.

#### HSB Line of Credit Resolution & Taxable Note 

A Resolution of the SCSD authorizing the issuance of a bond (in the form of a revolving line of credit note) in the aggregate principal amount not to exceed $5,000,000 was previously distributed to the Board for their review. The District intends to use the proceeds of the Note to finance and refinance the costs of capital projects (which shall include, without limitation, design, engineering, planning, fiscal, and legal expenses related thereto) and for other public purposes of the District permitted by the Enabling Act. Pursuant to a resolution of the Sanitary Commission adopted on July 9" 2020, the District previously issued, among other things, its $700,000 SCSD Taxable Note (the “2020 Note”) to Hebron Savings Bank (HSB). It is the intent of the Sanitary Commission and HSB that the 2020 Note be terminated and the Note supersede and replace the 2020 Note in its entirety. Mr. Bagley made a motion approving the Resolution and Taxable Note with Hebron Savings Bank in the amount of $5,000,000. Mr. Williams seconded the motion which carried to approval unanimously. A Resolution and Taxable Note will be presented to the Somerset County Commissioners for their approval on June 20" 2023. 

### Bulk Water Policy & Resolution 

A Resolution of the SCSD adopting the policy governing bulk water purchases was previously distributed to the Board for review. The SCSD Commission desires to adopt a formal policy, to include application and permitting procedures, to assist in the administration and management of bulk water purchases. This includes a permit fee of $10.00 and a deposit of $50.00 per permittee to be paid when the application is filed. Charges for bulk water purchases shall be in accordance with the rates set during the fiscal year budget and will begin July 1% of each year. Mr. Bagley made a motion approving the Bulk Water Policy and Resolution. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.

#### Request for courtesy credit 

During the May 11 2023 Commission meeting, Rochelle Duncan-Baylor, granddaughter of Ethel Pollitt (deceased) property owner of 30261 Bowland Hill Circle, requested a credit for unexplained water and sewer usage. The Board tabled Ms. Duncan-Baylor’s request in an effort to give her an opportunity to discuss her unexplained usage. 

Ms. Duncan-Baylor was present via telephone, she explained to the Commission she inherited her grandmother’s home which has been vacant since April 2022. After receiving the March 2023 quarter water and sewer bill she had someone (as she lives in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania) examine the property for any interior and exterior water leaks or damages which concluded with negative results. She is mystified by the amount of water usage (1,020,750 gallons) from 12/12/22 to 3/10/23 and is requesting a credit to reflect the average usage of this property prior to the bill as it has been a minimum bill for almost 2 years. When Ms. Duncan-Baylor first questioned the bill, Account Technician, Michael White immediately replaced the meter with a new auditable meter and sent the old meter to a third-party meter testing company (Mid America Meter, Inc.) which tested the meter and determined the meter does not meet AWWA standards because the low flow test was reading under (meaning it was under-read). The new meter installed at the property was read on 6/14/23, no water has passed through the meter since it was installed on 4/10/23. Mr. Stockus advised Ms. Duncan-Baylor that the usage may be a result of theft, as the old meter was tested and no usage has occurred since. Ms. Mister asked Ms. Duncan-Baylor if she had the property inspected by a Licensed Plumber in which she responded no. Ms. Mister explained if a leak is detected and repaired she will be eligible for a credit to the sewer portion, otherwise managements recommendation will be that the bill will stand. Ms. Mister also stated, with Board approval, she can offer Ms. Duncan-Baylor a 2 year payment agreement to pay the
