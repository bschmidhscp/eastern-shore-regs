# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY JANUARY 23, 2024** 

At 3:10 p.m., the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County met in regular session on a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster; carried by a unanimous show of hands in favor by all present Board members. Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for January 23, 2024</u>** 

On January 23, 2024, from 3:10 p.m. until 3:37 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Legal; Actions Taken: None; (2) Personnel; Actions Taken: None. 

The Board was advised for the need of a closed session. 

Therefore, at 3:10 p.m., on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated
