NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 

BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY ADOPTION OF THE SOMERSET COUNTY ZONING CODE AMENDMENTS 

Tuesday, February 11, 2025 at 2 PM County Commissioners’ Meeting Room, Room 111 11916 Somerset Avenue Princess Anne, Maryland 21853 

In accordance with Section 15 of the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance, Somerset County is considering amending sections of the Zoning Ordinance that pertain to uses, to include regulation for use of cannabis; including but not limited to growing, processing, and selling. The relevant sections of the Ordinance are: Section 5.7 Table of Uses, Section ‘d’ Commercial Uses, Section ‘e’ Industrial Uses, and Section 5.8 Supplemental Use Regulations. 

The Somerset County Planning Commission held a public hearing on November 7, 2024. Following discussion at the January 9"" meeting, the commission forwarded a recommendation to the Board of County Commissioners for its consideration. The County Commissioners will consider all public comment prior to making a final decision. 

#### Fair Summary 

The proposed changes include: 

- « Adding a new category in section 5.7(d), Commercial Uses to include the addition of cannabis growing, processing and/or selling. 

- Removing all references to Cannabis in section 5.7(e), Industrial Uses. 

- © Adding use regulations for cannabis to Section 5.8 Supplemental Use regulations. 

A detailed listing of the proposed revisions may be obtained by calling the Somerset County Department of Technical and Community Services at 410-651-1424, or by visiting the department website at https://www.somersetmd.us/departments/departments ~ n_ziplanning_ and_zoning/planning_commission.php 

Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County Ralph D. Taylor, County Administrator

Cannabis Licensee Locations-Restrictions Act that provides political subdivisions with limited power to establish zoning requirements for and otherwise regulate cannabis. As a result of this law, the Planning Commission (PC) was tasked with revisiting the compliance of<sup>Ordinance1195</sup> with State law. The PC concluded that Ordinance 1195 conflicts with State law. The recommended amendments to Ordinance 1195 were provided which were summarized in the notice of public
