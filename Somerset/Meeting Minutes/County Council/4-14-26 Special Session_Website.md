## BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY APRIL 14, 2026** 

At 1:00 pm, Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent today. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for April 14, 2026</u>** 

On April 14, 2026, from 1:11 p.m. until 1:55 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent today. County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Sheriff Ronnie Howard, Chief Deputy Paul Keplinger, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: None. 

Vice President Randy Laird called the meeting to order and asked for Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Public Works Director Woody Barnes came forward for the opening of sealed bids in regards to the Somerset County EMS Station #10 Renovations. 

Vice President Randy Laird opened each sealed bid and read them aloud as follows: 

- Evans Builders, LLC - $1,369,630.00 

- Beauchamp Construction - $1,460,000.00 

- Harkins Contracting, Inc. - $1,515,000.00 

- Whayland Company, LLC - $1,285,000.00 

- InSite Build, LLC - $1,822,000.00 

Mr. Barnes advised that the submitted bids will be reviewed and, following this, he will present his recommendation at a future meeting.

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 1:11 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
