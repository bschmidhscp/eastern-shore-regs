SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD July 9, 2024 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., Commissioner 

Darryl Webster and Commissioner Eldon Willing present. 

Also present were Deputy County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., County Roads Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, Roads employee Mr. Edward White and Executive Aide Lory Ebron. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of<sup>Allegiance.</sup> 

Minutes from the June 11, 2024 meeting were presented for approval; however, the Board requested more time to review. Minutes were not approved during today’s meeting.

###### Roads Division Discussion Items 

Mr. Barnes asked Mr. Charles Cavanaugh, Solid Waste and Maintenance Director and Mr. John Redden, County Engineer to come forward and join him. Mr. Barnes advised that last fall the County applied for the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Building Resilient Infrastructure and Committee (BRIC) Direct Technical Assistance (DTA). He explained that grant funding is awarded to assist with technical experience, conceptual design, and assistance through the grant application process. The letter from FEMA included within the agenda package advised that of the 170 submissions were received and prioritized, with Somerset County being selected to receive support. Mr. Barnes explained this support will target the seven miles of the Crisfield Dike, and offside road drainage on the east side of city limits. Mr. Cavanaugh added that this would be a three (3) year commitment with FEMA representatives on this initiative, also noting that the City of Crisfield is also on board. This support with work in conjunction with the large grant the City just received to address flooding in City limits. Vice President Randy Laird asked about mapping the tidal dike and if drones can be utilized to obtain a better view. Mr. Bares felt during this process drone work will be utilized as generally that is what is done to reach terrain not easily accessed. Work previously done was thru a Maryland Department of Planning grant for public drainage in the amount of $75,000. If not, he will be sure to make the recommendation for the use of drones. Mr. Cavanaugh noted that obtaining easements is already underway, with construction anticipating to begin in October 2026. Mr. Redden and Mr. Cavanaugh left the meeting at this time. 

Next, Mr. Barnes presented his follow up regarding the Public Hearing held on Tuesday, June 11, 2024 for the proposed road closure of Halls Creek Road (County Road 109/1,452 linear feet) requested by Vernon Vermillion and Gordon (Bunky) Ford. During the public hearing, one comment was received from Raymond Larrimore Jr. in opposition. No written comment was received during the 10-day public comment period. Mr. Bames provided an outline the proposed closure and maintenance performed by the County. He explained the property owners will be responsible to install a gate if the closure is approved, and the county will utilize the area owned by the Sanitary District for turnaround (done currently) while performing maintenance of the remainder of the road. This closure will be a savings to the county, as maintenance of that part of the roadway will be eliminated. Commissioner Willing advised that he has received no comments 

from area residents. The landowners will be liable for any legal or advertising fees. 2

Therefore, Commissioner Willing made a motion to approve the closure of the section of Halls Creek Road as petitioned by Vernon Vermillion and Gordon Ford. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Barnes next presented the request for road closure of Edelen Webster Road (County Road 73) as petitioned by John Jaeger and David Perdue to close the last 129 feet of the road. No comments were received during the public hearing or the 10-day written comment period. This proposed road purchase has an assessed value of $677.25, which the landowners are agreeable to pay. This is roadway the county is not maintaining, as it is waterfront property. There is a culvert that runs the length of the property that the landowner is willing to put in a perpetual easement to allow the county to maintain the culvert for future drainage issues. 

Commissioner Willing made a motion to approve the petition requested by John Jaeger and David Perdue regarding the proposed closure of 129 feet of unpaved road on Edelen Webster Road. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr. Barnes, for informational purposes, advised the Board the Weed Control MOU between Somerset County and the Maryland Department of Transportation, State Highway Administration. This annual agreement, in which Mr. Barnes was authorized to sign, will reimburse the County for weed control spraying performed up to $10,250.
