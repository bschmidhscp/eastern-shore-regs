## NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 

BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY TEXT AMENDMENTS TO THE 

SOMERSET COUNTY FOREST CONSERVATION ORDINANCE 

Tuesday, September 8, 2026 at 2:00PM 

Somerset County Office Complex County Commissioners Meeting Room (Room 111) 

11916 Somerset Avenue Princess Anne, Maryland 21853 

In accordance with the Forest Conservation Ordinance (#1029) of Somerset County Maryland, Chapter 21.1 (Amendments), Somerset County is considering amendments to the Somerset County Forest Conservation Ordinance to comply with amendments required by Stateof Maryland House Bill 1511 (2024) Members ofthe public are invited to attend the Public Hearing and comment on the draft amendments to the ordinance. The County Commissioners will consider all public comment prior to making a decision on the Ordinance amendments. 

#### Fair Summary 

The Maryland Forest Conservation Act (FCA) was passed by the General Assembly in 1991 and subsequently amended to conserve the State’s forest resources during development activities. The FCA requires identification of existing forest stands, protection of the most desirable forest stands, and establishment of areas where new forests can be planted. HB1511 (2024) significantly updated the Forest Conservation Program definitions, reforestation ratios, net forest gain initiatives and public notification requirements which will be addressed with this update. 

For further information please contact the Somerset County Department of Technical and Community Services at 410-651-1424 or by email at cfinney@somersetmd.us 

Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County Emest J. Leatherbury Jr. County Administrator Advertising Dates: 08/26/2026 & 09/02/2026

Mr. Drewer explained that this is the first major update to the ordinance since 1991. He advised that Maryland Department of Natural Resources has prepared a draft ordinance (included within the agenda package) to update the Somerset County Forest Conservation Program in June, with major changes to new definitions, reforestation, updated reforestation ratio’s and net forest gain initiatives. A public hearing is required to be held, and after hearing any comments, an ordinance will be drafted to formally adopt the updates.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 9-8-26 

Ms. Brittany Bozman, State Attomey’s Office/Child Support Division came forward at this time to present the CSEA/CRA FY27-023 Contract for Fiscal Year 2027. She advised that the total contract has increased $5,702.00 this year as compared to 2026 due to increases in postage costs, Clear Locating Service and staff salary increases over the last year. The County portion of the increase is $2,154.00 for FY27, with the total local share totaling $148,211. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve the CSEA/CRA FY27-023 Contract for Fiscal Year 2027. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 3-0. 

Mr. Leatherbury continued with discussion items at this time. 

A letter of support for a sign dedication was received from Mark Crampton, District Engineer, MDOT to dedicate a portion of US Route 13 in Somerset County, in honor of State Highway Administration employee Mr. Dipakkumar Patel, who tragically lost his life while performing his duties. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to authorize President Charles Laird to sign the letter of support drafted for the sign dedication request for Mr. Patel. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore, the motion carried 3-0. 

Last, Mr. Leatherbury presented a request for a tipping fee waiver received from Mr. Charles Fisher for the removal of home demolition debris at 13183 Hop Fisher Lane. Mr. Fisher advise in his letter that the elderly gentleman living there for free has passed away. The house needs to be taken down, and is close to neighbors so it can’t be burned. Mr. Fisher advised he will pay to have the home demolished and hauled away. 

President Laird asked Commissioner Webster his opinion being the request is in his district. Commissioner Webster asked if a similar request has been honored in the past. President Laird stated it has been done as a courtesy for county taxpayers in the past. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve the tipping fee waiver request from Mr. Fisher for 13183 Hop Fisher Lane. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore the motion carried 3-0.
