Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of September 12, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, September 12, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, and Maxine Landon, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, Josh Taylor, P.E., Davis Bowen & Friedel, Chris Derbyshire and Pete Bozick, P.E., George Miles & Buhr. Public Attendees included Valentina Downing, Marion Polk, and Cindy Perry. The Crisfield (Peripheral Area) Board position is currently vacant. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

##### General Information 

The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Board for review. The fourth quarter (FY24) revenues exceeded expenses by $374,819.98. The Commission had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. Princess Anne- Information Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project by stating the Pilot Testing Agreement/Proposal by Fluence (water treatment solution corporation) is no longer happening. Mr. Stockus explained, the company has been hard to negotiate with and have cost the District hundreds to possibly thousands of dollars in legal costs trying to negotiate the contract. They had additional costly requirements that were not discussed initially and they were not certain the study would be successful. Mr. Stockus stated it makes sense to change course to a Membrane Bioreactor Plant like the new WWTP on Smith Island, as our Plant Operators will be trained for this process. Mr. Stockus will be discussing this option with the Engineers at Davis, Bowen, & Friedel and report back to the Commission. The Board were in agreeance with Mr. Stockus’s decision. 

Mr. Stockus stated the District has not received official notification from MDE regarding their released draft of the FFY 2024 Intended Use Plan for the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF). The Princess Anne Color Removal Project ranked 5" on the priority scale, funding $5,090,200 of the requested $6,590,000 (leaving a shortage of $1.5 million). The funding package includes DWSRF base loan of $750,000, BIL (Bipartisan Infrastructure Law) general loan of $1,795,100, and principal forgiveness of $2,545,100. MDE’s comment period for the draft ended on August 7" 2024.

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating all inspections have passed and the cabinets and floors are being installed now. 

Ms. Stockus informed the Board, SunPower Corporation, the buyer of energy credits for the electricity generated by the solar panels at the PA WWTP, has filed for bankruptcy. Ms. Stockus anticipates assets to include contracts will be purchased by another company, but it’s unknown at this time. The panels are not generating a fraction of the money projected when the project was pitched to the District in 2019. This project was fully funded by MDE’s Energy Water Infrastructure Program Grant. 

##### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP) stating the WWTP Upgrade progress meeting was held on September 4!" 2024 on-site where construction of the Plant is 66% complete. Mr. Stockus stated there will be additional costs that were not anticipated at the beginning of the project as the existing drying bed pilings are not salvageable. A change order for this increased cost is anticipate in the next few months. The Subaqueous Force Main (SFM) project is at a standstill until Fall 2024 due to the end of year restrictions implemented by MDE.

#### Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular session minutes dated July 11" 2024 were previously distributed. On a motion by Ms. Landon and seconded by Mr. Williams, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular session minutes dated July 11" 2024. 

## Bid Opening for RFQ 

The District advertised a Request for Qualifications for Professional On-Call Engineering Services as it has been an extended period of time since awarded. The District received four responses from Rauch Inc. of Easton, Hillis-Carnes Engineering Associates of Delmar, George, Miles & Buhr LLC of Salisbury, and Davis, Bowen & Friedel Inc. also of Salisbury. | The professional services and hourly rates were read aloud. A review Committee will evaluate the firms based on the requirements and qualifications stated in the RFQ before a recommendation is brought to the Board. 

## PKS & Company Engagement Letter 

PKS & Company submitted an engagement letter to perform the annual Sanitary District Fiscal Year 2024 Financial Statement Audit. Ms. Landon made a motion to authorize the Chairman to execute the PKS & Company Engagement letter for Fiscal Year 2024 Audit as presented. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

##### 2024 Truck Purchase 

During the May 2024 Work Session Management discussed financing a 2023 Ford 150 Truck for the 2025 fiscal year for $36,130.00 which the Sanitary Board approved during the June 2024 Public Hearing for FY25 budget and rates. In an effort to save money, Management is requesting instead of financing the truck, the District purchase the truck outright with surplus funds from FY24. Mr. Williams made a motion to approve the purchase of the 2023 Ford 150 Truck from Hertrich Fleet Services in the amount of $36,130.00 by using surplus funds. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion unanimously carried to approval.

#### Approval of Estimate 

Reynolds Excavating has submitted a cost estimate for installing a new insertion valve on Beckford Avenue. This valve insertion will allow the District to isolate the shut off directly at the site and repair the leak, avoiding disconnection of multiple residents and businesses. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the cost estimate from Reynolds Excavating in the amount of $13,500.00. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

# Smith Island — Action 

### Request for time extension 

The Commission tabled the time extension request from M2 Construction for the WWTP until GMB can provide the Engineering cost associated with this extension. 

### Change Order 

Crofton Construction has presented the District with a change order for the following: 

1. Additional bore length for HDD #2 $94,589.00 2. Permit delay event 1 $68,292.70 3. Securing site & remobilization $324,000.00 4. Permit delay event 2 $48,160.85 Totaling $535,042.55 

GMB were present to discuss these items in detail. GMB and Management were in agreeance, due to the uniqueness of the Island and Crofton being the sole bidder of the project, in order to get this project complete it is best to comply with this change order contingent upon approval from MDE and USDA. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve change order #2 for the Subaqueous Force Main contingent upon approval from MDE and USDA. Mr. Williams seconded the motion, the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

#### Public Input 

Cindy Perry of Somerset Landing was present to thank the Sanitary Board and Staff for their efforts to receive funding for the Princess Anne Clean Water project which is aimed to help with the water quality in Princess Anne. 

On a motion by Ms. Landon and seconded by Mr. Williams, it was unanimously agreed to adjourn at 2:26 p.m.
