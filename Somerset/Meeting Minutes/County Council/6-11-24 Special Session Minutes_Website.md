SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS SPECIAL SESSION: TUESDAY JUNE 11, 2024 

# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY JUNE 11, 2024** 

At 2:34 p.m., on a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing, the Board entered into Somerset County Commissioners Special Session. President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing were all present for today’s meeting. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Services, joined the meeting to request the approval of the FY25 Community Development Block Grant Application and Resolution. Mr. Drewer advised that no written comments have been received in support or opposition from the public hearing held on May 28, 2024. He further advised that the deadline to submit this application was originally set for June 11, 2024 at 2pm; however, CDBG authorized an extension of the deadline to June 11, 2024 at 5pm (due to the Commissioner meeting schedule). 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to approve the FY25 Community Development Block Grant Application as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing, the motion carried. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to adopt Resolution #1201 (to support the submittal of the FY25 Community Development Block Grant Application for a County-wide blight removal program). By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing, the motion carried. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to approve a letter to Ms. Cindy Stone, CDBG, that will accompany the FY25 Community Development Block Grant Application and authorize Mr. Leatherbury’s signature on the letter. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing, the motion carried.

Mr. Drewer left the meeting. 

Mr. Chris Woodward, Director of IT, and Mr. Chad Michaels, Network Security Specialist, were now before the Board to request the approval of and MOU between the Maryland Department of Information Technology and Somerset County Government for Cybersecurity Assessment. Mr. Woodward advised that $40,000 has been budgeted for cybersecurity; however, the State has reached out with a pilot program called Cybersecurity Maturity Assessment that is designed to help identify and remediate cybersecurity gaps. This program will be at no cost to the County and will monitor policy and procedures only. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve President Charles Laird’s signature on the MOU agreement. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing, the motion carried. 

Mr. Woodward and Mr. Michaels left the meeting. 

Mr. Clint Sterling, Director of Recreation, Parks, and Tourism was now before the Board to present a letter of support for the Chesapeake Bay Passenger Ferry Feasibility Study Consortium. Mr. Sterling advised that Anne Arundel County is applying for the FY24 Federal Transit Administration Electronic or Low-Emitting Ferry Pilot Program to develop and implement the first routes of the proposed bay wide Chesapeake Bay ferry system. Mr. Sterling advised that this project (and the support of the application) will be at no cost to Somerset County, but will be a benefit as Somerset County is one of the proposed ferry ports. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the letter of support as written and authorize President Charles Laird’s signature on the letter as part of the Chesapeake Bay Passenger Ferry Feasibility Study Consortium. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing, the motion carried. 

Mr. Sterling left the meeting. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session.
