Next, Mr. Jesse Drewer came forward to request adoption of the 2025 Somerset County Comprehensive Plan which began twenty-nine years ago. The department has worked on updating the plan for two- and one-half years to have it be compliant with state law. Mr. Drewer advised that Somerset County will have the most current plan on the Eastern Shore upon adoption. One comment was received in favor of the plan from the Lower Shore Land Trust for encouraging land preservation in the plan. There were no questions from the Board. Mr. Leatherbury requested a motion to approve the 2025 Somerset County Comprehensive Plan and Resolution No. 1214. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve the 2025 Somerset County Comprehensive Plan and Resolution No. 1214. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0.
