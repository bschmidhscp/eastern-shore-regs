Somerset County Sanitary Commission Work Session Minutes of April 10, 2025 

A Work Session meeting of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, April 10% 2025 at 11:00 a.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Rich Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, and Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide. 

Ms. Mister presented the Commission with following budget highlights.
