## **C.** **<u>OLD BUISNESS:</u>** 

## **1. Review Contractor Qualification Forms for Housing Rehabilitation** 

Casey informed us that a Contractor Premium Installation Inc. has reached out to us requesting to review their newly submitted contractor form. It was a clerical error that labeled their company Premium Installation LLC and not Premium Installation Inc. 

The board reviewed documents that shows Premium Installation Inc. is in good standing with the state of Maryland and has provided all the necessary documents to be qualified.

**A Motion was then made by Mark Konapelsky to Approve Premium Installation Inc. as an approved Contractor. This was Seconded By Rosalie White  and the Motion was passed unanimously.**
