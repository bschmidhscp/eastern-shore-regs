Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of December 11, 2025 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, December 11, 2025 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, (joined meeting at 1:18 p.m.) and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Josh Taylor, General Manager, was absent. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating that the District is continuing to work on the design with Davis, Bowen, & Friedel, Inc. (DBF) and the treatment equipment manufacturers. The District will need approval from Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) on the design plans for the plant. Mr. Padgette anticipates this project lasting at least four years and hopeful to start construction in two years. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating the District is still working with MDE and DBF to finalize design. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Lead Service Line Project by stating the annual notifications required by MDE were mailed out to District's customers on 12/5/2025. The District is still working on purchasing the equipment, Swordfish (pipe line identifier tool), to help identify the lead pipes easier. Mr. Padgette is hopeful to be using this equipment by Spring 2026. 

##### Smith Island Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) is currently online and running with project completion at 95%. Mr. Padgette also stated that the force main is doing well and the decommissioning of the Tylerton plant is close to being done. Minor issues are still being worked through at this time with hopes to have them resolved before project completion.\ Fairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information

### Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular session minutes dated November 13" 2025 were previously distributed. On a motion by Ms. Somers and seconded by Ms. Elliott, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular session minutes dated November 13" 2025. 

#### Vehicle Financing Resolution 

Hebron Savings Bank has requested for the Sanitary Commission to provide a resolution authorizing the submittal of an application and execution of a vehicle loan agreement. The purchase of two work vehicles was budgeted for FY26 and previously approved by the Sanitary Commission. Last meeting, a motion was made to allow the General Manager to sign the resolution once received, not realizing it needed to be signed by the Sanitary Commission as well. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the vehicle financing resolution as presented; Ms. Elliott seconded the motion which carried unanimously to approval. 

## Hightide Well SCADA Communication Units (Emergency Purchase) 

The District currently has dry pair copper land lines, provided by Verizon, connected to and operating four wells. The District received a notice from Verizon stating the bill for the dry pairs would be doubling in cost and that they are no longer providing maintenance or repairs. The price to keep the 4 dry pair land lines is $10,500.00 per year with no way to repair if needed. The Hightide Well SCADA Communication Units will replace these dry pair land lines and be more cost-effective long term. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the purchase of four Hightide units from Freemire & Associates in the amount of $18,892.00; Ms. Elliott seconded the motion which carried unanimously to approval.

### Request for Allocation — 12139 Somerset Ave 

Thornton Properties, LLC has submitted a request for allocation of 2 EDU’s for his purposed duplex at 12139 Somerset Ave. Mr. Taylor has reviewed the request and is recommending the approval of the allocation for 2 EDU’s. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the request for allocation for 2 EDU’s for 12139 Somerset Ave. Ms. Elliott seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

### Plumber Cost Reimbursement Request — 11568 Beckford Ave 

Nicholas Slodowski, owner of 11568 Beckford Ave, has requested two plumbing bills from Roto Rooter dated 9/2/2025 and 9/9/2025 totaling $1,168.75 be reimbursed by the Sanitary District. The sewer lateral which is located on Linden Ave, was struck and damaged by a gas main installation. After gathering more information, it was determined the Sanitary District was at fault as a utility locate request was made in advance, however, the District made an assumption regarding the sewer lateral location which led to it not being marked accurately, causing the damage. Ms. Landon made a motion to reimburse the property owner at 11568 Beckford Ave for direct plumbing cost they incurred in the amount of $1,168.75. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

### Toilet Leak Credit Request — 12631 Somerset Ave Unit #1 

Patricia Robinson, owner of 12631 Somerset Ave Unit #1, is requesting a toilet leak credit for her running toilet repaired on September 27" 2025. Ms. Robinson had the leak fixed by a plumber at her property and applied for a leak credit however she could not provide a receipt due to the plumber not responding after many failed attempts. The District did a meter audit to confirm that the leak was repaired on the date that was given. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve a toilet leak credit up to $200.00 for 12631 Somerset Ave Unit #1. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.
