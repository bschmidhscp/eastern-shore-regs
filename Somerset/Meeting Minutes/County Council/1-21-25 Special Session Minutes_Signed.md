# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY JANUARY 21, 2025** 

At 2:14 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for January 21, 2025</u>** 

On January 21, 2025, from 2:14 p.m. until 3:28 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and voted in favor by a show of hands by all members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (3) To consider the acquisition of real property for a public purpose and matters directly related thereto. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Property; Actions Taken: None. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing 

to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

No minutes were presented today. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:14 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and carried on a vote in favor by show of hands by all members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (3) To consider the acquisition of real property for a public purpose and matters directly related thereto.
