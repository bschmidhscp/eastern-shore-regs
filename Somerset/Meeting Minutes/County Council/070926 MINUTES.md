Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of July 9, 2026 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, July 9, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Maxine Landon, Member, was absent. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

### General Information 

The District presented the Commission with a first read of the proposed Vehicle Use Policy for their review and discussion. The vehicle use policy is intended to reduce the risk of accidents, injuries, liability, and property damage. This policy applies to all SCSD employees operating a vehicle to conduct business. This policy, which includes Defensive Driving Training, will qualify the District for a discounted rate of insurance. The consensus from the Board was to move forward with the policy as an action item at the next SCSD Commission meeting. 

## Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade, Princess Anne Color Removal Project, and the Lead Service Line Project by stating Matthew Marshall, Department of Environment (MDE), performed on-site inspections for all three projects as part of MDE’s environmental review process and funding requirements. 

There were no other updates since the last commission meeting regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project. 

There were no other updates regarding the Lead Service Line Project since the last commission meeting.

## Smith Island Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the punch list inspection items are currently being addressed. The District is coordinating, along with MDE and USDA, a ribbon-cutting celebrating the completion of the SICWP. This ceremony is planned for October 2026, Mr. Taylor will have more information as the date approaches. 

# Fairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor informed the Board there were no other updates since the last commission meeting regarding the Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension Project. 

### Crisfield Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor informed the Board the City of Crisfield issued a Request for Proposal (RFQ) for a licensed operator to run the Crisfield Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) for five years. The District has no interest in pursuing this proposal request, however, SCSD will continue to work with the City towards negotiating the fifty-year agreement with the City of Crisfield, also known as, The 1977 Agreement, regarding the Crisfield Peripheral area water/sewer users.

## Approval of Policy & Resolution — Background Check Policy 

The District presented the Commission with a first read of the proposed Background Check Policy for their review during last month’s SCSD commission meeting. The Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) recently selected the SCSD for an audit of records. The District passed the CJIS audit with one requirement moving forward, establish a policy for the background checks. Management explained to the Board, the District conducts criminal background checks as part of its responsibility to protect public

health and promote safe drinking water and sewer systems. The District shall not conduct background checks on individuals at the initial application phase; instead, the District may only conduct a background check on an individual after presenting them with a conditional offer of employment. The policy applies to all Criminal History Record Information (CHRI) obtained for employment-related background checks on prospective or current employees. Mr. Williams made a motion to approve the Background Check Policy and Resolution as presented. Ms. Elliott seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

## Request for Leak Credit — 11302 Greenwood Road 

Nathen Morris, Property Owner of 11302 Greenwood Road, is requesting a leak credit for a repaired broken line under his house repaired in February 2026. The leak, which affected the December 2025 and March 2026 quarter’s water/sewer bill, was repaired by his brother-in-law, Franklin Robinson, a Maintenance Supervisor. Mr. Robinson had all the necessary parts to repair without purchasing, therefore, Mr. Morris was unable to provide a receipt for parts, prompting approval from the SCSD Commission. Management's recommendation is to approve the leak credit as the leak repair has been confirmed with the June 2026 meter reading, the consumption has returned to normal usage. Mr. Williams made a motion to issue a leak credit for 11302 Greenwood Road based on the leak policy. Mr. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

## Request for Leak Credit — 30259 Bowland Hill Circle 

Albert White, Property Owner of 30259 Bowland Hill Circle, is requesting a leak credit for a repaired seven-foot polyline in the driveway. The leak, which affected the December 2025 quarter water/sewer bill, was repaired by Jack Joseph, an unlicensed plumber, prompting approval from the SCSD Commission. Management's recommendation is to approve the credit request as decreased usage was confirmed with June 2026 quarter’s meter reading. Ms. Somers made a motion to issue a leak credit for 30259 Bowland Hill Circle based on the leak policy. Ms. Elliott seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.
