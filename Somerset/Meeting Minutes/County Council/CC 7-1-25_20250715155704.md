BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

July 1, 2025 

At 2:00 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., were absent from today’s meeting. 

Internal Auditor Sharon Muir, and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. Interim County Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr., was also absent from today’s meeting. 

Closed Session Summary Report 

On Tuesday, July 1, 2025 at 2:25 p.m., until 4:17 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing opened in Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (3) consider the acquisition of real property for a public purpose and matters directly related to the acquisition. All those present voted in favor by show of hands, therefore, the motion carried 3-0. President Charles Laird and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., and Interim County Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr were absent from today’s meeting. 

Ms. Sharon Muir, Internal Auditor, HR Director Jamie Manaia, Emergency Services Director Yvette Cross, Emergency Services Assistant Chris Hopkins, ChiefofEmergency Medical Services Joel Dixon, County Engineer John Redden (by phone), Public Works Director Woody Bames, Recreation and Parks/Tourism Director Clint Sterling, and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

Topics discussed: (1) Four Month Extension PT Temporary Emergency Services Dispatcher (2) Hire 911 Emergency Communication Specialist (3) Hiring for EMS Assistant Chiefs (4) Hire EMS Office Assistant III. Approved: (1) Extend PT Temporary EMS Dispatcher employment four months (2) Hire 911 Emergency Communication Specialist (3) Hire EMS Assistant Chiefs (4) Hire EMS Office Assistant III .

At 2:03 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird called a Public Hearing open to receive public comment on the Kingsbay Mansion-Chesapeake Bay Critical Area Growth Allocation Request. Vice President Randy Laird asked Mr. Drewer, Director/DTCS to come forward. Ms. Muir read 

the notice of public hearing into record as follows: 

NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 

BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY CHESAPEAKE BAY CRITICAL AREA — GROWTH ALLOCATION APPLICATION Tuesday, July 1, 2025 at 2PM County Commissioners’ Meeting Room, Room 111 11916 Somerset Avenue Princess Anne, Maryland 

Pursuant to the Somerset County Chesapeake Bay Critical Area Program and the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance, notice is hereby given that a public hearing will be held by the Somerset County Board of County Commissioners at the above referenced date and time. 

The purpose of the hearing is to consider a request by the applicant, David Carlin King and Gerald Philpott Jr. to expand operations to include a restaurant at 27999 Coulbourn Creek Road Marion Station Maryland 21838. 

The applicant is requesting growth allocation to intensify the usage of the property from Resource Conservation Area (RCA) to Limited Development Area (LDA). The applicant proposes no physical increase in the structure, but an intensification of use which is only permitted in the LDA. 

This public hearing is following a favorable recommendation from the Somerset County Planning Commission on June 5" 2025, The Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County will hold this public hearing prior to making a decision and forwarding a recommendation to the Critical Area Commission for concurrence and approval 

Fair Summary 

“The applicant proposes to utilize a portion of the 977.01 acres available for growth allocation in Somerset County for the establishment of a restaurant in a new Limited Development area.

He noted the staff report includes letters of support. If approved, the next step in the process is to forward the project to the Critical Area Commission for review and confirmation. 

Vice President Randy Laird asked if there were any proponents present wishing to speak. There were none. He then asked if there were any opponents present wishing to speak. There were none. Ms. Muir advised that written public comment will be taken for 10 days, or until Friday, July 11, 2025 at 4:30 p.m. The public hearing was declared closed at 2:08 p.m. 

Next, Mr. Drewer presented a review of the Nexamp Inc., Solar Energy Facility Zone project request for a floating zone. The public hearing to present the project for public comment was held on June 3, 2025. Three letters of support have been received since the hearing, and the applicants are present today should there be any additional questions. In order to proceed, Mr. Drewer requested the Board’s approval of<sup>theordinancegranting theapplicationof NexampInc.,</sup> for a Utility Scale Solar Energy Facility (SEF) Floating Zone. Staff recommendation is for approval of<sup>the SEFaspresented.</sup> 

With it noted that there were no comments received in opposition to the project, Commissioner Eldon Willing made a motion to approve Ordinance No. 1211 regarding SEF Zone approval for the Nexamp Inc. (Kemp Nursery Road) project. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye; therefore the motion carried 3-0. 

Mr. Drewer advised that the final site plan will be submitted to the Planning Commission for approval. 

Next, Mr. Drewer presented Ordinance No. 1212 to amend various zoning code text amendments. He explained the changes presented for adoption which will eliminate the Solar Energy Facility (SEF) Floating Zone, update definitions for solar facilities and energy storage devises and establish new zoning regulations for solar energy facilities that will bring our zoning code into compliance with SB931 (Renewable Energy Certainty Act) which will go into effect July 1, 2025 as required by State law. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve Ordinance No. 1212 as presented. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore, the motion carried 3-0.

Mr. Drewer presented the FY26 State of Maryland Critical Area Program grant, received to help offset the costs with administering the Critical Area Program. Somerset County has received $6,000 again this year, which is the same amount received for FY25, but $4,000 less than those funds received for FY24. Mr. Drewer requested the Boards approval to accept the FY26 State of Maryland Critical Area Program as presented. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to accept the FY26 Critical Area Program grant as presented for $6,000, authorizing Vice President Randy Laird to sign the agreement. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore the motion carried 3-0. 

Last, Mr. Drewer presented two requests for release of<sup>liens regarding funds received from</sup> the CDBG Housing Rehabilitation Grant Program. On August 20, 2009, $16,085.00 was used to fund repairs for property owned by Sharon Holden at 7672 Dividing Creek Road, Eden, Md. This ten-year lien expired August 20, 2019. Additionally, a second grant was issued to this same property owned by Sharon Holden on October 11, 2010 in the amount of $4,910.00. This grant had a five-year lien and expired on October 11, 2015. Mr. Drewer requested approval to authorize the Vice President to sign the releases as presented. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to authorize Vice President Randy Laird to sign the lien release in the amount of $16,085 against 7672 Diving Creek Road. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore the motion carried 3-0. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to authorize Vice President Randy Laird to sign the lien release in the amount of $4,910.00 against 7672 Diving Creek Road. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore the motion carried 3-0. 

Mr. Clint Sterling, Recreation and Parks/Tourism Director came forward to present an EDA Tourism grant in the amount of $112,320 which is 100% reimbursable. Mr. Sterling discussed the previous approach made the last couple of years to advertise, explaining that he desires to extend our reach regarding advertising for the county. The stated goal is to increase visitors to our county, who will in turn spend money in the county, and establish Somerset County as a travel destination. The target audience will be the Baltimore/Washington area using ads to be placed on ride share vehicles and box trucks capable of collecting device id’s and reaching 23 million people through their cell phones who will see our ads generated.
