Mr. Jared Parks, Lower Shore Land Trust, joined Mr. Drewer to present the FY2027 Application for the Dividing Creek Rural Legacy Area (DCRLA). 

Mr. Parks explained that he is presenting the same list that was presented this past February, noting there was a 75% cut to acquisition programs and no funds were awarded last year. He advised there are six properties in Somerset County and four in Worcester County. He added that he has no idea what the Statewide outlook will be for the program, which is based primarily on transfer tax. Approval was requested to submit the 2027 application to the Department of Natural Resources. If approved, the grant agreement will be presented to the Board of Commissioners for their approval and signature probably around the fall/winter time frame later this year. 

It was noted that the funds are used for easement purchases of qualified properties in Worcester and Somerset County. Mr. Parks was asked how the determination is made regarding awarding the grant, He explained that the application process works, broken into four quartered areas, and based on a point system, from a list of questions that are asked on the application. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve the submission of the 2027 Dividing Creek Rural Legacy Area grant application as presented. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 3-0. Mr. Parks and Mr. Drewer left the meeting at this time.
