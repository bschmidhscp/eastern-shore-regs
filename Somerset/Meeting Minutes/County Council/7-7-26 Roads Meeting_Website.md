**SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY JULY 7, 2026** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, and Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also present for today’s meeting were County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Mike Marvel, County Roads Assistant Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the May 12, 2026 meeting were presented for approval; however, the board requested more time for review. 

## **ROADS DIVISION** 

The meeting began with an informational discussion regarding a letter of support, drafted at the request of Mr. Moore from the Maryland Department of Transportation State Highway Administration (MDOT SHA) that supports SHA's request to the Federal Highway Administration for a Fiscal Year 2027 Off-System Bridge Program funding waiver. Mr. Marvel went on to advise that each year, a portion of SHA's funding for local bridge improvements is supported through federal Surface Transportation Block Grant Off-System Bridge funds. For Fiscal Year 2027, SHA anticipates that it will not require these additional federal funds because of the current scope of projects and available program balances. Mr. Marvel continued by advising that by approving the waiver, counties would gain greater flexibility in seeking funding assistance for critical bridge projects. In addition, the waiver could also potentially relax strict federal procurement policies and certain construction specifications, allowing the county to consider rehabilitation versus automatically defaulting to new construction. Mr. Marvel noted that no formal action was needed because the letter had already been submitted to meet the requested deadline. The board did, however, question if this waiver could potentially neglect that source of funding in the future 

and/or reduce any kind of support. Mr. Barnes addressed this by stating that, while he can’t speak

for SHA, his knowledge and past experiences in working through this program is much like the county’s state aid funding. He provided a brief explanation of the pool balances and how this should not affect future funding. 

Next, the discussion turned to the Smith Island bridge rehabilitation project. Mr. Marvel advised that the repairs have been completed and, also while the contractor was there, they were able to tar and chip an additional 1,000 feet. 

Following that, Mr. Marvel presented a request to extend Stone Contract 2019-1. Mr. Marvel advised that the current contract is with Vulcan Materials LLC and they have provided the county with favorable pricing for FY 2027. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to extend Stone Contract 2019-1 as presented. By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the board, the motion carried. 

Last under roads discussion items, Mr. Marvel advised that the FY26 line striping was completed in June. The Roads Department was able to double stripe approximately 155,000 linear feet and 8,800 linear feet of single stripe. The list of roads completed are: Flower Hill Church Road, College Backbone Road, UMES Loop Road, Old Princess Anne Rd, Lawson Barnes Rd, Cash Corner Rd, Freedom Town Rd, and Phoenix Church Rd. 

## **WATERWAYS** 

Mr. Marvel advised that he received the Ewell County dock plans and bid documents on June 11, 2026. He advised that they have been reviewed and sent back to Mr. Young (as he in the process of implementing our changes in those documents). The final draft for review should be received in the next two weeks and sent to DNR for their concurrence before putting the project out of bid. 

The board then questioned the bridges in Ewell, and Mr. Barnes advised the department is still waiting to hear back from the grant opportunity that the county partnered with Smith Island United with for the $800,000 to rehabilitate those two bridges on the island.
