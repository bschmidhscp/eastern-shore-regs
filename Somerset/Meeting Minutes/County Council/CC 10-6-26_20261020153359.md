# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 

FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

October 6, 2026 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were 

also present.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 10-6-26 

Ms. Cindy Tawes, Somerset County Dog Control came forward at this time to accept a certificate of appreciation for her 20 years of service to Somerset County. 

Next, Mr. John “Kevin” Collier came forward to accept a proclamation in recognition of his retirement, serving more than 33 years of dedicated service to Choptank Electric Cooperative. 

Ms. Karen Hughes, Ms. Karen Krieg, and Ms. Merland Omeus from the Life Crisis Center came forward to accept a proclamation acknowledging October 2026 as National Domestic Violence Awareness and Prevention Month, encouraging all citizens to embrace their moral obligation to prevent and respond to domestic violence and recognize the impact of domestic violence in our community. 

Last, Mr. Oscar Mejia Garcia came forward to accept a proclamation recognizing October 15, 2026 as White Cane Safety Day and call upon all citizens to recognize the white cane as a tool of independence for blind pedestrians on our streets. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer came forward at this time to present the bid award recommendation regarding comprehensive rezoning and zoning Ordinance updates. He explained that $70,000 was budgeted to update the plan, which once approved, will complete the process for twenty years. Three bids were received at our meeting held on September 8, 2026; The Zone Company for $130,000, Smith Planning and Design for $85,450 and Davis Bowen and Friedel (DBF) for $69,485. Following staff review for compliance the recommendation was made to award the bid to DBF for $69,485. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to award the bid to update the Comprehensive Rezoning and Zoning Ordinance to DBF for $69,485. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show ofhands, therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

Next, Mr. Drewer presented a request for release of lien, through the County’s Housing Rehabilitation Grant Program. The grant, in the amount of $10,085, with a 10-year lien, granted on April 11, 1988, expired on April 11, 1998 and was used to fund repairs at the property. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing made a motion to release the lien in the amount of $10,085. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands by those present, the motion carried 4-0. 

Last, a permit fee waiver request was received from Somerset County Long Term Recovery Committee Inc., in the amount of $240.00 for the elevation of a property located at 2957 Byrdtown Road. The project will raise the home to base flood elevation.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 10-6-26 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the permit fee waiver as requested by Somerset County Long Term Recovery Committee Inc. Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of hands, therefore the motion carried 4-0. 

Mr. Danny Thompson, Economic Development Director and Ms. LeeAnn Linton, Administrative Assistant came forward to present the Enterprise Zone Renewal Application and Resolution authorizing the designation of an Enterprise Zone within the City of Crisfield and Parcels of Land within Somerset County for approval. A Public Hearing was held by the County on August 25, 2026, and by the City of Crisfield on September 30, 2026. Mr. Thompson requested approval for the President signature on the cover page of the application as well as signatures on the Resolution, numbered 1236 as presented to complete the process, which is in place for 10 years. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the President's signature on the cover pageofthe application as well as signatures on the Resolution, numbered 1236 as presented. Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of. hands, therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

Last, Mr. Leatherbury presented two tipping fee waiver requests submitted by Somerset County Long Term Recovery Committee Inc., for debris removal for homes damaged due to tepetitive flooding located at 110 Maple Street, Crisfield MD and 2957 Byrdtown Road, Crisfield MD. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the tipping fee waiver requests as presented by Somerset County Long Term Recovery Committee Inc. Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of hands, therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

With no further business, the Board was advised of<sup>the needfor a closedsession.Therefore,</sup> at 2:19 p.m., upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, a motion was made to go into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. All those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 4-0. 

The Commissioner Regular Session and Closed Session Meetings concluded at 2:25 p.m.
