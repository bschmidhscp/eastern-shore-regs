# **SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY OCTOBER 28, 2025** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, and Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also present for today’s meeting were Interim County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Mike Marvel, County Roads Assistant Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Craig Mathies to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the September 30, 2025 meeting were presented for approval; however, the Board requested more time for review. 

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

Mr. Barnes began with a discussion regarding the closure of Edelen Webster Road. The closure involves a stretch of 0.024 miles (429 feet) of road, and the sale is to be made to John and Deborah Jagger, David and Sharon Purdue, and Tammy and Douglas Price. A public hearing was held on June 11, 2024, with a 10-day write-in period, during which no opponents came forward. At the July 9<sup>th</sup> meeting, the board unanimously voted to proceed with the formal closure. County Attorney Kirk Simpkins prepared the necessary deeds and settlement agreements, which were presented for signatures. A brief discussion was held regarding the perpetual easement for a 12inch cover pipe that aids in road drainage. 

Following this, a motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and carried by a vote in favor by all present members of the Board to move forward with signing the deed for the sale and closure of the discussed portion of Edelen Webster Road. 

Next, Mr. Barnes moved to the formal proceedings for the closure of a 0.27-mile stretch (1,452 feet) of Halls Creek Road. The sale of this road is to Gordon and Russell Ford, as well as Vernon and Sarah Vermillion. Similarly, a public hearing was held on June 11, 2024, with no opposition during the 10-day write-in period. At the July 9<sup>th</sup> meeting, the board voted unanimously to proceed. Once again, Mr. Simpkins prepared the deeds and settlement documents.

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and carried by a vote in favor by all present members of the Board to move forward with signing the deed for the sale and closure of the discussed portion of Halls Creek Road. 

Next, Mr. Barnes presented a request from Smith Island United and the residents of Tylerton seeking the Board’s commitment to participate in a partnership with the University of Maryland Sea Grant Extension and Watershed Restoration Program to apply for grants. These grants are aimed at developing plans and conceptual designs to address nuisance flooding in Tylerton. The Board's participation would involve providing technical support, expertise, and access to county resources like surveys, reports, and permits. No financial commitment is required. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to approve the letter of support. By a vote in favor by all present members of the Board, the motion carried. 

Mr. Barnes provided a copy of the snow policy for the upcoming winter season. The county’s snow removal responsibilities were reviewed, including damage to mailboxes during snow events. The policy had been successfully implemented in previous years without issue, and no changes were recommended. 

Next, Mr. Barnes provided an update on recent support from the Roads Department. The department had assisted with the Deal Island School’s fall festival by providing lights for the afterschool program. The department was also helping with traffic management for an upcoming Halloween event at the Civic Center, aimed at preventing traffic congestion. The board was informed that the event was scheduled for October 28-31, but weather might affect the plans. Concerns were raised about the safety of the event and insurance, to which Mr. Barnes advised the insurance agent was working on those details and the policy should be in place today. 

# **WATERWAYS** 

A discussion was held regarding the Tylerton Breakwater Project. Mr. Barnes advised that the final bid tabulation report indicated that Coastal Design and Construction was the lowest bidder at $456,800. The project will involve constructing a 160-foot by 4-foot wide stone breakwater in the community of Tylerton. The funds for the project are provided through a 100% grant from the Department of Natural Resources (DNR), with no financial obligation to the county. After reviewing the bid and receiving concurrence from the county engineer and DNR, the Mr. Barnes recommended awarding the project to Coastal Design and Construction.

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to award the bid to Coastal Design and Construction. By a vote in favor by all present members of the Board, the motion carried. 

Next, Mr. Barnes discussed a $25,000 maintenance grant received from the Department of Natural Resources. The funds will be used for general maintenance of county facilities, including dock and pier replacements at Fairmount, Deal Island, and Wenona. The grant offers more flexibility than previous funding sources, and additional resources available for county maintenance needs.
