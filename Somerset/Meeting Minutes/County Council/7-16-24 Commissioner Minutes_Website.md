SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS REGULAR SESSION: TUESDAY JULY 16, 2024 

# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **REGULAR SESSION TUESDAY JULY 16, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Somerset County Commissioners Regular Session to order and asked for Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Vice President Randy Laird was absent for the meeting. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for July 16, 2024</u>** 

On July 16, 2024, from 2:21 p.m. until 3:04 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) to consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Vice President Randy Laird was absent for today’s meeting. Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, Director of Technical and Community Services Jesse Drewer, Director of Human Resources Jamie Manaia, Director of Finance Faith Redmond, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Legal; Actions Taken: None; (2) Personnel; Actions Taken: 1) Salary Increase, 2) Termination. 

At this time, Mr. Richard Ward was invited to come forward for the Board to recognize his 

twenty years of service to the County.

SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS REGULAR SESSION: TUESDAY JULY 16, 2024 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Services, joined the meeting to request the approval of Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation (MALPF) rankings for FY25; a total of 18 applications. Mr. Drewer advised that no public comments were received from the recent public hearing (with the time period ending Friday July 12, 2024). 

With no further updates, nor written comments received from the public, a motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the MALPF Easement Applications (18). By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Next, Mr. Drewer advised that the public comment period for the Blight Removal Grant (CD-20-CD-25) ended on Friday July 12, 2024 and reported that no public comments were received.  Mr. Drewer continued by informing the board that this grant is now closed with the State and he will back before the Board once more at a future meeting with the final closeout letter from Ms. Cindy Stone. 

Mr. Drewer left the meeting at this time and Mr. Charles Cavanaugh, Director of Solid Waste and Maintenance, joined came forward. 

Mr. Cavanaugh advised that he was before the Board today to present and request the approval of the proposal for design from Davis, Bowen & Friedel, Inc. for the purpose of renovating the Somerset County Dispensary to serve as the new location for the Somerset County Board of Elections office. Several options for design will be prepared for the Board of Election to review. Mr. Cavanaugh advised that the estimated cost for this project is $36,350.00 (which is under the amount that was budgeted). 

Following a brief discussion, a motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the proposal from Davis, Bowen & Friedel, Inc. as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Cavanaugh then advised that for the need of a construction extension request for the purpose of the methane flare at the County Landfill. He explained that the new one is not generating enough methane and, due to the budget, will need to extend the construction by two years. This request will be presented via a letter addressed to MDE with President Charles Laird’s signature.

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the letter to MDE with the construction extension request and authorize the President’s signature on the letter. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Cavanaugh left the meeting. 

Next, Mr. Leatherbury reviewed correspondence received and sent. 

A request was received from the Crisfield Heritage Foundation for tax exemption status for 2024 on the following properties: Acct ID 12-9100 Ida Ward Linton Property, Acct ID 125741 Calvary Road Property, Acct ID 07-124708 1012 W. Main St. This exemption request totals $231.30. Mr. Leatherbury advised that this request has been in approved in the past. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the tax-exempt request for the Crisfield Heritage Foundation as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Leatherbury advised that the Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development has received an application for financial assistance through the Neighborhood Business Works Program for the property expansion of Crab & Cruise, Inc. The Board does not need to take any action on this. 

A fee waiver request was received from Eastern Shore Long Term Recovery for building permits (totally $430.00) for homes that have been affected by tidal flooding; with this request specifically for 3545 Freedomtown Road, Crisfield, Maryland. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to grant the fee waiver request as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Under discussion items, Mr. Leatherbury advised that the Tax Appeals Board has two vacancies (1 seat and 1 alternate). 

A pauper burial request was received from Anthony Ward funeral home for Ja’Zyiah Roberts.

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to approve the pauper burial request for Ja’Zyiah Roberts. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

A pauper burial request was received from Bradshaw and Sons Funeral Home for Connie Landon. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the pauper burial request for Connie Landon. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Leatherbury advised that Mosquito Control is almost at their service limit for spraying this season. No action is needed at this time, but with the number of new customers this year, and not enough staff to service if they go over their service limit, a discussion will need to be held in the future to ensure the department can properly service the customers of Somerset County. 

Mr. Leatherbury presented the Education Article §6-1009(d) Certification for President Charles Laird’s signature. This document (signed by both the County and Board of Education) acknowledges the fulfillment of the requirement to ensure teachers in the county received a 10% salary increase between July 1, 2019 and June 30, 2024. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the President’s signature on the Education Article §6-1009(d) Certification. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

The meeting was then opened for comments from the public; however, there were none. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to approve the meeting minutes from July 2, 2024. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session.
