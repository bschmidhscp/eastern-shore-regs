# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY MAY 14, 2024** 

At 2:57 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for the start of today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Ms. Jamie Manaia, Director of Human Resources, joined the meeting at this time to present 

the health insurance renewal for County employees. 

Mr. Taylor took a brief leave from the board room but shortly returned. 

Ms. Manaia advised that the cost for the County’s healthcare plan will increase by 22.7% this year. A discussion was held in regards to the increase with Ms. Manaia advising the increase was due to the prior year’s claims (both medical and pharmaceutical) from employees who participated in the healthcare plan. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the Dental and Vision plan as presented (effective 7-1-24). By a vote in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster; the motion carried. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the Pharmacy and Medical plan as presented (effective 7-1-24). By a vote in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster; the motion carried. 

Ms. Manaia left the meeting at this time. 

Mr. Taylor advised that Mr. Kirk Simpkins has provided the 5-year lease agreement (staring on 5-15-24) between the County and Catholic Charities; adding contingencies such as biannual reports of operations at the homeless shelter (moving to semi-annually), lessee responsible for all utilizes, and lessor responsible for maintenance of grounds and building. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve Vice President Randy Laird’s signature on the lease as presented. By a
