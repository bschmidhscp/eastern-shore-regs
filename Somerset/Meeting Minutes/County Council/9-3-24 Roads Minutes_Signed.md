# **SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY SEPTEMBER 3, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also present for today’s meeting were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the August 6, 2024 open meeting were presented for approval. A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the minutes as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Darryl Willing, the motion carried. 

# **BID OPENING** 

President Charles Laird opened the one sealed bid submitted for the Ewell County Dock Project (LG-1920-25); stating it was from George Young in the amount of $15,500.00. With no other bids submitted, Mr. Barnes recommended the Board approve the bid per the price and good standing with the contractor. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to award the bid to George Young for the Ewell County Dock Project (LG-1920-25) in the amount of $15,500 (pending approval from DNR). By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

President Charles Laird opened the one sealed bid submitted for the St. Peters Creek Project (LG-1919M-25); stating is was from George Young in the amount of $13,800.00. With no other bids submitted, Mr. Barnes recommended the Board approve the bid per the price and good standing with the contractor. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to award the bid to George Young for the St. Peters Creek Project (LG-1919M-25)

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

An update was provided on the FY25 Road Resurfacing Project. Mr. Barnes shared pictures of the completed roads and advised that line striping has been scheduled. He then advised that the tar and chip portion of the road resurfacing project will begin tomorrow. 

Mr. Barnes advised that Chesapeake Utilities will be running a natural gas line from the industrial park to Linden Avenue, and will be working around the school schedule to avoid any traffic delays.

# **DISCUSSION** 

Vice President Randy Laird inquired if the backside mower will be coming to his district in the near future, to which Mr. Beauchamp advised it is on the schedule. 

Vice President Randy Laird then inquired about paint on a manhole on Silver Lane, and Mr. Barnes advised that it was in regards to the tar and chip project. 

A question was presented with regards to the Tidal Dike Grant meetings, and Mr. Barnes advised that a kick-off meeting is planned with FEMA, and an in-person meeting will happen following that to address needs (sometime in November/December). 

With no further business to discuss, a motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and carried on a vote in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster to adjourn the Somerset County Roads Board meeting at 2:20 p.m.
