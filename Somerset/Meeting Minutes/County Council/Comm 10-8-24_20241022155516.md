BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, FOR SOMERSET COUNTY October 8, 2024 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

Closed Session Summary Report 

On Tuesday, October 8, 2024 from 2:20 p.m., until 2:49 p.m., President Charles Laird, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird opened in Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. Commissioner Eldon Willing and President Charles Laird voted in favor, therefore the motion carried 4-0. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., Ms. Lory Ebron, Executive Aide, Mr. Jesse Drewer, DTCS Director and Ms. Jamie Manaia, HR Director, Finance Director Faith Redmond, Tax Supervisor Willis Dryden and Warden Muir were also present. 

Topics discussed: (1) Hiring Planning and Zoning Assistant Director/Zoning Administrator and Tax Collections Clerk I (2) Probationary to Permanent Employee Status (3) Probationary status extension (4) Adopt Budget Grants Administrator position (5) Employee suspension. Topics Approved: (1) Approval to hire Planning and Zoning Assistant Director/Zoning Administrator (2) Approval to hire Tax Collections Clerk I (3) Approval to move probationary employees to permanent employee status (4) Approval to extend status of probationary employee (5) Approval to adopt Budget Grants Administrator position (6) Approval to suspend employees without pay.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 10-8-24 

Mr. Taylor presented meeting minutes for approval regarding the Commissioner Special and Closed Session meetings held on September 17, 2024 and the Commissioner and Closed Session meetings held on September 24, 2024. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve the minutes from the September 17, 2024 and September 24, 2024 meetings as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By showofhands the motion carried 4-0. 

First, Mr. Taylor asked Mr. Jesse Drewer and Mr. Willis Dryden to come forward. He advised that both Mr. Drewer and Mr. Dryden have graduated from the Academy for Excellence in Local Governance at the University of Maryland School of Public Policy. Mr. Taylor congratulated both gentlemen for this distinguished accomplishment. 

Next, Mr. Leatherbury presented Mr. Ralph D. Taylor with a certificate of appreciation in recognition for his twenty years of dedicated service to Somerset County. 

Mr. Taylor continued with correspondence and discussion items. 

Letters were sent to Governor Wes Moore regarding the Somerset County Property Tax Assessment Appeals Board nomination, to Secretary Carolyn J. Scruggs requesting a meeting, and last to Reverend Doctor Doretha S. Whittington regarding her appointment to the Somerset County Administrative Charging Committee. 

Regarding discussion items, Mr. Taylor requested formal board approval for the following: 

- The Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between The Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) and Somerset County to provide a framework for the collaboration of FEMA and Somerset County to carry out their respective roles and responsibilities with respect to FEMA’s Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities (BRIC) Program. This agreement sets forth the terms by which FEMA will provide BRIC Direct Technical Assistance to Somerset County and not intended to be legally binding. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to formally approve the MOU between FEMA. 

and Somerset County as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of 

hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

- Payment of a fine, to avoid any additional penalties, assessed to the County regarding Landfill violations in the amount of $10,000 after review of the documents provided by the Maryland Department of the Environment. 

Commissioner Willing made a motion to formally authorize payment of the Landfill 

violation fine to Maryland Department of the Environment (Reference # SP-25-3048/ Invoice # AC040841) per the Consent Order in the amount of $10,000. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 10-8-24 

- The IT Department request for approval to apply for a Cybersecurity grant application in the amount of $90,885. Deadline to apply was October 3, 2024. This funding will be used towards additional cyber security efforts. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to formally approve the Cybersecurity grant application in the amount of $90,885. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of 

hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

President Laird opened the meeting at this time for public comment. 

Pastor Dean DeFino, Antioch Church, Princess Anne MD came forward. 

Pastor DeFino introduced himself to the Board, advising he has been attending Town meetings, but also wanted to meet the Board of Commissioners in person, and share exciting things that are happening at the church. He explained work performed recently to the sanctuary, and noted that their plan is to next work on the fellowship hall which has not been used for years. He is interested in the development of the Town of Princess Anne, sharing what he feels the church can do to promote activities. He discussed plans for these activities which included concerts, movies, stage events and roller skating and activities that home schoolers can attend to fulfill requirements. A Youth Pastor has been hired and he is very excited about offering these events to parishioners as well as the public. Events are free to attend. 

Mr. Robert J. McIntyre, 28101 Mt. Vernon Road, Princess Anne MD came forward. 

Mr. McIntyre noted landfill fine paid, and also discussed Flair project at the Landfill feeling that the burning off of methane gas will help the world. 

Commissioner Mathies advised the Board of two proclamation requests he received. The first was to acknowledge the Class of 1974 Golden Hawk Union 50" Anniversary Celebration. The second was to acknowledge the Reverend DeAngelo Antonio Johnson, Pastor of Dames Quarter-St. Charles Charge. The event is scheduled for October 20, 2024 at UMES. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the proclamation request for the Hawk 50" Anniversary. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands the motion carried 4-0. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the proclamation request to acknowledge Reverend DeAngelo Antonio Johnson. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By showofhands the motion carried 4-0. 

With no further business, at 2:20 p.m., the Board was advised of the need to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland
