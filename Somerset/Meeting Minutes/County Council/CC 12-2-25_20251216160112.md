BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

December 2, 2025 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Interim County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., was absent from today’s meeting. Internal Auditor Sharon K. Muir and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

## Closed Session Summary Report

Also present were Internal Auditor Sharon K. Muir, Director of Recreation and Parks/Tourism Mr. Clint Sterling and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron. Board of Commissioners Meeting 12-2-25 

Mr. Woody Bares, Public Works Director, came forward to present a Clearinghouse Review project, MD20251121-0716/Replacement of the Jersey Island Wastewater Pump Station in the City of Crisfield. Mr. Barnes advised he has reviewed the project which has no negative impacts to the county. The recommendation was made to code the project C5 (It is consistent with our plans, programs and objectives). 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to code Clearinghouse Review Project MD20251121-0716/Replacement of the Jersey Island Wastewater Pump Station in the City of Crisfield as C5. Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of hands, therefore, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, DTCS Director came forward. First, he explained that in October, his office received notification from the Maryland Department of Natural Resources regarding additional funding available through the Climate Pollution Reduction Grant (CPRG) for design/build funding for shoreline and wetlands stabilization in Deal Island. This funding will provide construction funding for the Frank’s Island shoreline stabilization and also provide additional protection of the wetlands north of Frank’s Island, ultimately protecting the Deal Island Road Causeway. Property owners are very eager and optimistic about this funding. The grant application is due December 12, 2025. DTCS staff will prepare the grant application and manage the grant if awarded. Additionally, a resolution (No. 1216) supporting the grant application as well as a Letter of Support were also presented for consideration of approval. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve the grant application, Resolution No. 1216 and the Letter of Support as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show ofhands therefore, the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr. Drewer presented a request for waiver of building permit fees in the amount of $620.68 received from Salisbury Neighborhood Housing Services (NHS) who are working in partnership with the Parkside High School CTE Program for the placement of a new threebedroom, 1.5-bathroom home to be placed on Skipjack Circle in Princess Anne. The home will be an option for affordable housing for low-to-moderate income individuals and families. Mr. Drewer advised this is the third project presented by NHS within the county. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve the building fee waiver in the amount of $620.68 as presented. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show ofhands therefore, the motion carried 5-0.

Ms. Muir continued with correspondence and discussion items at this time. 

First, she reviewed the letter sent to Mr. John French, advising him of his appointment to the Somers Cove Marina Advisory Commission. 

Next, Ms. Muir presented the State of Maryland, Department of Human Services Child Support Administration Cooperative Reimbursement Agreement which is submitted every threeyears. The last submission presented and approved was on October 11, 2022. The grant, in the amount of $22,240 (less $844.00 Administrative Fee) will be used to serve paperwork for Child Support cases by the Sheriff's Office. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing made a motion to approve the Child Support Cooperative Reimbursement Agreement as presented. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show ofhands therefore the motion carried 5-0. 

With no further business, a motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster to adjourn the meeting at 2:11 p.m. and enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. By a show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. At 2:15 p.m., President Charles Laird advised of the need to re-enter into regular session. Therefore, Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to enter in Regular Session. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

President Charles Laird advised the Board of a letter he received from Ms. Ruby Ring explaining that there are two non-working, uninsurable chicken houses on her property that need to be torn down due to their condition. Living on a fixed social security income, she requests the Board’s approval to waive the Landfill Tipping Fee, explaining that any help the county could give would be greatly appreciated. The Board discussed the request, with Commissioner Mathies expressing his concerns that a precedent may be set if the request is approved. It was recalled that approval had been previously given for a tipping fee waiver for a chicken house destroyed by fire. Followinga brief discussion, consensus was received to approve this request, which is approved on a case-by-case basis. 

Commissioner Craig Mathies Sr., again noting his concern in setting a precedent, made a motion to approve the request presented by Ms. Ring to waive the landfill tipping fees regarding
