# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **REGULAR SESSION TUESDAY NOVEMBER 21, 2023** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County met in regular session. Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for November 21, 2023</u>** 

On November 21, 2023, from 2:43 p.m. until 3:31 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, Director of Recreation, Parks, and Tourism Clint Sterling, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Personnel; Actions Taken: 1) Hiring, 2) Permanent Employee Status; (2) Board Appointments; Actions Taken: 1) Appointments to Board of Zoning Appeals. 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird declared a Public Hearing open regarding the Adoption of the Somerset County Building Code. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer and Ms. Mary Phillips, Department of Technical and Community Services, were invited to come forward. Mr. Leatherbury read into record the Notice of Public Hearing pertaining to the adoption of the Somerset County Building Code. Mr. Drewer then

President Charles Laird then called on any proponents and/or opponents. With none seated in the audience, a motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to accept written comments for up to ten (10) business days. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

With no further business to discuss, the Public Hearing was called to a close at 2:04 p.m. 

At 2:05 p.m., President Charles Laird declared a Public Hearing open regarding the Adoption of the Somerset County Floodplain Ordinance. Mr. Drewer then advised that since there were no changes requested at the Special Session held on October 17, 2023, the amended Floodplain Ordinance will be adopted via ordinance and repeal ordinance 1084 (pending any changes presented/requested at today’s public hearing). 

President Charles Laird then called on any proponents and/or opponents. With none seated in the audience, a motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to accept written comments for up to ten (10) business days. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

With no further business to discuss, the Public Hearing was called to a close at 2:09 p.m. Mr. Drewer and Ms. Phillips presented the Special Loan Program Reimbursement Affidavit which will allow the Department of Technical and Community Services to recoup funds expended if an application is not approved. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the Special Loan Program Reimbursement Affidavit as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

A discussion was then held regarding the nuisance problem of domestic fowl crossing property lines and affecting surrounding property. Discussion included amending the nuisance/zoning code to include language that will assist the County with enforcing regulations

Under Discussion Items, Mr. Leatherbury advised that a Member needed to be selected for the MACo 2024 Legislative Committee. 

On a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, the Board voted to place Commissioner Darryl Webster in the position for the 2024 Legislative year. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

The Board was then advised of the following boards with vacancies that need to be filled: Administrative Charging Committee, Board of Zoning Appeals, Building and Plumbing Code Appeals Board, Seafood Board, and the Property Tax Assessments Appeals Board. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to appoint Mr. Phillip Holland to the Seafood Board. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried.
