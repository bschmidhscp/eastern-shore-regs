# SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY OCTOBER 17, 2023** 

At 2:29 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board opened in Special Session upon a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster. This motion carried on a vote in favor, by a show of hands by, President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for October 17, 2023</u>** 

On October 17, 2023, from 2:29 p.m. until 2:45 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Personnel; Actions Taken: Promotion. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a closed session. 

Therefore, at 2:29 p.m., upon a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment,
