**SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY MARCH 17, 2026** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with Vice President Randy Laird, and Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent today. 

Also present for today’s meeting were County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Mike Marvel, County Roads Assistant Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Vice President Randy Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the February 17, 2026 open and closed session meetings were presented for approval. A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the minutes as presented. By a vote in favor by all present members of the board, the motion carried. 

## **ROADS DIVISION** 

The meeting began with a discussion regarding budget planning for FY27. Mr. Marvel advised that, since no paving work was completed last fiscal year, all paving contracts for the upcoming fiscal year will need to put out for bid. Ideally, these bids would need to be completed in late April, May and June to keep ahead of the summer paving. Discussion was also held regarding the price increases of fuel in an effort to advise the board that any submitted bids will more than likely be higher than they have in the past. 

Mr. Marvel then advised that the fuel site upgrades are complete and provided photos for the board. 

A discussion was held regarding Edgehill Terrace and the upcoming construction of a new Popeye’s. The board was advised that SHA has proposed to close off the entrance to Edgehill Terrace, which would redirect all traffic to flow through the parking lot of Goose Creek and Popeye’s. The board expressed concern over this proposed closure and how it would affect

<!-- page 3 -->
emergency response to the residents of that area. With the board against this proposed closure, Mr. Barnes and Mr. Marvel advised they will pass along the board’s concerns to SHA. 

The board was then advised that on April 22<sup>nd</sup> the students of Greenwood Elementary School will participate in an Earth Day activity. Due to this, organizers have reached out to request the temporary closure of Dryden Road for the event, as well as a dump truck for any trash removal. The board advised they are okay with granting this request. 

Lastly, Mr. Marvel report that Mr. Beauchamp and other County Roads staff recently participated in career day and were received well. 

## **WATERWAYS** 

Mr. Marvel advised that the Tylerton Breakwater project is now complete.
