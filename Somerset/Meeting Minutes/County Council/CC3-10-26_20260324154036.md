#### NOTICE OF A PUBLIC HEARING 

The Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County will hold a Public Hearing on: 

Tuesday, March 10, 2026 at 2:00 P.M. Somerset County Commissioners Meeting Room 

Room 111 Somerset County Office Complex 11916 Somerset Avenue Princess Anne, MD 21853 

In accordance with the Annotated Code of Maryland, Environmental Article, Sections 9-662(1)(2), 9-724(c)(iii) and 9-726.1(d)(1), the Somerset County Commissioners will consider the adoption of an Ordinance authorizing the Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. to establish a tiered water reconnection charge. 

FAIR SUMMARY 

The proposed ordinance will allow the Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. to establish a tiered water reconnection charge: water reconnection charge - basic of $40.00, water reconnection charge — delinquent of $45.00, and water reconnection charge — after hours of $110.00. This charge will apply when water service is restored to a property and will be in addition to a disconnect fee for properties that are delinquent on water and/or sewer service bills.

Additionally, Mr. Drewer presented another release of lien request at 12959 James Lane, Princess Anne, MD in the amount of $5,170.00 to fund repairs through the county’s housing rehabilitation program. The five-year lien was made on August 5, 2016 and expired on August 5, 2021, and therefore is also satisfied.
