# **BOARD OF COMMISSIONER FOR SOMERSET COUNTY SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY MAY 27, 2025** 

At 2:26 pm President Charles Laird called the County Commissioners Special Session to order upon a motion made by Vice President Randy and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies that carried by a show of hands in favor by all present members of the board. 

Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Darryl Webster.  Commissioner Eldon Willing was not present for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were Interim County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Ms. Jennifer Trager of Catholic Charities was now before the Board to present the BiAnnual Shelter Report. 

Ms. Trager began by confirming that the Seton Center was open; however, they have put a notice out advising that they are out of the prevention funding (for evictions and utilities). 

Moving on, Ms. Trager advised that Bethany House has been open since October 2024. She further advised that their biggest difficulty at this time his hiring staff (due to the complicated hiring process). She continued by stating that one of the barriers is getting the State of Maryland to report to Wilmington when CPS background checks come through for staff (a Diocese requirement since children/families are involved). Ms. Trager advised that Bethany House did have to close during the month of April due to staffing shortages. She then advised that since October they have assisted nine families, of which seven went into permanent housing. Bethany House currently has three families. 

Commissioner Craig Mathies inquired the length of time permitted for families/individuals to stay at Bethany House. Ms. Trager advised that families are typically there for an average of 90 days; however, some families/individuals stay longer if they need more assistance. She advised of two new case managers (one at Seton Center and one at Bethany House) who will be in charge of assisting individuals/families with finding affordable housing. 

Commissioner Craig Mathies then inquired as to how understaffed they currently are. Ms. Trager advised that she has ten staff members as of right now but, ideally, she needs fifteen. 

The Board thanked Ms. Trager for the information provided today during the Bi-Annual Shelter Report.
