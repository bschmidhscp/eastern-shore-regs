# **SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY FEBRUARY 20, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also present for today’s meeting were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Vice President Randy Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the January 23, 2024 Open and Closed meetings were presented for approval; however, the Board requested more time review and to revisit later in the meeting. 

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

Mr. Barnes advised that the dead trees have been removed from County property on 278 South Somerset Ave. 

Mr. Barnes presented the 2023 FHWA Bi-Annual Bridge Inspection Report, advising of the following bridges in various poor conditions: East Creek (Fair), Marumsco Creek (Poor), Shanks Creek – March Rd (Serious), Shanks Creek – Smith Island Rd (Serious), and Kings Creek (Fair). A brief discussion was held in regards to the Smith Island Bridges with Vice President Randy Laird inquiring if the bridges were still receiving traffic from heavy equipment. Mr. Barnes advised it is back to normal traffic with the only “heavy equipment” traveling over the bridges to being Fire/Emergency and Fuel vehicles. Mr. Barnes advised he is working on the most costeffective way to repair both of those bridges at the same time with the Federal Bridge Program. 

Mr. Barnes advised of a request from the Marion VFD for a gas card that would permit them to use the Gas/USLD pumps located at the Somerset County Roads Department and the Gas/Bio-Diesel located at Maryland State Highway. Mr. Barnes reminded the Board that a similar request was presented in May of 2022, but was denied due to concerns of liability and security. It was then clarified that this recent request was to utilize the pumps during hours of operation only. Mr. Barnes advised the request to use the pumps at the Somerset County Roads Department during normal operating hours could be accommodated with the Board’s approval; however, the Board

Mr. Barnes advised of a request from Mr. Alan Schultz to pave a portion/or all of Georges Lane. # **WATERWAYS** 

Mr. Barnes advised of repairs needed to a 2500 sq ft area at the Dames Quarter loading ramp due to broken up asphalt. The cost for this repair will be covered under the Waterway Improvement Funds in the amount of $5,000. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the request as presented. Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster voted in favor; therefore, the motion carried. 

Mr. Barnes then advised of the misuse of County docks on Smith Island (Ewell, Tylerton, and Rhodes Point). At Ewell and Tylerton, a ferry is tying up and staying docked for hours (utilizing the dock for business purposes), and a houseboat has been occupying the dock at Rhodes Point for the past sixty days. The Board entered into a discussion on how to proceed with these current violations and ensuring they do not continue in the future. 

A motion was then made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to authorize Mr. Barnes to send letters to the parties in violation advising them of the rules outlined in Resolution 1066 and advise their vessels need to be moved. Furthermore, the Board authorized signage to be placed at the docks regarding the time limitations.  By a vote in favor by Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried as presented.
