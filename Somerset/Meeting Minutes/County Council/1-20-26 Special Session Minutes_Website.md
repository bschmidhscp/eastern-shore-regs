# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY JANUARY 20, 2026** 

At 2:25 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session on a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the Board. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were Interim County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary 

Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for January 20, 2026</u>** 

On January 6, 2026 from 2:25 p.m. until 3:08 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; (7) to consult with counsel for legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Interim County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Mr. Robin Cockey (via teleconference), Mr. Kirk Simpkins, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Personnel, Action Taken: Probationary to Permanent Employee Status; (2) Topic: Legal, Action Taken: None. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:25 pm, on a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (7) to consult with counsel for legal advice.

Board of County Commissioners For Somerset County
