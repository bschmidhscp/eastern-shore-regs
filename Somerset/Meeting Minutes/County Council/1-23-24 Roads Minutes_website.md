# **SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY JANUARY 23, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird. Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also present for today’s meeting were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the November 28, 2023 and December 19, 2023 meetings were presented for approval; however, the Board requested more time review and to revisit later in the meeting. 

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

Mr. Barnes advised that all work has been completed on the Whitehaven Ferry Bulkheading and Road Repairs project. Wicomico County is currently installing a better gate and it is estimated that the ferry will reopen in two weeks (weather permitting). 

Mr. Barnes informed the Board of the 1<sup>st</sup> Annual Noxious Weed Control Committee meeting approaching in March that will be held at the Road’s barn, and invited the members of the Board to attend that meeting to learn about the weed control plan that will be outlined for the year. 

Mr. Barnes advised that SHA has provided proposed changes for Somerset County, due to an unfortunate increase in accidents and fatalities. These changes would include the mandatory use of headlights in certain areas as well as an upgrade to signage throughout the County (48” stop signs and Look Again signs at certain intersections). 

An update was provided on recent weather events in the County and safety concerns regarding staff who are out in the unfavorable conditions tending to downed trees (etc). Mr. Barnes advised they are running into issues with numerous calls regarding “ETAs” and when roads will be reopened; which is not something they are able to accurately provide as each situation is different and sometimes involves the assistant of other entities (utility companies, etc) before the Roads department can do their part. Another issue they have encountered is motorists carelessly driving past Roads staff, whether by refusing to slow down or stop when directed to. With that

Vice President Randy Laird inquired about dead trees on a County owned property in Crisfield and inquired if the Roads department could drop them (as one has fallen on a neighboring property). A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to authorize Mr. Barnes to add this vehicle to his fleet. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Barnes then shared photographs of the new wheel loader and thanked the Board for allowing him to purchase it. 

# **WATERWAYS** 

Mr. Barnes advised that all work on the Webster’s Cove Marina Improvement project is now complete, submitted, and approved for reimbursement from DNR. 

Mr. Barnes then advised that the Dames Quarter Bulkheading project is complete, minus the paving which will be completed near the end of April because of the weather. He also advised that this is the first facility to utilize solar lighting. 

Mr. Barnes reminded the Board of the DWTC 50 Year Anniversary being held on February 2, 2024.

# **DISCUSSION** 

A discussion was held in regards to the placement of a pipe on Clarence Christie Lane (presented by President Charles Laird). Mr. Barnes advised that Mr. Beauchamp was contacted by a property owner on the North side of Clarence Christie Lane about a driveway permit. During this visit to the property, a discussion was held with regards to ponding water on Clarence Christie Lane that stages for long periods of time; therefore, a cross over pipe was proposed to alleviate the issue. Mr. Barnes advised that this was met with opposition from landowners from the South side of Clarence Christie Lane who expressed concerns. Mr. Barnes advised they investigated the concerns (as well as conferred with Mr. Cavanaugh) and find the cross over pipe to be a reasonable solution. 

Following this discussion, a motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to authorize Mr. Barnes to move forward with putting the pipe on Clarence Christie Lane. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Vice President Randy Laird requested repairs to the floodgate on William Maddox Road as it is not closing all the way. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster inquired if a tide gate has been considered next to the boat ramp at Webster’s Cove. Mr. Barnes advised he can investigate it and report back to the Board. 

Minutes from the November 28, 2023 and December 19, 2023 meetings were then approved upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, which was voted in favor and carried by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Somerset County Roads Board Closed Session.
