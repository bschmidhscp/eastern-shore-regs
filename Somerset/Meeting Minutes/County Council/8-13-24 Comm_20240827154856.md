Next, Mr. Drewer explained that in the 2024 Maryland General Assembly Session, Counties and the City of Baltimore gained the ability to establish by a law a special tax rate for vacant and abandoned property as passed with House Bill 2. He explained that this law dictates that vacant lots or improved property cited as vacant and unfit for habitation by a building inspector can be subject up to 2.5 times the rate for real property taxes. If established on or before December 1 of each year, a report to DHCD must be submitted. Mr. Drewer recommended the Commissioners hold a Work Session to discuss the bill further. The Board was in consensus to schedule a Work Session Meeting. 

Mr. Drewer then explained that as a component of the CDBG Housing Rehabilitation Program, site inspections for lead by third parties are completed per requirements. Due to program changes, grant funds are not allowed to be used for lead testing. This change will automatically disqualify homes built before 1968, a majority of Somerset County housing stock. It was noted that a $7,500 match is included each year, which is not subject to DHCD regulations and has been given CDBG approval from Ms. Cindy Stone to be used for lead inspecting purposes.
