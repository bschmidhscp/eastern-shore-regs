The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Commission for review. The first quarter revenues exceeded expenses by $409,215.41; the surplus is partially due to reimbursements received after the end of Fiscal Year 2023 from the United States Department of Agriculture and the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) for the Smith Island Clean Water Project. #### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project by stating MDE has commented on the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) stating their concern with this method being the most cost effective. A Pilot Testing Agreement has been drafted and forwarded to all Parties for their review. 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project, stating there has been no movement with the project since DBF’s letter of recommendation for treatment for Princess Anne water was disbursed to MDE for review. Maryland House Delegate, Charles Otto sent a letter to Governor Wes Moore endorsing the method of combining aquafers in an effort to reduce the tannins. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating construction will be breaking ground on November 13" 2023 with completion expected for January 2024. 

##### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP), stating the District has issued a Request for Quotes for mowing and maintaining the District's 2 lots associated with the Pump Station on Rhodes Point. All quotes must be accompanied with a Certificate of Liability Insurance, completed W-9, and submitted to the Sanitary District on or before November 15" 2023.

Princess Anne — Action 

# MDE Compliance/Enforcement Assessment 

MDE has issued a Compliance/Enforcement Assessment/Offer of Settlement to the Sanitary District for sanitary sewer overflows (SSOs) for the period of January 2021 — December 2022. The SSOs occurred 4 times in the 2-year pan in the areas of Deal Island Road, Route 413 intersection, and Beechwood Street. Rather than file a formal enforcement action in this case, MDE, in an attempt to provide a timely and equitable resolution, is offering the District an opportunity to settle, without admission of liability or concession, with a penalty payment in the amount of $2,986.00. MDE is requesting a response within 14 days of receipt (11/02/23). The Commission were in agreeance that settling was the best option for the District. Mr. Bagley made a motion to accept the settlement offer presented by MDE in the amount of $2,986.00. Ms. Somer’s seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

## Industrial Park Well Pump Replacement 

Mr. Ludy explained to the Commission, the Industrial Park/Washington High Well and Tower has failed and needs to be replaced. Numerous lightning strikes and power surges have caused control outages and stressed the well pump and motor as a result. Mr. Ludy provided the Commission with a proposal from Somerset Well Drilling to replace the well pump in the amount of $11,750.00. There may also be additional cost of $8,000.00 if the 2 inch galvanized drop pipe needs to be replaced when the pump is pulled. This well supplies a large volume of water supply to the Town of Princess Anne. Mr. Bagley made a motion to approve the proposal by Somerset Well Drilling for $11,750.00 for Industrial Park/Washington High well pump replacement and additional cost of up to $8,000.00 for pipe if needed when replacement is installed. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion unanimously carried to approval.

### Request for sewer credit 

Bonita Porter, Owner, Nelson’s Real Estate, is requesting a sewer credit for property located at 30472 Broad Street for unintentional water usage that didn’t enter the sewer system for treatment. Ms. Porter explained the Tenants of the property unknowingly left the faucet on outside for the entire July 4° 2023 holiday weekend causing usage of 129,760 gallons. The Sanitary District were unable to perform a meter audit due to the age of the meter, but confirmed usage is back to normal by meter readings performed in September and October 2023. Management is recommending crediting the sewer portion based on their average usage of 20,050 gallons since the water did not enter the sewer system. Ms. Somers made a motion to credit the sewer portion based on Management's recommendation. Mr. Bagley seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.

### Request for sewer credit 

Wayne Johnson, St. James United Methodist Church, 11146 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, was not present for his appointment at 1:45 pm; however Ms. Mister had previously spoken with Mr. Johnson and was aware of his request for a courtesy credit. Ms. Mister explained the Church had a high volume of unexplained consumption during June and September 2023 quarters, according to the meter audit, the leak stopped on or around July 27" 2023. The Church has no record of fixing any leak at the property and are not sure how the water was used. Since there is no explanation of usage, Management is recommending forgiving the interest currently accrued on their account and offering a 3- year payment plan with no interest to help make the balance more affordable for the Church. Mr. Bagley made a motion to offer a 3-year payment agreement with no interest and forgive all interest currently charged to their account based on Management’s recommendation. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval. 

Brett Davis, Simple Fiber, postponed his appointment with the Sanitary Board. 

Thomas Lilly, Property Owner, Marsh Road, Smith Island, was present at 2:15 pm to discuss the Rhodes Point Pump Station. Mr. Lilly owns a secondary home he rents out as an Airbnb across from the Pump Station and feels the appearance of the Pump Station is very unattractive and a deterrent to customers. Mr. Lilly suggested enclosing the Pump Station, however, Chris Derbyshire, GMB, stated enclosing the Pump Stations could not be permitted for proper function as they are not explosion proof. Mr. Lilly would like the Sanitary District to take measures to make the Pump Station more appealing. Mr. Stockus stated they have discussed placing lattice around the base of each Pump Station. Mr. Bagley made a suggestion of having a mural painted on the base of the Pump Stations to make them more visually appealing. Mr. Riggin coincided with Mr. Bagley’s suggestion and stated he could have a discussion with the Somerset County Arts Council to coordinate painting the murals. The Commission stated they would take steps within feasible reason to try to make the Pump Stations more appealing. 

No Public input received.
