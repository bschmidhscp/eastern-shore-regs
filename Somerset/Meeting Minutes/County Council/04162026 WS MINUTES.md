Somerset County Sanitary Commission Work Session Minutes of April 16, 2026 

A Work Session meeting of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, April 16! 2026 at 11:00 a.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Rich Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, and Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant. Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, was absent. 

Ms. Mister presented the Commission with following budget highlights.
