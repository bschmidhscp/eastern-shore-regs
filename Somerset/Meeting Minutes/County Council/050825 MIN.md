Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of May 8, 2025 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, May 8, 2025 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Josh Taylor, General Manager, was absent. The Crisfield (Peripheral Area) Board position is currently vacant. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Business — Information 

The Sanitary District held a budget work session on April 10° 2025. The Consensus of the Board was to move forward with advertising the following rates for quarterly water and sewer service, connection fees, bulk water sales and septage hauler fees for fiscal year 2026. Due to a projected budget shortfall, the District is recommending an across the board rate increase of approximately 10% to both water and sewer as well as a proposed rate increase in bulk water sales of 45 cents/1,000 gallons. Connection fees are also subject to an increase due to inflation. See advertised rate ad attached or by visiting the Sanitary District's page under Government on the Somerset County Website, www.somersetmd.us. 

The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Board for review. The third quarter (FY25) revenues exceeded expenses by $321,045.24. The Commission had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

Ms. Mister informed the Commission of the proposed amendment to the Employee Handbook, Sick Leave procedures. The drafted amendment, previously distributed for the Board’s review, includes the following additional language to the current employee handbook - sick leave verification: A Physicians’ certificate should identify any workrelated restrictions and timetables for the stated restrictions. The employee may resume their duties if those restrictions do not prevent them from performing the essential functions of theirjob as outlined in theirjob description. Employees who have exhausted their paid time off will be responsible for covering their health insurance premiums out of pocket. The consensus of the Board was to move forward with the proposed amendment to the Employee Handbook as presented; the amendment will be brought before the Board for approval at the next regular session. 

Ms. Mister informed the Commission of another proposed amendment to the Employee Handbook. The modification is an addition to the current On-call Policy in the employee handbook. The amendment draft, previously distributed for the Board’s review, includes

Mr. Mister informed the Commission of the proposed amendment to the Septage and Hauled Waste Disposal Policy to include the following additional language: Accounts which have been suspended and placed into collection status may be later reinstated, but only upon payment of all outstanding amounts owed, approval of the Superintendent, and entry into an Advanced Payment Agreement. The Permittee may request a reinstatement of a revoked permit. The request must be in writing and submitted to the Superintendent. The request must contain Permittee’s corrective actions to avoid future violations. Reinstatement of a suspended or revoked permit shall be in the sole discretion of the Superintendent, to be determined on a case by case basis. The consensus of the Board was to move forward with the proposed amendment to the septage and hauled waste disposal policy as presented. The policy amendment will be brought before the Board for approval at the next regular session. 

Ms. Mister informed the Commission of the proposed amendment to the Sewer Only Policy. The additions to the amendment include minor word changes as well as the following addition: Properties that were previously connected but no longer have a structure thereon may be eligible for “no charge” provided the sewer connection has been properly abandoned (sometimes referred to as capped) to prevent inflow. The consensus of the Board was to move forward with the proposed amendment to the sewer only policy as presented; the amendment will be brought before the Board for approval at the next regular session. 

Ms. Mister informed the Commission of the proposed amendment to the Connection Applications. The District is recommending increasing the standard cost of connection fees for water, sewer, and fire service. The price for parts has rapidly increased over the last few years and the District is finding that every connection, whether it be residential, multi-family or commercial, are costing more than the connection fee collected with the application. The shortage is billed to the customer as a miscellaneous invoice (cost overrun) once the connection is complete. The proposed amended connection applications, previously distributed for the Board’s review, includes an increase in connection fees across the board. The proposed rates are presented in the FY26 budget Advertisement attached. The consensus of the Board was to move forward with the proposed amendment to connection applications as presented. The amendment will be brought before the Board for approval at the next regular session.

Management, with guidance from Legal Counsel, have drafted an Advanced Payment Agreement for septage haulers who have lost their disposal privileges and would like to have their permit reinstated with the District. By entering into this advanced payment agreement, a bill will be issued to the permit holder every 30 days which outlines their disposal usage at the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant. The monthly invoice will be due upon receipt, any advanced funds in their account will automatically apply to the monthly bill. The agreement lists the Applicant's responsibility to maintain advanced payment, those who do not abide by the agreement will have their permit suspended. It was the consensus of the Board to move forward with the proposed Advanced Payment Agreement as presented. The Advanced Payment Agreement will be brought before the Board for approval at the next regular session. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) Enhanced Nutrient Removal (ENR) Upgrade by stating the District are holding monthly progress meetings with Davis, Bowen & Friedel (DBF) to discuss this project as it develops. DBF will be submitting request for qualifications (RFQs) for mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP), as well as geotechnical engineering services. 

Mr. Padgette stated to the Commission, there has been no movement with the Princess Anne Color Removal Project since last month’s commission meeting. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project by stating the old lab is permanently closed and the lab equipment has been moved to the new lab. The District is currently outsourcing lab services until the new lab is online. Mr. Padgette stated the floors are done and they are beginning to move the furniture in. The project punch list will be addressed during final walk through prior to project completion. 

#### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project stating the new WWTP is online, however the subaqueous force main (SFM) has not been connected from Tylerton to Rhodes Point. As stated in the last Commission meeting, the SFM pipeline has lifted from its intended position near the Tylerton connection. GMB has issued a Notice of Defect to Crofton Construction (Contractor for SFM) addressing this issue, once the District has more information the Board will be updated.

## Utility Truck emergency repair 

Mr. Padgette informed the Commission, the crane on the 2016 utility truck needed emergency repair. The crane is used to pull pumps and clean obstructions such as rags away from the impellors. A new crane installed on a truck with proper rigging costs around $13,000.00. Ms. Somers made a motion to retroactively approve the invoice from Meadows Hydraulics, Inc. in the amount of $6,243.40 for the emergency utility truck repair. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

### Princess Anne — Action 

## Maintenance Agreement 

The District's legal team, along with Management, have drafted a maintenance agreement to be executed between the District and the Somerset County Detention Center (SCDC). It has been a long-standing practice that the County requests the Sanitary District provide the labor and materials necessary for the maintenance and repair of their grinder pump which is a financial responsibility of the County. The agreement states the responsibilities of both parties and allows the District to be reimbursed for all expenses arising from the utilities necessary for the proper monitoring and operation of the Grinder. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the maintenance agreement between the SCDC and SCSD as presented. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion was unanimously carried to approval. 

##### Site Plan 

Bohler Engineering are requesting the approval of Wendy's restaurant site plan. Management has reviewed the plan and have provided Bohler with comments to address. Ms. Somers made a motion to authorize the General Manager to execute the site plan for Somerset Crossing contingent upon the District’s review and approval of requested changes. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously. 

#### Leak Credit 

Raja Properties LLC are requesting a leak credit for their property located at 11244 Old Princess Anne Road. The owners explained in their request, they had a busted pipe under the house they repaired themselves. A meter audit was performed and verified the leak to be subsided. Management is recommending the Board approvea credit for the March 2025 quarter based on the leak credit policy. Ms. Somers made a motion to approvea leak credit for 11244 Old Princess Anne Road based on Management's recommendation. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.
