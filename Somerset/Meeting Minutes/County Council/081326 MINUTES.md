Somerset County Sanitary Commission
Minutes of August 13, 2026

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday,
August 13, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex.
Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman,
Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member and Barbara Somers,
Member. Also in attendance were Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw,
Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior
Administrative Aide, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Josh Taylor,
General Manager, was absent. Tim Howard, Resident of the Crisfield Peripheral Area, was
the only Member of the Public.

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m.

General Information

Mr. Bagley was pleased to announce, Maxine Landon, has been reappointed by the
Somerset County Commissioners to serve another six-year term with the Sanitary District
Commission Board as the Commissioner for the Smith Island service area.

Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information

Mr. Padgette updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment
Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating the next step in moving
forward with the project is to prepare a RFP (Request for Proposals) for the Membrane
Bioreactor for the WWTP, with hopes of construction beginning in early 2027.

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal
Project by stating DBF (Davis, Bowen & Friedel) are 30% done with the design phase.
Once complete, the next step will be submitting the design plan to MDE, Stormwater
Management, for approval.

Mr. Padgette informed the Board, regarding the Lead Service Line Project, the District is
still awaiting final approval from the Maryland Board of Public Works to fund the Swordfish
equipment and support. Once approved, the District can order and begin utilizing the
Swordfish equipment.

<!-- page 3 -->
Smith Island Subdistrict — Information

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by
Stating a ribbon cutting ceremony is planned for October 21°! 2026. The event will
celebrate the completion of the new WWTP, Pump Stations, and Force Main Upgrade
also known as the Smith Island Clean Water Project. The ceremony will recognize the
funding agencies, engineers, and contractors while emphasizing the project's water-
quality, resiliency, and Chesapeake Bay restoration benefits.

Fairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information

There were no updates since the last commission meeting regarding the
Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension Project.

Public Input

No public input was received by the District.

Approval of Policy & Resolution — Vehicle Use

The District presented the Commission with a first read of the proposed Vehicle Use
Policy for their review and discussion at the July 9° 2026 board meeting. The vehicle use
policy is intended to reduce the risk of accidents, injuries, liability, and property damage.
This policy applies to all SCSD employees operating a vehicle to conduct business. This
policy, which includes Defensive Driving Training, will qualify the District for a discounted
rate of insurance. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the policy and resolution for the
vehicle use policy as presented. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried
unanimously to approval.

Request to waive accrued interest — 21228 Wharf Road

Ms. Mister explained to the Board, she spoke with Lory Ebron, Executive Aide for the
County Commissioners, regarding this outstanding debt service account. Ms. Ebron
explained this bill was missed as their Office thought the Tenant was paying it and the
Tenant thought the Landlord was responsible. The County Commissioners are requesting
the SCSD Board waive the accrued interest currently outstanding of $191.41 as they plan
to settle the balance on this account moving forward. Management recommends waiving
the accrued interest on this account as requested. Ms. Somers made a motion to waive
all accrued interest for the debt service account of 21228 Wharf Road as requested. Ms.
Elliott seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.
