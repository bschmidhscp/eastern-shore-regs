BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

April 23, 2024

Commissioner Mathies asked how much of an increase is being requested from the County. Ms. Johnson replied $1,950,000 with the inclusion of full School Resource Officer funding ($350,000). He asked how much is going towards teacher’s vs administration. Ms. Johnsonreplied 4% is spread out and will cover all of the employees. 

Commissioner Webster asked how much State Aid funding increased, shown as $39,339,140 for FY25. Ms. Johnson replied $1,578,000, noting that FY25 is the smallest increase showing over the 10-year period. Commissioner Webster asked how many teachers are Board Certified. Ms. Johnson replied two. The Board was told that there will be no cuts to programs if full funding is received as requested as much as possible. The Board was also advised that nonlicensed teachers will not have a position, as those positions will be cut as numbers are trimmed down, but the plan is for every program to continue. 

Commissioner Mathies noted the comment made on additional cuts, and requested projections regarding cuts and costs before a final decision on funding is made by the county. Dr. Gaddis advised that cuts will include downsizing, not filling administrative/board positions, and spending cuts. 

Vice President Randy Laird asked about teachers and administrative hiring made using COVID funding (30 positions), and now that those funds are depleted, will positions be eliminated. Dr. Gaddis confirmed that they will not be able to keep all the positions, however he noted that there has been a problem finding qualified applicants for teaching positions, and these grant funded people can be moved over to fill vacant positions (34 currently). Vice President Randy Laird also added that in years past, the Board of Education has presented only MOE or level funding, so the request for additional funding is unchartered territory for the County to consider. 

President Charles Laird expressed concern about finding qualified applicants. He then addressed those teachers in the audience today, expressing his appreciation for what they do not only for our county, but our schools and our children. It is his personal opinion to see good funding going towards keeping quality people here for our students. The Commissioners are elected, but the teachers earn their jobs, and it’s not taken lightly. Appreciation was expressed and they were thanked again by the Board of Commissioners for all they do. 

Dr. Gaddis, Mr. Davis and Ms. Johnson were thanked for presenting the Fy25 Proposed Operating Budget for the Boards consideration, and left the meeting at thistime.

Ms. Randi Merritt, Atlantic Virginia, and teacher at Crisfield High School, and President of the Somerset Teachers Association, came forward to address the Board in support of the Board of Education (BOE) Budget, requesting full funding as requested by the BOE. Teaching for over 40 years she noted how teaching shape’s the heart and the minds of the next generation. Full funding will help retain high quality staff. Two-thirds of Somerset County employees are county residents, and full funding will help those local families as well. Referencing the requirements to Blueprint, this funding will help to meet those requirements developing a strong core system that will meet our students needs. This additional funding will allow smaller class sizes that will allow teachers to give more individual instruction, updated technology and resources equipping classrooms with the demands of the 21" century, and will show that we believe in the value of education and the potential of our children, She asked that the Board consider the long-term impact of this full funding request, and invest in our education today. 

Mr. David Amold signed up to speak but had left the meeting.
