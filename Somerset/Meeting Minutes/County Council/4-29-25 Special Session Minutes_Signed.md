# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY APRIL 29, 2025** 

At 1:35pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the Board 1:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, and Commissioner Craig Mathies. Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster were not present for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

Internal Auditor Sharon Muir and Budget & Grant Administrator Cynthia Sapp were now before the Board to discuss the reallocation of ARPA funds. Ms. Muir advised that, as part of preparations for the upcoming annual ARPA expenditure report, they convened to resolve several funding allocation issues to ensure compliance with federal reporting guidelines. 

The first issue discussed involved the Detention Center's HVAC project, originally budgeted at $132,000. Although the work was completed, the funding was inadvertently charged to the County’s capital account rather than the ARPA account. Since the time window to correct this has passed, Ms. Muir proposed reassigning $125,000 from an existing ventilation project (already included in the ARPA system) as a substitute. This approach aligns with the original intention of the HVAC allocation and allows for accurate federal reporting. Utilizing this amount from ARPA will also allow the project to be removed from the FY26 budget plan, representing a reduction in future expenditures. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy to approve this reallocation. By a show of hands in favor of all present Board members, the motion carried. 

The second item involved remaining unspent funds (approximately $94,000) from various completed or partially completed ARPA projects. These amounts were small residuals resulting from budgeted amounts not being fully used, either due to cost savings or adjustments during implementation. Ms. Muir proposed consolidating these remaining funds and applying them to the already-approved airport hangar project. Since the airport hangar project is still open and active,
