SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD 

December 23, 2025 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., Commissioner Darryl Webster and Commissioner Eldon Willing present. 

Also present were Interim County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., Public Works Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Mike Marvel, Assistant County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Aide Lory Ebron. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the October 28, 2025 regular meeting was presented for approval. Upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing the minutes were approved as presented by show of hands. The motion carried 5-0. 

Closed Session Summary Report 

The Roads Board did not have a closed session meeting on this day.

Roads Division Discussion Items 

Mr. Marvel first updated the Board regarding the request for a tum Jane at the Crisfield Transfer Station on MD 413 made following a recent accident occurring at the location. He has been advised by the State office that a review has been performed, and in conclusion, it was determined that a sign will be put in place southbound identifying the Transfer Station location, a warming sign will be put up advising of left turning vehicles, and the centerline stripping will be extended. 

Recent community support efforts were discussed which included assistance with the Deal Island and Princess Anne parades, and the delivery of light plants to Asbury Church for their Christmas event.
