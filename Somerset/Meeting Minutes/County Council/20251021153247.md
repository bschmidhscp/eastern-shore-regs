She noted the importance of meeting with elected officials today to discuss the needs for the county and have dialog between the county and state offices. She noted the local bridge program, and their goals to improve the safety of our transportation network, preserving the transportation system and driving economic growth. More than $400 million has been secured for transportation revenue thanks to the Governor’s leadership, the work of the Maryland General Assembly and support from stakeholders. Ms. Sharon Muir, Internal Auditor, Mr. Joel Dixon, EMS Chief and Mr. Cory Polidore EMS Deputy Chief came forward to present two EMS contracts for Board consideration and approval. 

First, Ms. Muir advised they are presenting the Digitech Computer LLC Billing Service Agreement, which also includes Rider A (description of services, fees and Client responsibilities) and Rider B (business associate agreement regarding Protected Health Information). Ms. Muir explained that the County is contracting with Digitech Computer LLC to provide billing and collection services for the Emergency Medical Services (EMS) operation for ambulance transport services. The contract will also include reporting services (accounting and transport reports) and has a fee structure of 4.95% ofthe net monthly EMS billing collections with a minimum monthly fee of no less than $5,000, therefore the minimum cost for the year would be at least $60,000. The agreements being presented today also outline the responsibilities of both Digitech and the County regarding fulfillment ofthe requirements. Ms. Muir then explained that Rider B (Business Associate Agreement) addresses how PHI is provided, created or received between the County and Digitech and ensures privacy provision are adhered to per HIPAA and HITECH Act laws.

Next, Ms. Muir presented the Bay Area Receivables, Inc. (BAR), Customer Service Agreement for the Boards approval. She explained that the County is contracting with Bay Arca Receivables to render collection and recovery services to the County for any ambulance service billings that are referred by the County as being uncollectable by Digitech Computer LLC after their policies for collection have been utilized. She further explained that BAR complies with the provisions of the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act and abides by the code of ethics recommended by the American Collector's Association International. In exchange for their service, the County will by 35% of the account principle recovered by BAR on the County's behalf. The agreement outlines the terms and conditions for this service. Additionally, Ms. Muir is requesting approval for the HIPPA Business Associate Agreement governing the PHI ofclients who received ambulance services. This agreement is similar in form and governance to the agreement with Digitech for PHI. The County Attorney has also reviewed both of these documents and found them legally sufficient. 

Ms. Muir was asked by Commissioner Webster how long Digitech will attempt to collect payments due before it is turned over for collection. Ms. Muir explained 3 cycles, or 90 days. Commissioner Webster also questioned the fee of 35% of the account principle for payment. Ms. Muir advised that 35% is the standard, which Commissioner Webster agreed to be a true statement.
