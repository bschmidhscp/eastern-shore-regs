## Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of September 14!" 2023 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, September 14, 2023 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Chairman, Adam Riggin, Vice-Chairman, Charles Bagley, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant and Public Attendee, Edward Landon. 

Mr. Riggin called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Information 

The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Commission for review. The fourth quarter revenues exceeded expenses by $80,069.23; the surplus is partially due to the vacancy of the Assistant Superintendent Position, budgeted for Fiscal Year 2023. The Board had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

Mr. Stockus informed the Commission of a Notice of Proposed Class Action Settlement to resolve claims relating to PFAs (man-made chemicals) contamination of Public Water Systems. This Class Action Lawsuit has been sent to all Public Water Systems in the US that, as of June 30" 2023, are subject to the monitoring rules set forth in UCMR5 (large systems serving more than 10,000 people and small systems serving between 3,300 and 10,000 people). Mr. Ludy stated he hasn’t found any evidence of PFAs but will be vigilant if anything should progress. This Settlement information has been forwarded to SCSD Attorneys for their advisement. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project by stating the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) is still being reviewed by the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE). The Pilot Study is set to start mid November 2023. 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project, stating Josh Taylor, DBF, has drafted a letter to MDE with support from the Somerset County Commissioners, the Town of Princess Anne, and Property Owners in Princess Anne. The letter states DBF’s recommendation for treatment by combining Princess Anne’s two largest wells with carbon and blending that water with the PASE tower water. This treatment method would greatly reduce the tannins causing the dark color in Princess Anne water. The Somerset County Commissioners stated during their regular session held on 9/12/23 they were in full support and would contact the Governor’s Office for their support as well.

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating Tracy Gordy from the Town of Princess Anne asked to review the Site Plans for the project, we are awaiting approval to move forward from the Town of Princess Anne. 

#### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project, stating the Somerset County Commissioners were gracious enough to waive our building permit fee for construction of the new Waste Water Treatment Plant (WWTP) in the amount of $6,696.00. The WWTP upgrade construction is underway with the interior & exterior walls and roof trusses installed, along with the roof sheeting partially installed. The FRP (fiberglass reinforced plastic) tanks have also been delivered to the Island.

## Emergency Repair 

The Sewer Vacuum Jet Truck is being serviced and repaired for transmission and gear shifting issues by Stone’ Diesel & Truck Repair; the repair totaled $6,175.28. Mr. Bagley made a motion to approve invoice from Stone’s Diesel & Truck Repair for Sewer Jet Truck repairs in the amount of $6,175.28. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.

### Princess Anne — Action 

# Emergency Repair 

Mr. Ludy informed the Board, during a thunderstorm in August 2023, the Industrial Park Well sustained a power surge that is suspected to be due to lightning strike. The surge destroyed all communications and control circuits for the well. This repair invoice from John Funk Jr. in the amount of $5,441.21 is for the replacement parts and programming that were needed to get the well back on-line as soon as possible. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the invoice to Junk Funk Jr. in the amount of $5,441.21 for the Industrial Park Well Control Replacement. Mr. Bagley seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.
