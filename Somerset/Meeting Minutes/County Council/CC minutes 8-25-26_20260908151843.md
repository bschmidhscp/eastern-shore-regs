BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

August 25, 2026 

At 2:00 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., and Commissioner Eldon Willing. President Charles Laird and Commissioner Darryl Webster were absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were 

also present. 

Closed Session Summary Report 

A closed session meeting was not held today. 

# SE 

Vice President Randy Laird asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in 

Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Meeting minutes were presented for the Boards approval for the Commissioner and Closed Session meetings held on August 11, 2026. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing made a motion to approve the minutes as presented. Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 3-0. 

First, Ms. Erin Owens, Regent, Nanticoke Chapter, NSDAR was asked to come forward to accept a Proclamation recognizing September 17-23, 2026 as Constitution Week. Joining Ms. Owens were Ms. Wendy Reisten and Ms. Genene Murphy. 

Ms. Owens first explained that on behalf of the Nanticoke Chapter of the National Daughters of the American Revolution (NSDAR) a proclamation was requested to proclaim September 17" through September 23, 2026 as Constitution Week. She explained the history of the designation going back to 1956, signed by Dwight Eisenhower, advising the purpose of Constitution Week is to encourage all Americans to study the Constitution, and recognize our rights and freedoms. She thanked the Board of County Commissioners for their support of educational and patriotic initiatives to strengthen our community and honor our nation’s history. Mr. Leatherbury read the proclamation and presented it to Ms. Owens, Ms. Reisten and Ms. Murphy.

p.m. to receive public comment regarding the Somerset County and City of Crisfield Enterprise Zone Renewal. Mr. Danny Thompson, Economic Development Director came forward. Mr.

### NOTICE OF A PUBLIC HEARING 

## BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

## SOMERSET COUNTY AND CITY OF CRISFIELD ENTERPRISE ZONE RENEWAL 

The Board of County Commissioners will conduct a Public Hearing on August 25, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. in the afternoon in the Meeting Room of the Board of County Commissioners, Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

Fair Summary The purpose of the hearing is to receive public comment on the proposed Somerset County and City of Crisfield Enterprise Zone Renewal application for the designation of certain commercial areas as an Enterprise Zone under Title 5. Subtitle 7. Section 5-703, of the Maryland Annotated Code. A map of the Enterprise Zone Boundary can be reviewed at the Commissioner’s Office, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, Princess Anne, MD, of the County Office Complex.

County Administrator The ad was run in the County Times on Wednesday, August 12 & 19", 2026 

Mr. Thompson explained that the Enterprise Zone is a program that provides real property 

and state income tax credits for businesses located in Maryland. If within the designated zone, a business will receive credits, which can allow of job creations and investments. Somerset County has two zone designations; one in the City of Crisfield and one in the Town of Princess Anne. He advised that the Town has just renewed their application in June 2023, with the City preparing to file this fall. The application is required to be renewed every 10 years. He also advised that there are two types of<sup>taxcredits;a general income taxcredit ($1,000for qualified new employees)and</sup> a property tax credit deferred for business within the zone. He advised the County Tax Office processes these credits when received from the State.

Mr. Leatherbury advised that public comment will be taken for 10 days, until Friday, September 4, 2026 at 4:30 p.m. The public hearing was declared closed at 2:14 p.m. 

Ms. Manaia, HR Director came forward at the time to present the 2026 Integra Plan Supervision Agreements for Retiree and Dental/Vision for board approval. She explained that the cost had been presented at a previous meeting and she was here for the Boards approval for signature on the plans as presented. She stated that the plans remained essentially the same as last year, with a slight increase in administrative fees/rates. Attorney Simpkins has reviewed the agreements and found them legally acceptable. 

Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., made a motion to approve the Vice President's signature on the 2026 Integra Plan Supervision Agreements for Retiree and Dental/Vision as presented, Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye; therefore the motion carried 3-0. 

Continuing with discussion items, Mr. Leatherbury advised of two pauper burial requests received. The first was from the family of James M. Owen, a Somerset County resident who passed away on July 30" and having no life insurance. The family wishes to provide a simple cremation. The second was from the family of Keri Crump who passed away on August 12, 2026 with no life insurance. The family desires to have a basic funeral service and requests assistance. 

Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., made a motion to approve assistance in the amount of $250.00 payable to Anthony Ward Funeral Home to assist the Owen's family as requested. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. The motion carried 3-0. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing made a motion to approve assistance in the amount of $250.00 payable to Anthony Ward Funeral Home to assist the Crump family as requested. Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., seconded the motion. The motion carried 3-0. 

With no further business, at 2:18 p.m., a motion was made to enter into closed session made by Commissioner Mathies, and seconded by Commissioner Willing by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. ‘Those present voted aye therefore, the motion carried 3-0.
