# Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of April 13" 2023 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, April 13, 2023 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office, Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, and Josh Taylor, P.E., Davis Bowen & Friedel. Adam Riggin, Chairman, was absent. There were no Public Attendees. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

### General Business — Information 

Mr. Stockus discussed a few news articles including “Worcester County’s big poop problem” which states Worcester County is considering building a new wastewater pipeline to increase capacity along Route 611 (similar to the Fairmount Wastewater Treatment Plant Decommissioning and Sewage Transmission Project). 

The other article, “Septic tank permitting in Maryland faces backlogs, staff shortages” in the Bay Journal highlighted the septic permitting issues some residents are having in Maryland. The article featured Andrew Karolick, who voiced his concerns during Public Input at the February 9, 2023 Sanitary District Commission meeting. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus informed the Commission, there has been no movement since the last session regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR Upgrade Project. 

Mr. Stockus informed the Board, the Somerset County Board of Commissioners has received two recent complaints regarding brown water in Princess Anne. Mr. Stockus, Mr. Ludy, Mr. Bagley, and Mr. Williams were present to discuss the matter at the Somerset County Commissioner's Regular Session held on April 11, 2023. Mr. Stockus stated discoloration is typical in Princess Anne due to the tannins (natural fermented material) in the area; but assured the Board it is safe to drink. He explained, there are two options available to rectify the water discoloration; to invest in a filtration system at an estimated cost of $10 million, or obtain permission from the Maryland Department of the Environment to allow blending from the Revells Neck well, located in Westover (which was previously denied due to fluoride levels). Mr. Ludy discussed the maintenance performed to help with the issue but cited that most odor complaints are caused by resident’s hot water heater solenoid. The Somerset County Board of Commissioners thanked the gentlemen for their time and knowledge. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating an Advertisement for Bid will be published by the end of April for the Laboratory Fume Hood (and Accessories) and Laboratory Equipment. Mr. Ludy anticipates project completion by the end of September 2023.

### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SICWP) Subaqueous Force Main phase stating George, Miles & Buhr, on behalf of the SCSD, sent a written request to Crofton Industries (awarded Bidder) to inquire if they would consider extending the Bid acceptance period for their Bid of $2,442,000 received on November 23, 2021. This would avoid having to re-bid this phase of the project due to bid expiration. The District is awaiting Crofton Industries’ response.

## Princess Anne — Action 

## Request for additional credit 

During the Regular Session held on March 9 2023 the Commission took no action on the request for credit by Nicole Potosky for 11825 Beechwood Street pending the results of the meter accuracy test performed bya third-party meter testing company. 

The District received the test results on March 23" 2023 which showed the meter meets the AWWA standards. A copy of the test results was also mailed to Ms. Potosky on March 23" 2023 as well. The Board took no action on Ms. Potosky’s request for an additional credit because the meter was proven to be functioning properly.

## **<u>Approval of Plans</u>** 

Parker & Associates, on behalf of Market Lane LLC, have forwarded Comprehensive Development Plans for approval of Dollar General - Chase Plaza. Mr. Ludy and Mr. Stuckus reviewed the Plans and recommended the Board's approval contingent upon Parker & Associates adding a signature block for the Sanitary District's consent of water and sewer. Ms. Somers made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the Plans for the proposed Dollar General contingent upon the addition of SCSD block on the plans for ratification. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

## **<u>Smith Island - Action</u>** 

## **<u>Bond Resolution</u>** 

A Bond Resolution for the Smith Island Clean Water Project authorizing the issuance and sale of up to $3,600,000 of Bonds to be known as the "Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. Water Quality Bonds, Series 2023" was previously distributed to the Commission for review. The $1,600,000 Water Quality Bond, Series 2023A is a tax exempt loan with an interest rate of .80%.  The $2,000,000 Water Quality Bond, Series 2023B is a taxable loan (principal forgiveness). Ms. Landon made a motion to adapt of Resolution authorizing the issuance of both Bonds of Series 2023 for the Smith Island Road Clean Water Project. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval. 

## **<u>Transportation purchase</u>** 

During the regular session held on March 9<sup>th</sup> 2023, the Board approved the purchase of a 2001 Saturn sedan contingent upon passing inspection at Seller's Cost (John's Auto); for unknown reasons, the Department of Motor Vehicles stated the car was "red flagged" and would not pass registeration for the vehicle. 

Ms. Landon has since found another sedan, 2007 Mitzibishi Lancer, for a purchase price of $2,500 which also includes inspection cost. Ms. Somers made a motion to authorize management to purchase the 2007 Mitzibishi Lancer for $2,500 (inspection included) for personnel use on Smith Island. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

There was no Public Input. 

On a motion by Ms. Landon and seconded by Ms. Somers, it was unanimously agreed to adjourn at 1 :34 p.m.
