# **SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY OCTOBER 31, 2023** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird. Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird and Commissioner Eldon Willing were not in attendance for today’s meeting. 

Also present for today’s meeting were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Mr. Taylor introduced Mr. Ernie Leatherbury and welcome him on board as the new Deputy County Administrator. 

Minutes from the October 3, 2023 and October 17, 2023 meeting were presented for approval and approved on a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

Mr. Barnes provided an update on the NOAA Grant Application, advising that (due to the competitive nature of the grant) the County unfortunately lost out on obtaining the grant. The County can apply again in the future; however, the scope of work may need to be adjusted. 

Mr. Barnes then addressed a second NOAA Grant Application sponsored by Ducks Unlimited. Mr. Barnes summarized that this grant is to reestablish wildlife on the Martin Wildlife Refuge with an additional focus on shoreline resiliency; specifically, the 3,000 linear feet on Marsh Road and the Easter Point dredge spoil site. Additionally, they would like to include the work on the Ewell Basin and Rhodes Point Basin (originally included in the above NOAA Grant Application). Mr. Barnes further advised that a letter of support has been requested to submit with this application. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the letter of support as submitted for President Charles Laird’s signature. By a

A discussion was held regarding the White Haven Ferry bulkheading project and items that need to be addressed. Mr. Barnes requested permission from the Board to seek out a contractor in the area to complete these repairs. 

A discussion was held regarding the Deal Island Drainage, advising of permitting and easement issues. Mr. Barnes continued by advising that DNR is looking to secure funding through the NOAA Grant to 1. Identify the plan, 2. Permits and easements, and 3. Implementation. Through the $48,000 through the grant they can address options 1 and 2 (there are no funds to address option 3). This project will run through Somerset County Technical and Community Services. 

Mr. Barnes advised that the contractor for the Rhodes Point project has been awarded the job and work will begin around the first of November. This will cover the force main piping from Rhodes Point over to Tylerton. 

An update was provided on the recent USDA trip to Smith Island. The areas targeted during this trip were: ferry, shoreline resiliency, Tylerton bulkheading, trash removal, and bridges. Mr. Taylor then advised that he will keep the Board posted on a second trip that may occur sometime in December. 

Mr. Barnes requested permission to hire a Motor Equipment I Operator (a budgeted position). 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to grant permission for Mr. Barnes to hire a Motor Equipment I Operator. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried.

# **WATERWAYS** 

Mr. Barnes provided an update on the Webster’s Cove Improvement Project, advising the that the electric has been laid out and is good, and also advised that the floating dock has arrived (with ADA compliant railing). 

Mr. Barnes advised the 2500 cubic yards have been dredged for the Lower Wicomico Dredge project and the berm has been rehabilitated. A short video was provided for the Boards review. The video footage included a damaged straw bales barrier with some of them washed out, and a breeched ditch plug. A discussion was held and the Board inquired about the repairs, to which Mr. Barnes advised the contractor Contrell has subcontracted a landscaper to repair the breeched ditches and other various areas. Discussion continued with Mr. Barnes advising they will continue to monitor the situation. 

Mr. Barnes advised that the repairs to the Deal Island Marina (the fingers piers and repairs to the skipjack) have been completed.

Commissioner Eldon Willing inquired about the State cutting the road bed of Long Point Rd. Mr. Barnes advised that this process started years ago and is a solution for drainage issues (further advised that the process through the State takes a long time). 

Commissioner Craig Mathies commended the Roads Department on the speedy removal of a tree on the road. 

Vice President Randy Laird inquired on the status of an employee involved in an accident, to which Mr. Barnes advised he is well and the injury was not as bad as originally thought.
