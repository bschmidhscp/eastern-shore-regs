Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of July 10, 2025 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, July 10; 2025 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, Barbara Somers, Member, and Faith Elliott, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. There were no members of the public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

##### General Business — Information 

Mr. Taylor was pleased to announce the Somerset County Commissioners have appointed Faith Elliott as Sanitary Commissioner for the Crisfield Peripheral Area for a six year term. Ms. Elliott was present and welcomed by the Board and Management. 

Mr. Taylor informed the Board, the connection policy needs updating with improved language and clarity. The connection policy details the requirements for residential and commercial connections of water, sewer, and fire service. Management, with guidance from Legal Counsel, updated the policy as presented to the Commission. The consensus of the Board was to move forward with the proposed revision to the connection policy as presented. The amended connection policy will be brought before the Board for approval during the August meeting. 

#### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment (PA WWTP) Plant Enhanced Nutrient Removal (ENR) Upgrade by stating the District are holding monthly progress meetings with Davis, Bowen & Friedel (DBF) to discuss this project as it develops. DBF are evaluating the different ENR treatment options (Membrane Bioreactors, Dissolved Air Flotation, etc.) to determine which method of treatment will best suit for the PA WWTP upgrade. The PA WWTP was last upgraded as a Biological Nutrient Removal (BNR) treatment plant in 2001. 

Mr. Taylor stated to the Commission, there has been no movement with the Princess Anne Color Removal Project since last month’s commission meeting. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project by stating the District is currently outsourcing lab services until the new lab is online. Mr. Padgette stated the Lab is scheduled to be up and running in September 2025. The project punch list will be addressed during final walk through prior to project completion.

### Revision to Request for Services Agreement 

Mr. Taylor informed the Board during the July 10" 2025 Commission meeting, the request for services agreement needs updating. The agreement is a mutual legal binding document where the owner requests for the District to perform preliminary review services related to a project. The Agreement covers Engineering/Construction Administration expenses that are incurred for plan review fees and other expenditures during the plan review and construction phases of the project. Management, with guidance from Legal Counsel, have drafted a second read draft agreement, which includes but not limited to: Owner certification, scope of services, and reimbursement. The review team also felt the title, request for services, was too vague, therefore, they have renamed the agreement, Expense Reimbursement Agreement. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the Expense Reimbursement Agreement as presented. Mr. Williams seconded the motion which carried unanimously to approval.

### Request for one-time courtesy credit internal policy 

Ms. Mister requested approval by the Commission to implement a one-time courtesy credit issued by the Office Manager internal policy of up to $100.00 for eligible customer accounts. This credit would apply to penalty or miscellaneous charges in certain situations where the customer has made a good faith effort to pay but encountered an issue. This courtesy credit would be available once per account, for the duration of the ownership. If the property transfers to a new owner, the credit eligibility would reset. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the request for one-time courtesy credit internal Policy as presented. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

# Employee Health & Wellness Program 

Ms. Mister educated the Board of the health & wellness program being offered by Landmark Insurance, the District's health benefits provider. The wellness plan offers services that support your well-being and resilience in work and life with 24-hour telehealth, travel benefits and much more. Each employee is going to get a “bucket of money” to use towards worksite benefits. It is voluntary, employee paid benefit, so employees can decide if they want to participate or not. Employees can participate even if they are not on the District's health plan and their whole family will be covered. Open enrollment for this program is in September 2025. Ms. Landon made a motion to authorize the District to participate in the employee health & wellness program. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.
