# <mark>BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY November 7, 2023</mark> 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird, called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator-Clerk Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron was also present. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report</u>** 

On Tuesday, November 7, 2023 from 2:40 p.m. until 3:44 p.m., President Charles Laird, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, opened in Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. Commissioner Eldon Willing was also present. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent from today’s meeting. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Ms. Lory Ebron, Executive Aide were also present.

Tyshekia Wapels and Caroline Mollenauer, Fenix Youth Project came forward at this time to accept a proclamation recognizing November 2023 as National Runaway Prevention Month. 

Dr. John Gaddis, Mr. Jon Hill and Mr. Thomas Davis, Somerset County Public Schools came forward to present the FY25 Capital Improvement Plan. Dr. Gaddis thanked the Board for the invitation to attend, noting that there is no money attached to this year plan. He summarized the three priority projects that are being requested this year; Crisfield Academy and High School Systemic Renovations ($ 4 million), Somerset Intermediate School additions (no local share required) and Greenwood Elementary School Replacement ($70 million). Dr. Gaddis asked Mr. Hill to explain the proposed projects in more detail. The Board asked a few questions and a brief discussion was held regarding a centralized high school, with Dr. Gaddis discussing the functionality of a centralized high school, as well as elementary schools. He explained restrictions regarding state funding, and it was noted that due to changes being made and minimum attendance requirements, Deal Island Elementary may be eliminated due to its small number of students attending. The Board was then asked for their approval to authorize President Charles Laird to sign the letter acknowledging the Board has reviewed and approves of the FY25 Capital Improvement Plan. 

Commissioner Willing made a motion to authorize President Laird to sign the letter as presented. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Next, Attorney Kirk Simpkins came forward to present the proposed amendments to Ordinance No. 1180. Attorney Simpkins summarized the three amendments, previously presented, being made as required by the State and recommended moving forward. No written public comment had been received during the 10-day comment period. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Mathies to approve the amendments as presented for Ethics Ordinance No. 1180. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Mr. John Redden, County Engineer came forward to present the AECOM price proposal for a new T-Hangar and Apron Design project at the Crisfield-Somerset County Airport. Mr. Redden advised that we are moving forward with the project, and this phase is for the design of ten “U” hangars, 8 being a standard size and 2 larger sized hangars. The proposal also includes an

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve the lien release as presented. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Mr. Taylor continued with correspondence and discussion items. 

Tri-County Council for the Lower Eastern Shore of Maryland has requested Somerset County confirm it’s voting members, and also submit the nominees for the Executive Board positions. Mr. Taylor noted that Somerset County will hold the Chair (Commissioner Willing) and Vice Chair (Vice President Randy Laird) positions in 2024. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to nominate Commissioner Willing as TCC Executive Board Chair. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. Commissioner Willing abstained; therefore, the motion carried 3-0-1. 

Commissioner Willing made a motion to nominate Vice President Randy Laird as 3<sup>rd</sup> Chair on the TCC Executive Board. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. Vice President Randy Laird abstained; therefore, the motion carried 3-0-1. 

A letter was received from Maryland Association of Counties (MACo) requesting 2024 Legislative Committee Nominations (Member and Alternate). A brief discussion was held, and the matter was tabled until the full board is present. 

Mr. Taylor advised that notification was received from the Maryland Department of the Environment advising that a Virtual Meeting has been scheduled for November 8, 2023 at 6:00 pm, to receive public comment regarding the modification of hammermills to increase throughput and the installation of grain handling equipment at Mountaire Farms, Inc., Westover Feed Mill. 

A letter was received from Somers Cove Marina Commission advising that Mr. Kevin Wooster had to resign, and a replacement is needed.

Mr. Taylor next presented a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between The Somerset County Health Department and the Somerset County Detention Center. The MOU is for the mental health treatment for inmates, not to exceed $69,916.00, funded by a grant submitted through the Health Department.
