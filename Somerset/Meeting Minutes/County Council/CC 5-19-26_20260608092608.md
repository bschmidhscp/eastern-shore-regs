Board of Commissioners Meeting 5-19-26 

Ms. Muir explained they are before the Board today to present an amendment to the local abatement opioid plan. She explained that the County has received batches of monies thru the State, coming from the National Opioid Settlement. The Health Department is the only group putting in for the abatement plan and has been operating off of it for almost 2 years. After speaking with the State, it has come to our attention that the plan is for five years, instead of two. Therefore, the abatement plan is being amended for this reason and also because of funding that was going to be spent by June 30 will now be changed for a different use. Ms. Muir asked Ms. Weber to clarify further. 

Ms. Weber explained initially the plan was intended for 2 years. Their intention is to now hire a PT Peer, and software update for the overdose dashboard (although going forward we will be using the States dashboard). She also noted some staffing changes, keeping part of the Peer in there, noting a part of the salary for a Spanish speaking Peer, that will be working in Detentions, ECI and in the community. Additionally, a part salary for a Narcan coordinator is included, and vending machine health user supplies was also mentioned. 

Ms. Muir then requested a motion to approve this amended local abatement plan that the Health Department is utilizing through the County for our opioid funds that we accumulate. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to the amended local abatement plan as requested. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of hands, therefore the motion carried 4-0. Ms. Weber, Ms. Muir and Ms. Taylor left the meeting at this time. 

Mr. Leatherbury continued with correspondence and discussion items. 

Our office has received correspondence from the Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD) inviting the county to transfer our 2026 housing bond allocation in the amount of $1,192,638, to DHCD as has been historically done. These funds are used to issue bonds to fund housing programs or to issue mortgage credit certificates, creating affordable housing. It was noted that the county does not have the staff to adequately run this program. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve the 2026 housing bond transfer in the amount of $1,192,638. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0.
