Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of April 16, 2026 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, April 16, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, was absent. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Information 

The District presented a first read of an Extension Policy to the Commission. The policy is being updated to specify the timeframe of which payment is to be expected if an extension is granted. Other minor word changes were also made. With a consensus from the board, the policy will return next meeting as an action item. 

### Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating that the District is continuing to work with Davis, Bowen, & Friedel, Inc. (DBF) and Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) on design correspondence and adequate grant funding. The District is hoping to minimize any potential loan balance this project may accrue. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating the District is still working with MDE and DBF on the design phase but continue to see progress. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Lead Service Line Project by stating the District is continuing to work with MDE on this project and are working to collect and refine information that has been requested. 

### Smith Island Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) upgrade is reaching the end of completion. The District had a final inspection meeting on the island on 4/8/2026, which

## Fairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission in regards to the Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension Request/Petition by stating that he met with the County Commissioners last month to inform them of the bid award contingent upon funding. At this time, the District will be in contact with the USDA to inquire about a SEARCH grant. Once it is determined whether a SEARCH grant can be secured to fund the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) and Engineering Report (ER), the District will report back to the County Commissioners. If grant funding is not secured, the District will request that the County Commissioners consider funding the project. 

### Public Input 

Richard Crumbaker wanted to make a suggestion to Sanitary District Commissioners in regards to an annual EDU bill. After hearing that postage cost is increasing from the District's Budget Work session, Mr. Crumbaker suggested that customers should be offered an incentive or discount if they pay annually. He believes offering this option will be a way to save money on postage and will provide a way for the District to receive money ahead of time.

# Request for One Year, 0% Interest Payment Plan — 11696 Church St 

Gina Gerhard has requested a one year, 0% interest, payment plan for her property at 11696 Church St. Ms. Gerhard had a major leak at the property resulting in a high bill amount of $3,287.23. Management recommended to approve the payment plan to help alleviate the financial burden and make it more affordable for the customer. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve a one-year, zero percent interest payment plan for 11696 Church Street. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.
