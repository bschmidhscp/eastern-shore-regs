# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY FEBRUARY 17, 2026** 

At 2:39 pm, President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were Interim County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for February 17, 2026</u>** 

On February 17, 2026, from 2:39 p.m. until 3:07 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Interim County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, HR Director Jamie Manaia, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Personnel, Action Taken: Approval of Salary and Vacation Days. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:39 pm, on a motion made by Vice President Randy Laird, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
