Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of August 14, 2025 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, August 14, 2025 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, Barbara Somers, Member, and Faith Elliott, Member. Also in attendance were Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman and Josh Taylor, General Manager, were absent. Members of the public included, Stephen Masten, Paige Masten, Sue Ellen Nolan, Paula Compton, Diane Waller, Steve Hoffman, Frank Devein, Cindy Taylor, Jamie Taylor, and Anthony Stockus. 

The meeting was called to order at 1:00 p.m. 

###### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating the District is still in the planning phase and have been granted permission from MDE (Maryland Department of the Environment) to move forward with Design. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating this project is also still in the planning and design phase. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) Lab Project by stating the District is currently outsourcing lab services until the new lab is online. Mr. Padgette stated the Lab is scheduled to be up and running in September 2025. The project punch list will be addressed during final walk through prior to project completion. 

Mr. Padgette informed the Commission, the SCSD is participating in a federal and state mandated Lead Line Survey required by the State of Maryland and the Environmental Protection Agency. This survey involves multiple components, initial inventory and private side inspection. The District is planning to host a Town Hall in the future to inform the Public of this state mandated Lead Line Survey. 

###### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SICWP) stating the WWTP Upgrade project is 90% complete, a punch list inspection will be addressed during final walk through prior to project completion. The Subaqueous Force Main project is near completion and performing satisfactorily. The completion of the entire Smith Island Clean Water Project is projected for November 1, 2025.

##### Fairmount/Rumbley - Information 

On June 24'", the Sanitary District received a petition requesting extension of sewer service to Frenchtown/Rumbley. The petition was signed by approximately 26 property owners, which is a quarter of the 104 properties in the Frenchtown/Rumbley water service area. The District sent a letter to each of the petition signers letting them know that the petition will be placed on the agenda for introductory discussion during today’s Sanitary Commission meeting, and we welcome any input. Ms. Mister acknowledged that this is just the first step in the process which is addressed in the Maryland Annotated Code. The District will be sending letters to all 104 property Owners to inform them of this petition and request public input as well. Once that is done, the Sanitary District will be working with the Somerset County Commissioners to start the process of evaluating options to serve the area with sewer service. If the County Commissioners approve, the District will engage a professional engineering firm to prepare a Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) and an Environment Report (ER), that will evaluate the following: 

1. Different methods and options of serving the area with sewer. 

2. Health and Environmental impacts of the proposed project and different service options. 

3. Capital Costs to serve the area and financial impacts of the project. 

4. O&M (Operation and Maintenance) Costs to serve the area and long-term budget impacts of the project. 

The PER and ER will be used to determine if service to this area is physically and financially feasible, and plan/budget for any impacts. If the project moves forward, the PER and ER would be utilized to submit for grant and loan funding from various federal and state agencies. We will keep Somerset County Commissioner, Eldon Willing, representative for District 3, and the County updated as the planning phase of the project moves forward, and provide project updates at future Sanitary Commission meetings.
