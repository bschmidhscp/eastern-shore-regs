BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY January 16, 2024 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Darryl Webster, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr, 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present.

At 2:07 p.m., President Charles Laird declared the public hearing open to receive public input regarding the consideration to amend sections of the Zoning Ordinance that pertain to uses, to include regulation for the use of cannabis. 

Ms. Mary Phillips came forward to present the staff report. She explained that the Planning Commission amendment includes cannabis as an Industrial Use in Section 5.7(e), and adding Item #33 as an allowed Special Exception Use in the I-1 and I-1 Zones, with on-site consumption prohibited. Also, Section 17 definition (included within the agenda package) updates was also explained. 

Commissioner Webster asked if these establishments are allowed. Ms. Phillips stated they will only be open in industrial zones, reviewing the areas on the maps presented. These amendments are following how other counties are regulating Cannabis zoning. 

Commissioner Mathies questioned Carvel Hall, and the proposed Cannabis facility to be opened there. Ms. Phillips explained that location falls within City limits, and will therefore be required to follow city zoning regulations. 

President Charles Laird asked if there were any proponents present wishing to speak. There 

were none. 

President Charles Laird then asked if there were any opponents present wishing to speak. There were none, 

Written public comment will be taken for 10 days, or until Friday, January 26, 2024 at 4:30 p.m. The public hearing was declared closed at 2:11 p.m. 

Mr. Taylor continued with correspondence and discussion items. 

A letter was received from MDOT Secretary Paul J. Wiedefeld asking Somerset County’s support to seek solutions to make Maryland roadway’s safer for all travelers asking if the county had an interest in creating a local Strategic Highway Safety Plan. A discussion was held and it was noted that the majority of roadways in our county are rural. The Board was in consensus to take no action at this time. 

A letter was received from Tylerton Fire & Rescue Chief Christopher M. Marshall as follow up to their request to acquire the Crab Meat Co-Op building, located in Tylerton. The current space utilized by the fire company is not adequate, and they need more space. It was noted that the building was constructed with federal USDA funding, and therefore use restrictions may be in place. Mr. Taylor advised that he checking with Mr. John Sullivan, USDA State Executive Director. Additionally, he is checking to see if the property must be handled via sealed bid or

The meeting was next opened to receive public comment. Mr. Matt Lankford, 34283 Rehobeth Road came forward. He presented a handout he created to the Board, outlining audit information he has completed regarding the Board of Education. He reviewed his findings, questioning why the Board of Education requested funding last year when they had a $1.2 Million excess. He stated that the county can order an independent audit be performed to review the misuse of spending. He also questioned the spending of Covid money received, and the number of administrative staff hired. Mr. Lankford advised that the audits being performed are not looking at everything, criticizing tax increases, that are paying for high salaries and mis-spending. 

Vice President Randy Laird noted that the County has not increased taxes for many years now, and the increase seen in taxes is due to the higher state assessments that have been occurring, causing the increase, The Board thanked Mr. Lankford for the information presented.
