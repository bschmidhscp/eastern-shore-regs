BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY May 21, 2024 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., 

and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

Closed Session Summary Report 

‘A closed session meeting was not held today. 

— 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Mr. Leatherbury presented meeting minutes for approval regarding the Commissioner and 

Closed Session meetings held on May 7, 2024. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the meeting minutes as presented. 

Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Cavanaugh, Director of Solid Waste and Maintenance came forward to accept sealed bids received regarding Scrap Metal at the County Landfill. President Charles Laird opened the 

following sealed bids received as follows: 

Contractor: Bid Amount: Salisbury Scrap Metal/Joe Mayer $40 per ton Fitzgerald’s Auto $80 per ton 

Mr. Cavanaugh made the recommendation to award the bid to Fitzgerald’s Auto at $80 per 

ton. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to accept the bid from Fitzgerald’s Auto at $80 per ton as recommended. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Ms. Sharon Muir, Intemal Auditor, came forward to present a request to approve the LGIT sponsorship of the Somerset County Library System and the Somerset County Sanitary District as participants in the Local Government Insurance Trust Liability and Property Pools of insurance

She explained that pursuant to the LGIT Trust Agreement, the county must submit “Public Entity” sponsorship letters for the 2024-2025 renewal-year for these two component units that the county sponsors. Mr. Leatherbury continued with correspondence and discussion items. 

A request for the waiver oftipping fees was received from Mayor Taylor, City of Crisfield 

regarding demolition and clean up of the following surplus properties the county previously donated to the City as follows: 

- 332 W Chesapeake Avenue 

- 308 W Chesapeake Avenue 

- 321 W Chesapeake Avenue 

- 9N1* Street 

- 202 Laird Avenue 

- 329 Tyler Street 

- Vacant Lot Tyler Street (tree removal and grading) 

- Vacant Lot 308 Maryland Avenue (tree removal and grading) 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the Tipping Fee Waiver request as 

presented from Mayor Taylor. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Also, a Tipping Fee Waiver request was received from St. Paul’s Episcopal Church regarding debris from the remodeling/demolition of their church. Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the tipping fee waiver request for St. Paul’s Episcopal Church as requested. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands the motion carried 5-0. President Laird advised that Mr. Cavanaugh was asked to hold the invoice as the church was presenting the request for fee waiver. 

County Engineer John Redden was asked to come forward regarding the press release announcement from the Governor’s Office made regarding the $19.6 Million in Broadband Awards to provide internet access to 2,400 Maryland households. Somerset County will be receiving $1.1 Million. The purpose ofthis funding is to go towards running broadband internet to homes with long lanes. Mr. Redden explained that the three main providers are providing a match.

Mr. Leatherbury presented correspondence sent which included letters sent regarding Board of Zoning Appeals and Building and Plumbing Code Appeals Board appointments and the approval of a tipping fee waiver received from Princess Anne Volunteer Fire Company.
