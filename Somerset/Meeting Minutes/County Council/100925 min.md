Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of October 9, 2025 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, October 9, 2025 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon Member, (via telephone), and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, Ashley Stern, CPA, PKS & Associates, Alyssa Revell, Manager, PKS & Associates, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Information 

Alyssa Revell, Manager and Ashley Stern, CPA & Partner, PKS & Company, presented the Commission with the Fiscal Year 2025 Audit Report of the Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. Ms. Revell stated that this year the District had three audits, Independent Auditors’ Report, Government Auditing Standards Report, as well as a Uniform Guidance Report. Ms. Revell stated the Independent Auditors’ report is presented with an unmodified opinion, which is the highest level of assurance that can be given, the Government Auditing Standards report showed no significant deficiencies and no issues with no compliance. The Uniform Guidance report, required when an Agency exceeds $750,000 in federal funds during the year, stated the District complied with the types of compliance requirements that were required to be tested in relation to the Federal Grants. Ms. Stern wanted to point out that a clean audit opinion on Federal money is a rarity, typically only 10% of Agencies comply, and a testament to the District. Ms. Revell credited the Sanitary Staff for their administrative and organizational skills, making the Audit a pleasurable experience. The Commission had no questions for Ms. Revell or Ms. Stern. Due to the current U.S. Government shutdown at the office of management and budget, the District was presented with draft copies of the audit as the final audit package for any office with Federal Funds have not been released. Once available, a copy of the FY 2025 Somerset County Sanitary District, Inc. Audit may be obtained on the Somerset County Website, www.somersetmd.us. 

### Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating that it has been approved by the engineering team at Davis, Bowen, & Friedel, Inc. (DBF) and has now been passed

<!-- page 3 -->
along to MWIFA (Maryland Water Infrastructure Financing Administration) at MDE (Maryland Department of Environment) for their approval. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating DBF is currently reviewing the design proposal, once complete they will be forwarding to MDE for approval. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne WWTP Lab Project by stating that the project has been completed and plans to have the Board visit the new Lab in the near future. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Lead Service Line Project by stating that we are currently working with MDE to get approval to purchase our own equipment, to use long-term asa tool for leak detection, saving the district money and time when the inspection process of this project begins. 

Smith Island Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the subaqueous force main is performing well with Tylerton’s septage. The Waste Water Treatment plant is currently online and running but there are some glitches being worked out. Mr. Taylor stated that the pump station and force main are exceeding the design capacity. Mr. Taylor also updated Ms. Landon and assured her that her property will be restored before project completion. 

## Fairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regard the Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension Request/Petition by stating the Sanitary District will be making a request to be on the Somerset County Commission agenda to have a discussion on this potential project and how they would like to proceed in regards to the Plumbing Engineering Report (PER), Environmental Report (ER) and if they are willing to fund these reports. 

### Public Input 

No public input was received by the District.
