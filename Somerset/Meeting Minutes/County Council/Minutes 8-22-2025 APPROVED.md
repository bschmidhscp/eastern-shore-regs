# **<u>Minutes</u>** 

August 22, 2025 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Housing Board of Review meeting held on August 22<sup>nd</sup> 2025 at 9:00am in the meeting room of the Somerset County Technical and Community Services Office, Room 211, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland 21853. 

**<u>Board Members Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Eugene Borden Jesse Drewer, Director Rosalie White Casey Goldsborough, Housing Rehab Specialist Jackie DeMoss Stephanie Underwood, Housing Loan Specialist Mark Konapelsky, Building Inspector 

Casey Goldsborough opened the meeting at 9:00am 

### **A. NEW BUSINESS:** 

- **I. Bid Opening – 30003 Saint James Way Princess Anne, MD 21853** 

   - Ken Build LLC was the only bid received. The bid was opened for the work purposed with a bid cost of $9,715.00. However, the initial bid included an alternative bid to include the kitchen and dining room floor replaced for $13,115.00. 

- **II. Bid Review and Award – 30003 Saint James Way Princess Anne, MD 21853** A motion was made by Rosalie White and was seconded by Mark Konapelsky to approve the alternative bid for $13,115.00. the board voted unanimously to approved the alternative bid. 

- **III. Bid Opening – 26382 Burton Avenue Crisfield, MD 21817** 

Ken Build LLC was the only bid received. The submitted bid was for $23,585.00. 

- **IV. Bid Review and Award – 26382 Burton Avenue Crisfield, MD 21817** 

A motion was made by Eugene Borden and seconded by Rosalie White to approve the bid of $23,585.00. The board voted unanimously to approved the alternative bid.
