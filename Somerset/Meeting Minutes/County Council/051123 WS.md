Somerset County Sanitary Commission Work Session Minutes of May 11, 2023 

A Work Session meeting of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, May 11" 2023 at 11:00 a.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Adam Riggin, Chairman, Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent and Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide. 

Mr. Stockus presented the Commission with following budget highlights.
