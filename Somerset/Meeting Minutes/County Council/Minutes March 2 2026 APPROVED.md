# **SOMERSET COUNTY HOUSING BOARD OF REVIEW** 

Jesse Drewer, MPA, Director 

William Cornish, CFM, Assistant Director and Zoning Administrator 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Housing Board of Review meeting held on March 2<sup>nd</sup> 2026 at 9:00am in the meeting room of the Somerset County Office Complex, Room 216, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Board Members Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Eugene Borden Jesse Drewer, Director Jackie Wheatly Casey Goldsborough, Housing Rehab Specialist Stephanie Underwood, Housing Loan Specialist Mark Konapelsky, Building Inspector Dale Pusey, County Engineer 

Casey Goldsborough opened the meeting at 9:00am 

## **A. NEW BUSINESS:** 

**1. Election of Board Chair** 

At the beginning of the meeting it was addressed by Jesse Drewer that we have to elect a chair person for the Housing Rehabilitation Board. Jesse made the motion to nominate Jackie DeMoss as Chair. this was second by Eugene Borden and voted unanimously for approval. 

**2. Update on Somerset County Housing Rehabilitation Grant (MD-26-CD-27)** Casey Informed us the grant is currently under Environmental Review and we are exception to have our grant agreement with I the next couple weeks 

**3. Review Contractor Qualification Forms for Housing Rehabilitation The packets were by this order,** 

   - Esham Builders was opened and reviewed but was missing their insurance information and their MHIC license. 

   - Beauchamp Construction was opened and reviewed but was missing their insurance information and their septic installers license. 

   - Rob Mrohs HVAC & Propane Services was opened and reviewed all documents were included a heating and propane company. 

   - Tony Hilligoss/ J of all Trades was opened and reviewed all information was provided to be a lead inspector. 

   - East Coast Heating and Air was opened and reviewed all documents were included for a heating and propane company.

- ADJ/J&R was opened and reviewed but was missing their MHIC license. 

- Ken Build LLC was opened and reviewed but was missing their insurance information and their MHIC license.

After the Bids Were opened it was noted that a lot of contractors left out crucial information we needed for the board to approve them as a contractor. Also we noted we only had one septic installer, one pest control company, and zero well drilling companies. 

Jesse made a motion to wait to approve all contractors, reach out to all contractor that have submitted information and request any missing information and to reopen the request period for all Pest control companies, well drillers and Septic installer companies. The period will end on April 1<sup>st</sup> . This Motion was seconded by Eugene Borden was voted unanimously in favor of.
