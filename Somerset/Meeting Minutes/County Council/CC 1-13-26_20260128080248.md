### NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

Pursuant to the Somerset County Zoning Ordinance (Ordinance #1212), notice is hereby given that a public hearing will be held by the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to hear opponents and proponents on: 

## TUESDAY, JANUARY 13, 2026 @ 2:00pm. SOMERSET COUNTY OFFICE COMPLEX - COMMISSIONERS MEETING ROOM 11916 SOMERSET AVENUE, ROOM 111, PRINCESS ANNE, MD 21853 

‘The purpose ofthe hearing is to consider amendments to the established fee schedule for Planning & Zoning Fees, Building Permit Fees and Other Development Fees as established by Resolution #1181. 

Fair Summary 

The proposed changes to the fee schedule provide clarification on solar energy generation stations as outlined in the Zoning Ordinance, provide fees for energy storage devices, data centers and a fee for the technical advisory committee review. No other fees or changes are being amended or proposed. 

For further information about the proposed fees, please contact the Somerset County Department ofTechnical and Community Services at 410-651-1424 or view the proposed fee amendments in the DTCS office; Monday-Friday 8:30am-4:30pm from December 30", 2025 through January 13", 2026.

Ms. Jennie Challis, 8713 Courthouse Road, requested to address the Board. Ms. Challis advised she was here today of behalf of the School Board requesting the County Commissioners support the repeal of Maryland Education Article §3 -1204 which is causing problems for the way the School Board does its business. She stated we are the only county in the state of Maryland that has that to address and she was told that the County Commissioners will have to send the request to their lawyer. She advised she works for the State and knows how slow they can be. The Maryland General Assembly opens tomorrow, feeling this is a very simple request to ask them to repeal the article. She expressed her concern that per this law instituted in 1996, it is illegal for the Student School Board representatives to serve, which is not fair to those students, feeling we should take an urgent approach to this. By participating, these students can list their participation on their resume’s and collage applications. She asked the commissioners to put some urgency into this matter, advising she would be willing to work with Mr. Webster to help get it done and over to legislature. 

President Charles Laird explained that our office has spoken to the Superintendent and Senator Carozza, advising they have gotten everything they need to move forward with this. He explained that the Somerset County School Board, who are elected officials, are the local body required to proceed with the request to repeal that article, not the County Commissioners, who merely fund the School Board. The Board of County Commissioners is supportive of the School Board and students, but per legal counsel we were advised to not get involved with this legislative request. Ms. Challis repeated what President Charles Laird explained, to make sure she was understanding correctly, and it seems it was contradicting what she had last heard from the Board of Education, who say the County Commissioners are responsible. President Charles Laird again noted that the Board of Education is an elected board, representing the five (5) districts just as the Board ofCounty Commissioners, with our attorney advising that they have their own attomey and that we have heard through Senator Carozza that there is a legislative department that is available to guide them about the situation.
