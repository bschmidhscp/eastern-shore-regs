Somerset County Sanitary Commission 

Minutes ofJuly 13‘ 2023 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, July 13, 2023 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, and Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide. Chairman, Adam Riggin and Vice-Chairman, Charles Bagley were absent. Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times, attended via Teams. There were no Public Attendees. 

Mr. Stockus called the meeting to order at 1:10 p.m. 

# General Information 

During the February 9° 2023 Commission meeting Josh Taylor, Davis, Bowen & Freidel (DBF), informed the Commission he had submitted Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) State Revolving Fund Drinking Water Applications for the ‘Somerset County Sanitary District Lead Service Line Inventory’, ‘Jones Creek Watermain Crossing’, and ‘Princess Anne Water Supply and Treatment Improvements’ projects. The Sanitary District were recently informed they did not rank high enough to receive grant funding for either of these projects. The District plans to send MDEa letter thanking them for their consideration for SRF funding, and requesting that the five submitted Drinking Water/Clean Water projects be considered for any other funding opportunities within MDE’s resources. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project stating he is finalizing the Preliminary Engineering Report for submittal to MDE for their review. 

As mentioned during June’s Commission meeting regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project, Josh Taylor, DBF, is drafting a letter to MDE with his recommendation for treatment by combining Princess Anne’s two largest wells with carbon and blending that water with the PASE tower water. This treatment method would greatly reduce the tannins causing the dark color in Princess Anne water. Thereafter, we will be requesting letters of support to be addressed to the Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) from the Somerset County Commissioners, the Town of Princess Anne, and Property Owners in Princess Anne. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating the Somerset County Commissioners were gracious enough to give the District an additional $23,000 of American Rescue Plan grant money needed to help with the shortage of funds for the project.

### Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular and executive session minutes dated June 15‘ 2023 were previously distributed. On a motion by Ms. Somers and seconded by Mr. Williams, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular and executive session minutes dated June 15" 2023.

### Princess Anne - Action 

#### Award of Bid 

The SCSD placed an advertisement seeking bids for the Kjeldahl Nitrogen Analysis System and Accessories and the lon Chromatography System and Accessories for the PA WWTP. Lab Project. The recommended Bidder for the Kjeldahl Nitrogen Analysis System and Accessories was Velp Scientific with a bid total of $50,791.00. The recommended Bidder for the lon Chromatography System and Accessories was Fisher Scientific with a bid total of $34,144.13. Both bids were within the expected budget. Ms. Somers made a motion to award both bids to the recommended Bidders, Velp Scientific and Fisher Scientific for the PA WWTP Lab Project contingent upon supplemental funding. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

## Request for courtesy credit 

During the June 15'" 2023 Commission meeting, Rochelle Duncan-Baylor, granddaughter of Ethel Pollitt (deceased) property owner of 30261 Bowland Hill Circle, requested a credit for unexplained water and sewer usage. After speaking with Ms. Duncan-Baylor, the Commission tabled the request until this meeting to give her a chance to hire a Licensed Plumber to inspect the property for any possible leaks or explanation for the unexplained usage. The Sanitary District has not heard from Ms. Duncan-Baylor since the June Commission meeting; therefore, they took no action on her request for credit. Ms. Somers made a motion to offer Ms. Duncan-Baylor a 2-year interest free payment agreement for the current balance of $13,793.93. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval. 

##### Site Plan 

The Site Plan for Pro Coat office/workshop was previously distributed to the Sanitary District and the Town of Princess Anne for their review. Davis, Bowen & Friedel, on behalf of the Town of Princess Anne, reviewed the Plan and requested the Developer include documentation from the Fire Marshal stating no fire protection is required. Management is recommending the Commission authorize the Office Manager to ratify the Plan once this request is met. Ms. Somers made a motion to authorize the Office Manager to execute the Site Plan for Pro Coat contingent upon completion of requirements from Davis, Bowen & Friedel. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

#### Crisfield — Action 

#### Sewer credit 

Debra Jackson, on behalf of her mother, Pauline Jackson, requested a sewer credit for usage during the March 2023 quarter. Ms. Jackson explained her mother had a sewer blockage in her back yard, her brother used the garden hose water pressure to push the blockage out of the line, causing her bill to double in consumption. Because the water entered the sewer system and was treated, Management recommended taking no action on her request for credit, but offer a 2-year interest free payment plan. Ms. Landon made a motion to offer Ms. Jackson a 2-year interest free payment plan for her balance of $1,074.28. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.
