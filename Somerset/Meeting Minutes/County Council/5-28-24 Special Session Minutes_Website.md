# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY MAY 28, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the May 14, 2024 Somerset County Board of Health and Somerset County Commissioners Special Session were presented for approval; however, the Board requested more time to review them and asked to revisit them later in the meeting. 

President Charles Laird then call the Public Hearing for the “FY25 Community Development Block Grant Application – Blight Removal Grant” to order at 2:03 p.m. Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Services, and Mr. Casey Goldsborough, *need his position*, were invited to come forward as Mr. Taylor read the following notice into record: 

# **NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY FY 2025 COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT APPLICATION** 

# Tuesday, May 28, 2024 at 2:00 P.M. County Commissioners’ Meeting Room, Room 111 

# 11916 Somerset Avenue 

# Princess Anne, Maryland 

The Board of County Commissioners of Somerset County will conduct a Public Hearing to obtain the views of citizens on Community, Economic Development, and Housing Needs to be considered for submission of an application to the Maryland Community Development Block Grant Program (CDBG). Citizens will have the opportunity to discuss proposed projects and to provide input on other needs to be considered. The hearing will be held in the County Commissioners Meeting Room (Room 111), 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland 21853 at 2pm on Tuesday, May 28. The draft application will be available for the public to review on May 21 until June 4 in the Department of Technical and Community Services (Room 211) during normal business hours (8:30am-4:30pm). 

Citizens will be furnished with information including but not limited to: 

- the amount of CDBG funds available for State Fiscal Year 2025; 

- the range of activities that may be undertaken with CDBG funds; and 

- the proposed projects under consideration by Somerset County 

The Maryland Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Program is a federally funded program designed to assist governments with activities directed toward neighborhood and housing revitalization, economic development, and improved community facilities and services. It is administered by the Department of Housing and Community Development.

The Maryland CDBG Program reflects the State’s economic and community development priorities and provides public funds for activities which meet one of the following national objectives, in accordance with the federal Housing Community Development Act of 1974, as amended, that: 

- Benefit to low-and-moderate-income persons and households; 

- Aid in the prevention or elimination of slums or blight; 

- Meet other community development needs of an urgent nature, or that are an immediate threat to community health and welfare. 

All interested persons are encouraged to attend, but can review the proposed application in the Department of Technical and Community Services Office (Room 211) during normal business hours (8:30am-4:30pm), through June 4, 2024. Efforts will be made to accommodate the disabled and non-English speaking residents with 3 business days advance notice to Jesse Drewer, Director, 410-651-1424 

Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County Ralph D. Taylor, County Administrator 

Mr. Drewer advised that they are requesting $100,000.00 for this grant and the department 

is targeting ten (10) to fifteen (15) projects. He continued by stating that the current grant will expire at the end of June and has $78,307.44 remaining (after 32 projects completed), with a public hearing scheduled for July to close out the grant. Those funds will be returned to the State of Maryland and added to the new grant. Mr. Drewer then advised that utilizing the new funds within the two-year time frame will be more doable than the previous grant funds (of $300,000.00+). A brief discussion was held about the blight process (and why there were leftover funds) as some property owners were leery to enter into the agreement due to the five (5) year lien requirement on the property. Mr. Goldsborough advised that this time around they are looking at repeat nuisance violators and tax sale properties. The meeting was then opened to received comments from the public. With no members of the audience wishing to speak in favor or opposition, Mr. Taylor advised that written comments will be taken until 4:30 p.m. on June 7, 2024. Mr. Drewer then advised of a performance threshold requirement (starting with all grants as of FY25) that must be met for all grants; however, he is requesting a waiver from this threshold as there will be an overlap period between the current grant and the new grant. Mr. Drewer then requested President Charles Laird’s signature on the letter addressed to Ms. Cindy Stone requesting the waiver. A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve President Charles Laird’s signature on the letter to Ms. Cindy Stone. By a show of hand in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

The public hearing was then called to a close at 2:10 p.m. 

Mr. Goldsborough left the meeting.

Mr. Drewer advised of a request for a waiver of building permit fees from Robert and Sheila Lankford as they are replacing a doublewide mobile home on their property that was destroyed in a fire. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the waiver of building permit fees for Robert and Sheila Lankford as requested. By a show of hand in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Drewer advised of a request for release of lien for 8099 Woods Lane that was included in the County’s Housing Rehabilitation Grant Program in November of 1991. The fifteen (15) year lien expired in November of 2006 and, in order to be formally closed out and released, the actual release has to be approved by the County. 

Therefore, a motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, to authorize President Charles Laird’s signature on the release of lien for 8099 Woods Lane. By a show of hand in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Minutes from the May 14, 2024 Somerset County Board of Health and Somerset County Commissioners Special Session were approved on a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird. By a show of hand in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

With no further business, a motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to adjourn the Somerset County Commissioners Special Session at 2:16 p.m. By a show of hand in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried.
