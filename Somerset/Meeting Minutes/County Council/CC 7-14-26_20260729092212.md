Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the minutes as presented. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore the motion carried 4-0.

Next, Mr. Drewer presented Ordinance No. 1232 to reclassify the zoning for Map 23, Grid 03, Parcel No. 002 from Medium-Density Residential (R-2) to Agricultural-Residential (AR). The change was favorably recommended by the Planning Commission based on a substantial change in the character of the neighborhood. Additionally, the zoning change will allow the landowner to apply for the MALPF program. A public hearing was held on June 30, 2026, and no public comments were received. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve Ordinance No. 1232 has presented. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0.

Currently, there is approximately $67,000 remaining to be spent, with the grant ending on September 30, 2026. Mr. Drewer noted that unforeseen challenges with the hiring of staff at the shelter has caused delays, and recommends requesting the extension to allow time to spend the remaining funds. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to authorize President Laird to sign the letter requesting the extension regarding the CDBG for the Homeless Shelter. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of<sup>hands,themotioncarried4-0.</sup>

At 2:20 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Public Hearing open to receive public comment on the Establishment of the Somerset County Community Repair Reinvestment Fund (CRRF). He asked Mr. Drewer to come forward. Mr. Leatherbury next proceeded to read the notice ofpublic hearing into record as follows: 

NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY Community Repair and Reinvestment Fund 

Tuesday, July 14, 2026 at 2:20PM Somerset County Office Complex County Commissioners’ Meeting Room 11916 Somerset Avenue - Room 111 Princess Anne, Maryland 21853 

The Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County will conduct a Public Hearing to gather public feedback and consider adopting an ordinance to implement §1-322, Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis of the Annotated Code of Maryland. The purpose of the ordinance is to establish the purpose and uses for which money received from the State of Maryland's Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund (CRRF) may be used and disbursed. 

A Draft Copy of the proposed ordinance is available on the Department of Technical and Community Services website at: hutps://www.somersetmd.us/departments/departments_-_n_- zplanning and_zoning/index.php 

All interested persons are encouraged to attend, but can review the proposed ordinance in the Department of Technical and Community Services Office (Room 211) during normal business hours (8:30am-4:30pm), or online on the Department of Technical and Community Services website. 

Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County Emest J. Leatherbury Jr County Administrator Ad Date: 07/01/26 & 07/08/26 

Mr. Drewer advised that with the passage of “Question 4” in 2022, by Maryland voters, legalization to possess and consume cannabis in Maryland for individuals 21 and older was passed. In 2023 the Maryland General Assembly passed the Cannabis Reform Act (CRA) which allows for cannabis sales to adults 21 and older from licensed dispensaries. The CRA requires counties to establish a Community Reinvestment and Repair fund to provide financial support to communitybased organizations that were the most impacted. He explained the county will receive 5% of the statewide tax (previously 9% and now 12%) on cannabis sales noting the current balance in the fund totals $588,689, with restricted uses on what the funds can be spent on. The purpose of today’s hearing is to start the process, and allow Somerset County to begin expending the funds allocated. He summarized that the funding is intended to benefit low-income communities, and will be geared
