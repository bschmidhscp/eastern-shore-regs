BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY 

December 17, 2024 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

Closed Session Summary Report 

On Tuesday, December 17, 2024 from 2:18 p.m., until 3:08 p.m., President Charles Laird, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies Sr., seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster opened in Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Willing and President Charles Laird voted aye by show of hands, therefore, the motion carried 5-0. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Ms. Lory Ebron, Executive Aide, Ms. Jamie Manaia, HR Director, Ms. Faith Redmond, and Ms. Yvette Cross, Emergency Services Director were also present. 

Topics discussed: (1) Emergency Communication Specialist Hire (2) Employee termination. Approved: (1) Approval to hire Emergency Communication Specialist (2) Employee termination. 

—_—_ 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Willing to lead those present in Devotion and 

the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Mr, Taylor presented meeting minutes for approval regarding the Commissioner and Closed Session meetings held on December 3, 2024. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve the minutes as presented. Commissioner Craig Mathies Sr., seconded the motion. By show of hands those present voted in favor, therefore, the motion carried 5-0.

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director DTCS came forward to present two lien release requests. ‘The first request was for a CDBG Housing Rehabilitation Grant for work done at 30693 West Post Office Road, Princess Anne, MD to fund repairs in the amount of $6,450.00. Board ofCommissioners Meeting 12-17-24 

Mr. Clint Sterling, Director of Recreation and Parks/Tourism has advised that the Tourism Commission, consisting of seven (7) members who serve three (3) year terms have three members whose terms have expired; Mr. Tom Schisler, representing the City of Crisfield, Ms. Sandie Marriner, representing District 3 and Ms. Donzella Johnson representing District 1. Mr. Taylor also advised that there are currently three vacancies on this commission for District 2, 4 and the Town of Princess Anne which need to be filled. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to appoint Mr. Tom Schisler, representing the City of Crisfield, Ms. Sandie Marriner, representing District 3 and Ms. Donzella Johnson representing District 1 to serve on the Tourism Commission for a three (3) year term. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore, by show of hands the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr. Taylor presented the request for reappointments to the Recreation and Parks Commission. The following representatives have agreed to continue serving a three (3) year term on this nine (9) member board: 

- Mr. Robey French representing the At Large appointment 

- - Mr. Dale Turpin representing District 4 - Mr. Marshall Beacoat representing District 2 - Mr. James Webster representing the Board of Education - Mr. Charles Fisher representing the At Large appointment - Mr. Charles Laird representing the Commissioner’s Office 

Currently, this board has vacancies for District 1, 3 and 5. 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to reappoint Messers. French, Turpin, Beacoat, Webster, Fisher and Laird as members of the Recreation and Parks Commission as presented for a 3-year term. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore, by show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

A letter was received from Comcast advising about an internal company consolidation to streamline their cable corporate organization. Comcast of Delmarva, LLC is being transferred to Comcast Cable Communication Management, LLC. The letter advises that this change will have no impact to the county, as franchisor or to its customers. 

Letters sent to Nicole Carlozo, Maryland Department of Natural Resources and Bohler Engineering VA, LLC were reviewed. 

Next, regarding discussion items, Mr. Taylor presented the recommended fee increases, as summarized in the handout provided in the agenda package. It was noted that increases have not
