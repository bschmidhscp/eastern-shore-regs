# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY JULY 22, 2025** 

At 2:30 pm, President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in Special Session on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Craig Mathies were absent for today’s meeting. 

Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez was also in attendance. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for July 22, 2025</u>** 

On July 22, 2025, from 2:30 p.m. until 3:02 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(7) to consult with counsel for legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Darryl Webster. Commissioner Craig Mathies and Commissioner Eldon Willing were not present for today’s meeting. County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Legal, Action Taken: Proceed with proposal and notify public; (2) Topic: Mosquito Control, Action Taken: Emergency Contractual Hire. 

The Board was advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:30 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(7) to consult with counsel for legal advice.
