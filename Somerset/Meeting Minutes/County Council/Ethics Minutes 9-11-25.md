# SOMERSET COUNTY ETHICS COMMISSION 

11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111 

Princess Anne, MD 21853 

Minutes of the September 11, 2025 Meeting of 

Somerset County Ethics Commission 

10:00 a.m. 

Members Present: 

R. Dean Goldsborough 

Ernest Satchell William McInturff Elmer Barkley 

Staff Present: 

Kirk Simpkins, Attorney 

Lory E. Ebron, Executive Aide 

Rita Jones 

## Members Absent: None 

A meeting of<sup>theSomersetCountyEthics Commissionwas convened ontoday’s dateat10:00a.m.in Room</sup> 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. The annual 2025 Ethics Commission meeting was called to order by Chairman Goldsborough. The following business was addressed: 

Attorney Simpkins advised that the first order of business was to approve the minutes from the previous meeting held on September 26, 2024. The minutes from the September 26, 2024 open session meeting were reviewed by all members and presented for approval. Chairman Goldsborough called for a vote in favor. Upon a motion made by Mr. Satchell and seconded by Mr. MclInturff, the minutes were approved 5-0 by showofhands as presented. 

Mr. Simpkins next presented the 2025 Annual Certification Form (Form 26). He requested approval to submit Form 26 as presented certifying the stated certifications and providing the requested information. 

Ms. Jones made a motion to accept Form 26 as presented and file the form with the State Ethics Commission. Mr. Barkley seconded the motion. Those present voted aye by show of hands, therefore the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Goldsborough asked if anyone had any new business to present. No concerns were noted. 

With no further business, at 10:03 a.m., the meeting of the Ethics Commission adjourned upon a motion made by Mr. Satchell and seconded by Mr. MclInturff. The motion carried unanimously by those present.
