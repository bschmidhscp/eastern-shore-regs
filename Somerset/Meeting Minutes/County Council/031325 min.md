## Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of March 13, 2025 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, March 13, 2025 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Josh Taylor, Succeeding General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, Jason Loar, P.E., Davis Bowen & Friedel, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. The Crisfield (Peripheral Area) Board position is currently vacant. Robin Street was the only member of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Business — Information 

Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, was recognized for 20 years of service with the Sanitary District. Paige Coulbourne, Accounts Clerk II, was also recognized for 10 years of service with SCSD. The Commission presented both ladies with a plaque thanking them for their hard work and dedication through the years. 

Mr. Stockus was presented with a plaque by the Board recognizing his retirement from the Sanitary District after 10 years as the General Manager and previously as a SCSD Commission Member. Josh Taylor, Professional Engineer with Davis, Bowen & Friedel (DBF) for over 25 years, will be succeeding Mr. Stockus as General Manager. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Jason Loar, DBF, was present to update the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project stating after many months of review and comments the Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) has approved funding this project through BRNR. The next step moving forward with this project is to acquire engineering services. Mr. Loar, on behalf of DBF, has drafted a Proposal for Professional Engineering Services to be voted on during the action portion of this meeting. 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project stating the District's next step will be issuing a Task Order Request (TOR) for professional engineering services. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project by stating the old lab is permanently closed and the lab equipment has been moved to the new lab. The District is currently outsourcing lab services until the new lab is online. Mr. Padgette stated he has been unsuccessful

## Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular and executive session minutes dated February 43% 2025 were previously distributed for review. On a motion by Ms. Somers and seconded by Mr. Williams, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular and executive session minutes dated February 13" 2025. 

## Reappointment of Authorized Signers 

As stated in the past, the Sanitary District's insurance provider requires two signatures for all bank transactions. Currently Mr. Williams and Mr. Stockus are authorized signers, however with Mr. Stockus retiring, Mr. Taylor will need to be appointed as a signer. The District should also have an additional backup signer in the event Mr. Taylor or Mr. Williams are unavailable. Rev. Bagley was nominated and accepted being a signer for the District. Mr. Williams made a motion to appoint Rev. Charles Bagley, Josh Taylor, and Dennis Williams as authorized signers of the Sanitary District. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion was unanimously approved. 

### Princess Anne — Action 

## Emergency Repair 

Mr. Padgette informed the Commission, the sludge pump at the PA WWTP is inoperable. This pump moves the sludge to the belt press for dewatering and removing solids to keep the plant in compliance with the National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System Permit. The quote from Hill’s Industrial for the repair is in the amount of $7,373.48. Ms. Landon made a motion to retroactively approve the invoice from Hill’s Industrial as presented for the sludge pump repair. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

## Proposal for Engineering Services 

MDE recently approved the Princess Anne WWTP ENR upgrade preliminary engineering report (PER). This PER was completed by DBF on behalf of the Sanitary District to review options to upgrade the existing WWTP to ENR standards. The PER recommended

4 

upgrading the WWTP by inclusion of a Membrane Bio-Reactor (MBR) with other needed improvements in order to meet ENR discharge limits per the Districts’ discharge permit and associated consent order. A description of DBF’s proposed scope of services and associated fees totaling $677,665.00 were present with the proposal. MDE has determined they will cover 60% of construction costs through Enhanced Nutrient Removal (ENR) grant and the remaining 40% will be paid by local share. This preliminary eligibility determination is subject to change based on design review and will be adjusted based on actual bid prices. This proposal shall only be valid upon approval by both the MDE and the Board of Public Works (BPW) of whose intent is to provide grant funds for these phases of the project. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve DBF’s proposal for professional engineering services for the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR upgrade contingent upon grant funding from MDE and/or BPW. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion unanimously carried to approval. 

### Smith Island - Action 

# SCADA System Upgrade 

Mr. Padgette informed the Commission the new WWTP on the Island needs to be added to the District's alarm system. The initial quote from Shorite Controls, LLC is for $13,640.00 but the cost may be less depending on time and material used. MDE requires that alarms are present and functioning. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the quote from Shorite Controls, LLC in the amount of $13,640.00 for the SICWP. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

## Sewer Rate Reduction Request 

Brent Bradshaw, Resident of Rhodes Point, recently contacted our Office regarding his sewer bill he receives for 3231 Marsh Road. Mr. Bradshaw explained to the Utility Billing Specialist there hasn’t been a structure on this lot for years and he didn’t realize he was eligible for a rate reduction. Mr. Bradshaw has since had the sewer capped off in order to avoid receiving future sewer bills from the District. Management is recommending revising his last year of sewer bills to the sewer reduction rate since it's been verified to have no structure. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve revising the last year of sewer bills for 3231 Marsh Road at the sewer reduction rate. Mr. Williams seconded the motion which carried to approval unanimously. 

#### Crisfield — Action
