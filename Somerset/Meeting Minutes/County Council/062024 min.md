### Somerset County Sanitary Commission 

Minutes of June 20, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, June 20, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Richard Padgette, Succeeding Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, Josh Taylor, P.E., Davis Bowen & Friedel, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. The Chairman position is currently vacant. Maxine Landon, Member, was absent. There were no Public Attendees. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

##### General Information 

Mr. Stockus began the meeting by recognizing Starr Mister for her 25 years of service with the District and presenting her with a plaque. Mr. Stockus thanked Ms. Mister for her dedication and leadership through the years. 

## Princess Anne- Information 

Mr. Stockus informed the Board there has been no movement since April’s SCSD Commission meeting regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade. 

Mr. Stockus stated the District is still anticipating the release of the Intended Use Plan for the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund from the Maryland Department of the Environment to see if the District qualified for funding this year for the Princess Anne Color Removal Project. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating the drywall is going up now and final inspections are taking place. The Lab is expected to be online by August 2024. 

Mr. Stockus informed the Board, on June 1, 2024 the Princess Anne Police Department received a call stating a person at 29996 Deanwood Road was filling his swimming pool using a fire hydrant. Officer Strong of the Princess Anne Police Department was dispatched to the scene. He spoke with the owner, Steve Mason, and informed him he needed to contact SCSD when filling his pool. Our on-call Field Technician was also dispatched to the scene and informed Mr. Mason he was not authorized to use the hydrant. Mr. Stockus stated he is planning to file a formal complaint against Mr. Mason with the Princess Anne Police Department.

# Amendment to Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy 

Due to increasing inflation impacts, Management has recommended increasing the employee meal reimbursement amounts for breakfast from $8.00 to $15.00, lunch from $12.00 to $18.00, and dinner from $20.00 to $30.00. This policy was last amended on 2/13/2020. Ms. Somers made a motion to amend the Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy and Resolution as presented. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

#### Princess Anne — Action 

### Reimbursement of paid electric bills 

It was recently brought to the District's attention, the property on Clipper Circle where the District's lift station sits was transferred from Chase House Estates (previous owner) to the Somerset County Commissioners on 9/02/21. Prior to the transfer the utility bills were the owner's responsibility, Chase House Estates and Tawes & Associates split paying the electric bill for the lift station. Since transferred, Tawes & Associates have continued paying the bill. Management reviewed the Delmarva Power electric bills provided by Tawes & Associates and recommend reimbursing them from the time of transfer to present. Ms. Somers made a motion to reimburse Tawes & Associates from 9/2/21 to present for the electric bills they have paid to Delmarva Power for the Lift Station. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

##### Fairmount — Action 

### Request to use property for hunting 

Mr. Stockus received a request from, Robert Strausser, asking permission to hunt on 26760 Rumbley Road which abuts his property. This location, owned by SCSD, is where the Rumbley well house sits. Management stated their concerns with liability/insurance and setting a precedence. Mr. Bagley suggested the District confirm with their insurance provider whether there would be any insurance limitations that would prohibit hunting on this property. The Commission tabled Mr. Strausser’s request for further investigation.
