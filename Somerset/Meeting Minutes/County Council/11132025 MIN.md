## Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of November 13, 2025 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, November 13, 2025 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Chris Derbyshire, P.E., George, Miles & Buhr, LLC. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Information 

The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Board for review. The first quarter (FY26) revenues exceeded expenses by $138,078.91. The Commission had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

Mr. Taylor presented a draft of the 2026 Debt Service Budgets. The Sanitary District Management is proposing no change in the sewer rate of $30.19 per quarter. The District is proposing the water rate of $21.51 per in Princess Anne, a decrease of $0.14 in the Water Mains Developer Surcharge per EDU (Equivalent Dwelling Unit). The decrease in the water rate was possible due to the increase in customers in the Princess Anne District. The consensus of the Board was to move forward with advertising the following proposed rates for 2026 as presented below.

### Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating that the District is continuing to work on the design with Davis, Bowen, & Friedel, Inc. (DBF) and the treatment

<!-- page 3 -->
equipment manufacturers. Meetings are being held monthly with DBF for this project and the Commission will continue to be updated. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating the District is still working with Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) and DBF to finalize design. Mr. Taylor is hopeful to conclude the design of this project by the beginning of 2026. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Lead Service Line Project by stating the District was notified by MDE that there are additional annual notifications that need to be mailed out to District customers. The District staff and DBF are currently working together to have those letters completed and sent out soon. Mr. Taylor stated the District plans to apply for additional funding from MDE in January to help fund the cost of this project. 

Smith Island Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) is currently online and running but there are some start up and operational issues glitches being worked out. The WWTP Upgrade Project completion is anticipated for 12/22/25. 

## Eairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission in regards to the Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension Request/Petition by stating the Sanitary District has made a request to be on the Somerset County Commission agenda to have a discussion on if the County is able to fund the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) and Environmental Report (ER) for this project. Mr. Taylor also stated that he is preparing a request for quotation (RFQ) to send to the Districts on-call engineers, GMB & DBF, for preparation of the PER and ER. 

### Public Input 

No public input was received by the District.

## Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular session minutes dated October 9" 2025 were previously distributed. On a motion by Ms. Somers and seconded by Ms. Elliott, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular session minutes dated October 9'" 2025. 

### Vehicle Financing Resolution 

Hebron Savings Bank has requested for the Sanitary Commission to provide a resolution authorizing the submittal of an application and execution of a vehicle loan agreement. The purchase of two work vehicles was budgeted for FY26 and previously approved by the Sanitary Commission. Ms. Somers made a motion to allow the General Manager to sign the resolution once received by Cockey, Brennan & Maloney, the District's lawyer; Ms. Landon seconded the motion which carried unanimously to approval. 

Princess Anne Subdistrict — Action 

## Site Plan — Goose Creek (Popeye’s) Redevelopment 

Parker and Associates have presented the District with a request for approval of site plans for a standalone Popeyes to be built on Mt. Vernon Rd & Edgehill Terrace. Mr. Taylor informed the Commission that the redeveloped Popeyes should be in the final stages of plan review. Mr. Taylor has requested some items be revised prior to approval, he is hopeful all revisions will be done before the next meeting. Ms. Somers made a motion to allow the General Manager to sign the plans contingent upon revisions being completed. Ms. Elliott seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

John Rosebush, CMH Homes, Inc., has submitted a request for allocation on behalf of Somerset Grove subdivision for 79 lots on Old Princess Anne Rd & S. School Rd. Request for 0% Interest Payment Plan — 29993 Donnybrook Ln 

Jennifer Miller, owner of 29993 Donnybrook Ln, has requested an interest free 2-year payment plan to help her afford her water and sewer bill. Ms. Miller had a leak at her property during the September 2025 quarter which generated her bill to be $2,258.50. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve an interest free 2-year payment plan for 29993 Donnybrook Ln. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.
