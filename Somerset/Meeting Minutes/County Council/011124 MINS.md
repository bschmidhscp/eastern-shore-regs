Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of January 11, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, January 11, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Chairman, Adam Riggin, Vice-Chairman, Charles Bagley, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, and Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide. Bret Davis, Kirk Davis and Eric Davis were present for their appointments. There were no other Public Attendees. 

Mr. Riggin called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project by stating the Pilot Testing Agreement/Proposal provided by Fluence (water treatment Solution Corporation) has been reviewed and commented by SCSD’s Attorney and returned to Fluence for their comment/approval. Mr. Ludy is hopeful the pilot study will begin by mid-February 2024. 

Mr. Stockus stated there has been no movement regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project since the December 2023 SCSD Commission meeting. Mr. Stockus reiterated to the Board, the District is hopeful the recent concerns and support from local government Officials will help score higher this year when applying for funding through the MDE- State Revolving Fund. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating construction of the Lab building has stalled due to personnel issues with Pole Building LLC, the awarded contractor for the Lab building. The company is in the process of assigning a new Project Manager and hope to complete the floor next. Project completion is projected for May 2024. 

##### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP), stating Crofton Construction Services, Construction Contractorfor the Subaqueous Force Main, has begun horizontal directional drilling off the Island. Due to the weather conditions, barging the equipment was delayed for this project, therefore, Crofton will be asking for an extension in time to the project in the near future. The Force Main is projected to be complete by mid-April with entire upgrade completion anticipated for August 2024.

Princess Anne — Action 

### Request for sewer credit 

Scott Wade, owner of Process Integration, 11500 Progress Lane, requested the Board consider granting him a credit for some of the sewer usage charged to his water and sewer bill during the quarters of 9/30/23 and 12/30/23. Mr. Wade previously provided the Office Manager with documentation of whenhe irrigated his property during the two quarters which coincided with the audit performed on his water meter. Management's recommendation was to credit the 9/30/23 and 12/30/23 quarters sewer based on his average gallons (10,220) after irrigation gallons (7,462) have been deducted. Mr. Wade has paid to have an irrigation system installed to avoid this charge moving forward. Ms. Landon made a motion based on Management’s recommendation to credit the sewer portion as stated. Mr. Bagley seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.

During the 10/12/23 commission meeting Bret and Kirk Davis were present to request the Board consider allowing their company, Simple Fiber, to negotiate a deal to use SCSD Well Station property, in an effort to provide high speed fiber internet to the area, with the understanding that all expenses associated would be at Simple Fiber’s expense.

#### 2:00 p.m. 

During the 12/14/23 commission meeting, Eric Davis was present to discuss his increase in Equivalent Dwelling Units (EDUs) for 30511 Hickory Road. The District recently evaluated water usage on all commercial water and/or sewer accounts was based on average quarterly usage from December 2021 — September 2023. Mr. Davis was notified his EDUs were increasing by double and requested the Board reevaluate his usage starting from December 2023 quarter. Mr. Davis explained during the timeframe evaluated he had a contract with a company to house 70 of its employees, however, it ended up housing 120 people. The overcapacity caused all the utilities to triple. After failing to comply with the occupancy agreement, all the tenants were moved from the property by June 2023. Mr. Davis stated during renovation of the apartments, they discovered and repaired 4 toilet leaks. New tenants started moving in September 2023. The Commission understands Mr. Davis’ request, however they are inclined to follow the policy. Mr. Stockus suggested tabling Mr. Davis’ request for a reevaluation to get more information from him and possibly consult with Counsel regarding the policy. 

Ms. Mister explained to Eric Davis, she reviewed his account’s usage. She found that the District has been under billing the number of EDUs for Debt Service for this property up until this point. This was primarily due to the timing of the previous EDU study as his apartments were vacant during that time. Ms. Mister stated after subtracting the March 2023 quarter (when leaks occurred) his EDUs are calculated as 22, a reduction from 24 as performed in the 2024 commercial account evaluation. Mr. Davis understood and was satisfied with Ms. Mister’s evaluation. Ms. Landon made a motion to reduce the number of EDUs assessed from 24 to 22 for 30511 Hickory Road. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

There was no public input.
