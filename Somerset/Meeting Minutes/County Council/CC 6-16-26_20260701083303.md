Benefits 4.2 of the volunteer award program plan document, and complies under the requirements of the Intemal Revenue Code and the Maryland Tax General Annotated Code regarding length ofservice reward plans. There are no other changes to the VSAP Plan. The notice of amendment to the plan was advertised to the public on June 10, 2026. Ms. Muir stated that the Board recognizes the

Next, Ms. Muir presented Resolution No. 1229 for the Boards consideration regarding a Volunteer Personnel Workers’ Compensation Policy to be effective July 1, 2026. She explained that the policy will provide workers compensation insurance benefits to active volunteer firefighters and volunteer ambulance rescue an unpaid person authorized to perform volunteer firefighter or volunteer ambulance, rescue or advanced life support who are considered covered employees under Maryland Annotated Code Title 9-234. The adopted policy will deem that an unpaid person performing volunteer service to Somerset County shall be considered an employee of Somerset County for purposes of Workers’ Compensation Insurance benefits as provided for by law for injury or illness sustained by them while engaged in the performance of services for Somerset County. A public hearing was held on May 26, 2026. One proponent spoke in favor, and there were no opponents at the public hearing and no written or oral comments were received during the 10-day comment period. Ms. Muir advised she is before the board today requesting approval of Resolution No. 1229 to allow Somerset County to provide a volunteer Workers Compensation Insurance Policy as authorized by law. 

Commissioner Darry! Webster made a motion to approve Resolution No. 1229/Volunteer Personnel Workers Compensation Policy. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. Vice President Randy Laird abstained, as he is a Firefighter. The motion to approve carried 3-0-1.
