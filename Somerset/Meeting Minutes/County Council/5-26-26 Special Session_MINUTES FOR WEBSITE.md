## BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY MAY 26, 2026** 

At 2:00 pm, President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners to open in a Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for May 26, 2026</u>** 

On May 26, 2026, from 2:20 p.m. until 3:10 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Warden Keith Muir, HR Director Jamie Manaia, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: None; (2) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Standard Salary Increase: (3) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Approval of Job Description; (4) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: Approve Constituent Request; (5) Topic: Personnel; Action Taken: None. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in devotion and the pledge of allegiance. 

At this time, President Charles Laird declared the Public Hearing for the Volunteer Personnel Workers’ Compensation Policy open to receive public comment and invited Internal Auditor Sharon Muir to come forward to present. Mr. Leatherbury read the following notice into record: 

# **PUBLIC HEARING** 

# **Volunteer Personnel Workers’ Compensation Policy** 

Notice is hereby given that the Board of County Commissioners will hold a Public Hearing on: 

# **Tuesday, May 26, 2026** 

at **2:00 P.M.** In the County Commissioners Meeting Room Somerset County Office Complex, Room 111 11916 Somerset Avenue Princess Anne, Maryland 21853 

# <u>Fair Summary</u> 

The purpose of the public hearing is to consider providing Workers’ Compensation Insurance benefits for persons (volunteer firefighter or volunteer ambulance, rescue, or advanced life support worker) authorized by the Maryland Annotated Code Labor and Employment Title 9 – Workers Compensation Subtitle 2- Covered Employees and Employers, Section 9-234- Volunteer Fire or Rescue Company. 

The meeting will be held in-person to receive public comment. Individuals wishing to make oral or written comments are encouraged to attend the hearing. Anyone wishing to testify will be allowed to speak in person. The proposed Resolution will be made available at the public hearing. The document can also be viewed following the public hearing, during regular office hours, weekdays 8:30 a.m. – 4:30 p.m., at the Office of the Somerset

County Commissioners, Room 111, Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland.

He advised that this notice was advertised on May 6, 2026 and May 13, 2026. 

Ms. Muir then provided a summary of the Volunteer Personnel Workers’ Compensation Policy, advising that the County is considering offering workers’ compensation to the following eight volunteer fire companies: Princess Anne Volunteer Fire Company, Crisfield Volunteer Fire Company, Marion Volunteer Fire Company, Deal Island-Chance Volunteer Fire Company, Mt. Vernon Volunteer Fire Company, Fairmount Volunteer Fire Company, Ewell Volunteer Fire Company, and Tylerton Fire and Rescue (with a total of 239 active volunteer firefighters across the companies). Ms. Muir continued by advising that the county is in the process of gathering a quote through Chesapeake Employers Insurance for a separate policy specifically for volunteer personnel (to maintain separation from county personnel and volunteer personnel) and that it will, hopefully, be less costly in total than the individual policies the companies are now carrying themselves. 

Following this, President Charles Laird invited all proponents of this policy to come forward for public comment. 

Mr. John Barnette, Chief of Mt. Vernon VFD and President of the Somerset County Fire Chiefs Association, approached the podium and addressed the board, thanking them for this proposed policy that will financially aid the departments and alleviate the burden of the costly individual plans. He also expressed his support for the separate plan as to not affect the county employee plan. Mr. Barnette concluded his comment with a request for the Fire Chiefs to meet with Ms. Muir to discuss the quote and plan once it’s available. 

President Charles Laird then invited any opponents of this policy to come forward for public comment. There were none. 

Mr. Leatherbury then advised that written comments will be taken until Monday June 5, 2026 at 4:30 p.m. 

With that, President Charles Laird declared the public hearing closed at 2:07 p.m. 

Ms. Muir returned to the audience as Mr. Leatherbury invited Coach Joe Hubbs and the members of the Holly Grove Girls Basketball team to come forward to accept a proclamation from the Commissioners in honor of them becoming 2026 DVAC Women’s Conference Champions. 

Following this, Mr. Leatherbury invited Coach Lee Ramsey and the members of the Holly Grove Boys Basketball team to come forward to accept a proclamation from the Commissioners in honor of them becoming 2026 DVAC South Division Champions. 

Ms. Sherree Marshall, Director of Commission on Aging, came forward to request the Board’s approval to apply for the Senior Citizen Activity Grant in the amount of $37,920 (with a $7900 deduction). 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to authorize Ms. Marshall to apply for the Senior Citizen Activity Grant in the amount of $37,920 (with a $7900 deduction) as requested. By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the Board, the motion carried. 

Ms. Marshall then requested to apply for the Senior Care Program Grant (once available from the State) in the amount of $160,000.

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to authorize Ms. Marshall to apply for the Senior Care Program Grant (once available from the State) in the amount of $160,000. By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the Board, the motion carried. 

Ms. Marshall left the meeting at this time, and EMS Deputy Chief Cory Polidore came forward. 

Deputy Chief Polidore advised he was before the Board to request permission to accept the Whole Blood Grant award from the State of Maryland/MEIMMS in the amount of $58,338 (year one of a five year grant) to help implement the Whole Blood Program in Somerset County. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies to approve the acceptance of the Whole Blood Grant award as presented. By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the Board, the motion carried. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:20 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Craig Mathies, and voted in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
