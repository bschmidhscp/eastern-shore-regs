# 3-18-25 Special Session Minutes

<!-- page 1 -->
# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY MARCH 18, 2025** 

At 2:34 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session on a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and carried on a vote in favor by a show of hands of all present members of the Board. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was not present for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for March 18, 2025</u>** 

On March 18, 2025, from 2:34 p.m. until 2:48 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by a show of hands by all present members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305(b)(7) consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was not present for today’s meeting. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (7) Legal; Actions Taken: Confidential. 

The Board was advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:34 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Eldon Willing, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b)(7) consult with counsel to obtain legal advice.
