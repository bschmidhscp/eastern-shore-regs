# **SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY JUNE 11, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was absent for today’s meeting. 

Also present for today’s meeting were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Roads Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the May 28, 2024 meeting were presented for approval; however, the Board requested more time to review. 

# **PUBLIC HEARINGS** 

President Charles Laird opened the public hearing for the proposed road closure of Hall 

Creek Road at 2:00 p.m. Mr. Barnes read the following notice into record: 

# NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD PROPOSED ROAD CLOSURE Halls Creek Road 

Annotated Code of Maryland §12-512. Opening, altering, or closing roads by petition. (d) Independent authority, that the County Commissioners of Somerset County will hold a public hearing at 2:00 P.M. on Tuesday June 11, 2024 at the Somerset County Commissioners Office, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland 21853, to close a portion of a county road in Election District No. 06-001. 

The road closure under discussion is a .275 mile (1452’) unpaved portion of County Road #109 known as Halls Creek Road which begins approximately .715 (3775’) miles from intersection of MD 361 Fairmount Road and proceeds southeast to its terminus. 

The Public is invited to comment on the proposal and to offer objections or counter proposals. For information, contact the Somerset County Roads Department at 410-651-1930. 

# Somerset County Roads Department George W. Barnes Director 

Mr. Barnes provided a brief summary of the proposed closure before President Charles 

Laird opened the hearing up for public comment. 

Mr. Raymond Larrimore, P.O. Box 153, Upper Fairmount, Maryland addressed the Board 

in opposition of the proposed closure. Mr. Larrimore expressed his displeasure with the proposed closure of Halls Creek Road, stating how this would affect people from being able to use the dock at the landing. Mr. Larrimore concluded his public comment by sharing many fond memories from years past.

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

A discussion was held the FY25 Road Resurfacing Contracts. Mr. Barnes advised that he has the ability to extend the current contracts with their current pricing and requested the Board’s permission to do so. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to extend Contract # 2019-1 Stone Supply with Vulcan Materials for FY25. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies and Commissioner Eldon Willing, the motion carried. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Eldon Willing and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to extend Contract # 2020-2 Asphalt Emulsion with Slurry Pavers for FY25. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies and Commissioner Eldon Willing, the motion carried.

# **WATERWAYS** 

Mr. Barnes advised that the Waterway Improvement Fund will no longer be accepting applications for portable toilet grants at public boating facilities. Moving forward, the cost for the portable toilets will be the County responsibility; however, the year will be finished out with the current Atlantic Pumping contract). 

Mr. Barnes advised that the Dames Quarter Bulk-Heading project is now complete and shared photos of the completed project with the Board.

Commissioner Eldon Willing inquired if the SHA Waterjet was down, to which Mr. Barnes advised he was not aware but would find out.
