# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **WORK SESSION TUESDAY OCTOBER 15, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird call the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in a Work Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

No minutes were presented today. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Services, and Mr. Mark Konapelsky were now before the Board for a discussion on HB 0002 that would allow counties to “set a special rate for vacant lot or improved property cited as vacant and unfit for habitation or other authorized us on a housing or building violation notice.” Mr. Drewer further advised that this special rate “can be subject to up to 2.5 times the rate for real property taxes” and that, in order to be subjected to the special rate, the property would need to be cited by a building inspector. Mr. Drewer stated that while this is a great opportunity to improve vacant properties in the county, there are challenges that come with determining if a property is vacant or not as well as establishing said special tax rate and accounting for additional funds that would need to be considered. Mr. Drewer recommended that Somerset County not move forward at this time and see how other surrounding counties manage (should they decide to adopt HB0002). Mr. Drewer advised that HB0002 has been reviewed by County Attorney Kirk Simpkins as well, and Mr. Simpkins concurs with Mr. Drewer’s opinion. A discussion was held with Mr. Konapelsky adding that this bill was initially created for city settings but has made its way to rural setting where it unfortunately does not work; stating that this is why we have a Nuisance Coordinator. The Board thanked Mr. Drewer and Mr. Konapelsky for the information shared. 

Moving on, President Charles Laird initiated a discussion regarding campers and how, although the County has an ordinance pertaining to limitations with campers, he is receiving complaints that this ordinance is constantly being violated. President Charles Laird stated he

Mr. Drewer advised that his office will look into the complaints presented by President Charles Laird and also advised that his office is working through a transition period with employees; stating that the new Code Enforcement Officer would be starting this week. The discussion continued and concluded with President Charles Laird requesting a monthly report from Mr. Drewer’s office on code/nuisance violations for each Commissioner’s district. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing then advised that he was approached by the gentlemen who owns Frank’s Island and requested the County’s assistance in applying for a Shoreline Restoration Grant. A discussion was held with Mr. Drewer advising he was aware of this request; stating the DNR will only allow counties to apply for the grant (not privately owned or non-profit organizations). Mr. Drewer advised that he has a meeting planned with DNR following the Work Session meeting and advised he could gather more information regarding this topic and bring it back to the Board at a future meeting. 

Mr. Drewer and Mr. Konapelsky left the meeting. 

Mr. Taylor advised that the Secretary of Agriculture will be visiting the County on Monday. 

Vice President Randy Laird inquired on the status of the relocation of the Election Board. Mr. Taylor advised there are no further updates since the last meeting where the Board was advised that the report from the State was provided to the architect. A brief discussion was held with several Board members advising they have been approached by individuals stating that the Election Board is displeased with this action (of relocation) and safety concerns with the location of the proposed new office (Main Street Princess Anne). 

With no further business to discuss, a motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to adjourn the meeting. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster the motion carried and the meeting was adjourned at 2:25 p.m.
