SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY December 19, 2023 

At 1:45 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in Special Session with Commissioner President Charles Laird, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., Commissioner Darryl Webster and Commissioner Eldon Willing present. Vice President Randy Laird and County AdministratorClerk Ralph D. Taylor were absent from today’s meeting, 

Also, in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernest J, Leatherbury Jr., Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron and Roads Director, Woody Barnes, 

## Closed Session Summary Report 

On Tuesday, December 19, 2023 from 1:45 p.m. until 1:56 p.m., President Charles Laird, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, opened in Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code ofMaryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., was also present, and voted in favor; therefore, the motion carried 4-0. Vice President Randy Laird and County Administrator-Clerk Ralph D. Taylor were absent from today’s meeting. 

Deputy County Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr., Roads Director Woody Barnes, HR Supervisor Kim Widdowson and Ms. Lory Ebron, Executive Aide were also present. Topics discussed: (1) Probationary Status and Promotion (2) Hiring of Motor Equipment Operator I (MEO 1). Actions taken during closed session: (1) Approval to move probationary employee (Highway Worker I) to permanent employee status and promote to MEO | (2) Approval to hire MEOSe1. 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Willing to lead those present in Devotion and 

the Pledge of Allegiance. 

### Roads Division Discussion Items 

First, Mr. Barnes advised the Board that he has received two estimates regarding much needed repairs to White Haven Ferry Road. He explained the work to be performed to raise the toad surface to reduce water laying in the road, and reduce the abrupt approach onto and off the

<!-- page 3 -->
bridge. Bozman Paving and Grading LLC submitted a proposal for 2 options, with the lower being $45,000. Pruitt’s Resurfacing Solutions submitted a proposal of $23,600. Because the proposals are above his threshold authority, Board approval is required. Mr. Barnes recommended awarding the proposal to the low bidder Pruitt’s Resurfacing Solutions for $23,600. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to award the proposal for road repairs on White Haven Ferry Road to Pruitt’s Resurfacing Solutions for $23,600. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of hands, President Charles Laird and Commissioner Willing voted in favor; therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

#### Waterways Division Discussion Items 

Mr. Barnes explained that during his trip taken to Smith Island on December 6, 2023 with Vice President Randy Laird, Deputy County Administrator Emest Leatherbury Jr., hosted by Maryland Department of Natural Resources (DNR), the rehabilitation of the Ewell Basin, with the most vulnerable areas observed. During the visit, DNR advised of a FY23 grant opportunity available through the FT23 Resiliency and Restoration Fund; however, there was a December 14, 2023 deadline date for submission. Mr. Barnes completed the application, obtained letters of support and submitted the paperwork to apply for this $2.5 million grant, which is 100% funded (no match to the county) before the deadline date. Mr. Barnes requested formal approval to apply for the grant to be noted in Board records. 

Commissioner Willing made a motion to approve the submission of the FY23 Resiliency and Restoration Fund grant for the Ewell Basin as presented. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show ofhands, the motion carried 4-0. 

#### Correspondence 

Mr. Barnes shared an invite received from Delmarva Water Transport Committee, Inc., regarding their 50 Anniversary Luncheon being held on February 2, 2024 in Salisbury. Mr. Bames advised he will be attending, and invited any of the Commissioners to attend if they'd like, noting the RSVP is by January 19, 2024. He explained the committee is instrumental with dredging projects, include county representatives from Somerset, Wicomico, Worcester and Dorchester as well as those in private industry, noting they meet quarterly. 

#### Public Comment 

Mr. Robert J. McIntyre, 28101 Mt. Vernon Road requested to address the Board. He advised he goes across the White Ferry Haven Road constantly, noting the black top being placed keeps sinking down especially on one side, asking if something else can be used, adding black top has no structural integrity.
