# **SOMERSET COUNTY ROADS BOARD TUESDAY FEBRUARY 17, 2026** 

At 2:00 p.m., the Somerset County Roads Board met in regular session with President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, and Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also present for today’s meeting were Interim County Administrator Ernest Leatherbury, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, County Roads Superintendent Mike Marvel, County Roads Assistant Superintendent Gary Beauchamp, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Craig Mathies to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the January 20, 2026 meeting were presented for approval; however, the board requested more time for review. 

# **ROADS DIVISION** 

Mr. Marvel presented the cooperative agreement between the Maryland Department of Agriculture and Somerset County for the control and eradication of noxious weeds. The board was advised that this is simply a renewal of the agreement the county entered into previously, and Mr. Marvel recommended to continue with this provided service. 

A motion was made Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve this request and authorize President Charles Laird’s signature on the agreement. By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the board, the motion carried. 

An update was provided on the State Aid Funds with Mr. Marvel reporting an unobligated balance of $537,165.55. Mr. Marvel advised the plan is to allow this balance to grow in order to assist with bridge maintenance. 

Next, Mr. Marvel requested the approval of a sign placement for a received sign from the William C. Pomeroy Foundation for the White Haven Ferry. The board was provided with a sample of what the sign will look like (based on the sign placed on Smith Island to commemorate the Smith Island Cake) and proposed placement of said sign. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the sign and proposed placement. By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the board, the motion carried.

In regards to the BUILD Grant discussed in the previous Roads Board meeting, Mr. Marvel advised that the City of Crisfield is no longer pursuing this grant. 

A discussion was then held regarding Smith Island Bridges #21 and #22. Mr. Marvel presented the board with drafted letters of support addressed to Congressman Andy Harris and Senator Chris Van Hollen to urge their support for the rehabilitation of these bridges within the upcoming round of Congressionally Directed Spending Funds. Acquiring grant funding for the rehabilitation of these bridges has been unsuccessful in the past, which is the reason Mr. Marvel is now reaching out to Congressman Andy Harris and Senator Chris Van Hollen for their favorable recommendation to support our efforts in securing funding opportunities to complete this important rehabilitation project. A discussion was held regarding these bridges and prior funding attempts. The board was also advised that these letters of support are time sensitive, and Mr. Marvel requested their approval of the letters so Smith Island United could include them in their grant packet. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the letters of support as presented (with an amendment of the date on the letters from 2025 to 2026). By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the board, the motion carried.

# **DISCUSSION** 

Vice President Randy Laird inquired about pine trees growing in a ditch and asked Mr. Beauchamp if the Roads Department could be proactive with removing them. Mr. Beauchamp advised if they are within the county’s right of way they could. 

Commissioner Eldon Willing commended the Roads staff on their great work during the recent snow event. He then asked for Mr. Marvel to reach out to the State on smoothing the roads out over some culverts in Dames Quarter (Jerusalem Road). He also advised that a culvert on St. Stevens is backed up with rain water.

Commissioner Craig Mathies thanked Mr. Marvel for assisting a resident with his mailbox that was knocked over during snow removal by replacing it completely, as well as others on that street. 

President Charles Laird advised that he was approached by several homeowners in the Annamessick Ridge community praising the work of the Roads department during the recent snow event. **PUBLIC COMMENT** 

There were no public comments today. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the minutes as presented. By a show of hands in favor by all present members of the board, the motion carried. 

At the conclusion of the meeting, a motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird to adjourn Roads Board and enter in Roads Board Closed Session. Therefore, at 2:23 p.m., the motion carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the Board.
