# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY FEBRUARY 4, 2025** 

At 2:00 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was not present for today’s meeting. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for February 4, 2025</u>** 

On February 4, 2025, from 2:45 p.m. until 2:58 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and voted in favor by a show of hands by all members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305(b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.. 

Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was not present for today’s meeting. Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Personnel; Actions Taken: Hiring. 

Vice President Randy Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Minutes from the January 21, 2025 Special and Closed Session were presented for review and approval. A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve the minutes as presented. The motion carried by a vote in favor by all members of the Board. 

Ms. Dana Zucker, of Sugar Water Restaurant in Princess Anne, was now before the Board to provide some insight on her recent business ventures; from her bed and breakfast to the

Ms. Zucker advised of her vision of Agritourism (where visitors can visit the farm where bed & breakfast is located to learn the importance of plants and wetland restoration) and utilizing the building next to Sugar Water Restaurant (the Washington Inn) to house an accessible food store (stocked with all local produce, meats, fish, and poultry from local vendors and farms). Ms. Zucker left the meeting. 

Mr. Marty Koerner, Police Accountability Board, was before the Board to present the End of Year Report (December 2024) for the Somerset County Police Accountability Board. Mr. Koerner advised that the Board continued to meet quarterly in 2024 as mandated by the Maryland Police Accountability Act of 2021 and Somerset County Resolution No. 1,177, with the members of the Board consisting of: Dr. Kirkland Hall Sr., Bruce Parkinson, Julie Thomas, Ivan Lankford, and Chairman Martin Koerner. Additionally, the Somerset County Administrative Charging Committee (SCACC) members were selected as required: Reverend Charles Bagley, Pastor James Riley, Pastor Stephen Willing, Reverend Doctor Doretha Whittington, and Chairman Martin Koerner. Mr. Koerner advised that Reverend Doctor Doretha Whittington must complete the necessary Administrative Charging Committee training before participating as a voting member, with the earliest training opportunity available in Spring 2025. The Civilian Trial Board member is Cathy Landon. Mr. Koerner then advised of the Board’s key accomplishments in 2024, including: 

- The SCPAB met quarterly with law enforcement leadership, reviewed case outcomes, and discussed key law enforcement concerns within Somerset County. 

- The SCPAB responded to FOIA requests and media inquiries as needed. 

- Three SCACC members attended additional training hosted by the Maryland Police Training Commission to further develop their expertise. 

- The SCACC reviewed and issued decisions on all cases presented in 2024 in an efficient and timely manner. 

Mr. Koerner further advised that while the Maryland State Police and UMES Police 

Department report investigative findings to the State PAB rather than SCPAB, the Maryland State Police Princess Anne Barrack representative actively participates in SCPAB meetings,

Citizen engagement increased in 2024, with more residents using the complaint procedures. Only one minor case of officer misconduct was recorded, and a case review found no patterns or emerging accountability concerns. With no questions from the Board, Mr. Koerner left the meeting. 

Mr. Kirk Simpkins, County Attorney, and Ms. Flo Brotzman, SVN, were now before the Board to discuss the Westfield property. The Board was advised that two offers have been presented for this property; 1. Paul Thornton for $65,000 (cash) with no contingencies, a nonrefundable $15,000 deposit, and a $5,000 closing costs to new residents (for the homes he intends to build); and 2. Brett Davis for $100,000 with a 30-day study period (in which he could cancel the sale) and a $2,500 deposit. The Board entered into discussion regarding the property itself, mainly surrounding the estimated amount of lots/homes. At the conclusion of the discussion, the members of the Board opted to wait for all members of the Board to be present before accepting any offers. 

Mr. Simpkins and Ms. Brotzman left the meeting. 

Mr. Leatherbury advised that the Governor’s office has provided notice that members of the Liquor License Board (Mr. Bob Murphey, Mr. Frank Lusk, and Mr. Van Muir) are all up for renewal. With all three members advising they would like to continue serving, a motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to submit Mr. Bob Murphey, Mr. Frank Lusk, and Mr. Van Muir for a renewal of their terms with the Liquor License Board. The motion carried by a vote in favor by all members of the Board. 

There were no public comments for today’s meeting. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session.
