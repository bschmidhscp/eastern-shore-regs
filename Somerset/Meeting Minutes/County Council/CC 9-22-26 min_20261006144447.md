Board of Commissioners Meeting 9-22-26 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to approve the minutes as presented. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. Commissioner Eldon Willing abstained; therefor the motion carried 4-0-1. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer came forward at this time to present the bid award recommendation regarding comprehensive updates to the Critical Area Ordinance. He explained two bids were received at our meeting held on September 8, 2026. The county received a one time grant award in the amount of $25,000 to start the process. Following review of<sup>the twobids received bystaff,</sup> the recommendation was made to award the bid to Smith Planning & Design in the amount of $24,990 due to strict deadlines (June 30, 2027) and a prior history with the firm working with the County on other plans. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to award the bid to update the Critical Area Ordinance to Smith Planning & Design for $24,990. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of hands, therefore, the motion carried 5-0. 

Next, Mr, Drewer presented an ordinance to approve the Community Reinvestment and Repair Fund. One public comment was received, which was incorporated into the ordinance. Additionally, a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) requires approval, that establishes the Somerset County Health Department to administer the services. Mr. Leatherbury advised a motion is needed, noting the ordinance will be numbered No. 1235. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve Ordinance No. 1235 as presented. Vice President Randy Laird seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of hands. The motion carried 5-0. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve the MOU between Somerset County Health Department and Somerset County, Maryland as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of<sup>hands,the motioncarried5-0.</sup> 

Next, Mr. Clint Sterling, Director of Recreation and Parks/Tourism, came forward to present the FY 2027 Destination Marketing Organization Grant Agreement for approval. The grant, obtained every year, this year in the amount of $27,345 is used for tourism marketing efforts. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to authorize President Charles Laird to sign the FY2027 Destination Marketing Organization Grant Agreement. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye by show of hands, therefore, the motion carried 5-0.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 9-22-26 

President Charles Laird commended Mr. Sterling on the good job his department is doing with the Golf Course, and also complementing the upkeep of the Bike Trail. 

Mr. Woody Bames, Public Works Director came forward at this time. First, he presented a letter drafted to Ms. Mindy Lee, Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), which explained that the contract between Somerset County, the City of Crisfield and AECOM expired in June 2026. However, prior to the expiration, a scope of work to perform engineering and bid phase services for the rehabilitation of Runway 14/32 (Phase II) had been initiated, and not yet completed. Therefore, the letter requests to extend the contractual agreement with AECOM for one year, expiring in June 2027. A grant agreement has been approved, and budgeted. 

Vice President Randy Laird made a motion to proceed, and authorize President Charles Laird to sign the letter addressed to Ms. Lee to extend the AECOM contract for one year. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. By show of<sup>hands,the motioncarried5-0.</sup> 

Next, Mr, Barnes presented a proposal from EA Engineering for the Phase II new landfill expansion. He explained this phase is for the hydraulic study of the site. It would also revise the county’s 10-year Solid Waste Management Plan and allow an analysis of that report, and would include the permitting stage. The anticipated cost for this developmental phase is $753,500. He explained the purpose of<sup>the hydraulicstudy whichwillreviewsoil compactiondata to determine</sup> if the site is suitable for needed drainage. Mr. Barnes requested the Boards concurrence to move forward. 

Commissioner Craig Mathies made a motion to approve the proposal and agreement with EA Engineering regarding the Fairmount Road Landfill Expansion Phase 1] report as presented. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Barnes added that he has funding appropriated over the next 2-3 years to cover the cost of<sup>thisproject</sup>
