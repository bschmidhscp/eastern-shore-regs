# **SOMERSET COUNTY SEAFOOD RECONCILIATION BOARD MINUTES** _January 25, 2024_ 

The following are the minutes of the Somerset County Seafood Reconciliation Board meeting held on January 25, 2024, at 11:00 AM in the meeting room of the Somerset County Technical and Community Services Office, Room 211, in the Somerset County Office Complex, 11916 Somerset Avenue, Princess Anne, Maryland. 

**<u>Board Members Present: Somerset County DTCS Staff Present:</u>** Sue Heath (Chair) Jesse Drewer, Director John Barnette Phillip Holland Richard Evanusa 

Mrs. Sue Heath opened the meeting at 11:00am 

## **A. NEW BUSINESS:** 

- I. **Election of Chair:** Following a short discussion, Sue Heath was nominated to serve another year as the chair. 

- II. **Review of Ordinance #977:** Ordinance #977, Authorization to Harvest Seafood and Engage in the Seafood Industry Ordinance, was reviewed and discussed by members, with a focus on the Real Estate Transfer Disclosure being dispersed to real estate companies as a reminder of the ordinance. Mr. Drewer indicated he would send out a letter to all local real estate companies and the coastal realtors association. 

## **B. OTHER BUSINESS** 

Mr. Drewer asked if all member contact information could be provided to ensure staff could contact the board when scheduling the next meeting. 

## **<u>ADJOURNMENT:</u>** 

The Seafood Reconciliation Board adjourned at 11:28am
