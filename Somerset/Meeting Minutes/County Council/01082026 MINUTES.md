Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of January 8, 2026 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, January 8, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, and Maxine Landon, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, and Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant. Barbara Somers, Member, was absent. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

### Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating that the District is continuing to work on the design with Davis, Bowen, & Friedel, Inc. (DBF) and the treatment equipment manufacturers. The District has a meeting planned with Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) to discuss funding and cost of the project next week. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating the District is still working with MDE and DBF to finalize design. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Lead Service Line Project by stating the District is still currently working with MDE on this project and waiting to hear what next steps need to be taken. 

#### Smith Island Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) is currently online with minor issues being worked through prior to project completion. The District is waiting to hear from the USDA inspector to schedule the final punch list inspection. Mr. Padgette stated the force main from Tylerton to Rhodes Point is doing well and the deconstruction of the old plant is underway. Final completion date for this project has been scheduled for 2/4/2026. George, Miles, & Buhr, LLC (GMB) is drafting a letter to USDA for the District’s need for a boat purchase to transport Operators and supplies to the Island if there are leftover USDA funds when the project is complete.

## Fairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission in regards to the Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension Request/Petition by stating he is drafting a request for proposal (RFP) to be sent to the District's on-call engineers, DBF & GMB, in order to get a quote for engineering services. Once the RFP is returned and reviewed, the District will get a cost for the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) and Engineering Report (ER) to present to the County Commissioners, they will then determine whether they will be funding the reports.

### Approval of Dump Truck Repair 

The District's dump truck had a Department of Transportation (DOT) inspection in October 2024, finding multiple issues that needed to be addressed. Due to difficulties finding parts for the model and year of the truck, the repairs cost more than originally expected. The invoice provided by Stone’s Diesel & Truck Repair, Inc for the truck repair is in amount of $38,740.25, including a one-year warranty. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the dump truck repair invoice from Stone’s as presented; Mr. Williams seconded the motion which carried unanimously to approval.

# Request for Two Year, 0% Interest, Payment Plan — 30036 Donnybrook Ln 

Crystal Handy has presented the District with a request for a two-year, 0% interest, payment plan for her residence at 30036 Donnybrook Ln. Ms. Handy had a toilet leak which only made her eligible for a $200 sewer credit. Due to the leak carrying into multiple billing quarters, Ms. Handy requested additional time to pay the remaining balance of her bills. Management recommended granting the agreement as requested as well as waiving the current interest accrued. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve a two-year, 0% interest, payment plan for 30036 Donnybrook Ln. Ms. Elliott seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

Smith Island Subdistrict — Action 

## Request for Sewer Credit — 21228 Wharf Rd, Tylerton 

Somerset County Board of Commissioners, Owners of 21228 Wharf Rd, have submitted a request for a sewer vacancy credit. The County were unaware the previous tenants, Tylerton Crab Co-op, had not paid the bill since closing operations in 2019, leaving a balance of $8,550.48. Ernest Leatherbury Jr, Interim County Administrator, reached out to Mr. Taylor to see what could be done to minimize the balance. The District reached out to A&N Electric Co. and they were able to provide statements from September 30, 2022 — September 30,2025 showing electrical services were not being used during that timeframe. Ms. Landon made a motion to approve the sewer vacancy reduction rate for the period of September 30, 2022 — September 30, 2025 as well as waiving all interest accrued on the account as recommended by Management. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously.
