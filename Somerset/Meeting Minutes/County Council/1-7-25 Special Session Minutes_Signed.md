BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY JANUARY 7, 2025** 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for January 7, 2025</u>** 

On January 7, 2025, from 2:08 p.m. until 3:00 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and voted in favor by a show of hands by all members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; (3) To consider the acquisition of real property for a public purpose and matters directly related thereto; and (8) To consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals about pending or potential litigation. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, Director of Human Resources Jamie Manaia, Director of Dog Control Scott Sturgis, Director of Roads Woody Barnes, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Personnel; Actions Taken: Promotion x2; (2) Personnel; Actions Taken: Termination; (3) Property; Actions Taken: None; (4) Potential Litigation; Actions Taken: None. 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

No minutes were presented today.

SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS SPECIAL SESSION: TUESDAY JANUARY 7, 2025 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Services, Ms. Yvette Cross, Director of Emergency Services, and Ms. Kim Hopkins, Eastern Shore Long Term Recovery Committee (via phone) joined the meeting. 

Mr. Drewer presented a request from the Eastern Shore Long Term Recover Committee to submit a Notice of Interest and grant application to help support their mission goals to elevate and replace homes in Somerset County. Ms. Hopkins advised that the Committee cannot apply for this grant, and that only governments are eligible to apply for the funding and partner with eligible non-profit organizations. The goal for this application is to elevate ten homes at an estimate cost of $75,000.00, with the grant being submitted for $750,000.00. Mr. Drewer clarified they are before the Board today to request the approval to submit the Notice of Interest as the deadline to submit is January 10<sup>th</sup> (with them coming back to request approval to move forward with the grant application at a later meeting date). 

Ms. Hopkins left the meeting (ended the call). 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Vice President Randy Laird approving the Notice of Interest as presented. By a show of hands in favor by all members of the Board, the motion carried. 

Under public comments, Mr. Bob McIntyre commended Roads Director Woody Barnes for Polks Road. He then inquired about the area requirement for the raising of the homes, to which the Board answered it is not limited to one area specifically but countywide. 

The Board was then advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:08 pm, on  a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Vice President Randy Laird, and carried on a vote in favor by show of hands by all members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Approved by:
