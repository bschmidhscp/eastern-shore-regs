Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of February 8, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, February 8, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 216 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Adam Riggin, Chairman, Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, and Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide. Maxine Landon, Member, was absent. There were no other Public Attendees. 

Mr. Riggin called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Information 

The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Commission for review. The second quarter revenues exceeded expenses by $288,376.64; the surplus is partially due to reimbursements received after the end of last quarter from the United States Department of Agriculture and the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) for the Smith Island Clean Water Project. The Board had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus informed the Board there has been no change in status regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade since last month’s meeting. The Pilot Testing Agreement/Proposal is still being reviewed by Fluence (water treatment Solution Corporation). 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project stating the District has applied for funding through the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) — Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF) again this year. Mr. Ludy reduced the scope of plans for this project since last year in hopes the District will score more points for funding. The draft Intended Use Plan (IUP) for DWSRF will be available in Spring 2024 for public review/comment. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating the project is moving along, concrete floor has been poured, and project completion is projected for mid May 2024. 

#### Smith Island - Information

## Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular session minutes dated January 11'" 2024 were previously distributed. On a motion by Mr. Bagley and seconded by Ms. Somers, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular session minutes dated January 11 2024. 

# Emergency Shut-off Policy & Resolution 

The Commission were previously distributed a draft of the Emergency Shut-off Policy and Resolution for their review. The District reserves the right to interrupt and/or discontinue water service in the event of an emergency. Prior to interruption or disconnection, the District will make all reasonable efforts to determine if the property is occupied, and if so, the District will: 1) attempt to contact the occupants, 2) post a notice at the property, and 3) attempt to contact the owner of record. If the emergency is of such a nature as to not allow for advance notice of interruption or disconnection, the District will make all reasonable efforts to contact the occupants, post a notice at the property and contact the owner of record as soon as reasonably practicable. Ms. Somers made a motion to adapt the Emergency Shut-off Policy and Resolution as presented. Mr. Bagley seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

# Swimming Pool Policy & Resolution 

The Commission were previously distributed a draft of the Swimming Pool Policy and Resolution for their review. The District has established a swimming pool policy for customers who wish to fill their pool through their water service with the District or through their local fire department. The policy allows for credit on the sewer portion of a customer's bill provided they follow the procedures listed in the policy for commercial meter, residential meter, or fire department fill. Ms. Somers made a motion to adapt the Swimming Pool Policy and Resolution as presented. Mr. Bagley seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.

### Princess Anne — Action 

## Request for sewer credit 

Jason Wallace, 30043 Kristwood Way, requested the Board consider granting him a credit for some of the sewer usage charged to his water and sewer bill during the quarters of 9/30/23 and 12/30/23. Mr. Wallace stated in his request, during the 9/30/23 quarter he reseeded his yard and was watering to get it germinated and growing. During the 12/30/23 quarter, he topped off his pool. Management's recommendation was to take no action for the reason that the District are unable to verify how his water consumption was used. The Commission took no action on Mr. Wallace’s request, citing Management's recommendation. 

### Smith Island - Action 

## Approval of contract time extension 

Crofton Construction drafted a Change Order requesting a time extension for the SFM project. However, the Change Order is currently under review by funding agencies and is not ready for approval. Therefore, the Board tabled the request. 

### Lot Reconfiguration 

Horn Island Ventures, LLC are planing to consolidate their lots A and B of parcel 239 on Ocean Highway into one lot. During the 12/14/23 Commission meeting the Board approved the lot consolidation, however, Planning & Zoning required they show utility lines and right-of-ways. Mr. Bagley made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the revised Lot Reconfiguration. Ms. Somers seconded the motion; the motion carried unanimously to approval.
