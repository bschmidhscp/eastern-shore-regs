# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY APRIL 15, 2025** 

At 2:13 p.m., President Charles Laird called the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session on a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and carried on a vote in favor by a show of hands of all present members of the Board. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for April 15, 2025</u>** 

On April 15, 2025, from 2:20 p.m. until 3:10 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and voted in favor by a show of hands by all present members of the Board to enter into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3- 305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, and Commissioner Eldon Willing. Commissioner Darryl Webster was not present for today’s meeting. Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, Human Resources Director Jamie Manaia, Public Works Director Woody Barnes, EMS Coordinator Joel Dixon, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Topic: Salary, Action Taken: Approval of Salary for Promotion; (2) Topic: Part-Time Tourism Assistant, Action Taken: Hiring; (3) EMS Staff, Action Taken: Approval to Hire. 

The meeting began with Mr. Leatherbury advising of a Proclamation of State of Emergency for Somerset County. The nature of the proclamation is due to the urgent nature, limited timeframe, and lack of budgeted funding for the County to establish an ambulance squad. With that said, action is needed to expedite the procurement processes and the hiring of personnel, in order to best

Mr. Leatherbury then provided the recommendation, after consulting with County Attorney Kirk Simpkins, that President Charles Laird issue a Proclamation of State of Emergency for Somerset County, which will give the County the ability to purchase necessary equipment and hire personnel for the ambulance squad. 

President Charles Laird then advised that the issuance of this State of Emergency is a unanimous Board decision before reading the proclamation aloud and clarifying that the State of Emergency will be in effect for 30 days. 

Next, Mr. Leatherbury provided a request from Dr. Tasker-Mitchell, Superintendent of Somerset County Schools, for the Board’s assistance with nonrecurring costs for FY26 in the amount of $290,000. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to deny this request for assistance. The motion carried on a vote in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. Commissioner Craig Mathies abstained. 

The meeting was then opened up to the public for comment and Mr. Bob McIntyre inquired of the County would be taking over ambulance service for Crisfield as well. The Board advised they are not. 

The Board was advised for the need of a Closed Session. 

Therefore, at 2:20 pm, on a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster, seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing, and carried on a vote in favor by all present members of the Board, the Board entered into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland § 3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction.
