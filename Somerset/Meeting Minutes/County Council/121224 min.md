Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of December 12, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, December 12, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Maxine Landon, Member, was absent. The Crisfield (Peripheral Area) Board position is currently vacant. Scott Wade was in attendance for his appointment. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project stating the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) has been sent to Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) by Jason Loar, P.E., Davis, Bowen & Friedel (DBF) for their review and comments. 

Mr. Stockus stated there has been no movement regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project since November’s commission meeting where he announced SCSD received official notice of funding through MDE’s FFY 2024 Intended Use Plan for the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF). 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project by stating final invoices to be reimbursed through American Rescue Plan grant funds, allocated by the Somerset County Commissions, have been received. The remainder of items on the punch list is paused until after the holidays. 

#### Smith Island - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP) stating progress meetings were held on December 4" 2024 for both the Subaqueous Force Main (SFM) and Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) Upgrade projects. Crofton Construction, Contractor for the SFM, is on site plowing/drilling to install the pipeline from Tylerton to Rhodes Point with 75% of the project complete. Construction of the WWTP Upgrade continues with 81% completion. Entire project completion is projected for 6/25/25.

### Approval of Minutes 

The Sanitary Commission regular session minutes dated November 14!" 2024 were previously distributed. On a motion by Ms. Somers and seconded by Mr. Williams, it was unanimously carried to approve the regular session minutes dated November 14" 2024. 

## Execution of General On-call Services Agreement 

During the regular session commission meeting held on 10/10/24 the Sanitary Board awarded both Davis, Bowen & Friedel (DBF) and George, Miles & Buhr (GMB) for professional on-call engineering services. The District's Counsel, Cockey, Brennan & Maloney, have drafted an On-call Engineering Services Agreement to be executed separately between the District and both DBF and GMB. The Agreement has an initial term of three years with the right to renew for two one-year periods upon mutual agreement between the District and the Contractor. The Agreements were previously reviewed by Management prior to distribution to the Board. Ms. Somers made a motion to authorize the General Manager to execute the On-call Engineering Services Agreements between the District and DBF as well as the District and GMB. Mr. Williams seconded the motion; the motion carried to approval unanimously. 

Princess Anne — Action 

## Request for sewer credit 

The Commission tabled the sewer credit request for 11341 Stewart Neck Road until the District receives the required application to process. 

## Request for sewer credit 

The District received a leak credit application for 12113 College Place for a toilet leak repaired in August 2024. Subsequently an audit was performed by SCSD Staff to verify the leak has subsided. The Audit showed continuous use indicating an ongoing leak still remains at the property. It’s Management's recommendation to table the request for credit until verification that all leaks have been repaired. The Board tabled this request. 

1:45 p.m. Appointment — Action 

## Exception to Water Connection Policy 

Scott Wade, Owner of Process Integration (11500 Progress Lane), was present to discuss his proposed plans submitted by Wilkins-Noble LLC on November 11" 2024. The plans included a request to tap off the existing irrigation water line servicing his business in order to install a hose bib outside ofa proposed storage shed that will be built on the adjacent lot to his business.

<!-- page 5 -->
4 

The District reviewed Mr. Wade's plans and responded by stating water service cannot cross between different lots. In order to have the hose bib outside of the proposed building he would need to either: 

1. Consolidate lot 1 (11500 Progress Lane) with lot 15 and all water will go thru existing meter at 11500 Progress Lane. or 

2. Pay for water connection to proposed building on lot 15 and pay water usage & EDU bill quarterly. 

Mr. Wade was present, with plans/mylars in hand, to discuss his request with the Board. Mr. Wade explained that the proposed storage shed will be strictly a pole building with no running plumbing, however, he would like to have a hose outside for washing vehicles, etc. Mr. Wade stated subdividing the lots would be very expensive for him and time consuming to have approved by all the agencies of the county. 

Ms. Mister clarified the District's water/sewer connection policy is based on the standards set by our Engineer's as well as Legal Counsel. Ms. Mister explained the District adopted these standards to be more impartial and efficient, getting away from having to remember to monitor “special circumstances” such as this. If Mr. Wade were to sell lot 15 for instance, the water usage through the requested hose would be billed through the meter at 11500 Progress Lane. 

Mr. Wade stressed time is of the essence and asked if the Board would approve the plans contingent upona lot consolidation in the future. Ms. Marshall explained a clause would need to be added to the plans explaining the Board’s decision prior to executing. In the meantime, Mr. Stockus suggested the Commission and Management review the plans more in depth before making a decision via phone poll in an effort to expedite Mr. Wade’s consolidation process. The Board tabled Mr. Wade’s request. 

## 2:00 p.m. Public Hearing 

Mr. Bagley called the Public Hearing to order at 2:00 p.m. 

Ms. Mister announced the opening of the public hearing for the review and approval of the Calendar Year 2025 Debt Service Budget and Rates for the Somerset County Sanitary District. The Sanitary District Management is proposing no change in the sewer rate of $30.35 per quarter. The District is proposing the water rate of $21.65 per in Princess Anne and $20.25 in Fairmount and Crisfield, a decrease from current rate of $4.00/quarter ($16.00/year). This decrease in water rate was possible because two capital loans in Crisfield were paid off this year.
