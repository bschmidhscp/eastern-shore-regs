Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of February 12, 2026 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, February 12, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Maxine Landon, Member, was absent. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

#### General Information 

The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Board for review. The second quarter (FY26) revenues exceeded expenses by $85,953.43. The Commission had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

The District presented a first read of a Reserve Fund Policy to the Commission. The reserve fund is a continuing, non-lapsing account established to provide financial stability and to support appropriations that have become unfunded due to revenue shortfalls or other unforeseen financial circumstances. This policy is being created for transparency. With a consensus from the board, the policy will return next meeting as an action item. 

### Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating that the District is continuing to work on the preliminary design with Davis, Bowen, & Friedel, Inc. (DBF) and Maryland Department of Environment (MDE). The District is expecting it to take around 12 months for design and permitting to be completed, then another 6 months for the bidding process. Mr. Taylor estimates that it will take 18-24 months before construction begins. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating the District is still working with MDE and DBF on the preliminary design phase. A project meeting will be scheduled with the funding agency and the District's engineer within the next couple of months.

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Lead Service Line Project by stating the District is still currently working with DBF, MDE, and Electroscan (data collection and management program) on this project. If approved by the SCSD Board, the District will be purchasing equipment and partnering with Electroscan and DBF to utilize the equipment to finish inventory and be ahead of schedule for the lead service line that is being mandated by The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and MDE. 

Smith Island Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) upgrade is reaching the end of completion. The District has scheduled the final punch list inspection for 3/4/2026, which will include representatives from the District, GMB (George, Miles, & Buhr), MDE, USDA, and M2 Construction. It’s hopeful that project completion will be before the end of FY 2026. 

## Fairmount/Rumbley Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission in regards to the Rumbley/Frenchtown Sewer Extension Request/Petition by stating that a request for proposal (RFP) has been sent to the District's on-call engineers, DBF & GMB, in order to get a quote for engineering services for the Preliminary Engineering Report (PER) and Engineering Report (ER). Those quotes will be due on 3/5/2026. Once the RFP is returned and reviewed, the District will have a cost for the PER and ER to present to the County Commissioners, they will then determine whether they will be funding the reports. 

### Public Input 

No public input was received by the District.

#### Fairmount Subdistrict — Action 

## Request for Leak Credit — 27149 Fairmount Rd 

Carroll Smith has presented the District with a request for a leak credit at her rental property, 27149 Fairmount Rd. Ms. Smith had a leak under the kitchen sink of the trailer on property. The water has been disconnected at the meter but the leak has not been repaired due to the Insurance company condemning the property. Ms. Smith did inform the District that there are plans for the trailer to be torn down asa total loss. Management recommended to not offer a credit at this time and to wait until the trailer is demolished, as it goes against policy, that a leak must be repaired in order to receive a credit. Once that happens, the District's technicians can be sent out to the property to verify that the water is capped off, then a credit can be offered to the owner. The commission chose to take no action at the time until the trailer on the property is tore down and services are capped off.
