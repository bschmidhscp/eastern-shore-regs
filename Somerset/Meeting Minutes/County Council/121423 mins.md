Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of December14"* 2023 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, December 14, 2023 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Chairman, Adam Riggin, Vice-Chairman, Charles Bagley, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Earl Ludy, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, and Josh Taylor, P.E., Davis, Bowen & Friedel. Public Attendees included Cindy Perry and Eric Davis. Maxine Landon, Member, was absent. 

Mr. Riggin called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project by stating a Pilot Testing Agreement/Proposal has been drafted by Fluence (a water treatment solution corporation) for the Sanitary District's review, the proposal is currently being reviewed by SCSD’s Engineer and Attorney for their comment/approval. Mr. Ludy is hopeful the pilot study will begin in February 2024. 

Josh Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project, informing them the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) has responded to the blending proposal stating MDE generally does not accept blending to improve water quality, as operational challenges make it difficult to ensure effective mixing occurs to meet drinking water standards. MDE also stated water quality samples collected in October 2023 at the PASE wells exceeded standard levels for Fluoride, Total Dissolved Solids, and Sodium which will result in complaints of colored water and staining as well as taste and odor. MDE believes reverse osmosis (RO), which removes the contaminants of concern, is the best method for treatment. Mr. Stockus informed Doug Taylor, Somerset County Administrator, the Sanitary District will be appealing MDE’s decision to deny blending the wells. Josh Taylor is hopeful the recent concerns (Somerset Landing Petition) and support from local government Officials (Somerset County Commissioners and Delegate, Charles Otto) will help the District score higher this year when applying for funding through the MDE- State Revolving Fund. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating construction is underway on the Lab Building and the conduit for electric has been installed. Project completion is projected for April - May 2024.

## Appointment - Action 

Eric Davis was present to discuss his increase in Equivalent Dwelling Units (EDUs) for 30511 Hickory Road. The District recently evaluated water usage on all commercial water and/or sewer accounts to ensure that assessments accurately reflect property consumption per the EDU policy. The evaluation was based on average quarterly usage from December 2021 — September 2023. All commercial accounts with adjustments to their number of EDUs were sent a letter notifying them of the increase effective January 1, 2024. Mr. Davis requested the Board reevaluate his usage starting from December 2023 quarter. Mr. Davis explained during the timeframe evaluated he had a contract with a company to house 70 of its employees, however, it ended up housing 120 people. The overcapacity caused all the utilities to triple. After failing to comply with the occupancy agreement, all the tenants were moved from the property by June 2023. Mr. Davis stated during renovation of the apartments, they discovered and repaired 4 toilet leaks. New tenants started moving in September 2023. The Commission understands Mr. Davis’ request, however they are inclined to follow the policy. Mr. Stockus suggested tabling Mr. Davis’ request for a reevaluation to get more information from him and possibly consult with Counsel regarding the policy.
