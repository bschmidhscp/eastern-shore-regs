Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of April 10, 2025 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, April 10, 2025 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Interim Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor P.E., General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Chris Derbyshire, P.E., George, Miles & Buhr, and Heather Konyar, Legal Counsel, Cockey, Brennan, & Maloney. The Crisfield (Peripheral Area) Board position is currently vacant. There were no members of the Public present. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

##### General Business — Information 

Mr. Taylor informed the Commission of the proposed amendment to the Policy on Allocation of Water and Sewer Benefit Assessments. The amendment draft, previously distributed for the Board’s review, includes additions to the billing method, exempted properties. The additions include language allowing the District to determine exemptions on a case-by-case basis, and at the sole discretion of the Sanitary Commission. The consensus of the Board was to move forward with the proposed amendment to the Allocation Policy; the Policy will be brought before the Board for approval at the next regular session. 

#### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) Enhanced Nutrient Removal (ENR) Upgrade by stating, Jason Loar, Davis, Bowen, & Friedel (DBF), is submitting request for qualifications (RFQs) for mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP), as well as geotechnical engineering services. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project stating the District’s next step will be issuing a Task Order Request (TOR) for professional engineering services. 

Mr. Padgette updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project by stating the old lab is permanently closed and the lab equipment has been moved to the new lab. The District is currently outsourcing lab services until the new lab is online. Mr. Padgette stated the floors are going in now followed by finalizing plumbing. The goal is for the lab to be complete and operational in four weeks. 

During the Somerset County Board of Commissioners meeting held on February 25"" 2025, the Board unanimously voted to accept the highest bid for the purchaseoftheir

##### Smith Island - Information 

Chris Derbyshire, P.E., George, Miles & Buhr (GMB), was present to update the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project (SIWCP) stating the Subaqueous Force Main (SFM) pipeline from Tylerton to Rhodes Point has been fully installed. Mr. Derbyshire informed the Board, GMB was notified by the District's Sewer Operator on the Island, that the SFM pipeline has lifted from its intended position near the Tylerton connection. GMB is planning a site visit next week to investigate, once they know more they will be reaching out to Crofton Construction (Contractor for SFM) to address the issue. Mr. Derbyshire advised against paying most recent payment application #7 in the amount of $448,827.50 until they communicate with Crofton to resolve this issue.

### Waiver of charges request 

As previously stated during the information portion of this meeting, the Somerset County Commissioners recently approved the sale of their surplus properties located in Westfield Estates. The County Commissioners requested the Sanitary District waive all charges accrued to date for Westfield Estates totaling $436,818.48. If approved, the District must also release their lien on Westfield Estates in the amount of $197,017.83 for unpaid debt service and front foot assessment following the District's lawsuit victory against Malone Investments. Management recommended waiving the charges as the sale of Westfield Estates will generate revenue for the District through debt service billing and water and sewer service after developed. Ms. Somers made a motion to approve the waiver of charges for the County Commissioners in the amount of $197,017.83 and release the lien on Westfield Estates property previously filed by the Sanitary District. Ms. Landon seconded the motion; the motion was unanimously carried to approval.

Mr. Padgette informed the Commission, the UMES lift station pump needs rebuilding.
