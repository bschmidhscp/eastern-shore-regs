# Crisfield-Somerset County Airport Advisory Board 

October 2, 2023 

Members Present: Staff Present: Bryant Owens, Chairman Jessica Grangier, Office Assistant Chris Hartman. John Redden Guests Present: 

Guests Present: Mr. Donald Anthony 

## Danny Thompson 

On October 2"! 2023 at 3:00 p.m., a meeting was held for the Crisfield-Somerset County Airport Advisory Board at 11916 Somerset Ave., Room 11, Princess Anne, Maryland. Mr. Owens called the meeting to order. 

The first order of business was the 2023-2025 Disadvantage Business Enterprise Update. Mr. Redden stated that AECOM had updated the 2021-2023 document and his office had updated and returned for filing. The document is available on the county website as well as AECOM. 

Mr. Redden moved on to update the board on the 2024 Bipartisan Infrastructure Law Airport Terminal Program that he had been sent information on. He had followed up with FAA to see if Crisfield Airport (W41) was eligible for funding and it was determined since W41 was an unclassified airport, it was not eligible for funds. 

Mr. Redden provided an update on the Airport Avigation Easement Project. There is one property left that has items to be resolved. The property is owned by a handful of heirs with one missing. The lawyers are working out how to proceed with all parties. Mr. Redden advised that the parties were looking to sell the property and wanted the matter settled quickly. Mr. Lawson inquired if there was a possibility of the County/City acquiring the property for future interest. Mr. Redden said he would look into it. 

Mr. Owens then moved on the Airport Runway Rehabilitation project and Mr. Redden advised the following update: The MOU for the NEPA and building portion of the project was executed by the Board of County Commissioner, the City of Crisfield and both entities legal representatives. Mr. Redden believed the cost of this portion was around $150,000.00. Due to increased changes in critical area and floodplains the permitting process is more involved. 

Mr. Owens moved to the next item which was the wait list documents for the new airport hangars. Ms. Grangier went over the documents (Waitlist Policy, Waitlist Procedure, Waitlist Form and Internal Administrative Process). It was stated that the forms would go through the County’s legal department before getting Commissioner Approval and then City Approval. It was discussed that the County Engineer’s office and Ms. Grangier would handle the administrative side of the process for the time being. Mr. Hartman motioned to approve the documents. Mr. Wunder seconded and the motion passed unanimously. 

Mr. Owens then moved on with the Airport Strategic Plan. Mr. Hartman stated that the Fall class would be on Airport Management and the Spring class would be Airport Planning. He stated

<!-- page 2 -->
that the Spring class would be coming to the Office to interview various members of the Board about different aspects of the airport. 

The discussion then moved on to new business. Mr. Hartman spoke about the MDOT Smart Grant that UMES is working on to provide drone delivery of medicines from Crisfield Airport to Smith Island. He referenced a program that Accomack County, Virginia is running right now with drops to Tangier Island. Mr. Hartman also mentioned that the students were going to start conducting night flights and would potentially be refueling at night at Crisfield Airport. 

The discussion moved to public comments. Mr. Donald Anthony was in attendance. He currently has an airplane at the airport and wanted to make sure that his plan would fit in the new hangars. He provided specifications and Mr. Redden agreed to look into the dimensions and get back to him. He asked to be kept in the loop with any future meetings so he may show up and voice support for the project. 

The next meeting was discussed and scheduled for Monday, January 8", 2024 at 3:00 p.m. in room 216. 

With no further business, a motion to adjourn was made by Mr. Redden and seconded by Mr. Lawson. The motion carried unanimously and the meeting adjourned at 3:56 p.m. 

Respectfully Submitted: 

Approved:
