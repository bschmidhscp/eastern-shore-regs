##### Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of May 14, 2026 

A regular session of theSomerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, May 16, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Rev. Charles Bagley, Chairman, Faith Elliott, Vice Chairman, Dennis Williams, Secretary-Treasurer, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Josh Taylor, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Trish Raab, Administrative Assistant, Heather Konyar, Legal Counsel, Cockey, Brennen, & Maloney, and Richard Crumbacker, Somerset County Times. Maxine Landon, Member, was absent. There were no members of the Public present. Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. General Information The Sanitary District held a budget work session on April 16° 2026. The Consensus of the Board was to move forward with advertising the following rates for quarterly water and sewer service, connection fees, bulk water sales and septage hauler fees for fiscal year 2027. Due to a projected budget shortfall, the District is recommending an across the board rate increase of approximately 10% to both water and sewer as well as a proposed rate increase in bulk water sales of 60 cents/1,000 gallons. Other miscellaneous fees are also subject to a change as well. See advertised rate ad attached or by visiting the Sanitary District's page under Government on the Somerset County Website, www.somersetmd.us. 

The Financial Quarterly Analysis report was previously distributed to the Board for review. The third quarter (FY26) revenues exceeded expenses by $270,843.48. The Commission had no questions or comments regarding the quarterly report. 

The District presented a first read of a Water, Sewer, and/or Fire Service Connection Policy to the Commission. The policy is being updated for clarification and some minor word changes were made. With a consensus from the board, the policy will return next meeting as an action item. 

The District presented a first read of a Purchasing Policy to the Commission. The policy is being modified for transparency and to ensure the District follows all proper protocols when making purchases. Ms. Mister informed the Commission that the major change in this policy was updating a non-construction bid amount from $5,000.00 to $10,000.00 as well as sole source language being added. With a consensus from the board, the policy will return next meeting as an action item.

The District presented a first read of a Contractor Pre-Qualification Policy to the Commission. The policy is being created to ensure all contractors who are hired to perform work on District infrastructure must be experienced, licensed, insured and qualified. With a consensus from the board, the policy will return next meeting as an action item. 

The District presented a first read of a Surplus and Salvage Policy to the Commission. The policy is being updated for word changes and a section for Real Estate was added for when the District sells property. Now, there will be clear guidelines that will need to be followed before selling any property owned by the Sanitary District. With a consensus from the board, the policy will return next meeting as an action item. 

The District presented a first read of a Safety Policy to the Commission. The District has always had safety policies but this new policy is being created for clear clarification. With a consensus from the board, the policy will return next meeting as an action item. 

###### Princess Anne Subdistrict — Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant ENR (Enhanced Nutrient Removal) Upgrade by stating that the District is continuing to work with Davis, Bowen, & Friedel, Inc. (DBF) and Maryland Department of Environment (MDE) on required bid documents for this project. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Color Removal Project by stating the District is still working with MDE and DBF on the design phase but continue to see progress. The District has reached out to USDA Rural Development to see if any supplemental grants are available to hopefully minimize any loan amounts that could accrue from this project. 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Lead Service Line Project by stating the District is following state protocols and making submissions. The agreement with Electro Scan, Inc. to purchase the swordfish equipment to assist with this project has been done. Once our personnel are trained, the District will be able to use this equipment to identify lead lines. 

###### Smith Island Subdistrict - Information 

Mr. Taylor updated the Commission regarding the Smith Island Clean Water Project by stating the wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) upgrade is reaching the end of completion. Mr. Taylor spoke with a representative from George, Miles, & Buhr, LLC (GMB) and the punch list inspection is expected to be completed by the end of May. The project is expected to meet completion by the end of June.
