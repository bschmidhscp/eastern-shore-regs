BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY SPECIAL SESSION 

THURSDAY JANUARY 16, 2025 

At 2:00 p.m., the Board entered into Special Session. Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Darryl Webster and Commissioner Craig Mathies. 

Also, in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy Administrator Ernest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory Ebron. 

President Charles Laird asked Commissioner Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of<sup>Allegiance.</sup> 

At 2:04 a brief recess was taken. 

Closed Session Summary Report for January 16, 2025 

On January 16, 2025, from 2:15 p.m. until 3:54 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., seconded by Commissioner Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (7) consult with counsel to obtain legal advice. 

Present for the meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Eldon Willing, Commissioner Darryl Webster and Commissioner Craig Mathies. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Executive Aide Lory Ebron, County Attorney Kirk Simpkins. Topics discussed were: (1) Consult with Counsel (2) Ambulance Service in Somerset County (3) Request for Appropriation. Actions Taken: Approval to hire Emergency Services Coordinator (2) Emergency Services Ambulance Plan (3) Approval for appropriation request.
