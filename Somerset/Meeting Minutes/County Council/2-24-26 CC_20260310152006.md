President Charles Laird first asked to address calls and social media comments that he has been receiving regarding storage containers. Mr. Drewer advised he was aware of the comments and concerns, explaining that they are in response to a request made by Judge Price who asked for clarity to be advertised regarding the specifics of the zoning code regarding storage containers to make the public more aware. A discussion was held regarding the differences between Pods and storage containers. Mr. Drewer replied that in response to the Judge’s request, they have posted several zoning rules and regulations on social media, which resulted in the comments being made, and are also preparing an ad to run in the local paper. President Laird stated it would be beneficial to let the constituents know the county is providing this information to be helpful, and to write it in a way that is not harsh, as residents are interpreting what’s been on social media as nothing can be done on someone’s own property. He would like for it to be known that this is not the commissioners trying to take something from the citizens. He stated he understands their concerns because he has felt that himself on projects he has had. 

Commissioner Webster questioned ordinances online, asking why this specific one has become an issue. Mr. Drewer stated that he receives public comments on various codes, including

Commissioner Willing made a motion to approve Ordinance No. 1220 (the Somerset County Critical Area Ordinance amendment to include language for restaurants and retreats in the Resource Conservation Areas) as presented. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Next, Mr. Drewer presented a request for lien release regarding 31872 Flower Hill Church Road, Eden Maryland. The lien made on November 11, 1996, in the amount of $12,315.00 funded repairs at the property, and expired on November 11, 2001. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve the request for lien release as presented for $12, 315.00. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 4-0. 

Mr. Leatherbury continued with correspondence and discussion items. 

A letter was sent to Somerset County Health Officer, Danielle Weber regarding the Boards approval to install six (6) additional monitoring well sites at county owned properties. 

A request was received from Mr. John Barnette, during a previous meeting, asking the county for a letter of support, and an allocation in the amount of $65,000 to assist the fire company with their grant request to purchase a Tanker to better serve the residents of the county. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to approve an allocation in the amount of $65,000 to assist the Mt. Vernon Fire Company’s purchase of a new Tanker. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. By show of<sup>hands,the motioncarried4-0.</sup>
