Mr. Leatherbury asked Ms. Sharon K. Muir, Internal Auditor, to come forward to receive recognition from the Board of County Commissioners for her 45 years of dedicated service to the county. The Board expressed their gratitude to Ms. Muir and commended her for her many years of service. 

Next, Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Service came forward to present the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area Growth Allocation Request and adoption of Ordinance No. 1213 for 27999 Coulboum Creek Road (Kingsbay Mansion). A public hearing was held by the Planning Commission on June 5, 2025, noting that two letters were received in support. No other comments have been received following the Public Hearing held by the Board of County Commissioners. Mr. Drewer asked for the Boards approval of Ordinance No. 1213 as presented. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve Ordinance No. 1213 as presented. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. By show of hands, the motion carried 5-0. 

Mr. Danny Thompson, Economic Development Director, came forward at this time to inform the Board of the first round of Maryland Economic Development Corporation (MEDCO) Maryland Business Ready Sites Funding Program, established by Governor Moore’s economic competitiveness executive order. The press release was provided in the agenda package detailing the program. Mr. Thompson explained that there are two parts to this funding program; one titled “Characterization” and the second for “Infrastructure”. He advised he has submitted for grant funding regarding the Industrial Park, thanking Mr. John Redden, Ms. Linton, Choptank and Chesapeake Utilities for assisting him with the application. He advised the county was awarded $10,000 from the Characterization grant. This is a match grant however funding can be used that is already available from a previously awarded grant can be used for the match, and therefore be at no cost to the county. Mr. Thompson explained that a consultant will be hired, to evaluate what industry is best suited for the park, Mr. Thompson advised that he also applied for the infrastructure grant (ranging from $300,000-$500,000) but it was not awarded; however, following a meeting with MEDCO, the application was placed on a waiting list (additional funding in six months to one year) noting we won't have to re-apply. He was told the application was liked but there just was not enough funding to go around this time, noting is was diversified around the state. 

Mr. Chris Woodward, IT Director came forward at this time, and Mr. John Redden, County Engineer joined the meeting by phone. Mr. Redden advised that there was additional good news

regarding broadband for Somerset County. He explained they have worked with the state for the last 3-4 weeks, noting that the question has been asked regarding how many residents in the county are still left with no broadband service. He advised that through our three main providers, which he broke down individually, it is reported that there are 264 “very hard to reach” addresses that still do not have broadband. To address those areas the state has approved offering Starlink Satellite service to 64 extremely hard to reach locations. The Homestretch Grant will cover the cost to purchase equipment and installation fee (approximately $550.00) for these 64 locations. The next upcoming grant program is called the BEAD program. As with the other service providers the household will be responsible to pay the monthly service fee ranging from $80.00 -$160.00 per month, Mr. Woodward explained that Starlink is a very reliable and equitable service as compared to broadband. The Board was pleased to hear that there was an option provided to those users who have not been able to be serviced by the broadband ISP's thanking the Broadband team for their good work. Mr. Thompson, Mr. Woodward and Mr. Redden left the meeting at this time. 

Ms. Christina Robinson, 100% Triumph Events Coordinator, came forward at this time to present a funding request for consideration. She explained that 100% Triumph is a grass-roots Christian organization, located in UMES, that is dedicated to mentoring students toward academic excellence. The founders, Dr. Akintayo Emmanuel and Pastor Sarah Emmanuel dedicate the organization to developing well-rounded leaders through holistic support and disciplined focus. She advised that Governor Moore has expressed his support of<sup>theprogram.Theorganizationis</sup> planning to host an Outdoor Worship Experience in September with a goal of creating a welcoming space where individuals can take a break from the everyday demands of life to connect both spiritually and communally through live worship music and powerful testimonials. Their hope is this event will serve as a source of encouragement, unity and renewal. The anticipated cost to host the event is $100,000 - $125,000 and she is here today requesting $75,000 from the county in funding support to help bring the community event to life. 

Ms. Robinson was thanked for presenting the information about the event, being advised that the county would consider the request.
