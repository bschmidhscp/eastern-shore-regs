BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY December 16, 2025 

At 2:00 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird called the Board of County Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Regular Session. Also present for today’s meeting were Commissioner Darryl Webster, Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr. and Commissioner Eldon Willing. President Charles Laird was absent from today’s meeting. 

Interim County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury Jr., and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

### ClosedSummarySession Report 

On Tuesday, December 16, 2025, from 2:14 p.m., until 3:24 p.m., Vice President Randy Laird, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing entered into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3-305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (3) consider the acquisition of real property for a public purpose and matters directly related to the acquisition. Commissioner Craig N. Mathies Sr., was also present. All those present voted aye, therefore, the motion carried 4-0. President Charles Laird was absent from today’s meeting. 

Interim County Administrator Emest J. Leatherbury, Jr., HR Director Jamie Manaia, EMS. Chief Joel Dixon, Public Works Director Woody Bares, and Executive Aide Lory E. Ebron were also present. 

Topics discussed: (1) Letter of Intent/Mitigation (2) Employee Part time to Fulltime Status 3) Assistant Chief Hire. Approved: (1) Approval to sign letter of intent (2) Approval to move part time employee to fulltime (3) Approval to hire Assistant Chief.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 12-16-25 

Commissioner Eldon Willing made a motion to approve the minutes as presented. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

Ms. Starr Mister, Somerset County Sanitary District came forward to present a Resolution (No. 1217) authorizing guarantee of<sup>theloan for thepurchaseof two(2)vehicles(a2025FordF-</sup> 150 Work Truck and a 2025 Ford F-350 Work Truck) at a cost of $126,984. The request, previously presented in April 2025 was for three (3) trucks, but modified to now be two trucksand a trailer, presented by Mr. Josh Taylor during a meeting held on November 18, 2025. Ms. Starr explained that the bank is requiring the resolution authorizing the County Commissioners guarantee of the loan note for the two trucks. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve Resolution No. 1217 as presented. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 4-0. Ms. Starr left the meeting at this time. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Services came forward. He advised the Board of Commissioners that Mr. Brannigan Thornton, a licensed plumber, has contacted his office expressing his interest to serve on the Building and Plumbing Code Appeals Board which currently has a vacancy for a licensed plumber. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to appoint Brannigan Thornton to the Building and Plumbing Code ofAppeals Board for a three-year term. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

Mr. Woody Barnes, Public Works Director came forward to present a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) prepared by the Heath Department regarding an expansion to their parking lot. Mr. Barnes explained that their office is having more activity on Tuesday’s and Thursday’s and also need more parking area for vehicles that were being parked here at the county complex and asked to move due to our own expansion. The cost of the parking lot expansion will be approximately $35,000 (high estimate) and will be paid out of the Health Department’s budget. Mr. Barnes distributed an aerial view of the layout, advising that Ms. Weber is also asking approval to reallocate funding within her budget to cover the expense as well. 

Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., made a motion to approve the reallocation of funds in the amount of $35,000. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted 

aye, therefore the motion carried 4-0.

Board of Commissioners Meeting 12-16-25 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to approve the MOU between the Somerset County Health Department and the Somerset County Commissioners for the parking lot expansion project. Commissioner Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore, the motion 

carried 4-0. 

Mr. Leatherbury continued with correspondence and discussion items. 

A letter was sent to Salisbury Neighborhood Housing Services advising that their building permit fee waiver request was approved. Also, a letter was sent to Ms. Ruby Ring advised her that the request for a landfill tipping fee waiver was also approved. 

Regarding discussion items, Mr. Leatherbury requested the Boards approval to appoint Mr. Allan Webster to serve a (citizen representative) member on the ES Advisory Board. 

Commissioner Webster made a motion to appoint Mr. Webster to the ES Advisory Board as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye, therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

Next, formal approval was requested for two letters of support for the City of Crisfield; the first for the Long Point Living Breakwater Project and the second for the Southern Flood Mitigation Wetlands project. Both letters were approved by phone poll on December 4, 2025. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to formally approve both letters of support for the City of Crisfield as presented. Commissioner Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

A request for proclamation was presented for approval from the Big Brothers/Big Sisters of the Eastern Shore acknowledging January 2026 as National Mentoring Month. The proclamation will be presented at a meeting to be held in January. 

Commissioner Darryl Webster made a motion to approve the proclamation request as presented. Commissioner Mathies seconded the motion. Those present voted aye therefore, the motion carried 4-0. 

Last, Mr. Leatherbury advised the Board of an email he received from the Placement Director of the Governor’s Office of Appointments advising that Mr. Van B Muir Jr. is serving an expired term on the Somerset County Board of License Commissioners, and will continue to serve until he is reappointed or a successor named. Mr. Leatherbury advised he has spoken with Mr. Muir who would like to continue serving on this board.

Board of Commissioners Meeting [2-16-25 

Commissioner Mathies made a motion to recommend Mr. Van B. Muir Jr. be reappointed to serve on the Somerset County Board of License Commissioners. Commissioner Darryl Webster seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 4-0. 

Mr. John Barnette, Mt. Vernon MD and Chiefofthe Mt. Vernon Fire Department requested to address the board. First, Mr. Barnette questioned ifMr. Allan Webster was going to serve as the citizen representative on the ES Advisory Board. Serving on the Task Force Committee himself, he stated that the first step should be to designate a Chair, voted in by the members of the commission. The policy draft has not been formally adopted, and he is requesting that when the policies are presented, it be done in an open session meeting, requesting to be told when it will be occurring. He also noted seeing the facility being erected out back stating he is just making sure that everyone is on the same page regarding the new ES Advisory Board, and how it is supposed to work. 

With no further business, the Board was advised of the need for a closed session. 

Therefore, with no further business, upon a motion made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Craig N. Mathies, Sr., a motion was made to go into Closed Session by Authority of the General Provisions Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland §3305 (b)(1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction; and (3) consider the acquisition of real property for a public purpose and matters directly related to the acquisition. Commissioner Eldon Willing seconded the motion. All those present voted aye therefore the motion carried 4-0. 

The Commissioner Regular Session and Closed Session Meetings concluded at 3:24 p.m.
