# BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR SOMERSET COUNTY **SPECIAL SESSION TUESDAY NOVEMBER 12, 2024** 

At 2:00 p.m., President Charles Laird call the Board of Commissioners for Somerset County to open in Special Session. Present for today’s meeting were President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster. 

Also in attendance were County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez. 

# **<u>Closed Session Summary Report for November 12, 2024</u>** 

On November 12, 2024, from 2:16 p.m. until 2:52 p.m., at 11916 Somerset Avenue, Room 111, upon a motion made by Commissioner Craig Mathies, seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster, and voted in favor by a show of hands by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster to enter into Closed Session by Authority of State Government Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, Subtitle §3-305 (b) (1) the appointment, employment, assignment, promotion, discipline, demotion, compensation, removal, resignation, or performance evaluation of an appointee, employee, or official over whom it has jurisdiction;  (8) consult with staff, consultants, or other individuals about pending or potential litigation and; (14) discuss before a contract is awarded or bids are opened, a matter directly related to a negotiating strategy or the contents of a bid or proposal, if public discussion or disclosure would adversely impact the ability of the public body to participate in the competitive bidding or proposal process. 

Present for the meeting were Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing and Commissioner Darryl Webster. President Charles Laird was not in attendance for this closed session. County Administrator Ralph D. Taylor, Deputy County Administrator Ernie Leatherbury, County Engineer John Redden, Director of Recreation, Parks, and Tourism Clint Sterling, Director of Human Resources Jamie Manaia, Assistant Director of Emergency Services Gary Powell, and Executive Secretary Kimberly Lopez were also present. Topics discussed were: (1) Property; Actions Taken: None, (2) Personnel; Actions Taken: 1. Hiring, 2. Probationary Status to Permanent Status.

SOMERSET COUNTY COMMISSIONERS SPECIAL SESSION: TUESDAY NOVEMBER 12, 2024 

President Charles Laird called the meeting to order and asked Commissioner Eldon Willing to lead those present in Devotion and the Pledge of Allegiance. 

Mr. Clint Sterling, Director of Recreation, Parks, and Tourism, was now before the Board to present the FY25 Destination Marketing Organization Grant Agreement (funding the County receives yearly to assist with marketing efforts) in the amount of $28,237.00. Mr. Taylor advised that, due to a tight deadline and this being a grant that the Board has approved of yearly, the agreement has already been signed by him on behalf of the County, and they are before the Board today to seek formal approval. 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to formally approve the FY25 Destination Marketing Organization Grant Agreement. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Sterling left the meeting. 

Mr. Jesse Drewer, Director of Technical and Community Services, was now before the Board to advise that the County has been notified that the grant application submitted to the Maryland Department of Emergency Management and Federal Emergency Management Agency has been approved for the 617 W. Main Street Elevation Project in the amount of $97,112.80. This grant has been reviewed and found legally sufficient by County Attorney Kirk Simpkins. Mr. Drewer further advised that the current estimated cost for the elevation project is $104,000 and the homeowner is aware that they are responsible for paying the difference and the cost involved with relocation during the construction process. 

With not further questions, a motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to authorize the Board’s signature for the Hazard Mitigation Grant Program Award for 617 W. Main Street Elevation Project in the amount of $97,112.80. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Following this, Mr. Drewer presented a request from Ms. Cindy Stone, Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development, on behalf of Ms. Jennifer Trager of Catholic Charities/Seton Center to amend the agreement for the CDBG grant with regards to

Catholic Charities/Seton Center and the assistance with the Seton Center Homeless Shelter; requesting $152,880 for staff and $47,120 for food (whereas the current agreement covers 3 parttime employees and food at $76,440 for staff and $123,560 for food). This amended amount will ensure they can cover 3 part-time staff for the full 2 years of the contract while still assisting with food purchases. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Darryl Webster and seconded by Commissioner Eldon Willing to approve this request as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Drewer then presented a request from Salisbury Neighborhood Housing services (in partnership with the Crisfield Housing Authority) to waive the Building Permit Fees in the amount of $620.68 with regards to the Parkside High School CTE Program and the Maryland Department of Planning for the placement of a home located on Pear Street in Crisfield (to increase the options for affordable housing for low-to-moderate income individuals and families). 

A motion was made by Vice President Randy Laird and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the waiver of fees as presented. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Drewer left the meeting. 

Mr. Chris Woodward, Director of Information Technology, was now before the Board to present an amendment to the MOU regarding the Local Cyber Program. Mr. Woodward advised that this amendment is the second part of the process that allows the State to assist with developing missing policies, implement the procedures, and get the County up to a national standard. 

A motion was made by Commissioner Craig Mathies and seconded by Commissioner Darryl Webster to approve the amendment as presented and authorize the President’s signature. By a show of hands in favor by President Charles Laird, Vice President Randy Laird, Commissioner Craig Mathies, Commissioner Eldon Willing, and Commissioner Darryl Webster, the motion carried. 

Mr. Woodward also advised of notification regarding the award of a $100,000 grant to assist with cyber security. 

Mr. Woodward left the meeting.
