Somerset County Sanitary Commission Minutes of July 11, 2024 

A regular session of the Somerset County Sanitary Commission was held on Thursday, July 11, 2024 at 1:00 p.m. in Room 111 of the Somerset County Office Complex. Members present were Charles Bagley, Vice-Chairman, Dennis Williams, SecretaryTreasurer, Maxine Landon, Member, and Barbara Somers, Member. Also in attendance were Anthony Stockus, General Manager, Richard Padgette, Superintendent, Cody Bradshaw, Assistant Superintendent, Starr Mister, Office Manager, Jennie Marshall, Senior Administrative Aide and Earl Ludy, Professional Consultant, Megan’s Playground Farm. The Crisfield (Peripheral Area) Board position is currently vacant. 

Mr. Bagley called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m. 

### Princess Anne - Information 

Mr. Stockus updated the Board regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) ENR Upgrade Project by stating the Pilot Testing Agreement/Proposal provided by Fluence (water treatment solution corporation) was reviewed and commented by SCSD’s Attorney and returned to Fluence for Attorney review and comment. Attorneys for both parties are meeting to discuss/negotiate the contract on July 16" 2024. The pilot study is planned to begin by mid-February 2024. 

Mr. Stockus stated the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) has released a draft of the FFY 2024 Intended Use Plan for the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF). The Princess Anne Color Removal Project ranked 5" on the priority scale, funding $5,090,200 of the requested $6,590,000 (leaving a shortage of $1.5 million). The funding package includes DWSRF base loan of $750,000, BIL (Bipartisan Infrastructure Law) general loan of $1,795,100, and principal forgiveness of $2,545,100. MDE’s comment period for the draft ends on August 7"" 2024. MDE’s official notification of SRF funding to these projects will be made in Fall 2024. In the meantime, the SCSD will follow up with MDE thanking them for this funding opportunity and asking that the District be considered for the Drinking Water Supply Grant to bridge the gap in fully funding this project. 

Mr. Ludy updated the Commission regarding the Princess Anne Wastewater Treatment Plant Lab Project stating the Lab will be ready for operation once the painters contracted are available to complete the interior. The Lab is expected to be online by mid-August 2024. 

During the Commission meeting on June 20" 2024, Mr. Stockus informed the Board, on June 1, 2024 the Princess Anne Police Department received a call stating the resident at 29996 Deanwood Road was filling his swimming pool using a fire hydrant. Officer Strong of the Princess Anne Police Department was dispatched to the scene. He spoke with the owner, Steve Mason, and informed him he needed to contact SCSD when filling his pool. Our on-call Field Technician was also dispatched to the scene and informed Mr. Mason he

Fairmount/Rumbley — Action 

Request to use property for hunting 

During the Commission meeting on June 20" 2024 Mr. Stockus stated he received a request from Robert Strausser, asking permission to hunt on 26760 Rumbley Road which abuts his property. This location, owned by SCSD, is where the Rumbley well house sits. Management stated their concerns with liability/insurance and setting a precedence. Mr. Bagley suggested the District confirm with their insurance provider whether there would be any insurance limitations that would prohibit hunting on this property. The Commission tabled Mr. Strausser’s request for further investigation. 

Since the June Commission meeting Mr. Stockus reached out to SCSD’s insurance Agent to inquire whether there are any limitations applicable that would prohibit hunting on District/County property. The Agent stated they do not permit coverage for hunting and
