# Somerset County: By-Right Playbook

Keys: ZO = Zoning Ordinance (Ord. #1212, printed page numbers); Res 1218 = P&Z fee schedule eff. 2026-01-27; App = Subdivision Application 2025; CA Ord. = Critical Area Ordinance 05-2026; FCO draft = Forest Conservation Ordinance 2026 UPDATE DRAFT_v2; Comm digest / PC digest / BZA digest = `01. Digests/`; [MD] = `01. Maryland/00. Summary.md`. Everything marked **(estimate)** or **(not in corpus)** must be confirmed with DTCS (410-651-1424).

**Biggest caveat:** the ZO defers all plat procedure to a separate "County Subdivision Ordinance" [ZO p.4 (contents note); ZO § 9 site-plan text; ZO § 6.10 PUD text], which we could not find online or in the corpus. Everything about who decides, notice and timing is therefore evidence-based inference, not code text.

## 1. Bottom line

The path is the **Minor Subdivision (1 to 7 new lots)** [App p.1; Res 1218 p.3], which also equals Somerset's SB 236 septic "minor subdivision" number of 7 [MD §2]. Major (8+) goes sketch, preliminary, final through the Planning Commission (PC) [App p.1; PC digest 2025-02-06, 2025-05-01]. Who formally approves a minor is **not in the corpus**: DTCS staff take the application and fee, and the plat is then signed by the Sanitary Commission chairman (if sewer or EDUs are involved) [Comm digest 2024-01-11, 2025-10-09]; no pure 1 to 7 lot minor on existing frontage appears on a PC agenda in the 76-item PC digest, which suggests staff-level approval. **Public notice: none found.** No adjoining-owner letter, sign or published-notice rule for subdivisions appears anywhere in the corpus (the ZO notice rules cover variances, special exceptions and rezonings, not plats). Typical time to recorded plat is roughly **4 months (estimate; range 2 to 8)**, driven by perc season and Sanitary Commission/EDU timing. Planning-level county fees for a 3-lot minor are about **$500 to $2,600** depending on whether stormwater plan review applies.

## 2. Eligibility test

A parcel stays on the path only if all of these pass:

- [ ] **Zoning:** AR (1 ac, 100 ft width and depth), CO (5 ac, 200 ft; not useful), R-1 (20,000 sf, 85 ft), R-2 (10,000 sf, 80 ft), R-3 or MRC (8,000 sf, 80 ft) [ZO § 6.2 p.68; § 5.2 p.14]. These minimums assume public water and sewer; otherwise Health Department and County Water and Sewerage Plan standards control (septic lots in practice need more than the table) [ZO § 6.1.f p.66].
- [ ] **Density:** no per-acre cap in AR beyond lot size [ZO § 6.2]. Real limits are perc, tiers and Critical Area.
- [ ] **Lot count:** 7 or fewer new lots in total [App p.1; MD §2]. The county's Tier IV rule: minor subdivision on septic only, and no further subdivision of the minor or its remainder [Env. §9-206(l), MD §2]. Cap counted from: **not in corpus**; assume the original tract as of the county's 2012 SB 236 adoption date (estimate, confirm). The plat must carry the remaining-rights note.
- [ ] **Arterial frontage:** a new lot with its own driveway onto an existing arterial needs **200 ft** of width unless it shares access or fronts an interior/service road [ZO § 6.1.c p.66].
- [ ] **Existing road frontage, no new road:** the comp plan says subdivisions on minor collector or local streets with more than 2 lots need an internal road unless each lot has **600 ft of frontage** [2025 CompPlan p.234]. Treat this as the design rule: 3+ lots on a collector/local road need 600 ft each, or you trigger a road. A private 30 ft road to County Road Standards went to the PC for the Thornton 4-lot preliminary [PC digest 2024-05-02].
- [ ] **Flag/panhandle lots:** code requires 100 ft of road frontage; a 50 ft flag-lot access needed a BZA variance (granted with conditions) [BZA digest 2026-03-20]. Design every lot to 100 ft of frontage.
- [ ] **Septic tier:** tiers adopted 2012, no Tier IV exemption [MD §2]. Minor on septic is allowed in Tiers II to IV; the 2012 map may be revised toward Tier IV [PC digest 2025-07-10].
- [ ] **Sewer lots:** if the lot uses public sewer, a Sanitary District EDU must be available and approved (Crisfield's approval for Crisfield-area lots) [Comm digest 2023-03-09, 2023-04-13].
- [ ] **Critical Area (CA-1, about 42% of county):** RCA is 1 unit per 20 ac, no variance; 200 ft Buffer for new subdivisions on tidal water; 100 ft Buffer otherwise; plat records a protective easement and buffer planting [CA Ord. p.58, 103, 110, 114; MD §3]. LDA and IDA need the CA review fee and buffer plan. RCA parcels go to the Critical Area Commission [MD §3]. Intrafamily transfers: parcels of record 3/1/1986, 7 to under 12 ac get 2 lots, 12 to under 60 ac get 3 lots, DTCS Director approves [CA Ord. p.87-88].
- [ ] **AR plat note:** right-to-farm note on the plat and deed for each new AR lot [ZO § 6.1.g p.66-67]. Poultry setback 200 ft from adjacent poultry structure [ZO § 6.1.d p.66].
- [ ] **No MALPF/easement ground** without state owner/child lot limits [MD §8].
- [ ] **Not a PUD, cluster or TDR deal.** Cluster (20+ ac) and TDR run through the PC and subdivision-plan approval [ZO § 6.10 p.72-73; § 8.16 p.108-110].

## 3. Step-by-step path

All durations are **estimates** unless cited; no review-timing text exists in the corpus.

1. **Pre-application call to DTCS** (Dept. of Technical and Community Services, 11916 Somerset Ave, Suite 211, Princess Anne; 410-651-1424). Ask the Section 7 questions. Fee $0. 1 to 2 weeks.
2. **Tier and Critical Area screen** (us): check `Documents/Code Related/TierMap.pdf`, CA-1 overlay, flood zone, nontidal wetlands. Free; 1 week.
3. **Boundary survey and lot layout** (licensed surveyor). Design each lot with 100 ft frontage, no new road, 600 ft per lot if on collector/local road with 3+ lots. $ consultant cost. 3 to 6 weeks.
4. **Perc/soils testing** (Somerset County Health Department, with engineer/soil scientist). Each lot needs a 10,000 sf area for initial plus 2 replacement systems [MD §2; COMAR 26.04.02.04F]. **Season:** high-water-table soils must be tested when the water table is highest, usually Feb to Apr on the Shore [COMAR 26.04.02.04B; MD §1]. Somerset-specific season and fees: **not in corpus**; county raised septic fees Jan 2025 [Comm digest 2025-01-28]. Start by January. 4 to 10 weeks including Health Department scheduling.
5. **Forest Stand Delineation / forest conservation** (DTCS; Forest Conservation Ordinance). Applies at 40,000 sf or more of subdivision area [FCO draft p.19; MD §4]. Minor developments pay $200 (simplified FSD/FCP); declaration of intent for an exemption is $40 per project [Res 1218 p.3]. The draft ordinance would exempt "minor developments" meeting its Section 8.1 (under 5 acres, not more than 4 lots per acre) from FCP submittals [FCO draft Art. VIII, p.58]; adoption **not in corpus**. State reforestation is 1:1 as of 2026-07-01 [MD §4]. 2 to 4 weeks.
6. **Wetland delineation / stormwater (ESD) concept** (engineer). Stormwater plan fee is $2,000 + $25 per lot (2 reviews); waiver review $200 [Res 1218 p.3]. The state threshold is 5,000 sf of disturbance [MD §5]; the county's own threshold and whether a lots-only plat triggers a plan are **not in corpus**. Ask for a waiver at the plat stage; submit the full SWM plan only if staff require it. 3 to 8 weeks.
7. **Submit Subdivision Application** to DTCS: owner and applicant signatures, tax map/parcel/deed reference, plat sheets, fee $225 + $25 per new lot [App p.1; Res 1218 p.3]. Add CA review $150 if in Critical Area [Res 1218 p.4]. Staff review: **estimate** 3 to 6 weeks, plus resubmittals.
8. **Sanitary Commission / EDU step (sewer lots only).** Chairman signs the plat after EDU approval; minor subdivisions typically add 1 to 2 EDUs; reservation fee is 10% of EDUs requested [Comm digest 2024-01-11, 2025-10-09; Fees: Exhibit 1 FY27 p.2]. Precedent timing: Crisfield Highway minor tabled 2023-03-09 for a Crisfield 1-EDU approval, signed 2023-04-13 (about 5 weeks); Thornton phone-polled 2023-11-14, final signature 2024-01-11 (about 8 weeks) [Comm digest]. The Commission meets monthly, so plan a one-meeting delay. Septic-only lots skip this step.
9. **Health Department plat signature** (MDE sign-off by county health department) [MD §2; COMAR 26.04.03.02K]. 1 to 3 weeks after perc approval.
10. **Critical Area Commission comment** (RCA subdivisions only) [MD §3]. Adds an estimated 3 to 6 weeks.
11. **Staff sign-off and plat signatures** (DTCS / Planning). For lots the plat is not recorded until required county department approvals are obtained [PC digest 2025-05-01 conditions]. 1 to 3 weeks.
12. **Record the plat** at the Somerset County Circuit Court land records (plat cabinet/liber; fee follows Clerk of Court schedule [Res 1218 p.3]; amount not in corpus). The BZA digest shows a typical condition that plats be recorded within 1 year [BZA digest 2026-03-20]; confirm the minor-subdivision approval expiry.
13. **Soil Conservation District** sediment control is needed at building stage (not for a plat-only deal); we do not pull permits.

## 4. Public notice

- **Subdivision plats (minor):** no notice rule found. ZO notice provisions (Section 9 "Public Notices") apply to variances, special exceptions, appeals and rezonings, with mailed notice and 21-day administrative-variance notice [ZO § 9.2 p.119-120; BZA packets]. The Commissioners advertise rezonings [Comm digest 2026-01-27; Res 1218 p.2: rezoning $600 + advertising].
- **PC agenda:** majors and PUD final plats go to the PC with a 10-day pre-meeting submittal and a 30-day PC response [ZO § 6.10 p.88]. Minors appear to avoid the PC (inference from PC digest; Thornton 4-lot with a private road did appear [PC digest 2024-05-02]).
- **Variant that avoids notice:** keep to 7 lots, 100 ft frontage per lot, no variance, no new road, no rezoning. Any BZA variance triggers advertised notice and hearing [BZA digest]. The corpus cannot rule out a staff-practice notice (such as adjoining-owner letters); ask (Section 7).

## 6. Off-ramps

| Trigger | Result | Design around it |
|---|---|---|
| 8 or more lots | Major: sketch, preliminary, final at PC [App p.1]; on septic needs Tier III hearing [MD §2] | Stay at 7 total from the original tract |
| New road, or 3+ lots on collector/local road without 600 ft each | Internal road; PC review (Thornton 4-lot example) [2025 CompPlan p.234; PC digest 2024-05-02] | 100 ft of frontage minimum, 600 ft per lot if 3+ lots |
| Flag lot with less than 100 ft frontage | BZA variance, advertised hearing [BZA digest 2026-03-20] | Widen panhandle |
| Arterial driveway per lot | 200 ft width rule [ZO § 6.1.c] | Shared driveway or interior access |
| Any variance (lot size, width, setbacks) | BZA hearing; 61 of 64 granted 2019 to 2026 but adds notice and time [BZA digest] | Size lots to the table |
| Rezoning (for example R-2 to AR) | Commissioners/PC hearing [Res 1218; PC digest 2025-07-10] | Buy parcels already zoned correctly |
| Special exception | Board of Appeals hearing [ZO § 9] | Single-family only |
| Critical Area RCA beyond 1/20 ac, or growth allocation | No variance in RCA; growth allocation is discretionary [CA Ord. p.58, 71] | Avoid RCA; use LDA or outside CA |
| W&S Plan amendment or sewer extension | County plan amendment; MDE approval [MD §7] | Use septic or existing mains with EDUs |
| EDU unavailable at Sanitary District | Delay or denial [Comm digest] | Confirm EDUs before contract |
| Perc failure | Lot count reduces | Option contract contingent on perc |
| Tier remapping to Tier IV, or zoning rewrite | Rule change risk [PC digest 2025-07-10; Comm digest 2026-09-08] | Close within the current rules |
| Pending Aug 2026 zoning amendments and 2026 forest ordinance | Not yet confirmed adopted [Comm digest 2026-09-08; FCO draft] | Re-check before filing |
| MALPF/easement land | State lot limits [MD §8] | Check SDAT/MALPF first |
| Floodplain parcel | Separate floodplain ordinance (not in corpus) [ZO p.4] | Review FIRM |

## 7. Open questions to ask staff

1. **Who approves a minor subdivision** (Planning Director/DTCS Director vs. Planning Commission), and is there a PC agenda, sketch step or technical review for 1 to 7 lots?
2. **Is any notice given** (adjoining-owner letters, sign or advertisement) for a minor subdivision? Please send the Subdivision Ordinance.
3. **Cumulative cap:** is the 7-lot count from the original tract, from a specific date, and how are remainder lands and prior lots counted?
4. **Stormwater and forest:** at what disturbance size is a stormwater plan required for a plat-only minor, are waivers routine, and is the 2026 Forest Conservation Ordinance adopted (and does the minor-development exemption apply)?
5. **Timing and expiry:** realistic review times, approval expiry before recording, and Health Department perc scheduling and season in Somerset.
6. **Frontage rules:** does the 600 ft per lot (comp plan) apply as code, can lots share a driveway, and what is the minimum panhandle width (100 ft per code)?
