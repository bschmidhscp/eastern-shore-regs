President Breeding introduced the Board, and staff members seated at the table. He then turned the attention to County Attorney, Stewart Barroll, to formally read the public hearing advertisements into the record: Mr. Barroll stated that the following resolutions were introduced and held the first reading on Tuesday, February 6<sup>th</sup> , 2024. Advertisement for all the resolutions were published in the Star Democrat on February 11<sup>th</sup> and February 18<sup>th</sup> . 

On motion by Commissioner Porter, seconded by Commissioner Bartz, the County Commissioners voted to enter **Legislative Session and convene as the Board of Health.** The public hearings then opened for the following resolutions. 

Mr. Barroll stated that the Planning Commission received all four resolutions and sent the following letter in response _: The Planning Commission discussed the above Resolutions at their February 14_<sup>_th_</sup> _meeting . The Planning Commission unanimously agrees that adopting these moratoria supports and strengthens principle of Caroline County’s Comprehensive Water & Sewerage Plan (1992) and Comprehensive Plan (2010). The end goal of each moratorium is to protect surface and ground water supplies, maintain healthy air, water, and landscapes, and apply regulatory standards that accommodate the County’s diverse land uses. Taken together, these resolutions will help to insure environmental protection and quality of life for citizens. Following through discussion of each resolution, the Planning Commission recommended to the Caroline County Commissioners that the proposed resolution be adopted. In addition, the Planning Commission recommended that the County Commissioners enact Resolution #2024-003 DAF Moratorium Extension, 2024-007 DAF Moratorium Amended, 2024-004 Bio Solid Moratorium, 2024-005 Vegetable Processing Moratorium, and 2024-006 Livestock Manure Pit Moratorium as emergency ordinances to expedite the legislation implementation._

**Resolution #2024-004, Temporary Biosolids Moratorium** : This temporary moratorium halts the use of land or structures from storing biosolids in Caroline County until November 1, 2024. Biosolids being defined as treated sewage sludge that meets the standards for Class A or B sewage sludge. As defined, Biosolids do not mean grit or screenings collected at a wastewater treatment plan or ash generated by incineration of sewage sludge

**_Moratorium established_ .** A temporary moratorium in Caroline County prohibiting: the storage of any material or substance other than livestock manure in a structure previously designed and/or permitted for the storage of livestock manure; and the use of land or structures to store on a lot, as defined in §175-8 B. of Chapter 175 Zoning, of the Code of Public Local Laws of Caroline County, livestock manure which was not generated on the lot, shall be and is hereby established until November 1, 2024, from and after the effective date of this Resolution. 

**_Effective date_ .** This temporary moratorium shall be effective 10 days after adoption of this Resolution.
