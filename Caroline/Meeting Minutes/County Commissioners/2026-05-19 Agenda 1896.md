Public Comment 

- Closed Session: Under Maryland Code, General Provisions Article § 3-305(b)(4), the Board will meet in closed session to consider matters related to the proposal of a business or industrial organization seeking to expand or locate within Caroline County, and to discuss matters directly related to the negotiation, sale, or bidding process involving County property. 

Pursuant to § 3-305(b)(14), the Board will discuss the contents of and negotiation strategy related to a proposed contract before it is executed or awarded. 

Pursuant to § 3-305(b)(7), the Board will also meet in closed session to obtain legal advice regarding these matters. 

Additionally, pursuant to § 3-305(b)(13), the Board will discuss matters involving employee health information, the public disclosure of which is prohibited by law.
