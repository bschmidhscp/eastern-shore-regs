- Public Hearings: Public comment will be accepted in written format in advance of the meeting or by verbal testimony (when appropriate) at the meeting. Written comment may be submitted via USPS at 403 South 7th Street, Suite 210, Denton, MD 21629 or emailed to gboags@carolinemd.org.
