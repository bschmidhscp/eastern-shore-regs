# Planning Commission agenda 2023-11-08

<!-- page 1 -->
# **_Caroline County Planning Commission_** 



Hannah Cawley, Chairman Jeff Jackson, Vice-Chairman J. Travis Breeding, Commissioner Member Keith Bilbrough, Member Roger McKnight, Member 

Health & Public Services Bldg. 403 South 7<sup>th</sup> Street, Suite 210 Denton, Maryland 21629-1335 Telephone: 410-479-8100 Facsimile: 410-479-4187 

The Caroline County Planning Commission will hold its regular meeting on **November 8, 2023,** at **6:00 pm** . Meetings are held in the Health & Public Services Building, 403 South 7<sup>th</sup> Street, Room 111, Denton, MD.  Interested persons may also attend using video-conferencing link and call-in options (see meeting access instructions below). 

## **Agenda will include:** 

- Approval of the October 11, 2023 Meeting Minutes 

- <u>Review of Rules of Procedure</u> – Leslie Grunden 

- <u>Solar Energy System Legislative Bill</u> - Matthew Kaczynski 

## **Information and Discussion:** 

- Land Use Element Goals & Objectives – Leslie Grunden 

- BZA Update 

- Department Update 

- Planning Commissioners Open Discussion 

## **To access the meeting:** 

   - Click on the URL link: <u>https://www.carolinemd.org/667/Live-Meetings</u> Select _Planning Commission_ 

- Public Hearings:  Public comment will be accepted in written format in advance of the meeting or by verbal testimony (when appropriate) at the meeting.  Written comment may be submitted via USPS at 403 South 7<sup>th</sup> Street, Suite 210, Denton, MD 21629 or emailed to <u>cmcculley@carolinemd.org.  Written comments received by</u> the close of business the Friday prior to a meeting will be entered into the record and made available to the Board members prior to the meeting or hearing. 

- All speakers, including Board and staff members, must announce their name each time before they speak.  This means before asking a question or making a statement, the person must state their last name. 

**_Meetings are conducted in Open Session unless otherwise indicated.  All or part of the meetings can be held in closed session under the authority of the state open meetings law by vote of the Board members.  Breaks are at the call of the Boards Chairman.  Please note that the order of agenda items may change as time dictates or allows._**
