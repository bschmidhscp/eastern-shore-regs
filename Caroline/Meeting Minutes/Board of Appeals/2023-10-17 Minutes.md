Mr. Kaczynski reviewed the staff report: 

The applicant, Castle Hall 1781, LLC, has requested a Special Use Exception Approval for a Rural Special Event Venue. The property is located on Tax Map 8, Grid 20, Parcel 146, also known as 16070 Henderson Road, Goldsboro. 

Robert and Lisa Moser purchased the property in 2017, It consists of an existing dwelling and various accessory structures which are currently used for storage. The Moser’s, operating under the business name Castle Hall 1781, LLC, have plans to use the existing dwelling and the remainder of the property to host various types of events, which include weddings, family reunions, bridal and baby showers, as well as corporate events. The existing dwelling on-site is a historic home known as “Castle Hall” which was constructed in 1781, is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. The events to be held onsite are proposed to handle up to 200 guests, as outlined in the Event Management Plan. Onsite staff members will be provided to help coordinate any scheduled events. Outside staffing may also be provided depending on the type of event and/or number of guests, which may include event planners, catering staff, or even valet parking personnel. All parking for events is planned to be held onsite within a grassed parking area shown on the site plan, toward the rear of the property. Temporary restrooms will be brought on-site for events and then removed upon conclusion of the event. 

The proposed use of a Rural Special Event Venue requires a Site Plan Approval and a Special Use Exception Approval. Upon review, Staff has determined that the proposed use required a Minor Site Plan, therefore, any decisions regarding the minor site plan application shall be made by the Planning Director. If the Board of Zoning Appeals grants the Special Use Exception Application, the applicant will make any recommended changes and resubmit the revised site plan to the Planning Director for review and approval. 

Ms. Hardy administered the oath to Robert Moser (16070 Henderson Road, Goldsboro, MD). 

Mr. Moser told the Board that he and his wife wanted to make Castle Hall available for guests to use for a reasonable price. He noted that many similar places charge enormous fees. They also wanted guests to understanda bit about Castle Hall’s history. He noted that

their careful preparation for the application included considering the possible decibel levels of noise that might be heard by neighbors. No neighbors voiced opposition to the application. The Special Event Venue is their first priority. In the future they may consider building a pavilion. 

Mr. Moser submitted written comments (Applicant Exhibit 1, § 175- 141. Conditions for Approval). He read the exhibit to the Board and paused to answer any questions. Mr. Butler asked about the specific acreage of the property; Mr. Moser answered 5.73. 

The applicant used the projected site plan to show the entry from Henderson Road, the recently added garage, and the area delineated for parking as well as specific parking (2 closer to the house and 3 in larger parking area closest to the access point) for the handicapped. He pointed out the area for refuse and two storage sheds. He indicated the original portion of the house which dated back to 1781. The septic drain fields on the right side of the property would remain undeveloped. Three round concrete pads near the back of the property marked where grain silos once existed. He told the Board the project had been slowed down by Covid. 

Chair Hardy pointed out to Mr. Moser that the setback for a Special Use Exception was 150 feet from the property line. She wondered how this might affect his planning. She was concerned about event setup and whether that could accommodate the 150-foot setback. Mr. Moser did not feel that would be a problem. 

Ms. Hardy asked if members had any questions. Mr. Butler asked how large the wedding parties would be. Mr. Moser referred to the Business plan which listed 200 as the maximum size, but he thought 150 would be more likely. Ms. Hardy said that 200 sounded sensible for the property to accommodate. Mr. Moser commented that a neighbor across the street (15945 Henderson Road) had a golf cart business, and if additional parking was needed event participants could park there and be shuttled to Castle Hall. 

Board members agreed that Caroline County was in need of additional event venues. 

Chair Hardy observed that there was no one either in the audience or viewing virtually to comment.

§175-142 A(2)) A duly advertised public hearing has been held as prescribed by § 175179 of this chapter. The Notice of Public Hearing (Exhibit 1) was advertised on 10/04/23 and 10/11/23. 

§175-142 A(3)(a) Will not be detrimental to or endanger the public health, safety or general welfare. Mr. Mann cited Mr. Moser’s testimony indicating neither would be an issue. Ms. Hardy added that the hours of operation would not be going late into the night and that the closest neighbor was a % mile distant. 

§175-142 A(3)(b) Will not be injurious to the peaceful use and enjoyment of other property in the neighborhood and will not substantially diminish or impair property values in the neighborhood. Mr. Mann mentioned Mr. Moser’s concern that the noise level would not disturb his neighbors. Ms. Hardy stated that Mr. Moser’s restoration of Castle Hall would likely increase property values. 

§175-142 A(3)(c) Will not impede the normal and orderly development and improvement of the surrounding property for uses permitted in the zoning district. The Board concluded the project would not impede any development or improvement of nearby properties. 

§175-142 A(3)(d) Will not overburden existing public facilities, including schools, police and fire protection, water and sewerage, public roads, storm drainage and other public improvements. Ms. Hardy noted that temporary facilities would be provided at the events, so it would not overburden water and sewerage. 

§175-142 A(3)(e) Conforms in all other respects to this chapter and especially to the applicable regulations of the zoning district in which it is to be located. The Board confirmed the project complied with all aspects of zoning regulations. Chair Hardy believed that having Castle Hall as a Rural Event Venue would be an asset for the community. 

§175-142 A(3)(f) Will not adversely affect water quality or adversely impact fish, wildlife, or plant habitat within the critical area and that the granting of the special use exception will be in accordance with the "Critical Area Program for Caroline County," the critical area law and this chapter. The Board noted the property was not within the Critical Area. 

Ms. Hardy referred to Mr. Moser’s complete responses to §175-141 Conditions for Approval for a Special Use Exception. She reiterated Mr. Moser’s statement that he would not have a problem meeting the 150-foot minimum zoning setback (stipulated in §175-47 M).

Mr. Kaczynski recommended the Board consider whether they wanted to require the parking be screened from Henderson Road or neighbors. Mr. Mann asked that they view the aerial photograph. With the aerial photograph projected, Mr. Moser described what trees and bushes were currently in place that might provide screening. The Board determined that given the location of the parking lot (in the back), the distance from the road and neighbors (% distant), and the screening currently in place, that they would not require any additional screening. 

Referring to the Business Plan (Exhibit 4), Mr. Kaczynski referred to the mention of “multiday” events. Mr. Moser explained to the Board that if a wedding were taking place at Castle Hall, they would allow the bride and groom to spend the night after the wedding. They may also allow people setting up the night before to spend the night. The site would not be used for multi-day music festivals. 

Mr. Mann advised that should Mr. Moser want to serve alcohol; he must get a liquor license. However, some wineries and breweries have licenses to dispense their wines or beers on the property. 

Motion: Mr. Mann made a motion to approve the Special Event Venue for Castle Hall 1781 LLC, Application Number 23-0019, with the following conditions: 

- The Applicant must receive Final Site Plan Approval from the Planning Director prior to the issuance of any Building Permit and/or Zoning Certificate associated with the project. 

- A Building Permit and/or Zoning Certificate will be required prior to any proposed development and must be accompanied by all the necessary documentation and site plan requirements. 

- The Board’s decision shall be void one year from the date of approval unless a plat is recorded or a zoning certificate and/or building permit is issued and construction has begun. 

Second: Ms. Hardy seconded the motion. 

Vote: The vote was unanimous (3-0). 

The meeting adjourned at 6:55 pm.
