# § 49-2. Definitions.

As used in this Article, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**EMPLOYEE** An employee of the government of Caroline County.

**EMPLOYER** The government of Caroline County.

**PERSONNEL SYSTEM** The Caroline County Government Personnel System.
