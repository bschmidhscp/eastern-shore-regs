# § 89-2. Jurisdiction; applicability.

The provisions of this chapter shall apply in the unincorporated areas of Caroline County, Maryland. The governing body of any municipality may request application of this chapter within a municipality's corporate limits by submitting a duly adopted written request to the County Commissioners. Any agreement to apply this chapter within a municipality shall include provisions for coordinating the issuance of permits and plan approvals for development.
