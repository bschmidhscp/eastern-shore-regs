# § 62-7. Notification of annual tax levy required; collection, deposit and payment procedures generally.

A. Notice requirements. Important notices on the back of each tax bill and an insert included with the tax levy explains that taxes levied shall be due and payable on the first day of July of the year in which they are levied. The due dates for both annual and semi-annual payments in compliance with state law are stated.

B. Weekly report. The Director of Finance shall report to the County Commissioners weekly, the total taxes and cash receipts received, the expenditures made, and all the outstanding general fund balances in bank accounts.

C. The Finance Director will collect, receipt and remit taxes per § 4-201 of the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.
