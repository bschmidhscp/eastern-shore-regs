# § 75-5. Distribution of compensatory preservation contributions.

A. The Department shall remit all Contributions collected under this chapter to the Director of Finance.

B. The Director of Finance shall deposit all such Contributions into the Agricultural Land Preservation Fund for Caroline County, as maintained pursuant to § 75-2 of this chapter.
