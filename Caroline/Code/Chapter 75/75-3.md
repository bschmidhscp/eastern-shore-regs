# § 75-3. When compensatory preservation contribution is required.

A. Applicability. A Compensatory Preservation Contribution shall be required when any portion of the Project Area of a solar energy generating station/system or energy storage device is Vital Farmland.

B. Collection. The Department shall determine the amount of the required Compensatory Preservation Contribution at the time of final site plan approval, and it shall be collected prior to issuance of a building permit.
