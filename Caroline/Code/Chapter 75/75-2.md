# § 75-2. Creation of Agricultural Land Preservation Fund.

A. All Compensatory Preservation Contributions for agricultural land preservation collected by the County shall be deposited in a special account for the County Commissioners known as the "Agricultural Land Preservation Fund for Caroline County."

B. Money in the Agricultural Land Preservation Fund for Caroline County may only be used for the purchase of development rights on agricultural land in Caroline County through the purchase of agricultural land preservation easements, including, but not limited to, those by the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation pursuant to the provisions of Title 2, Subtitle 5, of the Agriculture Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

C. Money in the Agricultural Land Preservation Fund for Caroline County may only be used with the written approval of the County Commissioners of Caroline County.
