# § 98-1. Legislative authority; purpose; title.

This chapter is enacted pursuant to the authority contained in Article 25, § 3(t)(1) and (3), of the Annotated Code of Maryland. This chapter is enacted for the protection and the promotion of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Caroline County. This chapter shall be known as the "Caroline County Electrical Code."
