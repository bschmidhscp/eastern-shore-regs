# § 98-26. Shelved licenses.

*[Amended 2-8-2005 by Bill No. 2005-1 ; 6-12-2007 by Ord. No. 2007-3 ]*

A. Any master electrician, general electrician or limited electrician may shelve his or her license in inactive status. Thereupon, the licensee may not engage in any electrical work governed by this chapter.

B. A shelved license shall be reactivated upon written request by the licensee to the Board and payment of the ordinary renewal license fee for the applicable class.

C. A shelved license must be activated every two renewal periods, during the month of June, by application for renewal and payment of an inactive license fee, except for inspectors of approved inspection agencies in Caroline County. A shelved license shall become null and void if not so renewed.

D. The insurance required by the provisions of § 98-22 of this chapter need not be kept in force while a license is shelved.
