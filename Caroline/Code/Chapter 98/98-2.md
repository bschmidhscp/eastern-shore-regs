# § 98-2. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings indicated:

**BOARD** The Constructions Trade Board.[Amended 9-11-2012 by Bill No. 2012-6]

**CODE** The Caroline County Electrical Code.

**COUNTY COMMISSIONERS** The County Commissioners of Caroline County.

**ELECTRICAL CODE** The edition of the “National Electrical Code” published by the National Fire Protection Association as adopted by resolution of the County Commissioners.[Amended 2-8-2005 by Bill. No. 2005-1]

**ELECTRICAL WORK** Installing, erecting and repairing electrical wire or conductors, to be used for the transmission of electric current for electric light, heat or power purposes, and/or mouldings, ducts, raceways or conduits, for the reception or protection of such wires or conductors, and/or electrical machinery, apparatus, devices or fixtures, to be used for electrical light, heat or power purposes, or planning, estimating, laying out and supervising such electrical work or contracting such electrical work.

**ELECTRICIAN**

**SHELVED LICENSE** A license that is inactive, but can be reactivated by applying for renewal and paying the renewal license fee for the applicable class.[Added 6-12-2007 by Ord. No. 2007-3]
