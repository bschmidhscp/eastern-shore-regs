# § 74-4. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following words and phrases shall be defined as follows:

**ADVISORY BOARD** The Caroline County Agricultural Preservation Advisory Board.

**COUNTY COMMISSIONERS** The County Commissioners of Caroline County.

**FOUNDATION** The Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation.

**PLANNING COMMISSION** The Caroline County Planning Commission.
