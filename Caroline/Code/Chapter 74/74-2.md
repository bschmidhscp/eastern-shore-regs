# § 74-2. Purpose.

This chapter is adopted for the following purposes:

A. To provide for the preservation of agricultural lands through the voluntary establishment of agricultural preservation districts and for the voluntary sale of development rights easements on eligible agricultural lands within Caroline County.

B. To provide for the protection of normal agricultural activities within established agricultural preservation districts.

C. To provide procedures for the creation and recording of Official Agricultural Preservation District Maps.

D. To protect and provide for the public health, safety and general welfare of the citizens of Caroline County.
