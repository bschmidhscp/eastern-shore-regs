# § 103-9. Modifications to erosion and sediment control plans.

A. The Soil Conservation District may revise approved plans as necessary. Modifications may be requested by the owner/developer, the inspection agency, or the Planning and Codes Department in accordance with COMAR 26.17.01.09(H), Plan Modifications.

B. The Soil Conservation District may develop a list of minor modifications that may be approved as field revisions by the inspection agency. The Administration must approve any list of minor modifications prior to its implementation.
