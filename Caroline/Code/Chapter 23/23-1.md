# § 23-1. Creation; purpose.

A. There is hereby created a Department of Correction (the "Department") under the immediate direction of a Superintendent who shall be appointed by the County Commissioners.

B. The Department shall operate all programs pertaining to detention of persons under the jurisdiction of the County government who are awaiting trial, who have been convicted of a crime and sentenced by a court of competent jurisdiction for a term in the Caroline County Detention Center or who are being held for any other lawful purpose.
