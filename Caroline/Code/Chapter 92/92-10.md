# § 92-10. Other proceedings by County.

A. In addition to all other remedies provided by state or county law, the County may institute any other appropriate action or proceedings to enforce any provision of the County Code.

B. The imposition of a civil monetary fine or the payment thereof shall not in any way impede the authority of the County to utilize any action or proceedings to enforce any provision of the County Code.
