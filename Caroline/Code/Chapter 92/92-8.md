# § 92-8. Failure to file notice of intent to stand trial; failure to pay fine.

A. If a defendant fails to file a notice of intent to stand trial within the time required and fails to pay the fine for the infraction by the date indicated in the citation, the County shall send a formal notice of the infraction to the defendant's last known address.

B. If the County determines that the citation has not been satisfied within 15 days from the date of the notice, the defendant will be liable for an additional fine which shall be twice the amount of the original fine.

C. If the citation has not been satisfied within 35 days the County may request adjudication of the case through the District Court, including the filing of a demand for judgment on affidavit.
