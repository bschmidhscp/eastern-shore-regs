# § 92-2. Types of violations deemed civil infractions.

A. Any person who violates any provision of the Caroline County Code subject to civil fine or penalty shall, upon citation issued pursuant to this chapter, be deemed to have committed a civil infraction and shall pay to the County a civil fine in the amount prescribed.

B. Beginning with the day the citation is issued, each separate day of violation shall constitute a separate and distinct civil infraction, and each such infraction shall be subject to citation and fine.
