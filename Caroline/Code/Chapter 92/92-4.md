# § 92-4. Issuance of citations.

A. The County Commissioners shall designate one or more enforcement officials who are authorized to issue citations to those persons whom the official determines to have committed a civil infraction.

B. Citations shall be delivered in person or mailed to the last known address of the recipient of the citation. In addition to delivery or mail, a copy of the citation may be posted on the property where the violation exists or has occurred. The enforcement official who delivers, mails or posts a citation shall make an affidavit regarding such action.[Amended 12-17-2002 by Bill No. 2002-3]

C. A copy of the citation shall be retained by the issuing official and shall bear the official's certification attesting to the truth of the matter set forth in the citation.

D. The citation shall serve as notification that the person is charged with a civil violation of the County Code and has been assessed a civil fine, which shall be due and payable to the County, subject to the person's right to stand trial in the District Court.
