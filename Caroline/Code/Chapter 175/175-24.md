# § 175-24. Swimming or tennis clubs.

The following regulations shall apply to swimming or tennis clubs:

A. Any pumps and filters which are located above ground shall be at least 50 feet from adjoining properties.

B. The dispensing of food, beverages, candy and tobacco shall be by coin-operated machines only.

C. All lights shall be shielded to reflect or direct light away from adjoining property.

D. The parking layout and surfacing shall be approved by the County Engineer.
