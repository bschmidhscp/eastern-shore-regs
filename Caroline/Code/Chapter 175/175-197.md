# § 175-197. Text amendments.

A. Initiation. Proposed Zoning Ordinance text amendments may be submitted to the Planning Commission for review by any citizen, organization, governmental agency or by the Planning Commission on its own initiative.

B. The Planning Commission shall have the opportunity, at least two regularly scheduled meetings, to review proposed text amendments and to submit proposed findings and recommendations to the County Commissioners.[Amended 6-28-2016 by Ord. No. 2016-1]

C. The County Commissioners shall adopt all changes to this chapter in accordance with the procedural requirements of the Land Use Article, Title 4, of the Annotated Code of Maryland.[Amended 6-28-2016 by Ord. No. 2016-1]
