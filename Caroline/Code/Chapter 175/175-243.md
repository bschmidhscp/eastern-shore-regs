# § 175-243. Fees.

There shall be no fee imposed by the County in connection with a request for reasonable accommodation under this article or an appeal of a determination on such request to the County Commissioners, and the County shall have no obligation to pay the attorney's fees or costs of an applicant or of an appealing party, as applicable, in connection with the request or appeal.
