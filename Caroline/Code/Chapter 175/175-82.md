# § 175-82. Fences and walls.

A. Fences and walls do not require a zoning certificate and/or building permit.

B. Fences and walls are not subject to setback requirements from adjoining lots.[Amended 8-12-2014 by Ord. No. 2014-1]

C. A fence or wall not more than five feet in height may project into or enclose any required front yard. A fence or wall not more than eight feet in height project into or enclose any required side or rear yard.

D. All residential swimming pools, spas and hot tubs must comply with the barrier requirements located in Appendix G of the International Building Code as adopted under Chapter 88.[Amended 8-12-2014 by Ord. No. 2014-1]
