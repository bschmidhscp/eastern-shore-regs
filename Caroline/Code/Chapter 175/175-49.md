# § 175-49. Warehouse, mini-storage facilities (VC Zoning District).

*[Added 3-17-2026 by Bill No. 2026-002 ]*

A. Purpose and applicability. The purpose of this section is to establish supplementary regulations for warehouse, mini-storage facilities located within the VC Zoning District. These standards are intended to ensure that such facilities are compatible with surrounding land uses, are appropriately scaled to their sites, and minimize adverse impacts on adjacent properties. Warehouse, mini-storage facilities shall be permitted in the VC Zoning District subject to approval of a special use exception and site plan approval, in accordance with the applicable provisions of this chapter.

B. Building setbacks. Minimum building setbacks for warehouse, mini-storage facilities shall be as follows:

  (1) Adjacent to residentially assessed parcels or lots.

    (a) Front setback: 10 feet.

    (b) Side setback: 10 feet.

    (c) Rear setback: 10 feet.

  (2) Adjacent to commercially or industrially assessed parcels or lots.

    (a) Front setback: 10 feet.

    (b) Side setback: five feet.

    (c) Rear setback: 10 feet.

C. Maximum building size.

  (1) For parcels or lots of one acre or less in size, the maximum gross floor area permitted for warehouse, mini-storage buildings shall be 3,000 square feet. Where multiple buildings are proposed, the combined total gross floor area of all buildings on the parcel or lot shall not exceed 3,000 square feet.

  (2) For parcels or lots greater than one acre in size, the maximum permitted gross floor area shall be determined by the Board of Zoning Appeals (BZA) as part of the special use exception approval, based upon the size of the parcel or lot, the scale and intensity of development, and the size and character of adjacent and nearby buildings.

D. Building height.

  (1) The maximum permitted building height shall be two stories.

  (2) The Board of Zoning Appeals may approve a building of up to three stories in height where it finds that the additional height is compatible with surrounding development and will not adversely impact adjacent properties.

E. Landscape buffers. Landscape buffers shall be provided along all property boundaries where a warehouse, mini-storage facility abuts another parcel or lot, as follows:

  (1) Adjacent to residentially assessed parcels or lots. A single row of evergreen trees shall be provided, with trees having a minimum height of six feet at the time of planting and spaced 10 feet on center.

  (2) Adjacent to commercially or industrially assessed parcels or lots. A single row of evergreen trees shall be provided, with trees having a minimum height of six feet at the time of planting and spaced 20 feet on center.

  All required landscaping shall be shown on the approved site plan and shall be installed and maintained in accordance with the approved plan.

F. Lighting.

  (1) Adequate lighting shall be provided for parking areas, access drives, and other areas of pedestrian or vehicular activity to ensure safety for customers and employees and to deter vandalism and theft.

  (2) All exterior lighting shall be sensor-activated where practicable and shall be designed to be dark-sky friendly, including full cut-off or downward-directed fixtures that minimize glare, light trespass, and skyglow.

G. Fencing. Where fencing is proposed, the type, height, and location of fencing shall be shown on the site plan.

  Where fencing is not proposed, the Board of Zoning Appeals shall determine, as part of the special use exception approval, whether fencing is required based on the nature of adjacent uses, site conditions, and input provided by the applicant.

H. Long-term parking storage.

  (1) Long-term outdoor storage of boats, recreational vehicles (RVs), and other vehicles may be permitted as an accessory use to a warehouse, mini-storage facility, subject to the following standards:

    (a) No more than 10% of the total parcel or lot area may be utilized for long-term parking storage.

    (b) Long-term parking storage shall be limited to the rear of the parcel or lot.

    (c) Parking spaces shall be designed to accommodate large vehicles, with a typical space size of approximately 12 feet by 50 feet, allowing for an average of approximately seven vehicles per acre.

  (2) Screening. Areas used for long-term parking storage shall be screened from adjacent parcels by two rows of evergreen trees, each having a minimum height of six feet at the time of planting and spaced 10 feet on center.

  (3) Surface and marking. All long-term parking storage areas shall be paved or graveled with a surface suitable for larger vehicles and shall be clearly marked.

  (4) Environmental and safety measures. All vehicle storage areas shall include measures to prevent spills and hazards from gasoline, oil, or other fluids, including appropriate containment methods, drainage controls, and compliance with all applicable local, state, and federal environmental regulations and safety standards.
