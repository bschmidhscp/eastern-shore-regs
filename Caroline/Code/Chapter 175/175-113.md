# § 175-113. Political signs.

A. Political signs or posters may be erected upon private property, provided that such signs:

  (1) Do not interfere with pedestrian or vehicular traffic or cause or enhance a danger thereto by interfering with reasonable sight lines and distances; and

  (2) Do not, as a result of size, construction, or placement, present a physical danger to others.

B. Political signs are not subject to application requirements or permit fees.
