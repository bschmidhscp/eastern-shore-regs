# § 175-85. Accessory ground-mounted solar power electric generation structures.

*[Added 6-28-2016 by Ord. No. 2016-1 ]*

A. Permitted. Ground-mounted solar power electric generation structures shall be permitted in all zoning districts subject to the conditions set forth in § 175-85.[2]

> Note: [2] Editor's Note: This reference was updated from "§ 175-86" to "§ 175-85" with the permission of the County.

B. Limit. Accessory ground-mounted solar panels shall be limited to producing approximately the annual average electricity needed to support the property. Where a property owner owns multiple parcels, the electricity produced in support of the property may include all parcels (e.g., multiple properties used for agricultural production where one farmer owns or leases multiple parcels, lots, or tracts of land). Supporting documentation shall be provided.

C. Site plans. A site plan drawn to scale shall be provided identifying the square footage of panels to be installed, the proposed location and spacing of solar panels, and properties/structures to be served.
