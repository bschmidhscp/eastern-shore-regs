# § 175-81. Location.

A. Except as herein provided, no accessory building shall project beyond a required setback line along any County or state road.

B. No accessory building shall be erected within any required front yard, except as provided in this chapter.

C. Unless otherwise specified in this chapter, accessory buildings or structures less than 750 square feet in floor area may be placed or erected within the side and rear building setback lines but shall be no closer than five feet to the side or rear lot lines.

D. Accessory dwellings shall be located on a premises only as specifically provided in this chapter. Accessory dwellings shall be located within the building envelope and comply with all additional setback requirements.
