# § 175-166. Meetings; quorum.

*[Amended 6-21-2022 by Bill No. 2022-002 ]*

A. Meetings of the Planning Commission shall be held once each month or at the call of the Chairman and at such other times as the Planning Commission may determine.

B. The presence of three members of the Planning Commission shall constitute a quorum for the conduct of business.

C. An affirmative vote of three members shall be required to effect a decision or recommendation of the Planning Commission.
