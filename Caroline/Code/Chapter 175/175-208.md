# § 175-208. Limitations on DRRA.

A. A DRRA may not confer a right to develop contrary to or in derogation of the applicable laws in effect at the time the DRRA is executed.

B. A DRRA may not excuse a developer from obtaining any special use exception or variance otherwise required under the Zoning Code, or any other approval required by law.
