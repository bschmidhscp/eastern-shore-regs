# § 175-216. Recording.

A. A DRRA that is not recorded in the land records of Caroline County within 20 days after the day on which the parties execute the DRRA is void.

B. The parties to a DRRA, and their successors in interest, are bound to the DRRA after the DRRA is recorded.
