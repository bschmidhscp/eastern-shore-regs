# § 175-143. Special use exceptions restricted to designated uses; applicability of additional regulations and standards.

*[Amended 10-31-2017 by Ord. No. 2017-3 ]*

A special use exception shall be granted only for those uses specifically designated in this chapter. A special use exception shall be subject to all applicable additional restrictions, regulations, design and performance standards found in this chapter, including minor or major site plan approval.
