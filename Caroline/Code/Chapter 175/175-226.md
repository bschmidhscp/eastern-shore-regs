# § 175-226. Prohibition locations.

Except as provided elsewhere in this Article XXIV, gun club activities shall not be permitted in the VC, VN, R-1, R-2, R-3, and TDR Zoning Districts. This § 175-226 does not apply to land in the R Zoning District unless in a TDR area.
