# § 175-177. Minutes and records.

A. The Board of Zoning Appeals shall keep minutes of its proceedings. The minutes shall contain the exhibits presented at the hearing, a summary of all testimony or evidence presented and the decision of the Board.

B. All applications for hearings and the minutes shall be a public record.
