# § 175-246. Revocation of reasonable accommodation.

Any reasonable accommodation received shall be deemed revoked if the applicant or the property upon which the accommodation is granted is found in violation of any provision of the order granting the reasonable accommodation by a court of law, the Planning Commission, or the County Commissioners.
