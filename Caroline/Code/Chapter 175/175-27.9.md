# § 175-27.9. Accessory uses permitted.

*[Added 4-24-2018 by Ord. No. 2018-1 ]*

The following accessory uses may be permitted on a property where an extraction facility is approved:

A. The storage, sorting, stockpiling, distribution and sale of materials extracted on the premises or any materials permitted on the property by § 175-27.9B.

B. The crushing, treating, washing, mixing or processing of materials.

C. The storage, maintenance and repair of vehicles and equipment used in the mineral extraction operations.
