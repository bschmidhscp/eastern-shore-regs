# § 175-234. Process.

A. Any moratorium adopted by the Commissioners shall be adopted by resolution and shall contain at the minimum:

  (1) The public purpose(s) that necessitate(s) adoption;

  (2) The scope of the moratorium, which will be tailored based on the public purpose(s) to be accomplished and sufficiently specific to allow implementation by the Zoning Administrator and/or other affected public bodies, agencies or officials;

  (3) General goals of the moratorium, including but not limited to study and potential adoption of new plans, ordinances, and/or regulations and/or amendments of existing plans, ordinances, and/or regulations;

  (4) The duration of the moratorium which shall not be longer than one year and shall be tailored based on the goals of the moratorium; and

  (5) The conditions, if any, under which an exception to the moratorium may be granted.

B. Upon adoption, the County Attorney shall transmit a copy of the moratorium resolution to the Planning Commission, Board of Zoning Appeals, and Zoning Administrator, the department head of any other County agency affected by the moratorium and any legal counsel serving those bodies.
