# § 175-48. Cannabis facilities.

*[Added 4-8-2025 by Bill No. 2025-001 ]*

PREAMBLE: The following provisions are an exercise by Caroline County of the authority remaining to it to regulate cannabis facilities as prescribed in §§ 36-405 and 36-410 of the Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland. The existence of cannabis facilities in Caroline County is a result of State law and was never authorized or approved by the County Commissioners of Caroline County.

A. Cannabis dispensaries.

  (1) The closest portion of a cannabis dispensary building or structure shall not be located within 500 feet from the closest boundary of a parcel containing a pre-existing educational facility, religious facility, public or nonprofit park or recreation area, playground, civic community center or service club, library, registered family day care or licensed group day-care center.

  (2) The closest portion of a cannabis dispensary building or structure shall not be located within one-half mile of another cannabis dispensary building or structure.

  (3) The closest portion of a cannabis dispensary building or structure shall not be within 100 feet of the boundary of any parcel in a Residential Zoning District (i.e., an R-1, R-2, or MH Zoning District).

B. Cannabis growers.

  (1) The closest portion of a cannabis grower building or structure shall not be located within 1,200 feet from the closest boundary of a parcel containing a pre- existing educational facility, religious facility, public or nonprofit park or recreation area, playground, civic community center or service club, library, registered family day care or licensed group day-care center.

  (2) The closest portion of a cannabis grower building or structure shall not be within 600 feet of the boundary of any parcel in a Residential Zoning District (i.e., an R-1, R-2, or MH Zoning District); provided, however, that when the distance from a cannabis building or structure is being measured with respect to a parcel containing a residential structure within a Rural (R) Zoning District, the closest portion of the cannabis building or structure shall not be less than 300 feet from the boundary of such parcel or, failing such separation, shall not be less than 600 feet from the closest portion of any building or structure used principally as a residential dwelling.

C. Cannabis processors.

  (1) The closest portion of a cannabis processor building or structure shall not be located within 1,200 feet from the closest boundary of a parcel containing a pre-existing educational facility, religious facility, public or nonprofit park or recreation area, playground, civic community center or service club, library, registered family day care or licensed group day-care center.

  (2) The closest portion of the cannabis structure shall not be within 600 feet of the boundary of any parcel in a Residential Zoning District (i.e., an R-1, R-2, or MH Zoning District); provided, however, that when the distance from a cannabis building or structure is being measured with respect to a parcel containing a residential structure within a Rural (R) Zoning District, the closest portion of the cannabis building or structure shall not be less than 300 feet from the boundary of such parcel or, failing such separation, shall not be less than 600 feet from the closest portion of any building or structure used principally as a residential dwelling.

D. The setbacks listed under Subsection A B and C are in addition to any buffer, buffer yard, setback, or other design criteria generally applicable to a use in the permitted zoning districts.

E. Application. Compliance with the requirements of § 175-48 shall be determined as of the date of submittal of a completed application for a building permit and/or zoning certificate, site plan, or special use exception and any changes to the use of adjoining or neighboring property or to the size, type, number or location of structures or buildings on adjoining or neighboring property applied for, made after the date of submittal of a completed application for a building permit and/or zoning certificate, site plan, or special use exception shall be of no effect and shall not be given any consideration in determining compliance with the requirements of § 175-48.
