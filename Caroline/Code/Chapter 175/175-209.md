# § 175-209. Applicability.

A. Any person having a legal or equitable interest in real property located within the unincorporated areas of the County may petition the County Commissioners to enter into an agreement pertaining to the development of that property.

B. The County Commissioners, in accordance with this Article XXIII of the Zoning Code, may execute a DRRA with a person described in § 175-209A of this article.

C. The County Commissioners may include a federal, state, or local government jurisdiction or unit thereof as an additional party to a DRRA.
