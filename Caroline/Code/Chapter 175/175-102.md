# § 175-102. Height restrictions for certain public and semipublic uses.

Public or semipublic service buildings, hospitals, institutions, schools, churches and temples may be erected to a height not exceeding 75 feet when the required side and rear yards are each increased by at least one foot for every foot of additional building height above the height regulations for the zoning district in which the building is located.
