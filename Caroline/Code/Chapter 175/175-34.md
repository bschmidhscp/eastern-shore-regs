# § 175-34. Agricultural and fishery products processing plants.

*[Amended 8-19-2025 by Bill No. 2025-008 ]*

When permitted in the R, Rural Zoning District, the following additional regulations for agricultural and fishery products processing plants shall apply:

A. Any flour, feed and grain packaging, blending, storage or milling shall be located at least 200 feet from all lot lines.

B. Any commercial slaughtering and processing of farm animals, fish or seafood shall be located at least 500 feet from all lot lines.

C. Any rendering of animal by-products shall be located at least 1,000 feet from all lot lines.
