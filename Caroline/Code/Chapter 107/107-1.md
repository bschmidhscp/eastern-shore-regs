# § 107-1. Annual payments to volunteer companies authorized.

A. Criteria for receipt of funds; use. The County Commissioners of Caroline County are hereby authorized and empowered to make annual payments in any amount to the volunteer fire companies of Caroline County for the purchase, repair or maintenance of fire-fighting equipment, provided that the fire companies remain active bodies and active fire-fighting organizations for the benefit of the County.

B. Distribution. Payment made pursuant to this Article shall be distributed as determined by the County Commissioners from time to time in consultation with the Budget Director and the volunteer fire companies.
