# § 107-6. Funding of Program.

A. Annual appropriation. The Length of Service Award Program shall be funded in the Caroline County annual budget in the amount necessary to pay all reasonably anticipated benefits for the fiscal year.

B. Reserve fund balance. In addition to providing funding through the annual budget, the County Commissioners shall maintain a dedicated and restricted reserve fund balance of at least $15,000.
