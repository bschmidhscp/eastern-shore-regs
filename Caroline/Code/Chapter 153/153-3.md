# § 153-3. Prohibition; exceptions.

It shall be illegal to store or keep items defined as salvage, junk or scrap on any property except:

A. An industrially zoned property where items defined as salvage, junk or scrap are by-products of or are incidental to the business operation on the premises and are stored and screened from public view.

B. Where items defined as salvage, junk or scrap are incidentally included as outside storage and not restricted by zoning regulations and are screened from public view.

C. In a licensed salvage yard.
