# § 153-4. Outside storage.

Where specifically permitted by zoning, outside storage shall be permitted, provided that:

A. All items are stored and maintained in a neat and orderly manner.

B. No outside storage in other than a licensed salvage yard shall be within 25 feet of any side property line or within 40 feet of the front property line or public roads.

C. Motor vehicles and trailers without current license tags shall not be kept in other than licensed salvage yards unless permitted by the Caroline County Zoning Ordinance.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: See Ch. 175 , Zoning.
