# § 51-1. Purpose.

A. The purpose of this chapter is to ensure that the taxpayers of Caroline County receive full return on the investment of tax dollars; that Caroline County government operates openly, efficiently, and effectively in public purchasing; that vendors and potential vendors are treated fairly; and that local businesses are given the opportunity to provide goods and services where both cost effective and in the County's interest.

B. The goal of the Purchasing Ordinance is to determine not simply the best price for a good or service, but the best value. Another goal is make the process of doing business with the County open, transparent, and as simple as practicable. While some purchasing may be done through a central purchasing agent or office, County department heads also have authority to make purchases in conformance with this chapter and the adopted budget. To the extent possible, purchasing processes will fully utilize the County's website and other digital media.
