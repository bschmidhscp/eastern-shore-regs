# § 28-1. Number of districts designated; locations established for public elections.

A. Caroline County is divided into eight election districts according to the names, boundaries and limits heretofore established and as set forth on the Map of Caroline County Showing Topography and the Election Districts, revised as of 1950 by the State Department of Geology, Mines and Water Resources.

B. All elections for public office shall be held in each of said districts at the places heretofore established for that purpose, unless and until the locations are changed by the Board of Supervisors of Elections of Caroline County.
