# § 28-12. Descriptions of districts.

A. "Election districts" as used herein refers to Election Districts 01 through 08 as the same existed as of September 1, 2011, in the official records of the Board of Elections for Caroline County.

B. District 001 shall consist of all of Election Districts 01 and 07 and a portion of Election District 02, as illustrated on the Board of Education Election District Map attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference, and as further described in § 28-13.

C. District 002 shall consist of all of Election Districts 03 and 06 and the remainder of Election District 02 not placed in District 001.

D. District 003 shall consist of Election Districts 04, 05, and 08.
