# § 28-11. Number of districts; designation of districts.

A. There shall be three Board of Education districts in Caroline County.

B. Board of Education districts shall be drawn in accordance with applicable law as applies to drawing of election districts generally, including, but not limited to, compactness of districts, commonality of interests, natural or jurisdictional borders, and other relevant factors.

C. Board of Education districts shall be numbered 001, 002, and 003.

D. Board of Education districts shall be reapportioned in accordance with state law upon each decennial census of the United States.
