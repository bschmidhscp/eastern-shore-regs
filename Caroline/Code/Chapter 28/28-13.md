# § 28-13. Description of portions of Election District 02 assigned to Board of Education Districts 001 and 002.

A. That portion of Election District 02 assigned to Board of Education District 001 is as follows: All that portion of Election District 02 that lies generally north or above a line drawn through Election District 02 and described as follows:


|  | Commencing from a point where Ridgely Road crosses Forge Branch, from which point the line follows Ridgely Road northeast towards Greensboro for approximately 3,332 feet. From this point the line turns directly southeast and travels overland for 395 feet, east northeast 2,000 feet overland, east southeast 4,730 feet overland, east northeast 3,100 feet overland, northeast 3,265 feet overland, and 2,975 feet east southeast overland until it meets the intersection of Greensboro Road and Knifebox Road. From the intersection of Greensboro Road and Knifebox Road the line travels northwards along Greensboro Road to the intersection of Greensboro Road and Wheeler Drive. The line follows Wheeler Drive northeast and northwest to the intersection of Wheeler Drive and Whiteleysburg Road. From the intersection of Wheeler Drive and Whiteleysburg Road, the line follows Whiteleysburg Road east to the intersection of Whiteleysburg Road and the Maryland-Delaware state line. |

B. The remainder of Election District 02 not allocated to Board of Education District 001 is assigned to Board of Education District 002.

C. Descriptions and maps of the Board of Education districts shall be maintained in the Caroline County Department of Planning and Codes, the office of the County Attorney, and in the office of the Board of Elections for Caroline County.
