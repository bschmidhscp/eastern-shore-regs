# § 33-26. Omissions or deficiencies in reports.

A. After the Commission reviews a Lobbying registration or annual report filed under this chapter for compliance with the provisions of this chapter, the Commission shall notify the Lobbyist of any omissions or deficiencies in the Lobbying registration or annual report.

B. If a Lobbyist does not correct the omission or deficiency within 30 days after notification, the Commission shall refer evidence of noncompliance to the County Attorney for appropriate action under the enforcement provisions of this chapter.
