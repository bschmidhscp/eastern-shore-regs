# § 33-31. Termination, disciplinary action, or suspension.

In addition to any other enforcement provisions in this chapter, a Public Official who is subject to the provisions of this chapter and who is found by the Commission or a court to have violated its provisions is subject to:

A. Termination of employment or services;

B. Disciplinary action as may be warranted; and/or

C. Suspension from receiving payment of salary or other Compensation pending full compliance with the terms of an order of the Commission or a court.
