# § 33-19. Termination of registration.

A. A Lobbyist may terminate the Lobbying registration by providing the Commission with:

  (1) Written notice of termination; and

  (2) Reports required under this chapter that may be outstanding.

B. A termination is effective 30 days after the Commission receives a properly filed notice.
