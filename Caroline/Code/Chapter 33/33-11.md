# § 33-11. Ex parte communications prohibited.

A. This section does not apply to communication with an attorney representing the Board of Zoning Appeals for the purpose of seeking legal advice if the attorney is not a party to the matter.

B. A hearing officer, a member of the Board of Zoning Appeals, or an agent acting on behalf of a hearing officer or a member of the Board of Zoning Appeals may not engage in ex parte communication with any Person regarding a matter pending before the hearing officer or Board of Zoning Appeals at any time before disposition of the matter.
