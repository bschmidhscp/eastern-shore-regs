# § 33-15. Review of statements.

A. After a disclosure statement is submitted to the Commission, the Commission shall review the disclosure statement for compliance with this chapter and notify the filer of any omission or deficiency in the disclosure statement.

B. If the filer does not correct the omission or deficiency on the disclosure statement within 30 days after notification by the Commission, the Commission shall refer the matter, including the evidence of the omission or deficiency, to the County Attorney for appropriate action in accordance with the enforcement provisions of this chapter.
