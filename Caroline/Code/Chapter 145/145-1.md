# § 145-1. Definitions.

As used in this Article, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**COUNTY** The County Commissioners of Caroline County, Maryland.

**ENFORCEMENT OFFICER** Any state or County police officer or any person appointed by the County to enforce these regulations.

**VEHICLE** Any automobile, truck, motorcycle, boat, boat trailer or recreational vehicle.
