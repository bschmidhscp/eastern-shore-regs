# § 50-9. Budget administration.

A. Pursuant to § 18-7C(4) of the Caroline County Code, the County Administrator or his or her designee shall administer the County's budget where such responsibilities include but are not limited to:

  (1) Providing for the enacted budget to be reproduced and available to the public upon request;

  (2) Providing for the allotment of appropriations to control the rate of expenditure and prevent deficits;

  (3) Providing for the control of the transfer of appropriations in conformance with this chapter;

  (4) Establishing and maintaining revenue and expenditure account classifications so as to ensure a uniform system of general fund accounting and for enterprise fund accounting;

  (5) Ensuring that all expenditures are made in conformance with the adopted budget or adopted capital budget or in conformance with the amended budget where an amendment has been approved by a majority vote of the County Commissioners;

  (6) Requiring that the departments, allied agencies and entities funded by the County to prepare and transmit routine financial reports related to the use of County funds.

B. All disbursements of County funds shall conform to the approved budget. No obligation shall be incurred against, and no payment shall be made from, any budget account adopted unless there is a sufficient unencumbered balance in the account and sufficient funds are or will be available to meet the obligation.
