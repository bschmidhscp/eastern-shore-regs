# § 173-18. Reasons for discontinuance.

Water service may be discontinued for any one of the following reasons:

A. Use of water for purposes other than for consumption on the premises serviced.

B. Misrepresentation in the application for service.

C. Willful waste of water.

D. Failure to make repairs requested by the County.

E. Molesting or tampering with County property or seals on meters or other equipment.

F. Vacancy.

G. Nonpayment of bills when due.

H. Cross-connection of the water service pipes with any other water supply source.

I. Refusal of reasonable and safe access to the property.
