# § 173-2. Approval of entry.

The County Commissioners of Caroline County approve the entry of Caroline County into the North County Water & Sewer Authority (the "Authority") on the terms and conditions set forth in the proposed Articles of Incorporation ("Articles") for the Authority, which Articles are set forth as Attachment 1 hereto provided that all of the jurisdictions referenced in the Articles similarly agree to enter into and form the Authority.
