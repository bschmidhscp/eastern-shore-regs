# § 173-3. Execution of Articles of Incorporation.

Upon approval of the Articles by the participating local jurisdictions, the County Commissioners of Caroline County are authorized to, and shall, execute the Articles and to take all necessary and appropriate actions to cause the Articles to be filed with the Maryland State Department of Assessment and Taxation, and to take all reasonable and appropriate acts in furtherance of the creation of the Authority and for Caroline County's participation therein.
