# § 78-4. Applicability.

A. This Act applies to the unincorporated areas of the county, and to any Caroline County incorporated municipalities that have enacted this law.

B. This Act is not intended to establish standards which conflict with Caroline County's Right to Farm Ordinance[1] and generally accepted agricultural practices; however, this shall in no way condone cruel or abusive practices. In the event of conflict, for those owners subject to the Right to Farm Ordinance, the Right to Farm Ordinance shall prevail.

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: See Ch. 149 , Right to Farm.

C. This Act is not intended to establish standards which are stricter than those practices generally accepted in the horse industry. In the event of conflict, for those owners who are part of the horse industry, including the owners of pleasure horses, the generally accepted practices shall prevail; however, the standard is that applied to the industry as a whole, not confined to any particular segment or breed of the industry. Also, this provision shall in no way condone cruel or abusive practices.
