# § 128-9. Containers.

A. Containers.

  (1) An individual container or bulk container used for the storage of FPRs with a moisture content greater than 25% as certified by the Maryland Department of Agriculture shall have the following characteristics:

    (a) The container shall be constructed to be easily handled for collection.

    (b) The container shall be constructed of rust resistant and corrosion resistant materials.

    (c) The container shall be designed to prevent leaks.

  (2) Putrescible waste shall be stored in an individual container or bulk container that has the following characteristics:

    (a) The container shall be equipped with a tight-fitting lid or cover, or otherwise sealed.

    (b) The container shall be watertight, leak-proof, insect-proof and rodentproof.

  (3) All containers shall be clearly labeled as "Food Processing Residuals."

  (4) The total container height of a group of containers may not exceed nine feet. The maximum width and depth of a group of containers shall provide a configuration and aisle space which ensures access for purposes of inspection, containment and remedial action with emergency vehicles and equipment.

B. A structure used for the storage of FPRs with a 25% or less moisture content as certified by the Maryland Department of Agriculture shall have the following characteristics:

  (1) The structure shall be clearly labeled as "Semi-solid Food Processing Residuals."

  (2) The structure must be fully enclosed and weather-tight to contain odors, dust and runoff.

  (3) The structure shall have a stable impervious foundation, capable of supporting the total weight of the Semi-solid FPRs without movement, rolling or unacceptable settling. The foundation design and construction shall be based on sound engineering practices.
