# § 128-7. Nuisance minimization and control.

A. A person that stores FPRs shall:

  (1) Control and minimize the harborage, breeding, or attraction of vectors.

  (2) Take other measures necessary to control and minimize the presence of vectors.

  (3) Immediately take measures necessary to exterminate vectors.

B. A person storing FPRs shall also minimize, and control conditions not otherwise prohibited by this Chapter that are harmful to the public health, public safety, or the environment, or which create safety hazards, odors, dust, unsightliness, or other public nuisances.
