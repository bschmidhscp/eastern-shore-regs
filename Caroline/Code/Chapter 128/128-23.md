# § 128-23. Adoption of regulations by the department.

A. The Department shall adopt regulations to carry out this Chapter.

B. Regulations.

  (1) The regulations adopted under this Chapter shall address:

    (a) Alternative storage methods;

    (b) Control of pathogens and pollutants of concern identified by the Department;

    (c) Advertising requirements for public hearings and public information hearings;

    (d) Performance bonds, liability insurance, or other forms of security;

    (e) Procedures for notifying units of local government and other interested parties;

    (f) Adequate standards for storing FPRs;

    (g) The nature of any nearby surface water or groundwater;

    (h) The character of any affected area;

    (i) The character of nearby existing or planned land uses and transport routes;

    (j) Reasonable buffer areas to separate any home, agricultural land, or other property from land on which FPRs may be stored.

C. Fees.

  (1) Subject to Subsection C(2) of this subsection, the Department shall adopt regulations to establish and update permit application fees.

  (2) Fee schedule.

    (a) The Department shall provide an opportunity for public input on the development of a fee schedule under this Section.

    (b) A fee schedule developed under this Section shall include consideration of:

      [1] The anticipated costs of monitoring and regulating FPRs storage sites;

      [2] The anticipated costs of implementing this Chapter; and

      [3] The potential impact of the activities of an FPRs storage site on the environment and public health, safety, and welfare.
