# § 128-15. Expiration or renewal of permit.

A. Expiration.

  (1) Subject to Subsection A(2) of this section, a permit shall expire on the date the Department sets at the time the permit is issued or renewed.

  (2) The term of a permit may not last for more than one year.

B. Renewal.

  (1) Except as provided in Subsection C of this Section, the Department may renew a permit if the permit holder:

    (a) Is in compliance with:

      [1] The conditions of the permit; and

      [2] The requirements of this Chapter; and

      [3] All applicable regulations established by the Department;

    (b) Submits to the Department in a timely manner a renewal application on the form that the Department requires; and

    (c) Pays a renewal application fee.

  (2) The Department may refuse to renew a permit if:

    (a) The permit holder violates this Chapter, any regulation adopted by the Department under this Chapter, or any condition of the permit;

    (b) The Department determines that continued operation of any area covered by the permit would be injurious to the environment or public health, safety, or welfare;

    (c) The applicant is subject to an enforcement action, consent order, or ongoing investigation by the Department, the Maryland Department of Health, the Department of Agriculture, or the Department of the Environment, including, but not limited to actions regarding the containment of surface water, groundwater, or soil contamination;

    (d) The enforcement action, consent order, or ongoing investigation has not been withdrawn or resolved;

    (e) A notice of violation has been issued by the Department, the Maryland Department of Health, the Department of Agriculture, or the Department of the Environment; or

    (f) The Department determines that there is any other good cause.

C. Within 10 days after receiving an application for renewal of a permit to store FPRs in conjunction with an agricultural operation, the Department shall:

  (1) Publish notice of the application in a local newspaper that is primarily in circulation in Caroline County;

  (2) Mail a copy of the notice to:

    (a) The Health Officer of the Caroline County Health Department;

    (b) The County Commissioners of Caroline County and the County Administrator;

    (c) The chair of the legislative body and any elected executive of any municipal corporation within two miles of where the FPRs will be stored; and

  (3) Post the notice on the Department's website.

D. Within 15 days after receiving a notice under Subsection C of this Section, the County Commissioners or the elected executive or the legislative body of the municipal corporation may request that the Department hold a public hearing.
