# § 108-6. Abrogation and greater restrictions.

This chapter is not intended to repeal or abrogate any existing regulations and ordinances, including subdivision regulations, zoning ordinances, building codes, or any existing easements, covenants, or deed restrictions. In the event of a conflict between this chapter and any other ordinance, the more restrictive shall govern.
