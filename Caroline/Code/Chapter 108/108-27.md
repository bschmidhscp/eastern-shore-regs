# § 108-27. Critical and Essential Facilities.

*[Amended 11-25-2014 by Ord. No. 2014-4 ]*

Critical and essential facilities shall:

A. Not be located in Coastal A Zones or floodways.

B. If located in flood hazard areas other than Coastal A Zones and floodways, be elevated to the higher of elevations required by this chapter plus one foot: the elevation required by the building code, or the elevation of the two-tenths-percent chance (five-hundred-year) flood.
