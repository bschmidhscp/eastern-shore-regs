# § 108-23. Placement of fill.

A. Disposal of fill, including but not limited to earthen soils, rock, rubble, construction debris, woody debris, and trash, shall not be permitted in the special flood hazard areas.

B. Fill shall not be placed in Coastal A Zones except as provided in § 108-39.[Amended 11-25-2014 by Ord. No. 2014-4]

C. Fill proposed to be placed to elevate structures in flood hazard areas (A Zones) that are not Coastal A Zones shall comply with the floodways requirements in §§ 108-33A, 108-33B, and 108-33C and the limitations of § 108-34B.[Added 11-25-2014 by Ord. No. 2014-4]
