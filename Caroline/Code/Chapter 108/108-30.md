# § 108-30. Functionally dependent uses.

Functionally dependent uses shall be approved only by variance issued pursuant to Article VII. If approved, functionally dependent uses shall be protected by methods that minimize flood damage during the base flood, including measures to allow floodwaters to enter and exit, use of flood-damage-resistant materials, and elevation of electric service and equipment to the extent practical given the use of the building.
