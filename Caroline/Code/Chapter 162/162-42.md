# § 162-42. Fire protection facilities.

A. Fire protection services and facilities for the area in which the subdivision is located shall be adequate to protect the lives and property of the residents of the subdivision.

B. Where it deems necessary, the Planning Commission shall request an evaluation from the appropriate authority as to the adequacy of the fire protection services and facilities.
