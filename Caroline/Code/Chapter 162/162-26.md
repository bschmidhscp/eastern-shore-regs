# § 162-26. Sidewalks.

The Planning Commission may require the installation of sidewalks where:

A. Any lot is smaller than 20,000 square feet in area;

B. Two-family or multifamily residential development is proposed;

C. Commercial or industrial development is proposed; or

D. They are desirable because of the character of the neighborhood to continue sidewalks that are existing or proposed in adjoining subdivisions or to provide pedestrian access to community facilities, schools, shopping and recreation areas.
