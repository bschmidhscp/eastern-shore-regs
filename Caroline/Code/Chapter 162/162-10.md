# § 162-10. Appeals.

Any person aggrieved by the action of the Planning Commission pursuant to this chapter may appeal directly to the Circuit Court.[1]

> Note: [1] Editor's Note: Original Section I-15, regarding preliminary plat approvals existing prior to the effective date of this chapter, which section immediately followed this section, was deleted during codification.
