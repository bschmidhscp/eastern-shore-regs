# § 162-5. Criteria for determining number of lots; residual portions.

A. All lots, plats, sites or other divisions of land recorded after November 30, 1972, from an original lot, tract or parcel of land described in the land records of Caroline County as of said date shall be counted in determining the number of lots in the subdivision.

B. The residual or remaining portion of a lot, tract or parcel of land being subdivided shall be counted as one of the lots, plats, sites or other divisions of land created if it is less than 20 acres in area. At least one subdivision lot development right shall remain with the residual portion of the parcel.[Amended 12-12-1989 by Ord. No. 89-009]
