# § 162-40. Drainage outlets.

A. All subdivisions shall have a drainage outlet adequate in size and grade to efficiently remove stormwater and provide drainage for roads and all lots.

B. The County Engineer shall advise the Planning Commission as to whether an adequate drainage outlet exists.
