# § 132-64. Fire protection systems.

A. Installation and maintenance.

  (1) All fire protection systems and equipment, including smoke detectors, fire-suppression systems and portable fire extinguishers, shall be installed and maintained in proper operating condition and free of defects at all times.

  (2) Sprinkler heads shall be kept clean and free of corrosion and paint and may not be bent or damaged.

  (3) Fire extinguishers shall be visible.

B. Smoke detectors.

  (1) All housing units shall be provided with a minimum of one single-station smoke detector in the vicinity of each sleeping area.

  (2) The smoke detectors shall be installed and maintained in accordance with the state fire laws, Article 38A, § 12A, Smoke Detection Systems, of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

  (3) When actuated, the smoke detector shall provide an alarm suitable to warn the occupants within the unit.
