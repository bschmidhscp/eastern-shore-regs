# § 132-44. Residential structures in general.

A. Maintenance. The structure and its equipment and facilities shall be maintained in good repair and in a clean, safe, sound and sanitary condition so as not to pose a threat to the health, safety or welfare of the occupants.

B. Structural members. Supporting structural members shall be kept structurally sound, free of deterioration and maintained capable of safely carrying the imposed loads.
