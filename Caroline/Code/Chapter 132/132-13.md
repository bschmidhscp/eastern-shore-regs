# § 132-13. Waivers.

The Code Official may, on the basis of the religious practices of the occupant or other valid reason, waive application of this code, in whole or in part, to a unit of rental housing if:

A. The property owner files a written application justifying the need for the waiver;

B. Adequate notice of the application is afforded to the tenant of the unit;

C. The tenant is afforded an opportunity to comment on the application either in writing or in person; and

D. The waiver would not threaten the health or safety of a tenant.
