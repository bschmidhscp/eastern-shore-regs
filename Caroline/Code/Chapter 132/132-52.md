# § 132-52. Installation and maintenance.

All plumbing and plumbing fixtures, including the water supply and sewage systems, shall be properly installed, connected and maintained in a safe, clean and sanitary working condition and shall be kept free from obstructions, leaks and defects and shall be capable of performing the function for which they are designed. All repairs and installations shall conform with local plumbing code and environmental health requirements.
