# § 132-56. Sewage system.

The plumbing fixtures in each housing unit shall be properly connected to either a public or private sewage disposal system which has received all necessary and legally required approvals. The sewage system shall be installed and maintained to provide at all times sanitary waste disposal fee of health hazards and in conformance with environmental health regulations.
