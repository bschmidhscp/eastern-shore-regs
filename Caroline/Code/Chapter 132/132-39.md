# § 132-39. Petition for appeal.

A person affected by an order or decision of the Code Official which has been made in connection with the enforcement of this code may request and may be granted a hearing on the matter before the County Commissioners. A written petition requesting the hearing must be received by the Clerk to the County Commissioners within 30 days after service of notice of the order or decision. Within 10 days of receipt of the petition, the Code Official shall notify the petitioner, in writing, of the date, time and place of the hearing.
