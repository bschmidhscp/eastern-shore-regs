# § 132-26. Failure to comply.

If the property owner fails to comply with an order to vacate, close or demolish a condemned structure or to place out of service condemned equipment within the time period specified in an order, then the Code Official, after consultation with the County Attorney, may cause these to be accomplished through any available public agency or private persons. The cost shall be charged against the real estate upon which the structure or equipment is located and shall be a lien upon the real estate.
