# § 132-62. Accumulations and storage.

A. Flammable matter. Highly flammable or explosive matter, such as paints, volatile oils and cleaning fluids, or combustible rubbish such as waste paper, boxes and rags, may not be accumulated or stored on premises except in reasonable quantities consistent with normal usage.

B. Flammable liquids. A housing unit may not be located within a structure containing an establishment handling, dispensing or storing flammable liquids with a flash point of 100° F. or lower, except as provided for in the applicable local building code.
