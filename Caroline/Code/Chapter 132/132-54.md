# § 132-54. Water supply.

The plumbing fixtures in each housing unit shall be properly connected to either a public or a private water system which has received all necessary and legally required approvals. The water supply system shall be installed and maintained to provide at all times a supply of water to the plumbing fixtures and facilities which is free of contamination and in sufficient volume and at pressures adequate to enable them to function satisfactorily.
