# § 132-41. Status of orders during appeal.

The filing of an appeal petition shall stay the effect of the order or decision being appealed. If, following the hearing, the County Commissioners uphold the order or decision of the Code Official, then any deadline for compliance shall stand as previously imposed and shall be measured from the date of the decision of the County Commissioners on the appeal petition.
