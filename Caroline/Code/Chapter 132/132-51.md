# § 132-51. Required plumbing facilities.

Each housing unit shall include its own plumbing facilities which are adequate for personal cleanliness and the disposal of human waste. All plumbing fixtures shall be of nonabsorbent material and shall conform with local plumbing code requirements. The following minimum plumbing facilities shall be supplied:

A. Water closet and lavatory. Each housing unit shall contain within its walls a room separate from other habitable area which affords privacy and which contains a water closet supplied with cold running water and a lavatory supplied with hot and cold running water.

B. Bathtub or shower. Each housing unit shall contain a room which is equipped with a bathtub or shower supplied with hot and cold running water and which affords privacy.

C. Kitchen sink. Each housing unit shall contain a kitchen sink apart from the lavatory required under Subsection A. The sink shall be supplied with hot and cold running water.
