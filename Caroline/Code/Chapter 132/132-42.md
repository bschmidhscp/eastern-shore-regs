# § 132-42. Judicial appeal.

Within 20 days after the decision by the County Commissioners on an appeal, a person affected by an order or decision of the Code Official may appeal to a court of competent jurisdiction. Written notice to the County Commissioners shall be provided at the time of the filing of an appeal.
