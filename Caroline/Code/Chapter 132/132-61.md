# § 132-61. Means of egress.

A. Direct exit. Each housing unit shall have a safe, continuous and unobstructed means of egress directly to or by a common area that leads directly to the outside to an open area at grade.

B. Exit signs. All exit signs shall be illuminated and visible.

C. Emergency escape. Each sleeping room located in a basement shall have at least one openable window or exterior door for emergency egress or rescue which has received all necessary and legally required approvals or shall have access to two separate exits which have received all necessary and legally required approvals.
