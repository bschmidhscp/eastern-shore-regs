# § 151-4. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**ADDRESS COMMITTEE** The committee of persons appointed by the County Commissioners of Caroline County to develop guidelines and criteria for the implementation and maintenance of the new numbering system.

**COUNTY COMMISSIONERS** The County Commissioners of Caroline County, Maryland.

**DIRECTOR OF PLANNING** The person, or an authorized representative, designated by the County Commissioners to administer and enforce the provisions of this chapter.

**ROAD** A public or private way for vehicular traffic, including a right-of-way, street, avenue, drive, lane, terrace, boulevard, circle, court, parkway, highway or other similar terms.
