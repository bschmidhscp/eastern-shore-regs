# § 1-3. Saved from repeal.

The adoption of this Code and the repeal of laws above stated shall not affect the following acts, legislative and otherwise:

A. Appropriating money or transferring funds, promising or guaranteeing the payment of money or authorizing the issuance and delivery of any bond or other instruments or evidence of Caroline County's indebtedness.

B. Regarding the purchase, sale, lease, or transfer of property.

C. Levy or imposition of taxes, assessments, fees or charges.

D. Regarding subdivision of land, zoning, or dedication of property.

E. Compensation of officials and employees.

F. Resolutions.

G. Rights or liabilities established, accrued or incurred under a legislative provision prior to the effective date of this Act or any action or proceeding brought for the enforcement of such right or liability.

H. Any offense or violation of the law or any penalty, punishment or forfeiture which may result therefrom.

I. Any prosecution, indictment, action, suit or other proceeding pending or any judgment rendered prior to the effective date of this Act.

J. Unexpired licenses, permits, rights, easements or privileges.
