# § 1-2. Repeal of legislation; Code supersedes prior provisions.

A. Laws of a general and permanent nature adopted and in force on the date of adoption of this Act and not contained in the Code are hereby repealed as of the effective date of this Act.

B. This Act and Code shall supersede all other general and permanent laws enacted prior to the enactment of this Code, except as provided in § 1-3, Saved from repeal.
