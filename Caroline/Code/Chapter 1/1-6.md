# § 1-6. Supplementation, sale and distribution.

A. The County Commissioners shall make an appropriation to provide for the publication of this Code, which shall be offered for sale to the public.

B. The Code shall be supplemented at least annually so that it shall remain current.

C. A copy of the Code and its supplements shall be provided free of charge to the Caroline County Public Library for public inspection.

D. A copy of the Code and its supplements shall be provided to state agencies as may be required by state law.
