# § 158-10. Appeals.

Any person aggrieved by the action of any official charged with the enforcement of this chapter, as the result of the disapproval of a properly filed application for a permit, issuance of a written notice of violation, or an alleged failure to properly enforce the chapter in regard to a specific application, shall have the right to appeal the action to the Board of Zoning Appeals. The appeal shall be filed in writing within 60 days of the date of official transmittal of the final decision or determination to the applicant.
