# § 170-6. Transfer of ownership.

It shall be unlawful for the owner of any structure upon whom notice of violation has been served to sell, transfer, lease, mortgage or otherwise dispose of the property until the violations have been corrected or until the owner shall furnish the grantee, transferee, mortgagee or lessee with a copy of the notice of violation.
