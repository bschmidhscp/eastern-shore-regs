# § 170-5. Notice of violation.

A. Notification. The code official shall give notice of the existing violation to the owner of the affected structure and to its occupants.

B. Form. The notice shall:

  (1) Be in writing.

  (2) Include a description of the real estate sufficient for identification.

  (3) Include a statement of the reason or reasons why the notice is being issued.

  (4) Include an explanation of the owner's right to appeal in accordance with § 170-10 of this chapter.

  (5) Include a compliance order as hereinafter described.

  (6) Describe the penalties for failure to correct said violation.

C. Service. Notice shall be considered served when the notice is delivered to the owner personally or is sent by certified or registered mail, addressed to the owner at the last known address, with return receipt requested. Notice shall also be posted in a conspicuous place on or about the affected structure.
