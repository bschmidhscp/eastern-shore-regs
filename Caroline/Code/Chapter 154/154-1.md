# § 154-1. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**SANITARY CONSTRUCTION** The installation of any septic tank system or other on-site sewage disposal system and the practice of removing and disposing of the contents of septic tanks and other appurtenances.
