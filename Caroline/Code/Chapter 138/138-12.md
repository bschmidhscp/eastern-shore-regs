# § 138-12. Notice of violation.

A. When the code official determines that a violation exists, written notice shall be issued to the owner and/or responsible person.

B. The notice shall:

  (1) Identify the property;

  (2) Describe the violation;

  (3) Provide a time for correction in accordance with § 138-13;

  (4) Failure to comply may result in the issuance of a class a civil citation pursuant to Chapter 92 of the County Code.

C. Notice may be served by personal delivery, certified mail, posting on the property, or any other method reasonably calculated to provide notice.
