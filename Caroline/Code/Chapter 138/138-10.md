# § 138-10. Storage requirements.

A. Rubbish shall be stored in approved containers designed to prevent dispersal by wind, animals or other natural elements.

B. Garbage shall be stored in approved leakproof containers with close-fitting covers to prevent leakage, odors and infestation.

C. The owner of the premises shall be responsible for providing approved containers and ensuring removal of rubbish and garbage in a lawful manner.
