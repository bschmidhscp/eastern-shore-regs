# § 138-6. Responsibility.

A. The owner of the premises shall be responsible for maintaining exterior property in compliance with this chapter.

B. Nothing herein shall preclude enforcement against any occupant, operator or other person responsible for creating, maintaining or contributing to a violation.

C. Responsibility for compliance shall not be avoided by lease, contract or other agreement.
