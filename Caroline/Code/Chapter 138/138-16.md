# § 138-16. Repeat violations; accelerated enforcement.

A. For purposes of this section, a "repeat violation" shall mean a violation of this chapter occurring on the same premises within three calendar years of the date of a prior confirmed violation for accumulation of rubbish or garbage.

B. Upon determination of a repeat violation, the code official shall issue a notice of violation providing a compliance period of not less than 72 hours from the date of service.

C. No extension of time shall be granted for a repeat violation except in extraordinary circumstances. Any such extension shall be limited to a maximum of 10 additional calendar days beyond the initial seventy-two-hour compliance period and shall be granted only upon written request demonstrating compelling justification, as determined by the code official.

D. Upon expiration of the seventy-two-hour compliance period, the code official shall reinspect the premises in accordance with § 138-13.

E. If the violation remains uncorrected at reinspection, a civil citation shall be issued automatically and assessed at the repeat offense amount as set forth in Chapter 92 of the County Code.

F. Each day the violation continues thereafter shall constitute a separate repeat offense subject to the repeat offense citation.
