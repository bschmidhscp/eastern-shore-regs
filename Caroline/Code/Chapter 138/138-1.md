# § 138-1. Intent.

The County Commissioners recognize that Caroline is a rural, agricultural county with a strong tradition of respecting private property rights. The Commissioners also recognize that in developed portions of the County, properties on which rubbish and garbage have accumulated can diminish the quiet enjoyment of other properties in the vicinity and present a potential threat to public health, safety, and welfare. The provisions of this Chapter 138 shall apply to all the unincorporated areas in Caroline County, except as to agricultural material, implements, equipment, and machinery stored, maintained, or displayed on any property used for, or held for use for, agricultural business activities on the property. The legislative intent of this chapter is to provide a fair, balanced, and reasonable process to address the issue of the open-air accumulation of rubbish and garbage in the County. Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit or interfere with the lawful and permitted use of agricultural properties in accordance with Chapter 149, Right to Farm.
