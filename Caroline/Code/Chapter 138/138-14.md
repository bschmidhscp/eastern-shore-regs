# § 138-14. Reinspection.

A. Upon expiration of the compliance period stated in the notice of violation, the code official shall conduct a reinspection of the premises within 48 hours, excluding weekends and County-recognized holidays.

B. If the violation has been corrected, the case shall be closed.

C. If the violation has not been corrected, a civil citation shall be issued pursuant to Chapter 92 of the County Code.

D. Failure of the County to reinspect within 48 hours shall not invalidate enforcement actions or preclude the issuance of a civil citation.
