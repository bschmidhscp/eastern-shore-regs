# § 138-15. Civil infractions; penalties.

A. A violation of this chapter shall constitute a Class A civil infraction and shall be enforced in accordance with Chapter 92 of the County Code.

B. Each day that a violation continues after the expiration of the compliance period shall constitute a separate offense.

C. Issuance of a citation shall not preclude the County from pursuing abatement or any other remedy authorized by law.
