# § 138-13. Compliance timeframe.

A. Unless the code official determines that a shorter period of time is necessary due to the nature or severity of the violation, the responsible person shall be provided not less than 10 calendar days from the date of service of the notice to correct the violation.

B. The code official may establish a shorter compliance period when:

  (1) The accumulation presents a fire hazard;

  (2) The condition creates or contributes to infestation;

  (3) The condition poses a threat to public health or safety;

  (4) The violation has previously occurred on the same property; or

  (5) Weather conditions are likely to worsen the condition.

C. The code official may grant one extension of time, not to exceed 10 additional calendar days, upon written request showing good cause, provided that the violation does not pose a threat to public health or safety.
