# § 138-8. Definitions.

For the purposes of this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**APPROVED** Acceptable to the code official.

**CODE OFFICIAL** The official charged with the administration and enforcement of this chapter, or any duly authorized representative.

**EXTERIOR PROPERTY** The open space on a premises and on adjoining property under the control of the owner or operator.

**GARBAGE** Animal or vegetable waste resulting from the handling, preparation, cooking and consumption of food.

**OWNER** Any person, firm, corporation or other legal entity having legal or equitable interest in the property or otherwise having control of the property.

**PERSON** An individual, corporation, partnership or other group acting as a unit.

**PREMISES** A lot, plot or parcel of land, including the exterior property thereon.

**RUBBISH** Combustible and noncombustible waste materials, except garbage, including paper, rags, cartons, boxes, wood products, rubber, leather, tin cans, metals, glass, crockery and other similar materials. The term shall include treated, painted, stained, pressure-treated, or chemically treated lumber and wood products. The term shall not include natural vegetative materials, including tree branches, brush, leaves or yard trimmings.
