# Chapter 111. (Reserved)

*[Former Ch. 111, Foxes, adopted P.L.L., 1930, Art. 6, sec. 238; 1906, ch. 778; 1916, ch. 450; 1965, ch. 28; 1982, ch. 875, was repealed 5-13-2003 by Bill No. 2003-3. Section 2 of this bill also provided that Ch. 111 be reserved for future public local law pertaining to foxes and the hunting and/or trapping thereof.]*
