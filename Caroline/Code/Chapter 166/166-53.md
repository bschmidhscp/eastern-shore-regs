# § 166-53. Amount, eligibility and limitations.

A property tax credit of 100% is granted on real property owned by Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated, that meets the following criteria:

A. The property is owned by Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated, with the intention of relinquishing ownership in the near future; and

B. The property is used exclusively for the purpose of rehabilitation and transfer to a private owner in order to promote home ownership and the availability of affordable housing to the public; and

C. The property is not occupied by administrative, warehouse or other ancillary buildings owned by Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated.
