# § 166-55. Tax credit applicability period on eligible properties.

This real property tax credit shall take effect when an eligible property is transferred to Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated. However, if Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated, purchases the property rather than receiving it as a donation, the County shall reimburse Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated, for county real property taxes paid at time of settlement. The tax credit shall cease as of the date of transfer from Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated, to another owner. When the credit ceases, the property and its assessment shall be reinstated on the Caroline County real property tax roll, and taxes due shall be paid.
