# § 166-4. Evidence of consideration.

The consideration payable shall be described in:

A. The recitals or the acknowledgement of the instrument of writing; or

B. A statement, under oath, that accompanies the instrument of writing and that is signed by a party to the instrument of writing or by an agent of a party.
