# § 166-30. Evidence of consideration of debt.

The consideration payable, including the amount of an mortgage or deed of trust assumed by the grantee, or the principal amount of the secured debt incurred, shall be described in:

A. The recitals or the acknowledgment of the instrument in writing; or

B. An affidavit, under oath, that accompanies the instrument of writing and that is signed by a party to the instrument of writing or by an agent of a party.
