# § 166-81. Application and annual verification.

Each volunteer fire company shall certify, under oath, the active status of firefighters seeking a tax credit under this article and submit documentation to the Caroline County Fire and EMS Association no later than February 15 of the following year for verification and processing. A firefighter seeking a tax credit under this article must submit to the Fire and EMS Association:

A. An application in the form required by the Fire and EMS Association; and

B. During each subsequent year, the verification required by the Fire and EMS Association to confirm that the individual and property remain qualified for the tax credit.
