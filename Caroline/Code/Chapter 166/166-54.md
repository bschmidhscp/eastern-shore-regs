# § 166-54. Reporting requirements.

In order to receive credits, Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated shall annually on or by the first day of May submit a written report to the County Commissioners of Caroline County, the Caroline County Treasurer's Office, and to the Caroline County Assessment Office containing the following information on each of its real property holdings in Caroline County:

A. The property address, and the real property identification number on the property tax bill;

B. The date the property transferred to Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated; the amount of consideration paid to or received by Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated; the names of all parties to the transaction; the purpose of the acquisition by Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated; and the property's current use; and

C. Any other transactions involving these holdings.
