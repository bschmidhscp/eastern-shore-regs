# § 166-19. Exceptions.

The County Commissioners reserve the right to deny or postpone approval of an application if the applicant, or lessee in the case of leased property, is in default on payment of any Caroline County taxes or tax penalties, or State of Maryland taxes or tax penalties of which Caroline County receives a portion.
