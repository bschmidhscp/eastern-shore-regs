# § 166-23. Filing monthly reports required.

*[Amended 5-8-2007 by Bill No. 2007-1 ; 10-9-2007 by Bill No. 2007-5 ]*

Each operator or owner of a mobile home or trailer park in Caroline County, Maryland, shall file a written monthly return stating the total number of occupied mobile homes or trailers in the park, along with the total amount of tax due for the park. The return shall be filed no later than 15 days after the end of each calendar month and shall be on a form prescribed by the Comptroller. The operator or owner of the mobile home or trailer park shall be liable to the Comptroller of Caroline County for all taxes due.
