# § 166-76. Definitions.

In this article, the following terms have the meanings indicated:

**DWELLING** A house that is used as the principal residence of the homeowner and actually occupied or expected to be actually occupied by the homeowner for more than six months of a twelve-month period beginning with the date of finality for the taxable year for which the property tax credit under this article is sought.

**PUBLIC SAFETY OFFICER WHO IS AN ACTIVE VOLUNTEER FIREFIGHTER** A Caroline County volunteer firefighter who meets the eligibility criteria set forth in § 166-78 of this article.
