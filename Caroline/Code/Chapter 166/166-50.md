# § 166-50. Exemption from local taxation.

Pursuant to the grant of authority contained in Article 81, § 9C(f-1), of the Annotated Code of Maryland (1975 Replacement Volume and 1978 Supplement), real and tangible personal property owned by the Community Civic League, Inc., at its daycare center facility on Laurel Grove Road, shall be exempt from local taxation.
