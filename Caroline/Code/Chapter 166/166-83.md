# § 166-83. Record retention and inspection.

Each volunteer fire company shall retain all required documentation collected and/or submitted pursuant to this article for not less than five years. For continued participation in the program, these documents shall be made available for copying and inspection to the County Commissioners and/or the Department of Finance upon request.
