# § 166-80. Termination of credit.

The tax credit created by this article shall terminate and the firefighter will not be eligible if any of the following occurs:

A. The firefighter has failed to maintain active service status in the preceding calendar year, in which event the firefighter shall be ineligible to receive the tax credit until such time as the firefighter re-qualifies under § 166-78 of this article;

B. The firefighter no longer resides in or owns the dwelling for which the credit was granted; or

C. The volunteer fire company of the firefighter is delinquent in the required reporting to the state or County.
