# § 166-72. Definitions.

A. The definition of terms used in this Article shall be those provided under § 9-104 of the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, except as may otherwise be provided herein.

B. As used in this Article, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**THE STATE PROGRAM** The Maryland state property tax credit program established under Section 9-104 of the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.
