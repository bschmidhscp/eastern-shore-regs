# § 166-22. Determination, amount and collection of tax.

*[Amended 5-8-2007 by Bill No. 2007-1 ; 10-9-2007 by Bill No. 2007-5 ]*

All persons, firms or corporations renting, leasing or using any space, facilities or accommodations in any mobile home park or trailer park in Caroline County, Maryland, shall be required to pay a tax of $15 per month per mobile home or trailer rented, leased or using any space, facilities or accommodations in the park, whether the mobile home or trailer is in the park for the entire month or not. The tax shall be collected by the operator or owner of the park and remitted to the Comptroller of Caroline County, Maryland. Each month the park operator or owner shall be entitled to deduct 10% from the total monthly remittance due the Comptroller as compensation for collecting and remitting the tax. The deduction shall not be allowed unless the remittance due is received by the Comptroller within the time prescribed by this Article.
