# § 166-78. Eligibility.

A Caroline County Active Volunteer Firefighter is eligible for a tax credit under this article if the firefighter:

A. Is a member of one of the eight independent volunteer fire companies listed in § 107-4B of the Caroline County Code; and

B. Has maintained active service status as determined by Caroline County Fire and EMS Association for at least three consecutive calendar years accounting from January 1, 2022.
