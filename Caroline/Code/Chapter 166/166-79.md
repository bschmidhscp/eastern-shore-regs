# § 166-79. Amount of credit.

Subject to the conditions in this article, for the tax year 2026-2027, the tax credit is granted in the amount of $250 per dwelling. Subsequently, the amount of the tax credit shall increase by $50 each year until the credit reaches $500. The tax credit shall not exceed the amount of tax on the property.
