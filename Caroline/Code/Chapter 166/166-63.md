# § 166-63. Distribution and use of revenues.

A. Caroline County shall distribute the hotel rental tax revenue as follows:

  (1) From the total revenue, a reasonable sum for hotel rental tax administrative costs to the general fund of the County;

  (2) After distribution in Subsection A(1) of this subsection, the revenue attributable to a hotel located in a municipal corporation to the municipal corporation; and

  (3) The remaining balance to the general fund of the County.

B. All revenue generated to the County by the hotel rental tax is to be used exclusively for tourism development and Caroline County promotion efforts.
