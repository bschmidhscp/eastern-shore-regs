# § 166-25. Violations and penalties.

Any person, firm or corporation liable for the taxes imposed by this Article, who or which wilfully fails to collect, remit or pay the taxes imposed by this Article, wilfully fails to file any report required by this Article or wilfully makes any false statement or misleading omission in a report required by this Article, shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall be fined not more than $500 or imprisoned for not more than 30 days, or both, in the discretion of the court.
