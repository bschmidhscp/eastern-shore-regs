# § 166-52. Tax credit established; authority.

By virtue of the authority vested in the County Commissioners of Caroline County by the Tax-Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland, § 9-307(e) (1994 Replacement Volume and 1999 Supplement), a property tax credit against county real property tax imposed on certain qualifying unimproved and improved property owned by Caroline County Habitat for Humanity, Incorporated, is hereby established.
