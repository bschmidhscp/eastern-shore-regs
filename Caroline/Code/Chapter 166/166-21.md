# § 166-21. Definitions.

In this Article, unless otherwise clearly indicated by the context, the following words and terms have the meanings indicated:

**MOBILE HOME OR TRAILER** A structure that is transportable in one or more sections, built on a permanent chassis, and designed to be used as a dwelling, with or without a permanent foundation, when connected to the required utilities; includes the plumbing, heating, air-conditioning and electrical systems contained in the structure.

**MOBILE HOME PARK OR TRAILER PARK** A tract of land where spaces for two or more mobile homes or trailers used as living quarters are leased; includes all structures, vehicles, accessories and appurtenances used or intended as equipment in such a park.
