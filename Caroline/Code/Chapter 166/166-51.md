# § 166-51. Interest rate.

Caroline County shall charge interest on each tax at the rate of 1% for each month or fraction of a month that any of the following county taxes are overdue:

A. Full year real property taxes.

B. Half year real property taxes.

C. Final determination of estimated personal property tax.

D. Taxing district property tax.
