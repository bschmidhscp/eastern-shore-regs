# § 166-29. Rate of tax.

*[Amended 6-10-2003 by Bill No. 2003-5 ]*

For an instrument of writing dated August 1, 2003, and thereafter, the recordation tax rate shall be $5 for each $500 or a fraction of $500 of consideration payable on the principal amount the debt secured by an instrument of writing. The consideration includes the amount of any mortgage or deed of trust assumed by the grantee.
