# § 166-24. Failure to file.

*[Amended 5-8-2007 by Bill No. 2007-1 ; 10-9-2007 by Bill No. 2007-5 ]*

In the event that the operator or owner of any mobile home park or trailer park fails to file a return and the proper remittance within the time prescribed, or if there is reasonable cause to believe that an erroneous statement has been filed, the Comptroller of Caroline County shall proceed to determine the amount due and, in connection therewith, may make such investigations as may be necessary.
