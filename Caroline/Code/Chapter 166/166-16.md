# § 166-16. Credit eligibility period.

The five-year tax credit eligibility period shall be effective upon placement of the expanded or newly constructed property on the County tax roll and upon verification that the requirements for receiving the credit have been met. Taxes already paid shall not be refunded. Under no circumstances shall tax credits be extended or renewed beyond five years for the same expansion or new construction.
