# § 166-28. Imposition of tax.

*[Amended 1-12-2010 by Bill No. 2009-8]*

A recordation tax shall be imposed, determined, and collected on an instrument of writing by the Collector of Taxes before any such instrument of writing may be recorded with the Clerk of the Circuit Court for Caroline County.
