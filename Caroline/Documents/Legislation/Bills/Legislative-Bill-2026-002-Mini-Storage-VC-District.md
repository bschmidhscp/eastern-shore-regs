SECTION 1. A new Section 175-49 is hereby enacted, to read: 

ARTICLE V Supplementary Regulations 

§ 175-49. WAREHOUSE, MINI-STORAGE FACILITIES (VC ZONING DISTRICT) 

###### A. PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY. 



THE PURPOSE OF THIS SECTION IS TO ESTABLISH SUPPLEMENTARY REGULATIONS FOR WAREHOUSE, MINI-STORAGE FACILITIES LOCATED WITHIN THE VC ZONING DISTRICT. THESE STANDARDS ARE INTENDED TO ENSURE THAT SUCH FACILITIES ARE COMPATIBLE WITH SURROUNDING LAND USES, ARE APPROPRIATELY SCALED TO THEIR SITES, AND MINIMIZE ADVERSE IMPACTS ON ADJACENT PROPERTIES. WAREHOUSE, MINI-STORAGE FACILITIES SHALL BE PERMITTED IN THE VC ZONING DISTRICT SUBJECT TO APPROVAL OF A SPECIAL USE EXCEPTION AND SITE PLAN APPROVAL, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE APPLICABLE PROVISIONS OF THIS CHAPTER.

MINIMUM BUILDING SETBACKS FOR WAREHOUSE, MINI-STORAGE FACILITIES SHALL BE AS FOLLOWS:
