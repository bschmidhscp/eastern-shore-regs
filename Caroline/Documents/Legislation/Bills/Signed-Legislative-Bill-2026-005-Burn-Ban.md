AN ACT TO CREATE A NEW CHAPTER 124 OF THE CODE OF PUBLIC LOCAL LAWS OF CAROLINE COUNTY, ENTITLED AUTHORIZATION FOR DECLARATION OF AN OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN. Chapter 124 will consist of two Articles. Article I authorizes the County Commissioners to adopt an open-air burning ban in any part of the County, or throughout the County as a whole, during specific drought-related conditions based upon the recommendation of the Caroline County Director of Emergency Services; provides for notice to the public of the adoption of an open-air burning ban; and provides for a right of entry to extinguish a fire started in violation of an open-burning ban. Article II provides for judicial enforcement of Chapter 124 and for civil penalties.

##### § 124-1. DEFINITIONS. 

IN THIS CHAPTER THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED: 

- A. “BURN BARREL” MEANS A METAL CONTAINER USED TO HOLD COMBUSTIBLE OR FLAMMABLE WASTE MATERIALS SO THAT THEY CAN BE IGNITED OUTDOORS FOR THE PURPOSE OF DISPOSAL. 

- B. “BURNING BAN” MEANS A COMPLETE BAN ON ALL OPEN-AIR BURNING, INCLUDING THE BURNING OF HOUSEHOLD TRASH, GARBAGE OR RUBBISH, DECLARED BY THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS. 

- C. “CAMPFIRE” MEANS A SMALL OUTDOOR FIRE INTENDED FOR RECREATION OR COOKING BUT NOT INCLUDING A FIRE

#### INTENDED FOR DISPOSAL OF WASTE WOOD OR REFUSE. 

- D. “FIREWORKS” HAS THE MEANING SET FORTH IN THE PUBLIC SAFETY ARTICLE, TITLE 10, FIREWORKS AND SPARKLERS, § 10101, MD. ANN. CODE, AS AMENDED, MODIFIED, RECODIFIED OR REENACTED FROM TIME TO TIME. 

- E. “GARBAGE” MEANS ANIMAL OR VEGETABLE WASTE RESULTING FROM THE HANDLING, PREPARATION, COOKING AND CONSUMPTION OF FOOD. 

- F. “OPEN-AIR BURNING” MEANS KINDLING OR MAINTAINING A FIRE OF ANY MATERIAL IN THE OPEN WHERE THE PRODUCTS OF COMBUSTION ARE EMITTED INTO THE AMBIENT AIR WITHOUT PASSING THROUGH A STACK OR CHIMNEY, OR IN A RECEPTACLE OTHER THAN A BARBEQUE GRILL, FURNACE, INCINERATOR, OR OTHER EQUIPMENT CONNECTED TO A STACK OR CHIMNEY. OPEN AIR BURNING INCLUDES THE USE OF A BURN BARREL AND THE USE OF FIREWORKS, SPARKLERS, OR SPARKLING DEVICES, WITH THE EXCEPTION OF LAWFULLY PERMITTED MUNICIPAL OR LOCAL GOVERNMENT FIREWORKS DISPLAYS. OPEN-AIR BURNING DOES NOT INCLUDE THE USE OF PROPANE, ACETYLENE, NATURAL GAS, GASOLINE OR KEROSENE IN A DEVICE INTENDED FOR HEATING, CONSTRUCTION OR MAINTENANCE ACTIVITIES. 

- G. “PREMISES” MEANS A LOT, PLOT OR PARCEL OF LAND, INCLUDING THE EXTERIOR THEREON. 

- H. “RUBBISH” MEANS COMBUSTIBLE AND NONCOMBUSTIBLE WASTE MATERIALS, EXCEPT GARBAGE, INCLUDING PAPER, RAGS, CARTONS, BOXES, WOOD PRODUCTS, RUBBER, LEATHER, TIN CANS, METALS, GLASS, CROCKERY AND OTHER SIMILAR MATERIALS. THE TERM SHALL INCLUDE TREATED, PAINTED, STAINED, PRESSURE-TREATED, OR CHEMICALLY TREATED LUMBER AND WOOD PRODUCTS. THE TERM SHALL NOT INCLUDE NATURAL VEGETATIVE MATERIALS, INCLUDING TREE BRANCHES, BRUSH, LEAVES OR YARD TRIMMINGS.

##### OF AN OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN 

- § 124-2. ADOPTION OF AN OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN. 

   - A. AN EMERGENCY OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN SHALL TAKE EFFECT IMMEDIATELY UPON THE WRITTEN AND SIGNED DECLARATION OF SUCH BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, UPON CONSULTATION WITH THE DIRECTOR (OR ASSISTANT DIRECTOR) OF THE DEPARTMENT OF EMERGENCY SERVICES. IN THE PRESIDENT’S ABSENCE, THE VICE-PRESIDENT OR COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR MAY IMPLEMENT THE EMERGENCY OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN. 

   - B. BEFORE SUBMITTING A RECOMMENDATION, THE EMERGENCY SERVICES DIRECTOR SHALL CONTACT THE FIRE CHIEFS’ ASSOCIATION TO ASCERTAIN IF CONDITIONS WARRANT A BAN ON OPEN-AIR BURNING. THE DECLARATION OF AN OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN SHALL BE BASED UPON ATMOSPHERIC CONDITIONS, INCLUDING THE LACK OF APPRECIABLE RAINFALL, WIND CONDITIONS, AND ANY OTHER LOCAL CONDITIONS WHICH MAY BE DEEMED HAZARDOUS. THE DECLARATION MAY IMPOSE AN OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN IN ANY PART OR PARTS OF THE COUNTY OR THROUGHOUT THE COUNTY AS A WHOLE, BASED ON THE AFFIRMATIVE RECOMMENDATION OF THE EMERGENCY SERVICES DIRECTOR THAT PROLONGED OR UNUSUAL CONDITIONS EXIST THAT ARE CONDUCIVE TO THE EASY START AND SPREAD OF FIRE. 

   - C. A DECLARATION OF AN OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN SHALL: 

      - (1) INCLUDE FINDINGS AND DESCRIBE THE CONDITIONS THAT REQUIRE THE OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN; 

      - (2) IDENTIFY THE AREAS OF THE COUNTY IN WHICH THE OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN IS TO TAKE EFFECT; 

      - (3) INCLUDE THE DATES AND TIMES THE OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN IS TO TAKE EFFECT AND TERMINATE; 

      - (4) EXPIRE NO MORE THAN 45 DAYS AFTER THE DATE OF THE DECLARATION, BUT MAY BE EXTENDED OR RE-DECLARED FROM TIME TO TIME AS CONDITIONS WARRANT.

##### §124-4. NEGLIGENTLY CAUSING FIRES. 

- A. AN INDIVIDUAL OR CORPORATION MAY NOT CARELESSLY OR NEGLIGENTLY SET ON FIRE, OR CAUSE TO BE SET ON FIRE ANY WOODS, BRUSH, GRASS, GRAIN, OR STUBBLE IN VIOLATION OF THIS ARTICLE. 

- B. CAUSING A FIRE IN VIOLATION OF AN OPEN-AIR BURNING BAN OR ALLOWING THE FIRE TO ESCAPE TO THE INJURY OF ADJOINING LANDS, IS PRIMA FACIA PROOF OF CARELESSNESS OR NEGLECT WITHIN THE MEANING OF THIS SUBSECTION. 

- C. THE PROVISIONS OF THIS SECTION DO NOT CONTRAVENE OTHER PROVISIONS OF LAW RELATING TO THE LIABILITY FOR FIRES. 

#### §124-5. RIGHT OF ENTRY ON PRIVATE PROPERTY. 

A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER HAVING REASONABLE SUSPICION TO BELIEVE THAT OPEN-AIR BURNING IS OCCURRING IN AN AREA IN WHICH A BURNING BAN IS IN EFFECT MAY ENTER ON THE PUBLIC OR PRIVATE PROPERTY OF ANY PERSON TO EXTINQUISH THE FIRE. A CODE ENFORCEMENT OFFICER HAVING REASONABLE SUSPICION TO BELIEVE THAT OPEN-AIR BURNING IS OCCURRING IN VIOLATION OF A BURNING BAN MAY REQUEST ASSISTANCE FROM LAW ENFORCEMENT OR FIRE COMPANY PERSONNEL, WHO MAY ENTER ON THE SUBJECT PROPERTY TO EXTINGUISH THE FIRE. NOTHING IN THIS SECTION AUTHORIZES ANY LAW ENFORCEMENT OR CODE ENFORCEMENT OFFICER TO ENTER ANY DWELLING, STRUCTURE OR BUILDING.

§124-6. CIVIL PENALTIES; SERVICE OF CITATIONS. 

- A. A VIOLATION OF THIS CHAPTER IS A CLASS A CIVIL INFRACTION, PUNISHABLE BY A FINE OF $500 FOR A FIRST VIOLATION AND $1,000 FOR A SECOND OR SUBSEQUENT VIOLATION. 

- B. IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PROVISIONS OF CHAPTER 92 (“CIVIL INFRACTIONS”) OF THE CODE OF PUBLIC LOCAL LAWS OF CAROLINE COUNTY, A CAROLINE COUNTY CODE ENFORCEMENT OFFICER MAY SERVE A CITATION ON A PERSON WHO IS BELIEVED TO BE VIOLATING THIS ARTICLE. 

- C. PURSUANT TO §11-202 OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT ARTICLE, ANY POLICE OFFICER WHO IS AUTHORIZED TO ENFORCE THE LAWS OF THE STATE OF MARYLAND OR THE LAWS OF CAROLINE COUNTY, MARYLAND ARE AUTHORIZED TO SERVE A CITATION ON A PERSON WHO THE POLICE OFFICER BELIEVES IS VIOLATING THIS CHAPTER, AND THEY SHALL HAVE AUTHORITY TO ORDER ANY PERSON TO IMMEDIATELY CEASE ANY ACT VIOLATING A DECLARED OPEN AIR BURNING BAN OR ANY PROVISION OF THIS CHAPTER.

ALL PERSONS WHO COMMIT, INSPIRE, PARTICIPATE, OR ASSIST ANOTHER TO COMMIT ANY VIOLATION, OR WHO HAVE OWNERSHIP, POSSESSION OR CONTROL OF THE PREMISES ON WHICH ANY VIOLATION OCCURS, OR CUSTODY, SUPERVISION, OR CONTROL OF ANY MINOR WHO COMMITS ANY VIOLATION, SHALL BE JOINTLY AND SEVERALLY RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL SUCH VIOLATIONS.
