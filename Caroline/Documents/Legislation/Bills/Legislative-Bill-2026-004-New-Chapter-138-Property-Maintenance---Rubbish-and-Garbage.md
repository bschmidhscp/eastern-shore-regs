###### CHAPTER 138 

###### PROPERTY MAINTENANCE - RUBBISH AND GARBAGE 

§ 138-1. APPLICABILITY. 

THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS RECOGNIZE THAT CAROLINE IS A RURAL, AGRICULTURAL COUNTY WITH A STRONG TRADITION OF RESPECTING PRIVATE PROPERTY RIGHTS. THE COMMISSIONERS ALSO RECOGNIZE THAT IN DEVELOPED PORTIONS OF THE COUNTY, PROPERTIES ON WHICH RUBBISH AND GARBAGE HAVE ACCUMULATED CAN DIMINISH THE QUIET ENJOYMENT OF OTHER PROPERTIES IN THE VICINITY AND PRESENT A POTENTIAL THREAT TO PUBLIC HEALTH, SAFETY, AND WELFARE. ###### ARTICLE II 

###### RUBBISH AND GARBAGE REGULATIONS 

§ 138-9. ACCUMULATION PROHIBITED. 

EXTERIOR PROPERTY AND PREMISES SHALL BE KEPT FREE FROM ANY ACCUMULATION OF RUBBISH OR GARBAGE. 

§ 138-10. STORAGE REQUIREMENTS. 

- A. RUBBISH SHALL BE STORED IN APPROVED CONTAINERS DESIGNED TO PREVENT DISPERSAL BY WIND, ANIMALS OR OTHER NATURAL ELEMENTS. 

- B. GARBAGE SHALL BE STORED IN APPROVED LEAKPROOF CONTAINERS WITH CLOSE-FITTING COVERS TO PREVENT LEAKAGE, ODORS AND INFESTATION. 

- Cc. THE OWNER OF THE PREMISES SHALL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR PROVIDING APPROVED CONTAINERS AND ENSURING REMOVAL OF RUBBISH AND GARBAGE IN A LAWFUL MANNER.
