# Caroline County — Fee Findings

*Compiled 2026-10-11. Code current through 06-09-2026. FY27 = effective July 1, 2026.*

## Impact fee / excise tax
- Yes. Development impact fee, Ch. 167 (Bill 2018-2, adopted 10-9-2018). It replaced the old school excise tax (Ch. 166 Art. V, repealed 9-25-2018 by Bill 2018-1).
- **Single-family: $5,000 per dwelling, public school construction only** [§ 167-14.A]. Multifamily also $5,000/unit [§ 167-14.B]. Same figure in the FY2027 schedule [Fees: 55_FY2027-Fee-Schedule p.3].
- Age-restricted (55+): school fee waived, but $2,000/unit fire/EMS fee [§ 167-5.C(6)-(7), § 167-14.C].
- No in-town/out-of-town or subarea split: school and fire/EMS service areas are countywide, municipalities included, and the County collects in towns [§ 167-15, § 167-16, § 167-7.B].
- Paid before building permit or zoning certificate; rate fixed on the application date [§ 167-7.A]. Unpaid fees become a lien [§ 167-7.C].
- Exemptions: farm lot transferred from a farmer to a child or grandchild (waived; repaid if sold within 5 years); lots recorded before 12-14-1993 [§ 167-5.C(5), D(2)].
- Also: county transfer tax 0.5% of consideration [§ 166-3].
