# Caroline County, MD: Small-Subdivision Summary

Keys: "PC digest" / "BOA digest" / "Comm digest" = `01. Digests/`. Code is current through 06-09-2026. Statewide septic, Critical Area, BAT and forest rules are in [MD Summary]; only Caroline's local choices are listed here.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Caroline is a minor-subdivision county.** Zoning Administrator approves up to **4 lots**, no preliminary plat, one-step plat [§ 162-13; § 175-38.A]. Fees are low: $375/lot [Fees: FY2027 p.3].
- **Rural land (R district) is capped.** Minor subdivision: not more than 4 lots at 1 lot/acre gross, counted cumulatively from 11-30-1972; a residue under 20 acres counts as a lot [§ 175-38.A; § 162-5]. Plan for 3 new lots plus a small residue.
- **5+ lots in R only in the TDR receiving area** (max 50 lots, 1 right per 15 acres or TDR) [§ 175-38.B; § 175-44.D]. Over 90% of the county is Tier IV, minors only [Draft Plan pdf p.123, p.40]. 8-20 lot rural projects are largely unavailable outside receiving areas, villages and towns.
- **Impact fee is low:** $5,000 per home, schools only, countywide [§ 167-14.A; Fee Findings].
- **Surprise:** PC digest (100 items, 2023-10 to 2026-10) has zero residential subdivision plats; all items are comp plan, site plans (mostly mines), text amendments [PC digest]. Subdivision precedent is **not found in corpus**.
- **Risk:** draft Comprehensive Plan and a following comprehensive rezoning may change density and tiers [Draft Plan pdf p.44, p.123].

## 3. Subdivision process

| | Minor | Major / rural major |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | Up to 4 lots [§ 162-13; § 175-38.A] | 5+ lots [Fees p.3]; rural major 5-50, receiving area only [§ 175-38.B] |
| Decider | Zoning Administrator; appeal to PC [§ 162-13.C, I] | Planning Commission [§ 162-21.A; § 162-22] |
| Stages | Plat only; Health Officer signs water/sewer approval [§ 162-13.A, D] | Sketch (optional), TAC, preliminary (valid 3 yrs), final + improvement plans [§ 162-20 to 22] |

- **Requirements:** frontage on county/state road or 30-ft private right-of-way; dedicate 25 ft from road centerline [§ 162-15]. Right-to-farm covenant in R [§ 162-37]. Interim septic note [§ 162-18.B].
- **APFO:** roads, drainage, schools, fire, solid waste can support denial; paved/50-ft ROW road required for majors [§ 162-38 to 43].
- **Majors:** 5% recreation/open space, fee in lieu possible [§ 162-36].
- **Timing:** application at least 4 weeks before the PC meeting [§ 162-21.A(2)]. Minor timing and approval rates: **not found in corpus** (administrative, not minuted).

## 4. Overlays and constraints

- **Septic tiers:** Tier IV (>90% of county) minors only; Tier III = TDR receiving area and rural villages [Draft Plan pdf p.40, p.123]. Caroline kept its local 4-lot minor definition, no Tier IV exemption [MD Summary §2]. 
- **Critical Area:** 1,000 ft rule; local program **not found in corpus**; state rules [MD Summary §1]. Fees higher ($450/lot) [Fees p.3].
- **Forest conservation:** applies at 40,000 sf; thresholds 50% ag/resource, 25% medium-density residential, 20% high-density [§ 109-3; § 109-8]. State 1:1 rule since 7-1-2026 [MD Summary §1]; local code not yet updated in corpus.
- **Stormwater** (Ch. 158) and E&S (Ch. 103): thresholds not extracted. **Floodplain** Ch. 108 exists. **Groundwater:** lot count can be limited by the CWSP groundwater report [§ 175-38.D].
- **Ag preservation:** TDR program; MALPF/Rural Legacy easement land cannot sell rights [§ 175-44.C(2)]. New Ag Land Preservation Fund (Bill 2025-011, enacted 10-21-2025) targets solar [Comm digest].

## 5. Water and sewer

- **Setting:** county-run water in few places (Jonestown Water $400/yr) [Fees p.7]; towns (Denton, Greensboro, Federalsburg, Ridgely, Preston) run systems [Draft Plan]. Rural lots use well and septic.
- **Plan:** CWSP 1992 with amendments through 11-7-2023; areas S-1, S-3 [CWSP]. Amendments in digest: 4 in 3 years (Federalsburg 2026; Benedictine School community sewer 2024) [Comm digest].
- **Charges:** set by resolution; **not found in corpus** [§ 173-12]. Septic/perc fees: not found; Health Dept. 410-479-8000 [Fee Findings].
- Rural villages to be redesignated Tier II for sewer [Draft Plan pdf p.40].
